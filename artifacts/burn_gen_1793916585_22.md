## 0. Architecture Assumptions and an Up-Front Feasibility Flag

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743 at 400 MHz (ArduPilot default). Timer kernel clock 200 MHz on APB1 and APB2 timers. |
| ESC MCUs | 4× AT32F421 (AM32-class firmware), one per motor. This sets the 48 kHz PWM and decodes DShot. |
| Gate driver | 3-phase half-bridge driver with bootstrap, FD6288Q-class, 1 per motor. |
| Bus | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max. An 8S variant is noted where it matters. |
| MOSFETs | 40 V, 5×6 DFN, 2 in parallel per switch position. That is 12 per motor and 48 total. |
| Rating | **80 A burst per motor**, so 320 A peak bus current with all four at burst. |

> **Feasibility flag.** "Nano" usually means a 20×20 mm mount. You cannot place 48 power FETs, bulk capacitance and 320 A bus copper on 20×20 mm. A single 5×6 DFN occupies about 30 mm² before routing.
>
> The model below therefore uses a **30.5×30.5 mount (≈36×36 mm board)**. Section 3 shows that even that board is burst-limited to seconds.
>
> If 20×20 is a hard requirement, the realistic rating is roughly **35–45 A burst per motor**. Section 3.5 gives the scaling.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At any instant, two legs carry the phase current I_ph:

- **PWM ON:** HS(leg A) → motor → LS(leg B)
- **PWM OFF (freewheel, sync-rect):** LS(leg A) → motor → LS(leg B)

In both states, current flows through exactly **two switch positions in series**. Each position consists of n_p paralleled FETs.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)] , \qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

This corresponds to the typical ×1.5 rise at 125 °C. Check your datasheet's normalized curve.

The effective resistance of one position, including the package/PCB spreading term:

$$R_{pos} = \frac{R_{DS}(T_j)}{n_p} + R_{pkg,trace}$$

### 1.3 RMS current including PWM ripple

The ripple current amplitude is:

$$\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{\phi\phi}\, f_{sw}}$$

The RMS current through the conducting path is then:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

### 1.4 Conduction loss per motor

$$\boxed{P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{pos}}$$

Each of the 6 positions conducts on average 1/3 of the time-weighted loss. So the per-FET average is:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{P_{cond,motor}}{6 n_p}$$
