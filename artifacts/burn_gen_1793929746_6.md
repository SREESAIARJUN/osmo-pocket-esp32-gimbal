## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle with latency compensation

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{0}) \bmod 2\pi,\qquad 
\hat\theta_e = \theta_e + \omega_e\,T_d,\quad T_d = 1.5\,T_s$$

The $1.5\,T_s$ term covers one sample of computation delay plus half a sample of PWM zero-order hold. $\theta_0$ is found by encoder alignment (inject $V_d$ at $\theta_e = 0$, then read the encoder).

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = 
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\hat\theta_e & \sin\hat\theta_e\\ -\sin\hat\theta_e & \cos\hat\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.3 Plant and current PI with decoupling

The PMSM model in the rotor frame is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad 
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

Use the PI law with cross-coupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad 
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

With these feedforward terms each axis reduces to the first-order plant $1/(Ls+R)$. Pole-zero cancellation then gives a closed loop of $\omega_c/(s+\omega_c)$:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c = 2\pi\cdot 1.5\text{ kHz}\;(\le \omega_s/13)$$

Plugging in the assumed motor: $K_{p,i} = 19.8$ V/A and $K_{i,i} = 52{,}800$ V/(A·s).

### 1.4 Voltage limit and SVPWM

Limit the voltage vector to the linear SVPWM region, with d-axis priority:

$$|v| \le V_{max} = \frac{V_{dc}}{\sqrt3}\cdot 0.96$$

$$v_d^{sat} = \text{clamp}(v_d^*, \pm V_{max}),\qquad 
v_q^{sat} = \text{clamp}\!\left(v_q^*, \pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - (v_d^{sat})^2}\right)$$
