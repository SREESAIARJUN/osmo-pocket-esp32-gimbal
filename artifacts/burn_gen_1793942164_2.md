# STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): Electrothermal and DShot Timing Model

## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Assumptions

**Architectural clarification.** ArduPilot running on the STM32H743 does not commutate motors. A production-viable AIO splits the work as follows:

- **STM32H743 (flight controller).** Runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600 on 4 timer channels.
- **4× ESC MCUs** (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). Each one commutates one motor at a 48 kHz PWM rate and returns eRPM telemetry.
- **4× 3-phase gate drivers and 24 MOSFETs.** This is a 6S design.

**Design-defining assumptions.** Replace these with your BOM datasheet values. All equations stay symbolic, so the results rescale directly.

| Symbol | Meaning | Value used |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | Bus voltage (6S, loaded) | 24 V |
| I_ph | Burst phase current per motor | 80 A |
| f_sw | PWM frequency | 48 kHz |
| R_ds25 | MOSFET R_DS(on) at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | 1.5 mΩ (40 V, 3.3×3.3 DFN class) |
| α | R_DS(on) temperature coefficient | 0.0048 /K |
| Q_gs2 + Q_gd | Switching charge | 3 nC + 6 nC |
| Q_g | Total gate charge | 30 nC |
| Q_rr | Body-diode reverse recovery charge | 30 nC |
| C_oss,tr | Effective output capacitance | 600 pF |
| V_drv, V_pl | Gate drive voltage, Miller plateau | 10 V, 3.5 V |
| R_g,tot | External + internal gate resistance | 6 Ω |
| t_dt, V_F | Dead time, body-diode forward drop | 100 ns, 0.8 V |
| Board | Dimensions and copper | 30.5 × 30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz |

**Commutation model.** Six-step (trapezoidal) commutation with synchronous rectification, complementary PWM on the active leg.
