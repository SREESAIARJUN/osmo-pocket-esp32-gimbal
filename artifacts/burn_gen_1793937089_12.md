# STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): Power-Stage, Thermal & Bi-Dir DShot Architecture

## 0. Design Assumptions and Feasibility Flags

**Architectural correction.** The STM32H743 runs the flight-control side (ArduPilot). Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU, for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32. The H743 talks to them over bi-directional DShot600. Everything below uses this split.

**Feasibility warning.** Four channels at an 80 A burst on a nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board is not thermally credible beyond about 1–2 s. Section 3 shows the numbers. Treat 80 A as a short-burst rating with a 30×30 board and a bonded heat spreader, or derate.

| Parameter | Symbol | Assumed value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max (worst case for switching) |
| Phase current (burst) | I_ph | 80 A per channel |
| MOSFET | — | 40 V, 5×6 or 3.3×3 PDFN class |
| Rds(on) at 25 °C / 125 °C | R_ds | 1.5 mΩ / 2.25 mΩ (k_T = 1.5) |
| Turn-on + turn-off time | t_r + t_f | 10 + 10 ns (≈1.5 A gate drive, Q_gd ≈ 10 nC) |
| Body-diode forward voltage / dead time | V_f / t_dt | 0.8 V / 100 ns per edge |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr | 40 nC |
| Effective output capacitance | C_oss,eff | 1.5 nF |
| Total gate charge / gate drive voltage | Q_g / V_gs | 50 nC / 10 V |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| Commutation | — | 6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification (AM32 default) |

All results below are parametric. Substitute your real datasheet values.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

In 6-step drive with complementary PWM, exactly two MOSFETs are in the current path at any instant:
- one high-side (or its synchronous low-side partner during the off-time),
- one low-side in the return leg.

The per-channel conduction loss is therefore:

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2 \cdot R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\ \alpha \approx 0.005/\mathrm{K}$$

**Results at I_ph = 80 A**

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.5 mΩ | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| 125 °C | 2.25 mΩ | **28.8 W** | **115 W** |

Each FET carries current for 1/3 of the electrical period. The electrical frequency is about 3.5 kHz at 30 krpm with 14 poles, which is much faster than the package thermal time constant. The time-averaged loss per FET is therefore approximately P_channel / 6.

**Note on rating basis.** If "80 A" means battery current, then I_ph = I_batt / D. At D = 0.7, I_ph = 114 A and P_cond roughly doubles. Specify which current the rating refers to.

**Electrothermal feedback.** Because R_ds rises with T_j, the steady-state junction temperature satisfies:

$$T_j = T_a + R_{\theta} \cdot I^2 R_{ds,25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

Thermal runaway occurs if

$$R_\theta \cdot I^2 R_{ds,25}\,\alpha \ge 1$$

per FET. Check this condition with your real R_θ.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one leg hard-switches per PWM period: the high-side switches, and the low-side runs complementary in soft/ZVS-like fashion. Losses per channel:

| Mechanism | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw = ½·25.2·80·20 ns·48 k | 0.968 W |
| Body diode during dead time | V_f·I·2·t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·200 ns·48 k | 0.614 W |
| Reverse recovery | V·Q_rr·f_sw = 25.2·40 nC·48 k | 0.048 W |
| C_oss discharge | ½·C_oss·V²·f_sw | 0.023 W |
| Gate drive (2 FETs, in driver IC) | 2·Q_g·V_gs·f_sw | 0.048 W |
| **Total P_sw** | | **≈ 1.70 W** |

**Per-channel total at T_j = 125 °C:** ≈ 30.5 W. **Board total for 4 channels:** ≈ 122 W.

**Design levers**
- **Dead time.** Cutting it to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W per channel. Dead time is the dominant switching term after overlap.
- **Switching frequency.** Moving from 48 to 24 kHz halves P_sw. However, conduction dominates by about 17:1, so f_sw is not the lever that matters here.
- **R_ds(on) is the lever that matters.** Paralleling two FETs per switch halves P_cond.
