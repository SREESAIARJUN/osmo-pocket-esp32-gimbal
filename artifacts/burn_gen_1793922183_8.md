# STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC), Job #60: Power-Stage & Timer Architecture

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 does **not** generate the 48 kHz motor PWM.

- On a 4-in-1 design, the H743 is the flight controller running ArduPilot.
- The H743 sends **bidirectional DShot600** to four independent ESC MCUs. Typical parts are AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32.
- Each ESC MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM and runs commutation.

The analysis is split along that boundary.

**Feasibility warning.** "80 A burst" on a nano form factor (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) is thermally limited to roughly **1–3 seconds** with all four channels loaded. Section 3 derives this. Treat the 80 A figure as a transient rating, never a continuous one.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM).

- In any commutation sector, one phase's low-side FET is held on.
- The PWM'd phase alternates between its HS FET (duty D) and its LS FET (1−D).
- The motor current therefore always flows through **exactly two FETs in series**, independent of D:

$$
P_{cond,ESC} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)] \approx k_T R_{25}
$$

$$
P_{cond,ESC} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.5\,\text{mΩ} = \mathbf{19.2\ W}
$$
