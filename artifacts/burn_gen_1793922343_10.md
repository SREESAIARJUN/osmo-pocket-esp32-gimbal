## 0. Reference Assumptions

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 2–5 mH |
| Encoder | 14-bit magnetic (e.g. MT6701), SPI, sampled in the PWM interrupt |
| PWM / current loop | 20 kHz, center-aligned, 2-shunt or inline current sensing |
| Velocity loop | 4 kHz |
| Attitude / angle loop | 1 kHz |
| IMU | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, ODR 8 kHz (gyro) / 4 kHz (accel) |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 (STM32G4/H7 class), single-precision FPU |

The loop rates are separated by at least 4× in bandwidth, so each inner loop is close to unity gain within the outer loop's bandwidth.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke transform, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of the same rotation matrix.

### 1.2 Plant and PI Design (pole-zero cancellation)

The dq-axis voltage equations, with surface PM so L_d = L_q = L:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

The per-axis plant is G(s) = 1/(Ls + R). Choose a PI controller C(s) = K_p + K_i/s with:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c
$$

The PI zero then cancels the electrical pole, and the closed loop becomes first-order:

$$
\frac{i}{i^*}=\frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

Use ω_c ≈ 2π · 1–2 kHz for a 20 kHz loop. Keep ω_c · T_s ≲ 0.1 so that PWM and sampling delay (≈1.5 T_s) cost less than about 15° of phase.

**Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:**

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

**Delay compensation.** Use θ_e evaluated at the mid-point of the *next* PWM period:

$$
\theta_e^{pwm} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s
$$

**Voltage limiting.** Limit the vector circularly: |v_dq| ≤ V_bus/√3. Give d-axis priority, and give q-axis whatever remains.
