# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model (Job #29)

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In every production "FC + 4-in-1 ESC" stack, the architecture splits as follows:

| Function | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743 (480 MHz, M7) | ArduPilot |
| ESC, one MCU per channel (×4) | AT32F421 / STM32G071 / EFM8-class | AM32 / BLHeli_32 / BLHeli_S |
| 3-phase gate driver (×4) | FD6288Q-class | — |
| Power stage (×4) | 6× N-MOSFET per channel | — |

The **48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs**, not the H743. The H743 only produces **bidirectional DShot600** commands and decodes eRPM telemetry. I model both sides.

> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag.** 80 A per channel on a nano-format board is an **aggressive burst rating**, not a continuous one. The math below shows it is survivable only for a few seconds.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification

At any instant in the electrical cycle, two phases are energized:

- **PWM ON (fraction D):** current flows through the high-side FET of phase A, the motor, then the low-side FET of phase B.
- **PWM OFF (fraction 1−D):** the high-side of A turns off. After the dead time, the complementary low-side of A conducts. Current recirculates through the low-side of A, the motor, and the low-side of B.

In either state, **exactly two FETs carry the phase current I_ph**. This gives:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph,rms}^2 \left[R_{HS}D + R_{LS}(1-D) + R_{LS}\right] \approx 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

This holds when R_HS ≈ R_LS. The loss is **independent of D** with synchronous rectification.

### 1.2 Phase current versus battery current

$$
I_{ph} \approx \frac{I_{batt}}{D} \quad \text{(continuous conduction, lossless approximation)}
$$

An 80 A rating is normally a battery-side figure. At full throttle (D → 0.95–0.98), I_ph ≈ I_batt. At part throttle the phase current exceeds the battery current. **Worst-case conduction loss therefore occurs at high phase current with D < 1, not at the battery-current peak.** Size the design on phase current.

### 1.3 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

This gives a factor of 1.5 at 125 °C. Add the PCB copper and solder path:

$$
R_{path} = 2R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{Cu} + R_{shunt}
$$

Example values: R_Cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ per channel for 2 oz pours on short runs. R_shunt = 0.5 mΩ if per-phase sensing is used; AM32 often uses a single bus shunt.
