# STM32H743 AIO / 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Plan, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Assumptions and Corrections

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates DShot600. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32), and that MCU produces the 48 kHz PWM. The two timing domains are independent:

- DShot600 runs at about 8 kHz command rate on the FC side.
- The 48 kHz PWM is local to each ESC MCU.
