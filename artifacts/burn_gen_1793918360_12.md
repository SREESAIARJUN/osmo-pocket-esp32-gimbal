## 0. Architecture Clarification and Design Flags

Four points must be settled before the math, because they change the results.

| # | Issue | Resolution adopted in this document |
|---|-------|-------------------------------------|
| F1 | The H743 cannot run the 48 kHz bridge commutation for four motors under ArduPilot. ArduPilot has no native FOC or six-step bridge driver. | The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600. Four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421, running AM32) generate the 48 kHz PWM. 48 kHz is an AM32 setting, not an H743 timer. |
| F2 | "80 A burst" on all four channels at once means about 320 A from the pack. Nano-class motors cannot draw this. | 80 A is modelled as a **single-channel burst ≤ 1 s**. The four-channel worst case is computed only as a bounding envelope. |
| F3 | A 20 × 20 mm mount (≈25 × 25 mm board) cannot sink 4 × 80 A. | The board is thermally capacitance-limited. Section 3 quantifies the allowed burst duration
