# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage and DShot Engineering Model

This is a model-grade reference design. The equations are general. The numbers depend on the stated assumptions, especially MOSFET Rds(on), gate drive strength, and copper stackup. Before release, replace them with your chosen components' datasheet values and validate on a bench with a thermocouple or IR camera.

---

## 0. Architecture Corrections (Read First)

**1. The H743 does not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM.**
- In every production 4-in-1 design, each motor bridge is commutated by its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32.
  - Typical choices are the AT32F421 or STM32G071, one per motor or one per pair.
- The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to those MCUs.
- Commutating 4 sensorless BLDC motors at 48 kHz from the same core that runs the EKF3 is not a supported ArduPilot configuration.

**2. "80 A burst" must be per motor, short duration, and not simultaneous on all 4 motors.**
- 4 × 80 A = 320 A from a nano-format (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board is not physically achievable.
- The XT60 or solder pads, the battery leads, and the board's copper and thermal mass all rule it out.
- The model below shows why and quantifies the real limit.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent Rds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_ds = 1.5 × (1 + 0.006·75) = **2.175 mΩ**

### 2.2 Bridge current paths (one 60° commutation sector)

- **PWM phase, high-side (HS):** conducts I for duty D.
- **PWM phase, low-side (LS, synchronous rectification):** conducts I for (1 − D), minus dead time.
- **Return phase, LS:** conducts I continuously for the sector.
