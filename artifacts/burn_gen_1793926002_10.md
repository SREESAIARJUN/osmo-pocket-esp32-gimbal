# Job #48: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (80 A Burst, 48 kHz, Bidirectional DShot600)

**Scope:** Electro-thermal loss model, ArduPilot DMA/timer allocation, and reference netlists.

---

## 0. Scope Corrections and Assumptions

Several premises in the brief need to be stated precisely before any math is meaningful.

1. **Two processor domains.** ArduPilot on the STM32H743 does not commutate MOSFETs. The H743 runs the flight stack and emits bidirectional DShot600. Each motor channel (or a shared dual-core part) runs ESC firmware such as AM32 or BLHeli_32 on a dedicated MCU, which generates the 48 kHz PWM. This document models both domains.
2. **"80 A burst" is interpreted as 80 A phase current per motor, simultaneously on all four channels.** That is the worst case. At full duty it implies roughly 320 A from the battery. No XT60 or 12 AWG lead supports that for more than a moment. Treat the 4×80 A case as a **sub-5-second envelope**, not a rating.
3. **Placeholder component parameters.** Final MOSFET and driver part numbers were not supplied. I use a representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PDFN
