## 0. Assumptions and Interpretation

The brief does not name a MOSFET, gate driver, board size, or battery. These parameters change the results directly, so I state them up front. Every equation is parametric, so you can substitute datasheet values.

**Architecture interpretation.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. The 4-in-1 ESC uses four dedicated ESC MCUs, one per channel, running AM32 or BLHeli_32. A standard 4-in-1 does not commutate motors from the H743. The H743 drives the ESCs over bidirectional DShot600.
