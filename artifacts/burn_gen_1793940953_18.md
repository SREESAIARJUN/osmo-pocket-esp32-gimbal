## 1. System Architecture

```
            6S LiPo (25.2V max)
                │
   ┌────────────┴─────────────┐
   │  4-in-1 ESC (bottom half)│   4× AT32F421K8U7 running AM32 (or BLHeli_32-class)
   │  24 N-FETs (12 duals)    │   PWM 48 kHz, complementary w/ dead-time
   │  4× FD6288Q gate drivers │   Bidirectional DShot600 input
   └────────────┬─────────────┘
                │ 4× DShot signal + GND + VBAT + CURR
   ┌────────────┴─────────────┐
   │ STM32H743VIT6 (FC half)  │   ArduPilot, 480 MHz
   │ TIM1 CH1–4 → Motors 1–4  │   DMAMUX-routed bursts + input capture
   └──────────────────────────┘
```

The H743 does **not** commutate motors directly. Each channel's 48 kHz PWM is generated by its local ESC MCU. This is the standard and recommended partitioning, because it keeps the FC's timing jitter out of commutation.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| R_DS,25 (per FET, V_GS = 10V) | 1.5 mΩ |
| T_j design point | 110 °C |
| R_DS(110 °C) | 1.5 × 1.51 = **2.27 mΩ** |

### 2.2 Six-step (trapezoidal) commutation with synchronous rectification

- At any instant, exactly **two FET channels** carry phase current. During the PWM-on interval these are the HS of phase x and the LS of phase y. During PWM-off, the complementary LS of phase x carries current instead.
- With complementary PWM, the current path always contains two R_DS(on) elements, apart from dead-time.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

At full throttle (D → 1), the phase current approaches the battery current. At partial throttle the phase current is higher than the battery current:

$$I_{ph} \approx I_{bat}/D$$

Each of the 6 FETs conducts about 1/3 of the electrical period:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 2.3 Results

| Case | I_ph | P_cond per channel | P_cond per FET | P_cond, 4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: 20 A/ch | 20 A | 2·400·2.27m = **1.82 W** | 0.30 W | **7.3 W** |
| B: 80 A/ch | 80 A | 2·6400·2.27m = **29.1 W** | 4.84 W | **116 W** |

### 2.4 Additional I²R loss terms (Case B, per channel unless noted)

| Source | Estimate | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| PCB copper, 2 oz, ~0.3 mΩ path | 80² · 0.3m | 1.9 W |
| Current shunt, 0.25 mΩ, total current | 80² · 0.25m (Case A, total) | 1.6 W |
| Bulk cap ESR | I_cap,rms ≈ I·√(D(1−D)) = 40 A at D = 0.5 | see §4 |

## 3. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Parameters: V_bus = 25.2V, t_r + t_f ≈ 40 ns (FD6288Q, R_G ≈ 10 Ω), Q_rr ≈ 40 nC, C_oss ≈ 1 nF, Q_g ≈ 30 nC, t_dt ≈ 100 ns, V_SD ≈ 0.8V.

Only **one leg** is PWM-switched per commutation sector. That leg's HS switches hard, and its LS acts as a synchronous rectifier.

| Loss term | Formula | 20 A | 80 A |
|---|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap (HS) | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f | 0.48 W | **1.94 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f | 0.048 W | 0.048 W |
| C_oss discharge | ½·C_oss·V
