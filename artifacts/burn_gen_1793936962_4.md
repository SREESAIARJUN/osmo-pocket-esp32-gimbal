## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform**, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park** is the transpose of the rotation above.

### 1.2 Plant and Current PI Design

The PMSM voltage equations in the rotor frame are:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

Apply cross-coupling decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$
v_d = \text{PI}_d(e_d) - \omega_e L\, i_q,\qquad
v_q = \text{PI}_q(e_q) + \omega_e (L\, i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

Each axis is then the plant 1/(Ls + R). Pole-zero cancellation gives:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c
\;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \frac{I(s)}{I^*(s)} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

The setpoint is i_d* = 0 (surface PMSM, no field weakening needed).
