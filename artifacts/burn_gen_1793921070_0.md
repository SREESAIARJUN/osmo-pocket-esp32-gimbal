# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4‑in‑1 ESC (Job #25): Architecture, Loss Model, BDShot DMA Plan, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 should be the flight controller, not the ESC controller. Each ESC channel runs on its own small MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Reasons:

- Sensorless 6‑step commutation needs about 4 timer/comparator resources per motor.
- Four motors on the H743 would consume its advanced timers.
- Running commutation on the H743 would couple motor commutation faults to attitude control.

The model below therefore covers:

- **(a)** the power stage, per channel
- **(b)** the FC↔ESC DShot600 bidirectional link, driven by the H743 under ArduPilot
