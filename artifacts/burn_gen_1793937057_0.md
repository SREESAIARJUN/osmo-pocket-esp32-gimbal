# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #24): System Architecture & Mathematical Model

## 0. Assumptions and Corrections

Four points need settling before the math, because they change the results.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In an AIO design the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot, and the ESC side uses dedicated per-channel MCUs running AM32. This design assumes 4× AT32F421 + FD6288Q gate drivers. The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600. One H743 doing sensorless FOC/6-step for 4 motors plus the flight stack is possible in principle, but ArduPilot does not support it.
2. **"80 A burst" is interpreted as 80 A per channel.** Section 3 shows this is only survivable for about 1–2 s on a 20×20 board. If you meant 80 A total, divide the conduction numbers by 16 (I² scaling at 20 A per channel).
3. **No MOSFET was specified.** I use a representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PDFN part. Every result is written symbolically so you can substitute datasheet values.
4. **Supply is 6S.** V_bus,max = 25.2 V, so 40 V FETs are used.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Topology

In 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM, there are always two FETs in the current path:

- **PWM leg:** the high side conducts for D·T. The low side conducts for (1−D)·T (synchronous rectification).
- **Return leg:** the low side conducts the full period.

So the instantaneous path resistance is always 2·R_DS(on), independent of duty:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]
$$

Because R rises with the loss it causes, the loss and junction temperature must be solved together. In steady state (T_ref is the PCB/case reference temperature):

$$
T_j = T_{ref} + P_{FET}\,R_{\theta,j\text{-}ref}, \qquad
P_{FET} = \tfrac{1}{3}\, I_{ph}^2 R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

The 1/3 factor arises because each FET carries current for one-third of the electrical cycle on average. Over six commutation states, 2 of 6 FETs are conducting, and the total loss is shared across 6 FETs: P_FET = P_cond,ch / 6 = (2/6)·I²R.

Closed form (valid while the denominator is greater than 0, i.e. no thermal runaway):

$$
T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + k\,R_{\theta}\,(1 - 25\alpha)}{1 - k\,R_{\theta}\,\alpha}, \qquad k = \tfrac{1}{3} I_{ph}^2 R_{25}
$$

### 1.3 Numbers

k = (1/3)(6400)(1.2 mΩ) = **2.56 W** per FET at 25 °C.

With R_θ,j-pcb = 1.5 K/W and T_pcb = 90 °C (late in a burst):

$$
T_j = \frac{90 + 2.56\cdot1.5\cdot(1-0.1125)}{1 - 2.56\cdot1.5\cdot0.0045} = \frac{93.41}{0.9827} \approx 95.1\ ^\circ\text{C}
$$

R_DS(on)(95 °C) = 1.2 × (1 + 0.0045 × 70) = **1.58 mΩ**
