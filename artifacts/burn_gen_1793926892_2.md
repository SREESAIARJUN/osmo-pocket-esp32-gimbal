# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC (Nano Stack): Design Package, Job #41

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

The STM32H743 cannot drive the bridges directly. It runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. The 4-in-1 ESC needs its own commutation MCUs, either 4× STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32, or one multi-motor MCU. The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600 on four signal lines. All models below follow this split.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
In 6-step synchronous mode, two FETs are always in series with the motor current:
- the PWM leg, where the high side (HS) conducts for duty D and the low side (LS) of the same leg for 1−D;
- one continuously-on LS (or HS) of the return leg.

### 1.2 RMS current including ripple
The phase-current ripple is:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{ph} f_{sw}}, \qquad I_{rms} = \sqrt{I_{dc}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}}$$

For L_ph = 10 µH and D = 0.5:
- ΔI_pp = 25.2 × 0.25 / (10 µ × 48 k) = 13.1 A.
- I_rms = √(80² + 13.1²/12) = **80.09 A**, so ripple is negligible at burst.

### 1.3 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)] = 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega \times [1 + 0.0055 \times 85] = 2.20\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.4 Conduction loss per motor

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R(T_j) = 80.09^2 \times 2 \times 2.20\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{28.2\ W}$$

The instantaneous loss in the single worst-case FET (one in the conduction path) is 80² × 2.20 m = **14.1 W**. This value sizes the junction transient in §3.3.

**Thermal-runaway check.** R(T_j) depends on the loss it produces, so solve the coupled system iteratively:

$$T_j = T_c + P_{FET}(T_j)\, R_{\theta JC}$$

The loop converges if:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\, R_{\theta JC} = I^2 R_{25}\, \alpha\, R_{\theta JC} = 6400 \times 1.5\text{ m} \times 0.0055 \times 1.2 = 0.063 \ll 1$$

This is stable.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. The terms below are per motor.

| Loss term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 20 n × 48 k | **0.968 W** |
| C_oss (HS + LS) | 2 × ½ · C_oss · V² · f_sw = 1.5 n × 635 × 48 k | 0.046 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw = 40 n × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.048 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f · I · 2t_dt · f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 200 n × 48 k | 0.614 W |
| Gate drive (driver dissipation) | 2 · Q_g · V_GS · f_sw = 2 × 50 n × 10 × 48 k | 0.048 W |
| **Total switching-related loss** | | **1.72 W** |

**Totals at burst:**
- Per motor: 28.2 + 1.72 = **29.9 W**.
- Four motors: **119.7 W**.
- Bus copper and shunts add roughly 3–6 W more, depending on layout.

**Sensitivity.** Overlap loss scales linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz the switching-related loss drops to about 0.9 W per motor. Conduction loss dominates at about 94% of the total. Lower R_DS(on), or paralleled FETs, is therefore the effective lever. Lowering PWM frequency is not.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Network (Cauer, lumped)

```
P_FET ─[R_θJC 1.2K/W]─ T_case ─[R_spread ≈ 0.8K/W per FET]─ T_board(C_b) ─[R_θBA]─ T_amb
                          │                                   │
                       C_j (~0.02 J/K)                     C_board
```

### 3.2 Board thermal capacitance
Assumes 6 layers, 2 oz (70 µm) copper, ~70% copper fill.

| Board | Copper mass | C_Cu | + FR-4 / FET / cap mass | **C_board** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20×20 mm "nano" | ≈1.05 g | 0.40 J/K | +1.0 J/K | **≈1.4 J/K** |
| 30.5×30.5 mm | ≈2.4 g | 0.94 J/K | +2.0 J/K | **≈2.9 J/K** |

Steady-state board-to-air resistance R_θBA in prop wash (~10 m/s, h ≈ 60 W/m²K, two faces):
- 20×20: ≈ 21 K/W
- 30×30: ≈ 9 K/W

### 3.3 Burst duration limit
The board time constant τ = R_θBA·C_b is about 26–29 s. Bursts are therefore near-adiabatic:

$$t_{burst} \approx \frac{C_b\,(T_{b,max} - T_{b,0})}{P_{tot}}$$

**Inputs:**
- T_b,max = 110 − ΔT_jc − ΔT_spread.
- ΔT_jc + ΔT_spread for the worst FET ≈ 14.1 W × (1.2 + 0.8) ≈ 28 K, giving T_b,max ≈ 82 °C.
- T_b,0 = 45 °C at cruise.
