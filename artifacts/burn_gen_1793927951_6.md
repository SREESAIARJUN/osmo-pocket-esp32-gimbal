## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz, Ts_i = 50 µs)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle from mechanical angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Clarke transform (amplitude invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

Torque:

$$\tau = K_t\, i_q,\qquad K_t = \tfrac{3}{2} p \lambda_m$$

### 1.3 PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$v_d^* = PI_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q^* = PI_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

Pole-zero cancellation places the closed loop at a first-order response with bandwidth ω_ci:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}$$

Choose ω_ci = 2π·1.5 kHz, which is below f_pwm/10.
