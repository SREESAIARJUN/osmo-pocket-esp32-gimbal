# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4‑in‑1 ESC (Nano) — Loss, Thermal and Bidirectional DShot Model

## 0. Scope, Architecture Correction, and Key Finding

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 talks to those four MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. The design is therefore split into three parts:

1. Power stage (power loss model)
2. ESC MCU and gate drivers
3. H743 timer/DMA (signalling)

**Key finding.** On a nano board (20×20 mm mounting pattern, roughly 26×26 mm PCB), "80 A burst" can only be a **time-limited** rating:

- My model gives about **2–3 s at 80 A/channel**, all four channels loaded.
- It gives about **15–20 A/channel continuous** in strong prop wash.

Section 3 derives these numbers. Treat the label as a burst figure with a stated duration, not a capability.

**Assumptions.** All device parameters below are representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PQFN values. Replace them with the datasheet numbers of the parts you actually select and re-run the equations.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Commutation model.** The model assumes six-step BLDC commutation with synchronous (complementary) PWM, which is the AM32/BLHeli default.

- At any instant, two phase legs carry the full phase current I.
- The PWM leg alternates current between its high-side FET (for duty D) and low-side FET (for 1−D). Its loss is I²R·D + I²R·(1−D) = I²R, independent of duty.
- The non-PWM leg's low-side FET is held fully on, so its loss is also I²R.

**Temperature-dependent resistance:**

$$R_{DS}(T_j)=R_0\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

**Conduction loss per ESC channel:**

$$P_{cond}=2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)+I^2 R_{shunt}+I^2 R_{Cu}$$

**80 A case at T_j = 115 °C:**

- R_DS = 1.0 mΩ × 1.405 = 1.405 mΩ
- FET conduction: 2 × 6400 × 1.405 mΩ = **17.98 W**
- Shunt (0.25 mΩ): 1.60 W
- Phase copper (≈0.3 mΩ, 2 oz, short pours): 1.92 W
- **P_cond ≈ 21.5 W per channel**

**Electrothermal coupling.** R_DS depends on T_j, so iterate until it converges:

$$T_j^{(k+1)} = T_b + P_{FET}(T_j^{(k)})\,(R_{\theta jc}+R_{\theta cb})$$

This converges in 3–4 iterations as long as α·P·R_θ < 1. If that product reaches 1, the device is in **thermal runaway**. Check this condition explicitly.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

**Which FETs hard-switch.** Only the PWM leg switches. Its high-side FET hard-switches at both edges; its low-side FET soft-switches after the body diode has conducted through the dead time.

$$t_{r}\approx t_{f}\approx \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_g}=\frac{15\,\text{nC}}{0.5\,\text{A}}=30\ \text{ns}$$
