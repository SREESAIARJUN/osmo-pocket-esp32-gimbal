# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Design Basis (Job #53)

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Feasibility Flags

Three issues in the brief need to be resolved before the math is meaningful.

**1. The H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.**
- **What the H743 does:** It runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600.
- **What switches the MOSFETs:** Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example an STM32G071 or AT32F421. That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM, commutation, and dead-time.
- **Consequence:** The 48 kHz switching model below describes the ESC power stage. The DShot model describes the H743.

**2. "80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 mm) board is a marketing figure, not a design figure.**
- Section 3 shows the board heats at roughly 50 K/s at 4×80 A.
- 80 A is therefore valid only as a burst of about 1 s or less, with a thermal derate in firmware.
- Specify it that way: **80 A / 1 s burst, about 35–40 A continuous with airflow**. Otherwise the datasheet claim is indefensible.

**3. The device parameters below are labeled design assumptions, not vendor-verified values.**
- Replace them with the selected MOSFET datasheet values before sign-off.
- The equations are general. The numbers are a worked example.

## 2. Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Losses

In 6-step operation with synchronous PWM, two legs are active at any instant:

- **PWM leg:** The high-side FET conducts for D and its complementary low-side FET for (1−D). Current always flows through exactly one FET in this leg.
- **Sink leg:** One low-side FET conducts continuously.

So, independent of duty:

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

Include the ripple contribution:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}$$

For a typical 5″ motor (L_ph ≈ 15 µH) at mid-duty, ΔI ≈ 8 A. The ripple term is then ≈5 A², which is negligible against 6400 A².

**At T_j = 100 °C:**

$$R_{ds} = 1.0\,(1+0.0045\cdot 75) = 1.34\text{ mΩ}$$
