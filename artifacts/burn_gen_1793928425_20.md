## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]$$

| T_j | R_DS |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 0.80 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.07 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.16 mΩ |

### 1.2 Six-step current paths with complementary PWM

During any commutation sector, three devices carry current:

| Device | Role | Conducts for fraction of PWM period |
|---|---|---|
| Q_HS (PWM leg) | Switching | D |
| Q_LS (PWM leg) | Synchronous rectifier | 1 − D |
| Q_LS (return leg) | Static on | 1 |

Summing the conduction losses:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}\left[D + (1-D) + 1\right] = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

This result is **independent of duty cycle**, which is a useful design property.

Over one electrical revolution the loss rotates through all 6 FETs. At typical eRPM, the electrical period (well under 1 ms) is far shorter than the FET thermal time constant (tens of ms or more). The time-averaged loss per FET is therefore:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{2 I^2 R_{DS}}{6} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}}{3}$$

**At 80 A** (I² = 6400 A²):

| T_j | P_cond per channel | P_cond per FET |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 10.24 W | 1.71 W |
| 100 °C | 13.70 W | 2.28 W |
| 125 °C | 14.85 W | 2.48 W |

**Low-RPM caveat.** At very low eRPM, such as during startup or stall, averaging fails. The static low-side FET then sees the full I²R_DS (5.1–7.4 W) for an entire sector. That is the true worst case for the junction and is covered in §3.3.
