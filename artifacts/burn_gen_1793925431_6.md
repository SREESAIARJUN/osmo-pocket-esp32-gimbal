## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture split

An STM32H743 cannot drive 12 half-bridges with 48 kHz commutation and also run ArduPilot. The system is therefore a two-board (or AIO two-domain) design:

| Domain | MCU | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) at 400 MHz | ArduPilot | Attitude control; sends DShot600 and receives bidirectional eRPM |
| ESC ×4 | STM32G071 / AT32F421-class | AM32 (or BLHeli_32-class) | Commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path

In trapezoidal six-step drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM, two phase legs carry current at all times:
- One leg is PWM-switched, alternating high-side and low-side.
- One leg has its low-side MOSFET held on.

Because the switched leg uses synchronous rectification, the current always flows through exactly **two MOSFET channels in series**, independent of duty cycle D:

$$
P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot \left[2R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{pcb}\right]
$$

With a quasi-flat trapezoidal current during the burst, I_rms ≈ I_pk = 80 A.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]
$$

| T_j | R_DS | 2R_DS + R_pcb | P_cond at 80 A |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 2.30 mΩ | 14.7 W |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ | 2.98 mΩ | 19.0 W |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 3.20 mΩ | **20.5 W** |

### 1.3 Electro-thermal feedback (stability check)

Losses raise T_j, and higher T_j raises R_DS. The system is self-consistent only if the loop gain is below 1:

$$
\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\cdot R_{th,ja} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{25}\alpha \cdot R_{th,ja} < 1
$$

Per MOSFET, ∂P/∂T = 80² × 1.0 mΩ × 0.0045 = 0.0288 W/K. The runaway condition is therefore R_th,ja > 34.7 K/W per device. With R_th,ja ≈ 15–25 K/W on a 2 oz multilayer board, the design is **stable but sensitive**. Iterate the operating point numerically:

```
T_j(n+1) = T_amb + R_th · P(T_j(n))   # converges if loop gain < 1
```

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-switched leg contributes switching loss. That leg has one hard-switched high-side FET and one synchronous low-side FET.

### 2.1 Hard-switching (V–I overlap)

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = \tfrac12(25.2)(80)(30\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz}) = \mathbf{1.45\ W}
