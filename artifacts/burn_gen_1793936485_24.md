## 0. Architecture Clarification (Read First)

A literal reading of "STM32H743 4-in-1 ESC" implies the H743 commutates all four motors itself. **That is not feasible, and this design does not attempt it.**

| Constraint | H743 capability | Requirement for 4 motors |
|---|---|---|
| Complementary PWM with hardware dead time | TIM1 and TIM8: 3 CH/CHN pairs each, so 6 pairs total | 12 half-bridges |
| HRTIM | 5 units, 10 outputs | 24 gate signals |
| BEMF comparators | 2 (COMP1/2) | 12, or ADC-based zero-cross on 12 phases |

**Adopted architecture.** The design is a two-tier All-In-One (AIO) board:

- **Tier 1 (flight controller):** an STM32H743VIT6 running ArduPilot sends bidirectional DShot600 to the ESCs.
- **Tier 2 (ESC):** four AT32F421K8U7 MCUs running AM32. Each drives one FD6288Q 3-phase gate driver and 6 N-channel MOSFETs.

This is how every production H7 AIO and stack is built.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation Topology

AM32 uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM on the active high side. In each 60° electrical sector:

- Phase A high side is PWM'd at duty D, with its low-side complement conducting for (1 − D).
- Phase B low side is held fully on (static).
- Phase C floats (BEMF sense).

Each FET is active for 120° (1/3) of the electrical period.

### 1.2 Per-FET RMS Currents

With motor phase current I (ripple neglected, since L_phase·f_sw ≫ R):

```
I_HS,rms  = I · sqrt(D/3)
I_LS,sync = I · sqrt((1-D)/3)                 (synchronous complement, same leg)
I_LS,stat = I · sqrt(1/3)                     (static low side, other leg)
```

Because each leg spends one 120° window as "active high" and one as "static low", the low-side FET of a leg carries:

```
I_LS,rms = I · sqrt( (1-D)/3 + 1/3 )
```

### 1.3 Temperature-Dependent Resistance

```
R_DS(T_j) = R_DS,25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)],   α ≈ 0.0059 /K  →  k_T(110 °C) ≈ 1.5
```

### 1.4 Conduction Loss per Motor

```
P_cond = I² · R_DS(T_j) · [ D + (1-D) + 1 ] = 2 · I² · R_DS(T_j)
```

The result is independent of D. At any instant exactly two FET channels carry I.

**Numeric (80 A, T_j = 110 °C):**

| Case | R_DS(T_j) | P_cond / motor |
|---|---|---|
| Typical | 1.0 × 1.5 = 1.5 mΩ | 2 × 6400 × 1.5e-3 = **19.2 W** |
| Max | 1.3 × 1.5 = 1.95 mΩ | **25.0 W** |

**Parasitic copper and shunt (per motor):**

```
R_path ≈ 0.40 mΩ (bus planes + phase pads, 2 oz) → I²R = 2.56 W
R_shunt = 0.25 mΩ (DC link, sees I·D avg; at D≈0.95 burst ≈ I) → 1.60 W
```

**Total conduction per motor (typical): 23.4 W.**

**Per-FET average (typical):**

- HS: 6400 × 1.5e-3 × D/3 ≈ 3.04 W at D = 0.95
- LS: 6400 × 1.5e-3 × (1.05/3) ≈ 3.36 W
