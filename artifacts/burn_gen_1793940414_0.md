## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumed Parameters

**Topology correction.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does **not** commutate the motors. A 4-in-1 ESC needs its own commutation MCU(s), typically:

- 4× AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32, or
- 1–2× EFM8BB51 running BlueJay.

These receive DShot600 from the H743 and return eRPM telemetry over the same wire (bi-directional DShot).

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + k\,(T_j - 25)\right],\qquad k \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.2 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.8 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current paths

**6-step trapezoidal, complementary (synchronous) PWM:**
- At any instant, current flows through one high-side path and one low-side path in series.
- The chopping leg alternates HS/LS at duty D. Both conduct the full current I, because synchronous rectification puts the LS FET in place of the diode.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

**Sinusoidal/FOC** gives a similar result: 3 legs × I_rms,phase² × R_DS ≈ 1.5·I_pk²·R_DS, which is slightly lower.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_DS | Per conducting FET | **Per ESC channel** | All 4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.2 mΩ | 7.68 W | 15.4 W | 61 W |
| 125 °C | 1.8 mΩ | 11.52 W | **23.0 W** | **92 W** |

### 1.4 Thermal runaway check

Loss rises with temperature, which raises temperature further. The loop is stable only if:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}\,R_{th,ja} < 1 \;\Rightarrow\; 2I^2 R_{25}k\,R_{th,ja} < 1$$

At 80 A: 2 × 6400 × 0.0012 × 0.005 × R_th < 1, so **R_th,ja < 13 K/W per channel**.

A nano board in steady state is around 15–25 K/W per channel. **80 A is therefore not a continuous rating. It is strictly a transient (burst) rating.**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Hard-switching overlap loss (the chopping leg's HS FET)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

### 2.2 Output capacitance loss

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss}V_{bus}^2 f_{sw}\times 2 \approx 0.5 \times 1.5\text{n} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} \times 2 = \mathbf{0.046\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery (LS body diode recovers when HS turns on)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 30\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.036\ W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_F\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

This term is significant. AM32's dead-time setting (≈ 20–60 ticks, depending on MCU clock) should be minimized to whatever the scope-verified shoot-through margin allows.

### 2.5 Gate drive (taken from the driver/LDO, not dissipated in the FET)

$$P_g = Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} \times 6 = 50\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} \times 6 = 0.144\ \text{W per channel}$$

### 2.6 Total per channel at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

| Term | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction | 23.0 |
| Switching overlap | 0.97 |
| Dead time | 0.61 |
| C_oss + Q_rr | 0.08 |
| **Total (FETs)** | **≈ 24.7 W** |
| **×4 channels** | **≈ 99 W** |

**Conclusion:** at this current, switching is under 7% of the total. Raising f_sw from 24 to 48 kHz costs only ~1.6 W/channel, but it buys smoother low-throttle control and reduced current ripple. **R_DS(on) is the design lever. f_sw is not.**

## 3. Transient Thermal Model (Burst Duration)

### 3.1 Lumped Foster network per FET

$$Z_{th}(t) = \sum_{i=1}^{n} R_i\left(1 - e^{-t/\tau_i}\right)$$

Use the datasheet's Z_th(j-c) curve for the die. Add the board stage as an RC pair, then board-to-air convection under prop wash.

### 3.2 Board-level heat capacity (dominant beyond ~100 ms)

For a 30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz board of ~6 g, with c_p ≈ 0.6 J/g·K (mixed FR4/Cu): C_board ≈ 3.6 J/K, plus ~0.5 J/K from components. **C ≈ 4 J/K.**

Neglecting convection (conservative):

$$\frac{dT}{dt} = \frac{P_{tot}}{C}$$

| Scenario | P | dT/dt | Time from 40 °C → 110 °C board |
|---|---|---|---|
| All 4 ch @ 80 A | 99 W | ~25 K/s | **≈ 2.8 s** |
| 1 ch @ 80 A, 3 @ 30 A | ~38 W | ~9.5 K/s | ≈ 7 s |
| 4 ch @ 40 A (cont.) | ~22 W | ~5.5 K/s | Convection-limited |

**Steady state with prop-wash convection.** Assume h ≈ 60 W/m²K over ~2×9.3 cm² of area, so R_th,ba ≈ 9 K/W for the whole board. That gives a sustainable dissipation of about 70 K / 9 K/W ≈ **8 W total**, or roughly **~20–25 A continuous per channel**.

### 3.3 Junction rise above the board

$$\Delta T_{jb} = P_{FET} \times R_{th,jb} \approx 11.5\ \text{W} \times 2.5\ \text{K/W} \approx 29\ \text{K}$$

Keep T_board below ~120 °C so that T_j stays at or below 150 °C.

### 3.4 Specification recommendation

Rate the product **"80 A burst (≤ 2 s, single-channel-dominant), 45–55 A for 10 s, ~25 A continuous per channel."** An unqualified "80A ×4" would invite field failures. Additionally:

- Enable ESC temperature telemetry and ArduPilot `MOT_` current limiting / `BATT_` limits.
- Enable the AM32 current limit, using one shunt per ESC channel.

## 4. ArduPilot Bi-Directional DShot600 Timing and DMA Allocation

### 4.1 DShot600 frame math

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Bit rate | 600 kbit/s |
| Bit period | 1.667 µs |
| Frame (16 bits) | 26.7 µs |
| Bit encoding | ≈ 37.5% duty = "0", ≈ 75% duty = "1" |
| Telemetry response (GCR, 21 bits @ 750 kbit/s = 5/4 × bitrate) | ≈ 28 µs |
| Turnaround gap before response | ≈ 30 µs |
| Full bidir cycle | ≈ 85–90 µs |

**Max loop rate.** Bidir cycles fit comfortably within ArduPilot's 400 Hz–2 kHz output rates. `SCHED_LOOP_RATE` at 1–2 kHz is feasible on the H743.

**Timer clock.** The H743 runs at 400 MHz (480 MHz on rev V), with APB timer clocks of 200/240 MHz. ArduPilot computes the prescaler from `DSHOT_BIT_WIDTH_TICKS` (≈ 20 ticks/bit → ~12 MHz timer tick). The small rounding error is within DShot tolerance.

### 4.2 DMA requirements per timer

Bidir DShot on ArduPilot needs two DMA resources per timer group:
1. **TIMx_UP** DMA: burst output via the DMAR register, which drives all channels of that timer.
2. **TIMx_CHy** DMA: input capture of the GCR response. The line flips to input after the frame.

The H7's **DMAMUX** lets any request route to any of the 16 streams (DMA1/DMA2). The constraint is therefore stream count and sharing, not fixed mapping.
