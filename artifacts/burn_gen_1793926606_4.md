# STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): Power, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture Decision and Assumptions

**The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no in-FC 6-step or FOC motor control. The architecture is therefore:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──BDShot600 ×4──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421, AM32) ──► 4× gate driver ──► 4× 3-phase bridge (24 FETs)
```

The H743 owns the timer/DMA problem. The ESC MCUs own the 48 kHz PWM.

**Numbers marked [A] are design assumptions.** Replace them with datasheet values for your chosen parts and re-run Sections 1 to 3.
