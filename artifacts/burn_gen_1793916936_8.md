Read these flags before the numbers. Three of them change the design.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Bridge Topology Under 6-Step Synchronous PWM

At any instant, two legs carry the phase current I:
- One leg is the PWM leg. Current flows through its HS FET for D·T and through its LS FET for (1−D)·T.
- One leg is the static leg, with its LS FET fully on.

With synchronous rectification, a single FET always carries I, whatever D is. This gives the instantaneous bridge conduction loss:

$$
P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds(on)}(T_j)
$$

Temperature dependence of Rds(on):

$$
R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\text{–}0.006\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}
$$

Copper path loss (battery → bridge → phase pad) adds:

$$
P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{trace} \quad(\text{per phase pair, } \times 2 \text{ phases active})
$$

### 2.2 Numbers at the 80 A Burst

Candidate FET class: 40 V, PDFN3.3×3.3, R₂₅ = 1.2 mΩ max at V_gs = 10 V.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| R_ds(on) at T_j ≈ 125 °C (×1.55) | **1.86 mΩ** |
| P_cond,bridge = 2 · 80² · 1.86 mΩ | **23.8 W** |
| R_trace (2 oz, 8-layer, paralleled planes, est.) | 0.25 mΩ per path |
| P_Cu = 2 · 80² · 0.25 mΩ | **3.2 W** |

### 2.3 Per-FET Distribution

The per-FET loss depends on rotor speed.

**Spinning case (e-period ≪ die thermal τ ≈ 1–5 ms).** Average over a commutation cycle. Each of the 6 FETs takes 1/6 of the bridge loss:

$$
\bar P_{FET} = \tfrac{2I^2R}{6} = \tfrac{I^2R}{3} = 3.97\ \mathrm{W}
$$

**Stall or low-eRPM case (worst case).** The static LS FET carries the full current continuously:

$$
P_{FET,stall} = I^2 R = 11.9\ \mathrm{W}
$$

This is the design-limiting case. Desync or prop-strike protection must cut current within milliseconds.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. Write V for the bus voltage, I for the phase current, and f for the switching frequency.

### 3.1 Hard-Switching Overlap (V–I crossover)

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r + t_f) f, \qquad t_{r,f} \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_g}, \quad I_g = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}{R_g+R_{g,int}}
$$

Candidate values: Q_gs2 + Q_gd ≈ 9 nC, V_drv = 10 V, V_plateau ≈ 3.5 V, R_g = 10 Ω, R_g,int ≈ 1.5 Ω.
- Gate current: I_g ≈ 6.5 / 11.5 ≈ 0.57 A, but the FD6288 sink/source limits apply.
- Effective edge time: t ≈ 15 ns per edge.

$$
P_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 30\,\mathrm{ns} \cdot 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}
$$
