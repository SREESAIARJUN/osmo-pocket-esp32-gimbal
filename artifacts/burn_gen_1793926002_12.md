## 0. Assumptions and Corrections

Two framing corrections come first, because they change the design.

1. **The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In every production AIO board, the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071. That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. This document models both domains on that basis.

2. **"80 A burst per channel" is not physically achievable on a nano (20×20 mm) board, even transiently, for more than about 1 s.** The math in §3 shows why. I carry 80 A through every calculation so you can see exactly where it breaks. I then derive the real continuous and burst envelope.
