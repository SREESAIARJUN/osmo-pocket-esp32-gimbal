## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Finding

**Architecture.** ArduPilot on the H743 does not commutate motors. The H743 (flight controller) sends bidirectional DShot600 to the 4-in-1 ESC. The ESC's own MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32) generates the 48 kHz PWM, drives the gate drivers and returns eRPM telemetry. The model is partitioned accordingly:

| Domain | Owner | Covered in |
|---|---|---|
| Power stage losses, thermal | ESC MCU + MOSFETs | §1–§3 |
| DShot600 bidir timing, timers, DMA | STM32H743 + ArduPilot | §4 |
| Netlists | Both | §5 |

> **Key finding:** 80 A *per channel* on a 20×20 mm nano 4-in-1 is **thermally infeasible**, even for a 2 s burst (§3). 80 A *total* (20 A/channel) is feasible. Please confirm which spec is intended. Both cases are computed below.

## 2. Loss Model (per channel, per electrical commutation state)

In any 6-step state, one phase's high side (HS) PWMs at duty *D*. Its complementary low side (LS) synchronously rectifies for (1−D). Another phase's LS conducts continuously.

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{on}\left[D + (1-D) + 1\right] = 2\,I^2 R_{on}(T_j)
$$

The loss is independent of D (with ideal sync rectification and ripple neglected). Adding the ripple correction for a phase inductance L:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12},\qquad \Delta I = \frac{V_{bus} D(1-D)}{L f_{sw}}
$$

For L ≈ 20 µH and D = 0.5: ΔI ≈ 6.6 A, which adds about 3.6 A². That is negligible at 80 A and small at 20 A.

**Case A (80 A):**
$$P_{cond} = 2 \cdot 6400 \cdot 0.0024 = \mathbf{30.7\ W}$$

**Case B (20 A):**
$$P_{cond} = 2 \cdot 400 \cdot 0.0024 = \mathbf{1.92\ W}$$

At a realistic T_j ≈ 90 °C, R ≈ 2.1 mΩ, which gives 1.7 W.

**Copper loss:** P_cu = I²·R_cu gives **3.2 W** (case A) and **0.2 W** (case B).

### 2.2 Switching Loss at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd HS hard-switches. Its sync LS commutates through the body diode during the dead time.

| Term | Formula | Case A (80 A) | Case B (20 A) |
|---|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | 1.94 W | 0.48 W |
| Dead-time diode | V_F·I·2t_dt·f_sw | 0.61 W | 0.15 W |
| Reverse recovery | V·Q_rr·f_sw | 0.06 W | 0.06 W |
| C_oss (2 devices) | 2·½·Q_oss·V·f_sw | 0.05 W | 0.05 W |
| Gate (mostly in driver) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw | 0.05 W | 0.05 W |
| **P_sw total** | | **2.71 W** | **0.79 W** |

**Frequency sensitivity:** dP_sw/df ≈ 56 µJ per cycle (case A). Going 48 → 24 kHz saves about 1.3 W per channel but increases current ripple and acoustic noise.
