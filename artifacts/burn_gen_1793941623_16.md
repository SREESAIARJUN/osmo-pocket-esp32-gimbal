## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility

These findings constrain the rest of the design, so they come first.

1. **Partitioning.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU (AM32-class firmware on an AT32F421 or STM32G071) talking bidirectional DShot600 to the H743. Running 4 sensorless FOC/six-step loops on the H743 would break ArduPilot's scheduling model and its fault isolation.
2. **The 80 A rating must be per-channel and short.** On a 20×20 / 25.5×25.5 mm board:
   - 4 × 80 A = 320 A of battery current simultaneously. No XT60/XT90 pad, input capacitor bank or nano-scale copper pour survives that.
   - **Specification adopted:** 80 A per channel for ≤ 1 s bursts, ≤ 160 A total input, and about 35–40 A continuous per channel with prop-wash airflow. Section 3 derives these numbers.
3. **All device parameters below are stated assumptions** for a representative 40 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3 MOSFET and an integrated 3-phase half-bridge driver. Re-run the model with the datasheet values of the parts you actually select.
