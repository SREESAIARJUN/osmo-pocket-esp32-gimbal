## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current distribution, six-step with complementary PWM

In each 60° sector, one high-side (HS) and one low-side (LS) FET carry phase current I. The PWM leg alternates HS (for D) and its own LS (for 1−D).

Per leg, over one electrical cycle:
- HS conducts for D·(1/3) of the cycle.
- LS conducts for (1−D)·(1/3) + 1/3 of the cycle (the freewheel interval plus its own sector).
- Total leg conduction is 2/3 of the cycle, independent of D.

This gives the following RMS currents:

$$I_{HS,rms}^2 = \frac{D}{3}I^2,\qquad I_{LS,rms}^2 = \frac{2-D}{3}I^2$$

Summed over the three legs, exactly two FETs are always in series:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)}$$

### 1.2 Temperature-corrected R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\quad R_{25}=1.0\,\text{m}\Omega\ (\text{typ}),\ \alpha\approx0.004/\text{K}$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R ≈ 1.4 mΩ. I use **1.6 mΩ** to cover tolerance plus copper spreading and solder resistance.

### 1.3 Numbers at I = 80 A

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| P_cond per channel | 2 × 6400 × 1.6 mΩ = **20.5 W** |
| Worst FET (LS at D → 0) | (2/3) × 6400 × 1.6 mΩ = **6.8 W** |
| HS FET at D = 0.9 | 0.3 × 6400 × 1.6 mΩ = **3.1 W** |

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one leg per sector is PWM-switching. Its losses per channel are the sum of five terms.

**1. V–I overlap:**
$$P_{ov}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw}$$
Rise and fall times are set by Q_gd / I_gate. With Q_gd ≈ 8 nC and a ~0.5 A effective driver, t_r ≈ t_f ≈ 15 ns:
$$P_{ov}=0.5(25.2)(80)(30\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{k}) = \mathbf{1.45\,W}$$

**2. Dead-time body-diode conduction:** two dead-time intervals per period, V_F ≈ 0.8 V, t_dt = 100 ns:
$$P_{dt}=2 V_F I t_{dt} f_{sw} = 2(0.8)(80)(100\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{k}) = \mathbf{0.61\,W}$$

**3. Reverse recovery:** with Q_rr ≈ 30 nC:
$$P_{rr}=V_{bus}Q_{rr}f_{sw} = \mathbf{0.04\,W}$$

**4. Output capacitance:** with Q_oss ≈ 40 nC:
$$P_{oss}=2\cdot\tfrac12 Q_{oss}V_{bus} f_{sw} = \mathbf{0.05\,W}$$

**5. Gate drive (dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors):** with Q_g = 50 nC and V_GS = 10 V:
$$P_{g}=2 Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = \mathbf{0.05\,W}$$

**Total switching loss per channel at 80 A ≈ 2.2 W.**

Design note: dead time is the dominant non-overlap term. Trimming it from 100 ns to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W per channel. Validate against shoot-through on the FD6288-class driver propagation skew first.
