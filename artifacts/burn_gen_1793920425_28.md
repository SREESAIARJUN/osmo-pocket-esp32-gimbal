## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

During each 60° commutation sector:

- **High-side FET of phase A (PWM leg):** conducts for duty fraction D.
- **Low-side FET of phase A (synchronous rectifier):** conducts for fraction (1 − D), minus dead time.
- **Low-side FET of phase B (return leg):** conducts 100 % of the sector.

So **exactly two FETs carry the phase current at all times** (ignoring dead time). This makes conduction loss independent of D for a given phase current I_ph:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2 \, R_{DS(on)}(T_j) \;+\; I_{ph,rms}^2\,(R_{Cu,PCB} + R_{shunt,share})
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence of R_DS(on)

Use a linear fit to a typical normalized curve (≈1.5× at 110 °C for 40 V trench FETs):

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + k_T (T_j - 25)\right], \qquad k_T \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × 1.51 = **1.81 mΩ**.
