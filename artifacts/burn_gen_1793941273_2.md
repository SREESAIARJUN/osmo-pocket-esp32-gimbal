## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Key Findings

### 0.1 Architecture

A 4-in-1 ESC does not commutate motors from the flight controller MCU. The architecture modeled here is:

```
┌─────────────────────────────┐   4× bidir DShot600    ┌──────────────────────────────────┐
│ FC: STM32H743 (ArduPilot)   │ ─────────────────────> │ 4-in-1 ESC: 4× AT32F421 (AM32)   │
│ TIM1 CH1–4 + DMAMUX streams │ <───── eRPM GCR ────── │ 4× 3-phase gate driver           │
└─────────────────────────────┘                        │ 24× 40 V N-MOSFET (1 per switch) │
                                                       └──────────────────────────────────┘
```

The ESC MCUs run AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware with 48 kHz PWM. The H743 owns DShot generation, eRPM capture, and harmonic-notch consumption.

### 0.2 Headline Results

| Quantity | Value | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| Loss per ESC channel @ 80 A, 6S | ≈ 25 W | 90% conduction |
| Total board loss, 4 × 80 A | ≈ 100 W | Cannot be sustained on a nano PCB |
| Burst duration to Tj = 125 °C (40 °C ambient) | ≈ 1.5–2 s | Board heat capacity ≈ 3 J/K |
| Realistic continuous rating (with prop-wash airflow) | ≈ 25–30 A/ch | Thermally limited, not silicon limited |
| Switching-edge overshoot at 80 A | ≈ 36 V on a 40 V FET | Layout-critical margin |
| Input-cap ripple current | up to 40 A_rms/ch | Dominant electrolytic/MLCC stressor |

**"80 A burst" is defensible only as a sub-2-second rating.** Marketing it as continuous would be wrong. The analysis below shows why.
