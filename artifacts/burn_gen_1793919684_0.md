## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

The H743 should not commutate the motors. The architecture assumed here is the industry-standard partition:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight control | STM32H743VIH6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, mixing, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| ESC ×4 | STM32G071GBU6 (or AT32F421) | AM32 | Commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive ×4 | FD6288Q (3× half-bridge, bootstrap) | n/a | Gate drive, 10–12 V |
| Power stage | 24× 30 V N-MOSFET, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN | n/a | 6 FETs per motor |

**Design inputs. These are assumptions, so replace them with your BOM values.**
