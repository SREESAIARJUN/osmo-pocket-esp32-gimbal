**Architectural reality check (stated upfront).** 4 × 80 A burst on a "nano" footprint is thermally capacity-limited, not convection-limited. At full burst the board dissipates about 90 W. It survives only a few seconds (§3), so the 80 A figure must be a timed burst rating enforced in firmware.
