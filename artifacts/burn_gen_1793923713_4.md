### Architecture note

On a combined FC + 4-in-1 board, the 48 kHz commutation and PWM run on the **ESC MCU(s)**, not on the H743. Typical ESC firmware is AM32, BLHeli_32, or Bluejay, on an AT32F421, STM32G071, or EFM8.

The H743 running ArduPilot only:
- emits DShot600 frames, and
- captures the bidirectional eRPM telemetry.

These are two separate design domains, treated in §1–3 (power/thermal) and §4 (timers/DMA).

### Feasibility warning

80 A per channel on a ~36 mm "nano" board is a **burst rating measured in seconds**, not a continuous rating. The model below quantifies exactly how many seconds.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in six-step complementary PWM

At any instant, phase current flows through **two** FETs in series:

- **Phase A (switching leg):**
  - HS conducts for duty D.
  - LS conducts for (1 − D), as a synchronous rectifier.
- **Phase B (return leg):** LS conducts for 100% of the 60° sector.

So for one ESC:

$$
P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \left[ D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS} + R_{LS} \right] = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

With ripple neglected, I_rms ≈ I_pk for a flat-topped trapezoidal current block.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

R_DS(on) depends on T_j, and T_j depends on loss, so the two are coupled. Solve self-consistently:

$$
T_j = T_{board} + P_{FET}\,R_{\theta JC},\qquad P_{FET} = I^2 R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

Solving for T_j in closed form:

$$
T_j = \frac{T_{board} + I^2 R_{25} R_{\theta JC}(1 - 25\alpha)}{1 - \alpha I^2 R_{25} R_{\theta JC}}
$$

**Numerical result** (I = 80 A, T_board = 100 °C):

- I²·R_25 = 6.4 W
- α·I²·R_25·R_θJC = 0.005 × 6.4 × 1.5 = 0.048
- T_j = (100 + 6.4·1.5·0.875) / 0.952 = **113.8 °C**
- R_DS(on) = 1.0 × (1 + 0.005·88.8) = **1.44 mΩ**
- P_cond per conducting FET = 6400 × 1.44 mΩ = **9.2 W**
- P_cond per ESC (2 FETs) = **18.5 W**

**Thermal runaway criterion:**

$$
\alpha I^2 R_{25} R_{\theta JC} \ge 1
$$

With these parameters, runaway occurs at I ≈ 365 A. It is not a limit here, but it shrinks quickly if R_θJC or R_25 is higher (for example, cheaper 3×3 parts with R_θJC around 5 K/W).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the switching leg's HS/LS pair switches per PWM period. Computed at V = 25.2 V and I = 80 A.

| Loss term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw = 0.5·25.2·80·40e-9·48e3 | **1.94 W** |
| Reverse recovery (LS body diode) | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 50e-9·25.2·48e3 | **0.06 W** |
| C_oss charge | ½·C_oss·V²·f_sw = 0.5·1e-9·635·48e3 | **0.015 W** |
| Dead-time body diode conduction | V_f·I·2·t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·100e-9·48e3 | **0.31 W** |
| Gate drive (×2 switching FETs) | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f_sw = 2·40e-9·10·48e3 | **0.04 W** (dissipated in driver/R_g) |
| **Total switching, per ESC** | | **≈ 2.36 W** |

### Sensitivity to switching frequency

P_sw scales linearly with f_sw.

| f_sw | Switching loss | Conduction share of total |
|---|---|---|
| 24 kHz | 1.2 W | — |
| 48 kHz | 2.4 W | 89% |
| 96 kHz | 4.7 W | — |

At 48 kHz, conduction still dominates. Because of that, the 48 kHz choice (smoother current, lower acoustic noise, better low-throttle behavior) costs only about 1.2 W per ESC versus 24 kHz.

### Edge-rate caution

20 ns edges at 80 A give di/dt ≈ 4 A/ns. With 1 nH of commutation-loop inductance:

- V_overshoot = L·di/dt ≈ **4 V**
- 25.2 + 4 = 29.2 V peak, which is within 40 V margin.

Keep the half-bridge loop (HS drain → LS source → local MLCC) **under 1.5 nH**: place MLCCs adjacent to the FETs, with the GND return directly beneath on L2.

## 3. Board-Level Thermal Model

### 3.1 Total burst loss (4 ESCs at 80 A simultaneously)

| Source | Per ESC | ×4 |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 18.5 W | 74.0 W |
| FET switching | 2.4 W | 9.4 W |
| Current shunt (0.25 mΩ, on the shared battery bus) | — | see below |
| Phase copper (≈0.3 mΩ path) | 1.9 W | 7.7 W |
| Bus copper and battery pads (0.15 mΩ at 320 A) | — | 15.4 W |
| **Total** | | **≈ 107 W** |

**Shunt note:** if a single shunt sits on the 320 A battery bus, its loss is 320² × 0.25 mΩ = **25.6 W**. That is unacceptable. Use one shunt per ESC, or sense phase current via R_DS(on). The total above assumes per-channel or R_DS sensing.
