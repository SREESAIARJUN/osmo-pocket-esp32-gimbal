**Architectural point.** The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM. In a 4-in-1 stack:

| Domain | Device | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (ArduPilot) | Sends DShot600 commands, receives bidirectional eRPM, runs the notch filter |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421 / STM32G071 class (AM32 or BLHeli_32) | 48 kHz complementary PWM, commutation, gate drive |

So the switching-loss model applies to the ESC power stage. The DMA model applies to the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path

In six-step trapezoidal drive (AM32/BLHeli_32), two phases conduct per 60° sector.

- **Return-leg low side (LS):** on for the whole sector.
- **Active leg:** the high side (HS) is on for duty D; the synchronous LS of the same leg is on for (1 − D).

At any instant, exactly two channels carry the phase current:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j) + I_{rms}^2 R_{cu}
$$

The ripple correction comes from the motor inductance L_ph:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{dc}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-E_{bemf})\,D}{2L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}
$$
