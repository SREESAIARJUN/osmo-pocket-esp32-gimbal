# Job #44: STM32H743 Nano FC with 4-in-1 80 A ESC

## Architecture, Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, BDShot DMA Allocation, and Reference Netlists

---

## 0. Executive Findings

These conclusions shape everything that follows.

1. **The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors directly.** ArduPilot has no in-tree 6-step or FOC motor control for the H7. The correct architecture is:
   - H743 runs ArduPilot.
   - Four independent ESC MCUs run AM32, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071, one per motor.
   - They are linked on-board by **bidirectional DShot600** (BDShot).
2. **80 A per channel on a nano (20×20 mm) board is a transient rating, not a continuous one.** The model below gives roughly **1–2 s adiabatic** at 4×80 A before T_j reaches about 125 °C, and about **4–8 s** for a single motor. Size the ESC firmware current limit and the ArduPilot `MOT_` and `BATT_` limits accordingly. Do not advertise this as "80 A continuous."
3. **At 80 A, conduction losses dominate.** They are about 28 W per channel at hot R_ds(on). Switching losses at 48 kHz are about 2 W per channel.
4. **Input capacitor ripple current (up to about 40 A_rms per channel at D = 0.5) is the second hard constraint.** It is frequently overlooked on nano boards.

Values marked **[VERIFY]** depend on the final part selection and must be replaced with datasheet numbers.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}$$

An alternative form, closer to datasheet curves, is $R_{ds,25}\,(T_j/298)^{2.3}$ with T_j in kelvin.

At T_j = 125 °C with R_ds,25 = 1.5 mΩ (max):

$$R_{ds}(125) = 1.5\,(1 + 0.45) = 2.175\ \mathrm{m\Omega}$$

### 2.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous drive

During any 60° sector, current flows through one high-side and one low-side position. With complementary PWM:

- During t_on: HS_x conducts, and LS_y conducts.
- During t_off: LS_x (synchronous rectifier) conducts, and LS_y conducts.

There are therefore always two channel resistances in series. The total bridge conduction loss is independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 2.3 Per-device RMS current

Each switch carries current for 1/3 of the electrical cycle.

- HS_x conducts for fraction D of its active third.
- LS_x conducts for its own third plus a (1−D) share.

The worst-case single device is the low side:

$$I_{rms,LS} \approx I\sqrt{\tfrac{1}{3}(2 - D)}$$

At D → 0.5 this gives about 0.71 I = 57 A_rms.

$$P_{LS,max} \approx (57)^2 \times 2.175\,\mathrm{m\Omega} \approx 7.0\ \mathrm{W}$$

### 2.4 Per-channel results at I = 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C (typ 1.2 mΩ) | 1.2 mΩ | 15.4 W | 61 W |
| 25 °C (max) | 1.5 mΩ | 19.2 W | 77 W |
| 100 °C | 1.99 mΩ | 25.4 W | 102 W |
| **125 °C** | **2.175 mΩ** | **27.8 W** | **111 W** |

### 2.5 Additional copper and shunt losses (per channel)

- **Shunt:** 0.25 mΩ, which gives P = 6400 × 0.25e-3 = **1.6 W**. Use a bus shunt that is 1206 or wider with Kelvin sense.
- **Phase pad and trace copper:** about 0.3 mΩ effective, which gives **~1.9 W**.

**Conduction subtotal at 125 °C: ~31 W per channel.**

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

In 6-step drive, only one leg is hard-switched per PWM period. The per-channel terms are below, with V = 25.2 V, I = 80 A, and f = 48 kHz.

### 3.1 V–I overlap

$$t_r \approx t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_g} = \frac{10\,\mathrm{nC}}{1\,\mathrm{A}} = 10\ \mathrm{ns};\quad \text{use 15 ns each with Miller and Ls effects}$$

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r + t_f) f = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 30\mathrm{e}{-9} \cdot 48\mathrm{e}3 = 1.45\ \mathrm{W}$$

### 3.2 Body-diode reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V\,f = 50\,\mathrm{nC} \cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\mathrm{e}3 = 0.06\ \mathrm{W}$$

The Q_rr of 50 nC is the value at 80 A and high di/dt **[VERIFY]**.

### 3.3 Dead-time diode conduction (two transitions per period)

$$P_{dt} = V_F\, I\, (2 t_{dt})\, f = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 200\mathrm{e}{-9} \cdot 48\mathrm{e}3 = 0.61\ \mathrm{W}$$

### 3.4 Output capacitance

With E_oss ≈ 0.3 µJ at 25 V **[VERIFY]**:

$$P_{oss} = E_{oss}\, f \approx 0.3\,\mu\mathrm{J} \cdot 48\mathrm{e}3 = 0.015\ \mathrm{W}\ \text{per switching device}$$

### 3.5 Gate drive (two complementary gates switching)

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g\,V_{GS}\,f = 2 \cdot 40\mathrm{e}{-9} \cdot 10 \cdot 48\mathrm{e}3 = 0.038\ \mathrm{W}$$
