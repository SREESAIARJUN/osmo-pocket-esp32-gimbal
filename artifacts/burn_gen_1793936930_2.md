## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology current paths
Under 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification, one phase is PWM-driven (phase A), one is held low (phase B), and one floats.

- **PWM ON:** HS_A → motor → LS_B
- **PWM OFF:** LS_A (synchronous freewheel) → motor → LS_B

In both states exactly **two FETs carry I_ph in series**. The per-motor conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

The split between the high-side and low-side devices of phase A does depend on D:

$$P_{HS_A} = D\,I^2R,\qquad P_{LS_A} = (1-D)\,I^2R,\qquad P_{LS_B} = I^2R$$

### 1.2 RMS current including PWM ripple
The phase current carries a triangular ripple:

$$\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-E_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph-ph}\,f_{sw}},\qquad I_{rms}=\sqrt{I_{avg}^2+\frac{\Delta I^2}{12}}$$

For a typical 5" motor (L_ph-ph ≈ 25 µH), at D = 0.9 and 25.2 V with E ≈ 20 V:

- ΔI ≈ (5.2 × 0.9)/(25e-6 × 48e3) ≈ 3.9 A
- The ripple term is 3.9²/12 = 1.27 A², which is negligible against 6400 A².

Take **I_rms ≈ 80.0 A**.
