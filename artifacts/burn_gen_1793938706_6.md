## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4-class (Cortex-M4F, FPU, CORDIC optional), 170 MHz |
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC, e.g. GBM4108: 12N14P, pole pairs $p = 7$, R ≈ 10 Ω, L ≈ 3 mH |
| Position sensor | 14-bit magnetic encoder per axis (MT6701 / AS5048A) over SPI |
| Current sense | 2-shunt or inline, sampled synchronously at PWM center |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, separate SPI buses,
