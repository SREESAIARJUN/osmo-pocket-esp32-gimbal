# Job #54: STM32H743 Nano AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC

**Design Package Rev A: Loss Model, Thermal Budget, Bi-directional DShot600/DMA Plan, Netlist**

---

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Red Flags

Three findings change the design's scope.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no in-tree sensorless BLDC commutation. The correct partition is:

- **H743:** runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600 on 4 timer channels.
- **4× ESC MCUs:** AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32), running AM32 at 48 kHz complementary PWM, one per motor.
- **4× FD6288Q:** 3-phase gate drivers.
- **24× N-MOSFETs:** 6 per motor.

**2. "80 A burst" must be defined.** I model two cases:

- **Case A:** 80 A per motor. Total battery current is 320 A, which is not survivable on a 20×20 / 25.5×25.5 board beyond about 1 s.
- **Case B:** 80 A total, i.e. 20 A per motor. This is realistic for a nano AIO.

The math is written per-channel so either case drops out.

**3. Thermal capacity of a nano board is the binding constraint.** Steady state is not the limit. Section 3 shows Case A reaches the junction limit in about 1 to 2 s.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (per motor channel, six-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

### 2.1 Conduction losses

In each 60° sector, current flows through exactly one high-side (PWM'd) FET and one low-side FET. With complementary PWM, the high-side's freewheel interval is carried by its sync-rectifying partner. To first order, two FET R_ds sit in the current path at all times regardless of duty D.

**Temperature-corrected resistance:**

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.2 × 1.45 = **1.74 mΩ**.

**Channel conduction loss:**

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

**Per-FET average.** Each FET carries current for 1/3 of the electrical period:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \tfrac{1}{3}I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

This splits between the high-side and its complement in ratio D : (1−D).

| | Case A (80 A) | Case B (20 A) |
|---|---|---|
| P_cond,ch | 2·6400·1.74 m = **22.3 W** | 2·400·1.74 m = **1.39 W** |
| P_cond per FET (avg) | 3.71 W | 0.23 W |

### 2.2 Switching losses (48 kHz)

Only the PWM'd high-side hard-switches. The sync FET switches at near-zero V_ds (ZVS) after dead time.

**Transition times:**

$$t_{r}=\frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,src}} = 12\text{ ns},\quad t_f=\frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,snk}}=8\text{ ns}$$

**Overlap (V·I) loss:**

$$P_{VI}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw}$$

**Reverse recovery** (sync FET body diode recovers into the turning-on high-side):

$$P_{rr}=Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw}=40\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k}=0.048\text{ W}$$
