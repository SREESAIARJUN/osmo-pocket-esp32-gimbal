## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle from the mechanical encoder angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park:**

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant Model (Surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\psi_m$$

Electromagnetic torque:

$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\,\psi_m\, i_q \equiv K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.3 Current PI by Pole-Zero Cancellation

After decoupling, each axis is the first-order plant

$$G_i(s) = \frac{1}{Ls + R}$$

Choose the PI controller

$$C_i(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s},\qquad K_p = L\,\omega_c,\quad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

The PI zero K_i/K_p = R/L cancels the plant pole, giving the closed loop

$$T(s) = \frac{\omega_c}{s + \omega_c}$$

**Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:**

$$v_d^{*} = \text{PI}_d(i_d^{*} - i_d) - \omega_e L\, i_q$$

$$v_q^{*} = \text{PI}_q(i_q^{*} - i_q) + \omega_e (L\, i_d + \psi_m)$$

**Voltage limit (SVPWM linear region):**

$$\|v_{dq}\| \le V_{\max} = \frac{V_{dc}}{\sqrt3}$$

- d-axis has priority.
- v_q is limited to $\sqrt{V_{\max}^2 - v_d^2}$.
