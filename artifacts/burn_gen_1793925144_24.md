## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Feasibility Review

### 0.1 Architectural clarification

ArduPilot runs on the **STM32H743 flight controller**. It does not commutate motors. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM and returns eRPM telemetry over bi-directional DShot600.

The partition is:

```
[LiPo 6S] ─┬─> [4-in-1 ESC: 4× AT32F421 + 4× FD6288Q + 24× N-MOSFET] ──> 4 motors
           │          ▲  DShot600 bidir (1 wire/motor)
           │          │
           └─> [5V/10V BEC] ──> [STM32H743 FC running ArduPilot]
```

### 0.3 Feasibility flag

80 A per channel on a nano form factor is a **transient (burst) rating only**. The model below shows roughly **30 A continuous** and **80 A for about 2–4 s** from a cool board. On a 20×20 mm board, 80 A burst is not credible because of copper cross-section and thermal mass. I recommend 30.5×30.5 mm minimum.
