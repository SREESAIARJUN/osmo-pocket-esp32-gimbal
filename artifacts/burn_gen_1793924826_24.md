## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**Division of labor.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller. It runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600. It does **not** generate the 48 kHz motor PWM. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU, which runs AM32 and drives the 48 kHz complementary 6-step PWM. The model therefore splits across two silicon domains:

| Domain | Silicon | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| FC | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100) or VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot, DShot600 bidir, ESC telemetry decode |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32), AM32 firmware | 48 kHz PWM, commutation, eRPM GCR reply |
| Gate drive ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase half-bridge driver, QFN24) | High/low-side gate drive, bootstrap |
| Power stage ×4 | 6× N-MOSFET, 40 V, DFN3.3×3.3 | Three half-bridges per motor |

> **Engineering flag (read first).** 80 A × 4 on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is physically a **~1-second burst rating**, not a sustained one. The math below proves this. If the requirement is "80 A sustained," the platform must grow to at least 30.5×30.5 mm with 5×6 mm FETs.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

| T_j | Multiplier | R_ds |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 | 1.50 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.34 | 2.01 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.45 | 2.18 mΩ |

### 1.2 Six-step complementary (synchronous) conduction

During any commutation sector, two phases carry current:

- **PWM phase:** the high side conducts for fraction D. The low side conducts for (1 − D) as a synchronous rectifier.
- **Static phase:** the low side conducts 100 % of the time.

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 R_{ds}\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

With complementary switching, conduction loss is **independent of duty cycle**. Ripple adds to RMS current:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

For a typical 5" motor (L_ph ≈ 15 µH line-to-line, worst case D = 0.5, V_bemf ≈ V_bus/2):

ΔI ≈ (12.6 × 0.5)/(15e-6 × 48e3) ≈ 8.75 A → ΔI²/12 ≈ 6.4 A², which is negligible against 6400 A².

**Per motor channel at T_j = 100 °C:**
$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 6406 \times 2.01\text{ mΩ} = \mathbf{25.8\ W}$$
