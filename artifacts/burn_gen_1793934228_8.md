# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #8)

## 0. Architecture Baseline and Assumptions

ArduPilot cannot commutate a BLDC bridge directly. So this design is a **split architecture**:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (400 MHz, the ArduPilot default clock) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, bidirectional DShot600 master |
