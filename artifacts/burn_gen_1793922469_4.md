## 0. Architecture Clarification and Baseline Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning

The STM32H743 cannot directly commutate four BLDC motors at 48 kHz with sensorless back-EMF zero-cross detection while running ArduPilot. Every production H743 "4-in-1" AIO uses the following split:

| Domain | Device | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 / VIT6 | ArduPilot, sensor fusion, emits 4× bi-directional DShot600 |
| ESC (×4 or ×1 quad-capable) | AT32F421K8U7 / STM32G071GBU6 running AM32, or EFM8BB51 running BlueJay | 48 kHz complementary PWM, commutation, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap), one per motor | High-side and low-side drive, about 10 V Vgs |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET, 40 V, PDFN3.3×3.3 | 6 FETs per motor |

The model below follows this partition. If Job #48 truly intends H743 direct commutation, flag it. That requires a non-ArduPilot firmware architecture.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step Trapezoidal, Synchronous PWM)

### 1.1 Commutation Topology

During any 60° electrical sector:
- **Phase A (chopped):** HS on for D·T_sw. LS on for (1−D)·T_sw (synchronous rectification).
- **Phase B (sink):** LS held on continuously.
- **Phase C:** floating, used for B
