## 0. Architectural Assumptions and Corrections

These assumptions change the math, so they come first.

| # | Assumption / finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **The STM32H743 cannot be the motor-commutation MCU for 4 BLDC motors.** It has only two advanced timers with complementary outputs and dead time (TIM1, TIM8). HRTIM can produce complementary pairs, but 4× sensorless 6-step with BEMF comparators is not practical. | Architecture is H743 flight controller plus **4× dedicated ESC MCUs** (e.g. AT32F421 running AM32), linked by bidirectional DShot600. This is why bidirectional DShot exists in the design. |
| A2 | "80 A burst" is interpreted as **phase (motor) current per channel**, not battery current. | Battery current: I_bat ≈ D·I_ph. With 4 channels at D≈0.9, the pack sees about 290 A. |
| A3 | Bus voltage is 6S: V_bus = 25.2 V max. 48 kHz is the **ESC-side PWM**. DShot frame rate is independent of it. | Switching loss uses 25.2 V worst case. |
| A4 | Representative MOSFET is a 40 V N-channel in PDFN5×6. Substitute your datasheet values into §1. | R_ds(on),25 = 1.2 mΩ, Q_g = 50 nC, Q_rr = 50 nC, Q_oss = 40 nC, t_r + t_f ≈ 20 ns at the chosen gate drive, R_th,jc = 1.2 K/W |
| A5 | **"Nano" form factor is a red flag.** 24 FETs at 80 A will not fit a 20×20 mm board. | The model below assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm mounting pattern on a ~36×36 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz board**. At 20×20 mm, the 80 A burst rating is not credible (see §3.5). |

## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel = one 3-phase bridge, 6 FETs)

### 1.1 Conduction Path (120° trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

During any commutation sector, one phase is PWM'd (HS on for D, its LS on for 1−D under sync rectification) and one phase is held low (LS continuously on). The current path is therefore **always exactly two FETs in series**, independent of D:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

Temperature dependence of R_ds(on):

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}\ (\text{gives }\times1.55\text{ at }125^\circ\text{C})$$

At I_ph = 80 A and T_j = 125 °C (R = 1.86 mΩ):

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2 \cdot 6400 \cdot 1.86\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{23.8\ W}$$

Copper path (pad to FET to phase pad, ~0.4 mΩ effective): P_Cu = 6400 · 0.4 mΩ = **2.6 W**.

### 1.2 Switching Losses at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd half-bridge hard-switches. The held-low leg does not.

**Overlap (V–I crossover), hard-switched HS:**
$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 20\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.97\ W
