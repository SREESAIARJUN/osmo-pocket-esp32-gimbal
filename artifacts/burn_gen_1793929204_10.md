## 0. Key Finding: The 80 A Burst Rating Is Transient-Only

On a 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm board, 80 A per motor is a **sub-2-second burst rating**, limited by the board's heat capacity. It cannot be sustained, and the model below quantifies this. Battery-side current must also be bounded separately, because 4 × 80 A of phase current is not 320 A at the XT60 (see §2.5).

## 2. Thermal Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Conduction Path

In 6-step commutation, current always flows through one high-side and one low-side FET. Synchronous rectification splits the PWM-switched leg between its HS FET (duty D) and its LS FET (1−D). Both carry I_ph, so to first order the series resistance is always 2·R_ds(on):

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 \cdot R_{pcb}$$

### 2.2 Temperature-Dependent R_ds(on)

R_ds(on) rises with junction temperature, which creates the self-heating feedback loop:

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ /\text{K}$$

### 2.3 Numbers at 80 A Per Motor

| Condition | R_ds(on) | P_FET,cond per motor | Per FET (hot pair) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold (25 °C, typ) | 1.5 mΩ | 6400 × 3.0 mΩ = **19.2 W** | 9.6 W |
| Hot (125 °C, max) | 3.2 mΩ | 6400 × 6.4 mΩ = **41.0 W** | 20.5 W |
| Realistic design point (~100 °C, typ×1.45) | 2.2 mΩ | 6400 × 4.4 mΩ = **28.2 W** | 14.1 W |

### 2.4 PCB Copper Loss

Assume a 6-layer board with 2 oz outer and inner power planes. Each phase path has an effective resistance of about 0.5 mΩ, including the via farm and solder pads:

$$P_{pcb} = 80^2 \times 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega = 3.2\ \text{W per motor}$$

This term is frequently omitted from ESC ratings, but at 80 A it is significant.

### 2.5 Battery-Side Current

$$I_{bat} = \sum_{k=1}^{4} D_k \cdot I_{ph,k} + I_{aux}$$

Burst phase currents of 80 A occur at low D (current-limited acceleration) or at stall. At full throttle (D → 1), motor back-EMF limits phase current. Size the input for a realistic aggregate burst of about 150–180 A for under 1 s. Design the bulk capacitance to the ripple current requirement:

$$I_{C,rms} \approx I_{ph}\sqrt{D(1-D)} \;\Rightarrow\; \max = 0.5 \cdot I_{ph} = 40\ \text{A per motor at } D = 0.5$$

Four interleaved motors share the bank. Specify at least 1000 µF of low-ESR electrolytic off-board plus at least 4 × 10 µF X7R per bridge, placed at the FET drains.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM-switched leg incurs switching loss: one HS/LS pair per motor at any instant.

### 3.1 Hard-Switching V–I Overlap

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

### 3.2 Output Capacitance

$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac{1}{2} Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 30\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.018\ W}$$

### 3.3 Body-Diode Reverse Recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$
