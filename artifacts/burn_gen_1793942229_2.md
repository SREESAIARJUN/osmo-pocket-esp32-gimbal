## 0. Scope, Architecture Decision and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller (FC)**. It runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600. It does **not** commutate the motors.

Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU (baseline: **AT32F421K8U7** running AM32). That MCU generates the **48 kHz** 6-step PWM. This split is the industry norm and the only realistic option at 80 A burst. One H743 cannot run four ESCs, a full EKF and the IMU loops with deterministic commutation.
