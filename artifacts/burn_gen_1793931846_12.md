## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller** running ArduPilot. A 4-in-1 ESC needs its own motor-control MCU, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32, because the H743 cannot also commutate four motors under ArduPilot. The two boards communicate only through the DShot lines, ESC telemetry, the current-sense signal, and power.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Per-channel conduction loss

In 6-step operation with synchronous rectification, phase current always flows through exactly two MOSFETs:
- during the on-time: high-side of phase A plus low-side of phase B;
- during freewheel: low-side plus low-side.

Duty cycle therefore drops out of the conduction term:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_m^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) + I_m^2\,R_{cu}$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2(80)^2(1.5\times10^{-3}) + (80)^2(0.5\times10^{-3}) = 19.2 + 3.2 = \mathbf{22.4\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-device distribution

Each of the 6 FETs conducts one-third of the electrical cycle:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{2 I_m^2 R_{ds}}{6} = \frac{19.2}{6} = 3.2\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 Electro-thermal coupling

R_ds depends on T_j, so solve the following by fixed-point iteration (it converges in 3–4 steps):

$$T_j = T_{case} + P(T_j)\,R_{th,jc}$$

Thermal runaway occurs if:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}\,R_{th} \ge 1 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad 2I_m^2 R_{ds,25}\,\alpha\,R_{th,ja} \ge 1$$

With R_th,ja ≈ 12.5 K/W (section 4), the loop gain is 2(6400)(1e-3)(0.005)(12.5) = **0.8**. That is close to unity. At 80 A continuous, the board is near electro-thermal runaway, so 80 A can only be a time-limited burst.
