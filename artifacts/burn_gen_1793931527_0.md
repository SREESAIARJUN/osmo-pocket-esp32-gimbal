# STM32H743 AIO 4-in-1 ESC Flight Controller: Systems Architecture Package (Job #12)

## 0. Architectural Clarification and Assumptions

**Partitioning.** In a production 4-in-1 AIO design, the STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (an AT32F421 running AM32 in this design) that generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to each ESC MCU. The analysis therefore splits into:

| Domain | Owner | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction and switching losses at 48 kHz | ESC MCU + power stage | §1–§3 |
| Thermal model, transient and steady-state | Board + power stage | §4 |
| Bi-directional DShot600 timers and DMA | STM32H743 / ArduPilot | §5 |
| Netlists | Both | §6 |

**Reference power stage.** No MOSFET was specified, so the model uses a representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PDFN part. Substitute your datasheet values into the equations.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S, full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current per motor | I_pk | 80 A |
| R_DS(on) at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R₂₅ | 1.5 mΩ |
| R_DS(on) temperature coefficient | α | 0.0045 /K |
| Rise / fall time (with driver) | t_r / t_f | 12 ns / 10 ns |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 40 nC |
| Reverse recovery charge | Q_rr | 50 nC |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 50 nC |
| Body diode V_F at 80 A | V_F | 0.8 V |
| Dead time per edge | t_d | 100 ns |
| Junction-to-case thermal resistance | R_θJC | 1.5 K/W |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| Gate drive voltage | V_GS | 10 V |

**Topology.** The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal commutation (AM32) with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM:
- 6 MOSFETs per motor, 24 total.
- 1 FD6288Q-class three-phase gate driver per motor.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

During any 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct:
- **Phase A (PWM'd):** The high side Q_AH conducts for duty D. The low side Q_AL synchronously rectifies for (1 − D).
- **Phase B (sink):** The low side Q_BL is continuously on.

### 1.2 Loss equations

With low current ripple (I_rms ≈ I_avg = I):

$$P_{AH} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,D, \qquad P_{AL} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)(1-D), \qquad P_{BL} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$\boxed{P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)} \quad \text{(independent of } D\text{)}$$

Temperature dependence of R_DS(on):

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

### 1.3 Numerical evaluation at 80 A

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond per motor | P_cond, 4 motors |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.50 mΩ | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| 100 °C | 2.01 mΩ | 25.7 W | 102.9 W |
| 125 °C | 2.18 mΩ | 27.8 W | 111.4 W |

### 1.4 Hot-spot device

The most stressed device is the continuously-on low side, Q_BL:

$$P_{BL}(125\,°C) = 80^2 \times 2.18\text{ mΩ} = 13.9 \text{ W}$$

Commutation rotates this role through all six devices, so the time-averaged per-FET load is P_cond/6 × 2 ≈ 9.3 W. Short bursts, however, stress whichever FET holds the sector.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 V–I overlap (hard-switched high side)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(22\times10^{-9})(48\times10^3) = \mathbf{1.06\ W}$$

### 2.2 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery (low-side body diode)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.060\ W}$$

Qrr scales with di/dt and current, so treat this as a lower bound. Measure it on the bench.

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction (two edges per cycle)

$$P_{dt} = V_F I \cdot 2 t_d f_{sw} = 0.8\times80\times2\times100\text{n}\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated in driver and gate resistors, not the FET)

$$P_g = Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} \times 2\ \text{(HS+LS pair)} = 50\text{n}\times10\times48\text{k}\times2 = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

### 2.6 Switching loss summary per motor at 80 A

| Term | Power (W) |
|---|---|
| V–I overlap | 1.06 |
| Coss | 0.05 |
| Qrr | 0.06 |
| Dead time | 0.61 |
| **P_sw total (FET)** | **≈ 1.78** |
| Gate drive (driver IC) | 0.05 |

### 2.7 Sensitivity

- **Switching loss** is linear in f_sw. Moving from 24 kHz to 48 kHz adds about 0.9 W per motor at 80 A. This is small compared with conduction loss.
- **Dead time** is the dominant tunable term. At 48 kHz, reducing t_d from 100 ns to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W per motor.

## 3. Total Loss Budget at 80 A Burst

$$P_{motor}(T_j) = 2I^2R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] + P_{sw}$$

| Condition | Per motor | 4 motors | Efficiency (at 2.0 kW per motor input) |
|---|---|---|---|
| T_j = 25 °C | 21.0 W | 84 W | 98.9 % |
| T_j = 125 °C | 29.6 W | 118 W | 98.5 % |

Additional losses not in the FET model:
- **PCB copper:** about 0.3 mΩ per phase path on 2 oz × 8 layers, giving roughly 2–4 W per motor.
- **Bus capacitor ESR:** see §3.1.

### 3.1 Bus capacitor ripple current

$$I_{C,rms} = I\sqrt{D(1-D)} \le 0.5\,I = 40\text{ A per motor (at } D = 0.5)$$

With the four ESC MCUs running asynchronously, the uncorrelated combination is:

$$I_{C,rms,tot} \approx \sqrt{4}\times40 = 80\text{ A}$$

This requires:
- **Bulk:** a low-ESR electrolytic of at least 1000 µF / 35 V off-board.
- **Local:** 4–6 × 10 µF / 50 V X7R 1210 MLCC per motor.

## 4. Thermal Model

### 4.1 Network (Cauer approximation)

```
P_FET ──[R_θJC=1.5]──┬──[R_θCB=0.8]──┬──[R_θBA]── T_amb
                     C_j≈0.05 J/K     C_board
```

| Element | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| R_θCB (case → board, via thermal vias) | 0.8 K/W | Per FET |
| C_board | ≈ 4.5 J/K | 30.5 × 30.5 mm, 8-layer 2 oz board: ~5 g, c ≈ 0.6 J/g·K effective, plus FET and component mass |
| R_θBA, still air | ≈ 12 K/W | Whole board
