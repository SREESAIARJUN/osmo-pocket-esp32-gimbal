# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #11): Electro-Thermal and DShot Architecture

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Design Flags

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller only. It does not commutate the motors. Each motor is driven by a dedicated ESC MCU (4× AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 firmware. The FC sends bidirectional DShot600 to the ESCs. ArduPilot has no support for direct FOC or 6-step motor drive from the H7, so this partition is required, not optional.

**Design flags. Read these before layout.**

1. **Board size.** An 80 A per-motor burst on a nano (20×20 mm) board is not thermally or electrically credible. The math below assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz board**. It shows that even this size is limited to a burst of a few seconds.
2. **Bus capacitance.** On-board ceramics cannot absorb 48 kHz ripple at 80 A (Section 3.4). An external low-ESR bulk capacitor of at least 1000 µF is mandatory.
3. **Placeholder values.** All device parameters are representative placeholders, not values from a selected part. Replace every value in the Section 1 table with the chosen MOSFET's datasheet values; the equations remain valid.
