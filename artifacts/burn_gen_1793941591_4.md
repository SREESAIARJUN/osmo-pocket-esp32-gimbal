## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**Partitioning.** ArduPilot runs on the STM32H743 and does not commutate motors. The board is therefore an all-in-one (AIO) design with:

- **FC domain:** STM32H743VIH6 at 480 MHz, running ArduPilot. It outputs 4× bi-directional DShot600.
- **ESC domain:** 4× AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071), running AM32. Each drives its own 3-phase bridge (6 MOSFETs) through an FD6288Q gate driver.

**Form factor warning.** True "nano" (20×20 mm) cannot sustain 4×80 A. This model assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz outer / 2 oz inner** board, with one MOSFET per switch (24 FETs). The thermal results in §4 show why 80 A is a burst-only rating even on that board.
