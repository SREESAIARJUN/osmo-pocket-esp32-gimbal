# STM32H743 AIO / 4-in-1 ESC Nano FC: Power-Stage Model, Thermal Budget, and Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation

## 0. Scope Corrections and Assumptions

**Architecture correction.** The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz commutation PWM. In every production 4-in-1 design, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an AT32F421, STM32G071 or GD32E230. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to those MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. The model below is split into those two domains.

**Unverified inputs.** No MOSFET part number, board size or stackup was given for "Job #2". All numbers are **parametric with a representative device**. Substitute datasheet values before sign-off.

| Parameter | Symbol | Assumed value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery (6S, full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current, per channel | I | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET (40 V, DFN 3.3×3.3 class) | R_ds(on),25 | 1.2 mΩ |
| Rise + fall time | t_r + t_f | 20 + 15 ns |
| Body-diode reverse-recovery charge | Q_rr | 40 nC |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 50 nC |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 60 nC @ 10 V |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)], with α ≈ 0.005 /K for a 40 V trench FET.

At T_j = 110 °C:
R_ds = 1.2 mΩ × 1.425 = **1.71 mΩ**

A more accurate alternative is R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · (T_j[K] / 298)^2.3. Choose the model by fitting the datasheet R_ds vs T_j curve.

### 1.2 Six-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification

AM32 uses complementary PWM by default. At every instant, phase current flows through exactly two device positions: one high-side (or its complementary low-side during PWM-off) plus one low-side. This holds independent of duty D.

- **Per channel:** P_cond,ch = 2 · I² · R_ds(T_j)
- **Per FET, averaged over an electrical cycle:** P_cond,FET = P_cond,ch / 6 = I² · R_ds / 3
- **PWM-leg FET during its active 120° sector:**
  - High-side: P_HS = I² · R_ds · D
  - Low-side complement: P_LS = I² · R_ds · (1 − D)

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 110 °C

| Quantity | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| P_cond,ch | 2 × 6400 × 1.71e-3 | **21.9 W** |
| P_cond,FET (average) | 21.9 / 6 | **3.65 W** |
| Four channels, all at 80 A | 4 × 21.9 | **87.6 W** |

### 1.4 Copper losses (do not omit these)

**Trace resistance.** Use R_Cu = ρ·L / (w·t), with ρ(T) = 1.72e-8 · [1 + 0.00393(T − 20)] Ω·m.

- A 2 oz (70 µm) pour, 8 mm wide, 15 mm long gives about 0.46 mΩ per layer at 25 °C.
- Paralleling 3 power layers gives about 0.15 mΩ.
- At 80 A this is roughly 1 W per channel path.
- Battery pad to bus to phase pad: budget **~2 W per channel**.

**Vias.** Use about 0.5 mΩ per 0.3 mm plated via (25 µm plating). Use ≥ 20 vias per FET drain/source tie.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the leg currently being PWM'd switches, so one FET pair per channel at any time.

### 2.1 Loss terms (PWM-leg high-side FET)

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 35e-9 × 48e3 = **1.69 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw | 40e-9 × 25.2 × 48e3 = **0.048 W** |
| Output capacitance | ½ · Q_oss · V · f_sw | 0.5 × 50e-9 × 25.2 × 48e3 = **0.030 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f · I · 2t_dt · f_sw | 0.8 × 80 × 100e-9 × 48e3 = **0.31 W** |
| Gate drive (lost in driver/R_g) | Q_g · V_gs · f_sw | 60e-9 × 10 × 48e3 = **0.029 W** |

### 2.2 Totals

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| P_sw,ch | ≈ **2.1 W** (worst case at D ≈ 0.5) |
| At D → 1 (full-throttle burst) | P_sw → ≈ 0.3 W (only dead-time and occasional commutation edges remain) |

### 2.3 Sensitivity

- P_sw scales linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz it would be about 1.05 W per channel.
- 48 kHz is chosen for low-inductance motors (current ripple ΔI = V·D(1−D) / (L·f_sw)) and for reduced acoustic noise.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Total burst dissipation

P_total = 4 × (21.9 + 2.1 + 2.0 Cu) ≈ **104 W**.

This is the worst case: all four motors simultaneously at 80 A and T_j = 110 °C.
