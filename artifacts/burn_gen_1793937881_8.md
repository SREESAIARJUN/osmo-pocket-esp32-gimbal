# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal and Bi-Directional DShot600 Design Package

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architectural premise:**
- The STM32H743 is the flight controller.
- Each ESC channel has its own MCU (e.g., an STM32G071 running AM32, or an AT32F421 running BLHeli_32-class firmware) and gate driver.
- The 48 kHz figure is the **ESC firmware PWM frequency**. The H743 never drives gates. It sends bi-directional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry.

I have no prior project data for "Job #22", so the inputs below are explicit assumptions. Every equation is parametric, so you can substitute datasheet values for your actual MOSFET and driver.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification
At any instant, two phase legs carry current. One leg PWMs its high-side (HS) and low-side (LS) complementarily, and one leg holds its LS fully on. Each leg is active for 2/3 of the electrical cycle.

The cycle-averaged conduction loss for each switch in one leg is:

$$P_{HS}=\tfrac{1}{3}D\,I^2R_{DS}$$

$$P_{LS}=\tfrac{1}{3}\left[(1-D)+1\right]I^2R_{DS}$$

Summing over 3 legs, the duty cycle D cancels:

$$P_{cond,motor}=3\cdot\tfrac{1}{3}\left[D+(1-D)+1\right]I^2R_{DS}=2\,I^2R_{DS(hot)}$$

This makes physical sense: exactly two FETs are always in series with the winding.

### 1.2 Ripple correction
For phase inductance L_φ and ripple ΔI, replace I² with:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2+\frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I=\frac{V_{bus}D(1-D)}{2L_\phi f_{sw}}$$

Example: L_φ = 20 µH, D = 0.5 gives ΔI ≈ 3.3 A. The correction is 0.9 A², which is negligible at 80 A.

### 1.3 Numbers per motor
- FET conduction: P_cond = 2 × 80² × 1.2 mΩ = **15.36 W**
- Worst single FET (the LS at low D, conducting up to 2/3 of the time): (2/3) × 80² × 1.2 mΩ = **5.12 W**
- PCB copper path (pad, plane and solder, ≈ 0.5 mΩ round trip): 80² × 0.5 mΩ = **3.2 W**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Gate-charge-based transition times

$$I_{g,on}=\frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_g}=\frac{8}{11}=0.73\text{ A}\;\Rightarrow\;t_{on}=\frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,on}}=20.6\text{ ns}$$

$$I_{g,off}=\frac{V_{pl}}{R_g}=0.36\text{ A}\;\Rightarrow\;t_{off}=41.3\text{ ns}$$

### 2.2 Loss components (one switching leg per motor at any time)

| Term | Expression | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V_bus·I·(t_on + t_off)·f_sw | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 61.9 ns × 48 k = **2.99 W** |
| Dead-time body-diode conduction | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw | 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k = **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V_bus·f_sw | **0.06 W** |
| C_oss charge/discharge | ½·C_oss·V²·f_sw × 2 | **0.05 W** |
| Gate drive (dissipated in driver and R_g) | Q_g·V_drv·f_sw × 2 | **0.07 W** |
| **Total switching-related** | | **≈ 3.78 W** |

### 2.3 Per-motor and board totals

| | Per motor | ×4 (all motors at 80 A burst) |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 15.36 W | 61.4 W |
| Switching-related | 3.78 W | 15.1 W |
