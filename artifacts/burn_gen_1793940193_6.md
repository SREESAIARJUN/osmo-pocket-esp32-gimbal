## 0. Feasibility Constraints

These issues affect the whole design, so they come first.

| Issue | Finding | Resolution adopted |
|---|---|---|
| **"Nano" + 80 A burst** | A 20×20 mm board cannot physically place 24 power FETs rated for 80 A, nor dissipate about 90 W even briefly. Real 20×20 4-in-1 boards are 20–35 A class. | The power stage is modeled on a **30.5×30.5 mm (≈36×36 mm PCB)** footprint. The FC "nano" can stay 20×20 and be stacked. |
| **Where 48 kHz switching happens** | The H743 does not switch the FETs. Each ESC channel's own MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32) generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 sends DShot600 commands only. | Partitioned accordingly in Sections 4–5. |
| **Battery bus current** | 4 × 80 A = **320 A** through the battery pads. This is a connector and pad problem (XT90 class), not just a board problem. | 80 A treated as **per-channel, ≤2 s burst**. Simultaneous 4-channel burst is the worst case analyzed. |

**Assumptions.** These are typical values; replace them with datasheet values for your selected parts.

- 6S LiPo: V_bus = 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal
- MOSFET: 40 V, PDFN 5×6
  - R_DS(on) = 1.0 mΩ at 25 °C
  - Q_g = 50 nC, Q_gd = 12 nC, Q_rr = 50 nC, C_oss = 1.5 nF, V_SD = 0.8 V
- Gate drive: V_drv = 10 V, R_g(total) = 4 Ω
- Dead time t_dt = 100 ns per edge
- PWM f_sw = 48 kHz, 6-step trapezoidal with synchronous rectification

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ** (use 1.5 mΩ for margin).

### 1.2 Current path

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, **exactly two FETs carry the phase current at all times**:

- PWM on: HS(A) + LS(B)
- PWM off: LS(A) (sync rectifier) + LS(B)

The total is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

### 1.3 PCB copper loss

Phase trace: 10 mm long, 5 mm wide, 2 oz (70 µm), 4 layers in parallel.

$$R_{layer} = \frac{\rho L}{w t} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\times0.01}{5\times10^{-3}\times70\times10^{-6}} = 0.49\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$R_{4L} \approx 0.12\ \text{m}\Omega \;\Rightarrow\; P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.12\text{m} \approx \mathbf{0.8\ W/ch}$$

Copper resistance also rises about 0.39 %/K. Add roughly 25 % at 85 °C.

### 1.4 Shunt loss

A 0.25 mΩ shunt on the shared bus at 320 A dissipates **25.6 W**. This is not viable.

**Use per-channel 0.2 mΩ shunts** (1.28 W each at 80 A) **or Hall sensing** (e.g., ACS772). The netlist below uses one 0.2 mΩ shunt per channel.
