## 0. Assumptions and Key Findings

I don't have prior Job #6 documentation, so this baseline fixes the following assumptions. Change any of them and re-run the equations; every result below is parameterized.

| Parameter | Assumed value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal | 80 A class implies 6S |
| "80 A burst" | Per motor, ≤ 10 s, all 4 motors simultaneously (worst case) | Industry convention |
| Form factor | 30.5 × 30.5 mm mounting, ~36 × 36 mm board | See finding 3 |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), 6-step trapezoidal | AM32 supports 24–48 kHz |
| MOSFET | 40 V N-ch, R_DS(on) = 1.2 mΩ typ @ 25 °C, Q_g = 40 nC, Q_rr = 40 nC, Q_oss = 50 nC | Representative 40 V PQFN 3.3×3.3 / 5×6 class |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q-class 3-phase driver, ~1.2 A source / 1.5 A sink, t_r + t_f ≈ 20 ns at the FET | |
| ESC MCU | 4 × AT32F421K8U7 running AM32 | See finding 1 |
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100), 400 MHz, running ArduPilot | |
| PCB | 8-layer, 2 oz outer + 3 × 2 oz inner power planes | |

**Finding 1: Partitioning.** The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors directly. ArduPilot does not run sensorless BLDC commutation, and bidirectional DShot presupposes a separate ESC processor that returns eRPM.
- The architecture is therefore H743 (ArduPilot) → BDShot600 → 4 independent AM32 ESC MCUs.
- Four independent MCUs also give fault isolation: one ESC brown-out does not take down the other three.

**Finding 2: Continuous vs. burst rating.** "80 A burst" on this board is a thermal-capacitance rating, not a steady-state one.
- The model below gives roughly **35–38 A continuous per motor** with prop-wash airflow.
- 80 A on all four motors is sustainable for about **3 s** from a warm (60 °C) start.
- The 10 s burst holds only if the four motors are not all at 80 A at once, or the board starts cold.
- I recommend specifying the rating as "80 A burst (single motor, 10 s) / 60 A burst (all four, 5 s) / 35 A continuous". The derivation is in §1.4.

**Finding 3: Board size.** A true 20 × 20 "nano" board cannot carry 24 power FETs at this rating.
- Copper area for 80 A needs ≥ 30.5 mm hole spacing.
- At 20 × 20 mm, derate to about 40 A burst.
