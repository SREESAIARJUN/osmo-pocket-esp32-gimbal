## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Design Assumptions

These points affect every calculation below.

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** It is the flight controller and generates bi-directional DShot600. Each ESC channel runs its own commutation MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). The H743 cannot run four sensorless FOC/6-step loops while also running ArduPilot's EKF3 at 400 Hz with sufficient timing margin.
2. **"80 A burst" means per-channel phase current**, not battery current. At 80 A × 4 channels and high duty, battery current approaches 250–320 A, which exceeds an XT60 and most nano-class packs. The model treats 80 A as the per-motor burst design point.
3. **Form factor.** True "nano" (20×20 mm) cannot support 4×80 A. The thermal mass is too small, and burst time drops below 1 s. This document uses a **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz** board. The 20×20 numbers are given in §3.5 for comparison.
4. **Component parameters** below are representative design-basis values for a 40 V PDFN3.3×3.3 MOSFET class. Replace them with your selected part's datasheet values before tape-out. The model is parameterized for that purpose.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]$$

At a design junction temperature of T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{ds}(110) = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.006 \times 85) = 1.51\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous PWM

In each of the six commutation sectors, current flows through exactly two FET positions in series:
- the PWM'd phase, through either the HS FET (on-time, D) or the complementary LS FET (off-time, 1−D), and
- the static return phase's LS FET (on for the whole sector).

The series path therefore always contains two R_ds(on), regardless of duty:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.51\times10^{-3} = 19.3\ \text{W per channel}$$

Additional copper loss comes from phase pads, bus planes and shunt. Assume about 0.25 mΩ of effective path resistance:

$$P_{Cu,ch} = 80^2 \times 0.25\times10^{-3} = 1.6\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 Self-consistent junction temperature (iteration)

Because R_ds depends on T_j and T_j depends on loss, solve iteratively:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{board} + P_{FET}\!\left(T_j^{(n)}\right)\cdot R_{\theta,jb}$$

With R_θ,jb ≈ 3 K/W (junction to board, including the via array) and T_board = 95 °C, this converges in 3 iterations to T_j ≈ 108 °C. This confirms that 110 °C was a valid assumption.
