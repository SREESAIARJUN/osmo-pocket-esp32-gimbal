# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Architecture

I don't have the Job #3 requirements document, so I state every assumption explicitly. Substitute your actual MOSFET datasheet values into the equations. The structure of the model holds regardless of part choice.

---

## 0. Architecture Decision (Read First)

**The H743 should not commutate the motors directly.** ArduPilot does not run sensorless BLDC/FOC for propulsion. The H743 has only two advanced timers with complementary outputs (TIM1, TIM8) plus HRTIM, which is not enough clean 6-PWM resources for 4×3 half-bridges alongside everything else.

The correct architecture is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir (4 wires)──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / AM32 or BLHeli_32)
                                                         │
                                               3× half-bridge drivers + 6 FETs each (×4)
```

The 48 kHz switching frequency is set in the ESC firmware (AM32 `PWM_FREQ` / BLHeli_32 PWM setting), not by ArduPilot.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

In any 60° sector, current flows from bus to ground through one leg's high-side FET (HS) and another leg's low-side FET (LS). With complementary PWM at duty D:

- **On interval (D):** current flows through HS_x and LS_y.
- **Off interval (1−D):** current freewheels through LS_x (synchronous) and LS_y.

In both intervals, exactly two FETs carry the full phase current I. Therefore the total conduction loss per ESC is:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.3 Per-device distribution (over one electrical cycle)

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,D\,I^2 R_{ds}, \qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)\,I^2 R_{ds} + \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2 R_{ds}$$

The low-side FET is the hot device. At full throttle (D→1), P_LS ≈ P_HS ≈ I²R/3.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A

| Condition | P_cond per ESC | Per FET (D→1) |
|---|---|---|
| T_j = 25 °C | 2 · 6400 · 1.0 mΩ = 12.8 W | 2.13 W |
| T_j = 125 °C | 2 · 6400 · 1.45 mΩ = **18.6 W** | **3.1 W** |

**Parasitic copper and shunt.** Assume a 0.3 mΩ phase/bus copper path plus a 0.25 mΩ shared shunt at 4 × 80 A:

- Copper per ESC: 80² × 0.3 mΩ ≈ 1.9 W
- Shunt: 320² × 0.25 mΩ = 25.6 W

The shunt figure is unacceptable. **Use per-ESC 0.5 mΩ shunts** (3.2 W each, on board copper rather than in a FET), or use FET R_ds sensing.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg hard-switches. The freewheeling FET in a synchronous scheme switches with near-zero voltage.

| Loss term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap (hard switching) | P_VI = ½ · V_bus · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw = ½ · 25.2 · 80 · 35 ns · 48 k | **1.69 W** |
| Reverse recovery | P_rr = Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw | 0.06 W |
| Output capacitance | P_oss = ½ · Q_oss · V_bus · f_sw | 0.04 W |
| Gate drive (2 FETs per leg) | P_g = 2 · Q_g · V_drv · f_sw (mostly in driver/R_g) | 0.06 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | P_dt = V_f · I · 2t_dt · f
