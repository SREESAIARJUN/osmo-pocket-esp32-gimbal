# Job #57: STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC), Nano Form Factor

## Engineering Design Basis: Thermal, Switching, DShot/DMA, Netlists

---

## 0. Architecture and Key Findings

Two issues in the brief change the design, so they come first.

**Finding 1: The H743 should not commutate the motors.**
The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Six-step or FOC commutation of 4 motors at 48 kHz PWM belongs on dedicated ESC MCUs running AM32 (e.g. STM32G071 or AT32F421 class), one per motor. The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600. This partitioning is what makes the DShot/DMA section meaningful. Bidirectional DShot also requires that the ESC firmware supports it, which AM32 does.

**Finding 2: "80 A burst" on a nano board is thermally a seconds-scale rating, not a continuous one.**
The math in §3 shows:

| Condition | Result |
|---|---|
| All 4 channels at 80 A | ~76 W dissipated, heating the board at ~20 K/s adiabatically |
| Sustainable continuous rating (prop wash, 110 °C board limit) | ≈ 30 A per channel |
| 80 A on all channels | **~3 s maximum** |

The burst spec should be stated as **"80 A / 1–2 s, single or dual channel"**.

## 2. Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

The current path during PWM-on is HS(A) → motor → LS(B). During PWM-off it is LS(A) (synchronous) → motor → LS(B).

Leg A conducts through either HS or LS for the whole period (D + (1−D) = 1), and leg B's LS is always on. Therefore conduction loss is **independent of duty cycle**:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds(hot)} = 2(80)^2(1.12\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{14.3\ W}$$

Copper loss on the same path:

$$P_{cu} = 2\,I^2 R_{cu} = 2(6400)(0.15\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{1.9\ W}$$

**Electrothermal feedback.** R_ds rises with temperature, so the model is:

$$P_{cond}(T_j) = 2I^2R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006/K$$

Iterate this with the thermal model in §3.

### 2.2 Switching Loss at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches hard (2 transitions per period).

**V–I overlap:**
$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(40\text{ns})(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery** (the LS body diode conducts during dead time and then recovers):
$$P_{rr} = V Q_{rr} f_{sw} = 25.2(40\text{n})(48\text{k}) = 0.048\ W$$

**C_oss:**
$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss}V^2 f_{sw} = 0.5(1.5\text{n})(635)(48\text{k}) = 0.023\ W$$

**Dead-time diode conduction:**
$$P_{dt} = V_f I (2t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.9(80)(200\text{n})(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{0.69\ W}$$
