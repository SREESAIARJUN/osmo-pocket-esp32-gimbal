**Revision:** A (preliminary). **Status:** Engineering model. All component values are reference selections and must be verified against the vendor datasheets and the final layout before release.

## 0. Executive Summary

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Per-ESC loss at 80 A burst (Tj = 125 °C) | **~24.5 W** (conduction 19.2 W + switching 2.1 W + copper 3.2 W) |
| Worst case, all 4 channels at 80 A | **~98 W** into a ~1.3 J/K board |
| Board heating rate at 4×80 A | **~75–80 K/s**. Burst must be ≤ ~0.6–0.8 s from 40 °C start. |
| Steady-state continuous capability (20×20 mm, prop-wash cooled) | **~25–30 A per channel**, not 80 A |
| Battery current at 4×80 A, 6S | **320 A**. This exceeds XT60, pigtail, and pad ratings. |
| DShot600 bit time | 1.667 µs, frame 26.7 µs. Bi-dir reply at 750 kbit/s GCR. |
| Bi-dir DShot DMA streams required | 2 TIMx_UP + 2 TIMx_CHy (input capture) = **4 streams** of 16 |

> **Design-review flag:** "80 A burst" on a 20×20 mm nano board is only defensible as **≤ 1 s, one or two channels at a time**, with a firmware current limit. Marketing should not specify 80 A for four simultaneous channels. The thermal math in §3 shows why.

## 1. System Architecture

```
 6S LiPo (25.2 V max) ──► VBAT bus (XT60 / pads)
      │
      ├──► 4× ESC channel: [AT32F421 / AM32] → [FD6288Q 3-ph gate driver] → [6× N-MOSFET half-bridges] → Motor
      │         ▲ DShot600 bidirectional (single wire, per motor)
      │         │
      └──► 5 V/10 V bucks ──► STM32H743VIT6 (ArduPilot, 400 MHz)
                              ├─ TIM3_CH3/CH4, TIM4_CH1/CH2 → M1..M4
                              ├─ ADC: VBAT divider, ESC total current (shunt amp)
                              └─ SPI IMU, SDMMC, UARTs
```

**Why the H743 does not drive the FETs directly.** The H743 runs the ArduPilot flight stack. Each motor gets a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 firmware. That MCU handles commutation, 48 kHz PWM, and the bi-directional DShot reply. This is industry-standard partitioning: field-oriented or six-step commutation at 48 kHz is not run on the flight controller.

### 2.2 Conduction loss

In six-step commutation with **synchronous rectification**, the motor current always flows through exactly **two** FETs in series:

- During the ON interval, it flows through HS(phase A) and LS(phase B).
- During the freewheel interval, it flows through LS(phase A) and LS(phase B).

The total therefore does not depend on duty cycle D:

$$
P_{cond,ESC} = I^2 \cdot 2 \cdot R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = I^2 \cdot 2 \cdot k_T R_{DS,25}
$$

$$
P_{cond,ESC} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{19.2\ W}
$$

Each FET conducts on average 1/3 of the electrical cycle. Per FET:

$$
P_{cond,FET,avg} = \frac{19.2}{6} = 3.2\ \text{W}
$$

The **instantaneous** peak occurs in the LS FET of the fixed commutation phase during a 60° sector:

$$
P_{FET,peak} = 80^2 \times 1.5\times10^{-3} = 9.6\ \text{W}
$$

### 2.3 Switching loss (only one leg PWM-switches at a time)

**Hard-switching V–I overlap:**

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}
$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction (two edges per period):**

$$
P_{DT} = V_F \cdot I \cdot 2 t_{dead} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}
$$

**Reverse recovery:**

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}
$$

**Output capacitance:**

$$
P_{oss} \approx Q_{oss} V f_{sw} = 30\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.036\ W}
$$
