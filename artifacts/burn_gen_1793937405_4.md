# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #73): Power-Stage Thermal Model and Bi-Directional DShot Timer/DMA Architecture

I have no prior project files for Job #73, so this document states its assumptions explicitly. Every number below derives from a named parameter. Substitute your actual MOSFET datasheet values and the equations still hold.

---

## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** drive the MOSFETs directly. In an AIO "nano" design the partitioning is:

| Block | Device (reference choice) | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) @ 400 MHz | ArduPilot, sends DShot600, receives bidirectional eRPM telemetry |
| ESC MCU ×4 (or ×1 quad MCU) | AT32F421K8U7 / STM32G071 running AM32 | Commutation, 48 kHz PWM, BEMF zero-crossing |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) | High-side and low-side gate drive |
| Power stage | 6 FETs per motor (12 if paralleled) | Three-phase bridge |

So "48 kHz switching" happens on the ESC MCU and bridge. "DShot600 DMA" happens on the H743. The two domains connect only through 4 signal lines and a shared ground and power rail.

**Commutation scheme.** Assumed AM32-style trapezoidal commutation. In each 60° sector one leg PWMs complementarily (HS at duty D, LS synchronous rectification at 1 − D). A second leg's LS is held fully on, and the third leg floats for BEMF sensing.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Per-motor total

In any sector, current passes through exactly two conducting switch positions in series:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I^2 R_{ds,hot}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds,hot} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds,hot}
$$

This result is independent of duty, ignoring the dead-time fraction.

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{23.0\ W}
$$

### 2.2 Per-FET average over an electrical cycle

Each leg spends 1/3 of the cycle as the PWM leg, 1/3 as the static-LS leg, and 1/3 floating.

$$
P_{HS} = \frac{D}{3} I^2 R \qquad P_{LS} = \frac{1-D}{3} I^2 R + \frac{1}{3} I^2 R
$$

With I²R = 11.52 W at 80 A:

| D | P_HS | P_LS (worst FET) |
|---|---|---|
| 0.95 | 3.65 W | 4.03 W |
| 0.50 | 1.92 W | 5.76 W |
| 0.10 | 0.38 W | **7.30 W** |

**Takeaway:** the low-side FETs are the thermal hot spot, worst at partial throttle with high current (e.g., punch-outs with a large prop at moderate duty).

### 2.3 Paralleled FETs (2 per position, 24 FETs total)

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2 \cdot I^2 \cdot \frac{R}{2} = 11.5\ \text{W} \qquad P_{LS,worst} = 1.83\ \text{W per FET}
$$
