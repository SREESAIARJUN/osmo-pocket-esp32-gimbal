## 1. System Architecture

The 48 kHz switching happens on the ESC's own MCUs, not on the H743. The H743 only sends DShot600 commands and receives eRPM telemetry back.

```
 6S ─┬──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
     │                                                          │
 ┌───▼──────────────── 4-in-1 ESC ───────────────────────────┐  │
```

## 2. Power-Stage Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 2.1 Conduction losses

In 6-step synchronous PWM, current always flows through exactly two FETs in series:
- the active high-side and active low-side during the on-time;
- the low-side plus the synchronous-rectifying low-side during the off-time.

So conduction loss is effectively independent of duty cycle D:

$$
P_{cond}(T_j) = I_{pk}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{DS}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

R_DS depends on T_j, and T_j depends on P. Solve the two together (iterate, or solve in closed form):

$$
T_j = T_{pcb} + P_{FET}\,R_{\theta jc} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{converges in 2–3 iterations}
$$

**Numbers at I_pk = 80 A:**

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond (2 FETs) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W |
| 100 °C | 1.79 mΩ | 22.9 W |
| 125 °C | 1.98 mΩ | 25.3 W |

Copper and shunt in the current path add more. Assume 0.25 mΩ shunt + 0.35 mΩ trace/via/pad (2 oz, 8 layer):

$$
P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.60\,\text{m}\Omega = 3.8\ \text{W}
$$

### 2.2 Switching losses at 48 kHz

Only one FET per commutation state hard-switches; the synchronous FET switches at near-zero voltage.

**(a) V-I overlap (hard-switched FET):**
$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{pk}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = \tfrac12(25.2)(80)(24\text{ ns})(48\text{ kHz}) = \mathbf{1.16\ W}
$$

**(b) Output capacitance:**
$$
P_{oss} \approx Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}
$$
