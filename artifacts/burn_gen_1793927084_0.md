# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC (Job #37): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA, and Netlist

## 0. Architectural Ground Rules and Feasibility Flags

Three issues set the boundaries for the rest of this document.

1. **The H743 does not drive the power stage.** It runs ArduPilot only. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 (AT32F421 or STM32G071), its own 3-phase gate driver, and 6 MOSFETs. ArduPilot cannot commutate a BLDC, and 48 kHz complementary PWM on 12 half-bridges would consume most of the H7's advanced timers.
2. **The 80A burst is per channel, so the board can draw up to 320A.** That exceeds an XT60 (about 60A continuous), any practical common shunt, and the copper of a 20×20 or 30.5×30.5 board. This design treats 80A as a single-channel transient. Section 3 shows that 4×80A is time-limited to about 5 s and that the board has **no thermal steady state** at that load (thermal runaway criterion exceeded).
3. **The physics argues for 30.5×30.5 mm.** If "nano" means 20×20 mm, the 80A rating is not credible. The calculations use a 30×30 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz board. Re-derive them with the equations below if you change the format.
