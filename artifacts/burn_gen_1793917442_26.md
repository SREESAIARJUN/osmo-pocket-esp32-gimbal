# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Model (Job #28)

## 0. Read This First: Feasibility Findings

These findings come out of the math below. They drive the architecture, so they are listed up front.

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | 80 A per channel on 4 channels draws ≈ 290 A of battery current at high duty. That is about 5× an XT60 rating. | "80 A burst" must be specified as a **≤ 5–7 s single- or dual-motor transient**, not a 4-motor simultaneous rating. |
| F2 | Total dissipation at the 4×80 A burst is ≈ 76 W into a board with ≈ 7–8 J/K heat capacity. That is roughly a +100 K rise in 10 s. | The board is **burst-limited by thermal mass, not by MOSFET ratings**. |
| F3 | "Nano" (20×20 mm) cannot physically carry 24 power FETs plus 4 ESC MCUs plus the H743 at 80 A. | The model below assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm AIO** (36×36 mm outline). On 20×20 mm, derate to about 35–45 A burst. |
| F4 | The H743 is the *flight controller*. Each ESC channel still needs its own motor MCU (e.g. AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). The H743 does not commutate motors. | DShot600 runs H743 → 4 ESC MCUs over on-board traces. |
| F5 | The H743 shares a board with about 76 W of heat. | IMU and MCU need thermal isolation (split-plane or slot cut). An IMU heater/temperature-calibration strategy is mandatory. |

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path

With 6-step commutation and complementary PWM, the phase current always flows through exactly **two** FETs in series:
- During PWM-on: HS of phase A and LS of phase B.
- During PWM-off: LS of phase A (synchronous) and LS of phase B.

So the path resistance is 2·R_ds regardless of duty cycle D.

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]
$$

### 2.2 Ripple correction

The RMS current including inductor ripple is:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}
$$

with ripple

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-E_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}
$$

For a typical 5-inch-class motor with L_ph ≈ 15 µH, near full throttle (D ≈ 0.9, E ≈ 21 V):

$$
\Delta I \approx \frac{4.2 \times 0.9}{15\,\mu\text{H} \times 48\,\text{kHz}} \approx 5.3\ \text{A}
$$

The ripple term is 5.3²/12 ≈ 2.3 A², against 6400 A². This is negligible (< 0.05 %).

### 2.3 Numbers at T_j = 125 °C

$$
R_{ds} = 0.80 \times 1.5 = 1.20\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.20\,\text{m} = 15.36\ \text{W per channel}
$$

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period (HS and LS complementary share is folded in). Averaged over a cycle, that gives **≈ 2.56 W per FET**.

### 2.4 Electrothermal fixed point

R_ds depends on T_j, and T_j depends on power. Iterate:

$$
T_j = T_{ref} + P_{fet}(T_j)\cdot R_{th}
$$

$$
P_{fet} = P_0\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]
$$
