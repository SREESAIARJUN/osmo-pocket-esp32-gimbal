## 0. Scope, Architectural Clarifications & Design Basis

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 does not drive the power stage directly. In this design:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100) or VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot | Attitude control. Generates bidirectional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC (×4 channels) | 4× AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) | AM32 | 6-step commutation at 48 kHz complementary PWM |
| Gate drive | 4× FD6288Q (3-phase half-bridge driver) | — | Drives 24 N-MOSFETs |
| Power stage | 24× 40 V N-MOSFET, PDFN 3.3×3.3 | — | 4 × 3-phase bridges |

The 48 kHz PWM frequency is set in the AM32 firmware. The H743 only sets the throttle command and telemetry path.

### 0.2 Feasibility flag (read before anything else)

A "nano" form factor (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm mounting) rated at **80 A burst per channel** is at the edge of physical possibility.

- The analysis below shows about **29 W dissipated per channel at 80 A**, and about **114 W total** if all four channels burst at once.
- On a board with roughly 4 J/K of lumped heat capacity, that gives a burst window of **about 1–2 seconds**, not a continuous rating.

I define the rating accordingly:

> **80 A burst = ≤1.0 s per channel, ≤10 % duty cycle, 6S max, T_ambient ≤ 40 °C, ≥ 5 m/s prop-wash airflow.**
> Recommended continuous rating: **35–40 A/channel** (derived in §3.5).

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

At any commutation sector, two phases are active. One high-side FET is PWM'd at duty D, and the complementary low-side on that leg conducts during (1−D). The opposite leg's low-side FET is held on.

The motor current I_ph therefore always flows through exactly **two channels in series** (excluding dead time):

- During the on-time: HS(a) and LS(b).
- During the off-time: LS(a), synchronously rectified, and LS(b).

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]$$

At the design point T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{DS(on)}(125) = 1.3\,\text{mΩ} \times 1.60 = 2.08\,\text{mΩ}$$

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

Assuming low ripple (motor inductance ≫ L_crit at 48 kHz):

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

At I_ph = 80 A:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 6400 \times 2.08\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{26.6\ W}$$

**Per-FET distribution over one electrical revolution.** Each leg is active for 2/3 of the period, split between "PWM leg" and "return leg" sectors. For a high-side FET, averaged over a revolution:

$$P_{HS} = I^2 R\left[\tfrac{1}{3}D\right]$$

For the worst-case low-side FET:

$$P_{LS} = I^2 R\left[\tfrac{1}{3}(1-D) + \tfrac{1}{3}\right]$$
