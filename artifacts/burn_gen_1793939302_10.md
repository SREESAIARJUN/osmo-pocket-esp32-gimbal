## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

Nothing in the brief specifies the MOSFET, battery voltage, or burst duration, so I have fixed the assumptions below. Every number that follows changes if these change, so replace them with your BOM values.

**Architecture.** This assumes the standard AIO split. Two different MCUs do two different jobs:

- **STM32H743 (flight controller), running ArduPilot.** It produces 4× bidirectional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry.
- **4× ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 / STM32G071, running AM32 or BLHeli_32).** Each runs commutation and drives its own gate driver and 6 FETs.

The H743 does **not** commutate the motors. ArduPilot has no native 6-step motor-control path, and bidirectional DShot only makes sense FC→ESC.
