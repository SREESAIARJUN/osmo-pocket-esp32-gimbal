## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Caveats

**Architecture assumed.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller. It runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs. Each ESC MCU (AT32F421/STM32G071 class running AM32, or equivalent) generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM.

The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs directly. ArduPilot has no motor-commutation firmware. A single H743 doing 4× sensorless commutation plus flight control would also be an unvalidated, high-risk design.

**Component values are representative.** They describe a "40 V, ~1.5 mΩ DFN3.3×3.3 class" MOSFET and an FD6288-class gate driver. Every number marked ⚠ must be replaced with values from the datasheet of the part you actually procure before you sign off.

**Operating point.**

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery (6S, max) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current, per channel | I_b | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| Gate drive voltage | V_drv | 10 V |
| Ambient | T_a | 40 °C |
| Board | 20×20 mm "nano", 6-layer, 2 oz outer / 1 oz inner | |

**Headline finding.** On a 20×20 mm board, 80 A is a burst rating measured in roughly 1–2 seconds, not a continuous rating. The math below shows why. Write the datasheet rating accordingly. Firmware current and temperature limits are mandatory, not optional.
