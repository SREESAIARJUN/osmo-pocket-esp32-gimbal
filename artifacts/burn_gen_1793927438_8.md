# STM32H743 AIO 4-in-1 ESC (Job #56): Systems Architecture Package

**Scope:** Electrothermal model at 80 A burst, switching-loss model at 48 kHz, ArduPilot Bi-Directional DShot600 timer/DMA plan, reference netlists, and thermal verdict.

> **Headline finding:** The math below shows that **80 A per motor (320 A aggregate) cannot be sustained on a nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) AIO for more than about 1–2 s** without exceeding a safe junction temperature, even with good MOSFETs. I give the derivation, the allowable burst envelope, and design changes. Treat any marketing spec of "80A burst" on this form factor as a sub-second rating at best.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
In 6-step drive, two phases conduct at any instant. During the PWM on-time, current flows through high-side (HS) phase A and low-side (LS) phase B. During off-time, synchronous rectification moves the freewheel current to LS phase A and LS phase B.

The motor current therefore always sees **two R_DS(on) in series**. This holds regardless of duty cycle, with dead time excepted.

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{ds} = 1.0\,\text{mΩ}\,(1+0.0045\cdot100) = 1.45\,\text{mΩ}$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 80^2 \cdot 2 \cdot 1.45\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{18.6\ W}$$

### 1.3 Distribution across devices
Each of the 6 FETs carries current for one third of the electrical period. Positions are rotated, so the time-averaged loss is shared:

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{18.6}{6} = 3.1\ \text{W (average)}$$

The instantaneous loss in each conducting FET is I²R = 9.3 W. This matters for thermal ripple at low eRPM; see §4.4.

### 1.4 Board copper (often larger than expected)
Aggregate battery current is set by duty cycle D:

$$I_{bat} \approx D \cdot \sum I_{ph}$$

At D = 1 this is up to 320 A.

Example: a power plane of 4 layers × 2 oz (70 µm), with an effective path of 15 mm long × 18 mm wide.

$$R = \frac{\rho L}{A} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\cdot 0.015}{4\cdot 70\times10^{-6}\cdot 0.018} \approx 51\ \mu\Omega$$

The temperature-adjusted value is about 65 µΩ.
