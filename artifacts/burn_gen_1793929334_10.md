## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R = 5.6 Ω, L = 2.1 mH, λ_m = 0.0117 V·s/rad |
| Torque constant | K_t = 1.5·p·λ_m ≈ 0.123 N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701), read at 20 kHz |
| MCU | STM32G474 @ 170 MHz, FPU, 3-shunt current sensing |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, separate SPI buses, gyro and accel ODR 8 kHz |
| Bus | V_dc = 12 V |

Cascade timing:

```
 20 kHz  ── Current (FOC) PI loop  [i_d, i_q]       BW ω_c = 2π·1500 rad/s
  8 kHz  ── IMU sampling + vibration filters + dual fusion
  4 kHz  ── Velocity PI (inertial rate, from gyro)  BW ω_v = 2π·150 rad/s
  1 kHz  ── Attitude estimator + angle P/PI loop    BW ω_a = 2π·30 rad/s
```

Separation rule: ω_c ≥ 5·ω_v and ω_v ≥ 4·ω_a. Each inner loop then appears as unity gain plus a small lag to the loop above it.

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke transform:
$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_off:
$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant and decoupling

The motor in the rotor frame is:
$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

Feed-forward decoupling terms:
$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

With these applied, each axis reduces to G(s) = 1/(Ls + R).
