## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology

In trapezoidal six-step control with complementary PWM, at any instant during one 60° sector:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** the high-side FET conducts for a fraction D of each cycle. The low-side FET conducts synchronously for 1−D.
- **Phase B (return leg):** the low-side FET is held fully on.
- **Phase C:** floating, used for BEMF sensing.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

This corresponds to roughly 1.5× at 125 °C, which is typical for trench MOSFETs.

### 1.3 Channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms,HS}^2 R_{ds} + I_{rms,LS,sync}^2 R_{ds} + I_{rms,LS,return}^2 R_{ds}$$

With phase ripple Δi_pp superimposed on DC current I:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta i_{pp}^2}{12}$$

The PWM-leg terms sum to D + (1−D) = 1, so the result is **independent of duty**:

$$\boxed{P_{cond} = 2\,R_{ds}(T_j)\left(I^2+\tfrac{\Delta i_{pp}^2}{12}\right)}$$

**Ripple estimate.** With L_phase = 15 µH, worst case D = 0.5, and 6S:

$$\Delta i_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus} D(1-D)}{L f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \cdot 0.25}{15\times10^{-6}\cdot 48\times10^{3}} = 8.75\ \text{A}$$

This contributes only 6.4 A², which is negligible against 6400 A².

**Numeric results at I = 80 A:**

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per channel |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.000 mΩ | 12.8 W |
| 100 °C | 1.375 mΩ | 17.6 W |
| 125 °C | 1.500 mΩ | 19.2 W |

### 1.4 PCB copper loss (same current path)

For one 2 oz layer (R_□ ≈ 0.25 mΩ/□) and a phase path of about 4 squares across 3 paralleled layers:

$$R_{Cu} \approx \frac{4\cdot0.25}{3}\ \text{mΩ} \approx 0.33\ \text{mΩ}$$

$$P_{Cu} = 2\cdot80^2\cdot0.33\,\text{mΩ} \approx 4.3\ \text{W per channel}$$

Copper also has a temperature coefficient of +0.39 %/K.
