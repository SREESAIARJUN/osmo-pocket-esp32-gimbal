# STM32H743 Nano Flight Controller + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Model

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I don't have the original Job #26 requirements, so this model rests on stated assumptions. Each one is a parameter you can replace with datasheet values. The numbers below are worked examples, not certified ratings.

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU running ArduPilot. It is not the ESC controller. A 4-in-1 ESC uses four independent commutation MCUs, typically running AM32 or BLHeli_32/Bluejay. I model the system as two parts:

- **FC:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot, which generates bidirectional DShot600.
- **ESC:** 4 × (AT32F421/STM32G071-class MCU, AM32 firmware) + 4 × FD6288Q-class 3-phase gate driver + 24 × N-MOSFET.

> **Feasibility warning.** On a 20×20 board, 80A per channel is physically achievable only as a short burst. Section 3 quantifies this: roughly 1.2 s with all four channels at 80A, and about 15–20A per channel continuous without a heatsink. If marketing wants "80A," it should be specified as a burst rating with a stated duration.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology

The ESC uses 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation with synchronous rectification. At any instant, two legs carry the phase current I:

- One leg is the PWM leg. Current alternates between its high-side switch (for duty D) and low-side switch (for 1−D).
- The other leg is the return leg, with its low-side switch held on.

So at every instant, current flows through exactly two MOSFET channels in series. The loss is therefore independent of duty cycle, apart from dead-time effects, which are covered in §2.

### 1.2 Equations

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2\,\big(2R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{Cu} + R_{shunt}\big)$$

**Ripple correction.** At 48 kHz with a typical motor inductance of about 10 µH, the ripple current is ΔI ≈ 10–15 A peak-to-peak. RMS current then becomes:

$$I_{rms} = \sqrt{I^2+\Delta I^2/12}$$

This adds less than 0.3% at 80 A, so it is neglected.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

- R_ds(125) = 0.9 × 1.45 = **1.305 mΩ**
- MOSFET loss: 80² × 2 × 1.305 m = **16.7 W**
- Copper loss, assuming R_Cu ≈ 0.5 mΩ (phase pours plus vias): **3.2 W**
- Shunt loss (common 0.25 mΩ, carrying battery current I·D, about 50% duty): ≈ **0.8 W**

**Conduction subtotal ≈ 20.7 W per channel
