## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs `p = 7`), R ≈ 5 Ω, L ≈ 2 mH, K_t ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Encoders | 14-bit absolute magnetic (MT6701 / AS5048A), SPI |
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class, FPU, 3-shunt current sensing |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera payload (SPI, separate CS), mounted with known rotations R₁, R₂ and lever arms r₁, r₂ |
| Kinematic chain | Base → Yaw (z) → Roll (x) → Pitch (y) → Camera |

**Loop rates**

| Loop | Rate | Trigger | Feedback |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | ADC-injected ISR, PWM center | Phase currents, encoder angle |
| IMU sampling | 8 kHz | DRDY EXTI → SPI DMA | ICM-42688 ×2 |
| Velocity | 4 kHz | Timer | Gyro (filtered, decimated) projected to joints, with encoder PLL as fallback |
| Angle / attitude | 1 kHz | Timer | Fused quaternion |

**Bandwidth separation rule:** each outer loop runs at ≤ 1/5 to 1/10 of the bandwidth of the loop inside it.
- ω_c,current ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s
- ω_c,vel ≈ 2π·150 rad/s
- ω_c,angle ≈ 2π·20 rad/s

## 1. FOC Equations

### 1.1 Electrical angle
$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

θ_off is found at commissioning: inject d-axis voltage V_d at θ_e = 0, let the rotor settle, and record θ_m.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (power-invariant amplitude form, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform
$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant (PMSM dq model)
$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L \dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$
$$\tau = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q$$
