## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Coordinate transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a, \qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform** with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e, \qquad
v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 PMSM model in the dq frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda
$$

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32\,p\,\lambda\,i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$

### 1.3 Current PI controller with decoupling

The plant per axis, after decoupling, is G(s) = 1/(Ls + R).

Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole (pole-zero cancellation). The closed loop then becomes first-order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c, \qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\frac{I(s)}{I^*(s)} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

**Bandwidth limit.** The digital loop has roughly 1.5·T_c of delay (one sample of compute plus half a PWM period). Choose:

$$
\omega_c \le \frac{1}{3\,T_c}\approx 2\pi\cdot 1\,\text{kHz}
$$

This gives about 60° phase margin.

**Voltage commands** with cross-coupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L\, i_q
$$

$$
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L\, i_d + \omega_e\lambda
$$

**Saturation.** Limit the voltage vector to the inscribed SVPWM circle, |v| ≤ V_dc/√3. Give d-axis priority:

$$
v_d^* \leftarrow \text{clamp}\!\left(v_d^*,\,\pm V_{max}\right), \qquad
v_q^* \leftarrow \text{clamp}\!\left(v_q^*,\,\pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^{*2}}\right)
$$

**Anti-windup.** Use back-calculation with gain K_aw = 1/K_p (or simply conditional integration):

$$
x_{k+1} = x_k + K_i T_c\, e_k + K_{aw}\,(u_{sat}-u_{unsat})
