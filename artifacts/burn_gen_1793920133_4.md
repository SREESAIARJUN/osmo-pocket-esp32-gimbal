## 0. Architecture Clarification (Read First)

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller (FC)**. It does **not** commutate the motors. A production 4-in-1 ESC uses **four independent ESC MCUs** running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM and decode bidirectional DShot600.

```
┌────────────────────── FC (20×20 or 30×30 stack) ──────────────────────┐
│ STM32H743 @ 400 MHz (ArduPilot) │ IMU(SPI) │ Baro(I2C) │ OSD │ UARTs    │
│ TIM3_CH3/CH4, TIM5_CH1/CH2 ──► M1..M4 bidir DShot600 (single wire ea.) │
└───────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                │ 8-pin JST-SH: VBAT, GND, M1–M4, CURR, TLM
┌───────────────▼────────────── 4-in-1 ESC ─────────────────────────────┐
│ 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32) → 4× 3-ph gate driver          │
│ → 24× N-MOSFET (6 per motor) → phases A/B/C × 4                        │
│ Shared bus: 6S (25.2 V max), bulk + ceramic decoupling, shunt + amp    │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Feasibility flag.** 80 A per channel on a "nano" (20×20 mm mounting) ESC is **burst-only**. Section 2 shows that it is thermally limited to roughly 1–3 s. A continuous rating near 35–45 A is the realistic number for this form factor. The derivation below makes that explicit, so marketing claims can be defended.

**Commutation model:** 6-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) PWM, which is the AM32 default. At any instant, one high-side (HS) and one low-side (LS) device carry the phase current. On every PWM edge, one leg hard-switches.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

### 2.2 Per-device conduction loss

In the active commutation step with duty D:

- **HS (PWM leg):** conducts during D·T → $P_{HS} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,D$
- **Synchronous LS on the PWM leg:** conducts during (1−D)·T → $P_{LS,sync} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)(1-D)$
- **Return leg LS (static on):** conducts 100% of the step → $P_{LS,ret} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$

Total per ESC at any instant, independent of D (because synchronous rectification fills the off-time):

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)}$$

### 2.3 Self-consistent junction temperature (iteration)

At 80 A:

| Iteration | T_j assumed | R_DS | P_cond (ESC) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W |
| 1 | 100 °C | 1.61 mΩ | 20.6 W |
| 2 | 125 °C | 1.74 mΩ | **22.3 W** |

Each 6-step sector distributes the load across devices. Each MOSFET is a "return LS" for 1/3 of the electrical cycle, idle for 1/3, and PWM-leg for 1/3. The **worst-case single device** therefore averages:

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R + \tfrac{1}{3}\cdot\tfrac{1}{2}I^2R \approx 0.5\,I^2R = 5.6\text{ W @ 125 °C}$$

At low eRPM (punch-out from hover), the electrical period lengthens toward the thermal time constant of the die. Size for the **instantaneous** 11.1 W per device (I²R at 125 °C) on any device stalled in the return-leg role.

## 3. Switching Loss Model @ 48 kHz

### 3.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap (one hard edge pair per period)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 20\text{ ns} × 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{0.968\ W}$$

### 3.2 Reverse recovery (sync LS body diode → HS turn-on)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{ nC} × 25.2 × 48\text{ k} = \mathbf{0.036\ W}$$

Q_rr increases with di/dt and T_j. Derate ×2 at hot: **0.07 W**.

### 3.3 C_oss charge/discharge

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2} C_{oss} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} × 2 = 1.5\text{ nF} × 635 × 48\text{ k} = \mathbf{0.046\ W}$$

### 3.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction (two dead times per period)

$$P_{dt} = V_F I (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 × 80 × 100\text{ ns} × 48\text{ k} = \mathbf{0.307\ W}$$

### 3.5 Gate drive (2 switching FETs per ESC, dissipated in driver plus R_G)

$$P_g = 2 Q_g V_{GD} f_{sw} = 2 × 40\text{ n} × 10 × 48\text{ k} = \mathbf{0.038\ W}$$

### 3.6 Switching loss summary per ESC at 80 A

| Mechanism | Loss |
|---|---|
| V·I overlap | 0.97 W |
| Q_rr (hot) | 0.07 W |
| C_oss | 0.05 W |
| Dead-time diode | 0.31 W |
| Gate drive | 0.04 W |
| **Total switching** | **1.44 W** |

### 3.7 Frequency trade-off

Switching loss scales linearly with f_sw:

| f_sw | P_sw | Conduction share | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24 kHz | 0.72 W | 97% | Audible whine, higher current ripple |
| **48 kHz** | **1.44 W** | **94%** | Chosen: smooth low-throttle, quiet |
| 96 kHz | 2.88 W | 89% | Marginal gain, more EMI |
