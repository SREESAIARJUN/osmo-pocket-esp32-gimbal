## 1. Field-Oriented Control: the Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, two shunts, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θₑ = p·θₘ − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 SVPWM by Min/Max Zero-Sequence Injection

This method is equivalent to sector-based SVPWM and has no branches.

1. Compute the inverse Clarke phase voltages:
$$
v_a = v_\alpha,\quad v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$
2. Inject the zero-sequence offset:
$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_a,v_b,v_c) + \min(v_a,v_b,v_c)\big)
$$
3. Convert to duty:
$$
d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

The linear range is |v| ≤ V_bus/√3.

### 1.3 Current PI Gains by Pole-Zero Cancellation

**Plant.** Per axis (d or q), after decoupling:

$$
G_i(s) = \frac{1}{Ls + R}
$$

**Controller.**

$$
C_i(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s}
$$

Choose K_i / K_p = R / L so the controller zero cancels the electrical pole. The closed loop then becomes first-order:

$$
\frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_{c}}{s+\omega_c}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\boxed{K_p = L\,\omega_c,\quad K_i = R\,\omega_c}
$$

**Bandwidth choice.** Use ω_c = 2π·1.5 kHz. This stays below f_s/10 (20 kHz/10 = 2 kHz) to tolerate the 1.5-sample delay.

**Decoupling (cross-coupling feedforward)**, using electrical speed ωₑ = p·ωₘ:

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L\, i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L\, i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m,\quad \lambda_m = \frac{2K_t}{3p}
$$

**Voltage limiting.** Use circular limiting with d-priority:

1. Clamp v_d to ±V_max.
2. Compute v_q,max = √(V_max² − v_d²).
3. Clamp v_q to ±v_q,max.

### 1.4 Discretization and Anti-Windup

Use the backward-Euler integrator with back-calculation anti-windup, where u_sat is the limited output:

$$
I[k] = I[k-1] + K_i T_s\, e[k] + K_{aw}\,(u_{sat}[k-1] - u[k-1]),\qquad K_{aw} = \frac{1}{K_p}\cdot \frac{K_i T_s}{1}
$$

In practice, K_aw = K_i·T_s / K_p, which corresponds to a tracking time constant T_t = K_p / K_i.

## 2. Cascaded Angle → Velocity Control

### 2.1 Mechanical Plant

$$
J\dot\omega = K_t i_q - B\omega - \tau_{cog}(\theta) - \tau_{dist}
$$

With the current loop treated as ideal (ω_c ≫ ω_v), the velocity plant is:

$$
G_v(s) = \frac{K_t}{Js + B} \approx \frac{K_t}{Js}
