## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

At any instant one bridge leg (A) is PWM-switching and one leg (B) has its low side held on.

| PWM interval | Conducting devices | Number of devices |
|---|---|---|
| ON (duty D) | HS_A + LS_B | 2 |
| OFF (1 − D) | LS_A (synchronous rectification) + LS_B | 2 |

Two MOSFETs therefore always carry the phase current I. This makes per-channel conduction loss independent of duty cycle, to first order:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

### 1.2 Phase-current ripple correction

Motor phase inductance smooths the current, but at 48 kHz there is residual ripple. With L_ph ≈ 15 µH (typical for 5" class motors), at D = 0.5 and V_bus = 25.2 V:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{15\mu \times 48k} \approx 8.75\ \text{A}$$

The RMS current includes the ripple term:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 6.4 \approx 6406\ \text{A}^2$$

The ripple adds only about 0.1%, so it is negligible at 80 A. It matters only at light load.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds(on) | P_cond per channel | P_cond for 4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 0.80 mΩ | 2 × 6406 × 0.8 m = **10.2 W** | 41.0 W |
| 100 °C | 1.10 mΩ | **14.1 W** | 56.4 W |
| 125 °C | 1.20 mΩ | **15.4 W** | 61.5 W |

### 1.4 Electrothermal feedback

R_ds(on) rises with temperature, so loss and temperature feed each other:

$$T_j = T_a + R_{th}\cdot P(T_j) \;\Rightarrow\; T_j = \frac{T_a + R_{th}P_0(1-25\alpha)}{1 - R_{th}P_0\alpha}$$

Thermal runaway occurs if R_th · P_0 · α ≥ 1, where P_0 is the 25 °C loss.

For a steady state at 80 A (P_0 = 41 W for 4 channels), you would need R_th < 1/(41 × 0.005) = **4.9 K/W board-to-air**. A nano board without prop-wash has roughly 8 to 15 K/W.

**Conclusion: 80 A is thermally a burst-only rating. There is no steady-state solution at 80 A on all four channels.**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing high-side switch hard-switches. The synchronous low side turns on at zero voltage, after body-diode conduction.

### 2.1 Loss components (one channel)

**1. V-I overlap (hard switching):**
$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**2. Reverse recovery of the low-side body diode:**
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

**3. Output capacitance:**
$$P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2} Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 50\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.030\ W}$$
