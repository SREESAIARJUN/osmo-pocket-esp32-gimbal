### Headline findings

1. **Conduction loss dominates.** It is ≈ 29 W per ESC at 80 A and T_j = 110 °C. Switching loss at 48 kHz is ≈ 1.7 W.
2. **80 A on a 30.5 mm AIO is a transient rating, not a continuous one.** With all four channels at 80 A, the board heats at roughly 20–25 K/s adiabatically. The usable burst is about 2–3 s from a warm start. A single channel can sustain 80 A for about 8–10 s.
3. **The realistic continuous rating is about 20–25 A per channel**, limited by board-to-air convection. It is not limited by the MOSFETs.
4. **Bus capacitor ripple current approaches 40 A_rms per ESC at D = 0.5.** This is the most commonly under-designed item on AIOs.
