## 1. FOC Inner Loop (Current / Torque)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle from mechanical angle, with encoder offset θ_off found by d-axis alignment:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Amplitude-invariant Clarke transform, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

Inverse Park transform:

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 PMSM model and decoupled current PI

The dq-frame voltage equations are:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

The PI controllers act on the current errors. The cross-coupling and back-EMF terms are added as feedforward:

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m,\qquad \lambda_m = \tfrac{2K_t}{3p}$$

**Pole-zero cancellation tuning.** Choosing K_i/K_p = R/L cancels the electrical pole, so the closed loop becomes first order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \tfrac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10}$$

At 20 kHz this gives ω_c ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz, so K_p ≈ 20.7 V/A and K_i ≈ 52.8 kV/(A·s).

**Voltage limiting.** The dq voltage vector is circle-limited to

$$|v_{dq}|\le V_{bus}/\sqrt3$$

with d-axis priority.
