## 2. Field-Oriented Control Equations

### 2.1 Clarke and Park transforms

Clarke (amplitude-invariant):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park, with electrical angle θₑ = p·θₘ − θ_offset:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 2.2 Plant and decoupling

Motor model in the dq frame:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda$$

The feedforward terms are added to the PI outputs:

- v_d,ff = −ωₑ L i_q
- v_q,ff = +ωₑ L i_d + ωₑ λ

### 2.3 Current PI tuning (pole-zero cancellation)

$$K_p = L\,\omega_{c,i},\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_{c,i}$$

Discretized at T_s = 50 µs, with one-sample delay compensation θₑ ← θₑ + 1.5·ωₑ·T_s.

### 2.4 Voltage limit (circular)

$$\sqrt{v_d^2+v_q^2}\le V_{bus}/\sqrt3$$

v_d has priority; v_q receives the remaining voltage budget.
