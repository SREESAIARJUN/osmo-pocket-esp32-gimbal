## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Here θ_off is found at calibration by applying v_d = V_cal at θ_e = 0 and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, 3 shunts)

$$i_\alpha = \tfrac{2}{3}i_a - \tfrac13(i_b+i_c),\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_b - i_c)$$

With two shunts and i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

### 1.5 PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

**Pole-zero cancellation tuning.** The plant is 1/(R + sL). Placing the PI zero on the plant pole (K_i/K_p = R/L) gives a closed loop of first order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10}$$

At f_pwm = 20 kHz, choose ω_c = 2π·1500 rad/s, giving K_p = 19.8 V/A and K_i = 52 800 V/(A·s).

### 1.6 Discrete form and voltage limiting

Backward-Euler integrator with conditional integration (anti-windup):

$$u_k = K_p e_k + I_{k-1} + K_i T_s e_k$$
$$I_k = \begin{cases} I_{k-1} + K_i T_s e_k & \text{if } |u_k|\text{ not saturated or } \operatorname{sign}(e_k)\ne\operatorname{sign}(u_k)\\ I_{k-1} & \text{otherwise}\end{cases}$$

The voltage vector is limited to the linear SVPWM hexagon's inscribed circle. d-axis priority is used, with the margin factor 0.95 reserved for current sampling:

$$V_{max} = \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}\cdot 0.95,\qquad v_q^{max} = \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^{*2}}$$
