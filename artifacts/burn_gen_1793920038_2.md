# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion

I have no context on "Job #48", so I assumed a typical brushless gimbal platform. Every constant marked `// CONFIG` should be replaced with values from your hardware. The equations hold for any PMSM/BLDC gimbal motor.

## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class, single-precision FPU |
| Motor | 12N14P gimbal BLDC, p = 7 pole pairs, R ≈ 5–15 Ω, inline current sensing |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (MT6701 / AS5047P) on each axis |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, mounted symmetrically about the rotation centroid |
| Vbus | 12–16 V |

| Loop | Rate | Bandwidth target |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC, d/q PI) | 20 kHz | ~1.5–2 kHz |
| Velocity (PI + cogging feedforward) | 4 kHz | ~150–250 Hz |
| Attitude / angle (P/PI + rate feedforward) | 1 kHz | ~30–50 Hz |
| IMU sampling + filtering | 8 kHz ODR, decimated to 4 kHz | — |
| Attitude estimator (Mahony) | 1 kHz | — |

Each loop's bandwidth is kept at least 4–10× below the loop inside it. This lets each outer loop treat the inner loop as approximately unity gain.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Current Loop)

### 1.1 Motor model in the rotor (dq) frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d \frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L_q i_q
$$
$$
v_q = R i_q + L_q \frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

The electromagnetic torque, with $K_t = \tfrac{3}{2} p \lambda_m$ for a surface-mount motor where $L_d \approx L_q$, is:

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2} p \left[\lambda_m i_q + (L_d - L_q) i_d i_q\right] \approx K_t\, i_q
$$

The electrical angle is $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}$, and the electrical speed is $\omega_e = p\,\omega_m$.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, assuming $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

### 1.3 Park transform and its inverse

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix},
\qquad
\begin{bmatrix} v_\alpha \\ v_\beta \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & -\sin\theta_e \\ \sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} v_d \\ v_q \end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.4 Current PI controllers with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$
v_d^* = K_{p,i}\, e_d + K_{i,i}\!\int e_d\,dt - \omega_e L_q i_q
$$
$$
v_q^* = K_{p,i}\, e_q + K_{i,i}\!\int e_q\,dt + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$
