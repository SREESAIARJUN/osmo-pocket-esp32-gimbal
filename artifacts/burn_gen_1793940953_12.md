## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha\approx0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 150 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.625) = **1.63 mΩ**. Use 1.6 mΩ for the hot-case design.

### 1.2 Per-switch conduction duty in six-step

Each phase leg is active for 240° of the 360° electrical cycle:
- **120° as the PWM leg:** the high side conducts for fraction D, and the synchronous low side conducts for 1−D.
- **120° as the return (L_ON) leg:** the low side conducts 100%.

Per leg, averaged over one electrical cycle:

$$P_{HS}=I^2R\cdot\frac{D}{3},\qquad P_{LS}=I^2R\left(\frac{1-D}{3}+\frac{1}{3}\right)=I^2R\cdot\frac{2-D}{3}$$

Summing over 3 legs:

$$P_{cond,motor}=3\cdot I^2R\left(\frac{D}{3}+\frac{2-D}{3}\right)=\boxed{2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)}$$

This is independent of D, which makes physical sense: exactly two switches carry I_ph at every instant.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| Quantity | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Per motor | 2 × 80² × 1.6 mΩ | **20.5 W** |
| Worst single FET (low side, D→0) | (2/3) × 80² × 1.6 mΩ | **6.8 W** |
| Worst high-side FET (D→1) | (1/3) × 80² × 1.6 mΩ | 3.4 W |
| 4 motors | | **82 W** |

### 1.4 Copper conduction (not negligible)

2 oz copper has R_□ ≈ 0.25 mΩ/sq at 25 °C, and about 0.33 mΩ/sq at 100 °C.

Assume a phase path of ~3 squares per side and the bus path is shared. A paralleled 2-layer pour (outer + inner) gives about 0.5 mΩ effective per motor path:

$$P_{Cu}\approx 80^2\times0.5\,\text{m}\Omega\approx 3.2\ \text{W/motor}$$

This adds **~13 W total**. It is reduced by stitching phase pours on all 4 inner layers and by solder-bumping the exposed phase pads.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one leg per motor hard-switches at any time (the PWM leg). Losses per motor:

### 2.1 V–I overlap (hard switching, high side)

$$P_{VI}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5\times25.2\times80\times40\text{ns}\times48\text{k}= \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Body-diode reverse recovery

$$P_{rr}=Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw}=50\text{nC}\times25.2\times48\text{k}=\mathbf{0.06\ W}$$

The real value is often 2–3× higher at 80 A because Q_rr scales with di/dt and I_F. Measure it.

### 2.3 Output capacitance (C_oss) energy, both FETs in the leg

$$P_{oss}=2\cdot\tfrac12 C_{oss}V^2 f=1.5\text{nF}\times635\times48\text{k}=\mathbf{0.05\ W}$$
