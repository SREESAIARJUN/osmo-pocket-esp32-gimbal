## 0. Architecture Decisions and Their Limits

Three points change the shape of this design:

1. **The H743 should not run the motors directly.** ArduPilot has no motor-commutation firmware for flight-controller MCUs. Each ESC channel therefore gets its own MCU, either an AT32F421 or an STM32G071, running AM32. Those four MCUs generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM. The H743 only produces bidirectional DShot600 commands and reads eRPM telemetry back.

2. **"80 A burst" is only credible for one motor at a time, for a few seconds, on a 30.5 × 30.5 mm board.**
   - Four motors at 80 A is 320 A from the battery and about 106 W of heat into roughly 8 g of PCB.
   - Section 3 shows that this condition hits the junction-temperature limit in about 3 seconds.
   - On a 20 × 20 mm "nano" board, 80 A is not achievable with honest numbers. Target 35–45 A burst there.
   - **This document assumes a 30.5 × 30.5 mm stack, or an integrated 30.5 mm AIO.**

3. **Operating point:** 6S battery (25.2 V full charge), 6-step trapezoidal commutation, complementary (synchronous) PWM at 48 kHz, 100 ns dead time.

## 2. Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

In 6-step commutation, two phases conduct at any instant:

- **Phase A** is PWM-switched. The high-side FET conducts for duty fraction D, and the complementary low-side FET (synchronous rectification) conducts for 1 − D.
- **Phase B** is the return path. Its low-side FET is held fully on.

So current always flows through exactly two R_ds paths:

```
P_cond = I²·R_ds·[D + (1−D)] + I²·R_ds = 2·I²·R_ds(hot)
       = 2 × 80² × 1.92e-3 = 24.6 W
```

Add copper loss. Bus plus phase traces on 2 oz inner and outer layers are about 0.15 mΩ effective.

```
P_cu = I² × 0.15e-3 × 2 ≈ 1.9 W
```

### 2.2 Switching Loss (hard-switched high side)

**Voltage–current overlap:**
```
P_sw = ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw
     = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 30e-9 × 48e3 = 1.45 W
```

**Body-diode reverse recovery:**
```
P_rr = Q_rr·V·f_sw = 50e-9 × 25.2 × 48e3 = 0.06 W
```

**Output capacitance (C_oss) loss:**
```
P_oss = E_oss·f_sw = 1e-6 × 48e3 = 0.05 W
```

**Dead-time diode conduction (two dead-time intervals per PWM period):**
```
P_dt = V_f·I·2·t_dt·f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 200e-9 × 48e3 = 0.61 W
```

**Gate drive.** Two switching FETs per channel. This heat lands in the driver, not the FETs.
