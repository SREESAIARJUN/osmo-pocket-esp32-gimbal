# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Model (Job #14)

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I don't have Job #14's BOM, stack-up, or mechanical drawings, so this is a **parameterized model**. All numbers below use stated baseline values. Each assumption is tagged **[A#]** so you can substitute datasheet and measured values and re-run the math.

**Architecture clarification.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot. It is the *flight controller*, and it does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, which does the 48 kHz PWM and commutation. The H743 talks to those MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. If Job #14 instead intends the H743 to drive the gates directly, the DShot section is moot and the timer budget changes completely. Flag that early.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
In 6-step synchronous commutation, exactly **two FETs** carry phase current at any instant:
- one FET of the PWM'd leg (high-side during t_on, low-side during t_off), and
- the static low-side FET (or high-side) of the return leg.

Over a full electrical cycle each of the 6 FETs conducts for 120°.

### 1.2 Equations

$$R_{ds,hot} = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)] \approx 0.90\times1.40 = 1.26\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,FET,inst} = I^2 R_{ds,hot} = 80^2 \times 1.26\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{8.06\ W}$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds,hot} = \mathbf{16.1\ W}$$

$$P_{Cu,ch} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400\times0.5\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{3.2\ W}$$

Copper resistance check for [A10]: 2 oz ≈ 70 µm gives about 0.245 mΩ/□ at 25 °C. A 3 mm × 6 mm phase pour is about 2 squares, so ≈ 0.5 mΩ, rising ~0.39 %/K.

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

| Operating case | Per-FET dissipation | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Normal (rotating) | 16.1 W / 6 ≈ **2.7 W** average | Ripples at the commutation frequency |
| Stall or very low eRPM | Up to **8.06 W** in a single FET | Combine with the transient model in §3.3 |

Stall/low-eRPM is the actual design case. One FET can sit in the same state for tens of ms.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Only one leg hard-switches at a time in 6-step.

| Mechanism | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f = ½·25.2·80·30 ns·48 k | **1.45 W** |
| Dead-time body-diode | V_f·I·2·t_dt·f = 0.8·80·200 ns·48 k | **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f = 30 nC·25.2·48 k | **0.04 W** |
| C_oss | ½·C_oss·V²·f ×2 FETs | **0.05 W** |
| Gate drive (in driver, not FET) | Q_g·V_gs·f ×2 | **0.03 W** |
| **Subtotal** | | **≈ 2.2 W** |

Q_rr also inflates the turn-on overlap loss. Budget an extra +10–20 % on the overlap term until a double-pulse test is done.

**Sensitivity of the switching losses:**
- Switching loss scales linearly with f_sw.
- Dropping to 24 kHz saves about 1.1 W per channel but increases ripple current and acoustic noise.
- Cutting dead time to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W per channel. That requires verified driver propagation matching.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Total burst power

| Item | Per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 16.1 W | 64.4 W |
| Copper | 3.2 W | 12.8 W |
| Switching | 2.2 W | 8.8 W |
| **Total** | **21.5 W** | **≈ 86 W** |

Add about 1.5 W for the H743, regulators, and ESC MCUs. **P_total ≈ 87.5 W**.

**ESC efficiency at 80 A:** 21.5 / (25.2·80·D) gives roughly 1–2 % loss at high duty. That's fine electrically, but the board is tiny thermally.

### 3.2 Board-level lumped RC model

$$C_{th} \approx m_{FR4}c_{FR4} + m_{Cu}c_{Cu} \approx 4\ \text{J/K} \quad [A11]$$

$$R_{th,b-a} = \frac{1}{hA},\quad A = 2\times(0.0305)^2 = 1.86\times10^{-3}\text{ m}^2$$

| Airflow | h (W/m²K) | R_th,b-a | τ = RC | Steady-state allowable P (ΔT = 60 K) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Still air | 15 | 36 K/W | 144 s | 1.7 W |
| Prop-wash (in flight) | 100 | 5.4 K/W | 21.5 s | **11 W** |
| Prop-wash + Al heatsink plate (+6 J/K, 2× area) | 100 | 2.7 K/W | 27 s | 22 W |

**Burst response (prop-wash, no heatsink):**

$$\Delta T_{board}(t) = P\,R_{th}\left(1-e^{-t/\tau}\right)$$

With P·R = 87.5 × 5.4 = 472 K asymptote (physically unreachable; the FETs would fail long before):

| Burst duration | ΔT_board |
|---|---|
| 1 s | 21 K |
| 2 s | 41 K |
| 3 s | 61 K |
| 5 s | 97 K ✗ |

**Rule:** at T_amb = 35 °C and T_board,max = 100 °C, the **80 A ×4 burst is limited to about 3 s**. After the burst, the board needs roughly 3τ ≈ 60 s at low throttle to recover.

**Sustainable continuous current** at 11 W total:
- I²·(2·1.26 + 0.5) mΩ × 4 + switching ≈ 11 W
- This gives **I_cont ≈ 25–27 A per channel**.

Spec the product honestly as roughly "25 A continuous / 80 A 3 s burst
