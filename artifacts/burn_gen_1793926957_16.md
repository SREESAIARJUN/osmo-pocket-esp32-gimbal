## 0. Architectural Correction

The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM. A buildable architecture separates the two jobs:

| Domain | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) running ArduPilot | Attitude control. Emits 4× bidirectional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 (or EFM8BB51/STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware | 6-step commutation and 48 kHz complementary PWM. Encodes eRPM as GCR. |
| Gate drive (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase half-bridge driver, bootstrap) | Drives 6 N-FETs per motor. |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET, 40 V class | Three half-bridges per motor. |

Running ESC commutation on the H743 would require 12 complementary PWM outputs with dead-time, 4 BEMF comparator sets, and hard real-time commutation alongside ArduPilot's scheduler. ArduPilot does not support this.

### Feasibility caveat
On a 30.5×30.5 mm "nano" board, 80 A is achievable only as a time-limited per-motor burst. The continuous rating with prop-wash cooling is about 25–30 A per motor (derived in §4). The math below shows exactly why.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_DS = 1.6 × 1.3825 ≈ **2.21 mΩ**. At 125 °C: **2.32 mΩ**.

### 2.2 Six-step with synchronous (complementary) PWM

- **Chopping leg:** the high-side conducts for D·T_sw and the synchronous low-side for (1−D)·T_sw. The leg always carries I.
