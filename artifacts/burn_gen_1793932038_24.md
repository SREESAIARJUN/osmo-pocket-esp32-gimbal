### 0.1 Architecture clarification

An H743 cannot generate the 6-step commutation PWM for four motors (24 gate signals plus BEMF sensing) in any sensible way. The standard architecture, assumed here, is:

```
H743 (ArduPilot) --DShot600 bidir x4--> 4x ESC MCU (AM32) --> 4x 3-phase driver --> 24x MOSFET
```

The "48 kHz switching" is set in the ESC firmware (AM32 `PWM frequency`), not by the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step with synchronous rectification

During any 60° commutation sector, phase X is PWM'd and phase Y is held low:

- **PWM ON (fraction D):** HS_X and LS_Y conduct.
- **PWM OFF (fraction 1−D):** LS_X (synchronous rectifier) and LS_Y conduct.

Two FETs are always in series with the motor current, so:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j) \quad \text{(independent of } D\text{)}$$
