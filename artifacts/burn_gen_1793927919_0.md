# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Nano): Loss, Thermal, DShot and Netlist Design Package

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

No MOSFET part number, layer stack, or enclosure was specified. The models below are therefore **parameterized**, then evaluated with representative values. Substitute your datasheet values into the same equations. Items you must confirm against real silicon are marked **[VERIFY]**.

**Architectural constraint:** the H743 does not commutate the motors. Each motor needs a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, here 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600. The ESC firmware owns the 48 kHz PWM, dead time and commutation.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Bridge conduction

In 6-step with synchronous rectification, two phases conduct at every instant. Current always flows through exactly two channels in series: one high-side or its complementary low-side, plus the static low-side of the return phase.

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Evaluated: 2 × 80² × 1.45 mΩ = **18.6 W per motor**.

### 1.3 Per-device split

Each phase spends 120° high, 120° low and 120° floating. During the PWM phase:

- **High-side FET:** P_HS = D · I² · R
- **Low-side FET (sync):** P_LS,sync = (1−D) · I² · R
- **Static low-side of return phase:** P_LS,static = I² · R

Averaged over the electrical cycle, each FET carries one-third duty. That gives about **3.1 W average per FET**, regardless of D. Peak instantaneous dissipation in the static low-side is 9.3 W, which matters for junction transients (Section 4).

### 1.4 Copper and interconnect

Assume about 0.5 mΩ total path resistance (2 oz outer layers, paralleled inner planes, phase pads):

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{path} = 80^2 \times 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega = 3.2\ \text{W per motor}$$

### 1.5 Self-consistent junction temperature

R_DS rises with T_j, which raises loss, which raises T_j. Iterate:

$$T_j = T_a + P(T_j)\cdot Z_{th}$$

The loop converges when

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\cdot Z_{th} = P_{25}\,\alpha\,Z_{th} < 1$$
