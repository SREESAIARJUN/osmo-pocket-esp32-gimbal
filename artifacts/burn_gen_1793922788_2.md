# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal, and DShot Timing Design Package

**Scope:** Job #65, nano-class 6S stack
**Status:** Engineering model. Values marked **[A]** are assumptions that must be replaced with datasheet or measured values before release.

---

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs directly. The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32), which generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM through a 3-phase gate driver.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current per channel | I_ph | 80 A |
| Continuous current per channel | I_cont | 45 A **[A]** |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET (40 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3 or 5×6) | R_ds(on),25 | 0.80 mΩ **[A]** |
| R_ds(on) temperature coefficient (125 °C) | k_T | 1.5 |
| Rise / fall time at 80 A | t_r, t_f | 30 ns / 30 ns **[A]** |
| Gate charge | Q_g | 60 nC @ 10 V **[A]** |
| Body-diode reverse recovery | Q_rr | 50 nC **[A]** |
| Effective output capacitance | C_oss,eff | 2.0 nF **[A]** |
| Body-diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Dead time | t_dt | 200 ns per edge |
| Board footprint | — | 30.5×30.5 mm, 6-layer, 2 oz **[A]** |

> **Feasibility note.** 80 A per channel (320 A aggregate) on a "nano" board is only viable as a short burst. A 20×20 mm board cannot hold four 80 A half-bridge sets plus bulk capacitance. Section 3 quantifies the burst time limit.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology

With 6-step trapezoidal commutation and synchronous (complementary) PWM, current always flows through exactly two MOSFET channels:

- **ON interval:** high-side of phase A, then low-side of phase B.
- **OFF interval:** low-side of phase A (synchronous freewheel), then low-side of phase B.

So the conducting path resistance is independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds(on)}(T_j) \quad \text{(per channel)}$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{ds(on),25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /\mathrm{K}$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R = 0.80 \times 1.5 = 1.20\ \mathrm{m\Omega}$$

### 1.3 Burst value (80 A, hot)

$$P_{cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.2\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{15.36\ W}\ \text{per channel}$$

### 1.4 Copper and interconnect loss

Assume a per-channel path of 0.5 mΩ **[A]** (motor pad → phase node → FETs → GND/V+ planes):

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{3.2\ W}$$

Copper also has a positive temperature coefficient of about 0.39 %/K, which is worth roughly +20 % at 90 °C.

### 1.5 Battery current

$$I_{bat,ch} = D \cdot I_{ph} + I_{ripple}, \qquad I_{bat,total} \approx \sum_{k=1}^{4} D_k I_{ph,k}$$

At D = 0.85: I_bat ≈ 68 A per channel, or about 272 A total.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Per channel, one half-bridge is hard-switching at any time.

### 2.1 V–I overlap

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

$$= 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 60\times10^{-9} \times 48\times10^{3} = \mathbf{2.90\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\times10^{-9} \times 25.2 \times 48\times10^{3} = \mathbf{0.060\ W}$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2} C_{oss,eff} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 2\times10^{-9} \times 635 \times 48\times10^{3} = \mathbf{0.030\ W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

There are two dead-time windows per PWM period:

$$P_{dt} = V_f I_{ph} \cdot 2 t_{dt} f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 400\times10^{-9} \times 48\times10^{3} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

Two FETs switch per PWM period. This loss is dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FET die.

$$P_g = 2 Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 60\times10^{-9} \times 10 \times 48\times10^{3} = \mathbf{0.058\ W}$$

### 2.6 Per-channel totals at the 80 A burst

| Loss term | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction (FET) | 15.36 |
| Copper | 3.20 |
| V–I switching | 2.90 |
| Dead-time diode | 1.23 |
| Q_rr + C_oss | 0.09 |
| Gate drive | 0.06 |
| **Per channel** | **22.84** |
| **4 channels** | **≈ 91.4 W** |

Add about 0.5 W for the ESC MCUs and LDOs, and about 1 W for the H743 FC with its regulators. **Board burst total ≈ 93 W.**

### 2.7 Sensitivity to switching frequency

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial f_{sw}} = \frac{P_{VI} + P_{dt} + P_{rr} + P_{oss} + P_g}{f_{sw}} \approx 89\ \mathrm{\mu W/Hz}$$

Dropping from 48 kHz to 24 kHz saves about 2.1 W per channel (≈ 8.5 W total). The cost is more current ripple and audible noise. At 80 A the conduction loss dominates, so lower R_ds(on) or paralleled FETs give much larger gains than reducing f_sw.

**Option: parallel FET pairs.** Doubling FETs per switch halves R_eff, so P_cond drops to 7.7 W per channel. The board must then fit 48 FETs.
