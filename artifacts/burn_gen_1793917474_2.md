# STM32H743 Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #9): Power-Stage Thermal, Switching, and Bi-directional DShot600 Architecture

## 0. Architectural Clarification and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** It is the flight controller (FC) running ArduPilot. Each motor channel on the 4-in-1 ESC has its own small ESC MCU (typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM and runs the bridges. The H743 talks to the four ESC MCUs over bi-directional DShot600.

The model below therefore has two domains:
- **ESC power stage:** thermal and switching losses (Sections 1–4)
- **FC timer and DMA subsystem:** DShot (Section 5)

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

During each 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct:

- **Phase X (PWM leg):** the high side (HS) conducts for duty D. The low side (LS) synchronously rectifies for 1−D, with dead time in between.
- **Phase Y (return leg):** the LS is held on for the full sector.
- **Phase Z:** floating (BEMF sense).
