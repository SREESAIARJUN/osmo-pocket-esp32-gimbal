# STM32H743 Nano FC with Integrated 4-in-1 ESC (Job #34): Power-Stage Model, Thermal Budget, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation

---

## 0. Architecture Assumptions and an Early Warning

An STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFET bridges directly. It runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs, which run AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware on STM32G071 or AT32F421 parts. This document models both halves.

| Parameter | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal | Worst case for switching losses: 25.2 V |
| Board | 30.5 × 30.5 mm (FC+ESC stack) or 25.5 × 25.5 mm (AIO) | "Nano" form factor |
| PCB | 8-layer, 2 oz outer / 2 oz inner power planes | Required for this current |
| MOSFET (reference) | 40 V, 3.3 × 3.3 mm PQFN class | See §1.1 |
| Gate driver | 3-phase half-bridge driver (FD6288Q class), 10–12 V gate drive | |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), center-aligned | |
| Burst current | 80 A per phase-channel | Short duration only (see §3) |

> **⚠ Engineering verdict up front:** 80 A on all four channels simultaneously produces roughly **90–110 W of heat on a board of about 6 cm²**. This is achievable only as a **2–4 s transient** with prop-wash cooling. It is not a continuous rating. Any datasheet claiming "80 A continuous" on a nano 4-in-1 should be treated as marketing. The math below quantifies this.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 MOSFET Parameters (Reference Device, Parameterized)

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| R_DS(on),25 | 1.0 mΩ (V_GS = 10 V) | Typical at 25 °C |
| α_T | ≈ 0.0055 /K | Temperature coefficient of R_DS(on) |
| Q_g | 50 nC | Total gate charge |
| Q_gd | 10 nC | Miller charge |
| Q_oss | 50 nC @ 25 V | Output charge |
| Q_rr | 40 nC | Body-diode reverse-recovery charge (high di/dt) |
| V_SD | 0.8 V | Body-diode forward voltage |
| R_θJC | 1.5 K/W | Junction-to-case, steady state |

Temperature-dependent on-resistance:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha_T (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 135 °C:

$$R_{DS} = 1.0 \times (1 + 0.0055 \times 110) \approx 1.6\ \text{mΩ}$$

### 1.2 Current Paths in 6-Step Commutation with Synchronous Rectification

During each 60° sector, phase A sources current and phase B sinks it. Phase C floats and is used for BEMF sensing.

- **Phase A leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. The low-side FET carries the freewheel current for (1−D).
- **Phase B leg:** the low-side FET conducts 100% of the sector.

At every instant, exactly two FET channels are in the current path:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2 I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

This is independent of duty cycle, ignoring dead-time.

Per-FET average: each FET is active 1/3 of the electrical period, either as the switched FET or the full-sector FET.

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx \tfrac{1}{3}\cdot I^2 R_{DS}\cdot k_{role}, \quad k_{role}\in[D,1]$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.6\times10^{-3} = 20.5\ \text{W per channel}$$

$$P_{cond,4ch} = 82\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 Parasitic Copper and Shunt Losses (Often Forgotten)

| Element | Resistance | Current | Loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase trace + pad, per channel (2 oz, ~8 mm, multi-layer) | ≈ 0.15 mΩ | 80 A | 0.96 W × 4 = 3.8 W |
| Battery bus planes | ≈ 0.10 mΩ | up to 320 A (D → 1) | ~10 W |
| Shared current shunt | 0.2 mΩ | 320 A | **20.5 W ⚠** |

Battery current relates to phase current by I_bat ≈ D · I_phase.

**Design decision: use per-channel shunts of 0.5 mΩ.** At 80 A each, this gives 3.2 W per channel. It spreads the hot spot across four locations and gives each ESC MCU its own current-limit measurement. A single shared shunt cannot survive a 4 × 80 A burst.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM-switched high-side FET hard-switches. The synchronous low-side FET turns on after dead-time with its body diode already conducting, so it switches at near-zero voltage (ZVS).

### 2.1 Transition Times

Gate current through the driver plateau:

$$I_g \approx \frac{V_{drv} - V_{plateau}}{R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}} = \frac{10 - 3.0}{4.7 + 0.8} \approx 1.3\ \text{A}$$

$$t_{r,f} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}+Q_{gs2}}{I_g} \approx \frac{10+5\ \text{nC}}{1.3\ \text{A}} \approx 12\ \text{ns}$$

Add loop-inductance slowdown and use **t_r = t_f ≈ 15 ns**.

### 2.2 Loss Components (V = 25.2 V, I = 80 A, f = 48 kHz)

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f | ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 30 ns × 48 k = **1.45 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f | 40 n × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.05 W** |
| Output charge | Q_oss · V · f | 50 n × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.06 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_SD · I · 2t_dt · f (t_dt = 50 ns) | 0.8 × 80 × 100 n × 48 k = **0.31 W**
