## 0. Architectural Ground Truth and Assumptions

Three facts shape everything in this design.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1 design, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 (here an AT32F421K8U7, one per channel). That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. The loss model (§1–3) therefore applies to the ESC power stage. The DMA model (§4) applies to the H743.

2. **80A is a burst rating, not continuous.** No 20×20 or 30.5×30.5 board can dissipate 4×80A losses in steady state. §3 proves this numerically and derives the real continuous rating.

3. **Form factor.** "Nano" is taken as a 30.5×30.5 mm mounting pattern (≈36×36 mm board). A 20×20 board cannot physically route 80A phase currents. If 20×20 is a hard requirement, the burst rating must drop to about 40A.
