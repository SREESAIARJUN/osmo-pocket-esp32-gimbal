# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: 80 A Burst Electro-Thermal Model, 48 kHz Switching Losses, and Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Plan

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning
The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM. In this architecture, each ESC channel runs its own MCU (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class, e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071). That MCU closes the commutation loop and drives the gate drivers at 48 kHz. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 commands, receiving eRPM telemetry back on the same wire.

```
6S LiPo ─┬─ 4-in-1 ESC power stage (4 × 3-phase bridges, 24 MOSFETs)
         │      ▲ gate drive, 48 kHz complementary PWM
         │   4 × ESC MCU (AM32/BLHeli_32)
         │      ▲ bidirectional DShot600 (1 wire per motor, eRPM back)
         └─ BEC ── STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ── TIM3 CH1..CH4 + DMAMUX
```
