### Architecture correction

In ArduPilot designs, the **STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFET gates.** The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600 frames. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU that performs commutation:

- **ESC MCU:** AT32F421 or STM32G071, running AM32 or BLHeli_32.
- **Gate driver:** FD6288Q-class 3-phase driver.
- **Power stage:** 6 N-channel MOSFETs per motor.

The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs. The model below follows that split.
