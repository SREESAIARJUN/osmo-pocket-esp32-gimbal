## 0. Architecture Clarification & Design Basis

The STM32H743 cannot run the ESC commutation firmware. AM32 and BLHeli do not target the H7, and one MCU cannot close four sensorless BEMF loops at 48 kHz. The system is therefore partitioned as follows:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100, 480 MHz rev V) | ArduPilot, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) | AM32, 48 kHz complementary PWM, eRPM telemetry |
