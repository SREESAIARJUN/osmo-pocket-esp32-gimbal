# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control, Anti-Cogging Feedforward, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

**Assumptions.** I don't have the original Job #7 specification, so this design uses typical values. Replace them with your hardware's numbers:

- **Motors:** gimbal BLDC/PMSM, 12N14P (12 slots, 14 poles, so 7 pole pairs), roughly 10 Ω and 2 mH phase.
- **Sensing:** 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per axis, and two ICM-42688-P IMUs on the camera plate.
- **Inverter:** 3-phase, with 2-shunt current sensing.
- **MCU:** Cortex-M4F/M7 with an FPU.

Everything is in single-precision float with no heap allocation.

**Loop rates:**

| Loop | Rate | Ts |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | 50 µs |
| Velocity | 4 kHz | 250 µs |
| Angle / attitude | 1 kHz | 1 ms |
| IMU ODR (both) | 8 kHz | 125 µs |

## 1. FOC Inner Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, using electrical angle θₑ = p·θₘ − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 PMSM dq Model

$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e (L_d i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\,\lambda_m i_q \equiv K_t\, i_q \quad (\text{surface PM},\ L_d=L_q=L)
$$

### 1.3 Decoupled PI with Pole-Zero Cancellation

The cross-coupling terms are removed by feedforward:

$$
v_d^* = \underbrace{K_p e_d + K_i\!\int\! e_d}_{PI_d} - \hat\omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q^* = PI_q + \hat\omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

What remains is the plant 1/(Ls + R). Choosing

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c
$$

cancels the electrical pole at R/L, giving a closed loop of ω_c/(s + ω_c).

- Choose ω_c ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz, which must satisfy ω_c ≤ ω_s/10 at a 20 kHz sample rate.
- PWM plus computation adds about 1.5·Ts of delay. To compensate, advance the angle used in the inverse Park transform: θₑ,PWM = θₑ + 1.5·ω̂ₑ·Ts.

### 1.4 Voltage Limiting and Anti-Windup

The linear SVPWM region is a circle:

$$
\|v_{dq}\|\le V_{max}=\tfrac{V_{dc}}{\sqrt3}
$$

- **Priority:** the d-axis gets voltage first, then v_q is clamped to $\pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}$.
- **Anti-windup (back-calculation):**

$$
\dot I = K_i e + K_{aw}(v_{sat} - v_{unsat}),\qquad K_{aw}\approx K_i/K_p
$$

### 1.5 SVPWM via Min-Max Zero-Sequence Injection

$$
v_{0} = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_a,v_b,v_c)+\min(v_a,v_b,v_c)\big)
$$

$$
D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{dc}},\quad x\in\{a,b,c\}
$$

The phase voltages come from the inverse Clarke transform:

$$
v_a=v_\alpha,\quad v_b=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

## 2. Cascaded Velocity and Angle Control

### 2.1 Encoder Velocity: PLL Tracking Observer

Differentiating a quantized angle is noisy, so velocity comes from a type-2 PLL instead:

$$
e_k = \mathrm{wrap}_{\pi}(\theta_{m,k} - \hat\theta_k)
$$

$$
\hat\omega_{k+1} = \hat\omega_k + k_i T_s e_k
$$

$$
\hat\theta_{k+1} = \hat\theta_k + T_s(\hat\omega_{k+1} + k_p e_k)
$$

The gains are $k_p = 2\zeta\omega_n$ and $k_i=\omega_n^2$, with ζ = 1 and ω_n ≈ 2π·300 Hz.

### 2.2 Velocity Loop

Gimbal stabilization requires inertial rate feedback. The gimbal must hold the camera still in space while the base moves, so the velocity loop is closed on the gyro rate of the stabilized link, projected onto the joint axis:

$$
\omega_{fb} = \hat{\mathbf a}_j^{\mathsf T}\,\boldsymbol\omega_{gyro}
$$

The encoder PLL rate is used only for commutation and decoupling.

$$
G_v(s)=\frac{K_t}{J s + B}\cdot\frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
