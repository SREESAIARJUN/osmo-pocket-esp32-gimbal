## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Findings

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 cannot directly commutate 4 motors at 48 kHz *and* run ArduPilot. The correct architecture, consistent with ArduPilot's bi-directional DShot model, uses two tiers:

```
┌──────────────── FC (STM32H743, ArduPilot) ────────────────┐
│ TIM1 CH1–4 ──DShot600 BIDIR──► 4 × ESC MCU (AM32, AT32F421)│
│                                   │                       │
│                                   ▼                       │
│                     3-ph gate driver ×4 → 24 MOSFETs      │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Feasibility findings.** These are stated up front because they drive everything else.

| Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|
| 4 × 80 A simultaneous is about **81 W at 25 °C**, rising with temperature | A 20×20 board cannot meet it. **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, with a ≥5 g Al spreader is mandatory.** |
| Thermal loop gain > 1 at 80 A (Rds(on) tempco feedback) | **No steady-state 80 A exists.** 80 A is strictly a *time-limited burst*. |
| Burst limit (40 °C ambient, Tj ≤ 125 °C) | **≈7 s all-four-motors**. Rate the product
