## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Several points in the brief need correcting before any math is useful.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1 design, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot to those four ESC MCUs. Making the H743 commutate four motors and run ArduPilot at the same time is not supported by ArduPilot. The 48 kHz PWM is therefore an ESC-firmware parameter (AM32 `PWM_FREQ`), not an H743 timer setting.
2. **"80 A burst" must be qualified.** Is it per channel or total, and for how long? This design assumes **80 A phase current per channel, all 4 channels at once, on 6S**. Section 3 shows this is physically limited to about 2–4 seconds on a nano-format board. That limit is the most important result in this document.
3. **Battery-side reality check.** At full throttle (D→1), DC current approaches phase current: 4 × 80 A ≈ 320 A. XT60 or XT90 connectors and typical packs cannot deliver this. Expect pack sag to limit current first.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

In any commutation sector, two legs are active:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high-side switch conducts for duty D. Its complementary low-side switch conducts the freewheel current for (1 − D), minus dead time.
- **Leg B (return leg):** the low-side switch is held ON for the whole sector.

So at every instant, phase current flows through exactly **two channels' worth of R_ds(on)**:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot R_{ds(on)}(T_j)\cdot\big[D + (1-D)\big] + I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot R_{ds(on)}(T_j)
= 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j)
$$

This result is **independent of duty cycle**, which is a key property of synchronous rectification.

### 1.2 Including inductor ripple

The phase-current ripple at the PWM frequency is:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{(V_{in} - V_{bemf})\,D}{2L_{ph} f_{sw}}, \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}
$$
