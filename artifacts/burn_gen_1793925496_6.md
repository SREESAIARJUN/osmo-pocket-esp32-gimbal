## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller (FC)**. It does not commutate the motors. Each motor channel is driven by a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, which handles 48 kHz center-aligned PWM, back-EMF zero-cross detection and bidirectional DShot telemetry. The H743 runs ArduPilot and produces 4× bidirectional DShot600.

```
6S Battery ─► Shunt ─► VBAT bus ─┬─► 4× [ESC MCU + 3-ph gate driver + 6× MOSFET] ─► Motors
                                 └─► Buck 5V/3V3 ─► STM32H743 (ArduPilot)
                                                   └─ TIM1 CH1..4 ── bidir DShot600 ──► ESC MCUs
```
