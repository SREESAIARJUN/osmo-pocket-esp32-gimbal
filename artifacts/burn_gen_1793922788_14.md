## 0. Architecture Findings (Read First)

**Finding 1: The STM32H743 cannot directly commutate four sensorless BLDC motors.**

- The H743 has only two advanced-control timers with complementary outputs (TIM1, TIM8). That is enough for two 3-phase bridges.
- The HRTIM could supply more outputs, but the chip has only two analog comparators (COMP1/2) for BEMF zero-cross detection.

**Decision:** Use the standard split architecture:

- The **H743 runs ArduPilot** as the flight controller.
- **4× AT32F421 (or STM32G071) run AM32**, one per motor.
- The two are linked by **bidirectional DShot600**.

**Finding 2: An "80 A burst" rating on a nano (20×20 mm) board is a seconds-scale transient rating, not a thermal steady state.**

- Section 3 shows the board heats at about 35–40 K/s with all four channels at 80 A.
- Sustainable continuous current on 20×20 is roughly 15–20 A per channel.
- **Recommendations:**
  - Specify the burst rating as **"80 A, ≤1.5 s, 40 °C start, 105 °C PCB limit."**
  - Or move to a 30.5×30.5 mm mount if longer bursts are a hard requirement.

**Finding 3: Battery-side current.**

- Four channels at 80 A means 320 A instantaneous motor current.
- At realistic duty, that is about 200–300 A battery current.
- XT60 connectors and 12 AWG leads become the dominant loss and voltage-sag term. Section 3.5 covers the required external bulk capacitor.
