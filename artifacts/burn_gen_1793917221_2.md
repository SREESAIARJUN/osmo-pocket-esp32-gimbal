# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation

## 0. Assumptions and Architecture

I don't have the Job #67 requirements document, so every numeric result below depends on stated assumptions. Each one is parameterized so you can substitute datasheet values.

**System partitioning:** The STM32H743 is the flight controller (FC) MCU running ArduPilot. The 4-in-1 ESC carries its own per-motor MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Typical choices are STM32G071 or AT32F421; two dual-motor MCUs are also used. Bidirectional DShot600 is the only link between them, plus GND and an optional current-sense ADC line.

> **Honest headline:** 80 A *per motor* on a 20×20 nano board is a **thermal-mass-limited burst rating** (roughly 1–3 s), not a continuous rating. The math in §3 shows why. If the requirement is really "80 A sustained," the form factor must change.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
With 6-step commutation and synchronous rectification, current always flows through exactly **two** conducting MOSFETs in series:
- During PWM on-time: high-side of leg A and low-side of leg B.
- During off-time: the low-side of leg A (sync rectifier) replaces the high-side, so it is still two FETs.

Ignoring dead time (handled in §2), the conduction loss per ESC channel is:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]$$
