## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Design Basis

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Topology current paths

The design uses six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification. In every 60° electrical sector:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** HS conducts for D·T, LS conducts (synchronous) for (1−D)·T. The current is always in one FET of the leg.
- **Phase B (return leg):** LS is on for the full sector.
- **Phase C:** floating (BEMF sense).

So **exactly two MOSFETs carry I_ph at all times**, independent of duty:

$$
P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 \left[ D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS} + R_{LS} \right] \;\xrightarrow{R_{HS}=R_{LS}=R}\; 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature-corrected resistance

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

At a design junction temperature of T_j = 110 °C:

$$
R_{DS} = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 85) = 1.2 \times 1.383 = 1.66\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

### 1.3 Burst conduction loss

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.66\times10^{-3} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.66\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{21.2\ W\ per\ motor}
$$
