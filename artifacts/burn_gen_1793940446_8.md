## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flags

The architecture determines everything else, so it must be settled first.

1. **The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC.** It runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs. Each ESC MCU (AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 class, running AM32 or BLHeli_32) commutates its own bridge. Bidirectional DShot is only meaningful across that FC-to-ESC link. A single H743 commutating four motors is a different product and is not ArduPilot-supported.
