# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #51)

**Scope:** loss model at 80 A burst, 48 kHz switching analysis, thermal transient model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and reference netlists.

## 0. Architectural Corrections and Assumptions

These points change the design, so they come first.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is flight-controller firmware and does not run ESC commutation. A 4-in-1 ESC needs four ESC MCUs, typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 sends DShot600 to them and receives eRPM telemetry back. A single-MCU six-channel FOC design is possible, but it is not an ArduPilot target.
2. **"Nano" form factor and 80 A burst conflict thermally.** Section 4 shows that a 20×20 mm stack can carry roughly 80 A ×4 for only about 1–2 s. Treat 80 A as a short burst rating, not a continuous one.
3. **Assumed operating point:**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max (worst case for switching loss), 22.2 V nominal |
| Per-channel phase current (burst) | 80 A at near full duty, so I_phase ≈ I_DC |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal, complementary (synchronous) PWM at 48 kHz |
| MOSFET (representative 40 V, 5×6 or 3.3×3.3 class) | See below |

Representative MOSFET parameters (replace with your selected part's datasheet values):

| Symbol | Value | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| R_DS(on),25°C | 1.2 mΩ @ V_GS = 10 V | Q_g | 40 nC |
| k_T (R_DS multiplier at T_j ≈ 125 °C) | 1.5 | Q_gs2 (post-threshold) | 4 nC |
| V_plateau | 3.5 V | Q_gd | 8 nC |
| R_G,int | 1.0 Ω | Q_oss | 50 nC |
| Q_rr | 40 nC | V_SD (body diode) | 0.8 V |
| R_θJC | 1.2 K/W | | |

Gate driver assumptions: V_DRV = 10 V, external R_G = 4 Ω, so R_G,tot = 5 Ω. Dead time t_dt = 100 ns per edge.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology

In 6-step operation, exactly two phase legs conduct during each 60° sector:

- The **PWM leg**: high-side at duty D, low-side synchronous rectifier at (1−D).
- The **return leg**: low-side fully on.

### 1.2 Per-channel conduction loss

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS,hot}\big[\underbrace{D + (1-D)}_{\text{PWM leg}} + \underbrace{1}_{\text{return leg}}\big] = 2\,I^2 R_{DS,hot}
$$

$$
R_{DS,hot} = k_T\,R_{DS,25} = 1.5 \times 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega = 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega
$$

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.8\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{23.0\ W}
$$

### 1.3 Temperature coupling

R_DS depends on junction temperature, so solve iteratively:

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

$$
T_j = T_{board} + P_{FET}(T_j)\,R_{\theta,J\text{-}board}
$$

Converge by fixed-point iteration; it converges in 3–4 steps for this design.
