# 3-Axis Gimbal Motor Control & Sensor Fusion Design

This design covers the FOC current loop, cascaded angle/velocity control, anti-cogging feedforward, and dual ICM-42688-P vibration rejection, with a complete C++17 implementation.

I don't have the Job #26 spec sheet, so the motor, sensor, and timing parameters below are **stated assumptions**. They are typical for a 3-axis camera gimbal with gimbal-wound PMSM motors, 14-bit magnetic encoders, and an STM32G4/H7-class MCU. Replace them with your actual values; every gain is derived from them by formula.

**Loop rates and bandwidth separation.** Each loop runs about 5–10× slower than the loop inside it, and its bandwidth is set about 4–10× lower:

```
[20 kHz ISR]  ADC → Clarke/Park → PI(id,iq) → invPark → SVPWM
      ▲ iq_ref (+ cogging FF)
[4 kHz]       Velocity PI  ← fused gyro rate (dual IMU, notched)
      ▲ ω_ref (+ trajectory FF)
[1 kHz]       Angle P(+I)  ← attitude estimate (Mahony, dual IMU)
```

Target bandwidths:
- **Current loop:** ω_c,i = 2π·2000 rad/s.
- **Velocity loop:** ω_n,v = 2π·150 rad/s.
- **Angle loop:** ω_c,θ = 2π·30 rad/s.

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Plant model (rotor dq frame, surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

The electrical angle and speed are:

$$
\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}},\qquad \omega_e = p\,\omega_m
$$

### 1.2 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke transform, assuming balanced currents (i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.3 PI with pole-zero cancellation and decoupling

The plant in each axis is $G(s) = 1/(Ls+R)$. Choosing $C(s) = K_p + K_i/s$ with

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{c,i},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{c,i}
$$

cancels the electrical pole. The closed loop then reduces to a first-order system:

$$
T(s) = \frac{\omega_{c,i}}{s+\omega_{c,i}}
$$

Cross-coupling and back-EMF are removed with feedforward terms:

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(e_d) - \omega_e L\, i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(e_q) + \omega_e L\, i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

where $\lambda_m = \tfrac{2}{3}K_t/p$ for the amplitude-invariant transform.
