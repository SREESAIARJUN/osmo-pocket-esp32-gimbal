# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC with 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage and DShot Architecture Model

## 0. Scope, Partitioning and Stated Assumptions

No component selections came with Job #45, so this model is **fully parameterised**. Every number below comes from the reference design in §0.2, and each equation can be re-run with your actual datasheet values. The 80 A burst and timer/DMA figures must be re-checked against the final BOM before release.

### 0.1 Architectural correction

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. In any 4-in-1 design the bridges are driven by four (or one quad) dedicated ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to them over bidirectional DShot600. The design therefore has two partitions:

```
 [STM32H743 / ArduPilot] --4x BDShot600 (single-wire, bidir)--> [4x ESC MCU] --> [4x FD6288-class gate driver] --> [4x 6-FET bridge]
```
