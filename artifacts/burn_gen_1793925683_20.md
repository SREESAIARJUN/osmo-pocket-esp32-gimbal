## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**Architecture clarification.** The H743 does not commutate the motors directly. ArduPilot has no in-FC BLDC commutation. The standard and correct partition is:

```
[H743 FC: ArduPilot] --4x BDShot600--> [4x ESC MCU (AM32 / BLHeli_32)] --> [3-ph gate driver] --> [6x MOSFET] --> motor
```

| Parameter | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Bus voltage | 6S, V_bus = 22.2 V nominal (25.2 V max) |
| Burst current | 80 A phase current per channel, all 4 channels simultaneously (worst case) |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), 6-step trapezoidal |
| MOSFET | 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN class, R_ds(on),25 = 1.2 mΩ @ 10 V, Q_g = 40 nC, Q_gd = 8 nC, Q_oss = 50 nC, Q_rr = 40 nC, V_SD = 0.8 V, R_θJC = 1.5 K/W |
| Gate driver | ~1.5 A sink / 1 A source, V_drv = 10 V, giving t_r ≈ t_f ≈ 20 ns at 80 A |
| Dead time | t_dt = 200 ns per edge |
| Temperature | T_j,max design limit = 125 °C (FET rated 150 °C), T_amb = 40 °C |
| Temperature coefficient | R_ds(on)(T) = R₂₅·[1 + α(T − 25)], α ≈ 0.0055 /K, giving ×1.47 at 110 °C |
| Board | ~28×28 mm (20×20 mount), 6-layer, 2 oz outer / 2 oz inner power planes |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

During each 60° commutation sector, the motor current I flows through:
- The **PWM leg** (e.g., phase A): high-side on for D, low-side (sync rect.) on for (1−D) minus dead time.
- The **return leg** (e.g., phase B): low-side on for 100% of the sector.

So, ignoring dead time, **two R_ds(on) are always in series with I**, independent of D:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Numbers at 80 A

$$R_{ds}(110^\circ C) = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times [1 + 0.0055(85)] = 1.76\,\text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.76\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{22.5\ W\ per\ channel}$$

### 1.3 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\cdot I \cdot 2t_{dt}\cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 400\text{ns} \times 48\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$

### 1.4 Thermal coupling of Rds(on)

R_ds(on) depends on T_j, so solve iteratively:

$$T_j = T_{pcb} + P_{FET}(T_j)\,R_{\theta JC}, \qquad P_{FET} \propto R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

Thermal runaway is avoided at the FET level when $\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}R_\theta < 1$:

$$I^2 R_{25}\alpha\,R_{\theta,eff} = 6400 \times 1.2\text{m} \times 0.0055 \times 1.5 \approx 0.063 \ll 1$$

The FET level is stable. The real limit is the board's thermal mass (§4).

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the PWM-leg high-side hard-switches. The low-side commutates through its body diode (ZVS-ish), so its turn-on is near-lossless.

| Term | Formula | Value / channel |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f = ½·22.2·80·40 ns·48 k | **1.71 W** |
| C_oss | Q_oss·V·f = 50 nC·22.2·48 k | 0.053 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f = 40 nC·22.2·48 k | 0.043 W |
