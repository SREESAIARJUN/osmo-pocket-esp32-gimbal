## 0. Architecture Findings

Three points determine whether this design is feasible.

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | ArduPilot on an H743 cannot also commutate 4 BLDC motors at 48 kHz. | The ESC needs **4 dedicated ESC MCUs** (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32). The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to them over on-board traces. |
| A2 | 80 A per motor on a 20×20 mm board is **not thermally sustainable**. The board sees about 90 W total at 4×80 A. | "80 A" must be specified as a **time-limited burst** (≈1–2 s, derived in §3). Continuous rating is set by the steady-state model. |
| A3 | Input ripple current at 80 A is about 40 A RMS per bridge. | Bulk and local MLCC capacitance and the battery-lead inductance are first-order design constraints, not afterthoughts. |

**System block:**
```
VBAT(6S, 25.2V max) ─┬─ 4× 3-phase bridges (6 FETs each, 24 total)
                     ├─ 4× FD6288Q gate drivers
                     ├─ 4× AT32F421K8U7 (AM32)  ◄── DShot600 bidir ── STM32H743 (ArduPilot)
                     └─ Buck 5V/3.3V ── H743, IMU, baro, OSD
```

## 2. Loss Model per Bridge (One Motor)

### 2.1 Conduction Losses (Six-Step, Complementary PWM)

During any commutation sector, two phases carry current:

- The **high-side** FET of the PWM'd leg conducts for D·T.
- Its **low-side** complement conducts (synchronous rectification) for (1−D)·T.
- The **low-side** of the return leg conducts for 100 % of the sector.

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds,hot}\big[D + (1-D) + 1\big] = 2\,I^2 R_{ds,hot}
$$

Total conduction loss is therefore **independent of duty cycle**, though its distribution among FETs depends on D.

$$
R_{ds,hot} = k_T R_{ds,25} = 1.6\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.6\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{20.48\ W}
$$

Worst single FET (return-leg low-side, 100 % of sector):

$$
P_{FET,max,inst} = I^2 R_{ds,hot} = 10.24\ \text{W}
$$

Over one electrical revolution, each FET is "return-leg low" for 1/3 of the time. At high eRPM (electrical period much shorter than the junction τ of roughly 1–5 ms), the junction sees the average. At low eRPM with high current (startup, stall, prop-strike), it sees the instantaneous value. **Design to the instantaneous 10.24 W per FET for stall protection.**

### 2.2 Switching Losses (Hard-Switched Edge, Inductive Load)

$$
I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{src}+R_g} = \frac{10-4.5}{7} = 0.786\ \text{A}
$$

$$
I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{snk}+R_g} = \frac{4.5}{4} = 1.125\ \text{A}
$$
