## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and a Partitioning Note

You did not specify the MOSFETs, cell count, board size, or cooling. Every number below is **parametric**: the equations are general, and the worked values use a stated baseline. Replace the baseline values with your actual datasheet values before committing to layout.

**Architectural note.** In an AIO board, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and does **not** commutate the motors. The 4-in-1 ESC section uses one or more dedicated ESC MCUs running AM32, BLHeli_32, or Bluejay. Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 talks to them over bi-directional DShot600. So:

- §1–§3 (losses and thermal) concern the **ESC power stage**.
- §4 (DMA and timers) concerns the **H743**.

The hardware is shared; the firmware domains are not.
