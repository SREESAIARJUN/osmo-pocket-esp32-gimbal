**Feasibility flags. Read these before committing to a layout.**

1. **"Nano" and 80 A burst are incompatible.** A 20×20 mm board cannot fit 24 PDFN5×6 FETs, and it lacks the thermal mass for an 80 A burst. The math below assumes a 30.5 mm-class board. On a 20×20 board, divide the burst duration by roughly 2.5–3.
2. **"80 A burst" must be defined.** Four channels at 80 A from 6S is about 7 kW. That is a seconds-scale rating, not a sustained one. Section 3 quantifies the duration.
3. **Treat the netlist as a reference topology, not a fab-ready release.** Pin assignments must be re-verified against the STM32H743 datasheet (AF tables) and the current ArduPilot `hwdef` documentation.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

In each commutation sector, phase A (PWM leg) sources current and phase B (low-side "on" leg) sinks it.

- **High side of A** conducts for D·T_sw.
- **Low side of A** (synchronous freewheel) conducts for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Low side of B** conducts for the full T_sw.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

**Key result:** with synchronous rectification, conduction loss is **independent of duty cycle**. Dead-time diode conduction is handled separately in Section 2.
