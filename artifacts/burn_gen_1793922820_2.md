I don't have the Job #35 specification, so this design states its assumptions explicitly. Replace the values in the parameter table with your motor, mechanics and MCU data. **Bandwidth separation rule:** each outer loop's bandwidth is at most 1/5 to 1/10 of the inner loop's. Targets:

- Current loop: about 2 kHz
- Velocity loop: about 150–250 Hz
- Angle loop: about 20–40 Hz

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle, with $\theta_{off}$ found during alignment:

$$\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$$

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant and Decoupled PI

The motor in the rotor frame:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q, \qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

The controller adds decoupling and back-EMF feedforward to the PI outputs:

$$v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e \hat L\, i_q$$

$$v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e(\hat L\, i_d + \hat\lambda_m)$$

**Gains by pole-zero cancellation.** Choosing

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c, \qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

makes the closed loop first order with bandwidth $\omega_c$. Use $\omega_c = 2\pi \cdot 2000$ rad/s, which is safely below $f_{PWM}/10$.

**Delay compensation.** Rotate the inverse Park transform by $\theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s$ to account for the one-sample computation delay plus the half-sample PWM delay.

### 1.3 Voltage Limiting and SVPWM

Apply a circular limit:

$$|v_{dq}| \le \frac{V_{dc}}{\sqrt3}\,m_{max}, \quad m_{max} = 0.95$$

Prioritize $v_d$ and clip $v_q$ to $\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}$.

Then compute the inverse Park and inverse Clarke to get $v_a, v_b, v_c$, and apply min-max zero-sequence injection, which is equivalent to SVPWM:

$$v_0 = -\tfrac12\left(\max(v_{abc}) + \min(v_{abc})\right), \qquad D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{dc}}$$

### 1.4 PI Anti-Windup (Back-Calculation)

$$u = K_p e + I_k, \qquad u_s = \mathrm{sat}(u)$$

$$I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s e + K_{aw}T_s(u_s - u), \qquad K_{aw} \approx K_i/K_p$$
