**Architecture note.** In this architecture the H743 does not drive the MOSFETs. It sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs, and those MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM. The thermal model and the DMA model therefore apply to different silicon. Both are covered below.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /\text{K}$$

This α corresponds to a normalized factor of about 1.5 at 125 °C, which is typical for trench MOSFETs.

At T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS} = 1.2\,\text{mΩ}\times(1+0.005\times 85) = 1.71\ \text{mΩ}$$

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step mode with synchronous rectification

At every instant, the motor current flows through exactly two conducting devices:
- one in the PWM leg (either the high side for the D fraction or the low side for the 1−D fraction), and
- the low side of the sink leg.

Including PWM current ripple:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

Ripple is negligible at 80 A with typical motor inductance, so I take I_rms ≈ I.

**Total conduction loss per ESC channel:**

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)\; (+\, I^2 R_{trace})$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.71\,\text{mΩ} = \mathbf{21.9\ W}$$
