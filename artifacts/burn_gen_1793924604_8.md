## 0. Architecture Clarification and Design Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** It runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. Each ESC channel uses a dedicated low-cost MCU running AM32 (AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071) that generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. This is the only practical topology. The H743 has neither the comparator/ADC channel count nor the hard-real-time headroom to run 4× sensorless 6-step commutation alongside ArduPilot.

**The 80 A rating can only be a short burst on a nano board.** At 80 A on all four channels, the board dissipates about 110 W. A 30.5 × 30.5 mm board cannot sustain that, and Section 3 shows burst duration is limited to about 1.5–2 s. Market this as "80 A burst / ≈35–40 A continuous per channel" and lock it in firmware via AM32 current limiting.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-step, complementary PWM)

### 1.1 Commutation-state decomposition

AM32 drives trapezoidal 6-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM on the high-side leg and the lower phase's low-side FET held ON. Over one electrical period, each phase spends:
- **⅓ PWM-active:** HS on for D, LS synchronous rectifier on for (1−D)
- **⅓ low-side held on**
- **⅓ floating** (BEMF sensing)
