## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

During any 60° commutation sector, exactly two legs are active:
- **Switching leg:** the high-side (HS) FET conducts for D·T_sw, and the low-side sync FET for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Static leg:** the LS FET conducts continuously.

The instantaneous conducting resistance is therefore always 2·R_DS(on), independent of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph}^2 \left[ D R_{HS} + (1-D) R_{LS} + R_{LS} \right] = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$
