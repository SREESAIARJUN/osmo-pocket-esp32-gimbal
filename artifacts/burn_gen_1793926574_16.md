## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology

The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification (AM32 default), evaluated at peak current.

- At any instant, motor current flows through **exactly two conducting MOSFET channels**:
  - one in the PWM leg (high side during D, low side during 1−D), and
  - one in the static low-side leg.
- Therefore the bridge conduction loss is independent of duty cycle at constant phase current:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I^2 \, R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature Dependence (Self-Consistent)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 108 °C:

$$R = 1.2\times(1+0.006\times83) = 1.80\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\times 80^2 \times 1.80\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{23.0\ W\ per\ motor}$$

Per FET (time-averaged across the 6 positions): 23.0 / 6 ≈ **3.84 W**

### 1.3 Copper/Interconnect Loss

Assume 0.15 mΩ per current path (pads, vias, phase pour) and two paths in series:

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.30\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{1.9\ W\ per\ motor}$$

This is not negligible. Use 2 oz on the outer layers plus stitched inner power planes.

### 1.4 Thermal Runaway Criterion

Stability requires:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}\cdot R_{th} < 1 \;\Rightarrow\; 2I^2R_{25}\alpha \cdot R_{th,j-a} < 1$$

At 80 A:

$$\partial P/\partial T = 2\times6400\times1.2\text{m}\times0.006 = 0.092\ \text{W/K}$$

The bridge-level R_th must therefore be below **10.8 K/W**. This is satisfied with airflow, but marginal on a bench test without props.
