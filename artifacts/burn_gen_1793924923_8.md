## 1. Field-Oriented Control (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle with delay compensation

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s, \qquad \omega_e = p\,\omega_m
$$

The 1.5·Tₛ term compensates for one sample of computation delay plus half a PWM period of ZOH delay.

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant, ia + ib + ic = 0)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$
$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.3 Plant in the dq frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda
$$
