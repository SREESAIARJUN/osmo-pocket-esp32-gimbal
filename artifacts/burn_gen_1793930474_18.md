# STM32H743 FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model (Job #44)

## 0. Architectural Assumptions

Three points need to be settled before any numbers are meaningful.

1. **The STM32H743 cannot be the ESC controller.** Bi-directional DShot600 is a link *from* the flight controller *to* the ESCs. The architecture is therefore:
   - an **H743 flight controller** running ArduPilot, and
   - **four independent ESC MCUs** (AT32F421K8U7, running AM32), each driving one 3-phase bridge.

   Everything below assumes this split.

2. **80A burst does not fit a 20×20 mm "nano" board.** Four bridges need 24 MOSFETs. Even at 3.3×3.3 mm each, that is about 261 mm² of silicon footprint before drivers, caps and copper pours, on a board with only 400 mm² of total area. My recommendation:
   - **ESC board:** 30.5×30.5 mm (M3 or M2 mounting).
   - **FC board:** 20×20 or 30.5×30.5 mm, as a 2-board stack.

   If 20×20 is non-negotiable, derate to about 35–40A burst. The thermal model in §3 quantifies why.

3. **The MOSFET and timing parameters are placeholders.** They are representative of 40V, 3.3×3.3 DFN parts. **Substitute the selected part's datasheet values** before sign-off. All formulas are parameterized for this purpose.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

At T_j = 125°C: R_ds = 1.2 mΩ × 1.6 = **1.92 mΩ**.

### 1.2 RMS correction for ripple

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2\cdot0.9\cdot0.1}{10\,\mu H\cdot 48\,kHz} = 4.7\ \text{A}
$$

$$
I_{rms} = I\sqrt{1+\frac{1}{12}\left(\frac{\Delta I}{I}\right)^2} = 80\sqrt{1+0.00029} \approx 80.01\ \text{A}
$$

The ripple correction is negligible at this current, so I_rms ≈ I_DC.

### 1.3 Six-step commutation and per-FET duty

Each phase leg cycles through three equal states, each lasting 1/3 of the electrical period:

| Leg state | High-side FET | Low-side FET |
|---|---|---|
| High-active (chopping) | on for D | complementary, on for (1−D), plus dead-time diode |
| Low-active (return) | off | fully on |
| Floating (BEMF sense) | off | off |

Base term: I²R = 80² × 1.92 mΩ = **12.29 W**.

$$
P_{cond,HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,D\,I^2R_{ds} \qquad
P_{cond,LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,[(1-D)+1]\,I^2R_{ds}
$$

At D = 0.9:
- P_cond,HS = **3.69 W**
- P_cond,LS = **4.51 W**

At D = 1.0 (no chopping): both are 4.10 W, and switching loss is zero.
