**Architecture clarification.** In a 4-in-1 AIO, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates DShot. It does **not** generate the 48 kHz motor PWM. ## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path (Trapezoidal, Synchronous Complementary PWM)

At any instant two phases conduct:
- **PWM leg:** current flows in the high-side FET during D and in the synchronous low-side FET during (1−D).
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET is on for the full 60° sector.

So there are always exactly two R_DS(on) in series with the phase current:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{Cu}$$

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

### 1.2 Ripple Correction

$$I_{ph,rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12},\qquad \Delta I \approx \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

With L_ph ≈ 15 µH, ΔI is only a few amps. The correction is under 0.5 % and is neglected below.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

- R_DS(on) = 0.7 × 1.6 = 1.12 mΩ
- FET conduction: P_cond,FET = 2 × 80² × 1.12 mΩ = **14.3 W per channel**
- Copper: P_Cu = 80² × 0.5 mΩ = **3.2 W**

### 1.4 Per-FET Share

Over one electrical revolution, each of the 6 FETs carries current for 120°. So the time-averaged dissipation per FET is about 14.3 / 6 ≈ **2.4 W**.

Below roughly 1 k eRPM (startup, stall, punch-out from hover) the electrical period becomes comparable to the FET thermal time constant (~10 ms). In that regime treat the worst-case FET as dissipating up to 7.2 W.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. Each PWM period has one turn-on and one turn-off of the high-side FET, under hard switching against the low-side body diode.

| Term | Formula | Value at 25.2 V / 80 A / 48 kHz |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 20 ns × 48 k = **0.97 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 40 nC × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.05 W** |
| C_oss | ½·C_oss,er·V²·f_sw | 0.5 × 1.5 nF × 635 × 48 k = **0.02 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw | 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k = **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (6 FETs) | Q_g·V_GS·f_sw per switching FET | 60 nC × 10 × 48 k ≈ 0.03 W per FET, ~0.06–0.17 W total (in driver and R_g) |

**P_sw,ch ≈ 1.7 W per channel.**

Design notes:
- **Dead time** is the second-largest switching term. Tuning it from 100 ns to about 50 ns (AM32 `dead_time`) saves about 0.3 W per channel.
- **Switching frequency.** Losses scale linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz, P_sw ≈ 0.85 W, at the cost of more current ripple and audible noise.

## 3. Total Loss Budget at 80 A Burst

| | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 14.3 W | 57.2 W |
| Copper / shunt | 3.2 W | 12.8 W |
| Switching + dead time | 1.7 W | 6.8 W |
| **Total** | **~19.2 W** | **~77 W** |

**Efficiency check.** Battery current is I_bat ≈ D·I_ph. At full throttle with D ≈ 0.95, power in per channel ≈ 25.2 × 76 ≈ 1.9 kW. The loss is therefore about 1 %, which is fine electrically. The problem is purely thermal density.

## 4. Thermal Model

### 4.1 Lumped Two-Node Network

$$T_j(t) = T_{board}(t) + P_{FET}\,(R_{\theta JC} + R_{\theta,spread})$$

$$T_{board}(t) = T_a + P_{tot}\,\theta_{BA}\left(1-e^{-t/\tau}\right),\qquad \tau = \theta_{BA}\,C_{th}$$

### 4.2 Parameters for a 20×20 mm Board in Prop Wash

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Wetted area A | ≈ 8×10⁻⁴ m² (both sides, components included) |
| Convection coefficient h | ≈ 100 W/m²K (forced, prop wash) |
| θ_BA = 1/(hA) | ≈ **12.5 K/W** |
| Thermal mass C_th | ~6 g Cu + FR4 + FETs, ≈ **4 J/K** |
| τ | ≈ **50 s** |
| R_θJC | 1.5 K/W |
| R_θ,spread | ≈ 8 K/W (dense board, thermal vias, 2 oz inner layers) |
| T_a | 40 °C (summer, inside frame) |

### 4.3 Steady State

ΔT = 77 W × 12.5 K/W ≈ 960 K.

**There is no steady state at 80 A.** 80 A on a 20×20 board can only ever be a transient rating.
