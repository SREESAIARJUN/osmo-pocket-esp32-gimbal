## 0. Architecture Correction (Read First)

An STM32H743 cannot practically run ArduPilot and also commutate four motors at 48 kHz. The partitioning is therefore:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400–480 MHz | ArduPilot, sends BDShot600 to the four ESC MCUs |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 (AM32 firmware) | Trapezoidal 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, 250 V, bootstrap) | Drives 6 N-FETs per motor |
| Power FETs ×24 | 40 V, ~0.9 mΩ, PDFN 5×6 or 3.3×3.3 | Inverter legs |

**Feasibility flag.** 80 A per motor on a ~30×30 mm AIO board is a burst rating only, limited to a few seconds. Section 3 derives the actual time limit. Continuous rating will land around 35–45 A with good prop-wash airflow.

**Operating assumptions:**
- Battery: 6S, V_bus,max = 25.2 V
- Phase current: I = 80 A peak burst
- PWM frequency: f_sw = 48 kHz
- PWM scheme: complementary (synchronous) PWM

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step current path

In any 60° commutation sector, current flows through two phases:
- The **PWM phase**: its high side (HS) is on for duty D, and its low side (LS) is on for (1−D) as a synchronous rectifier.
- The **return phase**: its LS is on for 100% of the sector.

Instantaneous conduction power per motor:

$$P_{cond}(t) = I^2 R_{ds}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

**Key result:** with synchronous rectification, conduction loss is **independent of duty**. Two R_ds(on) are always in series with the motor current.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{ds} = 0.90 \times 1.383 = 1.245\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Ripple correction

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{ph-ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

Inputs: a typical 2306-class motor has L_ph-ph ≈ 20 µH. Worst case is D = 0.5.

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\times10^{-6}\times 48\,000} = 6.56\ \text{A}$$

$$\frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 3.6\ \text{A}^2$$

This is negligible against 80² = 6400 A². Ignore it at burst. It matters only at light load or with low-inductance motors.

### 1.4 Copper and shunt path

Assumed series resistances:
- PCB copper: R_pcb ≈ 0.35 mΩ per phase path, so ×2 per current loop
- Shunt: R_sh = 0.25 mΩ, in the common return

$$P_{Cu} = I^2(2R_{pcb} + R_{sh}) = 6400 \times 0.95\times10^{-3} = 6.08\ \text{W}$$

### 1.5 Conduction result (per motor, 80 A)

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.245\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{15.94\ W}$$

$$P_{cond,total} = 15.94 + 6.08 = \mathbf{22.0\ W}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM phase switches in each sector, so there are 2 hard transitions (one turn-on, one turn-off) per PWM period per motor.

### 2.1 Transition (V·I overlap) loss

The switching time is dominated by the Miller plateau:

$$t_{r,f} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_g}$$

Assumed values:
- Q_gd ≈ 8 nC
- FD6288 drive: ~1.2 A source, 1.5 A sink, reduced by the gate resistor R_g = 4.7 Ω

Resulting edge times: t_r ≈ 16 ns and t_f ≈ 14 ns.

$$P_{tr} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.452\ W}$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction

Inputs: t_dt = 50 ns, V_f = 0.8 V, two dead-time intervals per period.

$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\text{n}\times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.307\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

Assumed Q_rr ≈ 40 nC at 80 A and fast di/dt. Verify this value; it varies strongly with di/dt.

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

### 2.4 Output capacitance

Assumed Q_oss ≈ 60 nC at 25 V.

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V f_{sw} = \mathbf{0.036\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

Inputs: Q_g = 50 nC, V_gs = 10 V. Two FETs in the PWM leg switch each period.

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

Most of this dissipates in R_g and the driver, not in the FET die.

### 2.6 Switching result

$$P_{sw} = 1.452 + 0.307 + 0.048 + 0.036 + 0.048 = \mathbf{1.89\ W/motor}$$

### 2.7 Total loss at 80 A burst

| Term | Per motor | ×4 motors |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 15.94 W | 63.8 W |
| Copper + shunt | 6.08 W | 24.3 W |
| Switching (all) | 1.89 W | 7.6 W |
| **Total** | **23.9 W** | **95.7 W** |

**Efficiency check:**
- Output: 25.2 V × 80 A × 4 ≈ 8.06 kW electrical (at D → 1)
- Loss: ≈ 1.2%, so efficiency ≈ 98
