## 0. Architecture Correction and Design Assumptions

These assumptions shape every number below. I've recorded them explicitly so the review board can challenge them.

| # | Assumption | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **The STM32H743 is the flight controller and runs ArduPilot. It does not drive the gates directly.** Each of the 4 ESC channels has its own MCU (AT32F421K8U7 running AM32) generating 48 kHz complementary PWM. | ArduPilot is not ESC firmware. Bidirectional DShot is a FC↔ESC-MCU protocol. Running 4×3-phase FOC/6-step at 48 kHz on the H743 alongside EKF3 is not a supported ArduPilot configuration. |
| A2 | "Nano" means a **20×20 mm stack (M2 mounting holes, 16×16 mm for some variants)** with 3.3×3.3 mm PQFN MOSFETs. | Industry convention. |
| A3 | "80 A burst" means **80 A motor-phase current per channel for ≤1 s**. | This is the worst case for conduction loss. Battery current is lower: I_batt = D·I_ph. |
| A4 | Supply is 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max (22.2 V nominal). MOSFET V_DS rating is 40 V. | 40 V gives about 37% margin over V_max for inductive spikes. |
| A5 | Commutation is 6-step trapezoidal with synchronous rectification (AM32 default). | Two legs conduct at any time. One leg is PWM-switched. |

> **Principal-level verdict up front:** The physics in §3 shows that 80 A/channel on a 20×20 board with 3.3×3.3 FETs is achievable only as a **~0.5–1 s burst** with significant airflow. Continuous rating on this form factor is realistically **25–35 A/channel**. If the requirement is a genuine repeatable 80 A burst, move to 30.5×30.5 mm with 5×6 mm FETs or paralleled FETs. I'll show the numbers that support this, rather than just certifying the spec.

**Temperature scaling:**

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,(1.0045)^{T_j-25}$$

At T_j = 125 °C, the factor is (1.0045)¹⁰⁰ ≈ 1.566.

- Typ: 0.90 × 1.566 = **1.41 mΩ**
- Max: 1.20 × 1.566 = **1.88 mΩ**

### 1.2 Current Path per ESC Channel

In 6-step synchronous mode, phase current I_ph flows through exactly **one high-side and one low-side device in different legs at every instant**. The PWM-switched leg's current alternates between its HS and LS FET with duty D, while the other leg's LS FET conducts 100% of the time. So the conduction-loss sum is independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2\,\big[\,D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS}\,\big] + I_{ph}^2 R_{LS} + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

With R_HS = R_LS = R_DS:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2\,(2R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{Cu})}$$

R_Cu is the board copper path (phase pad → FET → bus → return): ≈0.25 mΩ for 2 oz inner + 3 oz outer copper on a 20×20 board.
