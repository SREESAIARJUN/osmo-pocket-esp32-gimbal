# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Nano Form Factor)
## System Architecture, Loss Model, DShot Timer Allocation, Netlists, and Thermal Analysis

**Document:** Job #2, Rev A (Preliminary Design Baseline)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Three Key Findings

### Architecture

- **Flight controller MCU:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot.
- **ESC MCUs:** 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071, one per motor, running AM32 firmware.
  - The H743 does not commutate motors. A 4-in-1 ESC runs one small MCU per channel.
- **Topology per motor:** 3-phase bridge with synchronous (complementary) PWM and trapezoidal 6-step commutation.

### Baseline Parameters

All component values are representative and must be re-verified against the selected datasheets before layout freeze.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S, full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current per motor | I_pk | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET (30–40 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3 class) R_ds(on) at 25 °C, V_gs = 10 V | R_ds25 | 0.70 mΩ |
| R_ds(on) temperature coefficient (125 °C / 25 °C) | k_T | 1.5 |
| Rise / fall time (driver-limited) | t_r / t_f | 10 ns / 10 ns |
| Dead time | t_dt | 50 ns |
| Body-diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Reverse-recovery charge | Q_rr | 40 nC |
| Energy-equivalent output capacitance | C_oss(er) | 1.5 nF |
| Total gate charge (10 V) | Q_g | 50 nC |
| Junction-to-case thermal resistance (exposed pad) | R_θJC | 1.5 K/W |
| Board | — | 30 × 30 mm, 8 layers, 2 oz copper on all layers |

### Key Findings

1. **80 A × 4 motors is only a transient rating.** Steady-state dissipation is about 72 W on a 30×30 board with roughly 5 J/K of thermal mass. That allows a burst of about 4–5 s from a 40 °C start without airflow. The full model is in §4.
2. **Battery-side current at full burst is about 300 A.** This exceeds XT60 and XT90 ratings and most 6S packs. The 80 A figure must be specified as per-channel and non-simultaneous.
3. **Bidirectional DShot600 on the H743 is straightforward because of DMAMUX.** It needs 6 DMA streams for 4 motors when using two timers with paired-channel input capture (§5).

## 1. Power Stage Topology

With complementary PWM, exactly two MOSFET channels are in the current path at all times:

- **ON-state:** high-side (HS) FET of phase A, then winding, then low-side (LS) FET of phase B.
- **OFF-state (freewheel):** synchronous LS FET of phase A, then winding, then LS FET of phase B.

So the instantaneous conduction resistance per motor is always 2 × R_ds(on), independent of duty cycle. Only the PWM leg switches, so there is one hard-switched leg per motor at any time.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 General Form

$$
P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot \left( 2R_{ds(on)}(T_j) + R_{Cu} + R_{shunt} \right)
$$

$$
R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /\mathrm{K}
$$

### 2.2 Worst Case at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

R_ds(on) at 125 °C = 0.70 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.05 mΩ**

| Term | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFET conduction | 80² × 2 × 1.05 mΩ | **13.44 W** |
| Copper path (phase pour + vias + pads, est. 0.5 mΩ) | 6400 × 0.5 mΩ | **3.20 W** |
| Phase/bus shunt (0.25 mΩ, if low-side sensing) | 6400 × 0.25 mΩ | **1.60 W** |
| **Total conduction, per motor** | | **≈ 18.2 W** |

### 2.3 Per-Device Distribution

Over one electrical revolution, each leg is the PWM leg for 1/3 of the time, the LS return for 1/3, and floating for 1/3.

- **HS FET during PWM:** loss = I²R × D
- **LS sync FET (same leg):** loss = I²R × (1 − D)
- **LS return FET:** carries current 100% of its interval

At burst current, D ≈ 0.9–1.0, so the worst-case device per sector is

$$
P_{FET,max} \approx I^2 R_{ds} = 6.7\ \mathrm{W\ (instantaneous)}, \qquad \bar P_{FET} \approx 13.44/6 = 2.24\ \mathrm{W\ (average)}
$$

**Self-heating feedback.** R_ds(on) rises with T_j, so conduction loss rises with T_j. The loop is stable only when

$$
\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\cdot R_{\theta} < 1
$$

With the Section 4 numbers, the loop gain is 13.44 × 0.005 / 1.5 × R_θ, which is approximately 0.045 × R_θ.

- **Stable** with heatsinking where R_θ,J-ambient < 20 K/W.
- **Thermally unstable** on a bare, still-air nano board, where R_θ is about 40 K/W total. This is why the burst duration must be firmware-limited.

## 3. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

### 3.1 Hard-Switching Overlap (one switching leg)

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\,\mathrm{ns} \times 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = \mathbf{0.968\ W}
$$

### 3.2 Dead-Time Body-Diode Conduction (two transitions per period)

$$
P_{dt} = V_f \cdot I \cdot 2 t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\,\mathrm{ns} \times 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = \mathbf{0.307\ W}
$$

### 3.3 Reverse Recovery

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\,\mathrm{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}
$$

The added I_rr also lengthens the HS turn-on overlap. Budget an additional ~0.1 W, giving **0.15 W**.

### 3.4 Output Capacitance

$$
P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2} C_{oss(er)} V^2 f_{sw} \times 2 = 1.5\,\mathrm{nF} \times 635 \times 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = \mathbf{0.046\ W}
$$

### 3.5 Gate Drive

This loss is dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FETs.

$$
P_g = 2 Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} = 2 \times 50\,\mathrm{nC} \times 10 \times 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}
$$
