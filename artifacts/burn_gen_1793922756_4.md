## 0. Baseline Assumptions and One Key Architectural Decision

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 should be the **flight controller only**. Each ESC channel gets its own small MCU running AM32 firmware.

- **Why not have the H743 commutate all four motors?** That would require 4 × 3 = 12 complementary PWM pairs plus 4 × 3 back-EMF (BEMF) zero-cross comparators. It would also need hard real-time commutation running alongside the ArduPilot scheduler. ArduPilot does not support this, and the H7 has no analog comparator per phase.
- **How the ESC requirements map:**
  - "48 kHz switching" is an ESC-side parameter.
  - "Bi-directional DShot600" is the H743 ↔ ESC-MCU link.
