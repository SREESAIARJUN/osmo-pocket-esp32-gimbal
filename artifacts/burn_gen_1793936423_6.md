## 0. Assumptions & Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4/H7-class Cortex-M4F/M7, single-precision FPU |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R = 5.6 Ω, L = 2.2 mH, K_t ≈ 0.09 N·m/A, J_load ≈ 4e-4 kg·m² (pitch) |
| Bus | 12 V, center-aligned PWM 20 kHz, 2-shunt or inline current sense |
| Encoder | 14-bit magnetic absolute (AS5048/MT6701-class), read every current cycle |
| IMU A (feedback) | ICM-42688-P on the camera plate. Measures camera inertial rate and attitude. |
| IMU B (reference) | ICM-42688-P on the gimbal base/handle. Measures disturbance rate and vibration spectrum. |
| IMU ODR | 8 kHz gyro, 1 kHz accel, SPI at 24 MHz |
