# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Model, Thermal Budget, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation

## 0. Assumptions and Scope

These assumptions drive every result below. Replace the placeholders with datasheet values for your real parts before sign-off.

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller (FC). It runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600 on 4 timer channels. The 4-in-1 ESC has its own per-channel MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32), which own the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 does not generate the motor PWM. ## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In any 60° commutation sector, current flows through 2 FETs in series:
- the PWM'd leg, alternating between HS (during D·T) and LS (during (1−D)·T), and
- the static LS of the return phase.

The PWM'd leg alternates but always has exactly one FET conducting. The instantaneous path resistance is therefore:

$$R_{path}(T_j) = 2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + R_{Cu}$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence (self-consistent)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2\,\big(2R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + R_{Cu}\big)$$

R_Cu is the board copper from the phase pad through the FETs to the bus. For 2 oz × 4 layers on a nano board, take R_Cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ.

**At T_j = 125 °C:** R_DS = 0.9 × 1.55 = 1.395 mΩ

$$P_{cond,ch} = 80^2 \times (2\times1.395 + 0.3)\,\text{m}\Omega = 6400 \times 3.09\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.8\ W}$$

Of this, the FETs dissipate 6400 × 2.79 mΩ = 17.9 W and the copper dissipates 1.9 W.

### 1.3 Per-FET average

Each of the 6 FETs conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle at full duty:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{17.9}{6}\times 2 = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}}{3} = \mathbf{2.98\ W}$$

The factor of 2 accounts for 2 conducting FETs among 6.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 V-I overlap (hard-switched FET)

Gate currents through the Miller plateau:

$$I_{G,on} = \frac{V_{DRV}-V_{pl}}{R_G} = \frac{10-3.5}{5} = 1.30\ \text{A}, \qquad I_{G,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_G} = 0.70\ \text{A}$$

Transition times:

$$t_r = \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{G,on}} = 10.0\ \text{ns}, \qquad t_f = \frac{13\ \text{nC}}{0.70} = 18.6\ \text{ns}$$

Overlap loss:

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5\times25.2\times80\times28.6\,\text{ns}\times48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.38\ W}$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction (synchronous FET)

$$P_{DT} = V_F\,I_{ph}\,(2t_d)\,f_{sw} = 0.8\times80\times400\,\text{ns}\times48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$

This term is as large as the overlap loss. Cutting dead time from 200 ns to 100 ns saves ~0.6 W per channel. Only do this if shoot-through margin is verified on a scope across temperature.
