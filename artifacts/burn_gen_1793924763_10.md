# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal (Job #52)

I don't have the Job #52 spec sheet, so this design rests on the assumptions in §0. Every gain is derived from symbols, so you can substitute your measured parameters.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC/PMSM, e.g. GBM4108: 12N14P → **p = 7 pole pairs**, R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 2–5 mH |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A or MA730) on each motor |
| MCU | STM32G4/H7 class with FPU, center-aligned PWM, synchronous ADC |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 24 MHz, ODR 8 kHz, FIFO or DRDY |
| Kinematics | Yaw (outer) → Roll → Pitch (inner, camera) |

**Loop timing**

```
                     ┌──────── 1 kHz ──────┐ ┌──── 8 kHz ─────┐ ┌──── 24 kHz ────┐
 θ_ref ──►(+)─► Kθ ─►(+)──► PI_ω  ─────────►(+)─► PI_d/PI_q ──► SVPWM ─► Inverter ─► Motor
          ▲ −      ω_ff  ▲ −     i_q,ff(cog)▲ −    ▲                                │
          │              │                  │ i_dq └── Park/Clarke ◄── ADC ◄────────┤
          │              │                  └──────────────── θ_e = p·θ_m ◄── Encoder
          │              └── ω_joint ◄─ J⁻¹(q) ◄─ Notch/LPF ◄─ Dual ICM-42688-P (8 kHz)
          └───────────── attitude (Mahony) ◄─────────┘
```

Two design choices drive this architecture:

- **The velocity loop uses filtered gyro rate (inertial) rather than encoder speed.** Stabilization means rejecting base motion. The encoder only sees the relative joint motion.
- **The encoder is used for commutation and cogging phase only.**

## 1. FOC Current Loop (Innermost, 24 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, with θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant (Surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q \equiv K_t\, i_q
$$

### 1.3 PI Current Control with Decoupling

Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole R/L. This gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10}
$$

For f_pwm = 24 kHz, use ω_c ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz to 2π·2 kHz.

**Control law (feedforward decoupling):**

$$
v_d^* = PI_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = PI_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

**Voltage limit.** Saturate to the circle |v_dq| ≤ V_dc/√3, giving q priority after d. Use back-calculation anti-windup (§2.4).

### 1.4 SVPWM (Min-Max Zero-Sequence Injection)

$$
v_{a,b,c} = \text{invClarke}(\text{invPark}(v_d^*, v_q^*)),\quad
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc})+\min(v_{abc})\big)
$$

$$
D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{dc}}
$$

**Gimbal-specific practice: voltage-mode fallback.** High-R gimbal motors often have current signals too small to measure well. In that case, set v_q* = R·i_q* + ω_e·λ_m. The rest of the cascade is unchanged.

## 2. Cascaded Angle/Velocity Control

### 2.1 Mechanical Plant per Axis

$$
J\dot\omega = K_t i_q - \tau_{cog}(\theta_m) - B\omega - \tau_{dist}
$$

### 2.2 Velocity Loop (8 kHz): PI, Optional D-on-Measurement, Feedforward

$$
i_q^* = \underbrace{K_{p\omega}e_\omega + K_{i\omega}\!\int e_\omega\,dt - K_{d\omega}\,\dot{\omega}_f}_{\text{feedback}}
+ \underbrace{\frac{J\dot\omega^*}{K_t}}_{\text{accel FF}}
+ \underbrace{i_{q,cog}(\theta_m)}_{\text{§3}}
$$
