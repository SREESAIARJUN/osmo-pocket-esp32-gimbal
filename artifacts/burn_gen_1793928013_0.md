# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal, and DShot DMA Architecture (Job #36)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and One Upfront Warning

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot only. Motor commutation runs on four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class, AM32 firmware). The H743 does not commutate the motors. It sends bidirectional DShot600 to the ESC MCUs and receives eRPM telemetry back.

**Assumptions.** These are stated assumptions, not datasheet values. Replace them with your selected MOSFET's datasheet numbers before sign-off.

| Parameter | Symbol | Assumed value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S, full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current per motor | I | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET on-resistance @ 25 °C (40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm class) | R_ds25 | 0.8 mΩ |
| R_ds temperature coefficient | α | ≈0.0055 /K (→ ×1.4 at 100 °C) |
| Total gate charge / Miller charge | Q_g / Q_gd | 60 nC / 12 nC |
| Output charge / reverse-recovery charge @ 80 A | Q_oss / Q_rr | 40 nC / 100 nC |
| Body-diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns |
| Effective rise/fall time (FD6288-class driver, Rg ≈ 5 Ω) | t_r, t_f | ≈20 ns each |
| Gate drive voltage | V_gs | 10 V |
| Copper path resistance per motor (pads + pours + vias) | R_cu | 0.3 mΩ |

> ⚠️ **Engineering verdict up front:** 4 × 80 A on a 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm board is **not thermally sustainable**. The math below shows "80 A burst" is roughly a **3-second** capability from a warm start. Do not ship with an 80 A continuous label. Section 3 derives this.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Operating mode.** The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM. During each 60° electrical sector, three devices carry current:

- the high-side FET of the PWM'd phase, for duty D;
- its low-side synchronous rectifier, for (1−D);
- the low-side FET of the return phase, at 100%.

**Conduction loss per motor:**

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

This result is independent of D, which is a useful property of synchronous rectification.

**Temperature-corrected on-resistance:**

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] \approx 0.8 \times 1.4 = 1.12\ \text{m}\Omega \quad (T_j = 100\,°C)$$

**Results:**

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.12\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{14.3\ W/motor}$$

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{cu} \times 2\ \text{paths} \approx 80^2 \times 0.3\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{1.9\ W/motor}$$

The copper term uses an effective lumped value. Note that copper loss is not negligible at this current; it is about 12% of FET conduction loss.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one FET pair hard-switches per motor per PWM cycle.

| Loss term | Equation | Value per motor |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw = ½·25.2·80·40 ns·48 k | **1.94 W** |
| Coss | ½·Q_oss·V·f = ½·40 nC·25.2·48 k | 0.024 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f = 100 nC·25.2·48 k | 0.12 W |
| Dead-time body diode | V_f·I·2·t_dt·f = 0.8·80·200 ns·48 k | 0.61 W |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | 2·Q_g·V_gs·f | 0.058 W |
| **Switching subtotal** | | **≈2.75 W** |

**Per motor:** 14.3 + 1.9 + 2.75 ≈ **18.9 W**

**Board total, ESC section:** 4 × 18.9 ≈ **75.7 W**

Add about 1.5–2 W for the H743, the ESC MCUs, the BEC buck converters and the LDOs.

**Design levers:**

- **Dead time.** Dead-time loss scales linearly with t_dt. Tightening it from 100 ns to 50 ns saves about 1.2 W across the board. The constraint is shoot-through margin; verify it with a double-pulse test.
- **PWM frequency.** At 24 kHz, switching losses roughly halve, saving about 5.5 W total. The cost is more current ripple in low-inductance motors.

**Bus capacitor ripple.** Per motor, worst case at D = 0.5:

$$I_{rms} \approx I\sqrt{D(1-D)} = 40\ \text{A}$$

The four motors' PWM is uncorrelated, so ripple sums roughly as √4. That gives about **80 A_rms total**. Use ≥4× 22 µF X7R per motor close to the FETs, plus a 1000 µF low-ESR bulk capacitor at the pigtail.
