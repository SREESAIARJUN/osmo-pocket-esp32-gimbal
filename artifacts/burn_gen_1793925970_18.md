## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM.** In any practical 4-in-1 design, each motor bridge is commutated by its own ESC MCU, such as an AT32F421 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600 to those four ESC MCUs. The document is split along that boundary.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step commutation

In any 60° sector, one phase leg is PWM-switched (high side at duty D, low side at 1−D with synchronous rectification). Another phase's low-side FET is held fully on. Two FETs are therefore always in the current path.

Each sector's instantaneous conduction loss is:

$$
P_{cond,ch}(t) = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[\,D + (1-D)\,] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

Because each FET takes each role for one-third of the electrical cycle, the per-device average is:

$$
\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)}{6} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)}{3}
$$

(Exact per-device values depend on D. High-side FETs see D/3 and low-side FETs see (2−D)/3 of I²R. At high throttle, D ≈ 1, so the split is nearly even.)

### 1.2 Temperature dependence (self-consistent)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

This gives 1.2 mΩ × 1.6 = 1.92 mΩ at 125 °C. The loss and junction temperature must be iterated together (§2.3).

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| P_cond per channel | 2 × 80² × 1.92 mΩ = **24.6 W** |
| P_cond per FET (average) | **4.10 W** |
| P_cond, 4 channels | **98.3 W** |
| Same at T_j = 25 °C | 15.4 W/channel, 61.4 W total |

Copper and shunt losses are additional. 1 mm of 2 oz, 10 mm-wide plane on 4 layers ≈ 0.06 mΩ. Budget about 0.3 mΩ of path per channel, which adds about 1.9 W/channel at 80 A.
