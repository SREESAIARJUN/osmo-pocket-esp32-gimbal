## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| T_j | Factor | R_DS(on) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 | 1.50 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.375 | 2.06 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.50 | 2.25 mΩ |

Use **R = 2.1 mΩ** (T_j ≈ 100 °C) as the burst design point. Iterate if the thermal result in §3 lands elsewhere.

### 1.2 Current paths in 6-step with complementary PWM

In each 60° sector, two phases are active:

- **Switching leg:** the HS FET conducts for D·T_sw. The LS FET synchronously rectifies for (1−D)·T_sw, less dead time.
- **Return leg:** the LS FET is on continuously.

At every instant the phase current I_ph flows through **exactly two channels**. The motor conduction loss is therefore independent of D (body-diode dead-time conduction is handled in §2):

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.1\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{26.9\ W}$$

**Per-FET distribution.** Each FET is active for 1/3 of the electrical cycle:

$$P_{FET,HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\left[D + (\text{LS-return share})\right] I^2 R, \qquad \bar P_{FET} = \frac{26.9}{6} \approx \mathbf{4.5\ W\ (average)}$$

**Instantaneous loss** while conducting is 80² × 2.1 mΩ = **13.4 W**. This peak drives the junction-to-case transient in §3.3.

### 1.3 Copper (PCB) conduction loss

Phase and bus copper, assuming 2 oz outer layers plus 2 × 2 oz inner planes in parallel and an effective ~1.0 mΩ per motor current loop:

$$P_{Cu,motor} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \approx 6.4\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 Current-sense shunt

A single 0.25 mΩ shunt sits in the shared battery return. Battery current per motor is I_bat ≈ D·I_ph.

At D = 0.9 with 4 motors: I_bat ≈ 288 A, so

$$P_{shunt} = 288^2 \times 0.25\,\text{m}\Omega = 20.7\ \text{W}$$

⚠️ **Red flag:** 288 A of battery current through an XT60/pads on a 20 × 20 board is not credible. See §3.5.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one leg per motor hard-switches per PWM period: the HS turns on against the LS body diode or channel.

### 2.1 V–I overlap (hard switching)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

With t_r + t_f ≈ 20 ns (FD6288 ~1.5 A source / 1.8 A sink, R_g = 0 Ω, Q_gd ≈ 6 nC):

$$P_{VI} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 40\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$
