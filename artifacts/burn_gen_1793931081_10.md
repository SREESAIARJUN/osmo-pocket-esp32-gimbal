### 0.1 Architectural clarifications

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In any ArduPilot-compatible 4-in-1, each ESC channel runs its own MCU. Typical choices are AM32 on STM32G071/AT32F421, or BLHeli_32. The H743 only generates bidirectional DShot600 and decodes the eRPM telemetry. The 48 kHz PWM, dead-time and commutation live in ESC firmware.

2. **"Nano" vs. 80 A.** 80 A burst per channel is not credible on a 20×20 mm board. This analysis assumes:
   - a 30.5×30.5 mm ESC (≈36×36 mm outline),
   - 8-layer construction with 2 oz copper, 12 MOSFETs,
   - an FC stacked on top.
