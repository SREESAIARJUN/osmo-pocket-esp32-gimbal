## 1. Loss Model (per motor, i.e. one 3-phase bridge of 6 FETs)

### 1.1 Conduction loss

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, the current always flows through **two FETs in series**: one high-side and one low-side (or the low-side complement during the off-time).

R_DS(on) rises with temperature:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × (1 + 0.005 × 75) = **1.375 mΩ**

Conduction loss is:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j) = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.375\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{17.6\ W}$$

This splits across the conducting FETs in proportion to duty. At high throttle (D → 1), the high-side FET of the active phase carries most of it, about **8.8 W in a single 3.3×3.3 mm package**. That is the hotspot.

**PCB copper loss** (often ignored, and significant at 80 A):

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{trace}$$

- Assume a 2 oz path of about 0.4 mΩ for phase + bus path: 80² × 0.4 mΩ ≈ **2.6 W**.
- Copper resistance also rises about 0.39 %/K.

**Note on R_DS(on) thermal runaway:** P_cond rises with T_j, which raises T_j further. Solve iteratively:

$$T_j = T_{board} + R_{\theta JC}\,P(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Switching losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-switched FET (one per bridge at any instant) incurs hard-switching loss.

**V-I overlap:**

$$P_{sw}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(20\text{ ns})(48\text{ kHz}) = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

**Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss}=Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw}=40\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.048\text{ W}$$

**Reverse recovery** (body diode of the complementary FET):

$$P_{rr}=Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw}=50\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k}=0.060\text{ W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two dead-time intervals per period):

$$P_{dt}=V_{SD}\,I\,(2t_{dead})f_{sw}=0.8\cdot80\cdot200\text{ns}\cdot48\text{k}=\mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**Gate drive** (complementary pair switching; loss mostly lands in the driver and gate resistors):

$$P_g = 2\,Q_gV_{GS}f_{sw}=2\cdot40\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k}=0.04\text{ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-motor and total budget at 80 A

| Term | W per motor |
|---|---|
| Conduction (2 FETs, 100 °C) | 17.6 |
| Copper | 2.6 |
| V-I switching | 0.97 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 |
| Q_oss + Q_rr | 0.11 |
| Gate | 0.04 |
| **Total** | **≈ 21.9 W** |

All four motors at 80 A gives **≈ 88 W**. The battery delivers 320 A, so on 6S the loss is about 1.1 % of roughly 8 kW.

**Design levers:**
- Conduction dominates by about 15×, so a lower R_DS(on) matters far more than switching speed.
- Dropping f_sw to 24 kHz saves only about 0.8 W per motor and adds acoustic noise and current ripple. 48 kHz is fine.
- Cutting dead time from 100 ns to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W per motor.

### 1.4 Thermal model

Use a two-node lumped RC model: FET junction to board copper, and board to air.

$$C_{b}\frac{dT_b}{dt}=P_{tot}-\frac{T_b-T_a}{R_{\theta BA}},\qquad T_j=T_b+R_{\theta JC}P_{FET}$$

Solution for a step in power:

$$T_b(t)=T_a+P_{tot}R_{\theta BA}\left(1-e^{-t/\tau}\right),\quad \tau=R_{\theta BA}C_b$$

**Thermal mass of a 20×20 AIO** (PCB, copper planes, FETs, capacitors, about 4 g):

C_b ≈ 4 g × ~0.75 J/(g·K) ≈ **3 J/K**

**Board-to-air resistance** with prop-wash airflow, 20×20 mm, both sides:

R_θBA ≈ 10–15 K/W. Take **12 K/W**, giving τ ≈ 36 s.

**Steady state is impossible.** At 88 W:

ΔT = 88 × 12 ≈ **1056 K**

**Burst analysis.** Because the burst is short compared with τ, the board heats almost adiabatically:

$$\frac{dT_b}{dt}\approx\frac{P}{C_b}=\frac{88}{3}\approx 29\text{ K/s}$$

**Hotspot junction:**

$$T_j = T_b + R_{\theta JC}\cdot P_{FET} \approx T_b + 1.5\times 8.8 = T_b + 13\text{ K}$$

For pulses under about 10 ms, use the datasheet Z_θJC(t) curve instead of R_θJC. The rise will be smaller.

**Allowed burst duration.** Start at T_a = 40 °C (warm day, idle board). Limit T_j to 125 °C for derating margin, which leaves the board 72 K of headroom:

| Load case | Max burst duration |
|---|---|
| All 4 motors at 80 A | 72 / 29 ≈ **2.5 s** |
| 1 motor at 80 A, 3 at 20 A hover | About 25 W total → **≈ 8 s** |
| Hover (~15 A per motor, ~1.5 W per motor, ~6 W total) | ΔT_ss ≈ 72 K. Marginal in still air, OK with prop wash. |

**Firmware protection:**
- Set the AM32 temperature limit to about 110 °C.
- Enable current limiting at about 60 A continuous-equivalent.
- In ArduPilot, monitor ESC telemetry temperature via `SERVO_BLH_*` / `ESC_TELEM`.

## 2. DShot600 Bi-Directional: Timing Derivation

**Forward frame (FC to ESC):**
- Bit rate: 600 kbit/s, so t_bit = 1.667 µs.
- Frame: 16 bits = 11-bit throttle + telemetry bit + 4-bit CRC, giving **26.7 µs**.
- In bi-directional mode the CRC is inverted and the line idles high (inverted signaling).
