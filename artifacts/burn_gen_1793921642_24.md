# STM32H743 AIO + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bidirectional DShot Design Package

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Feasibility Finding

I don't have the Job #21 BOM, stack-up, or mechanical drawing, so every number below comes from **declared assumptions**. Replace them with datasheet values and the equations will carry through. The netlists are reference topology. They are not fab-ready and need ERC/DRC and layout review.

**Key finding up front:** a **4 × 80 A simultaneous burst** is not thermally viable on a nano-class board for more than about 2 s, and the realistic continuous rating is **about 15 A per channel with prop-wash cooling**. Section 3 shows the derivation. The "80 A" figure is a short-burst rating, and it should be specified as such (for example, "80 A / 1 s, single-channel peak").

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At an initial guess of T_j = 110 °C: R_DS ≈ 1.0 × 1.47 ≈ **1.47 mΩ**. This is iterated in §3.4.
