## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.004\text{–}0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

With α = 0.004 K⁻¹ at T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × 1.40 = **1.40 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step current path

In each 60° sector, two phases carry current:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. The synchronous low-side FET conducts for (1 − D) during freewheel.
- **Phase B (static leg):** the low-side FET conducts 100% of the sector.

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2R_{DS}D + I^2R_{DS}(1-D) + I^2R_{DS} = 2I^2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

The result is independent of D. Synchronous rectification is what removes the D dependence.

At I = 80 A and R = 1.40 mΩ:

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2(80)^2(1.4\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{17.9\ W/motor}$$

### 1.3 Copper and interconnect loss

Assume 0.5 mΩ per motor current loop: phase pads, bus planes, and the shunt (0.25 mΩ, if fitted).

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.0005 = \mathbf{3.2\ W/motor}$$

### 1.4 Per-FET loss (for junction rise)

The static low-side FET conducts for 1/3 of each electrical cycle, i.e., two sectors out of six. While conducting, its instantaneous loss is I²R = 8.96 W.

- Junction rise during conduction: ΔT_jc ≈ 8.96 × 1.5 ≈ **13 K above case**.
- At these electrical frequencies the die thermal time constant (~ms) averages out. The junction therefore tracks the board temperature plus ~5–13 K.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. Per PWM period, there is one turn-on and one turn-off under current I and voltage V.

| Term | Equation | Value (V = 25.2 V, I = 80 A) |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f | 0.5·25.2·80·30 ns·48k = **1.45 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f | 40 nC·25.2·48k = **0.048 W** |
| Output capacitance | Q_oss·V·f (approximate) | 30 nC·25.2·48k = **0.036 W** |
| Dead-time body diode | V_SD·I·2t_dt·f | 0.8·80·200 ns·48k = **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (driver side) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f | 2·30 nC·10·48k = **0.029 W** |
| **Total** | | **≈ 2.17 W/motor** |

Design levers:

- **Dead-time diode loss** is a third of the switching total. Trimming dead time from 100 ns to 50 ns saves ~0.3 W per motor. First verify shoot-through margin on the scope at the hot temperature.
- **Gate drive power** largely dissipates in the driver IC and gate resistor, not in the FET.
- **Frequency scaling:** at 24 kHz, switching loss roughly halves to ~1.1 W. Lowering the frequency raises current ripple and acoustic noise.
