# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC: System Design Package (Job #66)

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU. It runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU, because the H743 does not drive the gates directly. A Cortex-M0+ part running AM32 is assumed (AT32F421 or STM32G071), with complementary PWM at 48 kHz.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

At any instant, two phases carry current.

- **PWM ON:** current flows through HS(A) → motor → LS(B).
- **PWM OFF, with synchronous rectification:** current flows through LS(A) → motor → LS(B).

So **two FETs are always in series** with the phase current, independent of duty cycle D. Dead time is the only exception and is treated separately in §2.

Over one electrical revolution, each FET conducts for at most 120°. Electrical periods are about 0.1–1 ms (for example, 14 poles at 30k RPM gives a 286 µs period). FET thermal time constants are in the ms–s range, so per-FET losses can be safely averaged over the period.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent conduction loss

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

**Distribution across FETs:**

- High-side FETs carry ≈ $I^2R\cdot D/3$ each.
- Low-side FETs carry ≈ $I^2R\,(1 + (1-D))/3$ each.
- **Low-side FETs run hotter at partial throttle.** Use them as the design-limiting device.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds(T_j) | P_cond per channel | P_cond for 4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 2·6400·1.0 mΩ = 12.8 W | 51.2 W |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ | 17.1 W | 68.4 W |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W | 74.2 W |

**PCB copper loss** must be added. Phase and bus copper is typically 0.3–0.5 mΩ per path, so add 2–3 W per channel. The model uses **R_pcb = 0.4 mΩ**, giving 2.6 W per channel.

### 1.4 Electro-thermal fixed point and runaway criterion

Define:
- $A = 2I^2R_{ds25}$
- $P_0 = P_{sw} + P_{pcb}$
- $R_\theta$ = the relevant junction-to-ambient thermal resistance

Then:

$$
T_j = T_a + R_\theta\left[A\,(1+\alpha(T_j-25)) + P_0\right]
$$

Solving for T_j:

$$
\boxed{T_j = \frac{T_a + R_\theta\,[A(1-25\alpha)+P_0]}{1 - R_\theta A \alpha}}
$$

**Thermal runaway** occurs when $R_\theta A\alpha \ge 1$.

At 80 A: A = 12.8 W, so runaway occurs at R_θ ≥ 1/(12.8·0.0045) = **17.4 K/W per channel**.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

With complementary PWM, only the **commutating high-side FET hard-switches** each cycle. The low-side FET commutates at near-zero voltage, because its body diode is already conducting during dead time.

### 2.1 V–I overlap loss

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw}
$$

$$
= 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 27\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.31\ W}
$$

Rise and fall times come from the gate drive: $t \approx Q_{gd+gs(th\to pl)}/I_{gate}$.

### 2.2 Reverse recovery of the opposite body diode

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 50\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.060\ W}
$$

The Q_rr-induced turn-on current spike also adds to the HS FET loss. Budget about 0.05 W.

### 2.3 Output capacitance loss

$$
P_{oss} = Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}
$$
