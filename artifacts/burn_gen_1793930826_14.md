# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal, and DShot Design Model

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

Some framing is needed first, because a few of the requirements shape the whole design.

1. **Architecture.** I'm modeling this as a 20×20 mm stack:
   - **FC board:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot.
   - **ESC board:** four independent ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class) running AM32, each with its own 3-phase driver and 6 MOSFETs.

   ArduPilot bidirectional DShot only makes sense with a separate ESC MCU. If you intend the H743 to drive the gate drivers directly (via HRTIM or TIM1/TIM8), the DShot section doesn't apply.

2. **"80 A burst" on a nano board is thermally a seconds-scale rating, not a continuous one.** The model below shows this quantitatively. A 20×20 board sustains roughly 20–30 A/channel continuously. Also note that 4 × 80 A = 320 A of battery current, which a nano stack's input connector, pads, and copper cannot carry. 80 A should be treated as a **single-channel transient** rating.
