### Two architectural corrections up front

1. **The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz switching.**
   - Each ESC channel's own MCU performs commutation and the 48 kHz PWM.
   - The H743 only emits DShot600 frames and decodes the eRPM telemetry that returns on the same wire.
   - The two domains share ground and power but no timing.
2. **"Nano" and "80 A burst" are in tension.**
   - The math below shows that a 20 × 20 mm board cannot survive 4 × 80 A for any useful duration.
   - I have therefore specified a 30.5 × 30.5 mm board. Even at that size, 80 A is a **few-second** rating, not a continuous one.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous PWM

At any instant, phase current flows through exactly two FETs:

| PWM state | Duty fraction | Conducting FETs |
|---|---|---|
| ON | D | HS(phase A) + LS(phase B) |
| OFF (freewheel) | 1 − D | LS(phase A) + LS(phase B) |

The duty terms cancel, so the conduction loss per motor channel is:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2\,R_{Cu}
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence of R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T_j = 100 °C:

R_DS = 0.9 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.31 mΩ**

### 1.3 Copper path resistance

The path runs battery pad → planes → FETs → motor pad.

$$
R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho_{Cu}(T)\,\ell}{w\,t\,n_{layers}}
$$

With ℓ ≈ 25 mm, w ≈ 6 mm, t = 70 µm, 4 paralleled layers, and ρ(100 °C) = 2.2 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m:

R_Cu ≈ **0.33 mΩ**

### 1.4 Loss at 80 A

| Term | Calculation | Loss per channel |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 2 × 6400 × 1.31 × 10⁻³ | **16.8 W** |
| Copper | 6400 × 0.33 × 10⁻³ | **2.1 W** |

Per-FET peak instantaneous loss: 6400 × 1.31 mΩ = **8.4 W**. Averaged over an electrical cycle, the per-FET loss is roughly ⅓ of the channel FET loss, about **2.8 W**.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Gate-charge transition times

Turn-on gate current:

$$
I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_{g,on}} = \frac{10 - 3.2}{4} = 1.70\ \text{A}
$$

Turn-off gate current:

$$
I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{g,off}} = \frac{3.2}{3} = 1.07\ \text{A}
$$

Transition times:

$$
t_{on} = \frac{Q_{gs2} + Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{14\,\text{nC}}{1.70\,\text{A}} = 8.2\ \text{ns}
$$

$$
t_{off} = \frac{14\,\text{nC}}{1.07\,\text{A}} = 13.1\ \text{ns}
$$

### 2.2 Loss components

Only the high-side FET of the active leg hard-switches. The synchronous low-side FET switches at near-zero voltage, so it contributes only dead-time diode loss.
