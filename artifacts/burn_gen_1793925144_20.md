# STM32H743 4-in-1 ESC Nano Flight Controller: Power Stage, Thermal, and DShot Design

## 0. Architecture Clarifications

Several points need to be settled first because they change the math.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 and *emits* bidirectional DShot600. Each of the four ESC channels needs its own commutation MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32), and that MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. This is an AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC) design with two firmware domains.
2. **"80 A burst" is treated as per-channel phase current.** If it means total battery current, the losses below drop by about 16×.
3. **Component parameters are representative placeholders.** Substitute your selected MOSFET's datasheet values into the equations.
4. **On a 20×20 mm nano board, 80 A per channel is only a sub-second capability.** Section 3 quantifies this. Recommend a 30.5×30.5 mm board or a lower rating.
