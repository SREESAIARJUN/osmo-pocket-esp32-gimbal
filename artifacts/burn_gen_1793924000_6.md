# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #52): System Model

## 0. Architecture Corrections

Three points in the request need correcting before the math is meaningful.

1. **The H743 does not generate the 48 kHz switching.** In a 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel has its own motor MCU running the commutation firmware (AM32 or BLHeli_32), for example an AT32F421 or GD32E230. That MCU produces the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to each ESC MCU over one bidirectional DShot600 wire. The two domains are modeled separately below.
2. **80 A per channel is not feasible on a nano board.** The math in §2 shows that a 20×20 mm board can carry 80 A on all four channels for only about 1 second, and roughly 17–25 A continuous. I give the model and the honest numbers rather than validating a marketing figure.
