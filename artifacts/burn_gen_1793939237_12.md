# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage and DShot Design Reference (Job #36)

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

These assumptions are stated up front because every number below depends on them. Substitute your actual BOM values into the formulas.

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Flight controller MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz, timer clock 200 MHz |
| ESC MCUs | 4× AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 does not commutate the motors directly. |
| Supply | 6S LiPo: 25.2 V full charge, 22.2 V nominal, 30 V absolute transient design limit |
| Commutation | Trapezoidal 6-step, synchronous rectification, complementary PWM at 48 kHz |
| MOSFET (reference part) | 40 V N-channel, PDFN 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 class: R_DS(on) 1.0 mΩ typ at 25 °C and V_GS = 10 V; Q_g = 50 nC; t_r = t_f = 20 ns; Q_rr = 40 nC; C_oss = 1 nF; V_SD = 0.8 V |
| Gate driver | 3-phase driver (e.g., FD6288Q class), V_drive approximately 10 V, dead time 100 ns |
| PCB | 30.5×30.5 mm, 8 layers, all 2 oz copper |
| Burst definition | 80 A phase current per motor for 2–5 s at 25 °C ambient, with forced airflow from the props |

> **Feasibility note:** "Nano" (20×20 mm) and "80 A burst per channel" are physically incompatible. The thermal capacitance calculation in §3 shows why. This design assumes a 30.5×30.5 mm board. A 20×20 mm variant should be derated to about 35–45 A burst.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /^\circ\text{C}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.0 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**.

If V_GS drops to about 7 V because the driver bootstrap sags, add roughly 10–15 %. This model uses 1.5 mΩ as the hot design value.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

At any instant, motor current flows through exactly one high-side path element and one low-side path element:

- During the ON portion of the PWM cycle (fraction D), current flows through HS(phase A) and LS(phase B).
- During the OFF portion (fraction 1−D), current freewheels through LS(A) via synchronous rectification and LS(B).

Therefore, at every instant, two R_DS(on) are in series with the motor current:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W\ per\ motor}$$

### 1.3 Distribution across the six FETs

Each phase leg conducts for 240° of every 360° electrical period: 120° high-side-active and 120° low-side-active.

**Averaged per FET** (the die thermal time constant is much longer than the electrical period above about 500 eRPM):

$$\bar P_{FET} = \frac{19.2}{6} = 3.2\ \text{W}$$

**Worst case** occurs at low duty cycle (D → 0, typical of a punch-out from hover). Here the low-side FET carries current for nearly its entire active window:

$$P_{LS,peak} = I^2 R_{DS} = 9.6\ \text{W} \text{ during its } 1/3 \text{ window, giving about } 3.2\text{–}4.8\text{ W average}$$

Design to **5 W per low-side FET** worst case.

### 1.4 Copper and shunt conduction losses

| Element | Resistance | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pad and trace, per path (2 oz, 4 parallel layers, ~8 mm) | ~0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| Low-side shunt (if fitted) | 0.5 mΩ | 3.2 W (consider omitting and using phase-current estimation instead) |
| Battery pad to bus, shared | 0.15 mΩ | I_bus² R (see §4) |

Excluding the shunt: **P_cond,total ≈ 20.8 W per motor**.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

### 2.1 Hard-switching V–I overlap

Only one hard-switched device exists per PWM period. In the commutating leg, the synchronous low-side FET turns on into a diode that is already conducting, so it soft-switches.

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

$$= 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Body-diode reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

Q_rr increases with di/dt and temperature, so budget **0.1 W**.

### 2.3 Output capacitance loss

$$P_{oss} = C_{oss} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 1\,\text{nF} \times 635 \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.030\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time diode conduction

There are two dead-time intervals per PWM period:

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I_{ph}\, (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive loss

This loss is dissipated in the driver IC and gate resistors, not in the FETs:

$$P_{gate} = Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} \times N_{switching} = 50\,\text{nC} \times 10 \times 48\,\text{k} \times 2 = 0.048\ \text{W per motor}$$

### 2.6 Per-motor loss budget at 80 A, 48 kHz, 6S full charge

| Loss term | W |
|---|---|
| FET conduction (2 × R_DS,hot) | 19.20 |
| Copper (no shunt) | 1.60 |
| Switching overlap | 1.94
