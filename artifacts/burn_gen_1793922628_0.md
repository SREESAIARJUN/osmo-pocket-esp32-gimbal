# STM32H743 AIO 4-in-1 ESC Nano FC (Job #21): Architecture, Loss Model & DShot DMA Plan

## 0. Feasibility Findings

These results drive every later section.

| Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|
| 80 A per motor, simultaneously on all four motors, is 320 A and about 8 kW from 6S. | Specify the 80 A rating as a **single-channel burst** of ≤1 s at 25 °C ambient. Specify a continuous rating separately (about 35–45 A on 30×30, about 25–30 A on 20×20). |
| A 20×20 mm board cannot shed about 100 W, even transiently, without Tj > 150 °C within about 2 s (see §3). | Recommend a **30.5×30.5 mm** ESC board with an integrated FC, or a stacked FC over the ESC. A 20×20 board is limited to ~40 A burst. |
| An H743 in "AIO" form does not commutate motors itself in the ArduPilot ecosystem. | The architecture is 1× H743 (ArduPilot) + 4× ESC MCUs (AM32 on AT32F421 or STM32G071), linked by **bidirectional DShot600**. |
| Bidirectional DShot needs **input capture**, and input capture is impossible on complementary `CHxN` pins. | Motor outputs must use non-N `TIMx_CHy` pins (see §4). |

All component values below are representative. Replace them with your selected MOSFET's datasheet values; the equations stay valid.
