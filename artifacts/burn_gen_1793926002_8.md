# STM32H743 Nano AIO (4-in-1 ESC + FC): Design Package, Job #33

## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Feasibility Flags

Three points about the spec need settling before the models, because they change the results.

1. **The H743 does not run the ESC.** In every production AIO, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot (the flight controller side). Each motor bridge is driven by its own ESC MCU, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32, or by one shared ESC MCU. So:
   - The **48 kHz PWM** is generated on the ESC MCUs.
   - **Bidirectional DShot600** is the link between the H743 and the ESC MCUs.
   
   The design below follows this partition.

2. **"80A burst" on a nano (20×20 mm) board is a time-limited rating, not a current limit.** At 80 A per motor the board dissipates about 160 W. That is only survivable for roughly 1 s or less (Section 3.4). Practical options:
   - Keep 20×20 mm and rate it 80 A for ≤ 1 s and about 25 A continuous with good airflow.
   - Move to 30.5×30.5 mm with 5×6 mm FETs for real 80 A capability.

3. **The netlist in Section 5 is a reference design.** Pin assignments, alternate functions, and device parameters are drawn from typical datasheet values. Verify every one against the specific silicon revision, package, and current ArduPilot `hwdef` conventions before layout.
