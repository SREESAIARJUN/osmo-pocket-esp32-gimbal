# 3-Axis Gimbal FOC Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion Design (Job #37)

**Assumed platform:** You didn't specify the hardware, so this design assumes the configuration below. Change the constants in `Config` to match your build.

- **MCU:** STM32G4-class, 170 MHz, with FPU and CORDIC.
- **Motors:** gimbal BLDC (12N14P), 3-phase inverter, low-side shunts.
- **Encoder:** 14-bit magnetic (e.g. AS5048/MT6701) on each joint.
- **IMUs:** two ICM-42688-P on the camera plate (IMU0 and IMU1), SPI at 24 MHz, ODR 8 kHz.
- **Axis order:** base, then Yaw (q₁), then Roll (q₂), then Pitch (q₃), then camera.

The design is ordered from the motor plant upward: motor model and current loop, cascaded attitude/rate control, anti-cogging, IMU filtering and fusion, implementation code, then commissioning and validation.

---

## 1. Rate Structure

| Loop | Rate | Feedback | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz (Ts = 50 µs) | Phase shunts, encoder θₑ | PWM duties |
| Rate (velocity) | 4 kHz | Fused, vibration-filtered gyro projected to the joint | i_q* |
| Attitude (angle) | 1 kHz | Mahony quaternion | ω* |
| IMU acquisition and filtering | 8 kHz | ICM-42688-P FIFO | Fused gyro and accel |

**Bandwidth separation rule.** Keep each loop well below the next inner loop and the filters below it:

- ω_c,i ≥ 5·ω_c,v
- ω_c,v ≥ 4·ω_c,θ
- Every filter's phase lag at ω_c,v must stay under 15°.
