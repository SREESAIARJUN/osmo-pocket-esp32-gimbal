# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Systems Architecture

**Document:** Job #12, Rev A (engineering derivation)

## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

**Architecture.** ArduPilot does **not** commutate motors. The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. Each of the four ESC channels needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. The design therefore uses two boards:

| Board | Contents |
|---|---|
| **FC** | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), IMU, baro, OSD, SD card |
| **ESC** | 4 × AT32F421K8U7 (AM32), 4 × FD6288Q 3-phase gate drivers, 24 power MOSFETs (48 if paralleled) |

**Assumed parameters.** Replace these with your BOM datasheet values.

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V (6S full) | Bus voltage (switching loss scales linearly with it) |
| I_burst | 80 A per ESC channel, 10 s | Burst current |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | PWM frequency |
| MOSFET | 40 V, R_ds(on),25 = 0.70 mΩ, Q_g = 50 nC, Q_oss = 60 nC, Q_rr = 50 nC, R_θJC = 0.8 K/W | e.g. a 3.3×3.3 / 5×6 class part |
| α | 0.005 /K | R_ds(on) temperature coefficient |
| t_r + t_f | 20 ns total at the FD6288 drive strength | Switching transition time |
| t_dt | 100 ns | Dead time per edge |
| V_SD | 0.8 V | Body-diode forward voltage |

**Up-front verdict.** A true 80 A burst per channel on a 20×20 or 30×30 "nano" footprint is thermally marginal. The math below shows you need either **2 paralleled FETs per switch position** or a burst limit of **≤ 3 s**.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C:

$$R = 0.70 \times 1.425 = 1.00\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Six-step (trapezoidal) commutation with complementary PWM

At any instant, two phase legs carry the motor current I:

- One leg is PWM'd with duty D: the high-side FET conducts for D, and the low-side FET synchronously rectifies for (1−D).
- The other leg's low-side FET is held fully ON.

Exactly two R_ds paths are therefore always in series with I, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I^2\,\frac{R_{ds}(T_j)}{n_p} + I^2 R_{shunt} + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

where n_p is the number of paralleled FETs per position.

Per-FET RMS current, averaged over an electrical cycle (each FET is active 1/3 of the cycle):

$$I_{HS,rms} = I\sqrt{D/3},\qquad I_{LS,rms} = I\sqrt{(2-D)/3}$$

The low-side carries more: it is "on" for its 1/3 freewheel window plus the (1−D)/3 synchronous period.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| Term | n_p = 1 | n_p = 2 |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 2·80²·1.0 mΩ = **12.80 W** | **6.40 W** |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ) | 1.60 W | 1.60 W |
| Copper (≈0.15 mΩ effective, 2 oz) | 0.96 W | 0.96 W |
| **Subtotal per ESC** | **15.36 W** | **8.96 W** |

The shunt is a real loss. Consider 0.2 mΩ, or use R_ds sensing if accuracy permits.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches. Its high-side and low-side FETs commutate once per PWM edge.

| Mechanism | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V·I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = ½·25.2·80·20e-9·48e3 | **0.968 W** |
| Dead-time body-diode conduction | V_SD·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·200e-9·48e3 | **0.614 W** |
| Reverse recovery | V·Q_rr·f_sw = 25.2·50e-9·48e3 | 0.060 W |
| Output capacitance | V·Q_oss·f_sw | 0.073 W |
| Gate drive (driver loss, not FET) | 2·Q_g·V_gs·f_sw = 2·50e-9·10·48e3 | 0.048 W |
| **Switching subtotal** | | **≈ 1.76 W** |

Sensitivity of the switching subtotal:

$$\frac{\partial P_{sw}}{\partial f_{sw}} \approx 36.7\ \mu\text{W/Hz}$$

Going from 48 kHz to 96 kHz adds about 1.76 W per ESC. **Keep 48 kHz.** Halving dead time (if the driver and FET allow it) saves about 0.3 W.

> **With n_p = 2:** overlap loss is roughly unchanged (the current splits, but Q_g doubles and the edges slow). Coss and Qrr losses double. Budget **≈ 1.9 W**.

## 3. Total Loss and Thermal Model

### 3.1 Power budget at 80 A

| | n_p = 1 | n_p = 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Per ESC | 17.1 W | 10.9 W |
| **4-in-1 total** (all 4 motors at 80 A) | **68.5 W** | **43.4 W** |
| Hottest single FET (PWM-leg LS at low D) | ≈ 6.4 W cond + 0.8 W sw ≈ **7.2 W** | ≈ **4.0 W** |

### 3.2 Junction rise above the PCB (steady-state, per FET)

$$\Delta T_{JC} = P_{FET}\cdot R_{\theta JC}$$

- n_p = 1: 7.2 W × 0.8 K/W = **5.8 K**
- n_p = 2: 4.0 W × 0.8 K/W = **3.2 K**

The die is not the bottleneck. **The board is.**

### 3.3 Board transient model (lumped RC)

$$T_{board}(t) = T_a + P_{tot}R_{\theta BA}\left(1-e^{-t/\tau}\right),\qquad \tau = R_{\theta BA}C_{th}$$

Board parameters (30×30 mm, 6-layer, 2 oz):

| Quantity | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Copper mass | ≈ 3.5 g |
| FR4 mass | ≈ 4 g |
| FET + solder mass | ≈ 2 g |
| C_th | 3.5·0.385 + 4·1.1 + 2·0.7 ≈ **7.2 J/K** |
| Prop-wash airflow (h ≈ 60 W/m²K, both sides, 1800 mm²) | R_θBA ≈ **9.3 K/W** |
| τ | ≈ **67 s** |

Because τ ≫ 10 s, the burst is effectively adiabatic:

$$\Delta T \approx \frac{P_{tot}\,t}{C_{th}}$$

| Case | ΔT after 10 s | T_board (T_a = 35 °C) | T_j | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| n_p = 1, 4×80 A | 68.5·10/7.2 = **95 K** | 130 °C | **≈ 136 °C** |
