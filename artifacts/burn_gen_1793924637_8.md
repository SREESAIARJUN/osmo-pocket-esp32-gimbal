# Job #49: 3-Axis Gimbal FOC Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion

## 0. Assumptions and Architecture

I have no project files for Job #49, so the design uses typical gimbal hardware. Every assumed value lives in a config struct you can change.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class (FPU, 170+ MHz), one per axis or one for all three |
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 14 pole pairs (`p=7`), 12 slots, R≈5 Ω, L≈2 mH, Kt≈0.08 N·m/A |
| Commutation sensor | 14-bit magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701) on each motor shaft |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI @ 24 MHz, 8 kHz ODR |
| PWM | 20 kHz center-aligned, 3-shunt or 2-shunt low-side sensing |

### Loop rates and bandwidths

Each loop's bandwidth is kept at least 4–5× below the loop inside it.

| Loop | Rate | Target bandwidth | Feedback |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (d/q PI) | 20 kHz | 1.0–1.5 kHz | Phase shunts, encoder θₑ |
| Velocity (PI + filtered D) | 4 kHz | 80–150 Hz | Fused gyro rate, projected to joint |
| Angle (P + rate FF) | 1 kHz | 15–30 Hz | Fused attitude (stabilize) / encoder (follow) |
| IMU fusion | 4 kHz in, 1 kHz attitude | — | 2 × ICM-42688-P |

## 1. FOC Inner Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

The amplitude-invariant Clarke and Park transforms are:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix},
\qquad \theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}
$$

### 1.2 Plant (PMSM in dq frame)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e (L_d i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\,\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q
$$
