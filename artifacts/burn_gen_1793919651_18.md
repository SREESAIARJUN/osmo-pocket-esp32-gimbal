# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #4)

**Scope:** Power-stage loss model (conduction at 80 A burst, switching at 48 kHz), board-level transient thermal model, ArduPilot bi-directional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and reference netlists.

> **Basis of estimate:** Component values below are representative, not tied to a specific part number. Every result is expressed symbolically first, so you can substitute values from your actual MOSFET datasheet.

---

## 0. Architectural Red Flags

Resolve these before layout. The math in §3 supports each one.

| # | Issue | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **4 × 80 A = 320 A total battery current** on a "nano" stack | An XT60 is rated about 60 A continuous. A 12 AWG lead drops about 0.3 V at 320 A over 6 cm. This is not a credible *simultaneous* rating. |
| 2 | **About 100 W of dissipation at 4 × 80 A** on roughly 36 × 36 mm | The adiabatic rise is about 18 K/s. The board hits its thermal limit in **≈ 4 s** (§3.4). |
| 3 | **Continuous rating** under propwash convection | Roughly 25 to 30 A per motor. 80 A must be specified as a **≤ 2 s per-motor burst** with a duty-cycle limit. |
| 4 | The H743 does **not** commutate the motors | The ESC needs its own MCU per motor (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). The H743 sends DShot only. |

**Recommendation:** Market the board as "**30 A continuous / 80 A 1-second burst per channel**". Alternatively, move to a 30.5 × 30.5 mm form factor with 2 oz copper on 8 layers and a bolt-on heat spreader.

**Topology:** 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM on the switching leg. At any instant, one leg PWMs (HS/LS complementary) while a second leg's low-side is held fully ON.

## 2. Loss Model (Per Motor, 6 MOSFETs)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

Current flows through the switching leg (HS for D, sync LS for 1−D) and through the static LS leg:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I^2\,\alpha_T R_{ds,25}$$

The result is **duty-independent** with synchronous rectification. Dead-time diode conduction is handled separately in §2.3.

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

Copper path loss:

$$P_{cu} = I^2 R_{cu} = 6400 \times 0.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{3.2\ W}$$
