## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step commutation

In each 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. The low-side FET conducts synchronous freewheel for (1 − D).
- **Phase B (return leg):** the low-side FET is on continuously.

So two R_DS(on) are always in series with the phase current, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I^2\, R_{DS}(T_j) \qquad R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right]$$

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period, split by D between its high-side and low-side roles. The average loss per FET is therefore:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \tfrac{1}{3}\cdot 2 I^2 R_{DS}\cdot\tfrac{1}{2}\cdot... \;\Rightarrow\; \overline{P}_{cond,FET} = \frac{2 I^2 R_{DS}}{6}$$
