## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 1.1 Conduction losses

With synchronous rectification in 6-step commutation, phase current always flows through exactly two FETs:
- during PWM-on: high-side of phase A, then low-side of phase B;
- during PWM-off: low-side of phase A, then low-side of phase B (complementary freewheel).

The conduction loss is therefore approximately independent of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\,/\text{K} \Rightarrow k_{T}(125^\circ\text{C}) \approx 1.5$$

Numerical values:
- R_DS(on) at 125 °C = 1.3 mΩ × 1.5 = 1.95 mΩ.
- **FET conduction loss** = 2 × 80² × 1.95 mΩ = **24.96 W**.
- **Copper loss**: assume 0.3 mΩ for phase and bus traces plus pad. 80² × 0.3 mΩ = **1.92 W**. This is a critical loss on a nano board.

Per-FET split (time-averaged over a commutation cycle):
- The low-side FET of the active leg carries current for a fraction (1 − D) of the PWM period.
- The high-side FET carries current for a fraction D.
- The static low-side FET carries current 100 % of the time during its 120° sector.

The worst-case single FET is the static low-side, which dissipates I²·R_DS(on) = **12.5 W** during its sector. Averaged over a full electrical cycle, each FET averages about 1/3 of that, roughly 4.2 W. However, **use the instantaneous 12.5 W against Z_θJC**, because sector durations at low eRPM can approach thermal time constants.

### 1.2 Switching losses (f_sw = 48 kHz, one hard-switched leg)

Only the PWM leg switches. The terms below are summed for that leg.

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw = ½ · 25.2 · 80 · 40 ns · 48 k | **1.94 W** |
| Dead-time body-diode conduction | V_f · I · 2t_dt · f_sw = 0.8 · 80 · 200 ns · 48 k | **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw = 60 nC · 25.2 · 48 k | **0.07 W** |
| Output capacitance (C_oss) | Q_oss · V · f_sw = 50 nC · 25.2 · 48 k | **0.06 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs, mostly dissipated in the driver) | 2 · Q_g · V_GS · f_sw = 2 · 60 nC · 10 · 48 k | **0.06 W** |
| **P_sw total** | | **≈ 2.74 W** |

### 1.3 Total ESC power budget at burst

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Per-channel loss (P_cond + P_Cu + P_sw) | 24.96 + 1.92 + 2.74 = **29.6 W** |
| Four channels at 80 A simultaneously | **≈ 118 W** |
| Delivered power, 4 × 80 A at ~23 V (sagged) | ≈ 7.4 kW |
| Implied efficiency | ≈ 98.4 % |

The efficiency is good. The problem is the absolute wattage dissipated in a ~1 cm³ volume.

**Optimisation levers, in order of sensitivity:**
1. Conduction loss scales with I² and dominates. Parallel FETs per switch position (R/2) or use a lower-R_DS(on) die. The 48 kHz switching frequency is not your problem.
2. Reducing f_sw from 48 to 24 kHz saves only about 1.3 W per channel and worsens current ripple and motor iron/copper ripple losses.
3. Reducing dead time to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W per channel. Only do this if driver propagation skew allows it.

## 2. Thermal Model

### 2.1 Network (Cauer, per FET to ambient)

```
P_FET ──[R_θJC 1.2]──┬──[R_θCB 0.5 (solder + thermal vias)]──┬──[R_θBA]── T_amb
                     │                                       │
                   C_j,case                              C_board
                   (~0.05 J/K)                           (~6 J/K, shared)
```

### 2.2 Junction rise above the board (fast transient)

For the worst-case static FET at 12.5 W:

$$\Delta T_{J-B} = P\,(R_{\theta JC} + R_{\theta CB}) = 12.5 \times 1.7 = 21.3\ \text{K}$$

This is reached within about 50–100 ms. Use the datasheet Z_θJC(t) curve for sub-10 ms pulses.

### 2.3 Board heat capacity (lumped)

Assumptions: 20×20 mm (or 30×30 mm) 6-layer board, 2 oz copper.

| Constituent | Mass | Specific heat | Heat capacity |
|---|---|---|---|
| FR4 | ~1.5 g | 1.1 J/g·K | ~1.6 J/K |
| Copper | ~2.5 g | 0.385 J/g·K | ~1.0 J/K |
| FETs and solder | ~2 g | — | ~1 J/K |
| **Total** | | | **C ≈ 4–6 J/K** (6 J/K used, optimistic) |

### 2.4 Burst survivability (adiabatic, worst case)

$$\Delta T_{board}(t) = \frac{P\,t}{C}$$

**All four motors at 80 A (118 W):**
- 10 s burst: 1180 J / 6 J/K = **197 K rise. Not survivable.** Solder reflow and FET T_j,max are both exceeded.
