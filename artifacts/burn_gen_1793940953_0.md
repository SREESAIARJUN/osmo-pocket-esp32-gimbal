# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: 80 A Burst Thermal, 48 kHz Switching, and Bidirectional DShot600 Design Package

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

No MOSFET part number, battery voltage, or board geometry was specified, so I've parameterized everything. Worked numbers use the reference values below. Substitute your datasheet values and the equations still hold.

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 does not switch the bridges. It sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class, e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071), which run the 48 kHz PWM. The two domains are modeled separately and joined at the DShot/telemetry interface.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

In any 60° commutation sector, phase A is PWM'd (high side HS_A on for D, synchronous low side LS_A on for 1−D) and phase B's low side LS_B is held on.

$$
P_{HS,A}=I^2R_{DS}(T_j)\,D,\qquad P_{LS,A}=I^2R_{DS}(T_j)\,(1-D),\qquad P_{LS,B}=I^2R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

The sum is independent of duty cycle:

$$
\boxed{P_{cond,motor}=2\,I^2\,\frac{R_{DS}(T_j)}{n_p}+I^2R_{Cu}}
$$

The temperature dependence of R_DS(on) is:

$$
R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]
$$

### 1.2 Worked numbers

Hot-die resistance at T_j ≈ 130 °C:

$$
R_{DS}(130)=0.90\,[1+0.0045\cdot105]=1.33\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

Silicon conduction loss per motor:

$$
P_{cond}=2\cdot 80^2\cdot 1.33\times10^{-3}=17.0\ \text{W}
$$

Copper path loss (2 oz, 8-layer, ~0.25 mΩ round trip estimate):

$$
P_{Cu}=6400\cdot 0.25\times10^{-3}=1.6\ \text{W}
$$

**Per-motor conduction total ≈ 18.6 W.**

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Over an electrical cycle, each of the six FETs carries current for about 1/3 of the time:

$$
\bar P_{FET,cond}\approx \frac{17.0}{6}=2.8\ \text{W}
$$

The instantaneous peak in the "held-on" LS position is 80² × 1.33 mΩ = **8.5 W**. Use the average for board-level thermals and the peak for die-level transients at low eRPM (see §4.4).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Gate current and transition times (Miller plateau method)

$$
I_{g,on}=\frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{drv}+R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}}=\frac{7}{5.2}=1.35\ \text{A}
$$

$$
I_{g,off}=\frac{V_{pl}}{5.2}=0.58\ \text{A}
$$

$$
t_{on}=\frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}}=8.9\ \text{ns},\qquad
t_{off}=\frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,off}}=20.7\ \text{ns}
$$
