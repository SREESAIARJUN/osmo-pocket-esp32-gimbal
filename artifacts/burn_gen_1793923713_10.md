# Job #63: STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC) Engineering Model

**Scope:** Conduction and switching loss model at 80 A burst and 48 kHz, thermal analysis, ArduPilot bi-directional DShot600 timer and DMA allocation, and a reference netlist.

The architecture assumes the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller and drives four independent ESC MCUs over DShot. The ESC side uses AM32-class firmware on an AT32F421 (or STM32G071), one per motor. The H743 does not commutate the motors. That is the only architecture under which bi-directional DShot is meaningful.

> **Feasibility flag:** The math in §3 shows that 80 A *per motor* on a nano-format (~20×20 mm mount, ~25×25 mm board) AIO is only achievable as a short transient of about 1–2 s. Continuous rating will be roughly 15–25 A per motor. I've written the model in closed form so you can re-run it against your actual FET datasheet and board stack-up, and the numbers will tell you where you land.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology

The design uses a three-phase bridge with 6-step trapezoidal drive and synchronous (complementary) PWM, as AM32 does by default. In every 60° commutation sector:

- **Phase A (PWM phase):** the high-side (HS) conducts for duty *D*, and the low-side (LS) conducts for *(1−D)* through synchronous rectification.
- **Phase B (sink phase):** the LS conducts continuously.
- **Phase C:** floating, used for back-EMF zero-crossing.

Because of synchronous rectification, **exactly two FET channels carry the phase current at every instant**, regardless of *D*. Dead-time intervals are the exception and are handled in §2.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25^\circ\text{C})\right],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.004\text{–}0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

Use the datasheet's normalized R_DS(on)-vs-T_j curve if available. At T_j = 125 °C the factor k_T ≈ 1.5.

### 1.3 Per-channel (one motor) conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2\,R_{trace}$$

### 1.4 Per-FET loss (worst case)

Over one electrical period:

- each HS conducts for 1/3 of the period at duty *D*;
- each LS conducts for 1/3 of the period as the sink, plus 1/3·(1−D) as the synchronous rectifier.

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}D\,I^2R_{DS},\qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(2-D)\,I^2R_{DS}$$

**The LS FET is the thermal worst case at all throttle levels below 100 %.** At D → 1 the two converge to I²R/3 each.

### 1.5 Worked numbers

**Assumptions:**

- 40 V N-MOSFET in DFN 3.3×3.3, R_DS,25 = 1.5 mΩ at V_GS = 10 V
- One FET per switch position, 12 FETs per channel pair, 24 total
- 6S battery: V_bus = 25.2 V (max)

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| I_ph (burst) | 80 A |
| R_DS at T_j = 125 °C | 1.5 mΩ × 1.5 = 2.25 mΩ |
| I² | 6400 A² |
| **P_cond per channel (FETs)** | 2 × 6400 × 2.25 mΩ = **28.8 W** |
| Trace/via path, ~0.4 mΩ (2 oz outer + internal planes) | 6400 × 0.4 mΩ = **2.6 W** |
| Per-FET, D = 1 | 6400 × 2.25 mΩ / 3 = **4.8 W** |
| Per-FET LS, D = 0.7 (mid-high throttle at 80 A, unusual) | (1.3/3) × 14.4 = **6.2 W** |

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

### 2.1 Loss components

Only the PWM phase switches. Per channel:

$$P_{sw,ch} = \underbrace{\tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw}}_{\text{V–I overlap (HS)}} + \underbrace{Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw}}_{\text{LS body-diode recovery}} + \underbrace{\tfrac12 C_{oss,eq}V_{bus}^2 f_{sw}\cdot 2}_{\text{Coss, HS+LS}} + \underbrace{2\,V_F I_{ph}\, t_{dead} f_{sw}}_{\text{dead-time diode}} + \underbrace{2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw}}_{\text{gate drive}}$$

### 2.2 Estimating transition times

Estimate the transition time from the Miller charge and the driver current:

$$t_{r,f} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g}},\qquad I_g \approx \frac{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}{R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}+R_{drv}}$$
