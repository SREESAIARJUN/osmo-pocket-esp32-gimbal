## 0. Architecture

ArduPilot does not generate 48 kHz commutation PWM. The system splits into two domains:

| Domain | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (ArduPilot) | Attitude control. Outputs 4× bidirectional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC (×4 channels) | AT32F421K8U7 / STM32G071 (AM32 or BLHeli_32) | Sensorless 6-step commutation with 48 kHz complementary PWM |
| Gate drive | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) ×4 | Drives 6 N-FETs per channel |
