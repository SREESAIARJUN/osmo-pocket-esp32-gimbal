## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Topology
The ESC uses six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous (complementary) PWM. At any instant exactly two phase legs carry the current:
- one leg is chopped (high side for duty D, low side for 1−D);
- one leg holds its low side on.

So the current path always passes through two MOSFET channels. Because body-diode conduction occurs only during dead time (treated in §2), we can write:

$$P_{cond,motor}(T_j) = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Ripple correction
For PWM ripple ΔI_pp:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

For a typical 5" motor, L_ph ≈ 15 µH, and the worst case is D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}D(1-D)}{L f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \cdot 0.25}{15\mu \cdot 48k} = 8.75\text{ A}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = 6400 + 6.4 = 6406.4\ \text{A}^2$$

The ripple term is negligible (+0.1%), which confirms that 48 kHz is ripple-adequate.

### 1.3 Values at T_j = 125 °C
- R_ds = 1.5 mΩ × 1.5 = 2.25 mΩ
- P_cond,motor = 2 × 6406 × 2.25e-3 = **28.8 W**
- Per FET (each conducts 1/3 of the electrical period): 28.8 / 6 = **4.8 W average**. The commutation frequency is in the kHz range, far faster than the thermal time constants, so averaging is valid.
- Four motors: **115.3 W**

### 1.4 Electrothermal stability (runaway criterion)

$$T_j = T_a + R_{\theta}\,P(T_j)$$

Linearising gives

$$\frac{dP}{dT_j}=2I^2R_{25}\alpha$$

The solution is stable only if

$$R_\theta\cdot 2I^2R_{25}\alpha < 1$$

At 80 A: 2 × 6400 × 1.5e-3 × 0.005 = 0.096 W/K. The effective R_θ from junction to ambient for the whole motor bridge must therefore be below **10.4 K/W**. Since that is a bridge-level figure, the board as a whole needs roughly ≤ 2.6 K/W. Steady state is marginal; burst operation is fine (§3).

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the chopped high side hard-switches. The synchronous low side turns on at near-zero voltage after the dead time, but its body diode recovers when the high side turns on.

| Term | Equation | Value per motor |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | ½·25.2·80·20n·48k = **0.968 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 40n·25.2·48k = **0.048 W** |
| C_oss discharge | ½·C_oss·V²·f_sw | 0.5·1.5n·635·48k = **0.023 W** |
| Dead-time diode | 2·V_f·I·t_d·f_sw | 2·0.8·80·100n·48k = **0.614 W** |
