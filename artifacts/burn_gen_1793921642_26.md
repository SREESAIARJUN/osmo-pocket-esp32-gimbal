## 0. Feasibility Findings

Read these before the derivations. The numbers below support a design review, and they show that the stated targets conflict.

| Issue | Finding |
|---|---|
| **80 A on a 20×20 mm board** | At 80 A on all four channels, the battery current approaches **~320 A** (D≈1). That is not physically supportable through 20×20 battery pads and copper. "80 A burst" must be a **per-channel, short-duration** rating, with 4-channel simultaneous current far lower. |
| **Burst duration** | The lumped thermal model in §3 gives **≈1.3 s** at 80 A on all four channels before T_j reaches 125 °C. This is an optimistic upper bound. |
| **Architecture** | The H743 does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel (or the 4-in-1 block) runs its own MCU, typically STM32G071 or AT32F421 with AM32/BLHeli_32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. |
| **Thermal coupling** | The STM32H743 shares the board with ~100+ W of transient dissipation. Commercial and industrial H743 grades are limited to T_A ≤ 85 °C (some to 105 °C, check suffix). IMU drift and baro error will couple to ESC heat. |

**Recommendation:** Rate the board at 40 A burst, or move to a 30.5×30.5 mm footprint for 60 A+. The models below are parametric, so they can be re-evaluated for either case.

**Component parameters are representative** of a 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PQFN MOSFET class. Replace them with the selected part's datasheet values before release.
