| ESC MCU | 1× AT32F421 or STM32G071 per motor, running AM32 |
| FC MCU | STM32H743 at 400 MHz running ArduPilot. It does **not** commutate the motors. |
| PWM frequency | 48 kHz |
| Dead time | 100 ns |

Three feasibility issues should be resolved first:

1. **The H743 does not drive the FETs.** On every production 4-in-1 design, each motor has its own ESC MCU. The H743 only sends DShot commands and receives eRPM telemetry. The architecture below reflects this.
2. **80 A per motor on 20×20 mm is a burst-of-seconds rating, not a sustained one.** Section 3 shows the board reaching its thermal limit in about 1.5 s at full 4×80 A.
3. **Four motors at 80 A means up to 320 A of battery current.** An XT60 (~60 A continuous) and a single 12 AWG lead cannot carry this. A real "80 A" nano stack sees roughly 80 A *total* in practice. Size the connector, leads, and input shunt for the realistic total-current envelope, not 4× the per-channel rating.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R<sub>DS(on)</sub>

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T<sub>j</sub> = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS(on)} = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.006 \times 85) = 1.2 \times 1.51 \approx 1.81\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous PWM

At any instant, two phases conduct:
- One leg is the **PWM leg**. Its HS or LS FET carries current, alternating with duty D.
- The other leg is **static**, with its LS FET held on.

With synchronous rectification there are always exactly **two FET channels in series** with the motor current (ignoring dead time):

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.81\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{23.2\ W\ per\ motor}$$

### 1.3 Distribution per FET

Over one electrical cycle, each leg is active for 240° (120° as PWM leg, 120° as static leg). The per-FET average is approximately:

$$\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6} \approx 3.9\ \text{W}$$

This approximation holds when the electrical period (about 0.2–2 ms at flight eRPM) is short compared with the die/case thermal time constant. The instantaneous peak in the conducting FET is:

$$P_{FET,pk} = I^2 R = 80^2 \times 1.81\,\text{m}\Omega = 11.6\ \text{W}$$
