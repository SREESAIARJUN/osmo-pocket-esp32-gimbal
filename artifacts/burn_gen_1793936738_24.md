## 0. Architecture Clarification and Baseline Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not switch the motors.** It runs ArduPilot and generates DShot. Each motor channel on the 4-in-1 ESC has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, which produces the 48 kHz complementary PWM and the commutation. The two halves of the design are therefore coupled only through:
- DShot signal lines (one per motor),
- telemetry and current sense,
- shared power and thermal paths.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

The design point is T_j = 110 °C (iterated in §3):

$$R_{DS}(110) = 0.75\,(1 + 0.005 \cdot 85) = 1.069\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

At any instant two phase legs conduct:
- **Non-PWM leg:** one FET is held on for the entire 60° sector.
- **PWM leg:** the high-side conducts for duty D and the synchronous low-side for (1−D). Outside dead time, exactly one FET in this leg carries I_ph.

So the current always passes through exactly two FET channels in series, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.069\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{13.68\ W}$$

Averaged over a full electrical cycle, each of the 6 FETs conducts for 1/3 of the period:

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} = \frac{13.68}{6} = 2.28\ \text{W}$$

### 1.3 Copper and shunt losses (per motor)

| Element | R (est.) | Current | Loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase path copper (2 legs × ~0.15 mΩ) | 0.30 mΩ | 80 A | 1.92 W |
| DC-link shunt (shared; per-motor share ≈ D·I) | 0.25 mΩ ÷ 4 share | ~72 A avg at D≈0.9 | ~0.3 W |
| **Total copper/shunt per motor** | | | **≈ 2.2 W** |

### 1.4 Ripple-current check (not a loss term)

Phase inductance makes the PWM ripple small relative to 80 A at 48 kHz, so treating I_ph as DC is acceptable within about 2%. Add the term back if you use low-L outrunners (< 10 µH):

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$
