## 0. Architecture Decision and Feasibility Summary

### 0.1 Topology

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller (FC)**. It does not commutate motors directly. Bidirectional DShot implies a **dedicated ESC MCU per motor** that decodes DShot frames and returns eRPM telemetry.

```
┌──────────────────────── FC board (20×20 / 25.5×25.5) ───────────────────────┐
│ STM32H743VIT6 @ 480 MHz · ArduPilot · TIM1 CH1–CH4 → bidir DShot600 ×4        │
└───────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                │ 8-pin stack: VBAT, GND, CURR, TLM, M1–M4
┌───────────────┴──────────── 4-in-1 ESC board ────────────────────────────────┐
│ 4 × AT32F421K8U7 (AM32) → 4 × FD6288Q 3-phase gate driver → 24 × 40 V MOSFET   │
│ PWM 48 kHz · complementary (synchronous) · shunt current sense                 │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 0.2 Feasibility of the Brief

The numbers below are derived in Sections 1 to 4.

| Requirement | Result | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 80 A burst per motor, 6S | ≈ 30 W loss per motor, ≈ 120 W per board | **Feasible only for about 3–4 s** from 40 °C ambient |
| Continuous rating on nano copper | ≈ 24 A per motor (all 4 loaded) | 80 A is a burst-only label |
| 48 kHz PWM | Switching ≈ 1.7 W per motor at 80 A | Acceptable; conduction dominates |
| Bidir DShot600, ArduPilot, H743 | 1 timer, 3 DMA streams | Fully feasible |

**Key finding:** this design is thermally capacitance-limited, not junction-limited. Any "80 A" marketing figure must state a duration (≤ 3 s at 40 °C ambient). An unqualified "80 A" claim on a nano 4-in-1 is not supportable physically.
