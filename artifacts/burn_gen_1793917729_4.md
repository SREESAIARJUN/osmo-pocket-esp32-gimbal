## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

Use the amplitude-invariant Clarke transform with two shunts:

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

The electrical angle comes from the mechanical angle, offset by an alignment angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{m,0})$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose.

### 1.2 Plant and PI Tuning (Pole–Zero Cancellation)

The motor electrical model in the dq frame is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

PI controller per axis:

$$C_i(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s}$$

Choosing the zero to cancel the electrical pole (K_i/K_p = R/L) gives:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_{ci}$$

The closed loop is then first order: $i/i^* = \omega_{ci}/(s+\omega_{ci})$.

- Choose $\omega_{ci} \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/10$. Here $\omega_{ci} = 2\pi \cdot 1.5\text{ kHz}$.
- One PWM period of transport delay costs $\omega_{ci}T_s = 27°$ of phase, which leaves roughly 60° of margin.

**Decoupling feedforward:**

$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

**Voltage limiting.** The vector $(v_d, v_q)$ is limited to a circle of radius $V_{dc}/\sqrt3$ (linear SVPWM region). Priority goes to $v_d$:

$$v_q^{max} = \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}$$

**Anti-windup (back-calculation):**

$$\dot x_I = K_i e + K_{aw}(v_{sat} - v_{unsat}),\qquad K_{aw} = \frac{1}{K_p}\cdot K_i$$

With this choice, the anti-windup time constant equals the integral time constant.
