## 0. Architectural Corrections and Assumptions

Three points need correcting before the math, because they change the model.

**A. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is a flight stack and does not run 6-step or FOC commutation at 48 kHz. On any H743 AIO, the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. Four dedicated ESC MCUs run AM32 or BLHeli_32 and generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. Typical choices are AT32F421, STM32G071, or GD32E230. The switching-loss model below applies to those ESC MCUs' bridges. The DMA model applies to the H743.

**B. 80 A on a nano board is a burst figure, not a rating.** "Nano" usually means a 20×20 mm board, sometimes 25.5×25.5 mm. As Section 3 shows, 80 A per channel produces roughly 80 W of board heat. On a 20×20 board that is survivable for only about 1–2 s. The continuous rating is roughly 25–35 A per channel. The burst must be defined as amplitude and duration together, for example "80 A / 1 s, 10% duty".

**C. All component values are representative placeholders.** Replace them with datasheet values from your actual BOM. The equations are exact for the stated topology. The numbers are only as good as the parameters fed into them.
