## 0. Architecture Correction and Key Findings

1. **The STM32H743 cannot be the ESC controller.** BLHeli_32 and AM32 do not run on the H7. The correct topology is:
   - The **H743 runs ArduPilot** and generates bidirectional DShot600.
   - **Four ESC MCUs** (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32), each with a 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs, drive the motors.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano footprint is thermally limited to about 2 s.** The board's heat capacity is roughly 3.4 J/K, and four channels at 80 A dissipate about 100 W. The realistic continuous rating is about **30–35 A per channel** with prop-wash cooling. Treat these figures as the specification, not the marketing number.
3. At 48 kHz, **conduction loss dominates (about 92%)**. Switching frequency is not the thermal problem; Rds(on) and copper are.
