## 0. Architecture Baseline and Stated Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** On every production 4-in-1 design, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM and decodes DShot. The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits four bidirectional DShot600 lines. This package assumes that split:

```
6S LiPo ─┬─► Power stage ×4 (6 FETs + 3-ph driver + ESC MCU, AM32 @ 48 kHz)
         │        ▲ DShot600 bidir (1 wire/motor)
         └─► 10V/5V/3V3 rails ─► STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ─► IMU/baro/OSD/UARTs
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.2 × 1.5 = **1.8 mΩ**.

Add the copper path: phase trace plus solder pads, R_Cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ per conducting path. Effective resistance:

$$R_{path} = R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{Cu} \approx 2.1\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Six-step current paths

During any 60° sector, phase A is PWM-ing and phase B is the return:

| Device | Conduction fraction within the sector |
|---|---|
| Q_AH (high-side, PWM) | D |
| Q_AL (sync rectifier) | 1 − D |
| Q_BL (return, held ON) | 1 |

Within the sector the current always flows through exactly two devices:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2 R_{path}[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2I^2R_{path}$$

**Per motor at 80 A:**

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.1\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{26.9\ W}$$

(FET-only share, excluding copper: 2 × 6400 × 1.8 mΩ = 23.0 W.)

### 1.3 Per-device average

Each FET is "active" for 120° of every 360° electrical cycle. At full throttle (D → 1):

$$P_{cond,FET} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\cdot\tfrac{1}{3} = 6400 \times 1.8\text{ m}\Omega / 3 = \mathbf{3.84\ W}$$

The high-side and low-side devices of a leg dissipate about equally at full throttle. At part throttle, loss shifts toward the low-side (sync) FET.
