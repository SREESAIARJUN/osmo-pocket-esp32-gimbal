1. **80 A ×4 is a seconds-scale burst, not a rating.** Simultaneous 80 A on all four channels dissipates about 95 W in a ~6 J/K board. That gives roughly 4 s from 50 °C to 110 °C PCB. Firmware current limiting is mandatory (§3.4).
