# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #31): Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bidirectional DShot Design Package

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 cannot drive the bridges directly at 48 kHz across 12 phases while also running ArduPilot. The correct partitioning is:

- **FC board:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot. It sends DShot600 and receives bidirectional (BDShot) eRPM telemetry.
- **ESC board:** four independent ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). Each one generates 48 kHz complementary PWM for its own 6-FET bridge.

**Assumed parameters.** The part values below are representative. Re-run the model with your actual BOM datasheet values.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S, full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current per motor | I_b | 80 A |
| MOSFET (40 V, 3.3×3.3 DFN class) R_ds(on) at 25 °C, V_gs = 10 V | R₂₅ | 0.90 mΩ |
| R_ds(on) temperature coefficient | α | 0.0045 /K (≈1.4× at 115 °C) |
| Rise + fall time at 80 A (gate R ≈ 4.7 Ω) | t_r + t_f | 20 + 20 ns |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 40 nC |
| Reverse-recovery charge | Q_rr | 30 nC |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 50 nC |
| Body-diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Dead time (per edge) | t_dt | 100 ns |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

With 6-step trapezoidal commutation and synchronous (complementary) PWM, current always flows through exactly two FETs in series:

- **During ON time (fraction D):** high-side of phase A, then low-side of phase B.
- **During OFF time (1 − D):** low-side of phase A (synchronous rectification), then low-side of phase B.

Either way, the conduction path contains two R_ds(on) values. So, independent of D:

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot \left[2R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{pcb}\right]$$

Two refinements apply:

- **Temperature dependence:** $R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]$
- **Ripple:** I_rms ≈ I_b, because the ripple term $\frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$ is under 1% at 48 kHz with typical motor inductance (≥ 10 µH).

### 1.2 Electrothermal fixed point

Because R_ds(on) depends on T_j, and T_j depends on loss, the conduction loss must be solved iteratively:

$$T_j = T_{pcb} + P_{FET}\, R_{\theta JC}$$

$$P_{FET} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Solving for T_j gives:

$$T_j = \frac{T_{pcb} + I^2 R_{25} R_{\theta JC}(1 - 25\alpha)}{1 - I^2 R_{25} R_{\theta JC}\,\alpha}$$

**Stability criterion (thermal runaway).** The denominator must stay positive:

$$I^2 R_{25} R_{\theta JC}\,\alpha < 1$$

At 80 A: 6400 × 0.0009 × 1.5 × 0.0045 = **0.039**. This is stable, with a large margin.

**Worked case at 80 A, T_pcb = 100 °C:**

1. Numerator: 100 + 6400 × 0.0009 × 1.5 × (1 − 0.1125) = 100 + 7.67 = 107.67
2. Divide by the denominator: T_j = 107.67 / 0.961 ≈ **112 °C**
3. R_ds(112 °C) = 0.9 × (1 + 0.0045 × 87) = **1.25 mΩ**

**Resulting conduction loss per motor channel:**

$$P_{cond} = 6400 \times (2 \times 1.25 + 0.25)\,\text{m}\Omega = 6400 \times 2.75\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{17.6\ W}$$

Of this, 16.0 W is in the FETs and 1.6 W is in the copper.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches hard. The commutation-static low-side does not.

| Term | Formula | Value at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½ · V_bus · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48 k = **1.94 W** |
| Body-diode dead-time conduction | V_f · I · 2t_dt · f_sw | 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k = **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw | 30 nC × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.036 W** |
| Coss | ½ · Q_oss · V_bus · f_sw | 0.5 × 40 nC × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.024 W** |
| Gate drive (2 switching FETs, in driver and R_g) | 2 · Q_g · V_gs · f_sw | 2 × 50 nC × 10 × 48 k = **0.048 W** |
| **P_sw total** | | **≈ 2.66 W** |

**Sensitivity.** P_sw scales linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz it is about 1.33 W; at 96 kHz about 5.3 W. 48 kHz is a reasonable compromise between acoustic and current-ripple performance and loss.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Total dissipation (all four motors at 80 A)

| Item | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 17.6 W | 70.4 W |
| Switching | 2.66 W | 10.6 W |
| Bus capacitor ESR, ripple ≈ 0.5·I, 3 mΩ | 4.8 W | (shared) ~5 W |
| **Total** | | **≈ 86 W** |

### 3.2 Transient (burst) model, lumped RC

The board is modeled as a single thermal mass with a path to ambient:

$$C_{th}\frac{dT}{dt} = P - \frac{T - T_a}{R_{\theta,SA}}$$

$$T(t) = T_a + P R_{\theta,SA}\left(1 - e^{-t/\tau}\right), \quad \tau = R_{\theta,SA}C_{th}$$

Assumed values:

- **Forced air** from prop wash: R_θ,SA ≈ 2.5 K/W, so τ ≈ 15 s.
- **Starting temperature:** T₀ = 40 °C.
- **Board limit:** 100 °C, which corresponds to T_j ≈ 112 °C.

The ramp toward the limit is calculated as follows:

1. Steady-state target = 40 + 86 × 2.5 = 255 °C. This is unattainable, so the burst must be time-limited.
2. Solve 60 = 215 × (1 − e^(−t/15)).
3. **t ≈ 4.9 s**

**Burst rating: ≈ 5 s at 80 A on all four motors simultaneously**, from 40 °C. With a single motor at 80 A and the others at cruise, the burst extends to about 15–20 s.

### 3.3 Continuous rating

Allow a 60 K rise at R_θ,SA = 2.5 K/W, which gives P_max = 24 W total, or 6 W per channel.

Solving 2.75 mΩ·I² + 0.033·I = 6 (switching loss scales with I, approximately 0.033 W/A) gives **I_cont ≈ 41 A per motor**.

The honest datasheet rating is therefore **"40 A continuous / 80 A 5 s burst."**

### 3.4 Junction check during commutation

At low eRPM, one FET can carry the full current for a long electrical sector:

- P_FET = 6400 × 1.25 mΩ ≈ 8 W
- ΔT_JC = 8 × 1.5 = 12 K

This keeps T_j at or below 125 °C if T_pcb is at or below 113 °C. Use 175 °C-rated parts for margin.

### 3.5 Required firmware protections

- **ESC side:** ESC temperature limit at 110 °C, with linear current foldback starting at 95 °C.
- **ESC side:** per-phase current limit of 85 A via the shunt and comparator.
- **FC side:** enable ESC telemetry. In ArduPilot, set `SERVO_DSHOT_ESC` to match the ESC type, and enable `BATT_*` and `ESC_*`
