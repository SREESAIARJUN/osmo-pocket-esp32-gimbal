## 0. Architectural Ground Rules and Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning

This design deliberately splits control into two domains:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 / VIT6 (400 MHz) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, DShot600 master, bidirectional eRPM capture |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6 (one per motor) | AM32 | Commutation, 48 kHz PWM, BEMF zero-cross detection, current limiting |

Do not attempt to commutate four motors from the H743 under ArduPilot. ArduPilot does not run commutation loops. The only electrical link between the domains is four DShot lines, plus optional current and voltage sense.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-Step Trapezoidal Commutation with Synchronous Rectification
