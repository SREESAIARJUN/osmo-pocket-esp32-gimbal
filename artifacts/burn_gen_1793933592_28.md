### Critical architectural flags

1. **The H743 does not generate the 48 kHz PWM.** On every production 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel has its own MCU running BLHeli_32 or AM32. The H743 sends DShot600 to those MCUs, and the 48 kHz switching happens in the ESC MCUs. The partitioning below follows that convention.

2. **80 A on a "nano" form factor is not physically achievable as continuous current.**
   - A true 20×20 mm board cannot carry four 80 A phases. Section 3 shows that even a 30.5×30.5 board reaches its thermal limit in roughly 2 s at 4×80 A.
   - The spec must read **"80 A burst, ≤ 2 s, single-motor or staggered"**, with a separately stated continuous rating (≈ 35–45 A per channel with airflow).
