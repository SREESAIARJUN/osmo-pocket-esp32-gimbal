## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

During each 60° sector, phase A is PWM'd (high-side/low-side complementary), phase B is held low-side ON, and phase C floats.

- High-side of A conducts for duty D.
- Low-side of A conducts (synchronous rectification) for (1 − D).
- Low-side of B conducts 100% of the time.

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{pos}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{pos} = 2\,I^2 R_{pos}
$$

The result is **independent of duty cycle**, which simplifies worst-case analysis. Here R_pos = R_DS(T_j) / n_p.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.2 × 1.6 = **1.92 mΩ**, so R_pos = 0.96 mΩ.

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.96\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{12.3\ W}
$$

| Configuration | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| 1 FET per position, 125 °C | 24.6 W | **98 W** (not viable on a nano board) |
| 2 FETs per position, 125 °C | 12.3 W | **49 W** |
| 2 FETs per position, 75 °C | 9.2 W | 37 W |

### 1.3 Electro-thermal fixed point

Solve iteratively:

$$
T_j = T_{board} + P_{FET}(T_j)\,R_{\theta JC}
$$

This converges in 3 to 4 iterations. If

$$
\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,R_{\theta,total} \ge 1
$$

you have **thermal runaway**. Check it explicitly:

$$
2 I^2 \frac{R_{25}\,\alpha}{n_p}\, R_\theta = 2 \cdot 6400 \cdot 3.6\times10^{-6} \cdot R_\theta = 0.046\,R_\theta
$$

The system is stable for R_θ < 21 K/W, which is fine.

### 1.4 Copper and shunt losses (often forgotten)

- **Copper.** 2 oz (70 µm) copper has a sheet resistance of about 0.245 mΩ/□ at 25 °C. A 3 mm wide × 10 mm long phase pour is 3.3 □, about 0.8 mΩ, giving roughly 5 W at 80 A. Use 3 oz or 4 oz copper on the power layers, stitch them in parallel, and keep phase pours short.
- **Shunt.** A 0.25 mΩ shunt dissipates 80² × 0.25 m = **1.6 W**.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Hard-switching V–I overlap

$$
t_{r,f} \approx \frac{n_p\,Q_{sw}}{I_g} = \frac{2 \cdot 15\,\text{nC}}{1\,\text{A}} = 30\ \text{ns}
$$

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12\, V_{bus}\, I\,(t_r + t_f)\, f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 60\,\text{ns} \cdot 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{2.90\ W}
$$
