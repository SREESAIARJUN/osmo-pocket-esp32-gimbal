# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #38): System Architecture & Analysis

## 0. Assumptions and Feasibility Findings

All numbers below are derived from the stated assumptions. **Replace the representative MOSFET and gate-driver parameters with your actual datasheet values before committing to layout.** The equations are written so you can do that directly.

### 0.1 Architecture decision: a two-board stack, not one PCB

Placing the H743 and four 80 A power stages on one 20 × 20 mm PCB does not close thermally (§2.6) or electrically, because ground bounce under ~10 V switching transients would corrupt the IMU. The baseline is:

| Board | Content |
|---|---|
| **FC (top)** | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) or STM32H743 in TFBGA100. IMU ×2, baro, flash/SD, OSD, 5 V/9 V/3.3 V regulation |
| **ESC (bottom)** | 4 × AT32F421K8U7 running AM32, 4 × FD6288Q 3-phase gate drivers, 24 × 40 V DFN3.3 MOSFETs, current shunt, bulk capacitance |
| **Interconnect** | 8-pin JST-SH: VBAT, GND, M1–M4 (bidirectional DShot), CURR, TLM |
