Loop bandwidths are separated by at least 5× per level:

- Current loop: f_c ≈ 2 kHz
- Velocity loop: f_c ≈ 150 Hz
- Angle loop: f_c ≈ 25 Hz

## 2. FOC Core Equations

### 2.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi,\qquad p = 7$$

### 2.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 2.3 Park transform

$$i_d = i_\alpha\cos\theta_e + i_\beta\sin\theta_e,\qquad i_q = -i_\alpha\sin\theta_e + i_\beta\cos\theta_e$$

### 2.4 Inverse Park transform

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 2.5 SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)

This is equivalent to conventional SVPWM:

$$v_{a,b,c} = \text{invClarke}(v_\alpha, v_\beta),\quad v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc}) + \min(v_{abc})\big),\quad D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}$$

The linear modulation limit is |v| ≤ V_bus/√3.

### 2.6 Current PI with pole-zero cancellation

Plant: G(s) = 1/(Ls + R). Choosing

$$K_p = L\,\omega_{cc},\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_{cc}$$

gives a closed loop of ω_cc/(s + ω_cc), a first-order response.

Decoupling feedforward:

$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L\, i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L\, i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

### 2.7 Torque

$$\tau = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q$$

## 3. Cascaded Velocity / Angle Control

### 3.1 Mechanical plant (per joint, rigid approximation)

$$J\dot\omega = K_t i_q - B\omega - \tau_{cog}(\theta_m) - \tau_{dist}$$

### 3.2 Velocity PI (design by bandwidth ω_v)

The velocity loop sees the current loop as approximately unity gain at ω_v. A PI zero placed at ω_v/4 gives roughly 60° phase margin:

$$K_{p,v} = \frac{J\,\omega_v}{K_t},\qquad K_{i,v} = K_{p,v}\,\frac{\omega_v}{4}$$

### 3.3 Angle loop

The plant seen by the angle loop is an integrator (1/s × the closed velocity loop):

$$K_{p,\theta} = \omega_\theta,\qquad K_{i,\theta} = K_{p,\theta}\,\frac{\omega_\theta}{10}$$

The small integral term removes steady-state error from cable spring torque and imbalance.

### 3.4 Discrete PI with back-calculation anti-windup (forward Euler integrator, T_s)

$$
\begin{aligned}
e_k &= r_k - y_k\\
v_k &= K_p e_k + I_{k} + u_{ff,k}\\
u_k &= \text{sat}(v_k, u_{min}, u_{max})\\
I_{k+1} &= I_k + T_s\big[K_i e_k + K_{aw}(u_k - v_k)\big],\qquad K_{aw} \approx \frac{K_i}{K_p}
\end{aligned}
$$

### 3.5 Optional derivative term

The derivative acts on the measurement, not the error, to avoid setpoint kick. It is filtered with a time constant N_f and discretized with Tustin:

$$D_k = \frac{2\tau_f - T_s}{2\tau_f + T_s}D_{k-1} - \frac{2K_d}{2\tau_f + T_s}(y_k - y_{k-1})$$
