# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Design Package (Job #49)

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architectural correction

The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM. It is the flight controller. It sends DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class, e.g. AT32F421). Each ESC MCU runs commutation and 48 kHz PWM into a 3-phase gate driver. The model below is split along that boundary.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Commutation basis.** Six-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) freewheeling means current always flows through exactly two MOSFET positions per motor, regardless of duty:
- During the on-time: the high-side of phase X and the low-side of phase Y.
- During the off-time: the sync low-side of phase X and the low-side of phase Y.

**Hot resistance:**

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] \approx R_{25}\,k_T = 1.8\ \text{m}\Omega$$

**Per-motor conduction loss:**

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2(80)^2(1.8\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{23.0\ W}$$

**Copper loss.** This is frequently ignored and is significant at 80 A:

$$P_{cu} = 2\,I^2 R_{cu} = 2(6400)(0.5\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{6.4\ W}$$
