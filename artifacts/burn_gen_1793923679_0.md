**Headline finding (stated up front):** 80 A × 4 motors cannot be sustained on a nano-class board. The model gives roughly **135 W of semiconductor loss** at burst. The board's thermal capacitance limits that burst to about **1–2 s** before the PCB reaches its limit. The spec should be restated as either "80 A for ≤1 s" or "55–60 A burst."

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

R_DS(T_j) = R_25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)], with α ≈ 0.0065 /K (≈1.65× at 125 °C)

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.6 mΩ × 1.65 = **2.64 mΩ**. Use 2.6 mΩ for burst calculations.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous PWM

In every 60° commutation sector two phases are active:

- **Static low-side FET:** ON for the whole sector, carries I.
- **PWM phase:** the high side (duty D) and the low side (1 − D, synchronous rectification) alternate. Together they carry I for 100% of the sector.

So **two FET channel resistances are always in series with the motor current**, independent of duty cycle:

P_cond,bridge = I_ph² · 2 · R_DS(T_j)

Each FET conducts for 120° of the electrical cycle.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A burst

P_cond,bridge = 80² × 2 × 2.6 mΩ = **33.3 W per motor**

Distribution across the six FETs (averaged over one electrical cycle, valid because electrical frequency is much faster than the die's thermal time constant):

- Static-LS role: I² R × (1/3) = 16.6 W × 1/3 ≈ 5.5 W
- PWM role: split between HS and LS according to D

The worst-case FET average is about **5.5–6 W**.

At high throttle (D → 1) the hottest devices are the high-side FETs, because they also take the switching loss (Section 3).

### 1.4 Parasitic copper and shunt loss (often forgotten)

- **Phase path:** 2 × (trace + via) ≈ 2 × 0.5 mΩ, so P = 80² × 1 mΩ = 6.4 W per motor.
- **Shunt:** 0.25 mΩ shared low-side shunt, so 80² × 0.25 mΩ = 1.6 W per motor (if a shunt is fitted).
- **Battery bus:** 320 A total. With 0.3 mΩ of pad-to-bridge copper, 320² × 0.3 mΩ = **30.7 W**.

The bus copper loss is comparable to one motor's FET loss. Design guidance:

- Use solid internal planes.
- Place battery pads centrally.
- Use ≥4 parallel layers for V_bus and GND.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Hard switching occurs in the PWM leg only: one HS turn-on and turn-off per PWM period.

### 2.1 V·I overlap (dominant term)

P_VI = ½ · V_bus · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw

Gate-current-limited transition times:

- t_r ≈ Q_gd / I_g,on
- t_f ≈ Q_gd / I_g,off

With FD6288Q drive (≈0.3 A source / 0.6 A sink), R_G = 10 Ω, and Q_gd ≈ 8 nC, the total is t_r + t_f ≈ 20 ns.

P_VI = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 20 ns × 48 kHz = **0.97 W**

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

During dead time the complementary FET's body diode conducts, then recovers.

P_rr = Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw = 30 nC × 25.2 × 48 kHz = **0.036 W**
