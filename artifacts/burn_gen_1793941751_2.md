## 1. FOC Inner Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

**Electrical angle:**

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

**Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):**

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant and decoupling

For a surface-mount PMSM ($L_d = L_q = L$):

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

The controller cancels the cross terms with feedforward:

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(e_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(e_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

Here $\lambda_m = K_e/p$, with $K_e$ in V·s/rad mechanical.

### 1.3 PI tuning by pole-zero cancellation

Place the PI zero on the electrical pole $R/L$. The closed loop then becomes first-order with bandwidth $\omega_c$:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

The integral gain is in continuous time. In discrete form, the integrator update is $x_{k+1} = x_k + K_i T_s e_k$.

Choose $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{\text{pwm}}/20$. At 20 kHz this gives about 2π·1000 rad/s. Gimbal motors are high-R, so 500–1000 Hz is plenty.

### 1.4 Voltage limit and SVPWM

**Circular voltage limit:**

$$|v_{dq}| \le \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}$$

The d-axis has priority. The q-axis gets the remaining headroom:

$$v_{q,\max} = \sqrt{V_{\max}^2 - v_d^2}$$

**SVPWM via min-max zero-sequence injection** (mathematically equivalent to sector SVPWM):

$$v_{a,b,c} = \text{invClarke}(v_\alpha, v_\beta)$$

$$v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc}) + \min(v_{abc})\big)$$

$$d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}$$

### 1.5 Anti-windup by back-calculation

Let $u$ be the unsaturated output and $u_{\text{sat}}$ the saturated output:

$$x_{k+1} = x_k + T_s\big(K_i e_k + K_{aw}(u_{\text{sat}} - u)\big),\qquad K_{aw} \approx K_i / K_p$$

## 2. Cascaded Angle → Velocity → Current Control

### 2.1 Per-axis mechanical model

$$J\dot\omega = K_t i_q - B\omega - \tau_{\text{cog}}(\theta_m) - \tau_{\text{fric}} - \tau_{\text{dist}}$$

- $K_t = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m$
- $\tau_{\text{dist}}$ includes gravity imbalance and base-motion coupling through cable stiffness and friction.

### 2.2 Feedback signal choice (important for gimbals)

| Loop | Feedback signal | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Commutation | Encoder angle | Only rotor-referenced measurement available |
| Velocity | Inertial rate of the camera about this joint axis: $\omega_j = \hat a_j^\top\,\omega_{\text{cam}}$ | Low noise, no differentiation, and it directly measures the quantity to null |
| Angle | IMU attitude error projected onto the joint axis | Same reasoning as the velocity loop |
| Fallback velocity | Encoder-differentiated rate (PLL) | Used when the IMU is faulted or during calibration |

Here $\hat a_j$ is the joint axis in the camera frame, computed from the encoder angles of the outer joints. This is what rejects base motion.

**Encoder velocity PLL** (2nd-order, critically damped, bandwidth $\omega_n$):

$$e = \operatorname{wrap}(\theta_{\text{meas}} - \hat\theta)$$

$$\hat\omega \mathrel{+}= T_s\,\omega_n^2\, e$$

$$\hat\theta \mathrel{+}= T_s(\hat\omega + 2\omega_n e)$$

### 2.3 Control laws

**Angle loop (1 kHz).** P control plus rate feedforward, with an optional small integrator:

$$\omega^* = K_{p\theta}\,e_\theta + K_{i\theta}\!\int e_\theta\,dt + \omega_{\text{ref}}$$

$$e_\theta = \operatorname{wrap}_{\pi}(\theta_{\text{ref}} - \theta)$$

**Velocity loop (4 kHz).** PI control plus inertia and friction feedforward, with a filtered derivative
