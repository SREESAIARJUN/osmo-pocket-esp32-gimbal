## 2. Field-Oriented Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 2.1 Transforms

The electrical angle comes from the mechanical angle, the pole-pair count $p$, and a calibrated encoder offset:

$$\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, using $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform:**

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 2.2 Plant model and decoupled PI

The motor in the rotating d/q frame:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d\dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L_q\dot i_q + \omega_e (L_d i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

The controller is a PI per axis plus cross-coupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e \hat L_q i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e(\hat L_d i_d + \hat\lambda_m)
$$

If the decoupling is exact, each axis reduces to $\frac{1}{Ls+R}$. Placing the PI zero on the electrical pole ($K_i/K_p = R/L$) gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth $\omega_{c,i}$:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_{c,i},\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_{c,i}
$$

The discrete (backward-Euler) integrator with back-calculation anti-windup is:

$$
I_k = I_{k-1} + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw}T_s\big(v^{sat}_{k-1}-v^{unsat}_{k-1}\big),\qquad K_{aw}=1/K_p
$$

**Voltage limit.** SVPWM can produce at most $|v_{dq}|\le V_{dc}/\sqrt3$ in the linear region. The q-axis gets priority:

$$
v_d \leftarrow \text{sat}(v_d, \pm V_{max}),\quad v_q \leftarrow \text{sat}\Big(v_q,\pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2-v_d^2}\Big)
$$
