## 0. Scope and Feasibility Finding

Three points shape the rest of this document.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.**
- In every production 4-in-1 design, each motor bridge is driven by its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 or EFM8BB51) running AM32 or BLHeli firmware.
- The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to those ESC MCUs.
- The 48 kHz PWM is therefore an ESC-firmware parameter. The H743 timer allocation only concerns DShot.

**2. "80 A burst" is a short transient on a nano-class board, not a rating.**
- On a 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm board, a continuous rating is physically impossible (§3).
- I model 80 A as a **per-channel burst**.
- All formulas are parameterized, so you can substitute your selected MOSFET.

**3. All component values below are representative, not from a specific datasheet.**
- Do not release the design to fab until the FET datasheet values, IR-camera data, and logic-analyzer captures confirm the model.

**Assumed MOSFET (typical 40 V PDFN 3.3×3.3, one FET per switch position):**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| R_DS(on) @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | 0.95 mΩ |
| R_DS(on) temperature coefficient α | ≈ 0.0045 /K |
| Q_g / Q_gd | 60 nC / 9 nC |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (Per Channel, 80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{DS} = 0.95\,\text{mΩ} \times 1.45 = 1.38\,\text{mΩ}$$

### 1.2 Six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification

In each 60° sector, two phases are active:

- **PWM phase:**
  - The high-side FET conducts for a fraction D of each period.
  - The complementary low-side FET freewheels for (1 − D).
- **Return phase:** the low-side FET is held on for the whole sector.

Total conduction loss per channel:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS} = 2 I^2 R_{DS}$$

This result is **independent of duty cycle** under synchronous rectification.

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.38\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{17.7\ W}$$

**Hottest single FET:** the continuously-on return FET dissipates I²R = 8.8 W for the duration of its sector. Averaged over a full electrical cycle, each FET carries 1/3 of the time on the return leg plus its PWM share. Peak junction swing, however, follows the sector dissipation, because sector duration is milliseconds versus a thermal time constant of tens of milliseconds.

### 1.3 Copper (PCB) loss

Assume 2 oz outer layers plus 2 oz inner power planes, with an effective phase and bus path of about 0.3 mΩ per channel:

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.3\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{1.9\ W}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Hard-switching V–I overlap

Gate-driver sink/source current is about 1 A, giving the Miller plateau time:

$$t_r \approx t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_g} \approx 9\text{–}15\ \text{ns}$$

Using 15 ns as the conservative value:

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD} \cdot I \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.05\ W}$$

### 2.4 Output capacitance

With C_oss,eff ≈ 1.5 nF:

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss} V^2 f = \mathbf{0.02\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

Two switching FETs per active PWM leg, V_drv = 10 V:

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{drv} f = 2 \times 60\text{nC} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$

This loss is dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FET die.

### 2.6 Per-channel and board totals at 80 A burst

| Term | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction (T_j = 125 °C) | 17.7 |
| PCB copper | 1.9 |
| Switching overlap | 1.45 |
| Dead time | 0.61 |
| Q_rr + C_oss + gate | 0.
