# STM32H743 Nano AIO: Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #50)

## Thermal, Switching-Loss, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Architecture Model

---

## 0. Feasibility Findings

These findings shape everything below.

| Item | Finding |
|---|---|
| **H743 role** | The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot only. It does **not** commutate the bridges. ArduPilot has no native FOC/6-step ESC firmware for direct FET drive. Each bridge needs its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32), receiving DShot600 from the H743. |
| **48 kHz** | This is the **ESC MCU's** bridge PWM frequency, not an H743 timer rate. The H743 timers generate DShot600 (600 kbit/s), which is unrelated to 48 kHz. |
| **"80A burst" on a nano (20×20 mm) board** | This is thermally possible only for about **1–2 s**, adiabatically. The derived continuous rating is **about 15–20 A per motor** in prop-wash airflow. This matches real-world 20×20 AIO products. I recommend specifying "80A peak (≤1 s), 18A continuous". Otherwise the rating is marketing, not engineering. |
| **Component values** | All MOSFET and driver parameters below are **assumed representative values** (marked ⚠). Substitute datasheet values for the selected parts before release. |

## 2. Conduction-Loss Model

### 2.1 Topology Assumptions

- Six-step trapezoidal (AM32 default), synchronous rectification ("complementary PWM" on).
- At any instant, one high-side FET and one low-side FET of the active phase pair carry the motor current *I*.
- With synchronous rectification, during PWM off-time the current freewheels through the complementary FET (channel, not diode). Current flows through exactly two FETs for the whole period, apart from dead-time.

### 2.2 Temperature-Dependent R<sub>DS(on)</sub>

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

⚠ Assumed FET: 40 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3, R<sub>DS,25</sub> = 1.0 mΩ (V<sub>GS</sub> = 10 V), Q<sub>g</sub> = 30 nC, Q<sub>rr</sub> = 40 nC, C<sub>oss</sub> = 900 pF.

At T<sub>j</sub> = 125 °C: R<sub>DS</sub> = 1.0 × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**.

### 2.3 Per-Channel Conduction Loss

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)]_{HS+LS} \times 2_{\text{(two legs)}} = 2 I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Add copper path resistance R<sub>Cu</sub> (pads, vias, phase pours). Estimated at about **0.6 mΩ per channel** on 8-layer 2 oz:

$$P_{cond,total} = I^2\,[2R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{Cu}]$$

| I (A) | 2R<sub>DS</sub>·I² @125 °C | R<sub>Cu</sub>·I² | **P<sub>cond</sub>/channel** | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 18 | 0.97 W | 0.19 W | **1.17 W** | 4.7 W |
| 40 | 4.80 W | 0.96 W | **5.76 W** | 23.0 W |
| 80 | 19.2 W | 3.84 W | **23.0 W** | **92.2 W** |

Note the battery-side input current at 80 A × 4 motors with D ≈ 0.85 is about 270 A through the XT60/pads. The input lead pad and copper alone would dissipate roughly 270² × 0.3 mΩ ≈ **22 W**. This is a second reason the 80 A figure is per-motor, non-simultaneous, and short-duration only.

## 3. Switching-Loss Model at f<sub>sw</sub> = 48 kHz

Let V<sub>bus</sub> = 25.2 V (6S full), I = 80 A. Hard-switched edges occur on the high-side FET. The low-side FET switches near-ZVS (synchronous).

### 3.1 V–I Overlap Loss

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

With FD6288Q (about 1.5 A peak source/sink), 4.7 Ω gate resistor, and t<sub>r</sub> + t<sub>f</sub> ≈ 25 ns (⚠ assumed):

$$P_{VI} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 25\text{e-}9 \times 48\text{e}3 = \mathbf{1.21\ W}$$

### 3.2 Reverse-Recovery Loss (Body Diode of Complementary FET)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{e-}9 \times 25.2 \times 48\text{e}3 = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

Q<sub>rr</sub> scales with I and di/dt. At 80 A, use about 2× the datasheet value, giving **≈0.10 W**.

### 3.3 Dead-Time Diode Conduction

AM32 dead-time ≈ 100 ns (⚠ assumed), V<sub>F</sub> ≈ 0.8 V, two dead-times per period:

$$P_{dt} = V_F I \cdot 2 t_{dt} f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{e-}9 \times 48\text{e}3 = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 3.4 C<sub>oss</sub> Loss

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 900\text{p} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.014\ W}$$

### 3.5 Gate-Drive Loss

Two switching FETs per active phase pair, but count all 6 gate drivers for margin. This loss is dissipated mostly in R<sub>g</sub> and the driver, not the FET:

$$P_g = 6 \cdot Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 6 \times 30\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.086\ W}$$
