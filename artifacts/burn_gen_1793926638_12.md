## 0. Architecture Corrections

Several points in the brief need correcting before the math is meaningful.

1. **The H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** In every ArduPilot-compatible 4-in-1 stack, each ESC channel has its own MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). That MCU performs 6-step commutation and generates the 48 kHz gate PWM. The H743 only sends DShot frames and receives eRPM telemetry. ArduPilot has no motor-commutation code. The loss model below therefore describes the ESC board, and the DMA model describes the FC board.

2. **"80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 mm) 4-in-1 is not physically credible as a simultaneous 4-channel rating.** Section 3 shows this quantitatively. That rating should either be single-channel for roughly 1 s or be rejected in design review.

3. **Parts are assumed, not verified.** I have no BOM for Job #60. The model below uses a parameterized representative 40 V / ~1 mΩ PQFN 3.3×3.3 MOSFET. Substitute your datasheet values into the equations; every result is written so it can be recomputed.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Conduction topology (6-step, complementary/synchronous PWM)

In any 60° commutation sector:

- **Two phases are active.**
- **The PWM leg** (high-side PWM, synchronous rectification) has its HS FET on for D and its LS FET on for (1−D), minus dead time.
- **The return leg** has its LS FET continuously on.
- **The floating leg** is off and used for BEMF sensing.

Each FET is part of an active conduction path for 1/3 of the electrical period. Two FET channels plus copper are always in series with I_ph.

### 2.2 Conduction loss

Per-FET RMS current at duty D:

$$I_{HS,rms}=I_{ph}\sqrt{\tfrac{D}{3}},\qquad I_{LS,sync,rms}=I_{ph}\sqrt{\tfrac{1-D}{3}},\qquad I_{LS,return,rms}=I_{ph}\sqrt{\tfrac{1}{3}}$$

Channel conduction loss, ignoring dead-time intervals:

$$P_{cond}=I_{ph}^2\,(2R_{ds,hot}+2R_{Cu}) = 6400\,(3.2+0.8)\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{25.6\ W}$$

- FET share: 20.5 W.
- Copper share: 5.1 W.

This is independent of D under synchronous rectification.
