# Job #51: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC
## Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, Bi-Directional DShot600 DMA Plan, and Reference Netlist

> **Scope.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller running ArduPilot. It does **not** commutate the motors. Each ESC channel uses a dedicated ESC MCU (AT32F421 class running AM32 is assumed). The H743 sends bi-directional DShot600 to those MCUs, and each ESC switches its bridge at 48 kHz. All numbers below rest on the stated assumptions. ## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path in 6-step commutation (synchronous PWM)

During each 60° sector, two phases conduct:

- **PWM leg:**
  - The high-side conducts for D·T_sw.
  - The complementary low-side conducts for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Return leg:** the low-side conducts for 100% of the sector.

So at any instant, exactly two channels are in series with the motor current. Ignoring dead time:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \left[ D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS} + R_{LS} \right] = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) \quad (R_{HS}=R_{LS})
$$

Temperature dependence closes the loop:

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \qquad \alpha = 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

### 2.2 Per-FET RMS current

Each phase's high-side is active for 1/3 of the electrical cycle and is PWM'd within that interval:

$$
I_{HS,rms} = I_{ph}\sqrt{D/3}, \qquad I_{LS,rms} = I_{ph}\sqrt{\tfrac{1}{3} + \tfrac{1-D}{3}}
$$

At D = 1:

$$
I_{rms} = 80/\sqrt{3} = 46.2\ \text{A per FET}
$$

### 2.3 Numerical result at 80 A, design point T_j = 110 °C

- R_ds(110 °C) = 1.0 × (1 + 0.005 × 85) = **1.425 mΩ**
- P_cond,FET (per channel) = 2 × 6400 × 1.425 m = **18.24 W**
- P_pcb (per channel) = 6400 × 0.5 m = **3.20 W**
- Per FET at D = 1: 46.2² × 1.425 m = **3.04 W**, sustained on its 1/3 duty share across the six FETs.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. The hard-switched device is the high-side; the low-side soft-switches after its body diode conducts.

| Term | Formula | Value @ 25.2 V, 80 A, 48 kHz |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw | ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 30 ns × 48 k = **1.452 W** |
| Coss | E_oss · f_sw | 0.38 µJ × 48 k = **0.018 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw | 40 nC × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.048 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V_f · I · 2t_dt · f_sw | 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k = **0.614 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | 2 · Q_g · V_gs · f_sw | 2 × 50 n × 10 × 48 k = **0.048 W** (mostly in R_g/driver) |
| **Total P_sw,ch** | | **≈ 2.18 W** |
