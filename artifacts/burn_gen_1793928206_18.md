# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #86): System Architecture and Design Model

## 0. Assumptions and Constraints

These points shape every calculation below.

1. **The H743 should not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot does not run ESC commutation firmware. A production 4-in-1 stack therefore uses:
   - **H743 flight controller**, which sends bi-directional DShot600.
   - **4× ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware at 48 kHz PWM.

   All the ESC math below applies to the power stage those MCUs drive.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano form factor is thermally marginal.** A 20×20 mm board cannot physically place 24 power FETs with adequate copper
