**Job #78: Architecture & Analysis Package (Rev A)**

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Key Findings

### Architecture

Two MCUs share the stack:

- **The STM32H743 is the flight controller.** It runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600.
- **The 4-in-1 ESC has its own MCU.** It runs the commutation firmware. The H743 does not drive gates directly. A typical choice is an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or an EFM8 running BLHeli_S/Bluejay. This document assumes AM32 on an AT32F421 because it supports 48 kHz PWM and bidirectional DShot natively.

### Assumed operating point (all equations are parametric, so substitute your own values)

### Key findings (stated up front)

1. **Loss.** At 80 A on all four channels simultaneously, total ESC dissipation is about 115 W.
2. **Burst limit.** A nano-format board cannot sink 115 W continuously. The real burst limit is set by board heat capacity, which gives **about 3 s for a simultaneous 4 × 80 A burst** starting from a 40 °C board.
3. **Continuous rating.** The defensible continuous rating with prop-wash airflow is **about 25–30 A per channel**.
4. **Implication.** "80 A" must be specified as a time-limited burst (≤ 2–3 s, 4 channels) or as a single-channel transient, never as a continuous rating.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology

With 6-step trapezoidal commutation and complementary PWM, two legs are active per 60° sector.

- **During the ON interval (D):** current flows HS(A) → motor → LS(B).
- **During the OFF interval (1−D):** current freewheels LS(A) → motor → LS(B), through the synchronous FET rather than the diode.

So at every instant **exactly two MOSFETs carry the full phase current I**, apart from the dead-time intervals covered in Section 2.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| T_j | R_ds |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ |

Design value: **1.5 mΩ**, which includes package and bond resistance.

### 1.3 Channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) + I^2\,R_{Cu} + I^2\,R_{shunt}$$

- **Copper path:** 2 oz outer + 2 × 1 oz inner paralleled, phase-pad-to-FET plus bus. Estimated R_Cu ≈ 0.4 mΩ round trip.
- **Shunt:** a single 0.25 mΩ shared bus shunt carries the *battery* current, ≈ D·I. Its loss is counted once in the board total, not per channel.

At I = 80 A:

| Term | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| FETs | 2 × 6400 × 1.5 mΩ | 19.2 W |
| Copper | 6400 × 0.4 mΩ | 2.56 W |
| **P_cond,ch** | | **≈ 21.8 W** |

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution

Each leg is active for 240° of 360°. Over that window, the HS conducts for D and the LS for (1−D) of the PWM period; the LS also carries the full current for the 120° "low" sector.

- **Average per FET:** ≈ 19.2 / 6 = **3.2 W**
- **Peak instantaneous per FET:** I²R = **9.6 W**
