# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #44): Architecture, Loss Model, and DShot Allocation

## 0. Architectural Assumptions and Feasibility

The "80A burst on a nano board" target needs a feasibility check before any schematics. In a 4-in-1 AIO:

- The **STM32H743** runs ArduPilot. It generates bi-directional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry.
- **Four ESC MCUs** (e.g., AT32F421K8U7 running AM32, or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32) do the commutation at 48 kHz PWM. The H7 does not switch the bridges.
- **12 half-bridges** (24 MOSFET dies) are needed: 3 per motor.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V (max), 22.2 V (nominal) |
| PWM frequency | f_sw = 48 kHz, center-aligned, complementary with dead time |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal (AM32 default) |
| Burst rating | 80 A phase current per ESC, ≤ 5 s, 25 °C ambient, prop-wash airflow |
| Continuous rating | Derived below; expect about 45–55 A |

**Feasibility verdict.** A true nano (20×20 mm) board cannot reject about 100 W of burst loss or carry 4 × 80 A through its copper. This design therefore assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz (70 µm) board**. Section 2.6 shows that even this is limited by heat capacity, not steady-state resistance. Treat "80A" as a time-limited rating, not a continuous one.

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] \quad\Rightarrow\quad R_{ds}(125^\circ C) = 1.2(1.45) = 1.74\ \text{m}\Omega$$

## 2. Loss Model

### 2.1 Conduction losses (6-step, complementary PWM)

In any 60° commutation sector with phase current I and duty D, three switches carry current:

| Switch | Conduction fraction | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| High-side, PWM phase | D | D·I²·R |
| Low-side, same phase (synchronous rectifier) | 1 − D | (1 − D)·I²·R |
| Low-side, return phase (fully on) | 1 | I²·R |

The losses sum to a duty-independent result, minus a small dead-time correction:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)}$$

At I = 80 A:

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per ESC | P_cond, 4 ESCs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W | 61.4 W |
| 100 °C | 1.61 mΩ | 20.6 W | 82.4 W |
| 125 °C | 1.74 mΩ | 22.3 W | 89.1 W |

Commutation rotates through all six switches each electrical cycle (≫ thermal time constants). The average per die is therefore:

$$P_{cond,die} = \frac{2I^2R}{6} = \frac{I^2R}{3} \approx 3.7\ \text{W @ 125 °C}$$

### 2.2 Switching losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM phase leg switches. Each ESC therefore has 2 active switches per sector, with one hard-switched transition per edge.

**(a) V–I overlap (hard-switched turn-on and turn-off)**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = \tfrac12(25.2)(80)(20\text{ ns})(48\text{ kHz}) = 0.968\ \text{W}$$
