## 0. Architecture Corrections

**The H743 should not commutate the motors.** It has only two advanced timers with complementary outputs (TIM1, TIM8, 3 pairs each) plus HRTIM (5 pairs). That gives 11 pairs, but you need 12 complementary pairs (4 motors × 3 phases). Running four sensorless back-EMF zero-cross loops alongside ArduPilot's scheduler is also not viable.

**Correct partition:**

| Block | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 / VIH6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421 or STM32G071 (one per motor) | AM32 or BLHeli_32 |
| Gate drive ×4 | 3-phase half-bridge driver (FD6288Q-class) | — |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET, 40 V, 5×6 DFN | — |

**"Nano" and 80 A are in tension.** On a 20×20 mm board, 80 A/motor is not physically achievable. The model below assumes a 30.5×30.5 mount (~36×36 mm board), 8-layer, 2 oz copper. Even there, 80 A is a **burst** rating of about 3–4 s, as Section 3 shows.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Assumed MOSFET Parameters (40 V, 5×6 DFN class)

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| R_DS(on) @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R₂₅ | 1.0 mΩ |
| Temperature coefficient | α | 0.006 /K |
| Switching charge (Q_gs2 + Q_gd) | Q_sw | 14 nC |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 60 nC |
| Reverse recovery charge | Q_rr | 40 nC |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 50 nC |
| Body diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Gate plateau voltage | V_pl | 3.5 V |
| Junction-to-board thermal resistance | R_θJB | ~1.2 K/W |

### 1.2 Temperature-Dependent On-Resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × (1 + 0.6) = **1.6 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Six-Step Trapezoidal Drive with Synchronous Rectification

At any instant, current flows through exactly two conducting devices:
- the PWM'd leg, either its high side (on-time) or its sync-rectifying low side (off-time), and
- the static low side of the return phase.

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j), \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

With ripple neglected (ΔI ≪ I at 48 kHz with typical motor inductance):

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.6\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{20.5\ W}$$

Per FET, averaged over the electrical cycle (6 FETs share the load):

$$P_{cond,FET} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS} = 3.41\ \text{W}$$

The instantaneous peak while a FET conducts is I²R = **10.2 W**. At 2–5 kHz electrical frequency this is far faster than the die thermal time constant (~ms), so the average governs.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Gate Drive Currents

With V_drv = 10 V and R_g,total = 4 Ω (external + internal):

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_g} = 1.63\ \text{A}, \qquad I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_g} = 0.875\ \text{A}$$

$$t_r = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,on}} = 8.6\ \text{ns}, \qquad t_f = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,off}} = 16.0\ \text{ns}$$

### 2.2 Loss Components

Conditions: one leg per motor switches at a time; V_bus = 25.2 V (6S full); I = 80 A; f_sw = 48 kHz; dead time t_dt = 50 ns.

| Mechanism | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | **1.19 W** |
| Body-diode conduction (dead time) | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw | **0.31 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw
