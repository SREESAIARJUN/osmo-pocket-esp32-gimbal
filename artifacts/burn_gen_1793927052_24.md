# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model for 80 A Burst, 48 kHz PWM, and Bidirectional DShot600

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility Flags

**Architecture.** Production 4-in-1 stacks do not let the H743 commutate the motors. The H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32-class firmware). Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. This document models that architecture. Having the H743 commutate four sensorless BLDCs directly would conflict with ArduPilot's real-time scheduler and is not supported upstream.

**Feasibility flags** (read before committing to layout):

| Issue | Finding |
|---|---|
| "Nano" (20×20 mm) with 80 A/motor | **Not feasible.** 24 MOSFETs capable of 80 A need about 720 mm² in PDFN5×6, which exceeds a 20×20 board even double-sided once drivers and caps are included. Use a **30.5×30.5 mm** ESC board. Treat the H743 FC as a separate stacked board, or accept a 3.3×3.3 FET with a lower rating. |
| 80 A burst | Thermally limited to **about 3–5 s with all four motors at 80 A** (§3). Continuous capability is about 35–45 A/motor with prop-wash airflow. |
| Input ripple | Up to **40 A RMS per motor** at D = 0.5. Onboard ceramics plus an **external low-ESR bulk cap are mandatory** (§4). |

**Assumptions used throughout.** Verify every device parameter against the datasheets of the parts you actually select.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_DC = 25.2 V worst case (full charge) |
| PWM | f_sw = 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous) six-step trapezoidal |
| Representative MOSFET (40 V, PDFN5×6) | R_DS(on) 0.7 mΩ typ / 1.0 mΩ max @ 25 °C; Q_g = 50 nC; Q_gd = 10 nC; Q_gs2 ≈ 7 nC; V_plateau ≈ 4.5 V; Q_rr ≈ 40–60 nC; C_oss(tr) ≈ 1.5 nF
