## 0. Architectural Ground Truth (Read First)

Three constraints shape the rest of this design.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** On a 4-in-1 AIO the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 to **four dedicated ESC MCUs**. Those MCUs run AM32 or BLHeli_32 and generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. I specify the AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32) for the ESC MCUs. STM32G071 is a drop-in alternative under AM32.

2. **"80A burst" on a nano board is a short transient rating, not a sustained one.** The math in §3 shows the board is **thermal-capacity limited to about 2–4 s** at 80A on all four channels at once. Treat 80A as a punch-out rating. Do not treat it as a sustained rating.

3. **Supply voltage:** 6S max (25.2 V full charge). This requires **40 V MOSFETs**. A 30 V part has insufficient margin against the commutation ringing measured on 6S (typically 32–36 V peaks).

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (Per ESC Channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Path in 6-Step Synchronous Commutation

During any commutation sector, one phase is PWM'd and one phase is held low. With complementary (synchronous) PWM at duty D, on the PWM'd leg:

- the high-side FET conducts for a fraction D of each period;
- the low-side FET conducts for the fraction (1−D) of each period, freewheeling.

The return leg's low-side FET conducts for 100% of the period.

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds,hot}\left[D + (1-D) + 1\right] = 2\,I^2 R_{ds,hot}
$$

This result is **independent of duty cycle**, which is a convenient property of synchronous rectification.

At 80A:

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.35\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{17.28\ W}
$$

Per-FET worst case: the return-leg low-side FET takes the full I²R:

$$
P_{LS,ret} = 6400 \times 1.35\text{ m}\Omega = 8.64\text{ W}
$$

Sector rotation spreads this across the three low-side FETs over each electrical revolution. The FET is still sized for the instantaneous 8.64 W because the sector dwell time can exceed the FET's ~1 ms thermal time constant at low eRPM.

Add **copper and trace loss**. The phase and bus copper path is estimated at 0.4 mΩ on 2 oz with stitched planes:

$$
P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.4\text{ m}\Omega = 2.56\text{ W}
$$

### 2.2 Switching Transition Times (Gate-Charge Method)

Rise time (turn-on, Miller-dominated):

$$
t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd}\,R_{g,tot}}{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}} = \frac{10\text{n}\times 5}{6} = 8.3\text{ ns}
$$

Fall time (turn-off):

$$
t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd}\,R_{g,tot}}{V_{plateau}} = \frac{10\text{n}\times 5}{4} = 12.5\text{ ns}
$$

The calculated t_r + t_f is 20.8 ns. Common-source inductance in the PDFN package plus layout parasitics typically adds about 40%. **Design value: t_r + t_f = 30 ns.**
