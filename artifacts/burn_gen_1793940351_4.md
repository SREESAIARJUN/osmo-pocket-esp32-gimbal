# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have the original Job #17 specification, so the design below rests on the stated assumptions. Every parameter that depends on them is isolated in a config struct, and the gain formulas are given in closed form so you can re-derive gains for your actual hardware.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs `p = 7`), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 2–5 mH |
| Position sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701 class) on each motor shaft |
| Current sense | 2- or 3-shunt low-side, sampled at PWM center (voltage-mode fallback in §1.6) |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with single-precision FPU (e.g., STM32G4/H7) |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera payload, SPI, 8 kHz ODR, data-ready interrupt |

### Loop rates

| Loop | Rate | Bandwidth target |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz, in the PWM ISR | ω_c ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz |
| IMU acquisition, filtering, fusion | 8 kHz | — |
| Rate (velocity) loop | 4 kHz | ω_v ≈ 2π·60–120 Hz |
| Attitude (angle) loop | 1 kHz | ω_θ ≈ ω_v / 5 |

### Signal flow

```
 setpoint ─► [Angle P + rate FF] ─ω*─► [Rate PI + accel FF] ─i_q*─►(+)─► [Current PI, dq] ─► SVPWM ─► motor
               ▲  (1 kHz)               ▲  (4 kHz)                 ▲ i_q,cog (anti-cogging FF, θ_m)
               │                        │                          │
           attitude q̂               gyro rate ω_axis            encoder θ_m ─► PLL ─► θ_e, ω_m
               └──── Dual ICM-42688-P → AAF → notch → LPF → consistency-checked fusion → Mahony
```

The design has three key structural choices:

- **The rate loop closes on the *inertial* gyro rate of the camera**, not on encoder velocity. That is what we are trying to null.
- **The encoder provides commutation and the cogging-table index**, plus a PLL velocity used for back-EMF decoupling.
- **Bandwidth separation of at least 5× between cascaded loops** lets each loop be designed as if the inner loop were ideal.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

The mechanical angle is corrected by an offset θ_off, found by calibration in §6:

$$
\theta_e = \mathrm{wrap}_{2\pi}\!\big(p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}})\big)
$$

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant)

With balanced currents ($i_a+i_b+i_c=0$):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

### 1.3 Park and inverse Park transforms

Park:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

Inverse Park:

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.4 Plant model and torque

PMSM dq voltage equations (surface-mount magnets, so $L_d=L_q=L$):

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

Torque:

$$
\tau = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$
