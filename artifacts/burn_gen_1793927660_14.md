# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #14)

**Scope:** Power-stage loss model (80 A burst, 48 kHz PWM), thermal transient model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and reference netlists.

## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Red Flags

### 0.1 Architectural clarifications

| Item | Clarification |
|---|---|
| **Who generates 48 kHz PWM** | The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and emits **DShot600 commands only**. The 48 kHz six-step commutation PWM runs on **four dedicated ESC MCUs** (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32/Bluejay). The H743 never drives gates. The architecture below reflects this. |
| **MOSFET parameters** | No FET part number was specified. I use a parameterized **example 40 V PQFN 3.3×3.3 FET** (6S-capable). All equations are symbolic, so you can substitute your datasheet values. |

### 0.2 Red flag: 80 A per channel on a nano AIO

A 4 × 80 A burst means **320 A battery current**. Before layout freeze, address these issues:

- **Battery connector:** An XT60 is rated about 60 A continuous. Even XT90 or AS150 class hardware is marginal for repeated 320 A bursts.
- **Distribution copper and shunts:** These dissipate tens of watts at 320 A (see §2.5).
- **Steady state:** The power stage dissipates more than 120 W at 80 A/ch (§2–3). A nano-format board (≈ 30 × 30 mm) cannot sustain this in steady state.

**Recommendation:** Specify the rating as **"80 A burst, ≤ 2 s, ≤ 10 % duty cycle"** and make the firmware current limit enforce it (§3.5).

## 2. Loss Model (Per ESC Channel)

### 2.1 Conduction topology: six-step, complementary (synchronous) PWM

In any commutation sector, current flows from phase A to phase B. The configuration is:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):** The high-side FET is on for duty D. The low-side FET is on for (1 − D), acting as a synchronous rectifier.
- **Leg B (static leg):** The low-side FET is on for 100 % of the sector.
