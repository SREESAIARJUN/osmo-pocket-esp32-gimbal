**Job #53, Rev A (engineering draft)**

**Scope:** 30.5×30.5 mm AIO board (H743 FC with 4×ESC), 6S nominal (25.2 V max), 80 A per channel burst, 48 kHz ESC PWM, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600.

> **Feasibility flag:** The model below shows that 80 A per channel is a **short-burst rating only**, measured in seconds. It is not continuous on this footprint. Derate marketing claims accordingly. ## 1. System Architecture

```
 VBAT (6S) ──┬── TVS + 4×470µF low-ESR bulk (off-board) + 8×10µF X7R/ch (on-board)
             ├── Buck 10V (gate-drive rail, 1A)
             ├── Buck 5V  (FC/periph, 3A)  ── LDO 3.3V (H743, sensors)
             └── 4× ESC channel:
                   ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 or BLHeli_32)
                   └─ 3-ph gate driver (FD6288Q class) ── 6× N-MOSFET (3 half-bridges)
 STM32H743 ── 4× DShot600 bidir lines (one per ESC MCU), single-wire, 3.3V
           ── CURR (summed shunt or per-channel) → ADC
           ── VBAT divider → ADC
```

The H743 does not switch the power stage. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by each ESC MCU. The H743 supplies DShot commands and receives eRPM telemetry.

## 3. Conduction-Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 3.1 Topology current paths

The ESC uses six-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM at duty D. During each 60° sector:

- **Phase A** high side (Q_AH) conducts for D·T. Its complement Q_AL conducts for (1−D)·T, freewheeling.
- **Phase B** low side (Q_BL) conducts for 100 % of T.

So for one ESC channel at phase current I:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R(T_j)
$$

The result is independent of D. Synchronous rectification makes the switching node always carry I through one FET.

### 3.2 Numbers at I = 80 A

| T_j | R(T_j) | P_cond per channel | P_cond for 4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.50 mΩ | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| 100 °C | 2.12 mΩ | 27.1 W | 108 W |
| 125 °C | 2.33 mΩ | 29.8 W | 119 W |

### 3.3 Per-FET worst case (FET always on within its sector)

$$
P_{FET,max} = I^2 R = 6400 \times 2.33\text{ m}\Omega = 14.9\text{ W (instantaneous, within a sector)}
$$

Averaged over a full electrical revolution, each FET is a "constant-on" device for 1/3 of the cycle and a PWM device for 1/3:

$$
\bar P_{FET} \approx \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R + \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R\cdot\tfrac{1}{2} \approx 0.5\,I^2R \approx 7.5\text{ W}
$$

The second term uses an average of D and (1−D).

### 3.4 PCB copper loss (often ignored, should not be)

Assume a 2 oz (70 µm) inner layer with an 8 mm wide, 12 mm long path. Copper resistivity ρ_Cu at 100 °C is 2.2×10⁻⁸ Ω·m.

$$
R_{trace} = \frac{\rho L}{wt} = \frac{2.2\times10^{-8}\cdot 0.012}{0.008\cdot 70\times10^{-6}} = 0.47\text{ m}\Omega \text{ per layer}
$$

With 4 parallel layers this gives about 0.12 mΩ, or **0.75 W per 80 A path**. Sum this over the phase out, the bus, and the ground return.

## 4. Switching-Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Each channel has one hard-switched device per PWM period: the active high side. The complementary FET switches with near-ZVS after dead time. V_bus = 25.2 V and I = 80 A.

| Loss term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap (hard switch) | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | ½·25.2·80·40 ns·48 k = **1.94 W** |
| Output capacitance | Q_oss·V·f_sw | 40 nC·25.2·48 k = **0.048 W** |
| Body-diode reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 50 nC·25.2·48 k = **0.060 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw | 0.8·80·200 ns·48 k = **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (2 switching FETs) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw | 2·50 nC·10·48 k = **0.048 W** (dissipated in driver and R_g) |
| **Total switching per channel** | | **≈ 2.71 W** |

**Sensitivity:**

- P_sw scales linearly with f_sw. Dropping to 24 kHz saves about 1.3 W per channel at 80 A, but raises current ripple.
- Ripple: ΔI = V·D(1−D)/(L·f_sw). With motor L = 15 µH and D = 0.5, ΔI = 25.2·0.25/(15 µH·48 kHz) = 8.75 A p-p. At 24 kHz it doubles to 17.5 A.
- The RMS ripple adds ΔI²/12 to the I² term, which is negligible at 80 A.
