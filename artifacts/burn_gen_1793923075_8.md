> **Scope note:** The STM32H743 is the flight-controller MCU. The ESC half-bridges are driven by a separate ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. ## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation topology

The model assumes trapezoidal (6-step) drive with synchronous rectification, which is AM32/BLHeli_32 default behaviour.

- At every instant, two phases conduct current I.
- The active path always contains exactly **one high-side-equivalent and one low-side-equivalent channel**.
- The PWM leg alternates HS ↔ LS (complementary switching).
- The static leg holds its low-side FET on.

So regardless of duty cycle D, the current sees **2 × R_ds,on** in series:

$$P_{cond,FET}(I,T_j) = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

**Distribution across devices.** Each phase leg carries current for 2/3 of the electrical period.
- Within the PWM leg, the HS FET gets fraction D and the LS FET gets fraction (1−D).
- The worst-case single device is the static low-side FET during its 1/3 conduction sector.

Average per-FET conduction over one electrical cycle:

$$\bar P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} D\, I^2 R_{ds}, \qquad \bar P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(2-D)\,I^2R_{ds}\ (\text{upper bound, static + sync-rect})$$

**Note on time scales.** At the electrical frequencies these motors run at (≈1–3 kHz), the FET die thermal time constant (~1–5 ms) is comparable to the sector time. For junction peak estimates, use the **instantaneous** I²R, not the average.

### 1.2 Electro-thermal fixed point

Junction temperature depends on R_ds, which depends on junction temperature:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + R_{\theta}\,I^2 R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

Solving for T_j:

$$T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + R_\theta I^2 R_{25}(1-25\alpha)}{1 - \alpha R_\theta I^2 R_{25}}$$

**Thermal runaway condition:** a stable solution exists only if α · R_θ · I² · R_25 < 1.
