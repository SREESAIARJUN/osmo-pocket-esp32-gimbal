# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #30): Architecture & Design Model

---

## 0. Architectural Ground Truth (Read First)

Several constraints shape everything below.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is a flight-control stack, not ESC firmware. The 4-in-1 needs dedicated ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. I specify **4× AT32F421K8U7** (AM32-supported, QFN-32). The H743 drives them over bidirectional DShot600.
2. **"Nano" and "80 A burst" conflict thermally.** Section 3 shows that 4 channels × 80 A cannot be sustained for more than about 1 s on a 30.5 × 30.5 mm board without forced airflow. A true 20 × 20 / 25.5 × 25.5 board cannot physically fit 24 power FETs of adequate R_DS(on). This design therefore assumes:
   - **Board:** 30.5 × 30.5 mm mounting, 36 × 36 mm outline, 8-layer, 2 oz inner / 2 oz outer.
   - **Battery:** 6S (25.2 V max, 21.0 V nominal), so 40 V MOSFETs are required.
   - **Ratings:** 80 A is a *per-channel, ≤ 1 s* burst with prop-wash airflow. Continuous rating is about 35–40 A per channel.
3. **Component values are representative.** Parameters are typical 40 V, 3.3 × 3.3 mm PQFN class. Re-run the model against the datasheet of the part you actually procure.

## 2. Loss Model: Single ESC Channel (Trapezoidal, Complementary PWM)

### 2.1 Conduction Topology

In 6-step commutation, two phases are active at any instant: phase A is PWM-driven (HS/LS complementary) and phase B is held LS-on.

- During D: current flows through HS_A and LS_B.
- During (1−D): current flows through LS_A (synchronous rectification) and LS_B.

The motor current therefore always flows through **exactly two conducting FETs**, independent of duty:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

**Temperature dependence** (linearized; the typical 40 V trench FET normalized curve is about 1.55 at 125 °C):

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$
