# STM32H743 FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model and Allocation Package (Job #34)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and One Architectural Correction

**Architecture.** In a stack that runs ArduPilot, the H743 does not commutate the motors. ArduPilot has no motor-commutation or FOC path. The partition is:

- **H743 (flight controller):** generates bi-directional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry.
- **ESC MCU per channel (AT32F421 / STM32G071 class, running AM32 or BLHeli_32):** generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM, handles BEMF zero-cross detection, and drives the gate driver and MOSFETs.

Everything below follows that partition. If Job #34 truly intends the H743 to drive gates directly, that is a custom-firmware project, not ArduPilot, and the DMA section would change completely.
