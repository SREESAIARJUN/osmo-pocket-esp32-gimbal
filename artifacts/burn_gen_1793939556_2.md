## 0. Architecture Clarifications

These points define the design boundaries.

1. **The H743 runs ArduPilot and does not commutate the motors.** Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU running AM32, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The 48 kHz switching frequency is an AM32 parameter on those MCUs. ArduPilot's only role is to generate bi-directional DShot600 and receive eRPM telemetry.
2. **"Nano" and "80 A burst" conflict.** A 20×20 mm board cannot route or dissipate 4 × 80 A. This design assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz (70 µm) board on all layers**. The analysis below shows that even at that size, "80 A burst on all four channels simultaneously" lasts about 1–2 seconds, not the 10 seconds typical of marketing claims.
3. **Device parameters are representative assumptions**, not a specific part number. Substitute real datasheet values for your selected MOSFET and re-run the equations.
