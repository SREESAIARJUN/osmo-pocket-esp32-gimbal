## 0. Scope and Architecture Clarification

**The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In every viable AIO (all-in-one) design, the work is split as follows:

| Function | Device | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Flight control (ArduPilot) | STM32H743VIT6 at 400 MHz (TIMx clock 200 MHz) | Loop rate 1–2 kHz |
| Motor commutation and 48 kHz PWM | 4× ESC MCUs (e.g. STM32G071 / AT32F421 running AM32 or BLHeli_32), or 1× quad-capable MCU | 48 kHz |
| Link between the two | Bi-directional DShot600, one wire per motor | 600 kbit/s down, 750 kbit/s (GCR) up |

The model below therefore has two independent parts:
- **Power-stage physics** (Sections 1–3), owned by the ESC MCU firmware and hardware.
- **DShot DMA allocation** (Section 4), owned by the H743 running ArduPilot.
