# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Systems Architecture Model (Job #34)

**Document class:** Engineering reference design, Rev A (pre-layout)
**Status:** Analytical model. All component values must be validated against the selected vendors' datasheets and bench thermal characterization before release.

---

## 0. Architectural Assumptions and Corrections

Several assumptions are stated up front because they drive every number that follows.

| # | Assumption / Correction | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU, not the ESC commutation MCU.** Each of the 4 ESC channels has its own MCU (AT32F421 running AM32, baseline). | ArduPilot does not perform BLDC commutation. Bidirectional DShot is an FC-to-ESC protocol, so it implies separate ESC MCUs. |
| A2 | **Form factor: "nano" ≠ 20×20 mm.** 80A burst per channel is not thermally or electrically achievable on 20×20 mm. The baseline is a **30.5×30.5 mm mount pattern on a ~36×36 mm board**, using an 8-layer, 2 oz/2 oz/1 oz internal stack. | The calculations below show 20×20 mm fails the burst budget by about 2.5×. |
| A3 | **Bus voltage:** 6S LiPo, 25.2 V maximum. Components are rated 40 V. | Standard for this power class. |
| A4 | **"80A burst" means 80A phase (motor) current per channel, for a limited duration.** It is not a continuous rating and is not battery current. | The duration is derived in §3. |
| A5 | **fsw = 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous) PWM, trapezoidal 6-step commutation.** | This matches the AM32 48 kHz PWM setting. |
| A6 | **MOSFET baseline:** a 40 V N-channel in DFN3.3×3.3 with the parameters listed in §1.1. These are class-typical placeholders, not a specific part number. | Substitute actual datasheet values. The model is fully parametric. |

### 1.2 Gate Driver

- **Part:** FD6288Q-class 3-phase half-bridge driver, one per channel.
- **Drive supply:** V_DRV = 10 V (from the 10 V rail in §5.4).
- **Source/sink strength:** ≈1.5 A / 1.8 A peak. Modelled as R_drv,on ≈ 4.5 Ω (source) and R_drv,off ≈ 2.5 Ω (sink).
