## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical model (PMSM, dq frame)

$$v_d = R\,i_d + L_d\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L_q i_q$$
$$v_q = R\,i_q + L_q\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e (L_d i_d + \lambda_m)$$
$$\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2}p\left[\lambda_m i_q + (L_d-L_q)i_d i_q\right] \;\overset{L_d=L_q}{=}\; K_t\, i_q,\qquad K_t = \tfrac{3}{2}p\lambda_m$$

Here $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}$ and $\omega_e = p\,\omega_m$.

### 1.2 Transforms

The Clarke transform is amplitude-invariant and assumes $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$:

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

The Park transform and its inverse:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix},\qquad
\begin{bmatrix} v_\alpha\\ v_\beta\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & -\sin\theta_e\\ \sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} v_d\\ v_q\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.3 Current PI with decoupling and back-calculation anti-windup
