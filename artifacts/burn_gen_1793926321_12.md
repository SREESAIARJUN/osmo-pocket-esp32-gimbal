# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture Model

**Scope:** 80 A burst power stage, 48 kHz switching, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600

---

## 0. Architecture Corrections Before the Math

Three points change the model, so they come first.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is a flight-control stack and has no BLDC commutation firmware. In every viable design, the H743 sends DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The model therefore has two layers:
   - **Power layer:** ESC MCU, gate driver and MOSFETs (48 kHz, losses, thermal).
   - **Control layer:** H743, DShot600 and DMA (timing and DMA allocation).
