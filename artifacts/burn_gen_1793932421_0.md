## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle
$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

The offset θ_off is found at commissioning: inject $V_d$ at $\theta_e=0$, wait for the rotor to settle, then record $\theta_m$.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$)
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform
$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 PMSM dq model
$$v_d = R i_d + L_d\dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$
$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\,\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q \quad(\text{SPM},\ L_d\approx L_q)$$

### 1.5 Current PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward
For a target current-loop bandwidth $\omega_{cc}$ (choose ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz at 20 kHz PWM), pole-zero cancellation gives:
$$K_{p,i}=\omega_{cc}L,\qquad K_{i,i}=\omega_{cc}R$$

The control law is
$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

with $i_d^*=0$.
