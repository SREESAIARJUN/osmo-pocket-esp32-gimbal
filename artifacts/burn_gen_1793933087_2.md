## 0. Architectural Assumptions and Feasibility Flags

The derivation depends on several assumptions. Two of them significantly affect the result.

| # | Assumption | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | The H743 runs ArduPilot only. The 4-in-1 ESC uses **4 dedicated ESC MCUs** (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32) driving gate drivers. | The H743 does not commutate motors. It only sends DShot. |
| A2 | 6S pack: V_bus,max = 25.2 V (design), nominal 22.2 V | Sets switching loss and FET voltage rating (40 V class) |
| A3 | "80 A burst" = 80 A **phase current per ESC channel**, ≤ 5 s | Battery current = D × I_phase. Total pack current at full throttle is about 4 × 80 A = 320 A, which is a lot. |
| A4 | 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM at 48 kHz | Determines how many FETs conduct at any moment |
| A5 | Reference FET parameters below are **placeholders**. Substitute values from your chosen datasheet. | All loss numbers scale directly with these |

> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag 1: "Nano" form factor.** On a 20×20 mm board, 80 A per channel is not thermally survivable beyond about 1–2 s even with airflow (see §3). The numbers below assume a **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz inner/outer copper** board. Treat 80 A as a sub-second to few-second burst rating, not continuous.
>
> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag 2: Connector/wiring.** 320 A aggregate burst from the pack will exceed XT60 ratings and cause large voltage sag. Size the input for the realistic sum, not 4 × burst.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path in 6-Step with Synchronous Rectification

At any commutation step, two phases are active (say A+ and B−).

- **PWM ON:** Current flows through Q_AH, the winding, and Q_BL. Two FETs conduct.
- **PWM OFF (synchronous):** Current freewheels through Q_AL, the winding, and Q_BL. Two FETs conduct.

So **exactly two channels carry I_ph at all times, independent of duty cycle**:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j) + I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{Cu}$$

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

For trapezoidal currents with flat tops, I_rms ≈ I_peak during conduction.

### 1.2 Electro-Thermal Self-Consistency

R_ds depends on T_j, and T_j depends on R_ds. The two must be solved together:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + P_{FET}(T_j)\cdot Z_{\theta}(t)$$

Substituting gives a closed-form solution, valid only when the denominator is positive. If it is not, the system is in thermal runaway.

$$T_j=\frac{T_{ref} + Z_\theta\,(k\,R_{ds25}(1-25\alpha) + P_{other})}{1 - Z_\theta\, k\, R_{ds25}\,\alpha},\qquad k = \tfrac{2I^2}{6}\ \text{(per-FET share)}$$
