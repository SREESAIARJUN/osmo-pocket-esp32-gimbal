**Topology.** The H743 flight controller drives four ESC MCUs over single-wire bi-directional DShot600. Each ESC MCU runs 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation with **complementary (synchronous) PWM** on the active high-side/low-side pair.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]^{\;}\quad\text{or, preferably,}\quad R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{25}\cdot k_T(T_j)$$

Take k_T from the datasheet's normalized curve. For the reference part, k_T(110 °C) ≈ 1.40, which gives **R_hot = 1.40 mΩ**.

### 2.2 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

During each 60° commutation sector, phase X switches (HS on for D, LS on for 1−D) and phase Y's low-side is held fully on.

- **PWM-leg HS loss:** P_HS = I²·R_hot·(D − 2t_dt·f_sw)
- **PWM-leg LS loss (synchronous rectification):** P_LS = I²·R_hot·(1 − D − 2t_dt·f_sw)
- **Static LS of the return phase:** P_ret = I²·R_hot

Per channel (ignoring the small dead-time fraction, which moves into the diode term):

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ch} \approx 2\,I^2 R_{hot}}\qquad(\text{independent of } D)$$

$$P_{cond,ch}=2(80)^2(1.40\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{17.9\ W}$$

**Worst single FET.** The return-phase low-side carries the full current for its whole conduction window:

$$P_{FET,max}=I^2R_{hot}=\mathbf{8.96\ W}\ (\text{during its 120° window})$$

Averaged over a full electrical cycle, each FET conducts for 120° (1/3). Thermal smoothing across commutation is only valid when the electrical period is much shorter than the FET's thermal time constant (~ms).
