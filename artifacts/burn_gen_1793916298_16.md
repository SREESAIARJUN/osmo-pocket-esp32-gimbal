# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, BDShot Timer Plan, and Reference Netlist (Job #29)

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Feasibility Flags

Four issues need resolving before any numbers are used.

1. **The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In a 4-in-1 design, the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits BDShot600. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32). That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM and drives a 3-phase gate driver such as the FD6288Q. The switching-loss model below therefore belongs to the ESC side. The DMA/timer model belongs to the H743 side.
2. **80 A per channel on a nano (20×20 mm mount) board is a burst rating, not continuous.** As shown below, total dissipation is about 100 W at 4×80 A. That is only survivable for roughly 1–2 s, and only with airflow. Market "80 A" nano ratings are usually 5–10 s marketing bursts at low duty.
3. **Battery-side current is extreme.** 4 × 80 A = 320 A peak at the pack. XT60 and XT30 connectors, and 12–14 AWG leads, are not rated for this. Expect motor and prop limits to cap real current far lower.
4. **Parameters are placeholders.** MOSFET and driver values below are representative of the 40 V, 3.3×3.3 DFN class. **Replace every parameter with datasheet values for your selected parts** before sign-off. The netlist is a reference topology, not a fab-verified release.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

**Temperature-dependent on-resistance:**

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.2 × 1.5 = **1.8 mΩ**.

**Per-motor conduction loss.** With complementary PWM, current always flows through exactly two FETs: either high-side A plus low-side B, or the synchronous low-side freewheel path. In both cases the path resistance is two R_DS(on).

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

- **FET term:** 2 × 80² × 1.8 mΩ = **23.0 W**
- **Copper term:** phase plus bus traces, estimated at R_Cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ (2 oz inner layers, short pours): 80² × 0.3 mΩ = **1.9 W**

**Per-FET distribution.** Each FET conducts during 120° of every 360° electrical cycle, so averaged over a cycle:

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} \approx \frac{23.0}{6} \approx 3.8\ \text{W}$$

**Thermal runaway check.** R_DS rises with T_j, and higher R_DS raises T_j. The loop is stable if:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\cdot R_{th,ja} < 1 \;\Rightarrow\; P_{cond}\,\frac{\alpha}{1+\alpha\Delta T}\,R_{th,ja} < 1$$

With 3.8 W, α = 0.005, and R_th,ja ≈ 10 K/W, the loop gain is about 0.13. This is stable, but sensitivity grows rapidly if airflow is lost.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd (hard-switched) leg sees full V·I overlap. The synchronous low side switches at near-zero voltage, after dead-time body-diode conduction.

| Loss term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V·I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw = ½ · 25.2 · 80 · 30 ns · 48 k | **1.45 W** |
| Reverse recovery | V · Q_rr · f_sw = 25.2 · 50 nC · 48 k | **0.06 W** |
| Output capacitance | Q_oss · V · f_sw = 40 nC · 25.2 · 48 k | **0.05 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V_f · I · 2 · t_dead · f_sw = 0.8 · 80 · 200 ns · 48 k | **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | 2 · Q_g · V_GS · f_sw = 2 · 50 nC · 10 V · 48 k | **0.05 W** (dissipated in driver and R_g) |

**Total switching-related loss: ≈ 2.2 W per motor.**

**Design levers:**
- Dropping f_sw to 24 kHz halves this term, to about 1.1 W. The cost is higher motor current ripple and more audible noise.
- Dead-time loss is the largest controllable term. Tighten t_dead to about 50 ns only after measuring the gate waveforms; shoot-through risk rises sharply.

## 5. Transient Thermal Model (Burst)

### 5.1 Foster network, junction to ambient (per FET)

$$T_j(t) = T_a + P_{FET}\sum_{i=1}^{3} R_i\left(1-e^{-t/\tau_i}\right) + \Delta T_{board}(t)$$

| Stage | R_i (K/W) | τ_i | Physical meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1.5 | 5 ms | die to package (R_th,jc) |
| 2 | 3.0 | 150 ms | pad to local copper spreading (thermal vias) |
| 3 | 6.0 | 3 s | local pour to board bulk |

### 5.2 Board bulk heating (lumped capacitance)

**Board heat capacity:**
- Assumed construction: 8-layer, 2 oz Cu, about 4.5 g total, roughly 40% Cu by mass.
- C ≈ m_Cu · 0.385 + m_FR4 · 1.1 ≈ 1.8 · 0.385 + 2.7 · 1.1 ≈ **3.7 J/K**
- Add 24 FETs, capacitors, and wire stubs: **C_board ≈ 5 J/K**

**Board-to-air resistance (convective):**
- Prop-wash airflow (h ≈ 50 W/m²K): R_th,ba ≈ 1/(h·A) = 1/(50 × 2 × 0.0009 m²) ≈ **11 K/W**
