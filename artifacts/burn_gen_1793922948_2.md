## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform:**

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)\quad(i_a+i_b+i_c=0)
$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 Plant in the dq frame (surface PMSM, $L_d=L_q=L$)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

The torque is

$$
\tau = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q .
$$

### 1.3 Current PI (pole-zero cancellation)

The PI controller is

$$
C_i(s)=K_{p,i}+\frac{K_{i,i}}{s}, \qquad K_{p,i}=L\,\omega_c,\quad K_{i,i}=R\,\omega_c .
$$

The PI zero at $R/L$ cancels the plant pole, which gives the open loop $\omega_c/s$. The closed loop is then a first-order system with bandwidth $\omega_c$.

**Delay constraint.** The PWM update plus computation delay is $T_d \approx 1.5\,T_{s,i}$. Phase margin stays at 60° or better only if

$$
\omega_c \le \frac{\pi/6}{T_d} \approx \frac{0.52}{1.5\cdot 50\,\mu s} \approx 7000\ \text{rad/s}.
$$

Choose $\omega_c = 2\pi\cdot1000$ rad/s.

**Decoupling plus back-EMF feed-forward:**

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(e_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(e_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m .
$$
