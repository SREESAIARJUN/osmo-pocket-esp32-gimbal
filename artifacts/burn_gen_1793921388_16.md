## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology

The model assumes six-step (trapezoidal) commutation with synchronous rectification. At any instant, two phases conduct:

- The **PWM leg**: the high-side (HS) FET during on-time, its complementary low-side (LS) FET during off-time.
- The **return leg**: the LS FET is on continuously for that 60° sector.

So the phase current always flows through exactly **two** FET channels.

### 1.2 Equations

RMS current including ripple (worst case at D = 0.5):

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12},\qquad \Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{pp}\,f_{sw}}$$

With L_pp = 15 µH: ΔI = 25.2·0.25 / (15e-6·48e3) = **8.75 A**. That gives ΔI²/12 = 6.4 A², which is negligible against I² = 6400 A² (0.1 %).
