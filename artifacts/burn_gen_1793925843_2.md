## 0. Architecture Note

**The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 as the flight controller. Each of the four ESC channels needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, for two reasons:

- **Timer limits.** The H743 has only two advanced timers with complementary dead-time outputs (TIM1, TIM8) plus HRTIM. That cannot cleanly drive 4 × 6 gates with independent BEMF zero-cross detection while also running ArduPilot.
- **Firmware support.** ArduPilot has no ESC commutation firmware.

The proposed architecture is:

```
[STM32H743 FC (ArduPilot)] --4× bidir DShot600--> [4× STM32G071 (or AT32F421) running AM32]
                                                     └─> gate driver ─> 6× N-MOSFET ─> motor
```

The H743 owns: DShot generation and eRPM capture, the harmonic notch, current and voltage sensing, and logging.

**Design assumptions.** All numbers below are worked from these. Replace them with your BOM datasheet values.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step, Complementary PWM)

### 1.1 Commutation scheme

AM32-style 6-step with synchronous rectification works as follows in each 60° sector:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) switches at duty D. The low-side (LS) is complementary at (1 − D).
- **Return leg:** the LS is held on for 100% of the sector.
- **Third phase:** floating, used for BEMF sensing.

Over one electrical cycle, each phase spends ⅓ as the PWM leg, ⅓ as the return leg, and ⅓ floating.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ /K \Rightarrow R(125°C) \approx 1.2\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Per-device averaged losses

Define the reference conduction loss:

$$P_0 = I^2 R_{hot} = 80^2 \times 1.2\text{m}\Omega = 7.68\ \text{W}$$

| Device | Expression | D = 0.5 | D = 0.95 |
|---|---|---|---|
| HS FET (per phase) | ⅓·D·P₀ | 1.28 W | 2.43 W |
| LS FET (per phase) | ⅓·(1−D)·P₀ + ⅓·P₀ | 3.84 W | 2.69 W |

### 1.4 Channel total

At any instant, two FETs carry the full current. Ripple is ignored, which is valid when L_phase·f_sw ≫ R.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 I^2 R_{hot} = 15.36\ \text{W}$$

This holds for every D.

Ripple correction, if needed:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{2L f_{sw}}$$

With motor L ≈ 15 µH, D = 0.5, and V_bemf ≈ 11 V, ΔI ≈ 4.9 A. The correction is about +0.03%, so it is negligible.

### 1.5 Copper and shunt conduction

These losses are not negligible at 80 A:

- **Phase pad and trace:** about 0.3 mΩ per path (2 oz, wide pours) → 2 × 6400 × 0.3m ≈ 3.8 W per channel.
- **Shunt:** 0.25 mΩ per channel → 1.6 W, or 0 W if you use MOSFET R_DS sensing.
