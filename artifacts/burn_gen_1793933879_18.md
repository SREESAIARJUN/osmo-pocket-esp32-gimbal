# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal and DShot Architecture (Job #4)

I don't have the Job #4 requirements, so the baseline assumptions are stated explicitly in §0. Every result below is a closed-form expression, so you can substitute your actual MOSFET datasheet values and re-run.

**Up-front warning:** At 80 A on all four motors, a nano-class board is thermally limited to a burst of about 2–3 s. It is not a continuous rating. §3 quantifies this.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{DS,25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\qquad \alpha\approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.2 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.8 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path with synchronous rectification

In six-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary PWM, the motor current always flows through exactly two devices:

- During the ON interval: the high-side of phase A and the low-side of phase B.
- During the OFF interval: the low-side of phase A (synchronous rectifier) and the low-side of phase B.

Conduction loss is therefore **independent of duty cycle**:

$$P_{cond,motor}=2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,motor}=2\times 80^2\times 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega=\mathbf{23.0\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-device average

Each device is in the path for about 1/3 of the electrical period, and the loss distributes roughly evenly:

$$P_{cond,FET}\approx P_{cond,motor}/6 = 3.84\ \text{W}$$

The worst device during a given 60° sector carries the full I²R = 11.5 W instantaneously. That matters at low eRPM, such as stall or punch-out from hover, because the sector dwell time approaches the thermal time constant of the die.
