# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan, and Netlists

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

No MOSFET, gate driver, stackup or enclosure was specified, so I used **representative parameters**. Each one is a named variable so you can substitute your datasheet values. The equations are the deliverable; the numbers are worked examples. Treat this as a design baseline, not a release-ready schematic: the netlists still need ERC/DRC and a layout review.

**Architectural clarification.** In a "4-in-1 ESC" stack, the STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by **four separate ESC MCUs** running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 only:

- emits bidirectional DShot600 frames, and
- captures the GCR eRPM telemetry that comes back.

Section 3 is therefore FC-side and Sections 1–2 are ESC-side.
