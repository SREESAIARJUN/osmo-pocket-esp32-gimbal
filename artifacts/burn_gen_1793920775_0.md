# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC (Job #59): Reference Engineering Model

**Scope and assumptions.** No component BOM was supplied, so every number below comes from stated parameter assumptions. Each section gives the formula first, then the worked value, so you can substitute your datasheet values directly. The netlists are a reference topology and must go through ERC/DRC and layout review before fabrication.

**Architecture note.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32, which gives native bidirectional DShot. The H743 does not drive the FETs directly. This split is assumed throughout.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /\mathrm{K}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**.

### 2.2 Instantaneous conduction path

With synchronous rectification, exactly two FETs carry the full phase current at every instant in 6-step operation:

- one in the PWM'd leg (the high-side during D, the low-side during 1−D), and
- one in the statically-on low-side leg.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.0015 = \mathbf{19.2\ W\ per\ channel}$$

### 2.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts for at most 1/3 of the electrical cycle in its active role. That gives:

- **Instantaneous worst-case per FET:** I²R = **9.6 W**
- **Cycle-averaged per FET:** ≈ 19.2 / 6 ≈ **3.2 W**, assuming even sharing; worst-case skew is about 4.5 W at low duty.

### 2.4 Copper and shunt contributions

These are often forgotten, but they add about 2.5 W per channel at 80 A:

- **Phase trace / plane:** about 0.3 mΩ round-trip, so 80² × 0.3 m = **1.9 W per channel**.
- **Shunt:** 0.5 mΩ in the bus path, with I_bus,rms ≈ I·√D. At D = 0.8: 80² × 0.8 × 0.5 m = **2.6 W total**.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 3.1 Transition times

Gate current at the Miller plateau:

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_g} = \frac{10 - 3.5}{5} = 1.3\ \mathrm{A}$$

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_g} = \frac{3.5}{5} = 0.7\ \mathrm{A}$$

Transition times, using Q_sw = Q_gs2 + Q_gd = 12 nC:

$$t_{on} = \frac{12\,\mathrm{nC}}{1.3\,\mathrm{A}} = 9.2\ \mathrm{ns}$$

$$t_{off} = \frac{12\,\mathrm{nC}}{0.7\,\mathrm{A}} = 17.1\ \mathrm{ns}$$

### 3.2 Loss components (PWM leg, per channel)

| Mechanism | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_on + t_off)·f = ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 26.3 ns × 48 k | **1.27 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f = 30 n × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.036 W |
| C_oss discharge | C_oss·V²·f = 1 n × 635 × 48 k | 0.030 W |
| Dead-time body-diode conduction | V_f·I·2t_dt·f = 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k | **0.61 W** |
| Gate charge (mostly in driver/R_g) | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f | 0.038 W |
| **Total switching-related** | | **≈ 1.99 W** |
