## 0. Architecture Baseline and Feasibility Flags

Several constraints in the brief interact, so the baseline is fixed explicitly before deriving anything.

| Item | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Commutation control | 4× dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421K8U7, running AM32) | ArduPilot does not drive bridge gates. The H743 generates DShot only. |
| H743 role | Flight control, DShot600 bidirectional to 4 ESC MCUs | Standard AIO partitioning. |
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V | Requires 40 V MOSFETs for ringing margin. |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), 6-step trapezoidal | AM32 default family. |
| "80 A burst" | Per-motor phase current, ≤ 5 s, not all 4 simultaneously at steady state | See §3: steady-state 4×80 A is thermally impossible on a nano footprint. |
| Form factor | 25.5 × 25.5 mm (M2, 20 × 20 hole pattern), 8-layer, 2 oz outer / 2 oz inner power | 16 × 3.3 × 3.3 mm FETs fit only on 25.5 mm or larger. |

> ⚠️ **Feasibility warning (Principal Architect sign-off item):** a true 20 × 20 mm board outline cannot carry 24 power FETs plus bulk capacitance at 80 A. The derivation below shows that 80 A is a *transient* rating limited by thermal capacitance. Marketing must quote it as "80 A burst (≤ 3–5 s, single motor)" with a continuous rating of approximately 35–40 A per motor.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path topology

In 6-step commutation with complementary PWM, exactly two phase legs carry current during each 60° sector:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) FET conducts for duty fraction D, and the low-side (LS) FET synchronously rectifies for (1 − D).
- **Fixed leg:** the opposite-side FET is on 100 % of the sector.

So at every instant, **exactly two FET channels are in series with the phase current**, independent of D. This gives a duty-independent bridge loss:

$$
P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$
R_{DS}(125) = 1.4\,\text{mΩ} \times 1.45 = 2.03\,\text{mΩ}
$$

### 1.3 Ripple correction

The phase current ripple is:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\, D(1-D)}{L_{LL}\, f_{sw}}
$$

With L_LL = 20 µH (typical 2207 motor, line-to-line), the worst case is D = 0.5:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\,\mu\text{H} \times 48\,\text{kHz}} = 6.56\,\text{A}_{pp}
$$

The RMS current then becomes:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 = 6403.6\ \text{A}^2
$$
