## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

### Two corrections up front

**1. The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.**
- ArduPilot is a flight-control stack. It does no motor commutation.
- The 48 kHz PWM, commutation and bi-directional DShot decoding happen in **four dedicated ESC MCUs** running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. Typical parts are the AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6.
- The H743 only generates DShot600 frames and captures the eRPM telemetry replies.
- This split is the only architecture ArduPilot supports. It also keeps the hard-real-time motor control isolated.

**2. "80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 mm mount, ~25×25 mm board) is thermally a burst of seconds, not a rating.**
- §2 shows the board reaches its thermal limit in roughly 2–4 s at full burst on all four channels.
- Specify the burst as **80 A / ≤2 s, single-channel-dominant**, and derate the continuous rating to roughly 25–35 A per channel with prop-wash cooling.

## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ** (1.5 mΩ is used below for margin).

### 1.2 Conduction loss

In 6-step operation, two phase legs are active in each 60° sector.

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. The complementary low-side FET conducts for (1−D) as a synchronous rectifier.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET is on for 100% of the sector.

Current therefore always flows through exactly two channels in series:

$$P_{cond}=I^2R_{HS}D+I^2R_{LS}(1-D)+I^2R_{LS}=2I^2R \quad(\text{for } R_{HS}=R_{LS})$$

$$P_{cond}=2(80)^2(1.5\text{ mΩ}) = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

Notes:
- Dead time slightly reduces this term, because the body diode carries the current instead (counted in §1.4).
- **Per-FET instantaneous loss:** the return-leg low-side FET carries I²R = **9.6 W** during its 120° conduction window.
- At eRPM well above 1 kHz, the die thermal time constant (roughly 1–10 ms) averages this over six FETs. The average is **3.2 W per FET**.
- At stall or startup (low eRPM, high current), one FET sits at 9.6 W. **This is the worst-case junction stress.**

### 1.3 Hard-switching loss (PWM leg only)

**V·I overlap:**

$$P_{VI}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw}=\tfrac12(25.2)(80)(40\text{ ns})(48\text{ kHz})=\mathbf{1.94\ W}$$
