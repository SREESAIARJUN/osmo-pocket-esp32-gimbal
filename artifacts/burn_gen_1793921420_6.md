# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control Loop, Anti-Cogging Feedforward, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

## 0. Assumptions

I don't have the Job #5 specification, so the design uses the typical brushless-gimbal assumptions below. Every number in the tables lives in one parameter struct (`GimbalParams`, §6.1), so you can update it to your actual hardware without touching the equations.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 (Cortex-M4F, single-precision FPU), 170 MHz |
| Motors | GM4108-class gimbal BLDC, 12 slots / 14 poles (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5 Ω, L ≈ 2.5 mH |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (MT6701 / AS5048A), SPI |
| Inverter | 3-shunt or 2-shunt current sense, Vbus = 12–16 V |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 24 MHz, gyro/accel ODR 8 kHz |

**Timing hierarchy.** Each loop's bandwidth is kept at or below one quarter of the bandwidth of the loop inside it.

| Loop | Rate | Target closed-loop bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current (d/q PI) | 20 kHz (center-aligned PWM ISR) | ~1.5 kHz |
| Encoder PLL + velocity PI + cogging feedforward | 5 kHz (every 4th PWM tick) | ~150 Hz |
| IMU fusion + attitude + angle loop | 1 kHz (IMU data decimated 8:1) | ~30 Hz |

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

Use amplitude-invariant Clarke and Park transforms. θ_e = p·θ_m is the electrical angle.

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant model

The PMSM in the dq frame (surface magnets, so L_d = L_q = L):

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Electromagnetic torque:

$$
\tau = \tfrac32\, p\, \lambda_m\, i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$
