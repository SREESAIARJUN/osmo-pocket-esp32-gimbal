## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz motor PWM.** In every production 4-in-1 design, and in ArduPilot's architecture, the work is split:

| Function | Silicon | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Attitude control, DShot600 command and eRPM telemetry decode | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot (custom `hwdef.dat`) |
| Commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, back-EMF sensing | 4× ESC MCUs (e.g. AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071) | AM32 or BLHeli_32-class |
| Gate drive | 4× 3-phase half-bridge drivers (FD6288Q class) | n/a |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET, 40 V, DFN3.3×3.3 or DFN5×6 | n/a |

**Assumptions** (all are parameters, so substitute your actual BOM values):

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S fully charged (worst case for switching loss) |
| I_ph | 80 A | Burst **phase** current per motor channel |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | ESC PWM frequency |
| R_ds(on),25 | 0.75 mΩ | Typ. @ V_GS = 10 V (representative 40 V trench FET) |
| k_T | 1.6 | R_ds(on) multiplier at T_j ≈ 125 °C |
| Q_g / Q_gd / Q_oss / Q_rr | 60 / 10 / 50 / 60 nC | Gate, Miller, output, reverse-recovery charge |
| t_r + t_f | 30 ns | Driver-limited (≈1–1.5 A gate drive) |
| t_dead | 100 ns | Per edge, two edges per period |
| V_SD | 0.8 V | Body-diode forward drop |
| T_amb | 35 °C | Under-frame air |

> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag:** A nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board **cannot sustain 80 A per channel continuously**. The model in §3 shows it is a **burst rating of a few seconds**, bounded by the board's thermal capacitance. The spec sheet and the firmware current limiting must reflect this.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology current paths
With 6-step trapezoidal commutation and synchronous rectification (complementary PWM), two phase legs carry I_ph at every instant:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side conducts for duty D and the low-side for (1−D). Both carry the same current, so the leg dissipates I²R for the whole period, independent of D.
- **Return leg:** the low-side is held ON, so it dissipates I²R continuously.

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}
$$

$$
R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{ds(on),25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)] \quad (\alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1} \Rightarrow k_T \approx 1.6 \text{ at } 125^\circ\text{C})
$$

### 1.2 Numbers at 80 A
- Hot R_ds(on) = 0.75 mΩ × 1.6 = **1.20 mΩ**
- Per conducting position: 80² × 1.2 mΩ = **7.68 W**
- MOSFET conduction per motor: 2 × 7.68 = **15.36 W**
- Copper path R_Cu (2 oz outer + 2 oz inner, pad → phase pad, ≈0.25 mΩ): 6400 × 0.25 mΩ = **1.6 W**

**P_cond,motor ≈ 17.0 W**

### 1.3 Per-FET averaging
Each FET carries current for about 1/3 of the electrical cycle. Electrical frequency is 1–3 kHz, which is far faster than the FET's thermal time constant (≈10 ms), so the FET sees the average:

$$
\bar{P}_{FET} \approx \frac{2 I^2 R}{6} = 2.56\ \text{W} \quad (\text{peak instantaneous } 7.68\ \text{W})
$$
