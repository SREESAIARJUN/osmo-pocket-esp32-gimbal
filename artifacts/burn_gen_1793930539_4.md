I have no prior context for "Job #69", so this package is self-contained. Every assumed parameter is labelled. Substitute your actual MOSFET datasheet values and the model recomputes cleanly. Several requirements as stated conflict with physics, and I flag those first.

## 0. Architecture Corrections

| Issue | Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| **H743 driving FETs directly** | ArduPilot does not perform BLDC commutation. The H743 outputs DShot; it does not run sensorless six-step control for 4 motors. | Use **4 dedicated ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). The H743 sends DShot600 to them over on-board traces. |
| **"Nano" form factor + 80 A** | 24 power FETs in PDFN5×6 need about 720 mm² of footprint. A 20×20 mm board offers about 800 mm² across both sides, before the gate drivers, MCUs, bulk caps and FC. | Use a **30.5×30.5 mm** stack, or a 20×20 board rated about 35–45 A continuous with 3.3×3.3 FETs. |
| **"80 A burst"** | The thermal analysis in §3 shows about 90 W of total dissipation at 4×80 A. Without a heatsink the board survives this for **seconds, not minutes**. | Specify 80 A as a **≤2–5 s burst** at a defined ambient and airflow. Pair it with a lower continuous rating. |

## 2. Loss Model (Six-Step Trapezoidal, Synchronous Rectification)

### 2.1 Conduction topology

At any instant, two phases conduct. Current path:
- HS FET (phase A) → motor → LS FET (phase B) during the on-time D.
- During the off-time (1–D), the current freewheels through the complementary LS FET (synchronous rectification).

So the motor current always flows through **exactly two R_DS(on) in series**, independent of D. Each FET carries current for 1/3 of the electrical period.

### 2.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

Evaluated at a design-point T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{DS}(125^\circ C) = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.40 = 1.40\,\text{m}\Omega$$

### 2.3 Ripple-corrected RMS current

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{DC}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph\text{-}ph}\, f_{sw}}$$

Example: a 5 µH phase-phase inductance at D = 0.5 with V_bemf ≈ 0.5·V_bus gives:
- ΔI_pp ≈ (12.6 × 0.5)/(5 µH × 48 kHz) ≈ 26 A
- The ripple term adds 26²/12 ≈ 56 A², which is +0.9 % on 6400 A². This is negligible at 80 A, but significant for low-inductance motors at light load.

### 2.4 Conduction loss

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j) = 2 \times 6456 \times 1.40\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{18.1\ W}$$

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} = \frac{18.1}{6} = 3.0\ \text{W (time-averaged over an electrical cycle)}$$

The instantaneous FET loss while conducting is 6456 × 1.4 mΩ ≈ **9.0 W**. This matters at low eRPM, where the electrical period becomes comparable to the FET thermal time constant (tens of ms). At startup or stall, use the instantaneous value.

### 2.5 Switching losses (only the PWM-ing leg switches)

| Term | Expression | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 20 ns × 48 k = **0.97 W** |
| C_oss | ½·C_oss·V²·f_sw | ½ × 1.5 nF × 635 × 48 k = **0.023 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 50 nC × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.060 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V_SD·I·2t_dead·f_sw | 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k = **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (to driver IC) | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f_sw | 2 × 60 nC × 10 × 48 k = **0.058 W** |
| **Total per motor** | | **≈ 1.72 W** |

Dead-time loss is about 35 % of switching loss. Cutting t_dead from 100 ns to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W per motor. Do this only after verifying shoot-through margin on a scope.
