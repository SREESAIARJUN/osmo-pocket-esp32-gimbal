# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Design Package

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Two points in the brief need correcting before the math is useful.

**1. The H743 cannot commutate the motors.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot (attitude control, EKF, DShot master). A "4-in-1 ESC" still needs **four dedicated ESC MCUs**, one per motor, running AM32 or BLHeli_32. These handle sensorless commutation, the 48 kHz PWM, and the bidirectional DShot reply. The architecture is therefore:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir x4──► 4× AT32F421K8U7 (AM32) ──► 4× FD6288Q ──► 4× 6-FET bridges
```

**2. "80A burst" on a nano (20×20 / 25.5×25.5 mm) board is thermally marginal.** The math in Section 2 shows this. Treat 80A as a ≤1–2 s single-channel transient rating, not a simultaneous 4-channel rating.

**Design assumptions** (change these and rerun the equations):

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_max = 25.2 V, nominal 22.2 V |
| MOSFET | 40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3, R_DS(on),25°C = 1.5 mΩ, Q_g = 40 nC, Q_rr = 40 nC, C_oss ≈ 1 nF, V_SD = 0.8 V |
| R_DS(on) temperature coefficient | α ≈ 0.0045 /°C |
| Edge times | t_r + t_f ≈ 35 ns (FD6288Q, ~1 A drive, Q_gd ≈ 8 nC) |
| Dead time | 300 ns per edge |
| f_sw | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM, 6-step trapezoidal |
| PCB | 25.5 × 25.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz copper on all layers |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Temperature-corrected on-resistance:**

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_DS = 1.5 mΩ × 1.3375 ≈ **2.0 mΩ**.

**Current path.** In 6-step operation with synchronous rectification, two FETs carry the full phase current at all times:
- During the on-time: high side of phase A plus low side of phase B.
- During the off-time: low side of phase A (sync rectifier) plus low side of phase B.

Ignoring dead time, the per-channel conduction loss is:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2(80)^2(0.002) = \mathbf{25.6\ W}$$

**Per-FET distribution.** Each phase is active for 2/3 of the electrical cycle. At high duty, the hottest FET carries:

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{I^2 R_{DS}}{3} = \mathbf{4.27\ W}$$

**Copper path (bus plus phase pads).** Assume roughly 0.3 mΩ of effective trace resistance:

$$P_{cu} = 80^2 \times 0.0003 \approx 1.9\ W \text{ per channel}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Losses are evaluated for the PWM-switching high side at I = 80 A, V = 25.2 V.

| Mechanism | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 35 ns × 48k | **1.69 W** |
| Dead-time body diode | V_SD·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 600 ns × 48k | **1.84 W** |
| Reverse recovery | V·Q_rr·f_sw = 25.2 × 40 nC × 48k | 0.05 W |
| C_oss discharge | ½·C_oss·V²·f_sw | 0.015 W |
| Gate drive (per FET, dissipated in driver/R_g) | Q_g·V_GS·f_sw = 40 nC × 10 V × 48k | 0.019 W |

Total switching-related loss is about **3.6 W per channel**.
