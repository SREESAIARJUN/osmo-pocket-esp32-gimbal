## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and a Feasibility Finding

The model below shows that **80 A per motor cannot be sustained on a nano (20×20 mm mount) board for more than about 1.5 s**, even with ideal MOSFETs. Resolve this with the customer before layout. Section 2.6 quantifies it. I carry both cases through:

- **Case A:** 80 A burst *per motor* (4 × 80 A = 320 A)
- **Case B:** 80 A burst *total*, 20 A per motor. This is the realistic nano rating.

## 1. Conduction Topology Model (6-Step, Synchronous PWM)

During each 60° commutation sector, exactly one leg is PWM-switched and one leg's low-side is held ON as the return path. Each leg spends 1/3 of the electrical period as the PWM leg, 1/3 as the return leg, and 1/3 floating (BEMF sensing).

**Per-FET RMS current** (DC motor current I, duty D):

$$I_{HS,rms}^2 = \frac{D}{3} I^2 \qquad I_{LS,rms}^2 = \frac{1}{3}I^2 + \frac{1-D}{3}I^2 = \frac{2-D}{3}I^2$$

**Per-leg sum:** (D + 2 − D)/3 · I² = (2/3)I
