### Architectural Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. The stack has two parts:

1. **FC (STM32H743, ArduPilot).** It generates bidirectional DShot600 on 4 timer channels and decodes the eRPM telemetry.
2. **4-in-1 ESC.** It has 4 independent ESC MCUs (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32). These do the 48 kHz PWM, commutation and dead-time.

The 48 kHz switching analysis below applies to the ESC power stage. The DMA/timer analysis applies to the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path

In 6-step synchronous commutation, the motor current flows through phase A and phase B at any instant:

- **On-time (D):** HS_A → motor → LS_B
- **Off-time (1–D):** LS_A (synchronous freewheel) → motor → LS_B

So the current always passes through exactly **two MOSFET channels in series**, whatever the duty cycle. This holds outside dead-time, which is treated separately in §2.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu,ph} + I_{bat}^2 R_{Cu,bus}$$

### 1.2 Temperature Dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| T_j (°C) | R_DS(on) (mΩ) |
|---|---|
| 25 | 1.00 |
| 100 | 1.375 |
| 125 | 1.50 ← design value (conservative) |

### 1.3 Numerical Result at 80 A

**MOSFET channels:** 80² × 2 × 1.5 mΩ = **19.2 W per ESC channel**

**Phase copper (pad → motor wire pad):**
- Assume 2 oz, 4 parallel layers, about 8 mm long × 4 mm wide.
- R ≈ ρL/(wt) = 1.72e-8 × 8e-3 / (4e-3 × 70e-6 × 4) ≈ 0.12 mΩ.
- Two phase paths ≈ 0.25 mΩ, giving 80² × 0.25 mΩ = **1.6 W**.

**Battery bus (shared):**
- I_bat,ch = D·I_ph. Worst case for bus copper is D → 1, so I_bat ≈ 80 A per channel and 320 A total.
- This is not physically deliverable by a typical 6S pack and XT60. In practice, current-limit firmware or the pack will sag first.
- With R_bus ≈ 0.15 mΩ and 320 A: **15 W**, concentrated near the pads. This is the strongest argument for limiting the 4-motor simultaneous burst.

**Per-FET split (6-step, averaged over an electrical revolution):**

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{19.2}{6} = 3.2\ \text{W}$$
