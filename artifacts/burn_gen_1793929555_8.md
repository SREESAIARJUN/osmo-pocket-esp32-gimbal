## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility

These constraints shape everything that follows:

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 and emits DShot600. Each motor needs its own ESC MCU, here **4× AT32F421 running AM32**. AM32 supports bidirectional DShot and a configurable 48 kHz PWM. A single H743 cannot run ArduPilot plus four sensorless commutation loops.
2. **80 A burst on a nano (20×20 mount, ~36×36 mm board) is thermally marginal.** Section 3 shows roughly **5 s of all-motor burst** before the board reaches its limit, and a sustained capability of only about **30 A per motor**. I recommend specifying the product as **35 A continuous / 80 A ≤ 3 s single-motor transient** and testing against that.
