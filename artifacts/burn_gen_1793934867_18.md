## 0. Architecture and Key Assumptions

**Architecture clarification:** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (assumed AT32F421, running AM32 at 48 kHz PWM), its own gate driver, and its own 6-FET bridge. The H743 sends bi-directional DShot600 to the four ESC MCUs.
