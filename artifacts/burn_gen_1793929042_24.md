## 0. Architecture Corrections (Read First)

Before the math, three requirements as stated would cause design failures if taken literally.

| Issue | Problem | Resolution adopted |
|---|---|---|
| **H743 as ESC controller** | ArduPilot runs on the H743 as the flight controller. It does not commutate motors. ESC firmware (AM32 / BLHeli_32) runs on separate MCUs. | **4× dedicated ESC MCUs** (STM32G071 or AT32F421, AM32 firmware). The H743 drives them via bidirectional DShot600. |
| **"Nano" + 80 A burst** | On a 20×20 mm board, 4×80 A produces about 80 W of loss (derived below) into roughly 1–2 J/K of thermal mass. That is thermal runaway in under 2 s. | **Minimum 30.5×30.5 mm mount** (about 37×37 mm board), 8-layer, 2 oz on all layers. |
| **"80 A" undefined** | Burst rating is meaningless without a duration and a cooling condition. | **Spec: 80 A per motor phase current, ≤5 s, from T_board = 50 °C, with prop-wash airflow. Continuous: 45 A.** |

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_J) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_J - 25)\right]$$

At T_J = 100 °C: R = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 75) = **1.34 mΩ**.

### 2.2 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

In each 60° sector, current flows through exactly **two** conducting devices:
- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET during t_on, or its complementary low-side FET during t_off.
- **Static leg:** the low-side FET of the return phase.

Synchronous rectification means the conducting path always has resistance R_DS, regardless of duty cycle D. The instantaneous channel loss is therefore **independent of D**:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_J)$$

The ripple correction for phase inductance L_ph is:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}$$

For a typical 5–10 µH motor at 48 kHz, ΔI is under 10 A. The correction is about 0.1%, so it is neglected.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.34\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{17.2\ W}$$

Each of the 6 FETs conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle on average:

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} = \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6} \cdot 2 = I^2 R_{DS}/3 \approx 2.9\ W$$
