## 1. Conduction Losses at 80 A

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 125 °C:

R_ds = 1.0 mΩ × 1.45 ≈ **1.45 mΩ** (use 1.5 mΩ for margin)

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous PWM

At any instant, phase current flows through exactly two devices in series:

- the high-side FET, or its complementary low-side FET during the PWM off-time, **and**
- the low-side FET of the opposite phase, which stays fully on.

This holds independent of duty cycle D, because synchronous rectification replaces the body diode outside dead time. Per ESC channel:

P_cond = 2 · I² · R_ds(T_j) = 2 × 80² × 1.5 mΩ = **19.2 W**

### 1.3 PCB copper path

Assume a 0.5 mΩ copper path (bus, phase pads and vias):

P_Cu = 80² × 0.5 mΩ = **3.2 W**

### 1.4 Per-device average

The six FETs share conduction over an electrical cycle, so each carries about 1/3 duty:

P_FET,avg ≈ 19.2 / 6 ≈ **3.2 W**

The instantaneous loss of an active device is about 9.6 W.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing leg switches hard. Its complementary FET switches at near-zero voltage (ZVS) through the body diode.

### 2.1 Transition time

I_g ≈ (V_drv − V_plateau) / (R_g,ext + R_g,int + R_drv) = (10 − 3.2) / (2.2 + 1.5 + 2.0) ≈ 1.2 A

t_sw ≈ Q_sw / I_g ≈ 12 nC / 1.2 A ≈ 10 ns (ideal)

Use **t_r = t_f = 20 ns** to account for source inductance, ringing and turn-on current overshoot.

### 2.2 Loss components

| Component | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V·I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw = ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48 k | **1.94 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_SD · I · 2t_dt · f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k | **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | V · Q_rr · f_sw = 25.2 × 50 nC × 48 k | **0.06 W** |
| Output capacitance (C_oss) | Q_oss · V · f_sw × 2 = 40 nC × 25.2 × 48 k × 2 | **0.10 W** |
| Gate charge | 2 · Q_g · V_drv · f_sw = 2 × 30 nC × 10 × 48 k | **0.03 W** (dissipated in the driver/R_g) |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 2.7 W** |

**Frequency sensitivity:** ∂P_sw/∂f_sw ≈ 56 µW/Hz. Moving from 24 kHz to 48 kHz costs about 1.35 W per channel at 80 A.

## 3. Total Loss Budget

| | Per ESC channel | 4 channels at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFET conduction | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| MOSFET switching | 2.7 W | 10.8 W |
| PCB copper | 3.2 W | 12.8 W |
| Bulk capacitor ESR (see §4.4) | ~0.5 W | ~2 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 25.6 W** | **≈ 102 W** |

Electrical efficiency at 80 A and 25.2 V: 1 − 25.6 / (25.2 × 80) ≈ **98.7 %**. Efficiency is good, but the absolute heat is very large for 4 cm² of board.
