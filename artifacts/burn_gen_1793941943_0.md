# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

## 0. System Assumptions

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 (Cortex-M4F, FPU, 170 MHz), center-aligned PWM |
| Motors | 3 × gimbal BLDC (e.g. GBM4108H), 11 pole pairs (22p), 24 slots |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per axis (AS5048A / MT6701) |
| Current sense | 2-phase low-side shunts, synchronous ADC at PWM valley |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera payload, on separate SPI buses, rigidly mounted with a known relative rotation |
| Axis order | Base → Yaw (z) → Roll (x) → Pitch (y) → Payload |

| Velocity | 4 kHz | 250 µs |
| Angle / attitude | 1 kHz | 1 ms |

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Motor model (dq frame, surface PMSM, L_d ≈ L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2} p\,\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q,\qquad \theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}
$$

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

With only two phases sensed, $i_c = -i_a - i_b$:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.3 PI current controllers with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$
v_d^* = K_{p,i}\,e_d + K_{i,i}\!\int e_d\,dt - \hat\omega_e \hat L\, i_q
$$

$$
v_q^* = K_{p,i}\,e_q + K_{i,i}\!\int e_q\,dt + \hat\omega_e \hat L\, i_d + \hat\omega_e\hat\lambda_m
$$

**Pole-zero cancellation tuning.** Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole at $R/L$. The closed loop then becomes first-order with bandwidth $\omega_{ci}$:

$$
K_{p,i} = \hat L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = \hat R\,\omega_{ci},\qquad
\omega_{ci} \le \frac{2\pi f_{PWM}}{10\text{–}20}
$$

At 20 kHz this gives $\omega_{ci} \approx 2\pi\cdot1.5\,\text{kHz}$.

**Delay compensation.** PWM update adds 1.5 Ts of transport delay. Rotate the inverse-Park angle forward to compensate:

$$
\theta_{e,\text{out}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s
$$
