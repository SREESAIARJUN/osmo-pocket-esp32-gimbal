# STM32H743 AIO FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #32): Power-Stage, Thermal and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**Partitioning decision.** The H743 does not commutate the motors. A nano AIO built around an H743 should follow the proven partitioning:

```
STM32H743VIT6 (ArduPilot) ──BDShot600 ×4──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421K8U7, AM32 firmware)
                                              └─► FD6288Q 3-ph gate driver ─► 6× N-MOSFET per motor
```

Running four sensorless FOC/trapezoidal loops with 48 kHz PWM on the H743, alongside the EKF, is possible in theory but a poor idea in practice:
- ArduPilot has no motor-commutation code.
- Fault isolation would be lost.
- Timer and DMA budgets would collapse.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Trapezoidal (6-step) commutation, synchronous rectification

In any 60° sector, phase A is PWM'd (HS_A at duty D, LS_A complementary), phase B's low-side is held on, and phase C floats.

| Device | Conduction time fraction | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| HS_A | D | D·I²·R_ds(T_j) |
| LS_A (sync rect.) | (1−D) − 2·t_dead·f_sw | ≈(1−D)·I²·R_ds |
| LS_B (return path) | 1 | I²·R_ds |

Summing these gives the bridge conduction loss:

$$P_{cond,bridge} \approx 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) \quad\text{(independent of D)}$$

The temperature-dependent resistance is:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1+\alpha_R (T_j-25)\right]$$

Each switch conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle in its active role. Averaged over the cycle, the loss per FET is P_cond,bridge / 6. The **peak** device is the static low-side, though, and it carries the full I² R for 120° electrical. At high eRPM the electrical period (~0.5 ms) is far shorter than the die thermal time constant, so averaging is valid for die temperature. For margin I still size on the worst sector.

### 1.2 Copper and shunt losses (often forgotten)

- **Shunt** (0.25 mΩ, DC-bus position): P_sh = I_bus²·R_sh. With I_bus ≈ D·I at full throttle (D ≈ 0.95): 0.25 mΩ · 5776 ≈ **1.44 W**
- **Copper path** (2 oz × 4 power layers, ~0.4 mΩ effective from motor pad to bus): 2·I²·R_cu ≈ **2.6 W**

### 1.3 Self-consistent junction temperature

Because R_ds depends on T_j, the loss must be iterated. Start from a first guess of T_j = 100 °C:

| Iteration | T_j (°C) | R_ds (mΩ) | P_cond,bridge (W) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 100 | 2.12 | 27.2 |
| 1 | 125 | 2.33 | 29.8 |
| 2 | 132 | 2.38 | 30.5 → converges |

**Result:** P_cond ≈ **30.5 W per motor at 80 A**, with the static low-side FET alone dissipating ≈ **15.2 W** during its sector.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one half-bridge switches per sector.

**Hard-switching V·I overlap:**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5\cdot25.2\cdot80\cdot35\text{ns}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.69\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery:**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$

Q_rr also adds turn-on overlap current, about 10% of P_VI. This is included in the total below.

**Output capacitance** (two FETs per switching leg):

$$P_{oss} = 2\cdot\tfrac12 C_{oss,eff} V^2 f = 1.5\text{n}\cdot635\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.05\ W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two edges per period):

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I\, (2 t_{dead}) f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot200\text{n}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**Gate drive** (dissipated in the FD6288Q and gate resistors, not the FETs):

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} = 2\cdot40\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.04\ W}$$

| Component | W per motor |
|---|---|
| V·I overlap (+10% Q_rr-induced) | 1.86 |
| Reverse recovery | 0.06 |
| C_oss | 0.05 |
| Dead time | 0.61 |
| **Total switching (FETs)** | **2.58** |
| Gate drive (driver IC) | 0.04 |

**Design levers:**
- **Dead time** is the biggest cheap win. Going from 100 ns to 50 ns saves 0.3 W per motor. Verify against the FD6288Q's internal ~200 ns fixed dead time; AM32 adds its own dead time on top. Measure on a scope.
- **Lowering f_sw** to 24 kHz halves the switching loss to ~1.3 W. The cost is higher phase ripple current and audible noise.
- **Faster edges** increase dV/dt ringing. The gate-resistor and snubber values in Section 5 target ~1.5 V/
