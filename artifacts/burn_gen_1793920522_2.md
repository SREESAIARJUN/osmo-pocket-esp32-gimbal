## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle: θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset) mod 2π.

**Clarke transform** (amplitude invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$ i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3} $$

**Park transform:**

$$ \begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix} $$

### 1.2 Plant model and decoupled PI control

Plant in the rotor frame:

$$ v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m $$

Control law (PI plus decoupling plus back-EMF feedforward):

$$ v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m $$

**Pole-zero cancellation tuning** for a current bandwidth ω_c (choose ω_c ≤ 2π·f_PWM/20, about 2π·1 kHz):

$$ K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c $$

This gives the closed loop i/i* = ω_c/(s+ω_c).

Set i_d* = 0, since gimbal motors are surface PM with no reluctance torque.

**Voltage limiting:** clamp the magnitude so that |v_dq| ≤ V_dc/√3, which is the linear SVPWM limit.

$$ v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e $$
