# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Job #44 Design Package

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Ground Rules

This package covers four areas:

1. Electro-thermal model of the ESC power stage at **80 A burst per motor**.
2. Switching-loss model at **f_sw = 48 kHz**.
3. **ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600** timer and DMA allocation on the STM32H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step commutation

At any instant two legs are active: one high-side and one low-side.

With complementary (synchronous) PWM at duty D:
- During the on-time, the high-side of phase A and the low-side of phase B conduct.
- During the off-time, the low-side of phase A (synchronous freewheel) and the low-side of phase B conduct.

So **exactly two channels carry I_ph at all times**, apart from dead time. The conduction loss per motor is therefore independent of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2 R_{DS}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

### 1.3 Copper path resistance

A 2 oz trace has a sheet resistance of about 0.25 mΩ/sq at 25 °C.

- Phase path: about 3 squares in parallel across 3 layers, giving ≈ 0.25 mΩ per leg.
- Round trip for one motor: R_Cu ≈ 0.5 mΩ, rising to 0.6 mΩ hot.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A and T_j = 125 °C

| Term | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFETs | 80² × 2 × 1.45 mΩ | **18.56 W** |
| Copper | 80² × 0.6 mΩ | **3.84 W** |
| **Conduction per motor** | | **22.4 W** |

### 1.5 Average loss per FET

Each FET conducts for 120° of the electrical period. The low-side also absorbs the freewheel interval.

- High-side average: P_HS = (1/3) · D · I² · R
- Low-side average: P_LS = (1/3)(1 − D) · I² · R + (1/3) · I² · R

At D ≈ 0.9 (burst), with I²R = 9.28 W:
- **P_HS ≈ 2.8 W**
- **P_LS ≈ 3.4 W**

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Only the PWM-ing leg hard-switches. Its high-side FET takes the V-I overlap, while the complementary low-side commutates through its body diode during dead time.

### 2.1 V-I overlap (hard switching, high-side)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

= 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48 kHz = **1.94 W**

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction (low-side)

$$P_{dt} = V_F \, I_{ph} \cdot 2 t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw}$$

= 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 kHz = **0.61 W**
