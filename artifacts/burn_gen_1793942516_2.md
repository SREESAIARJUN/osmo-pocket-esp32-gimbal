# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #53): Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Timer/DMA Architecture

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Findings

**Partitioning.** In a 4-in-1 stack, the STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.

- **H743 (flight controller, ArduPilot):** generates bi-directional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry.
- **ESC MCU (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class, e.g. STM32G071 or AT32F421, one per motor or a shared 4-channel part):** runs commutation at 48 kHz PWM through 3-phase gate drivers.

The loss and thermal model below applies to the ESC board. The timer/DMA model applies to the H743.

**Key findings:**

1. **80 A/channel is a burst rating only.** All four channels at 80 A dissipate about 115 W on the ESC. A nano board (30.5 mm or 20 mm pattern) can reject roughly 15–20 W continuously in prop wash. The usable burst is about **2–4 s** from a warm start. Continuous capability is about **28–32 A/channel**.
2. **Conduction loss dominates.** It is roughly 92% of the total at 80 A. The 48 kHz switching loss is about 2 W/channel.
3. **Bulk capacitance is mandatory.** Local MLCCs alone produce more than 20 V ripple at 80 A. A ≥470 µF low-ESR bulk capacitor is required at the battery pads.
4. **Motor timers on the H743 need 3 DMA streams** (TIM1_UP plus 2 input-capture streams). This is easily satisfied via DMAMUX on DMA1/DMA2. Timer channels must never land on BDMA.

**Caveat.** The netlists and part values below are a representative reference design for analysis. They are not a released, DRC/ERC-verified schematic. Verify component parameters against the specific datasheets you select.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At the design point T_j = 110 °C:

R = 1.4 mΩ × (1 + 0.005 × 85) = **2.0 mΩ**

### 2.2 Six-step, synchronous PWM

During each 60° commutation sector, current flows through two legs:

- **PWM leg:** the high side conducts for duty D. The low side (synchronous rectifier) conducts for 1 − D.
- **Static leg:** the low side conducts 100% of the time.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS} = 2 I^2 R_{DS}$$

The result is independent of duty, which is a useful property of synchronous rectification:

P_cond,ch = 2 × 80² × 2.0 mΩ = **25.6 W per channel**

**Copper and interconnect.** Battery pad, bus planes, and phase pads add about 0.25 mΩ per current path, twice per channel:

P_Cu,ch = 80² × 0.5 mΩ = **3.2 W**

### 2.3 Thermal runaway check (electro-thermal fixed point)

Solve T_j = T_c + P(T_j) × R_θJC iteratively. The loss per FET, averaged over an electrical revolution, is the channel loss divided by 6 FETs:

P_FET = 2 I² R(T_j) / 6

Convergence requires

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j} R_{\theta} = \frac{2I^2 R_{25}\alpha}{6} R_{\theta} = \frac{2 \cdot 6400 \cdot 1.4\text{m} \cdot 0.005}{6}\cdot 1.5 \approx 0.022 \ll 1$$

The junction-to-case loop is stable, so local runaway is not a concern. The risk is board-level heat accumulation (Section 5).
