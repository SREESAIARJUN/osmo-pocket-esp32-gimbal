# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control Loop, Anti-Cogging Compensation, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

I don't have the original specification for Job #17, so this design states its assumptions explicitly. Every parameter depends on these assumptions and is collected in one config block so you can substitute your real values.

## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset):

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

Inverse Park transform:

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 PMSM Model and Decoupling

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

The cross-coupling and back-EMF terms are cancelled by feedforward:

$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L\, i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L\, i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

### 1.3 PI Tuning by Pole-Zero Cancellation

After decoupling, each axis is the plant 1/(Ls+R). Place the PI zero on the plant pole:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

This gives the closed loop ω_c/(s+ω_c). PWM and computation add about 1.5·T_s of delay. For a phase margin of about 60°, choose

$$\omega_c \le \frac{1}{3\cdot 1.5 T_s}\approx 2\pi\cdot 700\ \text{Hz at } 20\ \text{kHz}.$$

The integrator is discretized as forward Euler (k_i·T_s). Anti-windup uses back-calculation, and the voltage vector is limited to the inscribed hexagon circle, |v| ≤ V_bus/√3.

### 1.4 SVPWM (Min-Max Injection, Equivalent to 7-Segment SVM)

$$v_{a,b,c} = \text{invClarke}(v_\alpha,v_\beta),\quad v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max v + \min v),\quad d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}$$
