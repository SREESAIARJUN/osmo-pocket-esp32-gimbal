## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Conduction topology

During each 60° commutation sector, exactly two half-bridges are active:
- **PWM leg:** HS and LS alternate with complementary switching. With synchronous rectification, one FET in this leg always carries I_ph, apart from dead time.
- **Fixed leg:** one FET held fully on.

Instantaneous conduction loss per motor channel is therefore:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot \left[R_{ds}(T_{j,PWM}) + R_{ds}(T_{j,fixed})\right] + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu,path} + I_{ph}^2 R_{shunt}
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence (requires iteration)

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]
$$

$$
T_j = T_{board} + P_{FET,avg} \cdot R_{\theta JC}
$$

Iterate until T_j converges. It typically converges in 2–3 passes. At T_j ≈ 115 °C:

$$
R_{ds} = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \cdot [1 + 0.0045 \cdot 90] = 1.405\,\text{m}\Omega
$$
