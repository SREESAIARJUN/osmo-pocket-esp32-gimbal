## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step commutation

At any instant, two phases are active:

- **PWM ON (fraction D):** current flows high-side A → motor → low-side B. Two FETs conduct.
- **PWM OFF (fraction 1−D):** current freewheels through the synchronous low-side A and low-side B. Two FETs conduct.

Because two FETs always carry the current, conduction loss does not depend on duty cycle under complementary PWM:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{ds,hot}$$

### 1.2 Ripple correction

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2\left(1 + \frac{r^2}{12}\right), \qquad r = \frac{\Delta I_{pp}}{I}$$

Ripple current is:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus} D (1-D)}{L_{ph} f_{sw}}$$

For L_ph = 20 µH and D = 0.5: ΔI ≈ 6.6 A, so r ≈ 0.08 and the correction factor is 1.0005. This is negligible.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.05\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{13.44\ W}$$

Copper loss must be added. A 2 oz layer at 35 mm length and 6 mm width with two parallel layers gives about 0.25 mΩ per phase path. That adds 80² × 2 × 0.25 mΩ ≈ **3.2 W**, spread across the board.

### 1.4 Temperature coupling (iterate)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

Solve P(T_j) and T_j(P) together by fixed-point iteration. It converges in 3–4 steps.
