# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #42): Power-Stage, Thermal and Bidirectional DShot Architecture

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

Three architectural points need stating before the models.

1. **The H743 does not drive the gates.** In every production AIO, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and talks DShot to four ESC MCUs (AT32F421/STM32G071-class, running AM32 or BLHeli_32). Each ESC MCU runs the 48 kHz PWM and commutation. The design below follows that split.
2. **"Nano" and 80 A are in tension.** The thermal model below shows that 80 A on all four channels at once cannot be sustained on any sub-36 mm board. I define "80 A burst" as **per channel, ≤ 1–2 s**, with a total-board burst current limit. This is quantified in §3.
3. **Component values are representative.** Parameters are typical of a 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFET and an FD6288Q-class 3-phase driver. Before layout, replace every parameter tagged ⟨P⟩ with the datasheet value of your selected part.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology accounting

Trapezoidal BLDC drive with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM) works as follows:
- In each 60° electrical sector, two phases conduct.
- The PWM leg alternates current between its high-side (fraction D) and low-side (fraction 1−D).
- The return leg's low-side conducts continuously.

This gives the per-motor conduction loss:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms,ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{rms,ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)
$$
