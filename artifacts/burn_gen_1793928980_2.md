# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have the specifications for Job #58, so this design uses the stated assumptions below. Every parameter is a named constant, so you can substitute your real values. The places you must check against your hardware are marked **⚠ VERIFY**.

## 0. Assumptions and Rate Plan

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (GM4108-class). Pole pairs p = 7, slots Z = 12 |
| Electrical params | R = 10 Ω, L = 4 mH, λ (flux linkage) ≈ 0.012 V·s, Kt = 1.5·p·λ |
| Encoder | 14-bit magnetic encoder (AS5048A/MT6701-class), SPI |
| MCU | STM32G4/H7, FPU, center-aligned PWM at 20 kHz |
| Phase current sensing | 2 or 3 low-side shunts. A voltage-mode fallback is provided |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera stage. Gyro and accel ODR 8 kHz. Both slaved to one external CLKIN for synchronous sampling |
