## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Assumptions

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| R₂₅ | 1.0 mΩ | R_DS(on) at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V |
| α | 0.0067 K⁻¹ | Tempco (≈1.5× at 100 °C, ≈1.67× at 125 °C) |
| R_cu | 0.5 mΩ | Phase/bus copper per current path (2 oz, internal + outer) |
| R_sh | 0.2 mΩ | Shunt, in the DC path |
| I_ph | 80 A | Burst phase current (≈ DC current at high duty) |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | PWM frequency |
| L_ph | 15 µH | Motor phase-to-phase inductance (typical 2207-class) |

### 1.2 Six-Step Synchronous Topology

At any instant, two phases conduct:
- **Phase A** is PWM'd. The HS FET conducts for D·T, and the LS FET conducts for (1−D)·T via synchronous rectification.
- **Phase B** has its LS FET on continuously for the 60° sector.

Exactly **two FETs are always in series** with the phase current, so the conduction loss is independent of D:

$$
P_{cond,FET}(T_j) = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]
$$

### 1.3 Ripple-Corrected RMS

$$
\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}},\qquad V_{bemf}\approx D\,V_{bus}
$$

The worst case occurs at D = 0.5:

$$
\Delta I_{max} = \frac{25.2\cdot 0.25}{15\,\mu\text{H}\cdot 48\,\text{kHz}} = 8.75\ \text{A}
$$

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6400 + 6.4 \approx 6406\ \text{A}^2
$$

Ripple is negligible at 80 A, so use I_rms ≈ 80 A.
