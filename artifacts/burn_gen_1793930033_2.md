## 1. FOC Current Loop (Innermost, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

**Electrical angle:**

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, assuming $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant Model (Surface PMSM)

$$v_d = R_s i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q$$

$$v_q = R_s i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

### 1.3 PI with Pole-Zero Cancellation and Decoupling

With the decoupling terms removed, each axis reduces to the first-order plant $G(s) = 1/(L s + R)$.

Choose the PI controller $C(s) = K_p + K_i/s$ with:

$$K_{p,i} = L_s\,\omega_{c,i},\qquad K_{i,i} = R_s\,\omega_{c,i}$$

The PI zero then cancels the electrical pole at $R/L$, and the closed loop becomes $\omega_{c,i}/(s+\omega_{c,i})$.

**Voltage commands with feedforward decoupling:**

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e \hat L_q i_q$$

$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e(\hat L_d i_d + \hat\lambda_m)$$

For a gimbal, set $i_d^* = 0$. These motors never reach field weakening.

### 1.4 Discretization (Backward Euler, Back-Calculation Anti-Windup)

$$u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\qquad u_k^{sat} = \text{sat}(u_k)$$

$$I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw}\,(u_k^{sat}-u_k),\qquad K_{aw}\approx \frac{1}{K_p}\cdot\frac{K_i}{1}\,T_s$$
