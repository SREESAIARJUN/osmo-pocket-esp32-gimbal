## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architectural Corrections

These corrections change the design, so they come first.

**1. The H743 cannot also be the ESC commutation controller.**
- Bi-directional DShot is a protocol from the flight controller to the ESC. It assumes the ESC has its own processor that commutates the motor and returns eRPM telemetry.
- Running four sensorless 6-step or FOC loops at 48 kHz on the same core that runs ArduPilot's EKF3 would also break ArduPilot's scheduling model.
- **Architecture:** STM32H743 (ArduPilot) → 4× AT32F421K8U7 (AM32 firmware), one per motor, over BDShot600.

**2. An 80 A burst on a 20×20 mm "nano" PCB is physically aggressive.**
- §3 shows that 80 A on all four channels dissipates about 100 W into roughly 2–3 J/K of board heat capacity.
- That load is survivable only for about 1 s.
- I model it as a time-limited burst, not a rating. The same math is given for 30.5×30.5 mm so you can choose the form factor deliberately.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path in 6-Step Complementary PWM

In any commutation sector, two phases conduct and the third floats. Take sector A+ B−:

- **Phase B, low-side (B_L):** on for 100 % of the sector. It carries I.
- **Phase A leg (PWM leg):**
  - A_H conducts during duty D.
  - A_L conducts during (1 − D) as the synchronous rectifier.
  - Dead time is handled separately in §2.4.

So at any instant exactly two MOSFET channels carry the full phase current:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{hot}\big[\underbrace{1}_{B_L} + \underbrace{D + (1-D)}_{A_H + A_L}\big] = 2\,I^2 R_{hot}
$$

This makes conduction loss **independent of duty cycle**, which is a useful property of synchronous 6-step.

### 1.2 Temperature-Dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R_{hot} = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha'(T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha' \approx 0.0048\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At a design junction temperature of T_j = 110 °C:

$$
R_{hot} = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.0048 \times 85) = 1.41\ \text{m}\Omega \;\rightarrow\; \text{use } 1.5\ \text{m}\Omega \text{ (margin for process spread)}
$$
