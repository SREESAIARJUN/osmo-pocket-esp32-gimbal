## 0. Architecture Correction

**The STM32H743 cannot be the ESC commutation controller in an ArduPilot system.** ArduPilot does not run 6-step or FOC motor commutation. The correct and only practical topology is:

```
[STM32H743 FC, ArduPilot] --4x BDShot600 (bidirectional, single wire)--> [4x ESC MCU, AM32/BLHeli_32-class]
                                                                              |
                                                                     [3-ph gate driver] -> [6x MOSFET] -> Motor
```

- **ESC MCU:** AT32F421 or STM32G071, one per motor, running AM32.
- **Commutation:** 6-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) PWM at 48 kHz.

The loss model below assumes this topology.

### Feasibility Flag

80 A per channel on a "nano" (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board is **burst-only and seconds-limited**, as Section 3 shows. Treat any marketing claim of "80 A" as a ≤2–10 s rating that depends on how many channels are loaded at once.
