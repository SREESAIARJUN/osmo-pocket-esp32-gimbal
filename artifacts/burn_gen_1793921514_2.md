# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Design Basis Document (Job #15)

**Scope:** Power-stage loss model (conduction at 80 A burst, switching at 48 kHz), transient thermal model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA plan, and reference netlists.

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility Findings

These decisions shape everything downstream, so they come first.

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | The STM32H743 runs **ArduPilot (flight control)**. It does not commutate motors. Each ESC channel needs its own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. | Architecture is H743 FC plus **4× AT32F421 (or STM32G071) running AM32** on the same PCB. The H743 talks to them over DShot600 bidirectional. |
| F2 | "Nano" (20×20 mm) **cannot** carry 4×80 A. 24 power FETs, bulk caps and 6S-rated copper need area. The thermal math in §3 shows that even 30.5×30.5 mm only holds 80 A on all four channels for about 4–5 s. | Baseline: **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz outer / 2 oz inner power planes**. The 20×20 variant is limited to roughly 35 A burst per channel. |
| F3 | "80 A burst" is interpreted as **80 A per channel, ≤5 s, all four channels simultaneously** (worst case). | All loss figures are per channel, then multiplied by 4. |
