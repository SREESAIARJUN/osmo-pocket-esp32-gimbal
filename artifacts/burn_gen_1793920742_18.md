# STM32H743 AIO 4-in-1 ESC Nano Flight Controller: Power-Stage and DMA Architecture (Job #24)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Architectural Corrections

These points shape every number in this document, so they come first.

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no sensorless 6-step BLDC commutation engine. The architecture is therefore:
   - **H743**: the flight controller. It emits **bi-directional DShot600** on 4 timer channels.
   - **4 × ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071, running AM32 or Bluejay-class firmware). Each one decodes DShot, commutates, and returns eRPM telemetry.

   All DShot traffic stays on the PCB.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board is thermally marginal.** The math in §3 shows the realistic burst duration with all four channels loaded is **about 3–5 s**. Treat 80 A as a **single-channel, ≤2 s** rating. Treat about 45–55 A as the honest 4-channel burst figure.
3. **The MOSFET and driver are reference parts.** Every value is parametric, so substitute your BOM's datasheet numbers into the equations.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths
Commutation is 6-step trapezoidal with **complementary (synchronous) PWM**, the AM32 default. During each 60° sector:
- Leg A switches: HS_A conducts for D·T, and LS_A synchronously rectifies for (1−D)·T.
- Leg B low side stays fully on.

The current therefore always passes through **exactly two channels in series**, regardless of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

The temperature dependence of R_ds(on) is:

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Evaluation at 80 A

- At T_j = 100 °C: R = 0.9 × (1 + 0.005·75) = **1.24 mΩ**
- MOSFET conduction: P = 2 × 6400 × 1.24 m = **15.9 W**
- Copper (phase pad → FET → bus, estimated 0.3 mΩ round-trip on 2 oz inner planes): P_Cu = 6400 × 0.3 m = **1.9 W**

**P_cond,ch ≈ 17.8 W per channel.**

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution
Over one electrical cycle, each of the 6 FETs carries current for about 1/3 of the time. At high eRPM the electrical period is in the ms range, which is shorter than the junction thermal time constant (tens of ms), so average power governs.

- Average conduction loss per FET: P_FET,avg ≈ 15.9 / 6 × 2 ≈ **2.65 W**. Each FET is in the conduction path 2 of 6 sectors at either full or chopped duty.
- Instantaneous peak per FET: I²R = **7.9 W**.

### 1.4 Thermal-runaway check
R_ds(on) rises with temperature, so P_cond and T_j must be solved iteratively:

$$T_j = T_{board} + P_{FET}(T_j)\cdot R_{\theta JC}$$

Stability requires:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,R_{\theta} < 1$$

Here ∂P/∂T = 2.65 × 0.005 / 1.375 ≈ 0.0096 W/K. With R_θ = 1 K/W the loop gain is about 0.01, so the junction is **stable**. The weak point is the board, not the junction (§3).
