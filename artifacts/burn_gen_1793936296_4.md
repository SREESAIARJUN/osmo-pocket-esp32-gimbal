# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have the Job #43 specification, so this design states its hardware assumptions explicitly. Every assumption is a `constexpr` or config field in the code. Change them to match your build.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 (170 MHz Cortex-M4F, single-precision FPU) |
| Motors | 3 × GBM2804-class gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5.6 Ω, L ≈ 2.0 mH |
| Encoders | 3 × AS5048A, 14-bit magnetic, SPI |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, separate SPI buses, 8 kHz ODR, data-ready driven |
| Bus | 12 V (3S) |
| Joint order | Yaw (base) → Roll → Pitch (camera) |

## 1. Timing Architecture

| Loop | Rate | Ts | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) PI ×3 | 20 kHz | 50 µs | TIM1/TIM8 update, centre-aligned ADC trigger |
| IMU acquisition + filtering | 8 kHz | 125 µs | ICM-42688 INT1 (data-ready) |
| Velocity PI ×3 | 4 kHz | 250 µs | Decimated from current ISR (÷5) |
| Attitude estimator + angle loop | 1 kHz | 1 ms | Decimated from IMU ISR (÷8) |

Each cascade stage needs enough bandwidth separation to stay stable:

$$
\omega_{c,\text{cur}} \ge 5\,\omega_{c,\text{vel}}, \qquad \omega_{c,\text{vel}} \ge 4\,\omega_{c,\text{ang}}
$$

Typical bandwidths are:
- Current loop: f_c,cur ≈ 1.5 kHz.
- Velocity loop: f_c,vel ≈ 150 Hz.
- Angle loop: f_c,ang ≈ 25 Hz.

## 2. FOC Current Loop Equations

### 2.1 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

### 2.2 Park transform

The electrical angle is θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset), where p is the number of pole pairs.

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 2.3 PMSM dq model and decoupling

The motor's dq-axis voltage equations are:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

The controller adds feedforward terms that cancel the cross-coupling and back-EMF:

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e \hat L\, i_q
$$
$$
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e(\hat L\, i_d + \hat\lambda_m)
$$

With decoupling, each axis plant becomes 1/(Ls+R). Pole-zero cancellation gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_p = \hat L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = \hat R\,\omega_c
$$
