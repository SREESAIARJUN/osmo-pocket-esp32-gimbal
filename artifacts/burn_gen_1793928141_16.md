# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A-Burst ESC: Engineering Design Package (Job #19)

**Document class:** Architecture / Derivation / Netlist, Rev A
**Scope:** Power-stage loss model (conduction + 48 kHz switching), transient thermal model, bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation under ArduPilot (ChibiOS), and the system netlist.

> **Stated assumptions.** Every number below follows from the parameter table in §1. Swap in vendor datasheet values for your selected MOSFET and re-run the equations. The structure of the model holds for any part. An 80 A per-motor burst on a nano-class (30.5 × 30.5 mm, M3) board is aggressive. The thermal section shows it is feasible only as a **time-limited burst**, not a continuous rating. A 20 × 20 mm build cannot meet this requirement, and §4.6 explains why.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation with complementary PWM, two phases are active in every 60° sector. The active-phase half-bridge alternates between the high-side FET (duty D) and the low-side synchronous FET (1 − D). The return-phase low-side FET conducts continuously.

At any instant, motor current therefore flows through **exactly two FETs in series**:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

This result is independent of D. Duty only redistributes the loss between the high-side and low-side FETs.

### 2.2 Temperature correction

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

| T_j | R_ds(T_j) | P_cond @ 80 A (per motor) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ | **17.2 W** |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W |

Worked example at 100 °C:

$$
80^2 \times 2 \times 1.34\,\text{m}\Omega = 6400 \times 2.68\times10^{-3} = 17.2\ \text{W}
$$

### 2.3 Parasitic copper loss

Model the phase trace plus pads at ≈ 0.15 mΩ per path (2 oz outer, 3 oz inner pours, stitched). Two segments per current path give:

$$
P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.30\,\text{m}\Omega = 1.9\ \text{W per motor}
$$

### 2.4 Per-FET distribution (thermal hot spot)

Each FET conducts during 120° of the 360° electrical cycle. Average conduction loss per FET is therefore about P_cond / 6 per motor.

The worst-case FET is the **low-side synchronous FET at low duty (D → 0)**, because it carries the full current for its entire 120° window:

$$
P_{FET,max} \approx \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{ds}(T_j) + P_{sw,FET} = \tfrac{1}{3}(6400)(1.34\text{m}) + \ldots \approx 2.9\ \text{W} + \text{switching}
$$

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the active half-bridge switches. That is one hard-switched commutation pair per PWM period.

### 3.1 Hard-switching overlap (V–I crossover)

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(35\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz}) = 1.69\ \text{W}
$$

### 3.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction (two edges per period)

$$
P_{dt} = V_{SD} \cdot I \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8(80)(200\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz}) = 0.61\ \text{W}
$$

### 3.3 Reverse recovery

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n}(25.2)(48\text{k}) = 0.06\ \text{W}
$$

### 3.4 Output capacitance

$$
P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n}(25.2)(48\text{k}) = 0.05\ \text{W}
$$

### 3.5 Gate drive (dissipated in driver and gate resistors, not the FETs)

$$
P_g = 2 Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2(60\text{n})(10)(48\text{k}) = 0.058\ \text{W per motor}
$$

|---|---|
| V–I overlap | 1.69 |
| Dead time | 0.61 |
