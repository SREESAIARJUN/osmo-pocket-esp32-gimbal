**Scope:** 80 A burst conduction model · 48 kHz switching model · ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA plan · reference netlists · thermal budget

## 0. Architectural Assumptions

These assumptions drive every number below.

| # | Assumption | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Two-processor architecture.** The H743 runs ArduPilot as the FC. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU (e.g., AT32F421/STM32G071 class running AM32 or BLHeli_32-style firmware). | ArduPilot does not perform motor commutation. The H743 talks to the ESCs over bidirectional DShot600. |
| A2 | 6S max: V_bus = 25.2 V. FETs rated 40 V. | 30 V FETs on 6S leave too little margin for inductive ringing. |
| A3 | Synchronous (complementary) 6-step trapezoidal PWM at f_sw = 48 kHz. | Typical AM32/BLHeli_32 configuration. |
| A4 | FET class: 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PQFN, R_ds(on),25 ≈ 1.2 mΩ @ V_gs=10 V, Q_g ≈ 30 nC, Q_gd ≈ 8 nC, Q_rr ≈ 40 nC, Q_oss ≈ 40 nC. | These values are parametric placeholders. Substitute the datasheet values of your chosen part. |
| A5 | Board: 20×20 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz on the power layers. | "Nano" form factor. |

> ⚠️ **Engineering reality check (carried through Section 3):** "80 A per channel" on a 20×20 board is achievable only as a **sub-second burst**. The math below shows this explicitly. It should be specified as such on the datasheet.
