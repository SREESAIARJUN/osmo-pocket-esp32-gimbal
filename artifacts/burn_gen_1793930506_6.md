## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Architecture Decisions

Your request leaves several parameters open. The assumptions below drive every number in this document. Change any of them and the equations in §1–§3 still apply, but the numbers must be recomputed.

| Parameter | Assumed value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal | Typical 80 A-class build |
| Burst current | 80 A per motor (phase/leg current), ≤10 s | "Burst" is defined as a transient, not continuous |
| Continuous rating target | 55 A per motor | Derived in §2.5 |
| PWM frequency | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous) | As specified |
| Commutation | Six-step trapezoidal (AM32 / BLHeli_32 class) | Standard for multirotor ESCs |
| Form factor | 36×36 mm PCB, 30.5×30.5 mm mounting | See the warning below |
| MOSFET | 40 V, R_DS(on) ≈ 0.9 mΩ typ @ 25 °C, 5×6 mm DFN class | Representative values, flagged ⚠ wherever used |
| Copper | 8-layer, 2 oz outer / 2 oz power inner | Required for current density |

> **⚠ Form-factor warning.** "Nano" usually means a 20×20 mm mount. **An 80 A burst on 20×20 is not physically supportable.** §2 shows that the 4-in-1 dissipates about 85 W at 80 A on all four channels. That cannot be spread from 12 MOSFET footprints on a ~400 mm² board without immediate thermal runaway. This design therefore uses a 30.5 mm mount. If 20×20 is mandatory, derate the burst rating to about 40 A, or split the design into a stacked FC + ESC.
