## 0. Assumptions and Key Findings

**Architecture.** A "4-in-1 ESC flight controller" is not a single MCU. The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates DShot. Four (or one quad-capable) ESC MCUs run AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, which owns commutation and the 48 kHz PWM. This document models both domains.

**Key findings to escalate:**

1. **80 A per channel on a nano (20×20 mm mount, ~25×25 mm) board is a seconds-scale burst rating, not a continuous one.** With all four channels at 80 A, the board heats at roughly 28 K/s (Section 3).
2. **5×6 mm FETs may not fit a nano layout.** If the layout forces 3.3×3.3 mm FETs, R_ds(on) is typically 1.5 to 2 mΩ, and conduction loss rises about 1.5 to 2×.
3. **Input capacitor ripple current is the hidden killer** (Section 2.6).

## 1. Commutation Model

The ESC uses six-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In each 60° sector:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) FET conducts for duty D. The low-side (LS) FET of the same leg synchronously rectifies for 1 − D.
- **Sink leg:** the LS FET is on continuously.
- **Third leg:** floating, used for back-EMF sensing.
