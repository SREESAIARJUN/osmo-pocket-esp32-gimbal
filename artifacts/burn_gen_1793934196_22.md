# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80A-Burst 4-in-1 ESC: System Model (Job #18)

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** In a 4-in-1 stack, each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). That MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and six MOSFETs. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to the four ESC MCUs. The H743 timer/DMA allocation (Section 4) is therefore decoupled from the 48 kHz power-stage PWM (Sections 1–3).

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
In 6-step mode with synchronous rectification, exactly **two MOSFETs carry I_m at every instant**:
- **PWM on-time:** high-side (HS) of leg A plus low-side (LS) of leg B.
- **PWM off-time:** LS of leg A (sync-rect freewheel) plus LS of leg B.

The DC bus current and phase current are related by:

$$I_{dc} = D \cdot I_m$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /\text{K}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.5 mΩ × 1.5 = **2.25 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2\, I_m^2\, R_{ds}(T_j) = 2 (89)^2 (2.25\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{35.6\ W}$$

Correction: using I_m = 89 A rather than 80 A, 2 × 7921 × 2.25 mΩ = **35.6 W**.

Sharing among the six FETs:
- Averaged over an electrical cycle, each FET carries **≈ 5.9 W**.
- During its active 120° segment, the static LS FET dissipates the full I_m²R ≈ 17.8 W.
- At typical electrical frequencies (1–3 kHz), the die thermal mass averages this to near the mean. Use the mean for steady state and the peak for the junction-ripple check (Section 3.4).

### 1.4 Parasitic copper losses (per channel)

| Element | R | P = I²R |
|---|---|---|
| Phase current-sense shunt | 0.25 mΩ | 80² × 0.25 m = 1.6 W |
| Power-plane / trace / via path (bus + phase) | ~0.4 mΩ | 89² × 0.4 m ≈ 3.2 W |
| **Subtotal** | | **≈ 4.8 W** |

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches. Each PWM cycle has one hard turn-on and one hard turn-off of the HS FET. The complementary LS FET soft-switches after its body diode conducts during the dead time.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_m (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(89)(30\text{ ns})(48\text{ kHz}) = \mathbf{1.62\ W}$$

Edge-time estimate from gate charge: t ≈ Q_gd / I_g = 8 nC / 1.5 A ≈ 5 ns intrinsic. The 15 ns figure includes R_g and loop-inductance damping.

### 2.2 Body-diode reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{ nC}\times 25.2 \times 48\text{ k} = 0.036\ \text{W}$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{ nC}\times25.2\times48\text{ k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$
