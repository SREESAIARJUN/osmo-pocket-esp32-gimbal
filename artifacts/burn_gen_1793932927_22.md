# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Model, DShot Timing, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors directly. It sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (AT32F421 running AM32 is assumed). Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and six N-channel MOSFETs, for 24 FETs in total.

**Operating point.**

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage | V_bus | 25.2 V (6S full charge). Design FETs for at least 40 V; 60 V gives more margin against ringing |
| Burst phase current | I_b | 80 A per motor |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| Board | n/a | 37 × 40 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz copper on all layers, 30.5 mm mounting pattern |
| MOSFET | n/a | Generic 5×6 DFN. Values below are placeholders; substitute datasheet values |

**MOSFET placeholder parameters:**

| t_r, t_f | 25 ns each |
| V_SD | 0.8 V |
| R_θJC | 1.0 K/W |

**Feasibility verdict.** 80 A per motor is a burst rating only on a board this size. The thermal model in §3 limits it to about 2 s, with roughly 30 A continuous under prop-wash cooling. Any "80 A" marketing figure should state burst duration.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In each 60° sector:
- **PWM leg:** the high side conducts for D·T, and the low side conducts as a synchronous rectifier for (1−D)·T.
- **Return leg:** the low side is held fully on.

So two FET channels carry the phase current I at every instant, regardless of duty cycle.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.2 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.8 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.8\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{23.0\ W}$$

### 1.4 Copper path loss

The current path includes the bus, phase pad, and solder joints. Estimate R_cu ≈ 0.5 mΩ per channel (8 layers of 2 oz, about 10 mm path):

$$P_{cu} = I^2 R_{cu} = 6400 \times 0.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{3.2\ W}$$

### 1.5 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts during 120° of the electrical cycle. The high-side FET carries D·I² R for that interval, and the low-side FET of the same leg carries the remainder.

- **At high eRPM**, losses average over all six FETs: about 23 / 6 ≈ 3.8 W each.
- **At low eRPM or stall** (punch-out from hover, a desync), the commutation period exceeds the thermal time constant of the die (about 10–50 ms). One FET pair then carries the full loss for the whole sector: up to I² R ≈ **11.5 W per FET**. This is the real burst killer.
