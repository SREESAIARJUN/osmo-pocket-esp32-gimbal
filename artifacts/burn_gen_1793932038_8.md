# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model, Job #49

**Document scope:** loss model for an 80 A burst, 48 kHz switching analysis, transient thermal model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA plan, and reference netlists.

**Status:** engineering baseline. Every component value tagged **[VERIFY]** must be checked against the chosen part's datasheet before layout release.

---

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Finding

An STM32H743 does not commutate the motors directly. ArduPilot does not run 4× sensorless 6-step FOC/trapezoidal loops. The correct partition is:

```
┌──────────────── STM32H743 (ArduPilot FC) ────────────────┐
│ IMU (SPI), Baro (I2C/SPI), OSD, SD (SDMMC), UARTs        │
│ TIMx CH1..4 ──► DShot600 bidir (1-wire per motor) ──┐    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┼────┘
                                                      ▼
┌──────── 4-in-1 ESC stage (same PCB stack or AIO) ─────────┐
│ 4× ESC MCU (AM32 on AT32F421 / STM32G071)  [or 1 MCU/2ch] │
│ each: 3-ph gate driver (e.g., FD6288Q) → 6 N-MOSFETs      │
│ PWM 48 kHz complementary, dead-time, BEMF comparators     │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Key finding (stated up front):** on a "nano" footprint (20×20 mm or 25.5×25.5 mm), **80 A per motor is not a continuous rating. It is a burst lasting roughly 1–3 s.** Section 3 derives this. If marketing requires "80 A", define it as a 1 s burst at a stated ambient and airflow, or the board will fail field returns.

## 2. Loss Model (Per Motor Channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Topology

With 6-step commutation and complementary (synchronous) PWM, exactly two legs carry I_m at any instant:

- **Leg A** alternates between the high-side FET (duty D) and the synchronous low-side FET (1 − D).
- **Leg B** holds its low-side FET on continuously.

Over one commutation, the RMS-weighted conduction path is always two R_ds in series, regardless of D (ignoring dead time):

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_m^2\, R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

Each of the 6 FETs conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period, so the per-FET average is:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \tfrac{2}{6}\,I_m^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Hot-spot FETs instantaneously carry the full I_m²R. This matters because the thermal time constant of a die (~1–10 ms) is comparable to the commutation period at low eRPM.

### 2.2 Switching Losses (Hard-Switched Leg Only)

Only the PWM'd leg switches. The low-side sync FET turns on after dead time (ZVS-like), so its switching loss is mostly diode-related.

| Term | Equation | Value @ baseline |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½ V_bus · I_m · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw | ½·25.2·80·22e-9·48e3 = **1.064 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw | 30e-9·25.2·48e3 = **0.036 W** |
| C_oss | ½ C_oss · V_bus² · f_sw | 0.5·1.2e-9·635·48e3 = **0.018 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V_f · I_m · 2 t_dt · f_sw | 0.85·80·200e-9·48e3 = **0.653 W** |
| Gate drive (dissipated in driver/R_g) | 6 · Q_g · V_drv · f_sw (upper bound) | 6·28e-9·10·48e3 = **0.081 W** |

$$P_{sw} \approx 1.77\ \text{W per motor}$$

The gate-drive term is an upper bound: only one leg switches per sector, so the real figure is about 1/3 of it.

**Sensitivity:** P_sw scales linearly with f_sw. Dropping to 24 kHz saves ~0.88 W per motor at 80 A, but increases current ripple and audible noise. At 80 A burst, conduction loss dominates by roughly 10×, so 48 kHz is acceptable.

### 2.3 Electrothermal Iteration (Conduction Is Temperature-Dependent)

Solve P_cond(T_j) and T_j = T_ref + P·Z_th self-consistently. At the end of the burst, take T_j ≈ 105 °C:

$$R_{ds}(105) = 1.6\text{m}\Omega\,[1+0.0055\cdot80] = 2.30\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond} = 2\cdot 80^2 \cdot 2.30\text{e-}3 = 29.4\ \text{W per motor}$$

Cold start (25 °C): 2·6400·1.6e-3 = **20.5 W**.

### 2.4 Copper and Shunt Losses (Often Forgotten, Not Negligible)

| Element | R (est.) | P at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase-pad + trace copper (2 oz, 4 parallel layers), per path | 0.3 mΩ ×2 | 3.8 W |
| Current-sense shunt (if per motor; usually one shunt for 4-in-1) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| Battery pad → bus plane | 0.15 mΩ (shared, 320 A!) | 15 W at 4×80 A |

### 2.5 Total at Burst (All 4 Motors at 80 A, End of Burst)

| Source | Per motor | ×4 |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (hot) | 29.4 W | 117.6 W |
| Switching + diode | 1.8 W | 7.1 W |
| Phase copper | 3.8 W | 15.4 W |
| Bus/battery pad (shared) | — | 15.4 W |
| **Total** | | **≈155 W** |

Input power ≈ 25.2 V × 320 A × D ≈ 7.7 kW, so efficiency is ≈ 98% electrically. Thermally, however, 155 W into a ~3 g PCB is the binding constraint.
