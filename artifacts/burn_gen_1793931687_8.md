## 0. Assumptions and Architectural Corrections

Three points shape everything below.

1. **The H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** In every production AIO design, each power stage is commutated by a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to those MCUs. Accordingly:
   - **48 kHz is the ESC-side PWM frequency**, set in the AM32 configuration.
   - **The DShot DMA timers live on the H743.**
