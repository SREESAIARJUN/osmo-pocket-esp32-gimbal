## 0. Scope and Assumptions

### 0.1 System architecture

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and acts as the flight controller. It does not commutate motors directly. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32, which handles 6-step commutation at 48 kHz PWM. The H743 talks to those MCUs over bi-directional DShot600. This is the only architecture ArduPilot supports for bi-directional DShot, and it is how every production FC/ESC stack is built.
