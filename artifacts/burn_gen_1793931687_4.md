## 0. Architecture Clarification and Baseline Assumptions

On any practical "STM32H743 4-in-1" board, the H743 does **not** commutate the FETs. Each ESC channel has its own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 running ArduPilot only:

- sends bidirectional DShot600 to the four ESC MCUs,
- decodes the eRPM telemetry they return, and
- reads current and voltage on its ADCs.

The model below follows that split.
