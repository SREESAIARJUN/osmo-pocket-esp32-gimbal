## 0. Architecture Assumptions and an Early Feasibility Warning

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Form factor | 20×20 mm mounting (≈26×26 mm board), 8-layer, 2 oz outer and 1 oz inner copper |
| Battery | 6S LiPo: 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal; MOSFETs rated 40 V |
| Flight controller | STM32H743 at 400 MHz (ArduPilot default clock), timer kernel clock 200 MHz |
| ESC control | **4 independent ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). The H743 does **not** commutate motors; it sends bidirectional DShot600. |
| PWM | 48 kHz complementary (synchronous rectification) 6-step trapezoidal drive |
| Burst rating | **80 A per channel**, defined here as ≤ 1 to 4 s (derived in §3) |
| MOSFET | ⚠ 40 V, 3.3×3.3 DFN, R_DS(on) = 1.5 mΩ at 25 °C and V_GS = 10 V, Q_g = 40 nC, Q_gd = 8 nC, Q_gs2 = 3 nC, Q_rr = 30 nC, Q_oss = 40 nC, R_θJC = 1.5 K/W |
| Gate driver | ⚠ 3-phase half-bridge driver, 10 V gate drive, ≈1.5 A source / 2 A sink |

> **Feasibility warning (the math is in §3).** Four channels at 80 A on a 20×20 board dissipate about 140 W. The board's thermal mass gives roughly **1 s** before copper and junction limits are reached. "80A burst" is therefore defensible only as a **single-channel or sub-second** rating. A spec of 4×80 A sustained is not physically achievable at this size. Marketing claims should be defined against the transient model below.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

In any commutation sector, two phases conduct:
- The **PWM leg** alternates between the high side (fraction D) and the low side in synchronous rectification (fraction 1−D, minus dead time).
- The **return leg** low side is on for the whole sector.

The bridge conduction loss per channel, at phase current I_ph, is:

$$
P_{cond,bridge} = I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot R_{DS}(T_j)\,\big[D + (1-D-2t_d f_{sw}) + 1\big] \approx 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Ripple correction

The PWM ripple at 48 kHz, with phase-to-phase inductance L_pp, is:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{pp}\,f_{sw}}, \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I_{avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}
$$

The worst case is D = 0.5. With ⚠ L_pp = 15 µH (typical for a 2207 to 2810 class motor) and V = 25.2 V:

ΔI = 25.2 × 0.25 / (15 µH × 48 kHz) = **8.75 A p-p**, so ΔI²/12 = 6.4 A².

At 80 A this changes I² by only 0.1%, so it is negligible at burst. The correction matters only at light load.

### 1.3 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.5 mΩ × 1.6 = **2.4 mΩ**.

### 1.4 PCB copper loss (often omitted, and significant here)

$$
R_{cu} = \frac{\rho_{cu}(T)\,\ell}{w\,t}, \quad \rho_{cu}(100^\circ C)=2.2\times10^{-8}\ \Omega\cdot m
$$

Assume ⚠ an effective 0.3 mΩ per channel. This covers pad, vias, and phase pour from the battery pad through the bridge to the motor pad, across 2 oz outer and 3 inner planes in parallel.

### 1.5 Numbers at the 80 A burst, T_j = 125 °C

| Term | Expression | Value per channel |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFET conduction | 2 × 80² × 2.4 mΩ | **30.7 W** |
| Copper conduction | 80² × 0.3 mΩ | **1.9 W** |

The electrical commutation rotates load among the 6 FETs. Thermal ripple at commutation frequency is negligible above about 1 k eRPM. The **average per FET** is therefore 30.7 / 6 ≈ **5.1 W**.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Gate-charge-based transition times

Take V_plateau ≈ 3.0 V, total gate resistance (driver plus external) R_g,on = 5 Ω, R_g,off = 4 Ω, and V_drv = 10 V.

$$
I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{g,on}} = 1.4\ \text{A}, \qquad I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{g,off}} = 0.75\ \text{A}
$$

$$
t_{on} \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = 7.9\ \text{ns}, \qquad t_{off} \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,off}} = 14.7\ \text{ns}
$$
