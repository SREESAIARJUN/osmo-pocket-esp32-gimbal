## 0. Assumptions and System Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 (Cortex-M4F, 170 MHz, FPU, CORDIC) |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs $p = 7$), $R_s = 5.6\,\Omega$ phase, $L_s = 1.9$ mH, $K_t = 0.075$ N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (MT6701 / AS5048A) over SPI, one per axis |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI, gyro ODR 8 kHz, ±2000 dps, accel ±16 g |
| Load inertia | $J_{pitch} = 2.0\times10^{-4}$, $J_{roll} = 3.5\times10^{-4}$, $J_{yaw} = 6.0\times10^{-4}$ kg·m² |
| Bus voltage | $V_{dc} = 16.8$ V (4S) |

**Loop scheduling (cascaded, integer-ratio, phase-locked to PWM):**

```
PWM / ADC trigger 20 kHz ─► Current loop (FOC PI, SVPWM, cogging FF)   T_i = 50 µs
                     ÷5 ─► Velocity loop (PI on gyro rate)             T_v = 250 µs (4 kHz)
                     ÷4 ─► Angle loop (P + FF on IMU attitude)          T_a = 1 ms   (1 kHz)
IMU DRDY 8 kHz          ─► Dual-IMU fusion + notch/LPF → decimate to 4 kHz
```

**Signal flow per axis:**

$$
\theta^* \xrightarrow{\;\text{angle P + FF}\;} \omega^* \xrightarrow{\;\text{velocity PI}\;} i_q^* + i_{q,cog} \xrightarrow{\;\text{FOC PI}\;} v_{dq} \xrightarrow{\;\text{SVPWM}\;} \text{inverter}
$$

- **Feedback:** angle and rate come from the fused camera IMU, projected onto each joint axis. The encoder supplies electrical angle (commutation), the cogging phase, and a fallback velocity.

## 1. Field-Oriented Current Control

### 1.1 Transforms

Use amplitude-invariant Clarke with 2-shunt or 3-shunt sensing, where $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off})$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

Inverse Park:

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

**Delay compensation.** The PWM update lands roughly $1.5\,T_i$ after sampling. Use the predicted angle in inverse Park:

$$
\theta_e^{pwm} = \theta_e + 1.5\,T_i\,\hat\omega_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI design (pole-zero cancellation)

The dq plant is:

$$
L_s\dot i_d = v_d - R_s i_d + \omega_e L_s i_q
$$

$$
L_s\dot i_q = v_q - R_s i_q - \omega_e L_s i_d - \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

Per axis this is $G_i(s) = \dfrac{1}{L_s s + R_s}$. Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole $R_s/L_s$, giving a closed loop of $\omega_{ci}/(s+\omega_{ci})$:

$$
K_{p,i} = L_s\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R_s\,\omega_{ci}
$$

**Bandwidth constraint:** $\omega_{ci} \le \dfrac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{20}$.

- At 20 kHz: $\omega_{ci} = 2\pi\cdot 1000 = 6283$ rad/s.
- This gives $K_{p,i} = 11.9$ V/A and $K_{i,i} = 35{,}186$ V/(A·s).

**Decoupling feedforward:**

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\hat\omega_e L_s i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \hat\omega_e (L_s i_d + \lambda_m),\qquad \lambda_m = \frac{2K_t}{3p}
$$

### 1.3 Discrete PI with back-calculation anti-windup

$$
e_k = r_k - y_k
$$

$$
u_k^{\text{unsat}} = K_p e_k + I_k + u^{ff}_k
$$

$$
u_k = \text{sat}(u_k^{\text{unsat}})
$$

$$
I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T e_k + K_{aw}\,T\,(u_k - u_k^{\text{unsat}}),\qquad K_{aw} = \frac{K_i}{K_p}
