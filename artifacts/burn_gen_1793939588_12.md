## 0. Architecture Assumptions

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot. It does not commutate the motors. ArduPilot has no ESC commutation firmware, and one H743 cannot practically close six-step/FOC loops for 4 motors at 48 kHz while also running the flight stack. The architecture is therefore:
