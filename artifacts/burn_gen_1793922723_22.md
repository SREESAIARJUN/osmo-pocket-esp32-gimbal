# Job #29: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC Architecture Package

**Scope:** 80 A burst loss and thermal model, 48 kHz switching loss model, ArduPilot bi-directional DShot600 timer and DMA plan, and reference netlists.

**How to use this package:** Every number below comes from stated assumptions. Before layout freeze, replace the MOSFET and driver parameters in §1 with values from your selected vendors' datasheets and re-run the equations. Expect corrections of ±20–30%.

---

## 0. System Partitioning

A 4-in-1 ESC does not run its commutation loop on the H743. The architecture is split:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 at 480 MHz | ArduPilot, DShot600 bidirectional master, telemetry decode |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (AM32) or EFM8BB21 (BLHeli_S/Bluejay) | 6-step commutation, **48 kHz complementary PWM** |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap, 20 V) | Half-bridge drive |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET, 40 V, 3.3×3.3 PQFN | 4 motors × 3 legs × 2 FETs |
| Form factor | 20×20 mm stack (M2 holes), 6-layer, 2 oz outer and 1 oz inner | "Nano" class |

**Electrical assumptions:**
- Bus: 6S Li-ion/LiPo, 25.2 V max and 22.2 V nominal.
- Burst: 80 A phase current per motor.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step, Synchronous Rectification)

In 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary PWM, each 60° sector works as follows:

- One leg runs PWM: the high side conducts for D, and the low side synchronously rectifies for 1−D.
- One leg holds its low side fully on.
- One leg floats (BEMF sensing).

Over one electrical cycle, each leg spends 120° in each role.

**Per-FET duty factors:**

$$k_{HS}=\frac{D}{3},\qquad k_{LS}=\frac{(1-D)+1}{3}=\frac{2-D}{3}$$

**Per-motor conduction loss.** Two FETs are always in series with the phase current:

$$P_{cond,motor}=2\,I^2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,LS}=\frac{2-D}{3}I^2R_{DS},\qquad P_{cond,HS}=\frac{D}{3}I^2R_{DS}$$

**At I = 80 A, T_j = 100 °C (R = 1.305 mΩ):**

- I²R = 6400 × 1.305 mΩ = **8.35 W**
- P_cond,motor = **16.7 W**

| D | P_HS | P_LS |
|---|---|---|
| 1.0 | 2.78 W | 2.78 W |
| 0.5 | 1.39 W | **4.18 W** (worst FET) |

The low-side FET is the thermal hot spot at partial throttle with high current, which occurs during punch-outs on heavy props.

**Copper loss (add):**
- Phase pad to FET bus: about 0.25 mΩ per path, giving 2 × 6400 × 0.25 m = **3.2 W per motor**.
- Shared V_BAT and GND planes: about 0.15 mΩ at 4 × 80 A = 320 A, giving **15.4 W board-wide**.

The board-wide figure assumes battery pads at the board edge and 2 oz planes. It is dominated by high-current paths, so pour both outer layers.
