# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion

I don't have the spec for "Job #2," so this design assumes a typical brushless gimbal. Section 0 lists every assumed value. Each one appears as a single parameter in the code, so you can substitute your hardware's numbers directly.

---

## 0. Assumptions

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3 × gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 5–10 Ω, L ≈ 2–4 mH |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A-class), SPI, read every PWM cycle |
| MCU | STM32G4/H7-class with FPU, center-aligned PWM at 20 kHz, inline/low-side current sense |
| IMU A | ICM-42688-P on the **camera/payload plate**. Primary attitude and rate feedback. |
| IMU B | ICM-42688-P on the **base/handle**. Vibration reference and disturbance feedforward. |
| IMU config | ODR 8 kHz, gyro ±1000 dps, accel ±8 g, on-chip AAF on, UI filter set to low latency |
| Gimbal order | Base → Yaw (Z) → Roll (X) → Pitch (Y) → camera |

### Loop rates

| Loop | Rate | Target bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | ≈ 1.5 kHz |
| Velocity / rate | 4 kHz | ≈ 80–150 Hz |
| Angle / attitude | 1 kHz | ≈ 10–25 Hz |
| IMU filtering and NLMS cancellation | 8 kHz (decimated to 4 kHz) | — |
| AHRS fusion | 1 kHz | — |

Keep a bandwidth separation of at least 5–10× between successive loops. This lets each inner loop be treated as unity gain when tuning the loop outside it.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

Here θ_off is found at calibration by applying a fixed d-axis voltage and reading the encoder once the rotor settles.

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms

Balanced three-phase currents (i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.3 Motor model in the dq frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q
$$

$$
v_q = R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

$$
\tau = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$

### 1.4 Current PI with decoupling and back-calculation anti-windup

Pole–zero cancellation tuning, with ω_c = 2π·f_c,i:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c
$$

This makes the closed current loop first order:

$$
\frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

Discrete form (forward Euler, sample time T_s = 1/20 kHz):

$$
e_k = i^*_k - i_k
$$

$$
u_k = K_p e_k + I_k + v_{\text{ff}}
$$

$$
v_d^{\text{ff}} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{\text{ff}} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

$$
I_{k+1} = I_k + T_s\big[K_i e_k + K_{aw}(\text{sat}(u_k) - u_k)\big],\qquad K_{aw} \approx K_i / K_p
$$

**Voltage vector limit.** Limit the dq vector to the inscribed circle of the SVPWM hexagon, |v| ≤ V_bus/√3. Give v_d priority:

$$
v_d \leftarrow \text{clamp}(v_d, \pm V_{\max})
$$

$$
v_q \leftarrow \text{clamp}\!\left(v_q, \pm\sqrt{V_{\max}^2 - v_d^2}\right)
$$

### 1.5 Inverse Park transform and SVPWM (min–max zero-sequence injection)

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

$$
v_a = v_\alpha,\qquad v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\qquad v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_a,v_b,v_c) + \min(v_a,v_b,v_c)\big)
$$

$$
D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{\text{bus}}}
$$
