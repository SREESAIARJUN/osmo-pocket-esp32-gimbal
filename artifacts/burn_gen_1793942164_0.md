## 0. Architectural Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the MOSFETs. In every viable 4-in-1 design, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The design divides into two domains:

| Domain | Owner | This document |
|---|---|---|
| 48 kHz PWM, commutation, gate drive | ESC MCU ×4 + 3-phase gate driver ×4 | §1–§4 (loss and thermal model) |
| Motor command and RPM telemetry | STM32H743 via bidirectional DShot600 | §5 (timer/DMA allocation) |

Both domains are delivered here.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path

In 6-step commutation, current always flows through exactly two switch positions in series. One is the PWM leg, where the high-side and low-side FETs alternate under synchronous rectification. The other is the static leg, held on. Neglecting dead time, the conducting path resistance is always 2·R_DS(on), **independent of duty cycle**:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period, so the average loss per FET is:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I_{ph}^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)}{3}$$
