## 0. Scope, Assumptions and a Key Finding

### 0.1 Architecture

In an "H743 4-in-1" all-in-one (AIO) board, the STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32). These MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The model below covers both domains.

### 0.3 Key Finding (Read Before Layout)

**80 A on a nano-format PCB is a time-limited burst, not a continuous rating.**

- With all four motors at burst, total dissipation is about 123 W.
- The adiabatic board slew is about 26 K/s.
- Endurance is about 1.6 s with all four motors loaded, or about 6 s with a single motor.

The derivation is in §3.
