**Feasibility warning.** A true "nano" footprint (20×20 mm mounting) cannot handle 4 × 80 A thermally, even in burst. Section 3 quantifies this. Treat 80 A as a **per-channel, short-burst, not-all-four-simultaneously** rating, or move to 30.5×30.5 mm with 5×6 FETs. The board as specified is good for roughly 25–30 A continuous per motor.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

R_ds(on)(T_j) = R_25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 110 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × (1 + 0.0055 × 85) = **1.76 mΩ**.

Add about 0.15 mΩ of package, solder, and copper parasitics per switch, giving **R_eff ≈ 1.91 mΩ**.
