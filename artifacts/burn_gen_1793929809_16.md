## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does not drive MOSFETs directly. The partitioning is as follows:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (ArduPilot) | Generates bidirectional DShot600 on 4 timer channels and decodes eRPM telemetry |
| ESC MCU (×4, or 1× multi-channel) | e.g. AT32F421 / STM32G071 (AM32 or BLHeli_32) | Commutation, 48 kHz PWM, dead-time, current limiting |
| Gate drive | 3-phase half-bridge driver ×4 (e.g. FD6288Q-class) ⟨A⟩ | 10 V gate drive with bootstrap |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET, 40 V | 3 half-bridges per motor |

The 48 kHz switching frequency is an ESC firmware setting (AM32 "PWM frequency"). It is not an H743 parameter.

**Electrical envelope ⟨A⟩:**
- Supply: 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal. Use 40 V FETs; 30 V parts are inadequate for 6S regen and ringing.
- Board: 30.5 × 30.5 mm 4-in-1, 6-layer, 2 oz copper on all layers.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Commutation Duty Factors

The model assumes trapezoidal 6-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM at duty D. Over one electrical cycle, each phase leg spends:

- **1/3 as the PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) conducts for fraction D and the low-side (LS) synchronous rectifier conducts for (1 − D).
- **1/3 as the return leg:** the LS is fully on.
- **1/3 floating:** BEMF sensing, no conduction.

The resulting per-FET conduction duty factors are:

$$\delta_{HS} = \frac{D}{3}, \qquad \delta_{LS} = \frac{1-D}{3} + \frac{1}{3} = \frac{2-D}{3}$$

### 2.2 Per-FET and Per-Motor Loss

Per-FET conduction loss, with I as the phase current (≈ DC during burst, ripple neglected):

$$P_{cond,HS} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\frac{D}{3}, \qquad P_{cond,LS} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\frac{2-D}{3}$$

Summing over all six FETs of one motor gives a result independent of D. Two FETs are always in series with the current:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 3\left[I^2R\frac{D}{3} + I^2R\frac{2-D}{3}\right] = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$
