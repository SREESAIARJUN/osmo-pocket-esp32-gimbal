## 0. Design Assumptions

Adjust these to your hardware. Every equation is parameterized, so only the constants change.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, e.g. GM4108: 12N14P, p = 7 pole pairs, R = 5.6 Ω, L = 2.4 mH, Kt ≈ 0.11 N·m/A |
| Cogging period | N_c = LCM(N_slots, 2p) = LCM(12, 14) = 84 cycles/rev |
| Encoder | AS5048A/MT6835, 14–21 bit absolute, read every PWM cycle |
