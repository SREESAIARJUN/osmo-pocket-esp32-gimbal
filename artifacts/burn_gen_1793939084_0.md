## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |

| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 2–5 mH |

```

Attitude estimator          1 kHz

```

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle is computed from the mechanical angle, the pole-pair count, and the calibrated encoder offset:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant and Decoupling

The surface-PMSM model in the rotating frame is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

Adding feedforward decoupling cancels the cross-coupling and back-EMF terms:

$$v_d^{*} = u_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{*} = u_q + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

After decoupling, each axis reduces to a first-order plant:

$$G(s) = \frac{1}{Ls + R}$$

### 1.3 Current PI Gains by Pole–Zero Cancellation

The PI controller is:

$$C(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s}$$

Choose $K_i / K_p = R/L$ so the PI zero cancels the plant pole. The open loop then becomes $K_p/(Ls)$, and the closed loop is first order with bandwidth $\omega_c$:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

**Stability limit.** Computation plus PWM update adds a delay of about $1.5\,T_s$. Keep $\omega_c \le \dfrac{1}{3 \cdot 1.5\,T_s}$. At 20 kHz this gives $\omega_c \le 2\pi \cdot 1.77$ kHz.

**Torque constant** (amplitude-invariant transform): $\tau = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q$.
