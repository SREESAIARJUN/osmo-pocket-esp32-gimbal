I don't have the Job #11 spec sheet, so every hardware number below is an **explicit, parametrized assumption**. All of them live in one `constexpr` config block. Replace them with your measured values before tuning.

|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 class (Cortex-M4F with FPU, 170 MHz) |

| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701 class), one per joint |

| Disturbance | Drone propeller vibration in the 80–400 Hz range, frequency time-varying |

## 2. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop, 20 kHz)

### 2.1 Electrical Angle

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

The offset θ_off is found at startup. Apply V_d along α (θ_e = 0), let the rotor settle, and record θ_m.

### 2.2 Clarke Transform (Amplitude-Invariant, Balanced Currents)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$
