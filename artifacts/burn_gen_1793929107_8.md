# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

## 0. Assumptions and Scope

I don't have the Job #70 requirements, so this model uses the parameters below. **Replace them with your actual BOM values before you trust any number.** The equations are general. The numbers are only as good as the inputs.

**Architecture clarification.** The STM32H743 cannot generate the 48 kHz commutation PWM. Four motors need 24 gate signals, closed-loop commutation, and ArduPilot running on the same core, which is not workable. The standard partition is:

- **H743 (ArduPilot):** sends DShot600 commands and receives bidirectional eRPM telemetry.
- **4× ESC MCUs** (e.g., STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32): generate the 48 kHz PWM, drive a 3-phase gate driver, and switch 6 MOSFETs per motor.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Temperature-dependent on-resistance:**

R_DS(T_j) = R₂₅ · [1 + α(T_j − 25)], with α ≈ 0.005 /K, so R_DS(120 °C) ≈ 1.5 mΩ.

**Current path.** Trapezoidal 6-step drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM has one high-side leg chopping and one low-side leg held on.

- During D: current flows through HS_a, then LS_b.
- During (1 − D): current flows through LS_a (synchronous rectification), then LS_b.

Two R_DS(on) are always in the path, independent of duty:

> **P_cond,motor = 2 · I² · R_DS(T_j)**

**At 80 A, T_j = 120 °C:**

P_cond = 2 × 80² × 1.5 mΩ = **19.2 W per motor**

**Copper and interconnect.** Add roughly 0.3–0.5 mΩ per path for copper, vias, and pads. At 0.4 mΩ:

80² × 0.4 mΩ = **+2.6 W per motor**, dissipated in the PCB itself.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the chopping high-side FET has hard-switched V–I overlap. The low-side FET switches at near zero voltage, aside from dead-time diode conduction.

| Term | Equation | Value per motor |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw = ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 30 ns × 48 kHz | 1.45 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_SD · I · 2t_dt · f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 kHz | 0.61 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw = 40 nC × 25.2 × 48 kHz | 0.05 W |
| C_oss | ≈ ½ · Q_oss · V · f_sw | 0.03 W |
| Gate drive (2 FETs switching) | 2 · Q_g · V_GS · f_sw | 0.06 W* |
| **Total P_sw** | | **≈ 2.2 W** |

\*Gate drive power is dissipated mainly in the driver and gate resistors, not the FETs.
