# STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #20): Power-Stage, Thermal and DShot Architecture

## 0. Feasibility Summary

Read this section before the detailed models.

| Item | Finding |
|---|---|
| **80A burst, all 4 channels** | Total power is 320A × 22.2V ≈ 7.1 kW. Dissipation is about 85 W on a 30.5×30.5 mm board. The board has no steady-state solution. It is limited to about **2–3 s** from a 40 °C start (§3.4). |
| **80A burst, single channel** | About **10–12 s**. Treat "80A" as a *single-channel, ≤10 s* rating and publish it that way. |
| **Topology** | The H743 is the flight controller. Commutation runs on **four dedicated ESC MCUs** (AM32-class, e.g. STM32G071). The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to them. Driving 24 gates directly from the H743 is not viable for 48 kHz complementary PWM with per-channel back-EMF ZC detection. |
| **Form factor** | Use a 30.5×30.5 mm stack with 3.3×3.3 DFN FETs. A 20×20 "nano" layout cannot carry 80A burst on any channel. |

**Parameter disclosure.** The device parameters below are representative values for a 40 V, 3.3×3.3 DFN, ≤1 mΩ MOSFET class. They do not come from a specific part. Substitute your selected datasheet values into the equations. The structure of every equation stays the same.
