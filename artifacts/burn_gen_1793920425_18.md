# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #36): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan, Netlists

## 0. Feasibility Findings and Assumption Register

Three findings shape the rest of this document.

1. **The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC.** The 48 kHz motor PWM runs on four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 running AM32, assumed). The H743 generates bidirectional DShot600 only. Any spec that has the H743 commutating motors should be rejected.
2. **80 A per channel cannot be sustained on a nano (20×20 or 25×25) board.** The model below shows about **100 W total dissipation**. At that load, 80 A is a **burst rating of roughly 1.5–2.5 s** at most. A realistic continuous rating is 25–35 A per channel. Marketing should state "80 A burst (≤2 s)".
3. **Four motors at 80 A means 320 A of battery current.** That exceeds any nano-board power entry (XT60 ≈ 60 A continuous) and the copper on the board. Burst is limited by the battery and the pads before the FETs.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation geometry
With 6-step trapezoidal drive and synchronous rectification, each FET carries phase current for 1/3 of the electrical period. At every instant, exactly one high-side and one low-side path conduct, either through the active FET or its complementary sync-rect partner.

### 1.2 Per-FET conduction loss

$$R_{ds,hot}=k_T R_{ds,25}=1.5\times1.2\,\text{m}\Omega=1.8\,\text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,FET}=\tfrac13\,I_{rms}^2 R_{ds,hot}=\tfrac13(80^2)(1.8\times10^{-3})=\mathbf{3.84\ W}$$

Ripple correction: assume motor phase inductance L = 20 µH and the worst-case duty D = 0.5.

$$\Delta I_{pp}=\frac{V_{bus}D(1-D)}{L f_{sw}}=\frac{25.2\cdot0.25}{20\mu\cdot48k}=6.6\,\text{A}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2+\Delta I^2/12 = 6400+3.6$$

The ripple adds 0.06 %, which is negligible.

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch}=2\,I^2R_{ds,hot}=2(6400)(1.8\text{m})=\mathbf{23.0\ W}$$

### 1.4 PCB copper loss
For 2 oz copper (70 µm), the sheet resistance is ρ/t = 1.72e-8 / 70e-6 ≈ 0.25 mΩ/□.

Each phase path is about 3 squares, paralleled across 4 inner and outer layers, giving about 0.19 mΩ per path. Two paths conduct at once:

$$P_{Cu,ch}\approx 2\cdot80^2\cdot0.19\text{m}\approx \mathbf{2.4\ W}$$

This is a significant term and is often ignored.
