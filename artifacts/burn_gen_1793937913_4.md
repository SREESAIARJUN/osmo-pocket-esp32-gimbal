# STM32H743 Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: 80 A Burst Design Package (Job #21)

## 0. Assumptions and a Feasibility Note

I don't have the original Job #21 requirements, so this package uses the baseline below. Every equation is parametric, so you can substitute your actual datasheet values.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal |
| Burst current | 80 A per motor, ≤ 3 s; 4 motors simultaneously (worst case) |
| Hover current | 15 A per motor |
| ESC PWM | 48 kHz, synchronous rectification, center-aligned |
| ESC firmware | AM32 on STM32G071 (one per channel). The H743 does not commutate motors. |
| FC firmware | ArduPilot, bidirectional DShot600 from the H743 |
| Power stage | 6 × 40 V N-MOSFET (PQFN 3.3×3.3) per channel, plus a 3-phase gate driver (FD6288Q class) |
| PCB | 8-layer, 2 oz on all layers, 36×36 mm outline (30.5×30.5 mm mounting) |

**Feasibility note:** On a true 20×20 mm "nano" board, an 80 A burst per channel is not physically viable. You cannot fit low enough R_DS(on), copper cross-section or thermal mass. The analysis below shows that even a 30.5 mm board can only sustain 4 × 80 A for about 2 s. Treat the "80 A" figure as a short burst rating, not a continuous one.

The 48 kHz switching happens on the ESC MCUs. The H743's role is DShot generation and telemetry capture (Section 3).

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| R₂₅ (max, V_GS = 10 V) | 1.5 mΩ (representative 40 V PQFN3.3 part) |
| R_hot at T_j = 125 °C | 1.5 × 1.5 = **2.25 mΩ** |

### 1.2 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

At any instant, current flows through exactly one high-side path and one low-side path. During PWM-off, the complementary FET replaces the active one, so the instantaneous conducting resistance is always 2·R_hot regardless of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{hot} + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

With R_Cu ≈ 0.5 mΩ (battery pad → bus → FETs → phase pad, 2 oz × 8 layers):

$$P_{cond,ch} = 80^2(2 \times 0.00225) + 80^2(0.0005) = 28.8 + 3.2 = \mathbf{32.0\ W}$$

### 1.3 Average loss per FET

Each switch conducts 120° of 360° (duty factor 1/3):

$$P_{cond,FET} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2 R_{hot} = \tfrac{1}{3}(6400)(0.00225) = \mathbf{4.8\ W}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

### 2.1 Hard-switching V–I overlap
