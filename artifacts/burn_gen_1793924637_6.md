**Loop rates and bandwidths.** Each loop runs at least 4–5× slower in bandwidth than the loop inside it.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

**Electrical angle.** θ_m is the mechanical angle and θ_off is the calibrated electrical offset.

$$\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant in the dq Frame (Surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

Here ω_e = p·ω_m and λ_m = (2/3)·K_t/p is the flux linkage.

### 1.3 Current PI with Decoupling and Back-EMF Feedforward

$$v_d^* = PI_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q^* = PI_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$
