# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80 A ESC (Job #63): Electro-Thermal and DShot System Model

## 0. Architectural Clarification

An STM32H743 cannot directly commutate 24 MOSFETs at 48 kHz across four motors. A 4-in-1 stack is therefore partitioned as follows:

| Domain | Device | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (or IHT6 in BGA for nano) running ArduPilot | Attitude control, bidirectional DShot600 master, eRPM telemetry |
| ESC (×4 channels) | 1× MCU per motor (STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32/BLHeli_32), 1× 3-phase gate driver (e.g., FD6288Q), 6× N-MOSFET | 48 kHz complementary PWM, commutation |
| Link | 4× DShot lines, 1× ESC telemetry UART, 1× current-sense ADC, VBAT, GND | |

The 48 kHz switching and 80 A losses below are ESC-side. The DMA and timer allocation is FC-side.

**Reference MOSFET (assumed; substitute your actual part).** Use a 40 V N-channel in a 3.3×3.3 mm package, with these parameters:
- R_DS(on) = 1.6 mΩ typical and 2.0 mΩ maximum at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V
- Q_g = 30 nC, Q_gd = 8 nC, Q_oss = 40 nC, Q_rr = 40 nC
- V_SD = 0.8 V
- R_θJC = 1.5 K/W

**Operating point:**
- 6S pack at V_bus = 25.2 V (full charge)
- f_sw = 48 kHz
- Burst current I = 80 A per motor
- Duty cycle D = 0.5 is used as the conservative case for ripple calculations.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology current path
With 6-step trapezoidal commutation and complementary (synchronous) PWM, exactly two MOSFETs carry the phase current at every instant:

- **PWM-on:** HS(A) + LS(B)
- **PWM-off (freewheel):** LS(A) + LS(B), plus the body diode during dead time

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period, so the per-motor conduction loss is:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Ripple-corrected RMS current
Phase ripple is computed with:
- Line-to-line inductance L_LL = 20 µH (typical 2207–2810 class motor)
- Back-EMF approximately D·V_bus

$$\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{LL} f_{sw}} = \frac{(25.2-12.6)(0.5)}{20\mu \cdot 48k} = 6.56\ \text{A}_{pp}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 \approx 6404\ \text{A}^2$$

At 48 kHz the ripple contribution is negligible (0.06 %). This is the main argument for 48 kHz over 24 kHz, where ΔI ≈ 13 A.

### 1.3 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| T_j | R_DS (typ) | R_DS (max) | P_cond per motor (max) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.60 mΩ | 2.00 mΩ | 25.6 W |
| 100 °C | 2.14 mΩ | 2.68 mΩ | 34.3 W |
| 125 °C | 2.32 mΩ | 2.90 mΩ | 37.1 W |

### 1.4 Electro-thermal fixed point (thermal runaway criterion)
Steady state requires:

$$T_j = T_a + R_{\theta JA}\,P_0\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

where P_0 is the loss at 25 °C. Solving for T_j:

$$T_j = \frac{T_a + R_{\theta JA}P_0(1-25\alpha)}{1-\alpha R_{\theta JA}P_0}$$

The stability condition is α·R_θJA·P_0 < 1.

On a 20×20 mm board with prop wash, R_θJA(board→air) ≈ 8–12 K/W for the whole board. With P_0 ≈ 100 W total, α·R·P_0 = 0.0045 × 10 × 100 = 4.5 ≫ 1. **No steady state exists at 80 A on all four motors.** 80 A is strictly a transient (burst) rating, and it must be bounded by time (Section 4).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

The gate driver is assumed to have 1.5 A source and 1.8 A sink capability, with R_g,ext = 2.2 Ω.

### 2.1 V-I overlap loss
Only the HS device of the PWM'd phase hard-switches. The LS device soft-switches through the body diode.

Rise and fall times are set by the Miller charge and the gate current:

$$t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{8\text{nC}}{0.55\text{A}} \approx 14.5\text{ ns},\qquad t_f \approx \frac{8\text{nC}}{0.65\text{A}} \approx 12.3\text{ ns}$$

Here I_g = (V_drv − V_plateau)/(R_drv + R_g), with V_plateau ≈ 4.5 V at 80 A.

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(26.8\text{n})(48\text{k}) = 1.30\text{ W}$$

### 2.2 Secondary switching terms (per motor)

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Coss | Q_oss · V_bus · f_sw | 40n × 25.2 × 48k = 0.048 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw | 0.048 W |
| Dead-time diode (t_dt = 100 ns ×2) | V_SD · I · 2t_dt · f_sw | 0.8 × 80 × 200n × 48k = 0.61 W |
| Gate drive (2 FETs toggling) | 2 · Q_g · V_drv · f_sw | 2 × 30n × 10 × 48k = 0.029 W (dissipated in driver/R_g) |
| **Switching subtotal** | | **≈ 2.04 W** |

### 2.3 Total loss budget at 80 A burst, T_j = 100 °C, max R_DS

| | Per motor | ×4 motors |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 34.3 W | 137 W |
| Switching + dead time | 2.0 W | 8.2 W |
| Shunt + copper (0.25 mΩ shunt + ~0.5 mΩ traces) | 4.8 W | 19.2 W |
| **Total** | **41.1 W** | **≈ 165 W** |

Conduction loss dominates by roughly 17×. To optimise, lower R_DS(on) (paralleled FETs or 5×6 packages if area allows) rather than lowering f_sw.

## 3. Input Capacitor Ripple

The RMS ripple current per motor bridge is:

$$I_{C,rms} = I\sqrt{D(1-D)} = 80 \times 0.5 = 40\ \text{A}$$

With four asynchronous ESC MCUs (PWM is not phase-interleaved), the worst case approaches correlated addition. Statistically, the sum is about √4 × 40 = 80 A RMS.

**Required decoupling:**
- On-board: ≥ 8× 10 µF/50 V X7R 1206 per bridge, placed within 3 mm of the HS drain and LS source loop.
- External: a mandatory 1000 µF/35 V low-ESR electrolytic (ESR ≤ 20 mΩ) on the pigtail.
