**Architecture note.** Production 4-in-1 boards do not commutate motors directly from the flight-controller MCU, and ArduPilot does not support that. The architecture is therefore:

- The **STM32H743** runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600.
- **Four dedicated ESC MCUs** (e.g. AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32) perform commutation and return eRPM telemetry.

**Feasibility flag (read before layout).** As the thermal results in §2 show, 4 × 80 A on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is a seconds-long burst rating only. Plan for one of:
- a 30.5×30.5 mm board,
- paralleled MOSFETs, or
- an explicit firmware burst limiter.

## 1. Loss Model, Per ESC Channel

### 1.1 Conduction losses

In 6-step operation with synchronous rectification, phase current always flows through **two conducting MOSFETs in series**:
- the PWM leg (high side for D, low side for 1−D), and
- the continuously-on sink leg low side.

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds(on)}\,k_T$$

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.5 = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

Copper (phase traces, bus planes, pads):

$$P_{cu} = I^2 R_{cu} = 6400 \times 0.3\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{1.92\ W}$$

The DC-link shunt carries I during the active portion. The worst case is D → 1:

$$P_{sh} = I^2 R_{sh}\,D \le 6400 \times 0.25\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{1.6\ W}$$

**Per-device distribution.** Over one electrical revolution, every FET spends one third of the time as "sink" and one third as the PWM leg. Averaged worst-case conduction loss per FET is about 19.2 / 6 ≈ **3.2 W**.

The instantaneous peak is higher. The sink-leg low side carries 80² × 1.5 mΩ = **9.6 W** for 1/3 of the electrical period. At high eRPM this period (< 1 ms) is far shorter than the die thermal time constant, so the averaged figure governs Tj. At stall or low RPM it does not, and the 9.6 W instantaneous figure must be used.

### 1.2 Switching losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches (one HS/LS pair per ESC).

**(a) V–I overlap (hard switching):**
$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

**(b) Reverse recovery of the complementary body diode:**
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

**(c) Output capacitance:**
$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 50\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.030\ W}$$

**(d) Dead-time body-diode conduction (two dead times per period):**
$$P_{dt} = V_f\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**(e) Gate drive (two switching FETs):**
$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 40\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.038\ W}$$
