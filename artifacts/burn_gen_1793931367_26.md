## 0. Architecture and Key Assumptions

**Architectural clarification:** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does **not** commutate the motors directly. Each of the 4 ESC channels has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. The H743 talks to those MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. This is standard practice: the H743 lacks 4× independent 6-step advanced-timer resources plus the BEMF comparators needed.

## 1. Electrical Operating Model

**6-step trapezoidal commutation.** Two phases conduct at any instant. The PWM phase alternates its high-side (HS) and low-side (LS) FETs with complementary PWM. The return phase holds its LS FET on.

- Phase (motor) current: $I_{ph}$
- DC bus current: $I_{bus} = D \cdot I_{ph}$
- **Every 80 A rating in this document refers to phase current.** Conduction loss is independent of D for this reason.

**Current ripple** (motor phase-to-phase L = 40 µH [A]):

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} \;\Big|_{D=0.5} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{40\mu \times 48k} = 3.28\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 0.90 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{ripple contributes } <0.02\%\ \text{(neglected below)}$$
