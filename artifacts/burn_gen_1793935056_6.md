# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #57): Power-Stage, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architectural Ground Rules and Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning: the H743 does not drive the MOSFETs

ArduPilot is flight-control firmware, not motor-commutation firmware. The 4-in-1 ESC therefore uses its own MCUs:

```
[STM32H743 FC] --DShot600 bidir (×4)--> [4× AT32F421 ESC MCU, AM32/BLHeli_32] --> [4× FD6288Q] --> [24× N-MOSFET]
```

The H743's job is DShot600 generation, eRPM telemetry capture, current/voltage ADC sensing, and the harmonic notch filter fed by eRPM.

### 0.2 The 80 A burst is thermally a burst only

On a nano form factor (~30×30 mm, 25.5 mm mounting pattern), 4 × 80 A cannot be sustained. Section 3 quantifies the burst limit, which is about 5–7 s at full load on all four channels.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology

AM32 and BLHeli_32 run 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM:

- At any instant, two phases conduct I_ph. The third phase floats for BEMF sensing.
- One leg is PWM-chopped. Its current alternates between the high-side FET (duration D) and the synchronous low-side FET (duration 1−D).
- The other leg's low-side FET is held on continuously.

So at every instant, exactly two FET channels carry I_ph, independent of duty cycle.

### 1.2 Equations

Temperature-dependent on-resistance:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

Conduction loss per motor channel:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Per-FET average (each FET conducts over 1/3 of the electrical cycle, sharing that with its complement):

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}$$

Per-FET peak, during its conduction window:

$$\hat P_{FET} = I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 51.2 W | 6.4 W |
| 100 °C | 1.49 mΩ | 19.1 W | 76.4 W | 9.5 W |

### 1.4 Copper path losses

These are frequently forgotten. Phase-pad and bus traces at about 0.15 mΩ per motor path in 2 oz copper add 2 · 80² · 0.15 mΩ ≈ 1.9 W per channel.

Use 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner copper, plus via arrays under each source and drain pad (≥12 × 0.3 mm vias per FET).

### 1.5 Phase vs. battery current

80 A here is **motor phase current**. Battery current per channel is approximately

$$I_{bat} \approx D \cdot I_{ph}$$

The ESC's 80 A burst rating should be stated as phase current, and current-limit firmware should act on the shunt reading, which measures battery-side current.
