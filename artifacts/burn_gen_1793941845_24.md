## 0. Executive Summary and Feasibility Flag

The headline result is that the specification is not feasible as written.

**80 A burst on all four channels simultaneously cannot be met on a nano form factor (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm).** The derivation below shows:

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Per-channel loss at 80 A, Tj = 125 °C | ≈ 20.4 W |
| Total ESC loss, all 4 channels at 80 A | ≈ 82 W |
| Simultaneous battery current | ≈ 320 A peak phase, ~160–240 A DC battery (duty-dependent) |
| Board ΔT after a 10 s burst (30×30 board, prop-wash cooling) | ≈ 114 K, giving ~154 °C board temperature at 40 °C ambient. **Fails.** |
| Board ΔT after a 5 s burst | ≈ 60 K, giving ~100 °C board and Tj ≈ 110 °C. **Marginal pass.** |

**Recommended re-specification (pick one):**
- **(A)** 80 A burst for ≤ 5 s on a 30.5×30.5 mm board, 6-layer, 2 oz inner/outer copper. Continuous rating 35–40 A per channel.
- **(B)** Keep the nano form factor and derate to 40–45 A burst per channel.
- **(C)** Keep 80 A per channel and parallel two FETs per switch position (48 FETs). This roughly halves conduction loss but does not fit a nano board.

The rest of this document models option (A) and states every assumption explicitly.

## 1. System Architecture

```
          6S LiPo (25.2 V max)
               │
     ┌─────────┴───────────────────────────────────────────┐
     │  VBAT bus — 2× 35V 1000µF low-ESR + 8× 10µF X7R/ch   │
     └──┬───────────┬───────────┬───────────┬───────────────┘
        │           │           │           │
   ┌────┴───┐  ┌────┴───┐  ┌────┴───┐  ┌────┴───┐
   │ ESC1   │  │ ESC2   │  │ ESC3   │  │ ESC4   │   AT32F421 (AM32 / BLHeli_32)
   │ 6×FET  │  │ 6×FET  │  │ 6×FET  │  │ 6×FET  │   + 3-ph gate driver
   └────┬───┘  └────┬───┘  └────┬───┘  └────┬───┘
        │ bidir DShot600 (single-wire, 3.3 V)
   ┌────┴───────────┴───────────┴───────────┴───┐
   │           STM32H743VIH6 @ 480 MHz           │
   │  TIM3 CH3/CH4 → M1/M2   TIM5 CH1/CH2 → M3/M4│
   └─────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Each ESC channel runs its own MCU (AT32F421 or similar) executing AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 is the flight controller only, and it communicates with the ESCs over bidirectional DShot600.
