## 0. Architecture Clarification and Baseline Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. ArduPilot has no field-oriented or 6-step ESC firmware for the FC MCU. The architecture is:

| Block | Device | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) @ 400 MHz | ArduPilot, emits bidirectional DShot600 on 4 timer channels |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) running AM32 | 6-step synchronous PWM at 48 kHz, BEMF sensing, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap, 1.5 A source / 1.8 A sink) | High-/low-side gate drive |
| Power stage ×4 | 6 × N-MOSFET, 40 V, 5×6 mm PDFN | 3-phase bridge |
