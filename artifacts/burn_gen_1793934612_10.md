# 3-Axis Gimbal FOC Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion

I don't have the Job #7 specification, so this design states its assumptions explicitly. Each one maps to a single constant in the code, so you can substitute your real hardware parameters.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 @ 170 MHz (FPU, CORDIC, 3× advanced timers) |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC (e.g. GBM2804H-100T), 12N14P → `p = 7` pole pairs, low-current, R ≈ 10 Ω, L ≈ 2.5 mH |
| Motor sensors | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per axis (AS5048A / MT6701), SPI |
| Current sense | 2-phase low-side shunts, ADC triggered at PWM center |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate. IMU1 is rotated 180° about Z relative to IMU0. Both share the CLKIN reference. |
| Kinematic order | Base → Yaw → Roll → Pitch → Camera |

**Loop rates.** Each outer loop runs at least 5× slower in bandwidth than the loop inside it.

|---|---|---|---|
