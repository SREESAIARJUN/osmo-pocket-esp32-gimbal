# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #42): Power-Stage Model, Thermal Budget, and Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Plan

## 0. Architecture Correction and Stated Assumptions

**Architecture.** On an AIO board, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. Commutation runs on four dedicated ESC MCUs (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32). The 48 kHz PWM, dead-time and BEMF sensing therefore live on the ESC MCUs. The H743 owns DShot600 generation, eRPM capture, and the DMA budget. The model below follows that split.

**Assumptions.** No MOSFET, driver, or board stack-up was specified, so I assumed a representative part set. Substitute your datasheet values into the equations; every result is shown symbolically first.
