# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture & Mathematical Model (Job #22)

## 0. Architectural Clarifications and Assumptions

Three issues need settling before the math, because they change the design.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** On every production "4-in-1 + FC" AIO board, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot to four (or one, for 4-channel firmware) dedicated ESC MCUs. Those are typically STM32G071, AT32F421, or EFM8BB51 running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. The H743 has no fast enough closed-loop path for sensorless 6-step BEMF zero-crossing on four motors while also running EKF3. This design uses:
- **FC MCU:** STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100) or VIT6 (LQFP100)
- **ESC MCUs:** 4 × AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32), one per motor, running AM32
- **Gate drivers:** 4 × FD6288Q (3-phase half-bridge driver, QFN24)
- **Power stage:** 24 × N-channel MOSFETs (6 per motor)

**2. 80 A burst on a "nano" (20×20 mm) board is not thermally credible.** The math in §3 shows this. Burst is defined below, and the model is built so it can tell you the burst *duration* limit rather than assume one. I recommend a **30.5×30.5 mm stacked** layout or a **25.5×25.5 mm AIO** for anything claiming >45 A burst per channel. The calculations are carried out for both.

**3. Component parameters are representative placeholders.** Replace every value flagged ⟨DS⟩ with the selected part's datasheet value before sign-off.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-Dependent On-Resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × 1.60 = **1.92 mΩ**.

### 1.2 6-Step Trapezoidal Commutation with Synchronous Rectification

In any 60° electrical sector, current flows through exactly one high-side path and one low-side path. With complementary (synchronous) PWM on the active high side, the PWM leg alternates between Q_H (for duty D) and Q_L of the same leg (for 1−D). The return leg's low-side FET conducts continuously for the full sector. In every sub-interval, **two channels are in series** with the load current:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

This total is independent of D, because synchronous rectification replaces diode conduction with channel conduction.

**Per-FET average.** Each of the 6 FETs carries current for 120° of 360° electrically, either fully or shared with its complement:

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} = \frac{P_{cond,ESC}}{6} \cdot \text{(sharing factor)} \approx \frac{2 I^2 R}{6}$$

The return low-side carries the full I²R for its 120°. A switching-leg FET carries D or (1−D) of that. The worst-case single FET is the **continuously-conducting low side**:

$$P_{cond,FET,max} = I^2 R \cdot \tfrac{1}{3}$$
