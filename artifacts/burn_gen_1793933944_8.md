# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #64): Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot Architecture

## 0. Assumptions and Scope

There is one architectural point to settle first. The STM32H743 is the **flight controller** MCU. It does not commutate motors. Each ESC channel (or a shared 4-channel ESC MCU set) runs AM32 or BLHeli_32 on its own MCU, such as the STM32G071 or AT32F421. The H743 sends bi-directional DShot600 to those MCUs over on-board traces. Everything below follows that partition.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step synchronous PWM, two legs are active during any commutation sector. The current always flows through exactly two MOSFET channels in series:

- During the on-time D: high-side of leg A, then low-side of leg B.
- During the off-time (1 − D): synchronous low-side of leg A, then low-side of leg B.

So, ignoring dead time, the channel conduction loss is independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence (self-heating feedback)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big]$$

The junction temperature depends on the loss, which in turn depends on R_DS. Solve the pair by fixed-point iteration (Section 3.3).

### 1.3 Ripple correction

Phase current ripple adds to the RMS value:

$$I_{ph,rms} = \sqrt{I_{avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}}, \qquad \Delta I_{pp} \approx \frac{(V_{bus}-E_{bemf})\,D}{2L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

For a typical 5″/7″ motor (L_ph ≈ 15–30 µH line-line) at 48 kHz, ΔI_pp is roughly 3–6 A. The correction is under 0.5 % at 80 A, so it is ignored below.

### 1.5 PCB copper loss

Copper losses are often omitted, but they are not small at this current. Assume a phase path of about 12 mm with an effective width of 6 mm, using 2 oz outer layers plus 2 internal 2 oz planes in parallel:

- R_sheet (2 oz, 70 µm) ≈ 0.245 mΩ/□ per layer.
- With 4 layers in parallel, ≈ 0.061 mΩ/□.
- 12 mm / 6 mm = 2 squares, so ≈ 0.12 mΩ per path segment.
- The battery bus plus phase pads total ≈ 0.3 mΩ per channel.
