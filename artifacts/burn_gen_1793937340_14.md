## 0. Architecture Clarification

**The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** A 4-in-1 ESC needs one dedicated ESC MCU per motor for 48 kHz complementary PWM, back-EMF zero-cross detection, and the bi-directional DShot telemetry encoder. The H743 has neither the advanced-timer count nor the comparator topology to drive 24 half-bridges sensorlessly while also running ArduPilot.

The architecture assumed throughout:

| Block | Device (reference choice) | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100) @ 480 MHz | ArduPilot, DShot600 bidir master |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6 | AM32 firmware, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate driver ×4 | Fortior FD6288Q (3-phase, 250 V, bootstrap) | High-side and low-side gate drive |
| Power stage ×4 | 6× 40 V N-MOSFET, PDFN 5×6 (or 3.3×3.3 on true nano) | Three half-bridges per motor |
| Supply | 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V | |

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

At any instant one phase leg is PWM-switched (high side and low side complementary), one low side is held on, and one phase floats.

**High-side FET (switching leg)**

$$P_{cond,HS} = I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)\,D_{eff}$$

**Low-side FET (same leg, synchronous rectification)**

$$P_{cond,LS} = I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)\,(1 - D_{eff} - 2t_{dt}f_{sw})$$

**Return-path low-side FET (held on for the full 60° sector)**

$$P_{cond,ret} = I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Each FET conducts for only 1/3 of the electrical cycle in its role. Per-device average loss is therefore the sector loss divided by 3. However, the **channel** total at any instant equals the sum of the active devices.

**Effective duty**

$$D_{eff} = D - 2t_{dt}f_{sw} = D - 0.0096$$

### 2.2 Numerical result: 80 A burst, D = 0.90, T_j = 100 °C (R = 1.49 mΩ)

| Term | Expression | Value |
|---|---|---|
| I²R | 6400 × 1.49e-3 | 9.54 W |
| HS conduction | 9.54 × 0.890 | 8.49 W |
| LS sync conduction | 9.54 × 0.100 | 0.96 W |
| Return LS | 9.54 × 1 | 9.54 W |
| **Channel conduction total** | | **≈ 19.0 W** |

The R_DS(on) temperature dependence creates positive feedback. Solve iteratively:

$$T_j = T_{board} + P(T_j)\,R_{\theta,j\text{-}board}$$

For burst estimates, use R at 100 °C (above) as the baseline and R at 125 °C (+10 %) as the worst case.

### 2.3 Copper and interconnect conduction

| Path | Assumed resistance | Loss @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Bus plane + phase pad + return (2 oz outer, 2 oz inner ×2) | ≈ 0.35 mΩ | 2.24 W |
| Motor wire solder joints and pads | ≈ 0.15 mΩ | 0.96 W |
| **Copper subtotal per channel** | | **≈ 3.2 W** |

Shunt loss is extra if a per-channel shunt is used. A shared 0.25 mΩ bus shunt at 4 × 80 A carries 320 A, giving **25.6 W**. A sustained 320 A bus is not credible on a nano board. Use either a Hall-effect current sensor or sense current at a lower burst assumption (see Section 5).

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches (one HS and one LS per channel at any time).

### 3.1 Hard-switching V-I overlap (HS turn-on and turn-off)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 3.2 Reverse recovery (LS body diode recovered by HS turn-on)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 50\text{ nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{ k} = 0.060\text{ W}$$

Diode current at recovery is near 80 A, so Q_rr scales up. Use the datasheet Q_rr at the corresponding di/dt and I_F. **Budget 0.15 W.**

### 3.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12\,C_{oss,eff}\,V_{bus}^2\,f_{sw} \times 2 = 2.0\text{ nF} \times 635 \times 48\text{ k} = 0.061\text{ W}$$

### 3.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 3.5 Gate charge (dissipated mainly in R_g and the driver, not the FET)

$$P_g = Q_g\,V_{GS}\,f_{sw} \times 2 = 50\text{ nC} \times 10 \times 48\text{ k} \times 2 = 0.048\text{ W}$$

### 3.6 Switching subtotal per channel

$$P_{sw,tot} \approx 1.94 + 0.15 + 0.06 + 0.61 = \mathbf{2.76\ W}$$
