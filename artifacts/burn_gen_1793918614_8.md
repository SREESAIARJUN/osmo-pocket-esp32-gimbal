**Scope:** loss model (conduction and 48 kHz switching), transient thermal model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and reference netlists.

## 0. Assumptions and Architectural Corrections

### 0.1 Architectural correction

The STM32H743 cannot be the ESC controller. It is the flight controller (FC). Each of the four motor channels needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 (for example, AT32F421 or STM32G071). The H743 talks to these MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. All DShot math below applies to the H743 ↔ ESC MCU link.

### 0.2 Feasibility warning

On a "nano" footprint, 4 × 80A is thermally a **burst-only** capability of a few seconds. Section 3 quantifies this. Do not market a continuous rating that this board cannot sustain.
