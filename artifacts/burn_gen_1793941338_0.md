## 1. FOC Inner Loop

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

Here $\theta_{\text{off}}$ is found at calibration by applying $v_d > 0,\ v_q = 0$ and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}\quad(i_a+i_b+i_c=0)
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.3 Plant and current PI design (pole-zero cancellation)

The PMSM voltage equations in the dq frame are:

$$
v_d = R_s i_d + L_s \dot i_d - \omega_e L_s i_q,\qquad
v_q = R_s i_q + L_s \dot i_q + \omega_e L_s i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Per axis, the plant is $G_i(s) = \dfrac{1}{L_s s + R_s}$. Choose the PI so its zero cancels the electrical pole:

$$
C_i(s) = K_{p,i} + \frac{K_{i,i}}{s},\quad
K_{p,i} = L_s\,\omega_{ci},\quad K_{i,i} = R_s\,\omega_{ci}
$$

The closed loop is then first order: $\dfrac{i}{i^*} = \dfrac{\omega_{ci}}{s+\omega_{ci}}$, with $\omega_{ci} = 2\pi\cdot1500$.

**Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward** (with $\lambda_m = \tfrac{2}{3}\tfrac{K_t}{p}$ for the amplitude-invariant transform):

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L_s i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L_s i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

### 1.4 Discrete PI with back-calculation anti-windup

Use a forward-Euler integrator with sample period $T$:

$$
\begin{aligned}
e_k &= r_k - y_k\\
u_k^{\text{raw}} &= K_p e_k + I_k + u^{ff}_k\\
u_k &= \operatorname{sat}(u_k^{\text{raw}})\\
I_{k+1} &= I_k + K_i T\, e_k + K_{aw}\,T\,(u_k - u_k^{\text{raw}}),\qquad K_{aw}\approx \frac{K_i}{K_p}
\end{aligned}
$$

The voltage vector is saturated **jointly**, with d-axis priority:

$$
|v_{dq}| \le \frac{V_{dc}}{\sqrt3}\cdot m_{\max},\qquad m_{\max}\approx 0.95
$$

### 1.5 Inverse Park and SVPWM (min/max zero-sequence injection)

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

$$
v_a = v_\alpha,\quad v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc})+\min(v_{abc})\big),\qquad
D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{dc}}
$$

This is equivalent to conventional SVPWM and reaches the full linear range $|v| = V_{dc}/\sqrt3$.

**Delay compensation.** PWM updates take effect one period later, so evaluate the inverse Park at $\theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_{pwm}$ (1 sample of compute delay plus ½ sample of PWM hold).
