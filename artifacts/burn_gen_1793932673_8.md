## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation topology

The ESC uses six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous (complementary) PWM, which is the AM32 default. In each 60° sector:

- **Switching leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T and the low-side FET for (1−D)·T.
- **Static leg:** the low-side FET is continuously on.
- **Third leg:** floating.

The phase current I flows through exactly two R_DS paths at every instant, independent of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)\,[\,D + (1-D)\,] + I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At the design point T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS}(110) = 1.0 \times (1 + 0.006 \times 85) = 1.51\ \text{m}\Omega$$
