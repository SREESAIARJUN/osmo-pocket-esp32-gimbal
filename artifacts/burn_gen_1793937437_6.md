```
 setpoint q_ref ─►│ quaternion error → J⁻¹ → P+FF │──►│ PI vel + AW   │──►│ +i_cog(θ)  PI_dq + decpl│──► SVPWM

                                                       ω_joint (encoder, PLL)      θ_e (encoder)
```

Design principles:

- The gimbal stabilizes the **camera attitude**, measured by the IMUs. The encoder is used for commutation, velocity, and the cogging lookup.
- Bandwidths are separated by at least 5× between loops: current ≈ 1.5 kHz, velocity ≈ 150 Hz, angle ≈ 25 Hz.

## 2. Field-Oriented Current Loop

### 2.1 Transforms

Use the amplitude-invariant Clarke transform with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off})$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

Inverse Park is the transpose of this matrix.

### 2.2 Plant in the rotating frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Electromagnetic torque:

$$
\tau = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q
$$

### 2.3 PI with decoupling (pole-zero cancellation)

With feedforward decoupling, $v_d^{*} = u_d - \omega_e L i_q$ and $v_q^{*} = u_q + \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda_m)$. Each axis then reduces to $G(s) = 1/(Ls+R)$.

Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole:

$$
C(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s},\qquad K_p = L\,\omega_c,\quad K_i = R\,\omega_c
$$

The closed loop becomes $\omega_c/(s+\omega_c)$.

- Account for the one-sample computation delay plus the PWM zero-order hold (≈ $1.5T_s$).
- Keep $\omega_c \le \frac{1}{3}\cdot\frac{2\pi}{1.5 T_s\cdot 2\pi}$, i.e. at most about 1/10 of the PWM frequency.
- At 20 kHz, $\omega_c = 2\pi\cdot1500$ rad/s gives roughly 60° phase margin.

Discretize with backward Euler:

$$
x_k = x_{k-1} + K_i T_s e_k,\qquad u_k = K_p e_k + x_k
$$
