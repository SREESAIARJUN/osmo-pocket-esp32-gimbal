> **Reality check, stated up front.** The model shows that a true 80 A continuous rating on a nano-class 4-in-1 is not thermally possible. 80 A is a *burst* rating limited by board heat capacity, typically to a few seconds. Section 3 quantifies this.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

In each 60° sector, phase X is PWM'd and phase Y is held low:

- **On-time (D):** current flows through HS_X, then the motor, then LS_Y.
- **Off-time (1−D):** current freewheels through LS_X (synchronous rectification), then the motor, then LS_Y.

Both paths always contain exactly **two** conducting FETs. Per motor:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph}^2 \left[ D\,(R_{HS}+R_{LS}) + (1-D)\,(2R_{LS}) \right] \;\xrightarrow{R_{HS}=R_{LS}=R}\; 2 I_{ph}^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

Temperature dependence:

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0063\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

### 1.2 Per-device RMS currents

Each FET's phase is active for 1/3 of the electrical period.

| Device | RMS expression | At D = 0.9, I = 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| High-side FET | $I_{HS,rms} = I\sqrt{D/3}$ | 43.8 A |
| Low-side FET (held-on sector + freewheel sector) | $I_{LS,rms} = I\sqrt{(1 + (1-D))/3}$ | 48.5 A |

### 1.3 Numbers at T_j = 100 °C (R = 2.2 mΩ)

| Quantity | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Per-motor conduction loss | 2 × 80² × 0.0022 | **28.2 W** |
| Single LS FET | 48.5² × 0.0022 | 5.17 W |
| Single HS FET | 43.8² × 0.0022 | 4.22 W |
| Copper/trace + shunt (0.5 mΩ effective path) | 80² × 0.0005 | 3.2 W per motor |

The shunt term is a single shared DC-link shunt per motor, and its loss scales with the DC current. Using I_ph as a worst case is conservative.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Hard-switching (V–I overlap) loss

Only the PWM'd high-side hard-switches. The LS FET turns on with zero voltage after the body diode has conducted.

Gate currents through the Miller region:

$$
I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_{src} + R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int}} = \frac{12-3}{10+4.7+1} = 0.573\ \text{A}
$$

$$
I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{sink} + R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int}} = \frac{3}{5+4.7+1} = 0.280\ \text{A}
$$

Switching transition times:

$$
t_{r} = \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{10\,\text{nC}}{0.573} = 17.5\ \text{ns}
$$

$$
t_{f} = \frac{10\,\text{nC}}{0.280} = 35.7\ \text{ns}
$$

Overlap loss:

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 53.2\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{2.57\ W}
$$
