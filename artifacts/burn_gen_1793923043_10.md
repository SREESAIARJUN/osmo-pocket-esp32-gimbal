# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #16): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Feasibility Problems to Resolve First

Two problems in the brief must be settled before the math is meaningful.

**1. The STM32H743 cannot be the ESC commutation MCU.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 and outputs DShot. Bi-directional DShot implies a separate ESC-side MCU that sends eRPM telemetry back. The baseline architecture is therefore:

- **Flight controller:** STM32H743VIT6, ArduPilot, bi-directional DShot600 output.
- **ESC:** 4× AT32F421 (or STM32G071) running AM32 firmware, PWM at 48 kHz, one MCU per motor.
- BLHeli_32 is discontinued, so AM32 is the supported path for bi-directional DShot with a configurable PWM frequency.

**2. "Nano" and "80 A burst" conflict.** A 20×20 mm 4-in-1 board only fits 3.3×3.3 mm FETs and roughly 2 oz copper. The model below shows that 80 A per channel is only survivable as a short burst, measured in seconds. It is not survivable as a continuous rating, and not with all four motors loaded at once.

My recommendation is a 30.5×30.5 mm mount, 8-layer 2 oz stack, and 40 V DFN3.3 FETs. The numbers below assume this. I give the 20×20 delta at the end.

## 1. System Block Architecture

```
 VBAT (6S, 25.2V max)
   │
   ├─► Bulk: 1× 35V 1000µF low-ESR (external, on XT60 pigtail) + per-ESC MLCC bank
   │
   ├─► ESC[1..4]: AT32F421 + FD6288Q gate driver + 6× 40V N-FET (3 half bridges)
   │       ▲  DShot600 (bidir, single wire, 3.3V, open-drain-ish w/ pull-up)
   │       │
   └─► FC: STM32H743 ── TIM1 CH1..CH4 ── M1..M4
           ├─ SPI1: IMU (ICM-42688-P), SPI4: OSD/flash, I2C: baro/compass
           ├─ ADC: VBAT divider, total current shunt amp
           └─ 5V/3.3V bucks (from VBAT)
```

Each ESC MCU also performs BEMF zero-cross sensing through dividers into its comparator inputs, plus one shared-current-shunt ADC channel.
