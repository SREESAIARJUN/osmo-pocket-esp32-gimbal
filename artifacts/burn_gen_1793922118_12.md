## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Conduction loss

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, the motor current always passes through **two** MOSFET channels in series. During PWM-on it flows through the high-side of phase X and the low-side of phase Y. During PWM-off it flows through the low-side of X (synchronous) and the low-side of Y. So, independent of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2 \cdot R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

**Ripple correction.** For phase inductance L_ph (line-to-line 2L_ph), the ripple current at duty D is:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{2L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}
\qquad
I_{rms}^2 = I_{dc}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}
$$

Example with L_ph = 10 µH and D = 0.5:
- ΔI = 25.2·0.25 / (2·10e-6·48e3) = **6.6 A**
- ΔI²/12 = 3.6 A²

This is negligible against 6400 A², so **I_rms ≈ 80 A**.

**Temperature-dependent resistance:**

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_0\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond @ 80 A (per channel) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ | 17.1 W |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ | **18.6 W** |

**Distribution across the six FETs.** Each phase leg carries current for 240° of 360°. Averaged over an electrical revolution (eRPM in kHz, far faster than thermal time constants), conduction loss spreads approximately evenly across the six FETs:

$$
P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{18.6}{6} \approx 3.1\ \text{W}
$$

There is a slight bias toward low-sides at low duty, because the LS conducts the freewheel current.

### 1.2 Hard-switching loss

Only the PWM-modulated leg hard-switches, and it does so twice per PWM period (turn-on and turn-off).

**Gate current during the Miller plateau** (V_drv = 10 V):

$$
I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}} = \frac{7}{5.7} = 1.23\ \text{A}
\qquad
I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{5.7} = 0.53\ \text{A}
$$
