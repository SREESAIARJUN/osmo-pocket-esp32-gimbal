## 0. Design Assumptions

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 class, Cortex-M4F, 170 MHz, single-precision FPU |
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R = 5.6 Ω, L = 2.1 mH, K_t ≈ 0.075 N·m/A |
| Position sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (e.g. AS5048A / MT6701) per axis |
| Inertia (axis + payload) | J ≈ 2.5×10⁻⁴ kg·m² (yaw), smaller for pitch/roll |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 24 MHz, gyro/accel ODR 8 kHz |
| Bus voltage | 12 V (3S–4S) |

**Loop rates:**

| Loop | Rate | Bandwidth target |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz (PWM center-aligned ISR) | ω_c ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s |
| Velocity | 4 kHz | ω_v ≈ 2π·120 rad/s |
| Angle / attitude | 1 kHz | ω_θ ≈ 2π·20 rad/s |
| IMU sampling | 8 kHz, decimated to 4 kHz after filtering | – |

**Bandwidth separation rule:** ω_c / ω_v ≥ 10 and ω_v / ω_θ ≥ 5. These ratios let each inner loop be treated as unity gain when tuning the next outer loop.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical model (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q, \qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2} p \lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q,\qquad \omega_e = p\,\omega_m,\qquad \theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}
$$

### 1.2 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform** (two shunts, using i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

**Park transform:**

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$
