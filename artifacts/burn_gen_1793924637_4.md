### Loop rate hierarchy

The design keeps a bandwidth separation of at least 5× between nested loops.

| Loop | Rate | Closed-loop BW | Trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| PWM / current (FOC) | 20 kHz | ω_c = 2π·1500 rad/s | ADC end-of-conversion ISR |
| IMU sampling + filtering | 8 kHz | AAF ≈ 1 kHz on-chip | IMU INT1 → SPI DMA |
| Velocity (rate) loop | 4 kHz | ω_v = 2π·150 rad/s | Every 5th current ISR |
| Angle (attitude) loop | 1 kHz | ω_θ = 2π·25 rad/s | Every 4th velocity tick |
| AHRS attitude update | 1 kHz | — | Same tick as angle loop |

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Torque Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical model (rotor dq frame, surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2} p \lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q,\qquad \theta_e = p\,\theta_m + \theta_{off}
$$

### 1.2 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform**, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform:**

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
