There is one architectural point to settle first. The STM32H743 runs **ArduPilot (the flight controller)**. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The 48 kHz figure is the **ESC PWM frequency**, which is set in the AM32 configuration and not by ArduPilot.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step with complementary (synchronous) PWM

In every 60° electrical sector, two phases conduct:
- One phase is held **low** (its low-side FET is fully on).
- One phase is **PWM'd** high: the high side is on for duty D, and the synchronous low side is on for (1 − D).

The motor current therefore always flows through **exactly two FET channels** (ignoring dead time):

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_b^2 \cdot 2 \cdot R_{hot}, \qquad R_{hot} = k_T R_{25}$$

### 1.2 Per-FET distribution over one electrical cycle

Each phase spends 1/3 of the cycle in the PWM-high role, 1/3 in the held-low role, and 1/3 floating:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I_b^2 R_{hot} D$$

$$P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I_b^2 R_{hot}\big[(1-D) + 1\big]$$

### 1.3 Numerical evaluation

$$R_{hot} = 1.45 \times 1.2 = 1.74\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.74\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{22.3\ W}$$

| Duty D | P_HS per FET | P_LS per FET | Hottest FET |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 (full-throttle burst) | 3.71 W | 3.71 W | 3.71 W (evenly shared) |
| 0.5 | 1.86 W | 5.57 W | **LS, 5.57 W** |

Note that 80 A at low duty is physically unlikely, because phase current scales with throttle.

### 1.4 Copper (PCB) conduction loss

The sheet resistance of 2 oz (70 µm) copper is:

$$R_\square = \frac{\rho_{Cu}}{t} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}}{70\times10^{-6}} = 0.246\ \text{m}\Omega/\square \;(25\,^\circ\text{C}),\quad \times 1.33 \text{ at } 110\,^\circ\text{C}$$
