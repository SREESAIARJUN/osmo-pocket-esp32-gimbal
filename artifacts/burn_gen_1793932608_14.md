## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 should **not** commutate the motors directly. Running four sensorless ESC loops on the flight controller MCU would conflict with ArduPilot's scheduler. The standard and recommended split is:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100, 480 MHz max, run at 400 MHz) | ArduPilot, DShot600 master, bi-directional telemetry decode |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) running AM32 | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, DShot slave |
| Gate driver ×4 | 3-phase bootstrap driver (e.g., FD6288Q class) **[A]** | 3 half-bridges per motor |
| Power stage | 24 × N-MOSFET, 40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 **[A]** | 6 per motor |
| Supply | 2–6S LiPo (max 25.2 V) | |

**Feasibility warning on the 80 A spec.** "80 A burst" on a nano form factor (20×20 or 30×30 mm mounting) is only achievable as a short transient (seconds), never as continuous current. Section 3 derives the actual time limit.

## 2. Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Losses

In 6-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM, two legs conduct at any instant:

- **Active-high leg:** current alternates between the high-side FET (duty D) and the synchronous low-side FET (1−D).
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET is fully on.

So the current always flows through exactly **two** R_ds(on), regardless of D.

$$P_{cond} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{ds(on)}(T_j) + I^2 R_{cu}$$

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) \approx R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1} \Rightarrow k_T = 1.5 \text{ at } 125\,°\text{C}$$

At 80 A:

| Condition | 2·R_ds | P_FET | P_Cu | **P_cond** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T_j = 25 °C | 2.4 mΩ | 15.4 W | 1.6 W | **17.0 W** |
| T_j = 125 °C | 3.6 mΩ | 23.0 W | 1.9 W (Cu +30 %) | **24.9 W** |

R_ds(on) rises with temperature, so conduction loss is a positive-feedback term. Section 3 iterates on it.

### 2.2 Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing half-bridge switches. That is one hard-switched commutation pair per PWM cycle.

**(a) V–I overlap (hard switching):**
$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 20\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2}C_{oss}V_{bus}^2 f_{sw}\cdot 2 = 1\text{n}\cdot 635 \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.03\ W}$$
