## Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, and Bi-directional DShot600 Timer/DMA Allocation

## 0. Feasibility Summary

**A nano-class AIO board cannot thermally sustain 80 A on all four channels.**

- **80 A is a per-channel burst rating** of roughly 1 to 4 seconds, depending on how many channels are loaded at once.
- **Sustainable continuous current is about 28 to 35 A per channel** with propwash airflow.
- **The thermal model in §3 derives both limits.** Marketing should not publish "80 A continuous."

**Two-processor architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU, nominally AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32. These ESC MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 sends bi-directional DShot600 commands to them and receives eRPM telemetry back.

**Status of part numbers.** All component values below are reference assumptions. Before layout freeze, substitute the parameters from the datasheet of the actual MOSFET you select.
