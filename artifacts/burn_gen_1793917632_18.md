## 1. Conduction-Loss Model (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

### 1.1 Current path

In any commutation sector one phase is PWM'd (complementary HS/LS) and one phase is held low-side ON:

- **PWM ON interval:** HS(A) + LS(B) conduct.
- **PWM OFF interval (synchronous freewheel):** LS(A) + LS(B) conduct.

So **exactly two channels conduct the phase current at all times**, independent of duty cycle, ignoring dead time.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.3 Per-motor conduction loss

Include the inductor ripple ΔI (triangular), so that I_rms² = I_ph² + ΔI²/12:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,R_{DS}(T_j)\left(I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}\right)$$

The ripple is set by the motor phase-to-phase inductance L_ll:

$$\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ll}\,f_{sw}}$$

For a 2207-class motor (L_ll ≈ 15 µH) at D = 0.5 and V_bemf ≈ 12 V:

- ΔI ≈ 9.2 A
- ΔI²/12 ≈ 7 A², which is about 0.1 % of 6400 A² and therefore negligible at 80 A.

### 1.4 Numerical result at T_j = 110 °C

- R_DS(110 °C) = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0055 × 85) = **1.47 mΩ**
- P_cond = 2 × 1.47e-3 × 6400 = **18.8 W per motor**

### 1.5 Per-device distribution

Over one electrical cycle each of the 6 FETs carries current about 1/3 of the time. The split between HS and LS within the PWM leg depends on D.

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Average per FET | 18.8 / 6 ≈ **3.1 W** |
| Instantaneous peak (held LS, full sector) | I²R = **9.4 W** |

The peak is held for one sector, t_sector = 1/(6·f_e). At f_e ≈ 2 kHz (≈ 34 k mech RPM, 14-pole) that is about 83 µs. The die thermal time constant is roughly 1–5 ms, so the junction tracks the **average** with ripple of about ±10 %.

## 2. Switching-Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches; the held-low leg is static. Per motor, the terms are:

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V_{bus}\,I_{ph}\,(t_r+t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

= 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40e-9 × 48e3 = **1.94 W**

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

The LS body diode conducts during dead time and is then hard-commutated by the HS turn-on:

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw}$$

= 30e-9 × 25.2 × 48e3 = **0.036 W**

### 2.3 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\,I_{ph}\,(2\,t_{dt})\,f_{sw}$$

= 0.8 × 80 × 100e-9 × 48e3 = **0.31 W**

### 2.4 Output capacitance and gate drive

- P_oss = Q_oss · V_bus · f_sw = **0.048 W**
- P_gate = 2 · Q_g · V_GS · f_sw = 2 × 40e-9 × 10 × 48e3 = **0.038 W**

P_gate is dissipated in the driver and R_G, not in the FET die.
