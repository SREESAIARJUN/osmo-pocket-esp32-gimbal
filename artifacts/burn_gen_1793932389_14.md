## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Check

Three issues need to be settled before the numbers mean anything.

**1. The H743 does not drive the power stage.**
- In a production ArduPilot design, the STM32H743 runs the flight stack and emits bidirectional DShot600.
- The 48 kHz bridge PWM is generated by dedicated ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32. This design uses four STM32G071 devices (one per motor), or one per pair on cost-reduced builds.
- ArduPilot has no field-oriented or six-step motor-drive layer. Putting the bridge on the H743 would be a custom-firmware project, not an ArduPilot configuration.
- This document therefore models two domains:
  - the ESC power domain (48 kHz switching, 80 A burst);
  - the FC domain (H743 DShot600 bidirectional timers and DMA).

**2. 80 A on a "nano" footprint is a burst rating, not a continuous one.**
- The calculation below shows roughly 108 W of total bridge dissipation at 4 × 80 A.
- A 30.5 × 30.5 mm board can only shed about 10–20 W continuously, even in prop-wash.
- The 80 A rating is therefore time-limited, to roughly 2–3 s, and the firmware must enforce it.
