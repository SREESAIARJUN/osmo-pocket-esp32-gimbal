# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture (Job #48)

## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

Two points in the brief need to be corrected before the derivation is meaningful.

**1. The STM32H743 cannot commutate the motors.** An H743 running ArduPilot cannot also run 4× sensorless 48 kHz commutation loops in practice, and ArduPilot does not support it. The architecture is therefore:

- **H743:** runs ArduPilot and generates 4× bidirectional DShot600.
- **4× STM32G071 (or AT32F421):** each runs AM32 firmware and drives 3 half-bridges.

**2. "80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 mm) footprint is not thermally viable** beyond about 1 s. The math in §2–§3 shows this quantitatively. I give the model so you can set a realistic rating.

**Design basis**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S, V_bus = 25.2 V max |
| MOSFET | 40 V N-ch, 3.3×3.3 DFN, R_ds(on) = 1.0 mΩ max @ 25 °C, temperature coefficient k_T ≈ 1.5 @ 125 °C, Q_g = 50 nC, Q_gd = 10 nC, Q_rr = 50 nC, Q_oss = 40 nC, V_SD = 0.8 V |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q, ~1 A source/sink, V_GS = 10 V |
| Modulation | 6-step trapezoidal, complementary (synchronous) PWM, f_sw = 48 kHz, dead time t_d = 100 ns |
| Board | 20×20 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz outer and inner power layers |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Which FETs conduct

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, current always flows through exactly **two FET channels in series**:

- one device of the PWM-ing leg: the high-side during D, the low-side during 1−D;
- the static low-side of the return phase.

Each FET carries current for 1/3 of the electrical period.

### 1.2 Equations

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /\text{K}$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \left(2R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{Cu} + R_{shunt}\right)$$

$$P_{cond,FET} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\cdot 2 \quad\text{(averaged over 6 FETs)}$$

### 1.3 Values at I = 80 A

| Term | Value | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| R_ds @ T_j = 125 °C | 1.5 mΩ | 2 × 80² × 1.5 m = **19.2 W** |
| Copper path R_Cu (pads, vias, planes) | ~0.4 mΩ | 2.56 W |
| Shunt (0.2 mΩ, single DC-link) | 0.2 mΩ | 1.28 W |
| **Total conduction per channel** | | **≈ 23.0 W** |
| **×4 channels (simultaneous burst)** | | **≈ 92 W** |

Per-FET average is about 6.4 W at 125 °C. This is already above the steady-state capability of a 3.3×3.3 DFN on a 20×20 board.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. Each switching event involves one hard-switched FET and one FET in dead-time diode conduction.

### 2.1 V–I overlap

Transition time is set by the Miller charge and the driver current:

$$t_r \approx t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_g} = \frac{10\,\text{nC}}{1\,\text{A}} \approx 10\,\text{ns} \;\;\rightarrow\;\; 20\,\text{ns each, including } Q_{gs2} \text{ and layout L}$$

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Body-diode reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V f_{sw} = 50\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.06\ \text{W}$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2} Q_{oss} V f_{sw} = 0.02\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time diode conduction

There are two dead-time intervals per period:

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD} \cdot I \cdot 2 t_d \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

Two FETs switch per period. This loss is dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors:

$$P_g = 2 Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 50\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.05\ \text{W}$$

### 2.6 Switching total

| | Loss |
|---|---|
| Per channel | **≈ 2.7 W** |
| ×4 channels | **≈ 10.7 W** |

**Sensitivity.** P_VI scales linearly with f_sw. Dropping to 24 kHz saves about 1.3 W per channel at 80 A, at the cost of current ripple and acoustic noise.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Total burst dissipation

$$P_{tot} = 4(23.0 + 2.7) \approx \mathbf{103\ W}$$

### 3.2 Transient network (Foster, per FET → board → air)

| Element | Value |
|---|---|
| Z_th,jc (3.3×3.3 DFN, 1 s pulse) | ≈ 1.2 K/W |
| Board thermal capacity, C_board | see below |
| R_th,board-air (20×20, prop-wash, ~10 m/s) | ≈ 8 K/W |

Board thermal capacity is built up as follows:

- **Copper:** 20×20 mm × 4 layers × 70 µm ≈ 112 mm³ ≈ 1.0 g, giving 0.39 J/K.
- **FR-4:** about 0.45 J/K.
- **24 FETs + passives:** about 0.35 J/K.
- **Total:** C_board ≈ 1.2 J/K.

### 3.3 Adiabatic board heating during a burst

$$\frac{dT}{dt} \approx \frac{P_{tot}}{C_{board}} = \frac{103}{1.2} \approx \mathbf{86\ K/s}$$

The junction runs above the board by:

$$\Delta T_{jc} \approx P_{FET} \cdot Z_{th,jc} \approx (6.4 + 0.9) \times 1.2 \approx 9\ \text{K}$$

### 3.4 Burst duration limit

Starting at T_board = 50 °C with T_j,max = 150 °C, the allowable board rise is about 90 K:

$$t_{burst,max} \approx \frac{90}{86} \approx \mathbf{1.0\ s}\ \text{(all 4 motors at 80 A)}$$

Iterate this calculation, because R_ds rises with temperature and makes the result slightly worse.

### 3.5 Steady-state capability

$$I_{cont} = \sqrt{\frac{(T_{max} - T_{amb})/R_{th,ba} - 4P_{sw}}{4 \cdot 2R_{ds} \cdot 1.25}} \approx \sqrt{\frac{100/8 - 4}{0.015}} \approx \mathbf{24\ A\ per\ channel}$$

### 3.6 Recommended rating

| Rating | Value |
|---|---|
| Continuous | 25 A per channel |
| Burst | 45 A for 10 s |
| Absolute peak | 80 A for ≤ 0.5 s |
