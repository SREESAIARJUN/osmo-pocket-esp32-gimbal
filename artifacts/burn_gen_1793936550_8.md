## 0. Architectural Assumptions and Scope

An STM32H743 cannot directly commutate 4 BLDC motors with the bidirectional DShot plus BEMF timing that AM32 or BLHeli_32 provide. The standard (and only practical) architecture is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot FC)  ──DShot600 BDShot──►  4× ESC MCU (AM32 on AT32F421K8U7)
                                                   └─► 4× FD6288Q gate driver ─► 24× N-MOSFET
```

All loss figures below depend on these baseline parameters. Replace them with your actual BOM values.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

### 1.1 Topology current paths
In each 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct:
- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) conducts for duty *D*. The complementary low-side (LS) conducts for (1−D), because synchronous rectification is used instead of the body diode.
- **Return leg:** the LS is held ON for 100% of the sector.

Instantaneous conduction loss per motor is therefore:

$$
P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

This result is independent of D, which is why complementary PWM is mandatory at 80 A.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0053\ /\mathrm{K} \Rightarrow k_T(100^\circ C)\approx 1.4
$$
