## 0. Architecture Corrections

Several requirements in the brief conflict with each other or with how this hardware works. The rest of the document depends on these decisions.

| Issue | Reality | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| "STM32H743 4-in-1 ESC" | The H743 runs ArduPilot (the flight controller). It does not commutate motors. A 4-in-1 ESC uses its own MCU(s), typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. | H743 is the FC. The ESC uses 4× AT32F421 running AM32. |
| "48 kHz switching" | This is ESC firmware PWM. It has no relation to the FC or to DShot. | Set in AM32 (`PWM_FREQ = 48 kHz`). |
| "80 A burst" on a nano board | Section 3 shows that a 20×20 mm stack cannot sustain 80 A/channel for more than about 1 s. | Rate it as **80 A for ≤1 s, 30 A continuous** (with prop-wash airflow), and use a 30×30 mm ESC footprint. |
| H743 on 20×20 | LQFP100 is 14×14 mm, which leaves no room for routing. | Use **STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100, 8×8 mm)**. |

Target topology: a two-board stack. The FC (20×20 or 30×30, H743) connects through an 8-pin JST-SH to the 4-in-1 ESC (30×30, 6S).

## 2. Loss Model (per ESC channel, one 3-phase bridge, 6 FETs)

### 2.1 Conduction Topology

With trapezoidal 6-step commutation and complementary (synchronous) PWM, motor current always flows through exactly two MOSFETs:

- **Active state (duty D):** high side of leg A, then low side of leg B.
- **Freewheel (1−D):** low side of leg A (synchronous rectifier), then low side of leg B.

So the conducting resistance is always 2·R_ds(on), independent of D:

$$
P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds(on)}(T_j), \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I_{pk}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}
$$

**Ripple.** With motor phase-to-phase inductance L ≈ 15 µH (typical 2207 motor) at D = 0.5:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus} D(1-D)}{L f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \cdot 0.25}{15\times10^{-6}\cdot 48\times10^{3}} = 8.75\ \text{A}
$$

$$
I_{rms}^2 = 6400 + 6.4 = 6406\ \text{A}^2 \quad (\text{ripple contributes } 0.1\%)
$$

**Hot conduction loss** (R_ds = 1.2 × 1.6 = 1.92 mΩ):

$$
P_{cond} = 6406 \times 2 \times 1.92\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{24.6\ W}
$$
