# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #44): System Architecture and Mathematical Model

## 0. Architectural Clarifications and Feasibility Flags

**System partitioning.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU, running ArduPilot. It does not commutate motors. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32. The 48 kHz PWM is an ESC-firmware parameter. DShot600 is the FC-to-ESC link. All models below follow this split.

**Feasibility flags.** These are what a design review would raise first:

| Issue | Finding |
|---|---|
| 80 A × 4 channels simultaneously | 320 A battery current. Two XT60s or an XT90 are needed. The battery-side shunt alone dissipates about 10 W at 0.1 mΩ. |
| Nano (20×20 mm) at 80 A | Steady-state operation is thermally impossible (see §2.4). "80 A" can only mean a short single-channel burst of about 1–2 s. |
| Gate driver | An FD6288-class driver (weak source current) dominates switching loss at 48 kHz. |
