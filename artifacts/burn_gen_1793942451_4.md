# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal and DShot Architecture

**Scope and assumptions.** Values marked *(assume)* are design inputs to confirm against your chosen parts' datasheets. Part numbers are candidates and must be verified for availability and footprint.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Bus voltage | 6S, V_bus = 25.2 V max (22.2 V nominal) |
| Burst current | 80 A per channel, phase current during conduction |
| ESC PWM frequency | f_sw = 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous) switching |
| Control split | H743 = flight controller only. Each motor runs on its own ESC MCU (AM32 on AT32F421, 4×). The H743 does not commutate motors. |

> **Feasibility flag (read first).** The math below shows that 80 A on all four channels cannot be sustained on a 20×20 board. It is a **sub-second to ~1 s burst rating**. Specifying "80 A burst" without a duration and duty cycle is the single biggest risk on this job. I recommend we publish **"80 A / 1 s, 1 motor; 4× 45 A / 5 s"** or move to 30×30.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology
Six-step (trapezoidal) commutation with synchronous rectification:

- At every instant, phase current flows through exactly **one high-side path and one low-side path**.
- During PWM off-time, current freewheels through the complementary FET's channel, not its diode, except during dead time.

So the series conduction resistance per channel is independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot \left(2R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + R_{shunt} + R_{Cu}\right)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\left[1+\alpha (T_j-25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

### 1.3 Representative MOSFET
40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3 mm. Six per channel gives 24 total, which is the only package class that fits a nano board.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| R_DS(on),25 @ V_GS = 10 V | 1.5 mΩ *(assume)* |
| R_DS(on) @ T_j = 125 °C | 1.5 × 1.6 = **2.4 mΩ** |
| Q_g | 40 nC |
| Q_rr | 30 nC |
| Q_oss | 25 nC |
| V_SD | 0.8 V |
| R_θJC (bottom) | 1.5 K/W |

### 1.4 Parasitics
- **R_Cu:** 0.3 mΩ for the phase pad, vias and bus planes on 2 oz inner layers *(assume)*
- **R_shunt:** 0.25 mΩ, shared bus shunt. It sees DC-link current, not phase current. Its loss is roughly I²·D·R and is booked separately.

### 1.5 At 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

$$P_{cond,ch} = 80^2 (2\cdot 0.0024 + 0.0003) = 6400 \times 0.0051 = \mathbf{32.6\ W}$$

Of this:
- **15.4 W** is in each conducting FET. This is the junction hotspot driver.
- **1.9 W** is in copper.
- Shunt at D = 1: 6400 × 0.00025 = **1.6 W**

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Per PWM period, one leg hard-switches: the high-side turns on and off, and the low-side commutates softly.

### 2.1 V–I overlap

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

With t_r + t_f = 20 ns (FD6288-class driver, ~1.5 Ω gate path):

$$P_{VI} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 20\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery (body diode of the synchronous FET)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{n}\cdot 25.2\cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.036\ W}$$

Hard-recovery snap also causes ringing. This sets the snubber and layout requirement in §5.

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.5\cdot 25\text{n}\cdot 25.2\cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.015\ W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction
Two dead-time intervals per period, t_dt = 100 ns *(AM32 configurable)*:

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD} I \cdot 2 t_{dt} f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot 80\cdot 200\text{n}\cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

Dropping t_dt to 50 ns halves this. Validate shoot-through margin with a scope at 25.2 V.

### 2.5 Gate drive
Dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors, not the FETs:

$$P_g = 2 Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2\cdot 40\text{n}\cdot 10\cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.038\ W}$$
