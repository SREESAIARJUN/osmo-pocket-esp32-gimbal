## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Architectural Flags

### 0.1 Architecture clarification (important)

On a "4-in-1 ESC" system the **48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC's own MCUs** (AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32), **not by the STM32H743**. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends **bi-directional DShot600** to the ESC MCU(s). The analysis is partitioned as follows:

| Domain | Owner | Analysed in |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction + switching losses @ 48 kHz | ESC MCU + gate drivers + MOSFETs | §1–§3 |
| Thermal (burst) | Shared PCB / stack | §4 |
| BDShot600 timer/DMA | STM32H743 / ArduPilot | §5 |
| Netlist | Both boards | §6 |

### 0.3 Red flag: "nano" form factor vs. 80 A

A "nano" (20×20 mm) or even 25.5×25.5 mm stack **cannot thermally sustain 4 × 80 A** under any realistic airflow. §4 quantifies this: 80 A is a seconds-scale burst rating only. I strongly recommend a **two-board stack (FC separated from ESC)** at ≥30.5×30.5 mm. Co-locating the H743 (T_j,max 125 °C for the industrial grade; the commercial grade is 105 °C ambient) on the same copper as 24 MOSFETs is a design-review blocker.

## 1. Current Waveform Model (Trapezoidal / 6-step BLDC)

During each 60° electrical commutation interval, two phases conduct. With complementary PWM at duty D on the high-side (HS) of the "active" phase and the low-side (LS) of the return phase held fully on:

| Switch | Conducts | Duty fraction |
|---|---|---|
| HS of PWM'd phase | I | D |
| LS of PWM'd phase (sync rectifier) | I | (1 − D) − 2t_dt·f_sw |
| Body diode of LS (dead time) | I | 2t_d
