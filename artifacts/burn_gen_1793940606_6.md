## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture correction
In a 4-in-1 ESC, the STM32H743 does **not** drive the MOSFETs. The ESC has its own MCU(s) running AM32 or BLHeli_32, which perform commutation and generate the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the ESC through bidirectional DShot600. The design therefore splits into two domains:

| Domain | MCU | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743 at 480 MHz | ArduPilot, DShot600 TX, eRPM capture via input capture, harmonic notch |
| ESC, one MCU per motor | AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, BEMF ZC, telemetry reply |

### 0.2 Form-factor flag
"Nano" usually means a 20×20 mm stack. **80 A burst per channel is not thermally credible on 20×20.** This model therefore assumes:
- a 30.5×30.5 mm mounting pattern,
- a 41×41 mm board,
- an 8-layer board with 2 oz power planes.

If 20×20 is mandatory, derate to roughly 35–40 A burst. Section 3 provides the scaling.
