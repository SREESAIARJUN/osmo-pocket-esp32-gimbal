# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC (Job #16): Design Model

## 0. Feasibility Findings

Three findings below change the architecture.

| # | Issue | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. Production 4-in-1 ESCs run a dedicated MCU per channel (AT32F421 / STM32G071 class, AM32 or BLHeli_32 firmware) because sensorless BEMF timing needs its own comparator and timers. | The H743 is the flight controller. It talks to 4 ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. The design below assumes this. |
| F2 | 80A × 4 on a nano (~26×26 mm) PCB is a transient rating only. §3 shows a ~3 s all-motor limit and about 22A continuous per channel. | The datasheet must state "80A burst, ≤ X s, duty-limited". Firm
