## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop (innermost, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle from the encoder

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

θ_off is found at commissioning: apply V_d with V_q = 0 and θ_e = 0, wait for the rotor to settle, then record θ_m.

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms

Clarke (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.3 Plant and current-loop PI design

The PMSM in the d-q frame is:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

**Decoupling.** Add a feed-forward term so each axis becomes a first-order plant 1/(Ls + R):

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

**Pole-zero cancellation.** Choose the PI zero to cancel the plant pole, giving a closed-loop bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c\quad (\text{i.e. } K_i/K_p = R/L)
$$

**Bandwidth limit.** With a 20 kHz loop plus 1.5 samples of PWM and computation delay, use ω_c ≤ 2π·f_s/20 ≈ 2π·1 kHz.

**Voltage limiting.** Clamp the voltage vector magnitude:

$$
\|(v_d, v_q)\| \le \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}
$$

Inside this circle, SVPWM is linear. Use back-calculation anti-windup (§2.3).

### 1.4 Inverse Park and SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\quad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

$$
v_a=v_\alpha,\; v_b=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\; v_c=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12\left(\max(v_a,v_b,v_c)+\min(v_a,v_b,v_c)\right),\qquad
D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

**Delay compensation.** Advance the angle used in the inverse Park by 1.5 sample periods:

$$
\theta_e^{\text{out}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s
$$

## 2. Cascaded Angle → Velocity → Current Control

### 2.1 Velocity estimation (encoder PLL observer)

Differentiating a 14-bit encoder directly is too noisy, so use a type-II PLL. Define the wrapped phase error:

$$
e = \operatorname{wrap}_{\pm\pi}(\theta_m - \hat\theta)
$$

Update equations:

$$
\hat\omega_{k+1} = \hat\omega_k + K_i^{pll} T_s\, e,\qquad
\hat\theta_{k+1} = \hat\theta_k + T_s(\hat\omega_{k+1} + K_p^{pll} e)
$$

Gains:

$$
K_p^{pll} = 2\zeta\omega_n,\qquad K_i^{pll}=\omega_n^2
$$

Use ζ = 1 and ω_n ≈ 2π·300 rad/s.

### 2.2 Loop structure and bandwidth separation

```
θ_ref ─►[Angle PI + ω_ff]─► ω_ref ─►[Velocity PI]─► i_q,ref ─(+ i_q,cog + i_q,ff)─►[Current PI]─► SVPWM
          ▲ θ̂ (IMU or encoder)        ▲ ω̂ (gyro + PLL blend)
```

| Loop | Equation |
|---|---|
| Velocity | $i_q^{ref} = K_{pv}\,e_\omega + K_{iv}\!\int e_\omega\,dt$ |
| Angle | $\omega^{ref} = K_{p\theta}\,e_\theta + K_{i\theta}\!\int e_\theta\,dt + \dot\theta^{ref}$ |

Errors are e_ω = ω_ref − ω̂ and e_θ = wrap(θ_ref − θ̂).

**Velocity PI gains.** The mechanical plant is ω/i_q = K_t/(Js + B). For a velocity bandwidth ω_v:

$$
K_{pv} = \frac{J\,\omega_v}{K_t},\qquad K_{iv} = \frac{K_{pv}\,\omega_v}{4}\;\;(\text{PI zero a factor 4 below crossover, }\approx 60^\circ\text{ PM})
$$

**Angle gains.**

$$
K_{p\theta} = \omega_\theta,\qquad K_{i\theta}=K_{p\theta}\,\omega_\theta/10
$$

The small integral term rejects static disturbances such as cable drag and imbalance.
