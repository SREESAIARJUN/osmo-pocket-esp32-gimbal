# Job #43: 3-Axis Gimbal FOC Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion

This specification covers:

- the FOC current loop,
- cascaded angle/velocity control in the inertial frame,
- anti-cogging feedforward with online learning,
- dual ICM-42688-P vibration rejection and attitude estimation,
- a complete C++17 implementation.
