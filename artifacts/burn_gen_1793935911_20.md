# STM32H743 AIO + 4-in-1 ESC, 80 A Burst: Loss Model, Thermal Budget, Bi-directional DShot600 Allocation, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

### Architecture
A 4-in-1 ESC does not run its power stages from the STM32H743. The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. Four dedicated ESC MCUs (AM32 on STM32G071 or AT32F421, or BLHeli_32) generate the 48 kHz PWM. This document models both sides and the interface between them.

### Baseline parameters
No MOSFET or driver part numbers were specified, so the worked numbers use a representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN FET. Replace the values in the table with your datasheet values. The equations stay valid.

> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag (summarized here, derived in §3):** 80 A on all four channels at once dissipates about 90 W into a nano-class PCB. That load can only be sustained for roughly 1–2 s without heatsinking. Treat "80 A burst" as a time-limited rating, and confirm in the spec whether 80 A is per channel or total.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Conducting path
In 6-step drive with synchronous rectification, exactly two FETs carry the phase current at all times:
- During PWM-on: the high-side (HS) FET of the driven phase plus the low-side (LS) FET of the return phase.
- During PWM-off: the LS FET of the driven phase (freewheel) plus the same LS FET of the return phase.

Including inductor ripple, the RMS current is

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

For a typical 2–5 µH motor at 48 kHz, ΔI_pp is about 10–20 A. The ripple term is then under 1 % of I² and can be neglected at 80 A.

### 1.3 Conduction loss per channel

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Each of the six FETs carries current 1/3 of the electrical cycle, so the per-FET average is

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.4 Electrothermal feedback

Losses raise junction temperature, which raises R_ds. Coupling the two (θ = effective thermal resistance or impedance):

$$T_j = T_a + \theta\,(P_0 + P_{sw}), \qquad P_0 = 2I^2R_{ds25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

$$\Rightarrow\; T_j = \frac{T_a + \theta\,[\,2I^2R_{ds25}(1-25\alpha) + P_{sw}\,]}{1 - 2\,\theta\,\alpha\,I^2R_{ds25}}$$

**Stability (thermal runaway) criterion:** the denominator must stay positive.

$$\theta < \frac{1}{2\alpha I^2 R_{ds25}} = \frac{1}{2(0.0055)(6400)(0.0013)} = 10.9\ \text{K/W per channel}$$

A nano board with no airflow typically sits at 8–15 K/W per channel to ambient. **Continuous 80 A is therefore at or beyond the runaway limit**, which is why the rating must be a burst rating.

### 1.5 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per channel | P per FET (average) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.30 mΩ | 16.6 W | 2.8 W |
| 100 °C | 1.84 mΩ | 23.5 W | 3.9 W |
| 125 °C | 2.02 mΩ | 25.8 W | 4.3 W |

### 1.6 Copper and interconnect loss (often omitted, but not negligible)

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 (R_{pour} + R_{pad} + R_{solder\ joint})$$

- 0.3 mΩ of phase pour plus pads adds about 1.9 W per channel at 80 A.
- Battery pad to bus plane carries the summed DC current, I_bus ≈ Σ(D·I). It needs ≥ 4 oz equivalent copper (multiple stacked layers plus via stitching).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-modulated leg switches. That is one hard-switched transition pair per PWM period per channel.
