# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC: 80 A Burst Thermal, 48 kHz Switching, and Bidirectional DShot600 Model

## 0. Three Architecture Corrections

Three points in the brief would cause a failed design if taken literally.

| Brief says | Engineering reality | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| H743 drives the 4-in-1 ESC | Four motors in sensorless 6-step need 24 gate signals, 4 independent commutation state machines and back-EMF comparators. The H743 has only TIM1 and TIM8 as advanced timers (3 complementary pairs each), so it can drive 2 motors cleanly. | **H743 = flight controller.** The ESC uses **4 dedicated MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32), connected over DShot. |
| 80 A burst on a "nano" (20×20 / 25×25 mm) | At 80 A × 4 the board dissipates about 104 W. Section 3.4 shows the board survives that for about 2.8 s. | Spec it as an **80 A, ≤1 s burst per channel**. Continuous rating is about **22–25 A per channel**. |
| "Bidirectional DShot DMA" | On the H7, the DMAMUX removes fixed stream mapping. The limit is stream *count* and contention with the IMU SPI, not request routing. | Section 4 gives the budget. |

All component values below are **representative**. Re-run the model with your actual MOSFET datasheet (R_DS(on) vs. T_j, Q_gd, Q_rr) before release.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation with complementary PWM, every instant has exactly **two MOSFETs in series** carrying the phase current I:

- **PWM on (D):** high-side of leg A, plus static low-side of leg B.
- **PWM off (1−D):** low-side of leg A (synchronous freewheel), plus static low-side of leg B.

The conduction loss is therefore **independent of duty cycle**:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 2.2 Electro-thermal fixed point

R_DS(on) depends on T_j, which depends on loss. Iterate the pair below:

$$T_j = T_{board} + \frac{P_{cond}}{6}R_{\theta,j\text{-}b}$$

Using T_board = 90 °C, R_θ,j-b = 4 K/W per FET, and a starting guess of T_j = 125 °C:

| Iteration | R_DS(on) | P_cond (2 FETs) | Avg per FET | T_j |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1.50 mΩ | 19.2 W | 3.2 W | 102.8 °C |
