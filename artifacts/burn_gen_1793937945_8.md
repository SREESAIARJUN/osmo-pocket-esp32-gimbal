# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Two Architectural Corrections

I don't have the original Job #70 requirements, so every input is stated explicitly. Replace the numbers in the parameter table with your real BOM and the equations will carry through.

**Correction 1: the H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1 design, each motor channel has its own small ESC MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM and the 6-step commutation. The H743 runs ArduPilot and only sends DShot600 frames. So the 48 kHz switching model below lives in the ESC MCU domain, and the DMA/timer model lives in the H743 domain.

**Correction 2: "80 A burst" on a nano form factor is thermally constrained, not electrically constrained.** The math below shows the FETs and copper can carry 80 A, but the board's thermal mass limits burst duration to seconds. Section 3 quantifies this, and it should drive the marketing rating.
