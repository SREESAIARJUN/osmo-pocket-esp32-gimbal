## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R = 5.6 Ω, L = 2.8 mH, Kt ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Commutation encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A-class) on each motor shaft |
| Inline current sensing | 2 phases, sampled synchronously at PWM center |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera payload (IMU-A and IMU-B), SPI, 8 kHz ODR |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with FPU (e.g., STM32G474 or H7) |

**Loop rates and target bandwidths**

```
 20 kHz  ── Current loop (FOC, d/q PI)                     BW ≈ 1.5–2 kHz
  4 kHz  ── Rate loop (fused gyro), cogging feed-forward   BW ≈ 150–250 Hz
  1 kHz  ── Attitude loop (quaternion error → rate cmd)    BW ≈ 20–40 Hz
  8 kHz  ── IMU sampling + vibration filter chain (decimated to 4 kHz)
```

Each loop's bandwidth is at least 5× slower than the loop inside it. This lets the inner loop be treated as unity gain when tuning the outer loop.

**Signal flow for one axis**

```
 q_ref ─►[Attitude P]─► ω_ref ─►(+)─►[Rate PID]─►(+)─► iq_ref ─►[FOC current PI]─► SVPWM ─► Motor

                       ω_cam (fused gyro)   iq_cog(θm)          ia, ib, θe (encoder)
                                 ▲
             IMU-A ─►[AAF→LPF→Notch]─┐
                                     ├─►[Dual fusion + fault detect]─► Mahony AHRS ─► q_cam
             IMU-B ─►[AAF→LPF→Notch]─┘
```

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Clarke and Park transforms

For a balanced machine, ia + ib + ic = 0.

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix},
\qquad \theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}})
$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI design (pole-zero cancellation)

The d/q-axis voltage equations are:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

After decoupling, each axis reduces to the first-order plant G(s) = 1/(Ls + R). Choose the PI controller C(s) = Kp + Ki/s with:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c
$$

This cancels the plant pole at −R/L. The closed loop then becomes first-order with bandwidth ω_c:
