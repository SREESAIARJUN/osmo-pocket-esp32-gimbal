> **Feasibility warning:** an 80 A per-channel burst on a nano board is aggressive. The thermal model in §3 shows it is **burst-only**, roughly 10 s with all four channels loaded. Publish the rating as **"40 A continuous / 80 A 10 s burst"**, not 80 A continuous.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At the design point T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS}(110) = 0.9\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.51 = 1.36\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous PWM

During any 60° electrical sector, phase A carries PWM (high side for duty D, low side for 1−D) and phase B's low side is held on for 100% of the sector.

- If dead time is neglected, the current always flows through exactly two channels in series. One is the HS or LS of the PWM leg; the other is the fixed LS.
- The conduction loss per ESC channel is therefore independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,FET} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,FET} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.36\,\text{m}\Omega = 6400 \times 2.72\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{17.4\ W}$$

### 1.3 Copper / interconnect loss

Assume a phase pad plus bus-plane path of about 0.3 mΩ per channel (2 oz outer, 2 oz inner, stitched):

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.3\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{1.9\ W}$$

### 1.4 Current-sense shunt

The shunt is in the common return: 0.25 mΩ total across 4 channels in parallel paths, about 1 mΩ equivalent per channel.

- Full model: P_shunt = (ΣI)² × R_shunt = (320 A)² × 0.25 mΩ = **25.6 W**.
