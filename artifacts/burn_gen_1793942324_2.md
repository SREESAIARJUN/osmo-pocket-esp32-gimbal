# STM32H743 + 4-in-1 80 A ESC Stack: Design Model (Job #20)

I don't have prior documents for Job #20, so this package rests on the **baseline assumptions in §0**. Every result is shown as a formula first and then evaluated, so you can substitute your actual BOM values. Items I'm uncertain about are flagged rather than presented as settled.

**Three findings change the architecture:**

1. **Board size.** A true 20×20 "nano" board cannot carry 80 A on four channels. This model assumes a 30.5×30.5 mm ESC board stacked under the FC.
2. **Burst duration.** 80 A on all four channels at once is a seconds-scale burst, about 4 s with prop-wash cooling. Only one channel at 80 A is thermally sustainable.
3. **Commutation controller.** The H743 runs ArduPilot only. Commutation runs on four dedicated ESC MCUs, because ArduPilot does not do sensorless BLDC commutation on the FC MCU.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology

With six-step (trapezoidal) commutation and complementary PWM, current always flows through exactly two channels in series:
- the high-side switch of the sourcing phase, or its synchronous low-side complement during the off-time, and
- the low-side switch of the sinking phase.

So the instantaneous conduction loss per ESC channel is:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]$$

### 1.2 Per-FET distribution

Each phase leg is active for 2/3 of the electrical period:
- the sourcing portion is split between HS (duty D) and LS (1−D),
- the sinking portion is all LS.

Averaged over one electrical cycle, per FET:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} D\, I^2 R_{DS},\qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)\, I^2 R_{DS} + \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS}$$

The low side is the hot device. At D → 0.5 it carries 3× the high-side conduction loss. Put the better FETs and more thermal vias on the low side.
