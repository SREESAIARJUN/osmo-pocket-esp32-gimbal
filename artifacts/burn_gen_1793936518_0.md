# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #9): Thermal, Switching, and DShot Design Package

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Feasibility Notes

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel runs its own MCU (AT32F421 running AM32 is assumed), and the H743 talks to it over bidirectional DShot600. The models below separate the two domains.

**Feasibility.** A full 80 A burst on all four channels at once means about 290 A from the battery at high throttle. That exceeds XT60/XT90 connectors and nano-class (20×20 / 25.5×25.5) copper. The model therefore treats "80 A burst" as **per-channel phase current lasting about 1–3 s**. Section 3.4 shows the math that forces this.
