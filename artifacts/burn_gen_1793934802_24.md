**Rev A: Engineering Derivation and Reference Netlist**

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Architecture Decisions

An H743 cannot practically run four sensorless FOC/6-step loops while also running ArduPilot, and ArduPilot has no native ESC firmware. The design is therefore partitioned as follows:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (rev V, 480 MHz) | ArduPilot; outputs bidirectional DShot600 |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) running AM32 | 6-step commutation at 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drivers (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap, 250 V) | 6 gates per motor |
| Power stage (×24 FETs) | 40 V N-ch, 3.3×3.3 mm PDFN, R<sub>DS(on)</sub> ≤ 1.3 mΩ max at 25 °C | 6S (25.2 V max) |
| Form factor | 30.5×30.5 mm mounting, ~36×36 mm board, 8-layer, 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner power planes | "Nano" class |

**Key assumptions.** Change these and re-run the equations.

* V<sub>bus</sub> = 25.2 V (6S, full charge).
* "80 A burst" means **80 A RMS phase current per motor**.
* T<sub>amb</sub> = 40 °C and T<sub>j,max</sub> design limit = 150 °C (175 °C absolute).

> ⚠ **Headline finding (Section 3):** 80 A/motor on a 30.5 mm board is a **~1.5–2.5 s burst rating**, not a continuous one. Thermally honest continuous current is **~25–35 A/motor**, depending on prop-wash airflow. Marketing must not publish "80 A" without a duration qualifier.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
In 6-step synchronous-rectified commutation, two phases conduct at any instant. During the PWM on-time the path is HS(A) → motor → LS(B). During the off-time it is LS(A, synchronous) → motor → LS(B). The current therefore always traverses **exactly two FETs** (excluding dead time):

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

Per-FET RMS current, with each phase conducting 2/3 of the electrical cycle:

$$I_{HS,rms} = I_{ph}\sqrt{\tfrac{D}{3}},\qquad I_{LS,rms}=I_{ph}\sqrt{\tfrac{1-D}{3}+\tfrac{1}{3}}$$

Note that the LS also carries the "B-side" sector.

### 1.2 Temperature coefficient
$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j)=R_{25}\,\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha\approx0.0045\text{–}0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

With R<sub>25,max</sub> = 1.3 mΩ, α = 0.006 and T<sub>j</sub> = 125 °C:
$$R_{DS(on)} = 1.3(1+0.6) = 2.08\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Ripple correction
Phase inductance for typical 5" motors (2207, 1750 KV) is L<sub>ph</sub> ≈ 15 µH line-to-line. Worst case is D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2\cdot0.25}{15\mu\cdot48k}=8.75\ \text{A}$$
$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{dc}^2+\frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400+6.4 \Rightarrow \text{+0.1 \% (negligible)}$$

### 1.4 Result at 80 A
$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\cdot80^2\cdot2.08\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{26.6\ W}$$

The worst single FET is the LS of the active-B phase, at ≈ **13.3 W** peak instantaneous and ≈ 8.9 W averaged over commutation sectors.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f<sub>sw</sub> = 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing high-side FET is hard-switched. The synchronous LS turns on after dead time via its body diode, giving quasi-ZVS.

### 2.1 Overlap (V–I crossover) loss
Example FET datasheet values: Q<sub>gs2</sub> = 4 nC, Q<sub>gd</sub> = 8 nC, V<sub>plateau</sub> = 3.5 V, R<sub>g,int</sub> = 1.0 Ω. The FD6288Q drives at V<sub>drv</sub> = 10 V with R<sub>g,ext</sub> = 2.2 Ω on and 1 Ω off via a diode.

$$I_{g,on}=\frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{drv,on}+R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}}=\frac{6.5}{0.5+2.2+1.0}\approx1.76\ \text{A}$$
$$I_{g,off}=\frac{V_{pl}}{R_{drv,off}+R_{g,off}+R_{g,int}}=\frac{3.5}{0.5+1.0+1.0}=1.40\ \text{A}$$
$$t_{sw}=\frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_g}\Rightarrow t_{on}=6.8\text{ ns},\ t_{off}=8.6\text{ ns}$$
