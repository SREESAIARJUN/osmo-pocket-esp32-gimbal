## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, assuming $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant model and PI design by pole-zero cancellation

The per-axis plant is

$$G_i(s)=\frac{1}{Ls+R}$$

Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole:

$$K_{p,i}=L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i}=R\,\omega_c$$

The open loop is then $\omega_c/s$, and the closed loop is first order:

$$T(s)=\frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

With the assumed numbers: $K_{p,i}=19.8\ \text{V/A}$ and $K_{i,i}=52{,}779\ \text{V/(A·s)}$.

### 1.5 Decoupling feedforward

Uses the electrical speed $\omega_e = p\,\omega_m$ and flux linkage $\lambda_m = \tfrac{2K_t}{3p}$:

$$v_d^{ff}=-\omega_e L\, i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff}=\omega_e L\, i_d+\omega_e\lambda_m$$

### 1.6 Voltage vector limiting

The linear SVPWM limit is $V_{max}=V_{bus}/\sqrt3$.

- **Priority to d-axis:** $v_d$ is clamped to $\pm V_{max}$, and then $|v_q|\le\sqrt{V_{max}^2-v_d^2}$.
- **Anti-windup:** back-calculation with gain $K_{aw}=1/K_p$.
