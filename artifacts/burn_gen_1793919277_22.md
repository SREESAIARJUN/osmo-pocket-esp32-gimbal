## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology current paths

In 6-step commutation, two phase legs conduct at any instant:

- **PWM leg:** the high side conducts for fraction D. The low side synchronously rectifies for (1 − D).
- **Return leg:** one low side is on for 100% of the commutation interval.

Phase current flows continuously, so:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 R_{hot}\big[D + (1-D)\big] + I_{ph}^2 R_{hot} = 2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{hot}$$

**Key result:** conduction loss is independent of duty cycle in synchronous 6-step. It depends only on phase current.

### 1.2 Numbers at 80 A

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.4\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{30.7\ W\ per\ motor\ channel}$$

**Peak loss in a single FET** (the return-leg low side during its 120° conduction window):

$$P_{FET,peak} = 80^2 \times 2.4\,\text{m}\Omega = 15.4\ \text{W}$$

**Electrical-cycle average per FET** (6 FETs per channel; switching losses from §2.6 included):

$$\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{34.4}{6} \approx 5.7\ \text{W}$$

The average is valid because the electrical period is much shorter than the die thermal time constant. For a 14-pole motor at 30 krpm, f_e = 3.5 kHz, so T_e ≈ 286 µs. The die's τ_th is in the ms range.
