## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Design Flags

### 0.1 Architecture

- The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to the ESC section.
- The ESC section uses 4× AT32F421 MCUs running AM32. Each one generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM for its motor.
- A single-MCU-per-4-motor approach is possible, but AM32 and BLHeli_32 tooling assume one MCU per motor.

### 0.3 Design Flags (read before committing)

1. **80 A is a 2–3 second burst rating, not a continuous one.** The thermal model in §4 shows a nano-format board can sustain about 40 A per motor continuously, and only with prop-wash airflow.
2. **4 × 80 A at full throttle is 320 A of battery current.** XT60 connectors, 12 AWG leads, and most 6S packs cannot deliver that. Realistic simultaneous peaks are 150–200 A. Size the main power path for the real peak and state the burst rating per motor.
3. **Bus capacitance is a hard requirement.** Input ripple current reaches tens of amps RMS (§2.5). An external low-ESR electrolytic of at least 1000 µF / 35 V on short leads is mandatory, in addition to on-board MLCCs and a TVS diode.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation Topology

The motors use six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM).

- At any instant, current flows through one high-side and one low-side path.
- During PWM on-time: high-side FET of phase A, then the motor winding, then low-side FET of phase B.
- During PWM off-time: low-side FET of phase A (synchronous freewheel), then the winding, then low-side FET of phase B.

Because synchronous rectification is used, the current always sees two R_DS(on) in series, regardless of duty cycle D.

### 1.2 Per-Motor Conduction Loss

Hot R_DS(on):

R_DS(on)(T_j) = R_25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)], with α ≈ 0.006 /K, which gives k_T ≈ 1.6 at 125 °C.

Total per-motor conduction loss:

P_cond = I² · (2 · R_DS(on)(T_j)) + I² · (R_cu + R_sh)

At 80 A and T_j = 125 °C:

- MOSFET term: 6400 × 2 × 1.6 mΩ = **20.48 W**
- Copper and shunt term: 6400 × 0.5 mΩ = **3.20 W**
- **Total P_cond ≈ 23.7 W per motor**

### 1.3 Per-FET Distribution

Each FET is in its conduction role for one third of the electrical cycle (120°). The duty-dependent split between high and low side is:

- High side: P_HS = (1/3) · D · I² · R_DS(on)
- Low side: P_LS = (1/3) · (2 − D) · I² · R_DS(on)

The low-side FET also carries the freewheel current. The worst case is low-side FETs at low duty with high current, for example during hard deceleration or under active braking.

At D ≈ 1 (full burst):

- P_HS ≈ P_LS ≈ 6400 × 1.6 mΩ / 3 ≈ **3.4 W per FET**
