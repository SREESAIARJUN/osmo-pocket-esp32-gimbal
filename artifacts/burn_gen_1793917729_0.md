## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (e.g. GBM2804-class), pole pairs **p = 7**, R ≈ 5.6 Ω (phase), L ≈ 1.8 mH (phase) |
| Torque constant | K_t ≈ 0.08 N·m/A (measure it, §2.6) |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A/MT6701-class), SPI, read every current-loop cycle |
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7-class with FPU, center-aligned PWM, ADC triggered at PWM center |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the **camera plate**, mounted symmetric about the pivot, SPI @ 24 MHz, 8 kHz ODR |
| Supply | 12–16 V |

**Loop rates and bandwidths**

| Loop | Rate | Target bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current (d/q) | 20 kHz | ~1.5–2 kHz |
| Velocity (rate) | 4 kHz | ~150–200 Hz |
| Angle (attitude) | 1 kHz | ~30–50 Hz |
| IMU sampling | 8 kHz, decimated to 4 kHz | |

Each outer loop runs at least 4–5× slower than the loop inside it. This keeps the loops dynamically decoupled.

**Cascade structure (per axis):**

```
θ_ref ──►[Angle P(I)]──ω_ref──►[Rate PI + LPF]──i_q*──►[Σ]──►[Current PI d/q]──►SVPWM──►Motor
             ▲                    ▲                     ▲  ▲
        θ_cam (IMU fusion)   ω_cam (filtered gyro)   i_cog_ff  i_ff(ω̇_ref)
```

For a stabilizing gimbal, the outer loops close on **inertial camera attitude and rate**, which come from the IMUs. They do not close on the encoder. The encoder is used for commutation, cogging compensation, and back-EMF decoupling.

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Clarke and Park transforms

The current loop uses the amplitude-invariant form with two-shunt or three-shunt sensing:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt{3}}\qquad (i_a+i_b+i_c=0)
$$

The electrical angle is computed from the mechanical angle:

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{m,0}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

The Park transform rotates into the rotor frame:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Latency compensation.** The voltage computed in cycle *k* is applied during cycle *k+1*. Rotate the inverse Park by a 1.5-sample advance:

$$
\theta_e^{inv} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s
$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI tuning by pole-zero cancellation

The PMSM model in the dq frame is:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Use a PI controller in parallel form, $C(s)=K_p + K_i/s$:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

The integrator zero ($K_i/K_p = R/L$) cancels the electrical pole. With $f_c = 1.5$ kHz, this gives $K_p ≈ 17$ V/A and $K_i ≈ 5.3\times10^4$ V/(A·s).

Keep $\omega_c T_s \lesssim 0.5$ for adequate phase margin once the 1.5-sample delay is included.

**Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:**

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\hat\omega_e \hat L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \hat\omega_e \hat L i_d + \hat\omega_e \hat\lambda_m,\qquad \lambda_m = \tfrac{2}{3}\frac{K_t}{p}
$$

### 1.3 Discretization and anti-windup

The integrator uses forward Euler with back-calculation anti-windup:

$$
\begin{aligned}
e_k &= r_k - y_k\\
u^{unsat}_k &= K_p e_k + I_k + u^{ff}_k\\
u_k &= \mathrm{sat}(u^{unsat}_k)\\
I_{k+1} &= I_k + T_s\left[K_i e_k + K_{aw}\,(u_k - u^{unsat}_k)\right],\qquad K_{aw}\approx K_i/K_p
\end{aligned}
$$

**Voltage vector limiting.** Prioritize the d-axis, then clamp the vector magnitude:

$$
|v_{dq}| \le V_{max}= \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}\,m_{max},\qquad m_{max}\approx 0.95
$$
