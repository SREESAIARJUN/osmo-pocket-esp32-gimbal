## 0. Architecture Correction and Critical Findings

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no native in-firmware 6-step or FOC ESC for an H7 flight controller. The correct architecture is:
- **STM32H743 runs ArduPilot** as the flight controller and generates bi-directional DShot600.
- **Four ESC MCUs** do the 48 kHz bridge PWM. Suitable choices are an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or an EFM8 running BlueJay. Each ESC MCU decodes DShot and returns eRPM telemetry.

The 48 kHz switching model in §3 therefore applies to the ESC MCU's PWM, not the H743's.

**2. "80 A burst" on a 20×20 mm nano board is thermally marginal.** §4 shows the following:

| Load case | Board heating rate | Time to limit |
|---|---|---|
| All four channels at 80 A (≈232 W) | ≈107 K/s | ≈0.6 s before the board reaches 105 °C |
| Single channel at 80 A | — | ≈2.5 s |

Rate the board as follows: **80 A ≤ 1 s single-channel burst, ≈35–40 A continuous with prop-wash airflow.** Otherwise, move to a 30.5×30.5 mm layout.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_ds = 2.5 × 1.45 = **3.625 mΩ**.

### 2.2 Six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification

During each electrical period, each phase spends 1/3 of the time PWM-high, 1/3 low, and 1/3 floating. Complementary PWM means the low-side FET carries the freewheel current during the off-time.

$$P_{HS}=\frac{I^2R_{ds}\,D}{3},\qquad P_{LS}=\frac{I^2R_{ds}}{3}\Big[(1-D)+1\Big]\cdot\tfrac{1}{1}\Big|_{\text{per }1/3\text{ windows}} = \frac{I^2R_{ds}(2-D)}{3}$$

Per channel, summed over 6 FETs:

$$P_{cond,ch}=3\!\left(P_{HS}+P_{LS}\right)=2\,I^2R_{ds}$$

This total is independent of D. The duty cycle only shifts loss between the high-side and low-side FETs. The worst single FET is the **low-side FET at low duty**, since (2−D)/3 → 2/3.
