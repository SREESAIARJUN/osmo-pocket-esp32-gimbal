**Architecture correction.** The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs. Each of the four ESC channels needs its own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example an STM32G071 or AT32F421 with an FD6288Q 3-phase driver. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the four ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. This is the only workable topology; the netlists below reflect it.

**Feasibility warning.** The math below shows that 80 A per channel on a single-FET 20×20 board is limited to roughly 0.5–1 s. Section 2.5 gives the corrective options.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology

The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification (AM32 complementary PWM):

- In each 60° sector, current flows through **two series FETs at all times**.
  - During t_on, the path is the high-side FET of phase A plus the low-side FET of phase B.
  - During t_off, the path is the low-side FET of phase A (synchronous rectifier) plus the low-side FET of phase B.
- Each FET carries current for 120° of 360°, i.e. 1/3 of the electrical period.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]$$

### 1.3 Per-channel and per-FET loss

Including PWM ripple ΔI_pp:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,R_{DS}(T_j)\left(I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}\right)$$

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}\cdot 2 = \frac{R_{DS}(T_j)\,I^2}{3}$$

The ripple term is computed from motor inductance L_m:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{2L_m f_{sw}}$$

For L_m = 10 µH, D = 0.95 and V_bemf ≈ 0.9·V_bus, this gives ΔI_pp ≈ 2.5 A. The term ΔI²/12 is about 0.5 A², which is negligible against I² = 6400 A².

### 1.4 Self-consistent solution

Iterate T_j until it converges (section 2.4 provides T_j):

| T_j (°C) | R_DS (mΩ) | P_cond,ch (W) | P_cond,FET (W) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | 1.20 | 15.4 | 2.56 |
| 100 | 1.56 | 20.0 | 3.33 |
| 125 | 1.68 | 21.5 | 3.58 |
| **150** | **1.80** | **23.0** | **3.84** |

**Design value: P_cond,ch ≈ 23 W per channel at T_j = 150 °C.**
