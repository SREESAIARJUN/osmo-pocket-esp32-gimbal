## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.004\ /^\circ C$$

| T<sub>j</sub> | R<sub>DS(on)</sub> |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.30 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.40 mΩ |

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous PWM

In each 60° commutation sector, current I flows through two phases:

- **Driven phase:** the high-side FET conducts for a fraction D of the period, and its complementary low-side FET conducts (synchronous freewheel) for 1−D. This phase leg therefore conducts I at all times.
- **Return phase:** the low-side FET conducts continuously.

So at every instant, exactly two R<sub>DS(on)</sub> drops are in series with the phase current:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

### 1.3 PCB copper resistance

Phase and bus paths of about 15 mm across 2 oz copper on 3 paralleled layers, with vias, give an estimated R<sub>Cu</sub> ≈ 0.5 mΩ per current loop.

### 1.4 Results at 80 A, T<sub>j</sub> = 125 °C

- FET conduction: 2 × 6400 × 0.0014 = **17.9 W**
- Copper: 6400 × 0.0005 = **3.2 W**, spread across the board

Per-FET averaging: each FET carries current for 1/3 of the electrical cycle. While conducting, it dissipates 6400 × 1.4 mΩ ≈ **9.0 W instantaneous**, averaging about **3.0 W** over the cycle.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f<sub>sw</sub> = 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing leg switches hard. With synchronous rectification, the low-side FET turns on into the conducting body diode, which is a soft switching event.

### 2.1 V–I overlap

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r+t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

The gate driver sinks/sources about 1 A, so t ≈ Q<sub>gd</sub>/I<sub>g</sub> ≈ 10–25 ns. Using t<sub>r</sub> = t<sub>f</sub> = 25 ns:

$$P_{VI} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 50\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{2.42\ W}$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction

With t<sub>dead</sub> = 100 ns, two dead-time intervals per period, and V<sub>F</sub> ≈ 0.8 V:

$$P_{DT} = V_F\,I\,(2t_{dead})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 50\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$

Note that Q<sub>rr</sub> grows with di/dt and current, so treat this term as being underestimated by about 2×.

### 2.4 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} \approx Q_{oss}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 40\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.05\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

$$P_{g} = 2\,Q_g\,V_{GS}\,f_{sw} = 2 \times 60\,\text{nC} \times 10 \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$
