### Architecture

This is an all-in-one (AIO) board. The STM32H743 is the flight controller and runs ArduPilot. It does **not** commutate the motors. Motor commutation is handled by four dedicated ESC MCUs, for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32. The H743 talks to them over bi-directional DShot600.

### Feasibility warning (read this first)

An 80 A burst rating on a 20×20 "nano" footprint is not physically defensible. You cannot place 24 DFN5×6 FETs plus gate drivers on that area. Even on 30.5×30.5 mm, 80 A per channel is a **seconds-long burst rating**, not a continuous one. Section 3 quantifies this. The model below assumes the 30.5 mm form factor and should be read as a burst-duration envelope.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in six-step with synchronous rectification

During any 60° commutation sector, two phase legs are active:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for a fraction D of the PWM period. The low-side FET carries freewheeling current for the remaining (1 − D), apart from dead time.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET is held on statically.

The motor current therefore always passes through exactly two R_DS(on) elements in series. This holds for any duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big]
$$

| T_j (°C) | R_DS(on) (mΩ) | P_cond per channel at 80 A (W) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | 1.00 | 12.8 |
| 75 | 1.25 | 16.0 |
| 100 | 1.375 | 17.6 |
| 125 | 1.50 | **19.2** |
| 150 | 1.625 | 20.8 |

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each leg is active in the high-side (PWM) role for 120° and in the low-side (static) role for 120° per electrical cycle. Averaged over a full cycle, each of the 6 FETs dissipates:

$$
\bar P_{FET} = \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6} \approx 3.2\ \text{W} \quad (T_j = 125^\circ\text{C})
$$

The instantaneous dissipation in an active FET can reach $I^2 R \approx 9.6$ W. The electrical period is far shorter than the die thermal time constant (~ms), so the average value governs junction temperature.

### 1.4 Additional conduction elements (do not omit these)

| Element | Value | Loss at 80 A per channel |
|---|---|---|
| Low-side shunt (if fitted) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| PCB copper path (pad → bus → phase pad), 2 oz × 4 layers | ~0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| Solder joints + phase wire pads | ~0.1 mΩ | 0.6 W |

**Total conduction loss per channel at T_j = 125 °C: ≈ 23.3 W.**
