# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Model

**Job #49 · Rev A**

## 0. Architectural Corrections and Assumptions

Before the math, three points that change the design:

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1 stack, each ESC channel has its own MCU, typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to the ESC MCUs. The switching-loss model (§2) belongs to the ESC board. The DMA model (§4) belongs to the FC.
2. **"80 A burst" is a per-channel, short-duration rating.** Four channels at 80 A on 6S is about 2 kW and about 320 A battery-side at full duty. No XT60 or XT90 harness supports that, and a 20×20 / 30×30 board cannot dissipate it. §3 shows how long the burst can actually last.
