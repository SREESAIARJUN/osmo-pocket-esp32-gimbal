# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion Design

I don't have the specification for "Job #5," so this design states its assumptions explicitly. Every parameter is a named constant, so you can substitute your real values.

## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 @ 170 MHz (FPU, CORDIC, HRTIM) |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs $p=7$), $R_s=5.6\,\Omega$, $L_s=2.1\,\text{mH}$, $K_t=0.08\,\text{N·m/A}$ |
| Joint encoder | 14-bit magnetic (MT6701 / AS5048A) on each axis |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera payload, SPI @ 24 MHz, ODR 8 kHz |
| Current sense | 3-shunt low-side, sampled at PWM center |

**Loop rates (all phase-locked to the PWM timer)**

| Loop | Rate | Bandwidth target |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC, d/q PI) | 20 kHz | 1.5–2 kHz |
| Velocity (PI + feedforward) | 4 kHz (÷5) | 80–150 Hz |
| Angle/attitude (P + rate feedforward) | 1 kHz (÷20) | 15–30 Hz |
| IMU acquisition + filtering | 8 kHz (DRDY interrupt) | — |
| AHRS fusion | 1 kHz | — |

Each loop's bandwidth is at least 5× below the one inside it, so the inner loop can be treated as unity gain when tuning the outer loop.

## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant). With $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform.** Here $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$
