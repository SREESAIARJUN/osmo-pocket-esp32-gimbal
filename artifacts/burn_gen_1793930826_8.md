## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Feasibility Flags

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller (FC)**. It runs ArduPilot and generates DShot. The 4-in-1 ESC needs **its own MCU(s)** running AM32 or BLHeli_32 commutation firmware, such as an STM32G071 or AT32F421. ArduPilot does not commutate motors. This document models both halves and the link between them.

The MOSFET and driver values below are **representative placeholders** for a 40 V, ~1 mΩ, DFN5x6-class part. Replace them with your BOM's datasheet values and re-run the equations. Every result is given symbolically first so you can do this.

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V (6S full charge, worst case) | DC link |
| I_ph | 80 A | Burst motor phase current, per ESC channel |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | PWM frequency |

**Feasibility flag:** 4 × 80 A in a "nano" footprint (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) is **burst-only**. Section 3 shows it is time-limited to roughly 2–5 s. Your hardware and marketing specs should state an explicit burst duration, not a continuous rating.
