# STM32H743 FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #44): Engineering Model and Reference Design

## 0. Architectural Assumptions

The H743 is the flight controller. It sends DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. It does **not** generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM. Each ESC channel has its own MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32, that drives the gate drivers. Mixing the two roles is the most common spec error on these jobs, so the model below separates them.

**Assumed parameters (replace with datasheet values):**

| Parameter | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | 6S, V_bus = 25.2 V | Full charge, worst case for switching |
| Burst current | I_ph = 80 A per motor, 4 motors simultaneously | Worst case |
| Duty | D = 0.9 | At burst |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal, 120° conduction | High-side PWM with synchronous rectification |
| MOSFET | Generic 40 V, 5×6 PDFN | R_ds(on) max 1.3 mΩ @25 °C; Q_g = 50 nC; Q_gs2 + Q_gd = 16 nC; Q_rr = 60 nC; Q_oss = 50 nC; V_plateau = 4.5 V; R_th,jc = 0.8 K/W |
| Gate drive | V_drv = 10 V | R_on,eff ≈ 6 Ω, R_off,eff ≈ 4 Ω (driver + R_g) |
| Dead time | t_d = 150 ns per edge | Body diode V_f = 0.8 V |

> ⚠️ **Form factor flag:** "Nano" usually means 20×20 mm. Six 5×6 FETs per channel (24 total) plus bulk capacitance will not physically fit at 20×20. Realistic 80A-burst 4-in-1s are 30.5×30.5 mm. The thermal section shows that heat is the binding constraint at either size.

## 1. Conduction Losses

### 1.1 Temperature-corrected R_ds(on)

R_ds(Tj) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α(Tj − 25)], with α ≈ 0.006/K for 40 V trench parts.

At Tj = 125 °C: R_hot = 1.3 mΩ × 1.6 = **2.08 mΩ**.

This must be iterated with Tj (see §4.4).

### 1.2 Per-FET duty fractions

Over one electrical cycle, each phase leg spends:

- 1/3 of the cycle **sourcing**: HS conducts D, LS conducts (1−D) via sync rectification.
- 1/3 of the cycle **sinking**: LS conducts 100%.
- 1/3 of the cycle **floating**.

This gives:

- **High side:** δ_HS = D/3
- **Low side:** δ_LS = (1−D)/3 + 1/3 = (2−D)/3

### 1.3 Conduction loss per FET

P_cond = I² · R_hot · δ, with I² · R_hot = 6400 × 0.00208 = 13.31 W.

| Device | δ | P_cond |
|---|---|---|
| HS FET | 0.300 | **3.99 W** |
| LS FET | 0.367 | **4.88 W** |

**Check:** two FETs are always in the current path, so the per-channel total should equal 2·I²·R = 26.6 W. The sum 3 × (3.99 + 4.88) = 26.6 W matches.

## 2. Switching Losses at f_sw = 48 kHz

### 2.1 Gate currents during the Miller interval

- I_g,on = (V_drv − V_pl) / R_on = 5.5 / 6 = 0.92 A
- I_g,off = V_pl / R_off = 4.5 / 4 = 1.13 A

### 2.2 Transition times

- t_on = (Q_gs2 + Q_gd) / I_g,on = 16 nC / 0.92 A = 17.4 ns
- t_off = 16 nC / 1.13 A = 14.2 ns

### 2.3 Hard-switching (V–I overlap) energy, HS FET

E_sw = ½ · V · I · (t_on + t_off) = ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 31.6 ns = **31.9 µJ**

### 2.4 Additional loss terms (per switching period, while the leg is active)

| Term | Formula | Power while active |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | E_sw · f | 1.53 W |
| LS body-diode reverse recovery (dumped into HS) | Q_rr · V · f | 0.073 W
