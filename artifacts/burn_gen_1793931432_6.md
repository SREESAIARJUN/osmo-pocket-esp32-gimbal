# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC "Nano" Stack (Job #63): Power, Thermal & DShot Architecture

> **Scope:** This document provides first-principles models with a representative component set. Every device parameter marked **[A]** is an assumption you must replace with your chosen MOSFET's datasheet values before release.
>
> The STM32H743 runs **ArduPilot as the flight controller**. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel uses its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 talks to those MCUs over bi-directional DShot600.

## 0. Executive Summary

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Conduction loss @ 80 A, Tj≈110 °C (per channel) | **≈ 31 W** |
| Switching + dead-time + Qrr + Coss @ 48 kHz (per channel) | **≈ 1.7 W** |
| Copper / connector loss (per channel) | ≈ 1.5 W |
| **Total per channel @ 80 A** | **≈ 34 W** (≈ 136 W with all four channels saturated) |
| Max 80 A burst duration (local junction limit, 30.5 mm board, no forced air) | **≈ 1.5–3 s** |
| Realistic continuous rating (nano/20×20 copper) | **35–45 A per channel** |
| DShot600 bidir frame cycle | ≈ 95 µs, so ≥ 8 kHz is possible. ArduPilot runs at the loop rate (400 Hz–2 kHz). |
| DMA streams required (4 motors, one timer) | 1× TIMx_UP + 2–4× TIMx_CHy (DMAMUX makes this conflict-free on H7) |

**Engineering verdict:**
- "80 A burst" on a nano footprint is a **transient-capacitance rating, not a thermal-resistance rating**. It is defensible only as a ≤ 2 s burst, with a stated starting temperature.
- A 20×20 mm board cannot hold 80 A for 10 s on any channel.
- On 30.5×30.5 mm, 10 s is still not viable without a heatsink and prop-wash airflow.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path
In 6-step commutation, at any instant one phase is high-side PWM, one is low-side ON, and one is floating. With complementary PWM:

- **Switching leg:**
  - The high-side (HS) FET conducts for duty D.
  - The low-side (LS) FET of the same leg conducts for (1−D), via synchronous rectification.
- **Static leg:** the LS FET conducts 100 % of the commutation sector.

The leg-current RMS is therefore always carried by **two channel resistances in series**, independent of D:

$$
P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \left[ D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS} + R_{LS,static} \right] = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

for matched FETs.

### 2.2 Ripple correction
Phase inductance L_ph ≈ 15 µH (typical 2207-class motor) **[A]**. Worst-case ripple is at D = 0.5:

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{ph}\, f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{15\mu \times 48k} = 8.75\ \text{A}
$$

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{dc}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 6.4 = 6406\ \text{A}^2
$$

Ripple adds only about 0.1 %, so it is negligible at 80 A. It is not negligible at low current.
