# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture, Loss Model, DMA Allocation and Netlists

**Job #9: Principal Systems Architecture Package, Rev A**

## 0. Feasibility Findings

Two constraints shape the whole design.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** A 48 kHz, 6-step or FOC bridge across 4 motors (24 FETs) needs 12 complementary PWM pairs with dead-time, BEMF comparators and per-motor current sense. The H743 has only TIM1 and TIM8 as advanced timers, enough for 2 motors at most. The standard and correct architecture is:
   - **H743 (FC):** ArduPilot, sending DShot600 to the ESC MCUs.
   - **4× ESC MCU** (AT32F421 or STM32G071, running AM32): 48 kHz PWM, then a gate driver, then FETs.
2. **"Nano" (20×20 mm) cannot sustain 80 A/motor.** Section 3 shows that on a 20×20 board, 80 A on all four motors is thermally limited to about 1 s. This package therefore specifies a **30.5×30.5 mm power stage** (the FC can stack at 20×20). It defines "80 A burst" as **≤1 s at 4 motors or ≤5 s at 1–2 motors**, from a 45 °C board start.

All device parameters below are **representative assumptions**, labeled as such. Substitute datasheet values for the selected parts before release.

## 2. Loss Model (per motor)

### 2.1 Conduction losses

In 6-step complementary PWM, current flows through two legs during each 60° sector:

- **Active leg:** the high-side conducts for duty D. The low-side (synchronous rectification) conducts for (1−D).
- **Return leg:** the low-side conducts for 100% of the sector.

Neglecting ripple (L_phase is large relative to 1/f_sw):

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Conduction loss is **independent of duty** with synchronous rectification. This is a key result.

With ripple included: I_rms² = I² + ΔI²/12, where ΔI = V_bus·D(1−D)/(L·f_sw). For L_phase = 15 µH:

ΔI_max = 25.2 × 0.25 / (15e-6 × 48e3) = 8.75 A

The correction is ΔI²/12 = 6.4 A², which is less than 0.1% of I². It is neglected.

**Temperature coupling (implicit; solve iteratively):**

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 1.2 × (1 + 0.0055 × 85) = **1.76 mΩ**

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.76\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{22.5\ W}$$

### 2.2 Switching losses (hard-switched edge, active leg)

**V–I overlap:**
$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = 1.94\ W$$

**Reverse recovery of the synchronous FET's body diode:**
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.036\ W$$

**Output capacitance:**
$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.015\ W$$
