## 0. Architecture Correction and Design Assumptions

### 0.1 MCU partitioning

The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors directly. In ArduPilot-compatible stacks:

- The **H743** runs ArduPilot and emits **bidirectional DShot600** to four ESC MCUs.
- **Each ESC channel has its own MCU** running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. Candidates are STM32G071, AT32F421 or GD32E230. Each ESC MCU does six-step commutation with 48 kHz complementary PWM.

The model below follows this two-tier architecture.

### 0.3 Feasibility flag (read first)

An 80 A burst on a "nano" (20×20 mm or 25×25 mm) 4-in-1 board is at the extreme edge of feasibility. The numbers in §1 show:

- Roughly **95–100 W of dissipation** if all four channels burst simultaneously.
- The thermal mass supports only **seconds** of burst.

Treat 80 A as a **per-channel, non-simultaneous, ≤ 2–5 s** rating. Treat roughly 35–45 A as the continuous rating.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Six-step synchronous-PWM conduction topology

During any commutation sector, two phases carry current:

- **Phase X (PWM leg):** high-side FET conducts for duty D. The complementary low-side FET conducts for (1 − D).
- **Phase Y (return leg):** low-side FET is ON for 100% of the sector.

Current I flows continuously through **exactly two FET channels** at every instant, ignoring dead time.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

This is **independent of duty** under synchronous rectification, which is a key insight. Duty only redistributes heat between the high-side and low-side FETs.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on), solved self-consistently

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)]$$

The junction temperature depends on the loss, which depends on R_DS(on). With $T_j = T_{pcb} + P_{FET}\,R_{\theta JC}$:

$$P_{FET} = \frac{I^2 R_{25}[1 + \alpha(T_{pcb} - 25)]}{1 - I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha\,R_{\theta JC}}$$

The denominator must stay positive. If it reaches zero, conduction loss runs away thermally.

**Numeric evaluation** (worst-case FET at 100% sector conduction, T_pcb = 90 °C):

- Numerator: 6400 × 1.2e-3 × [1 + 0.005 × 65] = 7.68 × 1.325 = **10.18 W**
- Denominator: 1 − 6400 × 1.2e-3 × 0.005 × 1.2 = 1 − 0.046 = **0.954**
- **P_FET ≈ 10.7 W**, which gives T_j ≈ 90 + 12.8 = **102.8 °C**
- R_DS(on) at T_j ≈ 1.2 × (1 + 0.005 × 77.8) = **1.67 mΩ**

### 1.4 Six-step averaging per FET

Each FET is active for one-third of the electrical period. Within that window:

- **High-side FET:** conducts D/3 of the period in its PWM sector, plus the return-leg duty depending on the scheme.
- **Low-side FET:** carries most of the heat at low duty, when the motor is loaded at low throttle.

For layout, assume each FET sees a worst-case **⅓·I²R + (1 − D)·⅓·I²R ≈ 5–7 W average at 80 A**. Size copper for this per device.
