Separate the bandwidths by at least 5–10× between loops: current loop around 2 kHz, velocity loop around 150–250 Hz, angle loop around 30–60 Hz.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control: Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle is θₑ = p·(θₘ − θ₀), where p is the number of pole pairs and θ₀ is the encoder offset found during alignment.

**Clarke transform** (amplitude invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 Plant and PI Design

The PMSM voltage equations in the dq frame are:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda$$

With the cross terms cancelled by feedforward, each axis behaves as the first-order plant 1/(Ls + R).

Choose the PI zero to cancel the plant pole. The loop then reduces to a pure integrator crossing over at ω_c:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

with the controller form

$$v^* = K_p e + K_i \textstyle\int e\,dt + v_{ff}$$

and the decoupling feedforward terms

$$v_{d,ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_{q,ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda$$
