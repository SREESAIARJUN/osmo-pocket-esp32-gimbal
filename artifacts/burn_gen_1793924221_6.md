## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{DS} = 1.5\,\text{mΩ} \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 100) = 2.18\ \text{mΩ}$$

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

In any commutation sector, current flows through exactly one high-side path and one low-side path:

- **PWM ON:** HS_A → motor → LS_B
- **PWM OFF:** LS_A (synchronous freewheel) → motor → LS_B

Because complementary PWM keeps a MOSFET (never the diode, except during dead time) in the path at all times, the conducting resistance is always 2·R_DS, **independent of duty cycle**:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.3 Per-FET loss

Each phase conducts for 2/3 of the electrical cycle. Within that, HS/LS sharing depends on duty D. Averaged per FET:

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} = \frac{P_{cond,motor}}{6} \quad\text{(average over the full electrical cycle)}$$

The worst-case single FET is the static low-side at high D. Its peak instantaneous dissipation is I²·R_DS while it conducts during its 1/3 sector.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A burst

| Quantity | T_j = 25 °C | T_j = 125 °C |
|---|---|---|
| P_cond per motor | 2 × 6400 × 0.0015 = **19.2 W** | 2 × 6400 × 0.00218 = **27.9 W** |
| P_cond per FET (avg) | 3.2 W | 4.65 W |
| Instantaneous, conducting FET | 9.6 W | 13.95 W |

**Copper loss must be added.** Phase traces and battery planes are not negligible. For 2 oz outer copper, R_□ ≈ 0.25 mΩ/□ at 25 °C. An 8-square path per motor gives about 2 mΩ:

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.002 \approx 12.8\ \text{W per motor}$$

This is comparable to MOSFET loss. **Use stitched inner planes (4 × 1 oz in parallel) to cut it below 0.7 mΩ (≈ 4.5 W).**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one leg per motor is hard-switching at any instant. The PWM'd high-side and its synchronous low-side complement form a single commutation cell.

### 2.1 Loss components

**(a) V–I overlap (hard turn-on and turn-off of HS)**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

**(b) Body-diode reverse recovery (LS diode conducts during dead time and is recovered when HS turns on)**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw}$$

**(c) Output capacitance (HS Coss discharged at turn-on, LS Coss charged)**

$$P_{oss} \approx Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw}$$

This is a conservative form that lumps both devices.

**(d) Dead-time body-diode conduction (two dead times per period)**

$$P_{dt} = 2\, V_{SD}\, I_{ph}\, t_{dt}\, f_{sw}$$

**(e) Gate drive (dissipated in the driver and R_g, not in the FET)**

$$P_{gate} = 2\, Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw}$$

### 2.2 Numbers

Conditions: V_bus = 25.2 V, I = 80 A, f_sw = 48 kHz, t_dt = 100 ns.
