**Three findings to settle before layout:**

1. **The form factor cannot hold 80 A per motor.** A "nano" 20×20 mm 4-in-1 cannot sustain 4×80 A, and a 30.5×30.5 mm board only manages it as a burst of about 1.5–2 s. The calculation in §3 shows this. The spec sheet must state *"80 A burst ≤ 1.5 s, 50 % duty cycle recovery"* or similar, never "80 A continuous."
2. **Switching frequency belongs to the ESC firmware.** The 48 kHz figure lives in the AM32 configuration, not the STM32H743. The H743 only produces DShot600 frames and decodes eRPM telemetry.
3. **DShot DMA buffers must not sit in DTCM.** On the H7, DMA1/DMA2 cannot reach DTCM, and ArduPilot already places DMA-safe buffers correctly. Any custom driver code must follow the same rule.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At the design point T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{DS(on),hot} = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.0045\times100) = 1.74\ \text{m}\Omega$$

Two further corrections:
- **Gate-drive derating.** If the driver supply sags to 7.5 V during a burst (bootstrap droop at high duty), R_DS(on) typically rises another 10–20 %. Mitigate with a bootstrap capacitor of at least 1 µF (X7R, 25 V) per phase.
- **Thermal feedback.** R_DS(on) rises with T_j, which raises loss, which raises T_j. Converge iteratively (§3.4).

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous commutation

At any instant two phases conduct:
- During t_on, one high-side and one low-side FET conduct.
- During t_off with complementary PWM, the high-side is replaced by its synchronous low-side partner, so current still flows through two channels.

The current therefore always passes through exactly **two R_DS(on)** in series, independent of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on),hot}$$
