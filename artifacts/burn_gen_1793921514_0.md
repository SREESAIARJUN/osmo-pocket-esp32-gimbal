## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × (1 + 0.006 × 85) = **1.81 mΩ**. Use 1.8 mΩ.

### 1.2 Current path per 6-step sector

In each 60° sector, phase current I_ph flows through one high-side leg and one low-side leg:

- **PWM leg (high side):** HS conducts for fraction D, and its complementary LS (sync rect) conducts for 1−D. Either way, one FET carries I.
- **Return leg:** LS conducts continuously for the sector.

So at every instant, **exactly two FETs carry I_ph**:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.8\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{23.0\ W\ per\ ESC\ channel}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET average

Each phase carries current 2/3 of the electrical period, split between its HS and LS. At D → 1, each FET conducts about 1/3 of the period:

$$P_{FET} = \tfrac{1}{3}\, I^2 R_{ds} = \tfrac{1}{3}(6400)(1.8\text{ m}\Omega) = \mathbf{3.84\ W}$$

Check: 6 FETs × 3.84 W = 23.0 W ✓

### 1.4 Copper (interconnect) loss

Assume a phase path of battery pad → FET → phase pad → return of [0.5 mΩ] per channel (2 oz copper, about 3 mm wide pours plus vias):

$$P_{Cu,ch} = I^2 R_{path} = 6400 \times 0.5\text{ m}\Omega = 3.2\ \text{W}$$

**Total conduction for 4 channels:** 4 × (23.0 + 3.2) = **104.8 W**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM high-side FET hard-switches. The LS sync-rect FET soft-switches through its body diode during dead time.

### 2.1 V-I overlap loss

Switching transition time from gate charge:

$$t_{r} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g}} + \frac{Q_{gs2}}{I_g} \approx 15\ \text{ns}\quad (t_f \approx t_r)$$

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

### 2.2 Body-diode reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{ nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}$$

Note: Q_rr scales with I and di/dt. At 80 A, multiply the datasheet Q_rr by measured/datasheet scaling, and expect 2–3× at this current.
