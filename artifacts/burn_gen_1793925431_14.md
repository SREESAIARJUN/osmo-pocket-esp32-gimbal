# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal & Bi-Directional DShot600 Design Model

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I don't have the original Job #28 requirements, so this model rests on the stated assumptions below. **Every number flagged (A) is a placeholder** to replace with your MOSFET datasheet, stack-up, and airflow data. The equations are parametric, so substituting real values propagates correctly.

### 0.1 One architectural point to settle first

The STM32H743 has only two advanced timers with complementary outputs and dead-time insertion (TIM1 and TIM8). It cannot run 4× sensorless 6-step/FOC commutation itself without HRTIM gymnastics and a large ISR load. The model therefore assumes the standard split:

- **Flight controller:** H743 running ArduPilot, emitting bi-directional DShot600.
- **4-in-1 ESC:** four dedicated ESC MCUs (e.g. STM32G071 or AT32F421 on AM32), each with a 3-phase gate driver and 6 N-FETs.

If you actually intend single-MCU control of all four motors, say so; the timer section changes completely.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Topology

In 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification, current always flows through exactly two FET channels:

- During the PWM on-time: one high-side (HS) FET and one low-side (LS) FET.
- During the off-time: two LS FETs (with complementary PWM).

Total channel conduction loss per ESC is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ESC}=2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j),\qquad R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Per-FET distribution

Each phase leg is active for 120° of each 360° electrical cycle.

| FET | Average conduction loss |
|---|---|
| High-side | $P_{HS}=\tfrac{1}{3}\,D\,I^2R_{DS}$ |
| Low-side | $P_{LS}=\tfrac{1}{3}I^2R_{DS}+\tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)I^2R_{DS}\cdot\tfrac{1}{1}$ (own 120° plus freewheel share) |

Summed over all 6 FETs, this gives $2I^2R_{DS}$.

### 1.3 Numbers at T_j = 125 °C (R_DS = 1.55 mΩ)

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| I²R_DS | 6400 × 1.55 m = **9.92 W** (instantaneous, one conducting FET) |
| P_cond, ESC | 2 × 9.92 = **19.8 W per motor** |
| Average per FET | 19.8 / 6 ≈ **3.3 W**, with ~9.9 W peak during its 120° window |

### 1.4 Copper loss (do not ignore)

Phase pads, the bus plane and the solder joints add roughly 0.3–0.5 mΩ (A) to the current path. At 80 A:

$$P_{Cu}\approx I^2 R_{trace}\approx 6400\times0.4\text{ m}\Omega\approx 2.6\text{ W per motor}$$
