# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #54): System Architecture Model

**Scope:** Power-stage loss model (80 A burst, 48 kHz PWM), thermal transient budget, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and reference netlists.

---

## 0. Architecture Constraints

**1. The H743 does not commutate motors.** In every production 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an STM32G071, AT32F421 or GD32E230. That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 frames. It receives eRPM telemetry back on the same wire.

**2. A 20×20 mm board cannot carry 4×80 A.**
- A 4-in-1 ESC needs 24 MOSFETs.
- At PDFN 3.3×3.3 size, those FETs alone take about 261 mm², or 65% of a 20×20 board, before drivers, MCUs, caps or mounting holes.
- This document therefore assumes a **20×20 H743 FC stacked on a 30.5×30.5 ESC**.
- The thermal results in §3 show that even that ESC is transient-limited at 80 A.
