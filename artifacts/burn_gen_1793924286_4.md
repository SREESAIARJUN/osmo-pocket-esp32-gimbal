# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal, and DShot Design

## 0. Assumptions and Key Finding

I don't have the Job #62 requirements document, so every assumption is stated explicitly and the math is kept parametric. Substitute your actual BOM values to recompute.

| Parameter | Assumed value | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max | Worst-case switching voltage |
| Form factor | 20×20 mm mount, ~25×25 mm PCB, 8-layer, 2 oz inner/outer copper | "Nano" class |
| MOSFETs | 40 V N-ch, 3.3×3.3 mm PQFN. R_ds(on) = 1.4 mΩ max at 25 °C (V_gs = 10 V). Q_g = 30 nC, Q_gd = 8 nC, C_oss ≈ 1 nF, Q_rr ≈ 30 nC | Typical best-in-class for this package |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q-class 3-phase driver, ~1 A source/sink | Common 4-in-1 choice |
| ESC MCU | AT32F421 (AM32 or BLHeli_32), one per channel | Bidirectional DShot capable |
| Commutation | Trapezoidal, complementary (synchronous) PWM, f_sw = 48 kHz, dead time 100 ns | |
| "80 A burst" | 80 A phase current per channel, all 4 channels simultaneously | Worst case |

**Key finding:** at 80 A on all four channels, the board dissipates about **119 W**. A 25×25 mm board can reject roughly **9 W** continuously, even in prop wash. The 80 A rating is therefore feasible only as a **~1–1.5 s burst**. Sustained capability is about **22–25 A per channel**. Section 2.4 gives the derivation and redesign options.

## 1. Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.4 mΩ × 1.6 = **2.24 mΩ**. This hot value is used throughout, and Section 2.3 checks it self-consistently.

### 1.2 Conduction loss

With 6-step commutation, current flows through one high-side leg and one low-side leg at all times.

With synchronous rectification, the PWM leg alternates between the high-side FET (fraction D of the period) and its complementary low-side FET (fraction 1−D). The returning phase's low-side FET conducts continuously. The current path therefore always contains exactly **two R_ds(on)**, independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.24\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{28.7\ W\ per\ channel}$$

Each FET conducts during one third of the electrical cycle. On average, then, each of the 6 FETs carries about 4.8 W. Instantaneously, the two active devices each carry about 14.3 W, and this peak drives the junction ΔT.

### 1.3 Switching loss at 48 kHz

There is one hard-switched leg per PWM period.

**Transition times.** These are set by the Miller plateau:

$$t_{r} \approx t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g}} = \frac{8\,\mathrm{nC}}{\sim0.8\,\mathrm{A}} \approx 10\ \mathrm{ns}$$

**V–I overlap loss:**

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

$$P_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\,\mathrm{ns} \times 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = 0.97\ \mathrm{W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two dead-time events per period):

$$P_{dt} = V_f I_{ph}\,(2t_{dead})\,f_{sw}$$

$$P_{dt} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\mathrm{ns} \times 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = 0.61\ \mathrm{W}$$

**Reverse recovery:**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw}$$

$$P_{rr} = 30\,\mathrm{nC}\times25.2\times48\,\mathrm{k} = 0.036\ \mathrm{W}$$

**Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss}V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 0.015\ \mathrm{W}$$

**Gate drive** (one complementary pair is switching):

$$P_{g} = 2\,Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} = 2\times30\,\mathrm{nC}\times10\times48\,\mathrm{k} = 0.029\ \mathrm{W}$$

This loss is mostly dissipated in the driver and gate resistors.

$$P_{sw,total} \approx \mathbf{1.66\ W\ per\ channel}$$

At 48 kHz, switching loss is only about 5% of the total. **Conduction dominates.** Lowering f_sw buys almost nothing. Lowering R_ds or I does.

### 1.4 Other losses at 80 A

| Source | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Shunt resistor (0.5 mΩ, if low-side sensed) | I²R | 3.2 W/ch |
| Phase copper (~0.3 mΩ path) | I²R | 1.9 W/ch |
| Bus capacitor ESR | I_rms,cap² · ESR, with I_cap,rms ≈ I·√(D(1−D)) ≈ 40 A at D = 0.5 across 4×(470 µF/35 V ‖ ceramics), ESR_eff ≈ 5 mΩ | ~8 W (shared, mostly off-board on the pigtail cap) |

### 1.5 Totals

| | Per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 28.7 W | 114.8 W |
| Switching + dead-time + Q_rr + C_oss + gate | 1.66 W | 6.6 W |
| Shunt + copper | 5.1 W | 20.4 W |
| **Total on-board** | **35.5 W** | **~142 W** |

The ~119 W headline figure counts FET losses only. The board must also absorb shunt and copper losses, which brings the total to about 142 W.
