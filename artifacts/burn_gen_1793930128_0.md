# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Model, DShot Timing, and Reference Netlist

Two things affect everything below:

1. **I have no access to a "Job #9" specification.** All design parameters are assumptions, stated explicitly so you can substitute your real values.
2. **The 80 A burst figure is a short-pulse rating, not a sustained one.** The thermal model shows a 20×20 mm board reaches its temperature limit in about 1–2 seconds at 4×80 A. Section 5 quantifies this.

---

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU (assumed AT32F421 running AM32) driving a 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs. The H743 talks to the ESCs over bidirectional DShot600.

> An H743 could in principle commutate motors directly (TIM1, TIM8, HRTIM), but not four 3-phase motors under ArduPilot. That is not a supportable design.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × 1.6 = **1.6 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step trapezoidal commutation

In 6-step commutation, two phases conduct at any instant:

- The **PWM leg** carries current through the high-side FET for duty D, and through the complementary low-side FET for (1−D). Synchronous rectification is assumed.
- The **return leg** carries current through its low-side FET continuously.

So exactly two R_ds are always in the path.

### 1.3 RMS current including PWM ripple

Ripple amplitude:

$$\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2\times0.25}{20\,\mu\text{H}\times48\,\text{kHz}} = 6.6\ \text{A}_{pp}\ \ (\text{worst case, } D=0.5)$$

RMS current:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 \approx 6404\ \text{A}^2$$

Ripple adds less than 0.1%, so it is negligible.

### 1.4 Conduction loss per ESC channel

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 12.8 W | 51 W |
| 125 °C | 1.6 mΩ | **20.5 W** | **82 W** |
