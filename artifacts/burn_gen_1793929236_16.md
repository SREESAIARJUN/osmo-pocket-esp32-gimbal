## 0. Feasibility Flag

The analysis shows that **"80A burst" on a 20×20 / 25.5×25.5 nano form factor is only valid as a short-duration, single-axis transient.** It is not a sustained rating. Section 3 quantifies this: with all four motors at 80A, the board reaches its thermal limit in about 1–2 seconds. Specify it on the datasheet as **"80A per motor, ≤1 s, single motor, Tamb 25°C, with prop-wash airflow."** Anything stronger will fail qualification.

## 2. Loss Model

### 2.1 Conduction Losses (6-step / trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

In 6-step commutation each phase leg conducts for 240° of 360°. That is 120° on the high side and 120° on the low side. Each individual FET therefore conducts for D_FET = 1/3 of the electrical period. At full throttle (duty → 1) the phase current ≈ I_pk.

**RMS current per FET:**

$$I_{rms,FET} = I_{pk}\sqrt{D_{FET}} = 80\sqrt{1/3} = 46.2\ \text{A}$$

**Temperature-dependent on-resistance:**

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)]$$

**Conduction loss per FET:**

$$P_{cond,FET} = I_{rms,FET}^2 \cdot R_{DS}(T_j)$$

**Closed-form self-consistent junction temperature.** Conduction loss rises with temperature, which raises temperature further. Solving the coupled equations gives:

$$T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + I_{rms}^2 R_{25}R_\theta(1 - 25\alpha)}{1 - I_{rms}^2 R_{25}\,\alpha\,R_\theta}$$

**Thermal runaway criterion:**

$$I_{rms}^2 R_{25}\,\alpha\,R_\theta < 1$$

At 80A with R_θJB = 1.5 K/W: 2133 × 0.0015 × 0.006 × 1.5 = **0.029**. This is stable at the junction-to-board level. The risk is board-level heat accumulation (Section 3), not local runaway.
