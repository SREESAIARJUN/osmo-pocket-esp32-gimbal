# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC Stack (Job #43): Electro-Thermal Model, Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Plan, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Two premises in the brief need correcting before the math.

**1. The H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** In a 4-in-1 stack, each ESC channel has its own MCU, typically an AT32F421 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM and does BEMF commutation. The H743 only:
- sends bidirectional DShot600 commands,
- receives eRPM telemetry,
- reads the battery current and voltage analog lines.

The switching-loss model below therefore belongs to the ESC firmware and hardware domain. The DShot and DMA model belongs to the H743 and ArduPilot domain.

**2. "Nano" and "80A per channel" are physically incompatible for anything beyond a few seconds.** Section 3 shows this quantitatively. I model a 30.5×30.5 mm board. A 20×20 mm board reduces burst time by about 55% (it has roughly 0.44× the area and heat capacity).
