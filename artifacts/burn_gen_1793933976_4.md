## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms
Phase currents, assuming balanced phases (i_a + i_b + i_c = 0).

**Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant):**

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θₑ = p·θₘ − θ_off:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant and decoupling
The d/q-axis voltage equations, with flux linkage λ and torque constant K_t = (3/2)·p·λ, are:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda)
$$

The decoupling feedforward terms cancel the cross-coupling and back-EMF:

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda)
$$

After decoupling, each axis reduces to G(s) = 1/(Ls + R).
