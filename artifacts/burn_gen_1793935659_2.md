# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Thermal, Switching, and Bidirectional DShot600 Design Package (Job #51)

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 is the flight controller. It generates DShot600 and decodes the bidirectional eRPM telemetry. It does **not** switch the MOSFETs.

Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). That MCU runs 6-step commutation with 48 kHz complementary PWM. The loss model below applies to that power stage.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-Dependent On-Resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At an assumed T_j = 100 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × 1.3375 = **1.605 mΩ**. This value is checked by the iteration in §4.4.

### 1.2 Per-Switch Duty Under 6-Step Commutation with Synchronous Rectification

Each phase leg spends 120° electrical as the PWM'd high side, 120° as the solid low side, and 120° floating.

**High-side FET.** It conducts only during the PWM on-time of its 120° segment:

$$P_{HS,cond} = I^2 R_{DS}\cdot\frac{D}{3}$$

**Low-side FET.** It conducts for the full 120° when its phase is the return path. It also freewheels for the (1−D) fraction during its own phase's PWM segment:

$$P_{LS,cond} = I^2 R_{DS}\cdot\frac{1}{3} + I^2 R_{DS}\cdot\frac{1-D}{3} = I^2 R_{DS}\cdot\frac{2-D}{3}$$

**Consistency check.** Summing both FETs in a leg gives 2I²R/3. Across three legs this is 2I²R, which matches the fact that exactly two FETs are always in the current path.
