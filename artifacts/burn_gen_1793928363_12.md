# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control Loop and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion Design

I don't have the original Job #23 specification, so this design states its assumptions explicitly in Section 0. All parameters are grouped in config structs so they can be changed to match your hardware.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class, FPU, 170+ MHz |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, e.g. GBM2804: 7 pole pairs, 12 slots, R ≈ 10 Ω, L ≈ 2–4 mH |
| Driver | 3-phase PWM at 20 kHz, center-aligned, low-side shunt current sensing |
| Encoders | 14-bit
