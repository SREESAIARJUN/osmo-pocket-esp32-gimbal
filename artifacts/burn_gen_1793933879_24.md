## 0. Architecture Clarification and Baseline Assumptions

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors directly. On an AIO or stacked 4-in-1 design, the architecture splits into two domains:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot | Attitude control. Emits bidirectional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 | AM32 | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, back-EMF zero-crossing detection. |
| Gate drive (×4) | FD6288Q (3 half-bridges each) | — | Bootstrap high-side and low-side drivers. |
| Power stage (×4) | 6 × N-MOSFET per motor | — | 3 half-bridges per motor. |

**Baseline electrical assumptions.** Replace these with your actual datasheet values.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current per motor | I_ph | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET R_DS(on) at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R₂₅ | 0.80 mΩ (40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3) |
| R_DS(on) temperature coefficient | α | 0.0045 /K |
| Switching charge (Q_gs2 + Q_gd) | Q_sw | 10 nC |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 30 nC |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 30 nC |
| Body-diode reverse recovery charge | Q_rr | 40 nC |
| Body-diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns |
| Copper path resistance per motor (phase in to phase out) | R_cu | 0.30 mΩ |

> **Feasibility flag.** If "80 A" means 80 A per motor, the full-throttle battery current is up to 4 × 80 × D ≈ 320 A. No nano-class connector (XT60/XT30) or 20×20 / 30×30 PCB can sustain that.
>
> This model treats 80 A as a **≤2–3 s per-motor burst rating**, which is how the industry uses the term. The thermal section below shows why that time limit is physics, not marketing.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step complementary-PWM current path

At any instant, two phases are active and the third floats:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T. With synchronous rectification, the low-side FET conducts for (1−D)·T.
- **Static leg:** the low-side FET is fully on for the whole 60° step.

The motor current therefore always flows through **exactly two FET channels plus PCB copper**:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph}^2\left[2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{cu}\right]
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]
$$

At T_j = 110 °C:

$$
R_{DS} = 0.80 \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 85) = 1.106\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

### 1.3 Per-motor and per-FET conduction loss

$$
P_{FET,motor} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.106\times10^{-3} = 14.16\ \text{W}
$$

$$
P_{cu,motor} = 80^2 \times 0.30\times10^{-3} = 1.92\ \text{W}
$$

**Per-FET distribution over one electrical revolution.** Each phase spends 1/3 of the revolution driven high, 1/3 driven low, and 1/3 floating:

| FET | Duty-weighted on-time | Loss at D → 1 |
|---|---|---|
| High-side | D/3 | I²R/3 = **2.36 W** |
