## 0. Feasibility Review

These issues determine whether the spec is achievable, so they come first.

| Issue | Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| **H743 cannot commutate the motors under ArduPilot** | ArduPilot only outputs DShot. It has no BLDC commutation firmware. | The architecture is an H743 flight controller plus **four dedicated ESC MCUs** (AT32F421K8U7 running AM32). The H743 speaks bi-directional DShot600 to them. |
| **"Nano" (20×20 mm) at 80 A burst ×4** | The four channels draw up to 320 A from the battery. That is roughly 1.75 kW of copper and FET loss density on a 400 mm² board. | **Not achievable** on 20×20 mm. The minimum credible format is a 30.5×30.5 mm two-board stack: a 6-layer FC board plus an 8-layer, 3 oz ESC board. The model in §2 shows that even this format is limited to bursts of about 2–3 s. |
| **48 kHz PWM at 80 A** | Switching loss is small compared to conduction loss (see §2). | Acceptable. AM32 supports 24–48 kHz PWM. |
| **Battery bus** | 320 A peak exceeds XT60 (60 A continuous) and XT90 (90 A continuous) ratings. | The burst rating is per motor and non-simultaneous. Specify **120 A total continuous / 200 A burst** at the battery pads with 10 AWG leads. |

## 2. Power-Stage Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Assumed operating point and FET parameters

The FET is a 40 V device in a PDFN 3.3×3.3 package (class representative, e.g. a 1.2 mΩ part).

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S, full charge |
| I_ph | 80 A | Burst phase current (trapezoidal, flat-top) |
| R_ds,25 | 1.2 mΩ | At V_GS = 10 V, 25 °C |
| α | 0.0045 /K | R_ds tempco |
| t_r + t_f | 20 ns | With 1.5 A/1.8 A FD6288Q drive |
| t_dt | 100 ns | Dead time per edge |
| V_SD | 0.8 V | Body diode forward voltage at 80 A |
| Q_rr | 40 nC | Reverse-recovery charge |
| Q_oss | 30 nC | Output charge |
| Q_g | 40 nC | Gate charge |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | PWM frequency |

### 2.2 Conduction loss

**Temperature dependence of R_ds(on):**

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{ds}(125)=1.2\,(1+0.45)=1.74\ \text{m}\Omega$$

**Conducting-path model.** With 6-step commutation and complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM, the current path at every instant contains exactly one high-side or sync-rectifying FET in the PWM leg plus one low-side FET in the return leg. This holds independent of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond}=I_{ph}^2\cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond}=6400\times 2\times 1.74\times10^{-3}=\mathbf{22.3\ W}$$

**Per-FET average.** Each FET carries current for 120° of the electrical cycle, which is 1/3 of the period. In the PWM leg, that conduction is shared between the high-side FET (fraction D) and the low-side FET (fraction 1−D).

- Long-term average per FET: P_cond/6 ≈ **3.7 W**
- Worst case, the low-side return FET: 22.3/2 × 1/3 ≈ **3.7 W**, with an instantaneous loss of 11.2 W

### 2.3 Switching loss at 48 kHz

| Term | Formula | Calculation | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hard-switching V·I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 20 ns × 48 kHz | **0.968 W** |
| Body diode during dead time | V_SD · I · 2 · t_dt · f | 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 kHz | **0.614 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f | 40 nC × 25.2 × 48 kHz | **0.048 W** |
| C_oss charge/discharge | ½ · Q_oss · V · f | 0.5 × 30 nC × 25.2 × 48 kHz | **0.018 W** |
| Gate drive (2 switching FETs, dissipated in driver/R_g) | 2 · Q_g · V_GS · f | 2 × 40 nC × 10 × 48 kHz | **0.038 W** |
| **Total switching** | | | **≈ 1.69 W** |

**Frequency sensitivity:** P_sw scales as ≈ 35 mW/kHz. At 24 kHz, P_sw ≈ 0.85 W, saving 0.84 W per channel at the cost of more current ripple. Ripple current is L-dependent; with motor L_ph ≈ 15 µH it is ΔI ≈ V·D(1−D)/(L·f) ≈ 8.75 A_pp at 48 kHz.

### 2.4 Copper, shunt, and capacitor losses (per channel)

**Phase copper.** The trace is 3 oz (105 µm), 6 mm wide, 15 mm long, doubled across 2 layers:

$$R=\frac{\rho L}{A}=\frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\times0.015}{2\times6\times10^{-3}\times105\times10^{-6}}=0.20\ \text{m}\Omega \;(\times1.39 \text{ at } 125^\circ\text{C})$$

- Loss with current flowing in 2 phases: ≈ 2 × 6400 × 0.28 mΩ = **3.6 W**

**Shunt.** One 0.25 mΩ shunt on the common return, for the total pack current: ~1.6 W at 80 A for a single channel. This is shared and is included in the board total below.

**Bus capacitor ripple.** Worst case is D = 0.5:

$$I_{C,rms}\approx I_{ph}\sqrt{D(1-D)}=80\times0.5=40\ \text{A}$$

- Spread over 4×10 µF X7R per channel (ESR ≈ 3 mΩ each): ≈ **1.2 W**
- Requirement: interleave PWM phase between channels (AM32 cannot synchronise, so rely on statistical cancellation) and add a low-ESR polymer bulk capacitor.
