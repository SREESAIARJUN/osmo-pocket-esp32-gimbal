# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control Loop, Anti-Cogging Feedforward, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

I don't have the Job #7 spec sheet, so the design is parameterized. The defaults below are typical for a camera gimbal, and every one of them is a constant in `GimbalConfig`. Replace them with your measured values.

## 1. Plant Model

### 1.1 Electrical (PMSM in the rotor dq frame)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

- Electrical angle and speed: $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$, $\omega_e = p\,\omega_m$.
- Torque for a surface PMSM: $\tau_m = \tfrac{3}{2} p \lambda_m i_q \equiv K_t i_q$.

### 1.2 Mechanical (one gimbal joint)

$$
J\dot\omega_m = K_t i_q - b\,\omega_m - \tau_{cog}(\theta_m) - \tau_{fric}(\omega_m) - \tau_{dist}
$$

The disturbance $\tau_{dist}$ comes from two sources:

- base motion coupled through bearing friction and cable drag;
- cross-axis inertia.

## 2. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 2.1 Transforms

**Clarke (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):**

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$
