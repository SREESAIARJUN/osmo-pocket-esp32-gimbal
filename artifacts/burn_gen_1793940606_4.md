## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R<sub>DS(on)</sub>

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0048\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

This value of α comes from the typical normalized curve of ≈1.6× at 150 °C.

At T<sub>j</sub> = 110 °C (iterated in Section 4):

$$R_{DS(on)} = 1.0\,\text{mΩ}\,(1+0.0048\cdot 85) = 1.41\ \text{mΩ}$$

### 1.2 Six-step inverter current paths

In 6-step commutation, exactly two phases conduct at any instant. One leg is PWM-switched and one leg's low side is held on.

**PWM leg (complementary), at duty D:**
- High-side FET conducts I for fraction D.
- Low-side FET (synchronous rectification) conducts I for fraction (1−D), minus dead time.
- Leg conduction loss: $P_{leg,PWM} = I^2 R\,[D + (1-D)] = I^2R$

**Static leg:** one FET on continuously, so $P_{leg,static} = I^2 R$.

**Per ESC channel, at any instant:**

$$\boxed{P_{cond,FET} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)}$$

This expression is independent of D. Synchronous rectification removes the duty dependence.

**Add PCB copper and phase-pad resistance.** Assume 2 oz outer and 2×2 oz inner layers in parallel, giving R<sub>Cu</sub> ≈ 0.35 mΩ for the bus-to-pad path per phase:

$$P_{cond,Cu} = 2\,I^2 R_{Cu}$$

**Current shunt.** Use one 0.25 mΩ shunt on the common DC return, carrying the average bus current $I_{bus} \approx D\cdot I$. Take a worst-case D = 0.9 at full burst:

$$P_{shunt} = (0.9\cdot 80)^2 \cdot 0.25\,\text{mΩ} = 1.30\ \text{W (shared for all 4 motors if one shunt; see note)}$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| Term | Expression | Per channel |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFET conduction | 2·80²·1.41 mΩ | **18.05 W** |
| Copper | 2·80²·0.35 mΩ | **4.48 W** |
| **Subtotal conduction** | | **22.5 W** |

The per-FET average over one electrical cycle matters for junction rise. Each FET conducts for roughly 1/3 of the electrical period. With the complementary split, the high-side and low-side FETs of a leg share the load:

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{18.05\ \text{W}}{6} \approx 3.0\ \text{W}$$

The commutation frequency at burst is about 1–3 kHz electrical for 14-pole motors. The junction thermal time constant (ms) averages this out, but the **instantaneous** conduction is I²R = 9.0 W per conducting FET. Use 9 W for the short-pulse Z<sub>θJC</sub> check in Section 4.4.

> **Shunt note:** A single total-current shunt at 4×80 A = 320 A peak gives P = 72²·4·0.25 mΩ... A single total shunt is not appropriate here. Use **per-ESC 0.25 mΩ shunts** (1.3 W each, 2512 size, 3 W rated) feeding each AM32's ADC. Then sum the currents on the H743 via ESC telemetry, or use a Hall sensor (e.g., ACS772-400) on the main bus for the BATT_MONITOR.
