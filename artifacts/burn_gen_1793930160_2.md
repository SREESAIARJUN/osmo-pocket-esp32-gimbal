# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model and Allocation Package

## 0. Architecture Constraints

These affect everything below.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In a 4-in-1 ESC, each motor's 6-step commutation and 48 kHz PWM run on a dedicated ESC MCU, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 to the ESC, then decodes eRPM telemetry from it.
2. **"80A" is treated here as per-motor burst.** At nano size (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm), 4 × 80 A = 320 A from the battery. That is far beyond the board's thermal capacity and any nano-class connector. The math below shows this rating is valid only for a few seconds. Treat it as a marketing burst number, not a design point.
3. **Every device parameter below is an assumption.** Replace them with datasheet values for your selected parts before freezing the design. The equations are the deliverable, not the specific numbers.

### Assumed parameters

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

**Current path.** With 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation and synchronous rectification (complementary PWM):
- At any instant, motor current flows through two legs in series.
- In each leg, exactly one FET conducts: the high-side during D, the low-side during 1−D.
- The conduction path is therefore always two R_DS(on) in series, independent of duty cycle, apart from the small dead-time interval.

**Per-motor conduction loss:**

$$P_{cond} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)} \cdot k_T = 80^2 \cdot 2 \cdot 0.75\text{m}\Omega \cdot 1.5 = \mathbf{14.4\ W}$$

**Per-FET loss.** Each phase is active for 120° of the electrical cycle, so a given FET conducts at most 1/3 of the time:

$$P_{FET,avg} \le \frac{I^2 R_{DS}k_T}{3} = 2.4\ \text{W}, \qquad P_{FET,inst} = 7.2\ \text{W}$$

**Copper and shunt loss:**

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \cdot 0.25\text{m}\Omega = 1.6\ \text{W}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing leg switches hard. The other active leg is static for each 60° sector.

**Switching time** (gate-charge method):

$$t_{sw} = \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_g} \approx 15\ \text{ns per edge}$$

**Hard-switching V·I overlap:**

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 30\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two edges per period):

$$P_{dt} = V_F \cdot I \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 200\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery:**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.05\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{oss} = 2 \cdot \tfrac12 C_{oss,er} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 1.5\text{n} \cdot 635 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.05\ \text{W}$$
