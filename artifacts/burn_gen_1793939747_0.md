## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle: θₑ = p·θₘ − θ_offset.

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform:**

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Torque: τ = (3/2)·p·λ_m·i_q = K_t·i_q.

### 1.3 Current PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$
v_d^* = PI_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = PI_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

The PI zero is placed on the electrical pole R/L. This gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10}
$$

With ω_c = 2π·1.5 kHz, L = 3 mH, R = 10 Ω: K_p = 28.3 V/A and K_i = 9.42×10⁴ V/(A·s).

**Delay compensation.** The PWM introduces 1.5·T_c of transport delay. Rotate by θₑ + 1.5·ωₑ·T_c in the inverse Park transform.

**Voltage saturation (circular limit).**

$$
|v_{dq}| \le \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}\cdot m_{max}
$$

Give priority to v_d, then limit v_q to √(V_max² − v_d²).
