# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal (Job #34)

## 0. Design Assumptions

I don't have the Job #34 specification, so the design rests on the baseline below. Every parameter is a `constexpr` or config field, so you can substitute the real values without restructuring the code.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5 Ω, L ≈ 2 mH, Kt ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Position sensing | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per axis (MT6701 / AS5048A class) |
| Current sensing | 2 inline shunts per motor, synchronous ADC sampling |
| MCU | STM32G474 class (Cortex-M4F, single-precision FPU, 170 MHz) |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 24 MHz, ODR 8 kHz |
| Loop rates | Current 20 kHz → Velocity 4 kHz → Attitude/Angle 1 kHz. IMU filtering at 8 kHz, fusion at 4 kHz. |

**Key architectural choice: velocity feedback.** The velocity loop is fed by the **inertial angular rate projected onto each joint axis**, taken from the fused gyro. It is not fed by the differentiated encoder.

- The gyro rejects base disturbances (the drone's own motion) directly at the high-bandwidth loop.
- The encoder is used for commutation, for anti-cogging phase, and as a fallback velocity source.

## 1. Inner Current Loop (FOC)

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle is θₑ = p·(θₘ − θ_off), where p = 7.

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, balanced phases):
$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform:**
$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant Model

The surface-PM motor has L_d = L_q = L:
$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

The electromagnetic torque is
$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q .
$$

### 1.3 Current PI with Decoupling (Pole–Zero Cancellation)

The control law, applied identically on both axes with x ∈ {d, q}:
$$
v_x^* = K_p^i e_x + K_i^i\!\int e_x\,dt + v_x^{ff}
$$

The cross-coupling feedforward terms cancel the speed-dependent coupling in the plant:
$$
v_d^{ff} = -\hat\omega_e \hat L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \hat\omega_e(\hat L i_d + \hat\lambda_m)
$$

**Gain selection.** Choose K_i/K_p = R/L so the PI zero cancels the electrical pole. The closed loop then becomes first order with bandwidth ω_ci:
$$
K_p^i = \hat L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_i^i = \hat R\,\omega_{ci},\qquad \omega_{ci}\le \frac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10}
$$

With f_pwm = 20 kHz, choose ω_ci = 2π·1500 rad/s. This gives K_p = 18.8 V/A and K_i = 47 100 V/(A·s).
