# STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation

**Job #61, Architecture Package Rev A**

## 0. Assumptions

Several parts of the request are ambiguous or physically aggressive. These decisions govern everything below.

| # | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **The H743 runs ArduPilot only. Each ESC channel has its own MCU** (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32). | The H743 could in principle commutate four motors, but no supported ArduPilot or AM32 path exists for that. Bi-directional DShot requires an ESC-side MCU to answer. |
| A2 | **"80 A burst" means per-motor phase current**, not battery current. | Phase current is what heats the FETs. Battery current is I_bat ≈ D·I_ph, which is about 76 A at D = 0.95. |
| A3 | Supply is 6S: 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal. | Worst case for switching loss. |
| A4 | Six-step trapezoidal drive with **complementary (synchronous) PWM** at 48 kHz. This is the AM32 default. | Determines the conduction-path model. |
| A5 | FETs are a representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PDFN part: R_DS(on) = 1.5 mΩ typ at 25 °C and V_GS = 10 V, Q_g ≈ 30 nC, Q_rr ≈ 40 nC, Q_oss ≈ 50 nC. | Placeholder values. **Substitute the selected part's datasheet values before release.** 40 V on 6S leaves thin margin; see §2.6. |
| A6 | The form factor is a "nano" board, taken as 20×20 mm (or up to 25.5 mm whoop) with 8 layers and 2 oz copper. | The H743 is only available in LQFP100 (14×14 mm) or larger, or TFBGA100 (8×8 mm). **TFBGA100 is effectively mandatory at 20×20 mm.** |

**Headline result:** a 20×20 board **cannot sustain 80 A per motor thermally**. It can survive 80 A **bursts of roughly 1 s with all four motors loaded**, and somewhat longer with a single motor. The numbers follow.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

R_DS(on)(T_j) ≈ R_DS(on),25 · [1 + α·(T_j − 25)], with α ≈ 0.006 /K, which is typical for trench MOSFETs in the 40 V class.

| T_j | R_DS(on) |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.50 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 2.18 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 2.40 mΩ (design value) |

### 1.2 Current paths in six-step with synchronous rectification

At any instant, two legs are active and one floats:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. The low-side FET (synchronous rectifier) conducts for 1 − D.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET conducts continuously for the 60° sector.

As a result, phase current I_ph always flows through exactly two channel resistances, independent of D (dead time excluded):

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.4\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{30.7\ W\ per\ ESC\ channel}$$
