## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In every production AIO of this class, the design is split into two layers:

| Layer | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (400/480 MHz) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) | Attitude control, emits bi-directional DShot600 to 4 ESC MCUs |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6 | AM32 (or BLHeli_32-compatible) | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |

> ⚠️ **Engineering flag:** Treat 80 A per channel on a 30.5 mm board as a burst rating of **about 1–2 s**, not a continuous capability. A 20×20 mm board cannot physically fit 24 PDFN 5×6 FETs. ## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Commutation Topology

AM32 uses trapezoidal 6-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM:

- At any instant, exactly **two FETs carry the full phase current I**:
  - one in the PWM leg (HS during t_on, LS during t_off via synchronous rectification);
  - one static LS (or HS) in the return leg.
- Each FET is active for 1/3 of the electrical period.
- Only **one leg is hard-switched** at a time.

### 1.2 Junction-Temperature-Dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{DS} = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 100) = 1.74\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Conduction Loss

Phase current RMS includes PWM ripple. The motor inductance L_ph and duty D set the ripple:

$$\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}, \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

For a typical 2207 motor, L_ph ≈ 15 µH. Take D = 0.5 and V_bemf ≈ 0.5·V_bus:

$$\Delta I = \frac{12.6 \times 0.5}{15\times10^{-6} \times 48\,000} \approx 8.75\ \text{A} \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6.4\ \text{A}^2 \;(\text{negligible against } 6400\ \text{A}^2)$$

Channel conduction loss:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) = 2 \times 6406 \times 1.74\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{22.3\ W}$$

Per FET, averaged over the electrical cycle: 22.3 / 6 ≈ **3.7 W**.

Conduction loss dominates. Halving R_DS(on) is worth far more than any switching optimization.
