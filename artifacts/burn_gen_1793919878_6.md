## 0. Architecture Assumptions

Several of these assumptions change the math, so they are stated before any results.

| Item | Assumption | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Topology | FC (STM32H743) + separate 4-in-1 ESC with 4× local ESC MCUs (AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32) | ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The H743 only sends DShot and receives eRPM telemetry. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs. |
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max (22.2 V nominal) | Sets switching loss and the MOSFET voltage rating (40 V class). |
| "80A burst" | 80 A per motor channel, phase current, ≤10 s | Per-channel, not total. |
| Form factor | "Nano" = 20×20 mm mounting (≈25×25 mm board) | Dominates the thermal result (see §3). |
| MOSFET | 40 V N-ch, PDFN 3.3×3.3 or 5×6, R_DS(on) = 1.0 mΩ typ @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | Placeholder. Substitute your selected part's datasheet values. |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal, complementary (synchronous) PWM, 120° conduction | Sets the conduction-loss distribution. |

> **Headline finding (details in §3):** An 80 A/channel burst on a 20×20 board is thermally viable only for about 1–2 seconds from a warm start. Continuous capability is about 20–25 A/channel with good prop-wash airflow. If 80 A burst for ≥5 s is a hard requirement, the board must move to 30×30 or larger with thicker copper and/or a heatsink.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ /^\circ\text{C}$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_DS ≈ 1.0 × 1.47 ≈ **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step current path

In six-step drive, current at any instant flows through **two series FETs** in the active path:

- **High side of the "+" phase.** It is PWM'd at duty D. During the off-time, current freewheels through the synchronous low-side of the same leg.
- **Low side of the "−" phase.** It is on for the full 60° sector.

Path resistance is therefore effectively 2·R_DS whether the PWM is on or off:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each of the 6 FETs per channel carries current for 120° of 360° (one third of the time). Under synchronous rectification, the high-side and low-side split that time according to D:

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}\ \text{(averaged over an electrical cycle)}$$

The worst case is D → 1 at full throttle, where the high-side FETs carry most of the loss.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A

$$P_{cond,ch} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

- Per FET average: ≈ 3.2 W.
- At D ≈ 1, the conducting high-side peaks at 6.4 W during its 120° window.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

One leg switches per sector (the PWM'd leg). All terms below are per channel.

### 2.1 V–I overlap loss (hard-switched edges)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

The rise and fall times are set by gate drive. Plateau-limited estimate:

$$t_{r,f} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}\,R_{G,tot}}{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}$$

With Q_gd = 8 nC, R_G = 5 Ω, V_drv = 10 V, and V_pl = 4 V, each edge takes about 6.7 ns. Allowing ~10 ns per edge for parasitics:

$$P_{VI} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

### 2.2 Output capacitance (Q_oss) loss

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 40\,\text{nC}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.05\ W}$$

### 2.3 Dead-time body-diode conduction and reverse recovery

$$P_{dt} = V_F I_{ph}\,(2t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8\times80\times200\,\text{ns}\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 30\,\text{nC}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.04\ W}$$

Dead time is assumed to be 100 ns per edge. AM32 allows this to be tuned; reduce it to the minimum safe value for your driver.

### 2.4 Gate drive loss

This loss lands in the driver IC, not the FETs:

$$P_{gate} = 2\,Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} = 2\times50\,\text{nC}\times10\times48\text{k} = 0.05\ \text{W}$$

### 2.5 Totals at 80 A, 25.2 V, 48 kHz

| Loss | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (T_j = 110 °C) | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| V–I switching | 0.97 W | 3.9 W |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 W | 2.4 W |
| Q_oss + Q_rr | 0.09 W | 0.4 W |
| Gate (driver) | 0.05 W | 0.2 W |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ, if per-channel) | 1.6 W | 6.4 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 22.5 W** | **≈ 90 W** |

**Key insight:** At 80 A, conduction loss dominates (>85% of the total). Changing f_sw from 24 to 48 kHz moves the total by only about 1.6 W/channel. **R_DS(on) and copper are the levers that matter.** 48 kHz is justified by motor current ripple and acoustics, not efficiency.
