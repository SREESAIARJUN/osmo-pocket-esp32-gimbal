## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Critical Findings

### 0.1 Assumed architecture

This design assumes the industry-standard topology:

- **FC board:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot. It generates DShot600 and receives bi-directional eRPM telemetry.
- **4-in-1 ESC board:** four independent ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. Each MCU drives one 3-phase bridge (6 N-FETs) through a 3-phase gate driver at **f_sw = 48 kHz**.

The H743 does **not** drive the MOSFETs directly. If you intended a single-MCU design where the H7 commutates all four motors, that is a different and much harder design (12 complementary PWM pairs plus BEMF/FOC on one die). Flag it and I will re-derive.

### 0.2 Critical findings (read first)

1. **80 A is physically a burst rating on a nano board, not a continuous rating.** At 80 A on all four channels, the power stage dissipates about 60 W. A 20×20 board in prop wash can reject about 4–6 W continuously. All four channels can sustain 80 A for **about 3 s**, starting from a warm board (§3.4).
2. **The DC bus is the real bottleneck.** Capacitor ripple reaches about 40 A_rms per channel, and plane/connector I²R adds several watts (§3.5).
3. **H743 + ArduPilot bi-directional DShot is well supported,** thanks to the H7's DMAMUX. The binding constraints are timer-channel pairing and DMA stream count (§4).

## 1. Power Stage Topology and Current Model

### 1.1 Commutation model

Each ESC uses 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation with synchronous complementary PWM. In any commutation sector:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) conducts for duty D, and the low-side (LS) synchronously rectifies for (1 − D).
- **Static leg:** one LS FET conducts continuously for the 120° sector.
- **Floating leg:** both FETs off (BEMF sensing).

### 1.2 Phase current

Assume ripple-free phase current I_ph (motor inductance ≫ ripple at 48 kHz for typical 5" to 7" motors).

| Quantity | Expression |
|---|---|
| HS FET RMS (PWM leg) | I_HS,rms = I_ph · √D |
| LS FET RMS (PWM leg, sync rect) | I_LS,rms = I_ph · √(1 − D) |
| Static LS FET RMS | I_static,rms = I_ph |
| Battery current per ESC (avg) | I_bat = D · I_ph |

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 0.70 × 1.5 = **1.05 mΩ**.

### 2.2 Per-ESC conduction loss

$$
P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}\,D + I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}\,(1-D) + I_{ph}^2 R_{DS} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

Conduction loss is **independent of duty** with synchronous rectification. This is the key simplification.
