# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have the original specification for Job #22, so this design states its hardware assumptions explicitly in §0. All gains are derived symbolically from plant parameters, so you can substitute your actual motor, encoder and inertia values.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

Power-invariant scaling is not used; amplitude-invariant scaling is.

**Clarke transform** (assuming $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):
$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off})$:
$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform:**
$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad
v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant in the d-q Frame

$$
v_d = R_s i_d + L_s \dot i_d - \omega_e L_s i_q
$$
$$
v_q = R_s i_q + L_s \dot i_q + \omega_e L_s i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

**Decoupling feedforward.** Adding the following terms makes each axis a pure first-order plant $G_i(s) = \dfrac{1}{L_s s + R_s}$:
$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L_s i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L_s i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

### 1.3 Current PI Gains (Pole-Zero Cancellation)

With $C_i(s) = K_p + K_i/s$, choose the PI zero to cancel the plant pole, $K_i/K_p = R_s/L_s$. The open loop then reduces to:
$$
L(s) = \frac{K_p}{L_s s}
\;\Rightarrow\;
T(s) = \frac{\omega_c}{s + \omega_c}
$$
$$
\boxed{K_{p,i} = L_s\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R_s\,\omega_c}
$$

For the assumed motor: $K_p = 22.6$ V/A and $K_i = 52{,}779$ V/(A·s).

**Delay constraint.** PWM plus computation adds roughly $1.5\,T_s = 75\,\mu$s of delay. The phase margin is approximately
$$
\text{PM} \approx 90^\circ - \omega_c \cdot 1.5\,T_s \cdot \tfrac{180}{\pi} = 90^\circ - 40.5^\circ = 49.5^\circ
$$
which is acceptable.

### 1.4 Voltage Limiting and Anti-Windup

The available voltage is the hexagon's inscribed circle:
$$
|v_{dq}| \le V_{max} = \frac{V_{dc}}{\sqrt3}\cdot m_{max},\qquad m_{max} = 0.95
$$

**Priority.** Allocate $d$ first, then give $q$ the remaining magnitude:
$$
v_q^{lim} = \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}
$$
