**Scope:** Power-stage loss model, thermal transient model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA plan, and reference netlists.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha_R (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C this gives R_DS = 0.65 mΩ × 1.5 = **0.975 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

During any commutation sector, two phases conduct:

- **PWM phase:** high-side (HS) on for duty D, synchronous low-side (LS) on for (1 − D).
- **Return phase:** LS fully on.
