## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Feasibility

These assumptions drive the numbers below. Change any of them and the equations still hold, but the results will move.

| Parameter | Assumed value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Form factor | 30.5×30.5 mm AIO, 8-layer, 2 oz on all layers | A true 20×20 "nano" cannot sustain 4×80 A. This is shown in §3. |
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max | Ripple and overshoot need FETs rated ≥ 40 V. |
| Burst rating | 80 A per motor phase-DC current, all 4 channels simultaneously | Worst case. Battery current is about 4 × 80 × D_eff. |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) PWM | Matches AM32/BLHeli_32 behavior. |
| PWM frequency | f_sw = 48 kHz | |
| MOSFET (per switch, one die) | 40 V, R_ds(on),25 = 1.2 mΩ @ V_gs = 10 V, Q_g = 30 nC, Q_gs2 + Q_gd = 8 nC, Q_rr = 50 nC, Q_oss = 25 nC, R_θjc = 1.2 °C/W, 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 DFN | Representative modern trench FET. Substitute your actual part's datasheet values. |
| Gate driver | 3-phase bootstrap driver (FD6288Q class), V_drv ≈ 10 V | |
| ESC MCUs | 4× STM32G071 (AM32) or AT32F421 | The H743 is the FC. It does not commutate motors. |
