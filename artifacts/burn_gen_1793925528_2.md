## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform:**

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)\quad(\text{assuming } i_a+i_b+i_c=0)$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θₑ = p(θₘ − θ_offset):

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park and inverse Clarke:**

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

$$v_a=v_\alpha,\quad v_b=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta$$

### 1.2 SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)

The zero-sequence offset and resulting duty cycles are:

$$v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_a,v_b,v_c)+\min(v_a,v_b,v_c)\big),\qquad D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}$$

This is linear up to $|v_{dq}| \le V_{bus}/\sqrt3$. Above that, the dq voltage vector is scaled down while preserving its angle (circular limit).

### 1.3 Current PI tuning by pole-zero cancellation

The plant is the motor's RL circuit:

$$G_i(s)=\frac{1}{Ls+R}$$

Placing the PI zero on the plant pole gives:

$$K_{p,i}=L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i}=R\,\omega_{ci}$$

Choose $\omega_{ci}=2\pi\cdot 1.5\text{ kHz}$, which is at most f_PWM/10.
