## 1. Control Architecture

```
            ┌──────────── 1 kHz ────────────┐┌──── 4 kHz ─────┐┌────── 20 kHz ───────┐
q_ref ──►(Σ)─► Attitude P/PI ─► ω_ref ─►J⁻¹─►(Σ)─► Vel PI ─►(Σ)─► i_q* ─► Current PI ─► SVPWM ─► Motor
           ▲                              ▲         ▲      ▲ i_ff(θ_m)    ▲  ▲ (Park)
           │ q̂ (Mahony)                    │ q̇̂ (gyro→joint)  anti-cogging │  │ i_abc, θ_e (encoder)
           └── Dual-IMU fusion ◄── notch/LPF ◄── ICM-42688-P ×2           └──┘
```

Two design choices drive the structure:

- **Attitude and rate feedback come from the camera-stage IMUs.** This is what actually stabilizes the payload.
- **Commutation and anti-cogging use the rotor encoder.** These are motor-frame phenomena, so they must be indexed by rotor position.

## 2. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 2.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant):**

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)\quad (i_a+i_b+i_c=0)$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}$:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 2.2 Plant model and PI tuning

The SPMSM (surface permanent magnet) dq model is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

**Decoupling feedforward.** Adding the cross-coupling and back-EMF terms back in leaves each axis as $1/(Ls+R)$:

$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e(L i_d+\lambda_m),\qquad \lambda_m = \frac{2K_t}{3p}$$

**PI tuning by pole-zero cancellation.** Choose the controller zero to cancel the motor pole, with current bandwidth $\omega_c$:

$$K_p^i = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i^i = R\,\omega_c$$
