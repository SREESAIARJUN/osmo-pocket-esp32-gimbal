## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller** MCU running ArduPilot. It does not commutate the motors. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. A typical choice is an AT32F421 or STM32G071 driving a 3-phase gate driver. The 48 kHz figure is therefore the **ESC MCU's PWM frequency**, and DShot600 is the FC-to-ESC link.

## 1. Conduction Losses at 80 A Burst

### 1.1 Temperature-corrected R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds(25)}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] = 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega\,(1+0.006\cdot75)=2.18\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

In any commutation step two legs conduct:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. The complementary low-side FET (synchronous rectification) conducts for (1−D).
- **Static leg:** the low-side FET conducts for 100% of the step.

$$P_{cond}=I_{rms}^2R_{ds}\,[D+(1-D)]+I_{rms}^2R_{ds}=2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}$$
