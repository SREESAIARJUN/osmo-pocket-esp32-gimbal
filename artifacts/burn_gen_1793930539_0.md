# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 80A-Burst 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #51)

**Scope:** Loss model, thermal model, ArduPilot bi-directional DShot600 timer and DMA allocation, and reference netlists.
**Status:** Engineering reference design. All component values are parametric and must be checked against the datasheets of the parts you actually procure. Validate by thermal-camera bench testing before flight.

---

## 0. Architectural Correction

**The STM32H743 must not commutate the motors.** A credible design is two-tier:

| Tier | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (480 MHz, 1 MB SRAM) | ArduPilot, sensor fusion, DShot master, telemetry |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421 or STM32G071, running AM32 | 6-step trapezoidal commutation, 48 kHz PWM, bi-dir eRPM reply |
| Gate drive (×4) | 3-phase half-bridge driver, e.g. FD6288Q class, ~5–10 V gate drive | High- and low-side drive with bootstrap |
| Power stage (×4) | 6× N-MOSFET, 40 V, DFN3.3×3.3 | 24 FETs total |

Running ArduPilot's EKF and four sensorless commutation loops on one core breaks hard real-time guarantees. It is also incompatible with ArduPilot's DShot/eRPM model, which assumes external ESCs. The rest of this document assumes this split.

**Form-factor warning (stated up front):** On a 20×20 mm or 25.5×25.5 mm board, "80A" is only a **transient (burst) rating**. Section 3 shows that continuous capability is roughly 15–25 A per channel, depending on airflow.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (Per Channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, phase current flows through exactly two FETs at any instant: one high-side and one low-side. The low-side FET carries the current during the PWM off-time. The total I²R therefore does not depend on duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = k_T \cdot R_{DS(on),25}
$$

The ripple correction (inductor ripple ΔI_pp) is:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}
$$

This correction is under 1% for typical motor inductance (L ≈ 10–20 µH) at 48 kHz, so it is neglected here.

$$
P_{cond} = 80^2 \cdot 2 \cdot 2.25\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{28.8\ W}
$$

Add trace and connector resistance:

- Assume about 0.5 mΩ of copper path per channel (motor pad plus internal planes): 80² × 0.5 mΩ = **3.2 W**.

### 2.2 Hard-Switching Loss

Only the PWM-active high-side FET switches hard.

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 20\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}
$$

### 2.3 Dead-Time (Body-Diode) Loss

$$
P_{dt} = V_{SD} \cdot I \cdot 2 t_{dead} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 200\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}
$$

### 2.4 Reverse Recovery and Output Capacitance

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\cdot 25.2\cdot 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}
$$

$$
P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{n} \cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}
$$

### 2.5 Gate Drive

Two actively switching gates per channel:

$$
P_g = 2\, Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2\cdot 60\text{n}\cdot 10\cdot 48\text{k} = 0.058\ \text{W}
$$

This power is dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FETs.

### 2.6 Channel Total at 80A

| Loss term | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction (FETs) | 28.80 |
| Copper path | 3.20 |
| Switching | 0.97 |
| Dead-time | 0.61 |
| Q_rr + Q_oss | 0.08 |
| Gate drive | 0.06 |
| **Total per channel** | **≈ 33.7 W** |
| **4 channels (all at burst)** | **≈ 135 W** |

Efficiency at 80 A, 50% duty: P_out ≈ 25.2 × 0.5 × 80 ≈ 1 kW, so η ≈ 96.7%. That figure looks good, but the absolute heat is what matters on a 4 cm² board.

**Key insight:** At 48 kHz, conduction loss dominates. It is about 30× the switching loss. Lowering f_sw would not help meaningfully. Reducing R_DS(on) by paralleling FETs or choosing a better package is the only effective lever.

### 2.7 Input Capacitor Ripple

The worst-case DC-link ripple per channel occurs at D = 0.5:

$$
I_{C,rms} = I\sqrt{D(1-D)} = 80 \cdot 0.5 = 40\ \text{A}_{rms}
$$

AM32 does not phase-interleave the four channels, so assume partial correlation, roughly 50–80 A_rms in total.

- **Requirement:** 4× (10 µF X7R 50 V 1206) placed locally per channel, plus a 1000 µF low-ESR electrolytic at the battery pads.
- **Electrolytic loss:** with ESR ≈ 15 mΩ, P = 50² × 0.015 ≈ 37 W if all ripple flowed through it.
