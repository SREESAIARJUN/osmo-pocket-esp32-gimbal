## 0. Assumptions and Corrections

Several assumptions shape everything below. Any one of them, if wrong, changes the numbers.

| # | Assumption / correction | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | The H743 runs **ArduPilot only**. It does not drive the gates. Each ESC channel has its own MCU (AT32F421 running AM32 is assumed). The H743 talks to those MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. | No ArduPilot target runs field-oriented control (FOC) or 6-step commutation on the H743 itself. "H743 4-in-1 ESC" really means an AIO board: an H743 FC plus 4 ESC MCUs. |
| A2 | 6S battery: V_bus = 25.2 V maximum, 22.2 V nominal. | Switching losses scale with V. |
| A3 | "80 A" is **phase current per channel, burst**, not continuous. | At 80 A the conduction losses alone make continuous operation thermally impossible on a nano board. See §3. |
| A4 | MOSFET is a **placeholder** 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN part (parameters in §1). | Substitute real datasheet values. The model is parametric. |
| A5 | Gate driver is FD6288Q-class (3 half-bridges, bootstrap). V_DRV = 10 V (from a 10 V LDO). | Sets gate current and therefore edge times. |
| A6 | 48 kHz complementary (synchronous) PWM, trapezoidal 6-step commutation (AM32 default style). | Determines which FETs conduct and for how long. |
