## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not drive the power stage.** In this design:

- **The H743 runs ArduPilot.** It sends bi-directional DShot600 to the ESC section and receives eRPM telemetry back.
- **The ESC section has its own MCU(s)** running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. These MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM, dead-time, and commutation.
- **ArduPilot cannot do sensorless commutation and flight control on one H743.** That function stays separate.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths
Under 6-step synchronous-rectified commutation, the motor current at any instant flows through:
- one high-side FET, which is either conducting or freewheeling through its complementary low-side FET, and
- one fully-on low-side FET.

So two R_ds(on) elements are always in series with the phase current, regardless of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot \left(2R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{cu} + R_{sh}\right)$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\,/\mathrm{K}\ \Rightarrow\ k_T(125^\circ\mathrm{C}) \approx 1.6$$

$$R_{ds}(125^\circ\mathrm{C}) = 1.6\ \mathrm{m\Omega}$$

### 1.3 Result at 80 A

| Term | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| FETs | 80² × 2 × 1.6 mΩ | 20.48 W |
| Copper | 80² × 0.5 mΩ | 3.20 W |
| Shunt | 80² × 0.25 mΩ | 1.60 W |
| **Conduction subtotal per channel** | | **25.3 W** |

Note the positive feedback: R_ds rises with T_j, so conduction loss is evaluated iteratively in §4. The 1.6× factor is the converged worst case at T_j = 125 °C.
