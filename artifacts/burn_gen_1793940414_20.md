**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 cannot practically commutate four BLDC motors. Only TIM1 and TIM8 have complementary outputs with hardware dead-time, which is enough for two motors at most. The standard and recommended architecture is:

## 1. Conduction Losses (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Topology insight

In 6-step commutation with complementary PWM, exactly two MOSFETs carry the phase current at every instant:

- the active high-side FET (during D) or its synchronous low-side partner (during 1−D) on the PWM'd leg, and
- the fully-on low-side FET of the return leg.

Conduction loss per channel is therefore duty-independent, excluding dead-time diode intervals:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2 \cdot \frac{R_{ds(on)}(T_j)}{n_p} + I^2 R_{shunt} + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

where n_p is the number of paralleled FETs per switch position.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{ds(on),25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.004\text{–}0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 100 °C with α = 0.0045:

$$R_{ds} = 1.2 \times 1.34 = 1.60\ \text{m}\Omega$$

Use the datasheet's normalized R_ds(on) vs T_j curve in preference to this linear fit.

### 1.3 Numbers (n_p = 1)

| Term | Expression | Value |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 80² · 2 · 1.60 mΩ | **20.5 W** |
| Shunt (0.5 mΩ, if per-channel) | 80² · 0.5 mΩ | 3.2 W |
| PCB copper and solder (~0.3 mΩ loop) | 80² · 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| **Total conduction** | | **≈ 25.6 W/ch** |

Because R_ds rises with temperature, conduction loss feeds back into itself. Solve iteratively:

$$T_j^{(k+1)} = T_{ref} + P(T_j^{(k)})\,Z_{th}$$

It converges in 2–3 iterations. If it diverges, you are in thermal runaway territory.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches. The high-side FET is hard-switched. The low-side FET commutates near zero voltage through its body diode, so its losses are dominated by reverse recovery and dead time.

### 2.1 Gate currents during the Miller interval

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{src}+R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}} = \frac{10-3}{5+4.7+1} = 0.65\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{sink}+R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}} = \frac{3}{2.5+4.7+1} = 0.37\ \text{A}$$
