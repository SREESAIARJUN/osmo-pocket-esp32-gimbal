## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx \frac{k_T - 1}{100} = 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_hot = 1.2 × 1.6 = **1.92 mΩ**. This value is used throughout as the conservative design case.

### 1.2 Per-leg current distribution in 6-step with synchronous rectification

Over one electrical cycle, each half-bridge leg spends:
- **1/3** of the cycle as the PWM leg: high-side on for D, low-side on for (1−D).
- **1/3** of the cycle as the return leg: low-side fully on.
- **1/3** of the cycle floating, used for BEMF sensing.

Averaged loss per FET over a cycle:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} D\, I^2 R_{DS}$$

$$P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)\, I^2 R_{DS} + \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(2-D)\, I^2 R_{DS}$$

Per channel (3 legs):

$$P_{cond,ch} = 3(P_{HS}+P_{LS}) = 2\, I^2 R_{DS} \quad (\text{independent of } D)$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, R = 1.92 mΩ

- I²R = 6400 × 0.00192 = **12.29 W**
- **P_cond per channel = 24.6 W**
- Worst-case single FET is the low side, at D → 0: (2/3)(12.29) = **8.2 W**. At D = 0.5 it is **6.1 W**.
- Worst-case high-side FET, at D → 1: **4.1 W**
