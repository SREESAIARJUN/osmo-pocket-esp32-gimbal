## 0. Assumed System Parameters

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC (e.g., GBM2804), 12N14P → pole pairs $p = 7$, $R_s = 5.6\,\Omega$, $L_s = 1.1\,$mH, $K_t \approx 0.06\,$Nm/A |
| Driver | 3-phase, 24 kHz center-aligned PWM, two low-side shunts (or inline), $V_{bus} = 12$ V |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (MT6701/AS5048A) per axis, SPI |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI @ 24 MHz, ODR 8 kHz, placed approx. symmetric about the payload CoM |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 (STM32G4/H7), single-precision FPU |

**Loop rates (integer-divided from the PWM ISR):**

| Loop | Rate | Feedback |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 24 kHz | shunts + encoder angle |
| Rate (velocity) | 8 kHz | IMU gyro projected on joint axis (+ encoder blend) |
| Angle (attitude) | 1 kHz | fused IMU attitude |
| Cogging LMS adaptation | 1 kHz | iq residual |

Each loop's bandwidth should be at least ~5–10× below the loop it encloses:
- Current loop: $\omega_{c,i} \approx 2\pi\cdot 2\,$kHz
- Rate loop: $\omega_{c,v} \approx 2\pi\cdot 150$–$250\,$Hz
- Angle loop: $\omega_{c,\theta} \approx 2\pi\cdot 20$–$40\,$Hz

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Electrical angle
$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi$$

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$)
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform
$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 PMSM model in the dq frame
$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

### 1.5 PI tuning by pole-zero cancellation
The plant is $G(s)=1/(Ls+R)$. Choose:
$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{c,i},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{c,i}$$

This gives the closed loop $\omega_{c,i}/(s+\omega_{c,i})$.
