## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Feasibility Flags

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. It does not commutate motors directly. Each ESC channel runs its own MCU (AM32 on AT32F421 or STM32G071 is assumed), which generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 issues bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry.
