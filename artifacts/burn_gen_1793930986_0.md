## 1. Plant Model

### 1.1 Electrical model (d-q frame)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

where ω_e = p·ω_m is the electrical speed and λ_m = 2K_t/(3p) is the flux linkage (amplitude-invariant transform).

### 1.2 Mechanical model (per axis)

$$
J\dot\omega_m = K_t i_q - b\,\omega_m - \tau_{cog}(\theta_m) - \tau_{d}
$$

τ_d collects external disturbances: cable drag, airframe vibration, and imbalance torque.

### 1.3 Cogging torque

Cogging torque is periodic in mechanical angle. Its fundamental index is the least common multiple of slots and poles:

$$
N_c = \operatorname{lcm}(N_s, 2p) = \operatorname{lcm}(12,14) = 84
$$

$$
\tau_{cog}(\theta_m) = \sum_{k=1}^{K}\left[a_k\cos(kN_c\theta_m) + b_k\sin(kN_c\theta_m)\right]
$$

In practice, magnet and slot asymmetry also produce low-order terms. These include the once-per-rev term (index 1), the pole-pair harmonic *p*, and the slot harmonic N_s. The compensator therefore uses a **general harmonic set** {h_1 … h_K}, with defaults {1, 2, p, N_s, N_c, 2N_c}.

## 2. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 2.1 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)
$$

### 2.2 Park transform and its inverse

$$
\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix},\qquad
\begin{bmatrix}v_\alpha\\ v_\beta\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & -\sin\theta_e\\ \sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}v_d\\ v_q\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 2.3 Electrical angle with latency compensation

The PWM update lags the angle sample by about 1.5 sample periods, so the angle is advanced to compensate:

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{0}) + 1.5\,T_s\,\omega_e
$$

θ_0 is the electrical zero offset found at calibration.
