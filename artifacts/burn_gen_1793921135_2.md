## 1. Field-Oriented Control (inner current loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle, where θ_off is found at calibration by aligning the d-axis:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant and current PI design

Dynamics in the dq frame:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L \dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_{pm}$$

Apply decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$v_d^* = u_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = u_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_{pm}$$

Each axis is then the first-order plant 1/(Ls+R).

Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole, $C(s)=K_p + K_i/s$ with $K_i/K_p = R/L$. The closed loop becomes first-order with bandwidth ω_cc:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{cc},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{cc}$$
