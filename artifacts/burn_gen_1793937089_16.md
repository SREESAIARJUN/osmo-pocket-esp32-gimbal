# STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC) — Job #62 Design Package

## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Feasibility Flags

These points must be settled before the numbers below mean anything.

1. **The H743 does not generate the 48 kHz motor PWM.** In every practical AIO, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends four bi-directional DShot600 streams to four ESC MCUs. These are typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32, and they generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. This document models both domains: FC timing and ESC power stage.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano/AIO footprint is only feasible for seconds.** Section 3 shows roughly 89 W of stage dissipation with all four channels at 80 A. Steady state is impossible on a 25–30 mm board. The spec must define the burst duration, duty, and number of motors simultaneously at 80 A.
3. **No MOSFET was specified.** I use a representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFET. All equations are parametric, so you can substitute the real datasheet values.
4. **Package size.** The H743 in LQFP100 (14×14 mm) is a poor fit for a 20×20 board. Use TFBGA100 or a 25.5/30.5 mm form factor. This document assumes **25.5×25.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz outer and inner layers**.
