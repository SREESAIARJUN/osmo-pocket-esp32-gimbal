# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Model (Job #4)

## 0. Assumptions and Architecture Notes

No prior design inputs for Job #4 were provided. Every result below rests on the stated baseline parameters. Replace them with datasheet values for your chosen parts and re-run the equations.

**The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** On every production AIO/stack design, the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 (for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071). That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM.

**80 A burst on a nano (20×20 mm) board is a transient rating, not a continuous one.** The thermal model in §4 shows the realistic burst duration is about 1–4 s. Treat that figure as a validation target.
