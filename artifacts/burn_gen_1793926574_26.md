# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #7): Power-Stage Model, DShot Timing, and Netlists

## 0. Feasibility Finding

**An 80 A per-channel burst rating cannot be met on a 20×20 mm board that also carries an STM32H743 and an IMU.** The derivation below shows why. Section 4 gives three ways to resolve it, and the rest of the document is written so the same models can be re-run for any of them.

Summary for the 20×20 single-board concept:

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total power-stage loss at 4 × 80 A (Tj ≈ 110 °C) | ≈ 105 W |
| Adiabatic heating rate of the whole board | ≈ 48 K/s |
| Time from 40 °C to a 110 °C board limit | ≈ 1.5 s (optimistic) |
| Sustainable continuous loss with prop-wash cooling | ≈ 5 W total (≈ 9–10 A per channel) |
| H743 / IMU co-location | Board temperature far exceeds IMU calibration stability and the MCU's 85 °C ambient grade |

## 1. System Architecture and Assumptions

```
6S LiPo (25.2 V max, 22.2 V nom)
   │
   ├─► 4-in-1 ESC: 4 × [AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32] → 3× half-bridge each (24 FETs total)
   │        ▲ bidirectional DShot600 (1 wire per motor, 3.3 V, open-drain-tolerant)
   │
   └─► BEC 5 V/2 A ──► FC: STM32H743VIT6 @ 480 MHz, IMU (ICM-42688-P), baro, flash, OSD
```

The H743 does not commutate the motors. Each channel runs its own ESC MCU. The H743 sends DShot commands and receives eRPM telemetry over the same wire.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (Six-Step, Synchronous/Complementary PWM)

### 2.1 Current paths
In 120° trapezoidal commutation, two phases conduct at any instant:
- **Phase A (PWM phase):** the high-side FET Q_AH conducts for D·T_sw. The complementary low-side FET Q_AL conducts the freewheel current for (1−D)·T_sw, minus dead time.
- **Phase B (sink phase):** the low-side FET Q_BL is on continuously for the whole 120° sector.

So the motor current I always flows through exactly two channels' worth of R_ds,on, whatever the duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

### 2.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]
$$

At T_j = 110 °C:

$$
R_{ds}(110) = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 85) = 1.2 \times 1.383 = 1.66\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

### 2.3 Numbers at 80 A

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.66\times10^{-3} = 21.2\ \text{W}
$$

$$
P_{cu,ch} = I^2 R_{cu} = 6400 \times 0.5\times10^{-3} = 3.2\ \text{W}
$$

### 2.4 Per-FET average
Each FET is active for 1/3 of the electrical cycle. Six FETs share 21.2 W, so the average is ≈ 3.5 W per FET. The instantaneous peak while conducting is ≈ 10.6 W per FET.

With R_th,j-c ≈ 1.5 K/W:
- Average junction-to-case rise ≈ 5 K.
