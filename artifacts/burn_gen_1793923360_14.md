## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Two points need to be settled before any equations are useful.

**1. The H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC.** ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The working architecture is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──bidir DShot600 ×4──► 4× ESC MCU (AM32 / BLHeli_32)
                                              e.g. AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071
                                              └─► 3-phase gate driver ─► 6 MOSFETs per motor
```

Everything below models the ESC power stage separately from the FC timer and DMA design.

**2. "80 A" on a nano (20×20 mm) board is a burst rating, not a continuous one.** Four motors at 80 A each draw 320 A from the battery. The math in §3 shows this is thermally survivable for about 2 seconds, not continuously. I will derive that limit rather than assume it away.
