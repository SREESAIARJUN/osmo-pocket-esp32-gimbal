# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #9): Power, Thermal & DShot Architecture

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and a Feasibility Check

The calculations below depend on parts I have selected as a reference design. Swap the parameters in §1 for your actual BOM and re-run the equations.

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Topology | H743 runs ArduPilot. Four independent ESC MCUs (AT32F421, AM32 firmware) each drive one 3-phase bridge. The H743 does **not** generate the 48 kHz PWM; it sends DShot600 commands. |
| Bus | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V maximum. Design to 40 V FETs, which gives 37% headroom for regen and ringing. |
| Burst rating | 80 A per channel, ≤10 s. Continuous rating is derived in §3. |
| Board | 30.5 × 30.5 mm, 8 layers, 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner power planes. |
| PWM | 48 kHz complementary (synchronous rectification), trapezoidal 6-step commutation, 100 ns dead time. |

**Feasibility warning:** "Nano" usually means a 20 × 20 mm board. Twenty-four FETs plus an H743 plus 80 A copper on 20 × 20 is not thermally credible. Section 3 shows that even 30.5 × 30.5 mm only supports a few seconds of 4 × 80 A. Treat "80 A" as a per-channel, single-motor-transient rating. Total simultaneous battery current must be limited by the shunt and firmware to roughly 150–180 A.

**Gate driver:** FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap), V_CC = 10 V, R_g,ext = 4.7 Ω on and off.

## 2. Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Conduction losses

Temperature-dependent on-resistance:

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At the design point T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 0.90 × 1.45 = **1.305 mΩ**.

In 6-step synchronous PWM, phase current always flows through exactly two FET positions:

- The **switching leg**: HS for duty D, then LS (synchronous) for 1−D. Together this is one R_ds for 100% of the period.
- The **static leg**: LS on for the whole 60° sector.
