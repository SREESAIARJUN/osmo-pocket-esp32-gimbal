**Architectural constraint:** The H743 does **not** commutate the motors. ArduPilot runs on the H743 and emits DShot. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, which generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. In this design that MCU is an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The design therefore has two firmware domains, and the thermal model below is driven by the ESC MCU's PWM scheme.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-Dependent R_ds(on)

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

$$
R_{ds}(125^\circ C) = 1.2 \times 1.5 = 1.8\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

### 2.2 Current Path in 6-Step with Synchronous Rectification

In any commutation sector, current flows through **one high-side leg and one low-side leg** of two different phases.

- **PWM on-time (D):** HS(phase A) → motor → LS(phase B)
- **PWM off-time (1−D):** LS(phase A) (sync-rect) → motor → LS(phase B)

Because sync-rect replaces the diode with a FET channel, **two R_ds(on) are always in series with the full phase current, independent of duty**:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

At I = 80 A:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.8\times10^{-3} = 6400 \times 3.6\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{23.0\ W}
$$

### 2.3 Per-FET Distribution

Each FET conducts during 120° of the 360° electrical cycle as the "active" leg. The off-phase low-side FET additionally carries freewheel current for the fraction (1−D).

$$
P_{HS,avg} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{ds} D, \qquad
P_{LS,avg} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{ds} + \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{ds}(1-D)
$$

At full-throttle burst (D → 0.95):

| FET | Instantaneous (during conduction) | Electrical-cycle average |
|---|---|---|
| High-side | 11.5 W | ≈ 3.6 W |
| Low-side | 11.5 W | ≈ 4.0 W |

At 80 A and a typical 5" craft, the electrical frequency is about 1–2 kHz. The die thermal time constant is about 1–5 ms, so the die partially tracks the instantaneous 11.5 W. Budget a ripple term of about **+5–8 K** on T_j above the average-based estimate.
