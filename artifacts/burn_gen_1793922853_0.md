## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class, single-precision FPU, 3× advanced timers |
| Motors | Hollow-shaft gimbal BLDC, e.g. GBM2804: 12N14P, R ≈ 5–10 Ω, L ≈ 2–4 mH |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701), SPI |
| Current sense | 2 or 3 low-side shunts per motor |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on separate SPI buses, shared 32 kHz CLKIN |

| Loop | Rate | Ts |
|---|---|---|
| PWM / current loop (FOC) | 20 kHz | 50 µs |
| IMU sampling (ODR) | 8 kHz | 125 µs |
| Velocity loop | 4 kHz | 250 µs |
| Attitude estimator and angle loop | 1 kHz | 1 ms |

The rate split follows a bandwidth-separation rule: each outer loop's crossover should be at most 1/4–1/5 of the inner loop's crossover.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = \left( p \cdot \theta_m - \theta_{e,0} \right) \bmod 2\pi$$

- $p$ is the number of pole pairs (7 for 14P).
- $\theta_{e,0}$ is the electrical offset, calibrated by aligning to the d-axis.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, 2-shunt)

$$i_\alpha = i_a, \qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt{3}}$$

With 3 shunts, first reject common mode: $i_a \leftarrow i_a - \tfrac{1}{3}(i_a+i_b+i_c)$, and similarly for $i_b$.

### 1.3 Park transform and its inverse

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \begin{bmatrix} v_\alpha \\ v_\beta \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & -\sin\theta_e \\ \sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} v_d \\ v_q \end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 PMSM model in the dq frame

$$v_d = R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e (L_d i_d + \lambda_m)$$

$$\tau = \tfrac{3}{2} p \left[\lambda_m i_q + (L_d - L_q) i_d i_q\right] \approx K_t\, i_q, \qquad K_t = \tfrac{3}{2} p \lambda_m$$

### 1.5 Current PI with pole-zero cancellation

Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole $R/L$:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci}, \qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}$$

The resulting closed loop is first order, $i/i^* = \omega_{ci}/(s+\omega_{ci})$.

- Pick $\omega_{ci} \le 2\pi f_s / 20$, so $f_{ci} \approx 1$ kHz at 20 kHz PWM.
- This leaves margin for the 1.5-sample delay (ZOH plus computation).

**Decoupling feedforward:**

$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L_q i_q, \qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e (L_d i_d + \lambda_m)$$

**Discrete form** (backward Euler, back-calculation anti-windup):

$$u_k = K_p e_k + I_{k-1} + u^{ff}_k$$

$$u^{sat}_k = \mathrm{sat}(u_k)$$

$$I_k = I_{k-1} + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw}\left(u^{sat}_k - u_k\right), \qquad K_{aw} \approx K_i / K_p$$

**Voltage limiting.** Apply a circular limit $\sqrt{v_d^2 + v_q^2} \le V_{bus}/\sqrt{3}$. Give d-axis priority, so $v_q^{max} = \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}$.
