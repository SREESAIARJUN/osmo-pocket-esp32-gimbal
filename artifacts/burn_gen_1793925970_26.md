**"Nano" form-factor warning:** 80 A per channel needs 24 FETs with milliohm-class R_ds(on). In 5×6 packages that does not fit on a 20×20 mm board. If "nano" means 20×20, you must accept 3.3×3.3 FETs (around 1.8 to 2.5 mΩ). Conduction loss then rises roughly 2× and burst capability drops to about 30 to 40 A. Everything below assumes 30.5×30.5 mm.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step synchronous commutation
In each commutation sector, two phases carry current:
- **Switching leg:** the high side conducts for duty D, and the low side (synchronous rectifier) conducts for 1−D.
- **Static leg:** one low-side FET conducts 100% of the time.

So, independent of D, current always flows through two series R_ds(on):

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha_{FET}(T_j - 25)]$$
