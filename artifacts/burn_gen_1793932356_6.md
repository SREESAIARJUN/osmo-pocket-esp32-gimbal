# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: 80 A Burst Power-Stage and Bidirectional DShot Design Model

**Scope:** This document covers the conduction-loss model, the 48 kHz switching-loss model, the transient thermal model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer and DMA allocation, and representative netlists.

**How to use the numbers:** No component-level datasheets for Job #26 were provided. Every device parameter below is a declared representative value, marked [P]. Substitute your BOM values into the equations. The structure of the model holds regardless; the numbers do not.

---

## 0. Architecture Clarification

An STM32H743 does not commutate the motors directly. The correct partitioning is:

```
[STM32H743 / ArduPilot] --4x bidir DShot600--> [4x ESC MCU, e.g., AT32F421/G071 running AM32]
                                                 |-- 48 kHz complementary PWM --> 3-phase gate driver --> 6 FETs/motor
                                                 |<- eRPM GCR telemetry (same wire)
```

- The 48 kHz switching frequency is generated by the ESC MCUs, not the H743.
- The H743's real-time burden is the DShot output plus input-capture DMA (Section 4).

### Reality flag: form factor vs. 80 A burst

At a true 20×20 "nano" footprint, 80 A per channel cannot be sustained for more than about 1–2 s. Section 3 is built on a 36×36 mm, 30.5 mm-mount, 8-layer board. If Job #26 is literally 20×20, scale the thermal capacitance down by about 3.2×.
