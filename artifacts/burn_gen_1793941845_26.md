# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage and DShot Design Model

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

Two separate processors are involved, and the requirements split between them:

| Requirement | Where it lives |
|---|---|
| 80 A burst conduction and thermal | ESC power stage (MOSFETs, copper, capacitors) |
| 48 kHz switching | ESC MCU firmware (AM32 / BLHeli_32 / Bluejay) driving the gate driver |
| Bidirectional DShot600 and DMA | STM32H743 running ArduPilot |

The H743 never switches the MOSFETs. It sends DShot frames to four ESC MCUs (for example AT32F421 running AM32), and each ESC MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM.

The model needs MOSFET parameters, so I use a representative 40 V PDFN5x6 part. **Substitute your actual datasheet values. The equations are general; the numbers are only as good as these inputs.**

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S fully charged (worst case for switching loss) |
| I_burst | 80 A | Per-motor DC (battery-side) current |
| R_ds,25 | 1.0 mΩ | At V_gs = 10 V |
| α | 0.0045 /K | R_ds temperature coefficient |
| Q_gs2 + Q_gd | 4 + 8 nC | Switching charge |
| Q_g | 40 nC | Total gate charge |
| Q_rr | 30 nC | Body-diode reverse-recovery charge |
| C_oss,er | 1.0 nF | Energy-related output capacitance |

## 1. Conduction Losses at 80 A Burst

### 1.1 Current relationships

The model assumes six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification:
- Two phases conduct at any instant.
- One high-side FET is PWM'd.
- One low-side FET is held on.
- The complementary low-side of the PWM'd leg conducts during the off-time.

Phase current during conduction, at duty D:

$$I_{ph} \approx \frac{I_{DC}}{D}$$

At burst (D → ~0.95–1.0), I_ph ≈ 80–84 A. I use **I_ph = 80 A at D ≈ 1** for conduction and evaluate switching at D = 0.9 (§2).

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Loss per conducting FET and per channel

Instantaneous loss in each conducting FET:

$$P_{FET,inst} = I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 6400 \times 1.45\,\text{m} = 9.28\ \text{W}$$

Each FET conducts for 120° electrical (1/3 of the period), so its average is:

$$P_{FET,avg} = \tfrac{1}{3}(9.28) = 3.09\ \text{W}$$

Per channel, two FETs are always in the current path:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = \mathbf{18.6\ W}$$

### 1.4 Copper loss (often underestimated)

Consider a phase trace on 2 oz copper (70 µm), 3 mm wide and 10 mm long:

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho L}{A} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\times 0.010}{3\times10^{-3}\times 70\times10^{-6}} = 0.82\ \text{m}\Omega$$

At 25 °C this is comparable to the FET itself. Copper has α_Cu ≈ 0.0039/K, so at 100 °C it reaches ≈1.06 mΩ.

The burst path includes the battery pad, bus, FET, phase trace, and phase pad. Assume an effective 0.5 mΩ after paralleling 4 inner layers plus stitching vias:

$$P_{Cu,ch} = 80^2 \times 0.5\,\text{m} = 3.2\ \text{W}$
