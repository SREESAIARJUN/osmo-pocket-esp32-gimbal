## 0. Architectural Assumptions

| Parameter | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal | 40 V MOSFETs required |
| ESC topology | 3 half-bridges per motor, trapezoidal 6-step with synchronous rectification | AM32/BLHeli_32-class firmware |
| ESC MCUs | 4× STM32G071 or AT32F421 (one per channel) | The H743 runs ArduPilot only. ArduPilot does not commutate motors. |
| PWM frequency | 48 kHz | |
| Burst current | 80 A per motor, ≤2 s | Continuous rating derived in §3 |
| MOSFET (representative) | 40 V PQFN 3.3×3.3 / 5×6, R_DS(on) = 1.0 mΩ max @ 25 °C, Vgs = 10 V, Qg ≈ 60 nC, Qgd ≈ 10 nC, Qrr ≈ 50 nC, Qoss ≈ 60 nC | Replace with your selected part's datasheet values |
| Board | 30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz copper on every layer | A 20×20 "nano" variant is evaluated in §3.5 |

**Key finding:** 80 A per channel can be met as a short burst only. Even on a 30.5 mm board it is not a continuous rating. A 20×20 mm board halves the burst window. The derivation follows.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × 1.6 = **1.6 mΩ**.
