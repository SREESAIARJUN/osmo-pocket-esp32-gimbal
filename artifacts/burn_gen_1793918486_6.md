# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture & Mathematical Model

**Document:** Job #1, Rev A (engineering baseline)
**Scope:** 80 A burst conduction losses, 48 kHz switching losses, transient thermal model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and reference netlists.

> **Status:** This is an analytical baseline, not a released design. Device parameters are representative values for the 40 V, ~1 mΩ PDFN5×6 MOSFET class. Before layout freeze, replace them with the datasheet values of your selected parts and validate with thermocouple/IR testing.

---

## 0. Architectural Ground Rules

1. **The STM32H743 does not drive the power stage.** It runs ArduPilot and emits DShot600. Each motor channel has a dedicated ESC MCU that performs commutation at 48 kHz:
   - Recommended: 4 × AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32.
   - Alternative: 1 × MCU per pair.
   - Merging commutation into the H743 is not supported by ArduPilot and is not recommended.
2. **"80 A burst" is defined as 80 A per-channel phase current.** Battery current is lower by the duty cycle: I_bat ≈ D·I_ph.
3. **Bus:** 6S. V_bus,max = 25.2 V, V_bus,nom = 22.2 V. All loss calculations use 25.2 V as the worst case.
4. **Form factor:** "Nano" is interpreted as a 25.5 × 25.5 mm (M2) or 30.5 × 30.5 mm board. Section 3 shows that **80 A on all four channels simultaneously is a seconds-scale burst rating, not a continuous rating.** Treat it accordingly in marketing and firmware limits.
