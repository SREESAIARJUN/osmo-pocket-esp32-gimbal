## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flag

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU. It does not commutate the motors. In any 4-in-1 ESC, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 (for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071), one per motor or one per pair. The data paths are:

- **H743 → ESC:** bidirectional DShot600 commands.
- **ESC → H743:** eRPM telemetry via GCR.

The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, not the H743. The model below reflects that split.

**Feasibility.** Stated plainly, the analysis in §3 shows the following:

- **Continuous operation is impossible.** On a 20×20 mm "nano" form factor, 80 A per channel is not a sustainable rating. All four channels at 80 A dissipate about 80 W in a board with roughly 5 J/K of thermal mass.
- **Burst operation is limited to seconds.** "80 A burst" is achievable only for about 2–4 s from a warm start. A realistic continuous rating is about 25–35 A per channel, with airflow.
- **Marketing must not exceed this.** The datasheet rating should not be stated more aggressively than this, or field failures (FET delamination, desaturation, fire) are likely.
