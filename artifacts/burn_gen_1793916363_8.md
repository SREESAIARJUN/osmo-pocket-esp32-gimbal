**Architecture clarification.** The H743 does not switch the bridge. It generates DShot600 frames and decodes bidirectional eRPM telemetry. The 48 kHz PWM is produced by the four ESC MCUs. The DMA analysis (§4) applies to the H743. The loss analysis (§2) applies to the ESC power stage.

**Feasibility flags. Read these first.**

1. **Continuous operation is not feasible.** On a 20×20 or 30.5×30.5 board, 4 × 80 A is a burst-only rating. Steady-state convection cannot remove about 80 W (§3.3).
2. **Battery and connector limits.** Four channels at 80 A means about 320 A of battery current, which exceeds XT60 and XT90 ratings and most packs. In practice, 80 A will be per-motor and transient.
3. **Voltage overshoot.** With 40 V FETs on 6S, layout loop inductance is the critical risk (§5.3).
