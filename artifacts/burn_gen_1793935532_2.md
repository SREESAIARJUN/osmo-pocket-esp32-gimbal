## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = \operatorname{wrap}_{2\pi}\!\left(p\,\theta_m - \theta_{e,0}\right)$$

- θ_m is the mechanical angle from the encoder.
- θ_{e,0} is found once at commissioning. Apply a fixed voltage vector with V_d > 0 and V_q = 0, let the rotor settle, then record p·θ_m.

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms

The Clarke transform is power-variant and uses two measured phases with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

The Park transform rotates into the rotor frame:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform rotates back:

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.3 Motor model and torque

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

$$\tau_e = \tfrac32\,p\,\lambda_m\, i_q = K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.4 Current PI design by pole-zero cancellation

The plant per axis is $G(s) = 1/(Ls+R)$. Choosing the PI zero to cancel the plant pole gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10}$$
