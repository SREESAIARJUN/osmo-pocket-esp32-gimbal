### Architecture

```
            ┌──────────────── 1 kHz ───────────────┐ ┌──── 4 kHz ────┐ ┌──── 20 kHz ────┐
θ*_cam ──►(Σ)─► Angle P + ω_ff ──► ω* ──►(Σ)─► Vel PI ─►(Σ)─► iq* ──►(Σ)─► Current PI ─► SVPWM ─► Motor
           ▲                             ▲              ▲ +iq_cog(θ)   ▲    (d/q decoupled)
           │                             │              │ +J·α*/Kt     │
     IMU attitude (Mahony)       IMU gyro rate (fused,   │       Clarke/Park(θe from encoder)
```

The stabilization loops close on the inertial (IMU) quantities. The encoder is used only for commutation (θe), cogging-map lookup, and joint-frame kinematics.

## 1. Current (Torque) Loop – FOC

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke transform:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

Park transform, with θe = p·θm − θoffset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 PMSM dq model

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

Torque is given by:

$$
\tau = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q
$$

### 1.3 PI with pole-zero cancellation

The plant is 1/(Ls+R). Choosing the PI zero to cancel the plant pole gives a closed loop of ωc/(s+ωc):

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10}
$$

Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

Set id* = 0.

### 1.4 Voltage limit and SVPWM

The linear-region limit is ‖v‖ ≤ Vdc/√3. Clamp vq with priority, giving vd its share first:

$$
v_d \leftarrow \text{clamp}(v_d, \pm V_{max}),\quad v_q \leftarrow \text{clamp}\left(v_q, \pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}\right)
$$
