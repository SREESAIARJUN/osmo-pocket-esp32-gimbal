## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous drive
In any commutation sector, one leg is PWM-switched (high-side at duty D, low-side freewheeling at 1−D) and one low-side is held on. Exactly two MOSFETs carry current I at every instant, whatever the duty cycle.

Per-FET RMS current over one electrical cycle:

- High-side: I²_rms,HS = I²·D/3
- Low-side: I²_rms,LS = I²/3 + I²(1−D)/3

Sum over the three legs:

$$\sum I_{rms}^2 = 3\left[\frac{I^2 D}{3} + \frac{I^2}{3} + \frac{I^2(1-D)}{3}\right] = 2I^2$$

This gives the channel conduction loss:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]$$
