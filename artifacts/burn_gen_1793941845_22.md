## 0. Assumptions and Architectural Corrections

Two points in the brief need correcting before the analysis.

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is a flight-control stack. It emits DShot commands and does not run field-oriented or six-step commutation. Each of the four ESC channels needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. This design uses the AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 drives four bi-directional DShot600 lines to those MCUs.

2. **80 A is a burst figure, not a continuous rating, on a nano form factor.** The analysis below shows that a 26 × 26 mm board can sustain roughly 20–25 A per channel continuously in prop-wash airflow. A simultaneous 80 A burst on all four channels is thermally limited to about 1 second. Treat this as a validated datasheet claim with a defined duration, not an open-ended spec.
