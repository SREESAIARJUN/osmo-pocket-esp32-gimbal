# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC with Integrated 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Model, DShot Timer Plan and Netlist

## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Assumptions

I have no prior context for "Job #42", so every parameter below is stated explicitly. Replace the placeholder values with your actual BOM datasheet figures before sign-off.

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600. It does **not** commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Typical choices are the STM32G071 or AT32F421, and a single-MCU quad design is also possible. That ESC MCU generates the **48 kHz** complementary PWM. The H743's timers are therefore spent only on DShot, not on the bridge.

> **Bottom line up front:** a 20×20 board cannot sustain 4 × 80 A. The math below shows about 130 W of dissipation into a board with roughly 2.3 J/K of heat capacity. That is a temperature rise of about 56 K/s. "80 A burst" is only defensible as a **sub-second, per-motor** rating, and firmware current limiting must enforce it.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C this gives R_DS = 1.5 × 1.6 = **2.4 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step current path

With complementary PWM, the motor current always flows through exactly **two** conducting devices:

- During the ON interval: high-side (HS) of phase A plus low-side (LS) of phase B.
- During the OFF interval: synchronous LS of phase A plus LS of phase B.

The total conduction loss per channel is therefore independent of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 2.4\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{30.7\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-device split over one electrical revolution

Each device conducts for 120° (1/3 of the revolution). Its share depends on its role:

| Device role | Conduction weight | Loss at D = 1 | Loss at D = 0.5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| HS (PWM'd) | D/3 | 5.1 W | 2.56 W |
| LS (synchronous, complementary) | (1−D)/3 | 0 W | 2.56 W |
| LS (static 120°) | 1/3 | 5.1 W | 5.1 W |

Averaged over the six devices, the loss is **≈ 5.1 W per MOSFET**.

### 1.4 Copper and shunt loss

**Current-carrying layers.** Four 2 oz layers in parallel give a sheet resistance of 0.25 mΩ/□ ÷ 4 ≈ 62 µΩ/□. An effective path of about 4 squares (bus → bridge → phase pad) gives:

$$P_{Cu} \approx 80^2 \times 0.25\,\text{m}\Omega \approx \mathbf{1.6\ W/ch}$$

**Shunt.** A 0.25 mΩ shunt adds another **1.6 W**. Consider measuring current at the MOSFET R_DS instead, or use a single shared shunt.

## 2. Switching Loss at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd half-bridge switches hard. Each term below is per channel.

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V·I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 20 ns × 48 k | 0.968 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 30 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.036 W |
| Coss (output capacitance) | ½·Q_oss·V·f_sw | 0.015 W |
| Dead-time body-diode conduction | 2·V_SD·I·t_dt·f_sw = 2 × 0.8 × 80 × 100 ns × 48 k | 0.614 W |
| Gate drive (2 switching FETs) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw = 2 × 30 nC × 10 V × 48 k | 0.029 W |
| **Total P_sw** | | **≈ 1.66 W** |

Notes:

- The overlap term is the dominant switching loss. It scales linearly with f_sw.
- The dead-time term is the second largest. Trimming dead time to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W/ch, provided the shoot-through margin is confirmed.

**Sensitivity to switching frequency:**

$$\frac{\partial P_{sw}}{\partial f_{sw}} \approx 34.6\ \mu\text{W/Hz}$$

- Going from 48 kHz to 24 kHz saves about 0.83 W/ch.
- That saving is small next to 30 W of conduction loss. **At 80 A, conduction dominates (about 95 %).** Changing f_sw is not the lever. R_DS(on) and burst duration are.

## 4. Thermal Model

### 4.1 Junction-level Foster network (per MOSFET)

$$Z_{th}(t) = \sum_{i=1}^{3} R_i\left(1-e^{-t/\tau_i}\right)$$

| Stage | R_i (K/W) | τ_i |
|---|---|---|
| Die → lead frame | 0.3 | 1 ms |
| Package → PCB pad | 1.2 | 50 ms |
| Pad → local copper spreading | 18 | 8 s |

These are placeholder values. Fit them to the datasheet Z_th curve and to a thermal-camera step test.

**Junction rise above local board temperature, at 5.4 W per device** (5.1 W conduction plus about 0.3 W switching on the active HS):

- t = 100 ms: Z_th ≈ 0.3 + 1.2(0.865) + 18(0.0124) ≈ 1.56 K/W, so **ΔT ≈ 8.4 K**.
- t = 1 s: Z_th ≈ 0.3 + 1.2 + 18(0.118) ≈ 3.62 K/W, so **ΔT ≈ 19.5 K**.

The die itself is fine. **The board is the bottleneck.**
