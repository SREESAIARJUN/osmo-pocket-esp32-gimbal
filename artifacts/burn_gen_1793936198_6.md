# STM32H743 FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC Stack: Loss Model, Thermal Budget, Bidirectional DShot DMA Plan, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Assumptions

### Architecture

An STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFET bridges directly in any practical 4-in-1 design. The intended stack is:

- **FC board:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot. It emits bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry.
- **ESC board:** four channels, each with its own small MCU (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Each MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and 6 N-MOSFETs at 48 kHz.

If Job #49 really specifies one H743 commutating all 12 half-bridges, raise that as a design-review issue. The H743 lacks the advanced-timer count for 4× complementary 3-phase PWM with independent BEMF zero-cross sensing, and ArduPilot has no such firmware.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Topology identity

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM, as in AM32 default).

- **During on-time:** current flows through the high-side FET of phase A, the motor, and the low-side FET of phase B.
- **During off-time:** current freewheels through the low-side FET of phase A (sync-rect), the motor, and the low-side FET of phase B.

So at every instant exactly two channels of R_ds(on) are in series, independent of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j) \quad\text{(without sync-rect, add } (1-D)\,I_{ph}V_{SD}\text{ instead)}$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha (T_j-25)\right] \;\approx\; R_{25}\left(\tfrac{T_j+273}{298}\right)^{2.3}$$

At T_j = 110 °C the factor is ≈ 1.58, so R ≈ 1.26 mΩ.

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution over one electrical period

Each leg spends 120° high-active, 120° low-active, and 60° + 60° floating:

| FET | Duty share | Loss term |
|---|---|---|
| High-side | (1/3)·D | I²R·D/3 |
| Low-side | (1/3)(1−D) + 1/3 | I²R·(2−D)/3 |

The low-side FET is the hotter device at low D. That matters for hover-to-punch transitions and for which FET you place over the thermal vias.

### 1.4 Numbers per channel at 80 A, T_j = 110 °C

| Item | Equation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFET conduction | 2 · 80² · 1.26 mΩ | **16.1 W** |
| Shunt | 80² · 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| Copper (phase + bus pours, est. 0.15 mΩ effective) | 80² · 0.15 mΩ | 0.96 W |
| **Conduction subtotal** | | **≈ 18.7 W / channel** |

Self-consistency check: R_ds(on) depends on T_j, which depends on P. Iterate with T_j = T_case + P_FET · R_θjc. This converges in 2–3 passes for this design. Thermal runaway occurs only when dP/dT · R_θ ≥ 1, which is far away here.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

With synchronous rectification, only the PWM'd leg hard-switches. Its high-side FET turns on into a conducting body diode.

$$P_{sw} = \underbrace{\tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f)f_{sw}}_{V\!-\!I\ overlap} + \underbrace{Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw}}_{C_{oss}} + \underbrace{Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw}}_{recovery} + \underbrace{2V_{SD}I_{ph}t_{dt}f_{sw}}_{dead\text{-}time} + \underbrace{N_{FET}\,Q_gV_{drv}f_{sw}}_{gate\ (driver)}$$

The gate term counts all 6 FETs as an upper bound. Only the PWM'd leg (2 FETs) switches at f_sw, so the true value is about 1/3 of this.

| Term | Numbers | Result |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | 0.5·25.2·80·24 ns·48 kHz | 1.16 W |
| C_oss | 40 nC·25.2·48 kHz | 0.05 W |
| Q_rr | 50 nC·25.2·48 kHz | 0.06 W |
| Dead-time diode | 2·0.8·80·60 ns·48 kHz | 0.37 W |
| Gate drive (6 FETs, dissipated in driver/R_g) | 6·60 nC·10·48 kHz | 0.17 W |
| **Switching subtotal** | | **≈ 1.8 W / channel** |

### Edge-time derivation

Use the Miller plateau to replace the assumed t_r and t_f with your own:

$$t_{r}\approx \frac{Q_{gd}\,(R_{drv,on}+R_g)}{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}},\qquad t_f\approx \frac{Q_{gd}\,(R_{drv,off}+R_g)}{V_{plateau}}$$

Design implication: switching loss is under 10% of total at 80 A. The loss budget is R_ds(on)-dominated, so spend BOM money on R_ds(on) and copper, not on faster edges. Faster edges worsen ringing and EMI into the H743's IMU
