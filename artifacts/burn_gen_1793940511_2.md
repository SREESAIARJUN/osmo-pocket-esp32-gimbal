## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}) \mod 2\pi$$

θ_offset is found at calibration. Align the rotor by injecting V_d at θ_e = 0, then record θ_m.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant)

With 3 shunts, enforce i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$i_\alpha = \tfrac{2}{3}i_a - \tfrac{1}{3}(i_b + i_c), \qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_b - i_c)$$

With 2 shunts:

$$i_\alpha = i_a, \qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant model

The surface-PMSM model in the dq frame is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

The torque is:

$$\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2}\,p\,\lambda_m\, i_q \equiv K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.5 PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

Choose the current bandwidth ω_c. A good starting point is ω_c = 2π·1.5 kHz, which is at most about 1/10 of the PWM rate.

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c, \qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c$$

The PI zero R/L then cancels the electrical pole. The closed loop becomes first order:

$$\frac{i}{i_{\text{ref}}} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

Discretized (backward Euler, with back-calculation anti-windup gain K_aw = 1/K_p):

$$e_k = i_{\text{ref},k} - i_k$$

$$u^*_k = K_p e_k + I_k + u_{ff,k}$$

$$u_k = \operatorname{sat}(u^*_k)$$

$$I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_c\, e_k + \frac{T_c}{T_{aw}}(u_k - u^*_k)$$

The feedforward terms are:

$$u_{ff,d} = -\hat\omega_e \hat L\, i_q, \qquad u_{ff,q} = \hat\omega_e(\hat L i_d + \hat\lambda_m)$$

**Voltage limiting.** Limit the dq vector to the SVPWM linear region:

$$\|v_{dq}\| \le V_{bus}/\sqrt3$$

Give priority to v_d:

$$v_{q,\max} = \sqrt{V_{\max}^2 - v_d^2}$$

**Delay compensation.** Rotate the inverse Park by the 1.5-sample transport delay:

$$\theta_{e,\text{out}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_c$$
