# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #44): Electrical, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility

Two constraints change the scope as written, so I'm stating them first.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.**
- ArduPilot is a flight-control stack with no BLDC commutation or FOC path. It outputs DShot.
- The correct topology is: H743 (ArduPilot) → bi-directional DShot600 → **4× dedicated ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32) → 3-phase gate drivers → MOSFET bridges.
- The 48 kHz figure is therefore the **ESC MCU's PWM frequency**, not an H743 timer.

**2. "80 A burst" on a nano form factor is not physically achievable × 4 channels.**
- The math below shows roughly **33 W per channel** at 80 A, which is about **135 W into a 20×20 / 25×25 mm board**.
- I model it rigorously and give the actual burst-duration limit. The honest result is that 80 A is a **≤1–2 s per-channel transient**.
- The realistic continuous rating is 35–45 A per channel on a 30.5×30.5 mm board with 2 oz+ copper.

**Design assumptions** (change any of them and re-run the equations):

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max (worst case for switching) |
| Burst phase current | I | 80 A per channel |
| PWM | f_sw | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification) |
| MOSFET | — | 40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3, R_DS(on),max = 1.6 mΩ @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V, Q_g ≈ 50 nC, Q_gd ≈ 10 nC, Q_rr ≈ 40 nC |
| R_DS(on) temperature coefficient | α | ≈ 0.005 /K (gives ~1.6× at 150 °C) |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns per edge |
| Body-diode forward voltage | V_F | 0.8 V |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation with synchronous PWM

At any instant two phases conduct. Take phase A high / phase B low:
- **Q_AH** conducts during the ON time, duty D.
- **Q_AL** (complementary sync-rectifier) conducts during (1−D).
- **Q_BL** conducts 100 % of the sector.

So the current always passes through exactly **two R_DS(on)** in series:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2\,\big[D\,R + (1-D)\,R + R\big] = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

This is independent of D, which is why sync rectification matters at high current.

Each of the 6 FETs carries current for 1/3 of the electrical cycle, so the per-FET average loss is:

$$
P_{cond,FET} = \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}\cdot\frac{6}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{2}\;=\;\frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}\quad\text{(averaged)}
$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]
$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond,ch at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.60 mΩ | 20.5 W |
| 100 °C | 2.20 mΩ | 28.2 W |
| 125 °C | 2.40 mΩ | **30.7 W** |
| 150 °C | 2.60 mΩ | 33.3 W |

**PCB copper adds loss.** Each phase path is roughly 15 mm of 2 oz copper, 3 mm wide (≈0.25 mΩ at 25 °C, 0.35 mΩ hot). Two path segments add:

$$
P_{Cu} \approx 80^2 \times 2 \times 0.35\,\text{m}\Omega \approx 4.5\ \text{W per channel}
$$

### 1.3 Closed-form junction temperature (electro-thermal feedback)

With P₀ = 2I²R₂₅ and steady-state Rθ (junction→ambient, per channel):

$$
T_j = T_a + R_\theta P_0\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
\;\Rightarrow\;
T_j = \frac{T_a + R_\theta P_0(1-25\alpha)}{1-\alpha R_\theta P_0}
$$

**Thermal-runaway criterion:** α·Rθ·P₀ < 1. In practice keep it below 0.5.

For P₀ = 20.5 W and α = 0.005, runaway occurs at **Rθ ≥ 9.8 K/W**. A nano board with no heatsink has Rθ ≈ 8–15 K/W per channel. **Steady-state 80 A therefore sits at or beyond runaway**, which confirms burst-only operation.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd high-side FET hard-switches. The low-side commutates softly through its body diode during dead time.

### 2.1 V·I overlap (hard switching)

Transition time is set by Q_gd and the driver current. For a 2 A driver into the Miller plateau, t ≈ Q_gd/I_g ≈ 5 ns ideal. Layout inductance and gate resistance raise this to t_r ≈ t_f ≈ 25 ns in practice.

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = \tfrac12(25.2)(80)(50\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz}) = \mathbf{2.42\ W}
$$

### 2.2 Secondary terms

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Reverse recovery | V·Q_rr·f | 25.2 × 40 nC × 48 k = 0.05 W |
| C_oss discharge | ½·Q_oss·V·f (Q_oss ≈ 40 nC) | 0.02 W |
| Dead-time diode | V_F·I·(2t_dt)·f | 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k = **0.31 W** |
| Gate drive (dissipated in driver) | Q_g·V_GS·f × 2 FETs | 50 nC × 10 V × 48 k × 2 = 0.05 W |
