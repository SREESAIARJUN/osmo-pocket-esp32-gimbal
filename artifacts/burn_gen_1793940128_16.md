# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC Stack: Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, and Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Allocation

I don't have a prior record of "Job #26," so this document is built on the explicit assumptions in §0. Every equation is parametric, so you can substitute your actual BOM values. Numbers marked **[A]** are assumptions that must be checked against the datasheets of your chosen parts.

**Key architectural point:** On a 4-in-1 stack, the STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. The 48 kHz PWM and the per-phase commutation run on **four separate ESC MCUs** (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). Bidirectional DShot600 is the link between them. The power-loss model (§1–3) applies to the ESC board. The DMA model (§4) applies to the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]$$

At an assumed T_j = 110 °C (to be iterated in §3.4):

$$R_{DS}(110) = 0.70 \times (1 + 0.0055 \times 85) = 0.70 \times 1.4675 = \mathbf{1.027\ m\Omega}$$

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation with synchronous rectification

At any instant, two phases conduct: one leg is "high" and one is "low." The PWM'd high leg alternates between its HS FET (time D) and its complementary LS FET (time 1−D, synchronous rectification). The current therefore always sees exactly one upper-path R_DS plus one lower-path R_DS:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2\,(2R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{Cu})$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 6400 \times (2 \times 1.027 + 0.5)\times10^{-3} = 6400 \times 2.554\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{16.35\ W}$$

Of this, 13.15 W is dissipated in the silicon and 3.20 W in the copper.
