# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion

I don't have the specification for Job #41, so this design rests on the assumptions in §0. Every formula is parameterised. To adapt the design, change the constants and re-run the tuning equations in §2–§4.
