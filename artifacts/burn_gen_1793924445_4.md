## 0. Baseline Assumptions

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|

| Position sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per axis (AS5048A / MT6701 class) |

| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 24 MHz, ODR 8 kHz, ±2000 dps / ±16 g |
| Gimbal kinematics | Yaw (base, $z$) → Roll ($x$) → Pitch ($y$, camera) |

**Loop rates:**

| Loop | Rate | Period |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | $T_c = 50\,\mu s$ |
| Velocity + anti-cogging | 4 kHz | $T_v = 250\,\mu s$ |
| IMU fusion, attitude, angle loop | 1 kHz | $T_a = 1\,ms$ |

IMU data is decimated from 8 kHz after filtering.

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):
$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}$:
$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.
