## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Architectural Corrections

I don't have access to prior Job #53 documents, so this package states its assumptions explicitly. Replace the bracketed values with your selected parts and re-run the equations. The model is built so that only the inputs change.

**Architectural corrections:**

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 and emits bidirectional DShot600. Each of the four ESC channels needs its own commutation MCU (or one quad-capable ESC MCU) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The 48 kHz PWM lives on the ESC MCU, not the H743. The design therefore splits into:
   - **FC domain:** H743, timers, DMA, DShot.
   - **ESC domain:** 4× ESC MCU, gate drivers, MOSFETs, 48 kHz PWM.
2. **Define what "80 A burst" means.** Hobby ratings usually mean battery current per ESC. The MOSFETs carry phase current, and I_batt ≈ D·I_phase. This package uses the conservative case, **80 A phase current at D ≈ 0.9–1.0**, where the two are nearly equal.
3. **Feasibility flag.** As Section 3 shows, 4 × 80 A on a nano board (≤30.5 mm) is only a seconds-scale burst rating. It is not thermally sustainable. Plan the design and the marketing claim accordingly.
