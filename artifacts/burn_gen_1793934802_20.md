## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.74 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in synchronous six-step

In each 60° sector, phase X is PWM'd and phase Y is held low-side ON.

- The high-side switch of X conducts for duty D.
- The complementary low-side of X conducts for (1−D).
- The low-side switch of Y conducts for 100% of the sector.

So, ignoring dead time, two R_DS(on) are always in series with the phase current:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Per FET, averaged over an electrical revolution (each switch is active for 120° = 1/3 of the time):

- High-side: $P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} D\, I^2 R$
- Low-side: $P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)\, I^2 R + \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R$

The low-side FETs therefore carry more loss than the high-side FETs. At full throttle (D → 1) the loss shifts to the high side. Place thermal vias under all six FETs, but prioritize the low-side FETs.
