## 0. Architectural Clarifications

1. **The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** ArduPilot sends bidirectional DShot600 *to* ESC MCUs. These are typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or BLHeli_32/Bluejay targets. The ESC MCUs perform 6-step commutation and BEMF sensing.
   - **Topology:** H743 (FC) → 4× DShot lines → 4× ESC MCU → 4× FD6288-class 3-phase drivers → 24 MOSFETs.
   - It can be a single PCB or a stack. This document treats it as one design with two domains.
2. **"Nano" plus "80A burst" is a thermal contradiction.** Section 3 proves it numerically. On a 20×20 or 25×25 mm board, 80A is a sub-2-second burst rating. Realistic continuous current is about 25–30A per channel. Specify the product that way, or move to a 30.5 mm board with external heatsinking.
