# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #49): Architecture, Loss Models, DShot Allocation and Netlists

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**ArduPilot does not commutate motors.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and *commands* the ESCs over bidirectional DShot600. Commutation at 48 kHz runs on four dedicated ESC MCUs. The architecture assumed throughout is:

```
VBAT (6S) ──┬── STM32H743 FC (ArduPilot) ── 4× DShot600 BIDIR ──┐
            │                                                    │
            └── 4-in-1 ESC: 4× AT32F421 (AM32 firmware, 48 kHz PWM)
                            4× FD6288Q 3-phase gate driver
                            24× N-MOSFET (6 per motor)
```

These parameters were not supplied, so they are stated assumptions. Swap in your BOM values; every equation below is parametric.

| Symbol | Meaning | Assumed value |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 6S nominal, used for switching loss | 25.2 V |
| I_ph | Burst phase current per ESC | 80 A |
| R_ds(on),25 | 40 V MOSFET, 3.3×3.3 mm PDFN | 1.0 mΩ @ V_gs = 10 V |
| k_T | R_ds(on) multiplier at T_j = 125 °C | 1.6 |
| t_r, t_f | Switching transition times (FD6288Q, ~1 A drive) | 15 ns each |
| Q_g / Q_rr / C_oss | MOSFET parameters | 50 nC / 50 nC / 1.5 nF |
| t_dead | Dead time (each edge) | 100 ns |
| V_f | Body diode forward voltage | 0.8 V |
| f_sw | PWM frequency | 48 kHz |
| Drive mode | Six-step trapezoidal, complementary PWM (synchronous rectification) | — |

> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag:** An "80 A per channel" rating on a nano (20×20 / 25.5×25.5 mm) board is a **seconds-scale burst rating**, not a continuous one. Section 2 proves this quantitatively. Treat the figure as a transient envelope.

## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Conduction Loss

In six-step drive with synchronous rectification, exactly two MOSFETs (one high-side, one low-side, in different legs) carry the phase current at every instant, independent of duty cycle D.

**Channel conduction loss:**

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds(on)}(T_j) \approx R_{25}\,\big[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\big],\ \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

**Hot case (T_j = 125 °C, R = 1.6 mΩ):**

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.6\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{20.5\ W}$$

**Cold start (25 °C):** 12.8 W.

Because R_ds(on) rises with temperature, conduction loss is a **positive feedback loop**. The self-consistent junction temperature is the solution of:

$$T_j = T_{pcb} + R_{\theta,j\text{-}pcb}\cdot \tfrac{1}{6}\cdot 2 I^2 R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

**Per-FET distribution** over one electrical cycle (each FET is active for a 120° window, i.e. 1/3 of the period):

| Device | Conduction share | Loss at 80 A, D = 0.9, hot |
|---|---|---|
| High-side (switching leg) | D/3 | 3.07 W avg |
| Low-side (sync-rect, same leg) | (1−D)/3 | 0.34 W |
| Low-side (static leg) | 1/3 | 3.41 W |
| **Worst single FET (averaged over its 2 roles)** | — | **≈ 3.4–3.8 W** |

### 1.2 Switching Loss at 48 kHz

Only one leg switches at a time; its high-side FET takes the hard-switching loss.

**V–I overlap:**

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(30\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz}) = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

Transition time from gate charge: t ≈ (Q_gs2 + Q_gd)/I_g. With 12 nC at 0.8 A, t ≈ 15 ns. This is very sensitive to gate resistor choice (Section 4).

**Reverse recovery:**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.060\ \text{W}$$

**Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss} V^2 f_{sw} = 0.5\cdot1.5\text{n}\cdot635\cdot48\text{k} = 0.023\ \text{W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction:**

$$P_{dt} = V_f I_{ph}\,(2 t_{dead}) f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot200\text{n}\cdot48\text{k} = 0.61\ \text{W}$$

**Gate drive** (dissipated mostly in the driver and R_g, not the FET):

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} = 2\cdot50\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

### 1.3 Total Loss Budget at 80 A Burst

| Component | Per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (hot) | 20.5 W | 82.0 W |
| Switching overlap | 1.45 W | 5.8 W |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 W | 2.4 W |
| Q_rr + C_oss + gate | 0.13 W | 0.5 W |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ, if per-channel) | 1.6 W | 6.4 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 24.3 W** | **≈ 97 W** |

**Key insight:** At 80 A, conduction is about 85 % of total loss. Raising f_sw from 24 to 48 kHz costs only about 2 W per channel. Reducing R_ds(on), for example by paralleling FETs or using a 0.7 mΩ part, is the dominant lever.

**Frequency sensitivity:**

$$\frac{\partial P_{tot}}{\partial f_{sw}} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r+t_f) + 2V_fIt_{dead} + Q_{rr}V + \dots \approx 44\ \mu\text{J per cycle}$$

That is about 0.044 W per kHz per channel.
