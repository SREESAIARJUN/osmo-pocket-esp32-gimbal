## 0. Architecture Clarification

The 48 kHz switching does **not** come from the STM32H743. In a 4-in-1 ESC, each of the four channels has its own ESC MCU (typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM.
