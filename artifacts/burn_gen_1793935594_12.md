# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal, and DShot Design Package (Job #25)

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

Two points need to be settled before the math.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1 stack, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot. Each motor has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071. That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. A single H743 doing 4-motor sensorless FOC or 6-step alongside ArduPilot is not a supportable architecture. This design therefore uses **H743 (FC) + 4× AT32F421 (AM32, 48 kHz PWM)**.
2. **"Nano" and "80 A" conflict thermally.** The thermal model in §3 shows that 80 A on all four channels is a burst of a few seconds, not a rating. This holds even on a 30.5 × 30.5 mm board, and a 20 × 20 board roughly halves the burst time. The analysis uses 30.5 mm and gives 20 mm scaling.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-step, synchronous complementary PWM)

### 1.1 Current path

In trapezoidal commutation, each 60° electrical sector has three phases with distinct roles:

- **High phase:** PWM at duty D. Current flows through the high-side FET (HS) for D·T_sw and through the low-side FET (LS, synchronous rectification) for (1–D)·T_sw.
- **Low phase:** the LS FET is on continuously.
- **Floating phase:** used for BEMF sensing.

So at every instant **exactly two FETs carry I** (ignoring dead time):

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] \approx k_T R_{25}$$

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2(80)^2(1.5\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

### 1.2 Per-FET distribution

Each phase spends 120° as the high phase and 120° as the low phase out of 360°. Per FET:

$$P_{HS} = \frac{D}{3}\,I^2 R_{DS}, \qquad P_{LS} = \left[\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1-D}{3}\right] I^2 R_{DS}$$

With I²R = 9.6 W:

| D | P_HS | P_LS |
|---|---|---|
| 0.95 (burst, near full throttle) | 3.04 W | 3.36 W |
| 0.50 | 1.60 W | 4.80 W |

**The low-side FET is the worst case at partial throttle under high current.** This happens, for example, during a punch-out from mid-throttle or with a stalled or loaded prop.

### 1.3 Copper loss

$$P_{cu} = 2 I^2 R_{cu} = 2(6400)(0.5\times10^{-3}) = 6.4\ \text{W per ESC}$$

This is per-board heat, not junction heat. It is a strong argument for 2 oz copper on at least 4 power layers.

## 2. Switching and Dynamic Losses at 48 kHz

Hard switching occurs only in the PWM'd (high) leg. Each component scales linearly with f_sw.

| Mechanism | Formula | Value @80 A, 25.2 V |
|---|---|---|

| Body-diode conduction in dead time | V_F·I·2t_dt·f_sw | 0.8·80·400 ns·48 k
