# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Loss, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot Allocation Model

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I don't have a design file or BOM for "Job #4", so this model is **fully parameterized**. Every number comes from the assumed parts below. Swap in your datasheet values and the equations still hold.

**Architecture correction:** ArduPilot on the H743 does **not** commutate motors. The H743 is the flight controller. It sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (one per channel, e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM. The loss model applies to the ESC half; the DMA model applies to the H743.

| Parameter | Symbol | Assumed value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S, full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current | I | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET (40 V, 5×6 mm DFN) R_DS(on) @25 °C | R₂₅ | 0.80 mΩ |
| R_DS(on) temperature coefficient | α | 0.0045 /K |
| Rise / fall time (with driver and R_g) | t_r, t_f | 15 ns / 15 ns |
| Reverse recovery charge | Q_rr | 50 nC |

| Body diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R = 0.80 × (1 + 0.0045 × 75) = **1.07 mΩ**. Use **1.2 mΩ** for design margin, which also covers PCB via and solder resistance.

### 1.2 Per-device conduction in 6-step synchronous PWM

Each phase leg is active for 120° (1/3 of the electrical period). Let D be the PWM duty cycle.

| Device | Conduction fraction | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| PWM leg high-side | D/3 | I²R·D/3 |
| PWM leg low-side (sync rectification) | (1−D)/3 | I²R(1−D)/3 |
| Return leg low-side (held ON) | 1/3 | I²R/3 |

Summed over the six devices, there are always **exactly two FETs in the current path**:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j), \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

Ripple adds less than 1% at 48 kHz with typical motor inductance (≥ 10 µH), so it is neglected.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.0012 = \mathbf{15.36\ W\ per\ ESC\ channel}$$

Worst-case single FET: about 2.56 W averaged. It reaches about 5.1 W instantaneous during its 120° window. The thermal mass of the die and package smooths this at any eRPM above roughly 100 Hz.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches hard. Per channel:

**(a) V–I overlap:**
$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.452\ W}$$

**(b) Reverse recovery** (low-side body diode recovering at HS turn-on):
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.060\ W}$$

**(c) Output capacitance:**
$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss,er} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 1.5\text{n} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.023\ W}$$

**(d) Dead-time body diode conduction:**
$$P_{dt} = V_f I \,(2 t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.307\ W}$$

**(e) Gate drive.** Two FETs switch per PWM cycle. This loss is dissipated mostly in R_g and the driver, not in the FET.
$$P_g = 2 Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 60\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.058\ \text{W}$$

**Switching total:** P_sw,ch ≈ **1.84 W** in the FETs, plus 0.06 W in the driver.

### 2.1 Channel and board totals at 80 A burst

| Item | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 15.36 W | 61.4 W |
| Switching | 1.84 W | 7.4 W |
| Gate drive | 0.06 W | 0.23 W |
| PCB copper (estimate: 0.15 mΩ effective path) | 0.96 W | 3.8 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 18.2 W** | **≈ 73 W** |

**Efficiency check per channel:** P_out ≈ 25.2 V × 80 A × D. At D = 0.9, P_out ≈ 1.8 kW, so efficiency is about 99%. The efficiency is excellent; the problem is heat flux into a ~30×30 mm board.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Network (lumped Cauer, per channel to shared board node)

```
P_FET ──[R_jc 0.8 K/W]──[R_solder+spread 2.5 K/W]──┐
                                                    ├── T_board ──[R_ba]── T_amb
(×6 FETs per channel, ×4 channels)       C_board ───┘
```

### 3.2 Board node

| Parameter | Estimate | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| C_board | ≈ 4 J/K | ~5 g FR4 + 2 oz Cu + 24 FETs + capacitor |
| R_ba (in prop wash, ~5 m/s) | ≈ 8 K/W | ~18 cm² two-sided area, h ≈ 35 W/m²K |
| τ = R_ba·C_board | ≈ 32 s | — |

**Steady state at full 80 A × 4:** ΔT = 73 × 8 = 584 K. **80 A continuous on all four channels is physically impossible on a nano board.** The rating can only be a burst rating.
