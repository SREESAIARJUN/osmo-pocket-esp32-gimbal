## 0. Assumptions and Design Review Flags

The derivations below are fully parametric. Where a number is needed I state an assumed value (marked **[A]**). Substitute the actual datasheet values once the BOM is frozen.

**Flags to resolve before layout:**

1. **"80 A burst" on a nano board is a short transient, not a rating.** The thermal model in §3 shows a 25.5 × 25.5 mm board survives roughly **2–3 s at 80 A on one channel**. It survives **under 1 s with all four channels at 80 A**. Continuous capability is **about 20–25 A per channel** unless the board is heatsinked or sits in strong prop-wash. The spec sheet should state the burst duration and the duty cycle.
2. **Architecture.** The H743 runs ArduPilot only. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, here an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The 48 kHz figure is the ESC PWM frequency. It is generated by the ESC MCUs, not by the H743. The H743 generates DShot600 and decodes the bidirectional eRPM telemetry.
3. **Aggregate current.** 4 × 80 A = 320 A is not feasible through the battery pads or connector. The design assumes per-motor bursts with a total bus limit of about **120 A**, enforced by the ArduPilot `BATT_*` limits and the ESC current limits.
