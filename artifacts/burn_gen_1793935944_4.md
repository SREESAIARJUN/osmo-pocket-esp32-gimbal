## 0. Assumptions and Architectural Clarifications

I don't have Job #38's BOM, stack-up, or enclosure data. This document uses **explicitly stated representative parameters**. Every equation is parameterized, so you can substitute your datasheet values. All values marked ⚙ must be verified against the selected parts.

### 0.1 Architecture

In a production ArduPilot 4-in-1 design, the **STM32H743 does not commutate the motors**. Each ESC channel runs its own MCU (AM32 on AT32F421/STM32G071, or BLHeli_32). The H743 sends bi-directional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. Therefore:

| Function | Owner |
|---|---|
| 48 kHz PWM, dead-time, commutation, current limit | ESC MCU firmware (AM32: `PWM frequency`, `current limit`) |
| DShot600 generation, eRPM capture, harmonic notch | STM32H743 / ArduPilot |
| Thermal protection | Both (ESC temp telemetry → ArduPilot logging/failsafe) |
