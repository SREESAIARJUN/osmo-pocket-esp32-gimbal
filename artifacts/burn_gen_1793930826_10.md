## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors directly. It runs ArduPilot, and the ESC runs as a separate subsystem:

```
                 ┌──────────────────────── FC (STM32H743, ArduPilot) ───────────────┐
 6S LiPo ──┬──►  │ TIM3_CH3/CH4, TIM4_CH1/CH2 ──► Bidir DShot600 (4 lines)          │
           │     └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
           │                                   │ ▲ (eRPM telemetry, GCR 750 kbit/s)
           ▼                                   ▼ │
   ┌── 4-in-1 ESC: 4 × ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 firmware) ──────────────┐
   │   each: 3-phase gate driver ─► 6 N-MOSFETs (3 half-bridges) ─► motor A/B/C     │
   │   PWM carrier 48 kHz, synchronous rectification (complementary + dead time)    │
   └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The 48 kHz switching and the 80 A conduction happen in the ESC power stage. The DShot/DMA work happens on the H743.
