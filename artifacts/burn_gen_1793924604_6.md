> **Architecture flag:** "Nano" plus 80 A per channel is physically aggressive. A 20×20 mm board cannot carry this. At 30.5×30.5 mm, 80 A is achievable only as a time-limited burst. Section 3 quantifies the limit. The deliverable is written for 30.5×30.5 mm.

**Partitioning.**
- The **STM32H743** runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600.
- Four ESC MCUs run AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware and generate the 48 kHz PWM. AT32F421 is the reference; the STM32G071 is an alternative.
- The H7 does **not** drive gates directly. Putting the H7 on the gates would contradict the requirement to use DShot.
