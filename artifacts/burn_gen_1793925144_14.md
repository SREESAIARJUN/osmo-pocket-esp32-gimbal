# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #37): System Architecture and Mathematical Model

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architectural correction.** The STM32H743 does not drive the power stage. It runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600. Each ESC channel is driven by its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, and that firmware generates the 48 kHz PWM. The model is therefore split into two domains:

- **Domain A:** H743 timer, DMA and DShot (Section 3)
- **Domain B:** ESC power stage, losses and thermal (Sections 1–2)

**Engineering warning.** 80 A per motor on a 20×20 or 30×30 nano board is a burst rating only, on the order of 1–2 seconds. It cannot be a continuous rating. The math below shows why.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.2 × 1.45 ≈ **1.74 mΩ**. This analysis uses 1.8 mΩ, which includes margin.

### 1.2 Six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification

In every 60° sector, current flows through two switch positions in series:

- the PWM'd high-side, or its complementary low-side during the off-time;
- the static low-side.

With complementary PWM, the PWM leg conducts I for a fraction D through the high-side and 1−D through the low-side. The I² R product is therefore independent of D:

$$P_{cond,bridge}=2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,bridge}=2(80)^2(1.8\text{ m}\Omega)=\mathbf{23.0\ W\ per\ motor}$$

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period on average, so the per-FET loss is:

$$P_{cond,FET}=\frac{P_{cond,bridge}}{6}\approx\mathbf{3.84\ W}$$

### 1.3 Copper and connection loss

$$P_{Cu}=I_{ph}^2 R_{path}=6400\times0.5\text{ m}\Omega=\mathbf{3.2\ W\ per\ motor}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

In six-step drive only one leg is PWM'd at a time. That leg is the hard-switched one.

### 2.1 V–I overlap loss

$$P_{VI}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw}$$

$$P_{VI}=0.5(25.2)(80)(30\text{ ns})(48\text{ kHz})=\mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr}=Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw}=40\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k}=\mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss}\approx\tfrac12 Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw}=\mathbf{0.030\ W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt}=V_{SD}\,I_{ph}\,(2t_{dead})\,f_{sw}=0.8(80)(100\text{n})(48\text{k})=\mathbf{0.31\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

This loss is dissipated in the driver and gate resistor, not in the FET:

$$P_g=2\,Q_gV_{gs}f_{sw}=2(40\text{n})(10)(48\text{k})=\mathbf{0.038\ W}$$

### 2.6 Switching total

$$P_{sw}\approx \mathbf{1.84\ W\ per\ motor}$$

This loss rotates among the three high-sides, so each high-side carries about 0.61 W on average.

**Sensitivity.** Switching loss scales linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz it falls to about 0.9 W. At 96 kHz it rises to about 3.7 W.

## 3. Loss Budget Summary at 80 A Burst

| Item | Per motor | 4 motors |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 23.0 W | 92.2 W |
| Switching | 1.84 W | 7.4 W |
| Copper | 3.2 W | 12.8 W |
| **Total** | **28.0 W** | **≈112 W** |
| Hottest FET (high-side) | 3.84 + 0.61 ≈ **4.45 W** | |

**Check of the battery side.** DC current is approximately D × I_ph. With D ≈ 0.9, that is 72 A per motor, or about 288 A total.

- An XT60 connector (~60 A continuous) cannot support this. An XT90 or AS150 class connector is required.
- Bulk capacitor ripple current is roughly

$$I_{C,rms}\approx I_{ph}\sqrt{D(1-D)}$$

  This gives about 24 A rms per motor. Use low-ESR polymer capacitors plus an external 1000 µF+ low-ESR electrolytic.

## 4. Thermal Model

### 4.1 Network (Cauer approximation)

```
P_FET ─[R_jc 1.5K/W]─ T_case ─[R_spread ~6K/W, C_local ~0.15J/K]─ T_board ─[R_ba]─ T_amb
                                                               [C_board]
```

### 4.2 Steady state: shows that continuous 80 A is impossible

Assume a board-to-ambient resistance R_ba of about 3 K/W. This value is optimistic and assumes strong prop-wash airflow over a 30×30 board.

$$\Delta T_{board}=112\text{ W}\times3\text{ K/W}=336\text{ K}$$

The board would far exceed the 130 °C limit of the FR4 Tg rating.

### 4.3 Adiabatic burst

Estimate the board's thermal capacity:

- ~5 g FR4 + copper at c ≈ 0.9 J/(g·K), plus ~16 MOSFETs and capacitors
- C_board ≈ 5 J/K

$$\frac{dT_{board}}{dt}=\frac{P_{tot}}{C_{board}}=\frac{112}{5}\approx22\text{ K/s}$$
