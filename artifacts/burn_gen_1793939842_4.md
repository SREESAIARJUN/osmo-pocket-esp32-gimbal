## 0. Architecture Correction (Read First)

The phrase "STM32H743 4-in-1 ESC" can mean two different designs. Only one of them works with ArduPilot.

| Option | Description | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A | The H743 runs ArduPilot **and** commutates 4 motors (12 half-bridges, BEMF sensing) | **Not viable.** ArduPilot has no ESC commutation firmware. Running 4× 48 kHz FOC/6-step loops alongside the EKF also breaks timing. |
| B | The H743 runs ArduPilot as the FC. **4× dedicated ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071, running AM32) receive bi-directional DShot600. | **Baseline for this document.** |

Everything below assumes **Option B**.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (6-step, synchronous rectification)

### 2.1 Which FETs conduct

During any 60° commutation sector, two phases carry current I:

- **PWM phase:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T. The complementary low-side FET (synchronous rectifier) conducts for (1−D)·T.
- **Return phase:** the low-side FET conducts for 100% of the sector.

### 2.2 Per-ESC conduction loss

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{hot}\left[D + (1-D)\right] + I^2 R_{hot} = 2\,I^2 R_{hot}
$$

The result is **independent of duty cycle** because synchronous rectification is used.

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.45\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{18.6\ W\ per\ ESC}
$$

### 2.3 Copper / interconnect loss

Assume an effective path resistance of R_Cu ≈ 0.25 mΩ per ESC (bus planes, phase pads, vias):

$$
P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.25\,\text{m}\Omega = 1.6\ W
$$

### 2.4 Self-consistent junction temperature

R_DS(on) depends on T_j, and T_j depends on loss, so the loss must be solved iteratively:

$$
R(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0053\ /\text{K}
$$

The model converges in 2–3 iterations. Use T_j = 110 °C as the design point.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

All terms are per ESC. Only the PWM leg hard-switches: one hard-switched FET pair per sector.

**(a) V–I overlap loss**

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}
$$

**(b) Reverse recovery loss**

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.06\ W
$$

**(c) Output capacitance loss**

$$
P_{oss} \approx \tfrac{1}{2} Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.024\ W
$$

**(d) Dead-time body-diode conduction**

$$
P_{dt} = V_f \cdot I \cdot 2 t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}
$$

**(e) Gate drive loss** (dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET)

$$
P_g = 2\, Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 50\text{nC} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\ W
$$

| Term | Per ESC | ×4 ESCs |

| Conduction | 18.6 W | 74.4 W |
| Copper | 1.6 W | 6.4 W |
