## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle is computed from the encoder:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Motor model in the dq frame

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L \dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

Torque, for a surface PMSM:

$$\tau = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q$$

### 1.3 Current PI with pole-zero cancellation and decoupling

The plant is G_i(s) = 1/(Ls + R). Choosing

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c$$

cancels the electrical pole at s = −R/L. The closed loop then becomes ω_c/(s + ω_c).

Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward are added to the PI output:

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

The set-points are i_d* = 0 and i_q* = i_q,ctrl + i_q,cog.

**Voltage limiting.** Limit to the inscribed circle of the SVPWM hexagon, |v_dq| ≤ V_bus/√3, with d-axis priority. Anti-windup uses back-calculation with gain K_aw = 1/K_p,i.

### 1.4 Inverse Park and SVPWM

Inverse Park:

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

Phase voltages:

$$v_a=v_\alpha,\quad v_b=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta$$

SVPWM is implemented by min-max zero-sequence injection, which is equivalent to classic sector SVPWM:

$$v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max(v_{abc})+\min(v_{abc})),\qquad d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_\text{bus}}$$

**Delay compensation.** The duty cycle is applied one PWM period after sampling. Advance the angle used for inverse Park:

$$\theta_e' = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_{pwm}$$
