**Architecture:** The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot600 to **four on-board ESC MCUs** (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32). Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs. The H743 does **not** commutate motors directly; ArduPilot has no FOC/6-step motor driver.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM
During each 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct:
- **Leg A (PWM'd):** the high side conducts for D·T_sw. The low side of the same leg conducts (sync rect) for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Leg B (return):** the low side conducts 100% of the sector.

So at any instant, exactly **two MOSFETs carry I_ph**, independent of D (ignoring dead time):

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

### 1.2 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond / motor | P_cond × 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 51.2 W |
| 110 °C | 1.51 mΩ | **19.3 W** | **77.3 W** |

**Per-FET average:** each of the 6 FETs carries current for about 1/3 of the electrical cycle, so P_FET,avg ≈ 19.3 / 6 ≈ **3.2 W**. The instantaneous FET in conduction dissipates 80² × 1.51 mΩ = 9.7 W.

### 1.3 Copper (PCB) conduction loss
Sheet resistance of 2 oz (70 µm) copper:

$$R_\square = \frac{\rho_{Cu}}{t} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}}{70\times10^{-6}} = 0.245\ \text{m}\Omega/\square \ (25\,°C),\quad \approx 0.33\ \text{m}\Omega/\square\ (110\,°C)$$

Example: a phase path 15 mm long × 10 mm wide is 1.5 squares. Paralleled across 2 layers, R ≈ 0.25 mΩ, so P ≈ 80² × 0.25 mΩ × 2 conducting paths ≈ **3.2 W/motor**.
