# STM32H743 AIO FC + 4-in-1 ESC: 80 A Burst Electrothermal Model, 48 kHz Switching, Bidirectional DShot600

## 0. Assumptions and Architecture Corrections

Before the math, three things need flagging. If they are wrong, the numbers below are wrong.

1. **The H743 should not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot does not run 6-step or FOC commutation. A practical architecture has the H743 run ArduPilot and emit bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32). Those ESC MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. I model it that way.
2. **"Nano" and "4 × 80 A" conflict thermally.** On a 20×20 or 30×30 mm board, 80 A per channel is only survivable as a short burst lasting seconds. Section 3 quantifies this.
3. **"Job #37" parameters were not provided.** All component values are stated assumptions. Replace them with your BOM datasheet values. All equations are parametric.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Current path.** With trapezoidal 6-step commutation and synchronous (complementary) PWM, current always flows through exactly two MOSFETs:
- During the on-time D: the high-side FET of the PWM leg, plus the low-side FET of the return leg.
- During the off-time (1−D): the low-side FET of the PWM leg (synchronous rectification), plus the same return low-side FET.

The result is independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2\,\big[2R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{cu}\big]$$

**Temperature dependence of R_DS(on):**

$$R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS}=1.2\times(1+0.0045\times85)=1.66\ \text{m}\Omega$$

**Result per channel:**

$$P_{cond}=6400\times(3.32+0.5)\times10^{-3}=\mathbf{24.4\ W}$$

Of this, 21.2 W is in the FETs and 3.2 W is in the copper.

**Electrothermal stability (runaway criterion).** Losses rise with temperature, and temperature rises with losses. The loop is stable only if:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}R_{\theta JA}<1,\qquad \frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}=2I^2R_{25}\alpha=0.069\ \text{W/K}$$

This requires R_θJA,eff < 14.5 K/W. That is satisfied, so there is no runaway. However, the steady-state temperature is the real problem (Section 3).

**Note on bus current.** The battery current per channel is I_bus ≈ D · I_ph, not I_ph. Make sure your "80 A" specification refers to phase current. A rating of 80 A on the bus would be far worse.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. Its two FETs share the switching events.

| Loss term | Equation | Value per channel |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V_bus·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw | 0.5·25.2·80·40n·48k = **1.94 W** |
| Dead-time body diode conduction | V_f·I·(2·t_dt)·f_sw | 0.8·80·200n·48k = **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V_bus·f_sw | 50n·25.2·48k = **0.06 W** |
| Output capacitance (C_oss) | ½·Q_oss·V_bus·f_sw | **0.02 W** |
| Gate charge (dissipated in the driver) | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f_sw | 2·60n·10·48k = **0.06 W** |
| **Total** | | **≈ 2.7 W** |

**Rise-time estimate.** The overlap time comes from the gate drive current:

$$t_{r,f}\approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_g}\approx 15\text{–}20\ \text{ns}$$

Doubling the gate resistance roughly doubles the dominant switching term. 48 kHz is a reasonable trade-off. At 96 kHz, switching losses would be about 5.4 W per channel.

**Total burst loss:**
- Per channel: about **27 W**.
- Board total at 4 × 80 A simultaneously: about **108 W**. Add a few watts for the ~40 A RMS bulk capacitor ripple current (Section 5).

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Steady state: not achievable at 80 A

For a 30×30 mm board in propwash, take R_θ(board→air) ≈ 2–4 K/W for the whole board.

ΔT = 108 W × 3 K/W ≈ 324 K. **Continuous 80 A is physically impossible on this board.**

The maximum continuous phase current per channel follows from:

$$4\,[I^2 R_{eq}+k_{sw}I]\,R_{\theta}=T_{j,max}-T_a-\Delta T_{JC}$$

Here k_sw ≈ 0.034 W/A, R_eq ≈ 3.8 mΩ, and R_θ = 3 K/W. Solving gives **I_cont ≈ 25–30 A per channel**, which is consistent with real "30–35 A" 20×20 ESCs.

### 3.2 Burst capability: Foster RC network

The thermal path is modelled as three series stages, each with its own thermal resistance and heat capacity:

```
Pd ─┬─[R1=1.0 K/W | C1=0.05 J/K]─┬─[R2=1.5 K/W | C2=1.5 J/K]─┬─[R3=3 K/W | C3=8 J/K]─ Ta
  junction→case          case→local Cu/FR4           board→air (shared, 4 ch)
```

The temperature response to a power step is:

$$T_j(t)=T_0+\sum_{i=1}^{3}P_iR_i\left(1-e^{-t/R_iC_i}\right)$$

The time constants are:
- τ₁ = 50 ms
- τ₂ = 2.25 s
- τ₃ = 24 s

**Junction-to-case rise.** Loss rotates through all 6 FETs over each electrical cycle, so each FET averages 27/6 ≈ 4.5 W. That gives ΔT_JC ≈ 4.5 K.
