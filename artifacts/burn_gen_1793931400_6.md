## 0. Architecture Baseline and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture correction

The STM32H743 cannot directly commutate 4 motors at 48 kHz alongside the flight stack under ArduPilot. ArduPilot provides no ESC firmware for the H7.

The workable architecture is:

| Block | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100) or VIT6 (LQFP100) at 480 MHz | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6 | AM32 or Bluejay-class |
