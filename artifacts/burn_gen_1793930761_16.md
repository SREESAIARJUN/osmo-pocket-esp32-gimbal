# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Model, Thermal Budget, and Bidirectional DShot Allocation

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

### Architecture clarification

ArduPilot runs on the **STM32H743 flight controller**. It does not commutate motors. In a 4-in-1 stack, each motor channel is driven by its own ESC MCU (typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). That ESC MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 sends DShot600 commands and receives bidirectional eRPM telemetry. The model below follows that split.
