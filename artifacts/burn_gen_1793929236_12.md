## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

**Topology.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate motors. Four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421K8U7, running AM32) handle commutation and receive bi-directional DShot600 from the H743 over on-board traces.

Using the H743 for both flight control and 4×BLDC commutation is not viable under ArduPilot. It has no 6-step BLDC motor driver.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

At the design point T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{ds}=1.5\,\text{mΩ}\times(1+0.005\times85)=2.14\ \text{mΩ}$$

### 1.2 Conduction path in 6-step synchronous PWM

During each 60° sector, three devices share the current:

- **High-side of the PWM leg:** conducts for the duty fraction D.
- **Low-side of the PWM leg:** synchronous rectification, conducts for (1 − D).
- **Low-side of the return leg:** conducts 100% of the time.

$$P_{cond}=I_{ph}^2R_{ds}\big[D+(1-D)+1\big]=2\,I_{ph}^2R_{ds}$$

The result is **independent of D**.

$$P_{cond}=2\times80^2\times2.14\times10^{-3}=\mathbf{27.4\ W}\ \text{per ESC}$$

**Parasitic copper.** Add copper loss of I²·R_cu. A typical nano layout has about 0.15 mΩ of copper in the phase path:

$$P_{cu}\approx 80^2\times0.3\,\text{mΩ}\approx 1.9\ \text{W}$$

### 1.3 Per-device instantaneous vs. averaged loss

- **Return-leg low-side:** 80² × 2.14 mΩ = **13.7 W instantaneous**.
- **Averaging over commutation:** each FET carries this role for about 1/3 of the electrical cycle. Electrical frequency exceeds 500 Hz at burst rpm, which is far faster than the junction thermal time constant (around 10 ms). The junction therefore sees roughly the **cycle-averaged** loss:

$$\bar P_{FET}\approx \frac{P_{ESC}}{6}$$

## 2. Switching-Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap

$$I_{g,on}=\frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}+R_{drv,src}}=\frac{12-3}{4.7+1.5+2.5}\approx1.03\ \text{A}$$
