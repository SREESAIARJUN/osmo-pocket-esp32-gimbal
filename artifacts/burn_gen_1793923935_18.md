# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #23): Power Stage Thermal Model, 48 kHz Switching Losses, and Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Architecture

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Design Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning: who generates the 48 kHz PWM

The H743 running ArduPilot does **not** commutate the motors. ArduPilot has no FOC or 6-step power-stage driver, and it would waste a 480 MHz flight-control MCU. The design is therefore partitioned as follows:

| Domain | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot. Emits bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421 or STM32G071 (one per channel) | AM32 or BLHeli_32 firmware. 48 kHz complementary PWM, BEMF commutation, DShot decode, eRPM reply. |
| Gate drive ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) or equivalent | Level shifting and gate current. |
| Power stage ×4 | 6 × N-MOSFET per channel (24 total) | 3 half-bridges per motor. |

The "48 kHz" figure is the ESC PWM frequency, an AM32/BLHeli_32 setting. The "DShot600" figure is the FC→ESC link. These are independent clocks, and the model below treats them separately.

### 0.2 Feasibility flag

80 A per channel is a **burst** rating only. On a 20×20 mm or even 30.5×30.5 mm board, steady-state 80 A × 4 is thermally impossible (see §3.4). Every result below is expressed as **time-to-limit**, not as a continuous rating.
