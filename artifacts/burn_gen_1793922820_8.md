# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Cascade Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

**Note on assumptions:** I don't have the Job #17 specification, so the design below rests on the assumptions in this table. Every assumption is a named constant in the code. Change them to match your hardware.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC, 14 poles (p = 7 pole pairs), 12 slots, R ≈ 10 Ω, L ≈ 2 mH, inline current sensing on 2 phases |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (MT6701/AS5048-class), one per joint |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI + FIFO, gyro/accel at 8 kHz ODR |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with an FPU |
| Gimbal order | Base → Yaw (z) → Roll (x) → Pitch (y) → Camera |
| Loop rates | Current 20 kHz, velocity 4 kHz, angle/fusion 1 kHz |

If your motors have no current sensing (common with high-R gimbal motors), see §1.5 for voltage-mode operation.
