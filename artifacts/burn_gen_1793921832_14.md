## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, motor current always flows through exactly **one high-side path and one low-side path**. During the PWM off-time, the low-side FET of the active phase freewheels, so the current path is still two FETs.

**Instantaneous conduction loss per ESC:**

$$P_{cond} = I_m^2 \cdot 2 R_{ds,hot}$$

$$P_{cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{23.0\ W}$$

Using the typical value (1.0 mΩ × 1.5 = 1.5 mΩ) gives 19.2 W.

**Per-FET loss.** Each FET conducts 1/3 of the electrical cycle:

- Peak while conducting: I²·R = 80² × 1.8 mΩ = **11.5 W**
- Cycle average: 11.5 / 3 = **3.84 W**

The HS/LS split depends on duty D. The high side carries D·I_m² R and the low side (sync rect) carries (1−D)·I_m² R. At burst (D ≈ 0.9), the high-side FETs dominate.

**Parasitic copper loss.** Battery pads plus phase pours plus shunt, assuming R_cu ≈ 0.4 mΩ and a 0.25 mΩ shunt:

$$P_{cu} = I_{bat}^2 (R_{cu}+R_{sh}) = 72^2 \times 0.65\,\text{m}\Omega \approx \mathbf{3.4\ W}$$

Here I_bat = D·I_m = 72 A.
