## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation, two phases conduct at any instant. With synchronous rectification:

- **PWM-on interval:** current flows through the high-side FET of phase A, the motor, then the low-side FET of phase B.
- **PWM-off interval:** current recirculates through the low-side FET of phase A and the low-side FET of phase B.

So **two FETs are always in series with the motor current**, independent of duty cycle D. Excluding dead time:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

Model Rds(on) as an exponential in junction temperature:

$$
R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\, e^{\alpha (T_j - 25)}, \qquad \alpha = \frac{\ln 1.6}{100} = 4.70\times10^{-3}\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

Because Rds(on) depends on Tj and Tj depends on loss, the model is coupled. Solve it as a fixed-point iteration:

$$
T_j^{(k+1)} = T_{pcb} + R_{th,j\text{-}pcb}\, P_{FET}\big(T_j^{(k)}\big)
$$

This converges in 3–4 iterations when $\alpha R_{th} P < 1$. That condition is the thermal-runaway criterion.

### 1.3 Copper path resistance

Estimate the bus and phase copper path with the sheet-resistance formula:

$$
R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho_{Cu}\, \ell}{w\, t\, n_{layers}}, \qquad \rho_{Cu}(T) = 1.72\times10^{-8}\,[1 + 0.00393(T - 20)]
$$

| Path input | Value |
|---|---|
| Effective length ℓ | 15 mm |
| Width w | 6 mm |
| Copper thickness t | 70 µm |
| Parallel layers carrying current | 4 |
| Temperature | 100 °C |

Result:

$$
R_{Cu} \approx \frac{2.26\times10^{-8} \times 0.015}{0.006 \times 70\times10^{-6} \times 4} \approx 0.20\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A

Rds(on) at Tj = 110 °C is 1.0 × e^(0.47×0.85) = 1.49 mΩ.

| Term | Expression | Per channel |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 2 × 80² × 1.49 mΩ | **19.1 W** |
| Copper | 80² × 0.20 mΩ | 1.3 W |
| **Conduction subtotal** | | **20.4 W** |

The heat is not shared equally between the two FETs:

- **Low-side FET:** conducts for the full period. It carries 80² × 1.49 mΩ = **9.5 W**.
- **High-side FET:** conducts for D·T. Its loss is D × 9.5 W.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the high-side FET is hard-switched. The low-side FET turns on into a conducting body diode, so it switches at near-zero voltage (ZVS).

### 2.1 V–I overlap loss

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = \tfrac12 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = 1.94\ \text{W}
$$

A more exact form separates the edges and accounts for the Miller plateau:

$$
t_{sw} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}\, R_{g,tot}}{V_{drv} - V_{plateau}}
$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

The low-side body diode recovers each cycle, and the high-side FET absorbs the loss:

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.06\ \text{W}
$$
