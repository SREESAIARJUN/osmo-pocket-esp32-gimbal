## Scope and Assumptions

No MOSFET, gate driver, or board stack-up has been specified. Every equation below is parametric, and the worked numbers use the reference values in §0.2. **Replace them with your datasheet values before sign-off.** Anything that depends on ArduPilot source details is flagged **[VERIFY]**.

### 0.1 Architecture Decision

On an AIO "nano" board, the STM32H743 is the flight controller only. Commutation runs on four dedicated ESC MCUs, typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 talks to them over bi-directional DShot600.

Running six-step or FOC commutation for four motors directly on the H743 is technically possible. It is not supported by ArduPilot, and it would consume every advanced timer.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current Path

Assume six-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM:

- **PWM-on (D):** High-side of phase X plus low-side of phase Y conduct.
- **PWM-off (1−D):** Low-side of phase X (synchronous rectification) plus low-side of phase Y conduct.

Two channels are therefore always in series, regardless of duty:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature Dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha_R (T_j-25)]$$
