# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC Nano Stack (Job #26)
## Loss Model, Thermal Model, Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Allocation, and Reference Netlists

---

## 0. Feasibility Findings

A "nano" form factor means a 20×20 mm mounting pattern, with a PCB of roughly 25×25 mm. I ran the numbers before committing to a design. The key findings are:

| Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|
| 4 motors × 80 A burst on 6S produces about **81 W** of total dissipation. | An 80 A burst is **thermally limited to about 1–1.5 s** from a 40 °C start. It is not a continuous rating. |
| Phase-leg copper carrying 80 A on a 25 mm board is heavily loaded. | You need 2 oz copper or heavier on at least 4 layers, with paralleled phase pours. |
| The ESC MCUs (AM32/BLHeli_32) must be separate from the H743. | The H743 runs ArduPilot and drives DShot. It does **not** commutate motors. |
| Bidirectional DShot cannot use `TIMx_CHyN` pins. | Input capture only exists on non-complementary channels. This constrains the pinout (§4). |

**Recommendation.** Market the design as **45 A continuous / 80 A ≤1 s burst per motor**. Alternatively, move the ESC to a 30×30 mm board. The rest of this document designs to the 80 A burst requirement and states where the margins become thin.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path

During any 6-step sector, one phase is driven high and one phase is held low. With complementary PWM:

- **Switching leg:** the high-side FET conducts during D·T. The low-side FET carries the freewheel current during (1−D)·T as a synchronous rectifier.
- **Sink leg:** the low-side FET is on for the whole sector.

So at every instant, **exactly two FET channels** carry the current I, regardless of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

The temperature dependence of R_DS(on) is modelled as:

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big]
$$
