# Job #61: 3-Axis Gimbal FOC, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual ICM-42688-P Fusion

**Basis of the numbers.** I don't have the Job #61 specification sheet, so this design uses typical brushless gimbal hardware:

- 12N14P motors (7 pole pairs)
- 14-bit magnetic encoders
- Two ICM-42688-P IMUs on the camera plate, sampled over SPI at 8 kHz ODR
- A Cortex-M4F/M7-class MCU

Every motor- and payload-specific number is a parameter in the code. Replace the defaults with your measured R, L, Kt, and J before tuning.

**Bandwidth separation.** Each loop is at least 4–5× faster than the loop around it:

| Loop | Rate | Closed-loop BW (target) |
|---|---|---|
| Current (d/q) | 20 kHz | 1.5–2 kHz |
| Velocity (gyro-based) | 4 kHz | 80–150 Hz |
| Angle | 1 kHz | 15–30 Hz |

**Feedback sources.**
- The velocity loop uses the **camera-frame gyro** mapped to joint space, not the differentiated encoder. This rejects base disturbances directly.
- The encoder is used for commutation and for indexing the anti-cogging map.

## 2. Field-Oriented Current Control

### 2.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke transform:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 2.2 PMSM model in the dq frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e (L_d i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

Torque (surface PM, so $L_d \approx L_q$):

$$
\tau = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$

### 2.3 PI tuning by pole–zero cancellation

Place the PI zero on the electrical pole $R/L$. The current loop then becomes first order with bandwidth $\omega_c$:

$$
K_p^{i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i^{i} = R\,\omega_c \quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

Keep $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/10$ to allow for the 1.5-sample transport delay.
