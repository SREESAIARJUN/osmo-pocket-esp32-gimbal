## 0. Architecture Corrections and Stated Assumptions

Three issues need settling before the math, because they change the design.

### 0.1 The H743 cannot also be the ESC MCU

ArduPilot runs on the STM32H743 as the flight controller. It does not run motor commutation.

- The 48 kHz PWM and commutation run on four dedicated ESC MCUs, one per channel, running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Typical parts are the AT32F421 or STM32G071.
- The H743 talks to those four MCUs over bidirectional DShot600.
- The stack is therefore an AIO-style FC + 4-in-1 ESC: two PCBs, or one PCB with five MCUs.

### 0.2 "Nano" (20×20 mm) cannot carry 80 A per channel

Section 2.6 proves this quantitatively. The model below assumes:

- a **30.5 × 30.5 mm mounting pattern**, with an approximately 36 × 36 mm ESC board;
- **80 A per channel burst** (≤ 5 s) and roughly **50 A continuous** with prop-wash airflow.

The FC board itself can stay 20×20.

### 0.3 Parameters are representative, not from a specific part

The MOSFET, gate-driver and PCB values below are typical for 40 V, 3.3 × 3.3 mm PQFN parts. Every equation is parametric. Replace the numbers with the datasheet values for the selected part before freezing the design.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{ds} = 1.5 \times 1.6 = 2.4\ \text{m}\Omega$$

The effective resistance of one switch position with N_p FETs in parallel is:

$$R_{pos} = \frac{R_{ds}(T_j)}{N_p} = 1.2\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

Two phases conduct at any instant, so the current always passes through two switch positions in series:

- **The PWM'd leg (say phase A):**
  - the high-side FET conducts for D;
  - the complementary low-side FET conducts for (1 − D), minus dead time.
- **The return leg (phase B):** the low-side FET conducts for 100 % of the sector.

The channel conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{pos} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.2\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{15.36\ W}$$
