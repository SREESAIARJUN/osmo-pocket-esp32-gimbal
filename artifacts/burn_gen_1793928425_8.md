## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** generate the 48 kHz commutation PWM. In every production 4-in-1 design, the split is as follows:

| Function | Device | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Flight control, DShot600 out, bi-directional DShot (BDShot) eRPM in | STM32H743 (ArduPilot) | 4 signal lines |
| 6-step/FOC commutation, 48 kHz PWM, dead-time | 4× ESC MCU (e.g., AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32) | One per motor |
| Gate drive | 4× 3-phase driver (e.g., FD6288Q class) | Bootstrapped high side |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFETs (6 per motor) | Synchronous rectification |

The model therefore has three decoupled domains: **power-stage thermal**, **ESC switching**, and **FC timer/DMA**.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Current path

With 6-step commutation and synchronous rectification, current flows through exactly two FETs at every instant, in both the PWM-on and PWM-off states:

- **On-state:** HS(phase A) → motor → LS(phase B)
- **Off-state:** LS(phase A) (synchronous freewheel) → motor → LS(phase B)

So the path resistance is always 2·R_ds(T_j) + R_cu, independent of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \left[ 2R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{cu} \right],\qquad
R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds25}\left[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right]
$$

### 2.2 Ripple correction

Phase-current ripple adds to the RMS current:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{pk}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12},\qquad
\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}
$$

Example: L_ph = 15 µH, V_bus − V_bemf = 3 V at full load, D ≈ 0.9.

$$
\Delta I \approx 3.75\ \text{A} \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 1.2\ \text{A}^2 \;(\text{negligible vs. } 6400\ \text{A}^2)
$$

### 2.3 Numbers per motor at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_FET (2 devices) | P_Cu | **P_cond** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 12.8 W | 3.2 W | **16.0 W** |
| 125 °C | 1.5 mΩ | 19.2 W | 3.2 W* | **22.4 W** |

\*Copper also rises about 0.39 %/K. At a 100 K rise, P_Cu ≈ 4.4 W.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one half-bridge is hard-switched per commutation sector. Its complementary FET switches with ZVS (zero-voltage switching) after the dead-time.

**3.1 Hard-switching V-I overlap**

$$
P_{sw}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw}=\tfrac12(25.2)(80)(30\text{ ns})(48\text{ k})=\mathbf{1.45\ W}
$$

**3.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction (2 edges per period)**

$$
P_{dt}=V_f I\,(2t_{dt}) f_{sw}=0.8\cdot80\cdot400\text{ ns}\cdot48\text{ k}=\mathbf{1.23\ W}
$$

This is the second-largest switching term. Cutting dead-time to 100 ns halves it, but only if driver propagation-delay matching allows.

**3.3 Reverse recovery**

$$
P_{rr}=Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw}=50\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k}=0.06\ \text{W}
$$

**3.4 Output capacitance**

$$
P_{oss}=\tfrac12 C_{oss}V_{bus}^2 f_{sw}=0.023\ \text{W}
$$
