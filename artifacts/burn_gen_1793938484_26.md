## 1. Conduction Loss Model

In 6-step complementary PWM, current at any instant flows through exactly two conducting devices:
- the fixed leg FET (100 % on during the 60° step), and
- either the PWM-leg high-side FET (duty D) or its synchronous low-side (1 − D).

**Total bridge conduction loss:**

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

**Average per FET.** Each of the 6 FETs carries the "fixed" role for 1/6 of the cycle and the "PWM" role for 1/6. So the per-FET average is

$$P_{cond,FET} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

**Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C (R_ds = 1.5 mΩ):**

| Quantity | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| P_cond,bridge | 2 · 6400 · 1.5 mΩ | **19.2 W** |
| P_cond,FET | 19.2 / 6 | **3.2 W** |

**PCB copper loss** (often forgotten, and large at 80 A):
- Phase path: about 15 mm, 4 mm wide, 2 oz (70 µm) on 2 paralleled layers.
- Sheet resistance ≈ 0.25 mΩ/sq per 2-oz layer, giving R ≈ (15/4)·0.25/2 ≈ 0.47 mΩ.
- Phase-trace loss: P = I²R ≈ 3.0 W at 25 °C, about 3.6 W hot.
- Add the battery-pad and bus-plane share.
- **Budget about 4 W per motor for copper.**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-leg device hard-switches during each step.

**(a) V–I overlap.** Gate current at the Miller plateau (V_pl ≈ 3.5 V, R_g,int ≈ 1.5 Ω):

$$I_{g} \approx \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}+R_{drv}} = \frac{10-3.5}{4.7+1.5+2} \approx 0.8\text{ A}$$

$$t_{r} \approx t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd}+Q_{gs2}}{I_g} = \frac{10\text{ nC}}{0.8} \approx 12.5\text{ ns}$$

Common-source and loop inductance roughly double this in practice. Use t_r + t_f = 40 ns.

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 40\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**(b) Reverse recovery** (body diode of the synchronous FET):

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{nC}\cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.060\ W}$$

**(c) Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} \cdot 2 = 25\text{nC}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.030\ W}$$
