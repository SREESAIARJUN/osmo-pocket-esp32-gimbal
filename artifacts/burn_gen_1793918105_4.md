## 2. FOC Equations

### 2.1 Electrical angle

Let $p$ be the number of pole pairs (7 for 14P) and $\theta_{off}$ the encoder-to-rotor alignment offset:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi$$

### 2.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 2.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 2.4 Plant model in the d/q frame

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L \dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q$$

$$J\dot\omega_m = \tau_e - \tau_{cog}(\theta_m) - B\omega_m - \tau_{fric} - \tau_{dist}$$

### 2.5 Current PI controller (pole-zero cancellation)

Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole $R/L$. With current-loop bandwidth $\omega_c$:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c$$

This gives a closed loop of $\frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$. Add decoupling and back-EMF feedforward terms:

$$v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(0-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

### 2.6 Voltage limiting and SVPWM

$$v_q^{max} = \sqrt{(V_{bus}/\sqrt3)^2 - v_d^2}$$
