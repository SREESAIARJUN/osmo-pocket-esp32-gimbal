## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step synchronous PWM
During any commutation sector, one phase is driven high and one is driven low. With complementary PWM:

- During the ON portion (duty D), current flows through the high-side FET of phase A and the low-side FET of phase B.
- During the OFF portion (1 − D), current freewheels through the low-side FET of phase A (synchronous rectification) and the low-side FET of phase B.

Either way, exactly two channels carry the full phase current I. Dead-time intervals are handled in §2.4. The conduction loss is therefore approximately independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2 \cdot \left[2R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + R_{Cu} + R_{sense}\right]$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]$$

This dependence creates a positive feedback loop: losses raise T_j, which raises resistance, which raises losses. Solve it as a fixed point:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + Z_{th}(t)\cdot P(T_j)$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution (worst-sector FET)
For RMS-equivalent loading over a full electrical cycle:

- Each phase leg conducts for 2/3 of the electrical period (120° conduction).
- Within that window, the high-side FET carries current for fraction D, and the low-side FET of the same leg carries it for (1 − D) on the PWM sector plus 100 % on the sinking sector.

At 80 A burst, the throttle is typically D ≈ 0.9–1.0. The worst case is the **low-side FET of the sinking phase**:

$$I_{rms,LS} = I\sqrt{\tfrac{1}{3}} \;(\text{sink sector}) \oplus I\sqrt{\tfrac{1-D}{3}}\;(\text{freewheel})$$

At D = 0.95:

$$I_{rms,LS} = 80\sqrt{\tfrac{1}{3}+\tfrac{0.05}{3}} = 80 \times 0.592 = 47.4\text{ A}$$

$$I_{rms,HS} = 80\sqrt{\tfrac{0.95}{3}} = 45.0\text{ A}$$

### 1.4 Numerical solution
Start at T_j = 100 °C, which gives R_DS = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 75) = 1.34 mΩ.

| Item | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Low-side FET conduction | 47.4² × 1.34 mΩ | **3.01 W** |
| High-side FET conduction | 45.0² × 1.34 mΩ | **2.71 W** |
| Per motor, 6 FETs (cycle average) | 80² × 2 × 1.34 mΩ | **17.2 W** |
| Copper path (R_Cu ≈ 0.35 mΩ, pads + planes) | 80² × 0.35 mΩ | 2.24 W |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ, if per-motor sensing) | 80² × 0.25 mΩ | 1.60 W |
| **Conduction total per motor** | | **≈ 21.0 W** |

**Check:** the six FET losses sum to I² × 2R for the instantaneous pair, which confirms the model is consistent.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Gate drive current and transition times
The driver sources 1.5 A and sinks 1.8 A (FD6288Q-class), with R_G(ext) = 4.7 Ω.

The Miller plateau sits at V_pl ≈ 4.2 V. The effective drive currents during the transition are:

$$I_{G,on} = \frac{V_{DRV} - V_{pl}}{R_{G,ext}+R_{G,int}+R_{drv,on}} = \frac{10-4.2}{4.7+1.2+3} \approx 0.65\text{ A}$$

$$I_{G,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{G,ext}+R_{G,int}+R_{drv,off}} = \frac{4.2}{4.7+1.2+2} \approx 0.53\text{ A}$$

The resulting voltage/current overlap times are:

$$t_{sw,on} = \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{G,on}} = \frac{14\text{ nC}}{0.65} \approx 21.5\text{ ns}$$

$$t_{sw,off} = \frac{14\text{ nC}}{0.53} \approx 26.4\text{ ns}$$

### 2.2 Hard-switching V-I overlap loss
Only the hard-switched high-side FET of the active leg sees this loss.

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_{on}+t_{off}) f_{sw}$$

$$= 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 47.9\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{2.32\text{ W}}$$

### 2.3 Capacitive and reverse-recovery losses

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2}C_{oss,eq}V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} \times 2\;\text{(HS + LS per leg)}$$

$$= 0.5 \times 1.2\text{ nF (energy-equivalent)} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} \times 2 = 0.037\text{ W}$$

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = 0.048\text{ W}$$

The Q_rr loss is deposited mostly in the opposite (high-side) FET at turn-on.
