## Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and ArduPilot Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation

## 0. Executive Summary and Feasibility Check

The analysis shows that **80 A per motor is achievable only as a short burst (about 2 s) on a nano-format board**. It is not a continuous rating.

| Metric | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Per-motor loss at 80 A burst (Tj ≈ 110 °C) | **≈ 22 W** | §2–§3 |
| Total ESC loss, 4 × 80 A | **≈ 89 W** | §3.6 |
| Adiabatic board heating rate | ≈ 26 K/s | §4.2 |
| Burst duration, 50 °C → 100 °C board | **≈ 2.0 s** | §4.3 |
| Continuous rating per motor (prop-wash cooling) | **≈ 20–30 A** | §4.4 |
| Battery bus current at 4 × 80 A | 320 A | No nano power entry (XT30/XT60) supports this continuously |

**Recommendation:** Market the board as "**25–30 A continuous / 80 A burst (≤ 2 s, per motor, not simultaneous on all four at full duty)**". Enforce this in AM32 firmware with current limiting plus a thermal derate (§6).
