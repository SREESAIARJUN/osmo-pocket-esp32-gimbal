# Job #53: STM32H743 Nano AIO with 80 A Burst 4-in-1 ESC

## Engineering Design Package, Rev A

**Scope:** Loss model, thermal model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer and DMA plan, and representative netlists.

**Caveat:** Device parameters are representative 40 V DFN3.3×3.3 values. Every calculation is parameterized, so substitute the final datasheet values before release.

---

## 0. Executive Summary

1. **Architecture.** The H743 cannot run four sensorless BLDC power stages itself while also running ArduPilot. Partition the design as follows:
   - **FC side:** STM32H743, running ArduPilot.
   - **ESC side:** 4× AT32F421 (AM32) or EFM8BB51 (Bluejay), one per motor.
   - **Gate drive:** 4× FD6288Q gate drivers.
   - **Power stage:** 24× N-MOSFETs.
   - **Link:** FC to ESCs via bidirectional DShot600 on TIM1 CH1–CH4.
