## 1. Electrical Operating Point

In 6-step commutation, two phases conduct at any time. With complementary PWM on the high-side (HS) of the "active" phase:

- **Active phase HS** conducts for fraction D of each period.
- **Active phase LS** conducts for fraction (1−D) (synchronous rectification), minus dead time.
- **Return phase LS** conducts for 100% of the step.

Battery current relates to phase current through the duty cycle:

$$I_{batt} \approx D \cdot I_{ph}$$

At an 80 A phase current and D ≈ 0.9 (full-throttle punch-out), each channel draws about 72 A from the battery, or about 288 A for four channels. This number drives the bulk capacitor, connector, and battery-lead sizing, so §4.3 treats it separately.
