# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC Stack: Design Model

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Stated Assumptions

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot on the flight controller. It does **not** commutate the bridges directly. Each ESC channel runs AM32 firmware on its own AT32F421 MCU. ArduPilot talks to the ESCs over bidirectional DShot600. Because of this split, the 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, while the H743 DMA budget covers only the DShot links.

None of your device-level parameters were supplied, so the assumptions below drive every number in this document. Substitute your actual datasheet values and re-run the equations.

| Parameter | Symbol | Assumed value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S, full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current per channel | I | 80 A (6-step, ≈ flat-top) |
| MOSFET (40 V, PQFN 5×6 class) | R_DS(on),25 | 1.0 mΩ @ V_GS = 10 V |
| R_DS(on) temperature factor at 125 °C | k_T | 1.6 |
| Gate charge | Q_g / Q_gs2 / Q_gd | 50 / 4 / 10 nC |
| Miller plateau | V_pl | 3.0 V |
| Output charge / reverse-recovery charge | Q_oss / Q_rr | 60 / 40 nC |
| Gate drive voltage, R_g(ext + int) | V_drv, R_g | 10 V, 4.7 + 1.0 Ω |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns per edge |
| Body diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Junction-to-case thermal resistance | R_θJC | 1.2 K/W |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| PWM scheme | — | Complementary (synchronous rectification) |

> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag.** "80 A on a nano-format 4-in-1" is a burst rating that is bounded by thermal mass, not by the silicon. Section 3 shows it holds for roughly 1–3 s, not continuously.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
In 6-step commutation two legs carry the phase current at any instant:
- One leg is in its "PWM" state (HS ↔ LS complementary).
- One leg is in its "low" state (LS held on).

With synchronous rectification, exactly one MOSFET per active leg conducts at any moment. The series path therefore always contains **2 × R_DS(on)**.

### 1.2 Hot R_DS(on)

R_hot = k_T · R_DS(on),25 = 1.6 × 1.0 mΩ = **1.6 mΩ**

### 1.3 Total bridge conduction loss per channel

P_cond = 2 · I² · R_hot = 2 × 6400 × 0.0016 = **20.5 W**

### 1.4 Distribution per switch
Each leg spends 1/3 of the electrical period as the PWM leg, 1/3 as the low leg, and 1/3 floating. At duty D:

- High-side: P_HS = I² R_hot · D/3
- Low-side: P_LS = I² R_hot · [(1 − D)/3 + 1/3]

| Case | High-side | Low-side | Worst switch |
|---|---|---|---|
| D → 1 (full-throttle burst) | 3.41 W | 3.41 W | — |
| D = 0.5 | — | 5.12 W | **Low-side (punch-out at partial throttle)** |

### 1.5 PCB copper loss
Assume 2 oz copper plus pads, with a phase path of about 0.3 mΩ:

P_Cu ≈ I² · 0.3 mΩ ≈ **1.9 W per channel**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Gate current during the Miller interval

| Edge | Gate current | Transition time (Q_sw = Q_gs2 + Q_gd = 14 nC) |
|---|---|---|
| Turn-on | I_g,on = (V_drv − V_pl)/R_g = 7/5.7 = **1.23 A** | t_on = 14 nC / 1.23 A = **11.4 ns** |
| Turn-off | I_g,off = V_pl/R_g = 3/5.7 = **0.53 A** | t_off = 14 nC / 0.53 A = **26.6 ns** |

### 2.2 Hard-switching energy (high-side of the PWM leg)

- E_on = ½ · V · I · t_on = ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 11.4 ns = **11.5 µJ**
- E_off = ½ · V · I · t_off = **26.8 µJ**

P_sw,VI = (E_on + E_off) · f_sw = 38.3 µJ × 48 kHz = **1.84 W**

### 2.3 Parasitic terms

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Reverse recovery (LS body diode) | Q_rr · V · f = 40 nC × 25.2 × 48k | 0.048 W |
| C_oss | ½ · Q_oss · V · f | 0.036 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f · I · 2t_dt · f = 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48k | **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (dissipated in driver/R_g) | Q_g · V_drv · f · 2 | 0.048 W |

### 2.4 Per-channel loss budget at an 80 A burst

| Loss | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction (silicon) | 20.5 |
| Switching (V·I) | 1.84 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 |
| Q_rr + C_oss | 0.08 |
| PCB copper | 1.9 |
| **Total per channel** | **≈ 24.9 W** |
| **4 channels at simultaneous burst** | **≈ 100 W** |

**Hottest device:** the high-side MOSFET at full throttle, at about 3.4 + 1.9 + 0.3 ≈ **5.6 W**.

**Sensitivity:** conduction dominates at about 82%. The highest-leverage changes are:
- Halving R_DS(on) by paralleling or a better die saves about 10 W per channel.
- Raising f_sw only scales the roughly 2.5 W of switching-related loss.
