## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C:

R_ds = 1.5 mΩ × (1 + 0.0045 × 85) = **2.07 mΩ**

### 1.2 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

In each commutation sector, two phases conduct:

- **Modulated leg:** the high-side conducts for duty D, and the low-side conducts (synchronous rectification) for 1 − D.
- **Return leg:** the low-side is on 100 % of the time.

So at any instant, exactly two channels carry the phase current. Conduction loss is therefore nearly independent of duty:

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.3 Ripple correction

The RMS current includes the inductor ripple:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph} f_{sw}}$$

Assume L_ph = 8 µH (line-to-line ≈ 16 µH) and D = 0.5 with V_bemf ≈ 12 V:

- ΔI ≈ 17 A
- ΔI²/12 ≈ 24 A², which adds about 0.4 % to I². This is negligible.

### 1.4 Result

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| P_cond per motor | 80² × 2 × 2.07 mΩ = **26.5 W** |
| P_cond for 4 motors | **106 W** |

### 1.5 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts for one-third of the electrical cycle. Consider full throttle with a 1900 KV motor (14 poles) on 6S:

- Electrical frequency f_e ≈ 5.6 kHz.
- This is much faster than the die thermal time constant (about 1 ms).

So the loss averages out across the six FETs:

- **Average:** P_FET,avg ≈ P_motor / 6.
- **Instantaneous:** up to 13 W per FET.
- **Low-RPM, high-current bursts** (punch-outs from hover, prop strikes): f_e drops and the averaging argument weakens. Treat this as a separate stall case (see Section 4.4).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Per motor, one leg is hard-switched per sector. The loss terms are:

$$
\begin{aligned}
P_{sw,VI} &= \tfrac{1}{2}V_{bus}I(t_r+t_f)f_{sw} \\
P_{rr} &= Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw} \\
P_{oss} &= E_{oss}f_{sw} \\

P_{gate} &= 2\,Q_gV_{drv}f_{sw} \quad\text{(dissipated mostly in } R_g\text{ and the driver)}
