# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #25): Systems Architecture Package

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility Flags

Three decisions shape everything below.

**A. Separate ESC microcontrollers.** ArduPilot outputs DShot to ESCs. It does not commutate motors. Two facts follow:

- The H743 is the flight controller only.
- Each of the four ESC channels has its own MCU running AM32. The example part is the AT32F421K8U7 (an AM32 target).

The H743 also cannot practically run four sensorless BLDC bridges itself. It has only two advanced timers with complementary outputs (TIM1, TIM8), plus HRTIM, and ArduPilot has no support for that topology.

**B. The 80A rating must be a per-channel burst, not a simultaneous one.** Section 3 shows that four channels at 80A simultaneously deliver about 107 W into a roughly 30×30 mm board. With no heatsink, that limits the burst to about 2 s. The rating must be specified as **80A per channel, ≤2 s, single channel or derated**. Otherwise the board needs a bonded heat spreader.
