## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the **flight controller**. It does not commutate motors directly. A real 4-in-1 ESC uses one ESC MCU per channel, typically an AT32F421, STM32G071, or GD32 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. These MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM.

The model therefore has two domains:

| Domain | Device | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Control | STM32H743 (ArduPilot) | Bi-directional DShot600 TX/RX, current/voltage ADC, telemetry |
| Power | 4× ESC MCU + gate driver + 6 FETs per channel | 48 kHz PWM, commutation, eRPM reporting |

## 2. Conduction Losses

### 2.1 Conduction Path

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, exactly two phases are active at any instant:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. The complementary low-side FET conducts for (1 − D).
- **Leg B (return leg):** the low-side FET conducts for 100% of the sector.

The motor current therefore always passes through **two FET channels in series**, regardless of D. Body-diode conduction occurs only during dead time and is handled in §3.

### 2.2 Hot-Junction Resistance

$$R_{ds,hot}=R_{ds,25}\cdot k_T = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.6 = 1.92\,\text{m}\Omega$$

### 2.3 Per-Channel Conduction Loss

$$P_{cond,FET}=I_{ph}^2\cdot 2R_{ds,hot}=6400\times 3.84\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{24.6\ W}$$

$$P_{cond,Cu}=I_{ph}^2\cdot R_{cu,path}=6400\times 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega=\mathbf{6.4\ W}$$

### 2.4 Per-FET Average

Each of the 6 FETs carries current for one-third of an electrical revolution, so the loss averages over the six devices:

$$P_{FET,avg}=\frac{24.6}{6}\approx \mathbf{4.1\ W}$$

The peak instantaneous dissipation in a single conducting FET is higher:

$$P_{FET,peak} = I^2 R_{ds,hot} = 12.3\ \text{W}$$

This peak lasts 120° electrical. At high eRPM it is filtered by Z_th(jc), whose time constant τ is about 1–10 ms. At low eRPM under high load, such as a stall or punch-out from hover, the junction tracks the peak. Use 12.3 W for low-speed transient checks.

## 3. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. The low-side synchronous FET turns on after its body diode has already clamped the node, so it switches at near-zero voltage.

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap (high-side) | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw = ½·25.2·80·30 ns·48 kHz | **1.45 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 40 nC·25.2·48 kHz | 0.048 W |
| Output capacitance | ½·Q_oss·V·f_sw | 0.018 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·100 ns·48 kHz | 0.31 W |
| Gate drive (dissipated in driver/R_g, not FET) | Q_g·V_gs·f_sw·2 | 0.038 W |
| **Total switching loss per channel** | | **≈ 1.83 W** |

- **Conclusion:** at 80 A, conduction loss dominates switching loss by roughly 13:1.
- **Implication:** raising f_sw from 24 kHz to 48 kHz costs only about 0.9 W per channel. It is justified by lower current ripple and lower motor iron/copper ripple loss.

### 3.1 DC-Link Capacitor Ripple

$$I_{C,rms}\approx I_{ph}\sqrt{D(1-D)}\;\Rightarrow\;\max = 0.5\,I_{ph}= \mathbf{40\ A_{rms}}\ \text{per channel (at } D=0.5)$$

This drives the following capacitor requirements:

- **Per channel:** at least 4× 10 µF/50 V X7R 1206 ceramics placed directly at the half-bridge. Account for DC-bias derating: a 10 µF part provides about 3 µF at 25 V.
- **Bulk:** a shared external low-ESR electrolytic of 470–1000 µF/35 V on the battery leads.

### 3.2 Loss Summary per Channel at an 80 A Burst

| Loss | W |
|---|---|
| FET conduction | 24.6 |
| Copper | 6.4 |
| Switching + diode | 1.8 |
| **Total** | **≈ 32.8 W** |
| **4 channels, all bursting** | **≈ 131 W** |

## 4. Thermal Model

### 4.1 Feasibility Check

Four channels at 80 A is up to 320 A of phase current. At full throttle (D → 1), the battery current approaches the same value, which is about 8 kW from a 6S pack. No 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 nano board can sustain this. **Treat "80 A" strictly as a sub-second burst rating** and validate it with the transient model below.

### 4.2 Network (Cauer-Approximated, Per FET)

```
P_FET ──[Rth_jc ≈1.5 K/W, τ≈5 ms]── Case/pad ──[Rth_cs ≈ 3 K/W via thermal vias]──
      Board copper node (C_board) ──[Rth_ba]── Ambient
```

**Board thermal mass.** Assume a 6 g board: roughly 2.5 g of copper on 2 oz × 6 layers, plus FR4, components, and solder.

$$C_{board}\approx 2.5\cdot0.385 + 3.5\cdot1.1 \approx \mathbf{4.8\ J/K}$$

**Convection.**
- In prop-wash: h ≈ 60 W/m²K over about 2×6.5 cm² of area, so R_th,ba ≈ 13 K/W.
- On the bench: R_th,ba ≈ 35 K/W.
