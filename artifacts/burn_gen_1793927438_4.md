# Job #52: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC
## Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Budget, Bi-Directional DShot600 DMA Allocation, and Reference Netlists

---

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Key Findings

### 0.1 Key findings

1. **An 80 A burst is a time-limited event on a nano-class board.** With all four channels at 80 A, total dissipation is about 92 W. The board reaches its thermal limit in about **1.5 to 2 s**.
2. **Sustainable continuous current is about 25 A per channel** in prop-wash cooling at a 50 °C ambient. Marketing should label the board **"25 A continuous / 80 A burst (≤1 s)"**.
3. **Conduction loss dominates.** At 80 A it is about 88% of the total, and in synchronous 6-step drive it is independent of duty cycle. Halving R_DS(on) by paralleling FETs is worth more than any change to the switching frequency.
4. **The DC-link ripple current (up to 40 A_rms per channel) is an undersized risk.** It must be addressed before layout freeze.
5. **DShot assignment:** place all four motors on **TIM1** (or TIM8) with BIDIR on CH1 and CH3. This consumes 3 DMA streams out of the H743's 16 general-purpose streams.

### 0.2 Architecture clarification

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU (AT32F421 running AM32, for example) that decodes DShot and drives the bridge. The H743 does **not** commutate the motors. A 48 kHz, 4-motor sensorless field-oriented control (FOC) or 6-step scheme on the flight-controller MCU, alongside ArduPilot, is not a supported or sensible configuration. If you intended that, flag it and we'll re-architect.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Commutation topology

In 6-step trapezoidal drive, each 60° electrical sector has:
- one phase high (PWM'd with duty D),
- one phase low (low-side switch held on),
- one phase floating.

With synchronous rectification, the PWM phase's high-side switch conducts for D and its low-side switch for (1 − D). The current always flows through exactly **two** channels in series:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[\,D + (1-D)\,] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\cdot 1 = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

**Duty cycle drops out entirely.** Conduction loss is the same at 30% and 100% throttle for the same phase current.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$
R_{DS} = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.0055 \times 100) = 1.55\,\text{m}\Omega
$$
