# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Reference Design Model

**Scope:** Thermal and loss model for 80 A burst, 48 kHz switching, ArduPilot BDShot600 DMA allocation, and reference netlists.

I don't have Job #68's actual BOM, stack-up or board outline, so I've stated every assumption explicitly. Swap in your real datasheet values and the equations carry through unchanged. One finding comes first because it shapes everything else.

> **Headline result:** On a nano (≤30×30 mm) board, 80 A per channel is **only a burst rating**, roughly 1–3 s depending on starting temperature. The board's convective sink supports about **25–30 A per channel continuous** in prop-wash. Section 2.6 derives this.

## 1. Electrical Operating Point

In 6-step commutation, two legs conduct at any instant:

- **Leg A** is PWM-switched, with the high side on for D and the low side (sync rectifier) on for 1−D.
- **Leg B** has its low side held ON continuously for that 60° sector.

With synchronous rectification, the motor current I_m always flows through exactly **two** FET channels in series. This holds regardless of D:

$$
I_{path} = I_m,\quad R_{path} = 2R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + R_{shunt} + R_{Cu}
$$

Per-FET RMS current over one electrical revolution (each FET is active for 120° of 360°):

$$
I_{HS,rms} = I_m\sqrt{\tfrac{D}{3}},\qquad I_{LS,rms} = I_m\sqrt{\tfrac{1}{3}\left(1 + (1-D)\right)\cdot\tfrac{1}{2}\cdot 2}\;\approx\; I_m\sqrt{\tfrac{2-D}{3}}
$$

The worst-case single FET is the low side at low D. In the 80 A burst case D ≈ 0.9–1.0, so the stress shifts to the high side.

## 2. Thermal Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ /\mathrm{K}
$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R_DS(on) = 1.0 × 1.6 = **1.6 mΩ**.

### 2.2 MOSFET conduction loss per ESC channel

$$
P_{cond,FET} = I_m^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 12.8 W |
| 100 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W |
| 125 °C | 1.6 mΩ | **20.5 W** |

### 2.3 Shunt and copper

$$
P_{shunt} = I_m^2 R_{sh} = 6400 \times 0.25\,\mathrm{m\Omega} = 1.6\ \mathrm{W}
$$

For the copper path, take ~10 mm effective path per phase through 2 oz (70 µm) copper, 5 mm wide, with 2 layers paralleled:

$$
R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho L}{w t} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\cdot 0.01}{0.005\cdot 70\times10^{-6}\cdot 2} \approx 0.25\ \mathrm{m\Omega}
$$

Hot copper (+0.39 %/K) adds about 1.3×, giving roughly 0.33 mΩ over two phases and **≈ 2.1 W**.

### 2.4 Diode / dead-time conduction

Dead time t_dt = 200 ns per edge, 2 edges per period, body diode V_F = 0.8 V:

$$
P_{dt} = V_F\, I_m \cdot 2 t_{dt}\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 400\,\mathrm{ns}\cdot 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = 1.23\ \mathrm{W}
$$

### 2.5 Switching losses at 48 kHz

**Hard-switching overlap loss** (leg A only, 1 edge pair per period):

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_m (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}
= 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 40\,\mathrm{ns}\cdot 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}
$$

**Reverse recovery** (body diode of the sync FET is recovered by the turning-on HS):

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\,\mathrm{nC}\cdot 25.2\cdot 48\,\mathrm{k} = 0.060\ \mathrm{W}
$$

**Output capacitance:**

$$
P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\,\mathrm{nC}\cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\,\mathrm{k} = 0.048\ \mathrm{W}
$$

**Gate drive** (2 switching FETs per period; this loss lands in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET die):

$$
P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{DRV} f_{sw} = 2 \cdot 50\,\mathrm{nC}\cdot 10 \cdot 48\,\mathrm{k} = 0.048\ \mathrm{W}
$$

**Switching subtotal ≈ 2.10 W per ESC.**

The sensitivity to switching frequency is linear. At 24 kHz this subtotal is ≈ 1.05 W; at 96 kHz it is ≈ 4.2 W. At 80 A, conduction dominates by roughly 10:1, so 48 kHz is a reasonable choice for current ripple and acoustic behaviour.

### 2.6 Total loss budget and burst duration

| Loss term (per ESC @ 80 A, T_j = 125 °C) | W |
|---|---|
| FET conduction | 20.5 |
| Shunt | 1.6 |
| Copper | 2.1 |
| Dead-time diode | 1.2 |
| Switching (all terms) | 2.1 |
| **Per ESC** | **27.5** |
| **×4 ESCs (all motors at burst)** | **≈ 110 W** |

#### Steady-state board sink capability

- Two-sided area: A = 2 × (0.036 m)² = 2.6×10⁻³ m².
- Prop-wash forced convection: h ≈ 80–120 W/m²K. Use 100.

$$
R_{\theta,BA} = \frac{1}{hA} = \frac{1}{100 \cdot 2.6\times10^{-3}} \approx 3.85\ \mathrm{K/W}
$$

With T_amb = 40 °C and a board limit of 110 °C (FR4/solder/capacitor margin), ΔT = 70 K:

$$
P_{cont,max} = 70 / 3.85 \approx 18\ \mathrm{W\ total}
$$

So the continuous rating is:

$$
I_{cont} \approx 80\sqrt{\frac{18}{110}} \approx \mathbf{32\ A/ch}
$$

This is optimistic. Derate to **~25–28 A** for still air at hover and for the FC's own ~1.5 W.
