## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_ds = 1.2 × (1 + 0.0055 × 75) = **1.695 mΩ**.

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = **1.86 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification

At every instant, current flows through exactly **two** conducting devices:
- the high-side FET of the sourcing phase (during PWM-on) or its complementary low-side FET (during PWM-off, synchronous freewheel), and
- the low-side FET of the sinking phase.

The duty cycle therefore cancels out of the total conduction loss:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\, I^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Averaged over one electrical revolution, each of the 6 FETs carries current for 1/3 of the cycle:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)}{3}$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 100 °C

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Instantaneous loss in one conducting FET | 80² × 1.695 mΩ = **10.85 W** |
| Total per ESC channel | **21.7 W** |
| Average per FET | **3.62 W** |
| Total, four ESCs | **86.8 W** |

### 1.4 Parasitic conduction terms (frequently forgotten)

| Element | Model | Value at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Current shunt, 0.25 mΩ (per ESC) | I²R | 1.6 W |
| Phase/bus copper, ~0.3 mΩ effective path | I²R | 1.9 W |
| Bus capacitor ESR (ripple ≈ 0.5·I at D = 0.5), 4 mΩ bank | I_rms²·ESR | 6.4 W total across bank |

### 1.5 Electrothermal coupling (fixed-point iteration)

Because R_ds depends on T_j, solve:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{case} + R_{\theta JC}\left[\frac{I^2 R_{ds,25}(1+\alpha(T_j^{(n)}-25))}{3} + \bar P_{sw,FET}\right]$$

Convergence condition:

$$\frac{I^2 R_{ds,25}\,\alpha\, R_{\theta JC}}{3} < 1$$

Here: 6400 × 1.2e-3 × 0.0055 × 1.5 / 3 = **0.021**. The iteration converges strongly, so there is no junction-level runaway. The runaway risk lives at the **board** level (Section 3), where the effective R_θ is about 10× larger.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing device in each ESC is hard-switched: one FET at a time, rotating every 60° electrical. Freewheel commutation of the complementary FET is quasi-ZVS apart from the dead-time diode conduction.

### 2.1 Transition times

$$t_r \approx t_f \approx \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_g} = \frac{12\text{ nC}}{1.5\text{ A}} = 8\text{ ns}$$

Use **10 ns** to account for common-source inductance in the package and layout.
