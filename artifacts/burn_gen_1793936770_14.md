## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Two points need to be settled before any equations, because they change the design.

**1. The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot does not contain ESC commutation firmware. A 6-step or FOC loop for 4 motors at 48 kHz would compete with the EKF and IMU scheduling. The architecture is therefore:

- **H743** runs ArduPilot (the flight controller) and emits bi-directional DShot600.
- **4 × AT32F421K8U7** (or STM32G071) ESC MCUs run AM32, one per motor.
- **4 × FD6288Q** 3-phase gate drivers.
- **24 × N-MOSFETs** in 3.3×3.3 mm PQFN.

**2. "80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 mm mounting, ~26×26 mm board) is a short-duration, thermally capacitance-limited rating.** The model below shows it is roughly **1.5–3 s per motor-group event**, not a sustained capability. The spec sheet should state it that way.
