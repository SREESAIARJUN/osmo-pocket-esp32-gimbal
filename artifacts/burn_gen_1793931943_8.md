## 1. Field-Oriented Control: Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

These are amplitude-invariant.

**Clarke:**
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)\quad(\text{assuming } i_a+i_b+i_c=0)$$

**Park,** with $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$:
$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park:**
$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant and decoupling

The SPM motor in the rotor frame obeys:
$$v_d = R_s i_d + L_d\dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q$$
$$v_q = R_s i_q + L_q\dot i_q + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

The cross-coupling and back-EMF terms are cancelled by feedforward:
$$v_d^* = v_{d,PI} - \hat\omega_e \hat L_q i_q,\qquad v_q^* = v_{q,PI} + \hat\omega_e \hat L_d i_d + \hat\omega_e\hat\lambda_m$$

Here $\lambda_m = \tfrac{2}{3}\tfrac{K_t}{p}$ for the amplitude-invariant transform, since $T_e = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q$.

After decoupling, each axis reduces to a first-order plant:
$$G_i(s)=\frac{1}{L s + R}$$

### 1.3 Gain design by pole-zero cancellation

Choose the PI controller $C_i(s)=K_p + K_i/s$ with zero at $K_i/K_p = R/L$. The closed loop then becomes first-order with bandwidth $\omega_{ci}$:
$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}$$

Pick $\omega_{ci} \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/20$ to account for the 1.5-sample computation and PWM delay. At 24 kHz this gives $\omega_{ci}\approx 2\pi\cdot 1200$, so:
- $K_p = 15.1$ V/A
- $K_i = 37\,700$ V/(A·s)
