- Current loop: ω_ci ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s
- Velocity loop: ω_cv ≈ 2π·150 rad/s
- Angle loop: ω_cθ ≈ 2π·15–25 rad/s

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform:**

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)\quad(\text{using } i_a+i_b+i_c=0)
$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant model in the rotating frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

Here ω_e = p·ω_m, and λ_m is the PM flux linkage, with K_t = (3/2)·p·λ_m.

**Electromagnetic torque:**

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2}p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q
$$

This holds for a surface-mount PMSM, where L_d ≈ L_q.

### 1.3 Current PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

**Pole-zero cancellation tuning.** Choosing

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}
$$

cancels the electrical pole at R/L. The closed loop then becomes a first-order lag with bandwidth ω_ci.

**Control law:**

$$
v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e \hat L\, i_q
$$

$$
v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e \hat L\, i_d + \hat\omega_e\hat\lambda_m
$$

**Setpoints:**

- i_d* = 0 (no field weakening on a gimbal).
- i_q* is the output of the velocity loop plus the anti-cogging feedforward (Section 3).

**Voltage limiting.** The command vector is limited to the linear-SVPWM circle:

$$
\sqrt{v_d^2+v_q^2}\le \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}
$$

The d-axis has priority, and back-calculation anti-windup acts on both integrators.
