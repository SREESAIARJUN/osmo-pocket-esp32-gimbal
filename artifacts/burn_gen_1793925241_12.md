# 3-Axis Gimbal Control: FOC, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Fusion

I have no specification for "Job #52", so this design uses the stated assumptions below. Every assumption is a named constant in the code, so you can substitute your motor, inertia and sensor-placement data directly.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 or STM32G4 with FPU, 3 axes on one MCU or one MCU per axis |
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5 Ω, L ≈ 2 mH, K_t ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A or MT6701), used for commutation and cogging phase |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera stage, mounted symmetrically about the rotation center, sharing a CLKIN |
| Loop rates | Current/FOC 20 kHz, IMU ODR 8 kHz, velocity 4 kHz, attitude/angle 1 kHz |
