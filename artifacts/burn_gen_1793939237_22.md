## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology
In 6-step trapezoidal drive, two half-bridges are active at any instant:
- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high-side conducts for duty D. The low-side synchronous-rectifies for (1−D), minus dead time.
- **Leg B (return leg):** the low-side is on continuously for the 60° sector.
- **Leg C:** floating (BEMF sensing).

Assuming ripple is small relative to I (true for 48 kHz and typical motor inductance), the current I flows through exactly one FET in each active leg at all times. This holds for any D. So:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

Each of the 6 FETs takes every role over an electrical revolution. The averages are:

$$
\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6} = \frac{I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)}{3},\qquad
\hat P_{cond,FET} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\ \text{(instantaneous, during its active sector)}
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]
$$

### 1.3 Worked numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per channel | Average per FET | Peak per FET |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W | 2.56 W | 7.7 W |
| 100 °C | 1.83 mΩ | 23.4 W | 3.90 W | 11.7 W |
| 125 °C | 2.04 mΩ | **26.1 W** | **4.35 W** | **13.1 W** |

### 1.4 Additional conduction paths
Do not ignore these. On nano boards they are often 20–40 % of FET loss.

| Path | Model | Example value |
|---|---|---|
| Shunt (if per-channel 0.5 mΩ) | I²·R_sh (low-side shunt sees current ≈ all the time) | 3.2 W/ch |
| Phase copper (2 oz, 5 mm wide, 8 mm long, ≈0.4 mΩ round trip) | 2·I²·R_cu | 5.1 W/ch |
| Solder pads + motor wire pads | ≈0.1–0.2 mΩ | 0.6–1.3 W/ch |

**Recommendation:** use one bus-current shunt or a Hall sensor instead of per-channel shunts. Use 3 oz or heavier outer copper with internal-layer paralleling of phase and bus nets.

## 2. Switching-Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. The low-side sync FET turns on after the body diode is already conducting (ZVS), so the hard-switching energy lands on the high-side.

$$
P_{sw,ch} = \underbrace{\tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw}}_{V\text{–}I\ overlap}
+ \underbrace{Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw}}_{reverse\ recovery}
+ \underbrace{Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw}}_{output\ charge}
+ \underbrace{2\,V_f\,I\,t_{dt}\,f_{sw}}_{dead\text{-}time\ diode}
+ \underbrace{2\,Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw}}_{gate\ (driver\ side)}
$$

| Term | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | 0.5·25.2·80·40 ns·48 kHz | **1.94 W** |
| Reverse recovery | 30 nC·25.2·48 kHz | 0.036 W |
| Q_oss | 40 nC·25.2·48 kHz | 0.048 W |
| Dead-time diode | 2·0.8·80·100 ns·48 kHz | 0.61 W |
| Gate (in driver/LDO, not FET) | 2·50 nC·10 V·48 kHz | 0.048 W |
| **Total P_sw per channel** | | **≈2.6 W** |
