## 0. Design Basis and Assumptions

I have no prior project files for Job #54, so this baseline rests on the assumptions below. Change any of them and the equations in §2–§4 still hold. Only the numbers change.

| Parameter | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max / 22.2 V nom | Drives FET V<sub>DS</sub> rating (40 V class) |
| Burst current | 80 A per motor channel, ≤ 10 s | Phase current; ≈ DC current at high duty |
| Form factor | 30.5 × 30.5 mm mount, ~36 × 36 mm PCB, 8-layer, 2 oz outer/inner power layers | "Nano" stack |
| Architecture | H743 FC drives 4× ESC MCUs (AT32F421 / STM32G071 class running AM32 or BLHeli_32) over bidirectional DShot600 | The H743 does **not** commutate motors directly |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal, complementary synchronous PWM | Standard AM32/BLHeli_32 |
| PWM frequency | 48 kHz | ESC MCU setting |
| MOSFET (reference) | 40 V, R<sub>DS(on)</sub> 0.9 mΩ typ @ 25 °C, Q<sub>g</sub> ≈ 60 nC, Q<sub>sw</sub> ≈ 20 nC, Q<sub>rr</sub> ≈ 60 nC, R<sub>θJC</sub> ≈ 1 K/W, 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 mm package | **Placeholder.** Substitute your selected part's datasheet values |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q-class 3-phase driver, ~1 A effective gate current at plateau | |

> ⚠️ **Headline engineering finding:** 80 A per channel on a 36 mm single-board 4-in-1 is a **burst-only** rating. The model below shows a thermally sustainable continuous rating of roughly **35 A/channel**, and an 80 A burst limit of roughly **5 s** from a 40 °C start. Market claims for nano ESCs must be validated against this.

