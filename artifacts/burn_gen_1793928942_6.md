## 0. Assumptions and Loop Rates

| Item | Value (parameterized) |
|---|---|
| Motor | 12N14P gimbal BLDC: pole pairs p = 7, slots Nₛ = 12 |

| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (MT6701 / AS5048A class) |
| PWM / current loop | 20 kHz, center-aligned, synchronous ADC sampling |
