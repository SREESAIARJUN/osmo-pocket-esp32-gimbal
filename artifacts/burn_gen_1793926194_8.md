# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion

**Scope note:** I don't have the specification document for "Job #13", so this design states its assumptions explicitly (§0). Every parameter that depends on your hardware is a named constant. Swap in your motor, encoder and MCU values and the equations still hold.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC (e.g. GBM2804-class), 12N14P → pole pairs `p = 7`, slots `Ns = 12` |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (MT6701 / AS5047P), SPI |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with FPU (STM32G4 / H7), center
