## 0. Architecture Clarification and Design Assumptions

Two points must be settled before any math, because they change the model.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In any ArduPilot-compatible 4-in-1 design, the H743 is the flight controller. It emits bidirectional DShot600 to four independent ESC MCUs, typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or EFM8 running BLHeli_S/Bluejay. Those MCUs run the 48 kHz PWM, back-EMF zero-cross detection, and eRPM telemetry. Running four sensorless BLDC loops on the H743 is not supported by ArduPilot and is not done here.

**2. "Nano" and 80 A burst are in tension.** A 20×20 mm board cannot reject this heat. This package assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm ESC board** with a **20×20 mm FC**. The thermal model in §3 shows that even at 30×30, 80 A is a **time-limited burst**, not a continuous rating.
