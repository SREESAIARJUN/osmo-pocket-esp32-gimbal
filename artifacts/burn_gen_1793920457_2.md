## 1. System Architecture and Assumptions

### 1.1 Architecture Correction

The STM32H743 should **not** run the motor commutation. ArduPilot cannot run 48 kHz six-step commutation for 4 motors alongside EKF3. The correct partition is:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) | Attitude control, EKF, bidir DShot600 master |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 (one per motor) | AM32 | Six-step complementary PWM at 48 kHz, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase half-bridge driver) | n/a | 12 V gate drive, internal bootstrap diodes |
| Power stage (×4) | 6 × N-MOSFET per motor (24 total) | n/a | Three-phase bridge |
