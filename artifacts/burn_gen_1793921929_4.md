## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = \operatorname{wrap}_{2\pi}\!\big(p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{m,0})\big)$$

θ<sub>m,0</sub> is the encoder offset. Calibrate it by applying a voltage vector at θ<sub>e</sub> = 0 and recording θ<sub>m</sub>. Also verify direction by sweeping θ<sub>e</sub> through +2π.

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.3 Motor model (surface PMSM, L<sub>d</sub> = L<sub>q</sub> = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

$$\tau = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.4 Current PI with decoupling (pole-zero cancellation)

Choose a closed-loop current bandwidth ω<sub>c</sub>. Keep it at or below about 1/10 of the PWM rate, e.g. ω<sub>c</sub> = 2π·1500 rad/s.

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c$$

$$v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e L i_d + \hat\omega_e\hat\lambda_m$$

With this choice, the PI zero cancels the electrical pole R/L. The closed loop then becomes first order: i/i* = ω<sub>c</sub>/(s + ω<sub>c</sub>).
