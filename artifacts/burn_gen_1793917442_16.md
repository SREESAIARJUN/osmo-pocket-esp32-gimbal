## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Topology

In 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification, two legs conduct at every instant:
- the commutated leg (HS or LS, static), and
- the PWM leg, where the HS and LS FETs alternate.

The phase current I flows through exactly one FET in each of the two active legs, ignoring dead-time. Conduction loss is therefore **independent of duty cycle**:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu}$$
