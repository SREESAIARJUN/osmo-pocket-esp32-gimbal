## 0. Architecture Boundary and Key Findings

**Partitioning.** ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The STM32H743 is the flight controller (FC) and drives 4× bi-directional DShot600 lines. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. The reference design uses AT32F421 parts. The 48 kHz switching frequency is configured in that ESC firmware, not in ArduPilot.

**Key findings:**

| # | Finding | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 80 A per channel is **burst only**. Total loss is about 98 W with all four channels at 80 A. | Burst is limited to **about 3–4 s** before the hottest FET reaches T_j ≈ 150 °C (see §3). |
| 2 | Four channels at 80 A draw **320 A** from the battery. | An XT60 or XT90 connector cannot carry this. Use direct solder pads, 10 AWG minimum, and an external low-ESR bulk capacitor. |
| 3 | Conduction loss dominates. It is about 79 % of the total, versus about 4 % for switching at 48 kHz. | Spend the design budget on R_DS(on) and copper. Pushing gate-drive speed buys little. |
| 4 | The DShot outputs must be on timer channels CH1–CH4, never CHxN. | The bidirectional input-capture path needs them. The pin map in §4 satisfies this. |

**Assumptions.** Every number below derives from the parameters in §1. Replace them with your actual MOSFET datasheet values and re-run the equations; the formulas are the deliverable.
