## System Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan, Netlist, and Thermal Analysis

## 0. Architectural Correction (Read First)

The STM32H743 cannot directly commutate four BLDC motors at 48 kHz while also running ArduPilot. The production-viable topology is:

| Domain | MCU | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz, LQFP100) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) | Attitude control, EKF, DShot600 master |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6 (one per motor) | AM32 | 48 kHz complementary PWM, sensorless commutation, eRPM telemetry |

All 48 kHz switching math below applies to the **ESC MCUs and power stage**. All DMA and timer math applies to the **H743**.

**"Nano" constraint flag.** A 20×20 mm or 25.5×25.5 mm AIO cannot sustain 80 A per motor. Section 3 shows that 80 A is a **≈2-second transient rating at most**, even with aggressive copper. This must be stated honestly on the spec sheet.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss (synchronous 6-step, complementary PWM)

During any 60° commutation sector, current flows from the PWM leg to the return leg:

- PWM leg: the high-side conducts for duty D, and the synchronous low-side freewheels for (1−D).
- Return leg: the low-side conducts for 100% of the sector.

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{hot}\big[D + (1-D)\big] + I^2 R_{hot} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds,25}\,k_T
$$

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.0\text{m}\Omega \times 1.5 = \mathbf{19.2\ W}
$$

**Per-FET stress.** The worst case is the high side at D→1, where a single FET carries the full 80 A for its 120° window. Instantaneous dissipation in that FET is 80² × 1.5 mΩ = **9.6 W**. Each FET's average over a full electrical revolution is about ⅓ of that, plus its share of freewheel conduction.

### 2.2 Hard-Switching (V-I overlap) Loss

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. Synchronous rectification makes the low-side turn-on a ZVS event after dead time.

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}
= 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ns} \times 48\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}
$$

### 2.3 Parasitic and Dead-Time Losses

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Output capacitance | Q_oss · V · f | 60 nC × 25.2 × 48k = **0.073 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f | 50 nC × 25.2 × 48k = **0.060 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V_f · I · 2t_dead · f | 0.8 × 80 × 100 ns × 48k = **0.307 W** |
| Gate drive (6 FETs) | 6 · Q_g · V_gs · f | 6 × 60n × 10 × 48k = **0.173 W** (dissipated in driver / R_g) |

### 2.4 Shunt and Copper Loss

Assumptions: 0.5 mΩ phase/bus shunt, plus about 0.6 mΩ of effective PCB copper per current path (2 oz, 6-layer).

$$
P_{Cu} \approx 80^2 \times 1.1\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{7.0\ W}
$$

This is significant and often ignored. Mitigation options are thicker outer copper, via-stitched pours, or removing the shunt and using total-current sensing only.

### 2.5 Totals

| Loss | Per ESC @ 80 A | ×4 ESCs |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FET) | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| Switching | 1.94 W | 7.8 W |
| Q_oss + Q_rr + dead time | 0.44 W | 1.8 W |
| Gate drive | 0.17 W | 0.7 W |
| Copper/shunt | 7.0 W | 28.0 W |
| **Total** | **≈28.8 W** | **≈115 W** |

Efficiency at 80 A, D ≈ 0.9: P_out ≈ 25.2 × 0.9 × 80 ≈ 1814 W per channel, so η ≈ **98.4%**. The efficiency looks excellent; the problem is entirely heat density.

### 2.6 Frequency Sensitivity

$$
\frac{\partial P_{total}}{\partial f_{sw}} = \tfrac{1}{2}VI(t_r+t_f) + Q_{oss}V + Q_{rr}V + 2V_f I t_{dead} + 6Q_gV_{gs} \approx 61\ \mu\text{W/Hz}
$$

Dropping from 48 kHz to 24 kHz saves only ≈1.5 W per ESC. **Conduction and copper dominate at 80 A.** 48 kHz is justified for low-inductance motors (current ripple and acoustic noise), not by losses.

### 2.7 Bus Capacitor Ripple

$$
I_{C,rms} = I\sqrt{D(1-D)} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{max at } D=0.5:\ 80 \times 0.5 = \mathbf{40\ A_{rms}}\ \text{per ESC}
$$

The four ESCs are not phase-synchronized, so the uncorrelated worst case is √4 × 40 = **80 A_rms**. A nano board cannot locally absorb this. The required mitigation is:

- ≥ 8 × 10 µF/50 V X7R 1210 ceramics per ESC, placed at the half-bridges.
- An external low-ESR electrolytic (≥ 1000 µF/35 V) on the battery pigtail. This is **mandatory**, not optional.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Junction Steady-State Delta

Assume R_θJC ≈ 1.5 K/W for a 3.3×3.3 PDFN on thermal vias.

The hottest FET (9.6 W conduction + ~1 W switching share) has

$$
\Delta T_{JC} \approx 10.6 \times 1.5 = 16\ \text{K}
$$

### 3.2 Board Thermal Capacitance (lumped)

The board is 30.5 × 30.5 × 1.6 mm, 6 layers, with 2 oz outer and 1 oz inner copper.

| Material | Volume | ρ·c_p | C |

| FETs, caps, solder | — | — | ~0.4 J/K |
