## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms
With amplitude-invariant scaling and $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$:

**Clarke**
$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

**Park**, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$:
$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Angle delay compensation.** The PWM update is applied about 1.5 samples after the ADC sample. Use $\hat\theta_e = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s$ for the inverse Park transform.

### 1.2 PMSM model in the dq frame
$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q
$$
$$
v_q = R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e (L_d i_d + \psi_m)
$$

Torque for a surface-mount PMSM ($L_d \approx L_q$):
$$
\tau = \tfrac32\,p\,\psi_m\, i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$

### 1.3 Current PI with decoupling
The control law is the PI output plus cross-coupling and back-EMF cancellation:
$$
v_d^* = K_{p,i}\,e_d + K_{i,i}\!\int e_d\,dt \;-\; \omega_e L_q i_q
$$
$$
v_q^* = K_{p,i}\,e_q + K_{i,i}\!\int e_q\,dt \;+\; \omega_e (L_d i_d + \psi_m)
$$

**Pole-zero cancellation tuning.** The plant is $1/(Ls+R)$. Choose $K_i/K_p = R/L$. The closed loop then reduces to a first-order system with bandwidth $\omega_{c,i}$:
$$
K_{p,i} = \omega_{c,i} L,\qquad K_{i,i} = \omega_{c,i} R,\qquad \omega_{c,i} \approx 2\pi \cdot (1\text{–}2\,\text{kHz}) \le \tfrac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10}
$$

**Discretization.** Use backward Euler for the integrator, $x_k = x_{k-1} + K_i T_s e_k$.

### 1.4 Voltage limiting and anti-windup
The linear SVPWM limit is $|v| \le V_{dc}/\sqrt3$. Give $v_d$ priority:
$$
v_d^{sat} = \text{clip}\!\left(v_d^*, \pm V_{max}\right),\qquad
v_q^{sat} = \text{clip}\!\left(v_q^*, \pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - (v_d^{sat})^2}\right)
$$

Back-calculation anti-windup:
$$
x_k \leftarrow x_k + K_{aw}\,(v^{sat} - v^*),\qquad K_{aw} \approx \frac{K_i}{K_p}\,T_s
$$
