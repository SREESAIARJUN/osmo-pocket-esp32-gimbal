# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model (Job #31)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Architecture Corrections

Several points in the request conflict with physics or with how ArduPilot works. They are resolved here so the rest of the model is sound.

| Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|
| **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** Bi-directional DShot assumes a separate ESC MCU per motor that decodes DShot and returns eRPM. | The architecture is H743 (ArduPilot) → DShot600 → 4× ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32). These MCUs → FD6288Q 3-phase gate drivers → 24 MOSFETs. The 48 kHz PWM belongs to the ESC MCUs. |
| **Nano (20×20 mm) cannot hold 80 A/channel.** The H743 (LQFP100, 14×14 mm), 24 power FETs, 4 ESC MCUs and the bulk capacitance do not fit. The thermal mass is also roughly 3× too small (see §2.6). | The design targets a 30.5×30.5 mm mount on a 36×36 mm board. A true nano variant should be derated to about 25 A burst per channel. |
| **80 A burst ×4 = 320 A battery current.** | Use XT90 or 10 AWG pads and 2× 1000 µF low-ESR bulk capacitors. A 6S pack at 320 A is about 8 kW, so this is a heavy-lift class drive. |
| **Part parameters.** | Datasheet values are representative of the 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN class. **Substitute your selected part's datasheet values** into the equations. |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path (6-step trapezoidal, complementary PWM)

At any instant one phase is high (PWM), one is low (on), and one floats.

- **PWM on-time (D):** current flows through HS(A) and LS(B), giving 2 × R_ds in series.
- **PWM off-time (1−D):** current freewheels through LS(A), which is synchronously rectified, and LS(B), again 2 × R_ds.

So, ignoring dead time, the channel conduction loss is independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$
