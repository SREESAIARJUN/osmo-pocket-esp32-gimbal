## 0. Architecture Corrections (Read First)

Three points in the brief need correcting before any math is meaningful.

1. **The H743 cannot also be the ESC.** Bi-directional DShot600 is a protocol *between* a flight controller and separate ESC MCUs. The correct topology is:
   - H743 running ArduPilot.
   - DShot600 bus.
   - Four ESC MCUs, either 4× AT32F421 running AM32 or 4× EFM8BB21 running BlueJay.
   - Gate drivers.
   - 24 MOSFETs (or 48 if paralleled).
   
   The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, not the H743.
2. **"80 A burst" must be defined.** This document assumes **80 A RMS phase current per motor channel**, which is the worst case. If you meant 80 A *total battery current*, per-channel losses drop by roughly 16×, and the design becomes comfortable.
3. **80 A/channel on a nano (20×20 or 25×25 mm) board is not thermally sustainable.** Section 4 shows it is viable only as a **burst of about 3–7 s**. This must be enforced in firmware and is not optional.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Six-step trapezoidal with synchronous (complementary) PWM

In any 60° sector, two legs are active:
- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high side conducts for D·T, and the complementary low side conducts for (1−D)·T.
- **Leg B (return leg):** the low side conducts continuously.

Instantaneous channel conduction loss:

P_cond,ch = I_ph² · R · [D + (1 − D) + 1] = **2 · I_ph² · R_ds,on**

This is independent of duty cycle, which is the key benefit of synchronous rectification.

### 2.2 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

| Configuration | R per switch position | P_cond per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 FET per position (6/ch) | 1.44 mΩ | 2·6400·1.44 m = **18.4 W** | 73.7 W |
| 2 FETs paralleled (12/ch) | 0.72 mΩ | **9.2 W** | 36.9 W |

### 2.3 Per-FET distribution

Each switch conducts for about 1/3 of the electrical cycle.

- Time-averaged loss per FET ≈ 2I²R/6 = I²R/3 = **3.07 W** (single-FET case).
- Peak instantaneous loss per FET = I²R = **9.2 W**. This matters for Z_th at low eRPM, such as stall or startup.

### 2.4 Copper loss (do not ignore)

For a phase path of 15 mm length × 4 mm width, 4 layers × 2 oz (70 µm) in parallel:

R_Cu = ρL/(w·t·n) = 1.72e-8 · 0.015 / (0.004 · 70e-6 · 4) ≈ **0.23 mΩ**
