## 0. Assumptions and Feasibility Issues

Three items need resolution before layout:

| Item | Issue | Resolution adopted |
|---|---|---|
| **"Nano" form factor + 80 A/ch burst** | A 20×20 mm board cannot physically hold 24 power FETs, 4 gate drivers and adequate copper for 80 A phases. Phase currents of 80 A also mean about 320 A battery current at full duty. | Two-board 30.5×30.5 mm stack: FC (H743) on top, 4-in-1 ESC below. If 20×20 is mandatory, derate to about 35 A burst. |
| **Who commutates the motors** | The H743 runs ArduPilot. It must not run four FOC/6-step loops itself. | The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to four per-channel ESC MCUs (AT32F421 class, AM32 firmware). The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs. |
| **Bus voltage** | Not specified. | 6S LiPo: 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal. 40 V FETs give 1.59× margin on V_max. |

Burst definition used throughout: **80 A phase-peak current per motor, all four motors simultaneously, starting at T_board = 45 °C.**

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology
Each channel is a 3-phase bridge with 6 FETs, giving 24 FETs total. The ESCs use 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM). At any instant:

- **Switching leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. Its complementary low-side FET conducts for (1−D).
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET conducts for 100% of the 60° sector.

### 1.2 Per-motor conduction loss

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\cdot 1 = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Key properties:
- The loss is independent of duty.
- Ripple adds a factor $(1 + \tfrac{1}{12}(\Delta I/I)^2)$. At 48 kHz with motor L ≈ 15 µH, ΔI ≈ 12 A, so the factor is 1.0019. This is negligible.

Temperature dependence of on-resistance:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

### 1.3 Numbers
FET class: 40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3, R_DS,25 = 0.80 mΩ at V_GS = 10 V, Q_g ≈ 40 nC, Q_gd ≈ 8 nC, Q_gs2 ≈ 4 nC, Q_rr ≈ 30 nC, Q_oss ≈ 25 nC.

| T_j (°C) | R_DS (mΩ) | P_cond per motor at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | 0.80 | 10.24 W |
| 100 | 1.07 | 13.70 W |
| **125** | **1.16** | **14.85 W** |

### 1.4 PCB copper conduction (often bigger than people expect)
- Sheet resistance of 2 oz (70 µm) copper: R_□ = ρ/t = 1.72e-8 / 70e-6 = **0.246 mΩ/□** at 20 °C, rising to about 0.31 mΩ/□ at 85 °C.
- Phase path from FET drain to motor pad is about 3 squares.
- Four 2 oz layers in parallel give 0.31 × 3 / 4 = **0.23 mΩ**, so P_Cu = 80² × 0.23 m = **1.5 W/motor**.
- On a single layer the same path would dissipate 5.9 W per motor. Use 4 layers × 2 oz on all power nets, stitched with ≥ 0.3 mm vias at ≤ 1 mm pitch.
