## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

Amplitude-invariant Clarke transform (three-phase to two-phase, using i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 Plant and Decoupling

In the rotor (dq) frame, with surface PMSM L_d = L_q = L:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Here λ_m = (2/3)·K_t/p is the flux linkage.

Feedforward decoupling cancels the cross-coupling and back-EMF terms:

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

After decoupling, each axis is a first-order plant G(s) = 1/(Ls + R).

### 1.3 Current PI Gains (Pole-Zero Cancellation)

$$
C(s)=K_p+\frac{K_i}{s},\quad K_p = L\,\omega_c,\quad K_i = R\,\omega_c
$$

These gains make the closed loop i/i* = ω_c/(s + ω_c).

**Bandwidth limit.** Keep ω_c ≤ 2π·f_pwm/10, because computation plus PWM update add roughly 1.5·T_s of transport delay.

### 1.4 Discretization (Backward Euler) with Clamping Anti-Windup

$$
u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\qquad
I_{k+1} = \operatorname{sat}\!\left(I_k + K_i T_s e_k\right)
$$

The integrator freezes whenever the output is saturated and the error has the same sign as the saturation.
