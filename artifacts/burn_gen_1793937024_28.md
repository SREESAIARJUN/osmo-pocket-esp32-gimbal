# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 80A ESC: Systems Architecture Package, Job #8

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and a Feasibility Flag

You did not specify a MOSFET part, board outline, or cell count, so every number below comes from the **parameter set in §0.2**. Swap in your datasheet values and the equations still hold. Validate the final numbers against your selected parts and a thermal test.

### 0.1 Architecture Decision

The STM32H743 acts as the **flight controller** running ArduPilot. It does not commutate the motors. Each of the four ESC channels has its own MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class, running AM32 or BLHeli_32), a 3-phase gate driver, and 6 MOSFETs. That makes 24 FETs in total.

Driving 4 motors directly from the H743 (using its HRTIM plus timers) is possible but not sensible. You lose ESC firmware maturity, and ArduPilot does not support it.

### 0.3 Feasibility Flag

The headline number is the problem. Four ESCs at 80 A means roughly **320 A phase current and ~83 W of semiconductor dissipation** on a ~7 cm² board.

The math below shows this is only survivable as a **short burst of about 2–3 s from cold** with prop-wash airflow. Treat "80A" as a **per-ESC, non-simultaneous, ≤ 2 s** rating, or move to a 30×30 mm format. Marketing that claims 80A continuous on a nano AIO is not physically supportable.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path (6-Step Trapezoidal with Synchronous Rectification)

At any instant, current flows through one high-side FET of phase *x*, the motor, and one low-side FET of phase *y*.

- During the PWM on-time: HS_x → winding → LS_y.
- During the off-time (complementary PWM): the free-wheel current passes through LS_x (synchronously rectified) → winding → LS_y.

In both states, **two channels conduct the full phase current**. Ignoring the dead time covered in §2.3:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_J)$$

### 1.2 Temperature-Corrected Resistance

The design is evaluated at T_J = 125 °C:

$$R(125^\circ C) = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega\,[1 + 0.004(100)] = 1.40\,\text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2(80)^2(1.40\times10^{-3}) = 17.9\ \text{W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET Distribution

Each FET conducts for 120° of each electrical cycle, i.e. a duty share of 1/3. Over 6 FETs carrying 2 conducting slots:

$$P_{FET,avg} = \frac{2 I^2 R}{6} = \frac{I^2 R}{3} = 2.99\ \text{W}$$

During its active interval, the instantaneous FET loss is I²R = 8.96 W.
