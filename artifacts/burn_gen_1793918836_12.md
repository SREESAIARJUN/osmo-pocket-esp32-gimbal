## 1. Field-Oriented Current Control

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform:**

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

With three shunts, enforce $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$ by subtracting the mean first.

**Park transform**, where θₑ = p·θₘ − θ_offset and p is the number of pole pairs:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 PMSM model in the dq frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q
$$

$$
v_q = R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e (L_d i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\left[\lambda_m i_q + (L_d-L_q) i_d i_q\right] \;\xrightarrow{\text{SPM}}\; K_t i_q,\qquad K_t = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m
$$

### 1.3 Current PI (pole–zero cancellation)

The plant per axis is $G_i(s)=\dfrac{1}{Ls+R}$.

Choose the zero of $C(s)=K_p + K_i/s$ at $R/L$. The open loop then becomes $\omega_c/s$, which gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10\text{–}20}
$$

**Decoupling feedforward:**

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L_q i_q
$$

$$
v_q^{ff} = \omega_e (L_d i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

u_k = K_p e_k + I_k + v^{ff}_k
