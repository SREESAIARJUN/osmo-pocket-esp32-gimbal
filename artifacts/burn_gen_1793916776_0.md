## 0. Architectural Assumptions and Red Flags

Several points in the brief must be settled before the math is meaningful.

| # | Issue | Resolution adopted |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The H743 does **not** commutate the motors. A DShot-controlled 4-in-1 ESC needs per-motor ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. | H743 is the flight controller and sends bidirectional DShot600 to 4 ESC MCUs (STM32G071 / AT32F421 class). |
| 2 | "80A burst" × 4 motors on 6S is ≈ 8 kW. A nano (20×20 mm) board cannot sink this for more than about 1 s (proven in §3). | 80A is treated as a **per-motor transient** rating. Burst duration is an *output* of the model, not an input. |
| 3 | MOSFET datasheet values are not specified. | Parametric model with stated assumed values. Replace them with your selected part's datasheet values. |
| 4 | 48 kHz PWM | Synchronous (complementary) PWM, trapezoidal 6-step commutation, dead time 100 ns. |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Per-leg current pattern (6-step, synchronous PWM)

Each half-bridge leg spends each electrical cycle as follows:

- **1/3 PWM-active.** The high-side (HS) FET conducts for duty fraction D. The low-side (LS) FET conducts for (1−D), acting as a synchronous rectifier.
- **1/3 low-side static ON.** The LS FET conducts 100% of the time.
- **1/3 floating.** This interval is used for back-EMF sensing.

### 1.2 RMS currents per FET (flat-top current I)

$$I_{HS,rms}^2 = \frac{D}{3}I^2 \qquad I_{LS,rms}^2 = \frac{(1-D)}{3}I^2 + \frac{1}{3}I^2$$

### 1.3 Total per motor (3 legs)

$$P_{cond,motor} = 3\left[\frac{D}{3} + \frac{1-D}{3} + \frac{1}{3}\right] I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = \boxed{2\,I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)}$$

This loss is **independent of duty** because synchronous rectification keeps the current flowing through a channel at all times.

### 1.4 Ripple correction

Phase ripple adds a correction factor:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})D}{L_{ph} f_{sw}}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

As an example, take L_ph = 15 µH and D = 0.5:

- V_bemf ≈ 12 V
- ΔI ≈ 9.2 A
- The correction is +7 A², which is about 0.1%. It is negligible at 80 A.

### 1.5 Temperature dependence and thermal runaway

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)\right]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.2 × 1.55 = **1.86 mΩ**.

### 1.6 Copper path loss

Two series phase paths plus the bus run, assuming 2 oz copper and about 0.4 mΩ effective:

$$P_{Cu} = 2I^2 R_{Cu} + I_{bus,rms}^2 R_{bus}$$

### 1.7 Numbers at I = 80 A

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond / motor | P_Cu / motor | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W | 5.1 W | 20.5 W |
| 125 °C | 1.86 mΩ | **23.8 W** | ~6.0 W (Cu α=0.0039) | **29.8 W** |

### 1.8 Worst single FET (D → 1)

At D → 1, the HS FET loses (1/3)·I²·R and the LS FET loses (1/3)·I²·R:

$$P_{FET} = \frac{1}{3}(6400)(1.86\text{ m}\Omega) = 3.97\text{ W}$$

Junction-to-case rise: ΔT_jc = 3.97 W × 1.5 K/W ≈ **6 K**. This is not the bottleneck. The board's thermal mass is (§3).

### 1.9 Runaway criterion

$$\frac{dP}{dT}\cdot R_{\theta,JA} = 2I^2R_{25}\alpha \cdot R_{\theta,JA} < 1$$

At 80 A: dP/dT = 0.084 W/K per motor. Stability therefore requires R_θ(motor→ambient) < 11.9 K/W. §3 shows this condition **fails** for sustained 80 A on a nano board. Firmware current limiting is mandatory.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM-active leg switches. That is one HS/LS pair per motor at any instant.

### 2.1 V–I overlap (hard-switched HS)

Assume a gate driver with ~1 A effective at the Miller plateau, giving t_r ≈ t_f ≈ Q_gd/I_g + t_ri ≈ 15 ns.
