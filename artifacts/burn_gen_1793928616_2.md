## 0. Design Verdict

At nano size, **80 A per channel is only possible as a short burst**. The model below shows about 25 W of loss per channel at 80 A, or about 100 W for the board. A 20×20 mm board has roughly 2 J/K of heat capacity, so it heats at about 50 K/s. **The usable burst is about 1 to 1.5 s.** Sustained current is limited to roughly **10 to 12 A per channel** in prop wash. Treat "80 A" as a burst rating, enforce it in firmware (Section 6), and state it that way in the datasheet.

**Architecture clarification.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It cannot also commutate four BLDC motors. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32. The H743 drives those MCUs over on-board bidirectional DShot600 traces.
