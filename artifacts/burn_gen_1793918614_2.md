## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous drive
At any instant, two phases conduct:
- **High-side (HS) FET** of phase A: PWM'd at duty D.
- **Low-side (LS) FET** of phase A: complementary, providing synchronous rectification during the (1–D) interval.
- **LS FET** of phase B: held fully on for the 60° sector.

The phase current I_ph therefore always flows through exactly **two FETs**, ignoring dead time:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

Battery-side current is $I_{bat} = D \cdot I_{ph}$.

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle (a 120° sector, shared between HS/LS by D). Averaged, this gives:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \tfrac{1}{3}\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Electro-thermal coupling
$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

This term depends on T_j, which in turn depends on dissipation. Solve iteratively as follows:

1. Assume T_j.
2. Compute P.
3. Update $T_j = T_{pcb} + P_{FET}\,R_{\theta,jc}$.
4. Repeat until |ΔT_j| < 0.5 K. This typically converges in 3–4 iterations.

At an end-of-burst target of T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS(on)} = 1.0\,[1+0.006(85)] = 1.51\ \text{m}\Omega$$

|---|---|---|
| P_cond per ESC (FETs) | 2 × 80² × 1.51 mΩ | **19.3 W** |
| P_cond per FET (avg) | (1/3) × 80² × 1.51 mΩ | **3.22 W** |
| PCB copper/solder path (≈0.5 mΩ) | 80² × 0.5 mΩ | **3.2 W** |
