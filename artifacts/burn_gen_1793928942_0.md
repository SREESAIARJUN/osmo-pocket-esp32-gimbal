## 1. FOC Current Loop (innermost, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}})$:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant Model and PI Design

The PMSM voltage equations in the dq frame are:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

With decoupling feedforward
$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q$ and $v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$,
each axis reduces to the first-order plant $G(s) = \dfrac{1}{Ls+R}$.

**Pole-zero cancellation design.** Choose a PI controller $C(s) = K_p + K_i/s$ with:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \frac{i}{i^*}=\frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

Choose $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/20$. At 20 kHz, set $\omega_c = 2\pi\cdot 1000$ rad/s.

**Discretization** (backward Euler on the integrator), with clamping and back-calculation anti-windup:

$$
\begin{aligned}
e_k &= i^*_k - i_k \\
u_k^{\text{unsat}} &= K_p e_k + I_{k-1} + v^{ff}_k \\
u_k &= \text{sat}\!\left(u_k^{\text{unsat}}\right) \\
I_k &= I_{k-1} + K_i T_c\, e_k + K_{aw}\,(u_k - u_k^{\text{unsat}}),\qquad K_{aw}=1/K_p
\end{aligned}
$$

### 1.3 Voltage Vector Limit and SVPWM

**Circular limit** (linear SVPWM region): $|v_{dq}| \le V_{dc}/\sqrt3$.
Give $v_d$ priority, then:

$$
v_q^{max} = \sqrt{(V_{dc}/\sqrt3)^2 - v_d^2}
$$

**SVPWM via min-max (zero-sequence) injection:**

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_a &= v_\alpha,\quad v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta \\
v_0 &= -\tfrac12\left(\max(v_a,v_b,v_c)+\min(v_a,v_b,v_c)\right) \\
D_x &= \tfrac12 + \dfrac{v_x + v_0}{V_{dc}}
\end{aligned}
$$

This is mathematically identical to sector-based SVPWM and has no branches on the sector.
