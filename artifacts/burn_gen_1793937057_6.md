# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, and Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation

## 0. Architecture Findings

Three issues affect everything below.

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | ArduPilot on the H743 does not commutate motors. It only emits DShot. | The 4-in-1 needs **4 ESC MCUs** (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). The H743 talks DShot to them over 4 signal lines. |
| A2 | 80 A per motor on a nano-class (20×20 / 25.5×25.5) board means about 320 A battery current and about 100 W of dissipation. | This is a **burst-only rating of about 1 s** (derived in §3). Continuous capability is about 25–35 A per motor. The datasheet must say so, or the product will fail in the field. |
| A3 | A single shared bus shunt at 320 A dissipates I²R ≈ 10 W even at 0.1 mΩ. | Use per-ESC Hall/shunt sensing at 0.2 mΩ, or estimate current from the duty cycle. |

All device values below are **parametric example values** (a representative 40 V, ~1.2 mΩ DFN3.3 MOSFET). Substitute your actual datasheet values. The equations are the deliverable; the numbers are a worked example.
