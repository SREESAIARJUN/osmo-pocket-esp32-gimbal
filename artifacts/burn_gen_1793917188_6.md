## 0. Architecture Decision and Feasibility Warnings

Three issues determine everything downstream.

**Correction 1: the H743 must not commutate the motors.** The H743 runs ArduPilot. Each of the four ESC channels needs its own commutation MCU, such as STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32. A single H743 cannot run four sensorless 48 kHz commutation loops alongside the ArduPilot scheduler with deterministic latency. The H743 talks to the ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600.

**Correction 2: "80 A burst" on a nano footprint is thermally limited by time, not by MOSFET rating.** The model in §3 yields roughly 70 W of dissipation with all four channels at 80 A. Without a heatsink, a 20×20 or 30×30 board survives this for a few seconds, never continuously. The spec must define burst duration, duty, and simultaneity.

**Assumptions.** Change these and re-run the equations.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path per ESC channel (6-step, synchronous rectification)

During each 60° sector, phase A is PWM-switched and phase B is held low:

- **On-time (D):** I flows through Q_AH and Q_BL.
- **Off-time (1−D):** I freewheels through Q_AL (synchronous) and Q_BL.

Q_BL conducts for 100% of the sector. Phase A conducts through Q_AH for D and through Q_AL for (1−D). Exactly two R_ds(on) are always in series, independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)\cdot(1 - 2t_{dt}f_{sw}) \;\approx\; 2I^2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Per-FET loss distribution

Sector rotation averages the loss across the 6 FETs. Each FET is in "PWM-high", "sync-low", or "held-low" roles for 1/3 of the electrical cycle. Average per-FET loss:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}$$

**Design hot-spot.** Do not design to the average. At low eRPM, near stall or punch-out, a single sector can persist for milliseconds, so one FET carries the full I²R_ds for that period. Use the full-current value for transient Z_th checks (§4.3).

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

Self-consistent junction temperature (iterated in §4): T_j ≈ 110 °C, so R_ds = 0.70 mΩ × [1 + 0.0055 × 85] = **1.03 mΩ**.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.03\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{13.2\ W}$$

Instantaneous single-FET loss: 80² × 1.03 mΩ = **6.6 W**.

### 1.4 Copper and shunt losses (often larger than designers expect)

Per-layer copper resistance:

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho L}{t\,w},\quad \rho=1.72\times10^{-8}(1+0.0039(T-20))\ \Omega\text{m}$$

For a phase run L = 12 mm, w = 4 mm, 2 oz (70 µm), at 80 °C: R ≈ 0.94 mΩ per layer. With 3 paralleled layers plus via stitching, R ≈ 0.31 mΩ. Two phase paths are in series, plus the share of the bus plane: **R_Cu,ch ≈ 0.8 mΩ**.

- Copper loss: P_Cu = 80² × 0.8 mΩ = **5.1 W/ch**
- Current shunt (0.25 mΩ on the low-side return): **1.6 W/ch**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-switched leg incurs switching loss. The held-low FET does not switch within the sector.

**(a) Hard-switching V–I overlap (high-side, inductive load)**
$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(40\text{ ns})(48\text{ kHz}) = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**(b) Dead-time body-diode conduction (low-side FET, 2 edges/period)**
$$P_{dt} = V_f I\,(2t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8(80)(200\text{ ns})(48\text{ kHz}) = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**(c) Reverse recovery** (charge Q_rr dumped into the high side at turn-on)
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 50\text{ nC}(25.2)(48\text{ kHz}) = 0.06\ \text{W}$$

**(d) Output capacitance**
$$P_{oss} = C_{oss(er)} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 1.8\text{ nF}(635)(48\text{ kHz}) = 0.055\ \text{W}$$

The ½ factor for each FET's charge/discharge is combined across the HS/LS pair.

**(e) Gate drive** (two switching FETs; dissipated in the driver and R_g, not the FET die)
$$P_g = 2Q_gV_{gs}f_{sw} = 2(60\text{ nC})(10)(48\text{ kHz}) = 0.058\ \text{W}$$

**Switching subtotal: P_sw,ch ≈ 2.7 W.**

**Sensitivity.** P_VI scales linearly with f_sw and edge time. At 24 kHz it halves (−1 W/ch). Slowing edges to 40 ns each for EMI doubles it. At 48 kHz, conduction dominates at 80 A, so 48 kHz is a reasonable choice for smoothness and acoustic performance.

## 3. Total Loss Budget at 80 A

| Loss term | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFET conduction (T_j ≈ 110 °C) | 13.2 W | 52.8 W |
| PCB copper | 5.1 W | 20.4 W |
| Shunt | 1.6 W | 6.4 W |
| Switching (V–I, dead time, Q_rr, C_oss) | 2.6 W | 10.6 W |
| Gate driver | 0.06 W | 0.24 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 22.6 W** | **≈ 90 W** |

Efficiency check: output ≈ 4 × 22.2 V × 80 A × D_eff. At full throttle, η ≈ 1 − 90/7100 ≈ **98.7%**. The percentage looks fine; the absolute heat in roughly 9 cm² is the problem.

Also check the battery side. Four channels at 80 A is up to 320 A phase current. DC bus current is I_phase × D, so near full duty it is about 300 A. That requires bulk capacitance (§5) and XT90-class leads, not a nano-class connector.
