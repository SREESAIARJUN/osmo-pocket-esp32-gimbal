# Job #57: STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 80A ESC

## Engineering Model and Reference Design, Rev A

This document covers the architecture, the conduction, switching and thermal models, the bi-directional DShot600 timer and DMA plan, and representative netlists.

Before the analysis, three corrections to the brief:

- **ArduPilot cannot commutate the motors.** It runs on the H743 and generates DShot, but it does not run ESC firmware. A working design therefore needs four ESC MCUs (or one MCU per channel pair) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. These receive DShot from the H743. The architecture below follows that split.
- **No MOSFET part number was specified.** All loss numbers use a stated representative 40 V device. They are formulas plus worked examples. You must re-run them against the datasheet of the part you actually select.
- **80 A on a nano-format board is limited by heat capacity, not by the silicon.** The model shows the burst lasts a few seconds, not indefinitely. That limit should be stated on the product label.

## 3. Conduction Loss Model

**Phase current.** In 120° 6-step commutation, each phase carries ±I for 2/3 of the electrical period:

$$I_{ph,rms} = I\sqrt{2/3} = 80 \times 0.816 = 65.3\text{ A}$$

**Per-device duty, with complementary PWM on the high side.** Over one electrical cycle, each leg spends:

- 1/3 of the period PWM-active,
- 1/3 held low,
- 1/3 floating.

This gives the following shares of conduction time:

| Device | Conduction share |
|---|---|
| High-side FET | D/3 |
| Low-side FET | (1−D)/3 (sync freewheel) + 1/3 (held low) |

$$P_{HS} = I^2 R_{hot}\frac{D}{3}, \qquad P_{LS} = I^2 R_{hot}\frac{2-D}{3}$$

**Bridge total.** Exactly two FETs are in the current path at all times, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2I^2R_{hot} = 2(80)^2(1.86\text{ m}\Omega) = 23.8\text{ W}$$

**Per-device split at burst (D = 0.9):**

- P_HS = 6400 × 1.86 m × 0.3 = **3.57 W**
- P_LS = 6400 × 1.86 m × 0.367 = **4.37 W**

**Worst case** is the low side at low duty with high current (stall or prop strike, D → 0): P_LS → 2I²R/3 = **7.9 W**. This case must be caught by firmware current limiting.

**Copper and shunt loss.** Assume a 0.25 mΩ shunt and 0.3 mΩ of bus/phase copper:

$$P_{Cu} \approx I_{ph,rms}^2 (0.55\text{ m}\Omega) = 2.3\text{ W per channel}$$
