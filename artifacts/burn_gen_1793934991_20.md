**Revision A: Design Basis Document**

## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Design Assumptions

Two architectural points shape the rest of this document:

1. **The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz motor PWM.** In every production 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel has its own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 firmware (for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071). That MCU runs the 48 kHz complementary PWM and commutation. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 commands to the ESC MCUs, receiving eRPM telemetry back over bidirectional DShot.
2. **80 A burst on a "nano" (20×20 mm) board is thermally marginal.** The model below shows this burst is only achievable for about 2 s with airflow. I recommend a 25.5×25.5 mm or 30.5×30.5 mm footprint if the 80 A rating must be marketed.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ /^\circ\mathrm{C}$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 75) = **1.34 mΩ**

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous commutation

The ESC uses trapezoidal 6-step commutation with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM:

- At any instant, two phases carry the motor current.
- During the PWM on-time (fraction D), current flows through the high-side FET of phase A and the low-side FET of phase B.
- During the off-time (1−D), current freewheels through the low-side FET of phase A (synchronous rectification) and the low-side FET of phase B.

Therefore exactly **two FET channels** are in the current path at all times, independent of D. RMS current includes the PWM ripple:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{pk}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

With typical motor inductance (~15 µH phase-to-phase) at 48 kHz, ΔI ≈ 8 A. The ripple term (5.3 A²) is negligible against 6400 A², so I_rms ≈ 80 A.

### 1.3 Conduction loss per ESC channel

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j) = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.34\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{17.2\ W}$$

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution (critical for junction temperature)

| Operating regime | Distribution | Per-FET loss |
|---|---|---|
| High eRPM (commutation fast vs. thermal τ) | Loss spread across 6 FETs | ≈ 17.2 / 6 = **2.9 W** |
| Stall / low-RPM high-torque (startup, punch-out from hover) | 2 FETs carry it for ms–s | ≈ 17.2 / 2 = **8.6 W** |

**Design case:** use 8.6 W per FET for the junction-temperature check and 17.2 W per channel for the board heat budget.
