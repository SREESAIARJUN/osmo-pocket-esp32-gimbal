## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.6 mΩ × 1.5 = **2.4 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path per channel, synchronous 6-step

At any instant, one phase is PWM-switching (HS on for D, complementary LS on for 1−D) and a second phase's LS is held fully on:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}\cdot 1 = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Synchronous rectification makes this **independent of duty cycle**. Asynchronous (diode) freewheeling would replace the (1−D) term with $(1-D)\,V_{SD} I$, which is much worse.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.4\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{30.7\ W}$$

### 1.3 Copper and shunt path

$$P_{Cu} = 2\,I^2 R_{trace} + I_{bus}^2 R_{shunt}$$

With R_trace ≈ 0.25 mΩ per phase path (2 oz, short pours)‡: P_Cu ≈ 3.2 W per channel. The shunt term (e.g., 0.25 mΩ) adds ~1.6 W at 80 A bus current.

### 1.4 Phase-to-bus current relation

$$I_{bus} \approx D \cdot I_{phase}$$

80 A phase at D = 0.6 is about 48 A drawn from the battery per channel. State whether your "80 A" rating refers to phase or bus current; marketing figures are usually ambiguous.
