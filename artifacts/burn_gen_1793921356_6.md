# STM32H743 Nano Flight Controller with 80 A Burst 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model (Job #11)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Key Findings

**System architecture.** This design uses a stacked architecture:

- **Flight controller (FC):** STM32H743 running ArduPilot. It generates bidirectional DShot600 on 4 motor outputs.
- **4-in-1 ESC:** 4 independent ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Each MCU drives one 3-phase bridge (6 N-channel MOSFETs).

The H743 is not used as the commutation controller. It has only two advanced timers with complementary outputs (TIM1 and TIM8), so it can drive at most 2 motors' worth of complementary PWM. It also lacks per-channel back-EMF comparator resources for 4 sensorless motors.
