I don't have the specification for Job #22, so this design uses the assumptions listed below. Every parameter is defined symbolically, so you can substitute your actual motor and inertia values.

| Parameter | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× gimbal PMSM (BLDC), e.g. GBM2804-class, 7 pole pairs, 12N14P |
| Rotor sensing | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per axis (AS5047/MA730-class) |

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Motor model in the dq frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d \frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L_q i_q
$$
$$
v_q = R i_q + L_q \frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e (L_d i_d + \lambda_m)
$$
$$
\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2} p \left[\lambda_m i_q + (L_d - L_q) i_d i_q\right] \;\xrightarrow{\;L_d = L_q\;}\; \tau_e = K_t\, i_q,\quad K_t = \tfrac{3}{2} p \lambda_m
$$

The electrical angle is θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset), and the electrical speed is ω_e = p·ω_m.

### 1.2 Transforms

**Clarke** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):
$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt{3}}
$$

**Park:**
$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park:**
$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

**Delay compensation.** PWM updates take effect about 1.5·T_s after sampling. Rotate the inverse-Park angle forward accordingly:
$$
\theta_e^{\text{pwm}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s
$$

### 1.3 Current PI with pole-zero cancellation

The plant per axis is G_i(s) = 1/(Ls + R). Choose the PI so that its zero cancels the electrical pole:
$$
C_i(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s},\qquad K_p = \omega_c L,\quad K_i = \omega_c R
$$

The closed loop is then first-order, i/i* = ω_c/(s + ω_c).

Discrete form (backward Euler) with back-calculation anti-windup:
$$
u_k = K_p e_k + I_k + u_{ff},\qquad
I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw}\left(\text{sat}(u_k) - u_k\right)
$$
with K_aw = K_i / K_p (equivalently 1/T_i).

**Decoupling feedforward:**
$$
u_{ff,d} = -\omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad u_{ff,q} = \omega_e (L_d i_d + \lambda_m)
$$
