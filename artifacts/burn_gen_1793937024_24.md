# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture Model (Job #27)

## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors.** It runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Production 4-in-1 ESCs use a dedicated low-cost MCU per motor (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32), or one MCU per pair of motors. The H743 cannot run four sensorless FOC/6-step loops at 48 kHz *and* ArduPilot within its real-time budget, and ArduPilot has no in-tree ESC commutation firmware. This topology is assumed below:

```
[H743 FC] --4x bidirectional DShot600--> [4x AT32F421 + gate driver + 6 FETs] --> M1..M4
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Commutation scheme.** 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At any instant, current flows through exactly one FET in each of the two active legs:
- During the on-time: high-side of phase A, low-side of phase B.
- During the off-time: low-side of A (synchronous freewheel), low-side of B.

The path therefore always contains 2 FET channels, independent of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I^2\, R_{ds,hot}, \qquad R_{ds,hot} = k_T R_{ds,25} = 1.44\ \text{m}\Omega$$

At 80 A:

$$P_{cond} = 2 (80)^2 (1.44\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{18.4\ W\ per\ motor}$$

**Per-FET distribution.** Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle. The worst-case instantaneous dissipation in one FET is I²R = 9.2 W. The average per FET is about 18.4 / 6 × 2 ≈ 6.1 W, because the two conducting slots rotate among the six FETs.

**Copper losses (not negligible).** Assume 0.3 mΩ of path resistance (pads, vias, 2 oz pours) per phase path:

$$P_{Cu} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.3\,\text{m}\Omega = 3.8\ \text{W}$$

> Note: R_ds is temperature dependent, which creates positive feedback. The fixed point is T_j = T_a + R_θ · 2I²R₂₅(1 + α(T_j − 25)). It diverges when 2I²R₂₅·α·R_θ ≥ 1, which is relevant for steady-state, not burst, operation.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the PWM-switched leg switches; one hard-switched transition pair occurs per PWM period.

| Term | Formula | Value @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f | ½·25.2·80·20 ns·48k = **0.97 W** |
| Coss | Q_oss·V·f | 50 nC·25.2·48k = **0.06 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f | **0.06 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V_SD·I·2t_dt·f | 0.8·80·200 ns·48k = **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (driver IC) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f | 2·60 nC·10·48k = **0.06 W** |
| **Total P_sw** | | **≈ 1.76 W/motor** |

**Total per motor at 80 A:** 18.4 + 3.8 + 1.76 ≈ **24 W**
**All four motors at 80 A:** ≈ **96 W**

Two design implications follow:
- Conduction dominates by ~10×, so choosing the lowest R_ds(on) matters more than switching speed.
- Halving dead time to 50 ns saves 0.3 W/motor. This is the cheapest switching-loss improvement available.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Steady state (shows 80 A cannot be continuous)

Assume a convective R_θ,board→amb of ≈ 5 K/W with strong prop-wash on a 20×20 board.

$$\Delta T = 96 \times 5 = 480\ \text{K}$$

This is physically impossible. **80 A is strictly a burst rating.**

The sustainable continuous current for all four motors, with a ΔT budget of 60 K (60 °C ambient-in-frame → 120 °C board), works out as follows:

$$4 \times 2.3\,I^2 \times 2.04\,\text{m}\Omega \cdot 5 \le 60 \Rightarrow I_{cont} \approx 28\text{–}30\ \text{A/motor}$$

The 2.3 factor approximates switching overhead, and 2.04 mΩ is FET plus copper at temperature.

### 3.2 Burst (adiabatic lumped capacitance)

**Board heat capacity:**
- FR4: 0.64 cm³ × 1.7 J/cm³K ≈ 1.1 J/K
- Copper: 6 layers × 70 µm × 4 cm² ≈ 0.17 cm³ × 3.45 J/cm³K ≈ 0.58 J/K
- FETs, caps and solder: ≈ 0.3 J/K
- **C_th ≈ 2.0 J/K**

**Board temperature, first-order:**

$$T_b(t) = T_{amb} + P R_\theta \left(1 - e^{-t/(R_\theta C_{th})}\right), \quad \tau = 5\cdot 2.0 = 10\ \text{s}$$

**Junction temperature** is the board temperature plus the FET transient: T_j = T_b + P_FET · Z_θJC(t). Take Z_θJC(≥100 ms) ≈ 1 K/W and P_FET,peak = 9.2 W, giving +9 K.

**Limits:** T_j ≤ 125 °C with derating, starting from T_b = 50 °C.

| Scenario | P_total | Board limit | Max burst time |
|---|---|---|---|
| All 4 motors at 80 A | 96 W | 116 °C | t = −τ·ln(1 − 66/480) ≈ **1.5 s** |
| 1 motor at 80 A, others 20 A | 24 + 3×1.9 ≈ 30 W | 116 °C | **≈ 2.6 s** to +66 K (asymptote 150 K) |
| All 4 motors at 60 A | 55 W | 116 °C | **≈ 2.8 s** |

**Spec line:** *"80 A burst, ≤1.5 s, all channels simultaneous; 30 A continuous with airflow."*

Firmware must enforce this limit. Configure the AM32 current limit plus temperature cutback, and set the ArduPilot `BATT_*` current limits accordingly.
