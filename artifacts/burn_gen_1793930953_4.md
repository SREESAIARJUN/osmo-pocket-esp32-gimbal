# STM32H743 AIO Nano Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture, Loss Model, and DShot DMA Plan (Job #23)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Headline Results

**Architecture.** The H743 does not commutate the motors directly. It sends bidirectional DShot600 to four independent ESC MCUs (AT32F421 running AM32). Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and 6 N-MOSFETs at 48 kHz complementary PWM.

**Design point.**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max (worst case for switching) |
| Burst current | 80 A per ESC (phase DC-link equivalent), duration-limited |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), dead time 100 ns |
| Board | 30.5 × 30.5 mm (25.5 mm "nano" variant noted), 8-layer, 2 oz inner / 3 oz outer power layers |
