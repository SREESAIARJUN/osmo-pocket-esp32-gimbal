## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α_R (T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 1.5 × 1.4675 ≈ **2.20 mΩ**

### 1.2 Six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous (complementary) PWM

In each 60° sector, current flows through one high-side leg and one low-side leg.

- **Switched leg:** the HS conducts for D·T. The complementary LS conducts for (1−D)·T, minus the dead time.
- **Fixed leg:** the LS conducts for 100 % of the sector.

So the instantaneous conduction path is always two FETs in series, independent of D:

P_cond,ESC = I² · [2·R_ds(T_j) + R_cu]

### 1.3 At 80 A, T_j = 110 °C

P_cond = 6400 × (4.40 + 0.40) mΩ = **30.7 W per ESC channel**

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution

Over one electrical cycle, each of the 6 FETs carries current for about 1/3 of the time.

- Average: P_FET,avg ≈ 30.7 / 6 ≈ **5.1 W**, including about 0.4 W of the copper share.
- Instantaneous during the conducting interval: I²·R_ds = **14.1 W**

The instantaneous figure matters only if the electrical period exceeds about 10 % of the FET's thermal time constant. At burst eRPM (>1 kHz electrical) it does not, so use the average.

### 1.5 Self-heating fixed point (thermal runaway check)

T_j = T_a + R_θ · P(T_j), with P(T_j) = k·[1 + α_R (T_j − 25)]

Solving gives:

T_j = [T_a + R_θ·k·(1 − 25α_R)] / (1 − R_θ·k·α_R)

The solution exists only if **R_θ · k · α_R < 1**. Here k is the 25 °C per-FET loss (about 3.6 W). This gives:

- Runaway limit: R_θ < 50 K/W. This is comfortably met locally.
- The board-level constraint in §3 is the binding one.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the switched leg's FET pair takes switching loss. Per ESC, the terms are:

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw | ½ · 25.2 · 80 · 30 ns · 48 k = **1.45 W** |
| Output capacitance | Q_oss · V · f_sw | 40 n · 25.2 · 48 k = **0.05 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw | **0.05 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f · I · 2·t_dt · f_sw | 0.8 · 80 · 200 n · 48 k = **0.61 W** |
| Gate charge (2 FETs, mostly dissipated in the driver) | 2 · Q_g · V_GS · f_sw | **0.05 W** |
| **Total P_sw** | | **≈ 2.2 W** |

**Takeaways:**

- At 48 kHz, switching loss is only about 7 % of conduction loss. Conduction dominates by roughly 14:1.
- Dead time is the largest controllable switching term. Cutting t_dt from 100 ns to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W per ESC.
- Going from 48 kHz to 24 kHz saves about 1.1 W per ESC. That is worth less than a 0.2 mΩ improvement in R_ds, so **pick FETs for R_ds, not Q_g**.

**Totals at 80 A burst:**

- Per ESC: 30.7 + 2.2 ≈ **32.9 W**
- Four motors: **≈ 132 W**
- Add the H743 (~0.5 W), BEC (~1–2 W), and ESC MCUs (~0.2 W): **≈ 135 W board dissipation**
