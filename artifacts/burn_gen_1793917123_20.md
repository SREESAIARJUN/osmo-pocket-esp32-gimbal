## 0. Architectural Clarification (Read First)

ArduPilot on the H743 **does not commutate motors**. A correct "H743 + 4-in-1" stack is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir x4──► 4x ESC MCUs (AM32/BLHeli_32, e.g. AT32F421 / STM32G071)
                                               └─► 4x 3-phase gate driver ─► 24x N-MOSFET
```

The H743's job is DShot generation, eRPM capture (for harmonic notch filtering), and current/voltage telemetry. The 48 kHz PWM and commutation run on the ESC MCUs. The model below covers both domains.

**Feasibility warning:** "Nano" (20×20 mm mount, ~26×26 mm board) with 80 A burst on *all four* channels is thermally limited to single-digit seconds (see §2.5). Treat 80 A as a per-channel burst rating, not a simultaneous rating.
