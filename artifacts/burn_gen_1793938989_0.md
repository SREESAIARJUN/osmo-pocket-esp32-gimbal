# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion

I don't have the Job #51 hardware spec, so this design is parametric. Every number below is a placeholder default. Replace it with your measured motor, encoder and frame data before closing any loop on hardware.

## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms
Use the amplitude-invariant Clarke transform with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = pp·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

Inverse Park is the transpose of the same matrix.

### 1.2 Plant (surface PMSM, L_d ≈ L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\psi_m
$$

Electromagnetic torque:

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32\,pp\,\psi_m\,i_q \equiv K_t\, i_q
$$
