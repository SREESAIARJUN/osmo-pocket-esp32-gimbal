# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual ICM-42688-P Vibration Rejection

I don't have the Job #46 specification, so this design uses the assumptions in §0. They are typical for a 3-axis camera gimbal. Every constant is a named parameter, so you can substitute your real values.

## 0. Baseline Assumptions

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class (FPU, ≥170 MHz) |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5–10 Ω, L ≈ 2–4 mH |
| Encoders | 14-bit magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701), one per joint |
| Joint order | Yaw (outer) → Roll → Pitch (inner, camera) |
| IMU A | ICM-42688-P on the camera plate (attitude / stabilization) |
| IMU B | ICM-42688-P on the handle/base (vibration reference, base-motion feedforward) |
| Loop rates | Current 20 kHz · Velocity 4 kHz · Angle/attitude 1 kHz · IMU ODR 8 kHz |

The two IMUs play different roles. IMU A measures the controlled payload. IMU B measures the disturbance source, which makes it an ideal reference for adaptive cancellation and for base-rate feedforward.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms

The power-invariant amplitude form, with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.3 Plant model (rotor dq frame)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

Torque is $\tau = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q$, using the $i_d = 0$ strategy for an SPM motor.

### 1.4 PI current controllers with pole-zero cancellation

For a desired closed-loop bandwidth $\omega_c$ (typically $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/10$, e.g. $2\pi\cdot 1.5\,$kHz):

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \frac{i_q}{i_q^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

The discrete form uses back-calculation anti-windup:

$$
u_k = K_p e_k + I_k + v_{ff},\quad u^{sat}_k=\text{sat}(u_k),\quad
I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw}(u^{sat}_k - u_k)
$$

with $K_{aw} \approx 1/K_p \cdot K_i T_s$ or simply 1 on a scaled integrator.

The decoupling feedforward is:

$$
v_{d,ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_{q,ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

The voltage vector is limited to the linear SVPWM hexagon-inscribed circle, $|v_{dq}| \le V_{dc}/\sqrt3$, with d-axis priority.
