Three points change the architecture and need correcting up front:

1. **The H743 does not generate the 48 kHz PWM.** In every production 4-in-1 design, each channel has its own ESC MCU (STM32G071 or AT32F421) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU does the commutation and the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to each ESC MCU. The model below follows this split.
2. **80 A burst on all four channels cannot fit a "nano" 20×20 mm board.** As shown in §3, roughly 90 W of loss would heat it beyond FET limits in about one second. This spec requires at least a 30.5×30.5 mm board, 8-layer 2 oz copper, and an airflow assumption. Alternatively, reduce the spec to "80 A burst on one channel" or "55 A burst on all four."
3. **"Job #23" carries no context I can see.** Everything here is derived from first principles.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R_ds = 1.0 × 1.6 = **1.6 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current paths in a trapezoidal six-step drive with synchronous rectification

During any 60° sector:
- One leg PWMs: its high-side (HS) conducts for D, its low-side (LS) for (1−D).
- One leg's LS is held on continuously.
- The third leg floats.

So the phase current always passes through exactly **two R_ds(on) in series**, regardless of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.3 Ripple correction

Ripple adds a small term:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{dc}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12},\qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

For a typical 5″/7″ motor with L_ph ≈ 15 µH, V_bus − V_bemf ≈ 4 V, D ≈ 0.85:

ΔI = 4 × 0.85 / (15e-6 × 48e3) ≈ 4.7 A, so the added term is ΔI²/12 ≈ 1.8 A² and negligible.

### 1.4 Results

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Per channel, T_j = 125 °C | P_cond = 80² × 2 × 1.6 mΩ = **20.5 W** |
| Per channel, T_j = 25 °C | 12.8 W |
| Copper/plane loss (assume 0.4 mΩ effective path per channel) | 80² × 0.4 mΩ = **2.6 W** per channel |
| Per-FET average | Each FET carries current about 1/3 of the electrical cycle: P_FET ≈ P_cond,ch / 6 ≈ **3.4 W** |
| Per-FET instantaneous | 80² × 1.6 mΩ = 10.2 W |

The per-FET average is valid because the electrical frequency (≥ 500 Hz at speed) is well above 1/τ_die (τ_die ≈ 1–3 ms).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches in each sector. Hard switching occurs on the HS turn-on and turn-off; the LS is soft-switched after its body diode conducts.

### 2.1 Voltage-current overlap

$$t_{r,f} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_g} + \frac{Q_{gs2}}{I_g} \approx \frac{10\,\text{nC}}{1.5\,\text{A}} + \text{ringing margin} \approx 15\ \text{ns each}$$
