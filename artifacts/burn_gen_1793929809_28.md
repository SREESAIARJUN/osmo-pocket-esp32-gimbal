# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture & Mathematical Model (Job #19)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Design Flags

### Architecture assumption

An STM32H743 cannot practically run four sensorless 6-step/FOC motor loops plus ArduPilot. The architecture is therefore:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──BDShot600 ×4──► 4× AT32F421 (AM32 firmware) ──► 4× FD6288Q gate drivers ──► 24 half-bridges (48 N-FETs)
```

The 48 kHz PWM is set in AM32. The H743 owns the DShot/telemetry timing.

### Engineering flags (read first)

1. **80 A per channel is a burst rating only.** The model below shows ≈120 W total dissipation at 4×80 A. No nano-format board can hold that continuously. The burst is time-limited to about 1–2 s.
2. **"Nano" form factor.** Forty-eight MOSFETs plus an H743 does not fit on 20×20 mm at an honest 80 A burst. Assume a 30.5×30.5 mm board (≈36×36 mm outline), 8-layer, 2 oz on outer/power layers. A 20×20 variant would need roughly a 45 A burst derating.
3. **MOSFET parameters are representative, not from a specific datasheet.** Substitute your selected part's values into the equations; the model is fully parametric.
