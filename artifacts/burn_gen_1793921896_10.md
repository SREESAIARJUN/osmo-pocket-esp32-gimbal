# STM32H743 AIO FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Nano): Power-Stage Model, Thermal Budget, and Bi-Directional DShot Allocation

**Basis:** I don't have the Job #58 BOM, stackup, or enclosure data. Every value below comes from a stated, labeled assumption, so you can substitute the real datasheet numbers and re-run the equations. The first conclusion matters most, so I've put it up front:

> **Feasibility flag:** On a nano-class board (~25 × 25 mm), 80 A per phase on all four channels simultaneously dissipates about **95 W** and is **only survivable for ~2 seconds**. At full throttle it would also draw about **320 A from the battery**, which no nano power entry can carry. Treat "80 A burst" as a short, single-or-dual-motor transient rating, enforced by firmware current limiting. Do not treat it as a continuous or all-motor spec.

## 2. Conduction-Loss Model

### 2.1 Per-leg derivation (6-step, complementary PWM)

Over one electrical period, each half-bridge leg spends time in three states:

- **120° as the PWM leg.** Current flows through the high side for duty D and through the low side (synchronous rectification) for 1−D. The leg therefore carries I for the full 120°.
- **120° as the sink leg.** The opposite-polarity switch is held fully on and carries I.
- **120° floating.** No current (BEMF sensing).

The leg therefore conducts I for 2/3 of the period, independent of D:

$$
P_{cond,leg} = \tfrac{2}{3}\, I^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = k_T R_{25}
$$

Per-device split, worst case on the low side at low duty:

$$
P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R\,(D + 1)\cdot\tfrac{1}{2}\ \text{(polarity-averaged)}, \qquad P_{HS} + P_{LS} = P_{cond,leg}
$$

### 2.2 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

$$
R = 1.6\ \text{mΩ}, \qquad I^2 R = 6400 \times 1.6\times10^{-3} = 10.24\ \text{W}
$$

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Per leg | 2/3 × 10.24 = **6.83 W** |
| Per ESC channel (3 legs) | **20.5 W** |
| All 4 channels | **81.9 W** |
| Hottest single FET | ≈ 3.5–4 W |

### 2.3 Copper loss (do not omit)

A phase trace or plane with 0.3 mΩ round trip at 80 A adds 1.9 W per active path:

$$
P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{trace}\cdot\tfrac{2}{3}\cdot 3 \approx 3.8\ \text{W per channel}
$$

Use 2 oz or heavier power layers, stitched in parallel.

## 3. Switching-Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches, so there is one switching node per channel at any instant.

### 3.1 Transition times (gate-charge method)

$$
t_{on} = \frac{(Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd})\,R_{g,on}}{V_{drv}-V_{pl}} = \frac{15\,\text{nC}\times10}{5.5} \approx 27\ \text{ns}
$$

$$
t_{off} = \frac{(Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd})\,R_{g,off}}{V_{pl}} = \frac{15\,\text{nC}\times 5}{4.5} \approx 17\ \text{ns}
$$

### 3.2 Loss components per channel

| Mechanism | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_on + t_off)·f = ½·25.2·80·44 ns·48 k | **2.13 W** |
| C_oss discharge | ½·C_oss,eff·V²·f | 0.02 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f·I·2t_dt·f = 0.8·80·200 ns·48 k | **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f | 0.06 W |
| Gate drive (lands in driver/R_g, 2 FETs) | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f | 0.05 W |
| **Per-channel total** | | **≈ 2.9 W** |
| **4 channels** | | **≈ 11.5 W** |
