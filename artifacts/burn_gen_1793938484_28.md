# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #1)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Two Architecture Flags

The request bundles three subsystems, so I fixed the interfaces first.

**Flag 1: The H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** In a 4-in-1 ESC stack, the H743 sends DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (AT32F421, STM32G071 or EFM8, running AM32, BLHeli_32 or Bluejay). The 48 kHz PWM, dead-time and commutation are generated by those MCUs. The model below is therefore split:
- **FC domain (H743):** DShot600 bidirectional timers and DMA (Sections 4 and 5).
- **ESC domain:** conduction, switching and thermal behaviour (Sections 1 to 3).

**Flag 2: "Nano" and 80 A burst conflict.** A 20×20 mm board cannot physically carry 80 A per phase; copper cross-section and thermal mass are both insufficient. I assume a **30.5×30.5 mm ESC footprint (≈36×36 mm board), 8-layer, 2 oz copper**, stacked under a compact H743 FC. If 20×20 is mandatory, derate to about 45 A burst. Section 3 shows the math behind this.
