## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Initial Feasibility Finding

### 0.1 Architecture clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. A production 4-in-1 ESC uses one small MCU per channel (AT32F421 / STM32G071 class running AM32 or BLHeli_32), each driving three half-bridges through a gate driver. This document models:

- **FC domain:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot, which generates bidirectional DShot600.
- **ESC domain:** 4× ESC MCU, 4× 3-phase gate driver, 24× N-MOSFET, with PWM at 48 kHz.

### 0.3 Feasibility finding (read first)

A "nano" form factor (20×20 mm mounting, ~25×25 mm board) **cannot thermally sustain 4 × 80 A bursts**. The math in §3 shows that even a 30.5×30.5 board (~36×36 mm) reaches the MOSFET thermal limit in roughly 10 s with only propwash cooling.

Viable options:

- **(a)** Rate the nano board at 4 × 40–45 A burst.
- **(b)** Move to a 30.5 mm board with 6–8 layers of 2 oz copper.
- **(c)** Keep 80 A with a burst limit of 3–5 s, enforced in ESC firmware.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

With 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation and synchronous rectification (complementary PWM), at any instant two phases conduct:

- **PWM phase leg:** the high-side conducts for D·T and the low-side for (1−D)·T. Exactly one FET of that leg carries I at every instant.
- **Return leg:** the low-side is held on for the full 60° sector.

So at every instant the current passes through **two R_ds(on) in series**, independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Ripple correction

Phase inductance L_ph is about 15 µH for a typical 5" motor. The peak-to-peak ripple is worst at D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus} D(1-D)}{L_{ph} f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{15\mu \times 48k} = 8.75\ \text{A}_{pp}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6400 + 6.4 = 6406\ \text{A}^2$$

The ripple contribution is 0.1%, so it can be neglected.

### 1.3 Temperature dependence (electro-thermal fixed point)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}[1 + \alpha_R (T_j - 25)]$$

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per channel |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 0.80 mΩ | 10.25 W |
| 100 °C | 1.19 mΩ | 15.25 W |
| 125 °C | 1.32 mΩ | 16.9 W |

Use **T_j = 125 °C** for burst design: **P_cond ≈ 16.9 W per channel**. Each conducting FET dissipates I²R ≈ 8.45 W while on.
