## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

The motor runs in an electrical frame that turns p times per mechanical revolution:

$$\theta_e = p(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi$$

where θ_off is the encoder offset found during calibration.

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park transform** (stationary frame to rotor frame):

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park transform** (rotor frame back to stationary frame):

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Motor Model and Current PI Controller

The surface-magnet PMSM equations (L_d = L_q = L) are:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

The electromagnetic torque is:

$$\tau = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q$$

**Decoupling.** Feedforward voltages cancel the cross-coupling and back-EMF terms:

$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

**Gain selection.** With decoupling in place, each axis behaves as the plant 1/(Ls+R). Choosing the PI gains so the controller zero cancels the plant pole gives:

$$K_p^i = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i^i = R\,\omega_c$$
