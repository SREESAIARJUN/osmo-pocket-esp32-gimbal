## 0. Feasibility Findings

Two constraints shape the rest of this design.

1. **The H743 cannot commutate the motors under ArduPilot.** ArduPilot is a flight controller stack and has no BLDC commutation firmware. The 4-in-1 power stage therefore needs four ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. This design uses 4× AT32F421K8U7, chosen because AM32 supports it and it handles 48 kHz PWM natively. The H743 communicates with these MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. The 48 kHz switching analysis applies to the ESC side.

2. **80 A per channel cannot be sustained on a nano (≤30×30 mm) board.** The model below shows roughly 90 W of total power-stage dissipation at 4×80 A. On a board of about 5 J/K thermal mass, that means a burst budget of about 2–3 s. The board must be specified as **80 A burst (≤2 s, ≥10 s recovery), approximately 35–40 A continuous with prop-wash airflow**. Section 3 derives the exact limits.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

```
R_ds(Tj) = R_ds25 · [1 + α·(Tj − 25)]
At Tj = 125 °C (max R_ds25 = 1.5 mΩ):  R_ds = 1.5 · (1 + 0.45) = 2.18 mΩ
At Tj = 100 °C (typ R_ds25 = 1.2 mΩ):  R_ds = 1.2 · 1.3375   = 1.61 mΩ
```
