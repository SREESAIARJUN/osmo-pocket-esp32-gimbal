## Electrical, Thermal and BDShot DMA Design Model (Rev A)

## 0. Scope and Constraints

These constraints shape every number below.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the STM32H743 flight controller. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32-class firmware). The 48 kHz PWM is an ESC-firmware parameter. The H743 only produces and decodes bidirectional DShot600. Running 4× sensorless FOC/6-step on the H743 alongside ArduPilot is not supported by ArduPilot.
2. **80A is a burst rating only.** Four channels at 80A means about 320A of phase current on a nano-class PCB. The thermal analysis in §5 shows this survives a few seconds, not continuously. Steady-state cooling cannot sustain 80A on any 20×20/26×26 mm board.
3. **Component parameters are representative, not authoritative.** They describe a class of 40V, ~1 mΩ, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFETs. Re-run the model with the selected part's datasheet curves before layout freeze.
