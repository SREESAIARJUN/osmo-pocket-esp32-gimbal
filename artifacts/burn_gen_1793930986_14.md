# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

**Assumptions** (I don't have the Job #2 spec, so adjust these to your hardware):

* Gimbal BLDC motors: 12N14P class (pole pairs `p = 7`). Each has a 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder and in-line or low-side current sensing.
* Yaw → Roll → Pitch joint order, from the base to the camera.
* Two ICM-42688-P IMUs on the camera plate, read over SPI at 8 kHz ODR.
* Single MCU (STM32G4/H7 class) with an FPU.
