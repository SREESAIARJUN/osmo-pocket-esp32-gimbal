# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #65): Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, and Bidirectional DShot600 Architecture

## 0. Architecture Constraints and Review Flags

**Flag 1: The H743 cannot be the ESC commutation MCU.** AM32 and BLHeli_32 do not run on the H7, and bidirectional DShot requires an independent MCU on each motor. The correct partition is:

- **STM32H743 (ArduPilot)** runs the flight controller and generates 4× bidirectional DShot600.
- **4× ESC MCUs** (e.g. AT32F421 running AM32) handle 48 kHz complementary PWM, commutation, and eRPM telemetry.

**Flag 2: 80 A per motor is not survivable on a nano board.** On a nano (20×20 mm mount, roughly 25×25 mm PCB), 80 A per motor means 320 A from the battery. The math in §2 shows this is a burst of about 1–3 s at most. Connector and battery-lead limits are exceeded first; an XT60 is rated around 60 A continuous. The model below is computed for both readings of the requirement:

- **Case A:** 80 A per motor.
- **Case B:** 80 A total, i.e. 20 A per motor.

Engineering sign-off should pick one.

## 1. Electrical Loss Model

### 1.1 Conduction topology

The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification. At any instant two phases carry the full phase current I:

- One leg alternates between its high-side FET (duty D) and its low-side FET (1−D).
- The other leg's low-side FET conducts continuously.

Two R_ds(on) values are therefore always in series with I:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

Averaged over six FETs, each FET carries P_cond / 6 = I²R/3. The instantaneous hot pair, however, carries I²R each for its 120° conduction window, which matters for the transient analysis in §2.
