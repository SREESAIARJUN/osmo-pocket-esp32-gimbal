# Job #19: STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC), 80 A Burst Power Stage

**Design Basis, Loss Model, Thermal Model, DShot DMA Plan and Netlists (Rev A)**

## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Red Flags

Three findings change the spec. They are listed before the math because they constrain everything that follows.

| # | Issue | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production AIO, the H743 runs ArduPilot (the flight controller). Each motor channel is driven by a dedicated ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071 class) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Bidirectional DShot600 is the link between the H743 and those four ESC MCUs. | The model uses this partition. |
| 2 | **"Nano" (20×20 mm) cannot carry 4×80 A.** Twenty-four power FETs plus drivers will not fit, and the copper cross-section is far too small. | Baseline is a **30.5×30.5 mm** board, 6-layer, 2 oz outer / 2 oz inner power planes. A true 20×20 board is limited to roughly 35–45 A burst per channel. |
| 3 | **"80 A burst" must have a duration and a simultaneity definition.** Four channels at 80 A is 320 A from the battery. An XT60 is rated about 60 A continuous. | The model is computed for the worst case (4 channels simultaneous).
