# Job #38: STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 80A-Burst 4-in-1 ESC

**System Architecture, Loss Model, DShot/DMA Allocation, Netlist, and Thermal Analysis (Rev A)**

> **About the numbers.** No MOSFET, driver or stack-up has been frozen, so this analysis uses a *representative* 40 V / 3.3×3.3 mm MOSFET class and a 30×30 mm stack-up. Every equation is parametric. Replace the bracketed `[assumed]` values with datasheet figures before design review. Where the analysis exposes a physical limit, that limit is stated plainly.

## 0. Executive Findings

1. **The STM32H743 does not switch the FETs.** An AIO board has two processor domains:
   - The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600.
   - Four dedicated ESC MCUs (AM32-class, e.g. AT32F421) run the 48 kHz commutation PWM.

   The loss model below therefore lives in the ESC domain. The DMA/timer model lives in the H743 domain.
2. **80 A is strictly a transient rating on a nano board.**
   - All four channels at 80 A dissipate about **115 W**.
   - A 30×30 mm board in prop-wash sustains roughly **8 W** continuously.
   - Predicted all-motor burst time from 40 °C is **about 1.5 s**.
   - Continuous capability is **about 20–23 A per channel** without a heatsink.
3. **The 40 V FET margin on 6S is thin.** Turn-off di/dt of about 10 A/ns across 1 nH of loop inductance produces about 35 V peak. This requires either a hard ≤1 nH power-loop layout plus snubbers, or a slower turn-off gate resistor (which trades Vds spike against switching loss; §2.3).
