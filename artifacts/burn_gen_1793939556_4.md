## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step with complementary PWM

At any commutation step, phase A is PWM-switched and phase B is held low:

- **On-time (D):** current flows Q_AH → motor → Q_BL
- **Off-time (1−D):** current freewheels through Q_AL (synchronous) → motor → Q_BL

Two FETs carry the full phase current *I* at every instant. This holds regardless of duty, apart from dead-time, which is handled in §2.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_J)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_J) = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_J)$$

$$R_{DS}(T_J) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_J - 25)]$$

### 1.2 Per-FET distribution

Across a full electrical cycle each FET conducts for 1/3 of the time on average (120° conduction). Within a step, however, the static low-side FET carries 100% and the PWM pair splits D / (1−D).

**Worst-case instantaneous FET** (the static low side during its 60° step):
$$P_{FET,peak} = I^2 R_{DS}$$

**Cycle-averaged per FET:**
$$\bar{P}_{FET} = \frac{2 I^2 R_{DS}}{6} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}}{3}$$

At eRPM ≥ ~20k, the commutation period (< 0.5 ms) is far shorter than the FET thermal time constant (~10–50 ms). Junction temperature therefore follows the **averaged** loss, with a small ripple.
