## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1 design, and in ArduPilot's model, the H743 is the flight controller. It sends bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs (STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32/BLHeli_32), which drive the gate drivers. ArduPilot contains no ESC commutation firmware, so the design must keep this split.
