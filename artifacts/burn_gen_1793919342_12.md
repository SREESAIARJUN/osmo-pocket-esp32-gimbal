## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Feasibility Flags

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and does not commutate motors. Each of the four ESC channels has its own dedicated MCU running AM32 (BLHeli_32-class firmware). The H743 sends DShot600 to each ESC MCU and receives bidirectional eRPM telemetry back. The 48 kHz figure is therefore the ESC-MCU PWM frequency, not anything the H743 generates.

**Feasibility flags:**

1. **"Nano" footprint vs. 80 A.** A 20×20 mm board cannot carry 4×80 A even transiently. The minimum credible footprint is 30.5×30.5 mm mounting (about 36×36 mm board). The analysis below uses 36×36 mm.
2. **"80 A burst" is a time-limited rating, not a thermal steady state.** Section 3 shows that all four channels at 80 A simultaneously is good for about 2 s. A single channel at 80 A is good for about 8–10 s.
3. **Supply current.** At full duty, 4×80 A phase current means roughly 320 A of battery current. Neither an XT60 nor any nano-class pack supports this. The realistic simultaneous rating is about 4×45–55 A, with an 80 A per-channel burst.
4. **Component values.** All component values are representative. Re-derive them against the specific MOSFET and driver datasheets you select.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α (T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 125 °C:

R_ds = 1.8 mΩ × 1.55 ≈ **2.8 mΩ**

### 1.2 Current path

With complementary PWM in 6-step commutation, motor current always flows through exactly two MOSFETs:

- During the PWM ON interval: HS(phase A) and LS(phase B).
- During the PWM OFF interval (synchronous freewheel): LS(phase A) and LS(phase B).

The ON/OFF split only changes which devices share the loss, not the total. The total conduction loss per ESC channel is therefore:

**P_cond = 2 · I_ph² · R_ds(T_j)**
