# STM32H743 Nano AIO: 4-in-1 ESC Power Stage and ArduPilot BDShot Design Package (Job #25)

## 0. Architectural Clarification

The STM32H743 cannot practically commutate four motors itself. ArduPilot has no ESC firmware, and 4× six-step control plus BDShot timing would exhaust its advanced timers. The design is therefore partitioned as follows:

| Block | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (400 MHz) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) |
| ESC MCU ×4 | STM32G071GBU6 or AT32F421K8U7 | AM32, 48 kHz PWM, BDShot enabled |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) | n/a |
| MOSFET ×24 | 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN, ~1.2 mΩ @ 25 °C, VGS = 10 V | n/a |

The FC-to-ESC link is bidirectional DShot600 on four single-wire lines.

**Form-factor caveat:** "Nano" here means a 20×20 mm mount on a ~25×25 mm PCB. I derive below that 80 A per channel is a **~2 s burst rating** on this board. It is not a continuous rating, and the numbers make that unavoidable.

All component values are design assumptions. Replace them with the datasheet values of the parts actually procured and re-run the equations.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Six-step synchronous topology

During each 60° commutation sector, one phase is PWM'd with complementary switching (HS for D, LS for 1−D). A second phase has its LS held fully on, and the third phase floats.

Motor current therefore always flows through exactly two conducting channels in series, ignoring dead time:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

This holds for any duty cycle D. With synchronous rectification, conduction loss is independent of throttle at a given phase current.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0067\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

This gives ≈1.5× at 100 °C, which is typical for trench MOSFETs.

| Tj (°C) | RDS(on) (mΩ) | P_cond per channel @ 80 A (W) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | 1.20 | 15.4 |
| 75 | 1.60 | 20.5 |
| 100 | 1.80 | 23.0 |
| 125 | 2.00 | 25.6 |

### 1.3 Copper loss

The sheet resistance of 2 oz (70 µm) copper is:

$$R_\square = \frac{\rho}{t} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}}{70\times10^{-6}} = 0.246\ \text{m}\Omega/\square$$

Assume each phase path is ~4 squares, paralleled on 4 layers, plus pad and via resistance. That gives R_cu ≈ 0.25 mΩ per path, with 2 paths in series:

$$P_{cu,ch} = 80^2 \times 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega = 3.2\ \text{W}$$

The current-sense shunt (0.25 mΩ, shared on V_bat−) is accounted for in §4.
