# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 80A ESC (Job #34): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Allocation, and Netlist

## 0. Feasibility Finding

**An 80A burst per channel on a nano-class (20×20 mm) board is a time-limited transient, not a rating.** On this footprint the board can only survive 80A for about 1–2 s before reaching its thermal limit. Continuous capability is roughly 20–25A per channel at moderate airflow. The rest of this document derives those numbers so the burst rating can be written into the datasheet honestly, as something like "80A / 1 s, 25A continuous."

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-Dependent Resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_0\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 100) = **1.45 mΩ**

### 1.2 Six-Step Commutation with Synchronous Rectification

During every 60° sector, current flows through exactly two legs (phase A in, phase B out).

- With complementary PWM, the PWM'd leg conducts through its high-side switch for D·T and its low-side switch for (1−D)·T. Either way, current always sees one R_DS.
- The other leg is static-on.

So, independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Each phase conducts 240° of 360° (2/3 of the time), split between its high and low side:

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}\ \text{(on average across the 6 FETs)}$$

The worst-case FET is the static-on low side, which carries a 1/3 share at full current:

$$P_{LS,worst} = \frac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS}$$

### 1.3 Results at 80A Burst, T_j = 125 °C

| Quantity | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Per channel | 2 × 80² × 1.45 mΩ | **18.6 W** |
| Worst single FET | 80² × 1.45 mΩ / 3 | **3.1 W** |
| 4 channels | 4 × 18.6 | **74.2 W** |

### 1.4 Electro-Thermal Feedback

Because R_DS rises with T_j, the loss feeds back on itself:

$$P(T) = P_{25}[1+\alpha(T-25)], \qquad T = T_a + R_{th}P(T)$$

Solving gives:

$$T_j = \frac{T_a + R_{th}P_{25}(1 - 25\alpha)}{1 - \alpha R_{th} P_{25}}$$

**Thermal runaway occurs if α · R_th · P₂₅ ≥ 1.** With α = 0.0045, the board is runaway-safe only if R_th · P₂₅ < 222 K.

At 80A, P₂₅ (4 channels) = 51.2 W, so the board-to-air R_th must stay below **4.3 K/W**. A 20×20 board in prop wash is about 12 K/W (see §3), so **80A continuous is unconditionally unstable.** The burst must be time-limited by firmware.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

In six-step, only one half-bridge per channel switches at any time. Per channel:

### 2.1 Hard-Switching (V-I Overlap)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

### 2.2 Dead-Time Body-Diode Conduction

Two dead-time intervals per PWM period:

$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse Recovery

Body diodes are usually soft at this voltage. With Q_rr ≈ 40 nC:

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.05\ W}$$
