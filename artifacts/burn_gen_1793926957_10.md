## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and does **not** commutate the motors directly. A production-grade 4-in-1 uses:

| Domain | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot, sensors, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| ESC ×4 | STM32G071 / AT32F421 class MCU running AM32 | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive ×4 | 3-phase half-bridge driver with bootstrap | 6 FETs per channel |
| Power stage | 24 × N-MOSFET, 40 V | 6S max (25.2 V) |

**Board assumption:** 30.5 × 30.5 mm mounting pattern, 36 × 36 mm outline, 8-layer, 2 oz copper on all layers, FETs on both sides.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step Trapezoidal, Synchronous Rectification)

### 2.1 Topology Basis
In any of the 6 commutation sectors, two phases conduct:
- The **PWM leg** alternates between its high-side FET (duty D) and low-side FET (1 − D) because of complementary PWM.
- The **return leg** conducts continuously through one FET.

At any instant, the current therefore passes through exactly **two R_ds(on) in series**.
