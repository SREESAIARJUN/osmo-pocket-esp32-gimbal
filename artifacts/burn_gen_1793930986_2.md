# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion

## 0. Design Basis and Assumptions

I don't have the Job #9 specification, so this design uses typical hardware for a 3-axis camera gimbal. Every parameter is a named constant, so you can substitute your own values without changing any equation.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 (170 MHz, single-precision FPU, CORDIC, HRTIM) |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R = 5.6 Ω, L = 2.1 mH, Kt ≈ 0.11 N·m/A |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (MT6701/AS5047P), SPI |
| Current sensing | 2-shunt low-side, sampled at the PWM valley |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI @ 24 MHz, ODR 8 kHz |
| Inverter | 20 kHz center-aligned PWM, V_bus = 16.8 V (4S) |

**Loop rate hierarchy (decimated from one PWM ISR):**

| Loop | Rate | Ts | Closed-loop bandwidth target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (d/q PI) | 20 kHz | 50 µs | ω_c = 2π·1500 rad/s |
| IMU fusion and filtering | 8 kHz | 125 µs | — |
| Rate (velocity) PI | 4 kHz | 250 µs | ω_v = 2π·120 rad/s |
| Attitude (angle) P/PI | 1 kHz | 1 ms | ω_θ = 2π·25 rad/s |

Each loop has about 5–12× bandwidth separation from the next, which keeps the cascade nearly decoupled.

**Gimbal-specific architecture:**
- The **encoder** is used for commutation, for the cogging table index, and as a fallback velocity source.
- The **camera-mounted gyros** close the rate loop.
- The **fused attitude** closes the angle loop.

This is the standard stabilization topology. Disturbances from base motion are rejected by the inertial rate loop rather than the joint loop.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

Here θ_offset is found during calibration by locking the rotor with i_d = I_align, i_q = 0, and reading the encoder.

**Latency compensation.** The encoder is sampled at t_k, but the PWM update applies about 1.5·T_s later (1 cycle for computation, 0.5 cycle for PWM averaging). Advance the angle accordingly:

$$
\theta_e^{\text{pwm}} = \theta_e + p\,\hat\omega_m\,(1.5\,T_s)
$$

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, 2 shunts)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.4 PMSM model in the dq frame

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_d &= R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q \\
v_q &= R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
\end{aligned}
\qquad
\tau = \tfrac{3}{2}p\,\lambda_m\, i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$
