# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal and DShot Design Model

Your request didn't specify the MOSFET, gate driver, supply voltage (cell count) or board size. This document uses a stated baseline, listed in §0, and keeps every equation parametric so you can substitute datasheet values.

The model leads to one conclusion you should see before reading further: **an 80 A per-channel burst on a nano-class 4-in-1 lasts seconds, not tens of seconds.** The thermal section quantifies this.
