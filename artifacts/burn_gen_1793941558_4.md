# STM32H743 AIO (4-in-1 ESC + FC) Nano: Electrothermal Model, DShot DMA Plan, and Netlist

## 0. Architecture and Feasibility

**0.1 Architecture.** The H743 runs ArduPilot only. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU. The H743 has only two advanced timers (TIM1, TIM8), which give 6 complementary pairs, but 4 motors need 12 bridge legs. Running back-EMF zero-cross commutation for four motors alongside the ArduPilot scheduler is also not viable. The design below is:

```
[H743 FC: ArduPilot] --4x bidir DShot600--> [4x AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 @ 48 kHz]
                                            --> 4x FD6288Q 3-phase gate drivers --> 24 MOSFETs
```

**0.2 Feasibility.** Section 2 shows that 80 A per channel on a 20×20 mm (nano) board is a **transient burst rating only**. It is limited by board thermal mass, not by the MOSFETs. If "80A" means total battery current, divide every loss figure by 16 (I² scaling at 20 A/channel). Confirm which interpretation applies before layout freeze.

**0.3 Parameters.** All values below are representative assumptions for a 30–40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFET (in the class of ~1.5 mΩ parts). Replace them with your chosen part's datasheet values.

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S fully charged |
| I_ph | 80 A | Burst phase current per channel |
| R_ds(on),25 | 1.5 mΩ | At V_gs = 10 V |
| k_T | 1.5 | R_ds(on) multiplier at T_j ≈ 125 °C |
| t_r + t_f | 35 ns | With ~1 A gate drive, Q_gd ≈ 8 nC |
| Q_g | 50 nC | Total gate charge |
| Q_rr | 40 nC | Body-diode reverse recovery charge |
| C_oss | 1.5 nF | Effective output capacitance |
| V_f | 0.8 V | Body diode forward voltage at 80 A |
| t_dt | 100 ns | Dead time per edge |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | PWM frequency |
| V_gs | 10 V | Gate drive voltage |

## 1. Loss Model (One ESC Channel, 6-Step, Synchronous Rectification)

In 6-step commutation with complementary PWM, two phases conduct at any instant. The current path always passes through one high-side-position and one low-side-position switch, so two R_ds(on) are always in series.

### 1.1 Conduction loss

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds(on),25}\,k_T = 2(80)^2(1.5\text{ m}\Omega)(1.5) = \mathbf{28.8\ W}$$

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle:
- Instantaneous dissipation while conducting: I²R = 14.4 W.
- Time-averaged dissipation per FET: P_cond / 6 ≈ 4.8 W.

**Copper and joint loss.** Assume 0.4 mΩ of trace, via, and solder resistance in the phase path:

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.4\text{ m}\Omega = 2.56\ \text{W}$$

### 1.2 Switching loss (hard-switched leg)

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw = 0.5·25.2·80·35 ns·48 kHz | 1.69 W |
| C_oss discharge | ½·C_oss·V²·f_sw | 0.023 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 0.048 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·200 ns·48 kHz | 0.61 W |
| Gate drive (2 switching FETs) | 2·Q_g·V_gs·f_sw | 0.048 W (dissipated in driver/R_g) |

$$P_{sw,total} \approx \mathbf{2.43\ W}$$

### 1.3 Channel and board totals

$$P_{ch} = 28.8 + 2.56 + 2.43 \approx \mathbf{33.8\ W}$$

$$P_{board} = 4 \times 33.8 = \mathbf{135\ W}\ \ \text{(all four motors at 80 A simultaneously)}$$

**Efficiency check.** Input power is 25.2 × 80 × D. At D = 1, that is 2016 W per channel, so η ≈ 98.3%. The loss is small relative to throughput but enormous relative to a 4 cm² board.

**Design lever.** Conduction loss dominates (85%). Raising f_sw from 24 to 48 kHz adds only about 1.15 W per channel. Halving R_ds(on), for example with paralleled FETs or a 5×6 package, saves about 14 W per channel. Spend board area on silicon, not on lower switching frequency.

### 1.4 DC-link capacitor ripple current

Ripple current peaks at D = 0.5:

$$I_{C,rms} = I_{ph}\sqrt{D(1-D)} = 80 \times 0.5 = \mathbf{40\ A_{rms}}\ \text{per channel}$$

This cannot be absorbed by local MLCCs alone. Use:
- 4× 10 µF/50 V X7R 1210 per channel, placed close to each bridge, plus
- an external 35 V/1000 µF low-ESR electrolytic on the battery pigtail.

## 2. Thermal Model

### 2.1 Steady state: not achievable at 80 A

A 20×20 mm board in prop wash has R_θ,board-amb ≈ 8–12 K/W. At 135 W this gives ΔT > 1000 K. **80 A per channel is not a continuous rating.**

Back-solving the continuous capability for ΔT = 60 K with R_θ = 10 K/W gives P_max = 6 W, or 1.5 W per channel:

$$I_{cont} \approx \sqrt{\frac{1.5}{2 \times 2.25\text{ m}\Omega}} \approx \mathbf{18\ A\ per\ channel}\ \ \text{(continuous, 6-layer 2 oz)}$$

### 2.2 Burst: adiabatic lumped-capacitance model

Board heat capacity (6 layers × 70 µm Cu at 70% fill, 1.6 mm FR-4):

| Element | Calculation | C_th |
|---|---|---|
| Copper | 117.6 mm³ → 1.05 g × 0.385 J/g·K | 0.41 J/K |

| Components (FETs, caps, MCUs) | — | ~0.5 J/K |
| **Effective total** | | **C_eff ≈ 1.55 J/K** |
