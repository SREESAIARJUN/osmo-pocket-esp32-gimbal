## 1. FOC Inner Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_c = −i_a − i_b):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park transform**, using electrical angle θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset):

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 PMSM voltage model and decoupled PI

The machine voltages in the rotating frame are:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

The controller adds cross-coupling and back-EMF feedforward terms to cancel the coupling:

$$v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

**Pole-zero cancellation gains.** The plant is 1/(Ls+R). Choosing the gains below makes the closed loop first-order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

With ω_c = 2π·1.5 kHz (below f_PWM/10), this gives K_p ≈ 19.8 V/A and K_i ≈ 52.8 kV/(A·s).
