# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #65): Architecture, Loss Model and Timer Allocation

**Revision A: engineering derivation. Values marked ⚠ are assumptions that must be checked against selected-part datasheets before layout freeze.**

---

## 0. Architectural Findings (Read First)

Three issues should be resolved at the design-review gate before any copper is committed.

| # | Finding | Severity | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | The STM32H743 should **not** commutate the motors directly. Four sensorless BLDC loops at 48 kHz, plus ArduPilot's scheduler, would require 24 complementary PWM outputs with dead-time and 12 comparator/ADC zero-cross paths. The H743 has only 2 advanced timers (TIM1/TIM8), which gives 6 complementary pairs, enough for 2 motors. | **Blocker** | Use a split architecture. The H743 FC sends bi-directional DShot600 to 4× ESC MCUs (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). Each ESC MCU generates its own 48 kHz PWM. |
| F2 | 80 A per channel on a "nano" (20×20 mm) footprint is not thermally sustainable. The analysis below shows about 70 W of total dissipation at full burst. | **High** | Specify 80 A as a **≤2 s burst** rating with an ~40 A continuous rating, or move to a 30.5×30.5 mm board with 5×6 mm FETs. |
| F3 | Single DFN3.3×3.3 FETs at 80 A approach clip/bond current limits for many parts. | Medium | Verify the "I_D package-limited" figure. Prefer 5×6 PowerPAK/LFPAK, or 2× parallel FETs per switch. |

The rest of this document models the board as specified, so the margins are visible quantitatively.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 0.85 mΩ × 1.45 ≈ **1.23 mΩ**.

### 2.2 Current path

In 6-step commutation, one phase is driven high and one is driven low at any instant. With synchronous rectification there are two states:

- **On-time (D):** HS(A) and LS(B) conduct.
- **Off-time (1−D):** LS(A) and LS(B) conduct (freewheel through the channel, not the diode).

In both states, exactly **two channels** carry I_ph:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$
