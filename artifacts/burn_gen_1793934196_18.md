## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does not drive the power stage directly. The correct partition for an ArduPilot AIO is:

| Domain | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100, 400–480 MHz) | ArduPilot. Generates 4× bidirectional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421 / STM32G071 class MCU per motor (AM32 / BLHeli_32) | Commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, dead-time, BEMF/zero-cross sensing |
| Gate drive | 3-phase half-bridge driver ×4 (e.g. FD6288Q class) | Bootstrap high-side drive, ~10–12 V Vgs |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET (6 per motor), 40 V class | Inverter |

The 48 kHz switching frequency belongs to the ESC MCUs. The H743 is concerned only with the DShot timing domain.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]$$

At the design point T_j = 100 °C:

$$R_{ds}(100) = 1.5 \times (1 + 0.006 \times 75) = 2.175\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 2.2 Six-step trapezoidal with synchronous rectification

During each commutation sector, two legs are active:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side conducts for D; the synchronous low-side conducts for (1 − D).
- **Return leg:** the low-side conducts for 100% of the sector.

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms}^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\left[D + (1-D) + 1\right] = 2\,I_{rms}^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Conduction loss is therefore **independent of throttle duty** at a given phase current, which is a useful property for the model.

### 2.3 Ripple correction

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{pk}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I \approx \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

For a typical 5″ motor (L_ph ≈ 15 µH line-to-line, D = 0.5, V_bemf ≈ 10 V):

$$\Delta I \approx \frac{15.2 \times 0.5}{15\times10^{-6} \times 48\,000} = 10.6\ \text{A}$$

The ripple term is ΔI²/12 ≈ 9.3 A², which is negligible against 6400 A² (+0.15%).

### 2.4 Copper and interconnect

Path resistance from the battery pad through the planes, MOSFET, and motor pad is R_cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ for 2 oz, 8-layer, with short paths. Of this, 2 × R_cu applies on the bus side and R_cu on the phase side.

$$P_{cu,motor} \approx I^2 \cdot R_{cu,path} \approx 6400 \times 0.3\times10^{-3} \approx 1.9\ \text{W}$$

### 2.5 Results at 80 A, T_j = 100 °C

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 6400 \times 2.175\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{27.8\ W}$$

| Device | Loss |
|---|---|
| Return-leg low-side FET (100%) | 13.9 W |
| PWM-leg HS + LS combined | 13.9 W (split D / 1 − D) |
