## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical Angle

With encoder count c (N_enc = 16384), mechanical offset θ_0, and direction sign s_d:

$$\theta_m = s_d\,\frac{2\pi c}{N_{enc}} - \theta_0,\qquad \theta_e = p\,\theta_m \bmod 2\pi$$

Latency compensation, applied to the PWM update half a period later:

$$\theta_e^{\,pwm} = \theta_e + \omega_e\,(1.5\,T_s)$$

### 1.2 Clarke and Park Transforms

Amplitude-invariant form, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.3 PMSM Model and Decoupled PI Control

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

PI with cross-coupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L\, i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L\, i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

**Gain selection by pole-zero cancellation.** For a target bandwidth ω_c (recommended 2π·1.5 kHz at 20 kHz, below f_s/10):

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c$$

This gives the closed loop $i/i^* = \omega_c/(s+\omega_c)$.
