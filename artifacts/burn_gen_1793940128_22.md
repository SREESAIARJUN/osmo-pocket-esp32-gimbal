## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Current path

In any 6-step sector, two phases conduct:
- **Leg A (PWM leg):** high-side is ON for D·T. The synchronous low-side is ON for (1−D)·T.
- **Leg B (return leg):** the low-side is ON for the whole sector.

So at every instant the current flows through exactly **two** MOSFET channels in series (ignoring dead-time, which is handled in §2.4):

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_J) + I_{ph}^2 \cdot R_{Cu}
$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_J) = R_{DS(on),25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_J - 25)]
$$

At T_J = 110 °C:

$$
R_{DS(on)} = 1.0\ \text{mΩ}\,[1 + 0.0045\cdot 85] = 1.38\ \text{mΩ}
$$
