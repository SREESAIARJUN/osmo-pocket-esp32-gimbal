## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\text{–}0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

From typical normalized curves (≈1.55× at 125 °C), α ≈ 0.0055. At T_j = 115 °C this gives R_DS ≈ 1.5 mΩ.
