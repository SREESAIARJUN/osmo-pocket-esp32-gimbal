## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class, FPU, 170+ MHz |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 2–5 mH |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701), read every current-loop tick |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 24 MHz, ODR 8 kHz |
| Kinematics | Handle → Yaw (z) → Roll (x) → Pitch (y) → Camera |
