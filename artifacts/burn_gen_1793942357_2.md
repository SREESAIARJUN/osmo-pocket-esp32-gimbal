# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

I don't have the specification for Job #43, so this design uses the **explicit assumptions in §0**. Everything is parameterized. Change the constants in `GimbalConfig` to match your hardware.

---

## 0. Assumptions and System Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 / H7-class Cortex-M with FPU (single precision) |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC (e.g. GBM2804H-100T): 12 slots / 14 poles, p = 7 pole pairs, R ≈ 10 Ω, L ≈ 2.5 mH |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701) per axis, read over SPI |
| Current sense | 2-phase low-side shunts, sampled synchronously at PWM center |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P. **IMU-C** sits on the camera plate (attitude feedback). **IMU-B** sits on the base/handle (disturbance feedforward and redundancy). |
| Axis order | Yaw (outer, z) → Roll (middle, x) → Pitch (inner, y) |

### Loop schedule

| Loop | Rate | Bandwidth target |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC dq PI) | 20 kHz | ω_c ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s |
| Velocity PI + cogging FF | 4 kHz (÷5) | ω_v ≈ 2π·150 rad/s |
| IMU read + filters + fusion | 8 kHz ODR, processed at 4 kHz (2 samples averaged) | — |
| Angle (attitude) loop | 1 kHz (÷4) | ω_θ ≈ 2π·25 rad/s |

Bandwidth separation is about 10× between loops, so each outer loop sees the inner loop as approximately unity gain.
