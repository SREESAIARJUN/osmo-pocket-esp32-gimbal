## 1. Loss Model

### 1.1 Conduction Losses (6-step, synchronous PWM)

In any commutation sector, two phases conduct:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T_sw. The synchronous low-side FET conducts for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Static leg:** the low-side FET conducts continuously.

Per ESC, with phase current I:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

The result is independent of D, because synchronous rectification always keeps exactly two channels in the path.

Temperature-dependent resistance:

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 135 °C: R = 1.5 mΩ × 1.495 ≈ **2.24 mΩ**.

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.24\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{28.7\ W\ per\ ESC}$$

PCB copper loss (phase busses and the DC link, about 0.3 mΩ effective per ESC path):

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.3\times10^{-3} \approx 1.9\ W$$

### 1.2 Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only one leg is PWM-switched per sector. Use V = 25.2 V and I = 80 A.

| Mechanism | Equation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 20 ns × 48 k | 0.968 W |
| Output charge | ½ · Q_oss · V · f_sw = 0.5 × 30 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.018 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw = 40 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.048 W |
| Dead-time body-diode conduction | 2 · V_SD · I · t_dt · f_sw = 2 × 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k | 1.229 W |
| Gate drive (two FETs switching) | 2 · Q_g · V_GS · f_sw = 2 × 50 nC × 10 × 48 k | 0.048 W (dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors) |
| **P_sw total** | | **≈ 2.31 W per ESC** |

Dead time is the dominant switching term. Reducing t_dt from 200 ns to 100 ns saves about 0.6 W per ESC. Only do this after verifying shoot-through margin against the driver's propagation-delay mismatch.

Sensitivity to switching frequency: going from 48 kHz to 24 kHz halves P_sw (saving about 1.15 W per ESC). The cost is higher phase current ripple and audible noise.

### 1.3 Total Loss Budget at 80 A Burst

| Item | Per ESC | ×4 (whole board) |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 28.7 W | 114.8 W |
| Switching | 2.3 W | 9.2 W |
| Copper | 1.9 W | 7.6 W |
| Gate driver and logic | 0.3 W | 1.2 W |
| **Total** | **33.2 W** | **≈ 133 W** |

Efficiency check: output power is about 4 × 80 A × 22.2 V × D. At D = 0.9 that is roughly 6.4 kW, so the inverter efficiency is about 98%. The efficiency is good, but the absolute heat is far beyond what this board area can reject continuously.
