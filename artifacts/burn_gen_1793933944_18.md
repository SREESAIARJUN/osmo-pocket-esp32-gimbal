### Risks Flagged Before Any Math

- **R1. "Nano" form factor is incompatible with 80 A.** On a 20×20 mm board, 80 A per motor is physically unachievable except for sub-second bursts (Section 2 quantifies this). The model below assumes 30.5×30.5 mm. A 20×20 mm board has roughly 44 % of the area and thermal mass, so burst times shrink proportionally.
