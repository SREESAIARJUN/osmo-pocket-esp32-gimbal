## 0. Architecture Review: Read This First

The analysis surfaced several system-level constraints. They decide whether the board ships, so they are stated up front.

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | The H743 runs ArduPilot only. Commutation (BEMF zero-cross detection and 6-step timing) runs on four dedicated ESC MCUs. Examples: AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or EFM8 running BlueJay. | The H743 drives DShot600 lines to the ESC MCUs, not gates. The DMA plan in §4 reflects this. Running four sensorless FOC/6-step loops and the ArduPilot scheduler on one H743 is not a supportable design. |
| A2 | "80 A" must be treated as a **per-channel burst** rating with a defined duration. On a 20×20 mm (nano) PCB it cannot be a continuous rating. | §3 shows a board-level adiabatic rise of about 55–60 K/s with all four channels at 80 A. Burst time is about 0.5–1 s before T_j reaches 125 °C. |
| A3 | 4 × 80 A = **320 A** of battery current at full simultaneous burst. | XT60 (≈60 A cont.) and typical 12 AWG leads are inadequate. Battery pads, copper pours and connector selection must be sized against a realistic simultaneous-burst profile (typically ~150–200 A peak in a punch-out). |
| A4 | All MOSFET parameters below are a **representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN device**. | Re-run the model with the selected part's datasheet values: R_DS(on) vs T_j curve, Q_gd, Q_rr at rated di/dt, and the Z_th transient curve. The equations are part-independent. |
