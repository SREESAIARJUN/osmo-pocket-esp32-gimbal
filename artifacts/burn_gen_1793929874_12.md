# STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): Burst Thermal, Switching Loss, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Architecture

**Job #79, Rev A: Engineering Analysis**

---

## 0. Executive Summary and Feasibility Flags

Three issues need to be settled before anyone commits to layout.

1. **Architecture.** The H743 does not commutate the motors. Bi-directional DShot is an FC-to-ESC protocol, so this design has:
   - one STM32H743 running ArduPilot, and
   - four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32) on the same PCB.

   If you intended the H743 to drive the gate drivers directly, DShot is unnecessary. That would also mean writing a custom motor-control layer inside ArduPilot, which is not a supported configuration.

2. **The "80 A" figure is a phase-current burst of about 1–2 seconds, not a continuous rating.** The model below shows:

   | Board | Realistic continuous rating per motor |
   |---|---|
   | 20×20 mm nano | ≈ 12–16 A |
   | 30.5×30.5 mm | ≈ 16–22 A |

   At 80 A the per-ESC dissipation is about 39 W. Only the board's thermal capacitance makes that survivable, and only for about 1.5 s.

3. **Component values are representative.** Every MOSFET and driver parameter below must be replaced with datasheet values for the parts you actually procure, then validated with a thermocouple or IR test. Treat this as a design model, not a qualified rating.

**Assumed operating point:** 6S LiPo (V_bus = 25.2 V max), f_sw = 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous) PWM, 14-pole motors.

## 1. MOSFET Loss Model

### 1.1 Reference device parameters

The reference device is a 40 V N-channel MOSFET in a 3.3×3.3 mm DFN package. A 40 V rating is mandatory on 6S because of regen and ringing headroom.

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| R_DS(on),25 | 2.0 mΩ | at V_GS = 10 V, T_j = 25 °C |
| α | 0.0045 /K | R_DS(on) temperature coefficient |
| Q_g | 30 nC | total gate charge |
| Q_gd | 6 nC | gate-drain (Miller) charge |
| Q_rr | 40 nC | body-diode reverse-recovery charge |
| Q_oss | 30 nC | output charge |
| V_F | 0.8 V | body-diode forward voltage |
| R_θJC | 1.2 K/W | junction-to-case (bottom pad) |
| T_j,max | 150 °C | maximum junction temperature |
| t_r, t_f | 15 ns each | rise and fall time (≈1 A gate driver into Q_gd) |
| t_dt | 100 ns | dead time per edge |

### 1.2 Conduction loss

Temperature-dependent on-resistance:

$$R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

**Topology.** In trapezoidal six-step drive with synchronous rectification, exactly two FETs are in the current path at any instant: one high-side or its complementary low-side on one phase, plus the static low-side on the return phase. The bridge loss is therefore independent of duty D:

$$P_{cond,ESC}=2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

**Worst single FET.** The worst case occurs at D → 1, when the high-side carries the full current for its 120° conduction window. A static low-side FET also carries full current for 120° of each electrical cycle. Short bursts are much shorter than the board thermal time constant, but the die time constant is about 10 ms, which is longer than one electrical period. The die therefore sees the per-electrical-period average.

For burst analysis, take the conservative bound in which one FET carries I² for the whole burst. This covers a stalled or low-RPM high-torque punch, where commutation is slow:

$$P_{cond,FET,max}=I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

**At 80 A, assuming T_j ≈ 130 °C** (R_DS = 2.0 × 1.4725 ≈ 2.95 mΩ):

$$P_{cond,FET}=6400\times2.95\times10^{-3}=18.9\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{cond,ESC}=37.8\ \text{W}$$

### 1.3 Switching losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-switched leg contributes.

| Mechanism | Formula | Value at 80 A, 25.2 V |
|---|---|---|
