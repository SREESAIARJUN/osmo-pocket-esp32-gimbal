Two architectural points affect the rest of the design:

1. **The H743 does not drive the gates.** A 4-in-1 needs 12 complementary PWM pairs. The H743 has only two advanced timers (TIM1 and TIM8), with 3 complementary pairs each, so it can drive at most 2 motors.
   - The correct partition is: H743 (ArduPilot) → DShot600 → four ESC MCUs. These are typically AM32 or BLHeli_32 running on an STM32G071 or AT32F421.
   - The 48 kHz PWM lives on the ESC MCUs.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano board is a seconds-long rating, not a continuous one.** Section 3 shows why.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C:

$$R_{ds} = 1.5 \times 1.375 = 2.06\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

During each 60° sector, two phases conduct:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) FET conducts for D, and the low-side (LS) FET conducts for (1 − D) through synchronous rectification.
- **Return leg:** the LS FET conducts for 100% of the sector.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{ph}^2 R_{ds} = 2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}$$

This result is independent of throttle duty when sync rectification is on.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.06\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{26.4\ W\ per\ channel}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET loss

Each FET takes every role over one electrical revolution, so the time-averaged loss per FET is:

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{26.4}{6} = 4.4\ \text{W}$$

The instantaneous peak while a FET is the return-leg LS is I²R = 13.2 W. This matters at low eRPM, where commutation sectors last milliseconds.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. Per channel:

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V·I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw = ½·25.2·80·27 ns·48 k | 1.31 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·100 ns·48 k | 0.31 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 40 nC·25.2·48 k | 0.05 W |
| C_oss (2 FETs per leg) | 2·½·C_oss·V²·f_sw | 0.05 W |
| Gate drive (driver-side, 2 FETs) | 2·Q_g·V_gs·f_sw = 2·50 nC·10·48 k | 0.05 W |
| **Total P_sw per channel** | | **≈ 1.77 W** |

### 2.1 Totals

$$P_{ch} = 26.4 + 1.77 \approx 28.2\ \text{W} \qquad P_{board} = 4 \times 28.2 \approx \mathbf{113\ W}$$

At 80 A, conduction is about 94% of the loss. The levers that matter, in order:

1. Lower R_ds(on) (parallel FETs or a larger package).
2. Copper and thermal mass.
3. The switching frequency itself.
