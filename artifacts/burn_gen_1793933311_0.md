## 1. Field-Oriented Control (inner current loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

θ_off is found at commissioning. Inject a d-axis current, let the rotor align, then read the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant in the rotating frame (SPMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

Torque is

$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.5 Current PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

Pole-zero cancellation sets the gains. The PI zero cancels the electrical pole R/L, which leaves a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{\text{PWM}}}{10}$$

At 20 kHz this gives ω_c ≈ 2π·2 kHz.

### 1.6 Voltage limiting and anti-windup

The voltage vector is limited to the inscribed circle of the SVPWM hexagon:

$$|v^*| \le \frac{V_{dc}}{\sqrt3}$$

Use back-calculation anti-windup on each integrator:

$$\dot x_I = K_i e + K_{aw}(v_{\text{sat}} - v_{\text{unsat}}),\qquad K_{aw} \approx \frac{K_i}{K_p}$$
