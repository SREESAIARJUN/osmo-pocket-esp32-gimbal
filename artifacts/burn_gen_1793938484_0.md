# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

**Scope:** Electrothermal model at 80 A burst, switching-loss model at f_sw = 48 kHz, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and reference netlists.

> **Note:** I have no prior context for "Job #12". Every device parameter below is a stated, labeled assumption. Substitute your actual MOSFET datasheet values into the equations. The structure of the model holds; the numbers move.

---

## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** drive the bridges directly. In this class of product the partition is:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (400 MHz) | ArduPilot, sends DShot600, receives eRPM via bidirectional DShot |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (AM32) or EFM8BB51 (Bluejay) | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, about 200 ns internal dead time) | Half-bridge drive |
| Power stage ×4 | 6× N-ch 40 V MOSFETs, PDFN 3.3×3.3 | Bridge |

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS ≈ 1.0 × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**.

### 2.2 Current path in 6-step commutation

In every 60° sector, exactly two switch positions carry I_ph:
- the PWM leg, where the high side conducts for duty D and the synchronous low side for (1−D);
- the static low side, which conducts 100%.

The total conducting resistance is therefore always 2·R_DS, independent of D:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W\ per\ ESC}
$$

Averaged over a full electrical cycle, each of the 6 FETs carries:

$$
P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}}{3} = \mathbf{3.2\ W}
$$

### 2.3 Copper (PCB) conduction

Assume a phase trace plus plane of 2 oz Cu, 4 mm wide, 15 mm long, two layers in parallel.

$$
R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho\,L}{w\,t\,n} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\times0.015}{0.004\times70\times10^{-6}\times2} \approx 0.46\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

Two phase paths are in series, giving 2 × 80² × 0.46 mΩ ≈ **5.9 W** at 25 °C (about +40% hot).

> **Copper is not negligible. It is about 30% of FET conduction loss on a nano board.**

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. Its low side is soft-switched (ZVS via body diode during dead time).

### 3.1 Transition times (gate-charge method)

$$
I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_g} = \frac{5.5}{5} = 1.1\ \text{A},\qquad
I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_g} = 0.9\ \text{A}
$$

$$
t_{on} = \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = 11.8\ \text{ns},\qquad
t_{off} = \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,off}} = 14.4\ \text{ns}
$$

### 3.2 Loss terms per ESC channel

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V·I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_on + t_off)·f_sw | ½·25.2·80·26.2 ns·48 k = **1.27 W** |
| Output capacitance | Q_oss·V·f_sw | **0.036 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | **0.048 W** |
| Dead-time body diode | V_F·I·2·t_dt·f_sw | 0.8·80·400 ns·48 k = **1.23 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f_sw | **0.038 W** (mostly in driver/R_g) |
| **Total** | | **≈ 2.62 W** |
