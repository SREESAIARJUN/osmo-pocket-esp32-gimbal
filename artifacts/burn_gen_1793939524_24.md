# STM32H743 AIO + 4-in-1 ESC: 80A Burst Power Stage, 48 kHz Switching, and ArduPilot BDShot600 Allocation

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

Two architectural points shape everything below.

1. **The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In a 4-in-1 design, each motor channel needs its own commutation MCU (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32) plus a 3-phase gate driver. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the four ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot. The model is partitioned that way.
2. **"Nano" plus 80A burst is thermally marginal.** Section 3 shows that 80A is a transient rating of roughly 1 s for all four motors at once. It cannot be a steady-state number on a 20×20 or 30.5×30.5 board. Size the burst spec from the thermal capacitance, not from the MOSFET datasheet.
