# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #27)

## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

The STM32H743 cannot drive the power stage directly. A commercially and technically sound 4-in-1 ESC design uses one ESC MCU per motor channel running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, such as the AT32F421 or STM32G071. The 48 kHz PWM is generated there. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. This document is partitioned that way.

**Operating point assumptions.** Change these and re-run the equations.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S, full) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current per channel | I_pk | 80 A |
| ESC PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET (40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3 class) | R_ds(on),25 | 1.5 mΩ @ V_gs = 10 V |
| R_ds(on) temperature coefficient | α | 0.0045 /K |
| Rise / fall time at gate drive used | t_r, t_f | 15 ns / 15 ns |
| Reverse recovery charge | Q_rr | 40 nC |
| Output capacitance (effective energy) | C_oss | 1.0 nF |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 50 nC |
| Body diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Dead time per edge | t_dt | 100 ns |
| Junction-to-PCB thermal resistance | R_θJ-PCB | 1.5 K/W |
| Board | — | 30.5 mm mounting class, 6-layer, 2 oz |

**Engineering warning, stated up front.** The analysis below shows that 80 A per channel on a nano-class board is a burst rating of roughly 1 second. It is not a sustained capability. Section 1.6 quantifies this. Do not publish a continuous 80 A rating.

## 1. Power Stage Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Commutation topology

A 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification has two phases conducting at any instant. Each phase uses complementary PWM.

- **PWM'd phase:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T_sw. The low-side FET conducts synchronously for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Return phase:** the low-side FET is on for 100% of the interval.

### 1.2 Conduction loss

R_ds(on) rises with junction temperature:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

Total conduction loss for one channel:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[\,D + (1-D) + 1\,] = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

The result is independent of duty cycle because of synchronous rectification.

| T_j | R_ds(on) | Loss per FET (I²R) | Channel P_cond |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.50 mΩ | 9.6 W | 19.2 W |
| 100 °C | 2.01 mΩ | 12.9 W | 25.7 W |
| 125 °C | 2.18 mΩ | 13.9 W | 27.9 W |

### 1.3 Switching losses at 48 kHz (hard-switched leg)

**V-I overlap:**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(30\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{k}) = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery:**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

**C_oss (both FETs on the switching node):**

$$P_{oss} = 2\cdot\tfrac12 C_{oss}V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 1\text{n}\cdot635\cdot48\text{k} = 0.030\ \text{W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction (two edges per period):**

$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot200\text{n}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**Gate drive.** This loss is dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FET:

$$P_{gate} = 2\,Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} = 2\cdot50\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 Per-channel and board totals at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

| Loss term | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 27.9 W | 111.6 W |
| V-I switching | 1.45 W | 5.8 W |
| Dead time | 0.61 W | 2.4 W |
| Q_rr + C_oss + gate | 0.13 W | 0.5 W |
| **Total** | **≈30.1 W** | **≈120 W** |

Conduction dominates at about 93% of the total. Two levers follow from this:

- **Halving R_ds(on)** with paralleled FETs or a 0.7 mΩ part cuts total loss by about 45%.
- **Dropping f_sw to 24 kHz** saves only about 1 W per channel. The case for 48 kHz is acoustic and current ripple, not efficiency.

### 1.5 Self-consistent junction temperature (electrothermal fixed point)

Because R_ds(on) depends on T_j, the junction temperature must be solved iteratively:

$$T_j = T_{PCB} + R_{\theta J\text{-}PCB}\left[I^2R_{ds,25}(1+\alpha(T_j-25)) + P_{sw,FET}\right]$$

Take P_sw ≈ 1.0 W, which is the worst FET, carrying most of the V-I and dead-time loss. Then:

$$T_j = \frac{T_{PCB} + R_\theta\,[I^2R_{25}(1-25\alpha)+P_{sw}]}{1 - R_\theta I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha}$$

- Denominator: 1 − 1.5·9.6·0.0045 = **0.935**.
- **Stability condition:** R_θ I² R₂₅ α < 1, otherwise thermal runaway occurs. The current design has margin, at 0.065.

For T_PCB = 90 °C:

$$T_j = \frac{90 + 1.5\,[9.6(0.8875)+1.0]}{0.935} = \frac{90+14.3}{0.935} \approx \mathbf{111.5\ °C}$$

### 1.6 Transient (burst) thermal model

**Board lumped heat capacity (30×30 mm, 1.6 mm, 6 × 2 oz):**

- FR-4: 1.44 cm³ × 1.85 g/cm³ × 1.1 J/gK ≈ **2.9 J/K**
- Copper: 9 cm² × 0.42 mm = 0.38 cm³ → 3.4 g × 0.385 ≈ **1.3 J/K**
- FETs, caps, and solder: ≈ **0.4 J/K**
- **C_th,board ≈ 4.6 J/K**

**Board-to-ambient resistance in prop wash** (forced convection at about 5–10 m/s, both faces): R_θPCB-A ≈ 3–5 K/W. Use 4 K/W.

**Two-node Foster model:**

$$T_j(t) = T_a + P_{tot}R_{\theta BA}\left(1-e^{-t/\tau_B}\right) + P_{FET}\,Z_{\theta JC}(t)$$

- Board time constant: τ_B = R_θBA · C_th = 4 × 4.6 ≈ **18 s**
- FET Z_θJC saturates to about 1.5 K/W within about 100 ms.

**Burst capability, 4 × 80 A (P_tot ≈ 120 W), T_a = 35 °C, limit T_j ≤ 150 °C:**

| Burst duration | Board ΔT | Junction ΔT over board | T_j |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 s | 120·4·(1−e^(−0.5/18)) ≈ 13 K | ≈21 K | ≈69 °C |
| 2 s | ≈50 K | ≈22 K | ≈107 °C |
| 3 s | ≈74 K | ≈23 K | ≈132 °C |
| 4 s | ≈96 K | ≈23 K | ≈154 °C (exceeds limit) |
| Steady state | 480 K | — | Destruction |

**Conclusions:**

- **Burst rating:** 80 A per channel, all four channels simultaneously, for ≤ 2 s, with a 10 s recovery.
