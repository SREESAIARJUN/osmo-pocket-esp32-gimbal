## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{ds(on),25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25^\circ\text{C})\big], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At a design-point junction temperature T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{ds(on)}(110) = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.0055 \times 85) = 1.47\ \text{m}\Omega$$

Add package and PCB spreading resistance per leg. A conservative estimate is R_pcb ≈ 0.3 mΩ for 2 oz outer layers plus 1 oz inner layers with a via stitch. This gives an effective R_eff ≈ 1.77 mΩ per conducting switch position.

### 1.2 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

During any 60° commutation interval, current flows through two phases:

- **Switching leg (PWM phase):** The high side conducts for D·T_s and the low side (sync rect.) conducts for (1−D)·T_s. Both carry the same phase current *I*, so the leg dissipates I²·R_eff regardless of D, split between the two FETs.
- **Return leg:** The low side is on continuously and dissipates I²·R_eff.

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2 R_{eff}$$

**Per-FET split** (worst case, low-side return FET):

$$P_{cond,LS,return} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 80^2 \times 1.47\text{m} = 9.41\ \text{W}$$

**Per motor at 80 A:**

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.77\text{m} = 22.7\ \text{W}\quad(18.8\ \text{W silicon} + 3.8\ \text{W copper})$$

Rotation over the electrical cycle spreads the load across FETs. Each FET conducts heavily for about 1/3 of the electrical period. For steady state, average per-FET silicon dissipation is about 18.8/6 ≈ 3.1 W. For transients shorter than the electrical period this averaging does not hold, but at more than 100 Hz electrical it does.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Gate-current-limited transition times

$$t_{on} \approx \frac{Q_{gs2} + Q_{gd}}{(V_{drv} - V_{pl})/(R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int})} = \frac{12\,\text{nC}}{7\,\text{V}/5\,\Omega} = 8.6\ \text{ns}$$

$$t_{off} \approx \frac{12\,\text{nC}}{V_{pl}/(R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int})} = \frac{12\,\text{nC}}{0.6\,\text{A}} = 20\ \text{ns}$$

### 2.2 Loss components (per switching leg, one motor)

| Term | Equation | Value @ 80 A, 25.2 V, 48 kHz |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_on+t_off)·f_sw | ½·25.2·80·28.6 ns·48k = **1.38 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 40n·25.2·48k = **0.048 W** |
| Output capacitance | ½·Q_oss·V·f_sw (×2 FETs) | **0.06 W** |
| Dead-time body-diode | V_SD·I·2·t_dt·f_sw | 0.8·80·200 ns·48k = **0.61 W** |
| Gate charge (dissipated in driver/R_g, not FET) | Q_g·V_drv·f_sw ×2 | **0.04 W** |

$$P_{sw,motor} \approx 2.1\ \text{W}\ (\text{FET}) + 0.04\ \text{W}\ (\text{driver})$$

**Sensitivity:**

- Dead-time loss is about 30% of switching loss. Cutting dead time to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W per motor, but only if the driver's propagation skew allows it. Verify on a scope.
- f_sw scales the switching loss linearly. At 24 kHz it would be about 1.05 W, at the cost of current ripple and acoustic noise.

### 2.3 Total at 80 A burst

| | Per motor | ×4 motors |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (Si) | 18.8 W | 75.2 W |
| Conduction (Cu) | 3.8 W | 15.2 W |
| Switching | 2.1 W | 8.4 W |
| **Total** | **24.7 W** | **~99 W** |

Electrical efficiency at this point is about 1 − 24.7/(25.2×80×~0.85 duty) ≈ 98.6%. That is good efficiency, but **99 W into a 30×30 mm board cannot be sustained.** This makes it a transient thermal problem.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Network (Cauer-equivalent, per hottest FET)

```
P_FET ──► [Rth_jc=1.2 K/W, Cth_j≈0.05 J/K] ──► case/pad
          ──► [Rth_pcb,local=8 K/W, Cth_cu,local≈0.15 J/K] ──► board mass
          ──► [Rth_board-amb (prop-wash) , Cth_board] ──► ambient
```

### 3.2 Board thermal mass

For a 30.5×30.5 mm board, 1.6 mm thick, 8-layer with 2 oz outer and 1 oz inner copper:

- Copper volume: about 9.3 cm² × (2×70 + 6×35) µm ≈ 0.33 cm³, giving 2.9 g × 0.385 J/g·K ≈ **1.1 J/K**
- FR4: about 1.5 cm³ × 1.85 g/cm³ ≈ 2.8 g × 1.1 J/g·K ≈ **3.1 J/K**
- Components and solder: about **0.8 J/K**
- **C_board ≈ 5 J/K** (the ESC section only, about 60% of this, is about 3 J/K effective)

The board-to-ambient resistance depends on airflow:

- Still air: R_th,ba ≈ 25 K/W
- In prop wash at 10–15 m/s: R_th,ba ≈ 6–8 K/W. **This must be measured.**

### 3.3 Burst duration limit

Board-level energy balance (adiabatic early phase):

$$\Delta T_{board}(t) = P_{tot} R_{ba}\left(1 - e^{-t/(R_{ba}C_{board})}\right)$$

Using P = 99 W, R_ba = 7 K/W, C = 3 J/K, τ = 21 s. The steady-state target of 693 K rise is not physical; the board fails long before reaching it. For t ≪ τ:
