## 0. Architectural Premise and Assumptions

The 4-in-1 power stage is **not** driven directly by the H743. Each channel uses its own ESC MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32-class firmware). The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to each ESC over one bi-directional DShot600 line.

This partitioning is mandatory for two reasons:
- ArduPilot does not do FOC or 6-step commutation for 4 motors.
- The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs.
