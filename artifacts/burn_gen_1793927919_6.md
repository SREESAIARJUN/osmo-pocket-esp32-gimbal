# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: 80 A Burst Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation

## 0. Scope and Architectural Assumptions

I don't have the Job #53 requirements document. Every assumption below is labeled so you can substitute your actual BOM values.

**Critical architecture point.** The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs. On every practical 4-in-1 design:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──BDShot600 ×4──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 or BLHeli_32)
                                          └─► 3-phase gate driver ─► 6 MOSFETs per motor
```

The 48 kHz PWM, dead time and commutation all live in the ESC MCU firmware. The H743's job is DShot generation, eRPM capture and filtering.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\text{–}0.006\ /^\circ C$$

At T_j = 100 °C with α = 0.006: R_DS = 1.0 × 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step conduction with synchronous rectification

During any commutation sector, two phases carry current I:

- **PWM phase:** the high side conducts for duty D. Its complementary low side conducts for (1 − D), minus dead time.
- **Fixed low-side phase:** conducts continuously.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}\,D + I^2 R_{DS}(1-D) + I^2 R_{DS} = 2 I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

With synchronous rectification, conduction loss is independent of duty. At 80 A and 100 °C:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.45\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{18.6\ W\ per\ motor}$$
