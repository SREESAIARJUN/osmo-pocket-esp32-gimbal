## 0. Architecture Clarification and Design Assumptions

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. In a 4-in-1 AIO:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir (1 wire/motor)──► 4× ESC MCU (AM32/BLHeli_32 class,
                                                          e.g. STM32G071 / AT32F421)
                                                          └─► 3-phase gate driver ─► 6 MOSFETs/motor
```

The 48 kHz switching frequency and the 80 A losses belong to the ESC MCUs and power stage. The H743 owns the DShot timers and DMA.
