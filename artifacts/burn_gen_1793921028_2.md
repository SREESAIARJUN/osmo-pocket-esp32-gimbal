# STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC) System Architecture: Job #44

**Loss model, DShot timing and DMA allocation, netlists, and thermal budget**

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Red Flags

### 0.1 Architecture assumption

An STM32H743 does not commutate motors directly. In this design:

- **The H743 runs ArduPilot** as the flight controller.
- **It emits four bidirectional DShot600 streams.**
- **Four dedicated ESC MCUs** run AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. Candidates are the AT32F421 or STM32G071, one per motor.
- **Each ESC MCU** drives a 3-phase gate driver and six N-channel MOSFETs at 48 kHz PWM.

### 0.2 Baseline parameters

All results are parametric. Substitute your actual BOM values.

| Symbol | Meaning | Baseline |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | Battery (6S full) | 25.2 V |
| I_pk | Phase burst current, per motor | 80 A |
| f_sw | ESC PWM frequency | 48 kHz |
| R_ds,25 | MOSFET R_DS(on) at 25 °C, 10 V gate | 1.0 mΩ (40 V, 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 class) |
| α | R_DS(on) temperature coefficient | 0.005 /K |
| t_r + t_f | Switching transition time at 80 A | 20 ns |
| t_dt | Dead time | 50 ns |
| V_SD | Body diode forward voltage | 0.8 V |
| Q_rr | Reverse-recovery charge | 40 nC |
| Q_oss | Output charge | 30 nC |
| Q_g | Total gate charge | 30 nC at 10 V |
| R_θJC | Junction to case (pad) | 1.5 K/W |

### 0.3 Red flags to resolve before layout

1. **80 A on a nano footprint (20×20 or 25.5×25.5) is a transient rating only.** Section 3 shows roughly 80 W of total dissipation at a full 4×80 A burst. That is survivable for seconds, not continuously.
2. **Package choice.** The smallest H743 package is TFBGA100 (8×8 mm, H743VIH6). LQFP100 is 14×14 mm and is impractical for a nano AIO. The netlists below use port names (PAx/PBx), which are package-independent. Map balls from the datasheet pinout table.
3. **Verify all ArduPilot specifics against the current `libraries/AP_HAL_ChibiOS/hwdef/` README.** This includes BIDIR marking rules and DMA sharing behavior, which have changed across releases.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α (T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**

### 1.2 Commutation topology

The ESC uses trapezoidal (6-step) commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM:

- At any instant, two phase legs carry the motor current I.
- In the PWM leg, current alternates between the high-side (HS) FET (duty D) and the low-side (LS) FET (1−D).
- Either way, exactly one FET per active leg conducts. Body-diode conduction occurs only during dead time (Section 2).

This gives:

P_cond,ch = 2 · I² · R_ds(T_j)

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts for 120° electrical out of 360°. The total loss is therefore shared by 6 FETs:

P_cond,FET,avg = P_cond,ch / 6 = I² · R_ds / 3

Peak instantaneous loss during a FET's conduction window = I² · R_ds.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per channel | Per FET (avg) | Per FET (peak) | 4-channel total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 12.8 W | 2.13 W | 6.4 W | 51.2 W |
| 100 °C | 1.375 mΩ | 17.6 W | 2.93 W | 8.8 W | 70.4 W |
| 125 °C | 1.5 mΩ | 19.2 W | 3.20 W | 9.6 W | 76.8 W |

### 1.5 Parasitic conduction (often forgotten, significant at 80 A)

| Element | Value | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Current shunt (per ESC) | 0.25 mΩ | I_bus²·R. At D = 0.8, I_bus ≈ 64 A → 1.0 W |
| PCB copper, phase path (2 oz, 8-layer stacked pours, ~0.3 mΩ) | 0.3 mΩ | 2 × 80² × 0.3 mΩ = 3.8 W |
| Solder and via interfaces | ~0.1 mΩ | ~1.3 W |

**Copper resistance rises about 0.39 %/K and must be included.** Copper can approach 30 % of FET loss, so specify inner layers at 2 oz minimum with stitched parallel pours and via arrays (≥ 20 × 0.3 mm vias per phase transition).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

In 6-step commutation, only one leg switches per sector. That leg contains one HS/LS pair in complementary mode.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V-I overlap

P_sw = ½ · V_bus · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw
= 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 20 ns × 48 kHz = **0.968 W**

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction (two dead times per period)

P_dt = V_SD · I · 2 · t_dt · f_sw
= 0.8 × 80 × 100 ns × 48 kHz = **0.307 W**

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

P_rr = Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw
= 40 nC × 25.2 × 48 kHz = **0.048 W**
