# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #5): Power Stage, Thermal and Bi-Directional DShot600 Design Package

## 0. Architectural Clarifications

These four points shape the rest of the design.

1. **The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In a 4-in-1 stack, each ESC channel has its own MCU, typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 to those MCUs. The **48 kHz switching frequency is an ESC-firmware setting**, not something the FC controls.
2. **80 A on a nano (20×20 mm) board is a burst rating measured in seconds, not a continuous rating.** The model below shows why and gives the burst time limits.
3. **All component parameters are representative assumptions** (e.g. 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFETs). Before release, substitute datasheet values for the actual BOM. Every equation is parametric so the numbers update cleanly.
4. **"80 A" is taken to mean phase current per motor channel.** Battery current is lower by roughly the duty cycle.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

In 120° trapezoidal drive, exactly one high-side and one low-side FET carry I_ph at any instant. With synchronous rectification, the PWM leg alternates high-side and low-side conduction but still carries I_ph through one FET at a time.

**Instantaneous conduction loss per channel:**

P_cond,ch = 2 · I_ph² · R_DS(T_j)

**Temperature dependence of R_DS:**

R_DS(T_j) = R_DS,25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)], with α ≈ 0.0062 /K

This gives 2.2 mΩ at about 100 °C. It creates a positive feedback loop, so iterate the result with Section 4.

**At 80 A and T_j = 100 °C:**

P_cond,ch = 2 × 6400 × 0.0022 = **28.2 W per channel**

**Per-FET average.** Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle:

- I_rms,FET = I_ph / √3 = 46.2 A
- P_FET = I_rms² · R_DS = 46.2² × 0.0022 = **4.7 W average**
- Peak during its conduction window is 14.1 W

**PWM ripple correction.** Phase current ripple adds a small term:

I_rms² = I_dc² + ΔI_pp² / 12

Ripple at 48 kHz:

ΔI_pp ≈ (V_bus · D · (1 − D)) / (L_ph · f_sw)

For a typical 5″ motor (L_ph ≈ 15 µH line-to-line) at D = 0.5, ΔI_pp ≈ 8.75 A. This adds about 0.2% loss, which is negligible at 80 A.
