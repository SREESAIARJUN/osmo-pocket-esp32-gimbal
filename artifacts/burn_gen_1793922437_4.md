## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ /^\circ\text{C}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ** (use 1.5 mΩ for margin).

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous commutation

In any 60° sector, current flows through two series switch positions:

- The PWM'd leg, alternating between the HS FET (duty D) and the LS FET (1−D, synchronous rectification).
- The LS FET of the sinking leg, held on for that sector.

The total conducting resistance is therefore always 2·R_DS, independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.0015 = \mathbf{19.2\ W\ per\ motor\ channel}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET is "the sink" for 120° of the 360° electrical cycle, so it carries the full I² term for one third of the time. The PWM-leg share redistributes but averages out:

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{19.2}{6} = \mathbf{3.2\ W\ average}$$

The instantaneous peak is ~9.6 W during the 120° conduction window. This matters at low eRPM, where the electrical period becomes comparable to the FET thermal time constant (~ms).

### 1.4 Self-consistent junction temperature

R depends on T_j, and T_j depends on P. Iterate the coupled equations:

$$T_j = T_{board} + P_{FET}\,R_{\theta,j\text{-}board}$$

Example: R_θ ≈ 3 K/W and T_board = 100 °C gives T_j ≈ 110–112 °C. This converges in about two iterations, so the 1.5 mΩ assumption is consistent.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches in each sector, so one hard-switched commutation pair per channel at f_sw = 48 kHz.

| Mechanism | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = ½·25.2·80·40 ns·48 k | **1.94 W** |
| Dead-time body-diode conduction | V_F·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·400 ns·48 k | **1.23 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 50 nC·25.2·48 k | 0.06 W |
| Output capacitance | ½·C_oss·V²·f_sw = ½·1.5 nF·635·48 k | 0.02 W |
| Gate charge (lands in driver / R_g, not the FET) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw = 2·60 nC·10 V·48 k | 0.06 W |
| **Total switching per channel** | | **≈ 3.3 W** |

**Design levers:**

- Dead-time loss is 37% of the switching budget. Reducing t_dt from 200 ns to 100 ns saves about 0.6 W per channel. Verify shoot-through margin on the scope at 25.2 V before doing this.
- Overlap loss scales linearly with f_sw. Dropping to 24 kHz saves about 1 W per channel, at the cost of current ripple and audible noise.

### 2.1 Channel and board totals at 80 A burst

$$P_{ch} = 19.2 + 3.3 = 22.5\ \text{W}, \qquad P_{board} = 4 \times 22.5 = \mathbf{90\ W}$$

Efficiency at 25.2 V × 80 A = 2016 W per channel: the ESC stage alone is **≈ 98.9 %**. Add roughly 2–4 W per channel for shunt, traces, and connector I²R.
