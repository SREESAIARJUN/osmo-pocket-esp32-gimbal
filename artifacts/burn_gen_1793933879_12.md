## 0. Architecture Correction

The STM32H743 does **not** switch the MOSFETs. A 48 kHz, 3-phase, 4-motor commutation loop on the H7 alongside ArduPilot is not a supported or sane design. The architecture is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) --4x bidirectional DShot600--> 4x ESC MCU (AT32F421 / AM32 firmware)
                                                      └─ 3-phase gate driver ─ 6x N-MOSFET ─ motor
```

- **48 kHz** is the ESC MCU's PWM frequency.
- **DShot600** is the H7 → ESC command and telemetry link.

The model treats these as two separate domains.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.65) = **1.65 mΩ** (use 1.6 mΩ as the design value).

### 2.2 Six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification

In every commutation sector, current flows through two series switch positions:

1. The PWM leg: the high-side for duty D, then the complementary low-side for 1−D.
2. The return leg: the low-side, fully on.

Because synchronous rectification puts a MOSFET (not a diode) in every interval, the total is **duty-independent**:

$$P_{cond,ch}=2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch}=2(80)^2(1.6\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{20.5\ W\ per\ motor\ channel}$$

**Per-FET average.** Each phase is active 2/3 of the electrical period, and the loss is shared across 6 FETs:

$$\bar P_{FET}\approx \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}= 3.4\ \text{W}$$

The electrical frequency is roughly 1–3 kHz. The junction thermal time constant is greater than 1 ms, so averaging over the electrical cycle is valid.

**FOC / sinusoidal alternative.** If the ESC firmware uses FOC, replace the factor 2·I² with 3·I_rms,ph² × R_ds, where I_rms = I_pk/√2. This gives 3 × 3200 × 1.6 mΩ = 15.4 W.

### 2.3 Copper loss

$$P_{cu}=I^2 R_{cu}=6400\times0.5\,\text{m}\Omega = 3.2\ \text{W per channel}$$
