# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture Derivation (Job #47)

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600 to the ESC MCU(s), typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32. Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM through 3-phase gate drivers. All loss math below applies to the ESC power stage. All DMA/timer math applies to the H743.

**Baseline parameters.** Replace these with datasheet values for your actual BOM.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S, full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current per motor | I_pk | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET (40 V, DFN 3.3×3.3, one FET per switch) | Rds(on)@25 °C | 1.2 mΩ |
| Rds(on) tempco factor at T_j = 125 °C | k_T | 1.6 |
| Rise / fall time at the driver's gate current | t_r, t_f | 20 ns / 20 ns |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns |
| Body diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Reverse recovery charge | Q_rr | 40 nC |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 30 nC |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 40 nC at V_gs = 10 V |
| Junction-to-case thermal resistance | R_θJC | 1.5 K/W |
| Current shunt | R_sh | 0.25 mΩ per motor |
| Ambient | T_a | 40 °C |

> **Feasibility flag (stated up front):** 80 A per motor on a nano (20×20 mm) board is thermally a short-burst rating only. Section 2 quantifies this. If "80A" means total across all four motors (20 A each), every loss term below drops by about 16× (I²) or 4× (linear), and the design closes comfortably.

## 1. Power-Stage Loss Model (per motor, one 3-phase bridge)

In 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) PWM, at any instant one high-side FET and one low-side FET carry I in the active phase pair. The PWM-ing leg alternates between the high-side (duty D) and the synchronous low-side (1−D).

### 1.1 Conduction loss

Hot on-resistance:

R_ds,hot = k_T · R_ds25 = 1.6 × 1.2 mΩ = **1.92 mΩ**

Per bridge:

P_cond = I²·R·D (HS, PWM leg) + I²·R·(1−D) (LS, sync) + I²·R (static LS, other leg)
     = **2·I²·R_ds,hot** (independent of D)

At 80 A:

P_cond = 2 × 6400 × 1.92e-3 = **24.6 W per motor**

Per FET, the worst case is the static low-side at full I²R: **12.3 W**.

Tempco is self-reinforcing:

R(T_j) = R_25·[1 + α(T_j − 25)], with α ≈ 0.006 /K

Solve P(T_j) and T_j(P) jointly. This matters in Section 2.

### 1.2 Switching (V-I overlap) loss

Only the PWM-ing leg switches.

P_sw = ½ · V_bus · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw
   = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40e-9 × 48e3 = **1.94 W**

### 1.3 Dead-time diode conduction

P_dt = V_f · I · 2t_dt · f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 200e-9 × 48e3 = **0.61 W**

### 1.4 Reverse recovery, Coss, and gate drive

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f = 40e-9 × 25.2 × 48e3 | 0.048 W |
| Coss | Q_oss·V·f = 30e-9 × 25.2 × 48e3 | 0.036 W |
| Gate drive (dissipated in driver) | 2·Q_g·V_gs·f | 0.038 W |

### 1.5 Shunt and copper losses

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Shunt | I²·R_sh = 6400 × 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| Copper | 2 oz Cu, ~0.3 mΩ path | ~1.9 W |

### 1.6 Totals at 80 A, 48 kHz, 6S

| Term | Per motor (W) | ×4 motors (W) |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (hot) | 24.6 | 98.3 |
| Switching | 1.94 | 7.8 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 | 2.4 |
| Q_rr + Q_oss | 0.08 | 0.3 |
| Shunt + copper | 3.5 | 14.0 |
| **Total** | **≈30.7** | **≈123** |

Efficiency at a full 80 A burst (~2 kW electrical per motor, accounting for duty) is about 98.5% per stage. Efficiency is not the problem; power density is.

**Frequency sensitivity:**

∂P/∂f_sw ≈ (P_sw + P_dt + P_rr + P_oss) / f_sw ≈ 55 µW/Hz per motor

So moving from 48 kHz to 24 kHz saves only about 1.3 W per motor. **Conduction dominates (~80%).** Reducing Rds(on), for example with paralleled FETs, is the effective lever. Two FETs per switch halve P_cond.

## 2. Thermal Model

### 2.1 Junction rise above the board (steady per-FET)

ΔT_jc = 12.3 W × 1.5 K/W = **18.5 K** above the PCB pad. Silicon is not the limit.

### 2.2 Board-level steady state

Required sink-to-ambient resistance for T_board ≤ 105 °C:

R_θ,BA ≤ (105 − 40) / 123 ≈ **0.53 K/W**

A 20×20 board in prop wash typically achieves about 8–15 K/W. **Steady-state 4×80 A is infeasible by about 20×.**

Sustainable continuous power at ~10 K/W is about 6.5 W total, which corresponds to roughly **I_cont ≈ 18–20 A per motor** with all four loaded.

### 2.3 Transient (burst) model: 2-node Foster/Cauer lumped network

Board thermal capacitance:
- Copper (~2.5 g × 0.385 J/g·K) ≈ 1.0 J/K
- FR4 (~2.5 g × 1.1 J/g·K) ≈ 2.8 J/K
- Components ≈ 0.4 J/K
- **C_B ≈ 4.2 J/K**

Single-pole model:

T_B(t) = T_a + P·R_BA·(1 − e^(−t/τ)), with τ = R_BA·C_B ≈ 10 × 4.2 = 42 s

For t ≪ τ this is quasi-adiabatic:

dT/dt ≈ P / C_B = 123 / 4.2 ≈ **29 K/s**

Coupled with Rds tempco, iterate R(T) to get an effective rate of about 35 K/s near 100 °C.
