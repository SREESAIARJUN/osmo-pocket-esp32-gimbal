## 0. Architectural Corrections

Three issues must be resolved before any math is meaningful.

### 0.1 The H743 cannot be the ESC and also emit bidirectional DShot

Bidirectional DShot is a protocol between a flight controller and ESC MCUs. A coherent architecture therefore needs two kinds of processor:

```
                ┌──────────────────────────┐
  VBAT ──┬──────│ STM32H743VIT6 (FC)       │── SPI1: IMU, SPI2: OSD, SPI3: Flash
         │      │ ArduPilot                │── I2C: Baro/Mag, UART×7
         │      └─┬───┬───┬───┬────────────┘
         │   DShot│600 bidir (1 wire each, open-drain-like inverted idle-high)
         │      ┌─▼─┐┌▼──┐┌▼──┐┌▼──┐
         └──────│ESC││ESC││ESC││ESC│   4× AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) running AM32
                │ 1 ││ 2 ││ 3 ││ 4 │   each → 3-phase gate driver → 6 MOSFETs
                └───┘└───┘└───┘└───┘
```

If you instead intend the H743 to commutate the motors directly, delete Section 3 entirely. In that case you need 12 complementary PWM outputs with dead-time (TIM1/TIM8 provide only 6 complementary pairs total) plus four BEMF/FOC loops. ArduPilot does not support that, so this document assumes the dual-MCU architecture above.

### 0.2 "Nano" and "80A burst" are in tension

A 20×20 mm board fits 24 MOSFETs only in 3.3×3.3 mm DFN packages, with roughly 1.3–2.0 mΩ R_ds(on) for 40 V parts. The math in Section 1 shows that 80 A per channel on such a board is a **seconds-scale** rating, not a continuous one. Recommendations:
- Use a 30.5×30.5 mm form factor.
- State "80A burst ≤ 2 s at T_board,start = 40 °C" explicitly.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.5 × 1.55 = **2.33 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Per-device conduction in 6-step with synchronous freewheel

Each phase leg conducts for 2/3 of the electrical cycle: +I for 120° and −I for 120°. During a leg's +I interval:
- The high-side FET conducts for fraction D.
- The low-side FET (synchronous rectification) conducts for fraction 1−D.

Per-FET average conduction power:

$$P_{cond,HS}=\tfrac{1}{3}\,D\,I_{rms}^2R_{ds}(T_j),\qquad P_{cond,LS}=\tfrac{1}{3}\,(1-D)\,I_{rms}^2R_{ds}(T_j)+\tfrac{1}{3}I_{rms}^2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

The second LS term arises because, in the complementary scheme, the "return" phase low-side stays on for its full 120°.

Per ESC, exactly two channel resistances are in series with the motor current at all times:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ESC}=2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)}$$

### 1.3 Ripple correction

PWM ripple raises I_rms above I_avg:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12},\qquad \Delta I_{pp}=\frac{(V_{bus}-E)\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

Example: L_ph = 15 µH line-to-line, V_bus − E = 6 V, D = 0.9.

ΔI_pp = 6 × 0.9 / (15 µH × 48 kHz) = **7.5 A**, giving ΔI²/12 = 4.7 A². This is negligible against 6400 A² (+0.07%).
