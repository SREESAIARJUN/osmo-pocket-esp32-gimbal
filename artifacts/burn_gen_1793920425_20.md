## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning: Who Generates the 48 kHz PWM

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot. It does **not** generate the 48 kHz motor PWM.

```
 6S LiPo ──► Bus caps ──► 4× [ESC MCU ──► 3-ph gate driver ──► 6 MOSFETs] ──► Motors
                              ▲  (AM32 / BLHeli_32, e.g. STM32G071 / AT32F421)
                              │  Bidirectional DShot600 (1 wire per motor)
                       STM32H743 (ArduPilot)
```

- **ESC MCUs** own commutation and the 48 kHz complementary PWM.
- **The H743** owns the DShot command and telemetry link, plus the eRP
