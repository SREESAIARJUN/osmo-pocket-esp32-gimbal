## 1. FOC Current Loop (innermost, T_i = 50 µs)

### 1.1 Transforms (power-invariant amplitude, 2-shunt sensing)

**Clarke transform** (with i_c = −i_a − i_b):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform** at electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_off:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant (SPMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\psi_m
$$

### 1.3 Decoupled PI controller (pole-zero cancellation)

Choose the PI zero to cancel the plant pole R/L. The closed loop then becomes first order with bandwidth ω_ci:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}
$$
