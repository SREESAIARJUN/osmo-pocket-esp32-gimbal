# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC (Job #17): Loss, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. It generates bidirectional DShot600. Each ESC channel runs its own commutation MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, BLHeli_32, or Bluejay. The 48 kHz figure is the **ESC's PWM frequency**, which is configured in the ESC firmware. The H743 does not set it.
