## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step synchronous commutation

Each phase leg is active for 2 of 6 sectors (120°). With complementary PWM at duty D:

- **High-side FET (phase x):** conducts for D of its 1/3 active interval.
- **Low-side FET (same phase):** synchronously rectifies for (1−D) of that interval. It also conducts 100% during the 1/3 interval when phase x is the return path.

The RMS currents over one electrical period are:

$$I_{HS,rms}^2 = \frac{D}{3} I^2, \qquad I_{LS,rms}^2 = \frac{(1-D)}{3} I^2 + \frac{1}{3} I^2$$

Summed over all 6 FETs of one channel, exactly **two FETs carry I at all times**, independent of D:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)}, \qquad R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\big[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\big], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0065\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

### 1.2 Results at 80 A burst (D ≈ 0.95)

| Case | R(T_j = 110 °C) | P_cond per channel | Hottest single FET (LS) | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Typical | 1.55 mΩ | 2·6400·1.55e-3 = **19.8 W** | (0.05/3 + 1/3)·6400·1.55e-3 = **3.5 W** | **79 W** |
| Worst (R_max) | 2.02 mΩ | **25.9 W** | **4.5 W** | **104 W** |

### 1.3 Parasitic copper and shunt losses

These are usually forgotten but are significant at 80 A:

| Element | Resistance | Loss per channel at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase/bus copper, 2 oz, 3 path segments | ≈0.5 mΩ | 3.2 W |
| Current shunt | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |

The shunt on a 4-in-1 board is usually common (DC link). Scale its loss by the sum of channel currents.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

In synchronous 6-step drive, only the PWM-ing high-side FET hard-switches. The low-side commutates via its body diode (ZVS), so it contributes dead-time and Q_rr losses instead.

### 2.1 Transition times (gate-charge method)

$$t_{on} = \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}},\quad I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{g,on}} = \frac{12-3.5}{10} = 0.85\ \text{A} \Rightarrow t_{on} = 22\ \text{ns}$$

$$t_{off} = \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,off}},\quad I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{g,off}} = 0.58\ \text{A} \Rightarrow t_{off} = 33\ \text{ns}$$
