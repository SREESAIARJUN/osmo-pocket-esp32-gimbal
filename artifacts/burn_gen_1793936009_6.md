## 2. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 2.1 Transforms

Electrical angle with encoder offset:

$$\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$$

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

Inverse Park is the transpose. Use θ_e advanced by half a PWM period, θ_e + 1.5·ω_e·T_s, to compensate for computation and PWM delay.

### 2.2 Machine model (surface PMSM, L_d ≈ L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

$$\tau_e = \tfrac32\,p\,\lambda_m\, i_q = K_t\, i_q$$

### 2.3 Current PI with decoupling and pole-zero cancellation
