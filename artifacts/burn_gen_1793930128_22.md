## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Assumptions

Some architectural facts shape everything below.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU. Typical choices are AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to those MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, not the H743.
2. **"Nano" plus 80 A is a severe constraint.** On a 20×20 mm or even 30×30 mm board, 80 A is achievable only as a short burst of seconds. Section 3 quantifies this. Don't let marketing put "80A" on the box without the burst duration attached.
3. **Every component value below is a parametric placeholder.** Replace each one with the datasheet values of the parts you actually select. The math is structured so you can substitute directly.
