## 0. Architecture Clarification and Design-Risk Flags

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own local MCU (typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). The interface between the two is 4× bi-directional DShot600, plus optionally one ESC telemetry UART, a current-sense ADC line, and VBAT.

**Critical risks to resolve before layout:**

| # | Risk | Consequence |
|---|------|-------------|
| R1 | 80 A per channel × 4 = **320 A battery burst**. Mass-loss on XT60/XT90 leads, bus bar, and input caps dominates. | A "nano" (20×20 / 25.5×25.5 mm) form factor cannot carry this continuously. The model below shows it is **burst-limited to ~5 s**. |
| R2 | Bus-cap ripple current at 320 A pulsed is roughly 0.5·I_bus RMS. | Need ≥ 1000 µF low-ESR external plus ceramics. On-board caps alone will fail. |
| R3 | A 6S overshoot at a 25.2 V bus with 40 V FETs leaves thin margin. | Layout loop inductance must be < 2 nH per leg, or move to 60 V FETs (higher R_DS(on)). |

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_DS = 0.75 × 1.383 = **1.04 mΩ**.

### 2.2 Six-step complementary (synchronous) PWM

In each 60° sector:
- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D, and the synchronous low-side conducts for (1−D).
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET is fully on.

The total bridge conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = I^2 R_{DS}[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS} = 2 I^2 R_{DS}$$

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.04\text{ mΩ} = \mathbf{13.3\ W}$$

### 2.3 Per-FET distribution

Averaged over 6 sectors, each leg is active 4/6 of the time.

- **Worst-case low-side FET** (low throttle, D → 0): ≈ I²R·(4/6) = **4.4 W**
- **High-side FET at D ≈ 0.9 (burst):** ≈ I²R·(2/6)·D + (return share) ≈ 2.0–2.5 W

### 2.4 Copper and PCB losses (often forgotten)

Assume a phase path of 0.3 mΩ and a bus share of 0.2 mΩ per channel:

$$P_{Cu} \approx 2 I^2 (0.3\text{ mΩ}) + I^2 (0.2\text{ mΩ}) = 3.84 + 1.28 = \mathbf{5.1\ W}$$

This heats the board directly and is included in the board-level thermal model.

## 3. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

### 3.1 Transition times (Miller-plateau model)

$$t_{on} \approx \frac{Q_{gd} R_{g,on}}{V_{drv} - V_{pl}} = \frac{14\text{n}\cdot 5}{7} = 10.0\text{ ns}$$

$$t_{off} \approx \frac{Q_{gd} R_{g,off}}{V_{pl}} = \frac{14\text{n}\cdot 4}{3} = 18.7\text{ ns}$$

### 3.2 Loss components (per channel, one hard-switched leg active)

| Component | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V·I overlap | ½ V I (t_on + t_off) f_sw | ½·25.2·80·28.7n·48k = **1.39 W** |
| Dead-time diode | 2 V_f I t_dt f_sw | 2·0.8·80·200n·48k = **1.23 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr V f_sw | **0.07 W** |
| C_oss | Q_oss V f_sw | **0.07 W** |
| Gate drive (driver + R_g) | 2 Q_g V_drv f_sw | **0.09 W** |
| **Switching subtotal** | | **≈ 2.85 W** |

**Design lever:** dead-time loss is about 45 % of switching loss. Reducing t_dt to 100 ns saves 0.6 W per channel, but only if the measured gate waveforms show no shoot-through margin violation.
