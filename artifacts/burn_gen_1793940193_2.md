## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path

With 6-step trapezoidal commutation and synchronous rectification, current always flows through exactly **two** FETs in series:
- one high-side or low-side FET of the PWM phase (complementary pair alternates by duty D), and
- the low-side FET of the return phase.

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot \left[ D \cdot R_{HS} + (1-D)\cdot R_{LS} + R_{LS,ret} \right] \approx 2 \, I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

### 2.2 Temperature-corrected resistance

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha = \frac{k_T - 1}{100} = 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.2 × 1.5 = **1.8 mΩ**.

### 2.3 Result at 80 A

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.8\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{23.0\ W}
$$

### 2.4 PCB copper loss (frequently omitted)

Phase pad to FET trace: 2 oz copper, effective 4 parallel layers, 5 mm wide, 8 mm long.

$$
R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho L}{w\,t\,n} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\cdot 0.008}{0.005 \cdot 70\times10^{-6}\cdot 4} \approx 98\ \mu\Omega \;(\times1.3 \text{ at } 100^\circ\text{C}) \approx 0.13\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

Over roughly 3 segments in the current path: P_Cu ≈ 80² × 0.39 mΩ ≈ **2.5 W per channel**.

### 2.5 Thermal-runaway check

Conduction loss rises with T_j, which raises R_DS(on). The loop is stable if:

$$
\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\cdot R_{\theta,total} < 1 \;\Rightarrow\; 2I^2 R_{25}\alpha \cdot R_\theta = 0.077\cdot R_\theta < 1
$$

This requires the effective R_θ per channel (junction to ambient) to stay below **~13 K/W**. Nano boards without airflow sit near this limit. This is a hard design constraint.

## 3. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the active FET hard-switches. The synchronous FET turns on after its body diode conducts, so it switches at near-ZVS. The penalty for that is dead-time diode loss and Q_rr loss.

### 3.1 Transition time

$$
t_{r} \approx t_f \approx \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_g} = \frac{12\ \text{nC}}{0.5\ \text{A}} = 24\ \text{ns}
