ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The 48 kHz switching runs on four ESC MCUs, and the STM32H743 only sends bi-directional DShot600 commands to them.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths

In 6-step commutation, current always flows through two phase legs in series: one high-side and one low-side. With synchronous rectification, the complementary FET carries the current during the PWM off-time, so the path is always two R_DS(on) in series, regardless of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

Each FET carries current for about 1/3 of the electrical period, so the per-FET share is:

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)}{3}$$

### 1.2 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per channel | P_cond per FET | Four channels |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.80 mΩ | 23.0 W | 3.8 W | 92 W |
| 100 °C | 2.41 mΩ | 30.8 W | 5.1 W | 123 W |
| 125 °C | 2.61 mΩ | 33.4 W | 5.6 W | 134 W |

### 1.3 Electro-thermal fixed point

Let P₀ be the conduction loss at 25 °C and R_th the steady-state thermal resistance to ambient. Then:

$$T_j = T_a + R_{th}\,P_0\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

Solving for T_j:

$$T_j = \frac{T_a + R_{th}P_0(1-25\alpha)}{1 - \alpha R_{th} P_0}$$

The stability (no thermal runaway) condition is:

$$\alpha\, R_{th}\, P_0 < 1$$

Check per FET with P₀ = 3.8 W:

- Stability limit: R_th < 1/(0.0045 × 3.8) = **58 K/W**. This holds for any sensible layout.
- With R_th(j-a, effective) ≈ 25 K/W and T_a = 40 °C, steady-state gives T_j ≈ 213 °C.

**Conclusion: 80 A is strictly a transient rating.** Section 3 quantifies how long it can last.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing leg (one complementary pair per channel) switches. Evaluated at V = 25.2 V, I = 80 A.

| Mechanism | Formula | Result |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw = ½·25.2·80·20 ns·48 kHz | 0.968 W |
| Coss charge | Q_oss·V·f_sw = 30 nC·25.2·48 kHz | 0.036 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 40 nC·25.2·48 kHz | 0.048 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | 2·V_SD·I·t_dt·f_sw = 2·0.8·80·50 ns·48 kHz | 0.307 W |
| Gate drive (dissipated in driver/R_g) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw = 2·40 nC·10 V·48 kHz | 0.038 W |
| **Total per channel** | | **≈ 1.40 W** |

### 2.1 Observations

- At 48 kHz, switching loss is only about 4% of conduction loss at burst. Conduction loss dominates.
- The frequency choice matters mostly at part throttle, for current ripple and motor efficiency.
- Dead-time loss is the second largest switching term. Trimming dead time from 50 ns to 25 ns saves about 0.15 W per channel.
  - Only do this if the driver's propagation-delay mismatch allows it.

### 2.2 Total burst loss

Burst dissipation, all four motors at 80 A, T_j ≈ 100 °C:

$$P_{tot} = 4(30.8 + 1.4) + P_{misc} \approx 129 + \sim3\ (\text{shunts, traces, LDOs}) \approx \mathbf{132\ W}$$

### 2.3 Copper loss check

Trace and plane resistance is a hidden killer. Check it before trusting the 3 W "misc" allowance.

- For a 0.2 mΩ effective bus path at 320 A total battery current at full duty:
  - 320² × 0.2 mΩ ≈ **20 W**.
- **Requirement:** the battery pads to FET drains must use stacked 2 oz pours on at least 4 layers, with via fields of at least 40 vias (0.3 mm) per FET group.
