## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3 × gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 5–12 Ω, L ≈ 2–5 mH |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A, MT6701 or similar), one per axis |
| Driver | 3-phase, low-side or inline shunts, center-aligned PWM at 20 kHz |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with FPU (STM32G4 or H7 class) |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI, gyro ODR 8 kHz |
| Kinematics | Yaw (z) → Roll (x) → Pitch (y), with the camera on the pitch link |
