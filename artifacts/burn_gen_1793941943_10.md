# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

I don't have any specifications for "Job #44". The design below is therefore built on explicit, stated assumptions (§0). Every gain and frequency is derived from symbols, so you can substitute your real motor and frame parameters. Register values for the ICM-42688-P are taken from the public datasheet; check them against the revision you are building with.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 1–3 mH |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701 class), SPI |
| Current sense | 2-shunt, low-side, sampled at PWM center |
| Bus voltage | 12–16 V |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, mounted symmetrically about the payload CoM, separate SPI buses |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with single-precision FPU (e.g. STM32G4/H7) |
