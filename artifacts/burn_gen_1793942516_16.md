# STM32H743 AIO + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #70): Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

These points change the math, so they come first.

1. **The H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** In a 4-in-1 ESC, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071. That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the four ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. Switching losses are therefore set by the ESC firmware's `PWM frequency` setting, not by ArduPilot.
