# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC (Job #19): Electro-Thermal Model, Switching Losses, BDShot DMA Plan, and Netlists

**Scope note:** No MOSFET part, board stack-up or cooling condition was specified, so I fixed a reference design (§0). Every equation is parametric, so you can substitute real datasheet values. Two findings set the physical limits of this job and should be read first:

1. **80A per channel is a short-burst rating only.** It is roughly 4 s from 40 °C on a 30.5×30.5 mm board. The board cannot sustain it, and §4 shows why.
2. **The ESC half-bridges are not driven by the H743.** The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs. Each ESC MCU (an AT32F421 running AM32) generates the 48 kHz PWM. This split is standard and is reflected in the netlists.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R_ds = 1.0 × 1.6 = **1.6 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

- Within each 60° sector, current flows through exactly two FET positions in series: one high-side path and one low-side path.
- In the PWM'd leg, current alternates between the HS and LS FETs, but always through one R_ds.
- So the instantaneous conduction resistance is 2·R_ds regardless of duty cycle.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.6\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{20.5\ W\ per\ channel}$$

Per FET, each device conducts on average about 1/3 of the electrical period:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{20.5}{6} \approx 3.4\ \text{W}$$
