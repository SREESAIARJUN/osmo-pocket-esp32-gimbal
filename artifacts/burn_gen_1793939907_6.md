# Job #59: STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC), Design Model

## 0. Architectural Corrections

Three assumptions in the brief need correcting before any math is meaningful.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** On every production 4-in-1 design, the FETs are driven by dedicated ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600 to those MCUs. The 48 kHz PWM, dead-time and commutation live in the ESC MCUs. This document models it that way. Driving 24 FETs, 4 sensorless BEMF observers and ArduPilot's scheduler from one H743 is not a viable architecture.

2. **"80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 mm) board is only a short transient rating.** Section 3 shows that 4 motors at 80 A dissipate about 60 W into roughly 4 cm² of PCB. That is survivable for about 1–3 s, not continuously. Any datasheet claim of 80 A on this footprint must carry a duration and a starting temperature.

3. **80 A is interpreted as phase current per motor.** In 6-step drive at partial throttle, phase current exceeds battery current (I_batt ≈ D·I_phase). FET heating is set by phase current.

## 2. Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

This couples the electrical and thermal models. Solve iteratively (Section 3.4).

### 2.2 Conduction loss, 6-step with synchronous rectification

In each 60° sector, exactly two legs carry I_ph. One is the PWM leg (HS during D, LS synchronous freewheel during 1−D). The other is the "on" low-side. The current path therefore always crosses two FET channels:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,(R_{DS}(T_j)+R_{pcb})$$

At T_j = 100 °C, R_DS = 0.70·(1 + 0.0055·75) = 0.989 mΩ:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \cdot 6400 \cdot (0.989+0.25)\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{15.9\ W}$$

Of this, about 12.7 W is in the silicon and 3.2 W is in copper. The copper share still heats the board.

Per-FET average (6 FETs share the duty roughly evenly over an electrical cycle):

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{2 I^2 R_{DS}}{6} = 2.11\ \text{W}$$
