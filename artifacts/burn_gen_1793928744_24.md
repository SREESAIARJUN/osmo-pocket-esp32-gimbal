## 0. Scope and Critical Assumptions

Four architectural corrections drive everything below.

| # | Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | ArduPilot does not run the ESC commutation loop. The H743 is the flight controller only. | Four dedicated ESC MCUs run AM32 (6-step, complementary PWM). Examples: STM32G071 or AT32F421. The H743 drives them over bidirectional DShot600. |
| A2 | "Nano" form factor | Assumed 20×20 mm (M2 mount), 6-layer, 2 oz outer and 1 oz inner copper. H743 in **TFBGA100** (H743VIH6, 8×8 mm). LQFP100 at 14×14 mm will not fit with 24 FETs. |
| A3 | Supply voltage | 6S Li-ion/LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V. Requires **40 V** MOSFETs for derating against inductive overshoot. |
| A4 | "80 A burst" | Interpreted as 80 A RMS phase current per channel, all four channels simultaneously, worst case. Section 3 shows this is **only ~1–2 s achievable** at 20×20. Treat the rating as a burst limit, not a capability. |

All device parameters below are **representative placeholders** for a 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PQFN-class FET. Substitute the selected part's datasheet values. The model is fully parametric.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]$$

At T_j = 100 °C:

$$R_{DS} = 1.2\times(1+0.0045\times75) = 1.605\ \text{m}\Omega$$

Use **1.74 mΩ** to include package and spreading resistance at 100 °C.
