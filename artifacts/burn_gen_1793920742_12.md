**Two architectural corrections up front:**

1. **The H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** The flight controller (STM32H743 running ArduPilot) sends bidirectional DShot600 to the ESC. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32) plus a 3-phase gate driver. Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM. The loss model applies to the ESC board. The DMA model applies to the FC.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano board is a short-duration thermal-mass rating, not a steady-state rating.** Four channels at 80 A on 6S is about 8 kW of throughput and about 100 W of heat on a board with roughly 5–6 J/K of heat capacity. The math below makes this explicit:
   - The burst is about 4 s from a warm start.
   - Continuous capability is about 34 A per motor in prop wash.
   - A true 20×20 mm "nano" format cannot meet 80 A. That is why 30.5 mm is specified.

## 2. Loss Model per ESC Channel

### 2.1 Conduction Losses (6-step, synchronous rectification)

In any commutation sector, two phases carry the motor current I:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. The low-side FET conducts for (1 − D) via synchronous rectification. Some body-diode time is handled separately in Section 2.2.
- **Static leg:** one low-side FET is fully on for the entire sector.
- **Floating leg:** off (BEMF sensing).

Instantaneous conduction power:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds(hot)}
$$

This is independent of D under synchronous rectification. At I = 80 A and R_ds(hot) = 1.5 mΩ:

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \times 6400 \times 0.0015 = \mathbf{19.2\ W}
$$

**Per-FET distribution.** Each FET carries current for 120° of the 360° electrical period, so the average per FET is 19.2 / 6 = 3.2 W. The instantaneous peak in the static low-side FET is I²R = 9.6 W.

**Copper and connection losses.** These cannot be ignored at 80 A. Assume:
- PCB trace and via path: about 0.4 mΩ per phase loop.
- Motor solder pad: about 0.2 mΩ.

This adds 6400 × 0.0006 × 2 phases ≈ **7.7 W**, spread across the board. This is a strong argument for 2 oz outer copper and via-stitched phase pours.

$$
