# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #75): Power-Stage Loss Model, DShot DMA Allocation, Netlists

## 0. Architecture and Critical Review

Two things need to be settled before any math.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 as the flight controller. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, because both firmwares handle bidirectional DShot and eRPM telemetry. This design uses 4× AT32F421 running AM32.

```
6S Battery ──► Power stage (4 × 3-phase bridges, 24 FETs)
                 ▲ gate drive (4 × FD6288Q-class 3-phase drivers)
                 ▲ 4 × AT32F421 (AM32)  ◄── bidir DShot600 ──  STM32H743 (ArduPilot)
```

**2. "80A burst" must be defined, because it drives the whole design.** If it means 80 A on all four channels simultaneously, the board sees 320 A of battery input at roughly 8 kW. A nano-format board (20×20 to 25.5×25.5 mm) cannot carry that input current through its battery pads and copper, and it cannot absorb the resulting ~85 W of heat for more than a few seconds.

This document therefore uses the following spec:
- 80 A per channel for ≤ 2 s bursts.
- Simultaneous 4-channel bursts are allowed, but they are thermally time-limited.
- Continuous rating is about 35–40 A per channel.

The numbers below show why this spec is necessary.

**Assumed MOSFET.** No MOSFET part was specified. All numbers use a representative 40 V, 5×6 DFN device. Substitute your part's datasheet values into the formulas.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds ≈ 0.9 × 1.55 ≈ **1.40 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification

**Key simplification:** at every instant, exactly two FETs carry the phase current. During the PWM-on interval, these are the high side of the PWM phase and the low side of the sink phase. During the PWM-off interval, the high side is replaced by the complementary low side of the PWM phase. Therefore, independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch}=2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)+I^2 R_{Cu}$$

**Per-FET distribution** (each phase is active for 1/3 of the electrical cycle; D is the PWM duty cycle):

| FET | Average loss |
|---|---|
| High side | $P = I^2 R\,\tfrac{D}{3}$ |
| Low side, complementary | $P = I^2 R\,\tfrac{1-D}{3}$ |
| Low side, sink phase | $P = I^2 R\,\tfrac{1}{3}$ (worst-case FET) |

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A and T_j = 125 °C

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Instantaneous loss in each conducting FET | 80² × 1.4 mΩ = **8.96 W** |
| Channel FET conduction loss | 2 × 8.96 = **17.9 W** |
| Copper loss (≈0.3 mΩ effective round-trip path, 2 oz inner layers) | 80² × 0.3 mΩ = **1.9 W** |
| Worst single FET, sink-phase low side (averaged over cycle) | 8.96 / 3 = **3.0 W** |
| **Channel conduction total** | **≈ 19.8 W** |
