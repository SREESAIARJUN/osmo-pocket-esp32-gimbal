# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have the specification for "Job #22", so this design rests on the assumptions in §0. Every parameter is a named constant that you can replace with your measured values. Register values for the ICM-42688-P are taken from the datasheet as I recall it (DS-000347). Verify them before you flash, particularly the AAF table.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal PMSM, 24N22P (GM4108-class), p = 11 pole pairs, R_s ≈ 10 Ω, L_s ≈ 4 mH, λ_m from K_v |
| Driver | 3-phase, 24 kHz center-aligned PWM, 2-shunt or inline current sensing |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701 class) per axis |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 24 MHz, ODR 8 kHz |
| Kinematics | Outer yaw (z), middle roll (x), inner pitch (y) |
