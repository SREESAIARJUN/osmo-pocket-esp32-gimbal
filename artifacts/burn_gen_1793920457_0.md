## 0. Design Basis and Clarifications

These points change the math, so they come first.

1. **The STM32H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** In every production 4-in-1 stack, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM and commutation. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 commands. The loss model in §1 to §3 applies to the ESC power stage. §4 applies to the H743.
2. **"80 A burst" is defined here as per-motor phase current for 2 s or less, one or two motors at a time.** Four motors at 80 A simultaneously would draw about 320 A from the battery. A nano-class power entry (XT60, 14 AWG pigtail, single pad pair) cannot carry that. §3.4 quantifies this.
3. **"Nano" is taken as a 30.5 × 30.5 mm board.** It uses 24 × PQFN 5×6 FETs and 6-layer 2 oz copper. A 20 × 20 board physically cannot fit 5×6 FETs for an 80 A burst rating.
