## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Findings

These findings change the design, so they come first.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** Bi-directional DShot is a protocol between a flight controller and *separate* ESC MCUs. The correct architecture is:
- **STM32H743** runs ArduPilot and emits DShot600.
- **4× ESC MCUs** (STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32) each drive one 3-phase bridge at 48 kHz PWM and reply with eRPM telemetry.

**2. 80 A per channel cannot be sustained on a "nano" board.** In 20×20 mm or 30.5×30.5 mm form factors, 80 A per channel is only achievable as a burst of roughly **1–2 s**. Section 3 derives this. A 10 s burst or a continuous rating near 80 A requires a larger board, heavier copper, or a heatsink.

**3. "80 A" is defined here as motor phase current during a conduction interval.** Battery current is D·I_ph. At full throttle, 4 × 80 A means up to **320 A from the battery**. The battery, XT90-class connector, and pigtail must be specified accordingly.

### Baseline Assumptions

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V |
| Board | 30.5×30.5 mm mounting, ~32×32 mm outline, 8-layer, 2 oz on all layers |
| MOSFET (×24) | 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PQFN, R_DS(on),25 = 1.1 mΩ @ V_GS = 10 V |
| MOSFET gate/charge | Q_g = 40 nC, Q_gd = 8 nC, Q_rr ≈ 50 nC, Q_oss ≈ 25 nC, V_SD = 0.8 V |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q-class 3-phase driver, V_CC = 10 V, ~1 A source / 1.5 A sink, R_g,ext = 2.2 Ω |
| PWM | f_sw = 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), t_dead = 50 ns |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal (AM32 default) |
