## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke transform, assuming balanced currents so that i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Electrical angle:

$$
\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}
$$

Park transform:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Electromagnetic torque:

$$
\tau = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q
$$

### 1.3 Decoupled PI controller (pole-zero cancellation)

The controller is

$$
v_d^* = \underbrace{K_p e_d + K_i\!\int e_d}_{PI} - \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q^* = K_p e_q + K_i\!\int e_q + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

Choose the gains so the PI zero cancels the plant pole at R/L:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c
$$

The closed loop then reduces to a first-order system:

$$
\frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

Keep ω_c ≤ 2π f_pwm / 10 to stay clear of the PWM/sample delay.
