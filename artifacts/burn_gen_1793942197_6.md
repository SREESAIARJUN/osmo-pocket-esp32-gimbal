**Architectural note.** The STM32H743 is the *flight controller* MCU. In a 4-in-1 ESC, each motor is commutated by its own ESC MCU, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The 48 kHz PWM and dead time are therefore ESC-firmware parameters. The H743 is responsible for DShot generation and bidirectional telemetry capture. The model below keeps those two domains separate.

**Feasibility flag (read first).** As §4 shows, 80 A per motor across all four channels simultaneously means roughly 90 W of heat on a 30 × 30 mm board. That is only survivable as a *time-limited* burst of about 2–3 s. It is not a continuous rating. The "80 A" figure should be defined in the spec as a burst current with an explicit duration.
