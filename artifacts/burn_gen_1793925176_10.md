## 0. Architectural Clarification and Assumptions

**The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** On an AIO board the STM32H743 is the flight controller running ArduPilot. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example 4× AT32F421K8U7 or one per motor. The H743 talks to these MCUs over bi-directional DShot600. This split governs both the netlist and the DMA plan.
