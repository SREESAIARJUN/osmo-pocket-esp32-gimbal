# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC: System Architecture Package (Job #11)

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

No spec sheet for Job #11 was provided, so every number below rests on stated assumptions (Table 0.1). All equations are parametric. Substitute your real MOSFET datasheet values and re-run Section 3.

There is one architectural point to fix first. In ArduPilot builds, the STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU. It generates bi-directional DShot600. The 48 kHz PWM and 6-step commutation are produced by the ESC's own MCU(s), such as AM32 on STM32G071/AT32F421, or BLHeli_32. The H743 does not drive gates directly. The package therefore covers two boards: the FC (H743, DShot/DMA) and the ESC (power stage, losses, thermal).
