# 3-Axis Gimbal FOC Control and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection: Design and C++ Implementation

> **Assumptions.** I don't have the "Job #11" specification, so this design rests on the typical hardware for a 3-axis gimbal, as listed below.
> - **Motors and encoder:** 12N14P gimbal BLDC motors (pole pairs p = 7) with a 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A or MT6701).
> - **MCU:** STM32G4-class at 170 MHz with an FPU.
> - **IMUs:** two ICM-42688-P units on SPI at 24 MHz, both rigidly mounted on the camera plate.
>
> Every hardware-dependent constant is a named parameter, so you can substitute your own values. Register bit-fields for the ICM-42688-P are from the DS-000347 datasheet. Confirm them against your silicon revision before you fly.
