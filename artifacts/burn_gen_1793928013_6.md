## 0. Architecture and Key Findings

### 0.1 System partitioning
- **STM32H743 (FC):** runs ArduPilot. It sends DShot600 frames and decodes the bidirectional eRPM telemetry.
- **Four ESC MCUs (one per motor):** run AM32 (or equivalent) on STM32G071 / AT32F421-class parts. They perform the 48 kHz complementary PWM and commutation.
- **Why not the H743 for commutation:** it does not commutate motors directly. Running four sensorless FOC/6-step loops plus ArduPilot on one die is not a supported configuration.

```
VBAT(6S) ─┬─ Bulk C ─┬─ ESC1 [MCU + 3ph driver + 6× FET] → M1
          │          ├─ ESC2 ...                          → M2
          │          ├─ ESC3 ...                          → M3
          │          └─ ESC4 ...                          → M4
          └─ BEC 5V/3V3 → STM32H743 FC
                 TIM3_CH3/CH4, TIM5_CH1/CH2 ⇄ DShot600 bidir (1 wire/ESC)
```

### 0.2 Key findings
1. **80 A per motor is a burst rating, not continuous, on a nano footprint.**
   - Each motor dissipates about **26 W** at 80 A, so all four together dissipate about **105 W**.
   - The board's adiabatic budget is about **2–3 s** of simultaneous 80 A.
   - Continuous capability with prop-wash cooling is about **25–30 A per motor**.
2. **Battery current at 4 × 80 A is about 300 A.** This exceeds XT60 ratings, typical 6S pack C-ratings, and most 12 AWG lead ratings. The burst limit is a protection envelope, not a design operating point.
3. **The electrothermal coupling is positive feedback** (Rds rises with temperature). Section 2.4 gives the closed-form stability criterion.
