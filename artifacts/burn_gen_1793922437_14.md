### Architecture clarification

ArduPilot running on the H743 does **not** commutate motors. The correct topology is:

- The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 to 4 dedicated ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32).
- Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver, which drives 6 MOSFETs.

Bidirectional DShot carries the eRPM telemetry back to the H743 for the harmonic notch filter.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

With complementary PWM in 6-step commutation:

- **Driven leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. The low-side FET of the same leg conducts for (1−D), minus dead time.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET is on continuously.

At any instant, the phase current I flows through **exactly two channels** (two FETs, or one FET plus a body diode during dead time). Therefore:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j)\cdot(1 - 2 t_{dt} f_{sw})$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence of R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ /\text{K}\ \ (\text{40 V trench, typical})$$

| T_j (°C) | R_ds(on) (mΩ) | P_cond per channel at 80 A (W) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | 1.20 | 15.4 |
| 100 | 1.61 | 20.6 |
| 125 | 1.74 | 22.3 |
| 150 | 1.88 | 24.1 |

Dead-time correction: with t_dt = 100 ns, the factor is (1 − 2·100 ns·48 kHz) = 0.99, which is negligible.

### 1.3 Distribution per FET

Each phase leg conducts for 2/3 of the electrical cycle. Averaged over many electrical revolutions, the loss spreads over 6 FETs:

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} = \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6} \approx 3.4\ \text{W at } T_j = 100\,°\text{C}$$

**Low-RPM / stall caveat:** at low eRPM (e.g., a stalled or yanked motor during a punch-out), one FET pair can carry the full current for milliseconds to seconds. In that case:

$$P_{FET,peak} = I^2 R_{ds(on)} \approx 10.3\ \text{W}$$

Design the thermal path for this peak value, not the average.

### 1.4 Electro-thermal coupling

Loss depends on junction temperature, and junction temperature depends on loss. Solve iteratively. The fixed point exists only if:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}\cdot R_{\theta JA} < 1 \;\Rightarrow\; 2I^2R_{25}\alpha\, R_{\theta,eff} < 1$$

At 80 A: 2·6400·1.2e-3·0.0045 = 0.069 W/K. Thermal runaway therefore occurs if the effective channel R_θ exceeds about **14.5 K/W**. A bare nano board in still air is around 15–25 K/W, which is why **80 A is burst-only**.

## 2. Switching-Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Per channel, only the PWM'd leg switches hard. The return-leg low-side FET is static.

### 2.1 V–I overlap (hard switching)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 40\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.06\ \text{W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery of the low-side body diode

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.05\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_F\, I\, (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot200\text{ns}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

This loss is dissipated mostly in the driver and the gate resistors:

$$P_{gate} = 2\, Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} = 2\cdot60\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k} = 0.06\ \text{W}$$

### 2.6 Switching total

$$P_{sw,ch} \approx 1.94 + 0.06 + 0.05 + 0.61 = \mathbf{2.66\ W\ per\ channel}$$

**Levers to reduce it:**
- Dead time is about 23% of switching loss. Tune it down to about 50 ns if the driver and FETs allow.
- 24 kHz PWM halves switching loss, at the cost of more audible noise and current ripple. AM32 also supports variable PWM frequency.
