**Two architectural points that shape the rest of this document:**

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1 stack, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The 48 kHz PWM is generated there, not on the H743.
