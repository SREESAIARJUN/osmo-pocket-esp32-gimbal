## 1. Conduction-Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current-path topology

In 6-step commutation with complementary PWM, every electrical cycle divides each leg's time into three roles:

| Leg role (each 1/3 of cycle) | High-side FET on-fraction | Low-side FET on-fraction |
|---|---|---|
| PWM leg | D | 1 − D (synchronous rectification) |
| Return leg (sink) | 0 | 1 |
| Floating leg | 0 | 0 |

### 1.2 Per-FET average conduction loss

For each FET:

$$
P_{cond,HS} = \frac{D}{3}\, I^2 R_{DS}(T_j), \qquad
P_{cond,LS} = \frac{(1-D) + 1}{3}\, I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

Summing all six FETs of one motor gives a result that is **independent of duty cycle**:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 3\left[\frac{D}{3} + \frac{2-D}{3}\right] I^2 R_{DS} = 2\, I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

The worst single device is the low-side FET at low duty, D → 0:

$$
P_{cond,LS,max} = \tfrac{2}{3}\, I^2 R_{DS}
$$

### 1.3 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T_j = 110 °C:

$$
R_{DS} = 1.2\ \text{m}\Omega \times 1.38 = 1.65\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

Add the PCB/copper path per conduction path. For 2 oz copper, about 4 mm wide pours, about 15 mm of path and vias, R_pcb ≈ 0.4 mΩ per FET path. This is counted as board heating, not FET heating.
