# FOC Control Loop and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have the original spec for Job #44, so this design rests on the assumptions in §0. Wherever a number depends on your hardware, it is computed from parameters rather than hard-coded. Change §0 and the derived gains follow.

## 0. Assumptions and Rate Plan

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 (170 MHz, single-precision FPU, CORDIC) |
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14 (12 slots, 14 poles, pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 10 Ω, L ≈ 2 mH |
| Encoder | 14-bit magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701) over SPI |
| Current sense | 2-shunt low-side. Voltage mode is also supported (§1.6). |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-
