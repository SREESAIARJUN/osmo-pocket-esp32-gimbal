## 0. Scope and Architecture Assumptions

**The H743 does not commutate the motors.** On every production 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel (or all four) runs on a dedicated small MCU, typically AM32 or BLHeli_32 on an STM32G071 or AT32F421. The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600 to those MCUs. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCU, not the H743.

**"Nano" and 80 A are in tension.** Section 3 shows that 80 A per motor is a seconds-scale burst rating on any 20×20 to 30×30 board, not a continuous one. All numbers below assume the following baseline. Change the inputs and re-run the equations.

|---|---|---|

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step synchronous PWM

At any instant two phases are active.

- **PWM-on interval (duty D):** current flows HS(A) → motor → LS(B).
- **PWM-off interval (1−D):** current freewheels LS(A, synchronous rectification) → motor → LS(B).

Either way, the current always passes through **two channels in series**. Per motor:

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph,rms}^2 R_{Cu}$$

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

Ripple correction for a triangular ripple Δi:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \Delta i^2/12$$

With motor L ≈ 10 µH, Δi is a few amps, so the correction is under 0.1 % at 80 A. It is neglected below.
