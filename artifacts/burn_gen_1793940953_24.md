# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Model (Job #17)

## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Assumptions

### Architecture boundary

The STM32H743 does not commutate the MOSFETs. The ESC uses four dedicated ESC MCUs, for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to those four MCUs. The 48 kHz switching frequency is therefore an ESC-firmware parameter (AM32 `PWM_FREQ`), not an H743 timer.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation, two switch positions always carry the phase current I: one high side and one low side of different legs. With complementary PWM, the "high side" position alternates with its own low-side partner during the off-time, but the current still crosses exactly two R_ds(on) at every instant.

The channel conduction loss is:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

Each individual FET carries this for about 1/3 of the electrical cycle, plus its synchronous-rectification share. Averaged, the per-FET loss is:

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left(\frac{T_j + 273}{298}\right)^{2.3}$$

| T_j (°C) | Multiplier | R_ds(on), using 1.0 mΩ max |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | 1.00 | 1.00 mΩ |
| 100 | 1.53 | 1.53 mΩ |
| 125 | 1.72 | 1.72 mΩ |

### 1.3 Burst evaluation at 80 A

Use the 1.0 mΩ max value at T_j ≈ 100 °C, giving R = 1.53 mΩ.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.53\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.6\ W}$$

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx 6.5\ \text{W per FET}$$

### 1.4 Additional copper and contact losses

- **Busbar/plane plus solder pads:** assume ~0.3 mΩ round-trip per channel. This adds 80² × 0.3 mΩ = **1.9 W per channel**, dissipated in the PCB rather than the FET.
- **Shunt:** 0.25 mΩ (shared DC shunt, total battery current). Battery current is about D·I_phase. At full throttle the 4 × 80 A burst through the shunt gives 320² × 0.25 mΩ = **25.6 W**. This is not viable, so use a Hall sensor or per-channel sensing. **Design decision: use a Hall-effect sensor such as ACS772, or a 0.1 mΩ Kelvin shunt (10 W burst, 2512 × 2 parallel).**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one leg switches per commutation sector (the PWM leg), so the following terms are per channel.

### 2.1 Gate currents

Turn-on (charging through the plateau):

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_{src} + R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int}} = \frac{12-3}{4+2.2+1} = 1.25\ \text{A}$$

Turn-off (discharging):

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{snk} + R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int}} = \frac{3}{2+2.2+1} = 0.58\ \text{A}$$

### 2.2 Transition times

The switching charge is Q_sw = Q_gs2 + Q_gd = 15 nC.

$$t_{on} = \frac{15\,\text{nC}}{1.25} = 12\ \text{ns}$$

$$t_{off} = \frac{15\,\text{nC}}{0.58} = 26\ \text{ns}$$

### 2.3 Loss components

**V–I overlap:**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V I (t_{on} + t_{off}) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 38\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{1.84\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery:**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V f_{sw} = 80\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.097\ \text{W}$$

In practice, add about 2× for the overlap current spike: **≈ 0.2 W**.

**Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2} C_{oss,er} V^2 f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 1.5\,\text{n} \times 635 \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.023\ \text{W}$$

**Body-diode conduction during dead time:**

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD} \cdot I \cdot 2 t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**Gate drive** (dissipated in the driver and resistors):

$$P_g = 2 Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} = 2 \times 50\,\text{n} \times 12 \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.058\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Switching subtotal

**P_sw,ch ≈ 1.84 + 0.2 + 0.02 + 0.61 + 0.06 ≈ 2.7 W per channel**
