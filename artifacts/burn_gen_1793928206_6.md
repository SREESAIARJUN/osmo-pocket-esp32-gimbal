### Architectural note (important)

The STM32H743 has only **two** advanced-control timers with complementary outputs and dead-time (TIM1 and TIM8). It therefore **cannot** natively drive four 3-phase bridges, which would need 12 complementary pairs.

The correct partitioning is:

- **H743**: flight controller only. It generates **4× bidirectional DShot600**.
- **4× ESC MCUs** (e.g., STM32G071 or AT32F421, running AM32/BLHeli_32): each drives one bridge from its own TIM1 at 48 kHz.

The power-loss math (Sections 1–3) applies to each ESC channel. The DMA allocation (Section 4) applies to the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step operation with synchronous rectification, two phases are energized in any 60° sector. Take high-side A and low-side B as the active pair:

- **During D·T_sw:** current flows through Q_AH, the motor, then Q_BL.
- **During (1−D)·T_sw:** current freewheels through Q_AL (synchronous), the motor, then Q_BL.

At every instant, **exactly two FETs carry I_ph**, ignoring dead time. The total channel conduction loss is therefore **independent of duty cycle**:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \, R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

Conduction loss and junction temperature are coupled, which gives a closed-form fixed point:

$$
T_j = T_{ref} + R_{th,eff}\,\big(P_0 [1+\alpha(T_j-25)] + P_{other}\big)
\;\Rightarrow\;
T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + R_{th,eff}\,(P_0(1-25\alpha) + P_{other})}{1 - \alpha R_{th,eff} P_0}
$$

Here P₀ = 2 I² R₂₅. **Thermal runaway** occurs if α · R_th,eff · P₀ ≥ 1.
