## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

With synchronous rectification in 6-step, at every instant two phases carry I_ph.

- **During PWM-on:** the current passes through the HS FET of leg A and the LS FET of leg B.
- **During PWM-off:** the current passes through the LS FET of leg A and the LS FET of leg B.

Either way, the current always crosses exactly **two** channel resistances. Bridge conduction loss is therefore independent of the duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

Use a linear temperature coefficient for R_ds,on:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.007\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

This gives about 1.7× at 125 °C. Check α against the R_ds vs. T_j curve of the chosen part.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | 2·I²·R_ds | Per 4-in-1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W | 61.4 W |
| 110 °C | 1.91 mΩ | 24.5 W | 98.0 W |

### 1.4 Copper loss

Assume a phase/bus path of about 15 mm at 6 mm width, 2 oz copper on two layers in parallel:

- Sheet resistance of 2 oz copper is about 0.25 mΩ/□.
- 15 mm / 6 mm = 2.5 squares, so 0.62 mΩ per layer, or about 0.31 mΩ for two layers.
- Count about 0.3 mΩ for the bus path plus the equivalent for the phase pad.
- R_Cu ≈ 0.4 mΩ ⟨P⟩, giving **P_Cu ≈ 6400 × 0.4 mΩ ≈ 2.6 W per ESC**.

Copper loss is not negligible at this scale. Stitch the B+/B− pours with dense via fields.

### 1.5 Electrothermal stability

Conduction loss rises with temperature, so check that the junction cannot run away:

$$\frac{dP}{dT_j} \cdot R_{\theta,JA,eff} < 1$$

Per FET, dP/dT = 24.5 W / 6 × (0.007 / 1.595) ≈ 0.018 W/K. With R_θ,eff ≈ 10 K/W local, the loop gain is 0.18, which is stable. The loop gain becomes problematic only above about 55 K/W, for example with a delaminated pad or a voided thermal attach.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches, and each switching event is one FET turning on while its complement turns off.

- Turn-on: I_g,on = (V_drv − V_pl) / R_g = (10 − 3) / 5 = **1.4 A**
- Turn-off: I_g,off = V_pl / R_g = 3 / 5 = **0.6 A**
