# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC (Job #45): System Architecture & Analysis

## 0. Architectural Corrections and Assumptions

Several points in the brief need clarifying before the math, because they change the model.

| Brief item | Reality | Design consequence |
|---|---|---|
| "STM32H743 4-in-1 ESC" | The H743 runs ArduPilot (FC side). Each ESC channel's commutation and 48 kHz PWM runs on a **separate ESC MCU** (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class, e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071). | The H743 never generates the 48 kHz bridge PWM. It sends DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. |
| "80A burst" | Assumed **80 A per motor, all four simultaneously** (worst case). The 320 A total is limited by the battery and XT60/XT90 connector. | The thermal model shows this is a **seconds-scale** rating on a nano board, not a continuous one. |
| "Nano" | Assumed a **30.5 × 30.5 mm** ESC, 8-layer, 2 oz copper. A 20 × 20 board roughly halves the burst time. | Board heat capacity is the dominant thermal term. |
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max (worst case for switching loss). | Use 40 V MOSFETs, with TVS clamping ≤ 36 V. |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Topology current path
The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM, which is the AM32/BLHeli_32 default. At any instant, motor current flows through exactly **two** MOSFETs in series:
- the high-side FET, or its complementary low-side FET during the PWM-off phase, of the driven phase; and
- the low-side FET of the return phase.

Both conducting devices share the same R_ds(on), so conduction loss is **independent of duty cycle**:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]$$

The phase current is treated as flat-topped DC, which is valid when L/R ≫ 1/f_sw. Ripple adds a factor of (1 + r²/12), where r = ΔI/I. At 48 kHz with typical motor inductance, r < 0.2, so the correction is under 0.4 %.

### 1.2 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | Per FET | Per channel (2 FETs) | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 6.4 W | 12.8 W | 51.2 W |
| 100 °C | 1.375 mΩ | 8.8 W | 17.6 W | 70.4 W |
| 125 °C | 1.50 mΩ | 9.6 W | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |

Per-FET duty sharing: the return low-side carries current for 1/3 of the electrical cycle, so averaged per device the loss is spread over 6 FETs. **Peak** junction stress, however, is set by the instantaneous 9.6 W. The thermal time constant of a DFN die (~ms) is shorter than a commutation period at low eRPM, so the peak value governs.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the hard-switched device of the PWM'd phase incurs switching loss. Per channel:

$$P_{sw} = \underbrace{\tfrac12 V I (t_r+t_f) f}_{\text{V–I overlap}} + \underbrace{Q_{rr} V f}_{\text{recovery}} + \underbrace{\tfrac12 Q_{oss} V f}_{\text{C}_{oss}} + \underbrace{2\,Q_g V_{GS} f}_{\text{gate (HS+LS)}} + \underbrace{2\,V_{SD}\, I\, t_{dead}\, f}_{\text{dead-time diode}}$$

With V = 25.2 V, I = 80 A, f = 48 kHz:

| Term | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| Overlap | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40 n × 48 k | **1.935 W** |
| Q_rr | 50 n × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.060 W |
| Q_oss | 0.5 × 40 n × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.024 W |
| Gate | 2 × 50 n × 10 × 48 k (dissipated mostly in driver and R_g) | 0.048 W |
| Dead time | 2 × 0.8 × 80 × 100 n × 48 k | **0.614 W** |
| **Total per channel** | | **≈ 2.68 W** |

### 2.1 Total ESC loss at the 80 A burst (T_j = 125 °C)

$$P_{ch} = 19.2 + 2.68 \approx 21.9\ \text{W}, \qquad P_{ESC} \approx 87.6\ \text{W}$$

Conduction is about 88 % of the loss, so **R_ds(on) is the dominant lever**, not switching speed. Raising f_sw to 96 kHz adds only about 2.7 W per channel. Paralleling FETs (2× per position) cuts conduction loss by 50 % if the layout allows it.

### 2.2 Bus capacitor ripple (often the hidden failure)

$$I_{C,rms} = I\sqrt{D(1-D)} \;\Rightarrow\; I_{C,rms,max} = 40\ \text{A per channel at } D = 0.5$$

With four channels interleaved at random phase:

$$\sqrt{4}\times 40 = 80\ \text{A}_{rms}\ \text{worst case}$$

The worst case occurs when all four channels sit at about 50 % duty.

- Use ceramics on each half-bridge: about 4–6 × 10 µF/50 V X7R 1206 per channel.
- Use a bulk low-ESR polymer/electrolytic of ≥ 1000 µF / 35 V at the pads.
- Ceramic ESR heating: about 80² × (ESR_eff ≈ 0.5 mΩ) ≈ 3.2 W, distributed across the array.
