# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model (Job #42)

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Several points in the brief need correcting before any numbers are useful.

1. **The H743 cannot commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is flight-control firmware, not ESC firmware. The correct architecture is:
   - STM32H743 as the flight controller, sending DShot600 to the ESCs.
   - Four dedicated ESC MCUs (e.g. AT32F421 running AM32) generating the 48 kHz PWM.
   - A 3-phase gate driver per motor (FD6288Q class).
   - Six MOSFETs per motor, 24 in total.
2. **80A per motor on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is not thermally achievable**, even as a burst, beyond a few seconds. Section 2 shows the math. It also implies a **320A** battery burst, which exceeds XT60 (~60A continuous) and almost any nano-scale power entry.
3. **All device parameters below are representative values for a 40V DFN3.3×3.3 MOSFET.** Substitute your actual BOM datasheet values. The equations are general; the numbers are only as good as the parameters.

| Symbol | Meaning | Assumed value |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 6S, full charge | 25.2 V |
| I_burst | Per-motor phase current | 80 A |
| R_ds(on),25 | MOSFET on-resistance at 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ (optimistic for 3×3) |
| k_T | Hot-resistance multiplier at T_j ≈ 110 °C | 1.5 |
| t_r + t_f | Rise + fall time | 20 ns |
| Q_rr | Body-diode reverse-recovery charge | 50 nC |
| C_oss(er) | Effective output capacitance | 1.5 nF |
| Q_g | Total gate charge | 60 nC |
| V_gs | Gate drive voltage | 10 V |
| t_dt | Dead time | 100 ns |
| V_f | Body-diode forward voltage | 0.8 V |
| f_sw | Switching frequency | 48 kHz |

## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 1.1 Conduction Loss

With 6-step trapezoidal drive and synchronous rectification, exactly two FETs (one high-side, one low-side) carry the phase current at any instant.

$$R_{ds,hot}=k_T\,R_{ds,25}=1.5\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond}=I^2\cdot 2R_{ds,hot}=80^2\cdot 3\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

Per-FET average: each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period, so 19.2 / 6 = **3.2 W per FET**.

The split between high and low side depends on PWM duty D:
- High-side share: D·I²R
- Low-side share: (1 − D)·I²R

The 80A case is near D ≈ 1.

### 1.2 Hard-Switching (V–I Overlap) Loss

$$P_{sw}=\tfrac12 V_{bus}I(t_r+t_f)f_{sw}=0.5(25.2)(80)(20\text{ns})(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

### 1.3 Reverse Recovery Loss

$$P_{rr}=Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw}=50\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k}= \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$

### 1.4 Output Capacitance Loss

$$P_{oss}=\tfrac12 C_{oss}V_{bus}^2 f_{sw}=0.5(1.5\text{n})(635)(48\text{k})=\mathbf{0.023\ W}$$

### 1.5 Dead-Time Body-Diode Conduction

There are two dead-time intervals per PWM period:

$$P_{dt}=V_f\,I\,(2t_{dt})f_{sw}=0.8\cdot80\cdot200\text{ns}\cdot48\text{k}=\mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 1.6 Gate Drive Loss

This is dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors, not the FETs. Conservatively assuming all 6 gates switch at f_sw:

$$P_g=6\,Q_gV_{gs}f_{sw}=6(60\text{n})(10)(48\text{k})=\mathbf{0.17\ W}$$

### 1.7 Copper and Shunt Loss

Per motor, assuming ~0.5 mΩ of phase copper and a 0.25 mΩ shunt:

$$P_{Cu}\approx 80^2(0.5+0.25)\,\text{m}\Omega \approx \mathbf{4.8\ W}$$

### 1.8 Totals

| Loss term | Per motor (W) | ×4 motors (W) |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 19.2 | 76.8 |
| Switching (overlap) | 0.97 | 3.9 |
| Reverse recovery + C_oss | 0.08 | 0.3 |
| Dead time | 0.61 | 2.4 |
| Gate drive | 0.17 | 0.7 |
| Copper + shunt | 4.8 | 19.2 |
| **Total** | **≈25.8** | **≈103** |

**Key insight:** at 48 kHz, losses are about 75% conduction. The switching frequency is *not* the problem; R_ds(on) and copper are.

Doubling f_sw to 96 kHz adds only about 7 W total. Halving R_ds(on) saves about 38 W.

## 2. Thermal Model

### 2.1 Junction-to-Case (Per FET)

Using R_θJC ≈ 1.5 K/W for DFN3.3×3.3:

$$\Delta T_{jc}=3.2\text{ W}\times1.5=4.8\text{ K}$$

The junction-to-case rise is negligible. **The bottleneck is the board, not the die.**

### 2.2 Board Lumped Thermal Capacitance (20×20 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz)

| Mass | Specific heat | Heat capacity |
|---|---|---|
| FR4 ≈ 1.6 g | 1.1 J/g·K | 1.76 J/K |
| Copper ≈ 1.0 g | 0.385 J/g·K | 0.39 J/K |
| 24 FETs + passives ≈ 2 g | ~0.7 J/g·K | 1.4 J/K |
| **Total C_th** | | **≈3.5 J/K** |

### 2.3 Transient Response (First-Order RC)

$$T(t)=T_0+P\,R_{\theta,BA}\left(1-e^{-t/(R_{\theta,BA}C_{th})}\right)$$

Assume a board-to-air resistance of R_θ,BA ≈ 10 K/W with prop wash. This gives τ = 35 s.

For bursts much shorter than τ, the heating is nearly adiabatic:

$$\frac{dT}{dt}\approx\frac{P}{C_{th}}=\frac{103}{3.5}\approx 29\text{ K/s}$$
