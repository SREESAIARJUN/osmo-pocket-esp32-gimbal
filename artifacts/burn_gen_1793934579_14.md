## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

Some ground rules first, because several of the numbers below depend on them.

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Each motor bridge is commutated by its own small ESC MCU, assumed to be an STM32G071 running AM32 (one per motor, or a 4-channel variant). The H743 never drives gates directly. ArduPilot cannot do sensorless 6-step commutation for 4 motors at 48 kHz PWM, so this is the only viable split.

**Assumed power-stage parameters.** These represent a typical 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm logic-level MOSFET class. **Replace them with your actual datasheet values.** The equations are written so you can substitute directly.

> **Up-front engineering verdict.** 80 A per channel on a nano-class board (≈25×25 mm, 20×20 mount) is achievable **only as a short, thermal-mass-limited burst of roughly 2–3 seconds**. It is not a continuous rating. Section 3 derives this limit.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths per commutation step

In any 6-step sector, two phases conduct.

- **Phase X (PWM'd):**
  - The high-side (HS) conducts for duty D.
  - The low-side (LS) conducts synchronously for (1 − D − 2t_dt·f_sw).
  - The LS body diode conducts during the two dead-time intervals.
- **Phase Y (return):** the LS is on for the full sector.

Using the phase current I:

$$
P_{cond,step} = I^2 R_{HS}\,D + I^2 R_{LS}(1-D) + I^2 R_{LS,Y} \approx 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

The result is **independent of duty**, because complementary PWM always keeps exactly two FET channels in the path. This is the key advantage over non-synchronous (diode-freewheel) modulation.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]
$$

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per channel @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W |
| 100 °C | 1.50 mΩ | 19.2 W |
| 125 °C | 1.67 mΩ | 21.4 W |

### 1.3 Per-device distribution

Over one electrical revolution, each of the 6 FETs conducts in 2 of 6 sectors as the "static" device, plus PWM shares. The averages and peaks are:

- **Average per FET:** P_cond / 6 × 2 ≈ **6.4 W** at 100 °C (the factor of 2 comes from total pair conduction).
- **Instantaneous worst case (LS fully on):** I²R = 80² × 1.5 mΩ = **9.6 W**. This is the value used for junction transients.

### 1.4 Parasitic copper and shunt losses (often ignored, and they dominate)

| Element | Assumed R | Loss @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase/bus copper path (2 oz, 4-layer paralleled pours, vias) | 0.5 mΩ effective | 3.2 W |
| DC-link shunt, per channel | 0.25 mΩ, carrying I·D (worst case D = 1) | ≤ 1.6 W |
