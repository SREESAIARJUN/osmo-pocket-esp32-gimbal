## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$
\theta_e = \operatorname{wrap}_{2\pi}\!\big(p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}})\big), \qquad \theta_m = \frac{2\pi\,N_{\text{enc}}}{2^{14}}
$$

Latency compensation uses the encoder sample age $t_d$ (typically about 1.5 PWM periods):

$$
\hat\theta_e = \theta_e + \omega_e\, t_d
$$

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.3 PMSM model and current PI with decoupling

The dq-frame machine model is:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

The control law (PI plus cross-coupling and back-EMF feedforward) is:

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

where $\lambda_m = K_t / (1.5\,p)$.

**Gains by pole-zero cancellation.** With target bandwidth $\omega_c$ (e.g. $2\pi \cdot 1.5$ kHz), the closed loop becomes $\omega_c/(s+\omega_c)$:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c
$$

**Discrete implementation** (forward Euler on the integrator, sample time $T_s$):

$$
u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\qquad I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s\, e_k + K_{aw}\,(\operatorname{sat}(u_k) - u_k)
$$

The back-calculation anti-windup gain is $K_{aw} = K_i T_s / K_p$.
