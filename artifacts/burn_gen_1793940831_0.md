# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have a spec for "Job #5", so the parameters below are reasonable defaults for a brushless gimbal. They are collected in one config struct so you can replace them.

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Motors | GM4108-class BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 10 Ω, Kt ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701) |
| Current sense | 2-phase inline shunts |
| MCU | STM32G4/H7 class with FPU |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, mounted point-symmetric about the camera's center of rotation |
| Gimbal chain | Base → Yaw (z) → Roll (x) → Pitch (y) → Camera |

**Loop rates**

| Loop | Rate | Target bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current (d/q) | 20 kHz | ≈ 1.5 kHz |
| IMU read and filters | 8 kHz | n/a |
| Velocity (inertial rate) | 8 kHz | ≈ 80–150 Hz |
| Angle (attitude) | 1 kHz | ≈ 15–25 Hz |

The bandwidth ratios are about 10× between current and velocity and 5–8× between velocity and angle.

## 1. FOC Core

### 1.1 Angles

Electrical angle from the mechanical (encoder) angle:

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{m,0}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

Here θ_{m,0} is found at startup by aligning the rotor with V_d > 0, V_q = 0.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

### 1.3 Park and inverse Park

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix},
\qquad
\begin{bmatrix} v_\alpha \\ v_\beta \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & -\sin\theta_e \\ \sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} v_d \\ v_q \end{bmatrix}
$$

**Delay compensation.** PWM update and ADC sampling add about 1.5 samples of transport delay. Compensate by rotating with a predicted angle:

$$
\theta_e^{*} = \theta_e + 1.5\,T_s\,p\,\omega_m
$$
