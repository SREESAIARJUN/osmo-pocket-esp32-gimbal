## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in synchronous six-step

At any instant, one phase is sourced (high-side), one is sunk (low-side), and one floats. With complementary PWM at duty D:

- **On-time (D):** current flows through HS_a → motor → LS_b.
- **Off-time (1−D):** current freewheels through LS_a (synchronous rectification) → motor → LS_b.

In both intervals, exactly **two MOSFETs carry the full phase current I_ph**. Ignoring the short dead-time intervals (handled in §2):

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

This holds regardless of D. That is why low-throttle, high-current conditions (prop-wash recovery, punch-outs from hover) are thermally as bad as full throttle.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence of R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| T_j (°C) | R_ds(on) (mΩ) | P_cond per channel @ 80 A (W) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | 1.20 | 15.4 |
| 75 | 1.56 | 20.0 |
| 100 | 1.74 | 22.3 |
| 125 | 1.92 | **24.6** |

R_ds(on) rises with temperature, so loss and temperature form a positive feedback loop. Solve self-consistently:

$$T_j = T_{board} + R_{\theta,j\text{-}b}\,P_{FET}(T_j)$$
