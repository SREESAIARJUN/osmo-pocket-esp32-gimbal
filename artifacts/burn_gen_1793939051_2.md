## 0. Architecture Assumption

The STM32H743 cannot be both the DShot master and the bridge commutator. Bidirectional DShot is a link between the flight controller and separate ESC MCUs. The standard architecture is therefore:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 480 MHz | ArduPilot. Generates bidirectional DShot600 and decodes eRPM. |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32 | 6-step sync-rectified commutation at 48 kHz PWM, one per motor |
| Gate drivers (×4) | 3-phase half-bridge driver (FD6288-class, 250 mA / 500 mA) or one 1:1 driver per phase | Drive 6 N-FETs per motor |
| Power FETs (×24) | 40 V N-ch, PDFN 3.3×3.3 | 4 motors × 3 phases × (HS + LS) |

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step, Complementary PWM)

During each 60° commutation sector, current flows through two series switch positions:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. Its synchronous low-side partner conducts for (1 − D).
- **Leg B (static leg):** the low-side FET is held on for the whole sector.

The conduction loss per motor, ignoring dead time, is:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

This result is **independent of duty**, which is why 80 A at low throttle heats the stage as much as 80 A at full throttle.

**Temperature dependence**

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]$$

Evaluated at T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.3 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.885 mΩ**.

**Results per motor at 80 A**

| Component | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 2 × 6400 × 1.885 mΩ | **24.1 W** |
| PCB copper | 2 × 6400 × 0.5 mΩ | **6.4 W** |

**Per-FET average.** Each FET conducts over a 120° electrical window. At high RPM the electrical frequency is several kHz, which is far faster than the junction thermal time constant, so the average is what matters:

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{1}{3} I^2 R_{ds} = \frac{6400 \times 1.885\,\text{mΩ}}{3} = 4.0\ \text{W}$$
