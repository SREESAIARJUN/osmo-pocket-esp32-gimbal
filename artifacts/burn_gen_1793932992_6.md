# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #63): System Design Package

**Scope:** Power-stage loss model (80 A burst, 48 kHz), thermal model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and reference netlists.

**Status:** Engineering derivation. Every number below rests on stated assumptions. Validate with the selected MOSFET datasheets, a thermal camera, and bench burn-in before release.

---

## 0. Architectural Reality Check (Read First)

Three constraints shape everything else.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** A 4-in-1 ESC needs dedicated commutation MCUs. This design uses 4× STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32, which supports a 48 kHz PWM setting. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to them over bidirectional DShot600.

2. **"Nano" and 80 A burst conflict.** At 80 A per motor, the bus draws up to 320 A. A 20×20 mm board cannot fit 24 low-R<sub>DS(on)</sub> MOSFETs, the copper cross-section, or the bulk capacitance.
   - **Recommendation:** a 30.5×30.5 mm mount, 8-layer PCB with 2 oz inner and 3 oz outer copper, and an aluminium heat-spreader plate.
   - **Rating:** treat 80 A as a time-limited burst (derived in §3), not a continuous rating.

3. **The FC and ESC sit on one PCB with one ground.** Star-ground the H743 analog and IMU domains away from the phase-current return paths. Wiring rules are in §5.

## 2. Power-Stage Loss Model

### 2.1 Conduction Topology (6-step, complementary/synchronous PWM)

At any instant, two phase legs conduct:
- The **PWM leg**: its high-side (HS) FET conducts for duty D. Its low-side (LS) FET synchronously rectifies for (1−D).
- The **return leg**: its LS FET conducts for 100 % of the 60° sector.

With synchronous rectification, the phase current always flows through exactly two channels:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

Each of the 6 FETs carries current 1/3 of the electrical cycle, averaged over position:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{2 I_{ph}^2 R_{DS(on)}}{6} = \frac{I_{ph}^2 R_{DS(on)}}{3}$$

### 2.2 Temperature-Dependent R<sub>DS(on)</sub>

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,(1+\alpha)^{(T_j-25)}$$

$$R(110\,°C) = 0.90 \times 1.0045^{85} = 0.90 \times 1.465 \approx 1.32\ \text{m}\Omega \;\rightarrow\; \text{use } 1.40\ \text{m}\Omega \text{ (margin)}$$

### 2.3 Conduction Loss at 80 A

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.40\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{17.92\ W\ per\ ESC\ channel}
