# STM32H743 AIO + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #25): Electro-Thermal Model, Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation, and Reference Netlists

## 0. Assumptions and Architecture Notes

I don't have the Job #25 specification, so the baseline assumptions are listed here. Change any of them and re-run the equations; every result below is parameterized.

| Parameter | Assumed value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max | Drives the 40 V MOSFET class |
| Board | 30.5 × 30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner power planes | The 20 × 20 case is covered in §2.6 |
| "80 A burst" | 80 A **per-motor phase current**, ≤ 5 s | It is not a continuous rating |
| ESC MCU | 4 × AT32F421 running AM32 (or BLHeli_32-class) | The H743 never drives gates directly |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q (3-phase, 3 half-bridges) | Or an equivalent bootstrap driver |
| MOSFETs | 40 V, R_DS(on) ≈ 1.2 mΩ max at 25 °C, PQFN 3.3 × 3.3 | Representative of Infineon/Toshiba/AOS 40 V trench parts |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), 6-step | |
| FC firmware | ArduPilot, H743 at 400 MHz, timer clock 200 MHz | 240 MHz if run at 480 MHz |

**Architectural constraint.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller. Each motor bridge is commutated by its own ESC MCU. The H743's job is limited to three things:
- generating four DShot600 streams,
- capturing the four bi-directional eRPM replies,
- reading the aggregate current sensor.

All loss math below therefore lives on the ESC side, and all DMA math lives on the H743 side.

## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 1.1 Conduction path in 6-step complementary PWM

In any commutation sector, phase A is PWM'd, phase B is held low, and phase C floats.

| PWM interval | Conducting devices | Fraction of time |
|---|---|---|
| ON | Q_AH + Q_BL | D |
| OFF (synchronous rectification) | Q_AL + Q_BL | 1 − D − 2t_dt·f_sw |
| Dead time | D_body(AL) + Q_BL | 2t_dt·f_sw |

As a result, **two channels are always in series with the phase current I**, independent of duty cycle (excluding dead time):

$$
P_{cond} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu}
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence of R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × 1.425 ≈ **1.71 mΩ**.

Copper path (MOSFET drain/source pads, phase pad, bus planes): R_Cu ≈ 0.4 mΩ per motor channel. This is estimated from 2 oz copper at 0.5 mΩ/sq/oz ÷ 2 and about 1.5 squares of effective path, with plane paralleling.

### 1.3 Conduction loss at 80 A

$$
P_{cond} = 2(80^2)(1.71\times10^{-3}) + 80^2(0.4\times10^{-3}) = 21.9 + 2.56 = \mathbf{24.4\ W}
$$

This creates a thermal-runaway loop: higher T_j raises R_DS(on), which raises P_cond. It must be solved iteratively (see §2.4).

### 1.4 Switching losses at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd high-side device hard-switches.

**(a) V–I overlap (hard-switched HS):**

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r+t_f)\,f_{sw}
$$

With t_r + t_f ≈ 40 ns (FD6288 at ~1.5 A source and sink, R_g = 10 Ω, Q_gd ≈ 12 nC):

$$
P_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(40\times10^{-9})(48\times10^3) = \mathbf{1.94\ W}
$$

**(b) Body-diode dead-time conduction (LS), with t_dt = 100 ns and V_F ≈ 0.8 V at 80 A:**

$$
P_{dt} = V_F\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8(80)(200\times10^{-9})(48\times10^3) = \mathbf{0.61\ W}
$$

**(c) Reverse recovery, with Q_rr ≈ 40 nC at 80 A and fast di/dt:**

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}
$$

The associated I_rr spike also raises HS turn-on loss by about 10 %. That is folded into (a) as margin.

**(d) Output capacitance, with Q_oss ≈ 45 nC:**

$$
P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw} = \mathbf{0.027\ W}
$$

**(e) Gate charge, with Q_g ≈ 55 nC and V_GS = 10 V, for HS + LS:**

$$
P_g = 2\,Q_gV_{GS}f_{sw} = \mathbf{0.053\ W}
$$

This loss is dissipated mostly in R_g and the driver, not the FET die.

### 1.5 Totals at 80 A, 6S, 48 kHz

| Loss term | W per channel |
|---|---|
| FET conduction (T_j = 110 °C) | 21.9 |
| PCB copper | 2.56 |
| Switching overlap | 1.94 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 |
| Q_rr + Q_oss | 0.08 |
| **FET die total** | **24.5** |
| **Channel total, including copper** | **≈ 27.1** |
| **Board, all 4 motors at 80 A** | **≈ 108 W** |
