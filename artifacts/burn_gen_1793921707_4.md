# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #62): Design Model, Rev A

**Scope:** loss and thermal model for 80 A bursts, switching losses at 48 kHz, ArduPilot bi-directional DShot600 timer/DMA plan, and the top-level netlist.

**Status:** engineering draft. All component parameters are stated assumptions. Substitute datasheet values for the selected parts before releasing this design.

## 0. Feasibility Verdict

At 80 A per motor, one ESC channel dissipates about **23 W**. The whole board dissipates about **95 W** when all four motors are at 80 A. A nano-class PCB (≈27 × 27 mm) has about 4 J/K of thermal mass and cannot reject that power continuously.

- **80 A is achievable only as a time-limited burst, about 1.5–3 s.** Firmware current and thermal limiting must enforce this.
- **Continuous rating is about 35–40 A per channel**, assuming prop-wash convection.
- If marketing requires "80 A burst," define it formally as: *80 A, ≤2 s, ≥10 s recovery at ≤30 A, T_amb ≤ 35 °C.*

The supporting math follows.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.41 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.55 mΩ |

### 2.2 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

In any commutation sector, current flows through exactly one high-side path and one low-side path.

- **Active phase:** during PWM on-time, current flows through HS_x. During off-time it flows through LS_x, the synchronous rectifier. Either way, one FET in that leg carries I.
- **Return phase:** LS_y is held on and carries I continuously.

The motor current therefore always passes through **two series FET channels**:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Per FET, each one conducts during its 120° sector, so the time-averaged loss per FET is about P_cond,ch / 6 when averaged over an electrical revolution.

### 2.3 Values at 80 A

| T_j | P_cond per channel | P per FET (avg) | Instantaneous P in a conducting FET |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 12.8 W | 2.13 W | 6.4 W |
| 100 °C | **18.0 W** | 3.0 W | 9.0 W |
| 125 °C | 19.8 W | 3.3 W | 9.9 W |

### 2.4 Copper and interconnect loss

Assumed per-channel interconnect resistance:

| Path segment | Resistance |
|---|---|
| Battery pads → bus planes (shared, 4 layers in parallel) | 0.15 mΩ |
| Bus → FET → phase pad | 0.25 mΩ |
| Motor wire solder joints | 0.05 mΩ |

Per-channel series total is R_cu ≈ 0.30 mΩ. The shared bus path carries the full sum current.

$$P_{cu,ch} = I^2 R_{cu} = 80^2 \times 0.30\,\text{m}\Omega = 1.92\ \text{W}$$
