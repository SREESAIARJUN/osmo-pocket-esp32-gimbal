## 0. Architecture Clarifications

These points constrain the rest of the design, so they come first.

1. **The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In a 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel has its own MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 only sends DShot600 commands and receives bi-directional eRPM telemetry. The 48 kHz switching frequency is an **ESC firmware setting**, not an ArduPilot parameter.
2. **"80A" must be defined.** I model **80 A phase (motor) current** at high duty (D ≈ 0.9–1.0), where battery current ≈ phase current. This is the worst case for conduction loss.
3. **Form factor.** True 80 A per channel on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is not thermally credible. The model assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm mount, ~36×36 mm board, 8-layer, 2 oz outer / 1 oz inner copper**. Section 3 shows that even this size supports 80 A only as a **burst of a few seconds**.
