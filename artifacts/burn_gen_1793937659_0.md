# Job #70: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC
## Electro-Thermal Model, Bidirectional DShot600 Timer/DMA Allocation, and Reference Netlist

This document derives the requested models and identifies three feasibility risks that must be resolved before layout.

---

## 0. Architectural Constraints and Feasibility Risks

| # | Issue | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot (flight control). It does not commutate the motors directly. | Each ESC channel needs its own MCU, such as AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32. The H743 sends DShot600 to those MCUs. |
| 2 | "Nano" on a 20×20 mm mount means a board of about 27×27 mm. Twenty-four 5×6 mm PQFN MOSFETs (about 30 mm² each, roughly 720 mm² total) do not fit alongside the gate drivers. | Realistic nano ESCs use 3.3×3.3 mm FETs with R_DS(on) of about 1.5–2.5 mΩ. Section 3 models both cases. |
| 3 | 80 A is achievable only as a short burst (a few seconds). | The thermal model shows that continuous capability is about 30–35 A per channel in prop-wash cooling. Any "80 A" marketing claim must state the burst duration. |

**Recommended topology:** stacked or split design. The FC board carries the H743, IMU, baro, OSD, and flash. A separate ESC board carries 4× AT32F421 MCUs, 4× FD6288Q gate drivers, 24 MOSFETs, and current sense. The boards connect through an 8-pin harness (VBAT, GND, M1–M4, CURR, TLM).

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C the multiplier is 1 + 0.0045 × 85 = 1.38. This gives:

- Case A: 1.38 mΩ
- Case B: 2.48 mΩ
