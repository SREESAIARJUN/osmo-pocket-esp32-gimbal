## 0. Architecture and Key Findings

### 0.1 System Partitioning

The STM32H743 cannot directly commutate 4 BLDC motors at 48 kHz while also running ArduPilot. The standard and only sane partition is:

```
┌──────────────── FC domain (STM32H743VIT6, 480 MHz, ArduPilot) ────────────────┐
│ IMU(SPI) · Baro(I2C) · OSD(SPI) · Flash/SD · UARTs · ADC(Vbat, Ibat)          │
│ TIM1/TIM8 → bi-directional DShot600 (×4) ───────────────┐                     │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────┘
                                                           │ 1 wire/motor, 3V3
┌──────────────── ESC domain (4× AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 or BLHeli_32) ──────┐
│ per motor: MCU → 3-phase gate driver → 6× N-MOSFET → phase A/B/C              │
│ 48 kHz complementary PWM, BEMF comparator, shunt current sense                │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 0.2 Headline Results

| Parameter | 5×6 mm FETs (≥30.5 mm board) | 3.3×3.3 mm FETs (true 20×20 nano) |
|---|---|---|
| Loss per channel at 80 A, 25.2 V, 48 kHz | ≈ 25 W | ≈ 38 W |
| Burst time, 1 motor at 80 A (others at hover) | ≈ 16 s | ≈ 9 s |
| Burst time, 4 motors at 80 A | ≈ 3.5 s | ≈ 2 s |
| Continuous rating (prop-wash cooled, ΔT = 65 K) | ≈ 25 A/ch | ≈ 18 A/ch |

### 0.3 Engineering Flag

**80 A is a short burst rating only, not a continuous one.** Physics, not layout, limits a nano-format board. The derivations below give the exact formulae, so you can re-run them with your chosen FET.
