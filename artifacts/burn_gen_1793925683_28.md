## 0. Architecture Review (Read First)

The model exposes three problems with the spec as written.

| # | Finding | Consequence | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | The STM32H743 cannot practically run 4 sensorless BLDC controllers *and* ArduPilot. It has only 2 advanced timers (TIM1/TIM8) with complementary outputs. 4 motors need 12 complementary pairs. HRTIM provides 10 outputs, which is still short, and the ArduPilot scheduler is not built for 48 kHz commutation ISRs. | Not implementable as a single MCU. |
