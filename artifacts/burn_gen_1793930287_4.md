## 0. Architecture Review: Three Issues to Resolve First

| # | Issue | Consequence | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A "Nano" form factor (20×20 mm) cannot carry 4 × 80 A. | 24 power FETs, bus copper, and bulk capacitance will not fit. Thermal mass is about 3 J/K, which allows a burst of under 2 s (see §3). | Use a 30.5×30.5 mm stack: the FC board on top, the ESC board below. Rate the ESC at 80 A burst for 5 s and about 45–55 A continuous. |
| 2 | The H743 does not commutate the motors. | ArduPilot has no BLDC commutation firmware for the FC MCU. | Give each motor its own ESC MCU (AT32F421K8U7, running AM32) that generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to these MCUs. |
| 3 | 48 kHz PWM is set on the ESC side. | It sets switching loss and bootstrap behavior, not FC timing. | Configure 48 kHz in AM32. The FC timer math in §4 is independent of the PWM frequency. |

## 2. Loss Model (per motor bridge, then ×4)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

This assumes six-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM.

At every instant, two FETs carry the phase current in series:
- the PWM'd leg, alternating between its high-side and low-side switch, and
- the static low-side switch of the returning phase.

The phase current therefore always flows through exactly 2 × R_DS(on):

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on),hot} = 2(80)^2(1.63\,\text{m}\Omega) = \mathbf{20.9\ W}$$

The loss is not shared evenly among the six FETs. Over one electrical cycle, each phase leg conducts for 2/3 of the time, so each FET averages:

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{20.9}{6} = 3.5\ \text{W}$$

The instantaneous peak per FET is

$$I^2 R = 80^2 \times 1.63\,\text{m}\Omega = 10.4\ \text{W}$$

which lasts for up to 1/3 of the electrical period. At 40 kHz eRPM-equivalent rates, this ripple is filtered by the die and package Z_th(t) (time constants under 1 ms). Use the average value for board thermal analysis.
