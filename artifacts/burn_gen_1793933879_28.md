## 0. Architecture Summary and Feasibility Finding

| Item | Decision |
|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz in ArduPilot default config, timer kernel clocks 200 MHz |
| ESC MCUs | 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 (one per channel). The H743 does **not** commutate motors. |
| Gate drive | 4× FD6288Q-class 3-phase driver (bootstrap high side), or equivalent |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET, 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN **[A]**: R_DS(on),25°C = 1.2 mΩ, Q_g = 60 nC, t_r + t_f ≈ 40 ns |
| Bus | 6S nominal (25.2 V max), 40 V FETs (≈1.6× margin, minimal for 6S; TVS clamp mandatory) |
| PWM | 48 kHz complementary (synchronous rectification), dead time 100 ns **[A]** |
| Protocol | ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 with EDT/RPM telemetry |
| Board | 30.5×30.5 mm, 6-layer, 2 oz on all layers recommended (see §4) |

**Feasibility finding:** The model in §4 shows that **80 A is a burst rating of roughly 2–3 s per channel, not a continuous rating**. On a true 20×20 "nano" footprint, the board thermal mass and convective area drop by about 57%, and the burst window falls to roughly 1 s. Two options follow:

- Keep "nano" and rate the product at **~55 A burst / 35 A continuous**, or
- Keep 80 A and move to a **30.5×30.5** footprint.

The remaining sections assume 30.5×30.5 and flag the 20×20 deltas.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current definitions
Let **I** be the motor phase current magnitude during conduction. For 6-step (120°) commutation at high duty, I ≈ I_DC per channel.

- Each switch conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period, so the switch RMS current is I/√3.
- At any instant, one high-side and one low-side switch are in series in the current path. With synchronous rectification, the freewheeling path uses the complementary FET's channel, not its body diode.

### 1.2 Temperature-corrected R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0065\ /\mathrm{K}\ [A]$$

At T_j = 105 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × 1.52 ≈ **1.82 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Per-switch and per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,sw} = \left(\frac{I}{\sqrt3}\right)^2 R_{DS(on)} = \frac{I^2 R}{3}$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 6\cdot\frac{I^2R}{3} = 2I^2R$$

**At 80 A, R = 1.82 mΩ:**
- P_cond,sw = 6400 × 1.82e-3 / 3 = **3.88 W per conducting FET (average)**
- P_cond,ch = 2 × 6400 × 1.82e-3 = **23.3 W per channel**

### 1.4 Copper and interconnect loss
Assume a phase path of about 0.3 mΩ (pads, vias, and the 2 oz plane to the motor pad) plus the bus plane share **[A]**. Two series paths give about 0.6 mΩ:

P_Cu = I² × 0.6 mΩ = **3.8 W per channel**

This term is frequently ignored and is not negligible.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM-switched FET in each commutation step incurs hard-switching loss. Its complement soft-switches after the dead time.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V-I overlap

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

= 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40e-9 × 48e3 = **1.94 W**

### 2.2 Output capacitance (Q_oss)

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12\,Q_{oss}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw}$$

With Q_oss = 40 nC **[A]**: P_oss = 0.5 × 40e-9 × 25.2 × 48e3 = **0.024 W**

### 2.3 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_F\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw}$$

= 0.8 × 80 × 200e-9 × 48e3 = **0.61 W**

### 2.4 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw}$$

With Q_rr = 50 nC **[A]**: P_rr = 50e-9 × 25.2 × 48e3 = **0.06 W**

### 2.5 Gate drive
This loss is dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not the FETs.

$$P_{g} = Q_g\,V_{GS}\,f_{sw}\times 2$$

= 60e-9 × 10 × 48e3 × 2 = **0.058 W per channel**

### 2.6 Duty-cycle note
P_sw scales with how often the FET actually switches. At D → 1 (full throttle), switching stops and P_sw → 0. The worst combined case is high current at mid-to-high duty, for example a punch-out at D ≈ 0.85. The full value is used here as the conservative bound.
