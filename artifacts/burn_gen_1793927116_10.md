## 2. Loss Model per ESC Channel

### 2.1 Commutation Topology

A trapezoidal (6-step) drive with complementary PWM is assumed. In each 60° electrical sector, the current paths are:

- **Phase A high-side (HS):** PWM-switched, duty D.
- **Phase A low-side (LS):** synchronous rectification, conducts during (1 − D) minus dead time.
- **Phase B LS:** held fully ON for the whole sector.
- **Phase C:** floating (BEMF sensing).

At every instant the current flows through exactly **two channel resistances in series**. In every sector, one FET hard-switches and one FET pair handles the diode/dead-time transitions.

### 2.2 Conduction Loss

The temperature-dependent on-resistance is:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

The instantaneous conduction loss of the channel, independent of D because HS and sync-LS alternate in the same leg, is:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2 R_{DS}(T_j)\cdot\left(1 - \frac{2 t_{dt} f_{sw}}{1}\right)$$

The dead-time correction is (1 − 2·50 ns·48 kHz) = 0.9952, so it is negligible here.

**Self-consistent junction temperature.** R_DS depends on T_j, and T_j depends on loss. Iterating with the Section 3 thermal model converges to T_j ≈ 105 °C at the end of a 2 s burst:

$$R_{DS}(105) = 1.6\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.0055 \times 80) = 2.30\,\text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 2.30\text{ m}\Omega \times 0.995 = \mathbf{29.3\ W}$$

For comparison, the cold start (25 °C) value is 80² × 2 × 1.6 mΩ = 20.5 W. Hence the **+43 % rise during the burst**, which is the positive-feedback term the thermal model must capture.

**Per-FET average.** Each of the 6 FETs conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period. Phase rotation spreads the load evenly:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = P_{cond,ch}/6 \approx 4.9\ \text{W}$$

**Per-FET peak.** While a FET is in its conduction window, its loss is I²·R_DS ≈ 14.7 W. This peak drives the short-time Z_th check in Section 3.3.

### 2.3 Switching Loss at 48 kHz

**(a) Hard-switching V–I overlap.** Only the PWM'd high-side FET in each sector hard-switches.

The transition times are set by the Miller plateau:

$$t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{Q_{gd}\,(R_g+R_{drv})}{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}, \qquad t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd}\,(R_g+R_{drv})}{V_{plateau}}$$

With V_drv = 10 V, V_plateau ≈ 3.5 V, R_g + R_drv ≈ 12 Ω:

- t_r ≈ 8 nC × 12 / 6.5 ≈ 14.8 ns
- t_f ≈ 8 nC × 12 / 3.5 ≈ 27.4 ns

The overlap loss is then:

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 42.2\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{2.04\ W}$$
