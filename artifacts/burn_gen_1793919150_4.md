# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #8): Power, Thermal and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architectural Correction and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors.** Commutating four BLDC motors at 48 kHz with BEMF zero-cross detection on one MCU, while running ArduPilot's scheduler, EKF3 and logging, is not a realistic design. The intended topology is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot FC) ──bidir DShot600 ×4──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 firmware)
                                                   └─► 4× 3-phase gate driver ─► 24× N-MOSFET
```

The H743 owns the DShot timers and DMA. The ESC MCUs own the 48 kHz PWM, dead-time and commutation.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step Trapezoidal, Complementary PWM)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0055 × 75) = **1.41 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Per-FET RMS current

Each electrical cycle has six 60° sectors. Each phase is the PWM'd high side for 120°, and the hard-on low side for 120°.

- **High-side FET (PWM'd for 120°):** $I_{rms,HS}^2 = I^2\cdot\frac{D}{3}$
- **Low-side FET:** hard-on for 120°, plus synchronous rectification for 120° at (1−D): $I_{rms,LS}^2 = I^2\cdot\frac{1+(1-D)}{3}=I^2\frac{2-D}{3}$

### 1.3 Per-channel total

During PWM-on, current flows through HS_A and LS_B. During PWM-off, it flows through LS_A and LS_B. Two FETs are always in series:

$$P_{cond,ch}=3\left[I^2R\tfrac{D}{3}+I^2R\tfrac{2-D}{3}\right]=2I^2R_{ds}(T_j)$$
