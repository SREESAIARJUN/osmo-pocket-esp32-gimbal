# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #39): Loss Model, Thermal Budget, Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation, and Netlists

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I don't have the Job #39 requirements package, so every input below is an explicit assumption. Swap in your real MOSFET datasheet values and the equations still hold. Numbers marked **[A]** are assumptions. Numbers marked **[D]** are derived.

### 0.1 Architecture (an important correction up front)

The H743 does not drive the 24 gate signals directly. In a standard ArduPilot stack:

- The **H743 is the flight controller**. It outputs bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs.
- **Each ESC channel has its own MCU** (STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). That MCU does commutation, the 48 kHz PWM, and dead-time.

The DShot section therefore covers H743 timers. The loss model covers the ESC power stage.
