## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Feasibility Constraints

These constraints shape every number in this document.

| # | Assumption / Constraint | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | The **H743 does not commutate motors**. Each channel uses a dedicated ESC MCU (AT32F421 running AM32) that receives DShot600 from the H743. | Commutation, dead-time, and 48 kHz PWM are owned by the ESC MCUs. The H743 owns DShot plus eRPM telemetry. |
| A2 | Supply is **6S**: 22.2 V nominal, **25.2 V** maximum. Switching math uses 25.2 V (worst case). | Use 40 V MOSFETs for ≥1.5× margin against ringing. |
| A3 | **"80 A burst"** means ≤ 2–3 s per channel with the board starting at 40 °C ambient. | 80 A is a transient rating, not continuous. See §3.5. |
| A4 | Form factor is **30.5 × 30.5 mm** ("nano"-class stack). | A 20 × 20 board cannot place 24 × 5×6 mm FETs. On 20 × 20, 80 A is not physically credible. |
| A5 | MOSFET parameters below are **representative of a 40 V, ~1 mΩ, 5×6 mm class device**. | Before freezing the BOM, replace them with your selected part's datasheet values. All equations are parameterized. |
| A6 | Scheme is 6-step trapezoidal with **complementary (synchronous) PWM** on the active leg, which is the AM32 default. | The loss topology in §2 follows from this. |

**Feasibility flag:** four channels at 80 A means **320 A from the battery**. No XT60 or XT90 lead and no 30 mm board copper sustains that. In practice, simultaneous full burst is limited by the battery and airframe, so the system-level budget in §3.6 treats 4 × 80 A as an absolute worst case.
