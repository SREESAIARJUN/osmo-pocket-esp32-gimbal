## 0. Architectural Clarification and Assumptions

The H743 should **not** commutate the motors directly. ArduPilot cannot run sensorless BLDC commutation, and a 48 kHz six-step loop on 4 motors would starve the EKF/IMU scheduler. The correct partition is:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 480 MHz | ArduPilot, sends DShot600 BiDir |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | STM32G071GBU6 (AM32 firmware) | Commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drivers (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase, VCC ≤ 20 V, VS ≤ 250 V) | Bootstrapped half-bridge drive |
| Power stage | 24 × N-MOSFET, 40 V, DFN3.3×3.3 | 6 per ESC |

**Design-basis assumptions.** Replace these with your BOM datasheet values; every equation is parametric.

> **Engineering flag:** "Nano" usually means a 20×20 mm board. 80 A per channel on 20×20 is not physically sustainable beyond fractions of a second. The thermal results below (on 30.5×30.5 mm) show that 80 A is a seconds-scale burst rating even on the larger board. Spec it as **"80 A, 2 s burst"**, not continuous.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step, Synchronous Rectification)

### 1.1 Current path

In any 60° sector of trapezoidal commutation, one leg PWMs (HS/LS complementary) and one leg holds its LS on. With synchronous rectification, the phase current always flows through **exactly two MOSFET channels**:

- During on-time D: HS(pwm leg) + LS(return leg)
- During off-time (1−D): LS(pwm leg, sync freewheel) + LS(return leg)
