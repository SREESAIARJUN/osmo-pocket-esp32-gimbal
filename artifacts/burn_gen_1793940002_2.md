## 0. Architectural Clarifications

These corrections define what the rest of this document models.

| Item | Clarification |
|---|---|
| **Who switches the FETs** | The STM32H743 does **not** drive the power stage directly. In practical 4-in-1 designs, each motor channel has a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, which commutates the motor and generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the ESC MCUs via bidirectional DShot600. This document models that split. |
| **"Nano" + 80A** | A 20×20 mm board cannot physically carry 4×80A. This design assumes a 30.5×30.5 mm AIO, 8-layer, 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner copper. **80A is a burst rating of a few seconds per channel, not continuous.** Section 3 derives the actual continuous rating. |
| **Component values** | FET, driver, and capacitor parameters below are representative assumptions for a 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PQFN-class design. Re-run every equation with the datasheet values of your selected parts before committing to layout. |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path in 6-Step Trapezoidal Commutation

During any 60° commutation sector, two phases carry current I. Assuming complementary (synchronous) PWM on the active leg:

- **ON interval (D·T):** current flows through HS(A) → motor → LS(B).
- **OFF interval ((1−D)·T):** current freewheels through LS(A) (synchronous) → motor → LS(B).

In both intervals, **exactly two FETs conduct**, regardless of duty cycle. Therefore:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature-Corrected R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{ds}(125) = 1.6\,\text{mΩ} \times 1.45 = 2.32\,\text{mΩ}$$

### 1.3 Burst Conduction Loss at 80 A

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.32\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{29.7\ W\ per\ channel}$$

### 1.4 Per-FET Distribution

Each FET conducts for 120° of each electrical revolution. At typical electrical frequencies (500 Hz–2 kHz), the commutation period is comparable to the die thermal time constant (~0.5–2 ms), so:

- **Average per FET:** P_cond,ch / 6 ≈ 5.0 W
- **Instantaneous peak for an active FET:** P_cond,ch / 2 ≈ 14.9 W. This sets junction ripple (Section 3.3).

### 1.5 Copper and Shunt Losses

- **Power-path copper** (estimated effective resistance of 0.4 mΩ per channel, VBAT → bridge → phase pad):
  $$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.4\,\text{mΩ} = 2.6\ \text{W}$$
- **Shunt** (0.25 mΩ, low-side, carries battery-side current I_bat = D·I):
  $$P_{shunt} = (D\,I)^2 R_{sh} \le 1.6\ \text{W at } D = 1$$
