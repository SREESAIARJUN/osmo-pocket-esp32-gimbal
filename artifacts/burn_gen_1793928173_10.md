## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Architectural Clarification

**What I could not access.** I have no Job #38 documents: no BOM, stackup, MOSFET selection or board outline. Every device parameter below is a stated placeholder (§0.2). The equations are general, so substitute your datasheet values and the numbers update directly. The model is written to be spreadsheet-ready for that purpose.

**Architectural clarification.** The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz motor PWM.

| Domain | Device | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743 running ArduPilot | Sends DShot600 commands and receives bidirectional eRPM telemetry |
| ESC (×4 channels) | ESC MCU (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32 | 48 kHz complementary PWM, commutation, BEMF sensing |
| Power stage | 3-phase gate driver + 6 MOSFETs per motor | 24 MOSFETs total |

Section 1 (losses) applies to the ESC power stage. Section 3 (DMA/timers) applies to the H743.
