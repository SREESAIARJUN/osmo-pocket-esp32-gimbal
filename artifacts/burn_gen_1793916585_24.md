## 0. Architecture Decision and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors.** In every production AIO of this class, the H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Four dedicated ESC MCUs run AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, each driving one 3-phase bridge at 48 kHz. Reasons:

- 4 × 3-phase complementary PWM with dead time would need 4 advanced-control timers. The H743 has only two (TIM1/TIM8), plus HRTIM.
- Sensorless BEMF zero-crossing for 4 motors would compete with the EKF/IMU loop.
- Isolating ESC firmware faults from the FC is a safety requirement.

The architecture is therefore H743 (ArduPilot) → 4 × bi-dir DShot600 → 4 × ESC MCU → 4 × gate driver → 24 MOSFETs.
