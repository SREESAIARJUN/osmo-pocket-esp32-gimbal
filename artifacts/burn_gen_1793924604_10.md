## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller** MCU. It does not commutate the motors. In any production 4-in-1 stack the ESC carries its own MCUs, typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The system therefore splits into two boards:

| Board | Function | Key ICs |
|---|---|---|
| FC (20×20 mm mount) | ArduPilot, sensor fusion, DShot master | STM32H743VIT6, ICM-42688-P, DPS310, AT7456E |
| ESC (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mount) | 4× 6-step BLDC drive | 4× AT32F421K8U7, 4× FD6288Q, 24× N-MOSFET |

**Sanity flag:** "80A per channel" on a nano form factor is only achievable as a **burst rating of a few seconds** (proven in §3). Four channels at 80A means 320A drawn from the pack. The design target is therefore **80A burst ≤ 2 s per channel, 35–40A continuous with prop wash.**

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation model

The drive is 6-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM on the active high side, as AM32 and BLHeli_32 do. In each 60° sector:

- **Phase X** (PWM leg): the high side conducts for fraction D and the low side conducts for (1 − D).
- **Phase Y** (return leg): the low side is fully on.
- **Phase Z**: floating, used for BEMF sensing.
