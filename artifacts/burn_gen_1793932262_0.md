## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class, FPU, 170+ MHz |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC (e.g. GBM2804H-100T): 12N14P → **p = 7 pole pairs**, R ≈ 10 Ω, L ≈ 2.5 mH (phase), Kt ≈ 0.08 Nm/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701), SPI |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P, SPI, gyro/accel ODR = 8 kHz, ±2000 dps / ±16 g |
| PWM | 20 kHz center-aligned, low-side shunt sensing |

**Loop rates (all phase-locked to the PWM timer):**

```
 20 kHz  │ Current (FOC) loop: Clarke → Park → PI(id,iq) → invPark → SVPWM
  4 kHz  │ Velocity PI + cogging FF + accel FF
  8 kHz  │ IMU sampling (DMA), AAF on-chip, notch + LPF, dual fusion
  1 kHz  │ Attitude estimator (Mahony), angle loop, gimbal kinematics
```

Design rule: each loop bandwidth should be ≤ 1/5…1/10 of its inner loop.
- Current loop: ω_c,i ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz.
- Velocity loop: ≈ 2π·150 Hz.
- Angle loop: ≈ 2π·20–30 Hz.

## 1. FOC Current Loop Equations

### 1.1 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt{3}}
$$

### 1.2 Park transform

The electrical angle is computed from the mechanical angle, with θ_off found at calibration:

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off})
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.3 Plant in the dq frame (surface PM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Torque: τ = (3/2)·p·λ_m·i_q = K_t·i_q.

### 1.4 PI tuning by pole-zero cancellation

With target bandwidth ω_ci, the closed loop becomes 1/(s/ω_ci + 1):

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}
$$

Discrete form (Tustin integrator, T_s = 1/20 kHz):

$$
u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\qquad I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s e_k
$$

### 1.5 Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$
v_d^* = PI_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = PI_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

### 1.6 Voltage limiting (circular) with back-calculation anti-windup

$$
V_{max} = \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}\cdot m_{max},\quad m_{max}\approx 0.95
$$

The d-axis has priority:

$$
v_d^{sat} = \mathrm{clamp}(v_d^*, \pm V_{max}),\qquad v_q^{sat} = \mathrm{clamp}\!\left(v_q^*, \pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - (v_d^{sat})^2}\right)
$$

Back-calculation anti-windup, with K_aw ≈ 1/K_p:

$$
I_{k+1} = I_k + T_s\left[K_i e_k + K_{aw}(u^{sat}_k - u_k)\right]
$$

### 1.7 Inverse Park and SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

$$
v_a = v_\alpha,\quad v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12\left(\max(v_{abc}) + \min(v_{abc})\right),\qquad D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

The min-max injection is mathematically identical to 7-segment SVPWM.

### 1.8 Angle latency compensation

The PWM update is applied ≈ 1.5 T_s after sampling, so use a predicted angle:

$$
\theta_e^{pwm} = \theta_e + 1.5\,T_s\,\omega_e
$$

$$
