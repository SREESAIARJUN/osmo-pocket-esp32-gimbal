## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Key Assumptions

Two architecture points must be settled before any numbers are meaningful.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is flight-control firmware, not ESC firmware. The realistic architecture is an H743 flight controller sending bi-directional DShot600 to a 4-in-1 ESC. The ESC uses four dedicated ESC MCUs (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or EFM8/BLHeli_S). Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM. Everything below assumes that split.

2. **80 A per channel on a "nano" board is a thermal edge case.** Section 3 shows that a nano-class board can survive 4 × 80 A only for **about 1–2 seconds**. Treat "80 A burst" as a sub-2-second rating unless the form factor or cooling changes.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current-Path Topology (6-Step, Synchronous)

In each 60° electrical sector, two phases are active:

- **Phase A (PWM phase):** the high-side FET conducts for D; the complementary low-side FET conducts for (1 − D).
- **Phase B (return phase):** the low-side FET is statically ON.
- **Phase C:** floating.
