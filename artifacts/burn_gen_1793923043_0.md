# STM32H743 + 4-in-1 ESC "Nano" AIO: Job #23 Architecture Model

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and a Feasibility Flag

The H743 cannot commutate four BLDC motors directly at 48 kHz with sensorless back-EMF. This design therefore uses the standard architecture:

- **H743** runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600.
- **4 × AT32F421** ESC MCUs run AM32 firmware.
- **4 × FD6288Q** three-phase gate drivers.
- **24 × N-MOSFETs**, one per bridge position (baseline).

| Parameter | Value (assumed; verify against the selected datasheets) |
|---|---|
| Bus | 6S, V_bus,max = 25.2 V |
| Burst current | I = 80 A per motor (phase DC-equivalent), burst duration t_b to be derived |
| PWM | f_sw = 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous) PWM, 6-step trapezoidal |
| MOSFET (per position) | 40 V; R_ds0 = 1.0 mΩ @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V; α = 0.006 /K; Q_g = 30 nC; Q_gd = 6 nC; Q_gs2 ≈ 4 nC; V_pl = 3.5 V; R_g,int = 1 Ω; Q_oss = 25 nC; Q_rr = 30 nC; V_SD = 0.8 V; R_th,jc = 1.5 K/W |
| Gate drive | V_drv = 10 V, R_src ≈ 6 Ω, R_sink ≈ 2 Ω, R_g,ext = 3.3 Ω |
| Dead time | t_dt = 100 ns per edge |
| Motor | L_LL = 20 µH line-to-line |
| Board | 30.5 × 30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz outer / 1 oz inner |

> **Feasibility flag:** 80 A per motor (≈ 320 A total on a 6S rail, about 8 kW) is not physically sustainable on a "nano" board. The model below shows this explicitly: 80 A is a seconds-scale burst rating, not a continuous one. A 20 × 20 mm board cannot meet it at all; the 30.5 mm board can only with 2 × paralleled FETs per position (see §1.7).

## 1. Loss Model

### 1.1 Conduction Topology

In 6-step operation with synchronous rectification, two phases are active at any instant:

- **PWM phase:** the high-side conducts for D·T_sw; its complementary low-side conducts for (1 − D)·T_sw.
- **Return phase:** the low-side conducts for 100 % of the interval.

Motor current therefore always flows through exactly two channel resistances, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds0}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$
