## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Topology
Assume six-step (trapezoidal) commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM, which is the AM32 and BLHeli_32 default. During any 60° electrical sector, current flows through two phases:

- **PWM'd phase:** high-side conducts for D, synchronous low-side for (1−D).
- **Return phase:** low-side conducts continuously.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 R_{hot}\big[D + (1-D)\big] + I_{ph}^2 R_{hot} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{hot}$$

The result is independent of D, which is a useful property of synchronous rectification.

### 1.2 Ripple correction
For motor inductance L ≈ 20 µH, ripple is worst at D = 0.5:

$$\Delta i_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus} D(1-D)}{L f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\,\mu\text{H} \times 48\,\text{kHz}} = 6.6\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta i_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{negligible } (+0.06\%)$$

### 1.3 Numbers
$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.74\ \text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{22.3\ W\ per\ channel}$$
