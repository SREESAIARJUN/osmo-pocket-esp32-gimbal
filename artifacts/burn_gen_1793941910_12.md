# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC (Job #52): System Model and Design Package

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Up-Front Warnings

The 4-in-1 ESC does not run on the H743. Each motor channel needs its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). The H743 is the flight controller. It generates bi-directional DShot600 and decodes the eRPM telemetry. The architecture below reflects that split.

**Reality check on "nano" + 80A:** 80A per channel is not a continuous rating on any 20×20 or 30×30 board. The model below shows it is a **thermally capacity-limited burst of seconds**. The firmware current limit, the burst timer, and the marketing claim should all derive from Section 3, not the other way round.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation topology
AM32/BLHeli_32 use 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In each 60° electrical sector:

- Leg X PWMs: the high side (HS) conducts for D·T, and the low side (LS) synchronously rectifies for (1−D)·T.
- Leg Y has its LS held fully ON.
- Leg Z floats and is used for BEMF sensing.

Motor current I_m flows through exactly two switch positions at every instant.
