## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, using electrical angle θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset):

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

Inverse Park transform:

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant in the dq frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

The cross-coupling and back-EMF terms are cancelled by feedforward:

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\hat\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \hat\omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

After cancellation, each axis reduces to a first-order plant:

$$
G_i(s) = \frac{1}{Ls + R}
$$

### 1.3 PI tuning by pole-zero cancellation

The controller is C(s) = K_p + K_i/s. Choose

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_{ci}
$$

This makes the closed loop exactly first order:

$$
\frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_{ci}}{s + \omega_{ci}}
$$

**Choosing the bandwidth.** Bandwidth is limited by the total loop delay. With 1 sample of compute delay plus 0.5 sample of PWM hold, T_d ≈ 1.5·T_s = 75 µs. For roughly 60° phase margin, use

$$
\omega_{ci} \le \frac{\pi/6}{T_d}
$$

This gives ω_ci ≈ 2π·1.1 kHz. Use **ω_ci = 2π·1000 rad/s**, which yields K_p = 13.2 V/A and K_i = 35 186 V/(A·s).

**Discretization.** Use backward Euler on the integrator, with back-calculation anti-windup (gain K_aw = 1/K_p).

**Voltage limiting.** Apply a circular limit, giving d-axis priority:

$$
|v_{dq}| \le \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}
$$

### 1.4 SVPWM via min/max injection

This method is equivalent to sector-based SVPWM:

$$
v_{abc} = \text{invClarke}(v_\alpha, v_\beta),\quad
v_{0} = -\tfrac12\big(\max v_{abc} + \min v_{abc}\big),\quad
D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

## 2. Cascaded Velocity / Angle Control

### 2.1 Mechanical plant (per axis)

$$
J\dot\omega = K_t i_q - B\omega - \tau_{cog}(\theta_m) - \tau_{dist}
$$

With the current loop treated as unity gain below 1 kHz:

$$
G_\omega(s) = \frac{K_t}{Js + B}
$$

### 2.2 Velocity feedback blending

The estimate ω̂ combines two sources through a complementary filter with crossover ω_x:

- **IMU gyro (inertial):** this is the *controlled* quantity for stabilization.

$$
