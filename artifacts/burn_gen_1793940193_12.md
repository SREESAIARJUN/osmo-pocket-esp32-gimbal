# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Model, Thermal Budget, and Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation

**Scope of Job #55:** loss model, thermal model, ArduPilot timer/DMA plan, reference netlists

## 0. Design Assumptions and Corrections

**1. The H743 does not run the ESC.**
- In an AIO nano design, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600.
- Each motor bridge is commutated by a dedicated ESC MCU, typically an AT32F421, STM32G071 or GD32 running AM32 or BLHeli_32.
- The **48 kHz PWM frequency is therefore an ESC-firmware parameter** (e.g. AM32 `PWM_FREQ`). ArduPilot never sets it.
- The model below keeps the two domains separate.

**2. "80 A burst" must be qualified.** The analysis shows that:
- A 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 board **cannot sustain 4 × 80 A simultaneously** for more than a few seconds.
- The limit comes from board thermal mass and the battery-entry copper, not from the MOSFET junctions.
- The design rating should be stated as "80 A per channel, ≤ X s, single-channel or diagonal-pair burst" (see §3.4).

**3. The device parameters below are representative placeholders.**
- They model a 40 V, 5×6 mm (or 3.3×3.3 mm dual-cool) trench MOSFET.
- **Replace every parameter in Table 1 with values from your selected part's datasheet** before sign-off.
- The equations are general. The numbers are only as good as Table 1.

**4. The netlists in §5 are a reference topology for schematic capture.** They are not a fabrication-verified design.
