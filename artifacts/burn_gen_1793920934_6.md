## 1. Loop Architecture

```
 θ_ref ─►(+)─► Angle P + ω_ff ─► ω_ref ─►(+)─► Velocity PI ─► i_q,fb ─►(+)─► i_q* ─► Current PI ─► SVPWM ─► Motor
         ▲ −                              ▲ −                          ▲ +  i_q,cog (anti-cogging FF)
         │                                │                            │
    θ̂_cam (attitude,               ω̂_cam (fused, notched            θ_mech (encoder)
     dual-IMU Mahony)               dual gyro, joint axis)
```

**Design choice:** stabilisation loops close on the inertial (camera-frame) gyro and attitude. Commutation and anti-cogging use the encoder angle, because cogging is a function of rotor position, not inertial attitude.

## 2. Field-Oriented Control Equations

### 2.1 Clarke and Park transforms

With phase currents i_a and i_b, and i_c = −i_a − i_b:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

$$
\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{e0},\qquad
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 2.2 Electrical model (dq frame)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

The electromagnetic torque is:

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$

### 2.3 Current PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

The pole of the plant 1/(Ls + R) is cancelled by choosing a zero at R/L. This gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c
$$

Choose ω_c ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz, which must satisfy ω_c ≲ 2π f_pwm / 10.

The voltage commands are:

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$
