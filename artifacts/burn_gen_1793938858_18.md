## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology
The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. During any PWM interval, current flows through **exactly two conducting channels**:

- either the high-side of the PWM leg plus the static low-side, or
- the synchronous low-side of the PWM leg plus the static low-side.

The dead-time body-diode interval is handled separately in §2.4.

### 1.2 Current and resistance terms
Phase RMS current, including PWM ripple:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

Ripple is worst at D = 0.5. With a typical 5" motor (L_ph ≈ 15 µH line-to-line):

ΔI_pp = 25.2 × 0.25 / (15e-6 × 48e3) ≈ **8.75 A**

This adds only 6.4 A² to 6400 A², so it is negligible at 80 A. It matters at light load for core loss.

Temperature-dependent on-resistance:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006/\text{K} \Rightarrow k_T(125^\circ C)=1.6$$

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j) + I_{rms}^2\, R_{Cu}$$

At 80 A and T_j = 125 °C:

| Term | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| FETs | 2 × 6406 × 1.6 mΩ | 20.5 W |
| Copper (R_Cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ, phase + bus path on 2 oz) | 6406 × 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| **P_cond, ch** | | **≈ 22.4 W** |

### 1.4 Per-FET average loss
Each of the 6 FETs carries 1/6 of the 2·I²R term on average (equal-share assumption):

P_FET,avg ≈ 20.5 / 6 ≈ **3.4 W**

Instantaneous dissipation in a conducting FET is 10.2 W. This drives the local junction rise in §3.3.
