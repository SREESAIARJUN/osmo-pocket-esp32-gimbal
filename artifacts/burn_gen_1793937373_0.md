## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Corrections

These assumptions frame everything below.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600 to those MCUs. Running 4× sensorless 48 kHz commutation on the H743 alongside the EKF3 and the 1–2 kHz gyro pipeline is technically possible but is not supported by ArduPilot. I treat it as out of scope.
2. **"80A burst" means 80 A phase current per channel, with all four channels loaded simultaneously.** This is the worst case. Section 2.5 shows that on a nano board this is a roughly 1-second adiabatic rating, not a sustainable one. I state this up front because many datasheets in this category omit it.
3. **Electrical baseline:**
   - 6S LiPo, worst-case bus voltage V_bus = 25.2 V
   - MOSFET rating 40 V, which gives only about 37% margin over 25.2 V once ringing is included
   - 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) PWM at f_sw = 48 kHz
4. **Board:** 25.5 × 25.5 mm outline (20 × 20 mm M2 mounting), 8-layer, 2 oz copper on all layers.

**MOSFET parameter set.** These are representative of best-in-class 40 V PDFN 3.3×3.3 parts. Substitute your chosen part's datasheet values.
