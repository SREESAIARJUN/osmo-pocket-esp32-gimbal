## 0. Architecture Clarification and Design Assumptions

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. In every production 4-in-1 stack, the architecture is:

```
[STM32H743 FC, ArduPilot] --4x bidirectional DShot600--> [4x ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071), AM32 or BLHeli_32]
                                                               |
                                                     3-phase gate drivers + 6 MOSFETs per motor
                                                     PWM @ 48 kHz (ESC-firmware generated)
```

The 48 kHz switching and 80 A conduction losses therefore live in the ESC power stage. The H743 owns DShot generation, eRPM capture, harmonic notch filtering and current/voltage telemetry.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path topology

The model assumes trapezoidal six-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM:

- **Active leg:** the high-side (HS) FET conducts for duty D. The low-side (LS) FET of the same leg conducts for (1−D) as a synchronous rectifier.
- **Return leg:** the LS FET is held on for the full 60° commutation sector.

At every instant, **exactly two channels** are in series with the motor current, independent of D. Freewheeling occurs through the LS FET of the active leg. The motor inductance holds the current approximately constant.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]$$

### 1.3 Instantaneous conduction power per motor channel

$$P_{cond} = I^2 \cdot 2R(T_j)$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × [1 + 0.0055 × 85] = **1.761 mΩ**

$$P_{cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.761\text{ m}\Omega = 6400 \times 3.522\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{22.54\ W}$$

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution

Over one electrical revolution, each leg is HS-active for 120° and LS-return for 120°. The per-FET average losses are:

$$\bar P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} D\, I^2 R, \qquad \bar P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\left[(1-D) + 1\right] I^2 R$$

**Worst case is the LS FET at low duty.** For example, a hard punch-out from low throttle gives D → 0.3:

$$\bar P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1.7)(11.27\text{ W}) = 6.39\text{ W}$$

The instantaneous peak in one FET is I²R = 11.27 W while it holds the full sector.
