# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #50): Power, Thermal, and DShot Timing Model

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Stated Assumptions

Three issues affect every calculation, so they are settled first.

**1. The H743 cannot directly commutate four motors with hardware dead-time.**
- It has only two advanced timers (TIM1, TIM8) with complementary outputs and a dead-time generator. HRTIM adds 10 outputs, but that is still short of the 24 gate signals needed.
- Bi-directional DShot is an FC-to-ESC protocol, so the requirement implies separate ESC MCUs anyway.
- **Architecture:** the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidir DShot600 to four on-board ESC MCUs. These are STM32G071 parts running AM32 at 48 kHz center-aligned PWM, each driving a 3-phase gate driver and 6 N-FETs.

**2. "Nano" (20×20 mm) does not physically fit this design.**
- 24 × PQFN3.3 FETs occupy about 261 mm², roughly 65% of one 400 mm² side, before the H743 (LQFP100/TFBGA), drivers, and four ESC MCUs.
- It also cannot hold 80 A bursts thermally (see §3).
- **Baseline:** a 30.5×30.5 mm two-board stack.
  - Board A: ESC, 8-layer, 2 oz outer and 2 oz inner power layers.
  - Board B: FC.
- Results are scaled to 20×20 where relevant.
