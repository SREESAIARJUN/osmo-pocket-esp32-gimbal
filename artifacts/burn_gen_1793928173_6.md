## 0. System Architecture Decision

The STM32H743 cannot practically run ArduPilot *and* perform sensorless commutation for four motors. The standard partition, adopted here, is:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100, 8×8 mm) or VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot, BDShot600 master |
| ESC MCUs ×4 | AT32F421 / STM32G071 class | AM32 firmware, commutation, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drivers ×4 | 3-phase half-bridge driver (FD6288Q class), 12 V gate rail | 6 FETs per channel |
| Power FETs ×24 | 40 V, ~1.0 mΩ typ, DFN 3.3×3.3 class | One FET per switch position |
| Form factor | 25.5×25.5 mm (20×20 mount), 8-layer, 2 oz on all layers | |

**Electrical assumptions:**
- Bus: 6S, 25.2 V maximum (used for worst-case switching loss).
- fsw = 48 kHz.
- Complementary (synchronous) PWM, trapezoidal 6-step.
