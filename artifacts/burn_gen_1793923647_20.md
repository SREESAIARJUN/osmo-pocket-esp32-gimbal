## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Architecture Corrections

### 0.1 Architecture clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. In every production 4-in-1 stack (AM32 or BLHeli_32), each ESC channel runs its own small MCU, such as the STM32G071 or AT32F421. These MCUs generate 6-step PWM, sense BEMF, and run the bidirectional DShot slave. The H743 runs ArduPilot and acts as the DShot **master**. The model below follows that partitioning:

```
[H743 FC] --DShot600 bidir (4 lines)--> [ESC MCU ×4] --> [3-ph gate driver] --> [6 FETs] --> motor
```

A single-MCU design, with the H743 driving 24 FETs directly, is possible in principle. It is not supported by ArduPilot or AM32, so it is out of scope.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths (6-step, synchronous/complementary PWM)

At any instant, two phases are active. During the on-time, current flows through the high side (HS) of phase X and the low side (LS) of phase Y. During the off-time with synchronous rectification, it flows through the LS of phase X and the LS of phase Y. With sync rect enabled, **exactly two FETs carry I_ph at all times**, apart from the dead time, when one body diode conducts instead of the X-phase FET.

With ripple included, the RMS current is:

$$
I_{rms} = I_{ph}\sqrt{1 + \frac{1}{12}\left(\frac{\Delta I}{I_{ph}}\right)^2}
$$

Ripple:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}
$$

For a typical 5" motor, L_ph is about 20 µH line-to-line. The worst case is D = 0.5, which gives ΔI ≈ 6.6 A. The ratio ΔI/I_ph = 0.08 adds only **0.05 %**, so ripple is negligible at 80 A.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on) (self-consistent)

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j) + I_{rms}^2\,R_{Cu}
$$

R_Cu is the copper resistance from the battery pad to the phase pad for one channel's loop. With 2 oz copper plus inner pours on 30×30 it is about 0.4 mΩ.

Copper loss is real and often ignored. At 80 A, 0.4 mΩ adds **2.6 W/channel** that heats the board directly.

### 1.3 Numbers

| T_j | R_ds | FET conduction (2 FETs) | + Cu | Total P_cond/ch | Per FET |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.50 mΩ | 19.2 W | 2.6 W | 21.8 W | 9.6 W |
| 85 °C | 2.00 mΩ | 25.6 W | 2.6 W | 28.2 W | 12.8 W |
| 125 °C | 2.33 mΩ | 29.8 W | 2.6 W | 32.4 W | 14.9 W |

### 1.4 Bus current vs phase current

The battery-side current is:

$$
I_{bus} \approx D\, I_{ph}
$$

So "80 A" must be specified carefully.

- 80 A *battery* current at D = 0.7 means I_ph ≈ 114 A, and conduction loss scales by 2×.
- This document assumes **80 A phase**.
- Make sure your spec sheet and current-limit firmware (AM32 `current limit`, ArduPilot `BATT_CURR_LIMIT`) use the same definition.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

In 6-step mode, only the PWM'd half-bridge switches; the other active phase is statically on. The model uses one hard-switched commutation pair per PWM period: HS turn-on/off with the LS acting as a synchronous rectifier.

### 2.1 Transition times (gate-charge method)

$$
I_{G,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_{G,on}} = \frac{10 - 4.5}{5} = 1.10\ \text{A}
$$

$$
I_{G,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{G,off}} = \frac{4.5}{4} = 1.13\ \text{A}
$$

$$
t_{on} = \frac{Q_{gs2} + Q_{gd}}{I_{G,on}} = \frac{13\ \text{nC}}{1.10} = 11.8\ \text{ns},\qquad t_{off} \approx 11.6\ \text{ns}
$$
