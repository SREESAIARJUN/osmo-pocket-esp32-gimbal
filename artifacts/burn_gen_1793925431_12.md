| Parameter | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max (22.2 V nominal) |
| Form factor | 25.5 × 25.5 mm board (20 × 20 mounting), 6-layer, 2 oz on all layers |
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIH6 at 400 MHz, running ArduPilot |

| ESC PWM | f_sw = 48 kHz, center-aligned |
| Gate driver | 1 × three-phase half-bridge driver per channel (FD6288Q class) |
| MOSFETs | 24 × 40 V N-channel in DFN3.3×3.3, R_ds(on) = 1.1 mΩ at 25 °C |
| Burst rating | 80 A per channel |

**Architectural note.** ArduPilot does not perform motor commutation. The H743 generates bidirectional DShot600, and four dedicated ESC MCUs commutate the motors. This partitioning is standard and is assumed for the rest of this document.

**Early warning.** As Section 3 shows, 80 A per channel on a 25 mm board is thermally a burst rating of roughly **1 to 3 seconds**, not a sustained one. The model below quantifies this so the rating can be specified honestly, for example "80 A / 2 s, 25 A continuous with airflow."

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-Step Current Path

In any commutation sector, two phases are active:
- One phase is PWM-switched (high side on, then the complementary low side synchronously rectifying).
- One phase has its low side held on.

At any instant the motor current flows through **two MOSFETs in series**, so for each channel:

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature Dependence

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ /\mathrm{K}$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{ds(on)} = 1.1\,\mathrm{m\Omega} \times 1.55 = 1.70\,\mathrm{m\Omega}$$

### 1.3 Burst Conduction Loss at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds(on) | P_cond per channel | P_cond, 4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.10 mΩ | 80² × 2.2 m = **14.1 W** | 56.3 W |
| 100 °C | 1.55 mΩ | **19.8 W** | 79.4 W |
| 125 °C | 1.70 mΩ | **21.8 W** | 87.0 W |

### 1.4 Parasitic Conduction Paths

These are added in series with the MOSFETs:

| Element | Resistance | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pad and via field (2 oz, about 40 vias) | about 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| Current shunt, 0.25 mΩ (if per-channel) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| Share of bus plane (4 × 80 A = 320 A total) | about 0.1 mΩ | about 10 W board-wide |

**Total conduction loss, worst case:** about 25 W per channel, about 100 W for the board at a simultaneous 4 × 80 A burst.

### 1.5 Electrothermal Feedback (Stability Criterion)

Because R_ds(on) rises with T_j, and T_j rises with loss:

$$T_j = T_a + R_{th} \cdot I^2 \cdot 2R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

Solving for T_j:

$$T_j = \frac{T_a + k(1 - 25\alpha)}{1 - k\alpha}, \qquad k = 2 R_{th} I^2 R_{25}$$

**Thermal runaway occurs when kα ≥ 1.** With R_th = 8 K/W and I = 80 A:

$$k = 2 \times 8 \times 6400 \times 1.1\times10^{-3} = 112.6\ \mathrm{K}$$

$$k\alpha = 0.62$$

The system is stable in the limit, but the steady-state point is far above T_j,max. This is why 80 A can only be a transient rating.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

In six-step with synchronous rectification, **only one half-bridge switches per sector**.

### 2.1 Overlap (V·I Crossing) Loss

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

With t_r = t_f = 10 ns, achievable with a 2 Ω gate resistor and about 1 A of driver drive:

$$P_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\,\mathrm{ns} \times 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = 0.97\ \mathrm{W}$$

### 2.2 Output Capacitance Loss

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\,\mathrm{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\mathrm{k} = 0.048\ \mathrm{W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse Recovery Loss (Body Diode After Dead Time)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\,\mathrm{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\mathrm{k} = 0.036\ \mathrm{W}$$

At 80 A, Q_rr grows with di/dt and forward current. Use 2× margin, giving **0.07 W**.

### 2.4 Dead-Time Diode Conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_f I \cdot 2t_{dt} f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 2 \times 50\,\mathrm{ns} \times 48\,\mathrm{k} = 0.31\ \mathrm{W}$$

### 2.5 Gate Drive Loss (Dissipated in the Driver and R_g)

$$P_g = 2 Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} = 2 \times 40\,\mathrm{nC} \times 10\,\mathrm{V} \times 48\,\mathrm{k} = 0.038\ \mathrm{W}$$

### 2.6 Switching Loss Summary per Channel at 80 A

| Term | Loss |
|---|---|
| V·I overlap | 0.97 W |
| Coss | 0.05 W |
| Qrr (with margin) | 0.07 W |
| Dead-time diode | 0.31 W |
| Gate drive | 0.04 W |
| **Total** | **about 1.44 W** |

**Interpretation.** At 48 kHz, switching loss is only about 6% of total loss at 80 A. The design is **conduction-dominated**. Lowering R_ds(on) matters far more than lowering f_sw. The 48 kHz choice is justified by reduced motor current ripple and acoustic benefits.

### 2.7 Bus Capacitor Ripple

$$I_{C,rms} \approx I\sqrt{D(1-D)} \Rightarrow \le 40\ \mathrm{A_{rms}}\ \text{per channel at } D = 0.5$$

The ESC PWM is not phase-interleaved, so the four channels add roughly in RSS:

$$I_{C,total} \approx 80\ \mathrm{A_{rms}}\ \text{(worst case)}$$
