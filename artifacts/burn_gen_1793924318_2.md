# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Reference Design (Job #69)

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Two points in the brief need correcting before any math is meaningful.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.**
- ArduPilot runs on the H743 and *sends* DShot600.
- The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC firmware (AM32 or BLHeli_32) running on dedicated ESC MCUs. This design uses 4× AT32F421 (STM32G071 is an alternative).
- The H743's role is the DShot timer/DMA engine and telemetry decode.

**2. "80A burst" on a nano board is ambiguous, and one reading is not physically achievable.**
- **Case A:** 80 A per channel, 4 channels simultaneously, about 100 W of loss. On a 20×20 mm board this is only survivable for about 1.5 s (derived in §2.6).
- **Case B:** 80 A aggregate, about 20 A per channel. This is feasible.

I calculate both cases and recommend designing to Case B, or moving to a 30.5×30.5 mm footprint for Case A.
