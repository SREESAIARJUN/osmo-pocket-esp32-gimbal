## 0. Architectural Ground Truth

### 0.1 The H743 does not commutate the motors

The STM32H743 has only two advanced-control timers (TIM1 and TIM8) with complementary outputs and dead-time insertion. That supports at most two 3-phase bridges, and it would starve ArduPilot of CPU and timer resources.

Every production 4-in-1 design uses this split:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot)  --DShot600 bidir x4-->  4x ESC MCU (AM32 / BLHeli_32)
                                                each: 3-phase driver + 6 MOSFETs
```

The ESC MCU is typically an STM32G071, AT32F421, or GD32E230. This document assumes that split.

### 0.2 "80 A" is a burst rating, not a continuous one

Section 3 proves that a 30.5 × 30.5 mm board cannot reject about 100 W continuously. Every 80 A figure in this document means **per-channel phase current for a time-limited burst**.

### 0.3 Baseline operating point

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V (design point), 22.2 V nominal |
| Burst phase current | I = 80 A per channel |
| PWM frequency | f_sw = 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), six-step trapezoidal |
| Ambient | T_a = 40 °C (in-frame, sun-loaded) |
