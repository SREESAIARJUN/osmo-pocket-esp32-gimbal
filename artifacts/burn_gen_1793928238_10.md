## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4-class (Cortex-M4F, 170 MHz, hardware FPU) |
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5 Ω, L ≈ 2 mH, K_t ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Position sensor | 14-bit magnetic encoder (MT6701/AS5048A class), SPI |
| Current sense | 2-phase low-side shunts, sampled in the PWM-center ADC trigger |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI, shared CLKIN for synchronous sampling |
| PWM | 20 kHz center-aligned |
