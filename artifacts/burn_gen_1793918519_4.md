## 1. FOC Inner Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle is computed from the mechanical angle, the pole-pair count and the calibrated encoder offset:

$$\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, valid when $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\-\sin\theta_e&\cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

**Delay compensation.** Rotate the inverse Park angle forward by 1.5 PWM periods to cover the computation delay plus the zero-order hold:

$$\theta_e^{out}=\theta_e+1.5\,\omega_e T_{pwm}$$

### 1.2 Space-Vector PWM (min/max injection)

1. Compute the phase voltages: $v_a=v_\alpha$, $v_b=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta$, $v_c=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta$.
2. Compute the common-mode offset: $v_0=-\tfrac12(\max+\min)$.
3. Compute the duty for each phase: $d_x = \tfrac12 + (v_x+v_0)/V_{bus}$.
