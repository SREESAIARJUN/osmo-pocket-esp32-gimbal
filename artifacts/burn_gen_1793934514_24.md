## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

Two points change the design before any math is done.

**1. The H743 cannot commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is not a motor-commutation firmware. In a 4-in-1 stack, the H743 sends DShot to **four ESC-side MCUs** (one per motor), such as STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. "Bi-directional DShot600" is the link between the two halves:

```
[STM32H743 FC] --DShot600 (bidir eRPM)--> [ESC MCU ×4] --6×PWM @48kHz--> [Gate drv] --> [3-phase bridge ×4]
```

**2. "80A burst" on a nano board is thermally a transient problem.** It is not a steady-state rating. The model below shows this quantitatively.

## 1. Conduction Losses

In 6-step drive, at any instant current flows through one high-side and one low-side FET in series. With synchronous rectification, the freewheel path also flows through FET channels rather than body diodes. The bridge therefore always presents **2 × R_ds(on)** to the phase current, independent of duty cycle.

$$R_{ds,hot} = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)] \approx k_T R_{ds,25} = 1.5\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,ESC} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds,hot} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

**Copper conduction.** A phase/bus path of about 10 mm, 3 mm wide, 2 oz copper (70 µm), using 2 paralleled layers:

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho L}{A} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\times 0.01}{2 \times 3\times10^{-3}\times 70\times10^{-6}} \approx 0.41\ \text{m}\Omega$$

The phase path and bus path combined are about 0.8 mΩ, so

$$P_{Cu} \approx 80^2 \times 0.8\times10^{-3} \approx \mathbf{5.1\ W/ESC}$$

This term is often ignored, but it is significant on a nano board.

**Per-FET average.** Each FET conducts about 1/3 of the electrical cycle. Electrical frequency is hundreds of Hz, which is much faster than the die thermal time constant, so the per-FET average is what matters thermally:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{19.2}{6} = 3.2\ \text{W}$$

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing leg hard-switches. With complementary PWM there is one hard-switched transition pair per PWM period per ESC.

**(a) V-I overlap.**

$$t_{r} \approx t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_g} = \frac{18\ \text{nC}}{1\ \text{A}} = 18\ \text{ns}$$

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 36\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.74\ W}$$

**(b) Reverse recovery.**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.060\ \text{W}$$
