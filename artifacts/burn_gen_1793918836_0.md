## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle from the mechanical encoder angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI Design

The PMSM stator dynamics in the rotor frame, with surface magnets so $L_d = L_q = L$, are:

$$L\dot i_d = -R i_d + \omega_e L i_q + v_d$$
$$L\dot i_q = -R i_q - \omega_e L i_d - \omega_e\lambda_m + v_q$$

The PI controller uses pole-zero cancellation with target closed-loop bandwidth $\omega_c$:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c$$

$$\Rightarrow\quad \frac{i_q}{i_q^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

Choose $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/20$. At 20 kHz this gives $\omega_c \approx 2\pi\cdot 1000$ rad/s.

Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$v_d = \text{PI}_d(e_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = \text{PI}_q(e_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

For the stated motor, $\lambda_m = \tfrac{2}{3}\,K_t/p$ (amplitude-invariant convention).

### 1.3 Voltage Limiting and Anti-Windup

Use a circular limit $V_{max} = V_{bus}/\sqrt3$ (linear SVPWM region), with d-axis priority:

$$v_d \leftarrow \text{sat}(v_d, \pm V_{max}),\qquad v_q \leftarrow \text{sat}\!\left(v_q, \pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}\right)$$

Back-calculation anti-windup:

$$x_I[k+1] = x_I[k] + T_s\left(K_i e[k] + K_{aw}(v_{sat}-v_{unsat})\right),\qquad K_{aw}=\frac{K_i}{K_p}$$

### 1.4 Discretization and Delay Compensation

Rotate the voltage vector forward by 1.5 samples to compensate for the computation plus PWM-hold delay:

$$\theta_{e,pwm} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s$$

### 1.5 SVPWM (Min-Max Zero-Sequence Injection)

$$v_{a,b,c} = \text{inverse Clarke}(v_\alpha, v_\beta)$$

$$v_0 = -\tfrac12\left(\max(v_{abc})+\min(v_{abc})\right)$$

$$d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}$$
