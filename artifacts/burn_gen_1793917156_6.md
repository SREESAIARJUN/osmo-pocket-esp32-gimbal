## 0. Architectural Clarifications and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. A production 4-in-1 AIO uses two processor layers:

- **H743 (ArduPilot):** Runs the flight controller and generates 4× bidirectional DShot600 streams.
- **4× ESC MCUs (one per motor):** For example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. These perform 6-step commutation at 48 kHz PWM and return eRPM telemetry.
