## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

In any 60° commutation sector, one phase is the high-side (PWM'd) leg, one is the low-side return, and one floats (used for BEMF sensing).

```
PWM ON  (fraction D):     HS_A ──► motor ──► LS_B        (2 FETs in path)
PWM OFF (fraction 1-D):   LS_A ──► motor ──► LS_B        (2 FETs in path, sync rect.)
Dead-time (2·t_dt per period): body diode of LS_A + LS_B
```

Two FETs are always in series with the phase current. Conduction loss is therefore nearly independent of duty cycle:

```
P_cond,ch = 2 · I_rms² · R_DS(on)(T_j)        [W per ESC channel]
```
