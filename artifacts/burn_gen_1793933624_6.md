# STM32H743 AIO + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #49): Design Model for 80 A Burst, 48 kHz Switching, and Bidirectional DShot600

## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Assumptions

Three points need settling before the math, because they change the design.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** On an AIO "FC + 4-in-1" board, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to **four separate ESC MCUs**. Each ESC MCU (for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32-class firmware) generates the 48 kHz PWM. All switching math below therefore applies to the ESC side.
2. **80 A on a small board can only be a short burst.** The thermal model in §3 shows a 30×30 mm-class board reaches its limits within about 1.5–3.5 s at 80 A per motor. The defensible rating is "80 A, ≤2 s burst; about 30 A continuous with airflow." The model below lets you prove whatever rating you choose.
3. **Component values are representative.** I have not tied them to specific MOSFET part numbers. Substitute your BOM's datasheet values into the equations; the structure of the model stays the same.
