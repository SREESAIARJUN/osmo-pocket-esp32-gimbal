# STM32H743 FC + 80A 4-in-1 Nano ESC: Electro-Thermal Model and DShot DMA Architecture

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architecture.** In a 4-in-1 ESC stack, the STM32H743 is the flight controller. It does not drive MOSFET gates. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32) and a 3-phase gate driver. The H743 talks to the ESCs over bi-directional DShot600. This document models both halves.

**Assumed parameters.** I don't know your MOSFET part or stackup. Every number below follows from these assumptions, so substitute your datasheet values.

| Parameter | Symbol | Assumed value |
|---|---|---|
| Max bus voltage (6S, full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current per motor | I_ph | 80 A |
| MOSFET R_DS(on) @25 °C, max | R₂₅ | 1.5 mΩ (40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN class) |
| R_DS(on) temperature coefficient | α | 0.0065 /K |
| Switching transition (rise + fall) | t_r + t_f | 12 + 10 ns |
| Reverse recovery charge | Q_rr | 30 nC |
| Energy-related output capacitance | C_oss(er) | 1.0 nF |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 40 nC @ 10 V |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns |
| Body diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| Duty cycle at burst | D | 0.95 |
| Junction-to-board resistance (per FET, via-stitched pad) | R_jb | 3 K/W |
| Board thermal capacitance (30×30 mm, 8-layer 2 oz, plus components) | C_b | ≈ 6 J/K |
| Board-to-ambient resistance in prop wash (h ≈ 100 W/m²K, two faces) | R_ba | ≈ 5.6 K/W |
