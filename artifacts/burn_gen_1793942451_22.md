## 0. Assumptions and Corrections

I have no prior project documents for "Job #25". Every assumption below is explicit and parameterized so you can substitute real datasheet values. Two points in the brief need correcting before the math is meaningful.

**1. The STM32H743 is not the ESC controller.** In every production 4-in-1 stack, the H743 is the flight controller running ArduPilot. Each motor is commutated by its own small MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. "48 kHz" is that MCU's PWM carrier frequency. The H743's job is to emit bidirectional DShot600 and decode eRPM telemetry. The architecture below follows this split.

**2. 80 A per channel does not fit a 20×20 mm "nano" board.** A 20×20 board physically cannot host 12 power MOSFETs, a driver, and 80 A copper. I have assumed a 30.5×30.5 mm ESC board stacked under a compact H743 FC.

Section 2 shows that even at 30.5 mm, 80 A is only sustainable for **about 2 seconds**. Treat "80 A burst" as a transient rating, never a continuous one.
