I don't have the original Job #47 specification. Every parameter below is therefore declared as an assumption so you can substitute your actual BOM values. The equations stay valid; only the numbers change.

## 0. Architectural Corrections

Two points in the brief need correcting before any modelling.

1. **The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** ArduPilot is a flight-control stack and does not run motor commutation. The correct topology is the H743 acting as the flight controller, sending bi-directional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs. Those MCUs (for example AT32F421 running AM32, or STM32G071) each drive a 3-phase gate driver and a 6-FET bridge. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, not the H743.
2. **80 A burst in a "nano" form factor is thermally marginal.** On a 20×20 mm board, 80 A per motor is not survivable for more than about 1 s. This design assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm** board, with 80 A defined as a **≤3 s burst** and 55 A as continuous with forced airflow. Section 3 quantifies this.
