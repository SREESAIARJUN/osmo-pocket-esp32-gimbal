## 0. Architectural Ground Truth

This constraint shapes everything below.

**ArduPilot on the H743 does not commutate motors.** ArduPilot is the flight controller. It emits DShot and consumes eRPM telemetry. Commutation requires a dedicated ESC MCU.

The architecture is therefore:

```
[6S Battery] ──► [Power stage ×4 (6 FETs each, 24 total)]
                        ▲ gate drive
               [ESC MCU ×4 (AM32 / BLHeli_32-class), e.g. STM32G071 / AT32F421]
                        ▲ DShot600 bidir (single-wire, 1 per motor)
               [STM32H743 FC running ArduPilot]
```

Alternatively, a single ESC MCU can drive all four motors (some designs do this). For this job I assume **4 independent ESC MCUs**, which is the standard and most robust choice. Every number below is parameterized so you can substitute your actual BOM.
