## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke transform:
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}\quad(i_a+i_b+i_c=0)$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset):
$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Motor model (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda)$$

$$\tau_e = \tfrac32\, p\, \lambda\, i_q \equiv K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.3 PI design by pole-zero cancellation

After feedforward decoupling, each axis reduces to the plant 1/(Ls+R). Choose:

$$C(s)=K_p+\frac{K_i}{s},\qquad K_p = L\,\omega_c,\quad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

This gives a closed loop of ω_c/(s+ω_c), a first-order response.

The decoupling feedforward terms are:
$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e(L i_d+\lambda)$$

**Discretization.** Use forward Euler for the integrator, `I += Ki·Ts·e`. This is adequate at ω_c·T_s ≈ 0.47.

**Transport delay.** The PWM update plus ZOH adds about 1.5 T_s of delay. Compensate by advancing the angle used in the inverse Park transform:
$$\theta_e^{\text{out}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s$$
