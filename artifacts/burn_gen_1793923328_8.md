## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, current always flows through exactly two MOSFET channels per motor channel at any instant:

- During PWM-on, it flows through the high-side of phase A and the low-side of phase B.
- During PWM-off, it flows through the low-side of phase A (synchronous freewheel) and the low-side of phase B.

Dead time is excluded here and handled in §3.4. The conduction path is therefore 2·R_ds(on) regardless of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

For trapezoidal drive with flat current, I_rms ≈ I_DC. Add a ripple term for accuracy:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{2L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}$$

For a 5–7" class motor, L_ph ≈ 15 µH (line-line 30 µH). At 50 % duty and V_bemf ≈ 12 V:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(25.2-12)(0.5)}{30\times10^{-6}\cdot 48\times10^{3}} = 4.6\ \text{A}$$

The ripple term is ΔI²/12 = 1.8 A². This is negligible against 6400 A², so I_rms ≈ I is valid.

### 2.2 Temperature dependence (closed-loop)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + k(T_j - 25)\right], \quad k = \frac{1.6-1}{100} = 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

Because loss depends on T_j and T_j depends on loss, this forms a fixed point:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + R_{\theta}\, P(T_j) \;\Rightarrow\; T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + R_\theta\left(P_0 - 25kP_0' \right) }{1 - R_\theta k P_0'}$$

where P₀' = 2I²R₂₅. **Thermal runaway** occurs if R_θ·k·P₀' ≥ 1. This is the stability criterion checked in §4.

### 2.3 Values at 80 A burst (per channel)

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond (2 FETs) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W |
| 100 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W |
| 125 °C | 1.60 mΩ | **20.5 W** |

### 2.4 Parasitic I²R terms (often missed, significant at 80 A)

- **Shunt:** I²·R_sh = 6400 × 0.25 mΩ = **1.60 W**
- **Copper (pads, planes, vias):** 6400 × 0.3 mΩ = **1.92 W**, rising ~30 % hot → **~2.5 W**
- **Solder joints / motor pads:** ~0.1 mΩ → **0.64 W**

**Total conduction loss per channel at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C ≈ 25.2 W.**

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Hard switching occurs on the PWM-ing high-side FET only. The low-side synchronous FET turns on with ZVS after the body diode has already conducted during dead time.

### 3.1 Transition times (gate-charge method)

$$t_{r,v} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}\, R_{g,on}}{V_{drv} - V_{pl}} = \frac{10\,\text{nC}\cdot 4.7}{7} = 6.7\ \text{ns}$$

$$t_{f,v} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}\, R_{g,off}}{V_{pl}} = \frac{10\,\text{nC}\cdot 2.2}{3.0} = 7.3\ \text{ns}$$

Add current-transition time (Q_gs2 portion) plus layout inductance. The realistic effective crossover time is t_on ≈ t_off ≈ **12 ns**. Validate this on the bench with a 1 GHz probe on V_ds.

### 3.2 V-I overlap loss

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I \,(t_{on}+t_{off}) f_{sw} = 0.5\cdot25.2\cdot80\cdot24\text{ns}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.16\ W}$$

### 3.3 Capacitive and recovery loss

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 35\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.042\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

Q_rr scales with di/dt and current. At 80 A, use about 1.5× the datasheet value: **~0.07 W**.

### 3.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

There are two dead-time intervals per PWM period:

$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot200\text{ns}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

This is a tuning lever. Dropping dead time to 50 ns (AM32 permits this with good layout) saves 0.3 W per channel.
