## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms
Electrical angle from mechanical angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a, \qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt 3}$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}$$

Inverse Park uses the transpose of the same matrix.

### 1.2 Plant and PI design (pole-zero cancellation)
The d-q voltage equations are:

$$v_d = R i_d + L \dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q, \qquad
v_q = R i_q + L \dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

After feedforward decoupling, each axis reduces to $G(s) = \dfrac{1}{Ls + R}$.
