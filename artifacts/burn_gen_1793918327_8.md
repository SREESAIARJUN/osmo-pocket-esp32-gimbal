# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC: 80 A Burst Thermal, 48 kHz Switching, and Bidirectional DShot600 Architecture

**Job #22: Design Basis Document, Rev A**

---

## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In every production-viable 4-in-1 stack, the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600. Four dedicated ESC MCUs run AM32 or BLHeli_32 and generate the 48 kHz PWM, commutation, and eRPM telemetry. This document models both halves.

```
 6S LiPo ──► [ESC board: 4× (MCU + 3-ph driver + 6 FETs)] ──► 4 motors
                   ▲  DShot600 bidir (1 wire/motor, inverted idle-low)
                   │
 [FC board: STM32H743 @ 480 MHz, ArduPilot]  ◄── 8-pin 0.8 mm JST-SH harness
```
