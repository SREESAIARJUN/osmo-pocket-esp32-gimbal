# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #58): Design Model

**Scope:** 80 A burst conduction and switching loss model at 48 kHz, thermal transient budget, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and reference netlists.

> **Engineering caveat.** The MOSFET, gate driver and PCB parameters below are representative assumptions, not values from a frozen BOM. Replace them with your datasheet values; the equations stay the same. Before tape-out, validate the netlists with ERC/DRC and validate the thermal model with a thermocouple or IR test on a populated board. The model shows that **80 A per channel on a nano (20×20 / 30×30) footprint is a seconds-scale burst rating, not a continuous rating.**

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Topology reduction

In 120° block commutation with complementary PWM:

- One phase is PWM'd: the high-side (HS) conducts for D and the low-side (LS) for 1−D.
- One phase has its LS fully on.
- One phase floats.

At every instant exactly **two FET channels are in series with the motor current**, regardless of D. Per phase leg, averaged over an electrical cycle:

$$
P_{leg} = \tfrac{1}{3}\left[I^2R\,D + I^2R\,(1-D)\right] + \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R = \tfrac{2}{3}I^2R
$$

$$
\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + I^2 R_{pcb}}
$$

### 2.2 Temperature dependence (self-consistent)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]
$$
