## 0. Architecture Correction and Design Assumptions

### 0.1 The H743 does not switch the bridges

ArduPilot does not perform BLDC commutation. The architecture is a **two-tier system**:

| Tier | Device | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (400 MHz) | ArduPilot. Generates 4× DShot600 and decodes bi-directional eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) running AM32 | 6-step commutation, **48 kHz PWM**, back-EMF zero-cross detection, DShot decode, GCR telemetry encode. |
| Gate drive (×4) | FD6288Q 3-phase half-bridge driver | Level shifting, bootstrap high-side drive. |
| Power stage (×4) | 6× N-MOSFET per motor (24 total) | Phase switching. |

The 48 kHz loss analysis therefore applies to the ESC MCU/bridge domain. The DShot/DMA analysis applies to the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step synchronous (complementary) PWM

In any 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct:
- **Phase A (PWM leg):** high-side conducts for D·T_sw. Low-side conducts synchronously for (1−D)·T_sw, ignoring dead time.
- **Phase B (return leg):** low-side is on continuously.

The current I_ph always flows through exactly **two** MOSFET channels:

$$
P_{cond,sector} = I_{ph}^2 R_{hot}\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{ph}^2 R_{hot} = 2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{hot}
$$

This result is **independent of duty cycle**, which is why low-throttle, high-current conditions (e.g., prop strikes, punch-outs from hover) are thermally as severe as full throttle.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence (positive feedback)

$$
R_{hot} = R_{ds(on),25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_hot ≈ 1.5 × 1.5 = **2.25 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Copper path resistance

Include the trace and plane resistance between the battery pads and the motor pads. For a 2 oz plane (sheet resistance ≈ 0.25 mΩ/□ at 25 °C, +0.39 %/K):

$$
R_{Cu} \approx 0.25\,\text{m}\Omega/\square \times N_\square \times [1+0.0039(T-25)]
$$

A realistic nano layout has about 2 squares in series per current path on paralleled layers, giving **R_Cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ** effective.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A burst

$$
P_{cond,FET}^{motor} = 2 (80)^2 (2.25\times10^{-3}) = 28.8\ \text{W}
$$
$$
P_{Cu}^{motor} = 2 (80)^2 (0.3\times10^{-3}) \approx 3.8\ \text{W}
$$
$$
\boxed{P_{cond}^{motor} \approx 32.6\ \text{W}} \qquad \boxed{P_{cond}^{4\,motors} \approx 130\ \text{W}}
$$

Each FET conducts on average about 1/3 of the electrical period:

$$
P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{I^2 R_{hot}}{3} \approx 4.8\ \text{W}
$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches in each sector (one hard-switched high-side FET, one synchronous low-side FET).

| Loss term | Equation | Value per motor |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap (hard switching) | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw | ½·25.2·80·40 ns·48 k = **1.94 W** |
| Coss discharge | Q_oss·V·f_sw | 30 nC·25.2·48 k = **0.036 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 40 nC·25.2·48 k = **0.048 W** |
| Dead-time body-diode conduction | V_SD·I·2t_dt·f_sw | 0.8·80·200 ns·48 k = **0.61 W** |
