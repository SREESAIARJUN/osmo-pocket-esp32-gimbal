**Engineering caveat (read first).** A nano-format (20×20 or 25×25 mm) board cannot dissipate 4 × 80 A continuously. The math below shows that 80 A is a thermal-capacitance-limited burst of roughly 1–3 s, depending on mass and airflow. It is not a rating. Treat the 80 A number as a transient spec and derive the label from Section 4.3.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

At any commutation step, one phase is PWM'd and one phase is held low:

- **PWM'd phase:** the high-side (HS) FET conducts for duty D. The complementary low-side (LS) FET conducts for 1−D.
- **Return phase:** the LS FET conducts for 100% of the step.
- **Floating phase:** carries no current (BEMF sensing).

The per-FET conduction energy over one PWM period is:

$$
P_{HS} = D\,I_{rms}^2 R_{DS}(T_j), \quad P_{LS,sync} = (1-D)\,I_{rms}^2 R_{DS}(T_j), \quad P_{LS,ret} = I_{rms}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

Summing these gives the per-channel conduction loss, which is **independent of D**:

$$
\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + I_{rms}^2 R_{Cu}}
$$

### 1.2 Ripple correction

The phase current ripple is:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{BEMF})\,D}{L_{ph} f_{sw}}, \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I_{avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}
$$

For a typical 5" motor (L_ph ≈ 15 µH line-to-line), with D = 0.5 and V_bus − V_BEMF ≈ 6 V at high load:

ΔI = 6 × 0.5 / (15e-6 × 48e3) ≈ 4.2 A, so ΔI²/12 ≈ 1.5 A².

This is negligible next to 6400 A², so I_rms ≈ 80 A.

### 1.3 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on) (self-consistent)

R_DS(on) rises with junction temperature:

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

Because T_j itself depends on dissipated power, the model is a fixed-point problem:

$$
T_j = T_{ref} + P(T_j)\,Z_{th} \;\Rightarrow\; T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + Z_{th}\, I^2 R_{25}(1-25\alpha)}{1 - Z_{th}\, I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha}
$$

**Thermal runaway criterion:** the solution only exists while

$$
Z_{th} I^2 R_{25}\alpha < 1
$$

For the return LS FET at 80 A:

I²R₂₅α = 6400 × 1e-3 × 0.0045 = 0.0288 W/K.

Runaway therefore occurs if the effective Z_th exceeds 34.7 K/W. That is plausible for a nano board in still air, so airflow is mandatory.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd high-side FET hard-switches. The LS FET switches at ~0 V (body-diode clamped), so its switching loss is soft.

### 2.1 Transition times

The gate plateau voltage is V_pl ≈ 4.5 V. With driver output 10 V, R_g,ext = 2.2 Ω and R_g,int = 1 Ω:

$$
I_{g,on} \approx \frac{10-4.5}{3.2} = 1.7\,\text{A (driver-limited to } \sim 1\,\text{A)}, \quad I_{g,off} \approx \frac{4.5}{3.2} \approx 1.0\,\text{A}
$$

The transition times follow from the gate charge:

$$
t_{r} \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_g} \approx \frac{4+8\ \text{nC}}{1\ \text{A}} = 12\ \text{ns}, \quad t_f \approx 12\ \text{ns}
$$
