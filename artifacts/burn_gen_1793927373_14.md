**Architectural correction 1: the H743 does not commutate the motors.** In a 4-in-1 ESC, each channel runs its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 with AM32, or BLHeli_32). The H743 only sends DShot600 frames and receives eRPM telemetry. The 48 kHz PWM and dead-time generation therefore live in the ESC MCUs. The H743 timer and DMA plan in Section 4 covers the DShot link only.

**Architectural correction 2: an 80 A continuous rating is not physically achievable on a nano-class board.** On a 20×20 mm or 30.5×30.5 mm board, 80 A is only possible as a time-limited burst. Section 3 quantifies the allowed duration.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology

The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification and high-side complementary PWM at duty D. Over one electrical cycle, each phase leg spends:

- **120° as the PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D, and the low-side FET conducts for (1 − D) as the synchronous rectifier.
- **120° as the static low leg:** the low-side FET conducts 100%.
- **120° floating:** 2 × 60°, used for back-EMF zero-crossing detection.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.2 × 1.45 = **1.74 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Per-FET average conduction loss

$$P_{HS}=\frac{I^2R_{DS}\,D}{3},\qquad P_{LS}=\frac{I^2R_{DS}}{3}\big[(1-D)+1\big]$$

### 1.4 Per-channel loss

Two FETs are always in series in the current path:

$$P_{cond,ch}=2\,I^2R_{DS}(T_j)+2\,I^2R_{cu}$$

Evaluated at the reference values:

- I² = 6400 A²
- FET conduction: 2 × 6400 × 1.74 mΩ = **22.3 W**
- Copper: 2 × 6400 × 0.3 mΩ = **3.8 W**
- Shunt (0.2 mΩ, one per channel): 6400 × 0.2 mΩ = **1.3 W**

**Worst single FET:** the low side at low duty.

$$P_{LS,max}\to \tfrac{2}{3}\cdot 6400\cdot1.74\,\text{m}\Omega = 7.4\ \text{W}\quad(D\to0)$$

At D = 1, the load is balanced at 3.7 W per FET. Note that high phase current at low duty only occurs during stall or hard deceleration, because

$$I_{batt} \approx D\cdot I_{ph}$$

An 80 A phase current at D = 0.3 draws only about 24 A from the battery. This is why stalled or blocked motors kill the low-side FETs first.

### 1.5 Ripple correction

The full RMS expression includes phase-current ripple:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2+\frac{\Delta I^2}{12},\qquad \Delta I=\frac{(V_{bus}-E)\,D}{L_{ph}f_{sw}}$
