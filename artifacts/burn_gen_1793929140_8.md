## 1. Field-Oriented Control (inner current loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle and transforms

Electrical angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{0}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, two shunts, with $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant and decoupled PI current control

The PMSM model in the dq frame (surface-mount, so $L_d = L_q = L$):

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

Control law (PI plus feedforward decoupling and back-EMF compensation):

$$v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e L i_d + \hat\omega_e \hat\lambda_m$$

Pole-zero cancellation tuning, with current bandwidth $\omega_c$ (rad/s):

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

Choose $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/20$, which gives ≈ 2π·1 kHz at 20 kHz.

Voltage limiting uses a circular limit, $\|v_{dq}\| \le V_{bus}/\sqrt3$ (linear SVPWM region). Saturate with the d-axis taking priority:

$$v_d \leftarrow \mathrm{clamp}(v_d, \pm V_{max}),\quad v_q \leftarrow \mathrm{clamp}\!\left(v_q, \pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}\right)$$

Anti-windup uses back-calculation: $I \mathrel{+}= T_s\,[K_i e + K_{aw}(v_{sat}-v_{unsat})]$ with $K_{aw} = 1/K_p$.
