## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude invariant, with i_c = −i_a − i_b):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, with θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant Model (SPMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

### 1.3 Current PI with Decoupling and Back-EMF Feedforward

$$
v_d^* = K_{p,i}e_d + K_{i,i}\!\int e_d\,dt - \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q^* = K_{p,i}e_q + K_{i,i}\!\int e_q\,dt + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

**Gain selection by pole-zero cancellation.** Choosing the gains below places a zero at s = −R/L. This turns the closed loop into a first-order system with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_{p,i}=L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i}=R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10}
$$

For f_pwm = 20 kHz, use ω_c = 2π·1500 rad/s.

### 1.4 Voltage Limiting and Anti-Windup

- The voltage vector is limited to the linear SVPWM circle, |v| ≤ V_bus/√3.
- d-axis has priority.
- The integrator is clamped using back-calculation:

$$
I_{k+1}=I_k + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw}(v_{sat}-v_{unsat})
$$

### 1.5 SVPWM by Min/Max Zero-Sequence Injection

$$
v_{abc} = \text{invClarke}(v_\alpha,v_\beta),\qquad
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max v_{abc}+\min v_{abc}\big)
$$

$$
d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$
