## 2. FOC Current Loop Equations

### 2.1 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)\qquad (i_a+i_b+i_c=0)
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix},
\qquad \theta_e = p\,(\theta_m-\theta_{off})
$$

### 2.2 PMSM dq model

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

$$
\tau = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$

### 2.3 Current PI by pole-zero cancellation

The plant is $G(s)=1/(Ls+R)$ and the controller is $C(s)=K_p + K_i/s$. Choosing

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c
$$

gives the closed loop $T(s)=\omega_c/(s+\omega_c)$.

With L = 2 mH, R = 5 Ω and ω_c = 2π·1500:

- K_p ≈ 18.8 V/A
- K_i ≈ 47 100 V/(A·s)

Keep ω_c ≤ ω_s/10 to tolerate the 1.5-sample PWM/ADC delay.

### 2.4 Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$
v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(e_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(e_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

### 2.5 Voltage limiting

Limit the vector circularly, d-axis priority:

$$
|v_d|\le V_{max},\quad |v_q| \le \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2},\quad V_{max}=\tfrac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}\cdot 0.95
$$

### 2.6 Inverse Park and SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\quad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

$$
v_a=v_\alpha,\; v_b=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\; v_c=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max+\min),\quad D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

### 2.7 Angle delay compensation

Use $\theta_e^{pwm} = \theta_e + \omega_e\cdot 1.5\,T_s$ for the inverse Park transform.
