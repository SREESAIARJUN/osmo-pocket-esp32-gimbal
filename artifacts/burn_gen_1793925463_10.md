## 0. Architecture Partition and Assumptions

### 0.1 Who switches the bridges

The STM32H743 does **not** drive the bridges. It generates bidirectional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry. Each of the four motor channels has its own ESC MCU running AM32 (or BLHeli_32-class) firmware. That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM, dead time, and BEMF commutation.

| Block | Device (reference choice) | Function |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100, 8×8 mm) | ArduPilot, DShot600 bidir, sensors |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32) | AM32, 48 kHz PWM, BEMF commutation |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q (QFN24, 3-phase, bootstrap) | HS/LS gate drive |
| MOSFET ×24 | 40 V N-ch, PDFN 3.3×3.3 (generic, see 0.3) | 3-phase bridges |
