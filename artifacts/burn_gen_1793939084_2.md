## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R = 5.6 Ω (phase), L = 2.1 mH, Kt ≈ 0.10 N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701), SPI |
| MCU | STM32G474 / H7, FPU, center-aligned PWM 20 kHz, ADC triggered at PWM center |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the payload (camera) plate, SPI at 24 MHz, ODR 8 kHz, ±2000 dps, ±16 g |
| Bus | 4S Li-ion, V_bus measured every PWM cycle |

### Loop rates and bandwidth separation

Each loop should be at least 5–10× slower than the loop inside it.

```
 Angle loop (1 kHz, ω_θ ≈ 2π·8 Hz)
   └─► Velocity loop (4 kHz, ω_v ≈ 2π·40 Hz), inertial rate from fused gyro
         └─► Current loop / FOC (20 kHz, ω_c ≈ 2π·1 kHz)
               + anti-cogging feedforward i_q,ff(θ_m)
               + optional base-disturbance feedforward
```

For stabilization, the velocity and angle loops close on **inertial** quantities from the IMU. The encoder is used for commutation, the cogging lookup, the rate PLL (fallback and blending), and the frame-to-joint mapping.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

Amplitude-invariant Clarke transform, assuming balanced currents (i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform with electrical angle θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset):

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 Plant and decoupling
