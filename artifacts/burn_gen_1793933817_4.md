## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical Angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

θ_off is found at commissioning. Inject i_d = I_align with θ_e = 0, wait until the rotor settles, then read θ_m.

### 1.2 Clarke Transform (amplitude-invariant; 3 shunts with zero-sum enforcement)

$$i_\alpha = \tfrac{2}{3}\left(i_a - \tfrac12 i_b - \tfrac12 i_c\right),\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_b - i_c)$$

### 1.3 Park Transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant in the d-q Frame

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

### 1.5 Current PI with Decoupling and Back-EMF Feedforward

Pole-zero cancellation gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c$$

The discrete form (sample time T_s), with feedforward, is:

$$v_q^{*} = K_{p,i} e_q + I_q + \underbrace{\omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m}_{\text{FF}},\qquad v_d^{*} = K_{p,i} e_d + I_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
