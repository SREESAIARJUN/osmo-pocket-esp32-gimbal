### Architecture correction (important)

The H743 should **not** commutate the motors directly. ArduPilot has no in-tree 6-step or FOC ESC firmware for driving four motors from the flight-controller MCU.

The correct architecture is:

- **H743 (ArduPilot)** sends bidirectional DShot600 to **4 dedicated ESC MCUs**.
- Each ESC MCU is, for example, an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or an EFM8 running Bluejay.
- Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs.
