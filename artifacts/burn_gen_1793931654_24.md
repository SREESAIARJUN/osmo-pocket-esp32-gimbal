## 0. Architecture Baseline and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the **flight controller** MCU. It does not commutate the motors. A 4-in-1 ESC uses **four dedicated ESC MCUs** (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32, one per motor. The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right] \quad\text{or use the datasheet curve directly}$$

At T_j ≈ 135 °C, k_T ≈ 1.6, so **R_hot = 1.6 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step current paths

At any instant, two phases conduct and one floats.

- In the **PWM'd leg**, the high-side (HS) carries current for fraction D. The synchronous low-side (LS) carries it for fraction (1 − D).
- In the **return leg**, the LS is fully on.

So the current always passes through exactly **two R_ds in series**, apart from the dead-time intervals.

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.6\text{ mΩ} = \mathbf{20.5\ W}$$

Each of the six FETs carries current for one third of the electrical period. Averaged over a full electrical cycle:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}}{3} = \mathbf{3.41\ W}$$

### 1.3 Copper, shunt, and connector conduction

| Element | Model | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus shunt (0.5 mΩ) | (D·I_ph)²·R_sh | (76)²·0.5 mΩ = 2.9 W per motor |
| PCB phase/bus copper (~0.3 mΩ path) | I²·R_cu | 1.9 W per motor |
