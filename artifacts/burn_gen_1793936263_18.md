# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #60): Power-Stage, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture Constraints

Three facts shape the rest of this document.

1. **The H743 cannot commutate the motors directly.** Four sensorless BLDC channels at 48 kHz PWM, each with BEMF zero-cross detection, would consume the H743's advanced timers, ADCs and interrupt budget while it also runs ArduPilot. Every production 4-in-1 design uses one small MCU per channel instead.
   - Architecture: **H743 flight controller → 4× bidirectional DShot600 → 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 → 4× three-phase bridges.**
   - The DShot section below covers the H743 side. The power section covers the ESC side.
2. **80 A is a single-channel burst, not a four-channel continuous rating, on a 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm board.** The thermal model in §3 shows this quantitatively. The board can survive about 1.5 s of all-four-channel 80 A from a 40 °C start. Its continuous rating is about 25 A per channel at realistic prop-wash cooling.
3. **The connector and wiring limit total current before the board does.** 4 × 80 A = 320 A of motor current. At full throttle (D ≈ 1) the battery current is ≈ ΣD·I_motor. An XT60 is rated about 60 A continuous, so 80 A must be treated as a transient per motor, not a system operating point.
