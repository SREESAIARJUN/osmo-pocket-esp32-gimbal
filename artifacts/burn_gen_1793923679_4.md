# STM32H743 Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Systems Architecture and Analysis Package

**Scope:** 80 A burst conduction model, 48 kHz switching-loss model, transient thermal model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and reference netlists.

**Status:** Design-intent draft. All component parameters are representative values for current 40 V trench MOSFETs. Before release, they must be replaced with values from the selected parts' datasheets.

---

## 0. Architecture Review: Read Before the Math

I found three issues that change the design and need to be resolved first.

| # | Finding | Consequence | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | The H743 is the **flight controller**, not the motor commutator. Running four 6-step or FOC loops at 48 kHz on the H743 while it also runs ArduPilot is not a supported configuration. | Architecture | Use an H743 FC plus 4× dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32, or BLHeli_32-class), connected over bidirectional DShot600. This document assumes this split. |
| A2 | "80 A burst" × 4 channels on a "nano" footprint. 24 power FETs in 5×6 mm DFN will not fit on a 20×20 mm board. | Mechanical / thermal | Minimum 30.5×30.5 mm ESC board with 3.3×3.3 mm or 5×6 mm FETs. Stack the FC separately at 20×20 or 30×30 mm. |
| A3 | Even on 30×30 mm, the thermal model (§3) shows that 80 A **simultaneous** on all four channels is limited to about 2–3 s. Continuous rating is about 40–45 A per channel. | Product spec | Specify the rating as "45 A continuous / 80 A for 2 s burst, all channels" and enforce it in firmware (§3.5). |
