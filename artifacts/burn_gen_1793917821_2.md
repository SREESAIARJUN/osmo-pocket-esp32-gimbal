## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Scope

- **Topology.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller and runs ArduPilot. Each of the four bridges is driven by its own ESC MCU, such as an AT32F421 running AM32, through a 3-phase gate driver. ## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 120 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 95) = **1.43 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Per-FET duty in 6-step with synchronous rectification

Each phase leg spends 1/3 of the electrical cycle as the high (PWM) leg, 1/3 as the low leg, and 1/3 floating. With PWM duty D:

| FET | Conduction fraction |
|---|---|
| High-side | δ_HS = D/3 |
| Low-side | δ_LS = 1/3 + (1−D)/3 |
| Sum per leg | 2/3, independent of D |

The per-FET losses are:

$$P_{cond,HS}=I^2R_{DS}\frac{D}{3},\qquad P_{cond,LS}=I^2R_{DS}\frac{2-D}{3}$$

The per-channel total (3 legs) is independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch}=3\cdot\tfrac{2}{3}I^2R_{DS}=2I^2R_{DS}$$
