## 1. Conduction Loss Model

In 6-step synchronous PWM, two phases are active at any instant:
- The **high phase** is PWM'd. The high-side FET conducts for duty D, and its complementary low-side FET conducts for 1−D.
- The **low phase** low-side FET is held on 100% of the time.

So exactly two FET channels carry current at every moment, independent of D (ignoring dead time):

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2 R_{ds}\,D + I^2 R_{ds}(1-D) + I^2 R_{ds} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Temperature dependence:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| Condition | R_ds | P_cond per motor | ×4 motors |
|---|---|---|---|
| 80 A, T_j = 25 °C | 1.3 mΩ | 2·6400·0.0013 = **16.6 W** | 66.6 W |
| 80 A, T_j = 125 °C | 2.0 mΩ | 2·6400·0.0020 = **25.6 W** | 102.4 W |

**Parasitic copper and shunt losses (per motor, 80 A):**
- Phase pad, via, and wire-pad path ≈ 0.25 mΩ per conducting path, giving 2·6400·0.25 mΩ = **3.2 W**
- Low-side current shunt, 0.25 mΩ (only if a per-channel shunt is fitted): 6400·0.25 mΩ = **1.6 W**

**Thermal runaway check.** Because R_ds rises with T_j, the steady-state solution exists only if:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,R_{\theta} = 2I^2R_{ds,25}\,\alpha\,R_{\theta} < 1$$

With R_θ ≈ 25 K/W (board to ambient, see §4), at 80 A: 2·6400·0.0013·0.005·25 = **2.08 > 1**. The board is **thermally unstable at 80 A continuous**. That confirms 80 A can only be a burst rating.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

The hard-switched device is the high-side FET of the PWM phase. The low-side FET is soft-switched because its body diode conducts during dead time.

### 2.1 V–I overlap

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5\cdot25.2\cdot80\cdot30\text{ns}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery of the complementary body diode

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{nC}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

Note that Q_rr scales with di/dt and I_F. At 80 A, datasheet Q_rr can be 2–3× the nominal value. Budget **0.15 W**.
