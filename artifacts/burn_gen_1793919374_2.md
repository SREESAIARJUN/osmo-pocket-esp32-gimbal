## 0. Assumptions and Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 @ 170 MHz, FPU, 3× advanced timers |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs $p = 7$), $R \approx 5\ \Omega$, $L \approx 2\ \text{mH}$, $K_t \approx 0.08\ \text{Nm/A}$ |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per axis (AS5048A / MT6701), SPI |
| Current sense | 2-shunt low-side, sampled at PWM center |
| PWM | 25 kHz center-aligned |
| IMU A | ICM-42688-P on the **camera plate** (attitude feedback) |
| IMU B | ICM-42688-P on the **base/frame** (disturbance reference) |
| Kinematics | Yaw (outer) → Roll → Pitch (inner, camera) |

**Loop rates (cascade separation ≥ 4–10× at each level):**

```
25 kHz  : ADC → Clarke/Park → current PI → SVPWM          (ISR, highest prio)
 8 kHz  : IMU A/B FIFO read → AAF → notch bank → rate signals
 4 kHz  : velocity PI (+ cogging FF, base-rate FF)  ×3 axes
 1 kHz  : attitude fusion (Mahony) → angle P/PI   ×3 axes
 50 Hz  : adaptive notch frequency supervision, cogging LMS commit, telemetry
```

## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

$\theta_{\text{off}}$ is found at calibration by applying $v_d > 0,\ v_q = 0$ and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, 2 shunts, $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a, \qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.4 Plant (surface PMSM, $L_d = L_q = L$)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q, \qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Torque: $\tau = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q$.
