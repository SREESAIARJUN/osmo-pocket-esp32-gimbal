## 0. Architecture Corrections

Three requirements, as stated, would cause problems. They should be resolved before any detailed design work.

| Issue | Problem | Resolution in this package |
|---|---|---|
| **The H743 cannot commutate motors under ArduPilot** | ArduPilot has no ESC commutation stack. The flight controller sends DShot commands.
