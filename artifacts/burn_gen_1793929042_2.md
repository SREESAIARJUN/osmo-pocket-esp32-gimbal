## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology

The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM, which is the AM32/BLHeli_32 default.

- During the **ON** interval, current flows through the high-side FET of phase A and the low-side FET of phase B.
- During the **OFF** interval, current freewheels through the low-side FET of phase A (synchronous) and the low-side FET of phase B.

So at every instant, **two FETs carry the full phase current** regardless of duty cycle D. Conduction loss is therefore approximately independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot \left[ D\cdot R_{HS} + (1-D)\cdot R_{LS} + R_{LS} \right] \approx 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

### 1.2 Numbers at 80 A, hot

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = 2 \times 9.6 = \mathbf{19.2\ W/channel}$$

**Copper path loss.** Assume 2 oz outer layers plus 2 oz internal planes and a phase/bus path resistance of about 0.5 mΩ per channel:

$$P_{Cu,ch} = 80^2 \times 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega = 3.2\ \text{W}$$

**Shunt loss** (if a per-channel 0.25 mΩ shunt is used; skip if sensing is low-side on a total shunt):

$$P_{shunt} = 80^2 \times 0.25\,\text{m}\Omega = 1.6\ \text{W}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Only the PWM'd half-bridge switches hard. One hard-switched leg per channel at any time.

### 2.1 V–I overlap

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12\, V_{bus}\, I_{ph}\,(t_r + t_f)\, f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss}\, V_{bus}\, f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

The synchronous FET's body diode conducts during dead time, then recovers.

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\, V_{bus}\, f_{sw} = 30\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I_{ph}\, (2 t_d)\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

This loss is dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET.

$$P_{g} = 2\, Q_g\, V_{GS}\, f_{sw} = 2 \times 40\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.038\ \text{W}$$

### 2.6 Switching subtotal

$$P_{sw,ch} \approx 1.94 + 0.05 + 0.04 + 0.61 + 0.04 = \mathbf{2.68\ W/channel}$$

**Design note on 48 kHz vs 24 kHz.** Halving f_sw saves about 1.3 W per channel at 80 A. That is small next to the 19 W conduction loss. 48 kHz is justified for low-inductance motors and smoother low-throttle behavior, not for efficiency. For a thermal win, spend the money on R_DS(on), not on lower f_sw.

## 3. Loss Budget Summary (80 A burst, T_j ≈ 125 °C)

| Loss term | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| Switching (total) | 2.7 W | 10.7 W |
| PCB copper | 3.2 W | 12.8 W |
| Shunt (optional) | 1.6 W | 6.4 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 26.7 W** | **≈ 106.7 W** |
| Without shunt | 25.1 W | 100.3 W |

**Efficiency check** at 80 A × 4 motors, 22.2 V nominal, D ≈ 0.9:

- Input power: P_in ≈ 4 × 80 × 22.2 × 0.9 ≈ 6.4 kW.
- Loss: ≈ 1.6 % (electrically fine).
- **Heat density is the problem:** about 100 W into roughly a 30 × 30 mm board.

## 4. Thermal Model

### 4.1 Network (per FET, Cauer-reduced)

```
P_FET ──[R_θJC=1.5]──●──[R_θCB≈2.0 (solder+vias)]──●──[R_θ,spread]──●──[R_θBA]── T_amb
                     C_j≈0.02 J/K                  C_board          (shared node, all 8 hot FETs)
```

The hottest FET (the synchronous low-side, which conducts 100 % of the cycle) dissipates:

$$P_{FET,max} \approx I^2 R_{hot} + P_{dt}/2 + P_{rr} \approx 9.6 + 0.3 + 0.04 \approx 10\ \text{W}$$

### 4.2 Junction-to-board rise (reaches steady state in ≈ 10–50 ms)

$$\Delta T_{jb} = P_{FET}\,(R_{\theta JC} + R_{\theta CB}) = 10 \times 3.5 = \mathbf{35\ K}$$
