## 0. Assumptions and Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 5.5 Ω, L ≈ 2.0 mH, K_t ≈ 0.09 N·m/A |
| Drive | 3-phase half-bridge, inline shunts on 2 phases, V_bus = 12 V |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701 class) on each joint |
| MCU | STM32G4 @ 170 MHz, single-precision FPU, CORDIC optional |
| IMU A | ICM-42688-P on the camera plate (attitude and feedback) |
| IMU B | ICM-42688-P on the handle/base (vibration reference and base-motion feedforward) |
| Joint order | base → yaw (z) → roll (x) → pitch (y) → camera |

**Loop rates (time-scale separation of at least 5× per stage):**

| Loop | Rate | Closed-loop bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | ~1.5 kHz |
| Velocity (gyro-based) | 4 kHz | ~150 Hz |
| Angle (attitude) | 1 kHz | ~20 Hz |
| IMU ODR | 8 kHz (decimated to 4 kHz) | — |
| Attitude estimator | 1 kHz | — |

**Why two IMUs in these locations.** IMU B sits upstream of the disturbance path, so it measures the vibration the handle injects. That gives three uses:
1. A clean reference for **tracking the dominant vibration frequency**, which then steers the notch filters on IMU A.
2. **Base-rate feedforward**, so joint velocity is computed relative to the moving base.
3. **Degraded-mode fallback** if IMU A fails its consistency check.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle with latency compensation

Raw mechanical angle from the encoder, with N_enc = 2¹⁴ counts:

$$\theta_m = \frac{2\pi \, c}{N_{enc}}, \qquad \theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$$

Measurement and PWM-update latency is about 1.5 sampling periods (one period of computation plus half a period of PWM zero-order hold). The angle used for the inverse Park transform is therefore advanced:

$$\hat\theta_e = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s, \qquad \omega_e = p\,\omega_m$$

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms

Two shunts, with i_c = −i_a − i_b (amplitude-invariant form):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.3 Plant and PI current controller

PMSM in the dq frame:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)$$
