**Flag 1: form factor vs. current.** A true 20×20 mm 4-in-1 typically sustains roughly 35–45 A continuous. The model in §3 reproduces this: 80 A is achievable only as a time-limited burst of about 3 s. Do not market it as continuous.

**Flag 2: battery current.** Four motors at 80 A phase current and high duty cycle draw up to about 300 A from the battery. That exceeds XT60 (≈60 A continuous) and XT90 ratings and stresses pack C-rating. Real hover-to-punch profiles rarely load all four motors at 80 A simultaneously. Size the input connector, bulk capacitance and copper for your actual mission profile.

**Flag 3: architecture.** The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs directly. Each ESC channel uses a dedicated ESC MCU (AT32F421 running AM32 shown here) and a 3-phase gate driver. The H743 talks to the ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.2 mΩ × 1.6 = **1.92 mΩ**.

Add the copper/solder parasitic per current path, R_cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ (2 oz inner + 2 oz outer, short pours). Effective value per switch: **R_eff ≈ 2.22 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step synchronous commutation

In each 60° sector, current flows through two legs:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side switch conducts for D·T. The low-side switch conducts for (1−D)·T via synchronous rectification. The current path through this leg is therefore always one R_DS.
- **Return leg:** the low-side switch is on for the full sector.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{eff}$$

At 80 A:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 6400 \times 2 \times 2.22\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{28.4\ W\ per\ channel}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET average

Each of the 6 FETs conducts about 1/3 of the electrical cycle (2 sectors of 6):

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6} \approx 4.7\ \text{W (avg)}, \qquad P_{cond,FET,peak} = I^2 R_{eff} = 14.2\ \text{W (instantaneous)}$$

The instantaneous peak matters for junction ripple at low eRPM. When the electrical period exceeds about 10 ms, T_j tracks the instantaneous value. Desync and stall cases are therefore the worst case.
