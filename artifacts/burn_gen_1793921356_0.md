# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #46): Power-Stage & DShot Architecture

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Design Warnings

Three facts determine the rest of the design:

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every viable 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel runs its own MCU (STM32G071 or AT32F421 with AM32/BLHeli_32). That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 only sends bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. The design therefore has two separate domains, with DShot as the only link between them.
2. **"80 A burst" is a thermal-transient rating, not a steady-state one.** A nano-format board (20×20 or 30.5×30.5 mm) cannot dissipate the 100+ W generated with four channels at 80 A. Section 3 derives how long such a burst can last.
3. **All four channels at 80 A means 320 A on the battery bus,** about 8 kW at 6S. That is not physically credible for a nano XT30/XT60 class input. The model below covers this worst case, but the realistic spec is **80 A per channel, non-coincident, ≤ 2 to 5 s.**
