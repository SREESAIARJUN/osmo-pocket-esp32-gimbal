**Architecture: the H743 should not commutate the motors.** In a production 4-in-1 stack, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot on the flight controller. The ESC board carries 4 independent ESC MCUs (STM32G071 / AT32F421 class running AM32 or BLHeli_32). The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600. Running 4× FOC/6-step at 48 kHz plus the flight stack on one H743 is technically possible, but ArduPilot does not support it, and you lose per-channel fault isolation. The rest of this document assumes the split architecture.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step with synchronous rectification

At any instant, one phase leg is PWM-switching and one leg's low-side is held ON. Current always flows through exactly **two** conducting channels in series:

- During the PWM on-time (D): the high-side of the PWM leg plus the low-side of the return leg.
- During the PWM off-time (1−D): the sync low-side of the PWM leg plus the low-side of the return leg. Body-diode conduction occurs only during dead time (see §2).

The conduction loss per ESC channel is therefore **independent of duty cycle** to first order:

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \cdot I_{ph}^2 \cdot R_{ds(on)}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence of R_ds(on)

$$
R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{ds(on),25} \cdot \left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0060\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

| T_j | Multiplier | R_ds(on) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 | 1.50 mΩ |
| 75 °C | 1.30 | 1.95 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.45 | 2.18 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.60 | 2.40 mΩ |

### 1.3 Burst conduction loss at T_j = 100 °C

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 2.18\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{27.9\ W\ per\ channel}
$$

Each conducting FET dissipates about 14 W. Because the return-leg low-side carries current for 1/3 of the electrical cycle and the PWM leg's two FETs share the remainder, the time-averaged loss per individual FET is roughly:

$$
P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{27.9}{6} \cdot \frac{6}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{2}\ ... \approx 4.7\text{–}9.3\ \text{W}
$$

The exact value depends on duty cycle. Use 9.3 W as the worst case for the low-side FETs at high throttle.

### 1.4 Parasitic copper and sense losses

These are usually forgotten and are not small:

| Element | Resistance | Loss @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Shunt (if used) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| Phase pad + motor wire solder joint | ~0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| PCB DC bus + phase copper (2 oz, ~15 mm, multi-layer) | ~0.4 mΩ | 2.6 W |
| **Subtotal** | | **≈ 6.1 W** |

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap

Switching transition times come from the gate driver current and Miller charge:

$$
t_{r} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g,src}} = \frac{8\,\text{nC}}{1.0\,\text{A}}\approx 8\,\text{ns},\qquad t_f \approx \frac{8\,\text{nC}}{1.5\,\text{A}} \approx 5.3\,\text{ns}
$$

Add di/dt-limited current commutation through loop inductance (~3 nH) and the gate resistor. Use a realistic **t_r + t_f ≈ 30 ns**.

$$
P_{sw,VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 30\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}
$$

### 2.2 Output capacitance

$$
P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{nC}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}
$$

### 2.3 Body-diode dead-time conduction

There are two dead-time intervals per PWM period:

$$
P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I_{ph}\, (2\,t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot200\text{ns}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}
$$

### 2.4 Reverse recovery

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{nC}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}
$$

Q_rr increases with di/dt and current. Derate ×2–3 if the datasheet value is specified at low di/dt, which gives ≈ 0.1 W.

### 2.5 Gate drive

Only the PWM leg's two FETs switch at f_sw:

$$
P_{gate} = 2 \cdot Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2\cdot50\text{nC}\cdot10\text{V}\cdot48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}
$$

This power is dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FET.

### 2.6 Switching loss summary (per channel, 80 A, 25.2 V)

| Term | Loss |
|---|---|
| V·I overlap | 1.45 W |
| Dead time (body diode) | 0.61 W |
| Reverse recovery (derated) | 0.10 W |
| C_oss | 0.05 W |
| Gate | 0.05 W |
| **Total switching** | **≈ 2.26 W** |

**Design insight:** at 48 kHz, switching loss is only ~8% of conduction loss. The 80 A burst is a **conduction- and copper-limited** problem. Raising f_sw to 96 kHz costs only ~2.3 W more per channel. Lowering R_ds(on) or adding parallel FETs is worth far more than faster switching.

### 2.7 Total loss and efficiency

| | Per channel | 4 channels (all at 80 A) |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FETs) | 27.9 W | 111.6 W |
| Copper / shunt / joints | 6.1 W
