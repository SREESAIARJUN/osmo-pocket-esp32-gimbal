## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Key Finding

This document uses representative datasheet-class values, not a specific vendor part. Every parameter is defined symbolically so you can substitute your selected part's numbers.

| Symbol | Meaning | Baseline value |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | Pack voltage (6S, full charge) | 25.2 V |
| f_sw | PWM frequency | 48 kHz |
| I_burst | Burst phase current | 80 A per channel (Case A) or 80 A total, 20 A per channel (Case B) |
| R_ds(on),25 | FET on-resistance at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | 1.2 mΩ (maximum, not typical) |
| k_T | Temperature coefficient of R_ds(on) | 0.0045 /K |
| Q_g, Q_gd | Total gate charge, Miller charge | 50 nC, 9 nC |
| Q_rr | Body-diode reverse-recovery charge | 40 nC |
| V_F | Body-diode forward voltage | 0.8 V |
| t_dt | Dead time | 100 ns |
| I_g | Gate driver source/sink current (effective) | ~0.8 A |
| FET package | 40 V-class FET (3.3 × 3.3 mm) | 24 FETs (4 channels × 6) |
| Board | 4-layer, 2 oz copper | 20 × 20 mm (nano) to 30.5 × 30.5 mm |

> **Key finding:** On a nano AIO with 3.3 × 3.3 mm FETs, an **80 A per-channel burst on all four channels at once is not thermally viable** for more than about 1–2 seconds. Section 3 shows the math.
>
> An **80 A total burst (Case B: about 20 A per channel)** is comfortably viable.
>
> **Pin down which definition of "80 A burst" the spec means before layout.** It changes FET count, copper weight, and board size.
