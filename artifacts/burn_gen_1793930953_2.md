## 1. Loss Model (Per Channel)

### 1.1 Conduction Topology in 6-Step

During any 60° sector:

- **Leg A (PWM'd):** the high side is ON for duty D, and the synchronous low side is ON for 1−D.
- **Leg B (return):** the low side is ON for 100% of the sector.
- **Leg C:** floating (used for BEMF sensing).

Conduction losses are therefore:

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2\big[R_{HS}D + R_{LS}(1-D)\big] + I_{rms}^2 R_{LS} \;\approx\; 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

This result is independent of D when the high-side and low-side FETs are identical.

### 1.2 Ripple Correction

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} \;\le\; \frac{25.2\cdot 0.25}{20\,\mu H\cdot 48\,kHz} = 6.6\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 \approx 6404\ \text{A}^2$$

The ripple contribution is negligible (<0.1%).
