## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Conduction topology per commutation sector

In each 60° sector of 6-step commutation with complementary PWM, two phases carry current:

- **PWM'd phase:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T_sw. The low-side FET synchronously rectifies for (1−D)·T_sw, minus dead time.
- **Return phase:** the low-side FET conducts continuously.

Phase current flows through exactly two channels at all times, so the per-motor conduction loss is:

$$
P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \, R_{DS}(T_j)\,\big[D + (1-D)\big] + I_{rms}^2 \, R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\, I_{rms}^2 \, R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

The result is independent of D, which is a key property of synchronous rectification. Dead-time intervals move current into the body diode; §2.4 accounts for that.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence and ripple

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$
