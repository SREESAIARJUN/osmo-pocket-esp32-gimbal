## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning

A 4-in-1 ESC does not commutate from the STM32H743. The design is a two-board stack:

| Board | MCU | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743 (400 MHz, TFBGA100) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, bidirectional DShot600 master, RPM-based notch filtering |
| 4-in-1 ESC | 4 × AT32F421K8U7 (one per motor) | AM32 | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |

### 0.2 Feasibility Warning

Running 80 A per motor on a 20×20 mm "nano" footprint is not physically viable. Total loss at 80 A on all four channels is about 90 W, and a 20×20 board has roughly 0.8 cm² of copper per channel. This analysis therefore uses the following geometry:
- **ESC:** 30.5×30.5 mm mounting pattern, 8-layer, 2 oz copper
- **FC:** 20×20 mm

All burst ratings are **time-limited**, as derived in Section 3.
