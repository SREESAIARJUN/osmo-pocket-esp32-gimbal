# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot Design Package

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Two Upfront Flags

I don't have prior context for "Job #26," so this package states its assumptions explicitly. Every number below can be re-run with your actual BOM values.

**Flag 1: Architecture.** The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs directly. It runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs, which run AM32 and perform the 48 kHz complementary PWM and BEMF commutation. This is the only practical topology. The H743 has neither the timer count nor the comparator resources to drive 24 FETs while running a flight stack.

**Flag 2: "80A burst" on a nano board.** The thermal model in Section 3 shows that 80A on all four channels at once is a **~2 to 3 second** event on a 30×30 mm board. It is not a continuous or 10-second rating. That case also draws ~320A at 6S (~8 kW) through one battery lead and connector, which no nano-class lead or connector supports. Treat "80A burst" as a **per-channel, short-duration** rating, and make sure marketing claims match Section 3.4.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path in 6-Step Trapezoidal with Complementary PWM

At any commutation step, one phase is chopped (HS/LS complementary at duty D) and one phase has its LS held on.

Instantaneous conducting resistance:

$$R_{path} = D\cdot R_{HS} + (1-D)\cdot R_{LS,sync} + R_{LS,static} = 2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

This is independent of D, because synchronous rectification keeps exactly two channels conducting.

### 1.2 Temperature-Corrected R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At the design point T_j = 110 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × 1.383 = **1.66 mΩ**

### 1.3 RMS Current Including Ripple

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{pk,avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

For a typical 5 to 10 µH phase inductance at 48 kHz, ΔI is about 10 to 20 A p-p. That adds ≤0.5% at 80A, so it is neglected for burst but retained for the low-current regime.

$$P_{cond} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.66\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{21.2\ W}$$
