## 0. Assumptions and Loop-Rate Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with single-precision FPU (e.g. STM32G474) |
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC (e.g. GBM2804/GM3506 class), 12N14P → pole pairs p = 7, slots Ns = 12 |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (MT6701/AS5047P), SPI |
| Current sensing | 2- or 3-shunt low-side, sampled at PWM center |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on separate SPI buses, mounted at r₁ ≈ −r₂ about the camera CoM |

The control runs in four nested tiers:

| Tier | Rate | Ts | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | 50 µs | Clarke/Park, d/q PI, SVPWM. Runs in the ADC ISR. |
| 2. Velocity | 4 kHz | 250 µs | PI on gyro/encoder rate, plus anti-cogging feedforward |
| 3. Angle | 1 kHz | 1 ms | P with velocity feedforward on attitude error |
| 4. IMU | 8 kHz ODR | — | Notch + LPF, dual-IMU fusion, Mahony AHRS, decimated to 4 kHz/1 kHz |

**Bandwidth separation rule:** each outer loop's crossover frequency is no more than about 1/4 to 1/5 of the inner loop's crossover:

$$\omega_{c,i} \approx 2\pi\cdot 1500\ \text{rad/s},\quad \omega_{c,v}\approx 2\pi\cdot 150,\quad \omega_{c,\theta}\approx 2\pi\cdot 30$$

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Coordinate Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park transform**, using electrical angle θₑ = p·θₘ − θ_offset:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park:**

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

**Latency compensation.** The commanded voltage is applied about 1.5·Ts after sampling. Use the extrapolated angle in the inverse Park transform:

$$\theta_e' = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s$$

### 1.2 Plant Model (SPMSM, with L_d ≈ L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

Electromagnetic torque:

$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q \equiv K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.3 Current PI with Pole-Zero Cancellation

With decoupling terms added, each axis reduces to G(s) = 1/(Ls + R). Choosing

$$K_{p,i}=L\,\omega_{c,i},\qquad K_{i,i}=R\,\omega_{c,i}$$

cancels the electrical pole at R/L, and the closed loop becomes first-order: i/i* = ω_c/(s + ω_c).

The control laws, including decoupling and back-EMF feedforward, are:

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

$$x_{k+1}=x_k + K_i T_s e_k \quad \text{only if the output is unsaturated, or } e_k \text{ drives it out of saturation}$$
