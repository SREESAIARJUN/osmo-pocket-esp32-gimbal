## 1. FOC Inner Loop

### 1.1 Electrical model (rotor dq frame)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

$$
\tau_m = \tfrac{3}{2} p \lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q,\qquad \omega_e = p\,\omega_m,\qquad \theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}
$$

Gimbal motors are surface-mount PM machines, so L_d ≈ L_q = L and the reluctance torque is zero. The d-axis current reference is therefore i_d* = 0.
