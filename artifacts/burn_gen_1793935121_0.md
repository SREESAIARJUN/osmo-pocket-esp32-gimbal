# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Fusion Design

> **Note on assumptions:** I don't have the Job #17 specification sheet, so every hardware-dependent value lives in one `Config` struct. The defaults assume:
> - **Motors:** 12N14P gimbal BLDCs (GBM2804-class)
> - **Encoders:** 14-bit magnetic (AS5048A/MT6701-class)
> - **MCU:** STM32G4/H7-class
> - **Inverter:** 20 kHz PWM with low-side current shunts
> - **IMUs:** two ICM-42688-P on the camera payload
>
> Replace these with your measured values before tuning.

---

## 1. Timing Architecture

| Loop | Rate | Target bandwidth | Input signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (d/q PI) | 20 kHz (PWM-synchronous) | ~1.5–2 kHz | Phase shunts, encoder θₑ |
| IMU sampling and filtering | 8 kHz (ODR) | n/a | 2× ICM-42688-P, SPI with FIFO or DRDY |
| Velocity (PI) | 4 kHz | ~60–120 Hz | Fused gyro projected onto the joint axis, blended with encoder rate |
| Angle/attitude (P + I) | 1 kHz | ~10–25 Hz | Mahony attitude quaternion |
| Anti-cogging learning | 4 kHz (gated) | n/a | Velocity-loop effort |

**Rule:** keep a 5–10× bandwidth separation between nested loops. The current loop must be at least 10× faster than the velocity loop. Total sensor delay in the velocity loop should stay under ~0.4 ms. Most of that budget is IMU filter group delay, which is why the filters below are kept low-order.

## 2. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 2.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, where θₑ = p·θ_m − θ_offset and p = 7 pole pairs:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

**SVPWM** (min-max zero-sequence injection, equivalent to sector-based SVPWM). The phase voltages are:

$$
v_a=v_\alpha,\quad v_b=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

The zero-sequence offset and resulting duty cycles are:

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc})+\min(v_{abc})\big),\qquad D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

The linear modulation range is |v_dq| ≤ V_bus/√3.

### 2.2 Current PI Design (Pole-Zero Cancellation)

The plant per axis is:

$$
G_i(s) = \frac{1}{Ls+R}
$$

Choosing the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole gives these gains:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_{ci}
$$

The closed loop then becomes first-order:

$$
\frac{\omega_{ci}}{s+\omega_{ci}}
$$

Choose ω_ci ≤ 2π·f_pwm/10. Gimbal motors have high R (~5–15 Ω) and low L (~1–3 mH), so the corner R/L is high and the loop is naturally well damped.

**Decoupling feedforward** (with ω_e = p·ω_m):

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

**Voltage limiting:** the output is limited as a circle. Priority goes to v_d, and v_q gets the remainder:

$$
v_q^{max} = \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}
$$

> **Note:** Many gimbals run open-loop voltage mode (v_q = R·i_q*) because shunt SNR is poor at the ~100 mA level. The code supports both modes via `useCurrentSensing`.

## 3. Cascaded Angle → Velocity → Torque Control

### 3.1 Attitude Error (Angle Loop)

Define the attitude quantities:
- **q_c:** camera attitude from the fusion filter.
- **q_r:** reference attitude.

The error quaternion and the rotation-vector error (taking the shortest path) are:

$$
q_e = q_r^{*}\otimes q_c,\qquad
\mathbf e = -2\,\operatorname{sgn}(q_{e,w})\,\mathbf q_{e,xyz}
$$

The commanded camera-frame angular rate (rate feedforward plus P + I on attitude error) is:

$$
\boldsymbol\omega^{*}_{c} = \boldsymbol\omega_r + K_\theta\,\mathbf e + K_{\theta i}\!\int\!\mathbf e\,dt
$$
