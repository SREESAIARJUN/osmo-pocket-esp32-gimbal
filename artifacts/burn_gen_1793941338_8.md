# Job #44: 3-Axis Gimbal FOC Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion Design

> **Assumptions.** I don't have the Job #44 spec sheet, so this design uses the defaults below. Every one is a single constant in `config.hpp`, so you can change any of them without touching the math.
>
> | Item | Assumed value |
> |---|---|
> | MCU | STM32G474 / H7 class (FPU, ≥170 MHz), center-aligned PWM, injected ADC triggered at the PWM valley |
> | Motors | Gimbal BLDC 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5 Ω, L ≈ 2 mH, Kt ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
> | Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701 class), one per joint |
> | IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 24 MHz, ODR 8 kHz each, sampled interleaved |
> | Loop rates | Current 20 kHz, Velocity 4 kHz, Joint angle 1 kHz, Camera attitude 1 kHz |
> | Kinematics | Pan (Z) → Roll (X) → Tilt (Y), camera at the tilt link |
