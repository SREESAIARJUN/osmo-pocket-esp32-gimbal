# 3-Axis Gimbal FOC Control and Dual-IMU Fusion: Design and Implementation

I don't have the Job #31 hardware spec, so I designed to the typical hardware for this class of gimbal. Section 0 lists every assumption. Change those values and the gain formulas (§2–§4) re-derive the controller automatically.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control Core

### 1.1 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

### 1.2 Park transform ($\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$)

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Latency compensation.** The PWM update is applied one period after sampling. Use the electrical angle advanced by 1.5 sample periods:

$$
\theta_e^{*} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s
$$

### 1.3 Machine model in the dq frame (SPMSM, $L_d=L_q=L$)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q
$$

### 1.4 Current PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

The PI zero is placed on the electrical pole, $K_i/K_p = R/L$. This cancellation leaves a first-order closed loop with bandwidth $\omega_{bi}$:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{bi},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{bi},\qquad \omega_{bi}\le \frac{2\pi f_{s}}{10}
$$

The control law adds the decoupling terms and back-EMF feedforward:

$$
v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

The setpoints are $i_d^*=0$ and $i_q^* = i_{q,\text{vel}} + i_{q,\text{cog}}$.

### 1.5 Voltage limiting and anti-windup

**Circular limiter.** The linear SVPWM range is $V_{max} = V_{dc}/\sqrt3$. Give $v_d$ priority:

$$
v_d^{sat} = \mathrm{clip}(v_d^*, \pm V_{max})
$$

$$
v_q^{sat} = \mathrm{clip}\!\left(v_q^*,\ \pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - (v_d^{sat})^2}\right)
$$

**Back-calculation anti-windup** (discrete, forward Euler):

$$
x_{k+1} = x_k + T_s\left[K_i e_k + K_{aw}\,(u^{sat}_k - u_k)\right],\qquad K_{aw} = \frac{K_i}{K_p}
$$
