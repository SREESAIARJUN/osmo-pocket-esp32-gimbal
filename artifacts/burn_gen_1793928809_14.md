**Loss Model, Thermal Budget, Bi-directional DShot600 DMA Plan, and Reference Netlist**
Revision A (architecture-level, pre-layout)

## 0. Assumptions and Key Findings

Every number below depends on component and layout choices that are not yet frozen. I state the assumptions explicitly so the model can be re-run when real datasheets and the stack-up are fixed. Treat the netlist as a reference topology, not a release BOM.

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and does not commutate motors. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 talks to the ESC MCUs over bi-directional DShot600. This is the only practical partitioning, because ArduPilot does not run sensorless BLDC commutation.

**Key findings:**

1. **The full 80 A rating is a burst rating only on a nano footprint.**
   - All four motors at 80 A: about 1.5 s before the junction limit is reached.
   - One motor at 80 A, the others at hover: about 4–5 s.
   - Realistic continuous rating: about 22–25 A per channel with prop-wash cooling.
2. **Conduction loss dominates.** At full throttle (D → 1), switching loss falls to nearly zero, so the worst case for switching loss is the region of about 85–95 % duty.
3. **Total burst draw is 320 A** (4 × 80 A). The battery, XT60/XT90 connector, pads, and bulk capacitors must be specified for this, not only the FETs.
