## 1. Electrical Operating Point

- 120° trapezoidal (6-step) commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM.
- At any instant, exactly two FETs carry the phase current: one high-side (HS) and one low-side (LS).
- Only one half-bridge is switching.

**Mode definitions:**

- **ON interval (duty D):** HS_A + LS_B conduct the motor current I.
- **OFF interval (1−D):** LS_A (synchronous rectifier) + LS_B conduct I.

**Phase current ripple** (L_ph-ph ≈ 30 µH, typical for a large 6S motor):

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}}$$

At D = 0.9:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{25.2 \cdot 0.09}{30\mu \cdot 48k} = 1.58\ \text{A}$$

**RMS current:**

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 0.21$$

The ripple contribution is negligible at 48 kHz.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0055\text{–}0.007\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

### 2.2 Instantaneous bridge conduction loss

Two FETs are always in the current path, regardless of D, because the bridge is synchronous:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

**Per-FET average.** Each FET is in the active pair for 1/3 of the electrical cycle. Within that window, its share depends on D:

- HS conducts for D.
- LS (same leg) conducts for 1−D.
- LS (return leg) conducts for 100%.

Summed over the cycle, every FET averages:

$$P_{FET,avg} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2 R_{DS}$$

The peak instantaneous loss (≈ I²R) still applies for junction transients.

### 2.3 Self-consistent solution

Tj is solved iteratively against the thermal model in §4.

| Tj (°C) | R_DS typ (mΩ) | R_DS max (mΩ) | P_cond typ (W) | P_cond max (W) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | 1.00 | 1.30 | 12.8 | 16.6 |
| 100 | 1.45 | 1.89 | 18.6 | 24.2 |
| 125 | 1.60 | 2.08 | 20.5 | 26.6 |

### 2.4 Parasitic conduction (frequently ignored, must not be)

| Element | R (mΩ) | Current | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase + bus copper, 2 oz × 4 layers, ~10 mm path | 0.30 | I | 1.9 W |
| Shunt (if per-channel low-side) | 0.25 | I (shared) | 1.6 W |
| Battery pad / XT60 wiring (board share) | 0.20 | 4I = 320 A | 20 W total (system) |

**Design rule:** at 320 A total, the battery pads and main bus need about ≥ 4 oz equivalent copper. Each 0.1 mΩ of bus resistance costs 10 W.
