## 0. System Architecture and Assumptions

```
 6S LiPo (25.2V max) ──► XT60 ──► 1000µF low-ESR + 4×(ceramic bank) ──► VBAT plane
                                                       │
   ┌──────────── FC (STM32H743VIT6 @ 400 MHz) ─────────┼───────────────┐
   │  TIM3_CH3/CH4, TIM5_CH1/CH2 ── bidir DShot600 ────┼──► ESC MCUs   │
   │  SPI1 IMU, SPI2 OSD/baro, UARTs, ADC (VBAT/CURR)  │              │
   └───────────────────────────────────────────────────┘              │
   ESC ×4: STM32G071 (AM32) ──► FD6288Q 3-ph driver ──► 6× N-MOSFET ──► motor
```

The H743 does **not** commutate the motors. Each ESC channel runs its own MCU (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class). The H743 only generates and receives DShot.
