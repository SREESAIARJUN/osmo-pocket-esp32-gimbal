## 0. Assumptions and Architecture Notes

I don't have your MOSFET part number, PCB stackup, or airflow data. Every number below comes from a declared parameter that you can replace. The equations are general, and the worked numbers are examples.

**Architecture correction.** ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The working architecture is:

- **STM32H743** runs ArduPilot and sends **bidirectional DShot600** to four ESC MCUs.
- **4× ESC MCUs** (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BlueJay) perform 6-step commutation with **48 kHz** complementary PWM.
- **Power stage:** 3 half-bridges per motor, 12 half-bridges total.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step, Synchronous Rectification)

In 120° trapezoidal commutation, each phase leg spends one third of the electrical period in each state:

- **HS-active (⅓ period):** the high-side switch conducts for duty D, and the low-side switch of the same leg conducts for (1−D) during synchronous freewheel.
- **LS-sink (⅓ period):** the low-side switch conducts 100%.
- **Floating (⅓ period):** no conduction.

Losses per leg and per ESC:

$$P_{leg} = I^2 R\left[\tfrac{1}{3}(D + 1 - D) + \tfrac{1}{3}\right] = \tfrac{2}{3}I^2R$$

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ESC} = 3\cdot\tfrac{2}{3}I^2R = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)}$$

The result is independent of duty because two switches are always in the current path.

R_DS(on) rises with junction temperature:

$$R(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)\right]$$

**Worked numbers at 80 A:**

- P_c0 = 2·80²·0.9 mΩ = **11.52 W** at 25 °C.
- At T_j = 110 °C: R = 1.28 mΩ, so P_cond = **16.4 W per ESC**.
- All four motors bursting together: about **66 W** of conduction loss alone.

**Ripple correction.** Phase current ripple ΔI_pp = V_bemf-limited / (L·f_sw). Conduction loss scales by (1 + ΔI_pp²/(12·I²)). This is negligible (<1%) at 80 A for typical 5" to 7" motors, where L is about 10–30 µH line-to-line.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the active PWM leg switches. That gives one hard-switched transition pair per PWM period per ESC.

| Term | Equation | Value at 80 A, 25.2 V |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw | ½·25.2·80·30 ns·48 k = **1.45 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V_SD·I·2·t_dt·f_sw | 0.8·80·400 ns·48 k = **1.23 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 50 nC·25.2·48 k = **0.06 W** |
| C_oss | Q_oss·V·f_sw | 30 nC·25.2·48 k = **0.04 W** |
| Gate drive | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw | 2·50 nC·10·48 k = **0.05 W** (mostly in driver) |
| **Total P_sw** | | **≈ 2.8 W per ESC** |

**Design levers:**

- **Dead time** is the second-largest term. Dropping from 200 ns to 100 ns saves about 0.6 W per ESC. Verify shoot-through margin with the actual driver propagation-delay mismatch before doing this.
- **Doubling f_sw to 96 kHz** doubles every row in the table, adding about 2.8 W per ESC.
- **Losses split between devices.** Overlap loss lands mostly in the high side. Dead-time and Q_rr loss land in the low side
