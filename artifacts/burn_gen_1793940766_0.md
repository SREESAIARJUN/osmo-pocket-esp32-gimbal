## 0. Architecture and Baseline Assumptions

**Architecture.** The H743 does not drive the gates directly. A production 4-in-1 design has:

- the H743 as flight controller, running ArduPilot and sending DShot600 at 3.3 V,
- one ESC MCU per motor (AT32F421 or EFM8BB21, running AM32 or BLHeli_S/32),
- a 3-phase gate driver per motor,
- 6 MOSFETs per motor, for 24 FETs in total.

The 48 kHz switching frequency is the ESC MCU's PWM.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths

In six-step synchronous PWM, during any 60° sector:

- one phase's high-side FET (HS) switches at duty D,
- its low-side FET (LS) conducts for 1−D (synchronous rectification),
- a second phase's LS FET is held on for 100% of the sector.

The motor current therefore always flows through exactly two FET channels in series:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2 I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

Conduction loss per channel is independent of duty cycle. Duty only changes how the loss is split between the HS and LS devices.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha = \frac{k_T - 1}{100} = 0.006\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}
$$

Because R_DS rises with temperature, loss and temperature are coupled. Solve the two equations together:

$$
T_j = T_{ref} + P(T_j)\,R_{\theta,eff}
\;\Rightarrow\;
T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + 2I^2R_{25}R_{\theta}(1-25\alpha)}{1 - 2I^2R_{25}R_{\theta}\alpha}
$$

**Thermal runaway** occurs when the denominator reaches zero, i.e. when 2I²R₂₅R_θα ≥ 1.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, using a conservative T_j = 125 °C

- R_DS = 1.0 mΩ × 1.6 = 1.6 mΩ
- Per FET in the conducting pair: P = I² × R_DS = 6400 × 0.0016 = **10.24 W** (time-averaged over commutation)
- Per channel: P_cond,ch = 2 × 6400 × 0.0016 = **20.48 W**

### 1.4 Copper and parasitic conduction

Assume 0.3 mΩ for the phase pad, trace, and solder joints, plus 0.15 mΩ for shared bus copper:

$$
P_{Cu,ch} = I^2 (R_{phase,path}) = 6400 \times 0.45\,\mathrm{m\Omega} \approx \mathbf{2.9\ W}
$$

This loss spreads into the copper rather than the junction. It still counts toward the board's heat budget.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the HS FET of the PWM'd phase hard-switches. The LS FET soft-switches, because its body diode conducts during dead time.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}
= 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}
$$

### 2.2 Body-diode reverse recovery

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.060\ \mathrm{W}
$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance (C_oss) loss

$$
P_{oss} \approx \tfrac{1}{2} Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} \times 2 = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \mathrm{W}
$$

### 2.4 Dead-time diode conduction

There are two dead-time intervals per PWM period:

$$
P_{dt} = V_F I\,(2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = 0.307\ \mathrm{W}
$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

This loss lands in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FET junction:

$$
P_g = 2\, Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 60\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.058\ \mathrm{W}
$$

### 2.6 Switching summary per channel at 80 A

| Term | Power (W) |
|---|---|
| V·I overlap | 1.94 |
| Q_rr | 0.06 |
| C_oss | 0.05 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.31 |
| Gate drive (driver side) | 0.06 |
| **Switching total** | **≈ 2.42 W** |

### 2.7 Total board loss at 80 A burst

| Location | Per channel (W) | ×4 channels (W) |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 20.48 | 81.9 |
| FET switching | 2.36 | 9.4 |
| Copper | 2.9 | 11.6 |
| Bulk cap ESR ripple (est.) | 0.2 | 0.8 |
| **Total** | **≈ 25.9** | **≈ 103.7 W** |

**Design insight.** Conduction loss is about 89% of FET loss. Lowering R_DS(on), for example by paralleling FETs, pays off far more than faster switching. Going to 24 kHz would save only about 1.1 W per channel and would increase current ripple.

Ripple check: with motor phase inductance L ≈ 15 µH, the worst case at D = 0.5 is

$$
\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}}{4 L f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2}{4 \times 15\mu \times 48\text{k}} \approx 8.75\ \mathrm{A_{pp}}
$$

This is acceptable and justifies 48 kHz
