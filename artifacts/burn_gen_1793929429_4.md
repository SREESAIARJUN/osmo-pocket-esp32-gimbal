## 1. Field-Oriented Control (current loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms
Use the amplitude-invariant Clarke transform with balanced currents (i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)
$$

The Park transform uses the electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_off, where p is the pole-pair count:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of that matrix.

### 1.2 Plant and PI design
The d/q voltage equations of the PMSM are:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

After decoupling, each axis reduces to the first-order plant G(s) = 1/(Ls + R). Pole-zero cancellation with a target closed-loop bandwidth ω_c gives:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \;\;(\text{so }K_i/K_p = R/L)
$$

The closed loop is then i/i* = ω_c/(s + ω_c). Choose ω_c ≤ 2π·f_s/20; at f_s = 20 kHz, ω_c ≈ 2π·1 kHz.

Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward terms are added to the PI outputs:

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\hat\omega_e \hat L\, i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \hat\omega_e \hat L\, i_d + \hat\omega_e \hat\lambda_m,\quad \hat\lambda_m = \tfrac{2}{3}\tfrac{K_t}{p}
$$

### 1.3 Voltage limiting and SVPWM
Limit the voltage vector circularly to |v| ≤ V_bus/√3, giving i_d priority. Then compute duty cycles with min-max (third-harmonic-equivalent) injection:

$$
v_{a,b,c} = \text{invClarke}(v_\alpha,v_\beta),\quad
v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max v + \min v),\quad
d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

### 1.4 Encoder angle and velocity observer (PLL)
The raw encoder angle is too noisy to differentiate directly, so a type-2 tracking loop estimates position and speed. The innovation e is the wrapped difference between measured and estimated angle:
