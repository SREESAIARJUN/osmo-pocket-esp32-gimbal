### Architecture correction

The STM32H743 should not drive the power stage directly.

- **Correct partition:**
  - The H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller and emits bidirectional DShot600.
  - Each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32 (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071), plus a 3‑phase gate driver and 6 FETs.
- **Board format:** whether this is an "AIO" board or an FC + 4‑in‑1 stack, the partition is the same.
- **"Nano" form factor:** 80 A per channel is only achievable as a short burst on a 30×30 board (see §3). On a 20×20 board it is not credible.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Bridge topology (6‑step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

In each 60° electrical sector, one phase is high, one is low, and one floats. With complementary PWM at duty D:

- During the on‑time, current flows through HS(a) and LS(b).
- During the off‑time, current flows through LS(a) (synchronous, carrying freewheel current) and LS(b).

So **two R_DS(on) are always in the current path, independent of D**:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature‑dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_0\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}\ \Rightarrow\ R_{DS}(125^\circ C)=1.92\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 RMS current including ripple

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-E)\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}, \qquad I_{rms}=\sqrt{I_{avg}^2+\frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}}$$

For a typical 5″–7″ motor (L_ph ≈ 15 µH line‑to‑line) at high load, ΔI_pp ≈ 10 A. The ripple term then adds less than 0.2 % to I_rms, so I_rms ≈ 80 A.

### 1.4 Conduction loss at 80 A

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2(80)^2(1.92\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{24.6\ W}$$

**Per‑FET average loss:** each FET conducts on average one‑third of the electrical cycle.

$$\bar P_{FET} = 24.6/6 \approx \mathbf{4.1\ W}$$

The instantaneous loss while conducting is 12.3 W. The commutation period is far shorter than Z_th(j‑c) at operating eRPM, so the average value governs.

**Copper loss:**

- Trace and plane resistance: R_cu ≈ ρL/(wt). Budget 0.3 mΩ per phase path, giving 80²·0.3 m = 1.9 W per channel.
- Shunt (0.2 mΩ, see §4): 1.3 W per channel.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the high‑side FET of the PWM'd phase is hard‑switched. The low side commutates through its body diode during dead time.

| Mechanism | Equation | At 25.2 V, 80 A, 48 kHz |
|---|---|---|
| V‑I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | ½·25.2·80·30n·48k = **1.45 W** |
| Body‑diode dead time | V_F·I·2t_dead·f_sw | 0.8·80·200n·48k = **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 40n·25.2·48k = **0.05 W** |
| Output capacitance | Q_oss·V·f_sw | 30n·25.2·48k = **0.04 W** |
| Gate drive (lost in the driver) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw | 2·50n·10·48k = **0.05 W** |
| **Total switching, per channel** | | **≈ 2.2 W** |
