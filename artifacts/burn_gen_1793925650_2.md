# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Electrothermal Model, DShot DMA Plan, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Architecture and Key Findings

**Architecture.** In a commercial "H743 4-in-1" stack, the STM32H743 is the flight controller. It runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to ESC MCUs, typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The ESC MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM that drives the power stage. ArduPilot does not commutate motors directly from the H7. The models below follow that partition.

**Assumptions.** Values marked **[A]** are assumptions. Replace them with datasheet values for your actual MOSFET and gate driver before committing.

**Key finding.** At 80A per channel on a nano footprint (20×20 or 30×30 mm), the stage dissipates about **24 W per channel and about 95 W total**. That is a **burst rating of roughly 1–3 seconds**, not a continuous rating. The math below shows why.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.2 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.74 mΩ**.

### 2.2 Current path

In 6-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM, two legs are always active:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T. The synchronous low-side FET conducts for (1−D)·T. Current flows continuously through one FET of this leg.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET is on for the full 60° sector.

So the instantaneous conduction path always contains **two R_ds(on)** in series.

### 2.3 RMS current including ripple

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{2 L_{ph} f_{sw}}$$

Example: L_ph = 15 µH [A] and D = 0.5 give ΔI ≈ 4.4 A. The ripple term adds only about 1.6 A², which is negligible at 80 A. Ignore it for low-inductance motors only after you check it.

### 2.4 Conduction loss per channel

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W | 61.4 W |
| 100 °C | 1.61 mΩ | 20.6 W | 82.5 W |
| 125 °C | 1.74 mΩ | **22.3 W** | **89.1 W** |

**Per FET:**
- Return-leg low-side FET: 11.1 W during its sector.
- PWM-leg pair: shares 11.1 W in the ratio D : (1−D).
- Over a full electrical cycle, each of the 6 FETs averages about P_cond,ch / 3 ≈ **7.4 W**.
- Peak instantaneous FET dissipation is about 11 W. This is the figure that matters for Z_th at commutation-period timescales above roughly 1 ms.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the hard-switched PWM FET (the high side, if PWM is on the high side) sees V·I overlap loss. The synchronous FET soft-switches after body-diode conduction during dead time.

### 3.1 Transition times

$$t_{on} \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{(V_{drv}-V_{pl})/R_{g,src}} = \frac{12\,\text{nC}}{1.17\,\text{A}} = 10.3\,\text{ns}$$

$$t_{off} \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{V_{pl}/R_{g,sink}} = \frac{12\,\text{nC}}{0.75\,\text{A}} = 16.0\,\text{ns}$$

### 3.2 Loss components (per channel at 80 A)

| Term | Expression | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_on + t_off)·f | ½·25.2·80·26.3 ns·48k = **1.27 W** |
| Output capacitance | ≈ ½·Q_oss·V·f | **0.027 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f | **0.036 W** (plus extra turn-on overlap; add about 10–20% to the overlap term) |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_SD·I·2t_dt·f | 0.8·80·100 ns·48k = **0.31 W** |
| Gate charge | Q_g·V_drv·f per FET | 0.019 W per FET, dissipated in the driver and R_g, not in the FET |
| **Total, P_sw,ch** | | **≈ 1.65–1.9 W** |

**Dependence on f_sw.** Switching loss is about 7% of conduction loss at 80 A. Going from 48 kHz to 24 kHz saves only about 0.8 W per channel. At burst current, **conduction loss dominates**. At hover current (about 10–15 A), switching loss becomes the larger share.

## 4. Thermal Model

### 4.1 Total loss with electrothermal feedback

$$P_{tot}(T_j) = 4\left[2 I^2 R_{25}(1+\alpha(T_j-25)) + P_{sw}\right] + P_{FC} + P_{BEC}$$

- P_FC ≈ 1.5 W (H743 at 480 MHz plus peripherals).
- P_BEC ≈ 1–2 W.
