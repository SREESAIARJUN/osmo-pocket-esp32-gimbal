## 0. Architectural Clarification

ArduPilot does not commutate BLDC motors. The H743 runs ArduPilot and *commands* the motors. Each of the four ESC channels runs its own firmware (AM32 or BLHeli_32) on its own small MCU, and that MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM.

```
 6S LiPo ──► Bus caps ──► 4× [ESC MCU ─► 3-ph gate driver ─► 6× N-MOSFET] ──► M1..M4
                              ▲ bidir DShot600 (1 wire/motor, half-duplex)
 STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──────┘  TIM3 CH1–CH4 + DMA (via DMAMUX)
```
