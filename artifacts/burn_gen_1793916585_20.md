## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Corrections

No part numbers or board constraints for Job #47 were provided, so every value below is a **stated assumption**. Replace these values with datasheet figures before sign-off. The equations remain valid when you do.

### 0.1 Architectural correction

ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The H743 is the flight controller and sends DShot600 commands. Each 4-in-1 channel needs its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 with Extended DShot Telemetry. A typical choice is an STM32G071 or AT32F421. This document models that two-tier architecture.
