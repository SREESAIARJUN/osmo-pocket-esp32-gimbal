## 0. Architecture Clarification and Design Basis

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. In every production 4-in-1 design:

| Function | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight control, DShot600 master, bidir telemetry decode | STM32H743VIH6 (400 MHz) | ArduPilot |
| Per-motor commutation, 48 kHz PWM, BEMF sensing | 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071) | AM32 / BLHeli_32 |
| Gate drive | 4× FD6288Q (or 12× half-bridge drivers) | — |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET (6 per motor) | — |

So the 48 kHz switching analysis applies to the ESC MCUs' bridges. The DShot timer analysis applies to the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-Dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × 1.51 = **1.81 mΩ**.
