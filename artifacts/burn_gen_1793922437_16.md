# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #41): System Architecture, Loss Model, DMA Plan, and Netlists

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Architectural Corrections

These points shape every number below, so they come first.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In any DShot 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel (or one shared ESC MCU per 4-in-1) runs its own firmware (AM32 or BLHeli_32) on a small MCU such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 frames. The ESC MCU sets the 48 kHz PWM. The two timing domains are independent.
2. **"80A burst" on a nano footprint is thermally transient-limited, not steady-state-limited.** The model below shows that all four channels at 80A cannot be sustained. The correct rating is a **time-bounded burst of about 3.5–4 s**.
3. **The MOSFET parameters are representative, not from a chosen part.** Replace them with the datasheet values for your selected part (for example, a 40V, ≤1 mΩ, 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 mm class device) before signing off.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path in 6-Step Commutation

At any instant, two phases conduct. One leg is PWM-switched, alternating between its HS and LS FET through synchronous rectification. The other leg's LS FET is held on. The current therefore always flows through **two FETs in series**:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \left( 2R_{DS,hot} + R_{pcb} \right)
$$

With temperature dependence, use a self-consistent junction temperature:

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0058\ /\mathrm{K}
$$

### 1.2 Numerical Result at 80 A

$$
P_{FET,cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.20\,\mathrm{m\Omega} = 6400 \times 2.4\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{15.36\ W}
$$
$$
P_{pcb} = 6400 \times 0.5\,\mathrm{m\Omega} = \mathbf{3.20\ W}
$$
$$
P_{cond,ch} = \mathbf{18.56\ W\ per\ channel}
$$

**Per-FET distribution.** Each FET conducts for about 1/3 of the electrical period. The peak instantaneous dissipation in the held-on LS FET is I²R_hot = **7.68 W**. The averaged figure per FET is P_cond/6 ≈ **2.6 W**.

The PCB copper term is 17% of conduction loss. At 80 A, copper is a design-critical component. Each phase and battery path needs multi-layer stitched pours with via arrays of at least 20 × 0.3 mm vias per transition.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches, so one hard-switched HS turn-on/turn-off pair occurs per period.

### 2.1 Gate Currents and Transition Times

$$
I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_{g,on}} = \frac{7}{6} = 1.17\,\mathrm{A}, \qquad
I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{g,off}} = \frac{3}{4.5} = 0.67\,\mathrm{A}
$$
$$
t_{on} = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,on}} = 12.9\,\mathrm{ns}, \qquad t_{off} = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,off}} = 22.5\,\mathrm{ns}
$$

### 2.2 Loss Components

| Mechanism | Formula | Result |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_on+t_off)·f_sw = ½·25.2·80·35.4 ns·48 k | **1.71 W** |
| Coss discharge (HS turn-on) | Q_oss·V_in·f_sw = 50 nC·25.2·48 k | **0.06 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V_in·f_sw = 40 nC·25.2·48 k | **0.05 W** |
| Dead-time body diode | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·200 ns·48 k | **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs, mostly lost in driver/R_g) | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f_sw | **0.06 W** |
| **Total switching, per channel** | | **≈ 2.49 W** |
