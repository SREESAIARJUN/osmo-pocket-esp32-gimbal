## 0. Architecture Clarification and Design Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. In a production 4-in-1 design:

- The **H743 (ArduPilot)** sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs and receives eRPM telemetry back.
- **Four ESC MCUs** (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32-class firmware) generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM and perform commutation.
- **Four 3-phase gate drivers** drive **24 N-channel MOSFETs** (6 per motor).

The 48 kHz switching analysis applies to the ESC MCUs and power stage. The DMA analysis applies to the H743.

### 0.2 Feasibility Flag

Four channels at 80 A on a nano (20×20 mm or 25.5×25.5 mm) board means up to about 300 A of battery current. Neither the copper, the connector, nor the board's thermal mass can sustain that. I model:

- **Case A:** one channel at 80 A burst (the realistic "punch-out" case for one motor).
- **Case B:** all four channels at 80 A (to show how long the board survives).
