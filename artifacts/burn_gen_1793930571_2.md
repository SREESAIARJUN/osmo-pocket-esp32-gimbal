## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke transform (with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform (θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset):

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

Inverse Park transform, using a delay-compensated angle:

$$\begin{bmatrix} v_\alpha\\ v_\beta\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\hat\theta_e & -\sin\hat\theta_e\\ \sin\hat\theta_e & \cos\hat\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} v_d\\ v_q\end{bmatrix},\qquad \hat\theta_e=\theta_e+1.5\,T_s\,\omega_e$$

The 1.5·T_s term covers one sample of computation delay plus half a sample of PWM zero-order hold.

### 1.2 Plant and PI design (pole-zero cancellation)

The dq-axis voltage equations for an SPM motor (L_d = L_q = L) are:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\psi_m$$

After adding decoupling and back-EMF feedforward, each axis reduces to 1/(Ls + R). A PI controller C(s) = K_p + K_i/s with zero placed on the electrical pole gives a closed loop of ω_c/(s + ω_c):

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c = 2\pi\cdot 1500\ \text{rad/s}$$

The rule of thumb ω_c ≤ ω_s/10 holds at 20 kHz.
