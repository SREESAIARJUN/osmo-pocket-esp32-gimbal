## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Risks

The STM32H743 cannot commutate the motors itself in this design. The board has two compute domains:

| Domain | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot, sends bidirectional DShot600 |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6 | AM32 firmware, 48 kHz PWM, six-step commutation |

**Feasibility warning:** 80 A per channel on a nano-class board is thermally burst-only. The model in §3 shows about 10 s to thermal limit with all four motors at 80 A. The derived limits are:

- **Battery current.** 4 × 80 A = 320 A, which needs an XT90 connector or soldered 10 AWG leads. An XT60 is not viable.
- **Continuous rating.** The defensible continuous rating is ≈ 35–40 A per channel.
- **Assumed component parameters.** Every component parameter below is a stated assumption. Replace each one with datasheet values for your selected parts before sign-off.

## 2. Loss Model (Per ESC Channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Topology

The ESC uses six-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In every sector, current flows through exactly two FETs in series:

- **PWM phase:** the high-side FET conducts during D and the low-side FET during (1−D). The current path is continuous.
- **Return phase:** the low-side FET is held fully on.

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)\qquad\text{(independent of duty D)}$$

### 2.2 Temperature-Corrected R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha_R(T_j-25)] = 0.70\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.45 = 1.015\,\text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.015\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{13.0\ W}$$

### 2.3 Copper Conduction

$$P_{Cu} = I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.30\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{1.92\ W}$$

### 2.4 Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only one half-bridge is switching per sector.

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw = ½ · 25.2 · 80 · 30 ns · 48 k | **1.452 W** |
| Output capacitance | Q_oss · V · f_sw = 60 nC · 25.2 · 48 k | 0.073 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw = 50 nC · 25.2 · 48 k | 0.060 W |
| Dead-time diode | V_F · I · 2t_dead · f_sw = 0.8 · 80 · 200 ns · 48 k | **0.614 W** |
| Gate drive* | 2 · Q_g · V_GS · f_sw = 2 · 80 nC · 10 · 48 k | 0.077 W |
| **P_sw total** | | **≈ 2.28 W** |

*Gate-drive power is dissipated in the gate resistors and the driver, not in the FET die.

### 2.5 Channel and Board Totals

$$P_{ch} = 13.0 + 1.92 + 2.28 \approx \mathbf{17.2\ W}$$

$$P_{board} = 4 \times 17.2 \approx \mathbf{69\ W}\ \text{(all motors at 80 A)}$$

Conduction loss dominates at 76 %. A lower-R_DS FET therefore gives a larger gain than any change to f_sw.
