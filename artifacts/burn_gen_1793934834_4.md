## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Engineering Caveats

Every number below comes from the stated assumptions. Swap in your actual MOSFET datasheet values and re-run the equations. The model is built so you can do that directly.

**Architecture clarification.** In a 4-in-1 ESC, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates DShot. Commutation is done by **four separate ESC MCUs** (e.g., AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). The H743 never drives gates directly. The netlist reflects this.

**"80A burst" is defined as 80 A RMS phase current per motor channel for ≤10 s.** This is a deliberately conservative reading. If 80 A means battery current, phase current is higher at partial throttle, because I_bus ≈ D·I_phase.

**System-level red flag.** Four channels at 80 A simultaneously means roughly 290 A from the battery. That exceeds XT60/XT90 ratings, typical 6S pack C-ratings, and anything a nano (20×20 / 30×30) board can dissipate for more than a few seconds. Section 3 quantifies this. Treat 80 A as a single-motor transient rating, not an all-motor rating.
