**Two corrections to the brief:**

1. **The H743 does not drive the FETs.** ArduPilot has no BLDC commutation firmware for the H743. The standard and supported architecture has two parts:
   - The H743 FC sends bidirectional DShot600 to four per-motor ESC MCUs.
   - The ESC MCUs (e.g. AT32F421 running AM32, or EFM8 running BLHeli_S/Bluejay) handle commutation at 48 kHz.

   The netlist below follows this architecture.
2. **"80 A" on a nano board is a burst rating, not a continuous one.** Section 3 shows that the board is thermally capacitance-limited to a burst of a few seconds. This is the most important design finding, and it should drive the product's spec sheet.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path (six-step, complementary/synchronous PWM)

At any instant two phase legs carry I_ph. In the PWM'd leg, current alternates between HS (duty D) and LS (1 − D). Because of synchronous rectification, exactly **two channels are conducting at all times**, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

### 1.3 Self-consistent junction temperature (electro-thermal feedback)

$$T_j = T_{ref} + R_{\theta}\cdot P(T_j)$$

Solving gives:

$$T_j - 25 = \frac{(T_{ref}-25) + R_\theta P_{25}}{1 - \alpha R_\theta P_{25}}, \qquad P_{25} = I^2 R_{ds,25}\cdot k$$

**Thermal runaway criterion:** $\alpha R_\theta P_{25} \ge 1$. Check this for every FET.

### 1.4 Per-FET worst case

Each FET is "assigned" for 120° of every 360° electrical cycle. Two cases matter:

- **HS at full throttle (D → 1)** carries the full current for 1/3 of the cycle:
  $$P_{FET,avg} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$
- **Instantaneous loss** while conducting: $I^2 R_{ds}$. This sets the junction ripple through Z_th(t_conduction).
