## 0. Scope and Assumptions

An STM32H743 cannot directly commutate four BLDC motors at 48 kHz while running ArduPilot. The architecture is therefore:

- **H743 flight controller** sends bidirectional DShot600 to **4× ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, configured for 48 kHz PWM).
- Each ESC MCU drives an **FD6288Q 3-phase gate driver** feeding a **6-MOSFET bridge**.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At $T_j = 125\,°C$:

$$R_{DS,hot} = 1.2\ \text{m}\Omega \times 1.45 = 1.74\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Conduction topology (6-step trapezoidal, complementary PWM)

During any commutation sector, two legs carry current $I$:

- **PWM ON:** the high-side (HS) of phase A and the low-side (LS) of phase B conduct, so the path resistance is $2R_{DS}$.
- **PWM OFF (synchronous freewheel):** LS of A and LS of B conduct, so the path resistance is again $2R_{DS}$.

The conducting resistance is therefore **duty-independent**, excluding dead time:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,motor} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j)}$$
