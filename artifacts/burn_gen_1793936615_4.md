# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

I don't have the original Job #45 specification, so this design is built on the baseline assumptions below. Every parameter is a named constant, so the design adapts directly if your hardware differs. **Verify the assumptions before use.**

## 0. Design Basis and Assumptions

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 10 Ω, L ≈ 2.5 mH (phase), Kt ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A/MT6701 class), SPI, read every current-loop tick |
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class, FPU, center-aligned PWM 20 kHz, ADC triggered at PWM center |
| Bus | 12–16 V. Current sensing on 2 or 3 low-side shunts |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on separate SPI buses, gyro/accel ODR = 8 kHz, FIFO or DRDY |
| Platform | Drone-mounted (propeller vibration 80–400 Hz plus harmonics), ESC RPM telemetry optional |

**Loop rate hierarchy** (each loop has roughly 5–10× bandwidth separation):

| Loop | Rate | Closed-loop bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | ~1.5 kHz |
| Velocity | 5 kHz | ~150 Hz |
| Angle (per motor) | 1 kHz | ~30 Hz |
| IMU filtering | 8 kHz | n/a |
| Attitude fusion and stabilization | 1 kHz | n/a |

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, where the electrical angle is

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}})
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$
