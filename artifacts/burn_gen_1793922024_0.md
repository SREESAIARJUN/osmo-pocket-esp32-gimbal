# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #9): Power, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. In every production 4-in-1 design, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, and that MCU drives the gate driver and MOSFETs. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to the four ESC MCUs. The 48 kHz figure is the ESC's PWM frequency (an AM32 setting). It is unrelated to DShot.

| Parameter | Value used | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | 6S, 25.2 V max | Worst-case switching |
| Burst current | 80 A phase current per channel, D ≈ 0.95 | "80A burst" rating |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology
With 6-step trapezoidal drive and synchronous rectification (complementary PWM), two phases conduct at any instant:
- **PWM phase:** the high-side FET conducts for D. The low-side sync FET conducts for (1−D).
- **Return phase:** the low-side FET conducts for 100% of the time.

The current therefore always flows through exactly two FET channels:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

### 1.2 At 80 A, T_j = 125 °C
$$R_{DS}(125) = 1.3\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.005 \times 100) = 1.95\,\text{m}\Omega$$
$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.95\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{24.96\ W}$$

### 1.3 Copper and interconnect
Assume ≈0.3 mΩ for the phase path (2 oz inner layers, pads, and the solder joints to the motor wire).
$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.3\times10^{-3} = 1.92\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 Hottest single device
The return-phase low-side FET conducts continuously during its 60° electrical sector. Short bursts can approach the sector-instantaneous value, so it is the worst case:
$$P_{FET,max} \approx 80^2 \times 1.95\ \text{m}\Omega = 12.5\ \text{W}$$
$$\Delta T_{JC} = 12.5 \times 1.5 = 18.7\ \text{K}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

### 2.1 Gate current and transition time
$$I_g = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{plateau}}{R_{drv} + R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int}} = \frac{10 - 3.5}{5 + 10 + 1} \approx 0.41\ \text{A}$$
$$t_{r} \approx t_{f} \approx \frac{Q_{gd} + Q_{gs2}}{I_g} = \frac{10\ \text{nC}}{0.41\ \text{A}} \approx 25\ \text{ns}$$

### 2.2 Loss components per channel
Only the PWM phase's half-bridge hard-switches.

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw = ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 50 ns × 48 k | **2.42 W** |
| Dead-time body diode | V_SD · I · 2 t_dt · f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k | **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw = 50 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | **0.06 W** |
| C_oss | ½ · C_oss · V² · f_sw = ½ × 1.5 nF × 635 × 48 k | **0.02 W** |
| Gate charge (2 FETs) | 2 · Q_g · V_g · f_sw | 0.03 W (mostly dissipated in driver/R_g) |
| **Total** | | **≈ 3.1 W** |

### 2.3 Sensitivity
- Switching loss scales linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz it is ≈1.55 W, a saving of ~1.5 W per channel at 80 A.
- Dropping R_g,ext from 10 Ω to 4.7 Ω roughly halves overlap loss. The cost is higher dV/dt ringing, which needs a layout or snubber review.

### 3.2 Transient (burst) model
A 20×20 mm stack is effectively a lumped thermal mass:
- Board + FETs + copper ≈ 4 g
- Effective heat capacity C_th ≈ 2.5 J/K (FR4 ≈ 0.9 J/g·K, Cu ≈ 0.385 J/g·K)

$$\frac{dT_{board}}{dt} \approx \frac{P - (T_{board} - T_a)/R_{\theta,BA}}{C_{th}} \approx \frac{126}{2.5} \approx 50\ \text{K/s}$$

Junction temperature:
$$T_j = T_{board} + P_{FET} \cdot Z_{\theta JC}(t)$$
