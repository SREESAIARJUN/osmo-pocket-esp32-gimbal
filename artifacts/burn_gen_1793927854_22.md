## 1. Electrical Operating Model (6-step, synchronous PWM)

In 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation, two phases conduct at any instant:

- One FET in the "static" leg is held fully ON for a 60° sector.
- The PWM leg alternates HS/LS complementarily at f_sw = 48 kHz, with dead time t_dt.

Because rectification is synchronous, **current always flows through exactly two channels in series** (except during dead time, when one is a body diode).

Definitions:
- I: phase current (DC-link current I_in ≈ D·I)
- D: duty cycle
- ΔI_pp: inductor ripple

The ripple is:

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{2L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}
$$

Worked example: L_ph = 15 µH (typical 2207-class line-to-line inductance of about 30 µH), D = 0.5, V = 25.2 V:

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{25.2 \cdot 0.25}{2 \cdot 15\times10^{-6} \cdot 48\,000} = 4.4\ \text{A}
$$

The RMS current through the series path is then:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 1.6 \approx 6402\ \text{A}^2
$$

Ripple is negligible at 80 A.

## 2. Thermal / Loss Model

### 2.1 Conduction Losses

Temperature dependence of on-resistance:

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

Using the max value at T_j = 100 °C:

$$
R_{DS} = 1.3 \times 1.45 = 1.89\ \text{mΩ}
$$

The typical value at 100 °C is 1.45 mΩ.

Per-channel conduction loss (two FETs always in series):

$$
P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

| Case | R_DS (100 °C) | P_cond per channel |
|---|---|---|
| Typical | 1.45 mΩ | 6402 × 2.90 mΩ = **18.6 W** |
