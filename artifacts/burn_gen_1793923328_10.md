## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, two FETs carry phase current at every instant:

- **On-phase leg:** the HS FET conducts for duty D; the complementary LS FET of the same leg conducts for (1−D).
- **Return-phase leg:** the LS FET conducts 100% of the 60° sector.

Path resistance is therefore always 2·R_DS(on), regardless of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\big[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R_DS(on) = 1.6 mΩ.

### 1.3 Ripple correction

The phase inductance of a typical 2207 motor is about 20 µH line-to-line. The worst-case peak-to-peak ripple is:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus} D(1-D)}{L f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \cdot 0.25}{20\mu \cdot 48k} \approx 6.6\ \text{A}$$

The RMS current then follows:

$$I_{rms} = \sqrt{I^2 + \Delta I_{pp}^2/12} = \sqrt{6400 + 3.6} \approx 80.02\ \text{A}$$

The ripple contribution is negligible.

### 1.4 Results per channel

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond / channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 12.8 W | 51.2 W |
| 100 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W | 74.2 W |
| 125 °C | 1.6 mΩ | **20.5 W** | **81.9 W** |

Because R_DS(on) rises with temperature, this term grows during the burst. That positive feedback is handled by the iterative solve in §3.3.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Per channel, one HS FET hard-switches per PWM period. The LS FET switches at near-ZVS because it commutates through its body diode.

### 2.1 Overlap (V·I crossover) loss

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 40\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = 1.94\ \text{W}$$

### 2.2 Body-diode reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{nC} \cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

Q_rr itself scales with di/dt and temperature. Budget about 2× at hot conditions, giving roughly 0.1 W.

### 2.3 Output capacitance loss

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss,eff} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 1.5\text{nF} \cdot 635 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.023\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_F \cdot I \cdot 2 t_{dt} f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 400\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = 1.23\ \text{W}$$

This is the second-largest switching term. Reducing dead time from 200 ns to 100 ns saves about 0.6 W per channel. Characterize shoot-through margin first.

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated in driver and R_g, not in the FET die)

Two FETs switch per channel:

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \cdot 50\text{nC} \cdot 10 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

### 2.6 Channel loss budget at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

| Term | W / channel |
|---|---|
| Conduction | 20.5 |
| V·I overlap | 1.94 |
| Dead-time diode | 1.23 |
| Q_rr (hot) | 0.10 |
| C_oss | 0.02 |
| Gate (driver side) | 0.05 |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ shared, battery current at D = 0.7) | ~0.8 W board total |
| **Total per channel** | **≈ 23.8 W** |
| **4 channels + shunt** | **≈ 96 W** |
