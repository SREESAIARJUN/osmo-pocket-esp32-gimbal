# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #42): Power Stage and DShot Design Package

**Scope:** 80 A burst conduction model, 48 kHz switching-loss model, transient thermal model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and a reference netlist.

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as an STM32G071 or AT32F421. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 to those four MCUs. The rest of this document assumes that partition.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in six-step with synchronous rectification

At any instant, the motor current flows through exactly two FETs:
- During the on-time D: high-side (PWM leg) → motor → low-side (fixed leg).
- During the off-time 1−D: low-side (PWM leg, synchronous rectification) → motor → low-side (fixed leg).

That gives the bridge conduction loss per channel:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{pcb}$$

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]$$

### 1.2 Distribution across the FETs (per electrical sixth-sector window of 1/3)

| FET role | Duty within its 1/3 window | Average loss |
|---|---|---|
| HS, PWM leg | D | I²R·D/3 |
| LS, PWM leg (synchronous rectification) | 1−D | I²R·(1−D)/3 |
| LS, fixed leg | 1 | I²R/3 |

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_DS(on) | 2·I²·R | Copper | P_cond per channel | Worst single FET |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.50 mΩ | 19.2 W | 3.2 W | 22.4 W | 3.2 W |
| 100 °C | 2.18 mΩ | 27.9 W | 3.2 W | 31.1 W | 4.6 W |
| 125 °C | 2.40 mΩ | 30.7 W | 3.2 W | **33.9 W** | **5.1 W** |

**Electrothermal feedback.** R_DS(on) rises with temperature, so the steady-state junction temperature is the fixed point of:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + R_{th}\cdot I^2 R_{25}\left[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right]$$

Solving for T_j:

$$T_j=\frac{T_{ref}+R_{th}I^2R_{25}(1-25\alpha)}{1-R_{th}I^2R_{25}\alpha}$$
