## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology

The ESC runs six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous (complementary) PWM. In each 60° sector:

- **PWM phase:** the high side (HS) conducts for fraction D. The low side (LS) conducts for (1 − D) as the synchronous rectifier.
- **Return phase:** the LS conducts for 100% of the sector.
- **Floating phase:** off, used for BEMF sensing.

### 1.2 Bridge conduction loss per channel

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{hot}\big[D + (1-D)\big] + I^2 R_{hot}\cdot 1 = 2\,I^2 R_{hot}
$$

This result is independent of D. Synchronous rectification removes the duty-cycle dependence.

$$
R_{hot} = k_T R_{25} = 1.5 \times 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega = 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega
$$

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.8\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{23.04\ W}
$$

### 1.3 Per-FET average

Each FET spends equal time in each role over one electrical revolution:

$$
P_{cond,FET} = \frac{2 I^2 R_{hot}}{6} = \frac{I^2 R_{hot}}{3} = \mathbf{7.68\ W}
$$
