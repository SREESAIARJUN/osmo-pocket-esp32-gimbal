# Job #9: 3-Axis Gimbal FOC Control Loop and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion

This covers the control and estimation architecture, the governing equations, and a complete C++17 implementation.

## 0. Assumptions

I don't have the Job #9 spec sheet, so the design is parameterized around a typical camera gimbal. Replace the values in `config::` with your measured values.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R = 5.6 Ω, L = 2.1 mH, K_t ≈ 0.09 N·m/A |
| Rotor encoder | 14-bit magnetic (MT6701/AS5048A), SPI, read every current-loop tick |
| MCU | STM32G474 @ 170 MHz, FPU. CORDIC is optional; a LUT is used here for portability |
| Current sensing | 2 or 3 low-side shunts, ADC triggered at PWM center |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, mounted at r₂ = −r₁ about the camera's center |
| Loop rates | Current (FOC): 20 kHz. Velocity: 4 kHz. Attitude/angle: 1 kHz. IMU ODR: 8 kHz |
