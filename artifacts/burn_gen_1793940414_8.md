## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. In a typical stack:

| Function | Silicon | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight control, DShot master, bdshot eRPM decode | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot |
| 4× ESC commutation at 48 kHz PWM | 4× AT32F421 / STM32G071 (one per motor) | AM32 / BLHeli_32 |
| Gate drive | 4× FD6288Q (3-phase, 250 V bootstrapped) | — |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET (6 per motor) | — |

The **loss model (§1–3)** applies to the ESC power stage. The **DMA allocation (§4)** applies to the H743.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (per motor, 6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss with Electrothermal Feedback

In 6-step commutation, two phase legs carry current at any instant. With synchronous rectification, each active leg has exactly one MOSFET conducting (high side during t_on, low side during t_off). Therefore **two channels always carry the full phase current**, regardless of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond}=2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j),\qquad R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

Self-consistent junction temperature (R_θ = junction-to-board thermal path per FET; see §3):

$$
T_j = T_{board} + R_\theta\cdot\frac{P_{cond}(T_j)}{6}\cdot k_{dist}
$$

Solving explicitly (linear in T_j):

$$
T_j=\frac{T_{board}+\frac{k R_\theta}{3} I^2 R_{25}(1-25\alpha)}{1-\frac{k R_\theta}{3}I^2R_{25}\alpha}
$$

**Thermal-runaway criterion.** A stable solution requires the denominator to stay positive:

$$
\frac{k R_\theta}{3} I^2 R_{25}\alpha < 1
$$

At 80 A with R_θ = 4.2 K/W and k = 1, this evaluates to 0.4 × 4.2 × 6400 × 1.5e-3 × 0.005 ≈ 0.08. That is stable with a large margin, so board temperature rather than local runaway is the limiter.

**Numbers at T_j = 100 °C:**
- R_DS = 1.5 × 1.375 = 2.06 mΩ
- P_cond = 2 × 6400 × 2.06e-3 = **26.4 W per motor**
