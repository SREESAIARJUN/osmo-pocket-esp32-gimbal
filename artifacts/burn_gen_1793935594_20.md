**Architecture clarification.** The H743 does not do the 48 kHz switching. Each ESC channel has its own gate-driving MCU (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32), which generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 only generates and receives bidirectional DShot600. The two halves are therefore modelled separately:

- Sections 1–3: ESC power stage.
- Section 4: H743 timer/DMA plan.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (Six-Step, Synchronous Rectification)

### 1.1 Current path

In any 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct:

- The **active leg** chops with duty D. Current flows through the high-side (HS) FET for D·T_sw and through the synchronous low-side (LS) FET for (1−D)·T_sw.
- The **return leg** LS is held on continuously for the full sector.
