# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan, and Netlists

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flags

**The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU, not the ESC controller.** A 4-in-1 ESC runs four independent motor-control MCUs, typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 talks to them over bi-directional DShot600 plus an optional serial telemetry line. The model below follows that split:

```
[STM32H743 FC] ──4× BDShot600──▶ [4× ESC MCU] ──▶ [4× 3-phase gate drv] ──▶ [24× MOSFET] ──▶ M1..M4
        ▲◀──── eRPM (GCR) ─────────────┘
        ▲◀──── CURR / VBAT analog, ESC serial telem ─┘
```

**Feasibility flags. Read these before committing to layout:**

1. **Burst duration is limited.** An "80 A burst" on all four channels at nano size (20×20 or 30×30 mm) is thermally limited to about 1–3 s, as quantified in §1.6. Continuous rating will realistically be 35–45 A/channel without forced airflow.
2. **The DC link needs a bulk capacitor.** It cannot be ceramics-only at 80 A. A ≥1000 µF low-ESR electrolytic at the pigtail is mandatory (§1.5).
3. **Every number here is an engineering estimate.** All MOSFET parameters are representative values for a 40 V, ~1 mΩ PDFN5×6 part. Substitute your selected part's datasheet values before sign-off.

## 1. Power-Stage Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Assumptions

| Symbol | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S full charge (design at worst case) |
| I_pk (burst) | 80 A | phase current, per channel |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | complementary (synchronous) PWM |
| R_DS(on),25 | 0.9 mΩ | @ V_GS = 10 V |
| α (temp. coeff.) | 1.6× @ T_j = 125 °C | → R_DS(on),hot = 1.44 mΩ |
| t_r, t_f | 20 ns each | with ~2 Ω gate resistors, FD6288-class driver |
| Q_g | 90 nC | total gate charge |
| Q_oss | 50 nC | |
| Q_rr | 60 nC | body diode |
| V_SD | 0.8 V | body-diode drop |
| t_dt | 100 ns | dead time per edge |
| R_cu | 0.5 mΩ | PCB copper path per channel (2 oz, internal + top) |

### 1.2 Conduction Loss

In 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification, current always flows through exactly **two FET positions in series**:

- The PWM leg (high-side for D, low-side synchronous rectifier for 1−D).
- The static low-side of the return phase.

Because the PWM leg conducts continuously across the switching cycle (alternating between HS and LS), the time-averaged loss is:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 \cdot R_{DS(on),hot} \cdot 2 = 80^2 \times 1.44\,\text{m}\Omega \times 2 = \mathbf{18.4\ W}
$$

Copper loss:

$$
P_{cu} = I^2 R_{cu} = 6400 \times 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{3.2\ W}
$$

Per-FET hot-spot: the static low-side FET carries the full 9.2 W during its 120° electrical interval. Averaged over rotation, each FET sees about 1/3 of the conduction loss of its phase. Size the thermal path for the 9.2 W instantaneous case, because commutation is slow relative to the die thermal time constant at low eRPM.

### 1.3 Switching Loss at 48 kHz

**Hard-switching V·I overlap**, on the PWM high-side:

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}
$$

**Output capacitance:**

$$
P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.06\ W
$$

**Reverse recovery:**

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 60\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.07\ W
$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two edges per cycle):

$$
P_{dt} = V_{SD} \cdot I \cdot 2 t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}
$$

**Gate drive.** This loss is dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET. Two switching FETs are active at any instant:

$$
P_{g} = 2 \cdot Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 90\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.086\ W
$$

### 1.4 Totals

| Loss term | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FET) | 18.4 W | 73.6 W |
| Copper | 3.2 W | 12.8 W |
| Switching (V·I) | 1.94 W | 7.8 W |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 W | 2.4 W |
| Q_oss + Q_rr | 0.13 W | 0.5 W |
| Gate drive | 0.09 W | 0.4 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 24.4 W** | **≈ 97.5 W** |

Efficiency at 80 A, 25.2 V, ~2 kW electrical per channel input: about 98.8 %. The losses are small in relative terms but enormous in absolute terms for a nano-sized board.

**Key insight:** at 48 kHz, conduction dominates by about 9:1. Dropping to 24 kHz saves only about 1.3 W/channel. Spending money on lower R_DS(on), or on paralleling FETs (2× per position halves P_cond), pays off far more than switching optimization.

**Optimal-frequency check.** Switching loss equals conduction loss only at f_sw ≈ 18.4 / (1.94 / 48k) ≈ 455 kHz. 48 kHz is deep in the conduction-dominated regime.

### 1.5 DC-Link Capacitor Sizing

**RMS ripple current** per channel (worst at D = 0.5):

$$
I_{C,rms} = I\sqrt{D(1-D)} = 80 \times 0.5 = 40\ A_{rms}
$$

For four channels with uncorrelated PWM phase, the combined ripple is ≈ 40 × √4 = **80 A_rms worst case** (if synchronized), and ≈ 40–60 A_rms typical.

**Voltage ripple:**

$$
\Delta V = \frac{I \cdot D(1-D)}{C \cdot f_{sw}}
$$

| C (effective) | ΔV |
|---|---|
| 20 µF (4× 10 µF/35 V X7R, DC-bias derated) | **20.8 V**. Unacceptable. |
| 1000 µF low-ESR electrolytic | 0.42 V (+ ESR × I: 15 mΩ × 40 A = 0.6 V) |

**Requirements:**

- **On board:** ≥8× 10 µF/50 V X7R 1210, placed directly across each half-bridge for HF loop current.
- **Off board, mandatory:** 1000–1500 µF / 35 V low-ESR at the XT60 pigtail. A TVS (SMBJ33A class) is also recommended against regen spikes.

### 1.6 Thermal Model

**Steady-state network** (per FET, PDFN5×6):

```
T_j ─[Rθjc 0.8 K/W]─ T_case ─[Rθ_pad/via 2.5 K/W]─ T_board ─[Rθ_board-amb]─ T_amb
```

The board-to-ambient resistance for a 30×30 mm, 6-layer, 2 oz board in prop wash (about 5–10 m/s) is roughly 1.0–1.5 K/W for the whole board. In still air it is about 4 K/W.

**Steady state at 97.5 W (whole board):**

$$
\Delta T_{board} = 97.5 \times 1.2 = 117\ K \quad\Rightarrow\quad \text{not sustainable}
$$

**Lumped thermal capacitance:**

| Material | Mass | c_p | C_th |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copper (planes + pads) | 4 g | 0.385 J/g·K | 1.54 J/K |
| FR4 | 3 g | 1.1 J/g·K | 3.3 J/K |
| FETs, components | 2 g | ~0.7 J/g·K | 1.4 J/K |
| Optional Al heat spreader | 3 g | 0.9 J/g·K | 2.7 J/K |
| **Total** | | | **≈ 6.2 J/K (8.9 J/K with spreader)** |
