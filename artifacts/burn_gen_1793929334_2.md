# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have the specification for "Job #35", so this design rests on the assumptions in §0. Every equation is parametric, so you can substitute your real motor, encoder and MCU values.

## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs $p=7$), $R_s≈5.5\,Ω$, $L_s≈2.0$ mH, $K_t≈0.08$ N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701 class), read over SPI |
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class with FPU. Center-aligned PWM at 20 kHz |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, separate SPI buses, gyro and accel ODR 8 kHz |
| Bus | $V_{dc}$ = 12–16 V |

**Loop rates and bandwidths.** Each loop's bandwidth is kept at least 5–10× below the loop inside it.

| Loop | Rate | Target bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | $ω_c ≈ 2π·1500$ rad/s |
| Velocity | 4 kHz | $ω_v ≈ 2π·120$ rad/s |
| Angle / stabilization | 1 kHz | $ω_p ≈ 2π·20$ rad/s |
| IMU fusion | 4 kHz | Decimated from 8 kHz after filtering |

**Signal flow per axis:**

```
θ_ref ─►[Angle P + vel FF]─► ω_ref ─►[Velocity PI]─► i_q,fb ─┐
                                                              (+)─► i_q* ─►[FOC current PI]─► SVPWM
                         i_q,cog = τ_cog(θ_m)/K_t  ──────────────┘
θ_meas, ω_meas  ◄── dual-IMU fusion (camera attitude/rate, projected onto the motor axis)
θ_e (commutation) ◄── encoder, latency-compensated
```

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle with latency compensation

$$
θ_e = p\,(θ_m - θ_{off}) + ω_e\,T_{lat},\qquad T_{lat}\approx 1.5\,T_{pwm}
$$

The $1.5\,T_{pwm}$ term covers sampling plus the zero-order hold of the PWM update. $θ_{off}$ is found at startup by applying a d-axis voltage vector and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (power-invariant amplitude form)

The second line uses $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$.

$$
i_α = i_a,\qquad i_β = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos θ_e & \sin θ_e\\ -\sin θ_e & \cos θ_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_α\\ i_β\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.4 PMSM model in the d-q frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - ω_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + ω_e L i_d + ω_e λ_m
$$

$$
τ_e = \tfrac32 p λ_m i_q = K_t i_q
$$

### 1.5 Current PI with pole-zero cancellation

The plant is $G(s)=\frac{1}{Ls+R}$. Choosing the gains below gives a closed loop of $\frac{ω_c}{s+ω_c}$:

$$
K_{p,i}=Lω_c,\qquad K_{i,i}=Rω_c
$$

**Decoupling plus back-EMF feedforward:**

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat ω_e \hat L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat ω_e \hat L i_d + \hat ω_e \hat λ_m
$$

Normally $i_d^*=0$.

**Voltage-circle limiting.** This gives d-axis priority:

$$
V_{max}= \frac{V_{dc}}{\sqrt3}\cdot m_{lim},\quad
v_d^{sat}=\text{clamp}(v_d^*,\pm V_{max}),\quad
v_q^{sat}=\text{clamp}\!\left(v_q^*,\pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2-(v_d^{sat})^2}\right)
$$

The modulation-limit factor $m_{lim}≈0.95$ leaves margin for current sampling.
