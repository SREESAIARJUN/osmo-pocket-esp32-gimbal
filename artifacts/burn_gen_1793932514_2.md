### Finding 1 (architecture)

The STM32H743 should **not** commutate the motors. Four motors need 12 complementary PWM pairs plus back-EMF zero-crossing comparators. The H743's TIM1 and TIM8 together provide only 6 complementary pairs. HRTIM could be stretched to cover more, but no maintained ArduPilot or AM32 firmware supports it.

The correct partition is:
- **H743** runs ArduPilot and outputs bidirectional DShot600.
- **4× AT32F421 or STM32G071** run AM32, one per motor, each driving one 3-phase gate driver.

The netlists in Section 4 follow this partition.

### Finding 2 (thermal)

**80 A per channel on a 20×20 board is a sub-second burst rating, not a continuous one.** The math in Section 3 shows:
- All four channels at 80 A simultaneously: about **0.6 s** before T_j reaches 125 °C.
- A single channel at 80 A (others at hover): about **2–3 s**.
