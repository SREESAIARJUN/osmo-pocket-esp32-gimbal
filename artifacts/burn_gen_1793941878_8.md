# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Thermal Model, Switching Losses, and Bidirectional DShot600 Timer Allocation

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Architectural Corrections

These three points shape every number below.

1. **The H743 does not generate the 48 kHz PWM.** In any ArduPilot AIO, the H743 runs the flight stack and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs. These are typically AT32F421 running AM32, or EFM8/BLHeli_S. The ESC MCUs commutate the bridges at 48 kHz. The model below treats them as separate domains.
2. **The component values are representative, not vendor data.** I can't verify the exact MOSFETs or driver on your Job #21 BOM. The parameters below match a typical 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFET used on 20×20 boards. Substitute your datasheet values into the equations. Every equation is parameterized.
3. **"80 A burst" on a 20×20 nano board is thermally a few-second, single-channel rating.** It is not survivable on four channels at once. Section 3 quantifies this. Battery-side current of 4 × 80 A at high duty would also exceed any XT60 or nano pad. Treat 80 A as a per-channel, non-simultaneous transient.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step trapezoidal with synchronous rectification

At any instant, current flows through two bridge positions: one in the PWM leg (HS or LS, alternating with PWM) and one in the static leg. Instantaneous loss per motor:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$
