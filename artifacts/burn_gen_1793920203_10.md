## 0. Architecture Clarification and Design Assumptions

**The STM32H743 cannot commutate the motors itself.** At 48 kHz PWM, four motors would need four independent 6-step or FOC commutation loops with back-EMF zero-crossing comparators. That is impractical alongside ArduPilot. The standard and correct partition is:

| Domain | Silicon | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100, 400–480 MHz) | ArduPilot | EKF, attitude control, DShot master |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8 or STM32G071 (one per motor) | AM32 | Commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive ×4 | 3-phase bootstrap driver (FD6288Q-class) | — | Drives 6 N-FETs per motor |
| Power stage ×4 | 6× 40 V N-FET, DFN3.3×3.3 | — | 24 FETs total |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) PWM, exactly two half-bridges carry the phase current at any instant:

- **PWM-on interval:** current flows through HS(A) → motor → LS(B).
- **PWM-off interval:** current flows through LS(A) (synchronous freewheel) → motor → LS(B).

Two channels are therefore always in series with the motor current, independent of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I^2 R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At a design point of T_j = 100 °C:

$$R_{ds(on)} = 1.2\ \text{m}\Omega \times 1.34 = 1.61\ \text{m}\Omega$$

At T_j = 125 °C, this rises to 1.74 mΩ. This value is used for the worst case.

### 1.3 Per-motor conduction loss at 80 A (T_j = 125 °C)

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.74\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{22.3\ W}$$

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution

Each phase leg is active for 2/3 of the electrical cycle. For 1/3 of the cycle it acts as the PWM leg, and for 1/3 as the low-side return.

- **Low-side FET:**
$$P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\, I^2 R + \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)\, I^2 R$$
- **High-side FET:**
$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\, D\, I^2 R$$

Summed over the 6 FETs, this returns 2I²R. Instantaneous dissipation while conducting is I²R = 11.1 W. The low-side FET is the hottest device, at about 7.4 W average when D → 0.

### 1.5 Parasitic resistive losses

$$P_{shunt} = 80^2 \times 0.25\ \text{m}\Omega = 1.6\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.5\ \text{m}\Omega = 3.2\ \text{W}$$

Note: the shunt carries DC-link current, which equals D·I_phase on average. The full-current figure used here is conservative.
