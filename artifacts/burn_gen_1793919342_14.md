## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Assumptions

Some ambiguities in the brief would cause design errors if left unstated.

**A. Two processor domains.** The H743 does not commutate motors. ArduPilot on the H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs, which run the 48 kHz 6-step PWM.

```
 STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir (4 lines)──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / AM32 class)
                                                        │ 48 kHz 6-step PWM
                                                        ▼
                                             4× 3-ph gate driver → 24× N-FET → motors
```

**B. Form factor reality check.** An 80 A-per-motor rating is not physically achievable on a 20×20 mm board, even as a burst. This design assumes a stacked 30.5×30.5 mm FC with a separate ESC board of about 36×36 mm. The thermal section shows the burst limits quantitatively.
