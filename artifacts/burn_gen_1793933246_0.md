## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

At any instant, two phases are active. Take phase A as the PWM phase and phase B as the low-side-on phase:

- **PWM ON (fraction D):** current flows V+ → Q_AH → motor → Q_BL → GND. That is 2 devices in series.
- **PWM OFF (fraction 1−D):** current freewheels Q_AL → motor → Q_BL. That is still 2 devices in series.

So, ignoring dead time, every instant has exactly two conducting R_DS(on) in the path, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

with

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Per-device distribution

Each phase spends 1/3 of the electrical period as the PWM leg, 1/3 as the LS-on leg, and 1/3 floating. The per-device average power is:

| Device | Duty-weighted conduction | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| High side (PWM leg) | D/3 | (D/3)·I²R |
| Low side (sync rectification + LS-on leg) | (1−D)/3 + 1/3 | ((2−D)/3)·I²R |

**Worst case: the low-side FETs at low duty.** At full throttle (D → 1) the high-side and low-side losses equalize at I²R/3 each.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

R_DS(on) at 125 °C = 1.2 × 1.5 = **1.8 mΩ**.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.8\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{23.0\ W\ per\ channel}$$

Per device:
- **7.68 W** at D ≈ 1
- up to **11.5 W** in the low-side FET at D = 0.5

### 1.4 Copper / interconnect

Assume R_Cu ≈ 0.5 mΩ per channel (2 oz inner pours, short paths, motor pads, share of battery pads). That adds:

$$80^2 \times 0.5\text{ m}\Omega = 3.2\text{ W per channel}$$

### 1.5 Current shunt

With a single 0.25 mΩ shunt carrying the total current of up to 320 A:

$$P_{shunt} = 320^2 \times 0.25\text{ m}\Omega = 25.6\text{ W}$$

This is unacceptable. **Per-channel shunts are required, or use a 0.1 mΩ shunt (10.2 W), or derive current in the ESC MCU and drop the board-level shunt.** Recommended: per-ESC 0.5 mΩ low-side shunts on the ESC MCU ADC. Each contributes 80² × 0.5 mΩ × (duty share ≈ 1) ≈ 3.2 W.
