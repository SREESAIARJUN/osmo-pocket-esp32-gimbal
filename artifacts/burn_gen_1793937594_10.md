## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Corrections

Several points in the brief need correcting or pinning down before any math is valid.

| Item | Assumption / Correction |
|---|---|
| **Who switches at 48 kHz** | The H743 does **not** drive the gates. Each motor channel runs its own ESC MCU (AM32 on STM32G071 or AT32F421 class) that generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 only emits DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. |
| **"80 A burst"** | Interpreted as **80 A per motor, all four simultaneously** (worst case). |
| **Bus voltage** | 6S LiPo: 25.2 V full, 22.2 V nominal. Calculations use 25.2 V. |
| **MOSFETs** | One 40 V, 5×6 mm DFN per switch (24 total). Representative parameters: R_DS(on) = 0.8 mΩ at 25 °C and V_GS = 10 V; Q_g = 80 nC; Q_rr ≈ 50 nC; Q_oss ≈ 60 nC; V_SD ≈ 0.8 V; R_θJC ≈ 1.2 K/W. **Replace these with your selected part's datasheet values.** |
