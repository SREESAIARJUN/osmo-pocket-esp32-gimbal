# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control Loop, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

I don't have the original specification for Job #29, so the design below is built on explicit assumptions (Section 0). Every gain is derived symbolically from plant parameters, so you can substitute your real motor, inertia and timing values.

## 0. Assumed Hardware and Timing

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5 Ω, L ≈ 2 mH, K_t ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Encoders | 14-bit absolute magnetic (MT6701 / AS5048A), one per joint |
| Current sensing | 2 low-side shunts per motor, ADC triggered at the PWM center |
| MCU | STM32G474 @ 170 MHz, single-precision FPU, CORDIC optional |
| IMU A | ICM-42688-P on the **camera plate**. This is the control feedback. |
| IMU B | ICM-42688-P on the **base/handle**, below the damper stage. This is the vibration reference. |
| Bus voltage | 12 V (3S) |

**Loop rates and bandwidth separation.** Each loop's bandwidth should be at least 5× below the loop inside it.

| Loop | Execution rate | Target bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current (d/q PI) | 20 kHz, in the ADC ISR | ω_c ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s |
| IMU sampling and filter chain | 8 kHz, SPI DMA on DRDY | — |
| Inertial rate (velocity) PI | 4 kHz | ω_v ≈ 2π·120 rad/s |
| Attitude (angle) P + FF | 1 kHz | ω_θ ≈ 2π·20 rad/s |
| Mahony fusion | 1 kHz | — |

**An architectural point that matters.** In a stabilizing gimbal, the velocity and angle loops close on **inertial** quantities: the camera gyro and the fused attitude. They do not close on encoder joint angles. The encoder is used only for:

- commutation (θ_e),
- the cogging LUT index,
- the kinematic Jacobian.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{m,0}) \bmod 2\pi$$

The offset θ_{m,0} is found at startup by applying a fixed V_d with θ_e = 0 and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$
