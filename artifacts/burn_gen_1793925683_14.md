## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Relationships

During any 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct the full phase current I_ph:

- **Phase A, high side:** PWM-switched, conducts for duty D.
- **Phase A, low side:** synchronous rectification, conducts for (1 − D).
- **Phase B, low side:** held on for the whole sector.

Battery current is related to phase current by I_batt ≈ D · I_ph. A "phase 80 A" rating and a "battery 80 A" rating are therefore very different at partial throttle, so specify which one you mean.

### 1.2 Bridge Conduction Loss

$$P_{cond,bridge} = I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

This result is **independent of duty** with synchronous rectification.

Adding ripple, where ΔI is the peak-to-peak phase-current ripple:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,R_{ds}(T_j)\left(I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}\right), \qquad \Delta I \approx \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

On-resistance versus temperature:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]$$

### 1.3 Worked Value at 80 A

Assume T_j ≈ 108 °C, so R_ds ≈ 1.5 mΩ:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W\ per\ channel}$$

Per-FET averaging over the electrical cycle gives 3.2 W per FET. This averaging is valid when the electrical period is at or below the die thermal time constant (~1–5 ms). Example: 100 k eRPM gives a 0.6 ms period, so averaging holds. At low eRPM under load (e.g. startup or a stall), individual FETs see peaks up to 2–3× the average. Treat those as separate peak cases.
