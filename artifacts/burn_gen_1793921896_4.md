# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: System Model (Job #64)

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architecture.** In a 4-in-1 stack the STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs. It runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32), a 3-phase gate driver, and 6 MOSFETs. The model below is split along that boundary.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Topology Argument

In 6-step commutation, two phases conduct at any instant: one high side and one low side.

With synchronous rectification, the current in the PWM-switched leg flows through the high-side FET for D·T and the complementary low-side FET for (1−D)·T. The leg conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{leg} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

The opposite leg is statically on, so it also dissipates I²R. The per-channel conduction loss is:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + I^2 R_{shunt} + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

### 1.2 Electro-thermal Coupling

R_DS depends on T_j, and T_j depends on the losses, so the loss must be solved iteratively:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)], \qquad T_j = T_{board} + P_{FET}\,R_{\theta JC}$$

At 80 A, with an assumed board temperature of 100 °C:
- First iteration gives T_j ≈ 105 °C, so R_DS ≈ 1.0 × 1.36 = 1.36 mΩ.
- Second iteration converges at **R_DS ≈ 1.38 mΩ**.

| Term | Formula | Value at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 2·80²·1.38 mΩ | **17.7 W** |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ) | 80²·0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| Phase/bus copper (~0.3 mΩ effective) | 80²·0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| **Conduction total** | | **≈ 21.2 W per channel** |

### 1.3 Per-FET Distribution

Each phase conducts for 2/3 of the electrical cycle. That conduction time splits between the high-side and low-side FETs of the leg. The average per-FET conduction loss is therefore:

$$\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{17.7}{6} \approx 2.95\ \text{W}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only one leg switches per PWM period in 6-step commutation. Its per-channel loss components are:

$$P_{sw,VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 20\text{ns}\cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

$$P_{dead} = V_f \cdot I \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot200\text{ns}\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{nC}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$

$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw}\ (Q_{oss}\approx25\text{nC}) = \mathbf{0.015\ W}$$

$$P_{gate} = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2\cdot40\text{nC}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k} = 0.038\ \text{W}$$

The gate-drive loss is dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FET.

**Switching total ≈ 1.66 W per channel.**

**Design levers:**
- Dead time is the largest controllable loss after V·I overlap. Reducing it from 100 ns to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W.
- Going from 48 kHz to 24 kHz halves all switching terms. The cost is more audible noise and current ripple.

### 2.1 Totals at 80 A Burst

| Conduction | 21.2 W | 84.8 W |

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Lumped RC Network (per channel, Cauer approximation)

```
P_FET ──[R_θJC 1.2 K/W]── Case ──[R_θCB 0.5 K/W (vias+solder)]── Board node ──[R_θBA]── Ambient
         C_j ≈ 0.02 J/K            C_case ≈ 0.1 J/K               C_board/4
```

### 3.2 Board Heat Capacity (whole stack)

- **Copper:** 30.5² mm² × 8 layers × 70 µm × ~70% fill ≈ 0.36 cm³. Mass ≈ 3.2 g, so C ≈ 3.2 g × 0.385 J/(g·K) ≈ **1.2 J/K**.
- **FR4:** 1.49 cm³ ≈ 2.8 g, so C ≈ 2.8 g × 1.1 J/(g·K) ≈ 3.1 J/K. About 50% of this is effectively coupled within 2 s.
- **FETs, caps, solder:** ≈ 0.5 J/K.
- **Effective C_board ≈ 3.2 J/K.**
