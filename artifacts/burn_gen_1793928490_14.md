# STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 80A ESC (Job #51): Architecture, Loss Models, DShot DMA, Netlists, Thermal

## 0. Architecture and Key Findings

### Architecture correction

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Candidate ESC MCUs are the STM32G071, AT32F421 or GD32E230. The ESC MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM to a 3-phase gate driver. The model below follows that split.
