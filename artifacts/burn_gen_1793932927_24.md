## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flags

**Topology.** The STM32H743 does **not** switch the power stage. The architecture is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) --[4x bi-dir DShot600, single wire each]--> 4x ESC MCU (AM32 / BLHeli_32)
                                                                   --> 3-phase gate driver --> 6 MOSFETs per motor
```

Two requirements live on different processors:
- **48 kHz PWM** is generated by each ESC MCU (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071).
- **DShot600 timer/DMA allocation** is an H743 and ArduPilot problem.

**Feasibility flag: form factor vs. current.**
- A "nano" stack (16×16 or 20×20 mm mounting) cannot dissipate the losses derived below, even in burst.
- 80 A burst per channel realistically needs a **30.5×30.5 mm ESC** (about 36×36 mm board).
- It also needs an 8-layer board with 2 oz inner copper and 3 oz outer copper, plus a clamped heatsink.
- This document therefore assumes:
  - a **20×20 FC** stacked on a **30.5×30.5 4-in-1 ESC**, or
  - a split-board design.
- Section 3 shows why the numbers force this.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (per channel)

### 2.1 Commutation Model

The ESC uses six-step trapezoidal BLDC with **complementary (synchronous) PWM** on the active leg. In any 60° sector:
- one high-side FET (leg A) and one low-side FET (leg B) carry the full current I;
- the PWM leg alternates conduction between its HS FET (fraction D) and its LS FET (fraction 1 − D);
- the third leg floats for back-EMF (BEMF) sensing.

Exactly **two FET channels carry I at every instant**, outside dead time.

### 2.2 Conduction Loss

Channel conduction loss:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)\cdot\left(1-\frac{2t_{dt}}{T_{sw}}\right)$$

Dead-time fraction: 2·200 ns / 20.83 µs = 1.92 %, so the bracketed factor is 0.981.

Per-FET average (each FET is active for 1/3 of the electrical cycle):

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\quad(\text{at } D\to 1,\ \text{burst condition})$$

Iterating to the thermal operating point (T_j ≈ 115 °C, see Section 3):

$$R_{ds}(115^\circ C) = 1.0\cdot(1+0.0045\cdot 90) = 1.405\ \text{m}\Omega$
