## 0. Architecture Assumptions

Several points in the brief need clarifying before any equations.

| Item | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Who commutates the motors | **4× dedicated ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 / STM32G071 class, running AM32) | ArduPilot does not run sensorless 6-step commutation. The H743 is the flight controller and sends DShot600 to the ESC MCUs. |
| 48 kHz | **ESC PWM frequency** on the bridge MOSFETs | This is an AM32 setting, not an H743 timer. |
| "80 A burst" | **80 A phase current per motor, burst** (≤ 5 s, see §3) | A nano board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) cannot sustain 80 A continuous. The math below proves this. |

| MOSFET | Parametric 3.3×3.3 / 5×6 DFN: R_DS(on),25 = 1.0 mΩ, Q_g = 30 nC, Q_gd = 10 nC, Q_rr = 30 nC, Q_oss = 40 nC, R_θJC = 1.0 K/W | Placeholder values. Substitute your selected part's datasheet values. |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

AM32 uses complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM. In any commutation sector:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** HS conducts for D·T_sw. LS conducts (sync rect) for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Phase B (sinking leg):** LS on continuously.
- **Phase C:** floating (BEMF sense).

So at every instant **exactly two MOSFETs carry the full phase current I_ph**, regardless of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature and ripple correction

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]^{\,}, \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS(on) ≈ 1.0 × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**
