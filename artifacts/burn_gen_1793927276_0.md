## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 @ 170 MHz, FPU | Any Cortex-M4F/M7 works |
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs) | R = 5.6 Ω, L = 2.1 mH, K_t = 0.085 N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (MT6701 / AS5048A) | Used for commutation and cogging phase |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI @ 24 MHz | ODR 8 kHz, gyro ±2000 dps, accel ±16 g |
| Bus voltage | 12–16.8 V (4S) | |
| Payload inertia | J ≈ 4.0e-4 kg·m² (pitch) | Identify per axis |

| Loop | Rate | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | PWM center-aligned ADC trigger |
| IMU acquisition + notch/LPF | 8 kHz | ICM-42688 INT1 (DRDY) |
| Velocity (inertial rate) | 4 kHz | Decimated from 20 kHz ISR |
| Attitude / angle | 1 kHz | Decimated |
| Cogging LMS adaptation | 4 kHz | Velocity ISR context |

The bandwidth separation follows the rule of thumb ω_c,inner ≥ 4–5 × ω_c,outer:

- Current loop: ω_i = 2π·1500 rad/s
- Velocity loop: ω_v = 2π·150 rad/s
- Angle loop: ω_θ = 2π·25 rad/s

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

θ_off is found at commissioning by applying a fixed d-axis current and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.4 PMSM model in the dq frame

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_d &= R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q\\
v_q &= R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
\end{aligned}
\qquad
\tau = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q
$$

### 1.5 Current PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

Pole-zero cancellation of the R-L plant gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_i:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_i,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_i
$$

Discrete form with step T_i = 1/20 kHz:

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_d^* &= K_{p,i}e_d + I_d - \hat\omega_e L i_q\\
v_q^* &= K_{p,i}e_q + I_q + \hat\omega_e L i_d + \hat\omega_e\lambda_m\\
I_{d,q}[k+1] &= I_{d,q}[k] + K_{i,i}T_i\,e_{d,q} + K_{aw}\big(v^{sat}_{d,q}-v^*_{d,q}\big)
\end{aligned}
$$

Voltage limiting uses a circular limit with d-priority, which is the inscribed circle of the SVPWM hexagon:

$$
V_{max} = \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}\,m_{max}
$$

$$
v_d^{sat} = \operatorname{clamp}(v_d^*, \pm V_{max}),\qquad v_q^{sat}=\operatorname{clamp}\!\left(v_q^*,\pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2-(v_d^{sat})^2}\right)
$$

The anti-windup gain is set to K_aw = 1/K_p,i, which is back-calculation with tracking time T_t = L/R.

### 1.6 Delay compensation

PWM update and ADC sampling introduce a 1.5·T_s transport delay. Advance the inverse-Park angle accordingly:

$$
\theta_e^{\text{inv}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\hat\omega_e T_i
$$

### 1.7 Inverse Park and SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta - v_q\sin\theta,\quad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta + v_q\cos\theta
$$

$$
v_a=v_\alpha,\; v_b=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\; v_c=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max+\min),\qquad d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

## 2. Cascaded Velocity / Angle Control

In a stabilizing gimbal, the controlled quantity is the **inertial** camera attitude and rate, not the joint angle. The loops are therefore assigned as follows:

- **Commutation:** encoder.
- **Velocity feedback:** fused IMU gyro rate (Section 4), projected onto the motor axis.
- **Angle feedback:** attitude estimate from IMU sensor fusion.
- **Encoder rate:** used only as a fallback and for cogging phase.
