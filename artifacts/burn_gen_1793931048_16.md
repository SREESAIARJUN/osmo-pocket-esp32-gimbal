## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

ArduPilot does not commutate motors. It produces DShot frames and consumes eRPM telemetry. The motor control loop must therefore run on dedicated ESC MCUs. This package uses the following baseline:

| Block | Assumption (verify against your BOM) |

| Gate driver | 3-phase half-bridge driver with bootstrap (FD6288Q-class), one per motor |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]$$

At the T_j = 125 °C design point:

$$R_{ds} = 1.2 \times 1.5 = 1.8\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current-path topology (6-step, synchronous)

In every 60° commutation sector, exactly one high-side leg and one low-side leg carry the phase current I.

- During PWM-on time, current flows through HS(A) and LS(B).
- During PWM-off time, the complementary LS(A) conducts instead of the HS(A) body diode.

So at every instant, **two MOSFET channels are in series with the motor current**, regardless of duty cycle.

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Each individual FET conducts for 120° of 360° electrical, which is 1/3 of the time:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)}{3}\quad(\text{averaged; HS/LS split depends on duty } D)$$

Per-FET split within a sector:

- HS(A) conducts for D.
- LS(A) conducts for (1−D).
- LS(B) conducts for 100%.

The worst-case FET is therefore the **static low-side** device:

$$P_{LS,peak\text{-}sector} = I^2 R_{ds}\ \text{for 1/3 of the period}$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.8\text{m} = \mathbf{23.0\ W}$$

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} = 6400 \times 1.8\text{m} / 3 = \mathbf{3.84\ W}$$

### 1.4 Ancillary conduction losses (often forgotten, and significant at 80 A)

| Element | Resistance | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Current shunt (if low-side bus shunt) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| PCB copper, phase pad to bus (2 oz, multilayer) | ~0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| Motor/battery solder joints and pads | ~0.1 mΩ | 0.6 W |
| **Subtotal per motor** | | **~4.1 W** |

### 1.5 Electrothermal coupling (fixed-point iteration)

R_ds depends on T_j, and T_j depends on the loss that R_ds creates. Solve iteratively:

$$T_j^{(k+1)} = T_{ref} + Z_{th}(t)\cdot P\big(R_{ds}(T_j^{(k)})\big)$$

Converge when |ΔT_j| < 0.5 K. The loop runs away (thermal runaway) if:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}\,Z_{th} \ge 1$$

Here ∂P/∂T_j = 2I²·R_ds25·α = 0.077 W/K per motor. That gives Z_th < 13 K/W as the stability bound, which this design satisfies.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

With complementary PWM, one leg per sector is hard-switched. Its HS FET switches on and off once per PWM period, and its LS FET switches with dead-time diode conduction.

### 2.1 V-I overlap loss (hard-switched HS FET)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

$$= 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction (LS FET)

There are two dead-time intervals per period, each with t_dt = 100 ns.

$$P_{dt} = V_f\,I\,(2\,t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$
