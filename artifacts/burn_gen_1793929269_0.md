# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #13): System Architecture & Math Model

## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Feasibility Flags

**Assumed partitioning.** The STM32H743 cannot practically commutate four BLDC motors and run ArduPilot at the same time. I'm assuming the standard split:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot, sends bi-directional DShot600 to the ESC MCUs |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32) | AM32 firmware, 48 kHz PWM, returns eRPM telemetry |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) | Drives 6 N-FETs per motor |
| Power stage | 24× 40 V N-MOSFET, PDFN 3.3×3.3 | Assumed R_DS(on) = 1.0 mΩ typ at 25 °C with V_GS = 10 V |

All device parameters below are **assumptions to be replaced with your selected part's datasheet values**. The equations are the deliverable; the numbers are worked examples.

**Feasibility flags. Please read these before committing to a spec:**

1. **"80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 mm) board is not thermally sustainable.** The model in §3 shows about 80 W of power-stage dissipation with all four channels at 80 A. That gives roughly 1.5 s of runway before reaching junction limits. Treat it as a *per-motor, sub-second* rating, or move to a 30×30 mm form factor.
2. "80 A" is interpreted as **per-motor phase current**. At high duty, battery current approaches 4 × 80 × D. That figure stresses the battery pads, the shunt, and the XT60 path far more than the FETs.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.004\ /\text{K}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × 1.40 = **1.40 mΩ** (used throughout).

### 1.2 Six-step (trapezoidal) commutation with synchronous rectification

In any 60° sector, exactly one leg is PWM-driven high, one leg is held low, and one floats.

- In the PWM leg, current alternates between the high-side FET (fraction D) and its synchronous low-side FET (fraction 1−D).
- The low leg's low-side FET carries the current continuously.

Therefore, **at every instant two FETs carry the phase current I**, independent of duty (dead time ignored):

$$\boxed{P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)}$$

Per-FET breakdown over one electrical cycle, for each leg:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}D\,I^2R,\qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)\,I^2R + \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R$$
