## 0. Architecture Corrections and Stated Assumptions

Three issues in the brief must be settled before the math means anything.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is a flight-control stack. It produces DShot frames and has no BEMF/6-step commutation layer. The correct architecture is:
   - STM32H743 as the flight controller.
   - Four ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware: AT32F421 or STM32G071, one per motor.
   - Bi-directional DShot600 between them.
2. **"80 A" must mean phase current per channel, burst only.** It cannot be a continuous rating on a nano form factor. Section 3 shows the board sustains roughly 20 A per channel continuously. 80 A is a burst lasting a few seconds and limited by thermal capacitance.
3. **"Nano" is taken as a 30.5 × 30.5 mm footprint.** A 20 × 20 mm board cannot physically host 24 power MOSFETs rated for 80 A burst plus an H743 in LQFP100/TFBGA. If the target really is 20 × 20 mm, the burst rating must drop to about 35–40 A.
