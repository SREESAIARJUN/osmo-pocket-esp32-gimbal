# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture, Loss and Thermal Model, DShot DMA, and Netlist

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Architectural Corrections

Some requirements in the brief need correcting before the math will be valid.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is not motor-control firmware and cannot run 4 × 3-phase commutation at 48 kHz with sensorless BEMF zero-crossing detection. The correct partition is:
   - **H743** runs ArduPilot. It sends bidirectional DShot600 commands to four ESC MCUs and reads their eRPM telemetry back.
   - **4 × ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071, running AM32) handle commutation and 48 kHz PWM.
2. **The "80A burst" rating is a transient, not a continuous rating.** Section 3 proves that a nano-format board (30×30 mm) cannot dissipate approximately 93 W steady-state. 80A per channel is achievable only for about 1–2 seconds, limited by thermal capacitance.
3. **The MOSFET part was not specified for Job #25.** I use a representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN device. Every equation is parametric, so you can substitute datasheet values directly.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_ds = 1.2 × 1.375 = **1.65 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

At any instant, the phase current passes through exactly two series FET positions:
- one high-side position (the PWM leg: HS during D, LS during 1−D, complementary), and
- one low-side position (held ON for the 120° interval).

Because the complementary PWM leg always has one FET conducting, the duty-cycle terms cancel:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

**At 80A, T_j = 100 °C:**

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.65\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{21.1\ W\ per\ channel}$$

### 1.3 Copper and shunt losses (per channel)

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 (R_{shunt} + R_{trace})$$

With a 0.25 mΩ shunt and 0.3 mΩ of effective trace/via resistance (2 oz, multi-layer paralleled):

$$P_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.55\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{3.5\ W}$$

> **Design note:** At 80A, copper loss is about 17% of FET loss. Paralleled inner planes and via-stitching are not optional at this current.

$$\frac{dP}{dT} = 2 I^2 R_{ds25}\,\alpha = 2(6400)(1.2\times10^{-3})(0.005) = 0.0768\ \text{W/K per channel}$$
