# Job #33: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC
## Design Package: Loss Model, Thermal Model, Bidirectional DShot600 Timer/DMA Plan, Netlists

> **Read first: scope and assumptions**
> - The STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. It generates DShot600 and decodes bidirectional eRPM telemetry. Each ESC channel's commutation runs on its own ESC MCU (AM32 on an AT32F421 in this design). 48 kHz is the **ESC PWM frequency**, not an H743 timer rate.
> - Device parameters below are a **representative 40 V PQFN 5×6 MOSFET** and a **FD6288Q-class 3-phase gate driver**. Every equation is parametric. Substitute your final BOM datasheet values before sign-off. Numbers marked ⚠ need bench verification.
> - 80 A **per motor** means up to 320 A at the battery. That is a burst-only rating. No nano form factor can dissipate it continuously, and the thermal section quantifies how long it can be sustained.
