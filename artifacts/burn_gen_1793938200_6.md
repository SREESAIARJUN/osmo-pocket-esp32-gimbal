## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Red Flags

### 0.1 Architecture

The H743 does **not** drive gates directly. ArduPilot has no BLDC commutation firmware. The standard topology is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──BDShot600 ×4──► 4× AT32F421 (AM32) ──► 4× FD6288Q ──► 24× N-MOSFET ──► 4 motors
```

### 0.2 Red Flags

**80 A per channel on a "nano" (20×20 mm mount) board is outside sustainable physics.** Section 3 shows:

- About 85–95 W of semiconductor loss.
- About 10 W of copper loss.
- About 320 A of battery current through solder pads.

The math below is fully parametric. Run it at 80 A per channel as specified, then use the scaling table (§3.5) to choose a realistic rating. If "80 A" means **total board burst (20 A/channel)**, the design is comfortable.
