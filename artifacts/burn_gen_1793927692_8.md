## 0. Architecture Clarifications

Several requirements in the brief conflict with each other. These need resolving before layout.

| Issue | Resolution adopted |
|---|---|
| **ArduPilot cannot commutate BLDC motors.** An H743 running ArduPilot sends DShot; it does not run the 48 kHz FOC/6-step loops. | AIO topology: the H743 is the flight controller. It drives 4 × AT32F421K8U7 ESC MCUs running AM32 firmware, one per motor. The 48 kHz PWM lives on the AT32s. |
| **"Nano" plus 4 × 80 A burst.** A 20×20 mm board cannot carry 4 × 80 A. | 30.5×30.5 mm mounting, 37×42 mm outline. 8-layer board with 3 oz outer and 2 oz inner copper. Plan on 2 FETs in parallel per switch (24 FETs) if you need more than a 2 s simultaneous burst (see §3). |
| **"80 A" is ambiguous.** | Defined here as **80 A per-channel phase current** (motor RMS ≈ DC in 6-step), at 6S (V_bus = 25.2 V nominal, 26.1 V HV-LiPo). |

**Status of all figures:** every value below is a parameterized first-pass model. The MOSFET numbers are representative of a 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PQFN class part. Substitute the datasheet values of your selected part and re-run the equations.
