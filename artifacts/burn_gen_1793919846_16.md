## 0. Architecture Clarification and Sanity Check

### 0.1 What runs where

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. The system is split into two parts:

| Block | Device | Function |
|---|---|---|
| FC | STM32H743VIT6 (400 MHz, ArduPilot) | Attitude control, mixer, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| ESC ×4 | STM32G071GBU6 or AT32F421 (AM32 firmware) | Six-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase half-bridge driver) **[VERIFY]** | Drives 6 N-FETs per channel |
| Power ×24 | 40 V N-MOSFETs, PDFN 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 | Inverter bridges |

### 0.2 The "80 A burst" figure

Before the math, three physical limits:

1. **Total battery current.** At full throttle, battery current per channel is roughly equal to phase current (D → 1). Four channels × 80 A gives **320 A, about 7 kW at 6S**. An XT60 is rated around 60 A continuous, and 12 AWG lead is around 80–100 A burst.
2. **Board size.** A 20×20 mm board cannot reject tens of watts steadily. As shown in §3, 80 A burst is a **thermal-capacitance (seconds-scale) rating**, not a thermal-resistance rating.
3. **Recommendation:** Specify **80 A per channel for ≤ 2 s, single-channel or diagonal-pair dominant**. Specify **total battery current ≤ 120–150 A** at the connector. Use a 30.5×30.5 mm form factor if "nano" permits.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] \quad\Rightarrow\quad R_{ds}(125^\circ C) = 1.5\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Six-step conduction with synchronous rectification

In each 60° commutation sector, one high-side (HS) leg is PWM'd complementarily with its low-side (LS) partner, and one LS leg in another phase is held on. The phase current I_ph therefore always passes through exactly **two** conducting FETs. During the on-time (D) the path is HS + static LS; during the off-time (1−D) it is sync-LS + static LS.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

This result is independent of duty cycle, because synchronous rectification eliminates diode freewheeling except during dead time.

Per-FET average: each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle (as an active or static switch), so the per-FET share is approximately P_cond,ch / 3 when averaged. The **instantaneous** dissipation of a conducting FET is I²R.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A, Tj = 125 °C

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

Instantaneous per conducting FET: 80² × 1.5 mΩ = **9.6 W**.

### 1.5 PCB copper and shunt conduction

- Phase + bus path resistance estimate: R_cu ≈ 0.4 mΩ (6-layer, 2 oz outer, 1 oz inner) **[extract from layout]**.
- Copper temperature coefficient is 0.0039 /K, giving about 0.5 mΩ at 85 °C.

$$P_{cu,ch} = 80^2 \times 0.5\ \text{m}\Omega \approx \mathbf{3.2\ W}$$
