## 1. Rigorous Equations

### 1.1 Field-Oriented Control (innermost loop, 20 kHz)

**Electrical angle.** θ_m is the mechanical angle and θ_0 the calibrated offset:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_0) \bmod 2\pi$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Plant in the dq frame.** For a surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$
$$\tau = \tfrac{3}{2}p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q$$

**Current PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:**

$$v_d^* = PI_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q^* = PI_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

**Pole-zero cancellation tuning.** With target bandwidth ω_c, the closed loop becomes 1/(s/ω_c+1):

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \tfrac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10}$$

With f_pwm = 20 kHz, choose ω_c = 2π·1500 rad/s.

**Voltage limiting.** The voltage vector is limited to the linear SVPWM hexagon's inscribed circle, |v| ≤ V_dc/√3. Priority goes to v_d:

$$v_q^{max}=\sqrt{(V_{dc}/\sqrt3)^2 - v_d^2}$$

**Inverse Park, then SVPWM.** Use min-max zero-sequence injection:

$$v_{a,b,c}\ \text{from inverse Clarke},\qquad v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max v + \min v)$$
$$D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{dc}}$$

### 1.2 Discrete PI/PID with Anti-Windup

The integrator is discretized by Tustin (trapezoid). The derivative acts on the measurement and is low-passed with time constant T_f:

$$u_P = K_p e_k$$
$$I_k = I_{k-1} + \tfrac{K_i T_s}{2}(e_k + e_{k-1}) + K_{aw}T_s(u_{sat,k-1}-u_{k-1})$$
$$D_k = \frac{2T_f - T_s}{2T_f + T_s}D_{k-1} - \frac{2K_d}{2T_f+T_s}(y_k - y_{k-1})$$
$$u_k = u_P + I_k + D_k + u_{ff},\qquad u_{sat}=\mathrm{clamp}(u_k)$$

The back-calculation gain is typically $K_{aw} = 1/T_i = K_i/K_p$.
