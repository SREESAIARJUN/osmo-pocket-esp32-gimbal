## 0. Architecture Clarification and Stated Assumptions

**ArduPilot does not commutate motors on the H743.** The H743 runs ArduCopter and sends bidirectional DShot600 to **four dedicated ESC MCUs** on the ESC board. Each ESC MCU runs AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware and performs 6-step commutation with complementary 48 kHz PWM. The architecture is therefore:

```
[H743 FC] --BDShot600 x4--> [ESC MCU x4 (AT32F421 / STM32G071)] --> [3-ph driver x4] --> [24 MOSFETs]
```
