# Job #45: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC
## Systems Architecture: Loss Model, Thermal Budget, Bi-directional DShot600 DMA Plan, Netlists

---

## 0. Executive Findings

These conclusions drive the rest of the document.

| # | Finding | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | **"80A" on a nano (30.5×30.5 mm) form factor is a burst rating only.** With realistic 40V FETs (single FET per switch), 80A per channel produces about **21 W per channel** of power-stage loss. That is about 84 W if all four channels burst together. | Burst duration is limited by thermal capacitance to about **2–3 s with all four channels at 80A**, or about **8–10 s for a single channel**. Continuous capability is about **30A per channel** in prop wash. |
| F2 | The FC (H743, ArduPilot) and the ESC are **separate control domains**. The H743 does *not* generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM. That is done by four ESC MCUs (AM32-class) or one per channel. | The H743 needs only 4× bi-directional DShot600 lines, telemetry, and current/voltage sense. |
| F3 | The H743 DMAMUX removes most F4/F7 stream conflicts. The only real constraints are **one TIMx_UP request per output timer** plus **input-capture DMA per bidir channel**. | Put all 4 motors on **TIM1 CH1–CH4**. The full allocation fits in 5 DMA streams. |
| F4 | All four channels at 80A means about **256 A battery current at 6S** (≈6.4 kW). Bus capacitors, the battery pad, and the shunt cannot support this on a nano board. | Specify the burst as **per-channel, non-simultaneous**, or move to a larger frame and paralleled FETs. |

**Assumptions.** You did not specify a MOSFET, gate driver, or cell count. I used a representative 40V shielded-gate FET and 6S. All parameters marked ⚠ must be replaced with your actual datasheet values before release. The equations are general; only the numbers change.
