### ⚠️ Headline finding: read before committing to layout

> **Simultaneous 80 A on all four channels of a 20×20 mm board is a short-duration burst rating only.**
>
> - Total dissipation is about **120 W**.
> - Adiabatic board heating is about **70 K/s**, so the board reaches its limit in **under 1 second**.
> - The steady-state sustainable dissipation in prop-wash is about **8–12 W**, which corresponds to roughly **20–25 A per channel continuous**.
>
> Two further recommendations:
>
> - **Do not integrate the H743 onto the same PCB as the power stage at 20×20.** The H743 (LQFP100 14×14 or TFBGA100 10×10) plus 24 power FETs, 4 ESC MCUs, 4 drivers and bulk capacitance does not fit in 400 mm² with an adequate copper pour.
> - **Recommended architecture:** a stacked pair, consisting of a 20×20 FC (H743) above a 20×20 ESC, connected by an 8-pin harness. Alternatively, move the ESC to 30×30.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.0048\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At T<sub>j</sub> = 150 °C:

$$
R_{DS(on)} = 1.4\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.60 = 2.24\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

In addition, model the board copper and solder path explicitly. For each phase path (battery pad → high-side FET → phase pad → low-side FET → ground), estimate R<sub>Cu</sub> ≈ 0.3 mΩ for 2 oz copper over 6 layers in a short path.

### 1.2 Current path in synchronous 6-step

During any commutation sector, exactly one high-side (HS) and one low-side (LS) FET on different legs carry the phase current I. With complementary PWM at duty D:

- **Active leg:** the HS FET conducts for time D·T<sub>s</sub> and its LS partner for (1 − D)·T<sub>s</sub>. Together they form one continuous R<sub>DS(on)</sub> in the path.
- **Return leg:** the LS FET conducts 100% of the sector.

So the current always passes through two R<sub>DS(on)</sub> in series:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2\left(2R_{DS(on)} + R_{Cu}\right)
$$

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 80^2\,(4.48 + 0.3)\times10^{-3} = 6400 \times 4.78\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{30.6\ W}
$$

FET-only portion: 6400 × 4.48 mΩ = **28.7 W per channel**.

### 1.3 Per-device average

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period, either as the return leg or shared as the PWM leg. The per-FET average is:

$$
P_{FET,avg} = \frac{28.7}{6} \approx 4.8\ \text{W}
$$

The instantaneous peak per FET is 80² × 2.24 mΩ = **14.3 W**. The ripple matters at low eRPM (see Section 3.3).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Per channel, only the PWM leg switches. Each PWM period contains one hard turn-on and one hard turn-off of the HS FET. The LS FET switches at ~0 V (ZVS via the body diode) during dead time.

### 2.1 V–I overlap loss

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}
$$

For the 1 A driver, Q<sub>gd</sub> ≈ 8 nC, and V<sub>plateau</sub> ≈ 4 V:

- t<sub>r</sub> ≈ Q<sub>gd</sub> / I<sub>g</sub> ≈ 8 ns; use 10 ns with margin.
- t<sub>f</sub> ≈ 10 ns.

$$
P_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}
$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$
P_{dt} = V_F\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}
$$

This assumes t<sub>dt</sub> = 100 ns per edge.

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

$$
P_{rr} = V_{bus}\,Q_{rr}\,f_{sw} = 25.2 \times 30\,\text{nC} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.036\ W}
$$

### 2.4 Output capacitance

$$
P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,C_{oss,eff}\,V_{bus}^2\,f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 1\,\text{nF} \times 635 \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.015\ W
