## 0. Architecture and Scope

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. It does not drive gates. Each of the four ESC channels has its own motor MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 firmware, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071. That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 sends bi-directional DShot600 commands and receives eRPM telemetry back.
