## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha\approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}\ \textbf{[A]}$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds ≈ 1.0 mΩ × 1.51 ≈ **1.51 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step synchronous commutation

In any 60° sector, two phase legs carry current:

- **PWM leg:** current flows through the high-side FET for D·T_sw and through the synchronous low-side FET for (1−D)·T_sw. Conduction loss is I²R_ds in both intervals, so this leg contributes **I²R_ds**, independent of D.
- **Static leg:** the low-side FET is on continuously and contributes **I²R_ds**.

So the per-channel conduction loss is:

$$P_{cond,ch}=2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Include PWM ripple:

$$I_{rms}^2=I_{dc}^2+\frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12},\qquad \Delta I_{pp}=\frac{(V_{bus}-E_b)\,D}{2L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

Example: L_ph = 15 µH **[A]**, D = 0.5, back-EMF E_b = 0.45·V_bus:

ΔI_pp = (25.2 − 11.3)(0.5) / (2 · 15e-6 · 48e3) ≈ **4.8 A**, so ΔI²/12 ≈ 1.9 A². This is negligible against 6400 A².

**At 80 A, T_j = 110 °C:**

$$P_{cond,ch}=2(6400+1.9)(1.51\times10^{-3})\approx \mathbf{19.3\ W}$$

Each FET conducts about 1/3 of the electrical cycle, so the average is about **3.2 W per FET**, with sector-peak dissipation of about 9.7 W.

### 1.3 Copper and shunt conduction

- Phase pad to motor wire, 2 oz copper, about 0.3 mΩ round trip **[A]**: 6400 × 0.3e-3 ≈ **1.9 W/channel**.
- The bus shunt is covered in §5.3. It is a large contributor.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 V–I overlap (hard-switched PWM leg only)

$$P_{VI}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw}$$

Transition time comes from the Miller plateau:

$$t_{r}\approx\frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}},\quad I_{g,on}=\frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{g}+R_{g,int}+R_{drv}}$$

With Q_gs2 + Q_gd = 14 nC **[A]**, V_drv = 10 V, V_pl = 3.5 V, and R_total = 10 Ω, I_g ≈ 0.65 A and t_r ≈ 21 ns. Turn-off with a faster sink path gives t_f ≈ 12 ns.

$$P_{VI}=0.5(25.2)(80)(33\times10^{-9})(48\times10^3)\approx\mathbf{1.60\ W}$$

### 2.2 Remaining switching terms

| Term | Formula | Values | Loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| Output capacitance | Q_oss·V·f | 40 nC **[A]** | 0.048 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f | 30 nC **[A]** | 0.036 W |
| Dead-time body diode | V_f·I·2t_dt·f | 0.8 V, t_dt = 50 ns **[A]** | 0.31 W |
| Gate drive (mostly in driver and R_g) | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f | 50 nC | 0.048 W |
| **Σ switching per channel** | | | **≈ 2.04 W** |

### 2.3 Per-channel and board totals at 80 A burst

| | Per channel | ×4 |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 19.3 W | 77.2 W |
| Switching | 2.0 W | 8.2 W |
| Phase copper | 1.9 W | 7.7 W |
| **ESC total (excluding shunt)** | **23.2 W** | **≈ 93 W** |

Efficiency check: P_out ≈ 320 A × 25.2 V × D_eff. Losses of about 93 W are roughly 1–2 % of electrical input at full throttle, which is plausible. The problem is heat density, not efficiency.

**Design lever:** conduction dominates at 89 %. Raising f_sw to 96 kHz adds only about 2 W per channel. Halving R_ds (paralleled FETs or a 0.5 mΩ part) saves about 9.6 W per channel.
