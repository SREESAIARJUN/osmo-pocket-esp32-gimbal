**Scope:** loss model, thermal model, bidirectional DShot600 DMA plan, and reference netlists.

I have no prior project record for "Job #49." Everything below is derived from first principles using stated assumptions, and every assumed parameter is marked so you can substitute datasheet values.

## 0. Architecture Facts and Assumptions

**The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In any ArduPilot AIO board, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot to four ESC MCUs. These are typically STM32G071, AT32F421 or GD32 parts running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The 48 kHz PWM, the 6-step or FOC commutation, and the MOSFET switching all live on the ESC MCUs. The DMA model in §4 covers the FC side. The loss models in §1–3 cover the ESC side.

**"80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board is an extreme claim.** The math below shows it is survivable only for about 2–4 s. Continuous capability is roughly 20–25 A per motor. Treat 80 A as a time-limited rating.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

At any instant, the motor current I flows through exactly two conducting switch positions:
- the PWM leg (high side for D, low side for 1−D), and
- the static low-side leg (on continuously).

Each leg spends 1/3 of the electrical period as the PWM leg, 1/3 as the static leg, and 1/3 floating.

### 1.2 Per-FET average conduction loss

- High-side FET: P_HS = I² · R(T_j) · D/3
- Low-side FET: P_LS = I² · R(T_j) · [(1−D)/3 + 1/3]

Per bridge (one motor):

  P_cond,bridge = 3[P_HS + P_LS] = **2 · I² · R(T_j)**

This result is independent of D.

### 1.3 Temperature-dependent resistance

  R(T_j) = R_25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 110 °C: R = 1.3 mΩ × 1.425 = **1.86 mΩ**.

### 1.4 Numbers at I = 80 A

- Per motor: P_cond = 2 × 80² × 1.86e-3 = **23.8 W**
- All four motors: **95 W**
- Hottest FET (low side, D = 0.9): 6400 × 1.86e-3 × 0.4 = **4.76 W**

### 1.5 PCB copper (add separately)

A 2 oz copper path of about 0.3–0.5 mΩ per motor current loop adds I²R_cu ≈ 2–3 W per motor at 80 A. This loss is distributed over the board rather than concentrated in the FETs.

### 1.6 Electrothermal coupling

R depends on T_j, and T_j depends on P. The fixed point is:

  T_j = T_ref + Z_th · 2I² R_25 [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

Solve by iteration (converges in 3–4 steps). Thermal runaway occurs if Z_th · 2I² R_25 · α ≥ 1.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches, so there is one hard-switched commutation pair per motor at any time.

### 2.1 Transition times (Miller-plateau model)

Turn-on gate current and time:
- I_g,on = (V_drv − V_pl) / R_g = (10 − 3.5) / 5 = 1.3 A
- t_on ≈ (Q_gs2 + Q_gd) / I_g,on = 10 nC / 1.3 A = 7.7 ns

Turn-off gate current and time:
- I_g,off = V_pl / R_g = 3.5 / 5 = 0.7 A
- t_off ≈ 10 nC / 0.7 A = 14.3 ns
