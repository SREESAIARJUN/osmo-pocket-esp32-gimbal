# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80 A ESC (Job #78): Electro-Thermal Model, DShot DMA Plan, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

### Architecture

The STM32H743 (FC) generates bidirectional DShot600 for four ESC MCUs. Each ESC MCU (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32) generates the 48 kHz 3-phase PWM locally. The H743 does not drive the gates.
