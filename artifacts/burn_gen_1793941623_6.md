# STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and Bidirectional DShot600 Timer/DMA Allocation

**Before relying on this document, note the following:**

- **Assumed architecture.** The H743 runs ArduPilot and produces 4× bidirectional DShot600. Each ESC channel is commutated by a dedicated ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class, running AM32 or BLHeli_32), and that MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 cannot directly commutate four motors at 48 kHz under ArduPilot.
- **Placeholder parameters.** Component values (R_DS(on), Q_g, Q_rr, R_θ) are representative of 40 V DFN3.3×3.3 MOSFETs. Replace them with the datasheet values of your selected parts before release.
- **Feasibility of the 80 A rating.** The math below shows that 80 A per channel on a nano-format board is a sub-second burst figure. It is not a sustained rating.
- **Unverified items.** The ArduPilot hwdef details should be checked against the current `libraries/AP_HAL_ChibiOS/hwdef` documentation and existing H743 board definitions. The netlist has not been through ERC/DRC.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation, exactly two phase legs carry I_ph at any instant: one sourcing and one sinking.

With synchronous rectification, each conducting leg routes current through either the high-side (HS) FET (fraction D of the period) or the low-side (LS) FET (fraction 1−D). The instantaneous conduction path therefore always contains exactly **two channels' worth of R_DS(on)**, independent of duty cycle. Dead-time diode conduction is treated separately.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

Each of the 6 FETs per channel conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period, split between HS and LS by D. The average loss per FET is therefore:

$$\bar P_{FET} = \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}$$

The worst-case single FET is the LS device at low D (e.g. a hard punch-out from low throttle):

$$P_{LS,max} \approx \tfrac{1}{3}\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}\,(1-D) \cdot 2$$
