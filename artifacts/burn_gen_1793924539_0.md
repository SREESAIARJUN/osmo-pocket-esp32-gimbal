I don't have the Job #42 requirements file, so every parameter below is marked as **[ASSUMED]** or **[DERIVED]**. Replace the assumed values with your BOM datasheet numbers. All equations are written to make that substitution easy.

## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the MOSFETs. ArduPilot has no in-tree sensorless BLDC commutation. The partitioning is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──BDShot600 ×4──► 4× ESC MCU (AM32 / BLHeli_32, e.g. AT32F421)
                                           └─► 3-phase gate driver ──► 6× N-FET per motor
```

The H743 handles timer and DMA allocation (Section 3). The ESC MCUs and power stage handle losses (Sections 1–2).

## 2. Loss Model (per Motor Channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

Assume 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM on the active phase. In every PWM sub-interval, phase current flows through exactly **two** conducting FETs:

- During the ON interval: high-side of phase A and low-side of phase B.
- During the OFF interval: low-side of phase A and low-side of phase B (synchronous freewheel).

So, independent of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond} = I_b^2 \cdot 2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

with the temperature-dependent on-resistance:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]$$

The worst-case path FET is the static low-side of phase B, which carries the full current all the time:

$$P_{FET,max} = I_b^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

**Evaluated values:**

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond (2 FETs) | Worst single FET |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 6.4 W |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ | 17.1 W | 8.6 W |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W | 9.3 W |

**Copper loss.** Assume ~0.3 mΩ of round-trip trace and pad resistance per phase pair:

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.3\,\text{m}\Omega = 1.9\ \text{W}$$

This is not negligible at nano scale. Verify it with an IPC-2152 or field-solver extraction.

### 2.2 Switching Losses at 48 kHz

**Hard-switching V·I overlap** (on the PWM'd leg only):

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_b (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = 0.968\ \text{W}$$

**Reverse recovery.** The low-side body diode conducts during dead time and is then recovered by high-side turn-on:

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.060\ \text{W}$$

**Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss} = C_{oss,er} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 1\text{n} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} = 0.030\ \text{W}$$

**Dead-time diode conduction.** There are two transitions per period:

$$P_{dt} = 2\,V_F I_b t_{dt} f_{sw} = 2 \times 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = 0.614\ \text{W}$$

**Gate drive.** Two FETs on the PWM'd leg; this power dissipates mostly in the driver and gate resistors, not the FETs:

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GD} f_{sw} = 2 \times 40\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.038\ \text{W}$$

**Switching subtotal:** ≈ **1.71 W** per motor.

### 2.3 Per-Channel and Board Totals at T_j = 125 °C

| Loss term | Per motor | ×4 motors |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FET) | 18.6 W | 74.4 W |
| Copper | 1.9 W | 7.7 W |
| Switching + dead time + Q_rr + C_oss | 1.71 W | 6.8 W |
| Gate drive | 0.04 W | 0.15 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 22.3 W** | **≈ 89 W** |

At 80 A, conduction dominates switching by about 11:1. Increasing f_sw from 24 to 48 kHz costs only about 0.85 W per motor. **The 80 A figure is the thermal problem, not 48 kHz.**

**Bus capacitor ripple.** The worst case is D = 0.5:

$$I_{C,rms} \approx I_b\sqrt{D(1-D)} = 40\ \text{A}_{rms} \text{ per motor}$$

With interleaving across 4 motors this does not cancel reliably, because the ESC MCUs are unsynchronized. This requires a bulk low-ESR polymer capacitor at the pack input (e.g. ≥ 1000 µF / 35 V low-ESR) plus local MLCCs per bridge. Budget for 40 A_rms per channel ripple.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Network Topology (Cauer Lumped Model)

```
P_FET ──[R_θJC=1.5]──●──[R_θCB≈2.0 (pad+vias)]──●── board node (C_board) ──[R_θBA]── T_amb
                    T_c                          T_b
```

- **Board heat capacity.** Copper (~2.0 g × 0.385 J/g·K) plus FR4 (~2.5 g × 1.1 J/g·K) plus FET packages gives **C_board ≈ 3.5 J/K**. [DERIVED, ASSUMED geometry]
- **Board-to-ambient.** R_θBA ≈ 8 K/W in prop-wash airflow, rising to about 25 K/W in still air. [ASSUMED]

### 3.2 Board Temperature Rise During Burst

$$\tau = R_{\theta BA}\,C_{board} = 8 \times 3.5 = 28\ \text{s} \gg t_b$$

Because τ ≫ t_b, the board is quasi-adiabatic during the burst:

$$\Delta T_b(t) = P_{tot} R_{\theta BA}\left(1 - e^{-t/\tau}\right) \approx \frac{P_{tot}\,t}{C_{board}}$$

$$\Delta T_b(2\,\text{s}) = 89 \times 8 \times (1 - e^{-2/28}) = 49\ \text{K}$$

### 3.3 Junction Rise Above Board (Worst FET, 9.3 W)

Over a 2 s burst, the junction-to-case and case-to-board stages are effectively at steady state, since their time constants are in the tens of milliseconds:

$$\Delta T_{jb} = 9.3 \times (1.5 + 2.0) = 32.6\ \text{K}$$

### 3.4 Junction Temperature

Assume a pre-burst board temperature of 60 °C (cruise heat soak in summer, 35 °C ambient):

$$T_j = 60 + 49 + 33 = 142\ \text{°C}$$

That is below the 175 °C absolute maximum, but above a 125 °C derating target.

The equation is coupled, because R_DS(T_j) feeds back into P. Iterate:

```
Tj0 = 125 → P = 89 W → Tj = 142 → R_DS = 1.53 mΩ → P ≈ 93 W → Tj ≈ 145 °C (converges)
```

### 3.5 Maximum Burst Duration to T_j = 150 °C

$$t_{max} = -\tau\,\ln\!\left(1 - \frac{150 - 60 - 33}{P\,R_{\theta BA}}\right) = -28\ln(1 - 57/744) \approx 2.2\ \text{s}$$

### 3.6 Engineering Verdict

**80 A per motor for about 2 s on a 20×20 board is marginal.** It is achievable only with:

- prop-wash airflow,
- FETs with ≤ 1.0 mΩ R_DS(on) at 25 °C, and
- thermal vias under every drain pad (≥ 20 × 0.3 mm filled vias).

If Job #42 means **80 A total**, i.e. 20 A per motor, losses fall by about 16×. In that case the design is comfortable. **Confirm which interpretation applies before layout.**

Firmware protection should include:

- an AM32 temperature limit around 110 °C (board NTC lags the junction by about 30 K),
- the ArduPilot `BATT_*` current limits, and
- motor current limiting in the ESC.
