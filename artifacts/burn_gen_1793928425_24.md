## 2. Loss Model (Per ESC Channel, Per Motor)

### 2.1 Conduction Losses

In six-step operation, at any instant two phases conduct:

- **PWM leg:** the high side conducts for fraction D. The synchronous low side of the same leg conducts for (1−D).
- **Return leg:** the low side conducts 100% of the 120° sector.

The PWM-leg contributions sum to unity regardless of D, so the conduction loss is duty-independent:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{eff}\left[D + (1-D)\right] + I^2 R_{eff} = 2\,I^2 R_{eff}
$$

$$
R_{eff} = k_T R_{ds(on),25} + R_{pcb} = 1.55(0.95) + 0.20 = 1.67\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

$$
P_{cond} = 2(80)^2(1.67\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{21.4\ W}
$$

(Square-wave current is assumed, so I_rms = I_pk within the conduction sector. Ripple adds about 1–3%, which is ignored.)

### 2.2 Hard-Switching Losses (One Hard-Switched FET per Period)

Gate currents during the Miller plateau:

$$
I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{drv,src}+R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}} = \frac{7.0}{8.2} = 0.854\ \text{A}
$$

$$
I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{drv,snk}+R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}} = \frac{3.0}{5.2} = 0.577\ \text{A}
