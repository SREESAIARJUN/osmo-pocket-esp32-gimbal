# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal, and DShot Design Model (Job #46)

## Scope and assumptions

Three points constrain the rest of this model.

1. **The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC.**
   - The H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600.
   - The 48 kHz PWM comes from the ESC firmware (AM32 or BLHeli_32), which runs on a separate ESC MCU per channel, assumed here to be an AT32F421-class part.
   - The architecture below follows that split.
2. **"Nano" and "80 A burst × 4" conflict physically.**
   - The math below shows a 30×30 mm board survives roughly 3–4 s of all-motor 80 A.
   - A 20×20 mm board cannot handle 80 A on all four motors simultaneously.
