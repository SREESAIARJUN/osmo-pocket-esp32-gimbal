The cascade is rate-separated by at least ~4–5× per stage:

```
θ_ref ─►[Angle P + ω_ff]─► ω_ref ─►[Velocity PI]─► i_q,ref ─►(+ i_q,cog)─►[Current PI d/q]─► SVPWM
          1 kHz                       4 kHz            ▲          20 kHz
                                                       │
                                            Anti-cogging FF (θ_m)
Feedback:  θ from IMU attitude (inertial) ; ω from fused, notch-filtered gyro ; i from shunts ; θ_e from encoder
```

In a stabilizing gimbal, the **velocity and angle loops close on inertial (IMU) feedback** projected onto each joint axis. The **encoder is used for commutation (θₑ) and for anti-cogging indexing (θₘ)**.

## 1. FOC Current Loop (Inner, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke (amplitude-invariant, 3 shunts)**

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park**, with θₑ = p·θₘ − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant and decoupling

For a surface PMSM (L_d ≈ L_q = L):

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

The controller adds cross-coupling and back-EMF feedforward so each axis reduces to $L\dot i + R i = v'$:

$$
v_d = v_d' - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = v_q' + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

### 1.3 PI gains by pole–zero cancellation

$$
C(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s},\qquad K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c
$$

The PI zero at $K_i/K_p = R/L$ cancels the electrical pole, giving a closed loop of $\omega_c/(s+\omega_c)$.

Choose $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/20$. For 20 kHz, f_c ≈ 1 kHz, so ω_c ≈ 6283 rad/s.

The limit leaves margin for the ~1.5 sample computation and PWM delay. The phase lag at crossover is $\omega_c \cdot 1.5T_s \approx 27°$.

### 1.4 Voltage limiting and anti-windup

Apply a circular limit $\sqrt{v_d^2+v_q^2} \le V_{dc}/\sqrt3$ with d-axis priority.

Use back-calculation anti-windup:

$$
x_I[k+1] = x_I[k] + T_s\left(K_i e[k] + K_{aw}(v_{sat}-v_{unsat})\right),\qquad K_{aw} = \frac{K_i}{K_p}
$$
