## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation structure

In 6-step (trapezoidal) drive with complementary/synchronous PWM:

- At any instant, motor current flows through **exactly two FETs in series**: one in the PWM-ing leg (HS during D, LS synchronous rectifier during 1–D) and the LS of the return leg (on for the full 60° sector).
- Each FET position is active for 1/3 of the electrical cycle.

So the per-motor conduction loss is independent of D, to first order:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]$$

The dead-time fraction (2·t_dead·f_sw ≈ 1.9 %) is carried by the body diode and is accounted for in Section 2.

### 1.2 Thermal-electrical coupling (fixed point)

R_ds depends on T_j, and T_j depends on loss. Solve iteratively:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + P_{FET}(T_j)\cdot Z_{\theta}(t)$$

For a burst analysis, evaluate at the end-of-burst junction temperature. Take **T_j = 125 °C** as the design corner:

$$R_{ds}(125) = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.005\times100) = 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{23.0\ W}$$
