## 0. Architecture Clarification (Read First)

An STM32H743 running ArduPilot does **not** commutate BLDC motors. ArduPilot has no ESC firmware. A "4-in-1 ESC nano FC" is therefore a two-domain system:

| Domain | Processor | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot | Attitude control. Emits DShot600 and receives bidirectional eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC (×4) | 4× AT32F421 / STM32G071-class (or 1× quad-core ESC MCU) | AM32 or BLHeli_32-class | Six-step commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, current sensing |

The two domains connect only through 4 DShot lines, GND, VBAT sense, current sense, and an optional ESC telemetry UART. This model treats the ESC power stage as the thermal-critical subsystem.

**Electrical baseline assumptions**

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (worst case, 6S full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst current per motor | I_ph | 80 A (phase, low-ripple approximation) |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET class ⚠ | — | 40 V N-ch, DFN3.3×3.3, R_DS(on),25 = 1.5 mΩ @ V_GS = 10 V |
| R_DS(on) temperature coefficient | α | ≈ 0.6 %/K → ×1.6 at 125 °C |
| Q_gs2 + Q_gd ⚠ | Q_sw | 7 nC |
| Q_g total ⚠ | Q_g | 25 nC |
| Q_rr ⚠ | Q_rr | 30 nC |
| Q_oss ⚠ | Q_oss | 15 nC |
| Body-diode V_f @ 80 A | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Dead time per edge | t_dt | 150 ns |
| Gate drive | V_drv | 10 V; I_g,on ≈ 0.5 A, I_g,off ≈ 1.0 A (effective, at the plateau) |
