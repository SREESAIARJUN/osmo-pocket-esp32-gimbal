## 0. Scope, Architecture and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 does not commutate motors directly. Every production 4-in-1 design delegates commutation to dedicated ESC MCUs. The architecture is therefore:

```
 6S LiPo ──► Power Stage (4 × 3-phase bridges, 24 FETs)
               ▲ gate drive
          4 × FD6288Q-class 3-phase gate drivers
               ▲ PWM (48 kHz, complementary, dead-time)
          4 × AT32F421 / STM32G071 ESC MCUs (AM32 firmware)
               ▲ bi-directional DShot600 (1 wire per motor)
          STM32H743VIT6 @ 480 MHz (ArduPilot) — FC
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation Topology

AM32 uses six-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. Over one electrical period, each phase spends:

- 120° as the **high leg**: the HS FET conducts for duty D, and the LS FET synchronously rectifies for (1−D).
- 120° as the **low leg**: the LS FET is fully on.
- 120° **floating**, used for BEMF zero-cross sensing.

### 1.2 RMS Current per FET

Phase current is a quasi-flat ±I for 2/3 of the period (ripple is neglected here and treated in §1.4).

**High-side FET:**
$$I_{HS,rms}^2 = \frac{D}{3} I^2$$

**Low-side FET:**
$$I_{LS,rms}^2 = \frac{1}{3}I^2 + \frac{(1-D)}{3} I^2 = \frac{(2-D)}{3}I^2$$

**Conduction loss per bridge:**
$$P_{cond,bridge} = 3\left[I_{HS,rms}^2 + I_{LS,rms}^2\right] R_{ds}(T_j) = 2 I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

The sum is independent of D: at every instant exactly two channels carry I.

### 1.3 Temperature-Dependent R_ds(on): Self-Consistent Solution

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + k(T_j - 25)\right], \quad k \approx 0.006\ /°C$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_ds = 1.0 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ**.

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.45\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{18.6\ W}$$

Hottest single FET (LS at D → 1):
$$P_{LS} = \frac{1}{3}(6400)(1.45\text{m}) = 3.09\text{ W}$$

At D = 0.5, the worst LS FET rises to 1.5/3 × 6400 × 1.45 m = **4.64 W**, while its HS partner drops to 1.55 W. **Low-side FETs dominate at partial throttle and high current.** This case occurs in hard punch-outs from mid-throttle.

### 1.4 Ripple and Copper Corrections

**Current ripple.** Assume L_phase ≈ 15 µH (typical 2207 motor, line-to-line):
$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus} D(1-D)}{L f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{15\mu \times 48k} = 8.75\text{ A}$$
$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} \Rightarrow +0.1\% \text{ (negligible)}$$

**Copper loss.** Assume 2 oz outer + 2 oz inner bus planes, with an effective path of ≈ 0.6 mΩ per conducting loop:
$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R = 6400 \times 0.6\text{m} = 3.8\text{ W per motor}$$

This heat is distributed in the board rather than the junction. It is not negligible.
