## 0. Feasibility Findings

Two findings shape everything below.

1. **The H743 cannot commutate the motors under ArduPilot.** ArduPilot outputs DShot. It does not run sensorless six-step or FOC for four motors. The only viable architecture is:
   - an H743 flight controller,
   - 4× dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32),
   - 4× 3-phase gate drivers,
   - 24 MOSFETs.

   The netlist in Section 4 reflects this.

2. **80 A is a burst rating, not a continuous one, on a nano form factor.**
   - Section 2.6 shows a 20×20 mm board can shed only about 5–8 W continuously, which is roughly 12–15 A per motor.
   - 80 A per channel produces about 100 W of total loss.
   - The board's thermal mass limits this to a burst of about 1–2 s.
   - If marketing needs "80 A continuous", the board must grow to at least 30.5×30.5 mm with a heatsink. That is a requirements conflict to escalate, not to engineer around.

**Modulation scheme.** The model assumes six-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In each sector:
- one phase is the switching leg: high side on for D, low side on for 1−D;
- one low side is statically on;
- the third phase floats for BEMF sensing.

## 2. Thermal / Loss Model

### 2.1 Conduction loss

R_DS(on) at temperature:

  R_DS(Tj) = R_DS,25 · [1 + α(Tj − 25)], with α ≈ 0.0059/K, so R_DS(110 °C) ≈ 1.5 mΩ

Current paths in any sector:
- **Switching leg:** the high side conducts for D and the synchronous low side for 1−D. Their sum is one full R_DS for the whole period.
- **Static low side:** one R_DS, 100% of the time.

Per channel:

  P_cond = I² · R_DS(Tj) · [D + (1−D) + 1] = 2 · I² · R_DS(Tj)

  P_cond = 2 · 80² · 1.5 mΩ = **19.2 W per channel**

At 48 kHz the inductor ripple is about 5–10% of I, so the RMS correction (1 + r²/12) is below 0.1% and can be ignored.

### 2.2 Copper loss

  P_Cu = I² · R_Cu · 2 = 80² · 0.5 mΩ · 2 = **6.4 W per channel**

This counts the in and out paths. It is the term most often forgotten. On a nano board, copper resistance is comparable to the MOSFET R_DS(on).
