## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

In each 60° commutation sector, one phase is high-side PWM and one phase is low-side ON.
- During the ON-time, current flows through HS(A) and LS(B).
- During the OFF-time, current flows through LS(A) (synchronous rectifier) and LS(B).

So at every instant, current I flows through exactly two channel resistances, independent of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence of R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{ds(on),25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.004\text{–}0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C with α = 0.005: R = 1.0 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Ripple correction

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - E_{bemf})\,D}{2L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}$$

**Example:** L_ph = 10 µH line-to-line effective, D = 0.8, back-EMF = 18 V.
- ΔI ≈ (7.2 × 0.8)/(20e-6 × 48e3) ≈ 6 A.
- The correction term is 3 A², which is negligible against 6400 A².

### 1.4 Result

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W\ per\ motor}$$

Thermal runaway check: R_ds rises with T_j, so solve iteratively. Stability requires ∂P/∂T_j × R_th < 1, i.e. 19.2 × 0.005/1.5 × R_th,j-a < 1. This holds for any realistic R_th.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Transition times

$$I_g = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{plateau}}{R_{g,tot}} = \frac{10 - 4}{5} = 1.2\ \text{A}$$

$$t_r \approx t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd} + Q_{gs2}}{I_g} \approx \frac{8 + 10\,\text{nC}}{1.2\,\text{A}} \approx 15\ \text{ns}$$

### 2.2 Loss components (per motor; only the PWM'd leg switches hard)

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f = 0.5·25.2·80·30e-9·48e3 | **1.45 W** |
| Dead-time body-diode | V_f·I·2·t_dead·f = 0.8·80·400e-9·48e3 | **1.23 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f = 50e-9·25.2·48e3 | 0.06 W |
| Output capacitance | Q_oss·V·f = 40e-9·25.2·48e3 | 0.05 W |
| Gate drive (driver dissipation) | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f = 2·60e-9·10·48e3 | 0.06 W |
| **Subtotal switching** | | **≈ 2.85 W** |

Dead time is the second-largest loss at 80 A. If the ESC firmware allows it, reducing t_dead to 100 ns saves about 0.6 W per motor.

### 2.3 Total ESC dissipation at 80 A burst

| Item | Per motor | 4 motors |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| Switching | 2.85 W | 11.4 W |
| Shunt (0.5 mΩ, if per-phase/low-side) | ~3.2 W | ~12.8 W* |
| PCB copper (bus + phase traces, ~0.3 mΩ) | ~1.9 W | ~7.7 W |
| **Total** | **~27 W** | **~109 W** (all four at 80 A) |

\*Many 4-in-1 boards use a single bus shunt instead. Adjust accordingly.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Network

```
Tj ──Rth,jc(≈1.5 K/W)── Tcase ──Rth,c-pcb(≈2 K/W via vias)── Tboard ──Rth,b-a(≈4 K/W)── Tamb
                                                      │
                                               C_board ≈ 6 J/K
```

### 3.2 Board heat capacity

- FR-4: 1.44 cm³ × 1.85 g/cm³ × 1.1 J/gK ≈ 2.9 J/K
- Copper (6 × 70 µm × 9.3 cm²): 3.4 g × 0.385 J/gK ≈ 1.3 J/K
- Components and solder: ≈ 1.5–2 J/K
- **Total C ≈ 6 J/K**
