## 0. Architectural Clarification (Important)

In a 4-in-1 stack, the **STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz motor PWM**. The partitioning is:

| Function | Device | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Flight control, DShot600 frames, eRPM decode | STM32H743 (ArduPilot) | 4× bidirectional DShot600 |
| Commutation, 48 kHz PWM, dead-time | 4× ESC MCU (e.g. AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 or BLHeli_32) | Gate-driver inputs |
| Gate drive | 4× 3-phase driver (e.g. FD6288Q class) | 24 gate nets |
| Power stage | 24× 40 V N-MOSFETs (6 per motor) | Motor phases |

All switching-loss math therefore depends on the ESC MCU's PWM settings. The H743 only governs command and telemetry timing.

**Feasibility flag.** "80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 or 30.5×30.5 mm) board is only physically defensible as a **per-channel, short-duration (≈1–5 s) rating**. As shown in §3, four channels at 80 A simultaneously would dissipate about 80 W into roughly 7 J/K of board. Specify the rating as *"80 A per channel, 5 s, T_board,start ≤ 50 °C"*, or reduce the claim.

## 2. Loss Model (Per Motor Bridge)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

**Temperature-dependent on-resistance:**

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 0.80 × 1.60 = **1.28 mΩ**.

**Current paths.** In 6-step operation with synchronous rectification, two phases are active at any instant:

- **PWM phase:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D, and its complementary low-side FET conducts for 1 − D. Current flows through exactly one of the two at all times.
- **Return phase:** the low-side FET is on continuously.

This gives:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS} = 2I^2R_{DS}$$

The result is independent of D, which is a useful property of synchronous 6-step drive.

$$P_{cond} = 2(80)^2(1.28\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{16.4\ W}$$

**Copper loss.** Assume a phase-path PCB plus solder resistance of R_cu ≈ 0.30 mΩ (heavy copper, multiple parallel layers):

$$P_{cu} = I^2 R_{cu} = 6400 \times 0.3\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{1.9\ W}$$
