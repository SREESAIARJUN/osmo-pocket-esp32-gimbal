# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Nano 20×20): Power-Stage Model, Thermal Budget, and Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation

## 0. Critical Findings

Three issues change the design and should be resolved before layout.

1. **Partitioning.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates DShot. It does not run the 48 kHz commutation PWM. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. This package uses one AT32F421 or STM32G071 per motor. The 48 kHz switching analysis applies to those MCUs.
2. **The 80 A rating is thermally unachievable as a continuous or all-motor rating on 20×20.** The model in §3 shows:
   - All four channels at 80 A: about **1.3 s** to reach the board thermal limit
