## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms
Phase currents $i_a, i_b$ give $i_c = -(i_a+i_b)$.

**Clarke** (amplitude-invariant):
$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park**, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}$:
$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI design
The dq voltage equations are:
$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Cancel the cross-coupling and back-EMF terms with feedforward. The remaining plant is $G(s)=1/(Ls+R)$. Pole-zero cancellation for a target bandwidth $\omega_c$ gives:
$$
K_p^{i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i^{i} = R\,\omega_c \quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{I}{I^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

The decoupled voltage commands are:
$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$
