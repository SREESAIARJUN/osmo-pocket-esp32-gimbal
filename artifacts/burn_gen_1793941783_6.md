## 1. System Architecture

```
            ┌──────────── 1 kHz ────────────┐ ┌──── 4 kHz ─────┐ ┌──────── 20 kHz ─────────┐
q_ref ──►(+)─► Attitude P(+FF) ─► ω_cam_ref ─► J⁻¹(θ) ─► θ̇_ref ─►(+)─► Vel PI ─► i_q* ─►(+)─► Current PI ─► SVPWM ─► Motor
          ▲                                        (+)◄─ i_cog(θ_m)     ▲               ▲   (dq, decoupled)
          │ q_cam                       θ̇_joint ◄── J⁻¹(θ)·ω_gyro ──────┘     i_dq ◄── Park(Clarke(i_abc), θ_e)
   ┌──────┴──────── Dual ICM-42688-P ── notch bank ─► PT2 LPF ─► inverse-variance fusion ─► Mahony AHRS
```

**Key design choice.** The velocity loop is closed on the **gyro-derived joint rate**, not on the differentiated encoder.

- The gyro gives disturbance-referenced inertial rate. This is what actually stabilizes the camera.
- The encoder is used only for commutation (θ_e), anti-cogging indexing, the Jacobian, and soft limits.

## 2. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 2.1 Transforms

Electrical angle with latency compensation. The PWM update applies 1.5 samples after the sample instant (ZOH + compute):

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s$$

Clarke (amplitude-invariant):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 2.2 Plant and decoupled PI

The dq model is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

With cross-coupling and back-EMF feedforward, each axis becomes $G(s) = 1/(Ls+R)$.

Pole-zero cancellation with target closed-loop bandwidth $\omega_{cc}$ gives:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_{cc},\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_{cc}\quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_{cc}}{s+\omega_{cc}}$$

Choose $\omega_{cc} \le 2\pi f_s/20$. At 20 kHz this is $\omega_{cc} \approx 2\pi\cdot 1000$ rad/s.

Discrete form (backward Euler integrator, back-calculation anti-windup):

$$
\begin{aligned}
e_k &= i^*_k - i_k \\
u_k &= K_p e_k + I_k + v_{ff,k} \\
\bar u_k &= \text{sat}(u_k) \\
I_{k+1} &= I_k + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw}(\bar u_k - u_k), \qquad K_{aw} = K_i/K_p
\end{aligned}
$$

The feedforward terms are:

$$v_{ff,d} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_{ff,q} = \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

**Voltage vector limit (circular).** $|v_{dq}| \le V_{dc}/\sqrt3 \cdot m_{max}$ with $m_{max} = 0.95$. The d-axis has priority:

$$v_q^{max} = \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}$$

### 2.3 SVPWM (min-max injection)

Inverse Park gives $v_\alpha, v_\beta$. Then:

$$v_a = v_\alpha,\quad v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta$$

$$v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max + \min),\qquad d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{dc}}$$
