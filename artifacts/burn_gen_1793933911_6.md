## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
In 6-step drive, current always flows through two phases. At any instant, two switch positions carry the full phase current I:

- **Active (on) interval, duration D·T_sw:** HS of phase A, then motor, then LS of phase B.
- **Freewheel interval, duration (1−D)·T_sw:** LS of phase A (synchronous rectification), then motor, then LS of phase B.

So, excluding dead time, exactly **two FET channels conduct I** at all times:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]$$
