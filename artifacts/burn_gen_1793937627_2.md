## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology

During any 60° commutation sector, two half-bridge legs carry current:

- **PWM leg:** current alternates between the high-side FET (duty D) and the synchronous low-side FET (1−D).
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET conducts continuously.

So at any instant, current flows through **exactly two R_ds(on)** in series, regardless of D.

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

### 1.2 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per motor | All 4 motors |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.50 mΩ | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| 100 °C | 2.06 mΩ | 26.4 W | 105.6 W |
| 125 °C | 2.25 mΩ | **28.8 W** | **115.2 W** |

### 1.3 Per-FET Distribution

Each FET conducts for 120° of the electrical cycle:

- **Return-leg low side:** I²R for the whole 120° window.
- **PWM-leg pair:** high side gets D·I²R, low side gets (1−D)·I²R.

At full throttle (D → 1), the six FETs average:

$$
\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,motor}}{6} = 4.8\ \text{W @ 125 °C}
$$

Peak instantaneous dissipation while conducting is I²R = 14.4 W. The junction RC time constant (~1–5 ms) only smooths this at moderate-to-high eRPM. At low eRPM under high current (stall, prop strike), use the peak value instead.

### 1.4 Electro-Thermal Feedback and Runaway Criterion

$$
P(T_j)=P_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\qquad T_j = T_a + R_{th}\,P(T_j)
$$

$$
\Rightarrow\ T_j = \frac{T_a + R_{th}P_{25}(1-25\alpha)}{1-\alpha R_{th}P_{25}}
$$

The system is stable only if **α·R_th·P₂₅ < 1**. With per-FET P₂₅ = 3.2 W and α = 0.005, runaway occurs at R_th > 62 K/W. That is not a risk for soldered FETs, but it becomes relevant if a FET delaminates or a joint voids.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. Per motor:

| Loss term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | ½·25.2·80·30 ns·48 k = **1.45 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw | 0.8·80·200 ns·48 k = **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 40 nC·25.2·48 k = **0.05 W** |
| C_oss charge | ½·Q_oss·V·f_sw | **0.02 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs, mostly in driver/R_g) | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f_sw | **0.03 W** |
| **Total P_sw per motor** | | **≈ 2.16 W** |

**Edge-time sanity check.** Miller plateau time is t ≈ Q_gd / I_g. For an FD6288-class driver plus R_g, I_g ≈ (10 − 3.5 V)/(R_drv + R_g) ≈ 0.5 A, giving t ≈ 16 ns. That is consistent with the 15 ns assumption.
