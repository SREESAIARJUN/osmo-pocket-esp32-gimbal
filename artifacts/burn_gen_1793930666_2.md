# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #16): Architecture, Loss Model, Thermal Budget, BDShot DMA Plan

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. In any ArduPilot bidirectional DShot system:

- **FC MCU (STM32H743):** runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 frames. It receives eRPM telemetry as GCR-encoded replies on the same wire.
- **ESC MCUs (×4, or ×1 for a quad-driver design):** for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32. These generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM, dead time, and commutation.

The DShot/DMA section therefore applies to the H743. The loss and thermal sections apply to the ESC power stage.

### 1.1 Current path in six-step / synchronous-rectified trapezoidal drive

- **PWM leg:** the high side conducts for D·T_sw. The complementary low side conducts for (1−D)·T_sw, minus dead time.
- **Return leg:** the low side is on for the whole sector.
