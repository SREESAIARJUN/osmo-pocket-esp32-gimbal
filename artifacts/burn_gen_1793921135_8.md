## 1. Loop Architecture and Timing

```
             1 kHz                  8 kHz                    20 kHz
 θ_ref ──► [Angle P + FF] ─ω_ref─► [Rate PI + FF] ─i_q*─► [Current PI dq] ─► SVPWM ─► Motor
             ▲                       ▲         ▲                ▲
             │ q̂ (Mahony)           │ ω̂_cam   │ i_cog(θ_m)     │ i_dq (Park)
             │                       │         │                │
     [Attitude filter] ◄── [Dual-IMU fusion + notch/LPF] ◄── 2×ICM-42688-P @ 8 kHz
                                               ▲
                                       [Anti-cogging LMS] ◄── encoder θ_m
```

**Bandwidth separation.** Each loop runs at about 5–10× the bandwidth of the loop outside it:

| Loop | Sample rate f_s | Target crossover f_c | Phase-margin target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (d,q) | 20 kHz | 1.5–2 kHz | ≥ 60° |
| Rate (gyro) | 8 kHz | 80–150 Hz (limited by the first structural mode) | ≥ 45° |
| Angle | 1 kHz | 10–20 Hz | ≥ 60° |

**Inner-loop feedback source.** The rate loop is closed on the **inertial gyro rate** of the camera stage, not on encoder speed. This is what rejects base motion. The encoders are used only for commutation, cogging compensation, and kinematics.

## 2. Motor Model and FOC Current Loop

### 2.1 PMSM model in the rotor (dq) frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d \frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L_q \frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2} p\,[\lambda_m i_q + (L_d - L_q) i_d i_q] \;\xrightarrow{L_d\approx L_q}\; \tau_e = K_t i_q,\quad K_t = \tfrac{3}{2}p\lambda_m
$$

Here $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}$ and $\omega_e = p\,\omega_m$.

### 2.2 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 2.3 Current PI controller with decoupling

The plant per axis, after decoupling, is $G_i(s) = \dfrac{1}{Ls + R}$.

Use pole–zero cancellation with crossover $\omega_{ci}$:

$$
C_i(s) = K_{p,i} + \frac{K_{i,i}}{s},\qquad K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}
$$

This gives the open loop $L(s) = \omega_{ci}/s$, so the closed loop is first order with bandwidth $\omega_{ci}$.

**Delay limit.** Computation plus PWM zero-order hold adds about $1.5\,T_s$ of delay. The phase margin is then

$$
\text{PM} = 90^\circ - \omega_{ci}\cdot 1.5T_s\cdot\tfrac{180}{\pi}
$$

Choosing PM ≥ 60° requires $\omega_{ci} \le \pi/(9T_s)$. At 20 kHz this gives $f_{ci} \lesssim 1.1$ kHz. A practical choice is $f_{ci} = 1$ kHz.

**Decoupling feedforward:**

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$
