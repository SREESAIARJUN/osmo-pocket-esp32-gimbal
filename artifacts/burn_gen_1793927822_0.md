## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step with synchronous PWM

In any commutation sector, two phases are active and one floats. With complementary PWM, current always flows through exactly **two FETs**:

- **During t_on:** HS(phase A) → motor → LS(phase B).
- **During t_off:** LS(phase A), synchronous freewheel → motor → LS(phase B).

The two-FET path holds regardless of duty cycle. Dead time is the only exception, and it is handled in §2.4.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{DS}(125) = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 100) = 1.74\,\text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

For trapezoidal drive, I_ph,RMS ≈ I_DC during the active sector. Ripple adds a small correction:

$$I_{RMS}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}$$

Typical 5" motor (L_ph ≈ 15 µH line-to-line), D = 0.85, V_bemf ≈ 19 V:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(25.2 - 19)(0.85)}{15\,\mu\text{H} \times 48\,\text{kHz}} \approx 7.3\,\text{A} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{correction} = 4.4\,\text{A}^2 \;(\text{negligible against } 6400\,\text{A}^2)$$

At 80 A:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.74\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{22.3\ W}$$

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution

Over one electrical revolution, each phase leg is HS-active for 1/3, LS-active for 1/3, and floating for 1/3.

- **PWM'd HS FET:** P_HS = I²R × D × (1/3)
- **Its complementary LS (freewheel):** P_LS,fw = I²R × (1−D) × (1/3)
- **Return-leg LS (fully on):** P_LS,ret = I²R × (1/3)

Each FET's average loss is therefore about P_cond,ch / 6 ≈ **3.7 W**. The instantaneous loss is I²R = **11.1 W** while conducting, and the transient thermal response uses that instantaneous value (§5.3).

### 1.5 Copper and shunt losses (often ignored, should not be)

**Phase and bus copper:** 2 oz, about 10 mm effective path, 3 mm wide, 2 parallel layers gives roughly 0.4 mΩ.

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.4\times10^{-3} \times 1.3\,(\text{temperature}) = 3.3\,\text{W per channel}$$

**Current shunt:** 0.25 mΩ single shunt on the aggregate low-side return.

$$P = 6400 \times 0.25\times10^{-3} = 1.6\,\text{W}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only **one leg** hard-switches per sector (the PWM'd HS and its sync LS).

### 2.1 Gate drive capability (FD6288Q)

| | Value |
|---|---|
| Source current | I_src ≈ 0.25 A |
| Sink current | I_sink ≈ 0.5 A |

These currents are weak, which is why external R_G is kept small (see the netlist).

The Miller-plateau transition time is approximately t ≈ Q_gd / I_G:

| | Formula | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Rise | t_r ≈ 8 nC / 0.25 A | 32 ns |
| Fall | t_f ≈ 8 nC / 0.5 A | 16 ns |

### 2.2 V–I overlap loss

$$E_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I \,(t_r + t_f) = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 48\,\text{ns} = 48.4\,\mu\text{J}$$

$$P_{sw} = E_{sw} f_{sw} = 48.4\,\mu\text{J} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{2.32\ W}$$

### 2.3 Capacitive and reverse-recovery losses

| Loss | Formula | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Output capacitance | P_oss ≈ ½ · Q_oss · V_bus · f_sw = 0.5 × 25 nC × 25.2 × 48k | 0.015 W |
| Reverse recovery | P_rr = Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw = 30 nC × 25.2 × 48k | 0.036 W |

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

AM32 dead time is t_dt ≈ 100 ns, with two transitions per period.

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I \cdot 2\, t_{dt}\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate charge loss (dissipated in driver and R_G, not in the FET)

$$P_g = 2\, Q_g\, V_{GS}\, f_{sw} = 2 \times 40\,\text{nC} \times 10 \times 48\,\text{kHz} = 0.038\,\text{W}$$

### 2.6 Per-channel switching total

$$P_{sw,ch} = 2.32 + 0.015 + 0.036 + 0.61 \approx \mathbf{3.0\ W}$$
