# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Loss, Thermal, and Bidirectional DShot600 Architecture

## 0. Assumptions and Corrections

I don't have a specification for "Job #58," so every number below comes from stated assumptions. Replace them with your own design inputs, and the equations carry through unchanged.

**Two architectural points to settle first:**

1. **The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC MCU.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 and generates DShot. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071. ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The netlists below follow this two-tier structure.
2. **80 A burst on a "nano" (20×20 mm) board is not physically credible.** The board cannot carry it in copper, FETs, or thermal mass. The calculations below assume a 30×30 mm, 6-layer, 2 oz board. Even then, the result in §3 shows 80 A is a seconds-scale burst rating, not a continuous one.
