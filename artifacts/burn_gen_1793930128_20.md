# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC Stack: Power-Stage Thermal Model, 48 kHz Switching Model, and ArduPilot Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation

**Job #15, Architecture Package Rev A**

## 0. Architectural Clarifications and Feasibility Flags

These must be resolved before layout.

| # | Issue | Resolution adopted |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The STM32H743 **does not** commutate the motors. A 4-in-1 ESC needs its own motor MCUs (one per motor), running AM32 or BLHeli_32. | 4× AT32F421 (or STM32G071) on the ESC board, running AM32 at 48 kHz PWM. The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to them. |
| 2 | "Nano" usually means a 20×20 mm stack. Twenty-four FETs that can carry 80 A each will not fit on 20×20 mm with adequate copper. | Bounded analysis on a **30.5×30.5 mm ESC** with the H743 FC on 20×20 or 30.5. A 20×20 variant is derated to ~45 A burst (see §3.7). |
| 3 | "80 A burst" is interpreted as **per motor, ≤10 s**. All four motors at 80 A simultaneously means 320 A from the battery, which is not a realistic flight condition. | Two load cases are analysed: **Case A** (all 4 motors at 80 A, worst case) and **Case B** (1 motor at 80 A, 3 at 30 A, realistic yaw/punch-out). |
