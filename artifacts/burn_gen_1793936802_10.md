## 0. Feasibility Findings and Design Assumptions

Several items in the requirement need to be stated precisely before the analysis.

| # | Issue | Resolution |
|---|-------|-----------|
| F1 | **The STM32H743 cannot commutate the motors itself in a usable way.** A 4-in-1 ESC needs four independent commutation loops at 48 kHz PWM, which means 4 × 3 complementary PWM pairs plus comparators. | Use an H743 flight controller with **four dedicated ESC MCUs** (AT32F421K8U7 running AM32, or EFM8BB21 running BLHeli_S). The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to them. This is industry-standard practice. |
| F2 | **"80A burst" must be defined per channel and in time.** 4 × 80 A = 320 A of battery current. No 20×20 or 30.5×30.5 board, XT60/XT90 connector, or flight pack sustains this for long. | Spec is defined as **80 A per channel for ≤ 5 s**, with any combination of channels. The 4-channel simultaneous case is analyzed as the worst case, and a burst-duration limit is derived in §3.5. |
