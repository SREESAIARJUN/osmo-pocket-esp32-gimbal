## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path analysis

In trapezoidal six-step drive with synchronous rectification, phase A is PWM'd and phase B is the low-side return:

- **PWM ON** (duty D): current flows V+ → HS_A → motor → LS_B → GND. Path resistance is 2·R_DS.
- **PWM OFF** (1−D): current freewheels LS_A (sync rect) → motor → LS_B. Path resistance is 2·R_DS.

Conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

with

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Copper loss

Phase and bus traces use 2 oz copper on 4 paralleled layers, about 6 mm wide and 8 mm long:

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho L}{w\,t\,n} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\cdot 0.008}{0.006\cdot70\times10^{-6}\cdot4} \approx 0.082\text{ mΩ}$$

Allowing for vias and terminations, R_Cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ per path.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_DS | P_FET,cond = I²·2R_DS | P_Cu = I²·0.3 mΩ | **Total per channel** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 12.8 W | 1.9 W | 14.7 W |
| 125 °C | 1.5 mΩ | 19.2 W | 1.9 W | **21.1 W** |

Per-FET distribution: the PWM'd leg splits its loss D : (1−D) between HS and LS, while the return LS carries the full I²R. The worst single FET (the return LS) dissipates 80²×1.5 mΩ = **9.6 W** during its 1/3 electrical-cycle conduction window. Its rotation-averaged value is about 3.2 W. This matters for junction ripple at low eRPM.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg hard-switches. The HS turns on into the LS body diode (hard switching), and the LS commutates near-ZVS after dead time.

### 2.1 Transition times (FD6288Q, R_g = 4.7 Ω)

$$t_{r} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g,src}} \approx \frac{10\,nC}{\sim0.7\,A} \approx 15\text{ ns}, \quad t_f \approx 12\text{ ns}$$

### 2.2 Loss components

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = ½·25.2·80·27 ns·48 kHz | **1.31 W** |
| Dead-time diode (2 × 100 ns) | V_SD·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·200 ns·48 kHz | **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | V·Q_rr·f_sw = 25.2·50 nC·48 kHz | 0.06 W |
| Output capacitance | ½·V·Q_oss·f_sw | 0.02 W |
| Gate drive (dissipated in driver/R_g) | 2·Q_g·V_gs·f_sw = 2·50 nC·10 V·48 kHz | 0.05 W |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 2.05 W/channel** |

### 2.3 Total per channel and per board

$$P_{ch} = 21.1 + 2.05 \approx 23.2\text{ W} \qquad P_{4ch} \approx 93\text{ W}$$

Add roughly 2 W for the bulk-capacitor ESR and the logic regulators, giving **P_total ≈ 95 W** at a simultaneous 80 A burst on all four motors.

### 2.4 Effect of switching frequency

The switching term scales linearly with frequency:

| Frequency | Switching loss per channel | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 24 kHz | ≈ 1.0 W | More audible noise and current ripple |
| 96 kHz | ≈ 4.1 W | |

48 kHz is a reasonable trade-off, and conduction loss dominates at about 90 % of the total.

### 2.5 Bulk capacitor ripple

$$I_{C,rms} \approx I\sqrt{D(1-D)} \Rightarrow 40\text{ A}_{rms}\ (\text{at } D=0.5)$$

This requires a 35 V, low-ESR bulk capacitor of at least 1000 µF on the pigtail, plus 4 × 10 µF X7R ceramics per channel at the half-bridge.
