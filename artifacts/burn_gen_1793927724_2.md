# STM32H743 Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Nano Stack), Job #53

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility Flags

**Flag 1: Partitioning.** The H743 should not commutate the motors directly. ArduPilot does not run ESC commutation firmware, and doing 4× sensorless FOC/6-step at 48 kHz on the same core as the EKF is not practical. The architecture is therefore:

- **FC:** STM32H743VIH6 running ArduPilot. It sends bidirectional DShot600 to the ESCs.
- **ESC:** 4× AT32F421K8U7 running AM32, each with an FD6288Q 3-phase gate driver and 6 N-MOSFETs.

**Flag 2: "Nano" plus 80 A.** On a 20×20 mm board, 80 A per channel is a **burst rating of seconds, not a continuous rating**. Section 3 quantifies this. Expect about 3 s at 80 A on all four channels and roughly 25–35 A continuous per channel, depending on airflow. Labelling this board "80A" without a time qualifier would be misleading.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

AM32 uses 6-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At every instant, the phase current flows through exactly **two** conducting channels in series:

- During on-time: HS(PWM leg) + LS(return leg).
- During off-time: LS(PWM leg, synchronous) + LS(return leg).

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 0.90 × 1.5 = **1.35 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j) = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.35\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{17.28\ W}$$

### 1.4 Per-FET breakdown (one commutation step, D = 0.95)

| FET | Expression | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| HS, PWM leg | D·I²R = 0.95 × 6400 × 1.35 m | 8.21 W |
| LS, PWM leg (sync) | (1−D)·I²R | 0.43 W |
| LS, return leg | I²R | 8.64 W |

Each FET conducts for 120° of the electrical cycle. Consider a typical burst speed of 30 k eRPM: the electrical frequency is 500 Hz and each conduction interval is 0.67 ms. This is shorter than the junction-to-board thermal time constant (several ms), so the **electrically averaged loss per FET** governs heating, with modest ripple on top.
