## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle
$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi
$$
$\theta_{\text{off}}$ is found at startup by applying a fixed $V_d$ vector and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant)
$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)\quad(\text{assumes } i_a+i_b+i_c=0)
$$

### 1.3 Park transform
$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.4 PMSM model in the dq frame
$$
\begin{aligned}
v_d &= R_s i_d + L_d\,\dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q\\
v_q &= R_s i_q + L_q\,\dot i_q + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m\\
\tau_e &= \tfrac32 p\left[\lambda_m i_q + (L_d-L_q) i_d i_q\right] \;\approx\; K_t\, i_q,\quad K_t=\tfrac32 p\lambda_m
\end{aligned}
$$

### 1.5 Current PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward
Plant per axis: $G(s)=\dfrac{1}{L s + R}$. Choose the PI zero to cancel the plant pole (pole-zero cancellation):
$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}
$$
This makes the closed loop first-order with bandwidth $\omega_{ci}$. Keep $\omega_{ci} \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/10$.

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_d^* &= \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L_q i_q\\
v_q^* &= \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e (L_d i_d + \lambda_m)
\end{aligned}
$$
$i_d^* = 0$ (surface-mount PMSM, no field weakening required).

### 1.6 Voltage limiting (circular) and anti-windup
$$
V_{max} = \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}\cdot 0.95,\qquad
\text{if } \sqrt{v_d^2+v_q^2} > V_{max}: \text{prioritize } v_d, \; v_q = \operatorname{sgn}(v_q)\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}
$$
Anti-windup uses back-calculation: $\dot x_I = K_i e + K_{aw}(v_{sat}-v_{unsat})$, with $K_{aw}=K_i/K_p$.

### 1.7 Inverse Park and SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)
$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\quad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$
$$
v_a = v_\alpha,\; v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\; v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$
$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max + \min),\qquad d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

### 1.8 Delay compensation
One PWM period of computational delay plus ½ period of PWM delay rotates the applied vector. Use this angle for the inverse Park:
$$
\theta_e^{\text{inv}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_{pwm}
$$

## 2. Cascaded Velocity / Angle Control

### 2.1 Mechanical plant (per axis, after joint decoupling)
$$
J\dot\omega = K_t i_q - B\omega - \tau_{cog}(\theta_m) - \tau_{dist}
$$
With the current loop modeled as $\frac{\omega_{ci}}{s+\omega_{ci}} \approx 1$ at velocity-loop frequencies:
$$
G_\omega(s) = \frac{K_t}{J s + B}
$$

### 2.2 Velocity PI (2-DOF, with acceleration feedforward)
$$
i_q^* = \underbrace{K_{p\omega} e_\omega + K_{i\omega}\!\int e_\omega\,dt}_{\text{feedback}} + \underbrace{\frac{J}{K_t}\dot\omega^*}_{\text{accel FF}} + \underbrace{\frac{\hat\tau_{cog}(\theta_m)}{K_t}}_{\text{anti-cog FF}}
$$
Tuning by the symmetric optimum, for crossover $\omega_{cv}$:
$$
K_{p\omega} = \frac{J\,\omega_{cv}}{K_t},\qquad K_{i\omega} = K_{p\omega}\,\frac{\omega_{cv}}{a^2},\quad a\approx 2\!-\!4
$$
