## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Stated Assumptions

No MOSFET part number, board dimensions, or layer stack were specified for Job #47. All numbers below use the representative parameters in the table. Every equation is written so you can substitute real datasheet values.

**Architectural clarification.** The 48 kHz switching does not run on the H743. In every production AIO design, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four (or one quad-channel) ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Those ESC MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The model treats them as two separate domains connected by four DShot lines.
