## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Conduction topology

In 6-step operation with complementary PWM, phase A is PWM-driven high and phase B is held low:

- **PWM on:** current flows through HS_A and LS_B.
- **PWM off:** current freewheels through LS_A (synchronous) and LS_B.

So exactly two MOSFET channels conduct the phase current at all times, independent of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence of R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.74 mΩ**.
