# Job #30: STM32H743 + Integrated 4-in-1 ESC Nano Stack

**Architecture Specification, Loss Model, DShot Timing, and Reference Netlist**

---

## 0. Architectural Clarifications

Three findings change the design before any math.

1. **The H743 cannot drive the motors directly.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 and sends DShot commands. Each motor bridge needs its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware to handle commutation and back-EMF zero-crossing detection. The architecture is therefore:
   `H743 (ArduPilot)` → `4× bidirectional DShot600` → `4× AT32F421 (AM32)` → `4× FD6288Q gate drivers` → `24× MOSFETs`.

2. **80 A burst on a 20×20 mm board is thermally limited by board heat capacity, not by the MOSFETs.** Section 3 shows that all four channels at 80 A simultaneously can be sustained for only about 1.2 s. The marketing "80 A burst" rating must be defined as one channel, ≤3 s, from a 40 °C start. Otherwise the board needs a 30×30 mm format or a bonded heatsink.

3. **Every parameter below is a stated assumption.** Re-run the model with the actual MOSFET datasheet values before layout freeze.
