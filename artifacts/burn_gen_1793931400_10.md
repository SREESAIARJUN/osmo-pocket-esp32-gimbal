**Architecture note.** In a "4-in-1 ESC" stack, each channel normally has its own small ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32/BLHeli_32. The H743 talks to those MCUs over bidirectional DShot. It does not drive gates directly. This model assumes that topology.

**Feasibility flag.** 80 A per channel × 4 channels on a 20×20 board is thermally a sub-second to ~2 s capability. It is not a sustained rating. Section 1.5 quantifies this. Treat "80 A" as a single-channel, short-burst figure in marketing and in arming/current-limit firmware.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

R_DS(on)(T_j) = R_DS(on),25 · [1 + α·(T_j − 25)], with α ≈ 0.006 /K for 40 V trench MOSFETs.

At T_j = 110 °C: R = 1.0 mΩ × 1.51 ≈ **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Per-device loss in 6-step synchronous operation

Each phase leg conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period (120°). Within that window:
- the high-side (HS) FET conducts for fraction D (duty cycle);
- the low-side (LS) FET conducts for fraction (1−D) as a synchronous rectifier.

The equations are:

- P_HS = I²·R(T_j)·D/3
- P_LS,sync = I²·R(T_j)·(1−D)/3
- P_LS,return = I²·R(T_j)/3 (LS of the return leg, on fully during its 120° window)

At any instant, current flows through exactly 2 FET channels. The total conduction loss per ESC channel is therefore:

**P_cond,ch = 2·I²·R(T_j) + I²·R_cu**

### 1.3 Numerical result at 80 A, T_j = 110 °C

- Silicon: 2 × 80² × 1.5 mΩ = **19.2 W**
- Copper path (assume R_cu = 0.6 mΩ for pads, vias, and planes per current loop): 80² × 0.6 mΩ = **3.8 W**
- **Total conduction per channel: P_cond ≈ 23.0 W**
- Worst single FET (HS at D→1): 80² × 1.5 mΩ / 3 = **3.2 W**

### 1.4 Junction rise above the board for the worst single FET

R_θJ-PCB ≈ R_θJC + R_θ(solder+via array) ≈ 1.5 + 3.5 = 5 K/W

ΔT_j-pcb = 3.2 W × 5 K/W ≈ **16 K**

The junction is therefore not the limiting factor. The board heat capacity is.

### 1.5 Board transient (lumped-mass) model

C_th = Σ m·c:

| Contributor | Calculation | Thermal capacity |
|---|---|---|
| Copper | 6 effective layers × 0.25 g = 1.5 g, × 0.385 J/g·K | 0.58 J/K |
| FR4 | 1.2 g × 1.1 J/g·K | 1.32 J/K |
| FETs, solder, components | | about 0.3 J/K |
| **Total** | | **≈ 2.2 J/K** |

The board temperature rise is:

**ΔT_board(t) = (P_tot · R_θ,amb) · (1 − e^(−t/τ)), with τ = R_θ,amb · C_th**
