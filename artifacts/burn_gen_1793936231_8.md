## 0. Architecture Decisions

Three facts about this request change the architecture.

1. **The H743 cannot also be the ESC controller.**
   - Four sensorless BLDC channels need 12 complementary PWM legs with dead-time, 4×3 BEMF comparators, and per-channel commutation timing. That would exhaust the H743's advanced timers (only TIM1/TIM8 have dead-time complementary outputs).
   - It would also conflict with ArduPilot, which owns the H743.
   - **Decision:** The H743 runs ArduPilot. Each ESC channel gets its own MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class, running AM32, which supports bidirectional DShot and EDT).

2. **"Nano" (20×20 mm) and 80 A burst per channel are thermally incompatible except for very short bursts.**
   - Section 3 shows roughly 1.5 s at 4×80 A on a 20×20 board.
   - **Decision:** Use a stacked layout: a 20×20 FC on top of a 25.5×25.5 or 30.5×30.5
