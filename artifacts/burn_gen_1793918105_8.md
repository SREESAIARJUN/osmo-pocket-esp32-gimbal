## Part 1: FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Coordinate transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_off:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 Plant model

The surface-mount PMSM model is (L_d = L_q = L, flux linkage λ, electrical speed ω_e = p·ω_m):

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda
$$

Torque and mechanical dynamics:

$$
\tau = K_t i_q,\quad K_t = \tfrac32 p\lambda,\qquad J\dot\omega_m = K_t i_q - B\omega_m - \tau_{cog}(\theta_m) - \tau_{dist}
$$
