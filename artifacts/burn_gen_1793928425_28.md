## 0. Architecture Corrections and Stated Assumptions

Three points need to be settled before any math, because they change the design.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no BLDC commutation firmware. The correct architecture is:
   - The **STM32H743** runs ArduPilot as the flight controller.
   - **4× AT32F421K8U7** ESC MCUs run AM32. AM32 supports up to 48 kHz PWM, so the 48 kHz requirement belongs on the ESC side.
   - The H743 talks to the ESC MCUs over **bidirectional DShot600**.
2. **"Nano" and "80 A burst" do not fit together on a 20×20 mm board.** The analysis below shows a 4-channel burst dissipation of about 95 W. A 20×20 board has roughly 1 s of thermal headroom at that level. This design specifies a **30.5×30.5 mm** board, 8-layer, 2 oz copper on the outer and power planes. The 20×20 numbers are shown for comparison.
3. **"80 A" is defined here as RMS motor phase current per channel, burst.** At full throttle (D ≈ 0.9), bus current per channel is about 72 A, so the total pack draw is about 290 A. **XT60/XT90 connectors are not acceptable.** Use direct-soldered 10 AWG leads and a low-impedance external bulk capacitor.
