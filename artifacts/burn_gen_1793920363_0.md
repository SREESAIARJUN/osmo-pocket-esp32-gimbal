# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have the original Job #44 specification, so this design states its assumptions explicitly. Every parameter is a `constexpr` or config field so you can substitute your real values.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 / Cortex-M4F at 170 MHz, single-precision FPU |
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 2–5 mH |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701) |
| Inverter | 3-phase, center-aligned PWM at 20 kHz, 2-shunt or inline current sensing |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on SPI at 24 MHz, gyro ODR 8 kHz, on the camera plate |
| Loop rates | Current 20 kHz, velocity 4 kHz, angle 1 kHz (per axis) |

## 1. Field-Oriented Control: the Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

The amplitude-invariant Clarke transform, assuming balanced currents (i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

The Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_off:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of that matrix.

### 1.2 Plant model in the dq frame

For a surface PMSM (L_d = L_q = L):

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Electromagnetic torque is:

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q \equiv K_t i_q
$$

### 1.3 Current PI with decoupling

Feedforward cancellation of the cross-coupling and back-EMF terms leaves a first-order plant on each axis:

$$
\frac{I(s)}{V(s)}=\frac{1}{Ls+R}
$$

Placing the PI zero on the plant pole (pole-zero cancellation) gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_p^{i} = \omega_c L,\qquad K_i^{i} = \omega_c R
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\frac{I}{I^*}=\frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

Choose ω_c ≤ 2π·f_pwm/10 to stay clear of PWM and computation delay. At 20 kHz this gives ω_c ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz.

The commanded voltages are:

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$
