## 0. Scope and Engineering Caveats

Three constraints shape the rest of this design.

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 and emits bidirectional DShot600. The 4-in-1 ESC needs its own MCU(s) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. This design uses one STM32G071 per motor channel. "4-in-1 nano FC" here means an AIO stack. The H743 has no role in 48 kHz commutation.
2. **80 A per motor is a burst rating only.** The math in §2–§3 shows roughly 95 W of dissipation at 4×80 A. No 20×20 or 30.5×30.5 board can sustain that. It must be treated as a time-limited burst (≈3–5 s, derived below), enforced in firmware.
3. **Component parameters are representative assumptions.** Values are for a 40 V, ~1 mΩ, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFET class. Before release, replace every value in the tables with the selected part's datasheet values at the corresponding temperature.

**Operating point.** 6S (V_bus = 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal), f_sw = 48 kHz, I_burst = 80 A per motor, T_ambient = 40 °C.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Conduction topology
With 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation and synchronous (complementary) PWM, two phases carry the full current I in each 60° sector:

| Interval | Conducting devices |
|---|---|
| PWM ON (D) | HS of PWM leg + LS of return leg |
| PWM OFF (1−D) | LS of PWM leg (sync. rect.) + LS of return leg |

The instantaneous conduction resistance is 2·R_DS(on) regardless of D, apart from dead time (§3.4). This gives:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2 \cdot R_{DS(on)}(T_j)\,\big[D + 1 + (1-D)\big] = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 2.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| R_DS(on),25 @ V_GS = 10 V | 1.0 mΩ (assumed) |
| R_DS(on) @ T_j = 125 °C | 1.0 × (1 + 0.6) = **1.6 mΩ** |
| PCB copper trace/plane, per path | ≈ 0.3 mΩ (2 oz, short pours) |

### 2.3 Numbers at 80 A

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.6\text{ m}\Omega = 20.5\ \text{W per motor}$$

$$P_{cond,Cu} = 80^2 \cdot 0.3\text{ m}\Omega \cdot 2 = 3.8\ \text{W per motor}$$

Averaged over a full electrical cycle, each FET conducts about 1/3 of the time. The per-device average is therefore about 20.5/6 ≈ **3.4 W**. The peak instantaneous value during its conduction sector is about 10 W.

### 2.4 Current-sense shunt
One low-side DC-link shunt per motor:

| R_shunt | P at 80 A | V at 80 A | Amp gain for 3.3 V full scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 mΩ | 3.2 W ✗ | 40 mV | 75 |
| **0.25 mΩ** (selected, 2512 or 3920) | **1.6 W** | 20 mV | **≈ 50–100** (INA181A4 = 200 V/V would saturate; use INA181A3, 100 V/V → 2.0 V) |

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. Its HS hard-switches, and its LS sees dead-time diode conduction and reverse recovery.

### 3.1 Hard-switching V–I overlap

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

The rise and fall times come from the gate charge and gate current:

$$t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}},\quad I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}{R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}+R_{drv,src}}$$

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Q_gd | 10 nC |
| V_drv / V_plateau | 10 V / 3.2 V |
| R_g,ext (on) / R_g,int / R_drv | 6.8 Ω / 1.0 Ω / 5 Ω |
| I_g,on | 6.8 / 12.8 = 0.53 A → t_r ≈ 19 ns |
| R_g,off (via Schottky bypass) ≈ 2.2 Ω total path ≈ 6 Ω | t_f ≈ 12 ns |

$$P_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 31\text{ ns} \cdot 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{1.50\ W}$$

### 3.2 Reverse recovery (LS body diode)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 60\text{ nC}\cdot 25.2\cdot 48\text{k} = 0.073\ \text{W}$$

Recovery current also adds to HS turn-on loss:

$$\approx \tfrac{1}{2} V I_{rr} t_{rr} f \approx 0.05\ \text{W}$$

### 3.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,C_{oss,er}\,V_{bus}^2\,f_{sw}\cdot 2 = 0.5 \cdot 1.5\text{ nF}\cdot 635\cdot 48\text{k}\cdot 2 = 0.046\ \text{W}$$

### 3.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = 2\,V_F\,I\,t_{dt}\,f_{sw} = 2\cdot 0.8\cdot 80\cdot 150\text{ ns}\cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.92\ W}$$

Dead time is set to 150 ns, about 2× the worst-case t_f plus driver skew.
