## 0. Scope and Architecture

The STM32H743 does not drive the power bridges directly. ArduPilot has no motor-commutation firmware, and four sensorless six-step or FOC loops at 48 kHz would conflict with the flight-control timing budget. The architecture is therefore:

```
            ┌────────────────────── FC SECTION ──────────────────────┐
 IMU/Baro ─▶│ STM32H743VIT6 @ 400 MHz  (ArduPilot)                    │
            │  TIM1 CH1..4 ──Bi-Dir DShot600──┐                      │
            └─────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────┘
                                              ▼  (4 single-wire links, 33 Ω series)
            ┌────────────────────── ESC SECTION (×4) ────────────────┐
            │ AT32F421K8U7 (AM32) ─▶ FD6288Q 3-ph gate driver ─▶     │
            │ 6× N-MOSFET (40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3) ─▶ Motor A/B/C        │
            │ Shared: bulk caps, 0.25 mΩ shunt + INA, TVS            │
            └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Design point (stated assumptions):**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Bus voltage | 6S LiPo: V_bus,max = 25.2 V, nominal 22.2 V |
| Burst current | 80 A per motor (phase DC-equivalent), ≤ 10 s |
| Continuous current | 55 A per motor (thermal model determines the real limit) |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), center-aligned |
| MOSFET (representative) | 40 V, R_DS(on),25 = 0.65 mΩ typ / 0.80 mΩ max @ V_GS = 10 V, Q_g = 52 nC, Q_rr = 40 nC, Q_oss = 30 nC, V_SD = 0.8 V, R_θJC = 1.2 K/W |
| PCB | 30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz outer / 3 oz inner power layers |
| Ambient | 35 °C |

> **Important note on "80A nano":** At 80 A, four motors draw 320 A from the battery. That figure is not physically sustainable on a nano footprint or from any normal pack. Treat 80 A as a per-channel transient rating. The thermal analysis in §3 shows the time window that is actually achievable.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\big],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

This is linearized from typical trench-MOSFET curves, where the normalized R_DS(on) is about 1.5 at 125 °C.

### 1.2 Six-step with synchronous rectification

The key invariant is that in any 60° commutation sector, current I flows through exactly two conducting channels:

- one high-side device in leg X, which alternates with its complementary low-side device during PWM-off;
- one fully-on low-side device in leg Y.

During PWM-off, current freewheels through the low-side device of leg X instead of its high-side device. The series count stays at two. Therefore the bridge conduction loss is independent of duty cycle D (dead time is handled separately):

$$
P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) \;+\; I^2 R_{Cu}
$$

**Per-FET RMS current** (useful for package hot-spot checks). Each leg conducts 2/3 of the electrical period, split between its high- and low-side devices:

$$
I_{RMS,HS}=I\sqrt{\tfrac{D}{3}},\qquad
I_{RMS,LS}=I\sqrt{\tfrac{1}{3}+\tfrac{1-D}{3}}=I\sqrt{\tfrac{2-D}{3}}
$$

This assumes the low side holds the "fully-on" role. AM32 rotates roles, which averages devices toward I²R/3 each.

**Copper path.** Phase pad → FET → bus planes → phase pad, using 3 oz inner layers plus 2 oz outer layers in parallel, with each path about 12 mm × 6 mm wide:

$$
R_{Cu}\approx \rho\frac{L}{W\,t_{eff}} = 1.72\times10^{-8}\cdot\frac{0.012}{0.006\cdot 175\times10^{-6}} \approx 0.20\ \text{mΩ (at 25 °C, +0.39\%/K)}
$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A (iterated in §3.4 to T_j ≈ 110 °C)

$$
R_{DS(on)}(110) = 0.65\,[1+0.0045\cdot 85] = 0.90\ \text{mΩ}
$$

$$
P_{cond} = 2(80)^2(0.90\times10^{-3}) = 11.5\ \text{W},\qquad
P_{Cu} = 80^2\cdot 0.20\times10^{-3}\cdot1.3 = 1.7\ \text{W}
$$

The 1.3 factor accounts for copper running at about 100 °C.
