### ⚠️ Feasibility flags (architect sign-off required)

1. **"Nano" vs. 80A.**
   - A 20×20 mm board cannot host 24 FETs of this class plus bulk capacitance at 80A.
   - A 30.5×30.5 mm board is the practical minimum, and 80A on it is a burst-only rating (seconds, see §3).
   - **Recommendation:** split the design into an FC board (STM32H743) and a separate 4-in-1 ESC board, connected by a stack harness.
2. **ESC MCU.** The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC's own MCUs (AM32 or BLHeli_32 on STM32G071 / AT32F421 / GD32), not by the H743. The H743 only sends DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry.
3. **H743 package.** Use LQFP100 (14×14 mm) for the FC; TFBGA100 forces HDI routing. LQFP100 on a 20×20 mm FC is feasible.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary PWM

At any instant, two phases carry current:

- **PWM on-time (D):** current flows through HS(phase A) → winding → LS(phase B). That is 2 FETs in series.
- **PWM off-time (1−D), synchronous rectification:** current flows through LS(phase A, body diode or channel) → winding → LS(phase B). That is again 2 FETs in series.

So at every instant two channels conduct the full phase current. Total conduction loss per ESC channel:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

For trapezoidal drive with low ripple, I_rms ≈ I_ph. Add ripple:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \quad \Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-2E)\,D}{2 L_{ph} f_{sw}}$$

With L_ph ≈ 15 µH (line-line 30 µH), D = 0.6, and back-EMF 2E ≈ 13 V:

- ΔI_pp ≈ (25.2−13)·0.6 / (30e-6 · 48e3) ≈ **5.1 A**
- Ripple adds ΔI²/12 ≈ 2.2 A², which is negligible against 6400 A².

### 1.2 Evaluated (R₂₅ max = 1.3 mΩ)

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per ESC channel |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.30 mΩ | 2·6400·1.30e-3 = **16.6 W** |
| 100 °C | 1.84 mΩ | **23.5 W** |
| 125 °C | 2.02 mΩ | **25.8 W** |

**PCB copper loss.** The phase pad, motor wire pad and bus copper add about 0.3 mΩ per path on 2 oz copper: 6400·0.3e-3·2 ≈ **3.8 W** per channel. Do not ignore it; it heats the same copper the FETs dump into.

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

- **Commutation:** each phase's HS and LS each conduct about 1/3 of the electrical period. Each LS also carries freewheel current during its partner phase's off-time.
- **Thermal averaging:** at 80A on a typical 14-pole motor (around 10k eRPM and above), the electrical period is about 6 ms or less. Each FET's thermal time constant is ≥ 10 ms, so losses average across the 6 FETs.

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond}}{6} \approx 4.3\ \text{W at } T_j=125^\circ C$$

The worst-case positions are the low-side FETs, which carry freewheel current. Apply a 1.3× imbalance factor: **≈ 5.6 W on the worst LS FET**.
