## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, one phase leg PWMs (HS ↔ LS alternating) and one leg's low side is held on. Exactly two FETs carry I_ph at all times:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

Battery current and phase current differ:

$$
I_{bat} = D \cdot I_{ph} \quad\text{(lossless approximation)}
$$

An "80 A burst" measured at the battery is therefore much more severe than 80 A at the phase when D < 1. **Define which one your spec means.** This model uses phase current, which is conservative for FET loss.

### 1.2 Temperature coupling (self-consistent solution)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

$$
T_j = T_{pcb} + P_{FET}\, R_{\theta,j\text{-}pcb}
$$

These form a positive feedback loop. Solving iteratively at the end-of-burst condition (T_pcb = 110 °C):

| Iteration | T_j (°C) | R_DS (mΩ) | P_cond,motor (W) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 110 | 1.425 | 18.24 |
| 1 | 122 | 1.485 | 19.01 |
| 2 | 123 | 1.49 | 19.07 (converged) |

**Design value: P_cond ≈ 19.1 W per motor at 80 A, hot.** The cold-start value (T_j = 40 °C) is 2·6400·1.075 mΩ = 13.8 W.
