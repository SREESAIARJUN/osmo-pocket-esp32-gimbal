# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #10)

## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Constraints

Several points in the brief need clarification before the derivation.

1. **The H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC MCU.** Commercial 4-in-1 ESCs run AM32 or BLHeli_32 on a small MCU per motor (STM32G071, AT32F421, or GD32E230). The H743 generates bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. This package treats the system as an H743 FC plus a 4×ESC stack, either AIO or two boards.
2. **"Nano" and "80A burst ×4" conflict.** A 20×20 mm board cannot dissipate the roughly 85 W that 4×80A produces (§3). I assume a **30.5×30.5 mm ESC board** with 5×6 mm PDFN FETs, a bonded aluminum spreader, and prop-wash cooling. The thermal model below sets the permissible burst duration.
3. **Electrical baseline:** 6S (25.2 V max), FET PWM at 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous) 6-step commutation as in AM32.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current | I | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| FET R_DS(on) @25 °C | R₂₅ | 1.0 mΩ (40 V, PDFN5×6 class) |
| Tempco factor @ ~125 °C | k_T | 1.5 |
| Gate charge / Miller charge | Q_g / Q_gd | 60 nC / 10 nC |
| Effective C_oss(er) | C_oss | 1.5 nF |
| Reverse-recovery charge | Q_rr | 40 nC |
| Body-diode V_f | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns |
| Driver gate current (Miller plateau) | I_g | ~1 A |

> These FET values are representative of 40 V / 1 mΩ parts such as BSC010N04LS-class devices. **Replace them with your selected part's datasheet values at the operating V_GS and T_j.**

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step complementary commutation
In each 60° sector, current enters through one phase and returns through another:
- **PWM phase:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. Its complementary low-side FET (synchronous rectifier) conducts for (1−D).
- **Return phase:** the low-side FET is held on for the full sector.

At any instant, **exactly two FETs carry I in series**, whatever the value of D. Over one electrical cycle, each of the 6 FETs therefore carries an equal share of the conduction-time integral.

### 1.2 Equations
$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)] \approx k_T R_{25} = 1.5\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\, I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2(80)^2(1.5\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{P_{cond,ESC}}{6} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}}{3} = \mathbf{3.2\ W}$$

### 1.3 Copper and parasitic adders
Phase-trace and bus-bar resistance (2 oz, ~3 mm wide, 15 mm path) is roughly 0.25 mΩ per path:

$$P_{Cu,ESC} \approx I^2 \cdot 2 \cdot 0.25\,\text{m}\Omega = 3.2\ \text{W}$$

This loss heats the PCB, not the junction. It adds to the board energy budget only.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the PWM leg switches hard: one high-side FET plus its synchronous partner.

### 2.1 V–I overlap (hard turn-on and turn-off)
Miller-limited transition time:

$$t_{r} \approx t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_g} = 10\ \text{ns}$$

To include the di/dt phase, use t_r + t_f ≈ 30 ns:

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = \tfrac12(25.2)(80)(30\times10^{-9})(48\times10^3) = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction
$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2 t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot200\times10^{-9}\cdot48\times10^3 = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery
$$P_{rr} = V_{bus} Q_{rr} f_{sw} = 25.2\cdot40\times10^{-9}\cdot48\times10^3 = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

### 2.4 Output capacitance
$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw}\times 2 = 0.046\ \text{W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive
This loss is dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FET:

$$P_{gate} = Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 60\,\text{nC}\cdot10\,\text{V}\cdot48\,\text{kHz} = 0.029\ \text{W per switching FET}$$

### 2.6 Summary per ESC channel at 80 A

| Loss | W per ESC | Concentrated in |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FETs) | 19.2 | All 6 FETs (3.2 W each) |
| V–I switching | 1.45 | PWM high-side |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 | Sync low-side |
| Q_rr + C_oss | 0.09 | PWM leg |
| Gate drive | 0.06 | Driver IC |
| **FET total** | **≈21.4** | |
| Copper | 3.2 | PCB |
| **Board total per ESC** | **≈24.6** | |
| **4-in-1 total** | **≈98 W** | |

**Worst-case single FET** (the PWM high-side): 3.2 + 1.45 + 0.05 ≈ **4.7 W**.

**Design insight:** at 80 A, conduction loss is about 90% of FET loss. Lowering f_sw to 24 kHz saves only about 1 W per ESC. **Cut R_DS(on) instead:** paralleling two FETs per switch halves conduction loss.

### 3.1 Two-tier RC network
```

```

### 3.2 Board thermal capacitance (30.5×30.5 mm)

| Element | Mass | c_p (J/g·K) | C (J/K) |
|---|---|---|---|
| FR-4, 1.6 mm | 2.7 g | 1.1 | 2.9 |
| Copper, 6 layers × 2 oz | 3.4 g | 0.385 | 1.3 |

Board-to-air resistance with prop-wash (h ≈ 60 W/m²K, two faces plus fins): **R_ba ≈ 2.5 K/W**.

Time constant:

$$\tau = R_{ba}C_b \approx 25\ \text{s}$$
