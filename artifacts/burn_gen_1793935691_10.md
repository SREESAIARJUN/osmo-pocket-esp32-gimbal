# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have the original Job #22 specification, so I based this design on the assumptions in §0. Every motor-, IMU- and MCU-specific constant is a named parameter, so you can substitute your values without changing the structure.

**Before integration, verify these items:**
- ICM-42688-P register and anti-alias filter (AAF) values against the current TDK datasheet.
- Gimbal joint ordering and Jacobian against your mechanical model.
- PWM and ADC triggering against your MCU's reference manual.
