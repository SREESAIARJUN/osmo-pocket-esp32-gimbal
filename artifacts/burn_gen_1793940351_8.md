# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion

I don't have the original Job #5 specification, so this design states its hardware assumptions explicitly. Every assumption is a constant in the code, so you can change any of them to match your actual hardware.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4-class (FPU and CORDIC), 170 MHz |
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R = 5.6 Ω, L = 2.1 mH, λ = 0.012 Wb |
| Position sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per axis (AS5048A / MT6701 class) |
| Current sensing | 2-shunt, sampled at the PWM center |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate (A and B). They are mounted rotated 180° about z relative to each other, and both are read over SPI at 8 kHz ODR. |
| Kinematic chain | Base → Yaw → Roll → Pitch → Camera |

| Loop | Rate | Target bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | ~1.5 kHz |
| IMU acquisition and filtering | 8 kHz | n/a |
| Velocity (rate) | 4 kHz | ~150 Hz |
| Angle (attitude) | 1 kHz | ~25 Hz |
| Cogging LMS adaptation | 4 kHz (gated) | slow (τ ≈ seconds) |

The loops are separated in bandwidth by a factor of 6 or more. This lets each inner loop be treated as unity gain when designing the loop around it.

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke (amplitude-invariant):**
$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)
$$

**Park, with electrical angle θₑ = p·θₘ − θ_offset:**
$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant Model (SPMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda
$$

$$
\tau_e = K_t\, i_q,\qquad K_t = \tfrac{3}{2}\,p\,\lambda
$$

### 1.3 Decoupled PI Control with Pole-Zero Cancellation

The control law adds feedforward terms that cancel the cross-coupling and back-EMF:

$$
v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e (L i_d + \lambda)
$$
