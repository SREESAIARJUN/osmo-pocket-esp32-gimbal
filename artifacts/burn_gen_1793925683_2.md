> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag.** 4 × 80 A on a 30.5 mm board is at the edge of physics. The model below shows about **85–90 W** of total burst dissipation. That is acceptable only as a **short transient (≈3 s)** using the board's thermal mass. It is not survivable as a continuous rating. A realistic continuous rating for this footprint is roughly **35–45 A per channel** with propwash airflow. Market the 80 A figure as a "≤ 3 s burst" with firmware enforcement (see §2.5).

## 1. Power-Stage Loss Model (per channel)

### 1.1 Temperature-Dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{hot} = 0.9 \times 1.6 = 1.44\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Conduction Loss

In 6-step operation, two phase legs conduct at any instant: one high side, and one low side of a different leg. With synchronous rectification, the PWM leg shares the current between its HS FET (duty D) and LS FET (duty 1–D). The total I²R in that leg is therefore independent of D:

$$P_{leg} = I^2 R\,D + I^2 R\,(1-D) = I^2 R$$

$$\boxed{P_{cond} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu}}$$

The copper path R_Cu (phase pad → FETs → bus planes → battery pad) is estimated at 0.3 mΩ *[A]* for 2 oz planes.

| Term | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 2 × 80² × 1.44 mΩ | **18.43 W** |
| Copper | 80² × 0.3 mΩ | **1.92 W** |
| **P_cond** | | **20.35 W** |
