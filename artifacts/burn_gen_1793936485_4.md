## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification, two legs carry phase current at any instant:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D, and the complementary low-side FET conducts for (1 − D).
- **Return leg:** one low-side FET is held on.

The total conduction loss is therefore:

$$P_{cond} = I^2\left[D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS} + R_{LS}\right] + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

With matched FETs (R_HS = R_LS = R_hot), this reduces to:

$$P_{cond} \approx 2\,I^2 R_{hot} + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

The result is **independent of duty cycle**, because the dead-time contribution is handled separately in §2.4.

### 1.2 Temperature-corrected R_DS(on)

$$R_{hot} = R_{25}\cdot k(T_j) = 0.70\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.5 = 1.05\,\text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Copper path resistance

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho\,L}{w\,t},\qquad \rho_{Cu,100°C} = 2.2\times10^{-8}\,\Omega\text{m}$$

Assumptions for the phase path from FET to pad:
- Length L ≈ 8 mm
- Width w ≈ 5 mm
- Two layers of 2 oz copper, so t = 140 µm

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{2.2\times10^{-8}\times 0.008}{0.005\times140\times10^{-6}} \approx 0.25\,\text{m}\Omega$$

The path traverses two phase legs plus the bus, so take R_Cu,total ≈ 0.5 mΩ.

### 1.4 Result per channel at 80 A

$$P_{cond} = 80^2(2\times1.05\text{m} + 0.5\text{m}) = 6400 \times 2.6\text{m} = \mathbf{16.6\ W}$$

Of this:
- FETs: 13.4 W
- Copper: 3.2 W

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches, so all of the following are per channel.

### 2.1 V–I overlap loss (hard switching, inductive load)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5\times25.2\times80\times20\text{n}\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery loss

The low-side body diode recovers when the high-side FET turns on.

$$P_{rr} = V_{bus} Q_{rr} f_{sw} = 25.2\times50\text{n}\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance loss

$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw}\times 2 = 40\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.05\ W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

There are two dead-time intervals per PWM period.

$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8\times80\times200\text{n}\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive loss

This is dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FETs.

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2\times60\text{n}\times10\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.058\ W}$$

### 2.6 Switching total per channel

P_sw ≈ 0.97 + 0.06 + 0.05 + 0.61 ≈ **1.69 W**

The gate drive loss is excluded from this total. At 48 kHz, dead time costs about 60% as much as V–I overlap. If your firmware and FET pair tolerate it, reducing t_dt to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W per channel.

## 3. Total Loss Budget

| Item | Per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 13.4 W | 53.6 W |
| Copper I²R | 3.2 W | 12.8 W |
| Switching (V–I + Q_rr + Q_oss + dead time) | 1.7 W | 6.8 W |
| Gate drive | 0.06 W | 0.24 W |
| **Total** | **≈18.4 W** | **≈73 W** |

Efficiency check per channel:
- Output power ≈ 25.2 V × 80 A × D. At D ≈ 0.9, that is about 1.8 kW.
- 18.4 W of loss gives roughly 99% electrical efficiency.
