## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Topology current paths

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM. At any instant, exactly two phases carry the phase current I:

- **PWM on (fraction D):** current flows through HS_x and then LS_y.
- **PWM off (fraction 1−D):** current flows through LS_x (sync rectifier) and then LS_y.

In both states, the current passes through **two R_DS(on) in series**. Conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle, apart from the dead-time intervals handled in 1.4.

### 1.2 Per-motor conduction loss

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]$$

where α ≈ 0.005 /K, which gives k_T = 1.5 at 125 °C.

At the 80 A burst:

| Case | R_DS | P_cond per motor |
|---|---|---|
| Cold (25 °C) | 1.0 mΩ | 2 × 6400 × 1.0e-3 = **12.8 W** |
| Hot (125 °C) | 1.5 mΩ | 2 × 6400 × 1.5e-3 = **19.2 W** |
| Nano DFN, hot | 2.5 × 1.5 = 3.75 mΩ | **48 W** |

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle, and the high-side/low-side split depends on D. The worst-case low-side FET carries current during:

- its own 1/3 of the cycle as the return path, at 100%, and
- 1/3 of the cycle as sync rectifier, scaled by (1−D).

$$P_{LS,avg} = I^2 R_{DS}\left[\tfrac{1}{3} + \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)\right]$$

At 80 A, 1.5 mΩ, and D = 0.5: **P_LS ≈ 4.8 W** and **P_HS ≈ 1.6 W**.

So the **low-side FETs run hottest at partial throttle**, which is counter-intuitive and worth putting in the layout notes.

### 1.4 Dead-time body-diode loss

Two dead-time edges per PWM period, each conducting through the body diode:

$$P_{dt} = 2\,V_F\,I\,t_d\,f_{sw} = 2(0.8)(80)(50\text{e-}9)(48\text{e}3) = \mathbf{0.31\ W/motor}$$

### 1.5 Board copper loss (often forgotten)

For a 4-layer stack with 2 oz copper on the power layers, the phase and bus paths come to roughly R_cu ≈ 0.4 mΩ effective per motor. That adds I²R ≈ 2.6 W per motor at 80 A, plus solder-pad and shunt contributions.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-modulated high-side FET hard-switches. The low-side commutates softly, apart from Q_rr.

### 2.1 Hard-switching overlap loss

$$P_{sw,VI} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(20\text{e-}9)(48\text{e}3) = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

To derive t_r and t_f from the gate driver:

$$t_{r} \approx \frac{Q_{gd} \cdot R_{g,tot}}{V_{drv} - V_{plateau}}$$

With the FD6288Q-class driver (≈ 0.3/0.6 A source/sink) and no external boost, expect t_r ≈ 20–40 ns. That **doubles or triples** this term. Verify on the bench with a 200 MHz+ probe.

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 40\text{e-}9 \times 25.2 \times 48\text{e}3 = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2} Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} \approx \mathbf{0.03\ W}$$

### 2.4 Gate drive

This loss is dissipated in the driver and R_g, not the FET:

$$P_g = Q_g\,V_{drv}\,f_{sw} \times 2 \text{ FETs} = 40\text{e-}9 \times 10 \times 48\text{e}3 \times 2 = \mathbf{0.04\ W/motor}$$

### 2.5 Per-motor and board totals at 80 A burst, T_j = 125 °C

| Loss term | 5×6 mm FET (1.0 mΩ) | Nano DFN (2.5 mΩ) |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 19.2 W | 48.0 W |
| Dead-time diode | 0.31 W | 0.31 W |
| Switching (V·I) | 0.97 W | 1.5 W (slower edges) |
| Q_rr + Q_oss + gate | 0.12 W | 0.12 W |
| Copper / shunt | 2.6 W | 3.5 W |
| **Per motor** | **≈ 23 W** | **≈ 53 W** |
| **Board (4 motors)** | **≈ 93 W** | **≈ 213 W** |

Efficiency at the burst point is P_out ≈ 25.2 × 80 × 4 ≈ 8 kW, giving η ≈ 98.8% for the 5×6 case and 97.4% for the DFN case. The efficiency looks excellent; the absolute watts are the problem.
