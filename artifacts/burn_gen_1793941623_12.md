# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #68): Reference Design Model

## Before You Use This

- **I have no access to Job #68's actual design files.** This is a first-principles reference model. Swap in your BOM's datasheet values: R_DS(on)(T), Q_gd, Q_gs2, Q_rr, R_θJC, and your stackup. The equations carry over; the numbers are only as good as those inputs.
- **The ESC switching frequency (48 kHz) belongs to the ESC MCUs, not the H743.** Each motor channel runs AM32 or BLHeli_32 on its own MCU. The H743 only produces bidirectional DShot600 and consumes eRPM telemetry.
- **80 A per channel on a nano-format AIO is thermally a seconds-scale burst, not a rating.** Section 3 quantifies this. Treat the result as a design-review finding.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Topology assumptions

- 3-phase bridge per motor, 6 N-MOSFETs, trapezoidal commutation.
- Complementary PWM, i.e. synchronous rectification.
- At any instant, phase current flows through exactly two conducting FETs:
  - PWM on: high side of phase X plus low side of phase Y.
  - PWM off: low side of phase X (synchronous rectification) plus low side of phase Y.
- Conduction loss is therefore approximately independent of duty cycle.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0065\text{–}0.0075\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

Reference device: 40 V, PDFN 5×6 (or 3.3×3.3), R_DS,25 = 1.0 mΩ at V_GS = 10 V.

At T_j = 110 °C: R_DS ≈ 1.0 × (1 + 0.0075·85) = **1.64 mΩ**.

### 1.3 RMS current including PWM ripple

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{LL}\,f_{sw}}$$

Worst case is D = 0.5, with 6S full charge V_bus = 25.2 V, L_LL = 20 µH, f_sw = 48 kHz:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{25.2 \cdot 0.25}{20\mu \cdot 48k} = 6.56\ \text{A},\qquad I_{ac,rms} = \frac{\Delta I_{pp}}{\sqrt{12}} = 1.89\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + I_{ac,rms}^2 = 6400 + 3.6 \approx 6404\ \text{A}^2$$

Ripple adds only about 0.06% and can be neglected at 80 A. It matters at light load.

### 1.4 Conduction loss per channel

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j) = 2 \cdot 6404 \cdot 1.64\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{21.0\ W}$$

Losses also go to copper. Assume about 0.3 mΩ of trace and pour in series per path (two paths):
