## 0. Architecture Correction and Feasibility Verdict

**The H743 does not commutate the motors.** A 4-in-1 ESC at 48 kHz with bidirectional DShot needs a dedicated ESC MCU per motor, or one MCU running all four channels. The partition used here is:

| Block | Device class | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743 (480 MHz max, run at 400 MHz) | ArduPilot. Emits bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AM32-class Cortex-M0+/M4 (e.g. G071 / F421 class) | Six-step commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, BEMF zero-cross detection |
| Gate driver ×4 | 3-phase bootstrap half-bridge driver, ~1 A source / 1.5 A sink | Drives 6 N-FETs per channel |
