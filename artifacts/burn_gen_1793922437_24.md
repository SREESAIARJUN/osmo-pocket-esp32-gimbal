# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #8): Systems Architecture Package

## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 cannot be the ESC commutation processor.** ArduPilot has no integrated 6-step or FOC motor-control path. Four 48 kHz bridges plus a flight stack on one core is not a supportable design. The correct partitioning is:

| Function | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight control, DShot master | STM32H743VIT6 @ 400 MHz | ArduPilot |
| ESC ×4 (commutation, 48 kHz PWM, bidirectional DShot slave) | 4× AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) | AM32 |
| Gate drive ×4 | FD6288Q-class 3-phase driver (or discrete half-bridge drivers) | n/a |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET, 40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3 | n/a |

**Model parameters.** These are placeholders. Replace them with the selected MOSFET datasheet values before release.

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S, full charge (worst case for switching loss) |
| I_ph | 80 A | Burst phase current per motor |
| R_ds(on),25 | 1.2 mΩ | At V_GS = 10 V |
| α | 0.005 /K | R_ds tempco, giving ≈1.5× at 125 °C |
| t_r, t_f | 20 ns each | With ~1 A driver, Q_gd + Q_gs2 ≈ 12 nC |
| Q_rr | 50 nC | Body diode reverse recovery |

**Modulation:** 6-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) PWM, which is the AM32 default.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology identity
In 6-step operation exactly two phase legs carry current at any instant. With synchronous rectification, whichever FET of the PWM leg is on carries the full phase current, during both the drive and freewheel intervals. Therefore, independent of duty D, **two R_ds(on) channels are in series with I_ph at all times** (dead time excluded):

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Evaluation at 80 A
| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per motor | ×4 motors |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W | 61.4 W |
| 100 °C | 1.65 mΩ | 21.1 W | 84.5 W |
| 125 °C | 1.80 mΩ | 23.0 W | 92.2 W |

Copper loss: 80² × 0.5 mΩ = 3.2 W per motor, 12.8 W total. This loss heats the board, not the junctions.

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution
Each of the 6 FETs conducts for 120° of each electrical cycle. Average conduction loss per FET at 125 °C is 23.0 / 6 = **3.83 W**.
