## 1. Field-Oriented Control (innermost loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle
$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

`θ_offset` is found at commissioning: inject `v_d > 0, v_q = 0` and read the encoder once the rotor has settled.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

### 1.3 Park transform
$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant (surface-mount PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)
$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$
$$\tau = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q$$

### 1.5 Current PI with decoupling (pole-zero cancellation)
Choose the closed-loop current bandwidth ω_c (e.g. 2π·1.5 kHz, below f_s/10):
$$K_{p,i}=L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i}=R\,\omega_c\quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{i}{i^*}=\frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

The discrete control law, with sample time T_s and error e = i* − i:
$$v_{d,q}=K_{p,i}\,e_{d,q}+x_{d,q}+v^{ff}_{d,q}$$
$$x_{d,q}[k{+}1]=x_{d,q}[k]+K_{i,i}T_s e_{d,q}\quad(\text{frozen if saturated, see below})$$
$$v_d^{ff}=-\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff}=\omega_e(L i_d+\lambda_m)$$
