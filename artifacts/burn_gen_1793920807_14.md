## 0. Architecture Corrections

Three items in the brief need correcting before any math.

1. **The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** ArduPilot cannot commutate motors. A working 4-in-1 design uses four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or BLHeli_32-class firmware), each with its own 3-phase gate driver. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to those MCUs. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, not the H743.
2. **"80A" must be defined per channel and time-limited.** The analysis below shows that 4 × 80 A continuous on a nano footprint is physically impossible. The usable spec is a burst rating with a defined duration, derived in §3.
