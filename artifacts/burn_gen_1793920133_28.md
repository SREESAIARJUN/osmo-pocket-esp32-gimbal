# STM32H743 Flight Controller + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Electro-Thermal and DShot Design Package

**Scope:** 6S input, 80A burst per channel, 48 kHz PWM, ArduPilot bi-directional DShot600.

---

## 0. Architecture Corrections Before Design

Three issues in the brief would sink this design if left in place.

**1. The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors.**
- Driving 4 BLDC motors needs 24 gate signals (12 complementary pairs) with hardware dead-time.
- The H743 has only TIM1 and TIM8 as advanced timers, giving 6 complementary pairs, which is enough for 2 motors. HRTIM adds 10 outputs, but sensorless back-EMF zero-crossing for 4 motors at 48 kHz on the flight MCU is not a viable design.
- **Resolution:** Use a stack architecture.
  - The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600.
  - Each ESC channel has its own MCU, either an STM32G071 or an AT32F421 running AM32.
- The netlists below follow this split.

**2. "Nano" and "80A burst" do not fit together.**
- On 20×20 mm, 80A per channel is not physically supportable (see §3).
- The thermal model below assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz** ESC board.
- At 80A, even this board is limited to a burst of seconds.
