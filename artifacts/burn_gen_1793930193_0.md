# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #61): Power-Stage Thermal Model, 48 kHz Switching Losses, and ArduPilot Bi-Directional DShot600 DMA Plan

## 0. Architectural Assumptions

These assumptions affect every number below, so check them first.

1. **The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC MCU.** No production 4-in-1 ESC runs on an H743. The ESC side uses one small MCU per motor, typically STM32G071, AT32F421 or GD32E230, running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 talks to the ESCs over bidirectional DShot600. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, not the H743. If you intend the H743 to drive 12 half-bridges directly, that is a different architecture. Its timer budget (4 × 3-phase complementary PWM plus BEMF/FOC sensing plus DShot) does not fit cleanly, and I'd advise against it.
2. **Battery:** 6S LiPo, 25.2 V maximum, 22.2 V nominal.
3. **MOSFETs:** a generic 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm (DFN/PDFN) N-channel. The model is parametric, so substitute your selected part's datasheet values.

4. **Board:** 36 × 36 mm, 30.5 mm mounting pattern, 8-layer, 2 oz outer and inner power layers. Note that "nano" usually means 20×20 mm, and 80 A per channel is physically impossible there (see §2.4).
5. **Commutation:** trapezoidal 6-step with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM, as in AM32 and BLHeli_32.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R<sub>DS(on)</sub>

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T<sub>j</sub> = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × (1 + 0.6) = **1.6 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous commutation

In each 60° sector, one phase is PWM-switched and one phase is held low.

- **PWM leg:** the high side (HS) conducts for fraction D. The low side (LS) synchronously rectifies for 1 − D.
- **Held-low leg:** the LS conducts 100%.

So at every instant, phase current I<sub>ph</sub> flows through exactly two MOSFET channels in series, independent of D. The per-channel conduction loss is therefore:
