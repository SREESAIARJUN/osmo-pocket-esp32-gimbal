# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #23): Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation and Reference Netlist

## 0. Scope, Architecture and Key Findings

### Architecture

The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFET bridges directly. A "4-in-1 ESC nano FC" is two subsystems on one board:

| Subsystem | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100, 8×8 mm) | Runs ArduPilot, generates bidirectional DShot600 on 4 timer channels |
| ESC ×4 | One AM32-class MCU per channel (e.g. AT32F421K8U7) + 3-phase gate driver + 6 N-FETs | Decodes DShot, runs 6-step commutation at 48 kHz, returns eRPM telemetry |

The loss and thermal model below covers one ESC channel and is then scaled ×4.
