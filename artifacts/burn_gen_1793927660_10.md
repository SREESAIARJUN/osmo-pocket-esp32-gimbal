## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In every production 4-in-1 design, and in this one, the work is split across two kinds of processor:

| Layer | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz under ArduPilot | Attitude control, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| ESC (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 (AM32) or equivalent BLHeli_32 MCU | 48 kHz complementary PWM, commutation, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive (×4) | FD6288Q 3-phase half-bridge driver | Bootstrap high-side drive with built-in dead time |
| Power stage (×4) | 6 N-channel MOSFETs per motor, 24 total | Three-phase bridge |

The "48 kHz switching" is generated by the ESC MCUs. The H743 only exchanges digital DShot frames with them. Keeping these separate prevents a common design error: trying to run motor PWM from the FC's timers.

**Design parameters used in this model.** These are illustrative values. Replace them with datasheet values for your chosen parts.

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S fully charged (worst case for switching loss) |
| I_ph | 80 A | Burst phase current per motor |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | PWM frequency |
| R_ds,25 | 2.0 mΩ | 40 V MOSFET in 3.3×3.3 DFN, V_GS = 10 V |
| α | 0.0067 /K | R_ds(on) temperature coefficient (about 1.57× at 110 °C) |
| t_r + t_f | 40 ns | Combined edge time with the chosen R_g |
| Q_rr | 30 nC | Body-diode reverse recovery charge |
| Q_oss | 40 nC | Output charge |
| Q_g | 50 nC | Total gate charge |
| V_F | 0.8 V | Body-diode forward voltage |
| t_dead | 100 ns | Dead time per edge |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

```
R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α (T_j − 25)]
```

### 1.2 Six-step trapezoidal drive with complementary PWM

During each 60° commutation sector, exactly one high-side FET and one low-side FET carry the phase current:

- **PWM leg:** the high side conducts for a fraction D of each period. During the remaining (1−D), its complementary low side conducts the freewheeling current.
- **Static leg:** the low side is fully on for the whole sector.

So the phase current always passes through exactly two R_ds(on) in series, independent of duty cycle. Including ripple:

```
I_rms² = I_ph² + ΔI²/12

P_cond,ch = 2 · I_rms² · R_ds(T_j)
```

The ripple term follows from the motor's phase-to-phase inductance:

```
ΔI = V_bus · D(1−D) / (L_ph-ph · f_sw)
```

For L ≈ 15 µH, D = 0.5, f = 48 kHz: ΔI ≈ 8.75 A, so ΔI²/12 ≈ 6.4 A². This is negligible against 6400 A².

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 110 °C

```
R_ds(110) = 2.0 mΩ × (1 + 0.0067 × 85) = 3.14 mΩ
P_cond,ch = 2 × 6406 × 3.14e-3        = 40.2 W per motor channel
```

**Per-FET distribution.** Each FET conducts during 2 of the 6 sectors per electrical cycle.

- The hottest device is the static low side: I²R = 20.1 W while it is active.
- Averaged over the electrical cycle, the bridge spreads about 40 W across 6 FETs, roughly 6.7 W each.
- On a small board, however, the thermal time constant of a single die (milliseconds) is shorter than one commutation sector only at very low eRPM. At low speed and high load (stall or punch-out from hover), individual FETs see the full 20 W pulses.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the PWM leg switches. The static leg commutates at the electrical frequency, which is negligible.

**(a) Hard-switching V·I overlap**

```
P_VI = ½ · V_bus · I_ph · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw
     = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40e-9 × 48e3 = 1.94 W
```

**(b) Body-diode dead-time conduction** (two dead-time intervals per period)

```
P_dt = V_F · I_ph · 2 · t_dead · f_sw
     = 0.8 × 80 × 200e-9 × 48e3 = 0.61 W
```

**(c) Reverse recovery**

```
P_rr = Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw = 30e-9 × 25.2 × 48e3 = 0.036 W
```

**(d) Output capacitance**

```
P_oss ≈ ½ · Q_oss · V_bus · f_sw = 0.024 W
```
