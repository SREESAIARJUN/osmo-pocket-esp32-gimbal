## 0. Scope and Architectural Corrections

I'll start with three findings that change the design:

1. **The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors.** Production 4-in-1 boards use one small MCU per motor running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Running four sensorless FOC/6-step loops at 48 kHz on the flight controller would conflict with ArduPilot's scheduler, and ArduPilot does not support it. The architecture is therefore **H743 (flight control) + 4 × AT32F421 (AM32)**, linked by bi-directional DShot600.
