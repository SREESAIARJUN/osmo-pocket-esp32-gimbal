## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.6 mΩ × 1.6 = **2.56 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Per-leg conduction in 6-step complementary PWM

Each phase leg spends one third of the electrical period in each of three states:

- **High-side PWM sector (1/3):**
  - HS conducts for fraction D.
  - The LS of the same leg conducts the freewheel current for fraction (1 − D).
- **Low-side active sector (1/3):** the LS is fully on.
- **Floating sector (1/3):** neither device conducts. This is the BEMF sensing window.

$$P_{HS} = I^2 R_{ds}\frac{D}{3},\qquad P_{LS} = I^2 R_{ds}\frac{(1-D)}{3} + I^2 R_{ds}\frac{1}{3}$$

$$P_{cond,leg} = P_{HS}+P_{LS} = \frac{2}{3}I^2 R_{ds} \quad\text{(independent of D)}$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

- I²R = 6400 × 2.56 mΩ = 16.38 W
- **P_cond,leg = 10.92 W**
- **Per ESC (3 legs): 32.8 W**
- **4-in-1 total: 131 W**

**Worst single device.** The low-side FET at low throttle (D → 0) carries (2/3)·I²R = **10.9 W**. At D = 1 the leg loss splits evenly, about 5.5 W per FET. Low-throttle, high-current conditions (prop-wash recovery, punch-out from a hover) are therefore the low-side thermal worst case.
