**Architectural note on the H743.** The H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It should **not** commutate the motors itself. ArduPilot has no in-process ESC firmware, and sensorless BEMF commutation for four motors at 48 kHz would consume the timer resources that bidirectional DShot needs.

The correct partition is:

- **H743 (FC):** sends bidirectional DShot600 commands.
- **4 × ESC MCU (AM32):** run commutation and return eRPM telemetry.

The DShot section (§3) assumes this partition.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current Path

With complementary PWM in 6-step commutation, exactly two FETs carry the phase current at all times. During the on-time these are HS(x) and LS(y). During the off-time they are LS(x), acting as a synchronous rectifier, and LS(y). Neglecting ripple, the instantaneous conduction loss per ESC channel is therefore independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature-Corrected R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005/\text{K} \Rightarrow R_{125} = 1.5\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Ripple Correction

The RMS current through a conducting FET includes PWM ripple ΔI:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{BEMF})\,D}{L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}$$

Taking a typical 5" motor (L_ph ≈ 15 µH line-to-line), at D = 0.5 and V_BEMF ≈ 0.5·V_bus:

$$\Delta I = \frac{12.6 \times 0.5}{15\,\mu\text{H} \times 48\,\text{kHz}} = 8.75\ \text{A} \Rightarrow \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6.4\ \text{A}^2$$

This adds about 0.1 % to I². It is negligible, which is one benefit of 48 kHz.

### 1.4 Results

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Per ESC channel, T_j = 125 °C | 2 × 80² × 1.5 mΩ = **19.2 W** |
| Per ESC channel, T_j = 25 °C | 12.8 W |
| Average per FET (each of 6 FETs conducts ~1/3 of an electrical cycle) | 19.2 / 6 ≈ **3.2 W** |
| PCB copper loss per phase (≈0.15 mΩ trace + via, ×3 path segments) | 80² × 0.3 mΩ ≈ **1.9 W** |
| Shunt loss (0.25 mΩ low-side current sense, ESC-level) | 80² × 0.25 mΩ = **1.6 W** |

## 2. Switching Losses at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing half-bridge (phase x) hard-switches. The static low-side LS(y) does not switch at f_sw.

### 2.1 V-I Overlap (hard switching)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.968\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse Recovery

The body diode of LS(x) conducts during dead time, then is hard-commutated by HS(x):

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.060\ W}$$

### 2.3 Output Capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} \cdot \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2 = 60\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.073\ W}$$

The factor of 2 accounts for both FETs of the leg. Because Q_oss charge energy is ≈ ½QV per device, this is a conservative estimate.

### 2.4 Dead-Time Body-Diode Conduction

$$P_{DT} = V_F I_{ph} (2 t_{DT}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 400\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{1.229\ W}$$

> This is the **largest switching-related term**, larger than V-I overlap. Reducing dead time from 200 ns to 100 ns saves 0.6 W per channel. Verify shoot-through margin on the bench first, measuring V_GS of both FETs with differential probes.

### 2.5 Gate Drive

Six FETs exist, but only two switch at f_sw at any moment:

$$P_g = 2\, Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} = 2 \times 45\,\text{nC} \times 10 \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.043\ W}$$

This loss is dissipated in R_g and the driver, not in the FET.
