## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

θ_off is found at commissioning by applying a d-axis voltage vector and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.4 PMSM dq model

$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Electromagnetic torque (surface PM, so L_d ≈ L_q):

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32\,p\,\lambda_m\, i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$

### 1.5 Current PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L_q i_q
$$
$$
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$
