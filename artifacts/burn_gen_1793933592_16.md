# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #23): Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bidirectional DShot Architecture

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architectural correction.** The STM32H743 cannot directly commutate four BLDC motors in this design. It runs ArduPilot and sends **DShot600** to four ESC MCUs, one per channel (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32, or BLHeli_32-class parts). Those ESC MCUs generate the **48 kHz** motor PWM. The two frequencies belong to separate subsystems, and the model keeps them separate.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.5 × 1.45 = **2.18 mΩ**. Use ~2.2 mΩ.

### 1.2 Instantaneous conducting set

In 6-step synchronous commutation, exactly two device paths carry I_ph at any instant:

- the active high-side (or its complementary low-side during PWM off-time), and
- the static low-side of the return phase.

The loss is therefore independent of duty D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Bus and phase currents are related by $I_{bus}\approx D\cdot I_{ph}$. Each FET conducts on average 1/3 of the electrical period, so:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\quad\text{(static low-side)}$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2(80)^2(2.2\times10^{-3}) = 28.2\ \text{W}$$

- Average per FET: ≈ 4.7 W. The instantaneous peak while conducting is 14.1 W.
- Four channels: **≈ 113 W**.

### 1.4 Parasitic copper and shunt losses

| Element | R | Loss/channel @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase + bus copper (2oz, inner layers) | ~0.4 mΩ effective | 2.6 W |
| Current shunt (if per-channel, 0.25 mΩ) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W × D² |

## 2. Switching-Loss Model at 48 kHz

One leg hard-switches per PWM period. The partner FET soft-switches through its body diode during dead time.

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{tot}} = \frac{5.5}{16}=0.34\text{ A},\quad I_{g,off}=\frac{V_{pl}}{R_{tot}}=0.28\text{ A}$$
