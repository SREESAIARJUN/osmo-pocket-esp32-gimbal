## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path topology
In 6-step commutation, two phases conduct at any instant. The current path always runs through exactly two "on" devices:
- one high-side (HS) switch in the sourcing leg, and
- one low-side (LS) switch in the sinking leg.

Complementary PWM alternates the current in the PWM leg between the HS FET (fraction D) and the LS FET (fraction 1−D). The total conduction resistance in the path therefore stays at 2·Rds regardless of duty cycle.

**Total bridge conduction loss (per motor):**
$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

**Per-MOSFET average:** each of the 6 FETs carries current for 120° of the 360° electrical cycle.
$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{2 I^2 R_{ds}}{6} = \frac{I^2 R_{ds}}{3}$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence of Rds(on)
$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.004\text{–}0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

A typical normalized datasheet curve gives about 1.5× at T_j = 125 °C.

### 1.3 Numerical results (I = 80 A)

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per FET | P_cond bridge | P_cond, 4 motors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 2.13 W | 12.8 W | 51.2 W |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ | 2.86 W | 17.2 W | 68.6 W |
| 125 °C | 1.50 mΩ | 3.20 W | **19.2 W** | **76.8 W** |

**Additional copper loss (not negligible at 80 A):**
- A 2 oz trace 3 mm wide × 10 mm long has R ≈ 0.8 mΩ, which adds about **5 W per motor** if not mitigated.
- Mitigation: stitch all power layers in parallel, keep the phase-pad-to-FET distance under 5 mm, and use bus-bar style pours.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. The HS FET hard-switches. The LS FET acts as the synchronous rectifier and turns on at zero voltage after dead time, but its body diode contributes reverse recovery.

### 2.1 Loss components

**(a) V–I overlap (HS FET):**
$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

The transition time follows from the Miller charge and the gate current:
$$t_{r,f} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_g},\qquad I_g \approx \frac{V_{drv} - V_{plateau}}{R_g}$$

With Q_gd ≈ 8 nC and I_g ≈ 0.8 A, each edge takes about 10 ns, so (t_r + t_f) = 20 ns.
$$P_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{ns} \times 48\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

**(b) Body-diode reverse recovery:**
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 30\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}$$

**(c) Output capacitance:**
$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 30\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.018\ \text{W}$$

**(d) Dead-time diode conduction** (two dead-time intervals per period, t_dt = 50 ns, V_f = 0.8 V):
$$P_{dt} = V_f\,I\,(2 t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = 0.31\ \text{W}$$

**(e) Gate drive** (2 FETs switching, Q_g = 40 nC):
$$P_g = 2\,Q_g\,V_{drv}\,f_{sw} = 2 \times 40\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.038\ \text{W}$$

### 2.2 Loss summary at 80 A, 25.2 V, T_j = 125 °C

| Component | Per motor | 4 motors |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FETs) | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| Switching (a–e) | 1.37 W | 5.5 W |
| PCB copper (mitigated estimate) | ~2 W | ~8 W |
| **Total** | **~22.6 W** | **~90 W** |

**Takeaways:**
- At 80 A the design is **conduction-dominated (>85%)**. Lowering f_sw from 48 kHz to 24 kHz saves only about 0.65 W per motor.
- Rds(on), copper area, and FET paralleling are the only meaningful levers.
- 48 kHz is justified on **acoustic and current-ripple grounds**, not efficiency grounds.

**Gate-loop design rule:** keep the loop inductance below 2 nH. At 80 A/10 ns, di/dt = 8 A/ns, and with 5 nH of power-loop stray inductance the overshoot is V = L·di/dt = 40 V. This **exceeds the 40 V FET rating**. Required countermeasures:
- 0402 bulk-bypass MLCCs (100 nF + 1 µF, X7R 50 V) within 1 mm of each half-bridge.
- R_g of at least 4.7 Ω on turn-on.
