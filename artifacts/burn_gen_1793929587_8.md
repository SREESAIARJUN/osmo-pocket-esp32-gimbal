## 0. Scope and Ground Rules

**Architecture assumption.** The H743 runs ArduPilot only. The 4-in-1 ESC section uses **four dedicated ESC MCUs** (AT32F421-class running AM32) feeding three-phase gate drivers. ## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.3 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.885 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current sharing in 6-step complementary PWM

Each electrical period has six 60° sectors. In each sector:
- The PWM leg's high-side (HS) switch conducts for duty D.
- The PWM leg's low-side (LS) switch synchronously rectifies for (1 − D).
- The return leg's LS switch conducts 100% of the time.

Each switch participates for 1/3 of the electrical period in a given role. Averaged per switch:

$$P_{HS} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS} D}{3}, \qquad P_{LS} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}\,[(1-D) + 1]}{3}$$

(This assumes the common scheme where the PWM is applied on the high side. With alternating-side PWM, the losses balance between HS and LS.)

Summed over the six switches of one ESC, the current I always flows through exactly two R_DS paths:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ESC} = 2 I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)}$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Per ESC | 2 × 6400 × 1.885 mΩ = **24.1 W** |
| Worst single FET (D → 1, HS) | 6400 × 1.885 mΩ / 3 = **4.0 W** |
| Worst single FET (low D, LS of PWM leg plus return role) | up to **8.0 W** |
| Four motors simultaneously | **96.5 W** |

The worst-case LS FET loss is the argument for alternating-side PWM, which caps any single FET at about 4 W.

### 1.4 Parasitic conduction terms (add these; they are not negligible on a nano)

- **PCB copper:** R_Cu = ρL / (wt). For 2 oz (70 µm) copper, a 5 mm wide, 10 mm long pour gives ≈ 0.49 mΩ. Two paths at 80 A add 2 × 6400 × 0.49e-3 ≈ **6.3 W per ESC**. Mitigate with 3 oz copper on inner layers, stacked pours, and short phase pads.
- **Shunt** (if per-ESC sensing is used): 0.25 mΩ × I_DC². This is only DC-link current, about 1.6 W at 80 A, D = 1.
- **Bus capacitor ripple:** worst at D = 0.5.

$$I_{C,rms} \approx I\sqrt{D(1-D)} = 40\ \text{A}$$

  With ESR_total = 5 mΩ, the capacitor bank dissipates **8 W**. Use an electrolytic plus polymer/MLCC split, and interleave the four ESC PWM phases (a 90° carrier offset, if the ESC firmware allows it) to reduce the shared-bus ripple.
