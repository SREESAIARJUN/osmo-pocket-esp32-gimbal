# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #40): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan, and Netlist

## 0. Ground Rules and Corrections

These points constrain everything below.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** A 48 kHz 6-step bridge with BEMF zero-cross detection needs a dedicated MCU per motor. The architecture here is **STM32H743 (ArduPilot) → 4× AT32F421 (AM32 firmware) → 4× 3-phase gate driver → 24 MOSFETs**. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the AT32s. The H743 only sends DShot600 and receives bidirectional eRPM telemetry.
2. **"80 A" must mean phase current.** Battery current is I_batt ≈ D·I_phase. All loss equations below use phase current.
3. **80 A on a 20×20/25×25 board is a transient rating only.** The math in §3 shows roughly 1–2 s of adiabatic headroom at 4×80 A. Anyone advertising it as continuous is wrong.
4. **MOSFET parameters are representative, not from a specific part.** They describe a 40 V, ~1.2 mΩ, 3.3×3.3 DFN-class device. Substitute the real datasheet values. Every equation is written symbolically so you can re-run it.
