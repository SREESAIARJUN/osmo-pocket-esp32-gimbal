# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Reference Design Analysis

**Scope:** Power-stage loss model (80 A burst, 48 kHz PWM), board thermal model, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA plan, and a reference netlist.

## 0. Architectural Assumptions

The H743 does not commutate the motors. It is the flight controller. Each ESC channel uses its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, and the H743 sends DShot600 to those MCUs. The analysis below depends on this split.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
In six-step operation, two phases conduct at any instant:
- One high-side FET (switched at duty D, complementary with its low-side partner).
- One low-side FET (held ON).

With synchronous rectification, current always flows through exactly **two channels of R_DS(on)** in series:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

With α ≈ 0.006 K⁻¹, this gives k_T ≈ 1.6 at 125 °C.

Ripple correction: I_rms² = I_dc² + ΔI²/12. With 48 kHz PWM and typical motor inductance (~20–50 µH phase-to-phase), ΔI is a few amps, so the correction is under 1%. It is neglected here.

### 1.2 At 80 A burst

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond per channel |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C (cold start) | 1.0 mΩ | 2·80²·1.0 m = **12.8 W** |
| 125 °C (hot) | 1.6 mΩ | 2·80²·1.6 m = **20.5 W** |

### 1.3 Copper and shunt losses
- Effective PCB path resistance per channel (phase pad → FET → bus → return) ≈ 0.3–0.5 mΩ.
  - P_Cu ≈ 80² · 0.4 m ≈ **2.6 W**
- Current shunt: either a single 0.25 mΩ shunt on the shared return, or a per-channel shunt.
  - Per channel: 80² · 0.25 m = **1.6 W**

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution
Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle:
- High-side, active leg: ≈ D·I²R
- Low-side, same leg: ≈ (1−D)·I²R
- Low-side, return leg: fully ON

The electrical period is far shorter than the die thermal time constant (ms range). Using the time-averaged value is therefore valid:

$$
\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{P_{ch,total}}{6}
$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Only the active (PWM) leg switches. That is one half-bridge per channel at a time.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V–I overlap

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}
= 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 40\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}
$$

### 2.2 Output capacitance

$$
P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{n}\cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}
$$

### 2.3 Body-diode reverse recovery

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\cdot 25.2\cdot 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}
$$

### 2.4 Dead-time diode conduction (two transitions per cycle)

$$
P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I\, (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot 80\cdot 100\text{n}\cdot 48\text{k} = 0.31\ \text{W}
$$

### 2.5 Gate drive
Dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET.

$$
P_g = 2\, Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2\cdot 40\text{n}\cdot 10\cdot 48\text{k} = 0.038\ \text{W}
$$

### 2.6 Frequency sensitivity
The frequency-dependent terms (P_sw + P_oss + P_rr + P_dt) total ≈ **2.33 W at 48 kHz**. They scale linearly with frequency:

| f_sw | Frequency-dependent loss |
|---|---|
| 24 kHz | 1.17 W |
| 48 kHz | 2.33 W |
| 96 kHz | 4.66 W |

Conduction loss dominates by roughly 9:1. At this current, **48 kHz is affordable**. Its benefit is smoother current and quieter motors; the thermal penalty is small.

## 3. Total Loss Budget at 80 A Burst (Hot, T_j = 125 °C)

| Term | Per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FET) | 20.5 W | 82.0 W |
| Switching + C_oss + Q_rr + dead time | 2.33 W | 9.3 W |
| PCB copper | 2.6 W | 10.4 W |
| Shunt | 1.6 W | 6.4 W |
| Gate drive / driver IC | 0.04 W | 0.15 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 27 W** | **≈ 108 W** |

**Efficiency check:**
- Input power ≈ 4 × 80 A × 25.2 V × D. At D ≈ 0.9 this is ~7.3 kW.
- η ≈ 98.5%.

Efficiency is not the problem. **Heat density is:** about 108 W from a 4 cm² board.

## 4. Thermal Model

### 4.1 Network (per FET, lumped)

```
P_FET → [R_th,jc ≈ 1.5 K/W] → case/pad → [R_th,pcb-spread ≈ 8–15 K/W per FET footprint]
      → board (C_board) → [R_th,ba] → ambient
```

### 4.2 Junction-to-board rise
- Per-FET dissipation ≈ 27 W / 6 ≈ **4.5 W**
- Junction-to-case rise: ΔT_jc = 4.5 × 1.5 ≈ **7 K** (fast, <10 ms)
- Local spreading rise: ΔT_spread ≈ 4.5 × ~4 K/W ≈ **18 K**
  - This assumes heavy via stitching under the drain pads into inner planes.
  - It is the dominant local gradient.

### 4.3 Board-level transient (the real limit)
Heat capacity estimate, assuming ~4.5 g of effective copper/FR4/component mass:
- Copper: ~2.5 g × 0.385 J/gK ≈ 0.96 J/K
- FR4 + components: ~2 g × ~0.9 J/gK ≈ 1.8 J/K
- **C_board ≈ 2.8 J/K**

Board-to-ambient thermal resistance:
- Prop-wash convection: h ≈ 60–100 W/m²K over ~8 cm² (both sides) → R_th,ba ≈ 12–20 K/W
- Thermal time constant: τ = R·C ≈ 35–55 s

Board temperature rise:

$$
\Delta T_{board}(t) = P R_{th,ba}\left(1 - e^{-t/\tau}\right)
\;\approx\; \frac{P}{C}\,t \quad (t \ll \tau)
$$

$$
\frac{dT}{dt} = \frac{108}{2.8} \approx 39\ \text{K/s}
$$
