## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in six-step complementary PWM

In any 60° commutation sector, one phase (A) is PWM-switched, one phase (B) is held low-side ON, and one phase (C) floats for BEMF sensing. With PWM duty D:

- **HS_A** (A high-side) conducts during D.
- **LS_A** (A low-side, synchronous rectification) conducts during (1 − D).
- **LS_B** (B low-side) conducts the full sector.

The motor current I_m is assumed continuous because the winding inductance is large relative to T_sw = 20.8 µs.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_J) = R_{DS,25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_J - 25)\right]$$

At T_J = 125 °C:

R_DS = 1.2 mΩ × (1 + 0.0045 × 100) = **1.74 mΩ**

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond} = I_m^2 R_{DS}(T_J)\,[D + (1-D)] + I_m^2 R_{DS}(T_J) = 2\,I_m^2\,R_{DS}(T_J)$$

This result is independent of D. Synchronous rectification makes the PWM leg look like one continuous R_DS.

At I_m = 80 A:

P_cond = 2 × 6400 × 1.74×10⁻³ = **22.3 W per channel**

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution

Each phase leg spends 1/3 of the electrical cycle as the PWM leg, 1/3 as the held-low leg, and 1/3 floating.

| FET | Average loss |
|---|---|
| Low-side FET | (1/3)·I²R·(1−D) + (1/3)·I²R |
| High-side FET | (1/3)·I²R·D |

**Worst-case single-FET instantaneous loss** occurs on LS_B during its held sector:

80² × 1.74 mΩ = **11.1 W**

For thermal purposes this is pulsed at the electrical frequency. At 30,000 eRPM the period is 2 ms, which is shorter than the FET die-plus-case time constant (~5–20 ms). Use the 1/3-averaged value for steady state and the instantaneous value for Z_θ(t) peak checks.
