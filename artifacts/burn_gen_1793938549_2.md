# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 80 A ESC: System Design Package (Job #70)

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Three issues in the brief need resolving before the math means anything.

1. **The STM32H743 does not run the ESC.** Production 4-in-1 ESCs use one small MCU per motor, typically AT32F421, STM32G071 or GD32 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Those MCUs do the commutation, the 48 kHz PWM and the back-EMF zero-cross detection. The H743 is the flight controller: it runs ArduPilot and talks to the four ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. This document models that split.

2. **80 A per motor on a "nano" board is a burst rating only.** It is never a continuous rating. Section 3 shows the copper heats at roughly 30–40 K/s at full burst, so 80 A is a 1–3 second capability. Treat the result as a design constraint, not a marketing number.

3. **Every part number, pin and coefficient here is a representative engineering assumption.** Before layout release, validate against:
   - the chosen MOSFET and driver datasheets,
   - the STM32H743 datasheet and reference manual (RM0433) alternate-function tables,
   - the current ArduPilot `hwdef` documentation.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in six-step commutation

At any instant, exactly two phases conduct:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** the high-side FET carries current for duty D. With synchronous rectification, the low-side FET of the same leg carries it for (1 − D).
- **Phase B (return leg):** the low-side FET is held ON for the whole 60° electrical sector.

So the motor current always passes through two FET channels in series, whatever the duty cycle.

### 1.2 Per-motor conduction loss

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 \left[ D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS} + R_{LS,ret} \right] + I^2 R_{Cu}
$$

With R_HS = R_LS = R_DS(on)(T_j):

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 \left( 2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + R_{Cu} \right)
$$

Temperature dependence of R_DS(on):

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

**Numbers at 80 A (I² = 6400 A²):**

| Term | R | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| FETs, T_j = 125 °C | 2 × 1.6 mΩ = 3.2 mΩ | **20.5 W** |
| PCB copper and solder (phase + DC-link path) | ≈ 0.5 mΩ | 3.2 W |
| **Per motor** | | **≈ 23.7 W** |
| **Four motors** | | **≈ 95 W** |

For comparison, at T_j = 25 °C the FET term is 12.8 W per motor. That gap creates the positive feedback loop in Section 3.4.

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts for about 1/3 of the electrical period (120°). At typical loaded speeds the electrical frequency is about 3.5 kHz (for example, 14-pole motor at ~30k RPM → 7 pole pairs). That is far faster than the junction thermal time constant (~1–10 ms), so the average power is what matters:

$$
\bar{P}_{FET} \approx \frac{20.5\ \text{W}}{6} \approx 3.4\ \text{W}
$$

The instantaneous conduction loss in a single FET is I² · R_DS(on) = 6400 × 1.6 mΩ ≈ 10.2 W.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. The return leg's low-side FET is static.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2}\, V_{bus}\, I\, (t_r + t_f)\, f_{sw}
= 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 35\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.69\ W}
$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

The complementary FET's body diode conducts during dead time and must recover when the opposite FET turns on:

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\, V_{bus}\, f_{sw} = 30\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}
$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$
P_{oss} \approx \tfrac{1}{2}\, Q_{oss}\, V_{bus}\, f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 40\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.024\ \text{W}
$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

There are two dead-time intervals per PWM period:

$$
P_{dt} = V_f\, I \cdot 2\, t_{dead}\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}
$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

Two switching FETs in the PWM leg:

$$
P_{g} = 2\, Q_g\, V_{drv}\, f_{sw} = 2 \times 40\,\text{nC} \times 10 \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.038\ \text{W}
$$

Most of this is dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FET.

### 2.6 Switching summary

| Loss component | Per motor (W) |
|---|---|
| V·I overlap | 1.69 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 |
| Q_rr | 0.04 |
| Q_oss | 0.02 |
| Gate drive | 0.04 |
| **Total switching** | **≈ 2.4** |
