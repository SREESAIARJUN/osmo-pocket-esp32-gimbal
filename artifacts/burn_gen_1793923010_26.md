**Feasibility flag (read first).** "80 A burst" on a nano-class 4-in-1 is a marketing-tier number. The model below shows it is only achievable as a short transient, about 1–2 s from a warm start. It is not achievable as a sustained rating. If "80 A" means the total across all four motors, everything below becomes comfortable. Confirm which definition the spec intends.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

With 6-step commutation and complementary PWM, two MOSFETs always carry the phase current:

- During the ON interval: the PWM-leg high-side FET plus the static low-side FET of the return phase.
- During the OFF interval: the PWM-leg low-side FET (synchronous rectification) plus the same static low-side FET.

Ignoring ripple, the path resistance is therefore constant regardless of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Including inductor ripple ΔI (peak-to-peak) gives the rms correction:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

At 48 kHz with typical motor inductance (~10–20 µH), ΔI is about 5–10 A at 80 A. That adds less than 0.2% and is neglected.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]$$

### 1.3 Electro-thermal fixed point and runaway criterion

$$T_j = T_{ref} + R_{\theta} \cdot \left(I^2 \cdot 2R_{ds25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] + P_{other}\right)$$

$$T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + R_\theta\left(2I^2R_{ds25}(1-25\alpha) + P_{other}\right)}{1 - 2\alpha I^2 R_{ds25} R_\theta}$$
