## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Topology basis

Assume six-step (trapezoidal) drive with **synchronous rectification** (complementary PWM). Under that scheme, current always flows through exactly two MOSFET channels in series:

- **During PWM on-time:** high-side of phase A, then low-side of phase B.
- **During PWM off-time:** synchronous low-side of phase A, then low-side of phase B.

The series resistance is therefore 2·R_DS(on) regardless of duty cycle (dead time excepted).

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0053\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

$$R_{DS}(110^\circ C) \approx 1.0 \times 1.45 = 1.45\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Conduction loss per ESC channel

$$P_{cond} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j) = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.45\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{18.56\ W}$$

### 1.4 Parasitic copper and connections

Assume ~0.25 mΩ of effective path resistance per channel (motor pads plus bus copper):

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.25\times10^{-3} = 1.6\ \text{W}$$

### 1.5 Per-device distribution

Each of the 6 FETs conducts for roughly 1/3 of the electrical cycle. The electrical period (~1–3 ms) is far shorter than the package thermal time constant, so losses average:

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{18.56}{6} \approx 3.1\ \text{W (average per FET)}$$

The commutating high-side FET carries the switching loss on top of this (Section 2).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Hard-switching (V–I overlap) loss

Only the PWM'd switch hard-switches each cycle:

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$
$$= 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\times10^{-9} \times 48\times10^{3} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance (C_oss) loss

Two devices per switching node:

$$P_{oss} = 2 \cdot \tfrac12 C_{oss(er)} V^2 f_{sw} = 1.5\text{n} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} = 0.046\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

Two edges per PWM cycle:

$$P_{dt} = V_F \cdot I \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

Dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FET die:

$$P_{gate} = 6 \cdot Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 6 \times 50\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.144\ \text{W}$$

### 2.6 Loss summary per ESC channel at 80 A burst

| Loss term | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction (2·R_DS, hot) | 18.56 |
| Copper / pads | 1.60 |
| Switching overlap | 1.94 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 |
| Q_rr + C_oss | 0.09 |
| Gate drive | 0.14 |
| **Total per channel** | **≈ 22.9 W** |
| **4 channels simultaneous** | **≈ 92 W** |
