# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: 80 A Burst Thermal, 48 kHz Switching, and Bidirectional DShot600 Design Package

## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

**The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no in-tree BLDC commutation for the H7. Every viable 4-in-1 design uses this split:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (400–480 MHz) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, DShot600 frame generation, eRPM capture |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 | AM32 | 6-step commutation, **48 kHz PWM**, bidirectional DShot decode and telemetry |
| Gate drive ×4 | 3-phase half-bridge driver (FD6288Q class) | — | Gate drive with bootstrap |
| Power ×24 | 40 V N-MOSFET, ~1 mΩ class | — | 6 per motor |

Design assumptions, which you must replace with your actual datasheet values:

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S full) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst current per motor | I_M | 80 A |
| R_DS(on) max at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R₂₅ | 1.2 mΩ |
| R_DS(on) temperature coefficient | α | 0.005 /K |
| Switching charge (Q_gs2 + Q_gd) | Q_sw | 12 nC |
| Effective gate current (source / sink) | I_g | 0.6 A / 1.0 A |
| Output charge at 25 V | Q_oss | 40 nC |
| Reverse-recovery charge at 80 A | Q_rr | 60 nC |
| Body-diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Dead time | t_dt | 150 ns |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| Phase inductance, line-to-line | L | 20 µH |
| Bus shunt per ESC | R_sh | 0.25 mΩ |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step with synchronous rectification
In each 60° sector, one phase's high side (HS) is PWM'd at duty D and one other phase's low side (LS) is held on. With complementary PWM, the PWM'd phase's LS conducts during (1−D).

$$P_{cond}=I_{rms}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\big[\underbrace{D}_{HS}+\underbrace{(1-D)}_{LS\,sync}+\underbrace{1}_{LS\,on}\big]=2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

The loss is **independent of duty**. This is why 80 A at partial throttle is just as hot as at full throttle.
