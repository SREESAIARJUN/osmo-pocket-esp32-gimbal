## 0. Feasibility Findings

These findings set the design envelope for everything below.

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | The H743 runs ArduPilot only. Commutation must run on dedicated ESC MCUs (4× AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or BLHeli_32-class firmware). | Bidirectional DShot is the H743↔ESC interface. The H743 never drives gates. |
| F2 | 80A per phase channel on a "nano" (20×20 mm) board is not physically achievable, even as a burst. At 30×30 mm it is achievable only as a time-limited burst of a few seconds. | Section 3 derives the burst time limit. Treat 80A as a 2–5 s rating, not continuous. |
| F3 | Four motors at 80A and full throttle draw about 320A DC bus current. | Battery leads, XT90-class connectors, and bus capacitors dominate the design. Copper loss is included in the model. |
| F4 | All MOSFET and driver parameters below are stated assumptions for a 40V, about 0.7 mΩ PQFN-class part. | Substitute datasheet values before release. The model is parametric. |

**Baseline assumptions**

- Supply: 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2V max, 22.2V nominal.
- MOSFET: 40V, R_ds(on),25 = 0.70 mΩ @ V_gs = 10V, Q_g = 40 nC, Q_gd = 8 nC, C_oss,eff = 1.5 nF, Q_rr = 50 nC, V_f = 0.8V.
- Gate drive: 10V, 1.5A effective peak, R_g,ext = 2.2 Ω.
- f_sw = 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous) PWM, dead time t_dt = 100 ns.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

With α ≈ 0.0055 /K:

$$R_{ds}(125^\circ C) = 0.70 \times 1.55 \approx 1.09\ \text{m}\Omega \;\rightarrow\; \text{use } 1.1\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current paths in 6-step trapezoidal drive

In any 60° commutation sector:

- Leg A is PWM'd: the high side conducts for fraction D and the low side for (1 − D) (synchronous rectification).
- Leg B holds its low side ON continuously.
- Leg C floats and is used for BEMF sensing.

So exactly two FETs carry the phase current I at every instant, regardless of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Over one electrical cycle, each FET carries current for 120°. Its RMS current depends on D. At full-throttle burst (D → 1), the losses equalise:

$$I_{rms,FET} = I\sqrt{1/3}, \qquad P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I^2 R_{ds}}{3}$$

### 1.4 Numbers at 80A burst, T_j = 125°C

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.1\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{14.08\ W}$$

$$P_{cond,FET} = 14.08/6 = \mathbf{2.35\ W}\ \text{(average)}$$

### 1.5 PCB copper loss (per phase path)

Assume 2 oz copper, a 6 mm-wide pour of 15 mm effective length, and 4 layers in parallel.

$$R_{sheet,2oz} \approx 0.25\ \text{m}\Omega/\square, \quad n_\square = 15/6 = 2.5$$

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{0.25 \times 2.5}{4} = 0.16\ \text{m}\Omega$$

Two phase paths are active, plus the bus share (≈0.1 mΩ, carrying the full motor current):

$$P_{Cu,motor} \approx 80^2(2\times0.16 + 0.1)\times10^{-3} \approx \mathbf{2.7\ W}$$

**This is not negligible: it is about 19% of FET conduction loss.**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Transition times (gate-charge method)

$$t_{r} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}+Q_{gs2}}{I_{g}} = \frac{8\,\text{nC} + 4\,\text{nC}}{1.5\ \text{A}} \approx 8\ \text{ns}$$

Common-source inductance and layout parasitics in a dense AIO add margin. Design value: t_r ≈ t_f ≈ 15 ns.

### 2.2 Hard-switching V·I overlap

Only the PWM'd high side hard-switches. The low side commutates with ZVS (zero-voltage switching) through its body diode.

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

### 2.3 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.4 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = V_{bus} Q_{rr} f_{sw} = 25.2 \times 50\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$
