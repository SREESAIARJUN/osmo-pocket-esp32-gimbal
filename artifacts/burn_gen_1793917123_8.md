# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bidirectional DShot600 Design Model

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I don't have the Job #26 design package (BOM, stackup, MOSFET selection, board outline). Everything below is a **parameterized reference model**. Every assumed value is marked **[A]**. Replace these with your datasheet and layout values. The equations still hold.

Two architectural points need to be settled first.

1. **The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC MCU.** The 4-in-1 ESC runs its own MCU (AT32F421, STM32G071, or EFM8 class) with AM32, BLHeli_32, or Bluejay firmware. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to the ESC.
2. **Reality check on "80 A burst" in a nano form factor (20×20 mm or 25.5×25.5 mm).** The math in §2–§3 shows roughly 16–17 W per channel at 80 A. With all four motors bursting, that is about 65 W in a board weighing a few grams. That is only survivable for seconds, and only with propwash airflow. Treat "80 A" as a short, single-event burst rating, not a continuous one. The model below quantifies the allowable burst duration.

## 1. Assumed Operating Point

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S, full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current (per ESC channel) | I_pk | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET | — | 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN **[A]** |
| R_DS(on) at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R₂₅ | 0.70 mΩ **[A]** |
| R_DS(on) temperature coefficient (25→125 °C) | k_T | 1.5 **[A]** |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 60 nC **[A]** |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 50 nC **[A]** |
| Rise / fall time with driver | t_r, t_f | 25 ns / 25 ns **[A]** |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns **[A]** |
| Body-diode forward voltage at 80 A | V_F | 0.85 V **[A]** |
| Gate drive voltage | V_GS | 10 V **[A]** (verify the driver can deliver it) |

Commutation is assumed to be 6-step trapezoidal with synchronous rectification and complementary PWM (AM32/BLHeli_32 default). At any instant, two legs carry the phase current. One leg is PWM-switched; the other is held on.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Hot R_DS(on)

$$
R_{hot} = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right] \approx k_T R_{25}
$$

$$
R_{hot} = 1.5 \times 0.70 = 1.05\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

### 2.2 Current path per channel

- **PWM on-time (fraction D):** current flows through HS(leg A), the motor, then LS(leg B). That is two FETs in series.
- **PWM off-time (1−D), synchronous rectification:** current flows through LS(leg A), the motor, then LS(leg B). That is also two FETs in series.

The current always sees two R_DS(on) in series, independent of D:

$$
P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{hot}
$$

At burst, phase current is roughly flat-top, so I_rms ≈ I_pk = 80 A:

$$
P_{cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.05\times10^{-3} = 13.44\ \text{W per channel}
$$

### 2.3 Copper and interconnect loss (often ignored, but significant)

A 2 oz outer layer plus a 2 oz inner pour has a sheet resistance of about 0.25 mΩ/□ per oz. For roughly 4 squares of effective path with paralleled layers, assume 0.3 mΩ **[A]** total, including solder joints and the motor-pad interface:

$$
P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.3\times10^{-3} = 1.92\ \text{W per channel}
$$

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one leg is hard-switched per PWM cycle. The other leg commutates at the electrical rate, which is negligible next to 48 kHz.

### 3.1 V–I overlap (hard switching of the HS FET)

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}
$$

$$
= 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 50\times10^{-9} \times 48\,000 = 2.42\ \text{W}
$$

### 3.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction (two dead-time intervals per cycle)

$$
P_{dt} = V_F \cdot I \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw}
$$

$$
= 0.85 \times 80 \times 200\times10^{-9} \times 48\,000 = 0.65\ \text{W}
$$

### 3.3 Reverse recovery

$$
P_{rr} \approx Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw}
$$

With Q_rr ≈ 40 nC **[A]**, this gives 40 nC × 25.2 V × 48 kHz = **0.05 W**. Q_rr rises with di/dt and temperature, so add margin.

### 3.4 Output capacitance

$$
P_{oss} \approx Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,000 = 0.06\ \text{W}
$$

### 3.5 Gate drive (HS and LS of the switching leg)

$$
P_g = 2 Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 60\,\text{nC} \times 10 \times 48\,000 = 0.058\ \text{W}
$$

This power is dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FET.

### 3.6 Per-channel total at 80 A burst

| Loss term | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction (2 × R_hot) | 13.44 |
| PCB copper / joints | 1.92 |
| V–I overlap switching | 2.42 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.65 |
| Q_rr + Q_oss + gate | 0.17 |
| **Total per channel** | **≈ 18.6 W** |
| **4 channels simultaneous** | **≈ 74 W** |
