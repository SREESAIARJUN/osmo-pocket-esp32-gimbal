# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #67): Engineering Model

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Several premises in the brief need correcting before the math is useful.

| Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|
| The STM32H743 does not drive the ESC bridges. ArduPilot does not run sensorless BLDC commutation. | The H743 is the flight controller. It sends bidirectional DShot600 to 4 ESC MCUs (e.g., 4× AT32F421 running AM32). Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM. |
| DShot and 48 kHz are unrelated. | DShot600 is the FC→ESC command link. 48 kHz is the ESC's MOSFET PWM frequency. |
| An 80 A burst on a "nano" (20×20 mm) board is at or beyond physical limits. | It is modelled below as a time-limited burst. The model shows it is achievable for roughly 1–2 s per event, and only with a 25.5×25.5 mm or larger ESC board. |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Temperature-dependent on-resistance**

R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 110 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × 1.425 = **1.71 mΩ**

**Current path per commutation step.** At any instant, two legs are active:
- Driven leg: the high side conducts for D·T, the low side for (1−D)·T.
- Return leg: the low side conducts 100 % of the time.

P_cond,ch = I²R·D + I²R·(1−D) + I²R = **2·I²·R_ds(T_j)**

This is independent of D, provided ripple is small compared with I.

**Per-FET average over one electrical cycle.** Each phase spends 120° high, 120° low and 120° floating.

- P_HS = I²R · D/3
- P_LS = I²R · (1−D)/3 + I²R/3

**Numbers at 80 A**

- I²R = 6400 × 1.71e-3 = 10.94 W
- P_cond,ch = **21.9 W per channel**
- At D ≈ 0.9: P_HS = 3.28 W and P_LS = 4.01 W. The **low side is the hottest device.**

**Copper loss.** Phase traces plus bus planes are about 0.3 mΩ per channel effective at 2 oz × 4 layers.

P_Cu ≈ 2 × 80² × 0.3e-3 ≈ **3.8 W per channel**

Do not ignore copper loss. At 80 A it is a significant term.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the PWM-ing leg switches. The bridge always has exactly one switching leg.

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f | ½·25.2·80·30e-9·48e3 = **1.45 W** |
| C_oss | ½·C_oss·V²·f | **0.023 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f | **0.048 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V_SD·I·2·t_dt·f | 0.8·80·200e-9·48e3 = **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (×2 FETs) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f | **0.048 W** (dissipated in driver/R_g) |

- P_sw,ch ≈ **2.18 W**
- Per FET (each HS switches for 1/3 of the cycle): ≈ 0.48 W on the high side

The dead-time diode loss is about 40 % of the overlap loss. Minimising dead time (AM32 setting) is worth roughly 0.3 W per channel.

**Total per channel at 80 A burst**

P_ch = 21.9 + 2.2 + 3.8 ≈ **27.9 W**

With all four channels at burst: **≈ 112 W.**

**Bulk capacitor ripple**

I_C,rms ≈ I·√(D(1−D)) = 80 × 0.3 = **24 A rms per channel at D = 0.9**

- Required: ≥ 4× 35 V low-ESR polymer/ceramic banks on the ESC.
- Required: an external 1000 µF low-ESR electrolytic at the battery pads.

## 3. Thermal Model

Steady state is impossible. A 20×20 board with prop-wash cooling has R_th,b-a ≈ 10 K/W, which would give ΔT > 1000 K at 112 W. The burst must therefore be treated transiently.

**Two-node Foster network**

- Node J (FET junction): R_th,j-b = 2.0 K/W (R_jc ≈ 1.0 plus solder/via array ≈ 1.0), τ_jb ≈ 10 ms
- Node B (board): C_th,B ≈ m_Cu·c_Cu + m_FR4·c_FR4 + m_FET·c_Si
