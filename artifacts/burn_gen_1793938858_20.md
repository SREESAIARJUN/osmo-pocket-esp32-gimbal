## 1. Electrical Model of One ESC Channel

In 6-step commutation at any instant:

- One leg is **PWM-switched**. Its high side conducts for D, and its low side synchronously rectifies for 1−D.
- One leg is **held low**. Its low side conducts 100%.
- One leg floats (BEMF sense).

The phase current I therefore always flows through **exactly two MOSFET channels in series**. Ripple is neglected because 48 kHz into motor inductances of ≥ 10 µH gives ΔI ≪ I at 80 A.

**Temperature-dependent on-resistance:**

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /\text{K}$$

$$R_{ds}(125^\circ C) = 1.0\,(1+0.5) = 1.5\ \text{m}\Omega$$

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Per channel

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 12.8 W |
| 100 °C | 1.375 mΩ | 17.6 W |
| 125 °C | 1.5 mΩ | **19.2 W** |

### 2.2 Per-FET distribution (needed for junction rise)

Over one electrical cycle, each of the 6 FETs takes an equal share:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{2 I^2 R_{ds}}{6} = 3.2\ \text{W @125 °C}$$

**Instantaneous worst case.** A stalled or low-eRPM rotor parks the current in one FET pair. The FET then sees up to I²R = 9.6 W until commutation. This drives the R_θJC transient check in §4.3.

### 2.3 Copper and shunt conduction (frequently underestimated)

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 (R_{trace,phase} + R_{trace,return}) ,\quad R_{Cu}= \rho\frac{\ell}{w\,t}\,[1+0.0039(T-20)]$$

The design target is **≤ 0.4 mΩ total path per channel**. That requires stitched phase pours on 4 layers of 2 oz (70 µm), w ≈ 4 mm, ℓ ≈ 12 mm, with ~0.42 mΩ per layer in parallel.

- P_Cu ≈ 80² × 0.4 m = **2.6 W/channel** (hot)
