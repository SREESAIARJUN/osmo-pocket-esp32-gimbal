## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

The STM32H743 should not commutate the FETs directly. Bidirectional DShot is a protocol between the flight controller and separate ESC MCUs. The architecture this document assumes:

| Block | Part (assumed) | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller MCU | STM32H743VIT6 @ 400 MHz (LQFP100) | ArduPilot, DShot600 TX + eRPM RX |
| ESC MCU ×4 | STM32G071GBU6 (or AT32F421) running AM32 | Field-oriented / 6-step commutation, BDShot responder |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) | 3× half-bridge drive |
| MOSFET ×24 (or ×48 paralleled) | 40 V, R_DS(on) ≈ 1.0 mΩ @ 25 °C, PDFN 5×6 or 3.3×3.3 | Power stage |
| Supply | 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V | |
| PWM | f_sw = 48 kHz, center-aligned, complementary, synchronous rectification | |

**Red flag on "80A burst" in a nano form factor.** A 20×20 mm or 25.5×25.5 mm board cannot sustain 80 A per channel. The math below shows this is thermally **burst-only, governed by heat capacity, not steady-state resistance**. I size for 80 A for ≤ 5–10 s and roughly 40–45 A continuous with airflow.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\text{–}0.008\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

Assumed values:
- R_DS,25 = 1.0 mΩ
- α = 0.0067 K⁻¹
- At T_j = 100 °C: **R_DS ≈ 1.50 mΩ**
- At T_j = 125 °C: **R_DS ≈ 1.67 mΩ**

### 1.2 Current path

With 6-step or sinusoidal synchronous drive, the phase current always flows through exactly **two switch positions in series**: one high-side and one low-side, in either the active or freewheel state.

For n_p FETs paralleled per switch position:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot \frac{R_{DS}(T_j)}{n_p}$$

For FOC, use the sinusoidal RMS (I_rms = I_peak/√2) across 3 phases:

$$P_{cond,ch}^{FOC} = 3 \cdot I_{rms}^2 \cdot \frac{R_{DS}}{n_p}$$

For six-step, with I_ph the DC-equivalent current in two active legs, the 2× form above applies. Six-step is used below because it is the worst case at burst.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 100 °C

| Config | P_cond per channel | P_cond ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| n_p = 1 (6 FETs/ch) | 2·80²·1.5 mΩ = **19.2 W** | 76.8 W |
| n_p = 2 (12 FETs/ch) | **9.6 W** | 38.4 W |

With n_p = 1, a single FET dissipates ≈ 9.6 W during its conduction window. In a 3.3×3.3 package this is not survivable beyond milliseconds. **n_p = 2 in PDFN5×6 is the minimum for an 80 A claim.** The remainder of this document assumes n_p = 2.

### 1.4 PCB copper conduction loss (often forgotten)

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 \cdot \frac{\rho_{Cu}(T)\, L}{w\, t}$$

Assumptions: 2 oz (70 µm) copper, 4 layers paralleled for the bus, 25 mm path, 8 mm width.

- R ≈ 1.72e-8 · 0.025 / (0.008 · 4 · 70e-6) ≈ **0.19 mΩ** per path, rising ≈ 0.25 mΩ hot
- Phase out + return gives ≈ 2 paths
- 80² · 0.5 mΩ ≈ **3.2 W per channel**

This is comparable to the FET loss. Use 3 oz copper or bus-bar solder strips if the budget allows.
