# STM32H743 Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #37): Systems Architecture Package

## 0. Architectural Clarifications

**1. The H743 is the flight controller MCU, not the ESC MCU.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 and emits bi-directional DShot600. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU. This design assumes four AT32F421 MCUs running AM32, because AM32 supports 48 kHz PWM and bi-directional DShot. The ArduPilot DMA allocation therefore covers DShot outputs and telemetry capture only. The 48 kHz switching is generated on the ESC MCUs.

**2. "80A burst" and "nano" are thermally incompatible.** The math in §3 shows that a 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm stack can survive 80 A per channel only for about 1–3 s with all four motors loaded. Continuous rating is roughly 15–25 A per channel with prop-wash cooling. The burst spec should be written as a duration-limited rating: **"80 A for ≤2 s at ≤50 °C board start temperature, single-axis transient."**

**3. All device parameters below are representative placeholders.** They are typical of 40 V PDFN3.3×3.3 and 5×6 MOSFETs used in 6S ESCs. Replace them with datasheet values from the selected FET before release. The formulas are exact; the numbers are illustrative.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous PWM)

### 1.1 Current path

In each 60° commutation sector:
- Phase A high-side (HS) is PWM'd at duty D.
- Phase A low-side (LS) is the complementary synchronous rectifier, conducting for (1 − D).
- Phase B LS is on for the whole sector (return path).

So exactly two FET positions carry I at any instant:

$$
P_{cond,bridge} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[\,D + (1-D) + 1\,] = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

The result is independent of D, because synchronous rectification removes the duty dependence.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 1.2 × (1 + 0.0053 × 85) = **1.74 mΩ**

### 1.3 Per-FET RMS over one electrical cycle

Each leg's HS conducts during its 120° window, and each LS during its 120° window:

$$
I_{rms,HS} = I\sqrt{D/3}, \qquad I_{rms,LS} = I\sqrt{\tfrac{1}{3}(1) + \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)}\ \text{(worst-case LS, return + sync)}
$$

With symmetric commutation and averaged over the cycle, the per-FET average is:

$$
\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{2I^2R_{ds}}{6} = \frac{I^2R_{ds}}{3}
$$

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 110 °C

- P_cond,bridge = 2 × 6400 × 1.74×10⁻³ = **22.3 W per ESC channel**
- Per-FET average = 3.71 W

Additional I²R losses (add to the board heat budget):

| Source | Resistance | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Shunt | 0.5 mΩ | 3.2 W |
| Copper traces/planes | ~0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
