### ⚠️ Three Architectural Red Flags

Resolve these before layout.

1. **"Nano" form factor vs. 24 MOSFETs.**
   - 24 × 5×6 DFN = 720 mm² of package footprint. That exceeds a 20×20 board (400 mm²) before you place a single routing channel.
   - A nano AIO forces option B (3.3×3.3 parts). Option B has roughly 50 % more R_ds(on), which makes the conduction loss worse.
   - **80 A per motor on a nano board is a ~1–2 second burst rating, never a continuous one.** Section 3 quantifies this.
2. **Total bus current.** 4 × 80 A = **320 A** burst.
   - An XT60 (~60 A continuous) is inadequate. Use XT90 or direct-solder 10 AWG.
   - The ground-return and V_bat copper (Section 5.4) becomes a design driver in its own right.
3. **Bulk capacitor ripple current.**
   - Input cap RMS current per motor is I·√(D(1−D)), which reaches **40 A RMS** at D = 0.5.
   - On-board MLCCs alone cannot support this. A low-ESR external electrolytic at the pads is mandatory.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path Topology

In 6-step operation with complementary PWM, exactly two phase legs are active at any instant:

- **PWM leg:** HS conducts for D·T_sw. LS conducts (synchronous rectification) for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Return leg:** LS conducts for 100 % of the sector.

So **two R_ds(on) are always in series with the phase current**, regardless of duty cycle.

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j) + I_{rms}^2 \cdot R_{Cu}$$

### 1.2 Ripple Correction

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{DC}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph} f_{sw}}$$

Take a typical 2207 motor with L_ph ≈ 20 µH, D = 0.5, and V_bus − V_bemf ≈ 6 V at load:

- ΔI_pp ≈ 3.1 A
- ΔI²/12 ≈ 0.8 A², which is negligible against 6400 A²

At 48 kHz ripple can be ignored. It only matters below ~24 kHz or with low-inductance motors (< 8 µH).

### 1.3 Temperature Coefficient

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0065\ /\mathrm{K}\ (\text{40 V trench})$$

At T_j = 115 °C the factor is ≈ 1.59. Use 1.6.
