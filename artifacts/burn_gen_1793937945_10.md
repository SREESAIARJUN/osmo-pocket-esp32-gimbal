## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility Constraints

Two issues in the brief change the design, so they are addressed first.

**1. The H743 cannot also be the ESC.** ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The correct split is:

| Function | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz, LQFP100) | ArduPilot |
| ESC, 4 independent channels | 4× AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6 | AM32 (or Bluejay on EFM8) |
| Link | Bidirectional DShot600, 1 wire per motor | — |

Using four independent ESC MCUs also contains faults: a single-channel failure does not take down the others.

**2. "Nano" and 80 A burst conflict.** A 20×20 mm board cannot place 24 power MOSFETs, gate drivers, and bulk capacitance while carrying 80 A per phase. This design therefore assumes:

- Two-board stack: 30.5×30.5 mm mounting pattern, ~36×36 mm outline.
- An aluminium heat-spreader on the ESC board.
- "80 A burst" means **one or two motors at a time for a few seconds**. Four simultaneous 80 A channels would demand ~320 A and ~7 kW from the pack, and the thermal numbers in §3 show this cannot be sustained.

**Part numbers are reference candidates.** Every parameter used below must be re-checked against the selected vendor's datasheet before layout freeze.
