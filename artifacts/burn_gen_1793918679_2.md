# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control Loop and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion Design

I don't have the Job #8 requirements document, so this design uses the stated **baseline assumptions** in §0. Every gain is derived from symbolic plant parameters. If your motor, encoder or MCU differs, substitute your values into the formulas; you should not need to re-tune by hand.

## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle
$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi,\qquad \hat\theta_e[k] = \theta_e[k] + p\,\omega_m T_{\text{lat}}$$
Here $T_{\text{lat}}$ is the encoder-to-PWM latency, typically 1.5·Ts. Compensating it removes the speed-dependent d/q cross-coupling error.

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$
$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.3 Plant and PI design by pole-zero cancellation
The plant is
$$v_{d} = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

With decoupling feedforward, each axis reduces to $G(s)=1/(Ls+R)$. Choose the PI controller $C(s)=K_p + K_i/s$ with
$$K_p^{(i)} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_i^{(i)} = R\,\omega_{ci}$$
The zero then cancels the electrical pole, and the closed loop becomes $\omega_{ci}/(s+\omega_{ci})$.

Choose $\omega_{ci} \le 2\pi f_s/20$. At 20 kHz this gives $\omega_{ci} = 2\pi\cdot 1000$ rad/s, so Kp = 13.2 V/A and Ki = 35 186 V/(A·s).

### 1.4 Discretisation, decoupling and anti-windup
Each axis uses a backward-Euler PI with back-calculation anti-windup:
$$u^* = K_p e + I[k] + v_{\text{ff}},\qquad u = \text{sat}(u^*)$$
$$I[k+1] = I[k] + T_s\big(K_i e + K_{aw}(u-u^*)\big),\qquad K_{aw} = K_i/K_p$$

The feedforward terms are
$$v_{d,\text{ff}} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_{q,\text{ff}} = \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda_m),\qquad \lambda_m = \tfrac{2K_t}{3p}$$

### 1.5 Voltage limit
The linear SVPWM region is a circle of radius $V_{\max} = V_{dc}/\sqrt3$. Saturation is d-priority:
$$v_d \leftarrow \text{clamp}(v_d,\pm V_{\max}),\qquad v_q \leftarrow \text{clamp}\!\left(v_q,\pm\sqrt{V_{\max}^2 - v_d^2}\right)$$

### 1.6 SVPWM by min-max injection
This is equivalent to sector-based SVPWM:
$$v_{a,b,c} = \text{invClarke}(v_\alpha,v_\beta),\qquad v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc})+\min(v_{abc})\big)$$
$$d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{dc}}$$
