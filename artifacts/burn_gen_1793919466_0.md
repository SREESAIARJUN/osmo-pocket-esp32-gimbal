## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

In each 60° sector, two phases conduct:

- **PWM leg:** the high side (HS) conducts for D·T_sw. The complementary low side (LS) conducts for (1−D)·T_sw, minus dead time.
- **Return leg:** the LS is continuously on.

So at every instant **exactly two channel-FETs carry I_ph** (ignoring the dead-time diode interval):

$$
P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]
$$

### 1.2 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts in 2 of 6 sectors (1/3 of the electrical period). Over a full electrical cycle:

$$
P_{HS,avg}=\tfrac{1}{3}D\,I^2R_{DS},\qquad P_{LS,avg}=\tfrac{1}{3}\left[(1-D)+1\right]\cdot\tfrac{1}{2}\cdot I^2R_{DS}\;\;(\text{sector-averaged})
$$

**The instantaneous peak per FET is what drives transient junction temperature:**

$$
P_{FET,inst}=I^2R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A burst

Assume T_j = 110 °C, so R_DS = 1.2 mΩ × (1 + 0.006 × 85) = **1.81 mΩ**.

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| P_FET,inst | 80² × 1.81 mΩ = **11.6 W** |
| P_cond per ESC channel | 2 × 11.6 = **23.2 W** |
| P_cond, 4 channels | **92.7 W** |

### 1.4 PCB copper loss (frequently omitted)

Bus-to-FET-to-phase-pad path, 2 oz outer layers plus stitched inner layers, R_Cu ≈ 0.4–0.6 mΩ per channel:

$$
P_{Cu}=I^2R_{Cu}\approx 80^2\times0.5\,\text{m}\Omega=3.2\ \text{W/ch}
$$
