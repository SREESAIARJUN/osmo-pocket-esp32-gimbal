## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation Topology

The drive is trapezoidal 6-step with **complementary (synchronous) PWM**, which is the AM32 default. At any instant, two phase legs carry current I:

- **PWM leg:** current alternates between HS (duty D) and LS (1–D, synchronous rectification).
- **Static leg:** the LS FET is held ON continuously.

The current path therefore always contains exactly two R_DS(on) in series, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{RMS}^2 \cdot 2 R_{DS(on),hot} \cdot \left(1 + \frac{(\Delta I/I)^2}{12}\right)$$

The ripple term:

$$\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

For a typical 5" motor (L_ph ≈ 15 µH line-line, D = 0.5, V_bus − V_bemf ≈ 5 V at load), this gives ΔI ≈ 3.5 A. The ripple term is then ≈ 1.00016, which is negligible.

### 1.2 Numeric Evaluation at 80 A

$$R_{DS,hot} = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.6 = 1.6\,\text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.6\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{20.5\ W/motor}$$

Per-FET distribution follows from each FET's conduction duty over an electrical period. Each switch is active for 1/3 of the period, half of that as the PWM'd device and half as the static device:

- **Worst-case FET** (low-side, low throttle, high current): approaches 80² × 1.6 mΩ × (1/3 + 1/3 × (1–D)) ≈ **5.1 W** at D = 0.5.
- **Average per FET:** 20.5 / 6 = **3.4 W**.

### 1.3 PCB Copper Loss

Assume 2 oz outer layers, 1 oz inner layers, and a phase-pad-to-FET path of ≈ 0.3 mΩ per leg:

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 0.3\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{3.8\ W/motor}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. The losses are made up of four components plus gate drive.

### 2.1 V–I Overlap

Gate current during the Miller interval:

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_{drv,src} + R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int}}, \quad I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{drv,snk} + R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int}}$$

Using V_drv = 10 V, R_src = 10 Ω, R_snk = 5 Ω, R_ext = 2.2 Ω, R_g,int ≈ 1 Ω:

- I_g,on = 6.5 / 13.2 = 0.49 A
- I_g,off = 3.5 / 8.2 = 0.43 A

Switching times:

- t_on = (Q_gs2 + Q_gd) / I_g,on = 15 nC / 0.49 A = **30.5 ns**
- t_off = 15 nC / 0.43 A = **35 ns**
