## 1. Loop Architecture and Timing

| Loop | Rate | Feedback | Output | Target bandwidth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz (PWM center-aligned) | Phase shunts | v_d, v_q → SVPWM | ω_c ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s |
| Velocity | 4 kHz | Fused gyro (camera frame → joint) | i_q* | ω_v ≈ 2π·120 rad/s |
| Angle | 1 kHz | Attitude estimate / encoder | ω* | ω_θ ≈ 2π·25 rad/s |
| IMU sample | 8 kHz ODR, FIFO/DRDY | — | Filtered ω, a | — |
| Attitude (Mahony) | 1 kHz | Fused gyro + accel | q̂ | ~0.5 Hz accel crossover |

```
θ*──►[Angle P + FF]──ω*──►[Velocity PI + FF]──iq*──►(+)──►[Current PI dq]──►[SVPWM]──►Motor
          ▲                      ▲                    ▲ iq_cog_ff        ▲
          │ θ̂ (Mahony)           │ ω̂ (dual-IMU,       │                  │ i_abc (shunts)
          │                      │  notched, LPF)     [Anti-cogging LMS(θ_m)]
```

The cascade separation rule is ω_c ≥ 8–10·ω_v and ω_v ≥ 4–5·ω_θ.

## 2. Field-Oriented Current Loop

### 2.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform:**

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)\quad (i_a+i_b+i_c=0)
$$

**Park transform**, with θ_e = p·θ_m + θ_off:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 2.2 Plant (SPMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

**Torque:**

$$
\tau = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q
$$

**Mechanics:**

$$
J\dot\omega_m = K_t i_q - B\omega_m - \tau_{cog}(\theta_m) - \tau_{load}
$$

### 2.3 PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

The control law is:

$$
v_d = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

Pole-zero cancellation of R/L gives first-order closed-loop current dynamics with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_{p,i}=L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i}=R\,\omega_c\quad(\text{continuous } K_i,\ \text{discretized as } K_i T_s)
$$

**Discrete PI (forward Euler) with back-calculation anti-windup.** Here sat() is the voltage-circle limit:

$$
u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\qquad
I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw}T_s(\text{sat}(u_k)-u_k),\quad K_{aw}=K_i/K_p
$$

**Voltage limit (circular).** SVPWM's linear region gives |v_dq| ≤ V_dc/√3. Prioritize d:

$$
v_d \leftarrow \text{clip}(v_d, \pm V_{max}),\qquad
v_q \leftarrow \text{clip}\!\big(v_q,\pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2-v_d^2}\big)
$$

**Delay compensation.** Use θ_e,pwm = θ_e + 1.5·ω_e·T_s for the inverse Park. This covers one sample of computation plus half a PWM period.

### 2.4 SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)

$$
v_a=v_\alpha,\quad v_b=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

$$
v_0=-\tfrac12(\max+\min),\qquad d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{dc}}
$$

This is mathematically identical to sector-based SVPWM and is branch-free.

## 3. Cascaded Velocity / Angle Control

### 3.1 Velocity loop (PI, gyro feedback)

The plant seen by the velocity loop, with the current loop treated as unity at these frequencies, is:

$$
G_v(s) = \frac{K_t}{Js+B}
$$

**Symmetric-optimum-style tuning.** Choose crossover ω_v, then set:

$$
K_{p,v}=\frac{J\,\omega_v}{K_t},\qquad K_{i,v}=\frac{K_{p,v}\,\omega_v}{a^2},\quad a\in[2,4]
$$

Use a = 3, which gives about 53° phase
