## 0. Architectural Ground Truths and Assumptions

Three architectural points shape everything below.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** Bidirectional DShot is a protocol between the flight controller and the ESC. A "4-in-1 ESC + H743" AIO therefore has five MCUs:
   - one STM32H743 running ArduPilot, and
   - four ESC MCUs, typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32. AM32 supports 48 kHz PWM and bidirectional DShot.

   Running ArduPilot and four sensorless BEMF commutation loops on one H743 is not a supported ArduPilot configuration.

2. **"80 A burst" is defined here as 80 A phase current per channel for a bounded duration.** It is not a continuous rating, and it is not 320 A from the battery. Section 3 shows that the nano form factor cannot sustain 80 A continuously. The math returns a burst time and a continuous rating instead.
