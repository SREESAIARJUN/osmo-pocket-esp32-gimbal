## 1. Field-Oriented Control (inner current loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle comes from the encoder, with the zero offset θ_off found at calibration:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, using i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant model and current controller

For an SPMSM (L_d = L_q = L):

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

The controller is a PI with cross-coupling and back-EMF feedforward decoupling:

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

Choosing the gains below makes the PI zero cancel the electrical pole (R/L). The closed loop then becomes first-order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{\text{PWM}}}{10}$$

At 20 kHz, ω_c ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz is a good starting point. Where λ_m = 2K_t/(3p) for the amplitude-invariant convention.

**Voltage limit.** The voltage vector is limited to the linear SVPWM circle:

$$\|v_{dq}\| \le V_{dc}/\sqrt3$$
