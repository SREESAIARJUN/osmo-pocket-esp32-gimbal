## 2. Power-Stage Loss Model (Per ESC Channel)

### 2.1 Topology and Conduction Pattern

The model assumes trapezoidal (6-step) drive with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM on the switching leg. In every 60° commutation sector:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET (duty D) alternates with the low-side FET (duty 1−D, synchronous rectification).
- **Return leg:** one low-side FET conducts continuously.
- **Floating leg:** idle (used for back-EMF sensing).
