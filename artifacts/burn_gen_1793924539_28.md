# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #48): Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Findings

**Architecture.** ArduPilot cannot commutate motors directly. So the H743 is the flight controller and emits bi-directional DShot600. Each of the four ESC channels has its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071. That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. All switching math below concerns the ESC MCU/FET stage. All timer and DMA math concerns the H743.

**Main finding.** At 80 A per channel, a single-FET-per-switch nano power stage (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) dissipates about **125–135 W total**. The board reaches its thermal limit in roughly **1–2 s** when all four channels are loaded simultaneously.

"80 A burst" is therefore defensible only under one of these conditions:
- (a) a burst lasting ≤1 s, or
- (b) a per-channel rather than simultaneous rating, or
- (c) a larger board or paralleled FETs.

Section 4 quantifies this.

All device parameters below are **representative assumptions**, not a specific part. Substitute datasheet values before release.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification

During any 60° sector, the PWM leg conducts through the high side for fraction D and the low side for 1 − D. The return leg's low side conducts continuously. The current path therefore always contains exactly two R_ds(on) in series:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

This is independent of duty cycle. Averaged over an electrical cycle, each of the 6 FETs carries about 1/6 of this, with the low-side static FET carrying slightly more at low D.

### 2.2 Electro-thermal coupling

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per channel (80 A) | P_cond for 4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.50 mΩ | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| 100 °C | 2.06 mΩ | 26.4 W | 105.6 W |
| 125 °C | 2.25 mΩ | 28.8 W | 115.2 W |

### 2.3 Parasitic copper and shunt losses

$$P_{par,ch} = I^2(R_{shunt} + R_{Cu}) = 6400 \times 0.65\,\text{m}\Omega = 4.2\text{ W}$$

### 2.4 Thermal stability criterion

Runaway is avoided if the loop gain stays below 1:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,R_{th} = 2I^2R_{25}\,\alpha\,R_{th,ja,eff} < 1$$

- Per FET: ∂P/∂T ≈ (2·6400·1.5e-3·0.005)/6 = 0.016 W/K.
- With an effective per-FET R_th,ja of about 25 K/W on a nano board, the gain is ≈ 0.4. This is stable, but losses grow about 1.67× from cold to hot, so the model should be iterated (§4.3).

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 3.1 Gate-current-limited transition times

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_{g,tot}} = \frac{10-3.5}{5} = 1.30\text{ A} \;\Rightarrow\; t_{on} = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,on}} = 7.7\text{ ns}$$

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{g,tot}} = 0.70\text{ A} \;\Rightarrow\; t_{off} = 14.3\text{ ns}$$
