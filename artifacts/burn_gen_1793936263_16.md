## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. ArduPilot has no sensorless BLDC commutation firmware, and running four 48 kHz six-step controllers on the FC MCU would conflict with the scheduler. The correct partitioning is:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight control | STM32H743VIH6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) | Attitude control, DShot600 master, eRPM consumer |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) | AM32 (or BLHeli_32-compatible) | Six-step commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, bidirectional DShot slave |
| Gate drive ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, 250 V, bootstrap) | n/a | HS/LS gate drive with dead time |
| Power stage ×4 | 6 × N-MOSFET per ESC (24 total) | n/a | 3-phase bridge |

The H743 talks to the four ESC MCUs over four single-wire bidirectional DShot600 links.
