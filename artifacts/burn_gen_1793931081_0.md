> **⚠ Key finding:** 80 A per channel on a 20×20 board is **thermally feasible only as a ~1–2 s burst**. The sustainable continuous rating is roughly **15–25 A/channel**, depending on prop-wash airflow. The math is in §3. If the requirement is 80 A continuous, the form factor must change (30.5×30.5 mm or larger, 5×6 mm FETs, a heatsink).

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 0.9 × 1.45 ≈ **1.3 mΩ**. Add ~0.2 mΩ for package and solder, giving **R_eff ≈ 1.5 mΩ hot**.

### 1.2 Bridge conduction with complementary 6-step PWM

In each 60° sector, two phases conduct:

- **PWM phase:** high-side (HS) conducts for D·T. With synchronous rectification, the low-side (LS) of the same phase conducts for (1−D)·T.
- **Return phase:** LS conducts 100% of the sector.

Two channels are therefore always in series with the motor current, independent of duty:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{eff}(T_j)}$$

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W/channel}$$

Per FET (each conducts ~1/3 of the electrical cycle on average): **≈ 3.2 W**.
