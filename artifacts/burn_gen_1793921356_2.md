## 0. Architecture Decision

The STM32H743 cannot directly commutate four 3-phase bridges. That requires 24 gate signals, 12 of them complementary with hardware dead-time. The H743 offers only:
- TIM1 and TIM8: 6 complementary pairs total
- HRTIM: 10 outputs

So the architecture splits into two tiers:

| Tier | Device | Function |
|---|---|---|
| FC | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot, IMU, nav, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) running AM32 | 6-step sync-rect commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase bootstrap driver) | 12 V gate drive |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET, 40 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3 | 4 × 3-phase bridges |

**Design assumptions** (all are parameters you can replace with datasheet values):

| Symbol | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S fully charged (worst case for switching) |
| I_burst | 80 A phase current, per motor | Burst ≤ 5 s |
| I_cont | 45 A | Continuous design point |

| k_T (R_DS temp coeff.) | 1.5 at T_j = 125 °C | R_DS(T) = R_25·[1 + α(T−25)], α ≈ 0.005/K |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous rectification

At any instant exactly two phase legs carry current, one sourcing and one sinking. With complementary PWM, the current always flows through exactly one FET in each active leg, either the high-side or the synchronous low-side. Therefore:

$$P_{cond,motor}(t) = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Each leg is active for 2/3 of the electrical period: 1/3 sourcing, 1/3 sinking. Within the sourcing interval, the high-side FET conducts for a fraction D of the time and the low-side FET for (1−D).

**Per-FET average conduction loss:**

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS} \left[D + (1-D)\cdot 0\right] + \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS}(1-D) \approx \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS}$$

At full throttle (D → 1) this simplifies to:

$$P_{HS} \approx P_{LS} \approx \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS}$$

### 1.2 Electro-thermal fixed point

R_DS(on) depends on T_j, and T_j depends on loss. Solve iteratively:

$$T_j = T_{board} + P_{FET}(T_j)\cdot R_{th,j\text{-}board}, \qquad R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + 0.005(T_j - 25)\right]$$

The loop converges when the gain $\frac{1}{3}I^2 R_{25}\cdot 0.005\cdot R_{th} < 1$. At 80 A with R_th = 16 K/W the gain is 0.145, so the solution is stable with no thermal-runaway risk at this operating point.

**Solution** (T_board = 80 °C, R_th,j-board,eff = 16 K/W):

| Iteration | T_j (°C) | R_DS (mΩ) | P_FET,cond (W) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 80 | 1.084 | 2.31 |
| 1 | 117 | 1.241 | 2.65 |
| 2 | 122 | 1.262 | 2.69 |
| ∞ | ≈ 123 | 1.266 | **2.70** |

**Per motor:** 6 × 2.70 / ... equivalently 2·I²·R_DS = 2 × 6400 × 1.266 mΩ = **16.2 W**
