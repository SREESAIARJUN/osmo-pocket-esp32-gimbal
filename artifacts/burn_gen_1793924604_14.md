# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #65): 80A Burst Thermal, 48 kHz Switching, and Bi-Directional DShot600 DMA

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

The 80A figure is the one that decides whether this board is feasible, so the model is parameterized throughout and the assumptions are stated up front. Swap in your actual datasheet values. **The schematics and numbers below are a reference design. They are not a release-ready netlist and must be checked against the chosen parts' datasheets and verified on hardware.**

**System partitioning.** The H743 runs ArduPilot. It does not commutate motors. Each ESC channel has its own MCU running AM32, which does the 48 kHz PWM and back-EMF commutation. The H743 talks to the ESCs over bi-directional DShot600.
