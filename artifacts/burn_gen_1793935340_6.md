## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Conduction losses

In six-step operation, one high-side path and one low-side path conduct at every instant. With synchronous rectification, the PWM-on state (HS conducts) and the freewheel state (complementary LS conducts) each place exactly **two FET channels** in series with the phase current. Duty cycle therefore cancels out:

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot \left(2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + R_{Cu} + R_{shunt}\right)$$

The temperature dependence of on-resistance is:

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 115 °C, R_DS(on) = 1.0 × 1.495 ≈ **1.50 mΩ**.

Inputs:

- Copper path (2 oz, 6‑layer, phase pads plus battery pours): R_Cu ≈ 0.40 mΩ.
- Low-side shunt: 0.25 mΩ. Its effective RMS current is about the phase current, so a ×1 weighting is conservative.

$$P_{cond} = 80^2\,(3.00 + 0.40 + 0.25)\times10^{-3} = 6400 \times 3.65\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{23.4\ W}$$

The split is 19.2 W in the FETs, 2.56 W in copper and 1.6 W in the shunt.

### 1.2 Switching losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg hard-switches in each sector. The switching transitions are approximately:

$$t_{r}\approx\frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}},\qquad t_{f}\approx\frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g,off}}$$

With Q_gd = 12 nC, a 1.2 A effective source current and a 1.5 A effective sink current, t_r ≈ 10 ns and t_f ≈ 8 ns. This assumes 4.7 Ω on / 2.2 Ω off gate resistors.
