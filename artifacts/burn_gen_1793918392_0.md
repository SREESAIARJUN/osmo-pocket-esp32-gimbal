## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle
$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}) \bmod 2\pi,\qquad \omega_e = p\,\omega_m$$

### 1.2 Clarke transform
This assumes balanced currents, $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$, with an amplitude-invariant scaling.
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform
$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant in dq (surface PMSM, $L_d = L_q = L$)
$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

### 1.5 PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward
$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

To tune by pole-zero cancellation for a target bandwidth $\omega_c$, place the PI zero at the plant pole $R/L$:
$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

The closed loop then becomes the first-order system $\omega_c/(s+\omega_c)$.

Keep $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/10$. At 20 kHz this gives $\omega_c \approx 2\pi\cdot 2000$ rad/s. With the assumed motor, $K_p = 25.1$ V/A and $K_i = 62{,}800$ V/(A·s).

### 1.6 Voltage limiting
Limit the voltage vector to the linear SVPWM hexagon's inscribed circle. Give $d$ priority, then fit $q$ into what remains:
$$V_{max} = \tfrac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3},\qquad |v_d^*|\le V_{max},\qquad |v_q^*|\le\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^{*2}}$$
