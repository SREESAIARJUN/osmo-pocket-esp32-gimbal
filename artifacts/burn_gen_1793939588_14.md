# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC (Job #71): Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation

## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Stated Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture

In every production 4-in-1 design, the STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs directly. The partition is:

```
[H743 Flight Controller] --4x bidirectional DShot600--> [4-in-1 ESC: 4x ESC MCU (AM32/BLHeli_32 class) + 4x 3-phase gate driver + 24 MOSFETs]
```

Two consequences follow:

- **The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, not by the H743.** The H743 only owns the DShot timers and DMA.
- **80 A is treated as per-motor burst current**, not total board current. That gives 320 A peak phase current and up to roughly 240 A battery current at high duty.
