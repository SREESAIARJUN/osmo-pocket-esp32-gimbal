# 3-Axis Gimbal FOC Control & Dual-IMU Fusion: Design Specification and Reference Implementation

## 0. Assumptions

I have no access to Job #45's specification, so these assumptions are explicit. Every one of them is a parameter in the code.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC (e.g. GBM2804-class), 12N14P, so pole pairs p = 7 |

| Rotor encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701-class), one per axis |
| IMU-P | ICM-42688-P on the **payload** (camera plate). Primary attitude feedback. |

| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with FPU (STM32G4/H7-class) |
| Loop rates | Current 20 kHz · Velocity 4 kHz · Angle 1 kHz · IMU ODR 8 kHz |

The rate hierarchy follows the rule that each outer loop runs at ≥5× the bandwidth of the loop it commands, and each inner loop has ≥4× the bandwidth of its outer loop.

## 1. Plant Model

### 1.1 Electrical model (rotor dq frame, surface PMSM, L_d ≈ L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q, \qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

- Electrical angle: $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}$
- Electrical speed: $\omega_e = p\,\omega_m$

### 1.2 Mechanical model (one axis)

$$
J\dot\omega_m = K_t\, i_q - B\,\omega_m - \tau_{\text{cog}}(\theta_m) - \tau_{\text{fric}}(\omega_m) - \tau_d(t)
$$

$$
K_t = \tfrac{3}{2}\,p\,\lambda_m \quad \text{(amplitude-invariant Clarke)}
$$

Here τ_d is the base-induced disturbance: reaction torque from base angular acceleration, cable spring torque, and payload imbalance under base linear acceleration.

### 1.3 Gimbal kinematics

The gimbal uses a yaw → roll → pitch outer-to-inner order. Payload angular rate in the payload frame is:

$$
\boldsymbol\omega_P = R_{P\leftarrow B}\,\boldsymbol\omega_B + \mathbf{J}_g(\boldsymbol\theta_j)\,\dot{\boldsymbol\theta}_j
$$

where θ_j are the joint (encoder) angles and J_g is the gimbal Jacobian. This relation is used both for rate decoupling and for mapping IMU-B into the payload frame.

## 2. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 2.1 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 2.2 PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$
v_d^* = K_{pc}\,e_d + K_{ic}\!\int e_d\,dt \;-\; \omega_e L\, i_q
$$

$$
v_q^* = K_{pc}\,e_q + K_{ic}\!\int e_q\,dt \;+\; \omega_e L\, i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

Pole-zero cancellation tuning makes the closed loop first-order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_{pc} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{ic} = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10\text{–}20}
$$

At 20 kHz, f_c ≈ 1.5 kHz, so ω_c ≈ 9425 rad/s, giving K_pc ≈ 17 V/A and K_ic ≈ 52 800 V/(A·s).
