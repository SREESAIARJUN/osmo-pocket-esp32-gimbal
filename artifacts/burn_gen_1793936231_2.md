# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model (Job #5)

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

Some assumptions need to be stated up front, because the requested combination has hidden constraints.

| Item | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Commutation MCU | 4× AT32F421 (AM32 firmware), one per ESC channel | The H743 does not commutate motors. ArduPilot runs flight control and sends bidirectional DShot600 to each ESC MCU. A single-MCU FOC design is a different project. |
| Bus voltage | 6S, 25.2 V max (design limit 30 V transient) | Use 40 V MOSFETs. |
| MOSFET | 40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3 mm, R_DS(on) ≈ 1.2 mΩ @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V, Q_g ≈ 60 nC | Values are representative. Re-run the model with your selected part's datasheet. |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q-class 3-phase driver with bootstrap, one per ESC | Industry-standard for 4-in-1 boards. |
| Form factor | 30.5×30.5 mm mount, ~36×36 mm board, 8-layer, 2 oz copper | 80 A burst on a 20×20 "nano" board is not thermally credible (see §2.4). |
| Switching frequency | f_sw = 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous) PWM | As specified. |
| Burst current | 80 A per motor (phase current, 6-step trapezoidal) | As specified. |

## 1. Loss Model (per ESC channel, 6 MOSFETs)

### 1.1 Conduction Losses

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, **exactly two MOSFETs carry the full phase current at every instant**:
- During t_on: HS(phase A) + LS(phase B).
- During t_off: LS(phase A) + LS(phase B).

Conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle to first order:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

The temperature coefficient is:

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0048\ /\mathrm{K} \;\Rightarrow\; \times 1.6 \text{ at } 150^\circ C$$

At 80 A with T_j = 150 °C, R = 1.92 mΩ:

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.92\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{24.6\ W}$$
