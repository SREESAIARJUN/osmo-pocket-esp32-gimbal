## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Architecture Corrections

The brief needs three clarifications before any numbers are meaningful.

1. **The H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC controller.** It generates DShot600. The four ESC channels each need their own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 cannot commutate four BLDC motors at 48 kHz and fly the aircraft at the same time. All loss math below applies to the ESC power stage. All DMA math applies to the H743.

2. **"80 A burst" is taken as 80 A phase current per channel, for up to 5 s.** At full throttle (D ≈ 0.95), phase current is roughly equal to DC current. Four channels at 80 A means about 320 A from the battery. On a nano form factor this is only feasible as a short burst, and the thermal section quantifies how short.
