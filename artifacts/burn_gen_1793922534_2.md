## 1. FOC Current Loop (Innermost, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle: θₑ = p·θₘ − θ_offset.

**Clarke** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park:**
$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park:**
$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI Design

The dq motor model:
$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

After feed-forward decoupling, each axis is first order: G(s) = 1/(Ls + R).

**PI design by pole-zero cancellation.** With C(s) = K_p + K_i/s and the zero placed at s = −R/L:
$$K_p = \omega_c L,\qquad K_i = \omega_c R \;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

**Bandwidth limit.** PWM and computation add about 1.5·T_s of delay, so choose ω_c ≤ 2π·f_s/20. At 20 kHz, f_c ≈ 1 kHz, giving ω_c = 6283 rad/s:
- K_p = 13.2 V/A
- K_i = 35 186 V/(A·s)
