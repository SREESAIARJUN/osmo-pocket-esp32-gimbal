# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #16): Reference Design Package

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and a Feasibility Warning

**System architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own MCU (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class, e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071). That MCU runs commutation at a 48 kHz PWM frequency. The H743 does not drive the gates.

**Feasibility warning.** "80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board is only survivable for **seconds**, and only when the battery path and the four channels are not all loaded at once. The math below shows that four channels at 80 A dissipate about 100 W on a board with roughly 6 J/K of thermal mass. Treat 80 A as a single-channel transient rating, not a continuous one.

|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S, full charge (worst case for switching loss)
