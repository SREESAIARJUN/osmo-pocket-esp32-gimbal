# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Power Stage, Thermal, and DShot Design (Job #6)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and One Architecture Correction

**Architecture correction.** The STM32H743 cannot practically commutate four motors at 48 kHz while also running ArduPilot. ArduPilot has no native ESC firmware path for this. The architecture that works is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot FC)  ──bidir DShot600 ×4──►  4 × ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 firmware)
                                                   └─► 4 × 3-phase gate driver ─► 24 MOSFETs
```

**Design basis.** These are assumptions. Change them and re-run the equations.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V (design V = 25.2 V for switching loss) |
| Burst current | 80 A per motor (phase/winding current, 6-step trapezoidal) |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous), dead time 100 ns |
| MOSFET (candidate class, 40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3) | R_ds(on),25 = 1.2 mΩ @ 10 V; Q_g = 40 nC; Q_gs2 ≈ 5 nC; Q_gd = 7 nC; V_plateau ≈ 3 V; R_g,int = 1 Ω; C_oss ≈ 900 pF; Q_rr ≈ 40–60 nC; R_θJC = 1.5 K/W |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q-class, V_DRV ≈ 10 V, source R ≈ 5 Ω, sink R ≈ 2.5 Ω, R_g,ext = 2.2 Ω |
| Board | 36×36 mm (30.5 mm mount), 6 layers, 2 oz copper |

**MOSFET voltage margin.** A 40 V part on 6S leaves about 15 V of headroom for switching spikes. That is acceptable only with tight power-loop layout and local ceramics; otherwise choose 60 V parts.

**Verification required.** Substitute the datasheet values for the MOSFETs you actually select. All pin assignments must be checked against the current datasheets and the AM32 / ArduPilot target files.
