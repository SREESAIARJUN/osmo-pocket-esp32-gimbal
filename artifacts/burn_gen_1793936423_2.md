## 1. FOC Current Loop (Innermost, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms
θₑ = p·θₘ − θ_offset is the electrical angle.

**Clarke** (amplitude-invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park:**

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park:**

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\quad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant (PMSM in the dq frame)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

Torque is $\tau = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q$.

### 1.3 PI with pole-zero cancellation
Each axis is treated as a first-order plant $G(s)=1/(Ls+R)$. Choosing $C(s)=K_p + K_i/s$ with

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

cancels the plant pole. The closed loop becomes $1/(s/\omega_c+1)$.

- Choose $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{loop}/10$. At 20 kHz this gives ω_c ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz.
- The cross-coupling and back-EMF terms are cancelled with a decoupling feedforward:

$$v_d^{ff} = -\hat\omega_e \hat L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \hat\omega_e(\hat L i_d + \hat\lambda_m)$$

### 1.4 Discrete PI with back-calculation anti-windup

$$u_k = K_p e_k + I_k + v^{ff},\quad u^{sat}_k = \mathrm{sat}(u_k),\quad I_{k+1} = I_k + T_s\left[K_i e_k + K_{aw}(u^{sat}_k - u_k)\right]$$

- Use $K_{aw} = K_i/K_p = \omega_{R/L}$.
- Saturation is applied to the voltage vector magnitude, with the d-axis given priority:

$$|v_d|\le V_{max},\quad |v_q|\le\sqrt{V_{max}^2-v_d^2},\quad V_{max} = \frac{V_{dc}}{\sqrt3}\cdot m_{max}\ (m_{max}\approx0.95)$$

### 1.5 SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)
This is mathematically equivalent to 7-segment SVPWM.

$$v_a = v_\alpha,\ v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\ v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta$$

$$v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max + \min),\quad D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{dc}}$$

## 2. Cascaded Angle and Velocity Control

### 2.1 Structure

```
θ_ref ─►[P + ω_ff]─► ω_ref ─►[PI + accel FF]─► i_q,ref ─►[Current PI]─► SVPWM
   ▲                    ▲                        ▲
   θ (IMU attitude      ω (gyro on joint axis)   + i_q,cog(θ_m) anti-cogging FF
      → joint space)
```

**Why gyro feedback in the velocity loop.** For stabilization, the variable to hold still is the camera's inertial rate, not the joint rate. Feeding back the gyro rate projected onto each motor axis rejects base disturbance directly at the bandwidth of the velocity loop.

**Joint-space mapping.** Let R_cam be the estimated camera attitude and R_ref the commanded one. The attitude error vector is

$$\mathbf e_R = \tfrac12\,\mathrm{vee}(R_{ref}^T R_{cam} - R_{cam}^T R_{ref})$$

The joint-angle error is $\mathbf e_q = J(\mathbf q)^{-1}\mathbf e_R$, where the columns of J are the motor axes expressed in the camera frame, determined by the gimbal kinematics.
