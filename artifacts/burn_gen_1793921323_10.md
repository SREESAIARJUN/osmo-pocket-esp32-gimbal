## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**The H743 does not drive the power stage.** In practice, a 4-in-1 ESC runs four dedicated motor-control MCUs (AM32 or BLHeli_32 firmware). The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to them. The H743 has neither the timer count nor the deterministic latency to run four sensorless FOC/6-step loops alongside the flight stack.

```
6S LiPo ──► Power stage (4 × 3-phase bridge, 24 MOSFETs)
              ▲ gate drive
          4 × ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32)
              ▲ bidir DShot600 (1 wire per motor)
          STM32H743VIT6 @ 480 MHz (ArduPilot)
```

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation model

The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM on the high-side of the active phase pair:

- The high-side of phase A conducts for duty D.
- The low-side of phase A synchronously rectifies for (1 − D).
- The low-side of phase B is statically on.

At any instant, exactly two MOSFETs carry I_ph:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph,rms}^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{ph,rms}^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

$$R_{ds}(125^\circ C) = 0.90 \times 1.45 = 1.305\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Ripple correction

The phase inductance (L_ph ≈ 15 µH for a typical 5″ motor) adds a triangular ripple on top of the DC phase current:

$$\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph} f_{sw}}, \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I_{dc}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

At D = 0.5 and V_bemf ≈ 12 V:

- ΔI = 13.2 × 0.5 / (15 µH × 48 kHz) ≈ 9.2 A
- The ripple term is 9.2² / 12 = 7.0 A², which is negligible against 6400 A² (+0.1 %).
