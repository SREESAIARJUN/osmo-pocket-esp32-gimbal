## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Feasibility Statement

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Form factor | 20×20 mm mount, approx. 26×26 mm stacked FC + ESC board (single board) |
| Bus | 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V (designed to 8S abs-max transients → 40 V FETs) |
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100) or VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz, ArduPilot |
| ESC MCUs | 4× AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) running **AM32**. The H743 does **not** commutate the motors. It sends bidirectional DShot600 to each ESC MCU. |
| Power stage | 6× 40 V N-FET per channel, 3.3×3.3 DFN, single FET per switch |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q (3-phase half-bridge, bootstrap) per channel |
| Modulation | 6-step trapezoidal, complementary synchronous PWM, f_sw = 48 kHz |
| Burst spec | 80 A per channel, all four channels simultaneously (worst case) |

> **Feasibility flag:** The model below shows that 4×80 A in a nano form factor is a **~2 second burst rating**, not a continuous rating. Continuous capability is roughly 25–30 A per channel with prop-wash airflow. Marketing should quote "80 A burst (≤2 s)" or the board will fail field returns.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

At any instant two phases are active. During a sector:
- **Phase A (PWM leg):** HS_A conducts for D·T_sw. LS_A conducts (synchronous rectification) for (1−D)·T_sw, minus dead time.
- **Phase B (sink leg):** LS_B is on for the full sector.

So, ignoring dead time, the motor current always flows through exactly **two** channels:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{rms}^2 \, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

Per-FET average over one electrical cycle. Each switch conducts in 2 of 6 sectors: 1 as a PWM device and 1 as a full-on sink. The resulting averages are:

$$
P_{cond,HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R\,\big[\tfrac{1}{2}(D) + \tfrac{1}{2}\big]\cdot 2 \quad\text{(general)},\qquad 
\bar P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}\
