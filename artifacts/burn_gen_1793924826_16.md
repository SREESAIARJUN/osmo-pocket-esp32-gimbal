## 0. Architecture Note and Assumptions

**The H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** In a 4-in-1 stack, the H743 sends DShot600 commands. A dedicated ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32) generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM, dead-time, and commutation. The loss model in §1–§3 applies to the ESC board. The timer/DMA model in §4 applies to the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Conduction topology

The motor uses six-step (trapezoidal) drive with synchronous rectification. At any instant, two phases carry I_ph:

- **PWM-on interval (D):** the high-side FET of leg A conducts, plus the low-side FET of the return leg B.
- **PWM-off interval (1−D):** the low-side FET of leg A conducts (synchronous freewheel), plus the low-side FET of leg B.

In both intervals, exactly two channels conduct. Duty cycle only moves loss between the high-side and low-side FETs. It does not change the total:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Numeric result at T_j = 125 °C

$$R_{ds}(125) = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.45 = 1.45\,\text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.45\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{18.6\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each leg spends 120° of the electrical cycle as the PWM leg and 120° as the return leg.

- **High-side FET:** P_HS = (1/3)·D·I²·R
- **Low-side FET:** P_LS = (1/3)(1−D)·I²·R + (1/3)·I²·R
