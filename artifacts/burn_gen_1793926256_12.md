## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Bridge topology

In 6-step mode, one high-side and one low-side path carry the phase current at every instant. With complementary PWM:

- The active high-side FET conducts for duty fraction D.
- Its synchronous low-side partner conducts for (1−D).
- The opposite-leg low-side FET conducts continuously during the 60° sector.

Therefore, **two R_ds(on) are always in series with the phase current**, regardless of D (dead time excluded):

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Ripple correction

With phase inductance L_ph and a centre-aligned ripple ΔI:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{dc}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I \approx \frac{(V_{bus}-E)\,D}{2L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

For typical 5″ motors (L_ph ≈ 15–30 µH) at 48 kHz, ΔI ≈ 3–6 A. This adds less than 0.1 % to I²R, so it is neglected below.

### 1.3 At 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per channel |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 2 × 6400 × 1.00 m = **12.8 W** |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ | **17.1 W** |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ | **18.6 W** |

**Parasitics.** Add these per channel (shunt and copper are evaluated at 80 A):

- **Shunt** (0.25 mΩ low-side or phase shunt): I²R = **1.6 W**
- **PCB copper path** (about 0.3 mΩ, 2 oz, 8-layer): **1.9 W**. This lands in the board, not the junction.

### 1.4 Thermal runaway criterion

Conduction power rises with temperature, so the electro-thermal loop is stable only if:

$$\alpha \cdot P_{cond,25} \cdot R_{th,ja,eff} < 1$$

With P_cond,25 = 12.8 W per channel and an effective per-channel R_th of 4 K/W (all four channels heating one shared board): 0.0045 × 12.8 × 4 = 0.23. The loop is stable, but the effective power gain is 1/(1−0.23) = **1.30×** over the cold estimate.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the high-side FET of the PWM leg hard-switches. The synchronous low-side commutates near-ZVS after the body diode picks up the current during dead time.
