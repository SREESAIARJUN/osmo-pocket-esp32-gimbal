## 1. Field-Oriented Control: Inner Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms
Electrical angle: θₑ = p·(θₘ − θ_offset).

Clarke transform, amplitude-invariant, using two phase shunts with i_c = −i_a − i_b:

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

Inverse Park transform:

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\quad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant and decoupled PI
The PMSM dq model is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

The controller adds cross-coupling and back-EMF feedforward to the PI outputs:

$$v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(e_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(e_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

With λ_m = (2/3)·K_t/p, each axis reduces to the first-order plant 1/(Ls+R). Choosing the zero to cancel the pole (Ki/Kp = R/L) gives a closed loop of ω_c/(s+ω_c):

$$K_{p,i}=L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i}=R\,\omega_{ci},\qquad \omega_{ci}\le \frac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10}\Rightarrow \omega_{ci}=2\pi\cdot 1.5\text{ kHz}$$
