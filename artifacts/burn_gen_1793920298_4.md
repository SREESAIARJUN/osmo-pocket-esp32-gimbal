**Architectural flags to resolve before layout:**

1. **The H743 does not drive MOSFETs.** It sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs. A single H743 cannot run four sensorless 48 kHz FOC/6-step loops while also running ArduPilot.
2. **"80 A × 4" on a nano board is a burst rating, not a thermal rating.** Section 3 shows it is limited to a few seconds, set by heat capacity rather than steady-state dissipation.
3. **Battery current.** I_bat ≈ D·I_phase. Four motors at 80 A and D ≈ 1 draw about 320 A. No XT60/XT90 lead or nano-board bus copper supports that. A realistic aggregate limit is 4 × 80 A only at partial duty, or one motor at a time (yaw/punch transients).

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification
At any instant, exactly two legs are active:
- **PWM leg:** HS on for D·T_s, LS (sync. rect.) on for (1−D)·T_s, minus dead time.
- **Static leg:** LS on continuously for the 60° sector.

So the phase current always flows through **two series MOSFETs**, plus copper.

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2\,\big(2R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + R_{Cu}\big)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)
$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}
$$
At T_j = 110 °C: R = 0.9 mΩ × 1.38 = **1.24 mΩ**. Use **1.3 mΩ** to include package and clip resistance.

### 1.3 RMS including PWM ripple
Phase-to-phase inductance L_pp ≈ 20 µH (typical 2207-class motor):

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{pp}\,f_{sw}} \;\Rightarrow\; \Delta I_{max}(D=0.5) = \frac{25.2\times0.25}{20\mu\times48k} = 6.6\ \mathrm{A}
$$
$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{DC}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 80^2 + 3.6 \approx 6404\ \mathrm{A^2}
$$
Ripple is negligible at 48 kHz, which is one benefit of the high PWM rate.

### 1.4 Results per motor channel @ 80 A

| Term | Value |
|---|---|
| 2 × R_DS(on),hot | 2.6 mΩ |
| R_Cu (pads, phase pours, vias) | ≈ 0.4 mΩ |
| **P_cond,ch** | 6404 × 3.0 mΩ = **19.2 W** (of which 16.6 W in silicon) |

### 1.5 Per-device distribution (worst device)
The static LS conducts the full current for 1/3 of the electrical period. The PWM leg splits current between HS (D) and LS (1−D).

- Instantaneous dissipation in a conducting FET: 80² × 1.3 mΩ = **8.3 W**.
- Time-averaged, per FET: ≈ 16.6 W / 6 = **2.8 W**.

At 80 A and 6S on a 2207 motor, the electrical frequency is about 1.5–2.5 kHz (sector ≈ 70–110 µs). That is far shorter than the package's thermal time constant (ms), so use the **average** value plus a small ripple term:

$$
\Delta T_{j,ripple} \approx P_{pk}\cdot Z_{th,jc}(t_{sector}) \approx 8.3\times0.15 \approx 1.2\ \mathrm{K}
$$

### 1.6 Current shunt
- **Per-motor shunt (0.5 mΩ):** 80² × 0.5 m = **3.2 W per channel**.
- **Single bus shunt (0.25 mΩ) at 320 A:** 25.6 W. Not acceptable.

**Recommendation:** use per-ESC 0.25 mΩ 2512 shunts (1.6 W each), or a Hall sensor (e.g. ACS-class / TMCS1100 class) on the bus, or rely on AM32's estimated current.

## 2. Switching Loss Model @ 48 kHz

Use V = 25.2 V and I = 80 A. Only the PWM-leg HS/LS pair switches.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V–I overlap (HS)
$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}
$$
Gate drive: FD6288Q sources ~0.3 A and sinks ~0.6 A, with R_g,ext = 4.7 Ω. Plateau-limited t_r ≈ 18 ns and t_f ≈ 10 ns.

$$
P_{VI} = 0.5\times25.2\times80\times28\,\mathrm{ns}\times48\,\mathrm{k} = \mathbf{1.35\ W}
$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery (LS body diode → HS turn-on)
$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V\,f_{sw} = 40\,\mathrm{n}\times25.2\times48\,\mathrm{k} = 0.048\ \mathrm{W}
$$
The induced extra HS turn-on loss is ≈ 1–2× P_rr, so allow **0.12 W** total.

### 2.3 Output capacitance
$$
P_{oss} = Q_{oss}\,V\,f_{sw} \approx 30\,\mathrm{n}\times25.2\times48\,\mathrm{k} = 0.036\ \mathrm{W}
$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction
Two dead-time windows per period, t_dt = 100 ns, V_F ≈ 0.8 V at 80 A:
$$
P_{dt} = V_F\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8\times80\times200\,\mathrm{n}\times48\,\mathrm{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}
$$
Tuning AM32 dead time down to about 50 ns (verify shoot-through margin on the scope) halves this.

### 2.5 Gate charge (dissipated in driver and R_g, not in the FET)
$$
P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2\times45\,\mathrm{n}\times12\times48\,\mathrm{k} = 0.052\ \mathrm{W}
$$
