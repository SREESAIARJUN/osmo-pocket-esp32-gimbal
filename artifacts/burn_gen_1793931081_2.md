# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #45): Electrical, Thermal and DShot Design Model

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning

The STM32H743 does not switch the MOSFETs. A production 4-in-1 ESC uses one ESC MCU per motor, running AM32 or BLHeli_32, to perform the 48 kHz commutation. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to each ESC MCU over bidirectional DShot600.

```
[6S LiPo] ─┬─ 4-in-1 ESC board (30.5×30.5 or 25.5×25.5)
           │    4× ESC MCU (AT32F421K8U7 / STM32G071GBU6, AM32)
           │    4× 3-phase gate driver (FD6288Q)
           │    24× N-MOSFET (6 per motor)
           │    Current shunt + amp, VBAT divider
           └─ 8-pin JST-SH stack connector ──► FC board (STM32H743VIH6, ArduPilot)
```

### 0.2 Baseline Parameters

These are assumptions. Replace them with your selected FET's datasheet values.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path

Trapezoidal 6-step drive with synchronous rectification works as follows:

- During each 60° commutation sector, phase current flows through exactly two FETs at all times.
  - During t_on: high-side of phase A plus low-side of phase B.
  - During t_off: low-side of phase A (synchronous rectifier) plus low-side of phase B.
- Conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle, except during dead time.

### 1.2 Equations

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha_R (T_j-25)]$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)\,(1 - 2 t_{dt} f_{sw})$$

### 1.3 Numbers at T_j = 125 °C

R_ds = 1.0 mΩ × 1.6 = 1.6 mΩ.

The dead-time correction factor is 1 − 2(200 ns)(48 kHz) = 0.981.

$$P_{cond} = 2 × 80^2 × 1.6\text{ mΩ} × 0.981 = \mathbf{20.1\ W\ per\ motor}$$

### 1.4 Copper and Shunt Losses

These add to the FET losses:

- **Phase traces:** 2 oz, ~0.4 mΩ total path, giving 80² × 0.4 m = 2.6 W per motor.
- **Battery-side current:** I_bat = D·I_ph. At D = 0.9, I_bat = 72 A per motor and 288 A total for four motors at burst.
  - This is beyond any realistic XT60 or nano pad.
  - In practice, all-motor 80 A coincidence is limited by battery sag.

### 1.5 Thermal Runaway Check

The loop gain is:

$$G = \frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,R_{th} = P_{cond}\frac{\alpha_R}{1+\alpha_R\Delta T}R_{th,j\text{-}brd}$$

This gives G ≈ 3.4 W × 0.0037 × 4 ≈ 0.05 ≪ 1. The design is stable per FET.
