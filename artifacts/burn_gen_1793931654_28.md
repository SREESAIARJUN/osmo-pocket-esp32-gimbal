# STM32H743 Nano FC + Integrated 4-in-1 ESC: Loss, Thermal, and DShot DMA Model

## 0. Architectural Ground Truth

Three assumptions in the request need correcting before the math is useful.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** Four 3-phase bridges need 12 complementary PWM pairs. The H743 has only two advanced timers (TIM1, TIM8) plus HRTIM, and ArduPilot does not support direct FOC or 6-step drive. The workable architecture is:
   - The **H743 runs ArduPilot** and emits bidirectional DShot600.
   - **Four ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or BLHeli_32-class firmware) generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM and return eRPM telemetry.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 mm) board is thermally marginal.** With realistic 3.3×3.3 mm FETs, 80 A on all four motors lasts roughly **1 s** before limits are reached (derived in §3). Treat 80 A as a single-motor transient or very short burst rating. Continuous capability will be around 25–35 A.
3. **The device parameters below are placeholders.** They represent a typical 40 V PQFN 3.3×3.3 FET and 6S operation. Substitute your chosen datasheet values; every equation is written parametrically so you can.
