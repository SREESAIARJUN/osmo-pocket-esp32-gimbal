## 1. Conduction Loss Model

In each commutation sector two phases conduct:

- **PWM leg:** current flows in the high-side for D·T and in the low-side for (1−D)·T. The loss is I²R·(D + 1−D) = I²R, independent of duty cycle.
- **Static leg:** the low-side is on continuously, giving another I²R.

The per-channel conduction loss is therefore:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2\, R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha_R (T_j - 25)]$$

**Electro-thermal coupling.** Because R_ds rises with temperature, solve iteratively:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{board} + P_{FET}(T_j^{(n)})\cdot R_{th,j\text{-}board}$$

Evaluated at the design point T_j = 100 °C:

- R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.005 × 75) = **1.375 mΩ**
- P_cond,ch = 2 × 80² × 1.375×10⁻³ = **17.6 W**

| T_j (°C) | R_ds (mΩ) | P_cond per channel (W) | P_cond, 4 channels (W) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | 1.000 | 12.8 | 51.2 |
| 75 | 1.250 | 16.0 | 64.0 |
| 100 | 1.375 | 17.6 | 70.4 |
| 125 | 1.500 | 19.2 | 76.8 |

**Per-FET average.** Conduction rotates across 6 FETs over an electrical cycle, so the average per FET is ≈17.6/6 ≈ 2.9 W. The instantaneous peak per FET is ≈8.8 W.

**Copper path.** For 4 × 80 A = 320 A total, the battery pads, bus planes and shunt add loss:

- Assuming 0.3 mΩ total bus path: 320² × 0.3 mΩ = **30.7 W**. This is not negligible.
- Use ≥4 layers of 2 oz copper for the bus.
- A 0.1 mΩ current shunt adds 10 W at 320 A. Consider a 0.1–0.15 mΩ shunt, or Hall sensing.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap (PWM-leg high-side)

Gate currents during the Miller plateau:

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{src}+R_g} = \frac{6.5}{7.2} = 0.90\ \text{A}, \qquad I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{sink}+R_g} = \frac{3.5}{6.7} = 0.52\ \text{A}$$

Transition times:

$$t_{on} = \frac{15\ \text{nC}}{0.90} = 16.7\ \text{ns}, \qquad t_{off} = \frac{15\ \text{nC}}{0.52} = 28.8\ \text{ns}$$

Switching loss:

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_{on}+t_{off}) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 45.5\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{2.20\ W}$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction (two edges per period)

$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2 t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 400\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$
