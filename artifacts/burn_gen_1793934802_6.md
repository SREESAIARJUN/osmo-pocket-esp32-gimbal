## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. In every production-grade AIO design, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 to four local ESC MCUs. Each ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32/Bluejay) generates the 48 kHz PWM. The model below follows that split:

```
VBAT ─┬─► 4× [ESC MCU → 3-ph gate driver → 6× N-MOSFET] ─► Motors 1-4
      └─► FC buck regulators → STM32H743 ──DShot600 (bidir)──► ESC MCUs
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds ≈ 1.2 mΩ × 1.6 = **1.92 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous commutation

At any instant, two phases carry current:
- **Phase A (PWM leg):** the high-side FET conducts for D. The low-side FET conducts synchronously for (1−D).
- **Phase B (return leg):** the low-side FET is held on.

Current therefore always flows through **two R_ds(on) elements in series**, independent of D. Neglecting ripple, which is small at 48 kHz with motor inductance of roughly 10–30 µH:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) + I^2 R_{trace}$$

Including ripple:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12},\qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

### 1.3 Numerical result at 80 A burst

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.92\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{24.6\ W}$$

Copper adds to this. A 2 oz trace 3 mm wide × 8 mm long is about 0.45 mΩ per path, which adds 2 × 6400 × 0.45 mΩ ≈ **5.8 W**. Board copper resistance is not negligible at this current; use multilayer paralleling and via arrays under the FET drains.

**Per-FET loss.** Each FET conducts for roughly 1/3 of the electrical cycle:
- Average: ≈ 4.1 W per FET
- Instantaneous while conducting: ≈ 12.3 W
