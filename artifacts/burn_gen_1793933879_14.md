## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Feasibility Findings

Several points in the requirement need clarifying before the math. Some of them change the design.

| # | Issue | Resolution |
|---|-------|------------|
| 1 | **The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** ArduPilot is flight-control firmware and has no FOC or 6-step commutation for gate drivers. | The H743 is the FC MCU. Each motor channel has a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as the AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600. The 48 kHz figure is the ESC PWM frequency. |
| 2 | **"80A burst" on a nano form factor** (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm). | The analysis below shows this is achievable only as a **per-motor, ~1–3 s transient**. It is not achievable on all four motors simultaneously beyond about 1–2 s. Treat it as a marketing-class rating unless the board grows or adds a heatsink. |
| 3 | Component parameters were not specified. | All device values are **stated assumptions** (§1). Re-run the equations with your actual MOSFET datasheet values. |

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Topology

In 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation with complementary PWM, two phases carry current at any instant:

- **PWM leg:** the HS FET conducts for D·T. Its complementary LS FET conducts for (1−D)·T, minus dead time.
- **Return leg:** the LS FET conducts continuously for the 60° sector.

So there are always **two R_DS(on) in series** with the motor current, whatever the duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2 \cdot R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

with

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1} \Rightarrow R(125^\circ C) \approx 1.5\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

### 2.2 Numbers at the 80 A burst

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{19.2\ W}
$$

Peak dissipation per individual FET (the continuously-on return LS):

$$
P_{FET,max} = 80^2 \times 1.5\ \text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{9.6\ W}
$$

Commutation rotates this load across all six FETs, so the time-averaged load per FET is lower: each FET carries current for 1/3 of the electrical cycle, about 3.2 W average. Junction ripple at low eRPM must still be checked (§5.3).

### 2.3 PCB copper loss (often forgotten)

Phase and bus traces: assume an effective 10 mm path, 2 oz outer layer plus 2 inner 1 oz layers in parallel, 4 mm wide.

$$
R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho L}{w\,t_{eff}} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\cdot 0.010}{0.004 \cdot 140\times10^{-6}} \approx 0.31\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

Over two current paths, ×1.3 at 80 °C:

$$
P_{Cu} \approx 80^2 \times 2 \times 0.31\text{m} \times 1.3 \approx \mathbf{5.1\ W/motor}
$$

This is significant. It is about 25 % of the FET conduction loss.
