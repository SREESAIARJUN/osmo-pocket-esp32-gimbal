## 0. Architectural Ground Truth

Three facts constrain everything else in this document.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1 design, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Typical parts are the STM32G071, AT32F421 or GD32E230. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to those four MCUs. The 48 kHz switching frequency is an AM32 parameter, not an H743 timer setting. This document models the system that way.
2. **"80 A burst" in a nano/AIO format is a transient rating, not a thermal steady state.** The math in §3 shows it is sustainable for roughly 1–3 s per channel. With all four channels at 80 A, total battery current is about 290 A. Treat the rating as a per-channel, short-duration, airflow-assisted number and label it that way on the datasheet.
3. **All device parameters below are stated assumptions.** Replace them with the datasheet values of the FETs you actually procure. The equations are the deliverable; the numbers are a worked instance.
