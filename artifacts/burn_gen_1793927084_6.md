# STM32H743 AIO "Nano" FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #30): Design Basis

This document derives the 80 A burst loss and thermal model, specifies the ArduPilot bi-directional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and provides reference netlists.

Before the math, two architectural points need to be settled.

**1. The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC.** A 4-in-1 ESC needs four dedicated motor-control MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32, one per motor. These handle commutation, the 48 kHz PWM, and DShot decoding. The H743 runs ArduPilot and generates DShot600. This document assumes 4× AT32F421K8U7 (AM32) on the ESC section.

**2. "Nano" and "80 A burst" conflict.** The model below shows that 80 A burst on all four channels cannot be dissipated on a 20×20 mm board. It is marginal even on 30.5×30.5 mm without a heatsink. I give the full model so the trade is explicit, then the derating rule I'd sign off on.

> **Parameter disclaimer:** MOSFET, driver and PCB values are representative of the 40 V / 5×6 mm PQFN class. Re-run every equation against the final datasheet before release. The model structure is the deliverable; the numbers are a first-pass bound.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C with the max datasheet value:

$$R_{DS(on)}(125) = 0.85\,(1+0.45) = 1.23\ \text{m}\Omega \;\Rightarrow\; \text{use } 1.1\text{–}1.25\ \text{m}\Omega$$

The design value is **R_hot = 1.2 mΩ**.

### 2.2 Bridge conduction

In 6-step synchronous operation, exactly two switches carry I_ph at every instant:

- the PWM-leg high-side, or its complementary low-side, and
- the fixed low-side of the return leg.
