# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion

I don't have the Job #42 specification, so this design states its assumptions explicitly (§0). Every parameter that depends on your hardware is collected in config structs, so you can swap in your real values without touching the algorithms.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4/H7-class Cortex-M4F/M7 with single-precision FPU (CORDIC optional) |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 2–5 mH |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701), one per joint |
| Current sense | 2 or 3 low-side shunts. A voltage-mode fallback is provided. |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 24 MHz, gyro/accel ODR = 8 kHz, INT1 data-ready triggering DMA |
| Kinematics | Yaw → Roll → Pitch chain (base → camera) |
