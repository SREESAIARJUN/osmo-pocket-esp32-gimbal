## 0. Architecture Assumptions

These assumptions determine every number in this document, so they come first. Change any of them and the equations in sections 1–3 still hold, but the results must be recomputed.

| Item | Assumption | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Form factor | 25.5 × 25.5 mm (20×20 M2 mounting), 8-layer, 2 oz inner/outer power layers | Typical nano AIO |
| Flight controller (FC) MCU | STM32H743VIT6 @ 480 MHz running ArduPilot | Per job spec |
| ESC MCUs | 4× AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) running AM32 | The H743 does **not** drive the gates. Running 4× 6-step or FOC plus ArduPilot on one core is not viable. |
| Gate driver | 4× FD6288Q (3-phase, 250 V, bootstrap) | Industry-standard ESC driver |
| MOSFETs | 24× 40 V N-ch, PDFN 3×3 | R_DS(on),25 = 1.5 mΩ @ V_GS = 10 V; Q_g = 45 nC; Q_gd = 9 nC; Q_rr = 40 nC; Q_oss = 30 nC; V_SD = 0.8 V; R_θJC = 1.5 K/W |
| Bus voltage | 6S, V_bus = 25.2 V max (worst case for switching loss) | |
| Commutation | Trapezoidal 6-step, complementary (synchronous) PWM, f_sw = 48 kHz, dead time t_dt = 100 ns | AM32 default style |
| Burst current | I_ph = 80 A per motor (phase/motor current), D = 0.8 at burst | |

**Key point up front:** in a 25.5 mm footprint, 80 A is a *burst* rating of roughly 1 second. It is not a continuous rating. Section 4 quantifies this.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At a design-point T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS}(110) = 1.5 \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 85) = 2.07\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current sharing in 6-step synchronous PWM

Each half-bridge spends one-third of the electrical cycle in each of three states:

- **"High" state** (PWM leg): the high-side FET conducts for D; the low-side FET synchronously rectifies for (1−D).
- **"Low" state** (return leg): the low-side FET conducts 100%.
- **Floating state:** no current (BEMF sensing).

Per-FET RMS-squared losses:

$$P_{HS} = I^2 R_{DS}\,\frac{D}{3}, \qquad P_{LS} = I^2 R_{DS}\,\frac{(1-D) + 1}{3} = I^2 R_{DS}\,\frac{2-D}{3}$$

Per leg:

$$P_{leg} = \tfrac{2}{3} I^2 R_{DS}$$

Per ESC channel (3 legs):

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)}$$

This result is independent of D: two FETs are always in series with the motor current.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, D = 0.8

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| I²R | 6400 × 2.07 mΩ = 13.25 W |
| P_HS per FET | 13.25 × 0.8/3 = **3.53 W** |
| P_LS per FET (worst FET) | 13.25 × 1.2/3 = **5.30 W** |
| P_cond per channel | **26.5 W** |
| P_cond, 4 channels simultaneous | **106 W** |

Note that battery current is D·I_ph ≈ 64 A per channel, not 80 A.

### 1.4 Copper and shunt losses (per channel)

- Phase trace/pad resistance ≈ 0.3 mΩ per path × 2 paths in series → 6400 × 0.6 mΩ = **3.8 W**.
- Shunt, 0.5 mΩ, on the bus: (64 A)² × 0.5 mΩ = **2.0 W**. This is the shared battery-return shunt, apportioned per channel.

Without copper and shunt losses, the error in the result is around 20%. Do not omit them.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

In 6-step, only **one leg PWMs at a time**, so switching loss per channel equals one leg's loss.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V–I overlap (high-side FET)

Transition times are driven by the Miller plateau charge. With FD6288Q I_source/I_sink ≈ 1.5/1.8 A through R_g = 10 Ω, the effective plateau current is ≈ 0.6 A:

$$t_r \approx t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{g,plateau}} \approx \frac{9\,\text{nC}}{0.6\,\text{A}} = 15\ \text{ns}$$

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery (low-side body diode, dumped into high-side FET)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$
