## 0. Architectural Ground Truth and Key Findings

Three architectural facts drive the rest of this document.

1. **The H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC.** Commutation at 48 kHz with back-EMF zero-cross detection on four motors cannot run on the H743 alongside ArduPilot. It also conflicts with ArduPilot's scheduler and its DShot output model. The correct architecture is:
   - **STM32H743** runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600 on four lines.
   - **4× ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class) run AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, one per motor.
   - **3-phase gate drivers** sit between the ESC MCUs and **24× N-channel MOSFETs** (6 per motor).

2. **80 A per channel is a burst rating only.** On a nano form factor it is thermally valid for roughly 1 second. The model below gives a defensible continuous rating of about **22–25 A per channel**. Any datasheet claiming "80 A" must state the burst duration and starting temperature.

3. **Vendor-specific numbers need confirmation.** Device parameters below are representative values for a 40 V PDFN3.3×3.3 MOSFET class. They must be replaced with the selected part's datasheet values before tape-out. Formulas and structure are exact. Numbers are engineering estimates and are marked as assumptions.
