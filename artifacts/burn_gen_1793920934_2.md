# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual ICM-42688-P Fusion

I don't have the Job #19 specification, so this design is parameterized. The assumed values are listed in §0. Replace them with your measured motor, encoder and inertia data. Every gain is derived from those parameters, not hand-picked.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Loop Timing

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC (2804/4108 class), pole pairs p = 7, slots N_s = 12, R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 1–3 mH |
| Current sensing | 2-phase inline shunts, sampled at PWM center |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A or MT6701 class), one per joint |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with FPU, 3 complementary-PWM timers |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, on separate SPI buses |
| Kinematics | Yaw (base) → Roll → Pitch (camera) |
