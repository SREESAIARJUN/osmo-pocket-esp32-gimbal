## 0. Assumptions and Scope

I don't have the Job #65 BOM, stackup, or MOSFET selection. Every number below derives from the parameter set in this section. The equations are general. Substitute your actual datasheet values and the results scale directly.

**What these results are and are not:**
- Treat them as **design-phase estimates**. They do not validate the hardware.
- **Netlist (§6):** reference topology only. It has not been through ERC/DRC or layout review.
- **DMA map (§5):** must be confirmed by ArduPilot's `dma_resolver.py` for your actual `hwdef.dat`.
- **DMAMUX request IDs:** verify against RM0433 for your silicon revision.

**Architecture assumed.** The H743 is the flight controller. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, which generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 cannot directly commutate four BLDC motors at 48 kHz while also running ArduPilot's scheduler. The H743's job is to send DShot600 and capture bidirectional eRPM telemetry.
