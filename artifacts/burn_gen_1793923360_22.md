## 0. Architectural Clarifications

These points change the math, so they come first.

1. **The H743 does not switch the bridges.** ArduPilot has no native FOC/6-step motor drive. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs. This baseline uses AT32F421 parts running AM32. Those ESC MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM. All switching-loss math below therefore applies to the ESC power stage, not the H743.

2. **"80 A burst" must be defined.** This document defines it as **80 A RMS-equivalent phase current per channel for t ≤ 5 s**.
   - If all four channels burst at once at high duty, battery current approaches 4 × 80 × D ≈ **300+ A**.
   - That exceeds XT60 (60 A continuous) and XT90 ratings, and most 6S packs.
   - The system bus is therefore specified separately (§3.6).

3. **"Nano" form factor vs. 80 A is the dominant risk.** On a 20 × 20 or 25.5 × 25.5 mm board the copper heat capacity is only a few J/K. The thermal section shows 80 A is achievable **only as a time-limited burst**, never continuously.
