# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage and DShot Architecture (Job #4)

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

The partitioning needs to be fixed first, because it changes where each part of the model applies.

- **The STM32H743 is the flight controller.** It runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs. ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The H743 never drives gates directly.
- **Each motor has its own ESC MCU.** The typical part is an AT32F421 or GD32E230 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU sets the 48 kHz PWM, dead-time and commutation.
- **The thermal and switching model applies to the ESC half-bridges.** The DMA model applies to the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths

AM32 and BLHeli_32 drive 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM.

- At every instant, phase current flows through **exactly two conducting MOSFET channels**:
  - the high-side of phase A *or* its complementary low-side, during the PWM off-time, and
  - the low-side of phase B.
- Assume high winding inductance, so phase current ≈ I_ph with small ripple. The effective conduction loss per motor channel is then:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

with ripple correction:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence (the critical coupling)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × 1.6 = **1.6 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Numerical result (80 A, negligible ripple)

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond / motor | P_cond / FET (on-state) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 12.8 W | 6.4 W |
| 100 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W | 9.3 W |
| 125 °C | 1.6 mΩ | **20.5 W** | **10.2 W** |

Add PCB copper and shunt losses:

- **Shunt:** if a 0.5 mΩ shunt sits in the return path, P_shunt = 80² × 0.5 m = **3.2 W** per motor.
- **Copper:** a 2 oz trace at roughly 0.3 mΩ effective adds about **1.9 W**. Include it; on nano boards it is not negligible.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-modulated leg switches. One hard-switched FET plus its complementary partner make up the switching event per commutation sector.

### 2.1 Components

1. **V-I overlap (hard switching):**
   $$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(40\text{ns})(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

2. **Output capacitance:**
   $$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

3. **Reverse recovery** (body diode of the sync-rect FET at turn-on of the opposite FET):
   $$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.060\ \text{W}$$
   Note that Q_rr rises with di/dt and T_j. A factor of 2–3× is realistic at high temperature.

4. **Dead-time body-diode conduction:**
   $$P_{dt} = V_F I_{ph} (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ns}\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

5. **Gate drive** (dissipated mostly in driver and gate resistors, not the FET):
   $$P_g = 2 Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2\times60\text{n}\times10\times48\text{k} = 0.058\ \text{W}$$

### 2.2 Total switching loss per motor

$$P_{sw} \approx 1.94 + 0.05 + 0.06 + 0.61 + 0.06 \approx \mathbf{2.7\ W}$$
