**Architecture clarification.** In an ArduPilot system the STM32H743 is the flight controller. It generates bidirectional DShot600 commands and receives eRPM telemetry. The 48 kHz PWM and commutation run on the ESC-side MCU(s), for example AM32 or BLHeli_32 firmware on an STM32G071 or AT32F421. ArduPilot does not drive MOSFETs directly.
