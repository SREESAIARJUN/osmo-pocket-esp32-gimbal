## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current distribution in a 6-step synchronous bridge

AM32 and BLHeli_32 use trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. Over one electrical period, each phase leg spends:

- **1/3 of the period PWM-active.** The high-side (HS) FET conducts for fraction D; the low-side (LS) FET freewheels synchronously for fraction (1−D).
- **1/3 of the period as the return leg.** The LS FET is fully on.
- **1/3 of the period floating.** The leg is off and used for BEMF sensing.
