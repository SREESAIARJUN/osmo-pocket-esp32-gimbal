# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model (Job #42)

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 flight controller. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The FC sends bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. This split determines both the DMA design (Section 4) and the netlist partitioning (Section 5).

## 1. Conduction Losses

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\left(\frac{T_j + 273}{298}\right)^{\beta},\quad \beta \approx 2.0\text{–}2.4$$

The design value at T_j = 125 °C is R_ds = 1.2 × 1.7 = **2.04 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step conduction paths

In each 60° commutation sector, two phase legs are active:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. The low-side FET conducts synchronously for 1 − D.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET is on continuously.

Losses per ESC channel:

$$P_{cond} = I^2R_{ds}\,D + I^2R_{ds}(1-D) + I^2R_{ds} = 2I^2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond} = 2(80)^2(2.04\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{26.1\ W\ per\ channel}$$

Each FET carries current for 120° of every 360° electrical cycle. The electrical frequency is in the kHz range, far faster than the FET thermal time constants, so the per-FET average is what matters:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{P_{cond}}{6} = 4.35\ \text{W}$$
