# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #33): Engineering Design Package

**Scope:** Loss model, thermal model, bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA plan, and reference netlists.
**Status:** Preliminary. All component values are design-intent and must be verified against the selected vendors' datasheets before layout release.

## 0. Architecture Reality Check

Two assumptions in the brief must be corrected before any math is meaningful.

**1. The STM32H743 cannot be the ESC controller under ArduPilot.**
ArduPilot is flight-control firmware. It does not commutate motors. The correct partition is:

- **STM32H743** (flight controller, running ArduPilot) sends bidirectional DShot600 to:
- **4× AT32F421 or STM32G071** (ESC MCUs, running AM32 or BLHeli_32), which drive:
- **3-phase gate drivers**, which drive:
- **6 MOSFETs per channel** (24 total).

The 48 kHz PWM frequency is an ESC-firmware setting (AM32 `PWM_FREQ`). ArduPilot has no control over it.

**2. "80 A" on a 20×20 mm nano board is a burst rating of roughly 1–2 seconds, not a continuous rating.**
Section 3 derives this from first principles. The defensible continuous rating is about **15 A per channel**. The datasheet should state both numbers along with the conditions under which they apply.

## 2. Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

In 6-step commutation, two phase legs conduct at any instant:

- The **PWM leg** alternates between the high-side FET (fraction D of each period) and the synchronous low-side FET (fraction 1−D).
- The **return leg** holds its low-side FET on continuously.

The current path therefore always passes through exactly **two R_ds(on)**, independent of duty cycle.

**Temperature-dependent on-resistance:**

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big]
$$

**Conduction loss, including PCB copper:**

$$
P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 \,\big[\,2\,R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{Cu}\,\big]
$$

**Copper resistance.** Each 2 oz (70 µm) layer has a sheet resistance of

$$
R_\square = \frac{\rho_{Cu}}{t} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}}{70\times10^{-6}} = 0.246\ \text{m}\Omega/\square
$$

With about 4 squares of path per channel spread over 3 paralleled power layers:

$$
R_{Cu} \approx \frac{4 \times 0.246}{3} \approx 0.33\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

**Results at 80 A:**

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per channel | P_cond, 4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 6400 × 2.33 mΩ = **14.9 W** | 59.6 W |
| 100 °C | 1.36 mΩ | 6400 × 3.05 mΩ = **19.5 W** | **78.1 W** |
