## 1. FOC Inner Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform**, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform**:

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad
v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI Design

The dq motor model is:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

**PI with pole-zero cancellation.** Choose the PI zero K_i/K_p = R/L. The closed loop then becomes first-order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{loop}}{10}
$$

With f_loop = 20 kHz, ω_c = 2π·2 kHz gives K_p = 27.6 V/A and K_i = 7.0×10⁴ V/(A·s).

**Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:**

$$
v_d^{*} = PI_d(e_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^{*} = PI_q(e_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m,\qquad \lambda_m = \tfrac{2}{3}\tfrac{K_t}{p}
$$

**Voltage limiting.** The vector is limited to the linear SVPWM region, |v_dq| ≤ V_bus/√3. Clamp v_d first, then give v_q the remaining headroom. When the vector saturates, feed the excess back to the integrator using back-calculation anti-windup.

### 1.3 SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)

This method is equivalent to classical 7-segment SVPWM:

$$
v_{a,b,c} = \text{invClarke}(v_\alpha,v_\beta),\quad
v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max v + \min v),\quad
d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

## 2. Encoder Velocity Estimation (PLL Observer)

Differentiating a quantized encoder angle at 4 kHz is far too noisy. Use a type-2 tracking PLL instead:

$$
e = \text{wrap}_{\pm\pi}(\theta_{meas} - \hat\theta),\quad
\dot{\hat\theta} = \hat\omega + k_1 e,\quad
\dot{\hat\omega} = k_2 e,\quad k_1 = 2\zeta\omega_n,\ k_2 = \omega_n^2
$$

Recommended settings are ζ = 1 and ω_n = 2π·400 rad/s. The PLL delivers velocity with essentially zero lag at constant acceleration.
