# STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #25): Power-Stage Losses, Thermal Model, and ArduPilot Bi-Directional DShot600 Timer/DMA Allocation

**Document:** SA-25-001, Rev A (engineering draft)
**Scope:** Flight controller (STM32H743, ArduPilot) driving a co-located 4-in-1 ESC, 6S, 80 A per-motor burst rating, 48 kHz ESC PWM.

---

## 0. Architecture Clarification

ArduPilot runs only on the STM32H743. It does not commutate motors. Each of the 4 ESC channels needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. The partitioning is:

```
[STM32H743 / ArduPilot] --4x BDShot600 (single-wire, bidirectional)--> [4x ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32)]
                                                                         └─> FD6288Q 3-ph gate driver ─> 6x N-MOSFET ─> motor
```

This partitioning keeps the 48 kHz PWM and commutation timing away from the H743's timing budget. The H743 owns only the DShot timers and DMA.

> **Feasibility flag.** The analysis below shows that "80 A burst per motor" on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is thermally limited to roughly **1–2 seconds** from warm. Treat 80 A as a transient rating with a stated duration. It is not a continuous-equivalent spec. Section 3.5 gives the numbers.
