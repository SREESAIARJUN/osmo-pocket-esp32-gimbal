## 0. Architecture Clarification and Baseline Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 does **not** generate the 48 kHz MOSFET PWM. In every production 4-in-1 AIO design:

```
┌──────────────────────┐   Bidirectional DShot600 (×4, 1-wire each)
│ STM32H743 (FC)       │──────────────────────────────┐
│ ArduPilot            │                              ▼
└──────────────────────┘        ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
                                │ 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071)        │
                                │ AM32 or BLHeli_32 firmware               │
                                │ 48 kHz complementary PWM, 6-step + BEMF  │
                                │ → 4× 3-ph gate driver → 24× N-MOSFET     │
                                └──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The H743's job is DShot generation and eRPM capture. The thermal and switching model applies to the ESC power stage.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path

With 6-step trapezoidal commutation and complementary (synchronous) PWM, two phases are active at any instant:

- **PWM leg:** the high side conducts for D·T_sw, and the synchronous low side conducts for (1 − D)·T_sw.
- **Static leg:** the low side is on for 100 % of the 60° sector.

The current therefore always flows through exactly **two channel resistances**, independent of duty cycle (ignoring dead time):

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + I_{rms}^2 R_{Cu}$$

### 1.2 Ripple Correction

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{pk,avg}^2\left(1 + \frac{r^2}{12}\right), \qquad r = \frac{\Delta I}{I}, \qquad \Delta I \approx \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{2 L_{ph} f_{sw}}$$
