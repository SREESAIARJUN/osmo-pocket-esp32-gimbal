# Design Package: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #8)

## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Feasibility Flags

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU (for example AM32 on an AT32F421 or STM32G071, or BLHeli_32). The H743 does not commutate motors. It sends bi-directional DShot600 and receives eRPM.

| Parameter | Value used |
|---|---|
| Bus voltage | 6S, 25.2 V maximum (22.2 V nominal) |
| Burst current | 80 A per motor (phase/DC-link equivalent, trapezoidal six-step) |
| PWM frequency | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification) |
| Gate drive | 12 V, half-bridge driver (FD6288Q-class) |
| MOSFET | 40 V, 5×6 mm, R_DS(on) = 0.70 mΩ at 25 °C, Q_gs2+Q_gd ≈ 12 nC, Q_g ≈ 60 nC, Q_rr ≈ 60 nC, R_θJC ≈ 1.2 K/W |
| Board | 30.5×30.5 mm mounting pattern, 8-layer, 2 oz power layers |

> ⚠ **Feasibility flags:**
> 1. A true 20×20 "nano" board cannot carry 4× 80 A. Sustained capacity at that size is about 30–40 A per channel. 80 A is achievable only as a short burst on a 30.5 mm board with 5×6 FETs (Section 3.5).
> 2. 4× 80 A means 320 A of battery current. The XT90 connector, battery C-rating and pigtails become the limit before the PCB does.
> 3. All pin numbers and DMA results below must be verified against the ST datasheet (DS12110) and ArduPilot's hwdef DMA resolver output before tape-out.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ /\text{K}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_hot = 0.70 × 1.6 = **1.12 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step current paths

In each 60° commutation sector, one phase is PWM-high, one is held low, and one floats.

- **PWM-high phase:** the HS FET conducts for D. The LS FET conducts for (1−D) because of synchronous rectification.
- **Held-low phase:** the LS FET conducts 100% of the sector.

So exactly two FETs carry I at every instant:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{hot},\qquad I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET averages

Over the electrical cycle, each phase spends 1/3 of the time high, 1/3 low and 1/3 floating.

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} D\, I^2 R_{hot},\qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(2-D)\, I^2 R_{hot}$$

### 1.4 Numbers at I = 80 A, ripple neglected (I²R_hot = 7.17 W)

| Duty D | P_HS | P_LS (hottest FET) | Per channel (6 FETs) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 (full-throttle burst) | 2.39 W | 2.39 W | 14.3 W |
| 0.5 (current-limited/stall) | 1.19 W | 3.58 W | 14.3 W |

Additional DC-path losses:

- **Copper and connections:** about 0.4 mΩ, giving 80² × 0.4 m = **2.6 W per channel**.
- **Shunt:** a 0.25 mΩ shunt gives **1.6 W**.
