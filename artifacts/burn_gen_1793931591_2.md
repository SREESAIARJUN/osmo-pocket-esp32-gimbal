## 1. FOC Inner Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke transform (two shunts, balanced, iₐ + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

Park transform with electrical angle θₑ = p·θₘ − θ_offset:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 Plant and decoupled PI

The surface PMSM model (L_d = L_q = L) is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

where λₘ = 2Kₜ/(3p).

The control law has three parts: a PI term, a cross-coupling cancellation term, and back-EMF feedforward.

$$v_d^* = PI_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q^* = PI_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$
