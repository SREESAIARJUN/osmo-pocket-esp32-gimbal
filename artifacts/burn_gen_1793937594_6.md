# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: 80A Burst Thermal, 48kHz Switching, and ArduPilot BDShot600 DMA Design

I don't have prior project files for "Job #2", so this is a standalone design built on the baseline assumptions in Section 0. Every equation is parameterized, so you can substitute your actual MOSFET datasheet values and stack geometry and re-run the numbers.

## 0. Architecture and Baseline Assumptions

**Architectural constraint.** ArduPilot running on the H743 cannot commutate motors directly. A 4-in-1 ESC still needs a local ESC MCU per channel (or a quad-capable MCU) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 talks to those MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. The design below is therefore a two-board stack (FC plus ESC), or a single board with five MCUs.

| Parameter | Symbol | Assumed value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max / 22.2 V nominal |
| Burst phase current (per motor) | I | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification) |
| MOSFET | — | 40 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3, R_ds(on),25 = 1.2 mΩ @ 10 V |
| R_ds(on) temperature coefficient | α | 0.006 /K (≈1.5× at 108 °C) |
| Switching times (with ~1 A-class driver, R_g ≈ 2.2 Ω) | t_r, t_f | 12 ns, 10 ns |
| Body diode | V_f, Q_rr | 0.8 V, 40 nC |
| Output charge, gate charge | Q_oss, Q_g | 30 nC, 40 nC @ 10 V |
| Dead time | t_d | 50 ns per edge |
| Board | — | 25×25 mm (20×20 mount), 6-layer, 2 oz (70 µm) all layers |
| Gate driver / ESC MCU | — | FD6288Q-class 3-phase driver / AT32F421 or STM32G071 (AM32) |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
In 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary PWM, the motor current always flows through exactly two MOSFETs, independent of duty D:

- **During D:** the high-side of the PWM leg and the low-side of the sink leg conduct.
- **During (1−D):** the low-side of the PWM leg (sync rectifier) and the low-side of the sink leg conduct.
