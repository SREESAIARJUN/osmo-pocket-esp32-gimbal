# Job #48: STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC)
## Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Budget, Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Plan and Reference Netlist

**Rev A, architecture-level.** Every component value below is a stated assumption. Replace each with datasheet numbers for the parts you actually pick before layout freeze.

---

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Ground Rules

Three facts frame everything below.

1. **The H743 does not drive the gates.** A 4-in-1 ESC needs a dedicated ESC MCU per channel. Typical choices are the AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or BLHeli_32-class firmware.
   - The 48 kHz PWM, dead-time, BEMF commutation and eRPM telemetry all live on those MCUs.
   - The H743 (ArduPilot) only emits DShot600 frames and decodes the GCR eRPM replies.
   - The two domains are coupled only by four signal lines, plus optional current-sense and telemetry lines.
2. **80 A on a "nano" footprint is a burst rating, never continuous.** The math in §3 shows the board is effectively adiabatic for burst durations. The limiter is the copper/FR4 heat capacity and the H743's own temperature ceiling, not the MOSFET junctions.
3. **"80 A burst" is defined here as 80 A RMS phase-pair current per ESC channel**, held for a burst time t_b, at 6S (V_bus = 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal).

## 1. Assumed Power-Stage Parameters

| Symbol | Meaning | Assumed value | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| V_bus | DC link | 25.2 V | 6S full charge (worst case for switching loss) |
| I_ph | Phase current (burst) | 80 A | per channel |
| f_sw | PWM frequency | 48 kHz | AM32 setting |
| R_DS(on),25 | MOSFET on-resistance at 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 40 V, DFN3.3×3.3 / 5×6 class |
| k_T | R_DS(on) temperature factor at T_j = 110 °C | 1.5 | typical normalised curve |
| t_r, t_f | Voltage rise/fall time (in circuit) | 20 ns each | derived in §2.3 |
| Q_rr | Body-diode reverse-recovery charge | 50 nC at 80 A | check at di/dt used |
| C_oss,eff (energy-related) | Output capacitance | 2 nF | |
| V_F | Body-diode forward drop at 80 A | 0.8 V | |
| t_dt | Dead time per edge | 100 ns | AM32 configurable |
| Q_g | Total gate charge | 60 nC | |
| V_drv | Gate-drive voltage | 10 V | FD6288Q-class driver supply |
| R_Cu | PCB + pad path resistance per conducting pair | 0.3 mΩ | 2 oz, 8-layer, via-farmed |

**Topology:** three half-bridges per channel, 6-step trapezoidal with synchronous rectification. At any instant exactly two MOSFETs carry I_ph in series: one high-side (HS) and one low-side (LS) of different phases. During PWM-off, the complementary FET on the chopped leg freewheels.

## 2. Loss Model per ESC Channel

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

With synchronous rectification, the current path always contains two channel-conducting MOSFETs, independent of duty cycle D.

$$
P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2 \cdot R_{DS(on),25} \cdot k_T
$$

$$
P_{cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.5 = 6400 \times 3.0\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W}
$$

Copper path loss:

$$
P_{Cu} = I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.3\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{1.92\ W}
$$

Note that R_DS(on) rises with T_j, so the model is self-heating. A coupled iteration is given in §3.4.

### 2.2 Switching Loss (Hard-Switched Leg)

Per PWM period there is one hard turn-on and one hard turn-off on the chopped device. A triangular V-I overlap approximation gives:

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}
$$

$$
P_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}
$$

Secondary switching terms:

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw = 50 nC × 25.2 × 48 k | 0.060 W |
| C_oss discharge | ½ · C_oss,eff · V_bus² · f_sw = 0.5 × 2 nF × 635 × 48 k | 0.030 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_F · I_ph · 2 t_dt · f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k | 0.614 W |
| Gate drive (2 switching FETs) | 2 · Q_g · V_drv · f_sw = 2 × 60 nC × 10 × 48 k | 0.058 W (dissipated mostly in R_g and the driver) |

### 2.3 Derivation of t_r and t_f

The Miller plateau sets the voltage transition time:

$$
t_{r,f} \approx \frac{Q_{gd} \, (R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int} + R_{drv})}{V_{drv} - V_{plateau}}
$$

Example values: Q_gd = 10 nC, R_g,total = 5.7 Ω, V_plateau = 4 V.

$$
t \approx \frac{10\,\text{nC} \times 5.7}{6\,\text{V}} \approx 9.5\ \text{ns (ideal)}
$$

Common-source inductance (≈ 0.5–1 nH from the DFN package plus layout) and loop ringing roughly double this in practice, hence the 20 ns assumption.

**Design lever:** halving R_g cuts P_sw roughly in half but raises dv/dt and ringing. The ringing threatens BEMF zero-cross detection and EMI into the IMU on the same board. Do not tune this blind; capture V_DS on the bench.

### 2.4 Per-Channel and Board Totals at Burst

| Loss | W per channel |
|---|---|
| Conduction (MOSFETs) | 19.20 |
| PCB copper | 1.92 |
| Switching (overlap) | 1.94 |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 |
| Q_rr + C_oss | 0.09 |
| Gate drive + driver IC | ~0.10 |
| ESC MCU, LDO, shunt | ~0.15 |
| **Total per channel** | **≈ 24.0 W** |
| **4 channels simultaneously** | **≈ 96 W** |

**Key insight:** conduction dominates by about 10:1. Raising f_sw from 24 to 48 kHz costs about 1.2 W per channel.
