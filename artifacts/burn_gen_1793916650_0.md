**Architectural flags you should accept or reject before layout:**

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** On every production AIO of this class, each bridge is driven by a dedicated ESC MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 talks to them over bi-directional DShot. This document assumes that architecture. Driving 4 bridges plus FOC directly from the H743 is possible, but it would consume the very timers needed for DShot and is not supported by ArduPilot.
2. **"80 A" on a nano board is a burst rating only.** The model in §3 shows a burst budget of roughly 1–1.5 s and a continuous rating near 25 A per motor with propwash cooling. If marketing wants "80 A continuous," the answer is no.
3. **Topology is assumed to be 6-step trapezoidal with synchronous rectification,** with complementary PWM on the active phase. This matches AM32's default mode.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R = 1.5 mΩ × 1.45 = **2.18 mΩ**

### 1.2 Current-path accounting for 6-step with synchronous rectification

In each 60° electrical sector, two phases conduct:

- **Phase A (PWM phase):** the high-side FET conducts for a fraction D of each PWM period; its low-side partner conducts for the remaining (1 − D) fraction.
- **Phase B (return phase):** the low-side FET conducts 100% of the sector.

Each FET is "assigned" for 120° per electrical cycle (1/3 of the time). Per-device average conduction loss:

$$P_{HS} = \frac{I_m^2 R_{DS}\,D}{3}$$

$$P_{LS} = \frac{I_m^2 R_{DS}\,(2 - D)}{3}$$

Summing over all six FETs gives the total bridge conduction loss, which is independent of D:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I_m^2 R_{DS}$$
