## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Feasibility Flags

Several facts constrain this design before any math is done.

| # | Issue | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | The STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. The 48 kHz commutation PWM is generated by four **separate ESC MCUs** (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32, or STM32G071), one per motor. | The H743 only generates DShot600 frames and captures eRPM telemetry. It never drives the gates. |
| F2 | 4 × 80 A burst means **320 A at the battery pad** (about 7.1 kW at 22.2 V). | No nano-class board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) can carry 320 A continuously. The XT60 is rated about 60 A continuous. "80 A" must be specified as a **per-channel, time-limited, non-simultaneous** burst rating (Section 4.4). |
| F3 | At 48 kHz with an 80 A phase current, the input capacitor RMS ripple reaches about 40 A per channel. | The input bank is the second-hardest thermal and reliability problem after the MOSFETs (Section 2.6). |
| F4 | Component values below (Rds(on), Qg, Rθ) are **design assumptions**. | Replace them with datasheet values from your selected parts and re-run the model. All equations are parametric. |

## 2. Loss Model (Per Channel, 6 MOSFETs)

### 2.1 Temperature-Dependent Rds(on)

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]
$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 100) = **1.45 mΩ**.

### 2.2 Conduction Loss

In 6-step operation, exactly two phase legs conduct at any instant. In the PWM leg, current alternates between the high-side FET (duty D) and the synchronous low-side FET (1 − D). The return leg's low-side FET conducts continuously during its 60° sector. The current path therefore always contains **two channel resistances in series**:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

Per-FET average loss is distributed over the electrical cycle. Each FET is active for 1/3 of the cycle, so:

$$
P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}\cdot\frac{\text{(sector weighting)}}{} \approx \frac{2 I^2 R_{ds}}{6}
$$

For the worst-case single FET, take the **instantaneous** sector loss for transient analysis:

$$
P_{FET,inst} = I^2 R_{ds}
$$

**At 80 A, T_j = 125 °C:**

- P_cond,ch = 2 × 6400 × 1.45e-3 = **18.6 W**
- P_FET,inst = 6400 × 1.45e-3 = **9.3 W** (during that FET's conduction sector)
- P_FET,avg ≈ **3.1 W** (averaged over the electrical cycle; low eRPM can lock a FET into one sector, so use 9.3 W for stall and startup)

### 2.3 Switching Loss (Hard-Switched PWM Leg)

Only the PWM leg switches. One hard turn-on and one turn-off occur per PWM period:

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}
$$

= 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40e-9 × 48e3 = **1.94 W**

### 2.4 Secondary Switching Terms

| Term | Equation | Value @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw | 30e-9 × 25.2 × 48e3 = **0.036 W** |
| C_oss discharge | ½ C_oss V_bus² f_sw | 0.5 × 1.5e-9 × 635 × 48e3 = **0.023 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f · I · 2t_dt · f_sw | 0.8 × 80 × 200e-9 × 48e3 = **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (2 switching FETs) | 2 · Q_g · V_gs · f_sw | 2 × 40e-9 × 10 × 48e3 = **0.038 W** (dissipated mostly in driver and R_g) |
