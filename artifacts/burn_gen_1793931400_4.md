## 0. Architectural Clarifications

| Issue | Finding | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| H743 on the ESC | ArduPilot does not perform sensorless BLDC commutation. The H743 is the flight controller. Each motor still needs a dedicated ESC MCU. | The AIO has an H743 FC plus 4× AT32F421 (AM32 firmware), linked by on-board DShot traces. |
| "Nano" + 80 A burst | Section 4 shows ~88 W total dissipation at 4×80 A, which a 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 board cannot sustain. | Use a 30.5×30.5 mm layout with 2 oz or more copper. Specify 80 A as a **single-motor or ≤2 s burst** rating. |
| Battery current shunt | At 4×80 A = 320 A, a 0.1 mΩ shunt dissipates 10 W. | Use a 0.1 mΩ 4-terminal shunt with an amplifier, and rate it for burst only (see §5). |

- 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max (22.2 V nominal)

- MOSFET "Q": 40 V, 5×6 PQFN, R_DS(on) = 0.9 mΩ @ 25 °C (V_GS = 10 V), Q_g = 60 nC, Q_sw = Q_gs2 + Q_gd ≈ 14 nC, Q_rr ≈ 40 nC, C_oss(tr) ≈ 2.5 nF, V_SD ≈ 0.8 V, R_thJC ≈ 1.2 K/W

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path Topology

In six-step commutation with synchronous rectification, two phases are active at any instant:
- **PWM ON (D):** the high-side FET of phase A and the low-side FET of phase B conduct.
- **PWM OFF (1−D):** the low-side FET of phase A (synchronous rectifier) and the low-side FET of phase B conduct.

**Key result:** the motor current always flows through exactly **two FETs in series**, independent of duty cycle (excluding dead time).

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{Cu}$$

With low inductance and a high f_sw/f_e ratio, ripple is small, so I_rms ≈ I_avg = 80 A.

### 1.2 Temperature-Dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 115 °C: R = 0.9 × (1 + 0.0045 × 90) = **1.26 mΩ**

### 1.3 Distribution Among the Six FETs (one motor, 80 A)

| FET role | Duty share | Loss expression | P (W) |
|---|---|---|---|
| HS, PWM phase | D/3 per electrical cycle* | I²R·D | 8.06·D |
| LS, sync-rect phase | (1−D)/3 | I²R·(1−D) | 8.06·(1−D) |
| LS, "return" phase | 1/3 continuous | I²R | 8.06 |

*Commutation rotates the roles among the FETs, so averaged over an electrical cycle each FET carries about 1/3 of the total.

Worst single-FET instantaneous loss is **I²R = 6400 × 1.26 mΩ = 8.06 W** (the return LS FET during its 120° window).

### 1.4 Totals per Motor at 80 A

| PCB copper | 6400 × 0.5 mΩ (bus, vias, phase pads; assumed) | **3.20** |

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing half-bridge switches hard: one HS/LS pair per motor.

### 2.1 Transition Times

The gate current during the Miller plateau, with R_G,total = R_drv + R_ext + R_g,int ≈ 2 + 4.7 + 1 = 7.7 Ω and V_plateau ≈ 3.5 V:

$$I_{G,on} = \frac{V_{DRV}-V_{pl}}{R_G} = \frac{10-3.5}{7.7} = 0.84\ \text{A}, \quad t_r = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{G,on}} = 16.6\ \text{ns}$$

$$I_{G,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_G} = 0.45\ \text{A}, \quad t_f = 31\ \text{ns}$$

**Design note:** a turn-off speed-up diode with 2.2 Ω brings t_f to about 12 ns. The baseline below omits it, as a conservative case.

### 2.2 Loss Components (V = 25.2 V, I = 80 A, f = 48 kHz)

| Mechanism | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw = ½·25.2·80·47.6 ns·48 k | **2.30 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 40 nC·25.2·48 k | **0.048 W** |
| C_oss | ½·C_oss,tr·V²·f_sw = ½·2.5 nF·635·48 k | **0.038 W** |
| Dead-time body diode | V_SD·I·2·t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·2·100 ns·48 k | **0.61 W** |
| Gate charge (driver/R_G) | 2·Q_g·V_DRV·f_sw = 2·60 nC·10·48 k | **0.058 W** |
| **Switching subtotal** | | **≈ 3.05 W** |

**Dead-time sensitivity:** each 10 ns of dead time costs 61 mW per motor at 80 A. AM32 dead-time settings should be tuned to about 60–80 ns once the measured t_f is known.

### 2.3 Frequency Trade-off

$$P_{sw}(f) \approx 63.5\ \mu\text{J} \times f_{sw}$$

| f_sw | 24 kHz | 48 kHz | 96 kHz |
|---|---|---|---|
| P_sw per motor (80 A) | 1.5 W | 3.05 W | 6.1 W |

48 kHz is justified by low-inductance (low-kV, high-pole-count) motor ripple and acoustics.
