## 0. Architectural Clarifications

**Two separate processor domains.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate motors. Each ESC channel (or a shared 4-channel MCU) runs its own firmware, typically AM32 or BLHeli_32, on a part such as the AT32F421 or STM32G071. That firmware sets the 48 kHz PWM and generates the bi-directional DShot telemetry reply. The H743's job is to:

- generate DShot600 frames, and
- capture the GCR eRPM replies.

**80 A burst sizing.** "80 A" is modeled here as **per-channel peak phase/bus current**. The analysis below shows this is a short-transient rating (≈1–3 s) on a nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board, not a sustained capability. If your spec means 80 A *total* (4 × 20 A), substitute I = 20 A. All losses scale with I² for conduction and with I for switching.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × (1 + 0.5) = **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous commutation

In trapezoidal 6-step drive, exactly two phases conduct at any time: one high-side path and one low-side path. With synchronous rectification (complementary PWM):

- During the **ON** interval (duty D), current flows through HS(A) → motor → LS(B).
- During the **OFF** interval, current freewheels through LS(A) (synchronously rectified) → motor → LS(B).

So two MOSFET R_DS(on) are always in series with the phase current, independent of duty cycle (dead time excepted).

### 1.3 RMS phase current including ripple

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{pk,avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

The ripple is set by the motor inductance:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph\text{-}ph}\, f_{sw}}$$

For a typical 2207-class motor, L_ph-ph ≈ 20 µH. At D = 0.5 and V_bemf ≈ 12 V:

$$\Delta I_{pp} \approx \frac{13.2 \times 0.5}{20\,\mu H \times 48\,kHz} \approx 6.9\,A$$

The ripple term adds 6.9²/12 ≈ 4 A². Against I² = 6400 A² this is negligible at 80 A, so I_rms ≈ 80.02 A.

### 1.4 Conduction loss per channel

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond (80 A) | P_cond (20 A) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 12.8 W | 0.80 W |
| 100 °C | 1.375 mΩ | 17.6 W | 1.10 W |
| 125 °C | 1.5 mΩ | **19.2 W** | 1.20 W |

Copper loss must be added. A 2 oz, 3 mm wide, 15 mm trace is about 1.2 mΩ, which adds roughly 7.7 W at 80 A. **This is why nano 4-in-1 boards need 2–3 oz copper and power pours on multiple layers.** Use 3 oz outer and 2 oz inner layers, and stitch the phase pours on at least two layers.
