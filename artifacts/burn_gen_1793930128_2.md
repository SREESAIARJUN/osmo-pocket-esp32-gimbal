# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #29): Power-Stage Model, Thermal Budget, Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation, and Reference Netlist

**Feasibility finding:** An 80 A per-motor burst on a 20×20 mm nano 4-in-1 is **not thermally survivable** beyond about 1–2 s, even with ideal MOSFETs. The math below shows why. I give a corrected configuration and keep 80 A as the design-stress case.

---

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

### 0.1 Who does what

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. It does not commutate the motors. The 4-in-1 ESC carries **four independent ESC MCUs** running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Typical choices are AT32F421 or STM32G071.

| Function | Silicon | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Attitude loop, DShot600 generation, eRPM capture | STM32H743 @ 480 MHz | Loop 1–2 kHz typical (bidir limit ~8–10 kHz) |
| 6-step commutation, **48 kHz PWM**, sync rectification | ESC MCU ×4 | 48 kHz |
| Gate drive | 3-phase half-bridge driver ×4 | 48 kHz |

So "48 kHz switching" is configured in **AM32 (PWM frequency)**, not in ArduPilot.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step trapezoidal with complementary PWM (sync rectification)

During each 60° electrical sector, two phases conduct:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** the high-side Q_AH conducts for duty D. The low-side Q_AL conducts for (1 − D) as the synchronous freewheel path.
- **Phase B (return leg):** the low-side Q_BL is ON for 100% of the sector.

Ignoring ripple (I_ripple ≪ I at 48 kHz with typical motor inductance):

$$
P_{cond,AH}=D\,I^2R_{DS}(T_j),\quad
P_{cond,AL}=(1-D)\,I^2R_{DS}(T_j),\quad
P_{cond,BL}=I^2R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

The instantaneous total per channel is **independent of duty**:

$$
\boxed{P_{cond,ch}=2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)}
$$
