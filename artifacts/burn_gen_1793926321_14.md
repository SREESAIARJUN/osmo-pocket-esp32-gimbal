# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #66): Power Stage Model, DShot DMA Plan, and Netlists

## 0. Architecture Corrections

Two issues in the brief need fixing before any math is meaningful.

**1. The H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.**
- ArduPilot does not perform BLDC commutation.
- The real architecture splits into two parts:
  - **Flight controller (STM32H743):** sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs and receives eRPM telemetry back.
  - **4-in-1 ESC:** four independent MCUs (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). Each MCU generates 48 kHz complementary PWM into a 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs.
- Section 2 (power stage) is therefore the ESC MCU's domain. Section 3 (DMA/timers) is the H743's domain.

**2. "80 A burst" on a nano board is a time-limited rating, not a steady-state rating.**
- Section 2.5 shows the board reaches thermal limits in about 1–3 s at full burst on all four channels.
- Four simultaneous 80 A channels also implies about 320 A from the battery. Neither an XT60 nor a typical 6S pack can deliver that.
- The burst rating is physically meaningful only per motor, as a transient.

All component values below are representative assumptions (marked **[A]**). Substitute datasheet values for your actual parts and rerun the equations; the model structure is what matters.
