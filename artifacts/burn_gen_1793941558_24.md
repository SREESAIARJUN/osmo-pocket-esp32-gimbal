## 1. Electrical Operating Model

**Commutation scheme.** Six-step trapezoidal with synchronous (complementary) PWM. In any 60° electrical sector:

- Phase X (PWM leg): the high-side FET is on for D·T_sw. The low-side FET conducts synchronously for (1−D)·T_sw, minus dead time.
- Phase Y (return leg): the low-side FET is on continuously.
- Phase Z: floating, used for BEMF sensing.

**Consequence.** Neglecting dead time, motor current always flows through exactly two channels in series. Conduction loss is therefore essentially independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

**Ripple correction.** With a flat-top phase current I_ph and peak-to-peak PWM ripple ΔI:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$
