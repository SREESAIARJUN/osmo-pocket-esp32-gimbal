## 1. FOC Inner Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

θ_off is found at calibration by applying v_d > 0 at θ_e = 0 and reading θ_m.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, balanced phases)

$$i_\alpha = i_a, \qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt{3}}$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant model in the dq frame

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q, \qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

### 1.5 Current PI with decoupling (pole-zero cancellation)

For a closed-loop current bandwidth ω_c (choose ω_c ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz, at most f_PWM/10):

$$K_p^{i} = L\,\omega_c, \qquad K_i^{i} = R\,\omega_c$$

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

The resulting closed loop is first order: i/i* = ω_c / (s + ω_c).

- **Voltage-mode fallback (no shunts):** v_q* = R i_q* + ω_e λ_m and v_d* = 0.
- **Voltage limit:** the vector magnitude is limited to |v| ≤ V_bus/√3 (the linear SVPWM region). When saturated, v_q is scaled while v_d is prioritized.

### 1.6 Inverse Park and inverse Clarke

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e, \qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

$$v_a = v_\alpha, \qquad v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2} v_\beta, \qquad v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2} v_\beta$$
