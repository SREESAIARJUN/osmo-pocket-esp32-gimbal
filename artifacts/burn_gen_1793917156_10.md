# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot Design Package

## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Stated Assumptions

Three clarifications shape everything that follows:

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In an AIO "FC + 4-in-1 ESC" board, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32, BLHeli_32 or Bluejay. Example MCUs are the AT32F421 and STM32G071. **The 48 kHz PWM frequency is an ESC-firmware setting.** ArduPilot does not control it.
2. **"80A" is ambiguous.** Vendors usually mean per-channel burst current, and they rarely specify phase versus battery current. Here, **80 A is treated as the phase (MOSFET) current per channel.** Battery current is lower: I_bat ≈ D·I_phase.
3. **The numbers below are a model, not a datasheet.** Every device parameter is a placeholder. Substitute your actual MOSFET and driver datasheet values; the equations are structured so you can recompute directly.
