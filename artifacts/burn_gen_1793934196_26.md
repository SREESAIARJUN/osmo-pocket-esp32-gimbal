## 0. Feasibility Flags

These are physical constraints the math below exposes. Read them before the detailed sections.

| # | Issue | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | **4 × 80 A at 6S is about 7.7 kW and 320 A battery current.** | An XT60 is rated about 60 A continuous. A "nano" (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board cannot carry or dissipate this continuously. 80 A must be a **per-channel, short-duration burst** spec with an explicit time limit (derived in §2.6). |
| F2 | **The H743 does not drive the FETs.** | A 4-in-1 needs 4 local ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32) or one dedicated motor-control MCU. The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to those MCUs. This architecture is used throughout. |
| F3 | **At 100 % duty there is no PWM switching loss.** | A true full-throttle burst has commutation losses only. The worst case for combined loss is **high current at D ≈ 0.85–0.95**, which is modelled below. |
| F4 | **Bulk capacitor ripple dominates at mid-duty.** | Per channel, I_C,rms ≈ I·√(D(1−D)), which is 40 A rms at D = 0.5 and 80 A. Low-ESR polymer plus MLCC banks are mandatory. |
