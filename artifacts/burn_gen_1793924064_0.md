## 1. Field-Oriented Control (inner current loop, T_s = 50 µs)

### 1.1 Transforms
**Clarke (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):**
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}$:
$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Motor model in the dq frame
$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)$$
$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q$$

### 1.3 Current PI with decoupling
Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole R/L. The closed loop then becomes first-order with bandwidth $\omega_{ci}$:
$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci},\qquad \omega_{ci}\approx 2\pi\cdot 1.5\text{ kHz}\ \ (\le \omega_{s}/20)$$

The controller adds feedforward terms that cancel the dq cross-coupling and back-EMF:
$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e(L i_d+\lambda_m)$$

**Delay compensation.** Measurement, computation and PWM update add about 1.5 sample periods of delay. Rotate the inverse Park transform by a predicted angle:
$$\theta_e^{\,\text{inv}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s$$
