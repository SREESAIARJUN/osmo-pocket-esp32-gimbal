# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC Stack: Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and Bi-Directional DShot600 DMA Plan

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Design Flags

I have no access to the Job #48 requirements package, so every number below rests on stated assumptions. Each equation is written so you can substitute your actual BOM values. Treat the numbers as preliminary until you validate them against datasheets and thermal tests.

**Architecture clarification.** An STM32H743 does not commutate the motors directly. The usual partition is:

- **Flight controller:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot. It emits bi-directional DShot600 on 4 timer channels.
- **4-in-1 ESC:** four small MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32 (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071). Each ESC MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM and reports eRPM.

The 48 kHz switching frequency is therefore an ESC-firmware setting. ArduPilot does not control it.

**Baseline assumptions (replace with your BOM):**

| Parameter | Symbol | Assumed value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max |
| Burst phase current per channel | I_ph | 80 A (conservative: treated as phase current, not battery current) |
| MOSFET R_DS(on) @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R₂₅ | 1.2 mΩ (40 V, 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 DFN class) |
| R_DS(on) temperature coefficient | α | 0.0045 /K |
| Switching transition times | t_r, t_f | 20 ns each |
| Dead time | t_d | 100 ns |
| Body-diode forward voltage | V_F | 0.8 V |
| Reverse-recovery charge | Q_rr | 30 nC |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 40 nC |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 50 nC |
| Gate-drive voltage | V_GS | 10 V |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| Commutation | — | 6-step trapezoidal, complementary (synchronous) PWM |

> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag: 4 × 80 A burst on a "nano" (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board is not credible.** Commercial nano 4-in-1 ESCs are rated about 35–45 A continuous and 45–55 A burst. Section 3 shows that 80 A per channel gives about 93 W total dissipation, which heats a nano board at roughly 26 K/s adiabatically. Confirm whether "80A" means total battery current, or move to a 30.5×30.5 mm board with a heatsink. My recommendation follows in Section 3.5.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × 1.3375 = **1.605 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current-path topology (6-step, complementary PWM)

In each 60° sector there are three legs:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D, and the low-side FET conducts (synchronous rectification) for 1−D.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET is fully on.
- **Floating leg:** both FETs are off (BEMF sensing).

So exactly **two FET channels are in series with the phase current at all times**, whatever D is.

Per-leg conduction (each leg is active for 2/3 of the electrical cycle):

$$P_{leg} = \tfrac{2}{3} I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}$$

Per ESC channel:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = 3 \cdot \tfrac{2}{3}\, I_{ph}^2 R_{DS} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)}$$

### 1.3 Distribution across the six FETs

Per FET, averaged over an electrical cycle:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} D\, I^2 R \qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(2 - D)\, I^2 R$$

Check: 3 × (P_HS + P_LS) = 3 × (2/3)·I²R... per leg pair, summing to 2I²R per channel. ✓
