# Gimbal Job #43: FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion Design

I don't have the Job #43 spec sheet, so this design uses explicit placeholder parameters. Everything that depends on hardware is collected in the `Config` structs so you can substitute real values.

**Assumptions:**
- **Motors:** 3× gimbal BLDC motors (e.g., GBM4108-class, 11 pole pairs, 12 slots).
- **Current sensing:** inline current sense on 2 phases.
- **Encoders:** 14-bit absolute magnetic encoders (AS5048A/MT6701-class), one per joint.
- **IMUs:** 2× ICM-42688-P rigidly mounted on the camera plate, symmetric about the plate center, on separate SPI buses. Both are sampled from one timer-triggered ISR.
