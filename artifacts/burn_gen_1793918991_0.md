# STM32H743 Flight Controller + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: System Design Package (Job #35)

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility

**System partitioning.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller. It runs ArduPilot and should not commutate motors. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 with bi-directional DShot support. Suitable parts are the AT32F421 and the STM32G071.

```
[6S LiPo] ─► [4-in-1 ESC: 4× (MCU + FD6288Q + 6× N-FET + shunt)] ◄─ 8-pin JST-SH ─► [H743 FC]
                                         ▲ DShot600 bidir (×4), Vbat, Curr, GND, (Tlm UART)
```

**Feasibility warning on "nano" + 80 A burst.**
- A 20×20 mm board cannot physically fit 24 power FETs with 80 A-capable copper. At best it fits 3.3×3.3 mm FETs on 2 oz copper, which supports about 35–45 A burst.
- This design assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm** ESC with 5×6 mm DFN FETs and 4-layer copper at 3/2/2/3 oz.
- **80 A is a 5–10 s burst rating, not continuous.** The thermal model below proves this.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-Dependent On-Resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_DS = 1.0 mΩ × 1.3825 = **1.38 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-Step Synchronous Rectification

With 120° trapezoidal drive and complementary PWM, current always flows through exactly two FETs:
- **During PWM-on:** the high-side FET of phase X and the low-side FET of phase Y conduct.
- **During PWM-off:** the low-side FET of phase X (synchronous freewheel) and the low-side FET of phase Y conduct.

The conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond}=I^2\,[\,2R_{DS}(T_j)+R_{Cu}\,]$$
$$=80^2\,(2.76+0.25)\,\text{mΩ}= \mathbf{19.3\ W\ per\ motor}$$

A FOC/sinusoidal driver gives the same result using I_rms = I_pk/√2 per phase across three phases.

### 1.3 Self-Consistent Junction Temperature

Because R_DS depends on T_j and T_j depends on loss, the conduction loss must be solved iteratively:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + P(T_j)\cdot Z_{th}(t)$$

Thermal runaway is avoided when:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}\,Z_{th} = I^2\,R_{DS,25}\,\alpha\,Z_{th} < 1$$

Per FET at 80 A: 6400 × 0.001 × 0.0045 × Z_th < 1, so **Z_th < 34.7 K/W**. This is easily satisfied. The design is stable, but the margin collapses if the FET loses its solder-void-free thermal pad.
