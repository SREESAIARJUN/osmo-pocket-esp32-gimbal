### Architectural caveats

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** On every production 4-in-1 design, each motor channel (or the whole 4-in-1) runs its own ESC MCU, typically AT32F421, STM32G071 or GD32 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600. This document models it that way. Making the H743 commutate four motors directly is not supported by ArduPilot.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano board is transient-only.** The thermal model in §3 shows about 133 W of total loss at 4×80 A. On a roughly 4–5 J/K board this sustains only about **2 s**. Real nano 4-in-1 boards that advertise 80 A use it as a marketing burst number. Design to the continuous figure (§3.4), and enforce limits in firmware.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous commutation

In each 60° electrical sector, phase A is PWM'd and phase B is held low:

- **HS_A** conducts for a fraction D of each PWM period.
- **LS_A** conducts for 1 − D (synchronous rectification), apart from dead time.
- **LS_B** conducts for the whole sector.

Conduction power per channel is therefore:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,D + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)(1-D) + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

Duty cycle drops out. That is a useful property of synchronous rectification.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

This gives a factor of 1.6 at 125 °C.

| Case | R_DS | P_cond per channel (80 A) |
|---|---|---|
| Cold, typ (25 °C) | 1.2 mΩ | 2·6400·0.0012 = **15.4 W** |
| Hot, max (125 °C) | 2.4 mΩ | 2·6400·0.0024 = **30.7 W** |

### 1.3 Parasitic copper and shunt

- **Phase and bus copper:** about 0.3 mΩ effective per current path on 2 oz internal layers, giving 2·6400·0.0003 = **3.8 W**. This loss is spread across the PCB.
- **Shunt (if per-channel):** 0.25 mΩ gives 6400·0.00025 = **1.6 W**.
