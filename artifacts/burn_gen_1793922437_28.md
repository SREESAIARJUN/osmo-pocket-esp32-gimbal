# STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Physics, BDShot DMA Plan, and Netlists

## 0. Architecture Corrections

Three points need to be settled before the math. Otherwise the model describes a board that cannot work.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is flight-control firmware. It outputs DShot and has no FOC or 6-step commutation stack. The "4-in-1 ESC" therefore needs four dedicated ESC MCUs, one per motor, running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. Recommended parts are the AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6. The H743 talks to them over on-board DShot600 traces.
2. **The 48 kHz switching frequency is an ESC-MCU parameter** (AM32 `PWM_FREQ`), not an H743 timer.
3. **An 80 A burst on a 20×20 mm nano board is thermally limited to roughly 1–2 s with all four motors loaded** (derived in §3). I model it honestly as a short-transient rating. Treat it as a peak spec, not a sustained one. Credible continuous ratings for this footprint are about 35–45 A per motor with prop-wash airflow.
