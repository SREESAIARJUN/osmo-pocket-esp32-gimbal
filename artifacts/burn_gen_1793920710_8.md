# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80 A ESC: Design Model (Job #13)

## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Feasibility Notes

**The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every viable 4-in-1 design, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs. Each ESC MCU runs AM32 or BLHeli_32 and handles 6-step commutation.

| Block | Assumed part (substitutable) |
|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 480 MHz, timer clock 240 MHz |
| ESC MCU (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071, running AM32 |
| Gate driver (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap, 250 V, ~1.5/1.8 A) |
| MOSFETs (×24) | 40 V N-ch, PDFN 3.3×3.3 or 5×6. R_DS(on),25 = 1.5 mΩ, Q_g = 60 nC, Q_gd = 10 nC, Q_rr = 30 nC, Q_oss = 40 nC, R_θJC = 1.5 K/W |
| Bus | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V (full charge) |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification) |

**Feasibility flag:** "Nano" plus "80 A per channel" is only credible as a short burst (≤ 1–3 s). The math below shows why. Continuous rating on a 20×20 or 30×30 board is roughly 35–45 A per channel with airflow. Treat 80 A as a thermally capacity-limited transient, not a steady state.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance
$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.5 × 1.45 ≈ **2.2 mΩ** (datasheet curves typically give ~1.5–1.6×; use 2.4 mΩ as the worst case).

### 1.2 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification
At any instant, exactly two phase legs carry the phase current I. In each conducting leg, the current flows through exactly one FET: either the high-side (during the D interval) or the complementary low-side (during the 1−D interval). Therefore:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) \quad\text{(independent of duty cycle)}$$

Averaged over the 6 FETs per channel (each leg is active 2/3 of the electrical cycle):

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} = \frac{2I^2R}{6} = \frac{I^2R}{3}$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A burst

| Quantity | R = 1.5 mΩ (cold) | R = 2.4 mΩ (hot) |
|---|---|---|
| P_cond per channel | 19.2 W | **30.7 W** |
| P_cond per FET (avg) | 3.2 W | **5.1 W** |
| P_cond, 4 channels | 76.8 W | **122.9 W** |

### 1.4 Copper and shunt losses (not negligible)
- **Shunt:** 0.25 mΩ shunt on the low-side return carries average bus current I_bus ≈ D·I. At D = 0.9: 0.25 mΩ × 72² ≈ **1.3 W** per channel.
- **Traces and planes:** Assume ~0.5 mΩ effective per phase path (2 oz, multi-layer stitched). 2 × 80² × 0.5 mΩ ≈ **6.4 W** per channel. This is often underestimated, so pour phase outputs on all layers.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. Hard switching occurs on one FET per transition pair.

### 2.1 V–I overlap loss
Transition times are set by the driver current through the Miller plateau:

$$t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_{drv,on}} \approx \frac{10\,nC}{1.5\,A} \approx 7\,ns,\quad t_f \approx \frac{10\,nC}{1.8\,A}\approx 6\,ns$$

Add gate-resistor and layout margin, and use t_r = t_f = 10 ns:

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r+t_f) f = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\,ns \times 48\,kHz = \mathbf{0.97\ W}$$

### 2.2 Output capacitance and reverse recovery
$$P_{oss} \approx Q_{oss} V f = 40\,nC \times 25.2 \times 48k = 0.048\ W$$
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V f = 30\,nC \times 25.2 \times 48k = 0.036\ W$$

The reverse-recovery current also adds to turn-on overlap. A ~+15 % margin on P_VI covers it.

### 2.3 Dead-time body-diode conduction
Two dead-time intervals per period, t_dt = 50 ns each, V_F = 0.8 V:

$$P_{dt} = V_F\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f = 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\,ns \times 48k = \mathbf{0.31\ W}$$

### 2.4 Gate drive (dissipated in the driver and R_g, not the FET)
$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f = 2 \times 60\,nC \times 10 \times 48k = 0.058\ W$$

### 2.5 Switching summary per channel at 80 A

| Term | W |
|---|---|
| V–I overlap (+15 % for Q_rr) | 1.11 |
| C_oss | 0.05 |
| Q_rr (diode) | 0.04 |
| Dead time | 0.31 |
| **Total** | **≈ 1.5 W** |

Conclusion: at 48 kHz, switching is about 5 % of conduction loss. Lowering f_sw to 24 kHz saves only ~0.7 W per channel but increases current ripple and acoustic noise. **The design is conduction-dominated, so spend budget on R_DS(on) and copper, not faster drivers.**

## 3. Total Loss Budget at 80 A Burst (4 Channels Simultaneously)

| Source | Per channel | ×4 |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction (hot) | 30.7 W | 122.9 W |
| Switching | 1.5 W | 6.0 W |
| Copper/planes | 6.4 W | 25.6 W |
| Shunt | 1.3 W | 5.2 W |
| Gate drive + logic + BEC | — | ~2 W |
| **Total** | **~40 W** | **~162 W** |

Efficiency check: P_out ≈ 4 × 25.2 × 72 A (avg bus) ≈ 7.3 kW, so η ≈ 97.8 %. Efficiency is fine; **heat density is the problem**.
