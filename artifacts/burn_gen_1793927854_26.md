## 0. Architectural Corrections

These points change the model, so they come first.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** The 48 kHz PWM, dead-time and six-step or sinusoidal commutation run on four dedicated ESC MCUs (one per motor, e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32-class firmware). The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to those MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. One H743 cannot close four 48 kHz commutation loops while also running ArduPilot.

2. **"Nano" form factor vs. 80 A per motor.** A 20×20 mm board cannot hold an H743 (LQFP100, 14×14 mm), 24 power MOSFETs, 4 gate drivers and 4 ESC MCUs.
   - **Baseline adopted:** 25.5×25.5 mm AIO (Ø2 mm holes, 20×20 pattern, 8-layer, 2 oz on all layers).
   - **Alternative:** a 20×20 two-board stack.
   - Section 3 shows **80 A is a burst rating (seconds), not continuous**, at any nano size.

3. **All numbers depend on the assumed MOSFET and driver parameters in §1.** Re-run the equations with your actual datasheet values. Treat the outputs as design estimates, not certified ratings.
