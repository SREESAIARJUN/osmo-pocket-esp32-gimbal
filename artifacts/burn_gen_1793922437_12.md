# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal and DShot Architecture (Job #23)

## 0. Feasibility Check and Assumptions

Two problems with the brief need resolving before the math.

**1. "Nano" form factor vs. 80 A burst.** A 20×20 mm board cannot carry 4×80 A, even briefly. The transient calculation in §3 shows this. This design therefore uses a **30.5×30.5 mm stack**:

- **Board A:** STM32H743 flight controller.
- **Board B:** 4-in-1 ESC.

**2. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** Each ESC channel uses its own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600. The 48 kHz figure is the ESC PWM frequency.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path
In 6-step synchronous PWM, exactly two MOSFETs carry phase current at every instant, dead time aside:

- During PWM-on: the high-side of the PWM leg and the low-side of the return leg.
- During PWM-off: the low-side of the PWM leg (synchronous rectification) and the low-side of the return leg.

So conduction loss is **independent of duty cycle**:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + I_{rms}^2\,R_{cu}$$

### 1.2 Temperature Dependence
$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega\,(1+0.005\cdot85) = 1.425\,\text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Ripple Correction
$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12},\qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

Take a typical 5″–7″ motor with L_ph ≈ 15 µH (line-to-line, ×2 phases in path), at worst-case D=0.5 and V_bemf ≈ 12 V:

$$\Delta I \approx \frac{13.2 \cdot 0.5}{30\,\mu\text{H}\cdot48\,\text{kHz}} \approx 4.6\,\text{A}$$

This makes ΔI²/12 ≈ 1.8 A², which is negligible against 6400 A². Higher f_sw is what keeps this small.

### 1.4 Result at 80 A
$$P_{cond,ch} = 2(6400)(1.425\text{m}) + 6400(0.3\text{m}) = 18.24 + 1.92 = \mathbf{20.2\ W}$$

Because R depends on T_j, the loss is self-reinforcing. Solve it iteratively with the thermal model in §3; the design is stable if dP/dT_j · Z_th < 1.
