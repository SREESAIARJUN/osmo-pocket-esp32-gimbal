## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path (6-step commutation, complementary PWM)

In any 60° sector, one high-side FET (phase X) is PWM-switched and one low-side FET (phase Y) is held on:

- **ON interval (D):** current flows through HS_X, the motor, then LS_Y.
- **OFF interval (1−D):** current freewheels through LS_X (synchronous) and LS_Y.

The total resistance in the path is therefore:

$$R_{path} = D\cdot R_{ds} + (1-D)\cdot R_{ds} + R_{ds} = 2R_{ds}$$

This result is **independent of duty cycle**. Conduction loss per ESC channel is:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

| T_j (°C) | R_ds (mΩ) | P_cond,ch at 80 A (W) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 | 1.00 | 12.8 |
| 80 | 1.25 | 16.0 |
| 125 | 1.45 | 18.6 |

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle. At full-throttle burst (D → 1):

$$P_{FET} \approx \frac{I^2 R_{ds}}{3} \approx 3.1\ \text{W at }T_j=125^\circ\text{C}$$

At partial throttle, the loss shifts toward the low-side FETs. The synchronous freewheel adds (1−D)·I²R on LS_X, so the **low-side FETs are the hot devices below ~70% throttle**. Place them on the best copper.

### 1.4 Parasitic copper

PCB phase and bus copper adds R_cu. For 2 oz copper (0.5 mΩ/sq at 70 µm) and roughly 1.5 squares per path:

$$P_{cu,ch} \approx I^2 R_{cu} \approx 6400 \times 0.75\text{m}\Omega \approx 4.8\ \text{W}$$

This is not negligible. It is the reason 4-in-1 nano boards use multiple paralleled internal planes.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Per channel, only the PWM leg switches hard. With synchronous rectification, one hard-switched FET pair per sector:

$$P_{sw,ch} = \underbrace{\tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw}}_{\text{V-I overlap}} + \underbrace{Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw}}_{\text{recovery}} + \underbrace{C_{oss}V_{bus}^2 f_{sw}}_{\text{2 FETs}\times\frac12 CV^2} + \underbrace{2V_F I_{ph} t_{dt} f_{sw}}_{\text{dead-time diode}} + \underbrace{2Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw}}_{\text{gate}}$$

| Term | Calculation | W |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | 0.5 · 25.2 · 80 · 20 ns · 48 k | 0.97 |
| Q_rr | 30 nC · 25.2 · 48 k | 0.04 |
| C_oss | 1.5 nF · 25.2² · 48 k | 0.05 |
| Dead-time diode | 2 · 0.8 · 80 · 100 ns · 48 k | 0.61 |
| Gate drive | 2 · 50 nC · 10 V · 48 k (dissipated mostly in driver and R_g) | 0.05 |
| **Total P_sw,ch** | | **≈ 1.7 W** |

**Sensitivity.** Overlap and dead-time losses scale linearly with f_sw. Going from 24 kHz to 48 kHz costs about 0.8 W per channel in exchange for lower current ripple and quieter motors. That is acceptable, because conduction dominates by roughly 11×.
