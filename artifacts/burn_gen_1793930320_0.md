## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle from the mechanical encoder angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a, \qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

Inverse Park transform:

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e, \qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant Model and Decoupled PI

For a surface-mount PMSM ($L_d = L_q = L$):

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q, \qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

The PI law with cross-coupling and back-EMF feedforward cancels the coupling terms and leaves a first-order plant $1/(Ls + R)$. Choosing $K_i/K_p = R/L$ cancels the plant pole (pole-zero cancellation). The closed loop then becomes a first-order system $\omega_c/(s + \omega_c)$:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c, \qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c, \qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{\text{PWM}}}{10}$$

The control law is:

$$v_d^* = K_p e_d + K_i\!\int e_d\,dt - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q^* = K_p e_q + K_i\!\int e_q\,dt + \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

The flux linkage follows from the torque constant: $\lambda_m = \tfrac{2}{3}\,K_t/p$.

### 1.3 Voltage Limiting with Anti-Windup

The available voltage with SVPWM is $V_{\max} = V_{\text{bus}}/\sqrt3$. Limit the voltage vector to a circle, giving priority to $d$:

$$v_d^{\text{sat}} = \text{clamp}(v_d^*,\ \pm V_{\max})$$

$$v_q^{\text{sat}} = \text{clamp}\!\left(v_q^*,\ \pm\sqrt{V_{\max}^2 - (v_d^{\text{sat}})^2}\right)$$

Use back-calculation for the integrator, with $K_{aw} \approx 1/K_p$ in volts-to-error units:

$$I_{k+1} = I_k + T_s\left[K_i e_k + \frac{K_i}{K_p}\left(v^{\text{sat}} - v^*\right)\right]$$

### 1.4 SVPWM via Min-Max Zero-Sequence Injection

$$v_a = v_\alpha,\quad v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta$$

$$v_0 = -\tfrac12\left(\max(v_{abc}) + \min(v_{abc})\right), \qquad D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{\text{bus}}}$$

## 2. Cascaded Angle and Velocity Control

```
θ_ref ─► [Angle P + FF] ─► ω_ref ─► [Velocity PI(D)] ─► i_q,ref ─► (+) ─► [Current PI] ─► Motor
             ▲                          ▲                          ▲
       θ (fused IMU)              ω (fused gyro)         i_q,cog(θ_m)  (anti-cogging)
```

For a gimbal, the angle and rate feedback come from the camera-plate IMUs. The encoder is used only for commutation and the cogging lookup. This is what gives inertial stabilization.

### 2.1 Velocity Loop

The plant from $i_q$ to $\omega$, with current-loop bandwidth $\omega_c \gg \omega_v$, is:

$$G_v(s) = \frac{K_t}{J s + B}$$

Use a PI controller with optional filtered derivative and acceleration feedforward:

$$i_q^* = K_{pv} e_\omega + K_{iv}\!\int e_\omega\,dt - K_{dv}\,\dot\omega_f + \frac{J}{K_t}\dot\omega_{\text{ref}}$$

Symmetric-optimum tuning, with crossover $\omega_v \approx \omega_c / 8$:

$$K_{pv} = \frac{J\,\omega_v}{K_t}, \qquad K_{iv} = K_{pv}\frac{\omega_v}{4}$$
