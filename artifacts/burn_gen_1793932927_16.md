## 0. Architecture Clarification and Design Assumptions

**Topology decision.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Bidirectional DShot requires a closed-loop ESC firmware on each motor channel, so the H743 does **not** commutate the motors directly. The power stage uses one ESC MCU per channel, running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware.

```
                    ┌────────────────────────── FC BOARD (20×20 / 25.5×25.5) ──┐
 VBAT ─┬─► 5V/10V BEC ─► 3V3 LDO ─► STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) ── IMU/Baro/OSD/Flash│
       │                                  │ TIM1 CH1..CH4 (bi-dir DShot600)      │
       │                                  ▼                                       │
       │   ┌───────── ESC BOARD ───────────────────────────────────────────────┐ │
       └──►│ 4 × [ ESC MCU ─► 3-ph gate driver ─► 6 (or 12) N-MOSFETs ─► motor ]│ │
           │ Bulk + MLCC DC link, shunt/current sense, NTC                     │ │
           └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘ │
```

**Baseline parameters.** Every number below is derived from these, so substitute your actual datasheet values where they differ.

| Symbol | Meaning | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 6S nominal / full charge | 22.2 V / 25.2 V |
| I_burst | per-channel burst (phase current at full duty) | 80 A |
| I_cont | per-channel continuous target | 45 A |
| f_sw | PWM frequency | 48 kHz |
| R_DS(on),25 | per FET, max @ V_GS=10 V | 0.90 mΩ (5×6 mm 40 V class) |

| V_SD | body diode forward drop @ 80 A | 0.85 V |
| t_dt | dead time (each edge) | 60 ns |
| T_amb | ambient (inside frame, propwash) | 40 °C |

> **Feasibility warning:** 80 A per channel on a nano (20×20 mm) footprint is at the physical limit. The math in §1 shows it is achievable only as a **short burst (≈1–3 s)**, with 2× paralleled FETs per switch or very low-R_DS(on) 5×6 devices, plus ≥2 oz copper. Treat 80 A as a transient rating, not a continuous one.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Six-step current path

In trapezoidal (six-step) commutation with synchronous rectification and high-side PWM:

- **During on-time (D):** current flows through HS FET (phase A), the motor, and the LS FET (phase B).
- **During off-time (1−D):** current freewheels through LS FET (phase A, synchronous), the motor, and the LS FET (phase B).

The phase-B low-side FET therefore conducts for 100 % of the 60° sector. The chopped leg shares the conduction between HS (D) and LS (1−D).

Average battery current relates to phase current by:

$$I_{batt} = D \cdot I_{ph} \quad\Rightarrow\quad I_{ph} = \frac{I_{batt}}{D}$$

At burst (full throttle) D → ~0.98, so I_ph ≈ 80 A.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\bigl[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\bigr],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ \mathrm{K^{-1}} \;\Rightarrow\; k_{125} = 1.55$$

$$R_{DS}(125\,°C) = 0.90 \times 1.55 = 1.395\ \mathrm{m\Omega}$$

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 \Bigl[\underbrace{D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS,A}}_{\text{chopped leg}} + \underbrace{R_{LS,B}}_{\text{return leg}}\Bigr] \approx I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

**Single FET per switch, at T_j = 125 °C:**

$$P_{cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.395\,\mathrm{m\Omega} = \mathbf{17.9\ W\ per\ channel}$$

**Two paralleled FETs per switch** (R_eff = R/2, with ~10 % current-sharing derate):

$$P_{cond} \approx 80^2 \times 2 \times 0.767\,\mathrm{m\Omega} = \mathbf{9.8\ W\ per\ channel}$$

### 1.4 Copper and shunt contributions (often forgotten)

| Element | R | P @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pad + trace (2 oz, ~6 mm, ×2 legs) | ~0.35 mΩ | 2.2 W |
| Current shunt (if per-channel, 0.25 mΩ) | 0.25 mΩ × D | 1.6 W |
| Shared DC bus share (¼ of 320 A on a 0.15 mΩ bus) | — | 3.8 W total / 4 |

Copper and shunt contributions add roughly **4 W per channel at burst**.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the **chopped leg** hard-switches. The return-leg LS FET is static within a sector.

### 2.1 Turn-on / turn-off (V–I overlap)

$$P_{sw,VI} = \tfrac12\,V_{bus}\,I_{ph}\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

$$= 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 33\,\mathrm{ns} \times 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = \mathbf{1.60\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery (LS body diode → HS turn-on)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 55\,\mathrm{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\mathrm{k} = \mathbf{0.067\ W}$$

The added current spike also increases HS turn-on loss. Add ~10 % margin to §2.1.

### 2.3 Output capacitance (HS and LS each, per cycle)

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} \approx 45\,\mathrm{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\mathrm{k} = \mathbf{0.054\ W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction (two edges)

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\,I_{ph}\,(2\,t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.85 \times 80 \times 120\,\mathrm{ns} \times 48\,\mathrm{k} = \mathbf{0.39\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated mostly in driver and R_g)

$$P_{g} = Q_g\,V_{GS}\,f_{sw} \times N_{switching\ FETs} = 48\,\mathrm{nC}\times 10 \times 48\,\mathrm{k} \times 2 = 0.046\ \mathrm{W}$$

With paralleled FETs this doubles to 0.092 W. It is negligible thermally but matters for the bootstrap and BEC budget (§4.3).

### 2.6 Switching total

$$P_{sw,total} \approx 1.60(1.1) + 0.067 + 0.054 + 0.39 = \mathbf{2.27\ W\ per\ channel}$$

**Frequency scaling note:** switching loss is linear in f_sw. Going 24 → 48 kHz costs about +1.1 W per channel at 80 A. In exchange, 48 kHz reduces current ripple (lower motor copper loss, quieter) and moves acoustic noise out of the audible band.

### 2.7 Current ripple sanity check

For a typical 5" 2207 1750 KV motor, L_ph-ph ≈ 25 µH:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} \Big|_{D=0.5} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{25\mu \times 48\mathrm{k}} = 5.3\ \mathrm{A_{pp}}$$

This is acceptable. RMS ripple adds less than 0.1 % to conduction loss.
