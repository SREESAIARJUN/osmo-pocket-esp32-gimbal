## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle from the mechanical encoder angle θ_m:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park** is the transpose of that matrix.

### 1.2 Current Plant and PI Design

In the rotor dq frame, for a surface PMSM with L_d = L_q = L:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

After feedforward decoupling, each axis reduces to the plant G(s) = 1/(Ls + R). Choosing the PI zero to cancel the plant pole, C(s) = K_p + K_i/s with K_i/K_p = R/L, gives open loop ω_c/s and a closed loop that is first-order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$
