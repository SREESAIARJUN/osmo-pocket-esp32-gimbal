**Architectural clarification.** On a 4-in-1 ESC the STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel, or all four, runs its own MCU (AT32F421/STM32G071 running AM32, or EFM8/BB51 running BLHeli_S/Bluejay). The H743 only generates DShot600 and decodes the bidirectional eRPM telemetry. The power-stage math in sections 1–3 therefore applies to the ESC half of the board. Section 4 applies to the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation, two phases conduct at any instant:

- **Switching leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D, and the low-side FET (synchronous rectification) conducts for 1−D.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET is held on continuously for the 60° sector.

The motor current I therefore always passes through exactly two R_ds(on) in series:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \left[ D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS,sw} + R_{LS,ret} \right] = 2\,I^2 R_{ds(on)}(T_j)
$$

This holds when all three resistances are equal.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

Temperature makes the model self-coupled:

$$
R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0065\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

### 1.3 Copper and interconnect

Add the copper and interconnect resistance R_pcb. Assume about 0.4 mΩ per channel path, covering pads, vias, and the phase pad to solder joint:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2\left[2R_{ds(on)}(T_j) + R_{pcb}\right]
$$

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 100 °C

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 80^2 \times (2 \times 2.3 + 0.4)\,\text{m}\Omega = 6400 \times 5.0\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{32.0\ W}
$$

The worst single FET is the continuously-on return-leg low side: 80² × 2.3 mΩ = **14.7 W**.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 V–I overlap

Estimate the transition times from gate charge:

$$
t_{r,f} \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_g}, \qquad I_g = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}{R_{g,int}+R_{g,ext}+R_{drv}}
$$

With V_drv = 10 V, V_plateau ≈ 3.2 V, and R_total ≈ 3.5 Ω, I_g ≈ 1.9 A. This gives t ≈ 4–5 ns from Q_gd alone. With layout inductance included, a realistic figure is **t_r = t_f ≈ 10 ns**:

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.97\ W}
$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery (low-side body diode)

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}
$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$
P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss,eq} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 800\text{p} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.012\ W}
$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction (two edges per period)

$$
P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2 t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}
$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

This loss is dissipated in the driver, not in the FET:

$$
P_{gate} = 2\,Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} = 2 \times 30\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.029\ \text{W}
$$
