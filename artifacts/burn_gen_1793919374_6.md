## 0. Assumptions

"Job #34" has no attached specification, so the design below rests on the assumptions in this table. Every one of them is a parameter in the code; change them to match your hardware.

|---|---|

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{m,0}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Here θ_{m,0} is the mechanical offset at which the rotor d-axis aligns with phase A. It is found at startup by injecting v_d with θ_e = 0 and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.3 Motor model (SPMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$
