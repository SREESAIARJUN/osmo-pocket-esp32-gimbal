**Architectural clarification:** the STM32H743 is the flight controller. The 4-in-1 ESC carries four dedicated ESC MCUs (STM32G071 or AT32F421 class, running AM32 or BLHeli_32-compatible firmware). These MCUs perform commutation and return eRPM telemetry over bi-directional DShot.

Doing 4-motor sensorless commutation on the H743 is technically possible: it has HRTIM, and TIM1/TIM8 are advanced timers. However, ArduPilot does not support this, so the design does not attempt it.
