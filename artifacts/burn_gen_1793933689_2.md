## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle, with encoder offset θ_0 found at alignment:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_0) \pmod{2\pi}$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 PMSM model in the dq frame

$$v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

Torque, assuming a surface PMSM so that L_d = L_q:

$$\tau = \tfrac32\, p\, \lambda_m\, i_q = K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.3 Current PI with pole-zero cancellation

The plant is G(s) = 1/(Ls + R). Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole:

$$C(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s},\qquad K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

The open loop then becomes L(s) = ω_c/s, so the closed loop is a first-order system with bandwidth ω_c.

Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

where λ_m = 2K_t / (3p).

**Discretization.** Use forward Euler on the integrator with sample time T_s. The total delay from computation plus PWM is about 1.5 T_s. This yields a phase margin of approximately:

$$\text{PM} \approx 90^\circ - \omega_c \cdot 1.5\,T_s \cdot \frac{180}{\pi}$$
