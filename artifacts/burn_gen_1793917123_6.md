# STM32H743 Nano AIO: 80 A Burst Thermal, 48 kHz Switching, and Bi-directional DShot600 Architecture

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In a 4-in-1 stack, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs. These are typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or BLHeli_32. The ESC MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM.

| Parameter | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | 6S, 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal | Switching loss scales with V |
| Burst current | 80 A per motor, phase current | Not battery current |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | ESC-side PWM |
| MOSFET (representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN) | R_ds(on),25 = 1.0 mΩ, Q_g = 30 nC, Q_gd + Q_gs2 ≈ 10 nC, Q_rr ≈ 50 nC, Q_oss ≈ 40 nC, V_SD ≈ 0.8 V | Substitute your datasheet values |
| Gate driver | ~1 A effective at the Miller plateau, 10 V V_GS | e.g. FD6288Q-class 3-phase driver |
| Dead time | 100 ns | ESC firmware setting |
| Board | 30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz outer and 1 oz inner | "Nano" stack |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Temperature-dependent on-resistance**

R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)], with α ≈ 0.004 /K for trench silicon.

| T_j | R_ds |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.30 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.40 mΩ |

**Per-leg loss with synchronous rectification**

With duty D, the high-side FET conducts D·I² R and the low-side FET conducts (1−D)·I² R. The sum per active leg is therefore **I²·R_ds**, independent of duty:

P_cond,leg = I_ph² · R_ds(T_j)

**Six-step (trapezoidal) commutation**

Two legs conduct at any instant, so P_cond,motor = 2·I_ph²·R_ds(T_j).

At 80 A and T_j = 125 °C:
- P_cond,motor = 2 × 6400 × 1.4 mΩ = **17.9 W**
- The worst-case single FET at D ≈ 1 approaches 6400 × 1.4 mΩ = **8.96 W**

**Sinusoidal FOC alternative**

With RMS phase current I_rms = I_pk/√2, the loss is P = 3·I_rms²·R. For an 80 A peak this gives about 13.4 W, slightly better. Most racing ESCs remain six-step, so 17.9 W is used below.

**Electro-thermal convergence**

Because R depends on T_j, iterate:

T_j(n+1) = T_ref + Rθ · I²R_25[1 + α(T_j(n) − 25)]

The loop has gain α·Rθ·P_25. It runs away when α·Rθ·I²R_25 ≥ 1. For a single FET at 80 A:
- I²R_25 = 6.4 W
- The runaway threshold is Rθ ≥ 1 / (0.004 × 6.4) ≈ **39 K/W**
- Nano layouts sit at roughly 10–15 K/W junction-to-ambient-local, so the iteration converges, but with about 1.3–1.5× amplification.

## 2. Switching and Ancillary Losses at 48 kHz

**Transition time**

t_sw ≈ Q_sw / I_g = 10 nC / 1 A = 10 ns per edge, so t_r + t_f = 20 ns.

| Loss term | Formula | Value (25.2 V, 80 A, 48 kHz) |
|---|---|---|
| V·I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f | ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 20 ns × 48 k = **0.97 W** |
| Dead-time body diode | V_SD·I·2t_dt·f | 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k = **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | V·Q_rr·f | 25.2 × 50 nC × 48 k = **0.06 W** |
| C_oss | V·Q_oss·f | 25.2 × 40 nC × 48 k = **0.05 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f | 2 × 30 nC × 10 × 48 k = **0.03 W** (dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors) |
| **Switching subtotal (one PWM-ing leg)** | | **≈ 1.72 W** |

**Per-motor and four-motor totals**

| Scope | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Per motor | 17.9 + 1.72 | **≈ 19.6 W** |
| All four motors at 80 A simultaneously | 4 × 19.6 | **≈ 78 W** |

**Sensitivity**
- Conduction dominates at about 91% of the total.
- Halving R_ds saves far more than dropping f_sw to 24 kHz, which would save only about 0.8 W per motor.
- 48 kHz is justified for current ripple and acoustic benefit, not for efficiency.

## 3. DC-Link Capacitor Ripple

This is the most frequently under-designed item.

**Input ripple per motor**

I_cin,rms = I_ph·√(D(1−D)), which peaks at D = 0.5 at 80 × 0.5 = **40 A RMS**.

**Four-motor total**

PWM is uncorrelated across ESC MCUs, so the currents add in RMS: √4 × 40 = **80 A RMS**, worst case.

**Ceramic budget**

A 1210 X7R 10 µF / 50 V part handles about 3–4 A RMS. At 25 V DC bias it retains only ~3 µF.

**Low-ESR electrolytic requirement**

The ripple voltage must stay within the FET margin:
- ΔV ≈ I_pk / (8·f·C), so ΔV < 1 V requires C ≳ 80 / (8 × 48 k × 1) ≈ 210 µF effective.
- Use **≥ 470 µF / 35 V** (or 1000 µF / 35 V for 80 A bursts) at the battery pads, plus 8–12 × 10 µF/50 V 1210 ceramics distributed at each half-bridge.
- Add a 33 V TVS (e.g. SMBJ33A-class) or SMBJ26A for 6S.

**Voltage margin:** 40 V FETs on 6S (25.2 V) leave 14.8 V for ringing. With loop inductance L_loop ≈ 3 nH and di/dt = 80 A / 10 ns, the spike is L·di/dt = **24 V**. This forces one of two remedies:
- A slower edge, R_g ≈ 4.7–10 Ω, accepting more switching loss, or
- A tighter commutation loop, under 1.5 nH, achieved with the ceramic placed directly across HS-drain and LS-source on an adjacent layer.
