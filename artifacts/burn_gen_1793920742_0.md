## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]$$

At the design point T_j = 100 °C:

$$R_{DS}(100) = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 75) = 1.2 \times 1.3375 = 1.605\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Six-step current paths with synchronous rectification

In each 60° sector, two phases are active. Take sector A+ / B− as the example:

- Phase B low-side (LS_B) conducts for 100 % of the sector.
- Phase A high-side (HS_A) conducts for fraction D of each PWM period.
- Phase A low-side (LS_A) conducts for fraction (1 − D), as the synchronous rectifier.

At every instant, exactly **two** channels carry the phase current I. Total channel conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch}(80\text{ A}) = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.605\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{20.5\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution (hot-spot analysis)

Each phase spends 1/3 of the electrical cycle as the PWM leg, 1/3 as the continuous-sink leg, and 1/3 floating. Averaged over a full electrical cycle:

| Device | Conduction fraction | P at 80 A, D = 0.9 |
|---|---|---|
| HS (per phase) | D/3 | 0.30 × 10.27 = 3.08 W |
| LS (per phase) | (1 − D)/3 + 1/3 | 0.367 × 10.27 = 3.77 W |
| Sum over 6 FETs | — | 20.5 W ✔ |

Here I²R = 10.27 W is the loss of one FET conducting continuously.

**The worst case is not the average.** At low eRPM (for example, a punch-out from hover), a single sector can last milliseconds. That is longer than the FET's thermal time constant (τ_jc ≈ 1–5 ms), so the LS FET effectively sees the full 10.27 W:

$$\Delta T_{jc,peak} \approx P \cdot Z_{\theta JC}(t_{sector}) \le 10.27 \times 1.5 = 15.4\ \text{K}$$

### 1.4 FOC alternative (sinusoidal)

For peak phase current Î with three phases always conducting:

$$P_{cond,ch} = \tfrac{3}{2}\,\hat I^{\,2} R_{DS}$$

For the same torque, Î ≈ 1.1 × I_6step, which gives roughly 18.6 W. That is about 10 % lower and spread evenly across the six FETs.

### 1.5 Iteration (electro-thermal coupling)

R_DS depends on T_j, which in turn depends on R_DS. Solve the coupling by fixed-point iteration:

$$T_j^{(k+1)} = T_{board} + \frac{P(T_j^{(k)})}{n_{FET}} R_{\theta JC} \quad \text{(per-FET path)}$$

Thermal runaway is excluded when the loop gain is below 1:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}\,R_{\theta} = 2I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha\,R_{\theta,eff} < 1$$
