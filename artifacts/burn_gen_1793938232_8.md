## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

### 0.1 The H743 does not drive the bridge

ArduPilot does not perform motor commutation. A 4-in-1 ESC with bidirectional DShot requires a dedicated ESC MCU per motor (or one multi-motor MCU) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The signal chain is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir──► 4× AT32F421 (AM32) ──► 4× FD6288Q gate driver ──► 24× N-MOSFET (4 × 3-phase bridge)
```

The 48 kHz switching frequency is a parameter of the **ESC firmware**, not the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Bridge conduction topology

With trapezoidal (6-step) commutation and complementary PWM (synchronous rectification), exactly **two FET-equivalent resistances are in series with the motor current at all times**:

- During the PWM on-time D, current flows through high-side (HS) + low-side (LS).
- During the off-time (1−D), current flows through the synchronous LS + LS.

Ignoring dead time (handled in §2.4), the conduction loss for one ESC channel is:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)$$

For trapezoidal drive with well-filtered current, I_rms ≈ I_b.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

Because loss raises T_j and T_j raises R_ds, the model is coupled. Solve it by fixed-point iteration:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{board} + P_{FET}\!\left(R_{ds}(T_j^{(n)})\right)\cdot R_{\theta,j\text{-}b}$$

**Iteration at 80 A, board at 100 °C, R_θ,j-b = 6 K/W per FET:**

| n | T_j (°C) | R_ds (mΩ) | P_cond per channel (W) | Avg per FET (÷6) (W) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 100 | 1.34 | 17.1 | 2.85 |
| 1 | 117 | 1.41 | 18.1 | 3.01 |
| 2 | 118 | 1.42 | 18.2 | 3.03 |

**Converged: P_cond ≈ 18.2 W per channel at R_ds ≈ 1.42 mΩ.**
