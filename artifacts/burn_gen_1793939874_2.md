## 0. Architectural Ground Truth

Three constraints shape everything else in this document.

1. **The H743 cannot commutate the motors under ArduPilot.** ArduPilot is not an ESC firmware. The board therefore consists of an H743 flight controller plus four dedicated ESC MCUs (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32). These communicate over bidirectional DShot600. The 48 kHz PWM lives on the ESC MCUs, not the H743.
2. **"Nano" (20×20 mm, M2) cannot sustain 4 × 80 A.** Section 3 quantifies this. 80 A is treated as a **time-limited burst rating** with firmware-enforced I²t protection. The recommended form factor is a 2-board stack: 20×20 FC on a 30.5×30.5 ESC. A 20×20 ESC is viable only near 4 × 35–45 A continuous.
3. **All numbers depend on stated assumptions.** Substitute the datasheet values for your chosen FETs. The equations are the deliverable; the numbers illustrate them.
