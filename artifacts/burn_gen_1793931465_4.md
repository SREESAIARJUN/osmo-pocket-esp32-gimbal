## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, balanced phases, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_off:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant Model and Decoupled PI Control

**Plant (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L):**

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

**Control law with decoupling feedforward:**

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

With decoupling, each axis reduces to $\frac{1}{Ls+R}$.

**Gain selection by pole-zero cancellation.** Choosing $K_i/K_p = R/L$ cancels the plant pole. The closed loop then becomes first order, $\frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c = 2\pi\cdot1500
$$
