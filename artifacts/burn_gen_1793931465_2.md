## 1. FOC Core (Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park transform**, with $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park** uses the transpose of this matrix.

**Delay compensation.** The PWM update lands roughly $1.5\,T_i$ after sampling, so the inverse Park angle is advanced:

$$\theta_e^{\,out} = \theta_e + 1.5\,T_i\,\hat\omega_e$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI Tuning

The motor in the dq frame:

$$v_d = R_s i_d + L_s\dot i_d - \omega_e L_s i_q,\qquad v_q = R_s i_q + L_s \dot i_q + \omega_e L_s i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

After decoupling feedforward, each axis reduces to $G(s) = \frac{1}{L_s s + R_s}$.

$$K_p = L_s\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_i = R_s\,\omega_{ci}$$
