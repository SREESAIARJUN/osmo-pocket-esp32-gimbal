## 1. Control Architecture

```
            ┌──────────────── 1 kHz ────────────────┐ ┌──── 4 kHz ────┐ ┌──────── 20 kHz ────────┐
 θ_ref ─►(+)─► Angle P + FF ─► ω_ref ─►(+)─► Velocity PI ─► i_q* ─►(+)─► Current PI ─► v_dq ─► SVPWM ─► Motor
          ▲                              ▲                    ▲  i_q,ff (anti-cogging, friction)
          │ θ_cam (Mahony, dual IMU)      │ ω_cam (dual-IMU gyro, notch + LPF)         ▲ i_dq (Clarke/Park)
          └───────────────────────────────┴──── encoder PLL: θ_m, ω_m ─────────────────┘
```

- **Stabilization loops.** The outer loop closes on the camera's *inertial* attitude, and the middle loop closes on the *inertial* rate. Both come from the dual-IMU estimator.
- **Encoder.** The encoder provides the electrical angle for commutation, the motor-relative angle for the cogging table, and the joint angle for the kinematic Jacobian.

## 2. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 2.1 Transforms

Use the amplitude-invariant Clarke transform with balanced phases (i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_off:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

Inverse Park transform:

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\quad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 2.2 Plant and decoupled PI

The PMSM dynamics in the rotor frame are:

$$L\dot i_d = v_d - Ri_d + \omega_e L i_q,\qquad L\dot i_q = v_q - Ri_q - \omega_e L i_d - \omega_e\lambda_m$$

The control law adds feed-forward decoupling to the PI outputs:

$$v_d = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

**Tuning by pole-zero cancellation.** With the decoupling applied, the plant is 1/(Ls+R). Choosing

$$K_p = L\,\omega_{cc},\quad K_i = R\,\omega_{cc}$$

makes the closed loop first-order with bandwidth ω_cc. Choose ω_cc ≤ 2π·f_c/10 (about 2π·2 kHz).

**Voltage limit.** The voltage vector is limited to the linear SVPWM circle |v_dq| ≤ V_bus/√3. The d-axis gets priority:

$$v_q^{max}=\sqrt{(V_{bus}/\sqrt3)^2 - v_d^2}$$

### 2.3 SVPWM by min-max zero-sequence injection

1. Compute the phase voltages:
$$v_a=v_\alpha,\; v_b=-\tfrac12v_\alpha+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\; v_c=-\tfrac12v_\alpha-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta$$
2. Compute the zero-sequence offset:
$$v_0=-\tfrac12(\max+\min)$$
3. Compute the duty cycles:
$$D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}$$

This is mathematically equivalent to sector-based SVPWM.
