# FOC, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual ICM-42688-P Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have the Job #44 specification sheet, so this design uses the assumptions below. Every assumption is a named constant in the code, so you can substitute the real motor and mechanical values.

| Parameter | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 14 poles (7 pole pairs), 12 slots, R ≈ 5 Ω, L ≈ 2 mH |
| Rotor angle sensor | 14-bit magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701 class) |
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class with FPU |
| Current sensing | Low-side shunts with ADC injected trigger |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on separate SPI buses, mounted symmetrically about the camera-platform centroid |

## 1. Loop Architecture and Timing

```
             IMU attitude (1 kHz)       IMU gyro rate (8 kHz)        encoder θm (20 kHz)
                    │                         │                          │
θ_ref ─►(Σ)─► Angle P ──►(Σ)─► Velocity PI ──►(Σ)─► Current PI (d,q) ─► SVPWM ─► Motor
         -▲     + ω_ff     -▲      + iq_cog ───┘  -▲
          └─ θ_IMU          └─ ω_IMU (fused,         └─ id, iq (Clarke/Park)
                               notch-filtered)
```

| Loop | Rate | Closed-loop bandwidth (target) | Feedback |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | ω_c ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s | Phase shunts + encoder θe |
| Velocity | 8 kHz | ω_v ≈ 2π·150 rad/s | Fused dual-IMU gyro, inertial frame |
| Angle | 1 kHz | ω_a ≈ 2π·25 rad/s | Fused AHRS attitude |
| Cogging adaptation | 8 kHz | μ-limited, below 1 Hz | Velocity-PI output (feedback-error learning) |

**Rule of thumb:** keep a bandwidth separation of at least 5–10× between nested loops.

**Why the IMU is used for stabilization:** the velocity and angle loops close on inertial IMU data, not the encoder. Encoder rate measures joint motion relative to the vehicle. Stabilization requires inertial rate of the camera. The encoder is used only for commutation and cogging phase.

## 2. Plant and FOC Equations

### 2.1 Electrical model (rotor dq frame)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

- Electrical angle: $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}$
- Electrical speed: $\omega_e = p\,\omega_m$

### 2.2 Mechanical model

$$
J\dot\omega_m = K_t i_q - B\omega_m - \tau_{cog}(\theta_m) - \tau_{dist}, \qquad K_t = \tfrac{3}{2}p\lambda_m
$$

### 2.3 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

### 2.4 Park and inverse Park

$$
\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix},
\qquad
\begin{bmatrix}v_\alpha\\ v_\beta\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & -\sin\theta_e\\ \sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}v_d\\ v_q\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 2.5 Delay compensation

The PWM update is applied about 1.5 sampling periods after the measurement. Advance the inverse-Park angle accordingly:

$$\theta_e' = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s$$
