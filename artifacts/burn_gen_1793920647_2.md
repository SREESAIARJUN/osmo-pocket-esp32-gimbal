## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

The offset θ_off is found at calibration by applying a d-axis current at θ_e = 0 and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant (SPMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

Here ω_e = p·ω_m and λ_m = 2K_t / (3p).

### 1.5 Current PI with decoupling (pole–zero cancellation)

Choose a current bandwidth ω_ci, typically 2π·1.5–2 kHz (below f_PWM/10). Then:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}\;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_{ci}}{s+\omega_{ci}}$$

The discrete law with back-calculation anti-windup is:

$$v_x^{PI}[k] = K_{p,i}e_x[k] + I_x[k],\qquad I_x[k+1] = I_x[k] + T_s\big(K_{i,i}e_x[k] + K_{aw}(v_x^{sat}-v_x^{unsat})\big)$$

with K_aw ≈ 1/K_{p,i}·ω_ci. Decoupling feedforward terms are added to the PI outputs:

$$v_d^* = v_d^{PI} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = v_q^{PI} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

### 1.6 Circular voltage limit (linear SVPWM region)

$$V_{max} = \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}$$

Priority goes to v_d:

$$v_d^{sat} = \text{clamp}(v_d^*, \pm V_{max}),\qquad v_q^{sat} = \text{clamp}\!\left(v_q^*, \pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - (v_d^{sat})^2}\right)$$
