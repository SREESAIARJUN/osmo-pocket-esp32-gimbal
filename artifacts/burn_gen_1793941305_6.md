# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC (Job #68): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot Allocation, and Netlists

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Feasibility Warnings

**System partitioning**

- The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU. It generates bidirectional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry.
- The 48 kHz PWM commutation runs on a separate ESC MCU on the 4-in-1 board: one MCU per channel, typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 class running AM32 or BLHeli_32.
- The 48 kHz switching analysis below applies to that ESC MCU, not the H743.

**Feasibility warnings**

1. **Size versus current.** 80A per channel is not achievable on a true "nano" (20×20 mm) board with 5×6 FETs; 24 FETs plus drivers will not fit.
   - Realistically this is a 30.5×30.5 mm board with 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 FETs.
   - It is burst-rated only. The numbers below show why.
2. **Separate ESC board.** The 4-in-1 ESC should be a separate board from the H743 stack. Do not co-locate the H743, IMU, or baro over the power stage.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology

Six-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At any instant one bridge leg is PWM-switching and one leg is held low-side ON:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side conducts for fraction D; the low-side (synchronous) conducts for fraction (1−D).
- **Return leg:** the low-side conducts 100%.

Per-channel conduction loss is therefore:

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{rms}^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I_{rms}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

This is **independent of duty** when synchronous rectification is active.

### 1.2 Ripple and temperature corrections

Phase-current ripple Δi with ratio r = Δi/I gives:

$$I_{rms} = I\sqrt{1+\frac{r^2}{12}}$$

For r = 0.3, I_rms = 1.0037·I, which is negligible. Use I_rms ≈ I.

The temperature dependence of R_DS(on) is:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + k_T(T_j - 25)]$$

### 1.3 Results at 80A

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per FET (2 FETs conducting) | P_cond per channel | P_cond, 4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 6.4 W | 12.8 W | 51.2 W |
| 100 °C | 1.5 mΩ | 9.6 W | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| 125 °C | 1.7 mΩ | 10.9 W | 21.8 W | 87.0 W |

**Positive feedback warning.** Conduction loss rises with T_j, which raises T_j further. Section 4 solves this coupling iteratively.

### 1.4 Shunt and copper losses

**Shunt** (one per channel, in the DC return):

$$P_{sh} = I^2 R_{sh} = 80^2 \times 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega = 3.2\ \text{W per channel}$$

**Copper traces and planes.** Assume a 2 oz outer layer plus 2× 2 oz inner layers in parallel, with an effective path of 0.3 mΩ per channel:

$$P_{Cu} \approx 1.9\ \text{W per channel}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. Each PWM period has one hard-switched turn-on and one turn-off on the high-side FET; the low-side switches with near-zero V_DS (soft) after dead time.

### 2.1 V–I overlap loss (hard-switched high-side)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.452\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery of the low-side body diode

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

The recovery current also adds overlap loss in the high-side, roughly +10–20% on P_VI. A conservative figure is P_rr,eff ≈ 0.25 W.

### 2.3 C_oss discharge

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2} C_{oss} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 1.5\text{nF} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.023\ W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2 t_d) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.614\ W}$$

Reducing t_d to 50 ns halves this. This is the best single tuning knob in AM32/BLHeli (dead-time setting), limited by shoot-through margin.

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET die)

$$P_g = Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} \times 2 = 60\text{nC} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} \times 2 = \mathbf{0.058\ W}$$
