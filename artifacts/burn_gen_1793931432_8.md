## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** perform the 48 kHz motor commutation. The partition is:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 at 400 MHz | ArduPilot. Generates bi-directional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421 or STM32G071 | AM32 firmware. 48 kHz complementary PWM, 6-step commutation, BEMF sensing. |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q-class 3-phase half-bridge driver | Bootstrap high side, 12 V gate drive. |
| Power stage ×4 | 6 N-channel MOSFETs per ESC (24 total, N_p = 1) | 3-phase bridge. |

Therefore, sections 1–3 apply to the ESC power stage and section 4 applies to the H743 timer and DMA subsystem.

**A physical constraint up front:** "80A" on a nano-class (20×20 or 30.5×30.5) 4-in-1 board is only achievable as a **short burst**. The math in section 3 shows that the realistic continuous rating is roughly 30–35 A per channel. This is consistent with what independent testing of commercial "80A" boards typically finds.

|---|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage | V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S, fully charged (worst case for switching) |

| MOSFET R_DS(on) at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R_25 | 1.5 mΩ (max) | 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm or 5×6 mm class |
| Temperature coefficient | α | 0.5 %/K | Typical trench MOSFET |

### 1.2 Hot R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,(1+\alpha)^{T_j-25} = 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.005^{100} = 1.5 \times 1.647 = \mathbf{2.47\ m\Omega}$$
