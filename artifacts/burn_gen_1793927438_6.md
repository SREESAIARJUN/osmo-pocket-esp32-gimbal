# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #55): Loss Model, Thermal Feasibility, Bi-Directional DShot Allocation, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Architecture Corrections

Two points in the brief need correcting before any math:

1. **The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In an ArduPilot stack, the STM32H743 is the flight controller. It sends DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (typically AM32 or BLHeli_32 class, e.g. STM32G071 or AT32F421). Those MCUs run the 48 kHz commutation PWM. The design is therefore two coupled subsystems:
   - The **FC (H743)**, which handles timers/DMA and DShot.
   - The **power stage (4× ESC MCU + gate drivers + 24 MOSFETs)**, which carries the losses and thermal load.

2. **An 80 A burst per motor on a "nano" (20×20 mm) board is not thermally achievable** for any burst longer than a fraction of a second. Section 3 proves this. Either the form factor or the current rating has to change. I give both the model and the resulting design limits.

**All component values below are representative design assumptions.** Verify every R_DS(on), Q_rr, Z_th, and pin mapping against the actual datasheets and your chosen ArduPilot version before layout.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (Six-Step Trapezoidal, Synchronous Rectification)

### 1.1 Current paths

At any instant, two phases conduct: one sourcing (high-side PWM'd at duty D) and one sinking (low-side fully on). With complementary PWM:

| FET | Conduction fraction of electrical period |
|---|---|
| High-side (sourcing leg) | D · (1/3) |
| Low-side, same leg (freewheel) | (1−D) · (1/3) |
| Low-side (sinking leg) | 1/3 |

The per-FET RMS currents, with phase current I_ph, are:

$$I_{HS,rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 \frac{D}{3}, \qquad I_{LS,rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2\left(\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1-D}{3}\right)$$

Summing over 3 legs gives the per-motor conduction loss:

$$\boxed{P_{cond} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)}$$

This is independent of D, because two R_DS(on) are always in series.

The temperature dependence of on-resistance is:

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) \approx R_{25}\,\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\text{–}0.007\ /\text{K}$$

### 1.2 Battery vs phase current

$$I_{batt} \approx D \cdot I_{ph}$$

If "80 A" is the battery current at D = 0.8, then the phase current is I_ph = 100 A, which raises conduction loss by 56%. **Spec the 80 A as phase current**, or derate accordingly.

### 1.3 Numbers (assumed: 40 V, ~1.0 mΩ-class PDFN5×6, one FET per position, T_j = 110 °C)

- R_DS(on, hot) ≈ 1.0 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**
- P_cond = 2 × 80² × 1.5×10⁻³ = **19.2 W per motor**
- Worst single FET (sinking low-side at low D): 80² × (2/3) × 1.5 m ≈ **6.4 W**
