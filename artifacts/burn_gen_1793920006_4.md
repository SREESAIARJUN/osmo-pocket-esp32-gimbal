## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

Three design decisions shape everything that follows.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot on the H743 is the flight controller. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32), driven by bidirectional DShot600. The H743 has no 24-channel, 48 kHz center-aligned commutation capability alongside a 4–8 kHz flight loop, and ArduPilot provides no ESC firmware. This is the only architecture that makes DShot meaningful.
2. **"80 A burst" means per-channel phase current, ≤10 s, non-simultaneous.** Section 3.5 shows why simultaneous 4×80 A is physically incompatible with a "nano" form factor.
3. **Form factor.** The FC board is 20×20 mm (M2). The ESC power stage must be **≥30.5×30.5 mm** (M3). This is derived in Section 3.6, not chosen for convenience.
