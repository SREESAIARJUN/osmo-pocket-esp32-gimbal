This document turns the requirements into numbers. It does not just restate them. One finding changes the program scope, so it comes first.

> **Key finding:** On a nano-class (20×20 / 25.5×25.5 mm) board, **80 A per channel is a ~1–1.5 second adiabatic burst rating, not a sustained one.** The calculated continuous capability is **~30 A/channel** with prop-wash cooling. Section 3 shows the derivation. Marketing specs must state a burst duration.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
In 6-step drive with synchronous rectification, two phases are energized at any instant:

- **During PWM-on:** the high-side of phase A and the low-side of phase B conduct.
- **During PWM-off:** the low-side of A (synchronous) and the low-side of B conduct.

So **two MOSFETs always carry the full phase current I**, regardless of duty cycle.

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha_T (T_j - 25)\right] \approx 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.5 = 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega \quad (T_j \approx 100\,°\text{C})$$
