**Bandwidth separation rule.** Each outer loop should be 4–10× slower than the loop inside it:
- current ω_c ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s
- velocity ω_v ≈ 2π·150 rad/s
- angle ω_p ≈ 2π·25 rad/s

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

θ_off is found at startup by aligning the d-axis. Inject V_d = V_align at θ_e = 0, wait for the rotor to settle, then record θ_m.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, two shunts)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant in the dq frame

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

### 1.5 Current PI with decoupling (pole-zero cancellation)

Choose the gains so the PI zero cancels the electrical pole R/L:

$$K_p^{(i)} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i^{(i)} = R\,\omega_c$$
