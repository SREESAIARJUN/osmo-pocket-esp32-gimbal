## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 does **not** drive the MOSFETs. In every production 4-in-1 AIO, the topology is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir (×4)──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / AM32)
                                                    │ 6× PWM @ 48 kHz
                                                    ▼
                                              FD6288Q 3-ph gate driver
                                                    ▼
                                              6× N-MOSFET half-bridges → motor
```

The H743 owns the timer and DMA problem (Section 4). The ESC MCU owns the 48 kHz complementary PWM, dead time, and commutation. The power stage owns the thermal problem (Sections 1–3).

### 0.2 Stated Assumptions

No MOSFET part number was specified, so the model is parameterized. A representative 40 V, 5×6 DFN device is used for the numbers. Substitute your actual datasheet values into the equations.

> **Engineering note on voltage rating.** On 6S, a 40 V FET has only about 15 V of margin against ringing. That is acceptable only with tight layout (Section 5.3) and snubbing. **60 V parts are recommended for 6S at 80 A**, at the cost of roughly 30–50 % higher R_DS(on). Re-run Section 1 if you switch.
