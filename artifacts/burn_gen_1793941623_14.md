## 0. Scope and Assumptions

### 0.1 System partitioning

ArduPilot runs on the STM32H743. It cannot also commutate four motors. The architecture is therefore:

- **FC:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot. It outputs bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs.
- **ESC:** 4× independent ESC MCUs running AM32 firmware, either AT32F421 or STM32G071. Each MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs.

### 0.2 80A burst at nano size

Treat "80A burst per channel" on a 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm board as a **time-limited rating**. Section 3 shows that this burst lasts on the order of 1–2 seconds before thermal limits are reached.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

AM32 uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At every instant, two phases conduct:

- **PWM'd leg:** the high-side (HS) FET conducts during D·T_sw. The complementary low-side (LS) FET conducts during (1−D)·T_sw, minus dead time.
- **Static leg:** the LS FET of the return phase is on for the full 60° sector.

As a result, the motor current always flows through exactly **two channels' worth of R_DS(on)**:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)$$

with

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

### 1.2 Electrothermal feedback (fixed point)

R_DS(on) depends on junction temperature, and junction temperature depends on loss. The two are coupled:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + Z_{th}\,P_{FET}(T_j)$$

Solving gives a closed form:

$$T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + Z_{th} k\,R_{ds,25}(1 - 25\alpha)}{1 - Z_{th} k\,R_{ds,25}\,\alpha}, \qquad k = \text{(I}^2\text{ share for that FET)}$$

**Thermal runaway** occurs if $Z_{th} k R_{ds,25}\alpha \ge 1$. Check this for stall and startup cases, where one FET carries the full current.
