## 1. Loss Model (per ESC channel, at 80 A)

### 1.1 Conduction Losses

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) PWM. In any commutation state:

- The **PWM phase** has its high-side FET on for duty D and its low-side FET on for (1 − D).
- The **return phase** has its low-side FET on continuously.

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}(T)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}(T) = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T)$$

This is independent of duty, which is a useful property of synchronous rectification.

R_DS(on) rises with temperature:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per ESC |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 2·6400·0.001 = **12.8 W** |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ | **17.1 W** |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ | **18.6 W** |

Additional copper losses must also be included:

- **Phase/bus copper:** a 2 oz polygon gives roughly 0.25 mΩ per path, so 6400 × 0.25 m ≈ **1.6 W**.
- **Current shunt:** 0.25 mΩ gives **1.6 W** if every ESC has one. Alternatively, use a single shared shunt on the bus. It still sees the full current, but you get one loss instead of four.

### 1.2 Switching Losses at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches.

**(a) V–I overlap (hard-switched high side):**

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 40\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**(b) Body-diode reverse recovery (low-side diode during dead time):**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n}\cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$

**(c) Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss} \approx Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\cdot 25.2\cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.05\ W}$$

**(d) Dead-time body-diode conduction (two edges per period):**

$$P_{dt} = V_F I (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot 80 \cdot 200\text{n}\cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**(e) Gate drive** (dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET):

$$P_g = Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} \times 2 = 50\text{n}\cdot 10\cdot 48\text{k}\cdot 2 = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

### 1.3 Totals at T_j ≈ 100 °C

| Loss | Per ESC | ×4 |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FET) | 17.1 W | 68.4 W |
| Switching + Qrr + Coss + dead time | 2.66 W | 10.6 W |
| Copper | 1.6 W | 6.4 W |
| Shunt (single shared bus shunt, 320 A) | — | ~25 W ⚠ |
| Gate drive | 0.05 W | 0.2 W |
| **Total (without shunt)** | **≈ 21.4 W** | **≈ 85.6 W** |

On the shunt: with a shared bus shunt, 320 A through 0.25 mΩ gives 25.6 W. Either use 0.1 mΩ (10 W), or sense current per ESC. I recommend per-ESC sensing via the ESC telemetry/EDT current, plus a 0.1 mΩ bus shunt or Hall sensor for the FC battery monitor.

**Hottest single FET:** the continuously-on return low-side FET carries I²R = 6400 × 1.34 m ≈ **8.6 W**. The PWM high-side FET carries roughly D·8.6 + 1.94 W, which is up to about **10.5 W** at D → 1.

**Frequency trade-off:** at 24 kHz the switching losses halve, saving about 5.3 W across 4 ESCs. 48 kHz buys quieter, smoother low-throttle operation at roughly a 12% thermal cost.
