# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #42): Power, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 belongs on the flight controller (FC). Each ESC channel runs its own BLHeli_32/AM32/Bluejay-class MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071. That MCU does the commutation and generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 only sends bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM.

**Assumed parameters.** Replace these with your BOM datasheet values before release.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max (worst case for switching) |
| Burst current per motor | I_ph | 80 A |
| MOSFET | — | 40 V, single FET per switch position |
| On-resistance at 25 °C | R_ds25 | 0.9 mΩ |
| On-resistance multiplier at 125 °C | k_T | 1.6 |
| Rise / fall time | t_r / t_f | 15 / 15 ns |
| Reverse-recovery charge | Q_rr | 50 nC |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 60 nC |
| Gate charge | Q_g | 60 nC |
| Gate drive voltage | V_drv | 10 V |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns |
| Body-diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Motor phase inductance (line-line) | L_ph | 15 µH |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous) PWM |

**Feasibility warning.** 80 A continuous per channel is not achievable on a 20×20 mm nano 4-in-1. The model below shows that 80 A is a short-burst rating only, limited by board thermal mass. Treat this as a design-validation model, not a certified rating.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\,/^\circ\mathrm{C}$$

This gives R_ds(125 °C) ≈ 1.44 mΩ.

### 1.2 Six-step synchronous bridge

At any instant two phases conduct.

- **PWM on-time:** current flows through HS_a and LS_b.
- **PWM off-time:** current flows through LS_a (synchronous rectification) and LS_b.

So the current always flows through two channels in series, whatever the duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.3 Ripple correction

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \cdot 0.25}{15\mu \cdot 48k} = 8.75\ \mathrm{A_{pp}}\ (D=0.5)$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6400 + 6.4 \approx 6406\ \mathrm{A^2}\quad(\text{ripple is negligible})$$

### 1.4 Result at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per ESC |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 0.90 mΩ | 11.5 W |
| 125 °C | 1.44 mΩ | **18.4 W** |

### 1.5 Per-FET distribution

Over one electrical cycle, each FET carries load current for roughly 1/3 of the time. The PWM'd high-side and its complementary low-side split that window by D and (1−D).

- **Hottest device:** the low-side FET at low duty (high-throttle burst means high D, so the high-side FET is hottest instead).
- **Design value:** P_FET,max ≈ I²·R·(1/3) ≈ **3.1 W**.

### 1.6 Copper and shunt losses

| Item | Value | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase plus bus copper | ≈ 0.3 mΩ effective | ≈ 1.9 W |
| Current shunt | 0.2 mΩ | 1.28 W |

The shunt figure is per channel if per-phase sensing is used. If a single bus shunt is shared, use the bus RMS current instead.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg hard-switches. Each term below is per ESC channel.

| Mechanism | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw = ½·25.2·80·30n·48k | **1.45 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 0.06 W |
| Output capacitance | Q_oss·V·f_sw (hard-switched, worst case) | 0.07 W |
| Dead-time body diode | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·200n·48k | **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (mostly in driver) | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f_sw | 0.06 W |
| **Total switching-related** | | **≈ 2.25 W** |

**Sensitivity:** dP_sw/dt_r ≈ 0.048 W per ns of edge time. Pushing edges slower for EMI costs heat quickly.

### Total per channel and per board (80 A burst, T_j = 125 °C)

$$P_{ch} \approx 18.4 + 2.25 + 1.9 + 1.28 \approx 23.8\ \mathrm{W}$$

$$P_{4\text{-}in\text{-}1} \approx 95\ \mathrm{W}\quad(\text{all four motors at burst simultaneously})$$

$$\eta \approx 1 - \frac{23.8}{25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot D}\quad(\approx 97.6\%\ \text{at}\ D = 1)$$

### Bus capacitor ripple (often the real failure point)

$$I_{C,rms} \approx I\sqrt{D(1-D)} = 40\ \mathrm{A}\ \text{per ESC at}\ D = 0.5$$

The four channels are not phase-interleaved, so this adds up. Recommended capacitance:

- On-board: ≥ 4× 10 µF/50 V X7R 1206 per channel, placed at the half-bridges.
- External: 1000 µF/35 V low-ESR electrolytic on the pigtail.
- TVS: SMBJ28A-class on the bus.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Junction-to-board (Foster RC, per FET)

$$T_j(t) = T_{board}(t) + P_{FET}\sum_i R_i\left(1 - e^{-t/\tau_i}\right)$$

With R_th(j-c) ≈ 1.2 K/W at 3.1 W, the junction sits only ≈ 4 K above the case. **The junction is not the bottleneck; board heat capacity is.**
