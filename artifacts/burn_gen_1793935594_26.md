**Architectural clarification.** The H743 does not drive the bridges. It runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 to four independent ESC MCUs. Those MCUs perform commutation at 48 kHz and return eRPM telemetry over the same wire (bidirectional DShot).

**Engineering flag.** "Nano" (a 20×20 mm pattern) combined with 80 A burst per ESC is not credible. The thermal model in §3 shows that even the 30.5 mm board is limited by heat capacity, not by junction resistance. Treat 80 A as a single-motor, few-second rating.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths with complementary (synchronous) PWM
In 6-step trapezoidal drive, phases A (PWM'd) and B (low side held ON) carry current at any instant.

- **PWM ON (duty D):** HS_A + LS_B conduct.
- **PWM OFF (1−D):** LS_A (synchronous freewheel) + LS_B conduct.

At every instant, current therefore flows through exactly **two MOSFET channels**, independent of D (dead time is handled separately):

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot \left[2R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{Cu}\right]$$

### 1.2 Ripple and temperature terms
$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}D(1-D)}{L_{ph} f_{sw}}$$

For a typical L_ph of 20 µH (line-line, 5″-class motor) at D = 0.5: ΔI_pp = 25.2·0.25/(20e-6·48e3) ≈ 6.6 A. This contributes 3.6 A², which is negligible against 6400 A².

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)]$$

### 1.3 Battery current vs. phase current
$$I_{batt} \approx D \cdot I_{ph}$$

An "80 A" rating must state which current it refers to. Here it is **phase** current, which is the thermally relevant quantity.

### 1.4 Numbers (T_j = 110 °C self-consistent, see §3)
- R_ds(110) = 0.9 mΩ × (1 + 0.005·85) = **1.28 mΩ**
- R_Cu (2 oz pour plus vias plus pads, phase path) ≈ 0.3 mΩ
- P_cond = 6404 × (2·1.28 + 0.3) mΩ = **18.3 W per ESC**
