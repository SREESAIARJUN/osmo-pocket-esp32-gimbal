## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flag

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. It does not drive MOSFETs. The 4-in-1 ESC section uses **four dedicated ESC MCUs** running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, for example STM32G071 or AT32F421. The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600. This is the only architecture ArduPilot supports for this function.

**Feasibility.** "80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 mm) form factor is not physically credible with four channels loaded simultaneously. Section 3 shows why. The model below assumes:

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Form factor | 30.5×30.5 mm board (≈ 36×36 mm outline) |
| Battery | 6S LiPo: V_bus,max = 25.2 V, nominal 22.2 V |
| Burst rating | 80 A per channel, ≤ 2–3 s, with prop-wash airflow |
| Continuous rating | Derived from the model, not asserted |

If 20×20 is a hard mechanical requirement, the honest rating is roughly 35–45 A burst per channel. Section 3.5 gives that derivation.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology and current path
In 6-step (trapezoidal) commutation with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM, the AM32/BLHeli_32 default):

- At any instant, exactly two phase legs carry current.
- In the PWM leg, current flows through either the high-side (HS) FET or its complementary low-side (LS) FET.
- The return leg's LS FET is held on.

The series resistance is therefore always **two FET channels plus copper**:

$$
R_{path}(T_j) = 2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + R_{Cu}
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence
$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{/°C (typ. trench, 40–60 V)}
$$

### 1.3 RMS current including PWM ripple
With peak-to-peak ripple ratio $r = \Delta I / I$:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I^2\left(1+\frac{r^2}{12}\right)
$$

The phase ripple is set by motor inductance:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{2 L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}
$$

For a typical 5" or 7" motor with $L_{ph}$ = 15–30 µH line-to-line, $r$ ≈ 0.1–0.3. Use $r$ = 0.3 as the worst case.

### 1.4 Per-channel conduction loss
$$
P_{cond} = I^2\left(1+\frac{r^2}{12}\right)\left[2R_{DS(on),25}(1+\alpha(T_j-25)) + R_{Cu}\right]
$$
