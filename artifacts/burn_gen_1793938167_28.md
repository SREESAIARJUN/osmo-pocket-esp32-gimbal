## 0. Architecture Corrections and Stated Assumptions

Two points in the brief need correcting before any numbers are meaningful.

**1. The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC MCU.**
ArduPilot does not commutate motors. A production-viable 4-in-1 uses:
- **H743**: runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600 on 4 timer channels.
- **4× ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class): run AM32 or BLHeli_32, generate the 48 kHz PWM, and drive the gate drivers.
