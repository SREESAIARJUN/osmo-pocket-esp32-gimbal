# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control Loop, Anti-Cogging Feedforward, and Dual ICM-42688-P Vibration Rejection

I don't have the original Job #5 specification, so this design states its assumptions explicitly (§0). Every parameter that depends on your hardware is a named constant you can change.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 / H7 class (FPU, ≥170 MHz), single-precision float |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, e.g. GBM2804: 12 slots / 14 poles (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5–10 Ω, L ≈ 2–4 mH |
| Rotor sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701), one per axis |
| Current sensing | Low-side 2- or 3-shunt |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on separate SPI buses: **IMU-A** on the camera payload, **IMU-B** on the payload with a different orientation/location, or on the base (see §4.4) |
| Kinematics | Yaw → Roll → Pitch (outer to inner) |

**Loop rates (cascade):**

```
 20 kHz  ── Current (FOC) PI loop, SVPWM            [ISR: ADC end-of-conversion]
  5 kHz  ── Velocity PI + cogging FF + PLL observer  [every 4th current tick]
  8 kHz  ── IMU sampling + vibration filters         [IMU data-ready / DMA]
  1 kHz  ── Attitude estimate + angle loop           [decimated from 8 kHz]
```

**Bandwidth separation rule:** each outer loop should be at least 5–10× slower than the loop it drives:

- ω_c,i ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s (current)
- ω_c,v ≈ 2π·150 rad/s (velocity)
- ω_c,θ ≈ 2π·15–25 rad/s (angle)

## 1. FOC Current Loop (Innermost)

### 1.1 Transforms

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)\qquad (i_a+i_b+i_c=0)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

Inverse Park:

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI Design

Motor model in the dq frame:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

**Pole-zero cancellation tuning.** Choose the PI zero at R/L. The closed loop then becomes first-order with bandwidth ω_c,i:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{c,i},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{c,i}
$$

**Discrete PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward.** Here T_i = 1/20 kHz:

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_d[k] &= K_{p,i}e_d + I_d[k] - \omega_e L i_q\\
v_q[k] &= K_{p,i}e_q + I_q[k] + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m\\
I_{d,q}[k+1] &= I_{d,q}[k] + K_{i,i}T_i\,e_{d,q} + K_{aw}\big(v^{sat}_{d,q}-v_{d,q}\big)
\end{aligned}
$$

**Voltage limiting (circular).** In linear SVPWM the maximum voltage is V_max = V_bus/√3. Priority goes to d:

$$
v_d^{sat} = \operatorname{clamp}(v_d, \pm V_{max}),\qquad
v_q^{sat} = \operatorname{clamp}\!\Big(v_q, \pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - (v_d^{sat})^2}\Big)
$$

**Computational delay compensation.** PWM updates take effect one period later, so the inverse Park uses the predicted angle:

$$
\theta_e' = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_i
$$

### 1.3 SVPWM (Min-Max Zero-Sequence Injection)

$$
v_{a,b,c} = \text{inverse Clarke}(v_\alpha,v_\beta),\quad
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc})+\min(v_{abc})\big),\quad
D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

## 2. Velocity and Angle Cascade

### 2.1 Velocity Estimation (PLL / Tracking Observer)

Differentiating a 14-bit encoder at 5 kHz gives quantization noise of about 2π/16384 · 5000 ≈ 1.9 rad/s, which is unacceptable. Use a type-II tracking loop instead.

The angle error is wrapped to (−π, π]:

$$
e_\theta = \operatorname{wrap}(\theta_{enc} - \hat\theta)
$$

$$
\hat\omega[k+1] = \hat\omega[k] + K_{i,pll}T_v e_\theta + T_v\,\frac{\tau_{cmd}}{J}\ (\text{optional accel FF})
$$

$$
\hat\theta[k+1] = \hat\theta[k] + T_v(\hat\omega[k] + K_{p,pll}e_\theta)
$$

Gains for a critically damped response at ω_pll (≈ 2π·400 rad/s):

$$
K_{p,pll} = 2\omega_{pll},\qquad K_{i,pll} = \omega_{pll}^2
$$

**Plant.** With i_q → τ = K_t i_q, the mechanical plant is

$$
J\dot\omega = K_t i_q - B\omega - \tau_{cog}(\theta) - \tau_{dist}
