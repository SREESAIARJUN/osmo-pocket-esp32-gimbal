## 1. Thermal and Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.5 × 1.5 = **2.25 mΩ**. Because R_DS rises with temperature, the model is solved iteratively (§1.7).

### 1.2 Conduction loss in 6-step with synchronous rectification

In any commutation sector, current flows through two series switch positions:

- One phase is PWM'd. Its high-side FET conducts for D and its low-side FET (synchronous) for 1–D.
- The return phase's low-side FET conducts 100 %.

So the instantaneous current path always contains exactly two FETs, independent of D. Neglecting ripple:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

With ripple included (ΔI = peak-to-peak phase ripple):

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

Ripple estimate (L_ph = motor phase-to-phase inductance, assumed **20 µH**, worst case D = 0.5):

$$\Delta I = \frac{V_{bat}\,D(1-D)}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\mu \times 48k} \approx 6.6\ \text{A} \Rightarrow \Delta I^2/12 = 3.6\ \text{A}^2\ (\text{negligible})$$

**At 80 A:**

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.5 mΩ | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| 100 °C | 2.06 mΩ | 26.4 W | 105.6 W |
| 125 °C | 2.25 mΩ | 28.8 W | 115.2 W |

**Per-FET peak:** while conducting, I²R = 6400 × 2.25 mΩ = **14.4 W**.

### 1.3 Switching loss at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd half-bridge hard-switches. The synchronous FET switches at near-zero V_DS (ZVS after dead time).

**a) V–I overlap.** Transition time is set by the Miller plateau:

$$t_{r} \approx \frac{Q_{gd} R_G}{V_{drv} - V_{pl}},\qquad t_f \approx \frac{Q_{gd} R_G}{V_{pl}}$$

With R_G,total ≈ 5 Ω, V_drv = 10 V, V_pl ≈ 4 V:

- t_r ≈ 8 nC·5/6 = 6.7 ns, practically ≈ 15 ns including the driver limit
- t_f ≈ 10 ns

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bat} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(25\text{ ns})(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{1.21\ W}$$

**b) Reverse recovery** of the synchronous FET body diode (it conducts during dead time):

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bat} f_{sw} = 40n \times 25.2 \times 48k = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

Q_rr scales with di/dt and I_F. Use the datasheet value at the actual conditions; it may be 2–3× higher at 80 A.

**c) Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bat} f_{sw} = 30n \times 25.2 \times 48k = \mathbf{0.036\ W}$$

**d) Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two dead-time intervals per period):

$$P_{dt} = V_F\, I_{ph}\, (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.85 \times 80 \times 200n \times 48k = \mathbf{0.65\ W}$$
