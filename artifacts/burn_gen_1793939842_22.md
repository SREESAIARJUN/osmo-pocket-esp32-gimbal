## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{ds}(125) = 1.2\,(1+0.45) = 1.74\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

During any commutation sector, current flows through **two switch positions in series**:

- **Active high-side leg:** HS conducts for duty D, and its synchronous LS conducts for 1 − D. The leg therefore always presents one R_ds.
- **Return low-side leg:** LS fully on, one R_ds.

The RMS-equivalent path resistance is independent of D:

$$P_{cond,FET} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,Cu} = I^2 \cdot R_{Cu},\qquad R_{Cu} \approx 0.30\ \text{m}\Omega \ \text{(bus plane + phase pour + pads, estimated)}$$

### 1.3 80 A burst, per motor channel

| Term | Expression | Value |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 80² × 2 × 1.74 mΩ | **22.3 W** |
| Copper | 80² × 0.30 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| **Conduction subtotal** | | **24.2 W** |

The hottest single die is the high-side FET of the active leg during high D (worst case D → 1):

$$P_{die,cond} = 80^2 \times 1.74\,\text{m}\Omega = 11.1\ \text{W}$$
