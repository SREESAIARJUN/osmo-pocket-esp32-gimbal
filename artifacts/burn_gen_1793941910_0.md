## 0. Assumptions and a Feasibility Flag

**Feasibility flag.** 80 A *per channel* on a 20×20 mm "nano" form factor cannot be sustained thermally. It is achievable only as a short burst of about 1–3 s, as Section 3 quantifies. ## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R_DS = 1.6 mΩ.

### 1.2 Leg duty decomposition (6-step, complementary PWM)

Over one electrical cycle, each half-bridge leg spends:

- **1/3 of the cycle as the PWM leg.** The high side (HS) conducts for fraction D and the low side (LS) for fraction (1−D) as a synchronous freewheel.
- **1/3 of the cycle as the sink leg.** The LS is fully on.
- **1/3 of the cycle floating.** This is the BEMF sense window, with no conduction.

The per-device average conduction losses are therefore:

$$P_{cond,HS}=\frac{D}{3}\,I^2R_{DS}(T_j)\qquad P_{cond,LS}=\frac{(1-D)+1}{3}\,I^2R_{DS}(T_j)=\frac{2-D}{3}\,I^2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,leg}=\frac{2}{3}I^2R_{DS}\qquad P_{cond,ch}=3\cdot\tfrac{2}{3}I^2R_{DS}=2\,I^2R_{DS}$$

This result makes physical sense: at any instant, exactly two FET positions are carrying the phase current.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C, D = 0.9

- I²R = 6400 × 1.6 mΩ = **10.24 W**
- P_HS = 0.3 × 10.24 = **3.07 W**
- P_LS = 0.367 × 10.24 = **3.75 W** (the hottest device)
- P_cond per channel = **20.5 W**
- P_cond for the full 4-in-1 = **81.9 W**

### 1.4 Copper and parasitic conduction

These terms are frequently omitted but are not negligible.

**PCB copper.** Use

$$R_{Cu}=\frac{\rho_{Cu}(T)\,L}{w\,t}$$

with ρ = 1.72 µΩ·cm × [1 + 0.0039(T − 20)].

- Example: a 15 mm phase path, 5 mm wide, 2 oz (70 µm) copper on 2 layers in parallel, gives about 0.37 mΩ at 100 °C.
- That is roughly **2.4 W per channel at 80 A**.
- **Design rule:** each phase and power path should have at least 4 oz total copper across layers, with via arrays of at least 20 × 0.3 mm vias at each layer transition.

**Current shunt.** If you use a 0.25 mΩ shunt on the shared bus at 320 A total, the loss is I²R = 25.6 W. That is unacceptable. Use a Hall sensor (e.g. ACS7xx-class) or a per-channel 0.1 mΩ shunt carrying 80 A (0.64 W each).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

At any instant, only one leg per channel is switching. Per-channel switching loss is therefore one leg's full switching loss, with no 1/3 factor.

### 2.1 Gate currents during the transition

$$I_{g,on}=\frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_g}=\frac{10-3.5}{6}=1.08\ \text{A}\qquad I_{g,off}=\frac{V_{pl}}{R_g}=\frac{3.5}{6}=0.58\ \text{A}$$

### 2.2 Voltage/current overlap times

$$t_{on}=\frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}}=\frac{14\ \text{nC}}{1.08}=13\ \text{ns}\qquad t_{off}=\frac{14\ \text{nC}}{0.58}=24\ \text{ns}$$
