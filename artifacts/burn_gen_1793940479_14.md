**Architecture.** The H743 is the flight MCU. It sends DShot600 to four independent ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32), and each ESC MCU drives a three-phase gate driver plus six N-channel MOSFETs. The 48 kHz PWM therefore lives in the ESC MCUs, not the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology
With six-step trapezoidal commutation, exactly two phase legs conduct during each 60° sector:
- One leg is PWM-modulated (high side at duty D, synchronous-rectifying low side at 1−D).
- One leg is held low (low side on 100 %).

Synchronous rectification means the PWM leg always has one FET conducting the full phase current. The conduction path therefore contains **two R_ds,on in series at all times**, regardless of D.
