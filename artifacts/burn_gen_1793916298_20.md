## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation, two phase legs are active in every sector:

- **PWM ON:** HS(A) → winding → LS(B)
- **PWM OFF (synchronous freewheel):** LS(A) → winding → LS(B)

Two R_ds(on) are therefore always in series with the phase current, independent of duty cycle D. Per channel:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Including ripple, where ΔI = V_bus·D(1−D)/(L·f_sw):

$$I_{rms} = I\sqrt{1 + \frac{1}{12}\left(\frac{\Delta I}{I}\right)^2}$$

For typical 5–15 µH motors at 48 kHz, ΔI/I < 0.1, so I_rms ≈ I (error < 0.1 %).

**Per-device split** over one electrical revolution:

| Device | Conduction share | Mean loss |
|---|---|---|
| High side | D/3 | — |
| Low side | (1−D)/3 + 1/3 | — |
| Single device, worst case | — | ≈ I²R·(2/3) |

In any active sector, the commutating low side carries the full current 100 % of the time. That device sets the junction-temperature peak.

### 1.2 Temperature-coupled R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]$$

T_j depends on loss, and loss depends on T_j. Solving by fixed-point iteration (Section 3.4) at T_j ≈ 115 °C gives R_ds = 1.5 × 1.405 = **2.1 mΩ**.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 2.1\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{26.9\ W}$$

Peak single-device loss in an active sector: 80² × 2.1 mΩ = **13.4 W**.
