### 0.1 Architecture Clarification

The H743 does **not** commutate the motors. DShot only makes sense between two processors, so the architecture is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir ×4──► 4× ESC MCU (AM32/BLHeli_32) ──► 4× 3-phase gate driver ──► 24× MOSFET
```

The ESC MCUs are typically STM32G071 or AT32F421 parts running AM32.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current Path

The ESC uses trapezoidal 6-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At any instant, two phases carry current:

- **Static leg (low-side on):** conducts I continuously for 1/3 of the electrical cycle.
- **PWM leg:** the high-side conducts for D, and the low-side (synchronous rectification) conducts for 1 − D.

The current therefore always passes through **exactly two channels in series**, independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Ripple Correction

With phase inductance L_ph and PWM leg duty D:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph} f_{sw}}, \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I_{DC}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

For a typical 5–8 inch motor (L_ph ≈ 15 µH line-to-line, worst case D = 0.5, V_bemf ≈ V_bus/2), ΔI_pp ≈ 8.75 A, so the ripple adds ≈ 6.4 A². This is negligible against 6400 A² (+0.1%), so I_rms ≈ 80 A.

### 1.3 Self-Consistent Junction Temperature

R_DS(on) depends on T_j, which depends on loss, so iterate:

| Iteration | T_j assumed | R_DS | P_cond,ch |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 25 °C | 1.10 mΩ | 14.1 W |
| 1 | 90 °C | 1.49 mΩ | 19.1 W |
| 2 | 110 °C | 1.61 mΩ | **20.6 W** |

### 1.4 Distribution Across FETs

Averaged over one electrical period, each of the 6 FETs carries about 1/6 of the total, ≈ 3.4 W. The worst FET is the low-side at low duty, which carries ≈ 1.5× the average, **≈ 5.2 W**.

The electrical frequency is about 1–3 kHz. That is far shorter than the die thermal time constant (~10 ms), so period-averaging is valid. At low eRPM (start/stall), however, the instantaneous 10.3 W per FET must be used instead.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Per channel, only the PWM leg switches hard: one high-side/low-side pair per period.

### 2.1 V·I Overlap (Hard-Switched Edge)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 30\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$
