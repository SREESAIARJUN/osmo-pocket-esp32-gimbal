# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #8): System Architecture and Mathematical Model

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, which owns the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 talks to the ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600 only. Running ArduPilot and four sensorless six-step loops on one H743 is not supported by ArduPilot, so this design does not do it.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.005 × 100) = **1.5 mΩ**.

Define the per-FET instantaneous conduction power:

$$P_0 = I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) = 80^2 \times 1.5\times10^{-3} = 9.6\ \text{W}$$

### 1.2 Six-step current distribution

The electrical cycle has 6 sectors. In each sector:

- One leg PWMs: the high-side (HS) FET runs at duty D, and its low-side (LS) FET synchronously rectifies for (1 − D).
- A second leg holds its LS FET static ON.
- The third leg floats (BEMF sensing).

Over one electrical cycle, each FET experiences:

| FET | Sectors as PWM switch | Sectors as sync rectifier | Sectors static ON |
|---|---|---|---|
| HS_x | 2 of 6 | 0 | 0 |
| LS_x | 0 | 2 of 6 | 2 of 6 |

The resulting per-FET average losses are:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\left(D\,P_0 + P_{sw}\right)$$

$$P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\left((1-D)\,P_0 + P_{dt} + P_0\right)$$

**The LS FETs are the thermal-limiting devices.**

### 1.3 Channel conduction total

At any instant, exactly two FETs carry I_ph, so:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{ds} = 19.2\ \text{W}\quad(\text{independent of } D)$$

Add the PCB copper loss. With R_cu,eff ≈ 0.3 mΩ for the bus plus phase path:

$$P_{cu} = 80^2 \times 0.3\times10^{-3} = 1.9\ \text{W/ch}$$
