# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #16): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot/DMA Allocation, Netlists

## 0. Architecture Decisions

The STM32H743 should **not** commutate the motors directly. Running 4× sensorless BLDC/FOC (12 gate signals, BEMF zero-cross detection, 48 kHz PWM per bridge) on the flight controller MCU alongside ArduPilot's EKF and scheduler is infeasible on timing and timer budget. The architecture is therefore:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIH6 / VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 | AM32 firmware, 48 kHz complementary PWM |
| Gate drivers (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase, 3 half-bridges) | Bootstrapped HS/LS drive |
| Power FETs (×24) | 40 V N-ch, ~0.7 mΩ class (PDFN 5×6) | 6 per motor |

**Operating point:** 6S (25.2 V full charge), 80 A per motor *burst*, f_sw = 48 kHz, synchronous rectification, 6-step trapezoidal drive.

> **Verify:** The MOSFET parameters below are representative of 40 V, 5×6 mm, sub-1 mΩ parts. Substitute your selected part's datasheet values: R_DS(on) at 125 °C, Q_g, Q_rr, Q_oss, t_r, t_f, and Z_th(j-c). A true "nano" board with 3.3×3.3 mm FETs (~1.5–2 mΩ) roughly doubles the conduction losses.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
In 6-step drive, two phases are energized at any instant. With complementary (synchronous) PWM, the current path is always one high-side FET plus one low-side FET:

- During t_on: HS(A) → motor → LS(B)
- During t_off: LS(A) body is enhanced (sync rect) → motor → LS(B)

Two channels are therefore always in series with the motor current, independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

### 1.2 Temperature-corrected R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ /\mathrm{K}$$

$$R_{DS(on)}(125^\circ C) = 0.70 \times 1.6 = 1.12\ \mathrm{m\Omega}$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| Term | Expression | Per motor |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 80² × 2 × 1.12 mΩ | **14.34 W** |
| Copper (phase + bus, ~0.5 mΩ, 2 oz, short pours) | 80² × 0.5 mΩ | **3.20 W** |
| **Subtotal** | | **17.5 W** |

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution
Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period, as either HS or LS. Total FET conduction loss is shared across 6 devices:

$$P_{FET,avg} = \frac{14.34}{6} \approx 2.39\ \mathrm{W}, \qquad P_{FET,peak} = I^2 R = 7.17\ \mathrm{W}\ \text{(while conducting)}$$

At low duty cycle the LS (sync-rect) FET carries most of the "off" current. The worst-case single FET is therefore the LS device at low D under high current (e.g., punch-out from hover). Design to the 7.17 W peak.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Parameters

V_bus = 25.2 V, I = 80 A, f_sw = 48 kHz. Representative FET values: t_r + t_f ≈ 20 ns (FD6288 ~1 A source / 1.5 A sink, R_g ≈ 2–4 Ω), Q_rr ≈ 60 nC, Q_oss ≈ 100 nC, Q_g ≈ 100 nC at V_GS = 10–12 V, dead time t_dt ≈ 200 ns per edge, V_SD ≈ 0.8 V.
