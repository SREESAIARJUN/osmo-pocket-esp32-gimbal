# STM32H743 FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #65): Design Model

I don't have the Job #65 BOM, MOSFET part number, board stackup or layout. Every number below comes from **stated assumptions**. The equations are exact for the model and parametric, so you can substitute datasheet values. Anything marked ⚠ needs verification against your actual parts before release.

## 0. Architecture Correction

On a 4-in-1 ESC the **STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz commutation PWM**. The partitioning is:

| Function | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight control, mixer, DShot600 TX/RX telemetry | STM32H743 (FC) | ArduPilot |
| 6-step / sinusoidal commutation at 48 kHz, BEMF sensing | 4 × ESC MCU (e.g. STM32G071 / AT32F421) | AM32 or BLHeli_32 |
| Gate drive | 4 × 3-phase half-bridge driver (e.g. FD6288Q class) | n/a |

If you intend the H743 to drive 24 gates directly at 48 kHz, that is a different, non-standard design. It is mostly infeasible with ArduPilot, which has no motor-commutation layer. Everything below assumes the standard partition.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

In 6-step with synchronous rectification, current always flows through **two conducting devices in series**: one in the PWM leg (HS during on-time, LS during off-time) and one in the static low-side leg.

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] \approx k_T R_{25} = 1.6\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2(80)^2(1.6\times10^{-3}) + (80)^2(0.5\times10^{-3}) = 20.5 + 3.2 = \mathbf{23.7\ W}$$

**Per-device distribution.** Each leg conducts 2/3 of the electrical cycle, split between HS and LS. Averaged over a cycle:

$$\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{2 I^2 R_{DS}}{6} = 3.4\ \text{W (conduction only)}$$

The instantaneous peak while conducting is I²R_DS = 10.2 W.
