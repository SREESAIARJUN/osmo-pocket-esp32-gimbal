# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Model, and ArduPilot Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation

This analysis has three outputs:

1. A per-channel loss model for 80 A bursts with 48 kHz PWM.
2. A transient thermal model that gives the allowable burst duration.
3. A conflict-free TIM and DMA plan for ArduPilot bi-directional DShot600, with a schematic-level netlist.

The models are parametric, so you can substitute your actual MOSFET datasheet values. All worked numbers use the reference parameters in Section 1. The headline finding is in Section 4: **80 A on all four channels at once is a burst rating of about 3 seconds on a nano-format board, not a continuous rating.**

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{DS} = 1.2 \times 1.45 = 1.74\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 2.2 Bridge conduction

With 6-step commutation and synchronous rectification, phase current always flows through exactly two MOSFET channels in series:

- On the **PWM leg**, current flows through the high-side switch for D·T_sw and through the low-side switch for (1−D)·T_sw.
- On the **static leg**, current flows through the low-side switch continuously.

Because both legs conduct the full current, the bridge conduction loss is independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

**At 80 A and T_j = 125 °C:**

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.74\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{22.3\ W}$$

### 2.3 Parasitic copper and shunt resistance

| Path | Resistance | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pad + vias + plane (per current path) | ~0.5 mΩ | 3.2 W |
| Current shunt (if fitted) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |

### 2.4 Per-MOSFET distribution

Each MOSFET conducts for a 120° window of the electrical cycle. During that window, its instantaneous conduction loss is:

$$I^2 R_{DS} = 11.1\ \text{W}$$

**Example timing:** at 30,000 rpm with a 14-pole motor (7 pole pairs):

- f_e = 3.5 kHz
- Electrical period T_e = 286 µs

This is far shorter than the die thermal time constant (milliseconds), so each die effectively sees the average loss:

$$\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{22.3}{6} = 3.7\ \text{W}$$

## 3. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

In 6-step commutation, only one leg is hard-switching at any instant.

| Mechanism | Formula | Value at 80 A, 25.2 V |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 20 ns × 48 kHz = **0.968 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f
