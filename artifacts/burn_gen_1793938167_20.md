**Feasibility check up front.** An 80 A rating on a nano/30×30 AIO can only be a **seconds-scale burst** rating. Continuous operation at that level is thermally impossible on this form factor, as §4 shows. The model below quantifies the burst time.

## 1. Current Definitions

For trapezoidal (120°) commutation, two phases conduct at any instant. With duty D:

$$I_{bat} = D \cdot I_{ph} \qquad\Rightarrow\qquad I_{ph} = I_{bat}/D$$

At low throttle with high load (prop-wash, punch-out from hover), phase current can greatly exceed battery current. The rating must therefore be specified in **phase** amps, which is why phase current is the model variable.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × (1 + 0.006·85) = **1.81 mΩ**

### 2.2 Per-FET distribution over one electrical cycle

Each phase spends 120° as the PWM leg (HS switching, LS synchronous) and 120° as the return leg (LS fully on):

| FET | Conduction fraction | Average loss |
|---|---|---|
| High-side | D/3 | P_HS = (D/3) I² R |
| Low-side | (1−D)/3 + 1/3 | P_LS = ((2−D)/3) I² R |

The sum over all 6 FETs is P_cond,total = 2 I²_ph R_ds(T_j), independent of D.

### 2.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 110 °C

- I²R = 6400 × 1.81 mΩ = **11.6 W**
- P_cond per ESC = **23.2 W**
- Worst single FET (LS at D → 0) = (2/3)(11.6) = **7.7 W**
- At D ≈ 1: HS = LS = 3.9 W

### 2.4 Copper and shunt losses (often forgotten)

**Phase trace** (2 oz, 4 parallel layers, 3 mm wide, 15 mm long):

$$R = \frac{\rho L}{w t n} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\cdot 0.015}{0.003\cdot 70\times10^{-6}\cdot 4} \approx 0.31\text{ mΩ}$$

Hot copper (+0.39 %/K) gives ≈ 0.4 mΩ, so P ≈ 2.5 W per phase path pair.

**Shunt** (0.25 mΩ, sees I_bat): at I_bat = 60 A, P = 0.9 W.

## 3. Switching Loss Model @ 48 kHz

### 3.1 Transition times (gate-charge method)

$$t_{ri} \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}\,R_g}{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}, \quad t_{fv}\approx\frac{Q_{gd}\,R_g}{V_{drv}-V_{pl}} \quad(\text{turn-on})$$
$$t_{rv}\approx\frac{Q_{gd}\,R_g}{V_{pl}}, \quad t_{fi}\approx\frac{Q_{gs2}\,R_g}{V_{pl}} \quad(\text{turn-off})$$

| Event | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Turn-on | (4 + 8 nC)·10 Ω / 6.5 V | ≈ **18 ns** |
| Turn-off | 12 nC·10 Ω / 3.5 V | ≈ **34 ns** |
