# STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture, Loss Model, and DShot Allocation (Job #4)

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Constraints

Two points shape the rest of this document.

1. **The H743 cannot run the ESC commutation.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 as the flight controller. Each motor needs its own commutation MCU running AM32. Bi-directional DShot600 is the link between the two, and the 48 kHz PWM is a setting on the ESC MCU, not the H743.
   - FC: STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100) or VIT6 (LQFP100), running ArduPilot.
   - ESC: 4 × STM32G071GBU6 or AT32F421K8U7 running AM32, plus 4 × FD6288Q gate drivers and 24 × N-MOSFETs.

2. **80 A burst on a 20×20 mm board is a transient rating only.** Section 3 shows it is limited to roughly 1–2 s at about 85 W of dissipation, with a junction-temperature risk. My recommended label is **"45 A continuous (airflow) / 80 A burst ≤ 1 s"**, enforced in firmware. If a true 80 A sustained rating is required, the ESC must move to a 30.5×30.5 mm or larger format.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A per Channel)

With trapezoidal 6-step commutation and complementary PWM, the phase current always flows through **two FET positions** in series:
- the high-side FET of the active phase, or its synchronous low-side FET during off-time, and
- the low-side FET of the return phase.

Temperature-dependent on-resistance:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

Conduction loss per channel, independent of duty cycle because synchronous rectification carries current in both PWM states:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

For flat-top trapezoidal current, I_rms ≈ I_pk. Adding 48 kHz ripple:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-E)\,D}{L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}$$

With motor phase-to-phase inductance L ≈ 15 µH and worst case D = 0.5, E ≈ 0:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.5}{15\,\mu\text{H} \times 48\,\text{kHz}} = 17.5\ \text{A} \;\Rightarrow\; \Delta I^2/12 = 25.5\ \text{A}^2$$

This adds less than 0.5 % to I², so it is negligible at 80 A.

**Self-consistent junction-temperature iteration**

Per-FET thermal path: R_θJ-board ≈ 5 K/W (R_θJC plus vias plus local copper). The board starts at 80 °C.

| Iteration | T_j (°C) | R_DS (mΩ) | P_cond per channel (W) | P per FET (W) | ΔT_j-board (K) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 100 | 1.50 | 19.2 | 9.6 | 48 |
| 1 | 128 | 1.69 | 21.6 | 10.8 | 54 |
| 2 | 134 | 1.73 | 22.2 | 11.1 | 55 |

The iteration converges at **T_j ≈ 135 °C with P_cond ≈ 22 W per channel** when the board is at 80 °C.

If the board reaches 95 °C or more, T_j exceeds 150 °C. **That board temperature is the hard burst limit.**

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only one leg per channel hard-switches during a commutation state. Values below are at V = 25.2 V, I = 80 A, f = 48 kHz.

| Term | Equation | Value (W) |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | 0.5·25.2·80·30 ns·48 kHz = **1.45** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_F·I·2t_dt·f_sw | 0.8·80·200 ns·48 kHz = **0.61** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 50 nC·25.2·48 kHz = **0.06** |
| C_oss (both FETs in leg) | 2·½·C_oss·V²·f_sw | **0.05** |
| Gate drive (taken in driver/LDO) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw | **0.06** |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 2.2 W per channel** |

Switching loss scales linearly with f_sw:
- 24 kHz: ≈ 1.1 W
- 96 kHz: ≈ 4.4 W

At 80 A, conduction dominates by about 10×. **48 kHz is therefore a sound choice** for current ripple and acoustic behaviour on low-inductance 20×20-class motors.
