### 0.1 Architecture Clarification ⚠

The STM32H743 does **not** drive the power stage directly. In every production AIO of this class, the topology is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 or BLHeli_32)
                                              └─► 3-phase gate driver ─► 6× N-MOSFET ─► motor
```

The 48 kHz PWM, dead time, and commutation all live in the ESC MCUs. The H743 is responsible only for the DShot command/telemetry link, current/voltage ADC, and the flight stack. The design below follows this split.

### 1.1 Current Path
