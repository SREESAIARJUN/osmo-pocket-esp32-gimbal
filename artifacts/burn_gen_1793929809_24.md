# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #23): System Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan, Netlists

## 0. Assumptions and Corrections to the Brief

I don't have Job #23's BOM, stackup, or enclosure data. Every numeric result below comes from the stated assumptions. Replace them with your datasheet values; the equations stay valid.

**Two corrections to the brief:**

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs as flight-control firmware on the H743. Each motor channel needs its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware for sensorless commutation, BEMF zero-cross detection and the 48 kHz PWM. A typical choice is the AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 talks to those four MCUs over bidirectional DShot600 on internal board traces. The architecture below reflects this.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) is thermally marginal.** Section 3 quantifies this. The usable burst at 4 × 80 A is about 3 s. It is not a continuous rating.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

At the design point T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{ds} = 0.9 \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 100) = 1.305\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current path

In 6-step commutation, two phase legs carry current at any instant:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts during D·T_sw. The low-side FET conducts in synchronous rectification during (1−D)·T_sw, minus dead time.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET conducts continuously for the 60° step.

Exactly two channel resistances are therefore always in series with I_ph, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.305\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{16.7\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts for 120° of the electrical cycle (1/3 duty). Averaged over a cycle at mid-duty:

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} = \frac{2 I^2 R}{6} = \mathbf{2.78\ W}$$

**Worst case (locked rotor or very low eRPM):** one FET carries 80 A continuously.

$$P = I^2 R = 8.35\ \text{W}$$

This is a fault case. AM32 stall protection must cut it within about 100 ms.

### 1.4 Copper loss

Phase pads, bus planes and vias are assumed to total 0.4 mΩ per path, with two paths in series:

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.8\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{5.1\ W/ESC}$$
