## 0. Scope, Architecture Correction, and Assumptions

**Architecture note.** The STM32H743 is the flight-controller MCU. It does not commutate the motors. A 4-in-1 ESC needs its own commutation controller(s). This design assumes AM32 firmware on 4× AT32F421 (one per motor). The H743 drives each ESC MCU over a single bi-directional DShot line.
