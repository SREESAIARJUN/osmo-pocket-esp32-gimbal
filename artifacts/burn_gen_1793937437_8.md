## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 1–4 mH |
| Sensing | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per joint, inline/low-side shunt current sensing |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 class (STM32G4/H7), single-precision FPU |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI + DMA, 4 kHz ODR |
| Gimbal order | Yaw (base) → Roll → Pitch (camera) |
