# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #8): Electro-Thermal Model, Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Plan, and Reference Netlists

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Three issues change the design, so they come first.

1. **The STM32H743 is not the ESC controller.** In a 4-in-1 ESC, each motor (or the whole ESC) is commutated by a dedicated small MCU, such as an STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 is the flight controller. It sources bidirectional DShot600 and decodes the eRPM telemetry. This document models it that way. Commutating four motors at 48 kHz from the H743 while also running ArduPilot is not a supported configuration.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 mm) board is a transient rating, not a thermal steady state.** Section 3 shows that four simultaneous 80 A channels dissipate about 100 W into roughly 1 J/K of board. That gives a usable window of well under one second.
3. **Component parameters are representative, not vendor-verified.** I have no confirmed BOM for Job #8. The MOSFET and driver values below are typical of 40 V DFN3.3×3.3 parts used on 6S ESCs. Every equation is parameterized, so substitute datasheet values before sign-off.
