## 1. Electrical Operating Model

The ESC uses trapezoidal (6-step) commutation with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM). At any instant two phases conduct:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** the HS FET conducts during D·T_sw and the LS FET during (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Phase B (return leg):** the LS FET is held fully on for the 60° sector.

Each FET conducts for 120° of the 360° electrical cycle, so its sector duty is 1/3.

Phase current during a sector is approximately the DC motor current I_M, since ripple is small at 48 kHz.

## 2. Conduction Losses at 80 A Burst

### 2.1 Temperature-Dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 0.90 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.27 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.40 mΩ |

Use **R_hot = 1.40 mΩ** for design.

Add the PCB copper path per phase loop (pad → pour → phase pad → shunt), estimated at R_Cu ≈ 0.6 mΩ total loop at 2 oz on a nano board.

### 2.2 Per-ESC Conduction Loss

During one sector:

$$P_{cond} = I_M^2\left[D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS} + R_{LS}\right] + I_M^2 R_{Cu} + I_M^2 R_{shunt}$$

With R_HS = R_LS = R_hot, the FET term reduces to **2·I²·R_hot**, which is independent of D.

At I_M = 80 A:

| Term | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| FETs | 2 × 80² × 1.40 mΩ | **17.9 W** |
| Copper | 80² × 0.6 mΩ | 3.8 W |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ) | 80² × 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| **Total conduction per ESC** | | **23.3 W** |

### 2.3 Per-FET Loss

Per-FET loss determines the junction temperature.

**Worst-case FET (LS):** it acts as the return leg for 60° and as the PWM-complementary leg for 60°, with low D at burst start.

$$P_{FET,max} \approx \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2 R_{hot}\cdot\frac{(1)+(1-D)}{2}\cdot 2 \;\;\to\;\; \le \tfrac{1}{3}\cdot 80^2\cdot1.40\text{m} = 2.99\ \text{W}$$

**Average FET:** 17.9 W / 6 = 2.98 W.

**Result:** about **3.0 W per FET**, plus the switching share from §3.

### 2.4 Electro-Thermal Stability (Runaway Check)

$$P(T) = P_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T-25)]$$

The system is stable only if the loop gain is below 1:

$$\alpha\,P_{25}\,R_{\theta,eff} < 1$$

Per FET:
- P₂₅ = 80² × 0.9 mΩ / 3 = 1.92 W
- R_θ,eff (junction → board → air, local) ≈ 25 K/W on a nano board with prop-wash convection

Loop gain = 0.0055 × 1.92 × 25 = **0.26**, which is below 1. The design is stable, but the steady-state temperature is unreachable before the transient limit (next section).

Self-consistent steady state:

$$T_j = T_a + \frac{P_{25}\,R_\theta\,(1-25\alpha) + ...}{1-\alpha P_{25} R_\theta} \Rightarrow \Delta T \approx \frac{1.92\cdot25}{1-0.26} = 65\ \text{K per FET locally}$$

This local figure is for an isolated FET only. All 24 FETs heat the same small board, so the board-level bulk rise (§2.5) dominates.
