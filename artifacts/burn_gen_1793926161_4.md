# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80 A ESC: Electrothermal Model, DShot600 Timing and DMA Allocation, Reference Netlist

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I don't have Job #42's actual BOM, stackup or schematic files. Everything below is a **parameterized reference model** using a representative component set. Replace the parameters in §1 with your datasheet values, and every result updates through the equations.

**Architecture note.** The H743 does not switch the MOSFETs. In a 4-in-1 stack:

- **H743 FC**: generates bidirectional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry.
- **ESC MCU** (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or Bluejay): generates the 48 kHz PWM.
- **3-phase gate driver**: drives 24 N-FETs (4 motors × 3 legs × HS/LS).

The loss model (§2–4) belongs to the ESC side. The DMA model (§5–6) belongs to the FC side.

**Feasibility flag.** 80 A per motor on a "nano" footprint (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm mounting, about 30×30 mm board) is only viable as a seconds-scale burst. §4 shows that thermal capacity, not R_DS(on), is the binding constraint.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

**Temperature-dependent resistance:**

R_DS(T_j) = R_DS25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

A more accurate fit is R_DS25·(T_j/298 K)^2.3. At T_j = 125 °C:

R_DS = 1.2 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.8 mΩ**

**Six-step trapezoidal operation with synchronous (complementary) PWM:**

- At any instant two phase legs carry the current I in series.
- Within the PWM-ed leg, current alternates between HS (duty D) and LS (1−D).
- The conducting path therefore always contains exactly two R_DS, independent of D.

The motor total is:

P_cond,motor = 2 · I² · R_DS(T_j) = 2 × 80² × 1.8 mΩ = **23.0 W**

Each FET conducts on average 1/3 of the electrical cycle. The per-FET average is:

P_cond,FET,avg = I² · R_DS / 3 = **7.7 W**

The worst-case distribution occurs at D → 1, where the HS of the active leg takes the full 120° window. The result is unchanged at I²R/3 per FET, but it concentrates on HS devices.

**Electrothermal fixed point.** R depends on T_j, which depends on P, so solve iteratively:

T_j = T_ref + P(T_j)·R_θ,e
