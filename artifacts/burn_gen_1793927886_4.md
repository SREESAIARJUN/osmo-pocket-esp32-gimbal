## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Which FETs conduct

In any commutation step, phase A is PWM'd and phase B returns to ground:

- **PWM on (duty D):** current flows through Q_AH, then the motor, then Q_BL.
- **PWM off (duty 1−D):** current flows through Q_AL (synchronous rectification), then the motor, then Q_BL.

So exactly **two FETs conduct at every instant**, regardless of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

The per-FET split depends on duty:

- Q_AH: I²R·D
- Q_AL: I²R·(1−D)
- Q_BL: I²R

**Q_BL, the low-side FET of the return phase, is the hottest device.** Over a full electrical cycle, the stress rotates among the six FETs.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.3 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.89 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds(on) | P_cond per channel | Hottest single FET (Q_BL) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.30 mΩ | 16.6 W | 8.3 W |
| 125 °C | 1.89 mΩ | **24.2 W** | **12.1 W** |

### 1.4 Parasitic copper and shunt losses (per channel)

| Element | Resistance | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Shunt (2 × 0.5 mΩ 2512 in parallel) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| Phase and bus copper (~0.3 mΩ effective) | 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |

The temperature dependence makes this a feedback loop. Solve it iteratively: P(T_j) feeds T_j = f(P) in Section 3 until it converges. Section 3 shows convergence at about T_j ≈ 125 °C at the burst limit.

## 2. Switching Losses at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg (Q_AH/Q_AL) switches. The parameters are V = 25.2 V, I = 80 A, and t_r + t_f = 20 ns effective (gate driver with ~2 A sink/source and a 4.7 Ω gate resistor).

| Mechanism | Formula | Result |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap (hard-switched high side) | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 20 ns × 48 k = **0.97 W** |
| Dead-time body-diode conduction (t_dt = 100 ns, two edges) | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw | 0.8 × 80 × 200 ns × 48 k = **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 40 nC × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.05 W** |
| Output capacitance | Q_oss·V·f_sw | 30 nC × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.04 W** |
| Gate drive (lost in the driver, not the FET) | Q_g·V_gs·f_sw × 2 | 40 nC × 10 × 48 k × 2 = **0.04 W** |
| **Total switching per channel** | | **≈ 1.7 W** |

**Key insight:** at 80 A, switching loss is only about 7% of conduction loss. Going from 24 kHz to 48 kHz costs about 0.
