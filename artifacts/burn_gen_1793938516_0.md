### Spec red flag: resolve before layout

If "80 A burst" means **80 A per motor**, the battery side sees up to ~320 A at high duty. That much current cannot pass through a 20×20 board's battery pads, an XT60, or 2 oz copper.

All 4-in-1 ESCs in this size class are realistically rated at 12–25 A continuous and 30–40 A burst per channel.
