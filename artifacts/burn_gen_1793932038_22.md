## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path in 6-step synchronous commutation

In any 60° sector, two phases conduct:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** the high side conducts for duty D. Its complementary low side conducts for (1 − D), minus dead time.
- **Phase B (return leg):** the low side conducts continuously.

So phase current I_ph always flows through exactly **two** channels in series, regardless of D:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot R_{DS}(T_j)\cdot\big[D + (1-D)\big] + I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

Dead-time conduction is handled in Section 3.4 as diode loss.

Ripple correction. With phase inductance L_ph and ripple ΔI:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12},\qquad \Delta I \approx \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\, f_{sw}}
$$

Take a typical 5-inch-class motor with L_ph ≈ 15 µH, near full throttle, where V_bus − V_bemf ≈ 3 V and D ≈ 0.9. Then ΔI ≈ 3.75 A and ΔI²/12 ≈ 1.2 A². This is negligible against 6400 A², so I_rms ≈ 80 A.

### 2.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$
R_{DS}(125) = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.005 \times 100) = 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega
$$

### 2.3 Numerical result

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{19.2\ W}
$$

### 2.4 PCB copper loss

The copper path per motor runs bus to high-side drain, phase node to pad, and low-side source to GND. Estimate R_Cu ≈ 0.25 mΩ effective at 80 °C, with copper α = 0.0039/K.

$$
P_{Cu,motor} = I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.25\times10^{-3} = 1.6\ \text{W}
$$

### 2.5 Shunt loss

The DC-link current is I_dc ≈ D·I_ph ≈ 72 A. Using a 0.25 mΩ shared bus shunt (one per 4-in-1, total I_dc,total ≈ 288 A at full 4-motor burst):

$$
P_{shunt} = 288^2 \times 0.25\times10^{-3} = 20.7\ \text{W}
$$

> **Design decision:** this is unacceptable. Use **per-motor sensing via R_DS(on)/phase-shunt-less AM32 current estimation**, or a 0.1 mΩ shunt (8.3 W, still severe). The recommended approach is a single 0.1 mΩ shunt sized for *average* current telemetry, accepting reduced burst accuracy. **[VERIFY]**

### 2.6 Paralleled-die option

With N dies in parallel per switch, R_eff = R_DS/N and P_cond scales by 1/N. With N = 2, P_cond,motor = 9.6 W. This is rarely possible on a 30.5 mm board with 24 switch positions.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. Its high side switches hard. Its complementary low side switches softly (ZVS through the body diode after dead time), apart from reverse recovery.

### 3.1 V·I overlap loss (high side)

Gate current during the Miller plateau:

$$
I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}} = \frac{10-3}{5.7} = 1.23\,\text{A}
\qquad
I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}} = \frac{3}{5.7} = 0.53\,\text{A}
