## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Corrections

I don't have prior Job #51 documentation, so every parameter is stated explicitly. Replace each one with datasheet values before release.

**Architecture correction.** ArduPilot cannot commutate motors directly from the H743. The only workable architecture is:

- **H743** runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600 on 4 timer channels.
- **4× ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class) run AM32 or BLHeli and drive the gate drivers and MOSFETs.

The H743 therefore owns DShot and DMA. The ESC MCUs own the 48 kHz PWM and dead-time.
