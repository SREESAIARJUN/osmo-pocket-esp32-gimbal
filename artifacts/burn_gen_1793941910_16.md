**Architecture:** The H743 FC sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (AT32F421 class, running AM32). Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and 6 FETs. The 48 kHz switching is generated by the ESC MCUs, not by the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

In each 60° sector, two legs are active:
- The **PWM leg** alternates the high-side (HS) FET (duty D) with the low-side (LS) FET (1−D, synchronous rectification).
- The **return leg** holds its LS FET fully on.

Outside dead time, phase current always flows through exactly two channels. Each channel has resistance R_DS(T_j). So per motor:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j) \cdot \left(1 - 2 t_{dt} f_{sw}\right)
$$

Per-FET average: each FET is in the active path for one-third of the electrical cycle.

$$
P_{cond,HS} = \frac{I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}\, D}{3}, \qquad
P_{cond,LS} = \frac{I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}\,\left[(1-D) + 1\right]}{3}
$$

The low-side FETs carry more loss than the high-side FETs whenever D < 1. They are the thermal hot spot at partial throttle with high phase current.

### 1.2 Temperature-coupled R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]
$$

$$
T_j = T_{board} + P_{FET}(T_j)\cdot R_{\theta,j\text{-}b}
\;\Rightarrow\;
T_j = \frac{T_b + R_\theta\left(k R_{25}(1-25\alpha) + P_{sw}\right)}{1 - R_\theta\, k\, R_{25}\, \alpha}
$$
