## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Constraints

Read this section first. Several numbers in the brief need to be pinned down or corrected before the math means anything.

| Item | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Topology | H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. **Four separate ESC MCUs** (AT32F421K8U7, AM32 firmware) each drive one 3-phase bridge. | The H743 does not have enough advanced-timer complementary channel sets (it has two: TIM1 and TIM8) for 4×3 half-bridges with BEMF zero-cross detection. Industry AIOs all use per-motor ESC MCUs. |
| Bus voltage | 6S Li-ion/LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V, 40 V MOSFETs | Leaves 37% margin for regen and ringing. |
| Form factor | **30.5×30.5 mm** board, 25.5 mm mount pattern | 24 FETs at 80A-burst class **cannot** fit a 20×20 "nano" footprint. On 20×20 the realistic burst rating is about 35–45 A. This is called out as a spec risk. |
| "80A" | **Burst, per channel, ≤ ~3 s** (Section 3 derives the limit). Continuous rating is about 21 A per channel with propwash cooling
