## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flags

Three points need to be settled before the math.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In a 4-in-1 AIO, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot600. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (typically an STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). That ESC MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM and handles bidirectional eRPM telemetry. The model below follows that split.

2. **80 A on a nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board is a burst rating only, not continuous.** With four channels at 80 A, the board dissipates about 95 W. A board this size has a heat capacity of only a few J/K. Section 3 shows the result: burst duration is bounded to roughly 1.5–3 s depending on airflow. Market "80A" nano claims usually mean 10 ms–1 s peaks.

3. **Assumed operating point.**
   - 6S LiPo: V_bus = 25.2 V maximum.
   - Trapezoidal 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification.
   - f_sw = 48 kHz.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (Per ESC Channel)

### 2.1 Conduction topology

In 6-step operation, one phase leg PWMs while another phase's low-side is held on. With synchronous rectification, the current path always passes through exactly two MOSFETs:

- **ON state:** HS(A) → motor → LS(B)
- **OFF state:** LS(A) (sync rect) → motor → LS(B)

The channel conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle, apart from small ripple effects:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)\cdot\left(1 + \frac{r^2}{12}\right)
$$

- r is the ripple ratio ΔI/I. At 48 kHz with typical 5" motor inductance (≈20–40 µH phase-phase), r ≈ 0.1, so the correction is +0.08%. This is negligible and is dropped.

The on-resistance rises with temperature:

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

**Temperature–resistance coupling.** Conduction loss heats the die, which raises R_ds, which raises loss. The fixed-point solution is:

$$
T_j = T_{ref} + Z_{th}\cdot P(T_j) \;\Rightarrow\; T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + Z_{th}\,I^2 R_{ds25}k\,(1-25\alpha)}{1 - Z_{th}\,I^2 R_{ds25}k\,\alpha}
$$

- k is the fraction of the channel loss carried by the FET in question (derived in 2.4).
- **Thermal runaway** occurs when the denominator reaches 0, i.e. when Z_th · I² · R_ds25 · k · α ≥ 1. This is a stability criterion for the layout's effective thermal impedance.

### 2.2 Conduction loss at 80 A

Evaluated at T_j = 125 °C, so R_ds = 1.55 mΩ:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.55\text{ m}\Omega = 6400 \times 3.1\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.8\ W}
$$

Copper and solder-joint loss:

$$
P_{cu} = 80^2 \times 0.3\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{1.92\ W}
$$
