### Key findings

1. **80 A per motor is not sustainable on a 20×20 mm board.**
   - With all four motors at 80 A, the board dissipates about **115 W**.
   - The adiabatic thermal budget is roughly **1.5–2 s** before the FETs reach their T_j limit.
   - Treat 80 A as a ≤1 s transient rating. A defensible continuous rating for this form factor is **about 35–45 A per motor**.
2. **Conduction losses dominate.**
   - At 48 kHz, switching losses are only about **7%** of total losses.
   - Lowering the PWM frequency therefore buys very little. Lowering R_DS(on) and improving copper buys a lot.
3. **Bus capacitor ripple current is a hidden failure mode.**
   - It reaches **≈ 40 A RMS per channel** at D = 0.5.
4. **DShot600 bidirectional on the H743 needs a specific timer layout.**
   - All four motors go on **one general-purpose timer** (TIM3), using non-complementary channels.
   - The configuration requires 5 DMA streams via DMAMUX: 1 × UP plus 4 × CHx.
5. **Architecture.** The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot to **four independent ESC MCUs** (AM32 or BLHeli_32-class). The H743 does **not** commutate the motors.
