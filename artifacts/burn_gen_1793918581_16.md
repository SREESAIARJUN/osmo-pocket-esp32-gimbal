# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #39): System Architecture, Loss Model, and DShot Allocation

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Feasibility Flags

Three problems with the brief need resolving before any numbers can be trusted.

**1. The H743 cannot be the ESC controller.** ArduPilot does not commutate motors, and the H743 cannot also run 4× sensorless FOC/6-step loops at 48 kHz alongside it. The correct architecture is:
- **STM32H743VIT6** running ArduPilot as the flight controller.
- **4× ESC MCUs** (AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071KBU, running AM32) driving **4× 3-phase gate drivers** and **24 MOSFETs**.
- The two sides communicate over **bidirectional DShot600**, one line per motor.

**2. 80 A burst on a "nano" board is thermally infeasible unless "burst" is tightly defined.** Section 3 shows that a 20×20 mm board absorbing ~80 W rises at about 35 K/s. The spec must be "80 A per channel, ≤1 s, single channel," or the board must grow to 30.5×30.5 mm with 5×6 mm FETs.

**3. The 48 kHz switching runs on the ESC MCUs, not on the H743.**

All component values below are **parametric placeholders**. Substitute datasheet values for the final FET and driver choices.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Assumptions

| Symbol | Meaning | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 6S, full charge | 25.2 V |
| I_ph | Phase current, burst | 80 A |
| R_ds,25 | Per FET at V_gs = 10 V | 1.0 mΩ (40 V, 5×6 class) |
| α | Temperature coefficient | ≈0.006 /K |
| T_j | Design point | 100 °C |
| R_cu | Phase trace + via + pad, per leg | 0.3 mΩ |

### 1.2 Temperature-Dependent R_ds(on)

R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)] = 1.0 · 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ**

### 1.3 Current Path

Assume 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification. At any instant two phases conduct, so current passes through exactly two FET positions:
- During PWM on-time: high side of phase A, then low side of phase B.
- During PWM off-time: low side of A (synchronous), then low side of B.

The series path therefore always contains two FETs:

P_cond,FET = I_rms² · 2 · R_ds(T_j)

The ripple contribution is I_rms² = I_dc² + ΔI²/12. With L_motor ≈ 15 µH, D = 0.9, and f = 48 kHz:

ΔI = V(1−D)D / (L·f) = 25.2·0.09 / (15e-6·48e3) ≈ 3.2 A, which is negligible: I_rms ≈ 80.0 A.

**Per motor:**

- P_cond,FET = 6400 · 2 · 1.45e-3 = **18.6 W**
- P_cond,Cu = 6400 · 2 · 0.3e-3 = **3.8 W**

**Distribution across FETs:** losses spread over 3 legs. At high duty, the high-side FETs take about 85%. The hottest FET dissipates roughly 18.6 / 3 · 0.85 ≈ **5.3 W** (average over an electrical cycle).

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Only the active leg's high-side FET hard-switches. The synchronous low-side commutates softly through its body diode during dead time.

### 2.1 Transition Times from Gate Charge

Assumed parameters:
- Q_gs2 = 3 nC, Q_gd = 6 nC, V_plateau = 3.0 V, V_drv = 10 V
- R_g,int = 1 Ω, R_g,ext = 4.7 Ω (turn-on), 2.2 Ω with a diode (turn-off)

Gate currents:
- I_g,on = (10 − 3) / 5.7 = 1.23 A
- I_g,off = 3 / 3.2 = 0.94 A

Transition times:
- t_on = 9 nC / 1.23 A = 7.3 ns
- t_off = 9 nC / 0.94 A = 9.6 ns
- **Σ ≈ 17 ns.** Allow a 1.5× margin for loop inductance: **26 ns**.

### 2.2 Loss Components (Per Motor)

| Term | Expression | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_on+t_off)·f = ½·25.2·80·26e-9·48e3 | **1.26 W** |
| Coss | Q_oss·V·f = 40e-9·25.2·48e3 | 0.05 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f = 40e-9·25.2·48e3 | 0.05 W |
| Dead-time diode | V_f·I·2·t_dt·f = 0.8·80·2·50e-9·48e3 | **0.31 W** |
| Gate drive | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f = 2·40e-9·10·48e3 | 0.04 W (in driver) |
| **Total switching** | | **≈1.7 W** |

The ring at turn-off (L_loop ≈ 2 nH, di/dt ≈ 80 A/9.6 ns) gives ΔV ≈ 17 V. That puts V_ds,pk at about 42 V, which is **over 40 V FET ratings**. Two fixes:
- Specify 60 V FETs, accepting roughly 1.4× higher R_ds; or
- Add an RC snubber (2.2 Ω + 2.2 nF per leg) and keep the loop under 1.5 nH using a 0402 100 nF decoupling capacitor at each high-side drain pad.
