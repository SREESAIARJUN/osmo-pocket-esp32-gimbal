## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Value (assumed) |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R = 5.6 Ω (phase), L = 2.2 mH, Kt = 0.08 N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (e.g. AS5048/MT6701), sampled at the current-loop rate |
| Inverter | 3-phase, center-aligned PWM at 20 kHz, low-side shunts, V_bus = 16.8 V (4S) |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 24 MHz, gyro/accel ODR 8 kHz, ±2000 dps / ±16 g |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 (STM32G4/H7 class), single-precision FPU |

**Rate hierarchy.** Each outer loop runs at least 5× slower than its inner loop, and loop bandwidths are separated by at least 5×.

| Loop | Rate f_s | Closed-loop bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current (d/q PI) | 20 kHz | ω_ci ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s |
| Velocity (PI + feedforward) | 4 kHz (IMU-synchronous: 8 kHz ODR, decimated 2:1) | ω_cv ≈ 2π·120 rad/s |
| Angle (P/PD) | 1 kHz | ω_ca ≈ 2π·20 rad/s |
| Attitude fusion | 4 kHz | — |

```
θ_ref ─►[Angle P]─ω_ref─►(+)─►[Vel PI]─i_q*─►(+)─►[Current PI]─► SVPWM ─► Motor
            ▲      ω_ff─┘          ▲        i_ff,cog ┘   ▲ (i_d*=0)
            │                      │                     │
     θ_meas (fused attitude)  ω_meas (gyro→joint axis)  i_abc → Clarke/Park(θ_e)
```

For stabilization, the **velocity feedback comes from the fused gyro rate projected onto each joint axis**, not from encoder differentiation. The encoder provides the electrical angle θ_e for commutation, the cogging phase, and the joint kinematics.

## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms
Phase currents are taken from two shunts, with i_c = −i_a − i_b.

**Clarke (amplitude-invariant):**
$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)
$$

**Park**, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:
$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park** applies the transpose.

### 1.2 Plant
For a surface-mount PMSM (L_d = L_q = L):
$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$
The electromagnetic torque is τ = (3/2)·p·λ_m·i_q = K_t·i_q.

### 1.3 PI design by pole-zero cancellation
The plant per axis is G(s) = 1/(Ls + R). Choose C(s) = K_p + K_i/s with K_i/K_p = R/L. The open loop then becomes ω_ci/s, and the closed loop is a first-order lag with bandwidth ω_ci:
$$
K_p = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_{ci}
$$
