## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Design-Review Flags

These assumptions frame every number below. Please confirm or correct them before layout.

**Assumed architecture (the industry-standard AIO split):**
- **STM32H743** runs ArduPilot only: the flight stack, EKF, IMU, OSD and logging. It does not commutate motors.
- **4× AT32F421K8U7** (or STM32G071) ESC MCUs run AM32. They do six-step commutation with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM at **f_sw = 48 kHz**.
- **4× 3-phase gate drivers** of the FD6288Q class drive **24× N-MOSFETs** in 3.3×3.3 mm PDFN packages.
- **6S nominal:** V_bus = 25.2 V max and 22.2 V nominal. Analysis uses 25.2 V as the worst case.
- **Form factor:** a 30×30 mm "nano" board with 8 layers and 2 oz copper.

**Red flags to resolve at the design review:**
1. **"80 A burst" on a nano board is thermally a ~2-second rating** when all four channels are loaded (derived in §3). It cannot be sustained. The datasheet must state the burst duration and the conditions.
2. **4 × 80 A at full throttle draws about 320 A from the battery.** That exceeds an XT60 (~60 A continuous / ~180 A burst) and most 6S packs. The *system* burst is pack-limited long before it is ESC-limited.
3. **MOSFET parameters below are representative** of a 40 V, 1.5 mΩ PDFN3.3 part. Re-run §1–§3 with the actual datasheet values (R_DS(on) vs T_j, Q_gd, Q_rr, Q_oss) once the part is selected.
4. **ArduPilot DMA details** are written against current AP_HAL_ChibiOS behavior (DMAMUX resolver, BIDIR pairing). Verify them against the release branch you ship.

### 1.2 Current-Path Topology

In six-step with complementary PWM, two phases conduct at any instant:

- **PWM on-time (D):** the high side of phase X and the low side of phase Y conduct.
- **PWM off-time (1−D):** the low side of phase X (synchronous rectifier) and the low side of phase Y conduct.
