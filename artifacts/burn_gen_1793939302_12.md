## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Feasibility Flags

Three things need to be settled before any numbers.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** On every production 4-in-1 design, each channel has its own ESC MCU (AT32F421, STM32G071 or EFM8) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and only *commands* the ESCs over bidirectional DShot600. The design below follows that split.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 mm) AIO is a thermal-capacitance rating, not a sustainable one.** Section 3 shows that 4 × 80 A at the same time gives a burst window of about 1–2 s. Battery-side current would be 320 A, which exceeds any XT30 or XT60 connector and typical 6S packs. The realistic interpretation is **80 A per channel, single-channel transient**, with about 35–45 A per channel continuous under prop wash.
