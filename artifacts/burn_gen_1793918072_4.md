# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #51): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot Timing, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Architectural Ground Rules

Three facts constrain the design.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot sends DShot commands. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 owns the four DShot lines and nothing downstream of them.
2. **80 A on a "nano" footprint is a burst rating, not a continuous one.** Section 3 shows that a 30×30 mm stack reaches thermal limits in about 6 s at 4×80 A. Continuous capability is about 30 A per channel.
3. **No specific MOSFET or gate driver was specified.** The model below is fully parametric. Re-run it with real datasheet values before committing to layout.
