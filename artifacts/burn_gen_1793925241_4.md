# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

I don't have the Job #4 specification, so this design states its assumptions explicitly. Every parameter is a named constant you can replace with your real values.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Hardware Baseline

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (7 pole pairs), R = 5.5 Ω, L = 2.0 mH, Kt ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701), read at 20 kHz |
| Current sense | 2-phase inline/low-side shunts, 12-bit ADC synced to PWM center |
| MCU | STM32G474 (FPU + CORDIC) or equivalent Cortex-M4F/M7 |
| Bus | 12–16 V |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI @ 24 MHz, ODR 8 kHz, placed symmetrically about the payload center |
| Axis order | Yaw (base) → Roll → Pitch (camera) |

## 1. Timing Architecture

| Loop | Rate | Ts | Target bandwidth |
|---|---|---|---|
| PWM / current (FOC) | 20 kHz | 50 µs | ~1.5 kHz |
| IMU sampling + vibration filters | 8 kHz | 125 µs | — |
| Velocity loop | 4 kHz | 250 µs | ~100–150 Hz |
| Attitude fusion + angle loop | 1 kHz | 1 ms | ~15–30 Hz |

**Bandwidth separation rule:** each outer loop should have a bandwidth of at most 1/5 to 1/10 of the inner loop's. This lets the inner loop be modelled as unity gain when tuning the outer loop.

## 2. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 2.1 Electrical angle

$$
\theta_e = \big(p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}})\big) \bmod 2\pi,\qquad p = 7
$$

θ_off is found at calibration: drive v_d = V_align at θ_e = 0, let the rotor settle, then record θ_m.

### 2.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, 2 shunts, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

### 2.3 Park transform

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

### 2.4 Plant model (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

with ω_e = p ω
