## 1. Loss Model, Per ESC Channel

### 1.1 Conduction losses

In 6-step synchronous mode, during each 60° sector:
- One leg's high-side (HS) switches at duty D.
- That same leg's low-side (LS) conducts synchronously for 1 − D.
- Another leg's LS is fully on.

Phase current I flows through exactly two channel resistances at all times:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{hot}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{hot} = 2\,I^2 R_{hot}$$

$$R_{hot} = k_T R_{25} = 1.5 \times 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega = 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.0018 = \mathbf{23.0\ W}$$

Notes:
- The result is independent of D, so it holds at any throttle where 80 A flows.
- The loss rotates across the 6 FETs. The average per FET is ≈ 3.8 W.
- At peak, a single LS FET dissipates I²R = 11.5 W for 120° of the electrical cycle. Electrical frequency is kHz-range, far shorter than the junction thermal time constant (~ms), so the average dominates.
- For sinusoidal/FOC drive, replace with $P = 3\,I_{rms}^2 R_{hot}$ with $I_{rms} = I_{pk}/\sqrt2$.

### 1.2 Switching losses at 48 kHz

There is one hard-switched commutation pair per PWM period.

**Overlap (V–I crossover):**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery** (LS body diode recovers when HS turns on):

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

**Coss charge** (both devices in the leg):

$$P_{oss} = 2 \times \tfrac12 C_{oss,eq} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 1.5\text{n} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} = 0.046\ \text{W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two edges per period):

$$P_{dt} = V_F\, I\, (2t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 400\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$

**Gate drive** (dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not the FETs):

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 40\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.038\ \text{W}$$
