**Architectural flags. Resolve these before layout.**

1. **The H743 should not commutate the motors directly.** ArduPilot has no field-oriented or 6-step motor-commutation firmware for the flight-controller MCU. The standard architecture uses four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). The H743 drives them over bidirectional DShot600. The netlist below follows this architecture.
2. **80 A per channel on a nano board is burst-only.** §3 shows sustained 80 A is thermally impossible at this size. A realistic burst rating is about 2 s.
3. **The battery-side system must also support 320 A.** An XT60 connector is rated for roughly 60 A continuous. At this current, the battery C-rating, the battery leads, and the pad-to-plane current density become the limiting factors, not the FETs.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

In each of the six commutation sectors, two phases are active. The "PWM leg" alternates between its high-side FET (for duty cycle D) and its low-side FET (for 1−D). The "return leg" low-side FET stays on for the whole sector.

As a result, two FETs are always in series with the motor current. Ignoring ripple, the per-channel conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond} = 2\, I_{rms}^2 \, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

The on-resistance rises with junction temperature:

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$
