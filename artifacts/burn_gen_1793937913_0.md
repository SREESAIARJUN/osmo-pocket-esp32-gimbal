# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC Stack (Job #28): Architecture & Analysis Package

## 0. Scope Corrections and Assumptions

Three issues change the architecture, so they come first.

1. **The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC MCU.** Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. That is typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071, at about 72 MHz with 48 kHz PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600.
2. **80 A is only a burst rating on a nano board.** It cannot be sustained, and Section 3.3 quantifies the burst window. Any datasheet claim of "80 A" should state the duration and the number of motors loaded.
3. **The parameters below are representative, not from a specific part.** Final numbers must be recomputed against the selected MOSFET datasheet.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × (1 + 0.006 × 75) = **1.45 mΩ**

### 1.2 Current path

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, the phase current always flows through exactly two MOSFETs in series:
- one device in the PWM leg (the high side during ON, the low side during OFF), and
- one statically ON device in the return leg.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

At I = 80 A:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.45\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{18.6\ W\ per\ channel}$$

### 1.3 Thermal–electrical feedback

R_DS rises with junction temperature, which raises loss, which raises temperature. Solve iteratively:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{board} + P_{FET}(T_j^{(n)})\cdot R_{\theta,J\text{-}board}$$

The loop converges if

$$\alpha \, P_{FET} \, R_{\theta} < 1$$

With P_FET ≈ 4 W and R_θ ≈ 3 K/W, the loop gain is 0.072, so it converges with no local thermal runaway. Runaway risk exists at the board level, not the die level (Section 3).

### 1.4 Parasitic copper and shunt

| Element | Resistance | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pads, vias, and internal planes | ≈ 0.5 mΩ effective | 3.2 W per channel |
| Single shared shunt, 0.25 mΩ, at 4 × 80 A = 320 A | 0.25 mΩ | 25.6 W |

The shared-shunt loss is prohibitive. **Use per-channel phase or low-side shunts** (0.5 mΩ, about 3.2 W each at burst), or use R_DS-sensing.
