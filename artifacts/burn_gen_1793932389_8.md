## 0. Architecture and Feasibility Flags

The requirement as worded contains three conflicts. Each is resolved here before the math, because they change the model.

| # | Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | The H743 cannot run four 48 kHz FOC/6-step commutation loops while also running ArduPilot. ArduPilot has no ESC firmware path, and 24 complementary gate outputs exceed the pin and timer budget. | **Split architecture.** The H743 is the flight controller and emits bi-dir DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own MCU (AT32F421 running AM32), which does the 48 kHz switching. This is the industry-standard 4-in-1 topology. |
| F2 | A "nano" (20×20 mm) board cannot dissipate the losses of 4×80 A. As shown in §3, it is about 160 W. | The FC is 20×20 mm (M2). The ESC is a 30.5×30.5 mm mount on a 36×36 mm board. Even then, **80 A is a burst rating of about 2 s**, not continuous. |
| F3 | 4 × 80 A = 320 A from the pack, about 8 kW at 6S. | XT90 connector, 10 AWG leads, and a 0.1 mΩ total shunt. The continuous rating is set separately at about 45 A per channel (see §3.4). |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{ds} = 2.0 \times (1 + 0.0055 \times 85) = 2.94\ \text{m}\Omega \approx 3.0\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step synchronous PWM

In any 60° sector, phase A is the PWM'd high side and phase B is the low side held on. During the high-side on-time (D), current flows through Q_AH and Q_BL. During the off-time (1−D), it freewheels through Q_AL (synchronous rectification) and Q_BL.

Therefore, at any instant, exactly two channels carry the full phase current I:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = I^2 R_{ds}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

The result is **independent of duty**, which is a useful property of synchronous rectification. The dead-time diode term is handled separately in §2.4.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 3.0\times10^{-3} = 38.4\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{cond,total} = 4 \times 38.4 = 153.6\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution

Over one electrical revolution, each low-side FET is held on for 120° and acts as the sync rectifier for another 120°×(1−D). The worst-case FET, at low D (high current, low speed, i.e. the punch-out start), is a low side:

$$P_{FET,max} \approx I^2 R_{ds}\left[\tfrac{1}{3} + \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)\right] \xrightarrow{D\to 0.3} 6400 \times 0.003 \times 0.567 = 10.9\ \text{W}$$

**FOC note:** for sinusoidal current with peak Î, use I_rms = Î/√2 per phase and P_cond ≈ 3·I_rms²·R_ds.
