## 0. Architecture Decisions and Stated Assumptions

Two points determine everything that follows.

**1. The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** Four 3-phase bridges need 12 complementary PWM pairs plus back-EMF zero-cross sensing. The H743 has only two advanced timers (TIM1/TIM8, 3 complementary pairs each), and ArduPilot has no native sensorless motor-commutation path. The architecture is therefore:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──BDShot600 ×4──► 4× AT32F421 (AM32) ──► 4× 3-ph gate driver ──► 24× MOSFET
```

**2. "80A burst" on a nano board is pulse-limited.** The math below shows it is a seconds-scale rating, not a continuous one. The numbers make this explicit rather than hiding it.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

In any commutation sector, one phase's high-side switch PWMs at duty D. Its complementary low side freewheels at (1−D), and a second phase's low side stays fully on. The sector current therefore always flows through exactly two channels:

$$
P_{cond}(D) = \underbrace{D\,I^2 R_{HS}}_{\text{HS on}} + \underbrace{(1-D)\,I^2 R_{LS}}_{\text{sync. rect.}} + \underbrace{I^2 R_{LS}}_{\text{return leg}}
$$

With matched devices (R_HS = R_LS = R):

$$
\boxed{P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)} \qquad \text{(independent of } D\text{)}
$$

The temperature dependence of R_ds(on) is modelled as:

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds(25)}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1} \;\Rightarrow\; k_T(125\,°\text{C}) = 1.6
$$

### 1.2 Copper and PCB parasitic resistance

Bridge-to-pad trace and via resistance is significant at this scale. For one layer:

$$

$$
