# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Loss Model, Thermal Budget, Bidirectional DShot Allocation, and Netlist

**Scope note:** I have no prior project context for "Job #69," so every component value below is a stated assumption you can replace with your BOM. The equations stay valid when you substitute your own values.

## 0. Feasibility Findings

1. **The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In a 4-in-1 ESC, each channel (or the whole board) has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 sends DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. The architecture below reflects that.
2. **80 A per channel is not sustainable on a 20×20 mm nano board.** The model below gives about **90–100 W total dissipation** with all four channels at 80 A. Adiabatic heating then limits the burst to about **1–1.5 s** with all four channels loaded, or about 3–4 s for a single channel.
3. **The input capacitor ripple current (~80 A RMS worst case) cannot be handled by on-board MLCCs.** A low-ESR external electrolytic at the pigtail is mandatory.

Treat "80 A" as a **≤1 s burst rating** and expect a continuous rating of roughly 35–45 A per channel with airflow.
