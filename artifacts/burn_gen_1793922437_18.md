## 0. System Architecture Decision

The STM32H743 should **not** commutate the motors directly. A 400–480 MHz H7 running ArduPilot is the flight controller. Commutation belongs on dedicated ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32:

```
          ┌──────────────────────── STM32H743VIT6 (ArduPilot) ───────────────────────┐
          │ IMU SPI1 | OSD SPI2 | Baro I2C | SD SDMMC1 | UARTs | ADC (VBAT, CURR)    │
          │ TIM3_CH3/CH4 + TIM5_CH1/CH2 → Bidirectional DShot600 (M1..M4)            │
          └────────┬───────────────┬───────────────┬───────────────┬─────────────────┘
                   │ DSHOT1        │ DSHOT2        │ DSHOT3        │ DSHOT4
              ┌────▼────┐     ┌────▼────┐     ┌────▼────┐     ┌────▼────┐
              │AT32F421 │     │AT32F421 │     │AT32F421 │     │AT32F421 │   AM32, 48 kHz PWM
              │ ESC MCU │     │ ESC MCU │     │ ESC MCU │     │ ESC MCU │
              └────┬────┘     └────┬────┘     └────┬────┘     └────┬────┘
               FD6288Q          FD6288Q          FD6288Q          FD6288Q    3-phase gate driver
               6× MOSFET        6× MOSFET        6× MOSFET        6× MOSFET  40 V, ~0.7 mΩ
```

**Electrical envelope:**
- 6S LiPo
- V_bus,max = 25.2 V
- Design V_bus = 25.2 V, which is the worst case for switching loss
- 40 V FETs provide about 60% derating against ringing

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Topology insight

With 6-step trapezoidal commutation and **synchronous (complementary) PWM**, at every instant exactly one high-side path and one low-side path carry the phase current I:

- During PWM-ON, the current flows through HS(A) and LS(B).
- During PWM-OFF, it freewheels through LS(A), which is the synchronous rectifier, and LS(B).

Either way, two channels conduct the full current. So, independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 2.2 Temperature-corrected resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

At Tj = 125 °C: R = 0.70 × (1 + 0.45) = **1.015 mΩ**

### 2.3 Per-FET distribution

Over one electrical cycle, each FET position conducts for 1/3 of the cycle, weighted by duty D for the high side and (1 − D) for the low-side synchronous rectifier:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R\,D \qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R\,(1-D) + \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R$$

The low side carries the extra term because of its fixed-on commutation third. **The low-side FETs are the hottest at low duty / high current**, for example during a punch-out from hover.

### 2.4 Numbers at 80 A, Tj = 125 °C

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.015\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{12.99\ W}$$

Worst single FET (LS, D → 0):

$$\tfrac{2}{3} \times 6400 \times 1.015\text{e-}3 = \mathbf{4.33\ W}$$
