# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC with 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot Architecture (Job #64)

## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

**Architecture correction.** The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs. In every production AIO design, the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits **bi-directional DShot600**. Four dedicated ESC MCUs (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32-class firmware) generate the **48 kHz** six-step PWM and drive 3-phase gate drivers. The model below follows this split.

**Rating reality check.** "80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 / 26×26 mm) board is only defensible as a **seconds-scale transient**. Four channels at 80 A means 320 A of battery current, which no XT30/XT60 nano-class battery path survives. The math in §4 shows roughly 2 s for all four channels and roughly 9 s for a single channel. Continuous capability is about 38 A per channel in prop wash.
