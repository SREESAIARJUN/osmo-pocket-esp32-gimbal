# Job #29: STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC) Design Package

This package covers the electrothermal model, the bi-directional DShot600 timer and DMA plan, the netlists, and the thermal budget.

## 0. Architecture and Key Assumptions

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and drives four ESC MCUs over bi-directional DShot600. Each ESC MCU runs AM32 firmware and controls one 3-phase bridge.

```
 VBAT ─┬─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
       │                                                          │
   [FC: STM32H743VIT6 @480 MHz] ──DShot600 bidir x4──► [ESC MCU AT32F421K8U7 x4]
       │                                                          │
       │                                       FD6288Q 3-ph gate driver x4
       │                                                          │
       │                                         6x N-MOSFET bridge x4 → M1..M4
```

| Parameter | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Input | 3S–6S (V_bus,max = 25.2 V) | Worst-case math is done at 6S |
| "80 A burst" | Per-channel **phase (motor) current**, ≤ 5 s | Industry convention |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), 6-step trapezoidal | AM32 |
| MOSFET (reference) | 40 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3 or 5×6, R_DS(on) = 1.0 mΩ max @ 25 °C, Q_g ≈ 60 nC, Q_gd ≈ 10 nC, Q_rr ≈ 40 nC | Substitute your selected part's datasheet values |
| Form factor | 30.5 × 30.5 mm stack | See the warning below |

> ⚠️ **Engineering flags (read first)**
> 1. **6S on 40 V FETs is marginal.** A 25.2 V bus plus L·di/dt ringing routinely exceeds 35 V. The board needs tight commutation loops and TVS clamping, or 60 V parts. Moving to 60 V parts roughly increases R_DS(on) by 1.5×, which scales the conduction numbers below.
> 2. **A true 20×20 "nano" cannot support 4 × 80 A**, even for bursts (see §3.4). The numbers below assume a 30×30 board. I give the 20×20 result for comparison.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM
During any commutation sector, phase A is PWM'd and phase B is held low:

- **PWM on (duty D):** current flows VBAT → HS_A → motor → LS_B → GND
- **PWM off (1−D):** current freewheels LS_A (sync rectified) → motor → LS_B

At every instant, the phase current I_ph passes through **exactly two conducting channels**. The bridge conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2 \, R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph,rms}^2 R_{Cu}$$

With trapezoidal currents and a well-matched motor, I_rms ≈ I_ph, so ripple is ignored. You can add a ripple term ΔI²/12 if the motor inductance is low.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ /\text{K}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × 1.6 = **1.6 mΩ**.

Copper path (pours, vias, solder pads, per channel): R_Cu ≈ **0.4 mΩ**. Use 2 oz outer layers and ≥ 2 oz inner planes.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A burst

| Term | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFETs | 2 × 80² × 1.6 mΩ | 20.48 W |
| Copper | 80² × 0.4 mΩ | 2.56 W |
| **P_cond per channel** | | **23.0 W** |

### 1.4 Distribution per device
The bridge has 6 FETs per channel. The losses spread unevenly:

- **Held low-side FET:** conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle at full current.
  - P = I²R/3 = 3.41 W average.
  - Peak instantaneous dissipation is 10.2 W.
- **PWM-leg FETs:** HS and LS share the remaining load in proportion to D and (1−D).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

### 2.1 Hard-switched high side (V–I overlap)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

Rise and fall times are set by the Miller plateau, t ≈ Q_gd / I_g. The FD6288Q sources about 1.5 A and sinks about 1.8 A, but the gate resistor limits this. With R_G = 4.7 Ω, take t_r ≈ t_f ≈ 20 ns.

$$P_{VI} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction (two transitions per period)

$$P_{dt} = V_F\, I_{ph}\, (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

The dead time is set by AM32 (`DEAD_TIME`, ~100 ns). Do not lower it below the measured driver plus FET delay skew.

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{ nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{ k} = \mathbf{0.05\ W}$$

### 2.4 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss,tr} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 1.6\text{ nF} \times 635 \times 48\text{ k} = \mathbf{0.02\ W}$$
