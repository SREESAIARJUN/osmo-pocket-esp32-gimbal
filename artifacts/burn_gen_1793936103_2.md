## 0. Baseline Assumptions

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class, single-precision FPU, ≥170 MHz |
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 2–5 mH |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701), SPI |
| Current sensing | 2-shunt or inline, sampled at PWM center |
| IMU | 2× ICM-42688-P, SPI at 24 MHz, ODR 8 kHz, ±2000 dps, ±16 g |
| PWM | 25 kHz center-aligned |

**Loop rates**

| Loop | Rate | Target bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 25 kHz | ≈ 1.5–2 kHz |
| Velocity | 5 kHz | ≈ 150–250 Hz |
| Angle / stabilization | 1 kHz | ≈ 20–40 Hz |
| IMU fusion | 8 kHz in → 1 kHz attitude | — |

Each loop should be at least 5–10× faster in bandwidth than the loop outside it.

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park** is the transpose of the matrix above.

**Latency compensation.** Rotate the angle used by inverse Park forward to account for one PWM period plus half a period of zero-order hold:

$$
\theta_{e,\text{out}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_{pwm}
$$

### 1.2 Plant model in the dq frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

**Torque:**

$$
\tau = \tfrac32 p\,\lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$

This is for a surface-mount PMSM, so the reference is i_d* = 0.

### 1.3 PI design by pole–zero cancellation

Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole R/L. The closed loop then becomes first order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c\quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

Pick ω_c ≤ 2π·f_pwm/15 to keep phase margin despite the ≈1.5 T_pwm delay.

**Decoupling feedforward:**

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

### 1.4 Voltage limiting and SVPWM

**Circular voltage limit.** Saturate in dq with priority to v_d:

$$
|v_{dq}| \le V_{bus}/\sqrt3
$$

**Min-max SVPWM** (equivalent to symmetric SVPWM). Compute the inverse-Clarke phase voltages, then subtract the common-mode offset:

$$
v_{0} = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_a,v_b,v_c)+\min(v_a,v_b,v_c)\big),\qquad
D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

## 2. Velocity Estimation (PLL Observer)

Differentiating a 14-bit encoder at 5 kHz produces quantization noise of 2π/16384 · 5000 ≈ 1.9 rad/s, which is unusable. Use a type-II PLL instead.

**Error and state updates:**

$$
e_k = \operatorname{wrap}(\theta_{meas,k} - \hat\theta_{k})
$$

$$
\hat\omega_{k+1} = \hat\omega_k + K_i T e_k,\qquad
\hat\theta_{k+1} = \hat\theta_k + T(\hat\omega_k + K_p e_k)
$$

**Gains:**

$$
K_p = 2\zeta\omega_n,\qquad K_i=\omega_n^2,\qquad \zeta = 0.707
$$

Use ω_n ≈ 2π·(3–5)× the velocity-loop bandwidth.

**Acceleration feedforward (optional).** Add T·τ_cmd/J into the ω̂ update to remove PLL lag during transients.

## 3. Cascaded Angle → Velocity Control

### 3.1 Structure

```
θ* ──►(+)──► C_θ ──►(+)──► C_ω ──►(+)──► i_q* ──► current loop ──► motor
       ▲−            ▲  ▲−           ▲  ▲
       θ̂             │  ω̂           │  └─ i_cog,ff(θ_m)   (Section 4)
                     │               └──── J·α*/K_t        (accel FF)
                     └─ ω_ff = ω* − ω_base (gyro base-rate feedforward)
```

For a stabilized gimbal, the "angle" is the **camera attitude error** from the IMU fusion (§5), projected onto each motor axis. The velocity feedback is the **IMU gyro rate** about that axis. The encoder is used only for commutation, cogging compensation, and as a fallback.
