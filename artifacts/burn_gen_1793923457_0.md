## 1. FOC Current Loop (Innermost, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle is computed from the mechanical angle and a calibrated zero offset:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{m,0}) \bmod 2\pi$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant Model and PI Design

The PMSM voltage equations in the dq frame are:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L \dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

With decoupling feedforward, the plant reduces to $G_i(s) = 1/(Ls + R)$.

**Pole-zero cancellation design.** Choose $K_i/K_p = R/L$, which cancels the electrical pole. The closed loop then becomes first-order with bandwidth $\omega_c$:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10}$$

For 20 kHz PWM, use $\omega_c \approx 2\pi \cdot 1.5\text{ kHz}$.

**Decoupling feedforward:**

$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m,\qquad \lambda_m = \frac{2K_t}{3p}$$
