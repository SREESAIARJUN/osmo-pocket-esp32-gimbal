## 0. Architecture Decision and Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning

The H743 does **not** commutate the motors directly. Driving four BLDC bridges directly would require 12 complementary PWM pairs (24 outputs). TIM1 and TIM8 together provide only 6 complementary pairs, and HRTIM would be needed for the rest, with no field-proven firmware. The board therefore uses the industry-standard split:

```
[STM32H743 FC: ArduPilot] --BDShot600 x4--> [4x ESC MCU: AM32 / BLHeli_32-class]
                                              --> [4x 3-ph gate driver] --> [24x N-MOSFET]
```

- **Controller:** STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100) or VIT6 (LQFP100).
- **ESC MCU:** AT32F421 class running AM32. Its pin mapping comes from the chosen AM32 target file.
- **Gate driver:** FD6288Q-class 3-phase driver with integrated bootstrap diodes, 250 V, about 1.5 A source / 1.8 A sink.
