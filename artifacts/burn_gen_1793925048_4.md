## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms
Use the amplitude-invariant convention, with electrical angle θₑ = p·θₘ − θ_offset.

**Clarke:**
  i_α = i_a
  i_β = (i_a + 2·i_b)/√3

**Park:**
  i_d =  i_α cos θₑ + i_β sin θₑ
  i_q = −i_α sin θₑ + i_β cos θₑ

**Inverse Park:**
  v_α = v_d cos θₑ − v_q sin θₑ
  v_β = v_d sin θₑ + v_q cos θₑ

### 1.2 Plant and PI Design (Pole-Zero Cancellation)
The dq voltage equations are:

  v_d = R·i_d + L·di_d/dt − ωₑ·L·i_q
  v_q = R·i_q + L·di_q/dt + ωₑ·L·i_d + ωₑ·λ_m

After decoupling feedforward (v_d,ff = −ωₑ·L·i_q, v_q,ff = ωₑ·L·i_d + ωₑ·λ_m), each axis reduces to G(s) = 1/(Ls + R).
