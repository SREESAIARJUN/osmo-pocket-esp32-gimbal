## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Definitions

| Symbol | Meaning | Reference value |
|---|---|---|
| I_ph | Motor phase current (burst) | 80 A |
| V_bus | Bus voltage | 25.2 V (6S full) |
| R_ds25 | R_DS(on) at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | 1.0 mΩ |
| α | R_DS(on) temperature coefficient | ≈ 0.0045 /K |
| R_cu | Copper path per conducting FET (pad + plane) | 0.15 mΩ |
| D | PWM duty | 0–1 |

Temperature dependence:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C this gives R_ds ≈ 1.45 mΩ. Adding R_cu gives an effective **R_eff ≈ 1.6 mΩ**.

### 2.2 Trapezoidal six-step with synchronous rectification

At any instant, exactly two half-bridges carry current:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high-side FET conducts for D·T_sw. The synchronous low-side FET conducts for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Leg B (return leg):** the low-side FET is held on for the full 60° sector.

So at every instant the current passes through exactly two channel resistances, regardless of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{eff}$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.6\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{20.5\ W}$$

### 2.3 Per-FET distribution (needed for junction rise)

Over one electrical revolution, each FET is active for 1/3 of the cycle:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R_{eff}\left(D + \tfrac{1}{2}\right),\qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R_{eff}\left(\tfrac{3}{2} - D\right)$$

| Case | Per-FET conduction |
|---|---|
| D = 1 (full throttle burst) | P_HS = P_LS ≈ **5.1 W** (worst-case loading) |
| D = 0.5 | HS ≈ 3.4 W, LS ≈ 6.8 W. **Low-side FETs run hotter at partial throttle.** |

### 2.4 Bus-side conduction (shared)

$$I_{bus} \approx D \cdot I_{ph} \cdot N_{motors}$$

At full burst, I_bus ≈ 320 A peak demand. This is unrealistic for a sustained period. Battery sag will cap it, but the copper must be sized for it:

- Battery pad-to-plane resistance must be ≤ 0.2 mΩ. At 320 A that already dissipates 20 W.
- **Requirement:** 2 oz inner planes (≥ 4 layers dedicated to V_bus/GND), plus direct-solder battery pads.
