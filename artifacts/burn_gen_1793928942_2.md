## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix},\qquad \theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}$$

**Inverse Park** is the transpose of the Park matrix.

### 1.2 Plant in the dq frame

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

### 1.3 Current PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

**Decoupled voltage commands:**

$$v_d = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

After decoupling, the plant becomes 1/(Ls+R). Placing the PI zero on the plant pole (pole-zero cancellation) gives:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}\quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{I}{I^*} = \frac{\omega_{ci}}{s+\omega_{ci}}$$

**Choosing the bandwidth.** Use ω_ci ≤ 2π·f_pwm/20. With computation delay (1.5·T_s for the PWM update), the phase margin is:

$$PM \approx 90^\circ - \omega_{ci}\cdot 1.5T_s\cdot\frac{180}{\pi}$$

At f_s = 20 kHz and ω_ci = 2π·1 kHz, PM ≈ 63°.

**Voltage limiting.** Use circular saturation:

$$|v_{dq}| \le V_{dc}/\sqrt3$$

d-axis priority is applied, and the PI uses back-calculation anti-windup.

### 1.4 SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)

$$v_x' = v_x - \tfrac12\left(\max(v_a,v_b,v_c) + \min(v_a,v_b,v_c)\right)$$
$$d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x'}{V_{dc}}$$
