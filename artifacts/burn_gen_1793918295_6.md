# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Electrothermal and DShot Architecture (Job #24)

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

Some framing corrections come first, because they change the math.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In a production AIO "nano" board, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600. Four dedicated ESC MCUs run AM32 and do the 6-step commutation, the 48 kHz PWM and the BEMF zero-cross detection. The ESC MCU in this design is the AT32F421K8U7 (120 MHz). Driving 12 half-bridges with BEMF sensing directly from the H743 is possible but not ArduPilot-supported.
2. **80 A is a burst rating only.** With four motors at 80 A, the board carries 320 A total. A nano board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) cannot dissipate the resulting ~65 W steadily. The analysis therefore uses a transient (heat-capacity) model.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

At every instant, the motor current flows through exactly two MOSFETs:

- **PWM ON:** high-side of phase A, then low-side of phase B.
- **PWM OFF (synchronous freewheel):** low-side of phase A, then low-side of phase B.

Total bridge conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle (excluding dead time):

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)\cdot(1 - 2t_{dt}f_{sw})$$

Temperature dependence:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 0.70 × 1.55 = **1.085 mΩ**.

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2(6400)(1.085\times10^{-3})(1-0.0096) = \mathbf{13.75\ W}$$

### 1.2 Per-FET distribution (hottest device)

Each phase is active for 4 of the 6 commutation sectors. The high-side MOSFET of the PWM-switched phase carries I²R·D during its 2 sectors. Near full throttle (D ≈ 0.95), over one electrical cycle:

$$P_{HS,avg} \approx \tfrac{2}{6}\,I^2 R\,D = \tfrac{1}{3}(6.94)(0.95) = 2.20\ W$$

Instantaneous dissipation within a sector is about 6.9 W. This value matters for Z_θJC at low eRPM, where sectors last longer than the device's thermal time constant.

### 1.3 Copper and interconnect conduction

| Path | Resistance (estimate) | Loss per ESC @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pad to FET, 2 oz × 4 layers | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| Shunt (if fitted, 0.25 mΩ) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| Shared bus planes (carry 320 A) | 0.05 mΩ | 5.1 W total → 1.28 W per channel |

The battery lead and connector are a hard system limit, not just a loss term. An XT60 is rated about 60 A continuous. A 320 A burst requires XT90-class connectors and 10 AWG or heavier leads, or a pad-solder battery connection.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Hard-switching transitions (high-side of the PWM phase)

The low-side commutates softly; its body diode conducts during dead time. Only the high-side sees V·I overlap.

Gate currents through the Miller plateau:

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{src}+R_g} = \frac{6.5}{5.2} = 1.25\ A$$

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{snk}+R_g} = \frac{3.5}{4.2} = 0.83\ A$$

Voltage transition times:

$$t_{v,on} = Q_{gd}/I_{g,on} = 9.6\ ns,\qquad t_{v,off} = Q_{gd}/I_{g,off} = 14.4\ ns$$
