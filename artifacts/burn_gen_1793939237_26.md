## 0. Architecture Clarification and Critical Findings

### 0.1 Partitioning
The STM32H743 is the **flight controller (FC)** running ArduPilot. ArduPilot does not perform motor commutation, so each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. This document assumes:

| Block | Device (reference) | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) or VIH6 (TFBGA100 for nano) | ArduPilot, bi-directional DShot600 master |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6 (QFN28) | AM32 firmware, 6-step commutation, BEMF sensing |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q-class 3-phase half-bridge driver | Bootstrap HS drive, ~1.0–1.5 A source/sink |
| MOSFET ×24 | 40 V N-ch, DFN3.3×3.3 (parameters in §1.1) | Power stage |
| Form factor | 20×20 mm mounting (≈25×25 mm board), 8-layer, 2 oz inner / 3 oz outer | "Nano" |

### 0.2 Critical Finding: 80 A Burst Is Duration-Limited
On a 20×20 board the **steady-state thermal resistance to ambient is about 10–15 K/W**. All four channels at 80 A dissipate about **110 W** (derived below). The design can only sustain 80 A as a **thermal-capacitance-limited transient of roughly 1–4 s**, depending on how many channels are loaded. Total battery current at full burst is 320 A (about 8 kW at 6S). That figure is not realistic for battery leads or connectors on a nano stack. The spec should be rewritten as:

> **80 A per channel, ≤2 s, single-channel burst; 4-channel simultaneous burst ≤1 s; continuous rating set by §3.4.**

### 1.2 Commutation Model (6-step, complementary/synchronous PWM)
- At any instant, **one high-side (HS) and one low-side (LS) FET carry I_phase**. With synchronous rectification, the complementary LS of the PWM'd leg carries the current during the off-time instead of the diode.
- Each FET is active for 120° electrical out of 360°, so its duty is 1/3 of the period. Within that window, conduction is shared between HS and LS of the PWM leg in a D : (1−D) ratio.
