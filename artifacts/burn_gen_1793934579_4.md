# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Reference Architecture Package

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I don't have access to prior Job #58 documents, so this package is built from stated assumptions. Each one is flagged so you can replace it with your actual BOM values and rerun the math. Treat the netlists as reference designs. They must pass ERC/DRC, a SPICE review of the gate loop, and thermal-chamber validation before fab release.

**Architectural note:** The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU (assumed AT32F421 running AM32), and that MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. This split is standard. The H743 lacks enough advanced-control timers and ADC sequencing headroom to run 4× sensorless FOC/6-step alongside ArduPilot's scheduler.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance
$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$
At T_j = 110 °C: R_DS ≈ 1.5 mΩ × 1.51 ≈ **2.27 mΩ**

### 1.2 Per-FET conduction loss
In 6-step commutation, each phase leg conducts for 120° of every 360° electrical. At the burst throttle (D → 1), the conducting high-side FET carries I_pk almost continuously during its 120° window. With synchronous rectification, the complementary low-side FET carries the current during (1−D).

High-side FET (active leg):
$$P_{cond,HS} = I_{pk}^2 \cdot R_{DS}(T_j) \cdot D \cdot \tfrac{1}{3}$$
Low-side FET (return leg, always on during its 120° window):
$$P_{cond,LS} = I_{pk}^2 \cdot R_{DS}(T_j) \cdot \tfrac{1}{3}$$
Synchronous rectification in the PWM'd leg contributes:
$$P_{cond,SR} = I_{pk}^2 \cdot R_{DS}(T_j)\,(1-D)\cdot\tfrac{1}{3}$$

At D ≈ 1, two FETs carry I_pk at every instant, so the per-channel total is:
$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{pk}^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2 (80)^2 (2.27\times10^{-3}) \approx \mathbf{29.1\ W}$$
Per FET (averaged over the electrical cycle): 29.1 / 6 ≈ **4.85 W**

Four channels: **≈116 W conduction.**

### 1.3 Parasitic copper and shunt losses
| Element | R | Loss @ 80A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pad + trace (per phase, 2 oz, ~0.15 mΩ) | 0.15 mΩ | 2 × 0.96 W per ch |
| Current shunt (shared, 0.25 mΩ, sees ΣI_dc) | 0.25 mΩ | depends on battery current |
| Battery pad → bus plane (0.1 mΩ) | 0.1 mΩ | I_bat² × 0.1 mΩ |

Battery current at burst ≈ ΣI_ph × D_eff. At full throttle with 4×80A, I_bat ≈ 300+ A. **Shunt and bus losses scale with I_bat², not I_ph²,** which is why many nano designs omit the shunt or use a Hall sensor (e.g., a 0.1 mΩ-equivalent ACS-class device) at this current level.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd FET in each active leg hard-switches. At D ≈ 1 this loss falls toward zero, so **worst-case switching loss occurs at partial throttle with high current** (e.g., hard punch-outs at D ≈ 0.6–0.8).

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap
$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{pk} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$
$$= 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Output capacitance
$$P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2} C_{oss(er)} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 1\text{ nF} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} = 0.015\ W$$

### 2.3 Body-diode reverse recovery
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{ nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.036\ W$$
Note: Qrr is current- and di/dt-dependent. At 80 A, datasheet Qrr may be 2–3× the nominal value.

### 2.4 Dead-time diode conduction
$$P_{dt} = V_f I_{pk} (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\text{ ns} \times 48\text{k} = 0.31\ W$$

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated in driver plus gate resistors, not the FET)
$$P_g = Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 50\text{ nC} \times 10\text{ V} \times 48\text{k} = 0.024\ W \text{ per FET}$$

### 2.6 Switching summary per channel (worst case, partial throttle)
| Term | Loss |
|---|---|
| V·I overlap | 1.94 W |
| Coss | 0.02 W |
| Qrr | 0.04–0.11 W |
| Dead time | 0.31 W |
| **Total** | **≈2.3–2.4 W/ch → ~9.5 W for 4 ch** |

Switching loss is under 10 % of conduction loss. **The 80A burst rating is a conduction/thermal-mass problem, not a switching problem.** Raising f_sw to 96 kHz would add only about 9 W total.

### 2.7 Gate-loop sanity check
$$t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gd} + Q_{gs2}}{I_{drive}},\quad I_{drive} = \frac{V_{DRV} - V_{plateau}}{R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int} + R_{drv}}$$
With V_DRV = 10 V, V_plateau ≈ 3.5 V, R_g,ext = 2.2 Ω, R_g,int = 1 Ω, R_drv ≈ 4 Ω, I_drive ≈ 0.9 A. With Q_gd + Q_gs2 ≈ 18 nC, t_r ≈ 20 ns, which is consistent with the assumption. Verify Miller-induced turn-on of the low-side FET: dV/dt ≈ 25.2 V / 20
