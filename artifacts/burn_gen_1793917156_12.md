**Document: Job #6, Rev A (Design Review Draft)**

## 0. Executive Summary and Design Review Flags

Before the math, three findings determine everything downstream.

| # | Finding | Severity |
|---|---------|----------|
| F1 | **The STM32H743 cannot commutate the motors.** A 4-in-1 ESC needs a dedicated MCU per channel (or one MCU per 2 channels on some designs) running AM32, BLHeli_32 or Bluejay. The H743 runs ArduPilot and *sends* DShot to those ESC MCUs. The architecture below uses 1× H743 (FC) plus 4× STM32G071 (ESC, AM32). | Architectural |
| F2 | **An 80 A burst per channel on a "nano" (20×20 mm) footprint is not thermally viable** for more than about 1–3
