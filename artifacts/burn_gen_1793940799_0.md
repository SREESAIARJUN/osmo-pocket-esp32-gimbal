## 0. Architecture and Key Constraints

**Topology.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does **not** commutate the motors. Each of the four motor channels has its own ESC MCU (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32-class firmware) and a 3-phase gate driver driving six N-MOSFETs. The H743 talks to each ESC MCU over bi-directional DShot600.

**Feasibility finding.** Read this before the math.
- The numbers below show that **80 A per motor is a burst rating of about 10 s** on a 30.5 × 30.5 mm board with a small heatsink.
- Continuous capability is **about 35 A per motor**.
- A true "nano" (20 × 20 mm) board cannot reach 80 A burst with single 5 × 6 mm FETs. That form factor should be re-specified or the burst duration and current derated.
