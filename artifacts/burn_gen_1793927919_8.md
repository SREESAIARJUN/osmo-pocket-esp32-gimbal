# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #59): System Architecture & Mathematical Model

This is an **engineering reference model**, not a fab-ready release. The numbers depend on the MOSFET, copper stack, and airflow assumptions stated in §1. Bench validation is required before you commit to an 80 A rating.

---

## 0. Architectural Clarifications

These shape every calculation that follows.

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 and sends DShot600 commands. Each ESC channel has its own MCU that does the 48 kHz PWM, commutation, and bidirectional telemetry. I've specified the **AT32F421K8U7 running AM32**, which is commonly used, open source, and supports 48 kHz and bidirectional DShot.
2. **"80 A burst" means per-channel phase current.** At full throttle, battery current approaches phase current, so four channels bursting together means about **320 A at the battery**. That is physically unrealistic for a nano-format board with an XT30/XT60 lead. The thermal model (§3) shows the all-four-motor burst window is only a few seconds.
3. **Bus voltage.** The design targets 6S (25.2 V max). I use 25.2 V for switching loss, which is the worst case.
