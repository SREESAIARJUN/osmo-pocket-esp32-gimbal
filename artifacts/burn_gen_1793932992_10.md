## 0. Architectural Clarification

Bidirectional DShot implies the following partitioning:

| Domain | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743 (480 MHz, ArduPilot) | Generates DShot600, captures GCR eRPM telemetry |
| ESC (×4) | One small MCU per motor (e.g., AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32) | Commutation, 48 kHz PWM, dead-time |
| Power stage (×4) | 3-phase gate driver (e.g., FD6288Q class) + 6 N-FETs | Inverter |

The H743 does **not** drive gates directly. Running 4×6 FETs with sensorless control on the FC MCU is possible in principle, but it is incompatible with the DShot telemetry loop you specified. The derivations below follow this partition.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Commutation topology

In 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification, two phases carry current at every instant:

- **PWM leg:** HS conducts for D·T_sw; LS conducts for (1−D)·T_sw (active freewheel).
- **Static leg:** LS (or HS) is on for the full 60° sector.
