## 0. Architecture Clarification and Baseline Assumptions

**Where the 48 kHz switching happens.** The STM32H743 does not switch the MOSFETs. It runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 frames. A separate MCU on each ESC channel (or one shared MCU) runs AM32 or BLHeli_32 and generates the 48 kHz PWM.

```
[STM32H743 / ArduPilot] --DShot600 bidir x4--> [ESC MCU x4] --> [3-ph gate driver] --> [6x MOSFET] --> motor
        ^                                            |
        +-------- eRPM telemetry (GCR, same wire) ---+
```

|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max (22.2 V nominal) |

**Feasibility flag.** 80 A per channel on a 30×30 mm or smaller nano 4-in-1 is a short-burst rating only. The thermal model below quantifies this.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation, two phase legs are active at any instant: one high-side (HS) leg and one low-side (LS) leg. With synchronous rectification:

- During the PWM on-time (duty D), current flows HS_x → motor → LS_y.
- During the off-time (1 − D), current freewheels through LS_x (sync-rect) → motor → LS_y.

So at every instant, the motor current passes through exactly two conducting channels, independent of D.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.3 RMS current including ripple

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12},\qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

Example: L_ph = 10 µH line-to-line, D = 0.9, V_bus − V_bemf ≈ 4 V.
- ΔI = 7.5 A, so ΔI²/12 = 4.7 A².
- This is negligible next to 80² = 6400 A². Use I_rms ≈ 80 A.

### 1.4 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2\left(2R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{Cu}\right)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 6400 \times (3.0 + 0.5)\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{22.4\ W}$$

This splits into 19.2 W in the MOSFETs and 3.2 W in the copper.

### 1.5 Per-MOSFET loss (worst case: HS, D → 1)

Each FET is active for 1/3 of the electrical cycle:

$$P_{cond,HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,D\,I^2 R_{DS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1)(6400)(1.5\,\text{m}\Omega) = \mathbf{3.2\ W}$$

At high eRPM the commutation period is short compared with the junction thermal time constant, so averaging over the cycle is valid. At low RPM with high current (stall or hard punch-out from standstill), use the instantaneous 9.6 W and the transient Z_th(t) instead.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

In 6-step operation, only one leg is PWM-switching at a time. All components below are therefore per channel.

### 2.1 V–I overlap loss (hard switching)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}$$

$$P_{VI} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 50\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance (C_oss) loss, both FETs in the leg

$$P_{oss} = 2 \times \tfrac{1}{2}\,C_{oss}\,V_{bus}^2\,f_{sw} = 1.5\,\text{nF} \times 635 \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.05\ W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_f\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated in the driver and R_G, not the FET)

$$P_{gate} = 2\,Q_g\,V_{GS}\,f_{sw} = 2 \times 60\,\text{nC} \times 10 \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$

### 2.6 Switching total

$$P_{sw,ch} \approx 1.94 + 0.06 + 0.05 + 0.61 = \mathbf{2.66\ W}\quad(\text{excluding gate drive})$$
