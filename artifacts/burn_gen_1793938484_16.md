# STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC, 20×20 mm) Design Model: 80 A Burst Thermal, 48 kHz Switching, and Bidirectional DShot600 DMA

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

Two points shape everything below.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In any AIO board the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 to four ESC MCUs. These are typically AT32F421 running AM32, or EFM8BB51 running Bluejay. Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs. The 48 kHz figure is the ESC-side PWM frequency, set in AM32/Bluejay firmware, not by ArduPilot.
2. **80 A per channel on a 20×20 board is a burst rating of about 1 s, not a continuous rating.** The math in §3 shows this. Treat "80 A" as a datasheet headline. The design-limiting number is the continuous rating, roughly 25–30 A per channel.

**Baseline parameters.** All are assumptions to be replaced with your selected parts.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S full) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Phase current, burst | I_ph | 80 A |
| ESC PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET (40 V, DFN3.3×3.3 class) | R_DS(on),25°C | 1.5 mΩ @ V_GS = 10 V |
| R_DS(on) temperature coefficient | k_T | ~1.5× at T_j = 125 °C, giving 2.25 mΩ |

| Ambient | T_a | 40 °C |

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

**Commutation scheme.** With 6-step trapezoidal commutation and complementary (synchronous) PWM, two phases conduct at any instant. Current flows through one high-side or synchronous low-side device on the PWM phase, plus one low-side device on the return phase. Over a full PWM period, two channel-resistances are always in series, regardless of duty cycle.

**Per-channel conduction loss:**

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{pcb}$$

$$= 80^2 (2 \times 2.25\text{ m}\Omega) + 80^2 (0.5\text{ m}\Omega) = 28.8 + 3.2 = \mathbf{32.0\ W}$$

**Per-FET average.** Each phase conducts for 2/3 of the electrical cycle, split between its high side and low side. Each FET therefore averages about 1/3 of the I²R:

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{80^2 \times 2.25\text{ m}\Omega}{3} \approx 4.8\ \text{W}$$

**Temperature coupling.** R_DS(on) depends on T_j, so iterate:

$$R(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At burst, 125 °C is the design point. Using the 25 °C value would understate loss by about 33%.

## 2. Switching-Loss Model at 48 kHz

With complementary PWM, only the high-side device on the PWM phase hard-switches. The low side commutates through its body diode during dead time, then turns on at near-zero V_DS.

**(a) Hard-switching V–I overlap:**

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

**(b) Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two edges per period):

$$P_{dt} = V_f I_{ph} (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**(c) Reverse recovery:**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{ nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{0.036\ W}$$
