## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

The H743 does not commutate motors directly. The standard and recommended topology is:

```
[STM32H743 FC] --DShot600 bidir (4 lines)--> [4x ESC MCU, e.g. AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 or BLHeli_32-class FW]
                                                   |-> 3x half-bridge gate driver -> 6x N-MOSFET per motor
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0055 × 85) = **1.47 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path with synchronous rectification (6-step / trapezoidal)

In any commutation sector, two phases are active:

- **PWM leg:** the high side conducts for D, the low side (synchronous) for (1 − D − 2t_dt·f_sw).
- **Return leg:** the low side conducts 100%.

The phase current therefore always flows through exactly two channels:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Including PWM ripple ΔI (triangular):

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

Ripple estimate, with motor phase-to-phase inductance L ≈ 15 µH and worst case D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}D(1-D)}{L f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \cdot 0.25}{15\mu \cdot 48k} = 8.75\ \text{A}$$

The ripple term ΔI²/12 = 6.4 A², which is negligible against 6400 A².

**Result at 80 A:**

$$P_{cond} = 6406 \times 2 \times 1.47\text{m} = \mathbf{18.8\ W\ per\ motor}$$

### 1.3 Dead-time body-diode loss

$$P_{dt} = V_f \cdot I \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 400\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$

### 1.4 Copper and shunt loss

| Element | Resistance |
|---|---|
| Board phase path (inner planes plus vias) | ≈0.4 mΩ |
| Shunt (shared bus, per motor share) | 0.25 mΩ (one 0.5 mΩ shunt per motor pair, or a 0.25 mΩ shared) |

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.4\text{m} = \mathbf{2.56\ W}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 V–I overlap loss (hard-switched high side)

The gate current during the Miller plateau is

$$I_g = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}{R_{g,int}+R_{g,ext}+R_{drv}}$$

With V_drv = 10 V, V_plateau ≈ 3.5 V, and 1.2 + 3.3 + 2 Ω = 6.5 Ω, I_g ≈ 1.0 A, so

$$t_{r}\approx t_f \approx \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_g}=14\text{ ns}$$
