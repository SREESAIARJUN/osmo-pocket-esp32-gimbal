## 0. Assumptions and Corrections to the Brief

The model is only as good as its assumptions, so these come first.

| # | Item | Position |
|---|------|----------|
| A1 | **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** | The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the FC. Each motor needs a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Example parts are the AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6, giving 4 MCUs, or 2 dual-motor MCUs. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by those MCUs. The H743 only generates and decodes bi-directional DShot600. |
| A2 | "80 A burst" | Interpreted as **80 A per motor, phase current, for ≤ 5 s**, not continuous. Section 3 shows that continuous 80 A is physically impossible on a nano board. |
| A3 | Bus voltage | 6S LiPo: V_bus,max = 25.2 V. Design MOSFET rating is 40 V. |
| A4 | Form factor | 30.5 × 30.5 mm AIO, 8-layer, 2 oz on all layers. A true 20 × 20 board makes Section 3 roughly 2.3× worse. |
| A5 | MOSFET | Representative 40 V, 0.7 mΩ (25 °C) N-ch, PDFN 3.3×3.3 class. Parameters: Q_g ≈ 60 nC, C_oss ≈ 2 nF, Q_rr ≈ 50 nC, R_θJC ≈ 1.2 K/W. Substitute the real datasheet values. |
| A6 | Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) PWM on one leg. Dead-time is 100 ns. |

All numbers below are first-order engineering estimates. They need validation by bench thermography and a calibrated FEA model before release.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 0.7 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.05 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path

In 6-step drive, two phases are always energized. The current therefore always flows through exactly **two conducting devices in series**. During PWM-off it flows through the HS and the synchronous LS of the chopping leg, which are complementary.

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.3 Per-device RMS

Each device conducts for at most 120° of the electrical cycle. The chopped leg's HS/LS share that interval by D and 1−D.

$$I_{RMS,FET,max} = I_{ph}\sqrt{\tfrac{1}{3}} = 80 \times 0.577 = 46.2\ \text{A}$$

$$P_{FET,worst} = 46.2^2 \times 1.05\,\text{m}\Omega = 2.24\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 Totals at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Per motor (6 FETs) | 80² × 2.1 mΩ = **13.44 W** |
| PCB copper (phase + bus traces, ≈ 0.3 mΩ effective) | 1.9 W |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ, bus-side; I_bus ≈ D·I_ph, D≈0.8) | 1.0 W |
| **Per motor conduction** | **≈ 16.3 W** |

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only one leg is hard-switched at any instant. Losses are referenced to that leg.

### 2.1 V–I overlap (hard-switched HS)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2}V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

Rise and fall times are estimated from gate charge with a ~1 A driver: t_r ≈ t_f ≈ 20 ns.

$$P_{VI} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_F\,I_{ph}\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = V_{bus}Q_{rr}f_{sw} = 25.2 \times 50\text{nC} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$

### 2.4 Output capacitance (HS + LS)

$$P_{oss} = 2 \cdot \tfrac{1}{2}C_{oss}V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 2\text{nF} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

This loss is dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FETs.

$$P_g = Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} \times 2 = 60\text{nC} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} \times 2 = \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$

### 2.6 Switching total

**≈ 2.7 W per motor**, plus ≈ 0.2 W ESC MCU/driver quiescent.

> **Sensitivity:** P_VI scales linearly with f_sw. Dropping from 48 kHz to 24 kHz saves ≈ 1.3 W per motor (≈ 5 W per board) at the cost of current ripple and audible/efficiency trade-offs at low throttle. AM32 allows variable PWM frequency, which is worth exploiting.
