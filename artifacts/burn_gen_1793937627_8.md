# STM32H743 Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model (Job #56)

## 0. Architecture Review

Three points need resolving before the math, because they change the design.

| Issue | Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| H743 as the ESC MCU | The H743 can't commutate 4 motors at 48 kHz with BEMF sensing while also running ArduPilot. ArduPilot has no ESC firmware path. | **H743 = flight controller only.** Use 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071 ESC MCUs running AM32. The H743 talks to them over bi-directional DShot600. |
| "Nano" form factor at 80 A | A 20×20 mm board can't carry 80 A sustained. There isn't enough copper area or thermal mass. | Specify **80 A burst at ≤ 2–4 s** on a 30.5×30.5 board (or a split 20×20 stack). Continuous rating is about 35–45 A per channel. Derivation in §2.5. |
| 4 × 80 A simultaneous | That is 320 A from the pack, beyond what most 6S packs and XT60/XT90 connectors can deliver. | Treat 80 A as a **per-channel** burst. Size the bus for about 200 A peak aggregate. |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 85) = **1.38 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

In any 60° sector, current flows through two legs:
- **PWM leg:** the high-side conducts for D·T and the synchronous low-side for (1−D)·T. The current path is always one FET, so the loss is I²R_ds, independent of D.
- **Return leg:** the low-side is on continuously, adding another I²R_ds.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.38\text{ mΩ} = \mathbf{17.7\ W}$$

### 1.3 Copper and PCB trace loss

Assume a 4-layer board with 2 oz copper on all layers and about 0.25 mΩ effective phase-path resistance (pad → via → layer → pad, ×2 phases):

$$P_{Cu,ch} = 80^2 \times 0.5\text{ mΩ} = 3.2\text{ W}$$

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution (for junction temperature)

Over one electrical cycle, each FET conducts 1/3 of the time, as either the PWM device or the continuous low-side. Worst case at full throttle (D → 1) is the low-side FET in continuous mode:

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2 R_{ds} = \tfrac{1}{3}(6400)(0.00138) = 2.94\text{ W}$$

During its conduction window the instantaneous dissipation is **8.8 W**.
