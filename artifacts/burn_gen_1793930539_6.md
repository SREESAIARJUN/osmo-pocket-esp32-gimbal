# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Design Model (Job #64)

## 0. Assumptions and Architecture Notes

I don't have Job #64's requirements document, so this model uses the baseline below. Every equation is parameterized, so you can substitute your actual values.

| Parameter | Symbol | Assumed value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max (22.2 V nominal) |
| Burst phase current (per ESC) | I_pk | 80 A, 10 s |
| Continuous current | I_cont | 55 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET (40 V, PQFN 5×6 class) | R_DS(on),25 | 0.70 mΩ typ / 0.90 mΩ max |
| Temperature coefficient of R_DS(on) | α | ≈ 1.5× at T_j = 125 °C |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 60 nC at 10 V |
| Reverse recovery charge | Q_rr | 50 nC |
| Body diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns per edge |
| Rise/fall time (driver dependent) | t_r + t_f | 20 ns combined |
| Junction-to-case thermal resistance | R_th,jc | 1.0 K/W |
| Board size | — | 30.5 × 30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz inner copper |

**Architecture correction.** The H743 does not commutate the motors. A 4-in-1 ESC has four dedicated ESC MCUs, for example AT32F421 or GD32E230 running AM32, or EFM8 running BLHeli_S/Bluejay. These MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to them. The model below treats each layer separately.

## 1. Power Stage Loss Model (per ESC channel)

Trapezoidal 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM) is used. At any instant, one phase high-side/low-side pair carries the PWM current and one low-side FET carries the return current.

### 1.1 Conduction Loss

With synchronous rectification, phase current flows through either the high-side (HS) or low-side (LS) FET of the switching leg for the whole PWM period. The I²R loss in that leg is therefore independent of duty cycle D:

```
P_cond,leg    = I² · R_DS(on)(T_j)                      [split D : (1-D) between HS and LS]
P_cond,return = I² · R_DS(on)(T_j)                      [the static low-side FET]
P_cond,ch     = 2 · I² · R_DS(on)(T_j)

R_DS(on)(T_j) = R_DS(on),25 · [1 + k(T_j − 25)],   k ≈ 0.005 /K
```

Worked values at 80 A:

| Case | R_DS(on) | P_cond per conducting position | P_cond per channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold (25 °C typ) | 0.70 mΩ | 4.48 W | 8.96 W |
| Hot (125 °C typ) | 1.05 mΩ | 6.72 W | **13.44 W** |
| Hot, max spec | 1.35 mΩ | 8.64 W | 17.28 W |

PCB copper adds loss. For a phase path of about 15 mm, 4 mm wide, with 4 paralleled 2 oz layers, R ≈ 0.25 mΩ, which adds about 1.6 W per channel at 80 A. **Do not omit this term.** On nano boards it is often 10–15% of total loss.

### 1.2 Switching Loss (hard-switched leg only)

```
P_sw = ½ · V_bus · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw
     = 0.5 · 25.2 · 80 · 20e-9 · 48e3      = 0.968 W
```

The edge times t_r and t_f are set by gate drive. Use t ≈ Q_gd / I_gate. With Q_gd ≈ 12 nC and about 1.2 A of effective driver current, each edge is about 10 ns. A typical FD6288-class driver sources only about 0.4 A, which gives ≈ 30 ns per edge and ≈ 2.9 W of switching loss.

**Driver selection is a first-order thermal decision at 48 kHz.**

### 1.3 Reverse Recovery, Dead Time, Coss, and Gate Losses

```
P_rr   = Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw                   = 50e-9 · 25.2 · 48e3        = 0.060 W
P_dt   = V_f · I · (2 · t_dt) · f_sw           = 0.8 · 80 · 200e-9 · 48e3   = 0.614 W
P_coss = ½ · C_oss,eff · V_bus² · f_sw · 2     = 0.5 · 1.5e-9 · 635 · 48e3 · 2 = 0.046 W
P_gate = Q_g · V_GS · f_sw · 2                 = 60e-9 · 10 · 48e3 · 2      = 0.058 W
```

P_gate is dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FETs.

Dead-time loss scales linearly with t_dt. Trimming dead time from 100 ns to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W per channel. Verify shoot-through margin on a scope before doing this.

### 1.4 Loss Budget at 80 A Burst (T_j = 125 °C)

| Loss term | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FETs) | 13.44 W | 53.8 W |
| Conduction (PCB copper) | 1.60 W | 6.4 W |
| Switching | 0.97 W | 3.9 W |
| Dead time / body diode | 0.61 W | 2.5 W |
| Reverse recovery + Coss | 0.11 W | 0.4 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 16.7 W** | **≈ 67 W** |

Efficiency check: output is about 80 A × 22 V × D. At D = 0.9 that is roughly 1.58 kW per channel, so ESC efficiency is about 99%. The absolute dissipation is still the problem.

### 1.5 Capacitor Ripple Current

```
I_cap,rms = I · √(D(1 − D))   → maximum at D = 0.5: 80 · 0.5 = 40 A_rms per channel
```

Four channels are not phase-interleaved, so this can approach 80 A_rms worst case across the bulk bank. Design for:

- Local MLCCs: at least 4 × 10 µF/50 V X7R per channel, placed less than 3 mm from each HS drain/LS source pair.
- A low-ESR polymer bulk capacitor (≥ 470 µF / 35 V) at the pad.
- External capacitance on the battery lead (≥ 1000 µF) for long leads.
