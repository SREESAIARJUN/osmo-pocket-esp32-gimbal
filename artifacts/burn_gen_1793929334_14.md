# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control Loop and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion Design

I don't have the Job #52 specification, so this design uses the assumptions below. Every assumption is a named constant in the code so you can substitute your real values. The equations are general. The numeric values (motor parameters, frequencies, and ICM-42688-P register values) must be checked against your hardware and the current TDK datasheet before you power anything up.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Loop Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs `p = 7`), `R = 5.6 Ω`, `L = 2.1 mH` (phase), `Kt = 0.08 N·m/A` |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (e.g. AS5048A / MA732), sampled synchronously with PWM |
| PWM / current loop | 20 kHz center-aligned. Current sampled at the PWM midpoint. |
| Velocity loop | 4 kHz (every 5 PWM periods) |
| Angle (attitude) loop | 1 kHz |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate. ODR 8 kHz, ±2000 dps, ±16 g. Separate SPI chip selects, shared FSYNC. |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 (single-precision FPU), e.g. STM32G4/H7 |

The control cascade runs as follows:

```
θ_ref ─►[Angle P + FF]─ω_ref─►[Velocity PI + FF]─iq_ref─►[Current PI (d,q)]─►SVPWM─►Motor
            ▲                      ▲          ▲ cogging FF            ▲
       fused attitude        fused gyro rate  │                 Clarke/Park(i_abc, θ_e)
       (camera IMU)          (notched, J⁻¹)   └─ τ_cog(θ_m)/Kt     θ_e from encoder
```

Two design decisions follow from this structure:

- **The encoder is used for commutation.** The motor's mechanical angle `θ_m` gives the electrical angle `θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset`.
- **The camera IMU is used for stabilization.** The stabilization loops close on the camera's inertial attitude and rate, not on the joint angle. The encoder serves only for commutation, joint limits, and cogging-table indexing.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, assuming balanced currents `ia + ib + ic = 0`):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

**Park transform:**

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant Model in the dq Frame (PMSM, Surface Magnets, L_d ≈ L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

The electromagnetic torque is:

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q
$$

### 1.3 Current PI with Pole-Zero Cancellation and Decoupling

Each axis has the plant `G(s) = 1/(Ls+R)`. Choose the PI controller as:

$$
C(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s}
$$

with gains:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_{cc},\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_{cc}
$$

The PI zero then cancels the electrical pole, and the closed loop becomes first-order:

$$
\frac{\omega_{cc}}{s+\omega_{cc}}
$$

**Bandwidth limit.** With 20 kHz sampling and roughly 1.5·Ts of total delay (computation plus PWM zero-order hold), keep:

$$
\omega_{cc} \le \frac{2\pi f_s}{20} \approx 2\pi\cdot 1\,\text{kHz}
$$

**Decoupling feedforward:**

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e L i_d + \hat\omega_e\hat\lambda_m
$$

The setpoints are `i_d* = 0` (no field weakening is needed for a gimbal) and `i_q* = i_q,vel + i_q,cog`.

**Voltage limiting.** Use a circular limit with q-axis priority
