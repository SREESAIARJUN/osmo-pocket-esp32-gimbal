These conclusions follow from the analysis below.

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | 80 A **per motor**, 4 motors on a nano (20×20 / 25.5×25.5 mm) board, dissipates about **210 W** at Tj = 125 °C. | F4 | The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. Each ESC channel needs its own motor-control MCU, running for example AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware on an AT32F421 or STM32G071. | The H743 only generates DShot and decodes bidirectional eRPM telemetry. Section 4 covers this interface. |

## 2. Loss Model: One ESC Channel, Six-Step Trapezoidal, Complementary PWM

### 2.1 Definitions and Representative Device Parameters

Values are for a representative 40 V N-MOSFET in a 3.3×3.3 mm DFN. **Replace every entry with the chosen part's datasheet maximums.**

| Symbol | Meaning | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | Battery voltage (6S full) | 25.2 V |
| I_ph | Phase current, burst | 80 A |
| f_sw | PWM frequency | 48 kHz |
| R_ds,25 | R_DS(on) max at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | 2.5 mΩ |
| α | R_DS(on) tempco | 0.006 /K |
| t_r, t_f | Current rise / fall time (with driver) | 8 ns / 6 ns |
| Q_rr | Body-diode reverse recovery charge | 20 nC |
| C_oss | Output capacitance at V_bus | 600 pF |
| Q_g | Total gate charge at 10 V | 25 nC |
| V_SD | Body-diode forward drop at 80 A | 0.8 V |
| t_dt | Dead time per edge | 100 ns |
| D | PWM duty | 0…1 |

**Temperature dependence of R_DS(on):**

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)] \quad\Rightarrow\quad R_{ds}(125^\circ C)=2.5\times1.6=4.0\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 2.2 Conduction Loss

In six-step commutation, two legs conduct at any time:

- **Static leg:** one FET held on for the 120° interval. It conducts I² for 100% of the time.
- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D. With complementary (synchronous) PWM, the low-side FET conducts for (1 − D) instead of the body diode.

Per channel:

$$P_{cond}= I^2R_{ds}\,[\underbrace{1}_{\text{static}} + \underbrace{D + (1-D)}_{\text{PWM leg}}] = 2\,I^2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

The result is **independent of duty**. This is the key property of synchronous rectification.

$$P_{cond}=2\,(80)^2(4.0\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{51.2\ W\ per\ motor}$$

**Per-FET peak:** a single FET carrying 80 A dissipates I²R = **25.6 W** while on. Averaged over the electrical cycle (on for 1/3 of it), this is about 8.5 W per FET.

### 2.3 Switching Loss (Hard-Switched PWM Leg)

**(a) V-I overlap:**
$$P_{VI}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(14\text{ns})(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{0.677\ W}$$

**(b) Reverse recovery.** This occurs when the high-side FET turns on into the conducting low-side body diode during dead time:
$$P_{rr}=Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw}=20\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k}=\mathbf{0.024\ W}$$

**(c) C_oss charge and discharge.** This applies to both devices in the leg:
$$P_{oss}=C_{oss}V_{bus}^2f_{sw}=600\text{p}\cdot635\cdot48\text{k}=\mathbf{0.018\ W}$$

**(d) Dead-time body-diode conduction.** There are two dead-time edges per period:
$$P_{dt}=V_{SD}\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw}=0.8\cdot80\cdot200\text{n}\cdot48\text{k}=\mathbf{0.614\ W}$$

**(e) Gate drive.** This is dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, for six FETs. It is conservative because only the PWM pair switches at f_sw.
$$P_g=6\,Q_gV_{gs}f_{sw}=6\cdot25\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k}=\mathbf{0.072\ W}$$

### 2.4 Totals at 80 A, Tj = 125 °C

| Loss term | Per motor (W) | 4 motors (W) | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 51.20 | 204.8 | 97.5 % |
| V-I switching | 0.68 | 2.7 | 1.3 % |
| Dead time | 0.61 | 2.5 | 1.2 % |
| Q_rr + C_oss + gate | 0.11 | 0.5 | 0.2 % |
| **Total** | **52.6** | **210.5** | |

**Design conclusions from the totals:**

- At 48 kHz, **conduction dominates by about 40×**. Reducing R_DS(on) is the only effective lever, for example by paralleling FETs or moving to 1.2 mΩ 5×6 parts. Nano footprints prohibit both.
- Increasing f_sw toward 96 kHz roughly doubles switching losses to about 2.6 W per motor. That is still minor in absolute terms but becomes significant at cruise.
- Shrinking dead time to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W per motor. This is only acceptable if the driver's propagation mismatch is below 20 ns.
