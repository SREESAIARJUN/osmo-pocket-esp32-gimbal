## 0. Architectural Correction (Read First)

The STM32H743 should **not** commutate the MOSFETs directly. ArduPilot has no on-FC ESC commutation firmware. The correct architecture is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot FC) ──bidirectional DShot600 ×4──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 firmware)
                                                          └─► FD6288Q gate driver ─► 6× N-MOSFET per motor
```

The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs (AM32 supports 24/48 kHz). The H743 only generates the DShot frames and captures eRPM telemetry.

### Key Feasibility Flag

As Section 2.5 shows, **4 × 80 A continuous on a nano board is physically impossible**. 80 A is a burst rating of a few seconds, and the firmware current limit must enforce it.

## 2. Thermal / Loss Model (Per ESC Channel)

### 2.1 Commutation Topology

With 6-step trapezoidal drive and complementary (synchronous) PWM, two conduction paths exist at all times:

- the PWM-ing leg: high side for duty D, synchronous low side for 1 − D;
- the opposite leg: low side on for the full 60° sector.

So the current always flows through **exactly two R_DS(on)** in series. Each FET carries current for 120° (1/3) of the electrical cycle.

### 2.2 Conduction Loss

Ripple current:

$$\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{LL}\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.9 \times 0.1}{20\mu \times 48k} = 2.36\ \text{A}$$

RMS phase current:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{pk}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6400 + 0.46 \approx 6400.5\ \text{A}^2$$

The ripple term is negligible at 48 kHz.
