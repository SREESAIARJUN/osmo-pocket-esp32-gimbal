## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In every viable 4-in-1 design, the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot. A dedicated ESC MCU generates the 48 kHz commutation PWM.
- Example ESC MCUs: AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or BLHeli_32.
- Driving 4×6 = 24 gates, sensing back-EMF, and running EKF3 on one H743 is not supported by ArduPilot. It would also wreck the loop-time budget.

The model below therefore splits into two sections:
- **ESC side:** power stage, 48 kHz switching
- **FC side:** H743 running ArduPilot, bidirectional DShot600
