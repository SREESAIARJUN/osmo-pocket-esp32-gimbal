# STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC), Job #65: System Architecture and Mathematical Model

## 0. Architecture Review: Read This First

Three of the stated requirements conflict with physics or with how these systems actually work. The model below is built so these conflicts are explicit rather than hidden.

| # | Issue | Resolution adopted in this document |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | **The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz gate PWM.** In ArduPilot systems the H743 runs the flight stack and emits DShot. Commutation and 48 kHz PWM run on per-motor ESC MCUs (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class, e.g. STM32G071 or AT32F421). | Two-tier architecture: H743, then DShot600 bidirectional, then 4× ESC MCUs, then gate drivers, then FETs. |
| R2 | **80 A burst × 4 motors on a "nano" (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board is not thermally or electrically survivable** beyond about 1 to 2 s with all motors at burst. That is roughly 74 W of ESC loss and 320 A of battery current. | Both thresholds are derived below. Burst is defined as a time-limited, single-or-few-motor transient. A 30.5×30.5 mm board is recommended if sustained high current is required. |
| R3 | ArduPilot BIDIR hwdef syntax and channel-pairing rules change between releases. | The allocation is given as a design intent. **Verify against `libraries/AP_HAL_ChibiOS/hwdef/` README for your target branch before layout freeze.** |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation topology

The ESC uses six-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) PWM, as in AM32 or BLHeli_32 "complementary PWM" mode. In any sector, phase A is PWM'd, phase B is held with its low side ON, and phase C floats.

- The high side of A conducts for D·T_sw.
- The low side of A (synchronous rectifier) conducts for (1−D)·T_sw.
- The low side of B conducts for 100 % of the sector.

Effective on-resistance per switch position:

$$R_{sw}(T_j) = \frac{R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]}{N_p}\cdot\frac{1}{\eta_{share}}$$

- α ≈ 0.006 /K, which gives k_T ≈ 1.6 at 125 °C.
- η_share ≈ 0.9 accounts for current imbalance between paralleled FETs (the PTC behaviour of R_ds is self-balancing, but layout asymmetry costs about 10 %).

Worked value:

$$R_{sw} = \frac{0.90\,\text{m}\Omega \cdot 1.6}{2 \cdot 0.9} = 0.80\ \text{m}\Omega \quad(\text{typ})$$
$$R_{sw,max} = \frac{1.20 \cdot 1.6}{2\cdot0.9} = 1.07\ \text{m}\Omega$$
