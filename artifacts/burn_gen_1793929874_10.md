## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architectural correction

The STM32H743 cannot commutate the motors under ArduPilot. ArduPilot has no in-tree sensorless BLDC commutation, and the H743's timer and ADC resources would be overcommitted anyway. The architecture is therefore split:

| Block | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100, 400 MHz) | ArduPilot | Attitude, nav, outputs bidirectional DShot600 |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | STM32G071 or AT32F421 class | AM32 (or BLHeli_32-compatible) | Sensorless 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drivers (×4) | 3-phase half-bridge driver (e.g., Fortior FD6288Q class) | – | 12 V gate drive, bootstrap high side |
| Power stage (×4) | 6× 40 V N-MOSFET per channel (24 total) | – | 6S bridge |

### 0.2 Design assumptions

All results scale with these assumptions; change them and re-run the equations.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max (22.2 V nominal) |
| Burst phase current per channel | I_ph | 80 A |
| Burst duration target | t_b | 5 s |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET R_DS(on) @25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R₂₅ | 1.0 mΩ |
| R_DS(on) tempco (normalized ≈1.5× at 125 °C) | α | 0.005 /K |
| Gate charge total / Miller | Q_g / Q_gd | 40 nC / 10 nC |
| Plateau voltage | V_pl | 3.5 V |
| Internal gate resistance | R_g,int | 1.5 Ω |
| Reverse recovery charge | Q_rr | 40 nC |
| Energy-equivalent output capacitance | C_o(er) | 1.5 nF |
| Body diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns |
| Board | – | 36 × 36 mm, 6-layer, 2 oz (70 µm) all layers, 30.5 mm mounting |
| Ambient (inside frame) | T_a | 40 °C |
| Junction limit / design target | T_j | 175 °C abs / ≤135 °C design |

> **Feasibility flag (read first):** 80 A × 4 channels at 6S is about 8 kW peak electrical. That is far beyond "nano" class (20×20 boards are typically 25–45 A). The math below shows a 36 mm board survives roughly 3 s, not 5 s, at full 4-motor burst. Section 2.6 lists what must change to meet 5 s.
