# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC with 80 A-Class 4-in-1 ESC: Design Basis Document (Job #24)

**Scope:** Conduction and switching loss model, burst thermal transient, ArduPilot bi-directional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and reference netlists.

## Feasibility Summary

A **20×20 mm (or 25.5×25.5 mm) nano** board **cannot** sustain 4 × 80 A. Section 3 shows ≈ 58 W of dissipation at that point. An unheatsunk nano PCB tolerates that for only about 2–4 s from a 40 °C ambient.

The design basis below therefore rates the board as follows:

| Rating | Value |
|---|---|
| Continuous, per channel | 35–45 A |
| Burst, per channel | 80 A for ≤ 2 s (single or diagonal pair) |
| Burst, all 4 channels at 80 A | ≤ 2 s, then thermal throttle via ESC temperature telemetry |

**Architecture note:** The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the **ESC MCU** (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32, or BLHeli_32-class silicon), not by the H743. The H743 only produces DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. Both domains are modeled separately.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path

With trapezoidal 6-step commutation and **complementary (synchronous) PWM** as used by AM32/BLHeli_32, two phases carry the phase current I at any instant. One leg PWMs between its high-side (HS) and low-side (LS) FET. The other leg holds its LS on. Therefore exactly **two FETs conduct** the phase current at every instant, with negligible ripple at 48 kHz for typical motor inductance.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2 \, R_{ds,hot}$$

The duty cycle D only divides the loss between HS and LS within the PWM leg. It does not change the total:

$$P_{HS} = D\,I^2R,\quad P_{LS,pwm} = (1-D)\,I^2R,\quad P_{LS,static} = I^2R$$

### 2.2 Temperature feedback (self-consistent)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0053\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

$$T_j = T_{ref} + Z_{th}(t)\cdot P(T_j)$$

This converges in about 3 iterations. **Thermal runaway** occurs if $\partial P/\partial T_j \cdot Z_{th} \ge 1$, i.e. $2I^2R_{25}\alpha Z_{th} \ge 1$.

At 80 A with Z_th = 3 K/W: $2\cdot6400\cdot0.0007\cdot0.0053\cdot3 = 0.14$. This is stable, with a margin of about 7×.

### 2.3 Numbers at 80 A

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{12.8\ W}$$

The worst single FET is the static LS: **6.4 W** instantaneous.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. Each PWM period has one turn-on and one turn-off of the current-carrying commutation.

| Term | Equation | Value per channel |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | $\tfrac12 V I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw}$ | 0.5·25.2·80·20 ns·48 k = **0.97 W** |
| Dead-time diode | $V_f I \cdot 2t_{dt} f_{sw}$ | 0.8·80·200 ns·48 k = **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | $Q_{rr} V f_{sw}$ | 40 nC·25.2·48 k = **0.05 W** |
| C_oss | $\tfrac12 C_{oss}V^2 f_{sw}$ | **0.02 W** |
| Gate drive (to driver, not FET) | $2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw}$ | **0.05 W** |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 1.65 W** |

### 3.1 Totals

| Case | Per channel | Board total (4 ch) |
|---|---|---|
| 80 A burst | 12.8 + 1.65 ≈ **14.5 W** | **≈ 58 W** |
| 40 A continuous | 3.2 + 0.8 ≈ **4.0 W** (R at ~90 °C) | **≈ 16 W** |

Conduction dominates (88 %), so **R_ds(on) and copper are the levers, not f_sw.**

Dropping to 24 kHz saves only about 0.8 W per channel. It also raises current ripple and audible noise.

### 3.2 Bulk capacitor ripple (often the real failure point)

$$I_{C,rms} \approx I\sqrt{D(1-D)} \;\xrightarrow{D=0.5,\ 80\text{A}}\; 40\ \text{A per channel}$$

- Channels are not phase-synchronized, so for 4 channels the combined ripple is ≈ 40·√4 ≈ **80 A rms**. That is the worst case. Interleaving helps somewhat.
- On-board MLCC banks of 8–12 × 10 µF/50 V X7R 1206 per channel carry the HF component.
- **An external low-ESR electrolytic (≥ 1000 µF/35 V) at the battery pads is mandatory.**

## 4. Burst Thermal Model

### 4.1 Two-node RC model

```
P_FET ──[Z_th,j-b(t)]── T_board ──[R_th,b-a]── T_amb
                            │
                         C_board
```

**Board heat capacity** (25.5×25.5 mm, 1.6 mm, 6-layer, 2 oz power layers):

| Material | Calculation | Heat capacity |
|---|---|---|
| Copper | ≈ 1.6 g × 0.385 J/g·K | 0.63 J/K |
| FR-4 | ≈ 1.9 g × 1.15 J/g·K | 2.2 J/K |
| FETs, solder, caps | | ≈ 0.3 J/K |
| **C_board** | | **≈ 3.1 J/K** |

**Board-to-air:** R_th,b-a ≈ 12 K/W in still air and ≈ 4 K/W with prop wash. Prop wash is the real airframe condition, and its time constant τ ≈ 12 s.

### 4.2 Board temperature during burst

$$T_b(t) = T_a + P R_{ba}\left(1 - e^{-t/R_{ba}C}\right)$$

With P = 58 W and R_ba = 4 K/W, the steady-state value is 232 K
