**Scope:** loss model at 80 A burst, 48 kHz PWM, 6S supply; ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation; netlists; thermal analysis.

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Baseline Assumptions

This is a two-processor system:

| Domain | Processor | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743 (480 MHz, LQFP100) | ArduPilot. Generates DShot600 and decodes bidirectional eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC (×4 channels) | AT32F421 / STM32G071 class running AM32 (or BLHeli_32) | Runs 6-step commutation at 48 kHz PWM. |

The H743 never generates the 48 kHz PWM. Switching losses are a property of the ESC MCU, gate drivers, and power stage.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

R_DS(T_j) = R₂₅ · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 125 °C, R_DS = 1.0 × 1.40 = **1.40 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step synchronous commutation

In trapezoidal six-step drive, two half-bridges conduct at any instant:

- **PWM leg:** the high side (HS) conducts for duty D; the complementary low side (LS) synchronously rectifies for (1 − D).
- **Return leg:** the LS is held fully on.

The PWM leg therefore always has exactly one FET in the current path. The total conduction loss per motor channel is independent of D (dead time ignored):

P_cond,motor = 2 · I_ph² · R_DS(T_j)
