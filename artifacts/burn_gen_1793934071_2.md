## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Key Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU running ArduPilot. It does not commutate the motors directly. Each of the 4 ESC channels has its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware (e.g., AT32F421 / STM32G071). The H743 sends **bidirectional DShot600** to those MCUs. This is the only architecture in which "DShot" and "4-in-1 ESC" are coherent together.
