## 0. Architectural Ground Rules and Assumptions

On a 4-in-1 ESC stack, the STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own low-cost MCU (for example, AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32/Bluejay) and that MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The thermal model applies to the ESC board. The DMA model applies to the FC board.
