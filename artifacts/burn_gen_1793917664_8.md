## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-Dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}\ \text{(rep.)}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 0.9 mΩ × 1.60 = **1.44 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current Path in 6-Step Synchronous PWM

At any instant, two phases carry current I, and current always flows through exactly two FETs:

- **PWM on:** HS(A) → winding → LS(B)
- **PWM off (synchronous freewheel):** LS(A) → winding → LS(B)

The conduction loss per channel is therefore independent of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Parasitic copper must be added. Assume a total R_cu ≈ 0.35 mΩ (bus planes plus phase pads) and a shunt R_sh = 0.2 mΩ (low-side shunt in the path ⅔ of the time, approximated as always in path):

$$P_{cond,total} = I^2\,[\,2R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{cu} + R_{sh}\,]$$
