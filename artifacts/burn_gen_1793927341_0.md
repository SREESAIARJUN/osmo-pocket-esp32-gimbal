## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. ArduPilot has no BLDC commutation firmware. The correct partitioning is:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, emits bi-directional DShot600 |
| ESC ×4 | One MCU per motor (e.g. AT32F421 / STM32G071) | AM32 or BLHeli_32 | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, returns eRPM telemetry |
| Power stage ×4 | 3-phase gate driver + 6 N-MOSFETs per motor | — | 24 FETs total |

**Feasibility flag:** 4 × 80 A simultaneous burst on a nano form factor (20×20 or 30×30 mm) is thermally marginal. Section 3 shows the board is limited to roughly **1–2 s** of simultaneous 80 A without heatsinking. Specify 80 A as a **per-motor, ≤1 s burst** rating, with a lower continuous rating.

## 2. Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.5 mΩ × 1.45 = **2.18 mΩ**.

### 2.2 Conduction losses (6-step, complementary PWM on the sourcing leg)

Each leg conducts for 240° of every 360° electrical:
- **120° sourcing:** the high-side (HS) FET is PWM'd with duty D, and the low-side (LS) FET synchronously rectifies for 1−D.
- **120° sinking:** the LS FET is fully on.

**HS RMS current:**
$$I_{HS,rms}^2 = I^2\frac{D}{3}$$

**LS RMS current:**
$$I_{LS,rms}^2 = I^2\left(\frac{1-D}{3}+\frac{1}{3}\right)$$

**Per-FET conduction loss:**
$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot R_{ds}(T_j)$$

At I = 80 A, D = 0.95, R_ds = 2.18 mΩ:

| FET | I²_rms (A²) | P_cond |
|---|---|---|
| HS | 6400 × 0.317 = 2027 | **4.42 W** |
| LS | 6400 × 0.350 = 2240 | **4.88 W** |
| Per leg | — | 9.30 W |
| **Per motor (3 legs)** | — | **27.9 W** |

**Cross-check:** At any instant, two FETs carry the full current in series:
$$P = I^2 \cdot 2R_{ds} = 6400 \times 4.36\text{ mΩ} = 27.9\text{ W}$$
This matches the per-leg sum.

**Low-throttle worst case for the LS FET:** As D → 0, I²_LS,rms → (2/3)I², giving 9.3 W in a single LS FET at 80 A. In practice, 80 A at low duty only occurs in a stall or desync. AM32's current limit and stall protection must catch this.

### 2.3 Switching losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd HS FET of one leg hard-switches at a time.

**Hard-switching V·I overlap:**
$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 40\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction (LS FET, two edges per period):**
$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 300\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.92\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery (dissipated in the HS FET at turn-on):**
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n} \cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.06\text{ W}$$

**Output capacitance:**
$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} \approx 0.05\text{ W}$$

**Gate drive (2 FETs, dissipated in driver and gate resistors, not in the FETs):**
$$P_g = 2 Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} = 0.048\text{ W}$$

### 2.4 Loss summary at 80 A burst

| Loss | Per motor | ×4 motors |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 27.9 W | 111.6 W |
| Switching | 1.94 W | 7.8 W |
| Dead time | 0.92 W | 3.7 W |
| Q_rr + Q_oss | 0.11 W | 0.4 W |
| **Total** | **30.9 W** | **≈123.5 W** |

Efficiency at burst: P_out ≈ 25.2 V × 80 A × 0.95 ≈ 1915 W per motor, so η ≈ 98.4 %.

**Conclusion:** Conduction dominates (~90 %). Raising f_sw to 48 kHz costs only about 1.5 W per motor relative to 24 kHz. The bigger trade-off is dead-time loss, which scales with f_sw. Keep t_dt at or below 150 ns.

### 2.5 Bus capacitor ripple

$$I_{C,rms} \approx I\sqrt{D(1-D)}$$

- At D = 0.95: 17.4 A per motor.
- At worst case D = 0.5: **40 A per motor**.

**Requirements:**
- ≥ 8 × 10 µF/50 V X7R 1206 ceramics per motor quadrant.
- A ≥ 1000 µF/35 V low-ESR electrolytic at the battery pads, mandatory for voltage-spike suppression.
