### Two architecture issues to resolve before layout

1. **The H743 does not switch the FETs.** ArduPilot has no native FOC/6-step motor drive. The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU (or one quad-capable ESC MCU per pair, depending on firmware). The 48 kHz switching analysis applies to the ESC MCU domain.
2. **"Nano" form factor vs. 4×80 A.** A 20×20 mm board cannot absorb 4×80 A bursts. There is no room for eight-plus 5×6 FET footprints per side, the phase copper, or the capacitor bank. Section 2.6 shows that even 30.5×30.5 mm only survives on the order of seconds. I strongly recommend specifying **80 A as a single-channel burst** (e.g., one motor saturating during a yaw transient), not all four simultaneously. Alternatively, rate 80 A per channel with an explicit duration curve.
