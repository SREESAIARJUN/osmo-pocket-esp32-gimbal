## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A per channel)

### 1.1 Current path

With 6-step trapezoidal commutation and synchronous rectification, two phase legs carry current at any instant:
- **PWM leg:** the high side (HS) conducts for D·T_sw, and the low side (LS) freewheels for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Return leg:** the LS is on continuously.

Every instant therefore has exactly two MOSFET channels in series. Conduction loss is **independent of duty cycle**, apart from dead-time body-diode intervals, which are counted in §2.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{rms}^2 R_{Cu}$$

### 1.2 Temperature coefficient

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_DS(on) = 1.0 × (1 + 0.006·85) = **1.51 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Ripple contribution

Worst-case ripple occurs at D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus} D(1-D)}{L f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \cdot 0.25}{20\mu \cdot 48k} = 6.56\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 \approx 6404\ \text{A}^2$$

Ripple adds only 0.06%, so it is negligible at 48 kHz.

### 1.4 Result per channel at 80 A

| Term | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFETs | 6404 × 2 × 1.51 mΩ | **19.3 W** |
| PCB copper and solder (R_Cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ, phase + bus path) | 6404 × 0.3 mΩ | **1.9 W** |
| **Conduction subtotal** | | **≈ 21.2 W** |

The MOSFET loss splits as ~9.7 W in each conducting FET. The return-leg LS takes the full 9.7 W. The PWM-leg HS and LS share theirs according to D.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the PWM leg switches. Its LS turns on after dead time with the body diode already conducting, so it is ZVS-like. Hard-switching losses therefore land on the HS.

### 2.1 V–I overlap (HS)

$$t_{sw} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_g},\quad I_g \approx \frac{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}{R_g} \approx \frac{10-3.5}{10\,\Omega}\approx 0.65\ \text{A} \Rightarrow t_r \approx t_f \approx 15\ \text{ns}$$

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 30\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery (LS body diode, dissipated in the HS)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\cdot 25.2\cdot 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} \approx Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.060\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction (LS)

$$P_{dt} = V_F \cdot I \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot 80\cdot 400\text{n}\cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$
