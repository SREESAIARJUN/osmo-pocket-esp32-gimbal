# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal & Bidirectional DShot Design Package (Job #27)

This package covers:

- Electrical loss model for 80 A burst at 48 kHz PWM
- Transient and steady-state thermal model
- ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer and DMA allocation
- Reference netlists

All numeric results depend on the component assumptions in §0. Re-run the equations with your selected MOSFET datasheet values before release.

## 0. Architecture Assumptions and a Feasibility Flag

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz, APB2 timer clock 200 MHz |
| ESC MCU (×4) | STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32. The H743 does not run commutation. |
| Gate driver (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap, VCC 5–20 V) |
| MOSFETs (×24) | 40 V N-channel, R_DS(on),25 = 1.0 mΩ, Q_g = 60 nC, t_r + t_f ≈ 20 ns, Q_rr = 50 nC, C_oss,eff ≈ 1.5 nF, R_θJC = 1.2 K/W |
| Battery | 6S, 25.2 V max (22.2 V nominal) |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal, complementary PWM (synchronous rectification), dead time 100 ns |
| Burst | 80 A per motor, all 4 motors simultaneously (worst case) |

**Feasibility flag:** A true 20×20 mm "nano" stack cannot carry 4×80 A. Three constraints rule it out:

- The copper cross-section is insufficient.
- Adiabatic board temperature rise exceeds ~20 K/s (see §2).
- Twenty-four 5×6 mm MOSFETs physically do not fit.

The minimum realistic format is **30.5×30.5 mm** with 3×3 mm (PQFN3.3) MOSFETs, and that only supports **≤2 s bursts**. The model below quantifies this so the burst duty limit can be enforced in firmware.

## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 1.1 Conduction losses

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, phase current always flows through exactly **two** MOSFETs in series:

- During ON: high-side of phase A and low-side of phase B.
- During OFF: low-side of phase A (synchronous) and low-side of phase B.

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

**Temperature dependence** (silicon, α ≈ 0.005 /K, giving about 1.5× at 125 °C):

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

**Ripple contribution:**

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{DC}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

The ripple term is evaluated with L_ph = 20 µH, D = 0.5 and V = 25.2 V:

- ΔI = 25.2·0.25 / (20e-6·48e3) = **6.56 A**
- ΔI²/12 = 3.6 A², which is negligible against 6400 A².

**Copper losses** (bus, phase pads and vias, estimated R_Cu ≈ 0.4 mΩ per current path):

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu}$$

### 1.2 Switching losses (one hard-switched leg at a time)

| Term | Equation | Value at 80 A, 25.2 V, 48 kHz |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw | ½·25.2·80·20e-9·48e3 = **0.968 W** |
| Dead-time body-diode conduction | V_f·I·2·t_dt·f_sw (V_f = 0.8 V) | 0.8·80·2·100e-9·48e3 = **0.614 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 50e-9·25.2·48e3 = **0.060 W** |
| C_oss | 2 × ½·C_oss·V²·f_sw | 1.5e-9·635·48e3 = **0.046 W** |
| Gate drive (2 switching FETs) | 2·Q_g·V_gs·f_sw (V_gs = 10 V) | 2·60e-9·10·48e3 = **0.058 W** (dissipated in the driver and R_g) |

$$P_{sw,total} \approx 1.75\ \text{W per motor}$$

The switching losses scale linearly with f_sw. Dropping to 24 kHz saves about 0.85 W per motor but raises ripple to 13 A and increases audible and current noise.

### 1.3 Total loss at 80 A burst

Evaluated at T_j = 25 °C (cold start) and T_j = 125 °C (hot):

| | Cold (R = 1.0 mΩ) | Hot (R = 1.5 mΩ) |
|---|---|---|
| P_cond (2 FETs) | 6400·2·1.0e-3 = 12.8 W | 19.2 W |
| P_Cu | 2.56 W | 3.0 W (Cu α = 0.0039) |
| P_sw | 1.75 W | 1.75 W |
| **Per motor** | **17.1 W** | **24.0 W** |
| **4 motors** | **68 W** | **96 W** |

The ESC efficiency at the burst point (hot) is about 96 W lost out of 4·80·~20 V ≈ 6.4 kW delivered, which is roughly 98.5%.

**Per-FET average** (each FET conducts about 1/3 of the electrical cycle; the high-side also carries the switching loss):

$$P_{FET,HS} \approx \frac{I^2 R(T_j)}{3}\cdot k + \frac{P_{sw}}{3}$$

- k ≈ 1.0 for the low-side, which conducts during both the PWM-off interval and its own active step.
- At the hot point: P_FET ≈ 6400·1.5e-3/3 + 0.58 ≈ **3.8 W peak-average** per FET.

### 1.4 DC-link capacitor ripple

Use worst case D = 0.5 per motor, with the four motors unsynchronized and adding as RMS:

$$I_{C,rms} = I\sqrt{D(1-D)} = 40\ \text{A per motor}, \qquad I_{C,total}\approx \sqrt{4}\cdot 40 = 80\ \text{A}_{rms}$$

Requirements that follow:

- Spread low-ESR MLCC bulk across all four channels (≥ 8× 10 µF/50 V X7R 1206 per channel).
