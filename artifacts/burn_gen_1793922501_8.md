## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Three issues in the request change the results, so I resolve them before the math.

| # | Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is a flight-control stack; it does not run ESC firmware. In every production 4-in-1 design, each motor channel has its own small MCU. | **Two-tier architecture.** The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600. Four AT32F421K8U7 MCUs (one per channel) run AM32 and generate the 48 kHz PWM. |
| 2 | **An "80A nano" ESC is a burst rating, not a continuous one.** A 20×20 mm board physically cannot reject 4 × 80 A worth of heat. Section 3 proves this. | Target a **30.5×30.5 mm** board. Rate it **80 A for at most 2 s per channel**, with a continuous rating derived in Section 3.4. A 20×20 variant is feasible only at about 35–40 A burst. |
| 3 | **48 kHz PWM is ESC-side**, not FC-side. | Switching losses are computed for the ESC MCU PWM. The FC timers run at DShot bit rate only. |
