# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control Loop and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion Design

This design covers the field-oriented control (FOC) current loop, cascaded angle and velocity control, anti-cogging feedforward, and dual ICM-42688-P vibration rejection, with a complete C++ implementation. I don't have the Job #56 specification, so the design is built on the assumptions in §0. Every equation is parametric, so you can substitute your actual motor, encoder and MCU values.

---

## 0. Assumed Hardware and Timing

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), e.g. GBM2804-class. R_s ≈ 5–10 Ω, L_s ≈ 2–4 mH |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701) on each axis |
| MCU | STM32G474 (Cortex-M4F, 170 MHz, FPU, CORDIC), one MCU driving all 3 axes, or one per axis |
| Driver | 3-phase driver (e.g. DRV8313), inline or low-side shunts, 20 kHz center-aligned PWM |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 24 MHz, ODR = 8 kHz, ±2000 dps, ±16 g |

**Loop rates:**

| Loop | Rate | Period |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | f_i = 20 kHz | T_i = 50 µs |
| IMU acquisition and filtering | f_imu = 8 kHz | |
| Velocity | f_v = 4 kHz | |
| Angle and attitude | f_θ = 1 kHz | |

Bandwidth separation is roughly 5–10× between successive loops.

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform** (assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad
v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 PMSM Model in the d-q Frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32\, p\,\lambda_m\, i_q \equiv K_t\, i_q \quad (i_d = 0 \text{ control})
$$

### 1.3 Current PI with Decoupling (Pole-Zero Cancellation)

The plant per axis is P_i(s) = 1/(Ls + R). With the PI controller

$$
C_i(s)=K_{p,i}+\frac{K_{i,i}}{s},
$$

choose the gains so the PI zero cancels the electrical pole:

$$
\boxed{K_{p,i}=L\,\omega_{c,i},\qquad K_{i,i}=R\,\omega_{c,i}}
$$

The closed loop then becomes first order:

$$
\frac{i}{i^*}=\frac{\omega_{c,i}}{s+\omega_{c,i}}
$$

Take ω_c,i ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz, which is below f_PWM/10 and accounts for the 1.5-sample computation-plus-PWM delay.

**Feedforward decoupling:**

$$
v_d^{ff}=-\omega_e L\,\hat i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff}=\omega_e(L\,\hat i_d+\lambda_m)
$$
