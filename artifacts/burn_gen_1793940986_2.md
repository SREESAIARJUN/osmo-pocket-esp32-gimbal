# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #6): Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot Design Package

## 0. Architectural Corrections

These points change the design, so they come first.

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every ArduPilot-compatible AIO, the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot. Each motor bridge is commutated by its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071. ArduPilot has no in-tree FOC or 6-step motor driver. The netlist below reflects this.
2. **80 A burst on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is only a ~1–2 second capability with 4 motors loaded.** Section 3 derives this. Do not put "80 A burst" on a datasheet without stating the duration and the number of loaded channels. The marketing figure only holds for one motor or a sub-second event.
3. **Every numerical result is parametric.** The values used (Rds(on), Qgd, Rth, and others) are representative of 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFETs. Substitute the real datasheet values and re-run the equations.

## 2. Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Losses

In 6-step operation with synchronous rectification, current always flows through exactly two FETs: the high side of one phase and the low side of another. During PWM off-time, the complementary low side of the PWM phase carries the current instead of the high side. Ignoring dead time, there are always two R_ds(on) in series:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

Each FET conducts for 120° of the electrical cycle:

$$P_{cond,FET,avg} \approx \frac{I_{ph}^2 R_{ds}(T_j)}{3}$$

Phase copper must be added. A 2 oz trace of 3 mm × 8 mm is roughly 0.2–0.3 mΩ, so add I²·2R_cu.

**Worked values at 80 A:**

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond / motor |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.50 mΩ | 19.2 W |
| 100 °C | 2.01 mΩ | 25.7 W |
| 125 °C | 2.18 mΩ | **27.9 W** |

Copper adds 6400 × 2 × 0.25 mΩ = **3.2 W**.

Because P depends on T_j and T_j depends on P, solve iteratively:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{board} + R_{th,jb}\,P_{FET}(T_j^{(n)})$$

This converges in 3–4 iterations. Thermal runaway occurs if α·P·R_th ≥ 1, which is not the case here (≈0.1).

### 2.2 Switching Losses at 48 kHz

**Gate-current-limited transition times:**

$$t_{on}\approx\frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{(V_{drv}-V_{pl})/(R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}+R_{drv,src})},\qquad t_{off}\approx\frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{V_{pl}/(R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}+R_{drv,snk})}$$

- I_g,on = 7 V / 11 Ω = 0.64 A, giving t_on ≈ **19 ns**
- I_g,off = 3 V / 11 Ω = 0.27 A, giving t_off ≈ **44 ns**

**Loss terms** (one switching leg per commutation sector):

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_on+t_off)·f = ½·22.2·80·63 ns·48k | **2.69 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f = 40n·22.2·48k | 0.043 W |
| Output capacitance | ½·Q_oss·V·f | 0.016 W |
| Dead-time diode | V_f·I·2t_dt·f = 0.8·80·200 ns·48k | **0.61 W** |
