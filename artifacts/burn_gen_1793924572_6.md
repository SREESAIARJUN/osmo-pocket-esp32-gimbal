# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #35): Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Budget, Bidirectional DShot600 Timer/DMA Plan, and Reference Netlists

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

A few things need stating up front, because they change the math.

1. **Architecture.** In practice the H743 does not drive the MOSFETs. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32-class firmware), and that MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to those MCUs. The model below is split the same way.
2. **"80 A burst"** is taken as **80 A phase current per channel, all four channels simultaneously, for a few seconds**. This is the worst case. If your rating means 80 A per channel with the others lower, or 80 A total battery current, scale P ∝ I².
3. **Bus voltage** is 6S: 25.2 V maximum, which is used for the switching calculations.
4. **The MOSFET is a placeholder.** It represents a 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN part:

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| R_DS(on),25 | 1.0 mΩ |
| α | 0.0045 /K |
| Q_g | 40 nC |
| Q_gs2 | 3 nC |
| Q_gd | 8 nC |
| Q_oss | 40 nC |
| Q_rr | 30 nC |
| V_SD | 0.8 V |
| R_θJC | 1.5 K/W |

Replace these with your datasheet values. Every equation is written parametrically so you can do that.

5. **Gate drive.** The driver is FD6288Q-class at 10 V, with R_g,ext = 4.7 Ω, R_g,int = 1 Ω, plateau voltage V_pl = 3.5 V, and dead time t_dt = 100 ns.
6. **Commutation.** The scheme is 6-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM on the high side of the "high" phase.

Anything marked **[verify]** must be checked against RM0433 or the component datasheet before layout or release.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance
$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C this gives R = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 85) = **1.38 mΩ**.

Add the PCB copper path per conducting leg, R_cu. For 2 oz copper on 4 layers, about 0.2 mΩ per leg is realistic on a nano board. It is kept separate below.

### 1.2 Topology identity (the key simplification)
In 6-step operation with synchronous rectification, at every instant exactly **one switch in each of the two active legs** carries the phase current I. This holds whether the high side is ON, or the low side is freewheeling during the PWM off-time. Therefore:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) \quad\text{(independent of duty D)}$$

**Per-device split.** Each phase is high for 120°, low for 120°, and floating for 2 × 60°. Over one electrical cycle, for phase PWM duty D:

- High side: δ_HS = D/3
- Low side: δ_LS = (1−D)/3 + 1/3 = (2−D)/3
- Check: the per-phase total is 2/3, and three phases × 2/3 = 2 conducting switches.

The worst-case device is therefore the **low side at low duty**:

$$P_{LS} = I^2 R \,\frac{2-D}{3}$$

At D = 1 (full-throttle burst), HS and LS each carry I²R/3.
