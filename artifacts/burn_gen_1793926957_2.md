## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 cannot drive four 80A bridges directly. It runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Each ESC channel uses its own motor MCU, for example an AT32F421 running AM32 or an STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The FC talks to each ESC MCU over bi-directional DShot600.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path

With synchronous complementary PWM in 6-step commutation, the current path changes with each part of the PWM period:

- **During t_on (fraction D):** current flows through the high-side switch of phase A and the low-side switch of phase B.
- **During t_off (fraction 1−D):** current flows through the low-side switch of phase A (synchronous rectifier) and the low-side switch of phase B.

Exactly two channels conduct at all times, ignoring dead time. The conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 \, R_{ds,hot}\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{ph}^2 \, R_{ds,hot} = 2\, I_{ph}^2 \, R_{ds,hot}$$

### 1.2 Hot on-resistance

$$R_{ds,hot} = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] \approx k_T \, R_{ds,25} = 1.6\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Ripple correction

The RMS current with PWM ripple is:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

For a typical 5–7" motor (L_ph ≈ 15 µH line-to-line), with V_bus − V_bemf ≈ 3 V at D ≈ 0.9:

$$\Delta I \approx \frac{3 \times 0.9}{15\,\mu H \times 48\,kHz} \approx 3.75\ \text{A}$$

The ripple term is therefore ΔI²/12 ≈ 1.2 A², which is negligible against 6400 A². This low ripple is one benefit of 48 kHz over 24 kHz.

### 1.4 Result per motor

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.6\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{20.5\ W}$$

### 1.5 Copper and connection losses (per motor)

| Element | R | P at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pad and trace (2 oz, ~8 mm, multilayer) | ~0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W per phase path; ×2 conducting phases = 3.8 W (shared plane, use ~2 W effective) |
| Shunt (if per-phase sensing) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |

For a total-current design with a single shared shunt, see Section 3.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Gate current and transition times

**Rise (turn-on):**

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{g,tot}} = \frac{10-3}{5} = 1.4\ \text{A}$$

$$t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{15\ \text{nC}}{1.4\ \text{A}} = 10.7\ \text{ns}$$

**Fall (turn-off):**

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{g,tot}} = 0.6\ \text{A}$$

$$t_f \approx \frac{15\ \text{nC}}{0.6\ \text{A}} = 25\ \text{ns}$$
