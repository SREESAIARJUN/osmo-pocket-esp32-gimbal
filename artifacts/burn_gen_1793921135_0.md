# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control Loop, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Fusion

I don't have the Job #48 specification, so this design states its assumptions explicitly (§0). Every motor, sensor, and timing constant is a parameter in one config struct, so you can substitute the real values without touching the algorithms.

## 0. Assumed Platform and Timing

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC (GBM2804-class), 12N14P → pole pairs p = 7, R ≈ 5.6 Ω, L ≈ 2.1 mH, Kt ≈ 0.045 N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (MT6701 / AS5048A) on each axis |
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class with FPU and center-aligned PWM |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, mirrored about the plate centroid |

**Rate hierarchy.** Each loop should be at least 5–10× faster than the loop outside it.

| Loop | Rate | Closed-loop bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| PWM | 40 kHz | – |
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz (Ts_i = 50 µs) | ω_ci ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s |
| IMU sample (ODR) | 8 kHz | – |
| Velocity | 4 kHz (Ts_v = 250 µs) | ω_cv ≈ 2π·120 rad/s |
| Angle / stabilization | 1 kHz (Ts_θ = 1 ms) | ω_cθ ≈ 2π·20 rad/s |

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle is θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_off) mod 2π. The encoder offset θ_off is found by aligning the rotor with a d-axis voltage.

**Clarke (power-invariant form not used; amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):**

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park:**

$$
\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant Model in the d–q Frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

Electromagnetic torque for an SPM motor:

$$
\tau = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q
$$

### 1.3 Current PI Tuning (Pole–Zero Cancellation)

The plant is G(s) = 1/(Ls + R). Choose the PI so its zero cancels the plant pole:

$$
C(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s},\qquad K_p = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_{ci}
$$

The closed loop then becomes the first-order system ω_ci/(s + ω_ci).

**Discrete form with back-calculation anti-windup** (K_aw = K_i/K_p):

$$
e_k = i^\*_k - i_k,\qquad
u_k = K_p e_k + I_k + v_{ff,k},\qquad
I_{k+1} = I_k + T_s\left[K_i e_k + K_{aw}(\mathrm{sat}(u_k) - u_k)\right]
$$

**Decoupling feedforward:**

$$
v_{d,ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_{q,ff} = \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

**Voltage limiting.** The voltage vector is limited to a circle of radius V_max = V_dc/√3 · m_max, with m_max = 0.95. When the limit is hit, d-axis priority is used:

$$
v_d \leftarrow \mathrm{clamp}(v_d, \pm V_{max}),\qquad
v_q \leftarrow \mathrm{clamp}\left(v_q, \pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}\right)
$$

### 1.4 SVPWM (Min–Max Zero-Sequence Injection)

$$
v_a = v_\alpha,\quad
v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad
v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc}) + \min(v_{abc})\big),\qquad
d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{dc}}
$$

### 1.5 Delay Compensation of the Angle

The PWM update lands about 1.5 T_s after the current sample. Advance the angle used in the inverse Park transform accordingly:

$$
\theta_e^{\,inv} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s
$$

## 2. Cascaded Angle → Velocity → Current Control

### 2.1 Mechanical Plant

$$
J\dot\omega = K_t i_q - \tau_{cog}(\theta) - b\,\omega - \tau_{dist}
$$

### 2.2 Velocity Loop (PI)

With the current loop treated as unity gain (because ω_ci ≫ ω_cv), the plant is K_t/(Js). The symmetric-optimum-lite tuning is:

$$
K_{pv} = \frac{J\,\omega_{cv}}{K_t},\qquad K_{iv} = K_{pv}\,\frac{\omega_{cv}}{4}
$$

This places the PI zero a factor of 4 below crossover, which gives roughly 60° of phase margin.
