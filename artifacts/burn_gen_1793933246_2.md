## 1. Commutation Topology Model

The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary PWM on the active high side. Under that scheme the motor current path always passes through **exactly two MOSFETs**:

- **PWM-on interval:** HS(phase A) → motor → LS(phase B)
- **PWM-off interval:** LS(phase A, synchronous rectification) → motor → LS(phase B)

This property makes conduction loss independent of duty cycle, given a fixed phase current. Only the split between high-side and low-side devices changes with duty.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS} = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times [1+0.0055\times 85] = 1.2 \times 1.468 = 1.76\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 2.2 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2\,R_{Cu}$$

R_Cu is the phase-path copper and solder resistance, covering the pad, two vias arrays, and the trace to the motor pad. For 2 oz copper, 3 mm wide, 8 mm per path, with parallel layers, a realistic estimate is **R_Cu ≈ 0.4 mΩ**.

$$P_{cond} = 2(80)^2(1.76\times10^{-3}) + (80)^2(0.4\times10^{-3}) = 22.5 + 2.6 = \mathbf{25.1\ W}$$

### 2.3 Per-device split

Each phase leg is active for 2/3 of the electrical period. With duty D:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS} D, \qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS}(1-D) + \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS}$$

At D → 1, the worst single device draws **⅓ × 80² × 1.76 mΩ × 2 ≈ 7.5 W peak-average**. This occurs in the LS devices because they spend more time conducting.

### 2.4 Self-consistency iteration

R_DS depends on T_j, and T_j depends on loss. Solve by fixed-point iteration:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_a + \Delta T_{board}(t) + P_{FET}\!\left(T_j^{(n)}\right) R_{\theta,j\text{-}b}$$

The iteration converges in 3–4 steps. If it diverges (thermal runaway), the burst rating is invalid at that duration.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing high-side FET is hard-switched. The synchronous low side commutates softly through its body diode during dead time.

### 3.1 Switching transition time

$$t_{sw} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}\,R_{g,tot}}{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}$$

With R_g,tot = 4.7 Ω external + 1.5 Ω internal + driver output resistance (≈ 15 Ω FD6288 source), V_drv = 10 V, and V_pl = 3.2 V:

$$t_r \approx \frac{9\text{nC}\times 21\Omega}{6.8\text{V}} = 28\text{ ns}, \qquad t_f \approx 22\text{ ns (stronger sink)}$$
