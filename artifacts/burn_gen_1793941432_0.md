### Architecture assumption

The H743 does not drive the FETs. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU (STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32), a 3-phase gate driver, and six MOSFETs. The H743 talks to each ESC MCU over one bidirectional DShot600 wire.

### Feasibility warning

A 20×20 mm board carrying 80 A per motor on all four motors at once is not thermally sustainable. Section 3 quantifies this: it is a burst rating of roughly 1 second, not a continuous rating. State this explicitly in the product rating.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step synchronous commutation

In each 60° electrical sector, two phases conduct:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. The low-side FET of the same leg conducts for (1−D) as the synchronous rectifier.
- **Leg B (return leg):** the low-side FET is on for the whole sector.

So the phase current always flows through exactly two FET channels in series, regardless of D:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

R_ds depends on junction temperature, and junction temperature depends on loss. Solve this self-consistently using the total thermal resistance R_θ derived in Section 3:

$$T_j = T_a + R_\theta\,P_{tot}(T_j)$$

Collecting terms gives a closed form:

$$T_j = \frac{T_a + R_\theta\,[P_{sw} + 2I^2 R_{25}(1 - 25\alpha)]}{1 - R_\theta\,2I^2R_{25}\alpha}$$

The denominator must stay positive. If it goes to zero or negative, the design is in thermal runaway.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per ESC | P_cond, 4 ESCs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.50 mΩ | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| 100 °C | 2.01 mΩ | 25.7 W | 102.9 W |
| 125 °C | 2.18 mΩ | 27.8 W | 111.4 W |

### 1.4 Per-FET loss

Each FET conducts for 120° of every 360° electrical cycle. Using the 125 °C value:

$$P_{FET,avg} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2 R_{ds} = \tfrac{1}{3}(6400)(2.18\text{ mΩ}) \approx 4.6\text{ W}$$

Averaging over the commutation cycle is valid here. A 14-pole motor at 30,000 rpm has an electrical frequency of 3.5 kHz, a period of about 286 µs. That is far shorter than the die-to-case thermal time constant (milliseconds).

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. It produces one hard turn-on and one hard turn-off per PWM period. The terms below are per ESC.

| Loss term | Equation | Value at 80 A, 26 V |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw = 0.5·26·80·30 ns·48 k | 1.50 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_SD·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·400 ns·48 k | 1.23 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 50 nC·26·48 k | 0.06 W |
