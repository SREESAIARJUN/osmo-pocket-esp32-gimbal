# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have the Job #35 specification sheet, so this design is parameterized. Every number marked **[A]** is an assumption you should replace with your hardware values.

## 0. Assumed Architecture

| Item | Assumption [A] |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G4 @ 170 MHz, FPU, 3× motor timers |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R = 5.6 Ω, L = 2.2 mH, Kt ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701), one per joint |
| Current sense | Inline 2-shunt, 12-bit ADC |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, separated by lever arm **r** ≈ 30 mm, SPI @ 24 MHz |
| Kinematics | Yaw (outer, z) → Roll (middle, x) → Pitch (inner, y) |

**Loop rates and timing**

| Loop | Rate | Ts | Bandwidth |
|---|---|---|---|
| PWM / current (FOC) | 20 kHz | 50 µs | ω_c = 2π·1500 rad/s |
| Velocity (joint) | 4 kHz | 250 µs | ω_v = 2π·120 rad/s |
| Angle / attitude | 1 kHz | 1 ms | ω_θ = 2π·25 rad/s |
| IMU sampling (ODR) | 8 kHz | 125 µs | consumed at 4 kHz after filtering |

The rule of thumb is to keep a separation of at least 4–5× between cascaded loop bandwidths: ω_c/ω_v ≈ 12 and ω_v/ω_θ ≈ 5.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle from the mechanical encoder angle θ_m:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{m,0}) \mod 2\pi$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park:**

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$
