# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #6): Power-Stage, Thermal and Bidirectional DShot Model

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**Who does what.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600. It does not commutate the motors.

Each of the four ESC channels has its own MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). That MCU:
- produces the 48 kHz complementary PWM,
- decodes DShot,
- returns eRPM telemetry.

ArduPilot has no native FOC or 6-step drive for a 4-in-1 power stage, so the losses below are set by the ESC MCU firmware, not by the H743.

**Baseline parameters.** These are representative values. Replace them with your actual BOM datasheet values before tape-out.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max (22.2 V nominal) |
| Burst current per motor | I_M | 80 A, ≤5 s |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz, center-aligned, synchronous rectification |
| MOSFET | – | 40 V N-ch, PDFN 3.3×3.3 or 5×6, one per switch position (6 per ESC, 24 total) |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

R_DS(T_j) = R_DS(on),25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 100 °C: R_DS = 0.80 · (1 + 0.0045 · 75) = **1.07 mΩ**

### 1.2 Per-ESC conduction loss

The active path is always one high-side switch plus one low-side switch. With synchronous rectification, the low-side of the PWMing leg carries the freewheel current during (1 − D), so the path resistance is ≈ 2R_DS regardless of duty cycle:

P_cond,ESC = I_M² · 2R_DS(T_j) = 80² · 2 · 1.07×10⁻³ = **13.7 W**

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each phase conducts for 2/6 of the electrical period: 1/6 on its high side and 1/6 on its low side, while sharing the PWM leg.

- **Instantaneous loss in a conducting FET:** I²R = 6.85 W
- **Average per FET:** ≈ 13.7 / 6 ≈ **2.3 W**. Worst case is ~2.8 W for the PWMing high-side at D near 1.

### 1.4 Copper and interconnect

Assume ≈ 0.5 mΩ of effective path resistance per ESC. This covers the battery pad → bus plane → FET → phase pad route, with 2 oz inner layers and parallel planes.

P_Cu,ESC = 80² · 0.5×10⁻³ = **3.2 W**

### 1.5 Current-sense shunt (if fitted)

A 0.5 mΩ shunt in the common return adds 3.2 W per ESC. Recommendation: use a single 0.25 mΩ shunt per board for total current, or use phase-current-free sensing.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the PWMing high-side FET hard-switches. The synchronous low-side commutates softly through its body diode during dead time.

### 2.1 V–I overlap loss

P_sw = ½ · V_bus · I_M · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw
= 0.5 · 25.2 · 80 · 30×10⁻⁹ · 48 000 = **1.45 W**

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction

P_dt = V_f · I_M · 2t_dt · f_sw = 0.8 · 80 · 200×10⁻⁹ · 48 000 = **0.61 W**

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

P_rr = V_bus · Q_rr · f_sw = 25.2 · 50×10⁻⁹ · 48 000 = **0.06 W**

The low-side Q_rr also increases high-side turn-on loss. Fold that into a margin: ×1.3 applied to P_sw gives ≈ **1.9 W**.

### 2.4 Output capacitance

P_oss = ½ · C_oss · V² · f_sw = 0.5 · 2×10⁻⁹ · 635 · 48 000 = **0.03 W**

### 2.5 Gate drive (driver dissipation, two FETs switching)

P_g = 2 · Q_g · V_GS · f_sw = 2 · 50n · 10 · 48k = **0.05 W**

This is dissipated in the driver and R_g, not in the FET die.

### 2.6 Loss budget per ESC at 80 A, 48 kHz, T_j = 100 °C

| Component | Power (W) |
|---|---|
| Conduction (MOSFETs) | 13.7 |
| Switching (with Q_rr margin) | 1.9 |
| Dead time | 0.61 |
| C_oss + gate | 0.08 |
| Copper | 3.2 |
| **Total per ESC** | **≈ 19.5 W** |
| **Total, 4 ESCs at 80 A simultaneously** | **≈ 78 W** |

**Frequency sensitivity.** Switching-related loss is ≈ 2.6 W per ESC at 48 kHz and scales linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz you would save ≈ 1.3 W per ESC (~5 W per board). The 48 kHz choice buys lower current ripple and acoustic benefits, not efficiency.

### 2.7 Input capacitor ripple

I_C,rms ≈ I_M · √(D(1 − D)). At D = 0.5 this is **40 A_rms per ESC**.

Because PWM phases are unsynchronized across the four MCUs, the board-level combined ripple is ≈ √4 · 40 = 80 A_rms worst case.

You need:
- ≥ 4× 10 µF/50 V X7R 1206 per ESC, placed at the half-bridges,
- plus an external low-ESR bulk electrolytic (≥ 1000 µF/35 V) on the battery leads.

On-board MLCCs alone cannot absorb this ripple.
