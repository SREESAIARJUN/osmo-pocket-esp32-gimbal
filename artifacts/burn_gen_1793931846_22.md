## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Flag

**The architecture needs one correction first.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. In any 4-in-1 design, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot to dedicated ESC MCUs. Those are typically 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or 4× EFM8/STM32F051 running BLHeli. The ESC MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM.

```
LiPo 6S ─┬─► 4-in-1 power stage (24 MOSFETs, 4× 3-phase drivers, 4× ESC MCUs)
         │        ▲ DShot600 bidir (1 wire/motor, half-duplex)
         └─► FC regulators ─► STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ─┘
```

**Feasibility warning.** As derived in §3, **80 A/channel burst on a nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) AIO cannot be sustained for more than about 1–3 s** without exceeding Tj = 150 °C. If "80 A" means *total board* current (≈20 A/motor), the design is comfortable. Please confirm which interpretation applies.

## 2. Loss Model (Per ESC Channel)

### 2.1 Commutation Topology

AM32/BLHeli use 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM). At any instant, two phase legs carry current:

- **PWM leg:** current alternates between the high-side (on-time, duty D) and the complementary low-side (off-time, 1−D).
- **Static leg:** one low-side FET is fully on for the whole 60° electrical sector.

So the conduction path always contains exactly **two R_DS(on)** in series, regardless of duty cycle.

### 2.2 Conduction Loss

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

$$P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

At T_j ≈ 135 °C: R_DS = 1.2 mΩ × 1.6 ≈ 1.9 mΩ (rounded to 1.8–1.9 mΩ).

$$P_{cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{23.0\ W/channel}$$

Distribution across FETs:

- **Static low-side FET:** 11.5 W during its sector.
- **PWM high-side and low-side pair:** 11.5 W combined.

Commutation rotates these roles, but at low eRPM or during a stall the hot spot does not move. Design for the **instantaneous single-FET value of 11.5 W**.

### 2.3 Switching Loss (PWM Leg Only)

**Hard-switching V–I overlap:**
$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{in} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ns} \times 48\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery:**
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{in} f_{sw} = 40\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

**Output capacitance:**
$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{in} f_{sw} \approx 0.018\ \text{W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction (2 edges per period):**
$$P_{dt} = V_F \cdot I \cdot 2t_{dt} \cdot f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**Gate drive** (dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET):
$$P_g = Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 50\text{nC}\times10\times48\text{k} = 0.024\ \text{W per switching FET}$$

$$P_{sw,total} \approx 1.94 + 0.05 + 0.02 + 0.61 \approx \mathbf{2.6\ W/channel}$$

**Design lever:** dead time contributes about 25 % of the switching loss. Cutting t_dt from 100 ns to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W/channel, but you must verify there is no shoot-through at hot V_th.

### 2.4 Copper and Interconnect Loss

Battery pads to the bus feed 4 × 80 A = **320 A peak**. Even a total bus resistance of 0.5 mΩ (pads, planes, vias) adds:

$$P_{bus} = 320^2 \times 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega = 51\ \text{W}$$

**This is the hidden killer in nano AIOs.** Phase-trace losses add roughly 1–3 W/channel depending on copper weight.

```
Tj ─[Rθjc 1.2 K/W]─ Tcase ─[Rθc-pcb 3.0 K/W: pad+vias]─ T_pcb ─[Rθpcb-a]─ Ta
```

Assumptions behind the values:

- **R_θpcb-a:** a 6-layer, 2 oz board with prop wash gives R_θpcb-a ≈ 4 K/W for the whole board **[ASSUMED]**.
- **C_pcb:** about 5 g FR-4 × 1.1 J/g·K plus about 1.5 g Cu × 0.385 J/g·K gives C_pcb ≈ 6 J/K.

### 3.2 Steady-State Check

$$\Delta T_{pcb,ss} = 150\ \text{W} \times 4\ \text{K/W} = 600\ \text{K}$$

This is impossible, so **80 A is strictly a transient rating.**
