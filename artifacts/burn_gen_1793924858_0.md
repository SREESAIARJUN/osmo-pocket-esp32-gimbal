## 0. Design Basis and Architecture

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 runs **ArduPilot** as the flight controller. It does **not** commutate the motors. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071. These are the standard parts for 4-in-1 ESCs, and both support bidirectional DShot with eRPM telemetry.

```
            ┌──────────────── FC SECTION ────────────────┐
 VBAT ──►5V/3V3 ─► STM32H743VIT6 ── SPI ─ IMU (ICM-42688-P)
                     │  TIM3_CH3/CH4, TIM5_CH1/CH2  (BDShot600, 1-wire, bidir)
                     ▼
            ┌──────────── ESC SECTION (×4) ──────────────┐
 VBAT ─► 10V LDO/buck ─► Gate driver (3-ph, bootstrap)
          3V3 ─► ESC MCU (AM32) ─► 6× PWM ─► driver ─► 6× N-MOSFET ─► motor
                     ▲ BEMF dividers (A,B,C,COM), shunt + amp
```
