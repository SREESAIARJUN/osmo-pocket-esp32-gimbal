## 0. Scope and Architecture

Two points shape the rest of the design.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is flight-control firmware, not ESC firmware. The sound architecture has two layers:
- **STM32H743** runs ArduPilot. It sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs and receives eRPM telemetry back.
- **4× AT32F421 (or STM32G071)** run AM32 or BLHeli_32. Each drives a three-phase gate driver and six MOSFETs.
