## 0. Architecture Correction (Read First)

The phrase "STM32H743 4-in-1 ESC" mixes two subsystems. They must be separated for the math to be valid.

| Subsystem | MCU | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) or VIH6 | ArduPilot | Generates DShot600 frames and decodes bidirectional eRPM telemetry |
| 4-in-1 ESC | 4× AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) | AM32 / BLHeli_32-class | 6-step or FOC commutation, **48 kHz PWM**, gate driving |

- The H743 never switches MOSFETs. The 48 kHz switching losses belong to the ESC MCUs.
- The DShot600 DMA allocation belongs to the H743.
- Both can sit on one PCB stack (AIO), but they are separate timing domains.

**Reality check on "80 A burst" at nano size (20×20 mm).** The model in §4 shows that 4 channels × 80 A dissipates about 125 W into roughly 2 J/K of board thermal mass. That is survivable for about 1 s, not as a sustained rating. The burst rating must therefore be specified with a duration and a channel count.
