## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke transform:

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset):

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 Plant model

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

### 1.3 PI design by pole-zero cancellation

Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole (K_i/K_p = R/L):

$$K_p^{(i)} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i^{(i)} = R\,\omega_c$$

With this choice the closed loop is first-order:

$$\frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

Add decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(e_d) - \hat\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(e_q) + \hat\omega_e L i_d + \hat\omega_e\lambda_m$$

### 1.4 Voltage limiting and anti-windup

SVPWM can produce at most a circle of radius V_max = V_bus/√3.

- **Priority:** d-axis first, then q-axis gets the remaining headroom: |v_q| ≤ √(V_max² − v_d²).
- **Anti-windup (back-calculation):** with K_aw = 1/K_p,
  $$\dot x_I = K_i e + K_{aw}(v_{sat} - v_{unsat})$$

### 1.5 SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)

$$v_{a,b,c} \leftarrow \text{inverse Clarke}(v_\alpha, v_\beta),\quad v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max + \min),\quad d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}$$
