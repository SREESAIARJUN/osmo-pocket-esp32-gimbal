# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal and DShot Timing Model

The prompt names no power-stage parts, board size or battery, so I fixed a reference design (§0). Every number derives from those parameters. If your BOM differs, substitute your datasheet values into the equations; the structure of the model does not change.

**Key architectural point:** the STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs. It sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class), and each of those runs the 48 kHz PWM. The model is split along that boundary.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_0\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 0.90 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.305 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.44 mΩ |

Because loss raises temperature and temperature raises R, this forms a feedback loop. It is solved in §4.4.

### 1.2 Six-step current-path accounting

In each 60° sector, two phases conduct.

- **PWM'd leg:** the high side (HS) conducts for duty D. The low side (LS) synchronously rectifies for 1 − D.
- **Sink leg:** the LS is fully on.

Each phase spends 1/3 of the electrical cycle as the PWM source, 1/3 as the sink, and 1/3 floating. Averaged over one electrical revolution:

$$
P_{HS} = I^2 R_{DS}\frac{D}{3}, \qquad
P_{LS} = I^2 R_{DS}\left(\frac{1-D}{3} + \frac{1}{3}\right)
$$

Per leg, P_HS + P_LS = (2/3) I² R_DS. Over three legs, this gives an important invariant:

$$
\boxed{P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)}
$$

This holds because there are always exactly two R_DS(on) in series in the current path.
