# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC (Job #66): Power, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

Four constraints shape the architecture. Each is either an assumption or a correction to the brief.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot cannot run six-step or FOC commutation at 48 kHz. The architecture is therefore:
   - **H743 (ArduPilot):** issues bi-directional DShot600 commands.
   - **4× ESC MCUs** (AT32F421K8U7 running AM32): each handles 48 kHz complementary PWM, commutation, and eRPM telemetry.
2. **"80A burst" means 80 A per channel.** Four channels at 80 A each is a 320 A battery draw. That exceeds an XT60/XT90 lead and any nano-size board's thermal mass for more than about one second. Section 3 derives the actual time limit.
3. **Form factor is 30.5 × 30.5 mm, not 20 × 20 mm.** A 20 × 20 board cannot place 24 power FETs plus bulk capacitance at this current. Treat 30.5 mm as the minimum.
4. **The MOSFET parameters are representative, not from a specific part.** They model a 40 V, roughly 1 mΩ part in a 3.3 × 3.3 mm (PDFN) package. **Replace them with your selected part's datasheet values.** All equations are written parametrically so you can do this.
