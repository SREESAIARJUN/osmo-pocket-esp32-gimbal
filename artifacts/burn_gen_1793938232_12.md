# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #57): Design Model

**Scope:** Power-stage loss model (80 A burst, 48 kHz PWM), thermal transient budget, ArduPilot bi-directional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and a reference netlist.

> **Engineering caveats**
> 1. **80 A on a nano board is burst-only.** On a 20×20 mm mount (≈25×25 mm board), "80 A per channel" is physically achievable only for roughly 1–2 s bursts. Section 3 derives the number. If the requirement is sustained 80 A, the board must grow to 30.5×30.5 mm or larger with heavier copper. Do not market a continuous 80 A rating on this form factor.
> 2. **Parameters are illustrative.** Part parameters are representative of the device class (40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFETs; FD6288Q-class drivers). Every number must be re-run against the datasheets of the parts you actually procure.
> 3. **The netlist is not fab-ready.** It is an architecture-level reference netlist for schematic capture. It is not a DRC-verified fabrication output.
