## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, motor current flows through exactly **two MOSFETs** at every instant:
- **PWM-on:** HS(phase A) → winding → LS(phase B)
- **PWM-off (freewheel):** LS(phase A) → winding → LS(phase B)

So the total conduction loss per motor is independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Per-device distribution

Each leg spends 1/3 of the electrical period as the PWM leg, 1/3 as the static low leg, and 1/3 floating. With duty cycle D:

$$P_{HS} = \frac{D}{3}\, I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) \qquad P_{LS} = \left[\frac{1-D}{3} + \frac{1}{3}\right] I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$
