# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC Stack: Power, Thermal, and DShot Architecture (Job #15)

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Constraints

Three issues must be settled before the math.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs flight control on the H743 and emits DShot. Commutation, with its 48 kHz PWM and zero-crossing or FOC loop, runs on four dedicated ESC MCUs: AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or BLHeli_32/Bluejay-class targets. Driving 24 MOSFETs at 48 kHz from the ArduPilot scheduler is not a supported or sane configuration. The design below is an H743 FC board stacked on a 4-in-1 ESC board, connected by an 8-pin harness.

**2. "Nano" and 80 A cannot be simultaneously true for sustained operation.** On a 20×20 mm ESC, 80 A per channel produces about 100 W of heat in roughly 4 cm² of copper. The model in §3 shows that 80 A is a **burst rating of about 1–2 s**, not a continuous one. I've modelled a 30.5×30.5 mm ESC (36×36 mm board outline) as the minimum credible platform. 20×20 numbers are given for comparison.

**3. Component values are representative, not vendor-locked.** MOSFET parameters are typical of 40 V, ~1 mΩ, 3.3×3.3 or 5×6 mm trench parts. Replace them with your selected part's datasheet values; every equation is parametric.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Path

In 6-step commutation with complementary PWM, phase current I always flows through exactly **two** MOSFETs in series:

- **ON interval (D):** high-side of leg A, then the motor, then the static low-side of leg B.
- **OFF interval (1−D):** the synchronous low-side of leg A replaces the high-side, then the motor, then the low-side of leg B.

Because both the high-side and synchronous low-side carry the same R_DS(on), the duty cycle cancels:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2\left[R_{HS}D + R_{LS,sync}(1-D) + R_{LS,B}\right] = 2I^2R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

with temperature dependence modelled as:

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) \approx R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)\right], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

### 1.2 Numbers at 80 A

| Condition | Calculation | P_cond per channel |
|---|---|---|
| Cold (T_j = 25 °C) | 2 · 6400 · 1.0 mΩ | **12.8 W** |
| Hot (T_j = 125 °C) | 2 · 6400 · 1.6 mΩ | **20.5 W** |
| Copper + shunt | 6400 · 0.5 mΩ | **3.2 W** |

### 1.3 Thermal Runaway Coupling

Conduction loss rises with junction temperature, so the loop must be closed:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + P(T_j)\,R_{th} \;\Rightarrow\; T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + P_{25}R_{th}(1 - 25\alpha)}{1 - P_{25}R_{th}\,\alpha}$$

This loop is only stable if **P₂₅·R_th·α < 1**. With per-FET P₂₅ ≈ 2.1 W and α = 0.006 K⁻¹, stability requires R_th < 79 K/W. That condition is satisfied, but the effective gain is about 10 %, so it must be included in the burst model.

### 1.4 Per-Device Distribution

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I^2 R_{hot}}{3} = \frac{6400 \times 1.6\text{ m}\Omega}{3} = \mathbf{3.41\ W}$$
