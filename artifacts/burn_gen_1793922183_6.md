# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC (Job #52): Architecture, Loss Model, Thermal Budget, Timer/DMA Plan, Netlists

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Key Conclusions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600. The 4-in-1 ESC uses four separate ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). Those ESC MCUs produce the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 never drives gates directly. This split matters for the DMA plan (Section 4) and the netlists (Section 6).
