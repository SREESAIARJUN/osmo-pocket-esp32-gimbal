## 0. Architecture Decision (Read First)

**The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no BLDC field-control stack, and running four sensorless six-step loops alongside the EKF and scheduler is not viable. The industry-correct and ArduPilot-compatible partition is:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz | ArduPilot | IMU, EKF, mixer, Bi-DShot600 master, harmonic notch |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421 / STM32G071-class (one per motor) | AM32 (or BLHeli_32-compatible) | 48 kHz PWM, BEMF commutation, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive ×4 | 3-phase bootstrap driver (FD6288Q-class) | n/a | Drives 6 N-FETs per channel |

The interface between domains is four Bi-DShot600 lines plus one shared current-sense analog line and one battery-voltage line into the H743 ADC.
