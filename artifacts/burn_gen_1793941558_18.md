## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Feasibility Flags

The numbers below depend on these assumptions. Change any of them and the math must be re-run.

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Topology | STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU (e.g. STM32G071 or AT32F421) running AM32, driving 6 MOSFETs through a 3-phase gate driver. The H743 does **not** commutate motors. |
| Battery | 6S, 25.2 V max |
| Burst | 80 A DC-link current per motor, trapezoidal 6-step with synchronous rectification |
| PWM | 48 kHz (an AM32 option) |
| MOSFETs | Generic 40 V, 3.3×3.3 DFN class with **assumed** parameters (Table 1). Replace them with your vendor datasheet values. |
| Board | "Nano" form factor, about 26×26 mm usable, 20×20 mm mounting, 6-layer, 2 oz copper |

**Flags to resolve before layout:**

1. **Burst ratings are time-limited.** Running four motors at 80 A simultaneously dissipates about 75 W on roughly 7 cm² of board. That is only sustainable for seconds (Section 4).
2. **Connector rating.** 4 × 80 A = 320 A of battery current, which no nano-format battery pad or connector can carry. The realistic interpretation is single-motor 80 A peaks with a total-current limit in AM32/ArduPilot.
3. **Placeholder parameters.** Every MOSFET and gate-driver value in this document is a placeholder for your datasheets, not a qualified design.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current waveform per FET (6-step, complementary PWM, sync rectification)

In each 120° electrical sector:
- One leg is PWM-switched. Its high-side (HS) conducts for duty D and its low-side (LS) for 1−D.
- Another leg's LS is held fully on as the current return.

Each leg spends 1/3 of the cycle as the PWM leg and 1/3 as the return leg. With flat-top current I (ripple neglected, so L/R ≫ 1/f_sw):

$$I_{HS,rms}^2 = I^2\frac{D}{3}, \qquad I_{LS,rms}^2 = I^2\left(\frac{1-D}{3}+\frac{1}{3}\right)$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 3\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)\,I^2\left[\frac{D}{3}+\frac{2-D}{3}\right] = 2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)\,I^2$$

Two things follow:
- The total is independent of D, because two FETs are always in series with the phase current.
- The LS devices are always the hotter ones.
