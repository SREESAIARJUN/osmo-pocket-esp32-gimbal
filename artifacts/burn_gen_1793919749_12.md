## 1. Field-Oriented Control

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{0}) \bmod 2\pi$$

$\theta_0$ is found at startup by aligning to the d-axis: apply $v_d$ with $v_q=0$ at $\theta_e=0$, then record $\theta_m$.

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt 3}\quad(\text{using } i_a+i_b+i_c=0)$$

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.3 Plant model in the rotor frame

$$v_d = R_s i_d + L_s\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L_s i_q$$
$$v_q = R_s i_q + L_s\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L_s i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m,\qquad \lambda_m = \frac{2K_t}{3p}$$

$$\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2}p\lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.4 Current PI with decoupling (pole-zero cancellation)

Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole $R_s/L_s$:

$$K_{p,i} = L_s\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R_s\,\omega_{ci}$$

The closed loop is then first-order, $i/i^* = \omega_{ci}/(s+\omega_{ci})$.
