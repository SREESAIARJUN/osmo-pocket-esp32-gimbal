# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

I don't have the specification for Job #46, so this design rests on the assumptions in §0. Every equation is parameterised, so you can substitute your actual motor, encoder, and MCU numbers. Values marked **[VERIFY]** must be checked against your datasheets or hardware before flight.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 10 Ω, L ≈ 2 mH, Kt ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701 class), one per joint |
| MCU | STM32G4 @ 170 MHz with FPU, center-aligned PWM 20 kHz, synchronized ADC |
| IMU-C | ICM-42688-P on the **camera plate**. Provides attitude and rate feedback. |
| IMU-B | ICM-42688-P on the **base/handle**. Provides the disturbance feed-forward and the vibration reference. |

| Loop | Rate | Target bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | ~1.5–2 kHz |
| IMU sampling + filtering | 8 kHz (ODR) | AAF ≈ 585 Hz |
| Velocity (inertial rate) | 4 kHz | ~60–100 Hz |
