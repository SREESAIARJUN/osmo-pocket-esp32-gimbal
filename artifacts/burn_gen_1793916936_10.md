> **Feasibility flag (important).** A true 20×20 "nano" cannot physically sustain 4 × 80 A. This model shows why. Treat "80 A burst" as a ≤1–2 s transient rating unless your thermal design is significantly better than these assumptions.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (Synchronous 6-Step / Complementary PWM)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.3 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.885 mΩ** (typical). With the max-spec part: **2.47 mΩ**.
