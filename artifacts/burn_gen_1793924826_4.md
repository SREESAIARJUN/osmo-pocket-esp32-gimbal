# STM32H743 FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Thermal, Switching, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Design Package

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Two premises need correcting before the math is meaningful.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In a 4-in-1 stack, each ESC channel runs its own MCU (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to each ESC MCU over bi-directional DShot600. The design below keeps that partition.
2. **80A per channel on a "nano" board is a burst-only rating.** At 4×80A the total loss is about 90–110 W. A 20×20 or 25×25 mm board cannot reject that continuously. Section 3 quantifies how long the burst can last.

The MOSFET parameters below are representative values for a 40V, 5×6 mm (PDFN) trench FET. They are not a specific datasheet. Replace them with the values for your chosen part.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Temperature-dependent on-resistance:**

R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 · (1 + 0.5) = **1.5 mΩ**

**Six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification.** At any instant, phase current flows through exactly one high-side path and one low-side path. Over a PWM period:

- The high-side FET of the "PWM phase" carries current for D·T.
- Its complementary low-side FET carries current for (1−D)·T.
- So the PWM leg as a whole conducts for the full period.
- The low-side FET of the "return phase" conducts for the full period.

This gives:

P_cond,ch = I² · R_ds(T_j) · [D + (1−D)] + I² · R_ds(T_j) = **2 · I² · R_ds(T_j)**

The result is independent of duty cycle, to first order.

**At 80 A, T_j = 125 °C:**

P_cond = 2 · 6400 · 1.5 mΩ = **19.2 W per channel**

**Ripple correction.** With PWM ripple ΔI the RMS current rises slightly: I_rms² = I² + ΔI²/12.

For a low-inductance motor at 48 kHz, take ΔI ≈ 10 A:

- I_rms² = 6400 + 8.3 = 6408.3 A²
- This adds less than 0.2 %, so it is negligible.

**Copper and shunt losses.** These are often larger than designers expect. The phase pad, trace and via path to the motor is about 0.3 mΩ per conducting path:

P_Cu ≈ 2 · 80² · 0.3 mΩ ≈ **3.8 W per channel**

A common current shunt on the battery bus is a separate problem:

- At 4 × 80 A = 320 A through 0.25 mΩ: P = 320² · 0.25 m = **25.6 W**
- Recommendation: use a 0.1 mΩ, 4-terminal shunt, which gives **10.2 W**.
- Alternatively, sense per channel with a lower total resistance.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

The high-side FET of the PWM leg hard-switches. The low-side FET switches at roughly zero voltage, because current is in the body diode during dead time.

**Hard-switching (V–I overlap):**

P_sw = ½ · V_bus · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw
= 0.5 · 25.2 · 80 · 40e-9 · 48e3 = **1.94 W**

**Reverse recovery** (the low-side body diode recovers when the high-side FET turns on):

P_rr = Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw = 50e-9 · 25.2 · 48e3 = **0.06 W**

Note that Q_rr grows with di/dt and with T_j. Budget 2–3× this value at 80 A, which gives about 0.15 W.

**Output capacitance:**

P_oss = ½ · C_oss · V_bus² · f_sw = 0.5 · 1.5e-9 · 635 · 48e3 = **0.023 W**

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two transitions per period):

P_dt = 2 · V_f · I · t_dead · f_sw = 2 · 0.8 · 80 · 100e-9 · 48e3 = **0.61 W**

**Gate drive** (dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET):

P_g = Q_g · V_drv · f_sw = 60e-9 · 10 · 48e3 = 0.029 W per switching FET, which is negligible.

### Per-channel total at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

| Loss term | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction (2 FET paths) | 19.20 |
| Hard switching | 1.94 |
| Reverse recovery (derated) | 0.15 |
| C_oss | 0.02 |
| Dead time | 0.61 |
| Copper (phase path) | 3.80 |
| **Channel total** | **≈ 25.7 W** |
| **4 channels** | **≈ 103 W** |
| Plus 0.1 mΩ bus shunt | +10.2 W, giving **≈ 113 W** |

**Frequency sensitivity:** dP/df_sw ≈ (1.94 + 0.15 + 0.02 + 0.61) / 48 kHz = **56.7 mW per kHz per channel**.

- Dropping to 24 kHz saves about 1.4 W per channel.
- Conduction loss dominates by about 7:1, so **R_ds(on) and copper are the levers that matter, not f_sw.**

### Worst-case single FET

The high-side FET on the PWM leg carries a share of the conduction loss plus all the hard-switching loss. Conservatively assign it a full path's conduction:

P_FET,max ≈ 80² · 1.5 m + 1.94 + 0.15 + 0.3 ≈ **12.0 W**

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3a. Steady state: proving it is burst-only

T_j = T_board + P_FET · (R_θJC + R_θCB) = T_board + 12 · 1.5 = **T_board + 18 K**

The board must therefore stay at or below about 107 °C for T_j ≤ 125 °C.

For a 30×30 mm 6-layer board with 2 oz copper in prop-wash airflow, board-to-ambient resistance is roughly R_θBA ≈ 1.5–3 K/W. At 100 W:

- ΔT_board ≈ 150–300 K
- **Continuous 80A operation is impossible.**

The sustainable continuous current is set by a ΔT_board of about 60 K, which allows roughly 30 W total:

- 4 · (2 · I² · 1.3 mΩ + I² · 0.6 mΩ + ~1 W) ≈ 30 W
- This gives **I_cont ≈ 32 A per channel.**
