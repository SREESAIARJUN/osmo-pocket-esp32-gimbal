# STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC (80 A Burst): System Architecture Model

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

I don't have prior context for "Job #25", so this model is built on the assumptions below. Every equation is parameterized, so you can substitute your own values.

| Parameter | Symbol | Assumed value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max (22.2 V nominal) |
| Burst phase current per motor | I_ph | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| Commutation | — | 6-step trapezoidal, complementary (synchronous) PWM |
| MOSFET (e.g., 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PQFN class) | R_ds(on),25 | 1.2 mΩ |
| R_ds(on) temperature factor at T_j = 125 °C | k_T | 1.7 |

| Body diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |

| Form factor | — | "Nano": 20×20 mm or 25.5×25.5 mm mounting |

**Architectural clarification.** The H743 does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and produces bi-directional DShot600. The two layers are modeled separately below.

## 1. Conduction Losses at 80 A Burst

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM
During any 60° electrical sector, current flows through exactly two conducting devices:
- one high side that is PWM'd (or its complementary low side during the off-time), and
- one low side held fully on in the return phase.

So at every instant, the bridge has 2 × R_ds(on) in series with I_ph.

### 1.2 Equations

Per-FET RMS currents. Each FET is active for 1/3 of the electrical period.

$$I_{HS,rms} = I_{ph}\sqrt{D/3}, \qquad I_{LS,rms} = I_{ph}\sqrt{\tfrac{(1-D)}{3} + \tfrac{1}{3}}$$

Total bridge conduction loss is independent of D under synchronous rectification:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

with $R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$, where α ≈ 0.007 /K gives k_T ≈ 1.7 at 125 °C.

### 1.3 Numbers (per ESC channel)

| Case | R_ds(on) | P_cond (FETs) | Copper (R_Cu ≈ 0.3 mΩ, 2 oz, short paths) | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cold (T_j = 25 °C) | 1.2 mΩ | 2·6400·0.0012 = **15.4 W** | 1.9 W | 17.3 W |
| Hot (T_j = 125 °C) | 2.04 mΩ | 2·6400·0.00204 = **26.1 W** | 1.9 W | 28.0 W |

All four motors at burst, hot: **≈ 112 W of conduction loss.**

Per-FET average when hot is ≈ 26.1 / 6 ≈ 4.35 W. The low side carries the worst case: at D → 0 its I_rms² factor approaches 2/3.
