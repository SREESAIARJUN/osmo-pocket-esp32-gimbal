# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, BDShot600 DMA Plan, and Reference Netlists

## 0. Feasibility Findings

These findings constrain everything below.

1. **The H743 should not commutate the motors.** Four sensorless BLDC channels need 24 gate signals, 4–12 BEMF comparators or ADC channels, and µs-level commutation timing. That cannot coexist with a 400 Hz+ attitude loop on one core. The architecture used here is the industry-standard split:
   - STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller.
   - Four dedicated ESC MCUs (STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32) drive the FETs.
   - The link between them is bidirectional DShot600.
2. **80 A × 4 on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is a burst of roughly 1–2 seconds, not a rating.** The thermal model in §3 shows this. A credible continuous rating on 20×20 is about 15–25 A per motor in prop wash. The 80 A figure is achievable only as a short, current-limited transient.
3. **Component values are representative.** Values like R_DS(on) and Q_gd come from a typical 40 V PQFN 3.3×3.3 FET class. Substitute your actual BOM datasheet values into the equations before sign-off. The equations are the deliverable; the numbers illustrate them.
