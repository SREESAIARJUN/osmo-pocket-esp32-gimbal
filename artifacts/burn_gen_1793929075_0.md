# STM32H743 Nano AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: 80A Burst Thermal, 48kHz Switching Loss, and Bi-Directional DShot600 DMA Design

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Corrections

I don't have prior context for "Job #5," so this document states every assumption explicitly. Substitute your actual BOM values into the equations. Each section is parameterized for that purpose.

**Architectural correction.** In an AIO with an STM32H743 and a 4-in-1 ESC, the H743 runs ArduPilot and does **not** commutate the motors. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32, BLHeli_32, or Bluejay. Typical choices are 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071 (AM32), or 4× EFM8BB21 (Bluejay). The H743 sends DShot600 frames and decodes the bidirectional eRPM replies. All of the "48 kHz" switching analysis applies to the ESC MCUs' PWM, not the H743.

**Feasibility flag.** 80A per motor on all four motors simultaneously is about 85W of dissipation in a 20×20 mm board. Steady state is physically impossible. Section 3 derives the actual burst duration, which is about 1 to 1.5 s from a warm start.
