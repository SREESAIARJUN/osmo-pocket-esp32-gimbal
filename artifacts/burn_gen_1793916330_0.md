## 0. Architecture Corrections

Two premises in the brief need to change before the math is meaningful.

### 0.1 The H743 cannot be the ESC commutation controller

ArduPilot requires the H743's timers to *generate* DShot. Separately, driving 4 × 3-phase bridges needs 12 complementary PWM pairs plus BEMF comparators per motor. The H743 has only TIM1/TIM8 (3 complementary pairs each) plus HRTIM, and it cannot also host a 1 kHz+ autopilot loop deterministically.

**Adopted architecture:**

| Block | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (400 MHz) | ArduPilot |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32, 120 MHz) | AM32 (supports bidir DShot600 and 48 kHz PWM) |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap, built-in dead time) | — |
| Power stage | 24 × N-MOSFET, 40 V, PQFN 3.3×3.3 | — |

### 0.2 "Nano" (20×20 mm) and 80 A per channel are thermally incompatible

The thermal model in §4 shows a 20×20 board sustains roughly 16 A/channel continuously and only 2–3 s at 80 A on all four channels. The model is therefore parameterised: §4 also gives results for a 30.5×30.5 board. I recommend specifying **80 A as a ≤2 s single/dual-motor burst**, or moving to 30.5×30.5.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path in 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM

At any commutation sector, two phases conduct. One leg's high-side (HS) is PWM'd at duty *D*. During OFF time, the complementary low-side (LS) on the same leg conducts synchronously. The return leg's LS is held ON.

So at every instant exactly **two FET channels** carry the phase current *I*:

- ON-time: HS_a + LS_b
- OFF-time: LS_a (sync) + LS_b
