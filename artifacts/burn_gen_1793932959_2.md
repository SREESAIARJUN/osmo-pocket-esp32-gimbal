# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #13): Systems Architecture & Mathematical Model

**Document scope:**
- Power-stage loss model (conduction at 80 A burst, switching at 48 kHz)
- Transient thermal model
- ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA plan (hwdef)
- Reference netlists

**Status:** Engineering reference design. All component parameters are stated as assumptions and must be verified against the datasheets of the parts you actually procure before layout release.

## 0. Architectural Clarification (Read First)

An STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFET bridges directly in any sane 4-in-1 design. The partitioning is:

| Domain | MCU | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100, 480 MHz max, run at 400 MHz) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) | Attitude control, generates 4× DShot600 and decodes bidir telemetry |
| ESC ×4 | 4× STM32G071 or AT32F421 (one per motor) | AM32 (or BLHeli_32-compatible) | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, BEMF sensing, eRPM reply |

The rest of this document models both domains.

**Feasibility flag:** 80 A per motor burst on a "nano" footprint (20×20 mm) is not physically credible. The copper cross-section and thermal mass are insufficient. This model therefore assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm AIO stack**:
- ESC board: 8-layer, 2 oz on all layers
- FC board: 6-layer, 1 oz

If you are truly constrained to 20×20, derate the burst to roughly 35–45 A. Section 3 shows why.

## 1. Assumed Power-Stage Parameters

| Symbol | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V (6S full), design point. Abs max 30 V transient | 40 V FETs, about 33% headroom against ringing |
| I_ph | 80 A burst, 55 A continuous target | Per motor |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | AM32 PWM setting |
| MOSFET | 40 V N-ch, PDFN 5×6 class | e.g., a 0.7 mΩ-class trench FET |
| R_ds(on),25 | 0.70 mΩ @ V_gs = 10 V | Assumption |
| α (tempco) | ≈ 0.0045 /K → R(100 °C) ≈ 1.0 mΩ | Typical trench silicon |
| Q_g | 50 nC | Assumption |
| t_r + t_f | 20 ns total, at R_g,ext = 3.3 Ω | Gate driver about 1.5 A source / 1.8 A sink |
