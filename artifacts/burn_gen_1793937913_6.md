# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with Integrated 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Model

**Revision A: analytical reference design. All component values are representative and must be verified against vendor datasheets before layout freeze.**

---

## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Up-Front Findings

This document makes four assumptions:

1. **The H743 is the flight controller only.** It does not commutate motors. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as an STM32G071 or AT32F421. The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600. This is the only architecture that ArduPilot supports for the H743.
2. **Battery:** 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V (4.2 V/cell). Design rating is 40 V MOSFETs.
3. **Switching:** f_sw = 48 kHz, 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM).
4. **80 A rating:** this is a per-channel, short-duration burst rating, not continuous.

**Finding 1: thermal feasibility.** A true 80 A × 4 burst on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is not thermally sustainable beyond about 3–4 seconds, even with ideal MOSFETs (§2.6).
- Treat "80 A burst" as a ≤2 s, single-or-dual-channel transient rating.
- Otherwise, move to a 30×30 mm footprint with a heatsink.

**Finding 2: bulk capacitor ripple.** At 80 A and D = 0.5, the bulk capacitors see about 40 A_rms of ripple per channel (§2.7). This, not the MOSFETs, is often the first failure point.
