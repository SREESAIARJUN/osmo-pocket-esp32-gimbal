# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #56)

## 0. Feasibility Constraints

These constraints shape every number below:

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to those MCUs over bi-directional DShot600. This is the correct and only practical partitioning.
2. **80A per channel is a burst rating, not continuous.** The model in §2 shows about 21W of dissipation per channel at 80A. On a 30.5×30.5mm ESC, the safe burst window is roughly 3s. On a true 20×20 "nano" ESC, 80A is not credible. Use 20×20 for the FC and 30.5×30.5 for the ESC.
3. **Part values are design assumptions.** Every MOSFET, driver and capacitor parameter is a representative value. Before layout freeze, re-run the model with the datasheet numbers of the parts you actually procure.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At the design point T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

Two FETs carry phase current at every instant:

- **Static leg:** one FET is held on for the whole 60° sector.
- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T. Its synchronous-rectifier low-side partner conducts for (1−D)·T.

The PWM-leg conduction therefore sums to one full R_ds regardless of duty cycle.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2\,(R_{ds,static} + R_{ds,PWM}) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

With R_Cu ≈ 0.15 mΩ for an 8-layer, 2 oz phase path:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 6400 \times (2.9 + 0.15)\,\text{m}\Omega = 18.6 + 0.96 \approx \mathbf{19.5\,W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET average

Over one electrical revolution, each of the 6 FETs conducts about 1/3 of the time:

$$\bar P_{FET,cond} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2 R_{ds} = \tfrac{1}{3}(6400)(1.45\text{m}) \approx 3.1\,W$$

The instantaneous peak per FET is 9.3 W. This matters at low eRPM, where sector duration approaches the FET's thermal time constant (see §3.3).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 V-I overlap (hard switching)

The transition time is approximately t_r ≈ t_f ≈ Q_gd / I_g ≈ 6.7 ns. Allowing for plateau and di/dt (L_s) effects, use a total overlap of t_r + t_f = 30 ns.

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(30\text{n})(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{1.45\,W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n}\times 25.2\times 48\text{k} = 0.06\,W$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.024\,W$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_f I_{ph}\,(2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8(80)(200\text{n})(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{0.61\,W}$$

This term is significant. Reducing dead time from 100 ns to 50 ns saves 0.3 W per channel, provided the driver's propagation matching allows it.

### 2.5 Gate drive

The gate-drive term below is dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET:

$$P_g = 2 \times Q_g V_{dr} f_{sw} = 2(60\text{n})(10)(48\text{k}) = 0.058\,W$$

### 2.6 Loss Summary

| Loss term | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FETs) | 18.6 W | 74.4 W |
| Copper / shunt path | 0.96 W | 3.8 W |
| V-I switching | 1.45 W | 5.8 W |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 W | 2.4 W |
| Q_rr + Q_oss + gate | 0.14 W | 0.6 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 21.8 W** | **≈ 87 W** |

At 80 A, conduction is about 90% of the loss. **Raising f_sw is cheap; R_ds(on) is everything.** Paralleling two FETs per switch (12 → 24 per channel) halves conduction loss but costs board area you do not have at 30.5 mm.

Efficiency check: the electrical input is 4 × 25.2 × 80 ≈ 8.06 kW at full duty, so η ≈ 98.9%.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Junction-to-board network (per FET)

$$\Delta T_{j-b} = \bar P_{FET}\,(R_{\theta jc} + R_{\theta c-b})$$

Average per-FET dissipation is about 21.8 / 6 ≈ 3.6 W. R_θjc is 1.2 K/W. R_θc-b is about 6 K/W through a thermal-via array (≥ 20 × 0.3 mm filled vias under the drain pad).

$$\Delta T_{j-b} \approx 3.6 \times 7.2 \approx \mathbf{26\,K}$$
