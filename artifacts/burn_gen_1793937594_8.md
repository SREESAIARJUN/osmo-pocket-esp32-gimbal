## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\quad \alpha\approx0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.8 mΩ**

### 1.2 6-step commutation with complementary PWM

In each 60° sector, one phase's high side is PWM'd at duty D, its low side rectifies synchronously for (1–D), and a second phase's low side is fully on. Each phase is driven high for 120° and low for 120° per electrical cycle.

Per-FET RMS-based average conduction loss:

$$P_{cond,HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS}\, D$$

$$P_{cond,LS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS}\,[1 + (1-D)]\cdot\tfrac{1}{2}\cdot 2 \;\;\to\;\; \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R_{DS}(2-D)\ \text{(worst-case LS, switching-leg rectification included)}$$

Per channel (always 2 conducting paths in series):

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

This holds independent of D when synchronous rectification is used and ripple is neglected.

**With ripple correction:**

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

where ΔI = V_bus·D(1–D)/(L_ph·f_sw).

For a typical 5" motor (L_ph ≈ 15 µH) at D = 0.5:

- ΔI = 25.2 × 0.25 / (15 µH × 48 kHz) ≈ 8.75 A
- ΔI²/12 ≈ 6.4 A², which is negligible against I² = 6400 A²
