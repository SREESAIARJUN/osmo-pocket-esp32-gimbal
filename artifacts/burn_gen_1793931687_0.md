# Job #17: STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Nano Form Factor)
## Electro-Thermal Model, Switching Analysis, Bi-Directional DShot600 DMA Allocation, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Executive Summary

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Conduction loss per channel @ 80 A, Tj = 125 °C | **19.2 W** |
| Switching and dead-time loss per channel @ 48 kHz, 6S | **2.2 W** |
| Total power stage loss, 4 × 80 A simultaneous | **≈ 86 W** (+ ~4 W copper) |
| Allowable 4 × 80 A burst duration on a 20 × 20 mm board | **≈ 2 s**, not the 10 s typically marketed |
| Allowable 1 × 80 A burst (others at 25 A) | ≈ 8–10 s (feasible) |
| DShot timer | TIM3 CH1–CH4, 5 DMA streams via DMAMUX (1 × UP + 4 × CHx) |

**Design finding:** A 20 × 20 mm board cannot sustain a 4 × 80 A burst for more than about 2 seconds. Section 3 derives this. Section 3.4 lists the options for fixing it.

**Assumed architecture.** In ArduPilot, bi-directional DShot is an FC-to-ESC protocol, so the design is split as follows:
- The STM32H743 is the flight controller running ArduPilot.
- Four independent ESC MCUs (AT32F421, running AM32) each drive a 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs.
- Commutation is 6-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) PWM.
