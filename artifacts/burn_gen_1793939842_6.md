## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current Path

In 6-step commutation with complementary PWM, phase current always flows through **exactly two conducting channels in series**:

- During D·T: high-side (HS) of phase A and low-side (LS) of phase B.
- During (1−D)·T: the synchronous LS of phase A (instead of its body diode) and LS of phase B.

The duty-independent result is:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

with

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds0}\,[1+\alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

### 1.2 Electrothermal Self-Consistency

R_ds depends on T_j, and T_j depends on loss. Solve by fixed-point iteration:

$$
T_j^{(k+1)} = T_{pcb} + \frac{P_{FET}(T_j^{(k)})}{1}\,(R_{\theta JC} + R_{\theta CB})
$$

This converges in 3–4 iterations. At T_j = 100 °C:

$$
R_{ds} = 1.0\,(1 + 0.0045 \cdot 75) = 1.34\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.34\text{e-}3 = \mathbf{17.1\ W}
$$

### 1.3 Dead-Time Body-Diode Conduction

$$
P_{dt} = 2\, V_f\, I_{ph}\, t_{dt}\, f_{sw} = 2 (0.7)(80)(200\text{ ns})(48\text{ kHz}) = \mathbf{1.08\ W}
$$

### 1.4 Copper and PCB Loss

Assume a 0.5 mΩ path resistance per channel (pours, vias, solder pads) **[A]**:

$$
P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{path} = 80^2 \cdot 0.5\text{e-}3 = \mathbf{3.2\ W}
$$

This loss is spread across the board rather than concentrated in the FETs, but it still contributes to board heating.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

### 2.1 Gate Drive Currents

$$
I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{DRV} - V_{pl}}{R_{drv,src} + R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int}}, \qquad
I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{drv,snk} + R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int}}
$$

Using R_drv ≈ 2 Ω (FD6288 effective) and R_g,ext = 2 Ω:

- I_g,on = 7 / 5 = 1.4 A
- I_g,off = 3 / 5 = 0.6 A

### 2.2 Transition Times

$$
t_{on} = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,on}} = 8.6\text{ ns}, \qquad t_{off} = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,off}} = 20\text{ ns}
$$

Common-source inductance on DFN parts with long traces adds about 30–50%. **Budget t_on + t_off ≈ 40 ns** as the conservative value.
