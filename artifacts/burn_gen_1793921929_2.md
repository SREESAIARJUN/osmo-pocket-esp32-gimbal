## 1. Loop Architecture and Rates

```
                 1 kHz                   4 kHz                      20 kHz
θ_ref ──►[Attitude P + FF]──ω_ref──►[Rate PI + accel FF]──iq_ref──►[Current PI dq + decoupling]──►SVPWM
              ▲                         ▲         ▲                   ▲
       fused attitude          fused gyro rate  cogging FF        ia, ib, θe
       (Mahony, dual IMU)      (joint-projected) (Fourier/LMS)     (encoder)
```

The loops are separated by bandwidth: the current loop runs at f_c ≈ 1.5 kHz, the rate loop at f_v ≈ 150 Hz, and the attitude loop at f_θ ≈ 15 Hz. This gives a ratio of ≥ 5–10× between adjacent loops, so each inner loop appears as approximately unity gain to the loop outside it.

## 2. Field-Oriented Current Control

### 2.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform** (with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset):

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 2.2 Plant model

Surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

Electromagnetic torque:

$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q$$
