# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bidirectional DShot Design (Job #55)

**Scope and caveats**

- **Design status.** This is a reference design. Every device parameter below is a stated assumption that must be replaced with values from your chosen datasheets. Pin assignments must be checked against the exact H743 package and the current ArduPilot `hwdef` documentation before layout.
- **Architecture.** The STM32H743 does **not** drive the MOSFETs. It runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600. Four dedicated ESC MCUs (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class, e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071) generate the 48 kHz PWM. The 48 kHz switching analysis therefore applies to the ESC MCUs, and the DShot/DMA analysis applies to the H743.
- **Feasibility flag.** 80 A per channel on a "nano" (20×20 mm) board is not thermally sustainable. Section 3 shows it is limited to bursts of about 1–3 s. A 30.5×30.5 mm board, or a split FC + ESC stack, is strongly recommended.
