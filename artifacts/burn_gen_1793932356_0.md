## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility Flags

Three issues need to be settled before the math.

| # | Issue | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **The H743 cannot commutate four BLDC motors under ArduPilot.** ArduPilot is a flight stack, not an ESC firmware. | The H743 runs ArduPilot and drives **4× bidirectional DShot600 links** to **4 dedicated ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class, running AM32). This is the standard "AIO" topology. |
| A2 | **An 80 A burst on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is not thermally credible.** The model in §3 shows about 85 W of dissipation at full burst.
