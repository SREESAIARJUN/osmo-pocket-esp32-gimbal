## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

Three issues need resolving before any numbers can be trusted.

**1. The H743 cannot commutate the motors under ArduPilot.** ArduPilot is a flight stack. It outputs DShot and does not run sensorless 6-step or FOC commutation. A "4-in-1 ESC" driven by an H743 therefore has two parts:
- **FC domain:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot, which generates bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry.
- **ESC domain:** four commutation MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32, e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071. Each drives a 3-phase gate driver and six or more MOSFETs.

The model below assumes this split. A single-MCU design would require custom firmware outside ArduPilot.

**2. "Nano" and "80A burst" conflict.** On a 20×20 mm board, four channels at 80A dissipate about 90 W. That raises the board temperature by over 50 K per second, which is not survivable. I have modelled a **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer board** and give the scaling so you can evaluate 20×20.

**3. MOSFET parameters are representative.** I used a generic 40 V DFN5×6 class device. Substitute your chosen part's datasheet values into the equations in §1–§3 before release.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-step, complementary PWM)

### 1.1 Current-path topology

At any commutation step, two phases conduct:
- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. The synchronous low-side FET conducts for 1−D.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET conducts continuously.

The instantaneous conduction loss per channel is:

$$
P_{cond}(t) = I^2 R_{eff}\,D + I^2 R_{eff}(1-D) + I^2 R_{eff} = 2\,I^2 R_{eff}
$$

This result is **independent of duty cycle**, which is a useful design property. With n paralleled FETs per position, R_eff = R_DS(on)(T_j) / n.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence and thermal runaway check

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

The junction temperature depends on its own losses:

$$
T_j = T_{ref} + R_{th}\cdot 2I^2R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

Solving gives:

$$
T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + 2I^2R_{25}R_{th}(1-25\alpha)}{1 - 2I^2R_{25}R_{th}\alpha}
$$

**Stability requires 2I²R₂₅·R_th·α < 1.** At 80 A, R₂₅ = 1 mΩ and α = 0.0045, this means R_th(channel→ref) < 8.7 K/W. Above that limit the channel runs away thermally.
