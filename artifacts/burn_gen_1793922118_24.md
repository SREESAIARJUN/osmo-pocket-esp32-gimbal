## 0. Key Findings and Assumptions

1. **The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In every production 4-in-1 stack, the H743 is the flight controller. Each ESC channel has its own MCU (for example STM32G071, AT32F421, or EFM8 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM, and the H743 talks to it over bidirectional DShot600. This document models both sides.
2. **80 A per channel is not a steady-state rating on a nano board.** The thermal model shows it is a burst of a few seconds. A true 20×20 mm "nano" board cannot carry 80 A per phase through its copper. The math below assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz** ESC. If Job #22 really requires 20×20 mm, the realistic burst rating drops to roughly 35–45 A.
3. **Operating point:** 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V at full charge (worst case for switching loss). 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification and complementary PWM.
