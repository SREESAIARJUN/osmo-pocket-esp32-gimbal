## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform** (assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θ_e = p(θ_m − θ_0):

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park** is the transpose of the matrix above.

**Inverse Clarke:**

$$v_a=v_\alpha,\quad v_b=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta$$

### 1.2 SVPWM via min-max zero-sequence injection

This is equivalent to 7-segment SVPWM and gives a linear range of |v| ≤ V_bus/√3.

$$v_0=-\tfrac12\big(\max(v_a,v_b,v_c)+\min(v_a,v_b,v_c)\big),\qquad D_x=\tfrac12+\frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}$$

### 1.3 Motor model in the dq frame (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

$$\tau = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q$$
