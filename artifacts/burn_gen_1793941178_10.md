# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control Loop, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

## 0. Assumptions

I don't have the Job #58 specification, so this design uses a typical brushless gimbal configuration. Every assumed value is a named constant in the code.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 1–3 mH |
| Position sensing | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per axis (AS5048A / MT6701 class) |
| Current sensing | 2-phase low-side shunts. A voltage-mode fallback is provided. |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with FPU (e.g., STM32G474), center-aligned PWM |
| IMU-A | ICM-42688-P on the camera payload (attitude truth) |
| IMU-B | ICM-42688-P on the base/handle (disturbance feedforward, vibration reference) |
| Kinematic chain | Base → Yaw (z) → Roll (x) → Pitch (y) → Camera |

**Loop rates**

| Loop | Rate | Ts |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | 50 µs |
| IMU sampling (both, SPI DMA) | 8 kHz | 125 µs |
| Velocity | 4 kHz | 250 µs |
| Angle / attitude | 1 kHz | 1 ms |
| Anti-cogging adaptation | 1 kHz | 1 ms |

Bandwidth separation is roughly 5–10× per cascade level: ω_i ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz, ω_v ≈ 2π·80–150 Hz, ω_θ ≈ 2π·10–25 Hz.

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Motor model (PMSM, dq frame)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d \frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L_q i_q
$$
$$
v_q = R i_q + L_q \frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$
$$
\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2} p \left[\lambda_m i_q + (L_d - L_q) i_d i_q\right] \approx K_t i_q, \qquad K_t = \tfrac{3}{2} p \lambda_m
$$

The electrical angle and speed follow from the mechanical encoder angle:

$$
\theta_e = p(\theta_m - \theta_{off}), \qquad \omega_e = p\,\omega_m
$$

### 1.2 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke** (with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):
$$
i_\alpha = i_a, \qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}
$$

**Park:**
$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park:**
$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e, \qquad
v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

**Delay compensation.** The PWM update lands about 1.5 Ts after sampling, so use a lead angle for the inverse Park:

$$
\theta_e^{*} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s
$$

### 1.3 SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)

$$
v_a = v_\alpha, \quad
v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta, \quad
v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$
$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12\left[\max(v_a,v_b,v_c) + \min(v_a,v_b,v_c)\right]
$$
$$
d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}, \qquad x \in \{a,b,c\}
$$

**Linear-region limit:**
$$
\sqrt{v_d^2 + v_q^2} \le \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}
$$

Saturation strategy: v_d
