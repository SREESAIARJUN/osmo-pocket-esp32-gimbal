# STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): Power-Stage Loss, Thermal, and DShot DMA Design Package (Job #73)

## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Assumptions

### Architecture clarification

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does **not** commutate the motors. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU running AM32 at 48 kHz. Typical candidates are the AT32F421 and the STM32G071. The H743 talks to those MCUs over bidirectional DShot600.

All loss math below applies to the ESC power stage. All DMA math applies to the H743.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### Six-step conduction pattern

At any instant, two FETs carry the phase current:

- During the PWM on-time: one high-side FET plus one low-side FET.
- During the off-time: the complementary low-side FET (synchronous freewheel) plus the low-side FET.

So the conducting resistance is always 2·R_ds, independent of duty cycle.

### Equations

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

$$P_{cond,ch}=I_{ph}^2\cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Each FET conducts for roughly one third of the electrical period:

$$P_{cond,FET}\approx\frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}\ \text{(average)}$$

The instantaneous FET loss while conducting is I²·R_ds.

### Evaluation at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond,ch | P per FET (avg) | P per FET (instantaneous) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 0.90 mΩ | 11.5 W | 1.9 W | 5.8 W |
| 100 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W | 2.6 W | 7.7 W |
| 125 °C | 1.31 mΩ | 16.8 W | 2.8 W | 8.4 W |

The design value is **P_cond ≈ 16.8 W per channel** at T_j = 125 °C.

### Additional copper loss (bus plus phase traces)

Assume about 0.3 mΩ effective per channel, which requires a solid 2 oz pour on 4 layers:

80² × 0.3 mΩ ≈ **1.9 W per channel**
