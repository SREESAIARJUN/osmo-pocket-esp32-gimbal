# STM32H743 AIO Nano Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: System Architecture Package (Job #26)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Key Findings

### Architecture assumption
The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. Like every production AIO design, each ESC channel runs on its own small MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the ESCs over bidirectional DShot600. This document models both domains.

### Key findings
1. **Conduction loss dominates.** At 80 A it is about 28 W per channel, versus about 1.35 W of switching loss at 48 kHz.
2. **"80 A burst" is a sub-second rating on a nano board.** With all four motors at 80 A, about 117 W is dumped into a board with roughly 2.8 J/K of heat capacity. The board reaches junction limits in about 0.8 s.
3. **The model predicts about 20 A continuous per channel.** This matches real-world nano AIO ratings, which cross-validates the model.
4. **The DShot design is clean.** Bidirectional DShot600 on TIM1 CH1–CH4 needs five DMA streams. The H743's DMAMUX and 16 general DMA streams accommodate this alongside the IMU, OSD, flash and UARTs.
