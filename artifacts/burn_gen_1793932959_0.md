## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

Several points in the brief need to be settled before the math is meaningful.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** It runs ArduPilot and emits four DShot600 streams. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU running AM32 (STM32G071 or AT32F421 class). That MCU, not the H743, produces the 48 kHz PWM. The two domains meet only at the DShot signal lines.

2. **"80 A burst" in a nano form factor is a transient rating, not a thermal operating point.** The model below shows that 4 × 80 A has no steady-state solution on a ~30 × 30 mm board. Burst duration must be specified and enforced in firmware.

3. **MOSFET parameters are placeholders.** I have not been given a BOM, so the model uses a representative 60 V, 3.3 × 3.3 mm DFN part. Every equation is parametric. Substitute your actual datasheet values before signing off.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous commutation

In each 60° electrical sector, two phases carry current I and one phase floats. With complementary PWM:

- **Non-PWM phase:** its low-side (or high-side) FET conducts I continuously.
- **PWM phase:** the high-side conducts I for duty D and the low-side conducts I for (1 − D).
