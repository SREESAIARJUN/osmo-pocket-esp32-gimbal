# STM32H743 Nano AIO FC + 4-in-1 80 A ESC: Systems Architecture Package (Job #40)

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Findings

**System partition.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600. It does **not** switch the power stage. Each ESC channel has its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or the BLHeli_32 target MCU). That ESC MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM through a 3-phase gate driver.

**Thermal finding.** At 80 A burst on all four channels, the power stage dissipates about **110 W**. On a 20×20 mm board this is thermally transient-limited to roughly **1 second**, not steady state. The numbers are in §3, and the mitigations are in §8.

**Device parameters are assumptions.** All MOSFET values below are representative of a 40 V, ~1 mΩ, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN device. Substitute your actual datasheet values. The model is written so you only need to change the inputs.
