## 0. Assumptions & Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32H7 class (Cortex-M7, FPU, 480 MHz) |
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 1–3 mH |
| Current sense | 2-shunt inline per motor (low-side also works) |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701) per joint |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI, ODR = 8 kHz, symmetric about the plate centroid |
| Gimbal order | Yaw (outer) → Roll → Pitch (inner, camera) |

**Loop rates (all timer-synchronous):**

```
PWM 20 kHz center-aligned ─┬─ ADC trigger ─► Current loop (FOC)       20 kHz
                           ├─ /5  ──────────► Rate (velocity) loop      4 kHz
                           └─ /20 ──────────► Angle loop + AHRS         1 kHz
IMU DRDY (8 kHz) ──► SPI DMA ──► per-IMU filters ──► fusion (decimate 2:1 to 4 kHz)
```

Bandwidth separation of about 5–10× per cascade level is the design rule:
- current ≈ 2 kHz
- rate ≈ 150–250 Hz
- angle ≈ 20–40 Hz

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{m,0}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

$\theta_{m,0}$ is found at startup by applying $v_d>0,\ v_q=0$ (rotor aligns to $\theta_e = 0$) and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.4 PMSM model in the dq frame

$$
\begin{aligned}
v_d &= R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q\\
v_q &= R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
\end{aligned}
\qquad
\tau = \tfrac32 p\,\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q
$$

### 1.5 PI tuning by pole–zero cancellation

The plant per axis is $G(s) = \dfrac{1}{Ls+R}$. Choosing

$$
K_p = \omega_c L,\qquad K_i = \omega_c R
$$

gives an open loop of $\omega_c/s$. The closed loop is first-order with bandwidth $\omega_c$. Choose $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/10$ (here $2\pi\cdot 2\,\text{kHz}$).

### 1.6 Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$
v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e \hat L_q i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e(\hat L_d i_d + \hat\lambda_m)
$$

### 1.7 Voltage limiting (circular, d-axis priority)

$$
|v_d^*| \le V_{max},\qquad |v_q^*| \le \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^{*2}},\qquad V_{max} = \tfrac{V_{dc}}{\sqrt3}
$$

Anti-windup uses back-calculation with the saturated value.
