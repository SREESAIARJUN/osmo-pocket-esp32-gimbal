## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Per motor

In 6-step drive, two phases conduct at any moment:
- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high-side (HS) is on for fraction D, and the synchronous low-side (LS) is on for (1−D).
- **Leg B (return leg):** the LS is on continuously for the 60° sector.

The current path always contains exactly two R_ds:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms}^2 R_{ds,hot}\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I_{rms}^2 R_{ds,hot}$$

**With synchronous rectification, conduction loss is independent of duty cycle.** Including inductor ripple:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I_{pp} \approx \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{2L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

For a typical 5" motor (L_ph ≈ 15 µH, D = 0.5, V_bemf ≈ 12 V):
- ΔI ≈ 4.6 A
- ΔI²/12 ≈ 1.8 A², which is negligible against I² = 6400 A².

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.8\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{23.0\ W}$$

### 1.2 Per-FET distribution over one electrical cycle

Each phase spends 120° as the PWM leg (high) and 120° as the return leg (low). The worst case is D = 0.5.

| FET | Duty-weighted on-time | Loss | At D = 0.5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| HS | (1/3)·D | (D/3)·I²R | **1.92 W** |
| LS | 1/3 + (1−D)/3 | ((2−D)/3)·I²R | **5.76 W** |
| Leg total | | (2/3)·I²R | 7.68 W |
| Three legs | | | 23.0 W ✓ |

**The low-side FETs are the thermal hot spot.** Place them on the copper with the best via stitching to the internal ground planes.

### 1.3 PCB copper loss

For 2 oz (70 µm) copper, ρ = 1.72×10⁻⁸ Ω·m, and a phase path of 15 mm long × 3 mm wide × 2 layers in parallel:

$$R_{cu} = \frac{\rho\,l}{w\,t\,n} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8} \times 0.015}{0.003 \times 70\times10^{-6} \times 2} \approx 0.61\ \text{m}\Omega \text{ (at 20 °C; ×1.3 hot)}$$

There are two path segments per motor, giving P_cu ≈ 2 × 6400 × 0.8 mΩ ≈ **10 W per motor**.

On a nano board, copper is the same order of magnitude as FET loss. Mitigations:
- Use 6–8 layers with 2 oz inner planes.
- Solder motor wires directly at the FET pads.
