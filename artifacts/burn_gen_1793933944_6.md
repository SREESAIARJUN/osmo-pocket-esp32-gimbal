## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Architectural Corrections

Three clarifications shape the rest of this design:

1. **Two MCUs, two jobs.** In a 4-in-1 ESC architecture, the STM32H743 is the flight controller running ArduPilot. It does not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM.
   - Each motor channel is commutated by an ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071.
   - The H743 sends throttle over bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry back.
   - The 48 kHz switching-loss model therefore applies to the ESC power stage, configured in ESC firmware (for AM32, `PWM frequency = 48 kHz`).
2. **Electrical operating point (assumed, since none was specified):**
   - Bus voltage: 6S. V_bus = 25.2 V at full charge, 22.2 V nominal.
   - Burst current: 80 A per channel.
   - Continuous current: 55 A per channel.
3. **"Nano" form factor vs. 80A burst is the dominant design risk.** Section 3 shows a 30×30 mm board is thermally capacitance-limited to roughly 1–3 s at a 4×80 A simultaneous burst. Treat "80A burst" as a time-bounded rating and say so on the datasheet.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At the design point T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{ds}(110) = 1.5\,(1 + 0.005 \cdot 85) = 2.14\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current path: trapezoidal 6-step with complementary (synchronous) PWM

Take the sector where phase A is PWM-high and phase B is low-side clamped:

- **On-time (D):** current flows through HS_A, then the motor, then LS_B.
- **Off-time (1−D):** current freewheels through LS_A (synchronous) and LS_B.

At every instant, exactly two FETs carry I. Excluding the dead time, the channel conduction loss is:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)}$$

Each phase leg spends 1/3 of the electrical cycle in each state (PWM-high, clamped-low, floating). Per-FET averages are:

$$P_{HS} = \frac{D}{3} I^2 R_{ds}, \qquad P_{LS} = \frac{(1-D) + 1}{3} I^2 R_{ds} = \frac{2-D}{3} I^2 R_{ds}$$

The low-side FET is the thermal hot spot at partial duty.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 110 °C (I²R = 6400 × 0.00214 = 13.7 W)

| Duty D | P_HS per FET | P_LS per FET | P_cond per channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.95 (full-throttle burst) | 4.34 W | 4.80 W | 27.4 W |
| 0.50 | 2.28 W | 6.85 W | 27.4 W |

**Copper path loss.** The path is pad to via array to plane to motor pad, roughly 0.35 mΩ per channel round-trip on 8L/2 oz. This adds 80² × 0.35 mΩ = 2.24 W per channel, dissipated in the PCB itself.

### 1.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

There are two dead-time intervals per PWM period, each with t_dt = 200 ns:

$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2 t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 400\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = 1.23\ \text{W/ch}$$

> **Design lever:** reducing dead time to 100 ns (AM32 setting, verified on a scope for shoot-through margin) halves this loss to 0.61 W.
