| # | Finding | Impact |

| F1 | The H743 is the **flight controller**. It does not commutate the motors. Bi-directional DShot assumes four (or one 4-channel) dedicated ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. | The architecture is H743 → DShot600 → 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 class) → 3-phase gate driver → 6 FETs per motor. |
| F2 | "Nano" form factor is taken as a 30.5×30.5 mm mounting pattern with a ~36×36 mm outline. 20×20 mm cannot physically host 24 power FETs at 80A ratings. | Board area sets the thermal mass. See §3. |

| F5 | The 48 kHz PWM is the ESC-side FET switching frequency. This is independent of the 600 kbit/s DShot link. | The two are modeled separately (§2 vs §4). |

## 2. Loss Model

### 2.1 Commutation Model

AM32 uses 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous complementary PWM ("comp PWM"). In every 60° sector:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the high side is switched at f_sw = 48 kHz with duty D. The low side is the complementary synchronous rectifier.
- **Leg B (return leg):** the low side is held ON for the full sector.
- **Leg C (floating):** OFF, used for back-EMF zero-cross sensing.

Each FET is therefore in each role for 1/3 of the electrical cycle.

### 2.2 Conduction Loss

At any instant, exactly two FET channels carry the full phase current I: one in the PWM leg (high side or synchronous low side) and one in the return leg.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_J)$$

Per-FET average, for a FET that is high side during its PWM sector:

$$P_{cond,HS} = \frac{1}{3}\left[D\,I^2R\right] + \frac{1}{3}\left[I^2R\right]_{\text{if return-leg role}}$$

In practice the topology rotates roles, so the average per FET is:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{2\,I^2R}{6} = \frac{I^2R}{3}$$

**At the 80A burst, T_J = 125 °C:**

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.92\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{24.6\ W}$$

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = 4.1\ W$$

The instantaneous FET in the return leg dissipates I²R = 12.3 W for the duration of its 120° conduction. At high eRPM this averages out across the electrical cycle. **At stall or low eRPM it does not.** Stall or desync at 80A is the worst case and must be current-limited (§6).

**Copper path loss.** Assume battery pad → bus → FET → phase pad ≈ 0.25 mΩ per current path, with 2 oz outer layers and 6 layers stitched:

$$P_{Cu,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.25\text{ m}\Omega = 3.2\ W$$
