## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Current / Torque Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle and latency compensation

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Between encoder sample and PWM update, roughly 1.5 PWM periods elapse: ½ for the sample, 1 for compute/update. The angle is therefore extrapolated:

$$\hat\theta_e = \theta_e + 1.5\,T_c\,\hat\omega_e, \qquad \hat\omega_e = p\,\hat\omega_m$$

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke, using $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$:

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\hat\theta_e & \sin\hat\theta_e\\ -\sin\hat\theta_e & \cos\hat\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.3 Plant model in the rotating frame

$$v_d = R_s i_d + L_s \dot i_d - \omega_e L_s i_q$$

$$v_q = R_s i_q + L_s \dot i_q + \omega_e L_s i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

### 1.4 Current PI with pole-zero cancellation and decoupling

Choose the controller zero to cancel the plant pole $R_s/L_s$:

$$K_{p,i} = L_s\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R_s\,\omega_c$$

The closed loop then becomes $\frac{i}{i^*} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$.

With $\omega_c = 2\pi\cdot1500$: $K_{p,i}=18.8$ V/A and $K_{i,i}=52{,}800$ V/(A·s). Note that $\omega_c T_c = 0.47$, which is the practical upper limit for $T_c=50\,\mu s$ given the 1.5-sample delay (phase margin ≈ 50°).

Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e L_s i_q$$

$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e (L_s i_d + \lambda_m),\qquad \lambda_m = \frac{2K_t}{3p}$$
