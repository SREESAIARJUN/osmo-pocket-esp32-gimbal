# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #12): Electro-Thermal & Firmware Architecture

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate motors. Each of the four bridges needs its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM and answer bi-directional DShot. The H743's job is to produce four DShot600 streams and decode four eRPM telemetry streams.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
In 6-step (trapezoidal) or FOC operation with synchronous rectification, the phase current always flows through exactly two conducting channels:
- the chopping leg, either its HS FET or its synchronous LS FET, and
- the static LS FET of the return phase.
