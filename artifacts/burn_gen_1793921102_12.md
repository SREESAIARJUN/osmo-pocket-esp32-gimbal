# Job #77: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80 A-Burst ESC

## Engineering Design Package, Rev A (Pre-Layout)

## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Assumptions

**Architecture.** In a 4-in-1 stack, the STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs. It runs ArduPilot and talks bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs that commutate the motors. This package uses:

| Block | Part (baseline) | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100) or VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot, DShot600 bidirectional |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32) | AM32 firmware, 6-step, 48 kHz PWM |
| Gate driver ×4 | Fortior FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) | HS/LS drive, ~1.0 A source / 1.5 A sink |
| MOSFET ×24 | 40 V, R_DS(on) ≤ 1.0 mΩ @ 25 °C, 5×6 PQFN (e.g. BSC010N04LS-class) | Power stage |
| Bus | 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V | |

**Physical reality check.** "Nano" (20×20 mm) and 4 × 80 A burst are not simultaneously achievable. Twenty-four 5×6 mm FETs need about 720 mm² of footprint before the driver, MCUs, and caps are placed. This package assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz (outer) / 2 oz (inner power) ESC board**, stacked under a 20×20 or 30.5 FC.

All numerical results scale with the parameter table in §1. Replace those values with datasheet values for your chosen FET before freezing the design.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step, Complementary PWM)

### 2.1 Commutation structure

In each 60° electrical sector, one leg is PWM'd (HS at duty D, LS synchronous at 1−D), one leg holds LS fully on, and one leg floats (BEMF sense). Each leg spends ⅓ of the electrical period as the PWM leg and ⅓ as the static-low leg.

**Instantaneous conduction loss:**

$$
P_{cond,inst} = I_m^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[\,D + (1-D)\,] + I_m^2 R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I_m^2 R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

The result is independent of D because synchronous rectification always provides a FET path.

**Per-FET average over an electrical period:**

$$
P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} D\, I_m^2 R_{ds}, \qquad
P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,(2-D)\, I_m^2 R_{ds}
$$
