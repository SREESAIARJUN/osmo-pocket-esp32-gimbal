## 1. FOC Inner Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical model (PMSM, dq frame)

$$
v_d = R_s i_d + L_d \frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad
v_q = R_s i_q + L_q \frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

The terms are defined as follows:
- $\omega_e = p\,\omega_m$ is the electrical speed.
- $\lambda_m = \tfrac{2}{3}\,K_t/p$ is the flux linkage (amplitude-invariant transform).
- The electromagnetic torque is $\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\,\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q$, assuming a surface PM motor with $L_d=L_q$.

### 1.2 Transforms

Clarke transform, amplitude-invariant, with $i_c = -i_a - i_b$:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$
