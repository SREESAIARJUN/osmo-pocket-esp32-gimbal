## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 / H7 class: Cortex-M4F/M7, FPU, hardware timers, ADC injected conversions |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 2–5 mH |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701) per joint |
| Current sense | 2- or 3-shunt, sampled at PWM center |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, mounted symmetrically about the pitch axis, SPI at 24 MHz |
| Kinematics | Yaw (outer) → Roll → Pitch (inner) → camera |

Loop rates use 5–10× bandwidth separation between nested loops:

```
PWM / current loop (FOC) : 20 kHz  (ADC-triggered ISR, ~8 µs budget)
Velocity loop            :  4 kHz  (every 5th current ISR)
IMU sampling + filtering :  8 kHz  ODR, decimated to 4 kHz
Attitude / angle loop    :  1 kHz
Cogging LMS adaptation   :  4 kHz  (runs with the velocity loop)
```

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant; for 2 shunts use i_c = −i_a − i_b):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform**, with electrical angle θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset):

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant Model (Surface PMSM, L_d ≈ L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

$$
\tau_m = \tfrac{3}{2}\, p\, \lambda_m\, i_q = K_t\, i_q
$$

### 1.3 Current PI by Pole-Zero Cancellation

Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole R/L. The closed loop then becomes first-order with bandwidth ω_cc:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{cc},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{cc}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\frac{i_q}{i_q^*} = \frac{\omega_{cc}}{s + \omega_{cc}}
$$

Choose ω_cc ≈ 2π · 1.5 kHz. Keep ω_cc < ω_PWM/10 to leave margin for the 1.5-sample computation and PWM delay.

Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$
v_d^* = PI_d(e_d) - \omega_e L\, i_q
$$

$$
v_q^* = PI_q(e_q) + \omega_e (L\, i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

### 1.4 Voltage Limiting and Anti-Windup

The usable voltage is limited by the linear SVPWM region:

$$
|v_{dq}| \le V_{max} = \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}
$$

Saturation priority:
1. Clamp v_d to ±V_max.
2. Give v_q the remainder: $\;v_q \le \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}$.

Back-calculation anti-windup, with K_aw = 1/K_p:

$$
\dot x_I = K_i\, e + K_{aw}\,(u_{sat} - u)
$$

### 1.5 Inverse Park and SVPWM (Min/Max Injection)

Inverse Park and inverse Clarke:

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad
v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

$$
v_a = v_\alpha,\qquad
v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\qquad
v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

Zero-sequence injection and duty computation:

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc}) + \min(v_{abc})\big),\qquad
D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

Delay compensation: use θ_e + 1.5 · ω_e · T_s in the inverse Park transform, to account for the one-sample computation delay plus half a PWM period.
