# Job #9: STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC
## Design Model: 80 A Burst Thermal, 48 kHz Switching, Bidirectional DShot600 DMA

---

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Key Feasibility Findings

| Item | Decision / Finding |
|---|---|
| Topology | The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot only. Commutation runs on 4× ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 with AM32). The H743 cannot do 4× sensorless 48 kHz commutation alongside the flight stack with deterministic timing. |
| Bus voltage | 6S design point: 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal. 40 V MOSFETs are required, which leaves ~37% derating headroom for ringing. |
| PWM | 48 kHz center-aligned complementary PWM with synchronous rectification. This is AM32's upper configurable limit. |
| **80 A burst** | **Per channel, not simultaneous.** 4 × 80 A means 320 A battery current and about 90 W of dissipation on a ~6.5 cm² board. That is feasible for about 2 s from a warm start. Ratings must say "80 A burst per channel, ≤10 s; 4-channel simultaneous ≤2 s". |
| Battery input | 320 A cannot pass through nano-sized pads. The realistic aggregate input is ~120–150 A peak, limited by pad and lead geometry. |

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

In each commutation sector, two legs carry the phase current:
- PWM-on: HS_A → motor → LS_B.
- PWM-off: LS_A (synchronous) → motor → LS_B.

So at every instant exactly **two FET channels** carry I_ph. Each of the 6 FETs conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period. The electrical frequency is about 1–3 kHz, which is much faster than the junction thermal time constant, so junction temperature follows the average.

### 2.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × (1 + 0.6) = **1.6 mΩ**.

### 2.3 PCB copper resistance

Sheet resistance of one 2 oz layer:

$$R_\square = \rho/t = 1.72\times10^{-8} / 70\times10^{-6} = 0.246\ \text{m}\Omega/\square$$

The phase path is about 3 squares on 4 parallel layers, taken twice (in and out). At 80 °C copper:

$$R_{Cu} \approx 2 \times \frac{3 \times 0.246}{4} \times 1.22 = 0.45\ \text{m}\Omega$$
