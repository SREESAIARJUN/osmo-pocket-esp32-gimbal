## 0. Architecture and Key Constraints

### 0.1 System partition

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does **not** commutate the motors directly. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or EFM8BB51 running Bluejay) with a 3-phase gate driver.
