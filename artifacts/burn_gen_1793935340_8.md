## 0. Design Basis

### Architecture decision

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot only. Motor commutation runs on four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class) under AM32. The H743 is not suited to direct motor control here, for three reasons:

- **Firmware support:** ArduPilot has no motor-commutation path for these ESCs.
- **Isolation:** commutation would compete with the EKF for timers and DMA.
- **Safety:** a single-MCU fault would take out all four motors.

The H743 drives four bi-directional DShot600 lines to the ESC MCUs.
