# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion

I don't have access to the original Job #17 specification. The design below uses the assumptions listed in §0. Every assumption is a named constant in the code, so you can change any of them in one place.

---

## 0. Baseline Assumptions

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3 × GM4108H-class outrunner, 24N22P (p = 11 pole pairs), R ≈ 11 Ω, L ≈ 4 mH |
| Mechanical angle encoder | 14-bit magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701 SSI) |
| MCU | STM32
