## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step Trapezoidal, Synchronous Rectification)

In any 60° sector, two phases conduct and one floats. The current path always goes through one high-side "position" and one low-side "position". Under complementary PWM with duty D:

- **Switched leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D. The low-side FET of the same leg (synchronous) conducts for (1−D).
- **Sink leg:** the low-side FET conducts for 100% of the sector.

The time-averaged conduction loss per motor channel is therefore independent of D:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \left[ D\,R_{DS} + (1-D)\,R_{DS} \right] + I^2 R_{DS} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

**Temperature dependence:**

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\left(1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right), \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1} \Rightarrow k_T = 1.6 \text{ at } 125^\circ\text{C}
$$

**At 80 A:**

| Condition | R_DS | P_cond per channel | P_cond for 4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold (25 °C) | 2.5 mΩ | 2·6400·0.0025 = **32.0 W** | 128 W |
| Hot (125 °C) | 4.0 mΩ | 2·6400·0.004 = **51.2 W** | **204.8 W** |

**Per-FET average.** Each of the 6 FETs carries current about 1/3 of the electrical period:

$$
P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{I^2 R_{DS}}{3} \cdot 2 \cdot \frac{3}{6}\cdot... \;=\; \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6} \approx 8.5\ \text{W (hot)}
$$

**Add PCB copper I²R losses.** For 2 oz, 8-layer, with about 1.5 mΩ of effective path resistance (phase + bus + shunt):

$$
P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} \approx 6400 \times 0.0015 = 9.6\ \text{W per channel}
$$

The shunt term depends on topology. A 0.25 mΩ shared shunt at 320 A total gives 25.6 W. **Use a per-channel or low-ohm current amplifier instead.**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Transition intervals (hard-switched high-side)

$$
t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}},\quad I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{DRV}-V_{plateau}}{R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}+R_{drv}}
$$

The calculation gives about 8 ns. With the common-source and loop inductance of a dense 4-in-1, use the **effective t_r = t_f = 20 ns**.

### 2.2 Energy per PWM period

$$
E_{sw} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r + t_f) + Q_{rr}V + Q_{oss}V
$$

| Term | Calculation | Energy |
|---|---|---|
| V·I overlap | 0.5·25.2·80·40 ns | 40.3 µJ |
| Reverse recovery (sync FET body diode) | 50 nC·25.2 V | 1.26 µJ |
| Output capacitance | 25 nC·25.2 V | 0.63 µJ |
| **Total** | | **≈ 42.2 µJ** |

$$
P_{sw} = E_{sw} f_{sw} = 42.2\,\mu J \times 48\,kHz = \mathbf{2.03\ W\ per\ channel}
$$

Only one leg PWMs per sector, so this is the per-channel figure.

### 2.3 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$
P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I\, (2 t_{dead}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 200\,ns \cdot 48\,kHz = \mathbf{0.61\ W}
$$

### 2.4 Gate drive (all 6 FETs, 2 switching at a time)

$$
P_{gate} = 2\,Q_g V_{DRV} f_{sw} = 2\cdot30\,nC\cdot10\,V\cdot48\,kHz = 0.029\ \text{W}
$$

This loss is dissipated in the driver and gate resistors.

### 2.5 Ripple contribution

At 48 kHz with motor inductance L ≈ 15 µH, the phase ripple is:

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V(1-D)D}{L f} \le \frac{25.2 \cdot 0.25}{15\mu H \cdot 48 kHz} = 8.75\ A
$$

The RMS correction ΔI²/12 ≈ 6.4 A² is negligible against 6400 A² (0.1%).

### 2.6 Per-channel budget at 80 A burst (hot)

| Loss | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction | 51.2 |
| Switching | 2.03 |
| Dead time | 0.61 |
| Gate | 0.03 |
| PCB copper | 9.6 |
| **Total per channel** | **≈ 63.5 W** |
| **4 channels** | **≈ 254 W** |
