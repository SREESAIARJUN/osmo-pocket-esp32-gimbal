## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology

During any 60° commutation sector, two legs carry the phase current I:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D, and the low-side FET (synchronous rectifier) conducts for 1 − D.
- **Static leg:** the low-side FET conducts 100% of the sector.

So at every instant, exactly two FETs carry I, independent of D (ignoring dead-time):

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j) + I_{rms}^2 R_{Cu} + I_{rms}^2 R_{shunt}$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

This gives a factor of about 1.6 at 125 °C. Use the datasheet's normalized R_ds(on) curve if available.

$$R_{ds}(125^\circ C) = 1.2 \times 1.6 = 1.92\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Parasitic copper and shunt

Copper path per channel (bus plane → FET → phase pad → return): about 0.3 mΩ for 2 oz copper over a short path. Shunt: 0.25 mΩ, if one shunt per channel is used.
