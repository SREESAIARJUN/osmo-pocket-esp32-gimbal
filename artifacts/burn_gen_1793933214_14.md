# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #39): Engineering Model

## 0. Architectural Corrections and Assumptions

Three issues need resolving before the math means anything.

**1. The STM32H743 cannot be the ESC controller.**
- It runs ArduPilot as the flight controller.
- Motor commutation (6-step or FOC at 48 kHz across 4 motors) needs dedicated advanced-timer complementary outputs with dead-time.
- The H743 has only TIM1 and TIM8 for that, which covers 2 motors, not 4.
- **Correct architecture:** H743 FC → bidirectional DShot600 → 4 × ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32) → 3-phase gate drivers → 24 MOSFETs.
- The DShot section below therefore covers the FC-side timers.

**2. "80 A burst" on a nano board is thermally marginal.**
- Read here as 80 A per motor for a short burst, which is 320 A aggregate.
- A 20×20 mm board cannot physically host 24 MOSFETs rated for this.
- I assume a 30.5×30.5 mm footprint, 8-layer PCB, 2 oz copper.
- Section 3 shows burst duration is limited to seconds.

**3. All component values below are representative, not verified part selections.** Substitute real datasheet values (Rds(on) vs T, Qg, Qrr, Coss, Zth curves) before release.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds ≈ 1.0 × 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification

During any 60° commutation sector:
- One leg is PWM'd complementarily. The current flows through either its high-side FET (for duty D) or its low-side FET (for 1−D), so exactly one FET conducts at any instant.
- The return leg's low-side FET is held on continuously.

So two FET channels always carry I:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) \quad \text{(independent of D under sync-rect)}$$

PCB copper resistance adds:

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu}, \quad R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho_{Cu}(T)\,\ell}{w\,t}$$

Assume R_Cu ≈ 0.4 mΩ effective per phase path (2 oz, paralleled layers, stitched vias).

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

| Term | Per ESC | ×4 |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction: 2 · 80² · 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W | 74.2 W |
| Copper: 80² · 0.4 mΩ | 2.6 W | 10.2 W |
| **Total conduction** | **21.2 W** | **84.4 W** |

**Per-FET average:** the six FETs share the load over an electrical cycle, so each sees about 18.6/6 ≈ 3.1 W average. The peak is 9.3 W while it is the static low-side FET.

At burst eRPM, the electrical period (≈ 0.3–1 ms) is much shorter than the die thermal time constant. The average value therefore governs junction rise.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg hard-switches. Per ESC:

**V–I overlap (hard switching of the high-side FET):**
$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 20\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.97\text{ W}$$

**Reverse recovery (low-side body diode, dissipated mostly in the high-side FET):**
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.048\text{ W}$$

**Output capacitance:**
$$P_{oss} \approx Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.036\text{ W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction (two dead-times per period):**
$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 200\text{n} \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.61\text{ W}$$

**Gate drive (HS + LS of the active leg, dissipated in the driver and gate resistors):**
$$P_g = 2\, Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} = 2 \cdot 45\text{n} \cdot 10 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.043\text{ W}$$

| Switching term | Per ESC | ×4 |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | 0.97 W | 3.88 W |
| Qrr + Qoss | 0.08 W | 0.34 W |
| Dead-time | 0.61 W | 2.46 W |
| Gate | 0.04 W | 0.17 W |
| **Total switching** | **1.71 W** | **6.85 W** |

**Design levers:**
- Dead-time is the second-largest switching term. Reducing t_dt to 50 ns (safe with a driver matched to Q_g) saves about 1.2 W board-wide.
- Raising f_sw to 96 kHz roughly doubles switching loss.
- At 48 kHz, conduction dominates by about 12:1. **Rds(on) is the primary selection metric.**

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Total burst dissipation

$$P_{tot} \approx 84.4 + 6.9 + P_{MCU/driver/BEC} (\approx 2\text{ W}) \approx \mathbf{93\text{ W}}$$

### 3.2 Lumped thermal network

```
Tj ──Rθjc(0.8°C/W per FET)── Tcase ──Rθ_pcb(spreading)── Tboard ──Rθ_ba── Tamb
                                                           │
                                                         C_board
```

**Board heat capacity (30.5×30.5×1.6 mm, 8L × 2 oz):**

| Material | Mass | Specific heat | Heat capacity |

| FR4 | 1.49 cm³ × 1.85 g/cm³ ≈ 2.75 g | 1.1 J/g·K | 3.0 J/K |

| FETs + passives | — | — | ≈ 0.5 J/K |
| **Total C_th** | | | **≈ 5.3 J/K** |

**Board-to-air resistance (forced convection, prop wash ~8 m/s, both sides):**
- h ≈ 60 W/m²K, A ≈ 2 × 9.3 cm²
- R_θba ≈ 1/(60 × 1.86e-3) ≈ **9 °C/W**
- With an aluminium heatsink plate and thermal pad: about 4 °C/W
