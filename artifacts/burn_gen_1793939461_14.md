## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle, with sampling-delay compensation (the PWM update lands about 1.5 T_s after sampling):

$$\theta_e = p(\theta_m - \theta_{0}) + \omega_e \cdot 1.5\,T_s$$

Clarke transform (two-shunt sensing, using $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant and decoupled PI

The motor's electrical model in the rotating frame:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda)$$

The controller is a PI plus feedforward that cancels the cross-coupling and back-EMF terms:

$$v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda)$$

Gains come from pole–zero cancellation, which makes the closed loop first order, $i/i^* = \omega_c/(s+\omega_c)$:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \approx 2\pi\cdot 1.5\text{ kHz}\ \ (\le \omega_{s}/10)$$
