## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Corrections

Three points need to be settled before the math is meaningful.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In a production 4-in-1 stack, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 to the ESC. Each ESC channel runs its own commutation MCU (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class firmware on an STM32G071/AT32F421). That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. Running 12 half-bridges plus BEMF zero-cross detection from the flight controller is technically possible, but no ArduPilot firmware supports it.

2. **"Nano" vs. 80A burst.** A 20×20 mm board cannot survive 80A per channel. The physics in §3 shows this. This design therefore assumes:
   - a **20×20 mm H743 FC**, stacked on
   - a **30.5×30.5 mm 4-in-1 ESC** (6-layer, 2 oz copper).

3. **Operating point.** "80A burst" means 80A per channel for up to 10 s, on 6S (25.2 V max).
