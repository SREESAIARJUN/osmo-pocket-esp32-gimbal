## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path

With six-step (trapezoidal) commutation and synchronous rectification, exactly two MOSFETs carry the phase current at every instant:

- In the PWM'd leg, either the high side (HS, during D) or the low side (LS, during 1−D).
- In the return leg, the LS is fully on.

So conduction loss is **independent of duty cycle**:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{rms}^2 R_{Cu}$$

### 2.2 Ripple contribution

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\, f_{sw}} \;\xrightarrow{D=0.5}\; \frac{22.2 \times 0.25}{20\,\mu\text{H} \times 48\,\text{kHz}} = 5.78\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{DC}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 2.78 = 6402.8\ \text{A}^2$$

The ripple adds 0.04 %, so it is negligible at 48 kHz. This is one benefit of the high f_sw.

### 2.3 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond (2 FETs) | + Copper | **Total / channel** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.81 W | 1.92 W | 14.7 W |
| 75 °C | 1.23 mΩ | 15.69 W | 1.92 W | 17.6 W |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ | 17.13 W | 1.92 W | 19.0 W |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.57 W | 1.92 W | **20.5 W** |

**Per-MOSFET peak** (a device conducting 100 % of a commutation step): about 9.3 W at 125 °C.
**Averaged over a commutation cycle:** each of the 6 FETs conducts about 1/3 of the time, giving roughly 6.2 W average per FET.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 3.1 Gate current during the switching intervals

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{DRV} - V_{pl}}{R_{g,on}} = \frac{12 - 3}{8.7} = 1.03\ \text{A}
\qquad
I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{g,off}} = \frac{3}{5} = 0.60\ \text{A}$$

$$t_{on} \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = 14.6\ \text{ns}
\qquad
t_{off} \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,off}} = 25.0\ \text{ns}$$
