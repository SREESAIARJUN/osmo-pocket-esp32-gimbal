## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform.** Assumes balanced phases, so i_c = −i_a − i_b:
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

**Park transform.** With electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:
$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park transform:**
$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\quad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI Tuning (Pole-Zero Cancellation)

The surface-PMSM d/q voltage equations are:
$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

Each axis is the first-order plant G(s) = 1/(Ls+R). Choose the PI controller C(s) = K_p + K_i/s with:
$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

The PI zero then cancels the plant pole, and the closed loop becomes ω_c/(s+ω_c). Pick ω_c ≤ 2π·f_pwm/20 ≈ 2π·1 kHz.

**Decoupling feedforward:**
$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

### 1.3 Voltage Limiting and Anti-Windup

The voltage vector is limited to the linear SVPWM circle |v| ≤ V_dc/√3. Priority goes to v_d, and v_q gets the remainder:
$$v_q^{max}=\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}$$

The integrator uses back-calculation anti-windup, with tracking gain K_aw = 1/K_p:
$$x_{k+1} = x_k + T_s\left[K_i e_k + K_{aw}\,(u^{sat}_k - u_k)\right]$$
