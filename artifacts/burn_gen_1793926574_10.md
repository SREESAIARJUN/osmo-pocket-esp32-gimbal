## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0048\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

(This gives ≈1.6× at 150 °C, typical for trench MOSFETs.)

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

During each 60° sector, two phases conduct:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for fraction D. The low-side FET (synchronous rectifier) conducts for 1−D.
- **Static leg:** the low-side FET conducts for 100% of the sector.

So at any instant, current flows through exactly **two FETs in series**, independent of D. Ignoring the dead-time fraction:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

With 48 kHz ripple, $I_{rms}^2 = I_{DC}^2 + \Delta I_{pp}^2/12$. For a typical 5–10 µH motor phase, ΔI_pp ≈ 4 A at 48 kHz, so the ripple term contributes about 1.3 A², which is <0.03%. It is negligible.

### 1.3 Self-consistent junction temperature

Solve iteratively. The FET's share of channel power raises T_j, which raises R_DS, which raises power.

$$T_j = T_{board} + P_{FET}\,R_{\theta,j\text{-}board},\qquad R_{\theta,j\text{-}board}\approx 1.2\ (\text{jc}) + 2.0\ (\text{pad/vias}) = 3.2\ \text{K/W}$$

Take T_board = 90 °C at end of burst:

| Iter | T_j (°C) | R_DS (mΩ) | P_cond,ch (W) | P per FET avg (W)* |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 100 | 1.36 | 17.4 | 2.9 |
| 1 | 99.3 | 1.357 | 17.4 | converged |

\*Each FET conducts about 1/3 of the electrical cycle, so channel power divides by 6. The **instantaneous** dissipation in a conducting FET is I²R = 6400 × 1.36 mΩ = **8.7 W**. This matters at low eRPM, where a sector lasts milliseconds. A FET with Z_θ ≈ 1 K/W at 5 ms then rises an additional ~9 K above the average.

**Result:** P_cond ≈ **17.4 W per channel** at 80 A.

### 1.4 PCB copper conduction loss

The path is battery pad → bus planes → FETs → phase pad. Path length is ~25 mm. Two 2 oz layers in parallel (70 µm each) are ~6 mm wide.

$$R_{cu} = \frac{\rho L}{A}\cdot[1+0.0039(T-20)] = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\cdot 0.025}{2\cdot 70\times10^{-6}\cdot 0.006}\cdot 1.27 \approx 0.65\ \text{m}\Omega$$

The current path includes supply and return segments, so roughly 2× path is shared among channels. Budget about **0.5 mΩ effective per channel**:

$$P_{cu} = 80^2 \cdot 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega \approx 3.2\ \text{W/channel}$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the **PWM leg** switches. Hard switching occurs on the high-side FET. The low-side FET switches at ~0 V (ZVS after dead time), but its body diode reverse recovery is absorbed by the high-side FET.

### 2.1 Gate current during Miller plateau

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{dr} - V_{pl}}{R_{drv}+R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}} = \frac{10-3}{0.8+2.2+1.0} = 1.75\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{total}} = \frac{3.0}{4.0} = 0.75\ \text{A}$$

$$t_{on} = \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{12\,\text{nC}}{1.75} = 6.9\ \text{ns},\qquad t_{off} = \frac{12\,\text{nC}}{0.75} = 16.0\ \text{ns}$$
