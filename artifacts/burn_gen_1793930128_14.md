## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

Two points need to be settled before any math.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** Bidirectional DShot is a protocol between the flight controller and *separate ESC MCUs*. This design therefore uses:

- **Flight controller:** STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100, 480 MHz) running ArduPilot.
- **ESC:** 4 × AM32-class MCUs (STM32G071GBU6 or AT32F421K8U7), each driving one 3-phase bridge with 48 kHz complementary PWM.

**2. "80 A burst" on a nano board is a transient rating, not a continuous one.** Section 3 shows that the continuous per-channel limit is roughly 30–35 A with all four motors loaded, and that 80 A on all four channels is sustainable for about 2 s.

**Design assumptions.** Every number below scales with these. Substitute your actual BOM values.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max (22.2 V nominal) |
| Board | — | 30.5 × 30.5 mm mount, ~36 × 36 mm outline, 8-layer
