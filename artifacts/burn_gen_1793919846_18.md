## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step with synchronous rectification
At any instant, two phase legs carry current:
- One leg PWMs complementarily: the high-side conducts for duty D, the low-side for 1−D.
- The other leg's low-side is held on.

The current therefore always passes through exactly **two FET channels in series**, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per motor | Per conducting FET (instantaneous) | Per FET (averaged over 120° conduction) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 6.4 W | 2.13 W |
| 100 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W | 9.3 W | 3.1 W |
| **108 °C (design point)** | **1.50 mΩ** | **19.2 W** | **9.6 W** | **3.2 W** |
| 150 °C | 1.75 mΩ | 22.4 W | 11.2 W | 3.7 W |

The electrical commutation frequency is in the kHz range, far faster than the die thermal time constant (ms). Averaging over the 120° conduction window is therefore valid.
