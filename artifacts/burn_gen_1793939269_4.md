## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Key Findings

**Architecture**
- ArduPilot DShot implies the STM32H743 is the **flight controller** and does not drive the gates.
- Each of the 4 ESC channels has its own ESC MCU (STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32/BLHeli_32), a 3-phase gate driver, and 6 N-FETs.
- The 48 kHz figure is therefore the **ESC MCU PWM frequency**.
- DShot600 is the link from H743 to ESC MCU.

**Operating point and parts**

| Parameter | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V (design point), 22.2 V nominal |
| Form factor | "Nano" = 20×20 mm mounting, ~25×25 mm board, 8-layer, 2 oz outer / 1 oz inner |
