## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning

On a 4-in-1 AIO board, the STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. The partitioning is:

| Function | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight control, DShot600 master, eRPM telemetry decode | STM32H743VIT6 (400/480 MHz) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) |
| Commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM reply | 4 × ESC MCU (e.g., AT32F421 / STM32G071) | AM32 or BLHeli_32 |
| Gate drive | 4 × 3-phase driver (e.g., FD6288Q class) | n/a |
| Power stage | 24 × N-ch MOSFET (6 per motor) | n/a |

The 48 kHz switching figure is therefore an ESC-firmware parameter. The H743's job is the DShot timer and DMA engine (Section 4).

### 0.2 Assumptions

No MOSFET or stack-up was specified, so the model is parameterised. Every number below should be re-run against the selected part's datasheet.

| Symbol | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S fully charged (worst case for switching loss) |
| I_ph | 80 A | Burst phase current per motor (≈ battery current at D → 1) |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | ESC PWM frequency |
| R_ds(on),25 | 1.2 mΩ (max, V_gs = 10 V) | 40 V trench FET, PDFN 5×6 class |
| α_R | 0.0045 /K | Typical R_ds(on) tempco |
| Q_gs2 + Q_gd | 13 nC | Switching charge (Miller) |
| Q_g,tot | 45 nC | Total gate charge |
| Q_rr | 60 nC | Body-diode reverse recovery charge |
| Q_oss | 40 nC | Output charge |
| V_F | 0.8 V | Body-diode forward drop |
| t_dead | 100 ns per edge | Typical AM32 dead time (2 edges per period) |
| V_drv, V_plateau | 10 V, 3.5 V | Gate drive voltage, Miller plateau |
| R_src / R_sink (driver) | 3 Ω / 1.5 Ω | Driver output resistance |
| R_g,ext / R_g,int | 2 Ω / 1 Ω | External / internal gate resistance |
| Board | 30.5 × 30.5 mm, 8L, 2 oz outer / 2 oz inner | "Nano/30×30" AIO |

> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag:** "Nano" plus 80 A per motor on 6S is at the edge of physical possibility. The math below shows it is achievable **only as a seconds-scale burst**, not continuously. This must be in the product spec.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in six-step synchronous PWM

At any commutation step, two phase legs conduct:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T_sw, and the low-side FET (synchronous rectification) conducts for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Fixed leg:** the low-side FET is fully on for the whole 60° step.

So at every instant the motor current passes through exactly **two R_ds(on)** in series (ignoring dead time):

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

with the temperature dependence

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha_R\,(T_j - 25)\right]
$$

### 1.2 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts during 120° of the electrical cycle:

- **Fixed-leg low-side FET:** 1/3 duty at full current.
- **PWM-leg FETs:** share D and (1−D) during their 1/3 duty.
