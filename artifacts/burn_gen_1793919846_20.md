## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification

In every 60° sector, one high-side (HS) FET and one low-side (LS) FET in different legs carry the phase current I_ph.

- During PWM-on, current flows through HS(a) and LS(b).
- During PWM-off, current freewheels through LS(a) and LS(b). Because rectification is synchronous, the FET channel conducts rather than the diode.

Therefore **exactly two R_DS(on) are in the current path at all times**, independent of duty cycle D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Phase RMS current with ripple

Peak-to-peak ripple current, with motor phase-to-phase inductance L_m = 15 µH (typical for a 5-inch 2306 motor):

$$\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-E)\,D}{L_m f_{sw}}$$

At D = 0.9 and back-EMF E = 21 V:

$$\Delta I = \frac{4.2 \times 0.9}{15\,\mu\text{H} \times 48\,\text{kHz}} = 5.25\ \text{A}_{pp}$$

The RMS value is then:

$$I_{rms}=\sqrt{I^2+\Delta I^2/12}=\sqrt{6400+2.3}=80.01\text{ A}$$

The ripple is negligible at this operating point.

### 1.3 Result at T_j = 110 °C

$$R_{DS}(110\,°\text{C}) = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.006 \times 85) = 1.51\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.51\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{19.3\ W\ per\ channel}$$

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution, worst device

The worst device is the HS FET in the PWM leg. It conducts for 120° of each electrical cycle.

$$P_{HS,cond} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,D\,I^2 R_{DS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(0.9)(6400)(1.51\text{m}) = 2.90\ \text{W (average)}$$

The electrical frequency is about 1–2 kHz. That is far faster than the junction thermal time constants (≥ 1 ms), so the averaged value is valid.
