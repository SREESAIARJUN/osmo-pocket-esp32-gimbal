## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0048 × 75) = **1.36 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

During each 60° sector, two phases conduct:

- **PWM phase:** the high side conducts for duty fraction D. The low side (synchronous rectification) conducts for 1 − D.
- **Return phase:** the low side is held on for 100% of the sector.

The motor current I_M therefore always flows through exactly two R_ds in series:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_M^2 R_{ds}\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I_M^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

This total is independent of D. Duty only changes which FET carries the loss.

### 1.3 Values at 80 A

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.36\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{17.4\ W\ per\ channel}$$
