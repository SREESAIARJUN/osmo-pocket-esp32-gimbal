# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 80A ESC: Electrical, Thermal and DShot Design Model

**Scope:** This document covers conduction loss, switching loss at 48 kHz, the thermal transient for an 80 A burst, bidirectional DShot600 timer and DMA allocation in ArduPilot, and a reference netlist.

**Caveat:** All numbers below come from the stated assumptions. Replace them with the datasheet values for the MOSFETs you actually select, then validate on hardware. Section 7 lists the tests.

---
