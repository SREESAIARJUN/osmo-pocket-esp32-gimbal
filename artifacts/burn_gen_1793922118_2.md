# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Thermal Model and Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation

**Revision:** A (architecture baseline)
**Scope:** Power-stage loss model at 80 A burst and 48 kHz PWM, transient thermal budget, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA plan, and reference netlists.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Corrections

Several points in the request need correcting or pinning down before any numbers mean anything.

1. **The H743 does not generate the 48 kHz PWM.** In a 4-in-1 stack, each motor's commutation and 48 kHz PWM come from the ESC's own MCU (e.g., an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). The H743 flight controller only sends DShot600 frames and receives eRPM telemetry. The two problems are treated separately below.
2. **"80 A burst" is interpreted as 80 A phase current per channel.** Battery current is lower by roughly the duty cycle: I_batt ≈ D·I_phase.
3. **MOSFET parameters are representative, not from a specific datasheet.** The model assumes a 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm device. Substitute your actual BOM part's values:

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| On-resistance @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R_ds,25 | 1.5 mΩ |
| Tempco multiplier @ T_j = 100 °C | k_T | 1.5 |
| Rise / fall time (with chosen R_g) | t_r / t_f | 20 / 20 ns |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 40 nC |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 30 nC |
| Reverse-recovery charge | Q_rr | 40 nC |
| Body-diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Junction-to-case thermal resistance | R_θJC | 1.5 K/W |

4. **Bus voltage is 6S:** 25.2 V full charge, about 22 V under load. The worst case of 25.2 V is used for switching loss.
5. **Physical scale:** a 20×20 mm "nano" 4-in-1 with 80 A per channel is far beyond what such boards are conventionally rated for (typically 25–45 A continuous). The model below shows exactly why. Treat 80 A as a sub-second transient only.

## 1. Power-Stage Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Conduction topology

The model assumes trapezoidal six-step drive with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM, which is the AM32 default. During each 60° commutation sector, phase current I flows through two switch positions:

- the **PWM'd leg**: the high-side FET for D·T, then the low-side FET (synchronous rectification) for (1−D)·T;
- the **static leg**: one FET held fully on.

Because synchronous rectification puts both PWM'd-leg devices in the current path at the same R_ds, conduction loss is essentially **independent of duty cycle**:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 \left[ D\,R_{ds} + (1-D)\,R_{ds} \right] + I^2 R_{ds} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0067\ \text{K}^{-1} \;\Rightarrow\; k_T(100\,^\circ\text{C}) = 1.5$$

At 80 A and T_j = 100 °C:

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.25\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{28.8\ W}$$

The conduction loss is split as 14.4 W in the static FET and 14.4 W shared between the PWM'd leg's HS and LS devices.

**Copper/interconnect loss.** Assume about 0.5 mΩ of total phase path (2 oz copper, vias, solder pads, motor-wire joints):

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{3.2\ W}$$

### 1.2 Switching loss at f_sw = 48 kHz

**Hard-switched V–I overlap, PWM'd leg only:**

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus}\, I\, (t_r + t_f)\, f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**Output-charge and reverse-recovery loss:**

$$P_{Q} = (Q_{oss} + Q_{rr})\, V_{bus}\, f_{sw} = 70\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.085\ W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two dead-time intervals per period, t_dt = 100 ns):

$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2\,t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**Gate drive** (2 FETs switched, V_drive = 10 V). This loss is dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FETs:

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g\, V_{drv}\, f_{sw} = 2 \times 40\,\text{nC} \times 10 \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.038\ \text{W}$$

### 1.3 Per-channel and board totals at 80 A burst

| Term | W/channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FETs) | 28.8 | 115.2 |
| PCB copper | 3.2 | 12.8 |
| Switching overlap | 1.94 | 7.8 |

| Dead-time diode | 0.61 | 2.4 |
