## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology

In each 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct:
- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D. The low-side FET synchronously rectifies for (1−D), ignoring dead time.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET conducts for 100% of the sector.

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Conduction loss is therefore **independent of duty cycle** under synchronous rectification.

### 1.2 Temperature Dependence

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

This gives about 1.6× at 125 °C, which is typical for trench MOSFETs.

| T_j (°C) | R_ds (mΩ) | P_cond per motor @ 80 A | ×4 motors |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | 1.20 | 15.4 W | 61.4 W |
| 90 | 1.67 | 21.4 W | 85.5 W |
| 110 | 1.81 | 23.2 W | 92.7 W |
| 125 | 1.92 | 24.6 W | 98.3 W |

### 1.3 Per-FET Loss

Each FET conducts during 120° of the 360° electrical cycle. At normal eRPM the electrical period is well below 1 ms, while the junction time constant is in the millisecond range or longer. Per-FET loss can therefore be averaged:

$$\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,motor} + P_{sw,motor}}{6}$$

> **Low-RPM exception:** at stall or startup the averaging assumption fails. A single FET can carry I²R = 80² × 1.81 mΩ = **11.6 W continuously**. The ESC firmware must cap current at low eRPM (AM32 stall protection / current limit).

### 1.4 Copper Loss (Do Not Ignore)

For 2 oz copper, R_□ ≈ 0.24 mΩ/□ at 25 °C, with a temperature coefficient of 0.0039/K.

**Phase path:** a 10 mm × 3 mm trace is 3.33 □. In parallel across 4 layers this is about 0.2 mΩ, rising to about 0.25 mΩ when hot.

- P = 80² × 0.25 mΩ × 2 phases = **3.2 W per motor**
- **12.8 W total**

**Battery input:** I_batt ≈ D × I_phase × 4. At D = 0.9 this is **288 A peak**, so the XT60/XT90 pad and lead resistance dominates. Even 0.3 mΩ total gives **25 W**.

This is why the rating is "burst".
