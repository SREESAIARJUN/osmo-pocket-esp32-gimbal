# STM32H743 Nano AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC), Job #6: Architecture, Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and DShot Timer/DMA Plan

## 0. Architectural Corrections

Three findings change the design, so they come first.

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | The STM32H743 has only **two advanced timers with complementary outputs** (TIM1, TIM8), giving 6 complementary pairs. Four 3‑phase bridges need 12 complementary pairs. HRTIM adds only 10 outputs. | The H743 should **not** commutate the motors. Use **four dedicated ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32) on the same PCB. The H743 runs ArduPilot and speaks **bidirectional DShot600** to them over internal traces. This is also the only architecture in which "DShot" is meaningful. |
| A2 | 80 A per channel on a nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board dissipates about **120 W** at full burst. | 80 A is a **≈1 s thermally limited burst rating**, not a continuous rating (see §3). The spec must state burst duration and starting temperature. |
| A3 | At 48 kHz, dead‑time diode conduction rivals hard‑switching loss. | Dead time must be ≤150 ns, with a low‑Qrr FET and tight gate loops. |

All component parameters below are **representative assumptions** (generic 40 V, 5×6 / 3.3×3.3 ESC MOSFET class). Replace them with your selected part's datasheet values. The equations stay valid.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Topology

In 6‑step commutation, at any instant one high‑side (HS) FET of phase X and one low‑side (LS) FET of phase Y carry the current. One leg is PWM'd:

- During D·T_sw: HS_x ON, LS_y ON
- During (1−D)·T_sw: LS_x ON (synchronous freewheel), LS_y ON

**Two FETs are always in the current path.** Each FET's share over an electrical cycle is 1/3, since each leg is active for 120° per switch type.

### 2.2 Conduction Loss

Per FET (worst case, D → 1):

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{1}{3}\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Per ESC (independent of D with synchronous rectification):

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

- P_cond,FET = (1/3)(6400)(1.86 mΩ) = **3.97 W**
- P_cond,ESC = 2(6400)(1.86 mΩ) = **23.8 W**

### 2.3 Switching Losses

Only the PWM'd leg switches, so it is one hard‑switched transition pair per T_sw.

**(a) V–I overlap (hard turn‑on and turn‑off of the HS FET):**

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(40\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz}) = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**(b) Dead‑time body‑diode conduction (LS_x diode, two edges per period):**

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I_{ph}\, (2 t_{dead})\, f_{sw} = 0.8(80)(300\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz}) = \mathbf{0.92\ W}$$

**(c) Reverse recovery (charge dissipated in the HS FET at turn‑on):**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\,\text{nC}(25.2)(48\,\text{k}) = \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$

**(d) Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2} C_{oss} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 0.5(1.5\,\text{nF})(635)(48\,\text{k}) = \mathbf{0.023\ W}$$

**(e) Gate drive (2 switching FETs; dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors):**

$$P_g = 2\, Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2(60\,\text{nC})(10)(48\,\text{k}) = \mathbf{0.058\ W}$$

### 2.4 Passive Path Losses

- Shunt: I² R_sh = 6400 × 0.5 mΩ = **3.2 W**
- Copper: I² × 2 × R_Cu = 6400 × 0.8 mΩ = **5.1 W** (on a nano board this term is **not negligible**)
- DC‑link capacitor ripple: I_C,rms ≈ I_ph √(D(1−D)) ≤ 40 A at D = 0.5. With ESR = 5 mΩ (bulk + ceramics), P ≈ **up to 8 W at mid‑throttle**. At burst (D → 1) this is ~0. Mid‑throttle ripple, not 80 A, is often what kills nano‑board capacitors.

### 2.5 Totals at 80 A Burst, 48 kHz, T_j = 125 °C

| Loss | Per ESC (W) | ×4 ESCs (W) |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FETs) | 23.8 | 95.2 |
| Hard switching | 1.94 | 7.8 |
| Dead time | 0.92 | 3.7 |
| Q_rr + C_oss + gate | 0.14 | 0.6 |
| Shunt | 3.2 | 12.8 |
| Copper | 5.1 | 20.4 |
| **Total** | **≈35.1** | **≈140** |

Worst single FET (the HS FET of the PWM'd leg, during its active third):

$$P_{FET,max} \approx 3.97 + \tfrac{1}{3}(1.94 + 0.06 + 0.02) \approx \mathbf{4.6\ W}$$

The dead‑time loss lands in the LS diode: about 0.3 W on that FET.

**Efficiency at burst:** P_out ≈ 25.2 × 80 × 4 ≈ 8.06
