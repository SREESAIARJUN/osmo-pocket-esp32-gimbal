## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Feasibility Flags

Several points in the brief need resolving before any numbers are meaningful.

| # | Issue | Resolution adopted |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | ArduPilot on the H743 does not commutate MOSFETs. It sends DShot to ESC MCUs. | The H743 is the flight controller only. Each ESC channel has its own MCU (AT32F421 running AM32) driving the gates. BDShot600 is the link between them. |
| A2 | "Nano" (20×20 mm) cannot hold an H743 (LQFP100 is 14×14 mm, TFBGA100 is 8×8 mm) **and** an 80A×4 power stage. | Two-board stack. The FC is 20×20 mm using the TFBGA100 package. The ESC is 30.5×30.5 mm. If a 20×20 ESC is mandatory, §3.5 shows why 80A becomes a burst of under 2 s. |
| A3 | "80A" is undefined. | I take it as **80A per channel DC phase current, burst**. Continuous is assumed lower. Bus is 6S: 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal. |
| A4 | MOSFET not specified. | Representative 40 V, 5×6 mm (PDFN) part. Parameters are in §1.1. Substitute your datasheet values; every equation is parameterised. |
| A5 | Commutation scheme. | 6-step trapezoidal (AM32 default) with complementary (synchronous) PWM at f_sw = 48 kHz. |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Device parameters (assumed)

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| R_ds,on(25°C) | 1.0 mΩ @ V_GS = 10 V | Typical value |
| α | 0.0045 /K | R_ds temperature coefficient (linearised) |
| Q_g / Q_gd / Q_gs2 | 50 / 10 / 5 nC | Gate charges |
| Q_rr | 40 nC | Body-diode reverse-recovery charge |
| C_oss,eff | 1.5 nF | Energy-equivalent output capacitance |
| V_SD | 0.8 V | Body-diode forward drop at 80 A |
| V_plateau | 3.5 V | Miller plateau voltage |
| R_θJC | 1.0 K/W | Junction to case |

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_ds

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1+\alpha (T_j-25)\right]$$

This expression is solved self-consistently with the thermal model:

$$T_j = T_{pcb} + P_{FET}(T_j)\,Z_{\theta JC}(t)$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R_ds = 1.45 mΩ.

### 1.3 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

During each 60° sector, the **PWM leg** alternates HS and LS FETs, with duty D and (1−D). The **return leg** keeps its LS FET on continuously. The motor current I therefore flows through exactly **two R_ds in series** at every instant, apart from dead time.

**Per channel (ESC):**

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) + I^2 R_{shunt} + I^2 R_{trace}$$

**At I = 80 A, T_j = 125 °C:**

| Term | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| FETs | 2 × 6400 × 1.45 mΩ | **18.56 W** |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ) | 6400 × 0.25 mΩ | 1.60 W |
| Copper (≈0.15 mΩ effective) | 6400 × 0.15 mΩ | 0.96 W |
| **Total conduction / channel** | | **21.1 W** |

**Per-FET worst case.** The return-leg LS FET conducts 100% of its 120° window, so its instantaneous dissipation is:

$$I^2 R_{ds} = 9.28\ \text{W}$$

Averaged over a full electrical cycle, each FET conducts for 1/3 of the time:

$$\bar P_{FET} \approx 9.28/3 \approx 3.1\ \text{W}$$

Bursts shorter than about 50 ms see instantaneous rather than averaged per-FET peaks. Low-eRPM, high-current operation (punch-outs from hover) is the worst case, because one FET holds current for long sectors.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

Worst case is V_bus = 25.2 V and I = 80 A. Hard switching occurs on the PWM leg only.

### 2.1 V–I overlap

The gate driver sources about 10 V. Total gate resistance R_g,tot = 4 Ω (driver plus internal plus external).

$$I_g \approx \frac{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}{R_{g,tot}} = \frac{10-3.5}{4} = 1.63\ \text{A}$$

$$t_{r} \approx t_{f} \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_g} = \frac{15\,\text{nC}}{1.63\,\text{A}} \approx 9.2\ \text{ns}$$

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(18.4\text{ns})(48\text{k}) = \mathbf{0.89\ W}$$

Real layouts add about 2–3 nH of common-source inductance. This slows the edges, so budget **×1.5 → 1.34 W**.
