## 0. Architecture Decisions

These points determine everything that follows.

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 and sends bi-directional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs, one per motor. The baseline part is the AT32F421 running AM32, which handles the 48 kHz PWM, commutation and back-EMF sensing. Bi-directional DShot only makes sense with this split. One H743 cannot run ArduPilot plus four sensorless FOC/6-step loops at 48 kHz.
2. **"80A burst" means per channel, for a limited time, and not all four channels at once indefinitely.** Four channels at 80 A from 6S is about 8 kW and 320 A of battery current. An XT60 or XT90 lead and a nano-class PCB cannot carry that. Section 4 quantifies the burst duration the board can actually survive.
3. **All numbers below are parametric.** Defaults are representative of 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFETs. Substitute your selected part's datasheet values and the equations still hold.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-corrected R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.45 = 1.74\,\text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current paths in 6-step with synchronous rectification

Consider the step where phase A is PWM-high and phase B is low:

| Interval | Fraction | Conducting FETs |
|---|---|---|
| ON | D | Q_AH + Q_BL |
| OFF (freewheel) | 1−D | Q_AL + Q_BL |

The per-FET losses are:

$$P_{AH}=I^2R_{ds}D,\quad P_{AL}=I^2R_{ds}(1-D),\quad P_{BL}=I^2R_{ds}$$

The total per channel is independent of duty cycle:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)}$$

At 80 A:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.74\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{22.3\ W}$$

The **worst single FET** is the continuously-on low side. It dissipates I²R_ds = **11.1 W** during that 60° electrical sector. Averaged over a full electrical revolution, each FET carries about 1/3 of the channel loss, roughly 7.4 W. The thermal model uses the sector peak because thermal time constants at the die level (milliseconds) are comparable to sector duration at low eRPM.

### 1.3 Shunt loss

The shunt sits in the common low-side return. Freewheel current circulates through the two low-side FETs above the shunt, so the shunt conducts only during ON:

$$P_{shunt} = I^2 R_{sh} D \le 6400 \times 0.25\text{m} = 1.6\text{ W (at } D=1)$$

### 1.4 Copper loss

For a 2 oz outer + 2 oz inner pour, assume an effective 0.3 mΩ per phase path, which gives about 1.9 W per channel at 80 A. **Use the inner layers as parallel current paths, with via arrays of at least 20 × 0.3 mm vias per FET pad.**
