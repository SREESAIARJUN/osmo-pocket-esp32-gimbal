# STM32H743 FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and Bidirectional DShot600 Timer/DMA Allocation

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**Architecture.** In a stacked FC/ESC design, the STM32H743 is the flight controller running ArduPilot. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU (assumed here: AT32F421 running AM32, or an EFM8/BLHeli_S equivalent). The H743 issues bidirectional DShot600 commands and receives eRPM telemetry. The loss and thermal model below applies to the ESC board. The DMA and timer model applies to the H743.

No datasheets or project files were provided, so all component values are representative assumptions. Replace them with your BOM's datasheet values before signing off.

## 1. Conduction Losses at 80A Burst

### 1.1 Temperature-corrected R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.425 = 1.71\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Commutation model (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

At any instant, two phases conduct.

- One high-side FET conducts during the PWM on-time (duty D).
- The complementary low-side FET in the same leg conducts during the off-time (1−D) via synchronous rectification.
- The static low-side FET in the return leg conducts for 100% of its 120° interval.

So the motor current always flows through exactly two FET channels. Per motor:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j) = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.71\times10^{-3} = 21.9\ \text{W}$$

Per FET, each device carries current for 1/3 of the electrical cycle:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}}{3} \cdot k_D,\qquad k_D \in \{D,\ 1-D,\ 1\}$$

The worst-case FET is the static low-side or the high-side at D→1:

$$P_{cond,FET,max} = \frac{6400 \times 1.71\text{ m}\Omega}{3} = 3.65\ \text{W}$$

### 1.3 Copper path loss

$$P_{cu} = I^2 R_{cu} = 6400 \times 0.5\ \text{m}\Omega = 3.2\ \text{W per motor}$$

### 1.4 Battery current vs. phase current

$$I_{bat} \approx D \cdot I_{phase}\cdot\eta^{-1}$$

The 80A phase rating approaches 80A battery current only at D≈1. Most "80A" burst events in flight occur at high throttle, so treat the phase and battery figures as approximately equal for worst-case planning.
