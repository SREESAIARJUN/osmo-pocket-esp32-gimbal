# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model (Job #5)

## 0. Feasibility Findings

Two points would change the design if left unexamined. The rest of this document assumes the corrected architecture below.

| Issue | Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| **Who drives the FETs** | The H743 should not commutate 4 motors directly. ArduPilot does not run ESC firmware, and DShot telemetry assumes per-ESC MCUs. | Use 4 dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071, running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware), each with a 3-phase gate driver. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to them. |
| **80 A on a "nano" footprint** | At 80 A per channel, the four channels together dissipate about 130 W. A 20×20 mm or 25.5×25.5 mm board can hold that for only about 1–2 s (derived in §3). | Specify **80 A as a ≤1 s burst rating**, about 35–40 A continuous with airflow. Alternatively, move to a 30.5×30.5 mm layout with 5×6 mm FETs. |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**Temperature dependence of R_DS(on)**

$$R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\quad \alpha\approx0.005\,/\mathrm{K}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.5 × 1.5 = **2.25 mΩ**.

**Bridge path.** In 6-step operation with synchronous rectification, the phase current always flows through exactly two FETs:
- the PWM leg, which alternates HS (on-time, fraction D) and LS (freewheel, fraction 1−D), and
- the commutated-leg LS, which conducts for the full sector.

$$P_{cond,ch}=I^2\big[D\,R_{HS}+(1-D)R_{LS}+R_{LS}\big]=2I^2R_{DS}(T_j)\quad(\text{for }R_{HS}=R_{LS})$$

**Results at I = 80 A**

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.50 mΩ | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| 100 °C | 2.06 mΩ | 26.4 W | 105.6 W |
| 125 °C | 2.25 mΩ | **28.8 W** | **115.2 W** |

**Copper losses.** Add phase and bus trace resistance. For 2 oz copper (≈0.25 mΩ/sq): a 4 mm × 10 mm pour is 2.5 sq ≈ 0.6 mΩ, giving about 3.8 W per channel at 80 A. **Internal-layer stitching and pour paralleling are mandatory.**

**Per-FET average.** The electrical frequency at 30k RPM is f_e = 30000/60 × 7 = 3.5 kHz. This is far faster than the thermal time constants, so each FET sees the average loss: P_FET ≈ 28.8 / 6 = **4.8 W**, and ΔT_JC = 4.8 × 1.5 = **7.2 K**. The junction is therefore not the bottleneck. **The board's heat capacity and spreading are.**
