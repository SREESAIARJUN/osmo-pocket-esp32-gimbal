# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Design Package (Job #27)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Corrections

### 0.1 Architecture

A 4-in-1 ESC does not commutate motors from the STM32H743. The system has two processor domains:

| Domain | Processor | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) | Sends bi-directional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry |
| ESC (×4 channels) | 1× or 4× AT32F421 / STM32G071 class | AM32 or BLHeli_32 | Commutation, 48 kHz PWM, BEMF sensing |

The math in Sections 1–3 covers the ESC power stage. Section 4 covers the H743 timer and DMA plan.
