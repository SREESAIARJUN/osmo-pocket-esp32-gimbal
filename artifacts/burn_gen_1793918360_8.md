## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

### 0.1 Who does what

In a 20×20 "nano" FC + 4-in-1 stack, the STM32H743 does **not** switch the MOSFETs. The split is:

| Function | Device |
|---|---|
| ArduPilot, attitude control, DShot600 frame generation, eRPM telemetry capture | **STM32H743** (FC) |
| 48 kHz PWM, commutation, BEMF sensing, bidirectional DShot response | **4× ESC MCUs** (e.g., AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32, or EFM8 running Bluejay) |
| Gate drive | 3-phase driver per channel (e.g., FD6288Q class) |
| Power stage | 6 N-ch MOSFETs per channel, 24 total |

The 48 kHz switching frequency is therefore an **ESC firmware parameter**. It does not affect the H743 timer configuration.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Conduction topology

The model assumes six-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM, which is the AM32/BLHeli_32 default. During each 60° electrical sector:

- **Switching leg:**
  - The high-side FET conducts for D·T.
  - The low-side FET (synchronous rectifier) conducts for (1−D)·T.
  - Together they carry the phase current I for the full period.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET conducts I continuously.

Therefore exactly **two R_ds(on) are in the current path at all times**, independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]$$

### 1.2 At 80 A, T_j = 100 °C

$$R_{ds(on)} = 1.2\,[1 + 0.005\cdot75] = 1.65\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.65\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{21.1\ W\ per\ channel}$$

$$P_{cond,total} = 4 \times 21.1 = \mathbf{84.5\ W}$$

### 1.3 Electrothermal feedback

R_ds(on) rises with temperature, and conduction loss rises with R_ds(on). Iterate:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{board} + P_{FET}(T_j^{(n)})\cdot R_{\theta JC}$$

Thermal runaway occurs when the loop gain reaches unity:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,R_\theta = 2I^2R_{25}\,\alpha\,R_{\theta,eff} \ge 1$$

With per-FET R_θ,eff = 1.5 K/W, the loop gain at 80 A is ≈0.14. The loop is stable, but losses run about 16% above the cold estimate.

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution

Averaged over a full electrical revolution, each of the 6 FETs takes about 1/6 of the channel loss:

- Average per FET: ≈21.1 / 6 = 3.5 W
- Instantaneous peak per FET: up to 10.6 W while it is the return-leg low-side

Because the electrical period is much shorter than the FET thermal time constant (ms vs. ~10–100 ms), the junction responds to the **average**:

$$\Delta T_{jc} \approx 3.5\cdot1.5 \approx 5\ \text{K}$$
