## 0. Architecture Decision and Scope

One correction to the brief comes first. The STM32H743 should **not** commutate the motors directly. ArduPilot has no native FOC or 6-step ESC firmware for the H7. The production-proven architecture is:

```
 ┌──────────────────────┐   4× bidirectional DShot600   ┌──────────────────────────────┐
 │ STM32H743VIT6 (FC)   │──────────────────────────────▶│ 4× ESC MCU (AM32 firmware)   │
 │ ArduPilot            │◀── eRPM telemetry (GCR) ──────│ e.g. AT32F421K8 / STM32G071  │
 │ IMU, baro, OSD, logs │◀── KISS/AM32 serial telem ────│ 4× 3-phase gate drivers      │
 └──────────────────────┘                               │ 24× N-MOSFET (6 per motor)   │
                                                        └──────────────────────────────┘
```

**Design targets (assumptions):**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Supply | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal |
| Current per channel | 80 A burst (≤ 5 s, see §3), 30 A continuous |
| PWM frequency | 48 kHz, centre-aligned, complementary with dead-time |
| Form factor | 30.5 × 30.5 mm mounting, ~36 × 36 mm board, 8-layer, 2 oz on all layers |
| MOSFET (⚠ example class) | 40 V, R_DS(on) = 0.70 mΩ at 25 °C, PDFN5x6/3x3 |

## 1. MOSFET Loss Model

### 1.1 Device parameters

| Symbol | Meaning | Value ⚠ |
|---|---|---|
| R_DS25 | On-resistance at 25 °C | 0.70 mΩ |
| α | R_DS tempco factor | R(T) = R_DS25 · (T_j/298)^2.3 → ≈ 1.50 × at 100 °C |
| t_r, t_f | Effective V-I overlap times at 80 A with driver | 20 ns + 20 ns |
| Q_rr | Body diode reverse-recovery charge | 60 nC |
| Q_oss | Output charge at 25 V | 100 nC |
| Q_g | Total gate charge at V_GS = 10 V | 100 nC |
| V_SD | Body-diode forward voltage at 80 A | 0.8 V |
| t_dead | Driver dead-time (each edge) | 200 ns |
| R_θJC | Junction to case thermal resistance | 0.5 K/W |

### 1.2 Conduction loss (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

At any instant, two phases carry the full phase current I.

- In the active PWM leg, the high-side FET conducts for D·T and the complementary low-side FET for (1−D)·T.
- In the return leg, one low-side FET is held on for the whole sector.

The instantaneous conduction loss per channel is therefore independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

At 80 A and T_j = 100 °C (R = 1.05 mΩ):

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.05\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{13.44\ W}$$

Copper and shunt loss must be added on top:

- **Phase-path copper.** Each path is about 15 mm long and 5 mm wide, using 4 paralleled layers of 2 oz copper. Sheet resistance is 0.245 mΩ/sq for 2 oz, so 3 squares ÷ 4 layers ≈ 0.18 mΩ per path. Two paths conduct, so the loss is 2 × 6400 × 0.18 m = **2.3 W**.
- **Shunt.** A 0.25 mΩ shunt (if per-channel current sensing is used) adds 6400 × 0.25 m = **1.6 W**.

**Per-FET view, for hotspot analysis.** Over one electrical revolution each FET conducts for 1/3 of the time. During a 5 s burst, however, the thermal time constant of a single FET (about 10–50 ms) is shorter than the commutation period at low eRPM. The worst-case per-FET sizing must therefore use instantaneous loss, not the averaged value:

$$P_{FET,inst} = I^2 R = 80^2 \times 1.05\,\text{m} = 6.72\ \text{W}$$

### 1.3 Switching loss at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. Using the hard-switching linear overlap model:

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

The remaining switching-related terms are:

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw = 60n × 25.2 × 48k | 0.073 W |
| Output capacitance | ½ · Q_oss · V_bus · f_sw = 0.5 × 100n × 25.2 × 48k | 0.060 W |
| Dead-time body-diode conduction | V_SD · I · 2 · t_dead · f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 2 × 200n × 48k | 1.23 W |
| Gate drive (mostly dissipated in driver/R_g) | 2 · Q_g · V_GS · f_sw = 2 × 100n × 10 × 48k | 0.096 W |

The dead-time term is significant. Trimming t_dead from 200 ns to 100 ns saves 0.6 W per channel. Validate the trim with a double-pulse test for shoot-through margin.

### 1.4 Per-channel and board totals at 80 A burst

| Loss term | Per channel | × 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 13.44 W | 53.8 W |
| Copper traces | 2.30 W | 9.2 W |
| Shunt | 1.60 W | 6.4 W |
| Hard switching | 1.94 W | 7.8 W |
| Q_rr + Q_oss | 0.13 W | 0.5 W |
| Dead-time diode | 1.23 W | 4.9 W |
| Gate drive | 0.10 W | 0.4 W |
| **Total** | **20.7 W** | **≈ 83 W** |

The efficiency check confirms these losses are plausible. Each channel delivers about 22.2 V × 80 A = 1776 W, so the loss is about 1.2 %, which is typical for 0.7 mΩ-class FETs.
