## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

This section separates what is derived here from what must come from datasheets you select.

**Architectural correction.** The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. In every production "H743 + 4-in-1 ESC" design:

- The **H743** runs ArduPilot and outputs bi-directional DShot600.
- **Four dedicated ESC MCUs** run AM32 (or BLHeli_32). Typical choices are AT32F421 or STM32G071, one per motor. They handle commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, and dead-time.

The loss model below therefore applies to the ESC power stage, and the DMA model applies to the H743.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation with complementary PWM, the current always flows through exactly two FETs:

- **On-time (D):** the high-side FET of phase X plus the low-side FET of phase Y.
- **Off-time (1−D):** the low-side FET of phase X (synchronous freewheel) plus the low-side FET of phase Y.

This gives:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Ripple contribution

The worst case for ripple occurs at D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{LL}\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\,\mu\text{H} \times 48\,\text{kHz}} = 6.56\ \text{A}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 \approx 6404\ \text{A}^2$$

Ripple adds less than 0.06% at 80 A and can be ignored at burst. It matters only at light load and on low-inductance motors.

### 1.3 Temperature dependence (self-consistent)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R_ds ≈ 1.2 mΩ × 1.65 = 1.98 mΩ:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 6404 \times 1.98\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{25.4\ W\ per\ channel}$$

### 1.4 Per-FET distribution

Each phase spends 120° high, 120° low, and 120° floating, so the six FETs share the loss roughly equally:

$$P_{cond,FET} \approx \tfrac{1}{3}\,P_{cond,ch} \approx 8.5\ \text{W}$$
