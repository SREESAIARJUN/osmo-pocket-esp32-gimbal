> **Architectural finding (read first):** At 80 A, the losses calculated below total about **94 W across four motors**. A 20×20 mm board can shed only about 5 W continuously in prop-wash airflow. The 80 A rating is therefore a **~1.4 s transient rating from a 40 °C start**, not a sustained one. Section 3 derives this. If the requirement is "80 A for 10 s" or longer, the board must grow to 30×30 mm with lower-R_DS(on) FETs, or the current spec must drop. Treat this as a requirements-review item, not a footnote.

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. The 4-in-1 ESC carries its own per-motor MCUs, typically 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 does not commutate motors directly, because six-step commutation of four motors at 48 kHz with BEMF zero-cross detection is not an ArduPilot function. The two halves connect over 4× bidirectional DShot600 plus current/voltage telemetry.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

At a design point of T_j = 110 °C:
$$R_{DS} = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1+0.0045\times85) = 1.66\ \text{m}\Omega$$

Because R depends on T_j and T_j depends on P_cond, the loop closes thermally. Iterate T_j → R → P → T_j. The loop converges in 2–3 passes when the stability condition $\alpha P R_{\theta} < 1$ holds, which it does here.

### 1.2 Six-step, complementary (synchronous) PWM

At any instant two phase legs are active:
- **PWM leg:** the high-side switches at duty D, and the low-side conducts during (1−D) as a synchronous rectifier.
- **Static leg:** the low-side is held fully on.
