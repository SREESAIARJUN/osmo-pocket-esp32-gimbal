## 2. Field-Oriented Control Equations

### 2.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

where $p$ is the number of pole pairs and $\theta_{\text{off}}$ is found by aligning the rotor to the d-axis with a fixed $V_d$ at startup.
