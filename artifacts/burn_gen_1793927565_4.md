## 1. Architecture and Loop Timing

```
             1 kHz                 5 kHz                     25 kHz (ADC ISR)
θ_ref ──►[Angle P + ω_ff]──ω_ref──►[Velocity PI + accel FF]──iq*──►(+)──►[Current PI dq + decoupling]──►SVPWM──►Motor
              ▲                         ▲                          ▲                ▲
              │ θ (IMU attitude          │ ω (IMU gyro,             │ iq_cog          │ id, iq (Clarke/Park)
              │  projected on axis)      │  notch-filtered)         │                 │
         Mahony/complementary    Dual-IMU fusion          Anti-cogging(θ_mech)   Encoder → θ_e (PLL)
```

Two design rules drive the structure:

1. **Bandwidth separation.** Each outer loop runs at least 5× slower in bandwidth than the inner loop:
   ω_c,i ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s, ω_c,v ≈ 2π·150 rad/s, ω_c,θ ≈ 2π·25 rad/s.
2. **Sensor roles.** For stabilization, the encoder is used for commutation. The **inertial** measurements (gyro rate, fused attitude) close the velocity and angle loops. That is what rejects base motion.
