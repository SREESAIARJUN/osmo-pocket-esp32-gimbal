# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #29): Reference Design Model

## 0. Feasibility and Assumptions

**Feasibility first:**

1. **Split of responsibilities.** The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs (AM32 or BLHeli_32 on a G071/AT32-class part), not by the H743. The H743 running ArduPilot only emits bidirectional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry. The two problems are coupled only through the connector and the PCB thermal mass.
2. **80 A on a nano board.** 80 A per motor on a 20×20 mm board is a short burst rating (≤ ~1 s, all four motors), not a continuous rating. Section 3 shows this numerically. If "80 A" is meant as continuous, you need a 30.5×30.5 mm board, a heatsink, or both.
3. **Parameter status.** All MOSFET and driver parameters below are representative values for a 40 V, DFN3.3×3.3 class device. Replace them with your selected part's datasheet values before sign-off. The netlists are reference topology, not a DRC/ERC-verified production design.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

R_DS(T_j) = R₂₅ · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 100 °C: R = 1.4 × 1.50 = **2.10 mΩ**

### 1.2 Per-motor conduction

In 6-step with synchronous rectification, exactly two switch positions carry I_ph at every instant:

- **PWM on:** HS_x and LS_y conduct.
- **PWM off:** LS_x (sync) and LS_y conduct.

P_cond,motor = 2 · I_ph² · R_DS(T_j) = 2 · 6400 · 0.0021 = **26.9 W**

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution (hotspot)

Each of the 6 FETs carries current for roughly 1/3 of the electrical period, so the average per FET is:

P_cond,FET ≈ (1/3) · I² · R = (1/3) · 6400 · 0.0021 = **4.48 W**

This averaging is valid only when the electrical period (≈ 0.3–1 ms at flight eRPM) is much shorter than the die thermal time constant (~ms). At low eRPM and high current (prop stall, startup) the instantaneous per-FET loss approaches 13.4 W. Desync and stall protection must cover that case.

### 1.4 Copper and shunt

| Loss source | Assumption | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| Phase/bus copper | ~0.5 mΩ per motor path | 80² × 0.5 mΩ = **3.2 W** per motor |
| Bus shunt | 0.25 mΩ, I_bat = ΣD·I_ph | ≤ 0.25 mΩ × (≈200 A)² ≈ 10 W at full burst |

The shunt figure is a strong argument for using the ESC's per-phase current sensing instead of a single bus shunt, or for a 0.1 mΩ shunt.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Gate currents during the Miller plateau

- **Turn-on:** I_g,on = (V_drv − V_pl)/(R_drv + R_g,ext + R_g,int) = 7/5.5 = 1.27 A, which the driver limits to **≈ 1.0 A**.
- **Turn-off:** I_g,off = V_pl/(R_total) = 3/5.5 = **0.55 A**

### 2.2 Transition times

- t_on ≈ (Q_gs2 + Q_gd)/I_g,on = 9 nC / 1.0 A = **9 ns**
- t_off ≈ 9 nC / 0.55 A = **16.4 ns**
