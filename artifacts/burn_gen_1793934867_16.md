## 0. Architecture Corrections and Scope (Read First)

Two points in the brief change the analysis, so I address them first.

**1. The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no in-tree sensorless BLDC commutation for four motors. In every production AIO, the H743 acts only as the flight controller. It sends DShot to four dedicated ESC MCUs, which run AM32 or BLHeli_32/Bluejay. Typical ESC MCUs are the AT32F421, STM32G071, or GD32E230.

The architecture modeled below is therefore:

```
H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir ×4──► 4× ESC MCU (AM32) ──► 4× 3-ph gate driver ──► 24× N-MOSFET ──► motors
```

**2. "80 A burst" on a 20×20 board must be bounded.** I model 80 A per channel. I then show mathematically that 4×80 A simultaneous is a sub-2-second adiabatic event on a nano board. The input path would carry 320 A, which is physically out of reach for the connector and board. The model sets the real burst envelope.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM, which is the AM32 default. In any 60° sector:
- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) conducts for D·T, and the synchronous low-side (LS) conducts for (1−D)·T. Exactly one device conducts at any instant.
- **Return leg:** the LS conducts continuously.
- **Floating leg:** not conducting (used for BEMF sensing).

Two devices therefore carry the phase current I at all times, independent of duty cycle.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond}=2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

At I = 80 A and T_j = 125 °C, R_ds = 1.2 × 1.6 = 1.92 mΩ:

$$P_{cond}=2(6400)(1.92\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{24.6\ W\ per\ channel}$$

**Per-device average.** Each of the 6 devices takes its turn over the electrical cycle, so the average is about 24.6/6 ≈ **4.1 W per device**.

**Why averaging is valid.** Consider a 14-pole motor at 30 k RPM:
- Electrical frequency f_e = 3.5 kHz, a period of 286 µs.
- The die thermal time constant is about 1–5 ms.
- Since the electrical period is much shorter, the averaged value holds.
- At low eRPM under high current (startup or desync), the average does not hold. Use the instantaneous 12.3 W per device instead.

### 1.4 Electrothermal fixed point and runaway criterion

Junction temperature depends on R_ds, which depends on junction temperature:

$$T_j = T_{pcb} + R_{\theta}\,P(T_j),\qquad P(T_j)=k\,I^2R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

Solving gives:

$$T_j=\frac{T_{pcb}+R_\theta kI^2R_{25}(1-25\alpha)}{1-R_\theta kI^2R_{25}\alpha}$$

**Stability condition:** $R_\theta\,k\,I^2R_{25}\,\alpha < 1$.
