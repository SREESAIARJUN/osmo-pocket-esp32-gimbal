## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Feasibility Check

### 0.1 System partitioning

The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs. A 4-in-1 ESC needs one dedicated ESC MCU per motor (or one multi-motor MCU) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz complementary PWM.

The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to the ESC MCUs.

```
[H743 ArduPilot] --DShot600 bidir x4--> [ESC MCU x4] --> [3-ph gate driver x4] --> [6 FETs x4] --> motors
```

### 0.2 What "80A burst" can mean on a nano board

| Interpretation | Feasible on a 20×20 mm nano? |
|---|---|
| 80 A total (≈20 A/motor) | Yes, with good copper. |
| 80 A per motor, ≈1–2 s burst | Marginal. The thermal mass limits it (see §3.4). |
| 80 A per motor, sustained | No. Total dissipation is ~80 W in a ~2 J/K board. |

Both cases are modeled below. The per-motor 80 A case is the stress case.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

Assume trapezoidal 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM). At any instant, two phase legs carry the phase current I:

- During the PWM on-time (D), the current flows through the high-side FET of leg A and the low-side FET of leg B.
- During the off-time (1−D), the current freewheels through the low-side FET of leg A (synchronous) and the low-side FET of leg B.

So exactly two FET channels are always in series with I, independent of duty cycle. This gives the per-motor conduction loss:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS(on)}(T_j)=R_{DS(on),25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

Here α ≈ 0.005 /K, which gives k_T ≈ 1.5 at 125 °C.

### 1.2 Per-device split (for hotspot sizing)

Each leg is active for 1/3 of the electrical period as the PWM leg and 1/3 as the return leg:

$$P_{HS}=\tfrac{1}{3}D\,I^2R, \qquad P_{LS}=\tfrac{1}{3}(2-D)\,I^2R$$

The low-side FETs run hotter at low throttle. Place them on the best thermal copper.

### 1.3 Copper loss

$$P_{Cu}=I^2R_{trace}, \qquad R_{trace}=\frac{\rho_{Cu}\,L}{w\,t}\,[1+0.00393(T-25)]$$

Example: a 2 oz (70 µm) outer layer plus a paralleled 2 oz inner layer, each 6 mm wide, over 15 mm, gives R ≈ 0.3 mΩ per path. A bus-to-motor round trip is about 2 × 0.3 = 0.6 mΩ.

### 1.4 Numbers (hot, k_T = 1.5 → R = 1.5 mΩ)

| Case | I per motor | P_cond per motor | P_Cu per motor | 4 motors total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: 80 A total | 20 A | 2·400·1.5 m = **1.20 W** | 0.24 W | **5.8 W** |
| B: 80 A per motor | 80 A | 2·6400·1.5 m = **19.2 W** | 3.84 W | **92 W** |

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. The return leg stays on, so there is one switching pair per motor.

### 2.1 Transition times

$$I_{g,on}=\frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{g}} = \frac{6.5}{5.3}=1.23\text{ A}, \quad t_{Miller}=\frac{Q_{gd}}{I_g}\approx 8.1\text{ ns}$$

Adding the Q_gs2 interval gives t_r ≈ t_f ≈ 12 ns. Turn-off is usually slower because the drive current is V_pl/R_g. Recompute t_f separately once the driver's sink resistance is known.

### 2.2 Loss terms per motor

| Term | Formula | Case B (80 A) | Case A (20 A) |
|---|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f | ½·25.2·80·24n·48k = **1.16 W** | 0.29 W |
| C_oss | Q_oss·V·f | 40n·25.2·48k = 0.048 W | 0.048 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f | 50n·25.2·48k = 0.060 W | ~0.02 W (Q_rr scales with I) |
| Dead-time diode | V_SD·I·2t_dt·f | 0.8·80·100n·48k = 0.31 W | 0.077 W |
| Gate drive | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f | 2·50n·10·48k = 0.048 W | 0.048 W |
| **Total P_sw per motor** | | **≈ 1.63 W** | **≈ 0.48 W** |

The gate-drive loss is dissipated in the driver and R_g, not in the FET.

### 2.3 Design levers

- **Overlap loss** scales with f_sw. Dropping to 24 kHz halves it, but costs acoustic noise and current ripple.
- **Dead-time loss** is significant at 80 A. Minimize t_dt to the driver's safe minimum, using shoot-through margin measured on the bench.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Total dissipation

| Case | Per motor | 4 motors | Plus FC, BEC, MCU (~2 W) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A (80 A total) | ~1.9 W | ~7.7 W | **~9.7 W** |
| B (80 A per motor) | ~24.7 W | ~99 W | **~101 W** |

### 3.2 Thermal network (per FET to ambient)

```
Tj --[Rth_jc ≈ 1.5 K/W]-- Tcase --[Rth_cb ≈ 1–3 K/W via-array]-- PCB --[Rth_ba]-- Ambient
                                                                   |
                                                               [C_board]
```

- **Via array:** Use 0.3 mm vias, 0.6 mm pitch, filled. Each via is roughly 70–100 K/W, so 20 vias in parallel give ≈ 4 K/W. Fill the exposed-pad area.
- **Board to ambient (whole 20×20 board, R_ba):**
  - No airflow: ≈ 20–30 K/W. This is impractical; nothing is sustainable at either case.
  - Prop-wash, 5–10 m/s, with h ≈ 50–80 W/m²K over 2 × 400 mm²: ≈ **8–12 K/W**.

### 3.3 Steady state (prop wash, R_ba ≈ 10 K/W)

- **Case A:** ΔT_board ≈ 9.7 × 10 ≈ 97 K. This is too hot.
  - Sustained 80 A total requires R_ba ≤ ~6 K/W. That means a heat spreader or clip-on sink, or a duty-limited current.
  - Realistic sustained rating: ~40–50 A total, with 80 A as a burst.
- **Case B:** Not physically sustainable on this form factor.
