## 0. Assumptions and Architecture Correction

**ArduPilot does not commutate motors.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller only. Each motor phase bridge needs a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32/Bluejay-class firmware. The correct architecture is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──4× bidir DShot600──► 4× ESC MCU (AM32 on STM32G071 / AT32F421)
                                              └─► 4× 3-phase gate driver ─► 24× N-MOSFET
```

The datasheet values below are baseline assumptions. Replace them with your actual BOM values. All equations are parametric.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_0\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]$$

$$R_{DS}(125^\circ\text{C}) = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.45 = 1.74\ \text{m}\Omega \approx 1.8\ \text{m}\Omega \text{ (used below)}$$

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

During each 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for duty D. The synchronous low-side FET conducts for (1 − D).
- **Static leg:** the low-side FET conducts 100% of the time.

Phase current therefore always flows through exactly two channels. This makes conduction loss **independent of duty cycle**:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Each FET is active for 120° of each 360° electrical cycle. Its averaged loss is:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}}{3}$$

### 1.3 Numerical results at 80 A

| Quantity | Formula | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Per FET (hot) | 80² × 1.8 mΩ / 3 | **3.84 W** |
| Per motor (6 FETs) | 2 × 80² × 1.8 mΩ | **23.0 W** |
| Copper/shunt path (≈0.5 mΩ per motor) | 80² × 0.5 mΩ | **3.2 W** |

### 1.4 Electrothermal feedback

Conduction loss and junction temperature depend on each other:

$$T_j = T_a + P(T_j)\,Z_{th}, \qquad P(T_j) = 2I^2R_0[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

Solving gives:

$$T_j = \frac{T_a + 2I^2R_0(1-25\alpha)Z_{th}}{1 - 2I^2R_0\alpha Z_{th}}$$

**Thermal runaway condition:** the solution diverges when

$$2I^2R_0\,\alpha\,Z_{th} \ge 1$$

At 80 A this gives Z_th,crit = 1 / (2 · 6400 · 1.2e-3 · 0.0045) ≈ **14.5 K/W** per motor-equivalent node. A 20 mm board in still air sits around 15–25 K/W. **Therefore, 80 A is thermodynamically a transient-only rating. Without airflow it is not steady-state stable.**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one leg per motor hard-switches at a time. Each loss component is calculated per motor.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

Rise time is set by Miller charge and driver current: t_r ≈ Q_gd / I_G. Driver current is I_G ≈ (V_drv − V_plateau)/(R_G,ext + R_G,int).

- **To halve P_VI:** double the gate current, or lower R_G.
- **Trade-off:** a lower R_G worsens dV/dt ringing and the risk of shoot-through.

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 40\text{nC}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} \approx \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_f\,I\,(2t_d)\,f_{sw} = 0.8\times80\times200\text{ns}\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

$$P_{g} = Q_g\,V_{GS}\,f_{sw}\times 2 = 50\text{nC}\times10\times48\text{k}\times2 = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

This loss is dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FETs.

### 2.6 Switching total and sensitivity

| Component | Per motor |
|---|---|
| V·I overlap | 1.45 W |
| Dead time | 0.61 W |
| Q_rr + Q_oss | 0.10 W |
| **Switching total** | **≈ 2.16 W** |

Sensitivity to switching frequency: ∂P_sw/∂f_sw ≈ 45 mW/kHz per motor.

- **24 kHz:** saves ~1.1 W per motor.
- **96 kHz:** costs +2.2 W per motor, but gives quieter, smoother low-throttle operation.

At 80 A, **conduction dominates (~10:1)**. The choice of switching frequency is a secondary lever.

## 3. Total Loss Budget at 80 A Burst

| Scenario | Conduction | Switching | Copper | **Total** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 motor at 80 A | 23.0 W | 2.2 W | 3.2 W | **28.4 W** |
| 4 motors at 80 A | 92.0 W | 8.6 W | 12.8 W | **≈113 W** |
| 4 motors at 40 A (cruise) | 23.0 W* | 4.3 W | 3.2 W | **≈31 W** |

\* At cruise, R_DS is cooler (≈1.4 mΩ at 70 °C), giving 2 · 1600 · 1.4e-3 · 4 ≈ 18 W of conduction; 23 W is the conservative figure.

Efficiency at 4 × 80 A: electrical input is roughly 25.2 V × 320 A × D. At full throttle this means ≈ **98.6%** inverter efficiency. The losses are small relative to throughput, but extremely large relative to the board's thermal mass.
