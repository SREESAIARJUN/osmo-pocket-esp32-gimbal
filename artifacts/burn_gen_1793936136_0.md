# 3-Axis Gimbal FOC Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion: Design and Implementation

I don't have the Job #14 specification, so I've used typical values for a brushless camera gimbal. Every assumption is a named constant in the code.

**Assumptions:**
- **Motors:** 12N14P gimbal BLDC per axis.
- **Encoders:** 14-bit magnetic (AS5048A/MT6701 class).
- **Drivers:** 3-phase with low-side current sensing.
- **MCU:** STM32G4/H7 class with a hardware FPU.
- **IMUs:** two ICM-42688-P units on the camera plate, connected over SPI.

## 2. FOC Current Loop

### 2.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

The Clarke transform converts measured phase currents to the stationary α/β frame:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}\quad(i_a+i_b+i_c=0)
$$

The Park transform rotates into the rotor frame, using electrical angle $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$ with $p = 7$ pole pairs:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 2.2 Plant and decoupled PI

The motor voltage equations in the dq frame are:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda
$$

Choose PI zero/pole cancellation, which makes the closed loop first-order with bandwidth $\omega_c$:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c
$$

Add cross-coupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(e_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(e_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda
$$
