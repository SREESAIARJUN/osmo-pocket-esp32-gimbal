## 0. Architectural Corrections and Assumptions

Several premises in the request need correcting before any numbers are meaningful.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is flight-controller firmware, not ESC firmware. A 4-in-1 ESC needs one dedicated ESC MCU per motor running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. Suitable parts are the AT32F421 or STM32G071, which handle 48 kHz PWM, BEMF zero-cross detection and bidirectional DShot telemetry. The H743 sends DShot600 frames to these four MCUs and receives eRPM back.
2. **"80 A burst" on a 20×20 mm (nano) board is a transient rating, not a thermal steady state.** The math in §3 shows the board can sustain it for seconds, not minutes. Treat any "80 A" figure as a marketing burst number unless it comes with a duration.
3. **The netlists below are reference-design level.** They are not a released, DRC-checked CAD export. Every part number must be checked against current datasheets before layout.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology

In 6-step commutation, two phase legs conduct at any instant:

- one leg is switched (high-side and low-side FETs alternate at duty D, complementary);
- one leg is held on (a single FET conducting continuously).

Ignoring dead time, **exactly two FETs carry the phase current at any instant.** This holds for every duty cycle D, because synchronous rectification moves the freewheel current from the body diode into the channel.

### 1.2 Equations

Temperature-dependent on-resistance:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}\ \Rightarrow\ k_{T}(125^\circ C)\approx1.5$$

Per-channel conduction loss (FETs plus copper):

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu}$$

Here R_Cu is the bus plus phase copper path. For a nano board with 2 oz inner planes and 3 oz outer layers, assume R_Cu ≈ 0.5 mΩ.

How the loss divides between the two FETs in the switching leg:

$$P_{HS} = D\,I^2R_{DS},\qquad P_{LS} = (1-D)\,I^2R_{DS}$$

The hottest single device is the FET in the held-on leg, which conducts 100 % of its 60° commutation sector. Averaged over a full electrical cycle, each FET conducts for one third of the time.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

- R_DS = 1.5 × 1.5 mΩ = **2.25 mΩ**
- FET conduction: P = 2 × 80² × 0.00225 = **28.8 W per channel**
- Copper: 80² × 0.0005 = **3.2 W per channel**
- Dead-time diode conduction (2 edges per PWM period):

$$P_{dt}=V_F\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw}=0.8\times80\times200\,\text{ns}\times48\,\text{kHz}=\mathbf{0.61\ W}$$
