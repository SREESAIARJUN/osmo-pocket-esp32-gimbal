## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology

In 6-step synchronous PWM, at any instant:
- one phase's high-side (HS) FET is PWM'd at duty D,
- the same leg's low-side (LS) FET synchronously rectifies for (1−D),
- a second phase's LS FET is held fully on (100%).

So the motor current I always flows through **exactly two R_DS(on) in series**, regardless of D (dead time excluded):

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2 \cdot R_{DS}(T_j)\cdot[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature Dependence

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

This gives a factor of about 1.6 at 125 °C, which matches typical datasheet normalized curves. T_j depends on P, which depends on R_DS(T_j), so you solve by fixed-point iteration:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{ref} + P\big(R_{DS}(T_j^{(n)})\big)\cdot Z_{th}$$

**Thermal runaway check:** a stable solution exists only if

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,Z_{th} = 2I^2 R_{DS,25}\,\alpha\, Z_{th} < 1$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A (T_j = 125 °C, R_DS = 1.2 mΩ × 1.6 = 1.92 mΩ)

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.92\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{24.6\ W\ per\ motor}$$

- Worst case using R_DS,max (1.5 mΩ × 1.6 = 2.4 mΩ): **30.7 W per motor.**
- Per-FET average over one electrical cycle: each FET carries current for about 1/3 of the cycle. That gives roughly 24.6 / 6 ≈ **4.1 W average**, with a **12.3 W instantaneous** peak during its active sector.

### 1.4 Copper and Interconnect Conduction (often ignored, and it shouldn't be)

| Path | R estimate | Current | Loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase pad + trace, per phase (2 oz, ~8 mm, multilayer parallel) | 0.3 mΩ | 80 A, 2/3 duty | ≈1.3 W per motor (2 phases active) |
| Battery pad → bus planes | 0.15 mΩ | I_bat (see below) | ≈ 3-15 W |

Battery current: I_bat ≈ Σ I_motor · D. At D = 0.9 with 4×80 A this is about 288 A, which is **not credible for a 30×30 board's XT60/pads.** Realistically the "80 A burst" figure is per-phase current at partial duty. Size battery pads and leads for **≥150 A burst aggregate** and document it.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

### 2.1 Hard-Switching Overlap (HS FET, inductive load)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12\,V_{bus}\,I\,(t_r + t_f)\,f_{sw}, \qquad t_{r,f} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}\,R_G}{V_{drv} - V_{plateau}}$$

With a ~1 A peak driver (FD6288Q-class) and Q_gd = 6 nC, t_r ≈ t_f ≈ 12 ns:

$$P_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 24\times10^{-9} \times 48\,000 = \mathbf{1.16\ W}$$

### 2.2 Output-Capacitance Loss

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss}V^2 f_{sw} \times 2 = 0.5\times1.2\text{nF}\times 25.2^2\times 48\text{k}\times2 = \mathbf{0.037\ W}$$

### 2.3 Body-Diode Reverse Recovery (LS diode → HS turn-on)

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V_{bus}\,f_{sw} = 40\text{nC}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

Note: Q_rr grows with di/dt and temperature. At 125 °C, budget about 2× this value (0.1 W).

### 2.4 Dead-Time Diode Conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\,I\,(2\,t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8\times80\times200\text{ns}\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$
