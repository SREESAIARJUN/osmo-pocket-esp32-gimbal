# Job #9: STM32H743 Nano AIO (Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC)

## Architecture, Loss Model, Thermal Budget, Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation and Netlists

**Document status:** Engineering baseline, Rev A. Every number is derived from the stated assumptions. Swap in vendor datasheet values before layout freeze.

---

## 0. Executive Findings

Four findings drive the rest of the design.

1. **The H743 must not commutate the motors directly.** Four 3-phase bridges need 24 gate signals. The H743 has only two advanced timers (TIM1 and TIM8, 6 complementary outputs each) plus HRTIM (10 outputs), which totals 22. It also has no hardware for four independent BEMF zero-cross detectors.
   - **Baseline:** the H743 runs ArduPilot. Four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421K8U7 running AM32) commutate at 48 kHz. Bidirectional DShot600 links the two over on-board traces.
2. **An 80 A phase-current burst is electrically achievable but thermally time-limited.** With all four motors at 80 A, total dissipation is about **107 W**. The adiabatic ceiling on a nano-class PCB is about **4 s**, and the realistic hot-spot limit is **≈1–2 s**.
   - The "80 A burst" rating must be specified as **80 A per motor, ≤1 s, 10 % duty, with an AM32 current limit enforced**.
3. **The on-board capacitors cannot absorb 80 A of ripple.** An external low-ESR electrolytic of at least 1000 µF / 35 V on short leads is a **mandatory** part of the system, not an option.
4. **Bidirectional DShot600 on the H743 needs 4 DMA streams** (2 timers × UP + CCx), arranged with ArduPilot's channel-pair `BIDIR` rule. DMAMUX removes the fixed stream conflicts, so the only real constraint is the stream budget.
