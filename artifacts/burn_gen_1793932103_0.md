# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage and DShot Architecture Model

## 0. Architectural Assumptions and Risks

These assumptions and risks change the design, so they come first.

| # | Item | Position |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Partitioning** | The H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU (STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32). The H743 does not commutate FETs, because ArduPilot has no BLDC commutation stack. |
| A2 | **Bus** | 6S. V_bus,max = 25.2 V, so the design uses 40 V FETs (30 V parts have too little margin for ringing on 6S). |
| A3 | **"80 A burst"** | Means per-channel phase current for ≤10 s. **It is not a concurrent rating.** 4 × 80 A at full duty is about 320 A of battery current, which is beyond XT60 (60 A continuous) and XT90 ratings and well beyond what a nano board's copper can carry. |
| A4 | **Form factor** | 30.5 × 30.5 mm mounting (36 × 36 mm board) is assumed for the numbers below. A true 20 × 20 "nano" roughly halves thermal mass and copper area. **An 80 A burst on 20 × 20 is not credible.** Expect about 35 to 45 A burst there. |
| A5 | **Placeholder FET** | 40 V, R_DS(on),25 = 1.0 mΩ @ 10 V, PDFN 5×6, Q_g = 60 nC, Q_rr = 50 nC, R_θJC = 1.2 K/W. Replace with datasheet values for the selected part. |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology current paths
The model assumes trapezoidal 6-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In each 60° sector:
- **Source phase:** the high-side FET switches at duty D, and the low-side freewheels for (1−D).
- **Sink phase:** the low-side FET conducts continuously.
- **Floating phase:** off (BEMF sensing).

At any instant, exactly two channel FETs carry the phase current I. The duty split only changes *which* FET carries it.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.004\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

This gives about 1.4× at 125 °C.

### 1.3 Channel and per-FET conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period, as either the active FET or the freewheel path. So the time-averaged loss per FET is:

$$\bar P_{cond,FET} = \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}\quad(\text{balanced, } D\to 1)$$

At partial throttle the loss shifts toward the low-side FETs, because they carry the freewheel (1−D) share. Size the low-side FET for worst case:

$$P_{LS,max} \approx I^2 R_{DS}\left[\tfrac13 + \tfrac13(1-D)\right]$$

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C
- R_DS = 1.0 × 1.4 = 1.4 mΩ
- P_cond,ch = 2 × 6400 × 1.4 mΩ = **17.9 W per channel**
- Per FET (average) ≈ 3.0 W

Add the copper path. Assume 2 oz copper, 6 parallel layers, and roughly 15 mm of path per phase at about 0.25 mΩ effective. That adds I²R ≈ 1.6 W per channel.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing FET pair hard-switches. The model has five terms.

**(a) V–I overlap (hard switching):**
$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

With t_r + t_f = 40 ns (FD6288-class driver, ~1 A source, R_g ≈ 4.7 Ω):

0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48 k = **1.94 W**

**(b) Reverse recovery** (the low-side body diode recovers when the high-side turns on):
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{nC}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = 0.06\text{ W}$$

**(c) Output capacitance** (Q_oss ≈ 40 nC):
$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} \times 2 = 0.05\text{ W}$$
