## 0. Architecture Correction and Assumptions

**Architectural correction.** ArduPilot running on the STM32H743 cannot commutate the motor bridges. It has no 6-step or FOC ESC firmware path. The only architecture consistent with "ArduPilot + bidirectional DShot600" is therefore:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──DShot600 bidir──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421K8U7, AM32 firmware)
                                              └─► FD6288Q 3-phase gate driver
                                                    └─► 6× N-MOSFET half-bridges per motor
```

> **Engineering flag (stated up front):** 80 A per channel on a nano footprint is achievable only as a short transient. Treat "80 A burst" as ≤1 s and enforce it in software (§6).

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-Step Trapezoidal, Synchronous Rectification)

### 1.1 Per-MOSFET conduction

In 6-step commutation, each phase leg is active for 2/3 of the electrical cycle:

- For 1/3 of the cycle it is the PWM-switched high side, at duty D.
- For 1/3 of the cycle it is the continuously-on low side.

With complementary (synchronous) PWM, the low side of the PWM leg conducts during the off-time.

$$P_{HS} = I^2 R_{hot}\,\frac{D}{3}$$

$$P_{LS} = I^2 R_{hot}\left[\frac{1-D}{3} + \frac{1}{3}\right] = I^2 R_{hot}\,\frac{2-D}{3}$$

Per leg:

$$P_{leg} = I^2 R_{hot}\cdot\frac{2}{3}$$

Per motor channel (3 legs):

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{hot}$$

This is physically consistent: exactly two FETs are in series with the winding at every instant.

### 1.2 Numbers at 80 A

- I² R_hot = 6400 × 0.0024 = **15.36 W**
- **P_cond,ch = 30.7 W per motor**
- **P_cond,total = 122.9 W for all four motors**

Worst single FET is the low side at D → 0 (low-throttle, high-current transient):

$$P_{LS,max} = 15.36\times\frac{2}{3} = 10.2\ \text{W}$$

At D = 0.5 this falls to 7.7 W.

### 1.3 Copper and shunt conduction (frequently forgotten)

**Bus/phase copper.** Use 6 layers × 2 oz with a parallel ~4 mm wide, ~15 mm path.

- R_sheet(2 oz) ≈ 0.25 mΩ/□.
- Per layer: 3.75 squares → 0.94 mΩ.
- With 4 power layers in parallel: ≈ 0.23 mΩ.

Loss is computed per path at 80 A: 6400 × 0.23 m = **1.5 W per channel**. Allow roughly **6 W total**.

**Current shunt.** The shunt carries the battery current, not the phase current.

- I_bat ≈ D · I_phase. Worst-case burst is about 4 × 80 × 0.8 ≈ 256 A.
- A 0.1 mΩ shunt dissipates **6.6 W** and produces 25.6 mV.
- Recommendation: 2× 0.2 mΩ 2512 shunts in parallel, Kelvin-routed to an INA-class amplifier with gain 50 → 1.28 V full-scale into the H743 ADC.
