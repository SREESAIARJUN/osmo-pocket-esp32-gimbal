## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I don't have the Job #5 requirements document, BOM, or stackup, so every parameter below is a stated assumption. Replace the values in **bold** with the real datasheet and stackup numbers and the equations carry through unchanged.

**Architecture assumed:** an STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs (for example, AM32 on AT32F421 or STM32G071), one per motor. Each ESC MCU drives a 3-phase driver and 6 MOSFETs, so the board has 24 FETs total. The H743 itself does not commutate the motors.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification (the AM32/BLHeli_32 default) means current always flows through **two** FETs in series:
- During on-time: one high-side FET plus one low-side FET.
- During off-time: the freewheeling path is low-side plus low-side.

So the conducting path resistance is independent of duty cycle:

$$R_{path}(T_j) = 2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + R_{Cu}(T_{pcb})$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]$$

At a design point of T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS(on)} = 1.5\,\text{mΩ}\times(1 + 0.005\times 85) = 2.14\ \text{mΩ}$$

### 1.3 Copper loss
The sheet resistance of 2 oz (70 µm) copper at 80 °C is:

$$R_\square = \frac{\rho_{Cu}(1+0.0039\,\Delta T)}{t} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\times1.23}{70\times10^{-6}} \approx 0.30\ \text{mΩ/□}$$

Assume about 3.3 squares of effective path across the bus entry, phase pad, and return, with 2 layers paralleled on each net. That gives **R_Cu ≈ 1.0 mΩ**. This term is routinely ignored and is about 20% of total loss here.

### 1.4 Ripple correction
For a current ripple of ΔI peak-to-peak:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

Motor inductance at 48 kHz keeps ΔI small, under 10% of I. The correction is below 0.1%, so it is neglected.
