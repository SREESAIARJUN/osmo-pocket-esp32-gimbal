## 0. System Architecture

A 4‑in‑1 ESC does **not** commutate motors from the H743. The partitioning is:

```
            ┌────────────── STM32H743 (FC, ArduPilot) ──────────────┐
 IMU/SPI ──►│ TIM3_CH3/CH4, TIM5_CH1/CH2  ── Bidir DShot600 (×4) ──┼──► ESC MCU ×4 (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 or BLHeli_32)
            └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘          │ 48 kHz complementary PWM
                                                                                ▼
                                                  3 × half‑bridge per motor = 24 N‑MOSFETs total
```

- Each ESC channel is a 6‑step trapezoidal drive.
- PWM is on the high side with **synchronous (complementary)** low‑side rectification.
- Switching frequency is f_sw = 48 kHz.
