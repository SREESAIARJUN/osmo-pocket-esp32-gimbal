## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1 stack, including AM32 and BLHeli_32 designs, the FC MCU sends DShot commands. Each ESC channel, or a shared ESC MCU per motor, runs its own commutation firmware. This document assumes:

| Block | Device (reference design) | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz | ArduPilot, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) | AM32 firmware, 48 kHz complementary PWM |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, 250 V, bootstrap) | Drives 6 N-FETs per motor |
| Power FETs ×24 | 60 V N-ch, PDFN 5×6 class | 6S-capable bridge |
| Current sense | 0.25 mΩ shunt + INA186A3 (100 V/V) | Battery current telemetry |

**Electrical operating point (stated, not given in the brief):**

- 6S LiPo: V_bus = 24 V for loss calculations; 25.2 V max; 60 V FETs for >2× margin against regen spikes.
- f_sw = 48 kHz, six-step trapezoidal, synchronous (complementary) PWM, dead time t_dt = 100 ns.
- Burst: I_phase = 80 A per motor, all four motors simultaneously (worst case).

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in six-step synchronous PWM

During each 60° commutation sector, exactly two phases carry current:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T_sw. Its complementary low-side FET conducts for (1−D)·T_sw, minus dead time.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET conducts continuously for the sector.

With synchronous rectification, the phase current always flows through **exactly two channels in series**, regardless of D:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

Temperature dependence:

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]
$$

### 1.2 Evaluation at 80 A

| T_j (°C) | R_DS(on) (mΩ) | P_cond per motor (W) | P_cond, 4 motors (W) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | 1.60 | 20.5 | 82 |
| 100 | 2.44 | 31.2 | 125 |
| 125 | 2.72 | 34.8 | 139 |

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET is the "return" device for 2 of 6 sectors and part of the PWM leg for 2 of 6 sectors. Averaged over an electrical revolution, loss spreads roughly evenly across the six devices:

$$
P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,motor}}{6} = 5.8\ \text{W @ } T_j = 125\,^\circ\text{C}
$$

> **Caveat (low-rpm / stall):** at very low electrical frequency (startup, prop strike), one FET can sit in the return role for tens of ms and see the full I²R ≈ 17 W. Size the overcurrent and desync protection for this case.

### 1.4 Ancillary conduction losses

| Element | Model | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery shunt (0.25 mΩ), I_batt = D·ΣI_ph; worst case D=0.8, 4×80 A → 256 A | I²R | **16.4 W** (!) |
| PCB copper, 2 oz, ~0.3 mΩ effective per phase path | 80²·0.3 mΩ ×4 | 7.7 W |
| Motor lead solder joints (~0.1 mΩ each, 2 per path) | — | ~5 W total |

**Recommendation:** at 256 A, a single 0.25 mΩ shunt dissipates 16 W. Use 0.1 mΩ (6.6 W), or per-motor sensing with 4 × 0.5 mΩ, where each sees ≤ 64 A battery-side (≈2 W each).
