# STM32H743 Nano AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Thermal, Switching and DShot Timer Architecture

**Document:** Job #1, Rev A (engineering baseline)
**Status:** Analytical model. All component values are stated assumptions and must be checked against the selected parts' datasheets and bench thermography before release.

---

## 0. Feasibility Findings

Three findings frame the rest of the design.

1. **The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** On every production AIO, each ESC channel (or one shared ESC MCU) runs its own commutation firmware, typically AM32 or BLHeli_32, on an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 to the ESC MCU or MCUs. This architecture keeps that split. Driving 12 gates from the H743 is not viable: ArduPilot has no motor-commutation layer, and doing so would break fault containment.

2. **80 A is a burst rating, not a continuous one.** On a nano board (20×20 mm mounting, about 26×26 mm outline), the model in §3 gives:
   - roughly **1.5–2 s** of 4×80 A burst before the hot spot reaches 110 °C, starting from 40 °C;
   - a **continuous limit of about 16 A per motor** with prop-wash cooling.

   This is consistent with commercial nano AIOs, which are rated 20–35 A. If a "true 80 A" label is a marketing requirement, it must be defined as a burst of ≤1 s at a 10% duty cycle.

3. **Voltage class.** The design assumes 6S (25.2 V maximum) with 40 V MOSFETs. 8S would require 60 V parts with roughly 2× the R_DS(on) per footprint, and the conduction model would not close.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel, 6 FETs)

### 2.1 Conduction losses

With six-step trapezoidal commutation and complementary (synchronous) PWM, two phases conduct at every instant:

- the PWM'd high-side FET, at duty D;
- its synchronous low-side complement, at 1 − D;
- the fully-on low-side FET of the return phase, at 1.

Including current ripple:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12},\qquad
\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}
$$

$$
P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] = 1.0 \times (1+0.0065\times 85) \approx 1.55\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

The ripple term is small. For a 5 µH phase inductance, D = 0.8 and V_bemf = 18 V, ΔI ≈ 24 A, so ΔI²/12 ≈ 48 A² compared with 6400 A².

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \times 6448 \times 1.55\times10^{-3} \approx \mathbf{20.0\ W}
$$

Copper losses add:

$$
P_{Cu} = 2\,I^2 R_{trace} = 2 \times 6400 \times 0.3\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{3.8\ W}
$$

These copper losses go directly into the board, which matters thermally.

### 2.2 Switching losses at 48 kHz (one switching leg active at a time)

| Term | Expression | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw = ½ · 25.2 · 80 · 50 ns · 48 k | 2.42 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw = 40 nC · 25.2 · 48 k | 0.05 W |
| C_oss | ½ · C_oss · V² · f_sw = ½ · 1.2 nF · 635 · 48 k | 0.02 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_SD · I · 2 · t_dt · f_sw = 0.8 · 80 · 200 ns · 48 k | 0.61 W |
| Gate drive (2 FETs per PWM leg) | 2 · Q_g · V_GS · f_sw = 2 · 50 nC · 10 · 48 k | 0.05 W |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 3.15 W** |

Note that 80 A at 25 ns edges gives di/dt ≈ 3.2 A/ns. With 1 nH of loop inductance, the turn-off overshoot is about 3.2 V. Bulk capacitance must therefore sit within 3 mm of each half-bridge (§5).

### 2.3 Totals at 80 A burst

| Scope | Loss |
|---|---|
| Per channel | 20.0 + 3.8 + 3.15 ≈ **27 W** |
| Four channels | ≈ **108 W** |
| Ancillary: BEC, H743, gate drivers, ESC MCUs | +1.5 W |
| **Board total** | ≈ **110 W** |

Efficiency check: the electrical power delivered is about 4 × 80 A × 22.2 V × D ≈ 5.7 kW at D = 0.8, so the ESC efficiency is about 98%. That is reasonable. The problem is heat density, not efficiency.

### 2.4 Closed-form loss versus current (for derating firmware)

$$
P_{ch}(I) = \underbrace{2(R_{DS,hot}+R_{trace})}_{a\,=\,3.7\,\text{m}\Omega}\,I^2 \;+\; \underbrace{\left[\tfrac12 V(t_r+t_f)f_{sw} + 2V_{SD}t_{dt}f_{sw}\right]}_{b\,\approx\,0.038\ \text{W/A}}\,I \;+\; \underbrace{c}_{\approx 0.07\ \text{W}}
$$
