**Architectural correction.** ArduPilot does not run ESC commutation on the H743. The real topology is as follows:

- The **STM32H743** is the flight controller running ArduPilot. It emits 4× bidirectional DShot600.
- Each of the **4 ESC channels** has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071. That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM and decodes DShot.

The model below follows this split: power-stage math for the ESC side, timer/DMA math for the H743 side.

> ⚠️ **Feasibility flag.** An "80 A" rating on a nano-class (20×20 or 30×30 mm) 4-in-1 is a **transient burst rating, never a continuous rating**. Section 3 proves this quantitatively. Treat 80 A as a ≤5 s event.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step trapezoidal drive

At any instant two phases conduct: one high-side (HS) path and one low-side (LS) path. With complementary PWM at duty D:

- The PWM'd leg's **HS FET** conducts for fraction D.
- That leg's **LS FET** conducts for fraction (1−D), as synchronous freewheeling.
- The opposite leg's **LS FET** conducts 100 % of the 60° sector.

The motor current I therefore always flows through exactly **two R_DS(on) in series**:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = I^2 \left[ D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS,sync} + R_{LS,ret} \right] = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

This assumes matched FETs, and it is independent of D. Per FET, averaged over an electrical cycle, each switch is active for 120° = 1/3 of the time:

$$I_{rms,FET} = I\sqrt{1/3}\ \text{(LS return)},\qquad P_{FET,avg} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS}$$

The instantaneous per-FET peak, ≈ I²R, governs short-term junction rise.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence (the iteration)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}$$
