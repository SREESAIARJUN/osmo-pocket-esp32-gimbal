## 0. Architecture Assumptions

The H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600. It does **not** drive the bridges. Each ESC channel has its own ESC MCU (e.g., an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32), and that MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. This is the only practical topology. The H743 cannot run 4× sensorless FOC/6-step commutation and the flight stack with deterministic timing.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-step, synchronous)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R = 0.90 × 1.3825 ≈ **1.24 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Per-FET conduction loss

Each phase leg spends one third of the electrical period in each state: high (PWM), low (sink), and floating. During the high third, the HS FET conducts for duty D and the LS FET conducts for (1−D) as the synchronous rectifier. During the low third, the LS FET is fully on.
