# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #6): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan, Netlists

## 0. Architectural Corrections and Stated Assumptions

Three points need settling first because the rest of the math depends on them.

1. **The H743 does not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM.** In any ArduPilot-compatible design, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU that does commutation, 48 kHz PWM, and eRPM telemetry encoding. I assume one AT32F421 per channel running AM32, which supports selectable PWM frequency. Bluejay on an EFM8 also supports 48 kHz. Putting FOC/6-step for four motors on the H743 would break ArduPilot's motor-output model. I would not do it.
2. **4 × 80 A burst on a "nano" footprint is thermally a short-duration rating.** Section 3 shows it is limited to about 1–1.5 s from a warm start. Continuous ratings are roughly an order of magnitude lower without a heatsink.
3. **Design parameters.** The table below lists them. They are representative values. Substitute your exact MOSFET datasheet values into the formulas, which are written generically.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max / 22.2 V nominal |
| Burst phase current per motor | I | 80 A (flat-top, trapezoidal) |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFETs | — | 24 × 40 V N-ch, PDFN3333 |
| On-resistance | R_ds,on @25 °C, V_gs = 10 V | 1.2 mΩ |
| Rds temperature coefficient (linearised) | α | 0.005 /K |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 50 nC |
| Gate-drain charge | Q_gd | 10 nC |
| Post-threshold gate-source charge | Q_gs2 | 6 nC |
