### Loop Rates and Bandwidths

Bandwidths are separated by a factor of 5–10× between successive loops.

| Loop | Rate | Closed-loop BW |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC, d/q PI) | 20 kHz | ~1.5–2 kHz |
| Velocity (PI + anti-cogging FF) | 4 kHz | ~150–250 Hz |
| Angle / stabilization (P + rate FF) | 1 kHz | ~20–40 Hz |
| IMU acquisition + filtering | 8 kHz | — |
| Attitude estimator | 1 kHz | — |

## 1. FOC Current Loop (Inner Loop)

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform** (assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform** (θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset):

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant and Decoupling

The PMSM model in the rotor frame is:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d\dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L_q\dot i_q + \omega_e (L_d i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

The speed-dependent cross terms are cancelled with feedforward, which reduces each axis to a first-order plant 1/(Ls + R):

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e(L_d i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

Here λ_m = Kt/(1.5·p) for the amplitude-invariant transform.

### 1.3 PI Gains by Pole-Zero Cancellation

The PI zero is placed on the electrical pole (s = −R/L), which gives the closed loop ω_c/(s + ω_c):

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c = 2\pi f_c
$$

A reasonable starting point is f_c ≈ f_PWM / 10–20 (1–2 kHz at 20 kHz PWM).

### 1.4 Discrete Implementation and Delay Compensation

**Discrete PI** (backward Euler) with back-calculation anti-windup:

$$
u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\quad u_k^{sat}=\text{sat}(u_k),\quad
I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw}(u_k^{sat}-u_k)
$$

Use K_aw ≈ K_i/K_p, the reciprocal of the integral time constant.

**Transport delay.** Computation plus PWM update adds about 1.5·T_s of delay. Compensate by advancing the angle used in the inverse Park transform:

$$
\theta_e^{pwm} = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s
$$
