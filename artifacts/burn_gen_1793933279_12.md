# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #64): Power-Stage Thermal Model, Switching Losses, and Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Allocation

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Warning

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU. It does not commutate motors. Each 4-in-1 ESC channel uses a dedicated ESC MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32), a 3-phase gate driver, and 6 MOSFETs. The H743 talks to the ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600. The models below follow that partitioning.

**Feasibility.** "80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 mm mount, ~25×25 mm board) 4-in-1 is thermally marginal. Section 3 shows it is only a few seconds of all-motor burst before board copper saturates. It is achievable only as a short transient on a 6–8 layer, 2 oz board. Treat 80 A as a per-motor transient rating, not a continuous one. If continuous ratings above ~35–40 A are required, move to a 30.5×30.5 mm form factor.

**Status of the figures.** All component values below are representative parameters for a 40 V PDFN5×6 MOSFET class. Before release, substitute the actual datasheet values for the selected part. The equations are exact; the numbers are only as good as the inputs.
