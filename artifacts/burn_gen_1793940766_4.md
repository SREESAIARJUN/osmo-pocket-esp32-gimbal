## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Feasibility Check

### 0.1 Partitioning

An STM32H743 cannot run four sensorless 6-step/FOC motor loops at 48 kHz while also running ArduPilot. The architecture therefore uses two tiers:

| Tier | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz | ArduPilot | Attitude control, bidirectional DShot600 master |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6 | AM32 | 48 kHz complementary PWM, commutation, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrapped, 20 V) | n/a | 12 MOSFETs per ESC |
| Power stage | 48 N-ch MOSFETs, 40 V, PDFN3.3×3 | n/a | 6 per motor |

### 0.2 Feasibility Warning

> **80 A per motor, even as a burst, on a 20×20 / 25.5×25.5 mm "nano" board is at the edge of physical feasibility.** The math below shows roughly **125 W of dissipation per motor-group at 80 A**. The board can survive this only as a heat-capacity-limited transient lasting a few seconds. It cannot be a continuous rating.
>
> Section 3 derives the realistic burst window. **Recommendation:** spec it as **80 A / 2 s burst, 40 A continuous (with airflow)**, or move to 30.5×30.5 mm.

### 0.3 Operating Point

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage | V_bus | 25.2 V (6S full charge, worst-case switching) |
| Burst phase current | I_ph | 80 A (flat-top approximation) |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz (AM32 setting) |
| R_DS(on) at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R₂₅ | 1.5 mΩ ⚠ |
| R_DS(on) temperature coefficient | α | ≈ 0.0045 /K, so R(T) = R₂₅·[1 + α(T_j − 25)] |

| Output charge | Q_oss | 40 nC ⚠ |
| Gate charge | Q_g | 40 nC at 10 V |

## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 1.1 Commutation Model

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM:

- In each 60° sector, phase A's high side is PWM'd at duty D, and its low side is the synchronous rectifier during (1−D).
- Phase B's low side is held ON for the whole sector.
- Each MOSFET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period (120°).

At any instant, exactly **two** channel resistances are in the current path:

- HS_A + LS_B during the on-time
- LS_A + LS_B during the off-time

### 1.2 Conduction Loss

Per motor:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Per device, high side of the PWM leg:

$$P_{cond,HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,D\,I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Per device, low side of the PWM leg:

$$P_{cond,LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,(1-D)\,I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j) + \tfrac{1}{3}\,I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

The second term in the low-side expression is the interval when that low side is the "held" leg.

Evaluated at T_j = 125 °C, where R_DS = 1.5 mΩ × 1.45 = 2.18 mΩ:

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.18\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{27.9\ W\ per\ motor}$$

### 1.3 Switching Loss (hard-switched PWM leg only)

**Overlap (V–I crossover) loss:**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = \tfrac12(25.2)(80)(24\text{ ns})(48\text{ k}) = \mathbf{1.16\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery.** The low-side body diode conducts during dead time and is hard-commutated:

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = 0.036\ W$$

**Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss} \approx Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = 0.048\ W$$

**Dead-time diode conduction (two edges per period):**

$$P_{dt} = 2\,V_F I_{ph} t_{dt} f_{sw} = 2(0.8)(80)(50\text{n})(48\text{k}) = 0.307\ W$$

**Gate drive** (2 active switching FETs). This is dissipated mostly in the FD6288Q and the gate resistors:

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2(40\text{n})(10)(48\text{k}) = 0.038\ W$$

**Switching total:** P_sw ≈ **1.55 W per motor** at D < 1.

At D → 1 (full throttle) there is no PWM switching, so P_sw → 0. **The worst case is therefore D ≈ 0.9–0.95 combined with 80 A.**

### 1.4 Bulk Capacitor Ripple

$$I_{C,rms} = I_{ph}\sqrt{D(1-D)}$$

| Duty D | Ripple current per motor |
|---|---|
| 0.5 | 40 A (theoretical max) |
| 0.9 | 24 A |

With four motors on one bus and staggered PWM phases, the aggregate is lower but still more than 30 A_rms.

**Requirements:**
- 1× low-ESR polymer per motor plus the main pack capacitor.
- ≥ 4× 10 µF/50 V X7R 1210 MLCCs per half-bridge pair, placed at < 3 mm loop length.
- Capacitor loss: P = I²·ESR. With ESR_eff = 3 mΩ, that is 24² × 0.003 ≈ **1.7 W** total.

### 1.5 Shunt and Copper

**Phase-current or bus shunt.** At 0.25 mΩ, 80 A gives 1.6 W.
- Recommended: one 0.2 mΩ 2512 shunt on the DC bus per board, not per phase.

**Copper.** Assume 4-layer, 2 oz outer / 1 oz inner, with an effective path of 2 mΩ per motor from pad to motor wire.

$$P_{Cu} = I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.002 \approx 12.8\ \text{W/motor}$$

> ⚠ This is frequently the hidden killer. **Mandatory:**
> - Exposed copper on the phase pads, solder-loaded.
> - Motor wires soldered directly at the MOSFET drains.

Target R_Cu < 0.5 mΩ, which gives 3.2 W.
