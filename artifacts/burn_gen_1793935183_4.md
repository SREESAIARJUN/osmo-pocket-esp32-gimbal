| ESC MCU | AT32F421 / STM32G071 per channel, running AM32 | See note 1 |

**Note 1: Architectural correction.** The H743 should **not** commutate the motors directly. ArduPilot contains no ESC firmware. A single MCU running four sensorless BEMF loops alongside the flight stack would also be a real-time and safety risk.

The industry-standard partition is used instead:
- The **H743 runs ArduPilot**.
- It sends **bidirectional DShot600** to **four dedicated ESC MCUs** on the same PCB (AIO) or on a stacked board.

**Note 2: "Nano" plus 80 A is a contradiction, and §2.6 shows why.** A 20×20 mm board cannot thermally sustain 80 A. The math below holds 80 A only as a short burst and recommends a 30.5×30.5 mm board for this rating.

## 1. Power Stage Topology and Operating Model

Each channel is a 3-phase half-bridge driven by 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At any instant within a commutation step:

- **Active (high-side PWM) phase:**
  - The high-side FET conducts during duty D.
  - The low-side FET conducts during (1−D), less dead time.
- **Return phase:** the low-side FET is fully on.

So motor current always flows through exactly **two** conducting channels: one switching leg plus one static low-side.

Definitions:
- I_m = motor phase current (DC within a step for trapezoidal drive; I_rms ≈ I_m)
- D = PWM duty
- f_sw = 48 kHz
- V = V_bus

## 2. Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R ≈ 1.5 × R_25 = **1.5 mΩ**.

### 2.2 Conduction loss per channel

The current path always contains two R_DS(on) in series:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_m^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

At 80 A, T_j = 125 °C:

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

At T_j = 25 °C it is 12.8 W. The positive temperature coefficient creates electrothermal feedback, which is solved in §2.7.

**Distribution across the 6 FETs.** Each phase is active-high for 1/3 of the electrical cycle and returns low for 1/3:
- Average per FET ≈ P_cond / 6 ≈ **3.2 W** at 80 A.
