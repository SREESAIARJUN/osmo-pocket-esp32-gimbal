# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #15): System Architecture & Design Model

**Revision A: preliminary engineering model. All component values are assumptions to be validated on the bench.**

---

## 0. Architecture Clarifications

Three assumptions shape the rest of the model.

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** It runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600. Each motor is driven by its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. This model assumes an AT32F421, an STM32G071, or one dedicated MCU per channel. The 48 kHz PWM frequency is an ESC-firmware parameter, not an H743 timer.
2. **"80 A burst" means 80 A per motor phase current for a short duration.** This is not a continuous rating. The thermal analysis below shows that on a nano form factor it is a seconds-scale rating.
3. **Power stage assumptions:**
   - 6S (25.2 V max)
   - 40 V N-MOSFETs in PDFN3.3×3
   - 6 FETs per channel, 24 total
   - 30.5×30.5 mm mounting, ~36×36 mm board, 8-layer, 2 oz copper

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-Dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.5 mΩ × 1.6 = **2.4 mΩ**

### 2.2 Six-Step Commutation with Synchronous (Complementary) PWM

During any sector, three switches carry current:

- The PWM'd high-side, which conducts for duty D
- Its complementary low-side, which conducts for (1−D) as a synchronous rectifier
- The opposite-leg low-side, which conducts 100% of the time

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds}\left[D + (1-D) + 1\right] = 2 I^2 R_{ds}$$

The result is duty-independent, which is the worst-case-friendly property of complementary PWM.

At I = 80 A, T_j = 125 °C:

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.0024 = \mathbf{30.7\ W\ per\ channel}$$

The loss is spread over two conducting devices, about 15.4 W each.

### 2.3 Parasitic Conduction Paths

| Source | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| Shunt | 80² × 0.5 mΩ | 3.2 W |
| PCB copper (≈0.3 mΩ phase path, 2 oz, multilayer paralleled) | 80² × 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |

**Total conduction-related loss: ≈35.8 W per channel @ 80 A.**

## 3. Switching Loss Model @ 48 kHz

In six-step operation, only one leg hard-switches per sector.

### 3.1 Overlap (V-I Crossover) Loss

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 25\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.21\ W}$$

### 3.2 Reverse Recovery Loss

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

### 3.3 Output Capacitance Loss

$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac{1}{2} Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = \mathbf{0.018\ W}$$

### 3.4 Dead-Time Body-Diode Conduction

Two dead-time events occur per PWM period:

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD} I \cdot 2 t_{dt} f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 400\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$

> **Design lever:** reducing t_dt to 100 ns (if the gate driver and layout permit) halves this loss.

### 3.5 Gate Drive Loss (Driver IC, Not the FETs)

$$P_g = Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} \times N_{switching} = 40\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} \times 2 = 0.038\ \text{W}$$

### 3.6 Frequency Sensitivity

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial f_{sw}} = \frac{P_{sw} + P_{rr} + P_{oss} + P_{dt}}{f_{sw}} \approx 52\ \mu\text{W/Hz}$$

Going from 24 kHz to 48 kHz costs about **+1.25 W per channel at 80 A**. That is under 4% of total loss. **At burst current, conduction dominates (~93%).** The 48 kHz choice is justified by lower current ripple, smoother low-throttle control, and inaudible operation.

### 3.7 Per-Channel Loss Budget @ 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

| Loss | W |
|---|---|
| MOSFET conduction | 30.7 |
| Shunt + copper | 5.1 |
| Switching (overlap) | 1.21 |
| Dead-time diode | 1.23 |
| Q_rr + Q_oss | 0.07 |
| **Total** | **≈38.3 W** |
| Efficiency @ ~1.9 kW in | ≈98.0% |

## 4. Thermal Model

### 4.1 Steady-State Junction Rise Above Case (Per FET)

$$\Delta T_{jc} = P_{FET} \cdot R_{\theta JC} = 16 \times 1.5 \approx \mathbf{24\ K}$$

This is acceptable. **The bottleneck is board bulk temperature, not junction-to-case.**

### 4.2 Lumped Board Thermal Capacitance (36×36 mm, 1.6 mm, 8L/2 oz)

| Element | Calculation | Heat capacity |
|---|---|---|
| FR-4 | 2.07 cm³ × 1.9 J/(cm³·K) | 3.9 J/K |
| Copper (≈65% fill × 8 × 70 µm) | 0.47 cm³ × 3.45 J/(cm³·K) | 1.6 J/K |
| FETs, caps, solder | — | ≈0.6 J/K |
| **C_th, total** | | **≈6.1 J/K** |

Convective resistance with prop-wash at 5–10 m/s is R_θBA ≈ 4–6 K/W. Use **5 K/W**.
