## 1. FOC Current Loop (innermost, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

`θ_off` is found at startup by applying `v_d` at `θ_e = 0` and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, two shunts, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant in the dq frame

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

where $\omega_e = p\,\omega_m$ and $\lambda_m = \tfrac{2}{3}K_t/p$ (amplitude-invariant).

### 1.5 PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

$$v_d^* = K_{p,i}e_d + K_{i,i}\!\int e_d\,dt \;-\; \omega_e L\, i_q$$

$$v_q^* = K_{p,i}e_q + K_{i,i}\!\int e_q\,dt \;+\; \omega_e L\, i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

**Gain selection by pole–zero cancellation.** The plant is $1/(Ls+R)$. Choosing a closed-loop bandwidth $\omega_{ci}$:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}\quad\Rightarrow\quad \frac{I}{I^*}=\frac{\omega_{ci}}{s+\omega_{ci}}$$

Use $\omega_{ci} \le 2\pi f_s/20$. At $f_s = 20$ kHz this gives $\omega_{ci} = 2\pi\cdot 1$ kHz, so $K_{p,i} = 13.8\ \text{V/A}$ and $K_{i,i} = 35\,186\ \text{V/(A·s)}$.

**Discrete form (forward Euler on the integrator) with back-calculation anti-windup:**

$$u_k = K_p e_k + I_k + u_{ff},\qquad \bar u_k = \text{sat}(u_k)$$

$$I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw}(\bar u_k - u_k),\qquad K_{aw}=1/K_p \ \text{(typ.)}$$

### 1.6 Voltage vector limit (circular)

$$|v| = \sqrt{v_d^{*2}+v_q^{*2}} \le V_{max} = \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}\cdot m_{max},\quad m_{max}=0.95$$

Give $d$ priority: $v_q^{max} = \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}$.
