**Risks to accept or reject before committing to layout:**

1. **"80 A" on a nano footprint is only a burst rating.** The math in section 3 shows a thermal time limit of about 3 s with all four channels at 80 A. Continuous capability is roughly 35–45 A per channel. Marketing should not print "80 A continuous."
2. **Input ripple current, not MOSFET loss, is the likely first failure.** Section 4 derives up to about 80 A RMS of ripple, which on-board ceramics cannot carry alone. An external low-ESR electrolytic is mandatory.
3. The DShot bit timing derived below is for **ArduPilot on the H743 at 400 MHz**. Verify the derived prescaler against the ArduPilot release you ship, because `RCOutput` timing constants have changed between versions.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

At any commutation step, two phases are active:
- **Leg A (PWM leg):** the current alternates between the high-side FET (duty D) and the low-side FET (1 − D) through synchronous rectification.
- **Leg B (return leg):** the low-side FET is held on for the full step.

With a phase current I_ph that is effectively DC over one PWM period (motor L/R ≫ 1/f_sw):

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2\Big[R_{HS}(T_j)\,D + R_{LS}(T_j)(1-D)\Big] + I_{ph}^2 R_{LS}(T_j) \;\approx\; 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

The approximation holds for matched HS/LS devices and is independent of D. That is why conduction loss dominates at high throttle.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence of R_DS(on)

Using a linear fit valid from 25 to 150 °C for 40 V trench FETs, with α ≈ 0.006 K⁻¹:

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,\big[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\big]
$$

Add the parasitic resistance of the PCB copper and solder per conduction path. For 2 oz outer copper plus 2 oz inner pours over a 6–8 mm path, R_pcb ≈ 0.3 mΩ per leg path. That loss heats the board, not the junction.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, with T_j assumed at 110 °C (closed iteratively in section 3)

- R_DS(110) = 1.0 × (1 + 0.006 × 85) = **1.51 mΩ**
- P_cond,FET total = 2 × 80² × 1.51 mΩ = **19.3 W per channel**
- P_cond,PCB = 2 × 80² × 0.3 mΩ = **3.8 W per channel** (board heating)
- With all four channels at 80 A: 77 W in the FETs and 15 W in copper.
