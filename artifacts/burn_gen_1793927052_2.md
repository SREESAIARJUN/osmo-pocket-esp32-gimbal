## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.0 × 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Per-channel conduction

In 6-step drive, two FETs carry phase current at any instant:
- one high-side FET switching at duty D,
- one low-side FET that is either static or synchronously rectifying.

With complementary PWM, the PWM leg always has one FET on (HS during D, LS during 1−D). Ignoring dead time:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot R_{DS}(T_j)\cdot 2$$

Add the copper-trace and shunt contribution:

$$P_{Cu,ch} = I_{ph}^2 (R_{trace} + R_{shunt})$$

Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C:

| Term | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFET conduction | 80² × 1.45 mΩ × 2 | **18.56 W** |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ) | 6400 × 0.25 mΩ | 1.60 W |
| Trace/plane (≈0.15 mΩ effective) | 6400 × 0.15 mΩ | 0.96 W |
| **Subtotal per channel** | | **21.1 W** |

Per FET, each conducts about 1/3 of the electrical cycle (120°). The average is about 18.56/6 ≈ **3.1 W** per FET. The instantaneous peak while conducting is 80² × 1.45 mΩ = **9.3 W**.

### 1.3 Ripple correction

The phase-current ripple adds to RMS:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{dc}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12},\quad \Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})D}{L_{ph} f_{sw}}$$

For a typical 5" motor with L_ph ≈ 15 µH, D = 0.9, V_bus − V_bemf ≈ 3 V:

$$\Delta I \approx \frac{3 \times 0.9}{15\,\mu\text{H} \times 48\,\text{kHz}} \approx 3.75\ \text{A}$$

The correction is ΔI²/12 ≈ 1.2 A², which is negligible against 6400 A². This is one reason to choose 48 kHz.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Transition times

Gate plateau voltage V_pl ≈ 4.5 V and driver resistance R_g,total ≈ 4 Ω (driver plus external plus internal).

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_g} = \frac{10-4.5}{4} = 1.38\,\text{A},\qquad I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_g} = 1.13\,\text{A}$$

$$t_r \approx \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{13\,\text{nC}}{1.38} \approx 9.4\,\text{ns}$$

$$t_f \approx \frac{13\,\text{nC}}{1.13} \approx 11.5\,\text{ns}$$

Use a **realistic layout-derated value of t_r + t_f = 40 ns**. Common-source inductance roughly doubles ideal transition times.

### 2.2 Loss terms (one hard-switching leg per channel)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw}$$

$$P_{dt} = V_F\, I_{ph}\, (2 t_{dt})\, f_{sw}$$

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw}$$

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw}$$

$$P_{gate} = 2\,Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw}\ \text{(dissipated in the driver)}$$
