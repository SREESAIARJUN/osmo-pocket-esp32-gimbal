## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Topology and Conduction Path

- Three-phase half-bridge, six-step trapezoidal drive.
- Complementary (synchronous) PWM is used on the active phase pair.
- At any instant, the motor current flows through **exactly two MOSFETs in series**:
  - **On-time:** HS(x) + LS(y).
  - **Off-time:** LS(x) + LS(y), via synchronous rectification.
  - **Dead time:** body diode of LS(x) + LS(y).

### 1.2 Symbols and Example Values

| Symbol | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I_pk | Phase (DC) current, burst | 80 A |
| ΔI | Peak-to-peak ripple current | see 1.3 |
| V_bus | DC link | 25.2 V |
| f_sw | PWM frequency | 48 kHz |
| D | Duty cycle (≈ V_motor/V_bus) | 0.5 worst case for ripple, ~0.9–1.0 at full throttle |
| R_ds,25 | R_ds(on) at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | 1.0 mΩ |
| α | R_ds temperature coefficient | 0.0045 /K |
| T_j | Junction temperature | 110 °C (design point) |
| t_r, t_f | V_DS rise and fall times (driver-dependent) | 20 ns each |
| t_dt | Dead time per edge | 200 ns |
| V_SD | Body diode forward voltage at I_pk | 0.8 V |
| Q_rr | Reverse recovery charge | 50 nC |
| Q_oss | Output charge at V_bus | 60 nC |
| Q_g | Total gate charge at 10 V | 60 nC |
| V_drv | Gate drive voltage | 10 V |
| L_ph | Motor phase-to-phase inductance | 20 µH (typical 2207–2807 class) |

### 1.3 Ripple and RMS Current

Ripple current:

ΔI = (V_bus − V_bemf)·D / (L_ph · f_sw) = V_bus · D(1−D) / (L_ph · f_sw)

Worst case is at D = 0.5:

ΔI = 25.2 × 0.25 / (20 µH × 48 kHz) = **6.6 A pp**

RMS current through the conduction path:

I_rms² = I_pk² + ΔI²/12 = 6400 + 3.6 ≈ 6404 A²

At 48 kHz the ripple contributes less than 0.1 % to conduction loss. Ripple matters much more for capacitors (§1.7).

### 1.4 Temperature-Corrected R_ds(on)

R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

At 110 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 85) = **1.38 mΩ**

### 1.5 Conduction Loss

The current always passes through two series FETs. The PWM duty only redistributes loss between HS and LS:

P_cond = 2 · I_rms² · R_ds(T_j)

P_cond = 2 × 6404 × 1.38e-3 = **17.7 W per channel**

Per-device split at duty D:

- Active-phase HS: D·I²R
- Active-phase LS (sync rect.): (1−D)·I²R
- Return-leg LS: I²R (always on during that 60° sector)

Averaged over six sectors, each of the 6 FETs dissipates about **P_cond / 6 × 2 ≈ 5.9 W** while it is in an active sector. The **return-leg LS is the hot spot**.

Add the PCB copper path:

P_Cu = I² · R_trace

At 2 oz with ~1.5 mΩ total loop in a nano layout: 80² × 1.5e-3 = **9.6 W**. This is a large term that is often ignored. Use internal planes and stack layers.

### 1.6 Switching Losses (hard-switched edges, inductive load)

1. **V–I overlap (one switching FET per PWM period):**
   P_VI = ½ · V_bus · I_pk · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw = 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40e-9 × 48e3 = **1.94 W**

2. **Dead-time body-diode conduction (two edges per period):**
   P_dt = V_SD · I_pk · 2·t_dt · f_sw = 0.8 × 80 × 400e-9 × 48e3 = **1.23 W**

3. **Reverse recovery:**
   P_rr = Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw = 50e-9 × 25.2 × 48e3 = **0.06 W**
   This term is small, but the di/dt ringing it causes is what kills FETs (see §1.8).

4. **Output capacitance:**
   P_oss ≈ Q_oss · V_bus · f_sw (charge-based approximation, since C_oss is nonlinear) = 60e-9 × 25.2 × 48e3 = **0.07 W**

5. **Gate drive (HS + LS pair switching):**
   P_g = 2 · Q_g · V_drv · f_sw = 2 × 60e-9 × 10 × 48e3 = **0.06 W**
   This loss lands in the gate driver and its bootstrap, not in the FET.

**Switching total ≈ 3.4 W per channel**

**Design note on 48 kHz:** dead-time loss scales with f_sw·t_dt. At 48 kHz, 200 ns of dead time is ~1.9 % of the period on each edge. Tighten t_dt to ~100 ns if the driver permits; that saves ~0.6 W per channel at 80 A. The minimum controllable on-time is t_on,min ≈ 2·t_dt + t_r, which sets the low-throttle resolution: ≈ 1.1 % duty at 48 kHz.

### 1.7 DC-Link Capacitor Ripple

Capacitor RMS current per channel:

I_C,rms ≈ I_pk · √(D(1−D))

At D = 0.5 this is **40 A_rms per channel**.

The four ESCs are asynchronous, so the ripple adds in RMS:

I_C,total ≈ √4 × 40 = **80 A_rms** (worst case)

Consequences:

- MLCCs alone cannot carry this. Use **≥ 4× 10 µF/50 V X7R 1210 per channel**, placed tight to each half-bridge.
- Add a **low-ESR bulk electrolytic of ≥ 1000 µF/35 V on the battery leads**. This is mandatory, not optional.
- Check MLCC DC-bias derating: about −60 % at 25 V on a 50 V X7R.
