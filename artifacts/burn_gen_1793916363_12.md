# STM32H743 FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #61): Power-Stage, Thermal and DShot Timer/DMA Design Package

## 0. Scope and Feasibility Findings

This package contains equations, worked numbers and reference sub-circuit netlists. It is not a fab-ready board. MOSFET parameters are placeholders and must be replaced with your selected part's datasheet values before release.

Three issues should be resolved before layout:

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---------|-------------|
| 1 | The H743 is the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. A 4-in-1 ESC uses its own MCU (e.g. AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). | The H743's job is DShot generation and telemetry capture only (Section 4). |
| 2 | 4 × 80 A = 320 A battery current, about 64 W in the power stage. A "nano" (≤20×20 mm) footprint cannot sink this, and no common nano connector carries 320 A. | 80 A must be a time-limited burst. The model below gives about 14 s from a 40 °C board and about 25 A per motor continuous. |
| 3 | At 80 A with D = 0.5, DC-link ripple current is about 40 A RMS per motor. | The capacitor bank and low-ESR bulk capacitor are a primary thermal element, not an afterthought. |
