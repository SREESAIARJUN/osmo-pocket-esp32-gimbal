# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #53): Reference Design Model

This is a reference design and analytical model. It is not a fabrication release. The netlists are block-level and component-class level. Part values assume a representative 40 V, ~1 mΩ, 3.3×3.3 mm MOSFET class. Before tape-out, re-run every equation with your actual datasheet values, and validate with thermocouple and IR testing on a prototype.

Two architectural constraints shape the whole design:

1. **Commutation runs on dedicated ESC MCUs.** The H743 runs ArduPilot, which does not commutate BLDC motors. Each channel therefore needs its own ESC MCU (e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600. That link is what the timer and DMA allocation covers.
2. **"80 A, all four channels, nano footprint" only works as a seconds-scale burst.** Section 3 quantifies this.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (Six-Step Trapezoidal, Synchronous Rectification)

### 1.1 Current paths
In six-step commutation, each 60° sector has three states:
- One phase is PWM'd high, with its high-side switching at duty D and its low-side conducting during (1−D) for synchronous rectification.
- One phase is held low.
- One phase floats.

Over a full electrical cycle, each leg spends ⅓ in each state.

Per-leg conduction energy fraction:

$$
P_{leg} = I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\left[\tfrac{1}{3}\big(D + (1-D)\big) + \tfrac{1}{3}\right] = \tfrac{2}{3} I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

Per ESC channel (3 legs), this reduces to two FETs always in series:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence
$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ /^\circ\text{C}
