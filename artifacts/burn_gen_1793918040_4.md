# Job #34: STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Nano)
## Design Model: Conduction and Switching Losses, Thermal Transients, ArduPilot BDShot600 DMA, Reference Netlists

## 0. Architecture Clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. In every production AIO and 4-in-1 design, the work is split as follows:

| Function | Device | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Flight control, ArduPilot, DShot600 master | STM32H743VIT6 (400/480 MHz) | 1–2 kHz loop |
| Motor commutation, 48 kHz PWM, BEMF sensing | 4× ESC MCUs (STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32, or EFM8 running BLHeli_S/Bluejay) | 48 kHz carrier |
| Gate drive | 4× 3-phase half-bridge drivers (e.g., FD6288Q class) | — |
| Power stage | 24 N-MOSFETs (6 per motor) | — |

The model therefore has three coupled domains:
1. Power stage losses at 48 kHz (ESC side).
2. Thermal transient response of a nano-format PCB.
3. The DShot600 bidirectional link, with timer and DMA allocation on the H743.
