# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Engineering Model and Design Package

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

Several of these assumptions change the numbers materially, so they come first.

| Item | Assumption | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Topology | STM32H743 runs ArduPilot. **Four separate ESC MCUs** (AT32F421 or STM32G071, running AM32 or BLHeli_32) drive the bridges. | ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The H743 only emits bidirectional DShot600 and reads eRPM telemetry. |
| Bus voltage | 6S, 25.2 V maximum. FETs rated 40 V. | |

## 1. Loss Model, Per Motor Channel

### 1.1 Conduction Losses

In 6-step drive, two phases conduct in each 60° sector:

- **The PWM leg.** Current flows through the HS FET during D·T and through the LS FET (synchronous rectification) during (1−D)·T.
- **The static leg.** Its LS FET carries full current for the entire sector.

The conduction loss per motor is therefore:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{ds} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

The result is independent of duty, which is a useful property for burst analysis. Including temperature dependence:

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /\mathrm{K}
$$

At 80 A:

| Tj | Rds | P_cond |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 0.90 mΩ | 2·6400·0.9 m = **11.5 W** |
| 125 °C | 1.35 mΩ | **17.3 W** |

Add the PCB copper path. Assume about 0.3 mΩ round trip in phase and bus traces:

$$
P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{trace} = 6400 \cdot 0.3\,\mathrm{m} = 1.9\ \mathrm{W}
$$

### 1.2 Switching Losses at f_sw = 48 kHz

These losses apply to the PWM leg only.

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V·I overlap | ½·V·I·(tr+tf)·f = 0.5·25.2·80·40 ns·48 k | **1.94 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Qrr·V·f = 50 n·25.2·48 k | 0.06 W |
| Coss | ½·Qoss·V·f = 0.5·60 n·25.2·48 k | 0.04 W |
| Dead-time body diode | Vsd·I·2·t_dt·f = 0.8·80·2·200 n·48 k | **1.23 W** |
| Gate drive (dissipated in driver, not FET) | Qg·Vgs·f·2 = 60 n·10·48 k·2 | 0.06 W |

$$
P_{sw} \approx 3.3\ \mathrm{W}
$$

**Design lever:** dead-time diode loss is about 40% of switching loss. Cutting dead time to 100 ns with a fast driver saves about 0.6 W per motor.

### 1.3 Total Per Motor at 80 A (Hot)

$$
P_{motor} = 17.3 + 1.9 + 3.3 \approx \mathbf{22.5\ W}
$$

This is an efficiency of about 98.9% at 2 kW per motor.

The worst single FET is the LS of the static leg. It carries 8.6 W instantaneous while active, and its average over the electrical cycle is about 3.5–4 W including its switching share.

### 1.4 Bus Capacitor Ripple

The ripple current in the bus capacitors is:

$$
I_{C,rms} = I\sqrt{D(1-D)} \;\Rightarrow\; 40\ \mathrm{A_{rms}}\ \text{per motor at } D = 0.5
$$

With unsynchronized ESC PWM, the four channels add roughly in RSS: about 80 A rms total in the worst case. Size the capacitors accordingly:

- MLCC bank near each bridge: at least 10 × 10 µF/50 V X7R 1210.
- Plus one 1000 µF low-ESR bulk capacitor on the pigtail.

## 2. Thermal Model

### 2.1 Lumped Board Model

**Thermal capacitance** (36×36 mm, 8L/2 oz, about 70% copper fill):

- Copper: 1.296e-3 m² · 0.56 mm · 0.7 · 8960 kg/m³ = 4.6 g → ×385 J/kgK = **1.75 J/K**
- FR-4: 1.296e-3 · 1.6 mm · 1850 = 3.8 g → ×1100 = **4.2 J/K**
- FETs, capacitors, solder: about **0.3 J/K**

The total is **C_th ≈ 6.2 J/K**.

**Thermal resistance to ambient:** prop wash gives h ≈ 60 W/m²K over both faces, about 2.6e-3 m². This yields **R_th,BA ≈ 6.4 K/W**.

**Time constant:** τ = R·C ≈ **40 s**.

### 2.2 Junction Stack

$$
T_j(t) = T_a + P_{tot}R_{BA}\left(1 - e^{-t/\tau}\right) + P_{FET}\,(R_{\theta JC} + R_{\theta C\text{-}PCB})
$$

Use R_θJC ≈ 1.0 K/W and R_θC-PCB ≈ 1.5 K/W (thermal via array under the drain pad). The FET-local rise is then about 4 W × 2.5 = **10 K**.
