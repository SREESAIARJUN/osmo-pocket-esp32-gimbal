# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model (Job #18)

**Scope:** 80 A burst conduction and 48 kHz switching loss model, thermal transient analysis, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer and DMA allocation, and representative schematic netlists.

---

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. ArduPilot has no motor-commutation firmware.

- The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends **bidirectional DShot600** to four ESC MCUs.
- The ESC MCUs are AT32F421 or STM32G071 parts running **AM32**.
- AM32 generates the **48 kHz** complementary PWM to the gate drivers.

The math below is split along that boundary.

### 0.2 Feasibility Warning

80 A per motor (320 A aggregate) on a 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm "nano" board is a **transient-only rating**. Section 3 shows the thermal limit is about **2 seconds**, and the continuous capability is roughly 30–35 A per motor. Market the burst figure with a time qualifier, or reviewers and customers will reject it.
