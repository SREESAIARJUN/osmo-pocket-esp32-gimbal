# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #30): Architecture, Loss Model & DShot Allocation

## 0. Architectural Corrections

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.**
- The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot600.
- Each motor channel needs its own ESC MCU to do the commutation and the 48 kHz PWM. Suitable parts include the STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32, or the EFM8BB51 running Bluejay.
- The loss models below are therefore properties of the ESC power stage. The H743's job is the DMA and timer allocation (Section 4).

**2. 80 A on a 20×20 mm board is a burst-only rating of about 1 second.**
- The math below shows a sustainable rating of about 20 A continuous per channel.
- Any datasheet or marketing claim of "80 A" must be qualified with a duration and duty cycle.
- If 80 A sustained is a real requirement, the form factor must grow to 30.5×30.5 mm or larger and be heat-sunk.

All numbers below are parametric. Replace them with your selected MOSFET's datasheet values.

## 1. Design Assumptions

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max |
| Burst phase current | I | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET class | — | 40 V, DFN3.3×3.3 (24 devices must fit on 20×20) |
| R_DS(on) @ 25 °C | R₂₅ | 2.0 mΩ (max) |
| Temperature coefficient @ T_j ≈ 125 °C | k_T | 1.45 → **R_hot = 2.9 mΩ** |
| Rise / fall time (FD6288Q-class driver, R_g ≈ 10 Ω) | t_r, t_f | 25 ns each |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns |
| Body diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Reverse recovery charge | Q_rr | 50 nC |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 30 nC |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 35 nC @ V_GS = 10 V |
| Shunt resistance | R_sh | 0.5 mΩ |
| Copper and solder path, per channel | R_cu | 0.3 mΩ |
| Thermal resistance, junction to case | R_θJC | 1.8 K/W |

**Commutation model:** 6-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) PWM, as AM32 does by default.
- At any instant, current flows through one high-side path and one low-side path, i.e. 2 R_DS(on) in series.
- Only one half-bridge is PWM-switching at a time.
