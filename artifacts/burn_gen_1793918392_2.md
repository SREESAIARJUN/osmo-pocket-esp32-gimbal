## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Inner Current Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Frame Transforms

Electrical angle (with encoder offset θ₀ found at calibration):

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_0) \bmod 2\pi$$

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 PMSM Plant (dq Frame)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q,\qquad \lambda_m = \frac{2K_t}{3p}$$

### 1.3 PI Current Controller with Decoupling

Pole–zero cancellation of the electrical pole R/L gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c$$

Choose ω_c ≤ 2π·f_PWM/10. At 20 kHz that is ω_c ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s.

Control law with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e L i_d + \hat\omega_e \lambda_m$$

Voltage limit circle (linear SVPWM region), with d-axis priority:

$$v_{max} = \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3},\quad v_d^{sat} = \text{clamp}(v_d^*, \pm v_{max}),\quad v_q^{sat} = \text{clamp}\!\left(v_q^*, \pm\sqrt{v_{max}^2 - (v_d^{sat})^2}\right)$$

Back-calculation anti-windup, with K_aw ≈ 1/K_p:

$$x_I[k+1] = x_I[k] + T_s\left(K_i e[k] + K_{aw}(u^{sat}[k]-u^*[k])\right)$$
