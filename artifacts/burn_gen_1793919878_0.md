# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan, and Netlists (Job #2)

## 0. Architecture Decision and Scope

**Topology.** The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. Twelve half-bridges with sensorless BEMF commutation need tightly timed complementary PWM and comparator/ADC zero-cross detection. That load does not belong on the flight controller.

The architecture is:

```
LiPo 6S ─┬─► 4× power stage (6 FETs each) ◄── 4× gate driver ◄── 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / AM32)
         │                                                          ▲  bidirectional DShot600 (1 wire each)
         └─► 12V/5V/3V3 regulators ─► STM32H743 FC ─────────────────┘
```

**Design envelope.** All numbers below follow from these stated assumptions. Swap in your FET datasheet values and the model still holds.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Bus voltage | 6S: 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal |
| Phase current | 80 A burst (≤10 s), continuous rating derived in §3 |
| FET (per switch, 1 die) | 40 V PQFN 3.3×3.3, R_DS(on) = 1.0 mΩ @25 °C/10 V, Q_g = 30 nC, Q_gs = 8 nC, Q_gd = 6 nC, Q_oss = 25 nC, Q_rr = 30 nC, V_th ≈ 2.5 V, V_plateau ≈ 3.5 V |
| PWM | 48 kHz, synchronous (complementary) six-step, dead time 100 ns |
| Gate drive | 10 V, R_g,on,total = 5 Ω, R_g,off,total = 3 Ω |

**Form-factor reality check.** A "nano" board (20×20 or 25×25 mm) cannot dissipate 4 × 80 A continuously. The model below gives the burst duration and the true continuous rating. Label the product accordingly, e.g. "35 A cont / 80 A 5 s burst". Do not label it "80 A".

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\text{ K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C, R_DS = 1.0 × 1.50 = **1.50 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step with synchronous rectification

At any instant, two phases carry current I:
- The PWM leg conducts through its HS FET (during D) or its LS FET (during 1−D).
- The opposite leg's LS FET is fully on.

So the current path always contains **exactly two R_DS(on)**, independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j)$$
