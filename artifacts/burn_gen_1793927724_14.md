## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Design Findings

**Architecture assumed.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32), a 3-phase gate driver, and 6 N-MOSFETs. The H743 does **not** commutate the motors. It sends bidirectional DShot600 to the four ESC MCUs.

**Design findings, stated before the math:**

1. **80 A on all four channels at once cannot be sustained on a nano (20×20 / 30.5×30.5) board.**
   - The model in §3 gives about **2–3 s** of 4-motor burst before the junction limit.
   - It gives about **20–25 A per channel** continuous in prop wash.
   - "80 A burst" should be specified as **single-channel, ≤ 1–2 s, defined start temperature**, or the board will fail qualification.
2. **4 × 80 A phase current at high duty means about 290 A of battery current.** That exceeds any XT60/XT90 lead, battery pad, or nano-board power plane. The battery-side rating must be derated separately (§2.6).
3. **All component values below are representative.** They are typical of 40 V DFN3.3×3.3 MOSFETs and FD6288-class drivers. Substitute your selected part's datasheet values into the equations; the structure of the model does not change.
