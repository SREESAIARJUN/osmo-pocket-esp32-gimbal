## 1. Field-Oriented Control (inner current loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

θ_off is found at startup by injecting a d-axis current vector at θ_e = 0 and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (power-invariant amplitude form, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.4 Plant model (SPMSM, L_d ≈ L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q
$$

### 1.5 Current PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

Use pole-zero cancellation so that the closed loop is first-order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_p^{(i)} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i^{(i)} = R\,\omega_c
$$

Choose ω_c ≤ 2π·f_pwm/10. At 20 kHz this gives about 2π·1.5 kHz.

$$
v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q
$$

$$
v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

Set i_d* = 0. Limit the voltage vector to a circle:

$$
\sqrt{v_d^2+v_q^2}\le \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}
$$

Give v_d priority, then
$$
v_{q,\max}=\sqrt{V_{max}^2-v_d^2}
$$

**Note on high-resistance gimbal motors.** With R > 10 Ω and low current (< 1 A), current sensing SNR is often poor. In that case set `useVoltageMode = true`. The current loop is then bypassed with

$$
v_q = R\,i_q^* + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

### 1.6 Inverse Park and SVPWM (min/max zero-sequence injection)

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad
v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

$$
v_a=v_\alpha,\quad v_b=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc})+\min(v_{abc})\big),\qquad
D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$
