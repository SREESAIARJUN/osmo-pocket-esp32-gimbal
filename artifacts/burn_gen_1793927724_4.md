## 0. Architecture Corrections

Three issues in the brief need to be settled first, because the rest of the math depends on them.

1. **The H743 should not commutate the motors.** No production ESC firmware runs FOC or six-step on the flight-controller MCU under ArduPilot. ArduPilot sends DShot; it does not drive gates.
   - The correct architecture is an AIO ("all-in-one") board: the H743 runs ArduPilot, and four dedicated ESC MCUs (STM32G071 or AT32F421) run AM32 at 48 kHz PWM.
   - The bi-directional DShot600 link runs between the H743 and those four MCUs.

2. **"80 A burst" must be defined.** This document assumes:
   - 80 A per motor, 1–2 s burst, 6S (25.2 V max).
   - All four motors loaded at once as the worst case.
   - That is 320 A and about 7 kW from the pack.
   - Section 4 shows that a 20×20 "nano" board cannot do this. A 30.5×30.5 board can survive it for only about 1–2 s.

3. **Every component below is a representative parametric choice.** The MOSFET and gate-driver parts are placeholders. Substitute datasheet values before release.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current paths

In any 60° sector:
- the **source phase** conducts through the high-side FET (HS) for duty D, and through the low-side FET (LS) as a synchronous rectifier for 1 − D;
- the **sink phase** conducts through its LS FET continuously;
- the **third phase** floats.

So two FET channels always carry I. The instantaneous conduction loss per motor is:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j)
$$

### 2.2 Inductor ripple correction

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12},\qquad
\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}
$$

Example: L_ph = 15 µH (a typical 2306 motor) and D = 0.5, with V_bus − V_bemf ≈ 12 V:
- ΔI ≈ 8.3 A
- the ΔI²/12 term ≈ 5.8 A², which is under 0.1 % of I² = 6400 A².
- Ripple is negligible at 48 kHz. This is one real benefit of 48 kHz over 24 kHz.

### 2.3 Per-FET distribution

Each phase spends 1/3 of the electrical cycle as source, 1/3 as sink, and 1/3 floating.

$$
P_{HS} = \frac{D}{3}\,I^2 R_{hot},\qquad
P_{LS} = \frac{(1-D) + 1}{3}\,I^2 R_{hot} = \frac{2-D}{3}\,I^2 R_{hot}
$$

At 80 A, I²·R_hot = 6400 × 0.00225 = **14.4 W**.

| Duty D | P_HS per FET | P_LS per FET | Per leg | **Per motor** | **4 motors** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 | 4.80 W | 4.80 W | 9.6 W | **28.8 W** | **115 W** |
| 0.5 | 2.40 W | 7.20 W | 9.6 W | 28.8 W | 115 W |

**Key results:**
- Conduction loss does not depend on D. Only the split between HS and LS shifts.
- At low throttle the low-side FETs are the thermal hot spot.
