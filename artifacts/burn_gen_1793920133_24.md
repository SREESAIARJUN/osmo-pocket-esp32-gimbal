## 0. Architectural Decision

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It cannot also commutate four BLDC motors with sensorless BEMF timing while meeting ArduPilot's real-time constraints. ArduPilot has no ESC firmware path for this.

The architecture is therefore:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──BDShot600 x4 (on-board traces)──► 4× AT32F421 (AM32 firmware)
                                                              │
                                                              ├─ FD6288Q 3-phase gate driver
                                                              └─ 6× N-MOSFET half-bridges ── Motor n
```

- **Form factor:** "nano" is taken as a 25.5 × 25.5 mm stack (20 × 20 mm M2 mounting) of two boards.
  - FC board: H743 and sensors.
  - ESC board: 4 MCUs, 4 drivers, 24 FETs.
- **Electrical envelope [A]:**
  - 6S maximum (25.2 V full, 4.35 V/cell HV gives 26.1 V).
  - 40 V MOSFETs.
  - 80 A per-channel *phase* current burst (≤ 5 s).
  - 35–40 A continuous.

## 1. MOSFET Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Commutation topology

AM32 uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In any sector:

- **PWM leg (e.g., phase A):** HS on for duty D, LS on for (1−D). Current always flows through one of them.
- **Static leg (e.g., phase B):** LS on for the full sector.
- **Floating leg (phase C):** off, used for BEMF sensing.

The instantaneous current path is therefore always **two series R_DS(on)**:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \left[ D\,R_{HS}(T_j) + (1-D)\,R_{LS}(T_j) + R_{LS}(T_j) \right] + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}
$$

With matched FETs (R_HS = R_LS = R):

$$
\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \,\big(2R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + R_{Cu}\big)}
$$

The expression is independent of D. The 2× R term applies at every duty cycle.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\big[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\big],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0065\ \text{K}^{-1}\ \textbf{[A]}
$$

This gives about 1.65× at 125 °C. Since R rises with T, which in turn depends on P, the junction temperature must be solved iteratively (Section 4.3).

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution (worst-case FET)

Over one electrical revolution, each FET:

- serves as the static LS for 1/6 of the time,
- serves as the PWM-leg LS for 1/6 of the time, and
- serves as the PWM-leg HS for 1/6 of the time.

Averaged over the revolution:

$$
P_{FET,avg} = \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}\cdot 2 = \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{3}\quad(\text{per HS/LS pair split evenly, D-dependent})
$$

For the thermal transient, use the **peak instantaneous** value. The static-leg LS dissipates I²R for a full sector. At low eRPM (e.g., a stalled or slow prop on a punch-out) the sector can last milliseconds, so:

$$
P_{FET,peak} = I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches. That gives one hard-switched transition pair per PWM period per channel, with V_bus = 25.2 V.

### 2.1 Components

**1. V–I overlap (hard switching, HS turn-on/off with inductive load)**

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r + t_f) f_{sw}
$$

**2. Body-diode reverse recovery (LS diode during dead time, recovered at HS turn-on)**

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw}
$$

**3. Output capacitance (Coss of both FETs in the leg, energy-equivalent)**

$$
P_{oss} = 2\cdot\tfrac12 C_{oss,er} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw}
