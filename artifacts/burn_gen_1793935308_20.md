## 0. Scope, Partitioning and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architectural Partitioning

The H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and outputs bi-directional DShot600. Each motor bridge is commutated by the ESC MCU, which runs AM32 or BLHeli_32 and generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. This document models both domains.

```
 VBAT 6S ──┬──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
           │                                                          │
     [5V/3V3 BEC]                                    [Bulk + MLCC bank]
           │                                                          │
  ┌────────▼────────┐  BiDir DShot600 (×4)     ┌──────────────────────▼───────┐
  │  STM32H743VIT6  │─────────────────────────►│ ESC MCU ×1 or ×4 (AM32)      │
  │  ArduPilot      │◄── GCR eRPM telem ───────│ TIM1 48 kHz compl. PWM + DT  │

  └─────────────────┘                          └──────────────────────────────┘
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current Paths per Commutation Interval

In each 60° electrical sector, exactly two phase legs carry current. Leg A is PWM-active and leg B is the return.

| Device | Conducts during | Duty | Loss |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q_A,HS | PWM on-time | D | I²R·D |
| Q_A,LS | Synchronous freewheel | 1−D | I²R·(1−D) |
| Q_B,LS | Entire sector | 1 | I²R |

Summing the three devices gives the per-motor conduction loss:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = I_m^2 R_{ds,hot}\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I_m^2\,R_{ds,hot}
$$

Key result: **with synchronous rectification, conduction loss is independent of throttle duty.** It depends only on phase current.

The hot on-resistance is:

$$
R_{ds,hot} = k_T\,R_{ds,25} = 1.5 \times 1.0\ \text{m}\Omega = 1.5\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

Silicon conduction loss per motor:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{19.2\ W}
$$

Each phase leg is active 2/3 of the electrical cycle, so the average per FET is:

$$
P_{FET,avg} = \frac{19.2}{6} = 3.2\ \text{W}
$$

The worst-case instantaneous FET is the return-leg LS, at 9.6 W during its sector. This averages out at electrical frequency, since T_elec ≪ τ_th of the die-to-copper path.

### 1.2 Copper Loss

$$
P_{cu,motor} = I_m^2 R_{cu} = 6400 \times 0.4\times10^{-3} = 2.56\ \text{W}
$$

### 1.3 Self-Consistent Junction Temperature (Iterative Form)

R_ds(on) depends on junction temperature, and junction temperature depends on loss, so the two must be solved together:

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.0059\ /\text{K}
$$

$$
T_j = T_{pcb} + P_{FET}(T_j)\cdot R_{\theta,j\text{-}pcb}
$$

Converge by fixed-point iteration (3–4 passes is sufficient). At α = 0.0059 /K and T_j = 110 °C, k_T ≈ 1.5, which is consistent with §0.2.
