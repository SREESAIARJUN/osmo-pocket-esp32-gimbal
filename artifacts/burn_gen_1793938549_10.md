## 0. Architectural Clarification

ArduPilot bidirectional DShot means the H743 does **not** commutate the motors. The partition is:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot. Generates DShot600 frames and decodes the eRPM telemetry returned on the same wire. |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 (QFN32) | AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. Runs 6-step commutation at **f_sw = 48 kHz**. |
| Gate drivers (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) | Drive the 6 MOSFETs per ESC. |
| Power stage | 24 MOSFETs (6 per ESC) | 3-phase half-bridges. |

The 48 kHz switching-loss model (§2) therefore applies to the ESC side. The DMA/timer model (§3) applies to the H743.

## 2. Power-Stage Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Topology

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In each 60° sector:

- The active phase high-side FET conducts for D·T_sw.
- Its complementary low-side FET conducts for (1−D)·T_sw.
- The return phase low-side FET conducts continuously.

So the current always flows through **exactly two FET channels in series**, regardless of duty cycle D.

### 2.2 Temperature-Dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) |
|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ |

### 2.3 Conduction Loss

$$
P_{cond} = I^2\,[\,2R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{sh} + R_{cu}\,]
$$

At 80 A and T_j = 100 °C:

| Term | Calculation | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFETs | 6400 × 2 × 1.34 mΩ | 17.15 W |
| Shunt | 6400 × 0.25 mΩ | 1.60 W |
| Copper | 6400 × 0.30 mΩ | 1.92 W |
| **P_cond per ESC** | | **20.7 W** |

Per-FET average: the electrical frequency (kHz range) is far faster than the thermal time constants, so the loss averages evenly across the six FETs. That gives 17.15 / 6 ≈ **2.86 W per FET**.

### 2.4 Switching Loss at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd phase switches, so each term below is per ESC.

**(a) V·I overlap loss (hard-switched edges)**

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}
$$

**(b) Output-capacitance loss**

$$
P_{oss} \approx Q_{oss} V f = 30\text{ nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{ k} = 0.036\ W
$$

**(c) Reverse recovery** (the low-side body diode recovers on every high-side turn-on)

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V f = 40\text{ nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{ k} = 0.048\ W
$$

Note: Q_rr grows with di/dt and current. Derate ×2 for margin, giving about 0.1 W.

**(d) Dead-time diode conduction** (two dead-time intervals per period)

$$
P_{dt} = 2 V_F I\, t_{dt} f = 2 \times 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ k} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}
$$

This is the second-largest term. Reducing dead time to 100 ns (AM32 setting) halves it, provided the gate driver can guarantee no shoot-through.

**(e) Gate drive** (two FETs per period, dissipated in the driver and gate resistors)

$$
P_g = 2 Q_g V_{GS} f = 2 \times 30\text{ nC} \times 10 \times 48\text{ k} = 0.029\ W
$$

**Switching total per ESC:** P_sw ≈ 1.94 + 0.04 + 0.10 + 1.23 + 0.03 ≈ **3.34 W**
