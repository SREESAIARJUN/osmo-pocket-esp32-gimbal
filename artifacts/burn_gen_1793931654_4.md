# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Model, DShot Timer Allocation, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I don't have a spec sheet for "Job #23," so every number below comes from stated, typical-class assumptions. Replace them with your actual BOM values. The equations are general; the worked numbers are illustrative.

**Architecture.** On every practical design of this type, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot to four dedicated ESC MCUs. Typical ESC MCUs are the AT32F421, STM32G071 or EFM8, running AM32 or BLHeli_32. **The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, not by the H743.**

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

In each 60° sector, one phase is PWM'd (high-side FET with duty D, low-side FET synchronous for 1−D). A second phase's low-side FET conducts continuously.

**Two FETs always carry I_ph.** Total conduction loss per ESC channel is therefore independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Each FET's conduction duty over one electrical cycle:

| Device | Conduction fraction |
|---|---|
| High-side | δ_HS = D/3 |
| Low-side | δ_LS = (1−D)/3 + 1/3 |

$$P_{cond,FET} = I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)\,\delta$$

At D → 1 (full-throttle burst), the worst case is δ = 1/3 for both high-side and low-side FETs.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 105 °C: R_ds = 1.5 mΩ × 1.52 = **2.28 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Worked numbers at 80 A, T_j = 105 °C

| Quantity | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Per channel | 2 × 6400 × 2.28 mΩ | **29.2 W** |
| Per FET (worst) | 6400 × 2.28 mΩ / 3 | **4.86 W** |
| All 4 channels at burst | 4 × 29.2 W | **~117 W** |

Include PCB copper resistance. A 2 oz, 3 mm-wide, 10 mm trace is about 0.8 mΩ round trip, adding roughly 10 W per channel at 80 A. **Copper is often the second-largest loss term on nano boards.**

## 2. Switching-Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches. Per channel:

$$P_{sw,ch} = \underbrace{\tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f)f_{sw}}_{V\text{–}I\ overlap} + \underbrace{Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw}}_{recovery} + \underbrace{Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw}}_{C_{oss}} + \underbrace{2V_f I_{ph} t_{dt} f_{sw}}_{dead\ time} + \underbrace{2Q_gV_{gs}f_{sw}}_{gate\ (driver)}$$

| Term | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Overlap | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 30 ns × 48 kHz | 1.452 W |
| Q_rr | 25.2 × 40 nC × 48 kHz | 0.048 W |
| Coss | 25.2 × 30 nC × 48 kHz | 0.036 W |
| Dead time (low-side FET body diode) | 2 × 0.8 × 80 × 50 ns × 48 kHz | 0.307 W |
| Gate (dissipated in driver/R_g) | 2 × 40 nC × 10 × 48 kHz | 0.038 W |
| **Total per channel** | | **≈ 1.88 W** |

Each phase is the PWM leg one-third of the time, so the **average switching loss per high-side FET is about 0.5 W**.

**Sensitivity.** Overlap loss scales linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz you save about 0.75 W per channel; at 96 kHz you add about 1.5 W. At 80 A, conduction dominates by roughly 15:1, so 48 kHz is a reasonable choice for motor smoothness.

**Total burst dissipation:**

| Scope | Result |
|---|---|
| Worst-case FET | 4.86 + 0.50 ≈ **5.4 W** |
| Per channel | 29.2 + 1.9 (+ ~10 copper) ≈ **31–41 W** |

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Steady-state with electro-thermal feedback

$$T_j = T_a + P_0[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]\,R_{\theta ja}$$

$$\Rightarrow\; T_j = \frac{T_a + P_0 R_{\theta ja}(1-25\alpha)}{1 - P_0\,\alpha\,R_{\theta ja}}$$

Here P_0 is the conduction loss at 25 °C plus switching loss.

**Thermal-runaway condition:** P_0 α R_θja ≥ 1.

**Worked example.**
- Inputs: per-FET P_0 = 6400 × 1.5 mΩ / 3 + 0.5 = 3.7 W.
- Thermal resistance: R_θja ≈ 35 K/W for a PDFN on a 4-layer nano board in still air.
- Runaway check: P_0 α R_θja = 3.7 × 0.0065 × 35 = 0.84. **This is close to runaway; the denominator is 0.16.**
- Result: the calculated T_j is about 1000 °C, so 80 A continuous with no airflow is not viable.
- With prop-wash: R_θja ≈ 12 K/W gives T_j ≈ 25 + 3.7 × 12 × 0.84 / (1 − 0.29) ≈ **78 °C (stable)**.
