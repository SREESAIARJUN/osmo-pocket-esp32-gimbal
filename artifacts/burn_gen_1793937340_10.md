# Job #48: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC, Systems Architecture Model

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Early Risk Flags

**Architecture clarification.** In a production 4-in-1 stack, the STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU. It runs ArduPilot and generates DShot. The 48 kHz commutation PWM is produced by a separate ESC MCU (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. This document models both sides and the link between them.

**Placeholder parameters.** No MOSFET part number was specified. All numbers below use representative parameters for a 40 V PDFN3.3×3.3 FET, so you can substitute your datasheet values directly into the equations.

| Symbol | Meaning | Assumed value |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | Battery voltage, 6S full charge | 25.2 V |
| I_pk | Burst phase current per motor | 80 A |
| R_ds,25 | FET on-resistance at 25 °C, V_gs = 10 V | 1.0 mΩ |
| α | Temperature coefficient of R_ds | 0.005 /K |
| t_r + t_f | Rise + fall time with a ~1.5 A gate driver | 40 ns |
| Q_rr | Body-diode reverse recovery charge | 40 nC |
| C_oss,eff | Effective output capacitance | 1.0 nF |
| V_f | Body-diode forward voltage | 0.8 V |
| t_dt | Dead time | 100 ns |
| Q_g | Total gate charge | 50 nC |
| R_θJC | Junction-to-case | 1.5 K/W |
| R_θ,spread | Local spreading on the board | ~8 K/W per FET |
| f_sw | PWM frequency | 48 kHz |
| T_amb | Ambient inside the frame | 40 °C |

**Risk flag.** An 80 A rating on a nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) 4-in-1 is a burst-only figure. Section 3 shows it is thermally limited to about 1 second. Treat the 80 A spec as a marketing ceiling, not a design point, unless you add a heatsink and real airflow.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous rectification)

### 1.1 Commutation scheme

The model assumes the AM32 / BLHeli_32 default scheme:
- At any instant, one high-side FET is PWM'd at duty D.
- Its complementary low-side FET in the same leg is switched synchronously, at duty 1 − D.
- The low-side FET of the second active phase is held fully ON.

Each phase leg is active for 2 of the 6 commutation sectors: one sector as the "PWM" phase and one as the "return" phase.

### 1.2 Per-FET RMS current over one electrical cycle

The current is assumed flat at I during conduction (trapezoidal approximation).

$$I_{HS,rms}^2 = I^2\frac{D}{3}$$

$$I_{LS,rms}^2 = I^2\left[\frac{1-D}{3} + \frac{1}{3}\right] = I^2\frac{2-D}{3}$$

### 1.3 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.005 × 85) = **1.425 mΩ**.

### 1.4 Conduction loss per motor

Two FETs are always in the current path, so the total is independent of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.425\text{m} = \mathbf{18.24\ W}$$

### 1.5 Per-FET distribution

The low-side FET is the hot device, and its worst case occurs at partial throttle.

| D | P_HS (W) | P_LS (W) |
|---|---|---|
| 1.00 | 3.04 | 3.04 |
| 0.95 | 2.89 | 3.19 |
| 0.50 | 1.52 | **4.56** |

**Design note.** In the real world, D = 0.5 at 80 A only happens during hard punch-outs or desync recovery. Simulate your actual throttle and current profile rather than assuming the table's worst row is typical.

### 1.6 Copper losses (often larger than designers expect)

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{trace}$$

Assume 0.4 mΩ per phase path (2 oz copper, about 3 mm wide, about 8 mm long, with parallel layers). That gives 2 × 6400 × 0.4 m = **5.1 W per motor**.

**Mitigation:** stitch phase pours on at least 3 layers and use at least 20 vias of 0.3 mm diameter per FET drain/source pad.
