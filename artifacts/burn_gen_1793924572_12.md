# STM32H743 AIO Nano Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Model, DShot DMA Plan, and Netlists

**Before using these numbers:** I don't have the Job #24 requirements package, BOM, or stackup. The analysis below uses explicit, labelled assumptions. Every equation is parametric, so you can substitute datasheet values. Three things must be confirmed before layout:

1. Whether 80 A is **per channel** or **total battery current**. I model both, and the outcomes differ fundamentally.
2. The final MOSFET part number.
3. The PCB stackup and form factor. I assume 20×20 mm "nano."
