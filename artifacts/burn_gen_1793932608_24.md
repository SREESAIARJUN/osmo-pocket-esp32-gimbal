# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #31): Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation

## 0. Assumptions and Architecture Notes

**Architecture.** On every production 4-in-1 design, the STM32H743 runs the flight controller (ArduPilot). It does not commutate motors. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or an EFM8 running BLHeli_S/Bluejay. The H743 talks to the ESCs only through DShot. This document follows that split.

**Sanity flag.** An "80A burst" rating on a 20×20 mm nano board is physically marginal; Section 3 quantifies this. Treat 80A as a 1–4 s rating, never a continuous one.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

**1.1 Temperature-corrected on-resistance**

R_DS(on)(T_j) = R_25 · [1 + α_R (T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 110 °C the multiplier is 1.38:
- Typical: R_hot = 0.90 × 1.38 = **1.24 mΩ**
- Maximum: R_hot = 1.20 × 1.38 = **1.65 mΩ**

**1.2 Current path in 6-step drive**

Two phases conduct at all times. Each current path therefore passes through exactly one high-side (HS) and one low-side (LS) channel. During PWM-off, the synchronous LS of the PWM'd leg takes over from the HS.

The total bridge conduction loss is independent of duty cycle D:

P_cond,ESC = 2 · I_ph² · R_hot

| Case | Per ESC | ×4 ESCs |
|---|---|---|
| Typical | 2 × 6400 × 1.24 mΩ = **15.9 W** | 63.5 W |
| Maximum | 2 × 6400 × 1.65 mΩ = **21.1 W** | 84.5 W |

**1.3 Per-FET distribution**

Each leg conducts for 120° out of 360°. The PWM'd HS conducts for a fraction D of that window. At full-throttle burst (D → 1), the per-FET loss is:

P_FET ≈ I_ph² · R_hot / 3 = **2.65 W typ / 3.52 W max**

That figure applies to each HS and each LS FET, averaged over one electrical revolution. The electrical period is far shorter than the die thermal time constant, so averaging is valid above roughly 1000 eRPM. At stall or very low speed it is not valid; a locked rotor puts the full I²R into one FET pair.
