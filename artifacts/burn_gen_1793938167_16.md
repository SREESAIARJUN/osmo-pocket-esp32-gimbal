### 0.2 Red Flags to Resolve Before Layout

1. **"Nano" plus 80A burst conflict.** 20×20 mm 4-in-1 boards are realistically limited to about 35–45A burst. 80A per channel × 4 equals 320A from the battery. This needs at least 30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2–3 oz copper, and FETs in parallel or with sub-1 mΩ resistance. I treat "nano" as the FC form factor and the ESC as 30×30 unless told otherwise.
2. **Current shunt dissipation.** A common 0.25 mΩ shunt at 320A dissipates **25.6 W**. Use 0.1 mΩ (10.2 W at burst) or a Hall sensor (ACS-class / TMCS) with an external amplifier.
3. **Battery side.** 320A × 25 V ≈ 8 kW. XT60 connectors and 12 AWG leads are inadequate; this is XT90 / 10 AWG territory. Confirm the burst is per motor, not simultaneous on all four.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Topology Analysis

Trapezoidal (6-step) commutation with complementary PWM has two phases active in each 60° sector:

- **High-side FET of the PWM phase** conducts for fraction D.
- **Low-side FET of the same phase** conducts for fraction (1 − D) via synchronous rectification, minus dead time.
- **Low-side FET of the return phase** conducts 100%.

Per-sector conduction loss (independent of D, to first order):

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

Dead-time correction: during 2·t_dt·f_sw the body diode conducts instead of the channel (handled in Section 2.4).

### 1.2 Temperature-Dependent R_ds(on)

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per channel at 80A |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 2 × 6400 × 1.0 m = **12.8 W** |
| 100 °C | 1.375 mΩ | **17.6 W** |
| 125 °C | 1.50 mΩ | **19.2 W** |

### 1.3 Interconnect (PCB + Solder) Losses

Assume a phase-path resistance of 0.25 mΩ per conducting branch, covering 2 branches **[A]**. 8 layers × 2 oz with an 8 mm pour × 15 mm length gives about 0.1 mΩ per layer group; add vias and solder.

$$
P_{Cu} = 2 I^2 R_{trace} = 2 \times 6400 \times 0.25\text{ m} = 3.2\text{ W}
$$

Copper tempco is 0.0039 /K, so at 100 °C board temperature this rises to ≈ 4.1 W.

### 1.4 Per-FET Distribution (Determines Hotspot)

Over one electrical revolution, each of the 6 FETs in a channel is:

- the 100% return low-side for 1/3 of the time,
- the PWM high-side for 1/3 × D, or
- the sync-rect low-side for 1/3 × (1 − D).

Per-FET average and instantaneous loss (at 125 °C, 1.5 mΩ):

- Instantaneous, while conducting: I²R = 9.6 W.
- Average, low-side FET: (1/3)(1 + (1 − D)) × 9.6 W. At D = 0.9 this is **3.5 W**.
- Average, high-side FET: (1/3) D × 9.6 W = **2.9 W** at D = 0.9.

At high throttle the low-side FET is the hotspot.
