## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Findings

### 0.1 Partitioning

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. A production-grade stack is partitioned as follows:

| Block | Device | Function |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743VIH6 / VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot, sensors, DShot600 bidir master |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | AT32F421 or STM32G071 class **[REP]** | AM32 / BLHeli_32-class firmware, 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drivers | 3-phase half-bridge driver ×4 (FD6288Q class) **[REP]** | Bootstrap high-side drive |
| Power FETs | 24× 40 V N-ch, PDFN 5×6 or 3.3×3.3 **[REP]** | 6 per motor |

### 0.2 The "80 A burst" rating on a nano (20×20 mm) board

The thermal model in §3 shows that **80 A per channel is a sub-second rating on a 20×20 mm board.** At 80 A on all four channels, the board dissipates about 100 W against a heat capacity of about 2.3 J/K. That is roughly 40 K/s of temperature rise.

A defensible spec is one of these:
- **80 A for ≤1 s, single-digit duty**, or
- **~25–35 A continuous** on a 30.5×30.5 mm board.

Design the firmware current limiter around this (§3.5).

## 2. Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 2.1 Conduction topology

With 6-step commutation and synchronous rectification, exactly **two FETs carry I_ph at every instant**, outside of dead time:

- **On-state (D):** HS of phase A + LS of phase B
- **Off-state (1−D):** LS of phase A + LS of phase B

### 2.2 Conduction loss

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,[1+\kappa\,(T_j-25)]$$

With κ ≈ 0.0059 /K, which gives α_R ≈ 1.5 at 110 °C:

$$P_{cond} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

Per-device split at D = 0.9:

| Device | Expression | Loss |
|---|---|---|
| HS_A | I²R·D | 8.64 W |
| LS_A | I²R·(1−D) | 0.96 W |
| LS_B | I²R (always on in this sector) | 9.6 W |

Commutation rotates these roles every 60° electrical, so averaged over a revolution **each FET sees P_cond/6 ≈ 3.2 W**. Size the junction for the in-sector peak, however. Each sector lasts 1/(6·f_e), which is milliseconds at low eRPM. That is comparable to the package thermal time constant.

**Electrothermal coupling.** R_DS(on) depends on T_j, which depends on P. Solve by fixed-point iteration:

$$T_j^{(k+1)} = T_{board} + R_{\theta jb}\,P\!\left(R_{DS}(T_j^{(k)})\right)$$

The iteration converges if R_θjb · ∂P/∂T_j < 1. Here ∂P/∂T ≈ 2·I²·R₂₅·κ ≈ 0.075 W/K and R_θjb ≈ 2 K/W, so the loop gain is ≈ 0.15. The iteration is stable, with no local runaway.
