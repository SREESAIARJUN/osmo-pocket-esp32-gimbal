## 0. Architecture Assumptions

Two points need to be settled before the math.

1. **The H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC.** On every production 4-in-1 design, each motor channel (or each pair) has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The 48 kHz PWM is an ESC firmware parameter. The H743's job is the DShot600 link plus bidirectional eRPM telemetry. This package assumes 4× AT32F421 running AM32 and a 3-phase gate driver of the FD6288Q class.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) board is a transient rating only.** The model in §3 shows that a sustained 80 A on all four channels is thermally impossible on this form factor. Treat 80 A as the ≤1–2 s burst limit per channel and derive the continuous rating from the thermal model.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation and Current Paths

The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous (complementary) PWM, which is the AM32 default.

In each 60° electrical sector, one phase high-side FET (HS) is PWM'd at duty D. Its complementary low-side FET (LS) on the same leg conducts for (1 − D). The LS of the return phase is held fully on.

Current therefore always flows through exactly **two** FETs in series.

### 1.2 Per-FET Average Loss over One Electrical Revolution

Each leg is active for 120° (high side) plus 120° (low side) out of 360°.

$$P_{HS} = \frac{D}{3} I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

$$P_{LS} = \frac{(1-D) + 1}{3} I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = \frac{2-D}{3} I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Summing over three legs gives the channel total:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 3\left[\frac{D}{3} + \frac{2-D}{3}\right] I^2 R = \boxed{2 I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)}$$

This is independent of D, but the **worst-case single FET is the low side at low D**: P_LS,max → (2/3)·I²R.

### 1.3 Temperature-Dependent R_DS(on), Solved Self-Consistently

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_0\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \qquad T_j = T_{pcb} + P_{FET}\, Z_{th,j\text{-}pcb}(t)$$

Substituting gives a closed-form solution:

$$T_j = \frac{T_{pcb} + k Z_{th} R_0 (1 - 25\alpha)}{1 - k Z_{th} R_0 \alpha}, \qquad k = \frac{2-D}{3} I^2$$

**Stability condition:** if k·Z_th·R₀·α ≥ 1, thermal runaway occurs. At I = 80 A, D = 0.3, and Z_th = 1.5 K/W:

k·Z_th·R₀·α = 3627 × 1.5 × 0.0016 × 0.005 = **0.044**, which is stable.
