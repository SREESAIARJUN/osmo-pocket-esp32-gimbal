## 0. Architecture Clarifications

Two points shape the rest of this document.

1. **The H743 is the flight controller, not the commutation engine.** ArduPilot does not commutate BLDC motors. The 4-in-1 ESC section therefore needs four local ESC MCUs (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32). AM32 supports a 48 kHz PWM setting, which is where the 48 kHz figure applies. The H743 drives those MCUs over bidirectional DShot600.
