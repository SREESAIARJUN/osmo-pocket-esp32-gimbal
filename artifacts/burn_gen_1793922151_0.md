## 1. Conduction Losses at 80 A Burst

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

In any commutation sector, two phases carry the full phase current I:
- **PWM phase leg.** The high-side FET conducts for duty D. The low-side FET conducts for (1 − D) via synchronous rectification. Either way, one FET in the leg carries I at every instant.
- **Grounded phase leg.** The low-side FET conducts continuously.

So at every instant exactly two FET channels carry I:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{DS}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+0.006(T_j-25)]$$

### 1.2 Burst case (I = 80 A)

Assuming low ripple, I_rms ≈ I_dc. With motor inductance around 10 µH, 48 kHz ripple adds less than 2 %.

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per motor | P_cond, 4 motors |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.50 mΩ | 80² × 2 × 1.5 m = **19.2 W** | 76.8 W |
| 100 °C | 2.18 mΩ | **27.9 W** | 111.6 W |
| 125 °C | 2.40 mΩ | **30.7 W** | 122.9 W |

**Temperature feedback.** Self-consistent T_j comes from iterating P(T_j) → T_j. Rising R_DS creates positive feedback. The stability condition is:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,Z_{th} < 1 \;\Rightarrow\; (2I^2R_{25}\cdot0.006)\,Z_{th} = 0.23\,Z_{th} < 1$$

This holds for Z_th < 4.3 K/W, which a single FET on a cold board satisfies at short timescales. It is violated once the whole board heat-soaks. That makes the burst duration limit fundamentally thermal-runaway-bounded, not just temperature-bounded.

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

The total of about 30.7 W per motor rotates among 6 FETs:
- **Average per FET:** ≈ 5.1 W.
- **Instantaneous per conducting FET:** ≈ 15.4 W.
