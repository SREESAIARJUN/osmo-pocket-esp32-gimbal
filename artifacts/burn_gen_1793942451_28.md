## 0. Architectural Corrections Before Derivation

Three issues in the brief must be resolved first. Everything downstream depends on them.

| # | Issue | Resolution adopted |
|---|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | **The STM32H743 does not run the ESCs.** ArduPilot runs on the H743. Bidirectional DShot is a link *from* the FC *to* ESC MCUs. | **H743 runs the FC. Four AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) MCUs run AM32**, one per motor channel, each with a 3-phase gate driver and 6 MOSFETs. |
| 2 | **"80 A" must be per channel and burst, not continuous.** The math in §3 shows ~60–80 W of board dissipation at 4×80 A. No 30×30 mm board survives that continuously. | Spec: **80 A burst ≤ 5 s per channel (all four simultaneously), 50 A continuous with ≥8 m/s prop-wash airflow.** |
| 3 | **"Nano" (20×20 mm) cannot carry 4×80 A.** It lacks the area for 24 FETs, the copper cross-section and the thermal mass. | **30.5×30.5 mm mount, roughly 37×37 mm board.** A 20×20 variant is derated to about 35 A burst (§4.5). |

Voltage class: **6S** (25.2 V max, with about 35 V transients). This requires **40 V MOSFETs**.
