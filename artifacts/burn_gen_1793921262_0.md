## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_off:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.2 Plant (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Electromagnetic torque:

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q
$$

### 1.3 PI current control with decoupling

$$
v_d^* = PI_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e \hat L i_q
$$

$$
v_q^* = PI_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e(\hat L i_d + \hat\lambda_m)
$$

**Pole-zero cancellation tuning.** Placing the PI zero on the plant pole R/L gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_ci:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}
$$

- Choose ω_ci ≤ 2π·f_i/20. That is about 2π·1 kHz at a 20 kHz PWM.
- This leaves margin for the 1.5·T_i computation-plus-PWM delay.

**Discretization (forward Euler on the integrator, back-calculation anti-windup):**

$$
u_k = K_p e_k + x_k
$$

$$
x_{k+1} = x_k + K_i T e_k + K_{aw}\,(\mathrm{sat}(u_k)-u_k)
$$

with K_aw = K_i/K_p, i.e., the inverse of the PI time constant.

**Circular voltage limit.** V_max = V_bus/√3 (the linear-region SVPWM limit). The d-axis has priority:

$$
v_d \leftarrow \mathrm{clamp}(v_d,\pm V_{max})
$$

$$
v_q \leftarrow \mathrm{clamp}\!\left(v_q,\ \pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2-v_d^2}\right)
$$

### 1.4 SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)

Inverse Park gives v_α and v_β. Then:

$$
v_a = v_\alpha,\quad v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc}) + \min(v_{abc})\big)
$$

$$
d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

## 2. Velocity Estimation (Encoder PLL)

Encoder differencing is too noisy at 14 bits and 4 kHz, so use a type-2 PLL on the wrapped mechanical angle.

**Phase error:**

$$
\varepsilon = \mathrm{wrap}_{\pm\pi}(\theta_{meas}-\hat\theta)
$$

**Update equations:**

$$
\hat\omega \mathrel{+}= T\,k_i\,\varepsilon
$$

$$
\hat\theta \mathrel{+}= T(\hat\omega + k_p\varepsilon)
$$

**Gains:**

$$
k_p = 2\zeta\omega_n,\qquad k_i = \omega_n^2
$$

with ζ = 0.707 and ω_n ≈ 2π·150 Hz.
