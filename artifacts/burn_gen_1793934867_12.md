## 1. Conduction Loss Model

In any 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** the high-side (HS) conducts during D·T. The synchronous low-side (LS) conducts during (1−D)·T.
- **Phase B (return leg):** the LS is fully on.

Because of synchronous rectification, the current always flows through exactly **two** channel resistances, independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] \approx k_T R_{ds,25}$$

PWM ripple adds a small correction to the RMS current:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I_{pp}=\frac{V_{bus}D(1-D)}{L_{ph} f_{sw}}$$
