# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #53): Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Design Package

> **Engineering review findings (read first)**
>
> 1. **80 A per motor on a nano (20×20 / 25.5×25.5) stack is a transient rating only.** The model below shows roughly 1–4 s of adiabatic headroom at 80 A on all four motors, and roughly 20–28 A per motor continuous at realistic airflow. Treat 80 A as a burst number and enforce it in ESC firmware.
> 2. **The power input cannot carry the 4×80 A burst current.** At 6S, four motors at 80 A means up to 320 A battery current (about 8 kW). An XT30/XT60 lead and 20 mm copper cannot carry that. The input must be specified separately (see §5.4).
> 3. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** A 4-in-1 ESC runs its own MCUs (for example 4× STM32G071 or AT32F421 on AM32, or BLHeli_32). The H743 runs ArduPilot and produces bi-directional DShot600. The 48 kHz figure is the ESC PWM frequency, not anything on the H743.
> 4. **Device parameters are representative placeholders.** They are typical of 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PQFN MOSFETs. Replace them with your chosen part's datasheet values and re-run the equations.

## 3. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 3.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0055 × 100) = **1.55 mΩ**. Use **1.6 mΩ** to cover the spread of maximum datasheet values.

### 3.2 Current path

With trapezoidal (6-step) commutation and synchronous rectification:

- At every instant, phase current flows through exactly one high-side path and one low-side path in series.
- Each switch conducts for 120° electrical, which is 1/3 of the cycle.

So the per-motor conduction loss is:

$$P_{cond}=I^2\,(2R_{ds}+R_{Cu})$$

R_Cu is the PCB copper and solder resistance in the phase path. It is roughly 0.3 mΩ on a nano board with 2 oz internal pours.

$$P_{cond}=80^2(2\times1.6+0.3)\,\text{m}\Omega = 6400\times3.5\text{ m}\Omega = \mathbf{22.4\ W\ per\ motor}$$

This splits into 20.5 W in the MOSFETs and 1.9 W in the copper.

## 4. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Assume one hard-switched transition pair per PWM period per active bridge (the high side switches hard; the synchronous low side switches softly with diode commutation). Losses are evaluated at V_bus = 25.2 V and I = 80 A.

| Loss term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = 0.5·25.2·80·40n·48k | 1.94 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 50n·25.2·48k | 0.06 W |
| Output capacitance | Q_oss·V·f_sw = 40n·25.2·48k | 0.05 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_SD·I·2t_dead·f_sw = 0.8·80·200n·48k | 0.61 W |
| Gate drive (6 FETs, mostly in driver) | 6·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw = 6·50n·10·48k | 0.14 W |
