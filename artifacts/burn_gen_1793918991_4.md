# Job #47: STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC, 80 A Burst

## Architecture Model, Loss Derivation, DShot DMA Plan and Netlists (Rev A)

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Feasibility

Three issues determine everything downstream.

**1. The H743 should not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 and *emits* bidirectional DShot600. Commutation at 48 kHz PWM must run on four dedicated ESC MCUs, one per motor, running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. Example parts are the AT32F421 or STM32G071. The reasons:
- ArduPilot has no sensorless BLDC commutation stack.
- Four motors × 48 kHz PWM with BEMF zero-cross detection would consume the H743's advanced timers and starve the DShot DMA.

The rest of this document assumes the topology **H743 (ArduPilot) → DShot600 bidir → 4× ESC MCU → 4× three-phase bridge**.

**2. 80 A per motor on a 20×20 mm nano board is burst-only, and short.** Section 3 shows roughly **2 s** to reach the PCB thermal limit from 40 °C. Continuous capability on 20×20 is about 25–35 A per motor. If 80 A must be sustained for more than about 2 s, move to a 30.5×30.5 stack with separate FC and ESC boards.
