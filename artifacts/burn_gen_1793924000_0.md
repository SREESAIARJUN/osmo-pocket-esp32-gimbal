# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC: Design Model (Job #65)

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

No MOSFET part number, layer stack, or enclosure was specified. All numbers below are **parametric**, and the example values are typical for 20×20 mm, 6S-class designs. Substitute datasheet values before release.

**One architecture point to settle first:** ArduPilot bi-directional DShot is an FC→ESC protocol. The H743 therefore runs ArduPilot and generates DShot. Each motor is commutated by a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as an AT32F421. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by those ESC MCUs, not the H743. The H743 does not drive gates directly.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Per-FET R_DS(on) with self-heating

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + k(T_j - 25)],\quad k \approx 0.005\ /\mathrm{K} \Rightarrow R_{125} \approx 1.5\ \mathrm{m\Omega}$$

This is a positive feedback loop: losses raise T_j, which raises R_DS, which raises losses. Solve it iteratively, as in §3.4.

### 1.2 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

In any commutation sector, two phases carry current I:
- The **PWM phase**: the HS FET conducts for D·T, and the LS FET (synchronous rectification) conducts for (1−D)·T.
- The **return phase**: the LS FET is on 100%.

Instantaneous conduction loss per motor channel is therefore independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I_{rms}^2 R_{DS}$$

Ripple correction: inductor ripple ΔI adds I_rms² = I² + ΔI²/12. For typical motor inductance of about 10 µH, f = 48 kHz, and D = 0.5:

ΔI = V·D(1−D)/(L·f) ≈ 13 A, giving I_rms ≈ 80.35 A, about +0.9% loss (negligible).

|---|---|---|---|

### 1.4 Per-FET share (for junction temperature)

Each FET position is active for 1/3 of the electrical cycle. The worst case is the LS FET on the return phase, at 100% duty within its sector:

$$P_{FET,peak} = I^2 R_{DS} = 6400 \times 1.5\,\mathrm{m\Omega} = 9.6\ \mathrm{W}\ \text{(while active)}$$

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx \tfrac{1}{3}\cdot 9.6 + \tfrac{1}{3}\cdot 9.6(1-D) \approx 4.8\ \mathrm{W}\ (D=0.5)$$

**Important:** at low eRPM (startup, punch-out from hover), sectors are long relative to the junction thermal time constant. You must use the 9.6 W peak, not the average, in that regime.

### 1.5 Copper and interconnect losses (often larger than designers expect)

$$R_{trace} = \frac{\rho_{Cu}\,L}{w\,t}(1 + 0.0039\,\Delta T),\quad \rho_{Cu} = 1.72\times10^{-8}\ \Omega\mathrm{m}$$

Example: 2 oz copper (70 µm), 4 parallel power layers, 6 mm wide, 15 mm path:

R ≈ 1.72e-8 × 0.015 / (0.006 × 70e-6 × 4) ≈ 0.15 mΩ, giving **≈1 W per channel at 80 A**.

Add the battery pads and the bus-cap ESR ripple loss (§2.6).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM phase switches. Per channel, per PWM cycle:

### 2.1 V–I overlap (hard switching, HS FET)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 40\,\mathrm{ns} \cdot 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = 1.94\ \mathrm{W}$$

The datasheet t_r/t_f are at a reference R_G. To estimate them for your gate driver:

$$t_{r} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}+Q_{gs2}}{I_{G}},\quad I_G = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}{R_{G,ext}+R_{G,int}+R_{drv}}$$

### 2.2 Dead-time body-diode conduction (LS FET, both edges)

$$P_{dt} = 2\,V_F\,I\,t_{dt}\,f_{sw} = 2 \cdot 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 100\,\mathrm{ns}\cdot 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = 0.61\ \mathrm{W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\,\mathrm{nC}\cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = 0.048\ \mathrm{W}$$

Q_rr scales with di/dt and I. Re-check it at 80 A from the datasheet curve.

### 2.4 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 25\,\mathrm{nC}\cdot 25.2\cdot 48\,\mathrm{kHz} = 0.030\ \mathrm{W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated in the driver and R_G, not the FET)

$$P_G = Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw}\times 2_{\text{(HS+LS switching)}} = 40\,\mathrm{nC}\cdot10\cdot48\,\mathrm{kHz}\cdot2 = 0.038\ \mathrm{W}$$
