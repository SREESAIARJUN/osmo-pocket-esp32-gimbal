# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 80 A ESC: Electro-Thermal Model, Bidirectional DShot600 DMA Allocation, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

The model rests on three architectural decisions:

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In a 4-in-1 ESC, each channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 runs ArduPilot and speaks bidirectional DShot600 to those four MCUs. Commutating four sensorless BLDC bridges from the H743 alongside ArduPilot's scheduler is not a viable design.
2. **"Nano" with 80 A burst is not credible.** A 20×20 mm stack cannot carry 4×80 A, either through copper or thermally (see §3). The model therefore uses a **30.5×30.5 mm ESC board**, with the FC on 20×20 or 30.5 mm mounting.
3. **Treat "80 A" as a time-limited burst.** The model derives the actual time limit (§3.4).
