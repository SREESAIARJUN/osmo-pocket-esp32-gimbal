## 0. Architecture Decisions and Stated Assumptions

The H743 should **not** commutate the motors directly. ArduPilot does not run ESC commutation, so the AIO uses a split architecture:

| Block | Choice | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) or VIH6 (TFBGA100) for nano | ArduPilot target; DMAMUX gives flexible DMA routing |
| ESC MCU | 4× AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32) running AM32 | Supports bidirectional DShot600 and a configurable PWM frequency (48 kHz) |
| Gate driver | 4× FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) **[VERIFY]** | Industry-standard for 4-in-1 ESCs |
| MOSFETs | 24× 40 V N-ch, PQFN 3.3×3.3, R_DS(on) ≈ 1.5 mΩ @ 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V **[VERIFY]** | PQFN 5×6 does not fit 24 devices on a nano footprint |
| Bus | 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V | Worst case for switching loss |
