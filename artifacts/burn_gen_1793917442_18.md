# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #37): Systems Architecture Package

## 0. Feasibility Constraints

Four constraints shape the rest of this design.

1. **The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In every production 4-in-1 stack, each motor channel is commutated by a dedicated ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. The power-stage model (§1–3) applies to the ESC board, and the DMA model (§4) applies to the FC.
2. **80 A is a burst rating per channel, not continuous.** Four channels at 80 A means 320 A from the battery. That exceeds XT60 (~60 A continuous), and XT90 and 10 AWG leads are still marginal. The thermal model below shows that the all-channel burst window is about 3 seconds.
3. **"Nano" form factor.** A 20×20 mm FC is achievable. An ESC that survives an 80 A burst requires at least a 30.5×30.5 mm board with 6-layer, 2 oz copper.
4. **The netlists below are reference designs.** They have not been through layout, DRC, or hardware validation. All device parameters are stated as assumptions so you can substitute your actual datasheet values.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R = 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 85) = **1.38 mΩ**.
