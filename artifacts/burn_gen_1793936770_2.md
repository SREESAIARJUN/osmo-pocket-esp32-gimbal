### ⚠ Design review flags

1. **80 A × 4 on a 25.5 mm board is not thermally sustainable.** §3 shows an adiabatic limit of about 2 s. Treat "80 A" as a single-motor transient rating, not a simultaneous one.
2. **System current.** 4 × 80 A phase current can mean up to about 320 A DC at high duty. An XT60 (60 A continuous) and 12 AWG leads cannot support this. The spec needs a defined *total* burst figure.
3. **Bulk capacitor ripple** (§2.6) is around 40 A<sub>RMS</sub> per motor at D = 0.5. This needs off-board low-ESR electrolytics plus on-board MLCCs.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}$$

At T<sub>j</sub> = 100 °C: R = 1.0 mΩ × 1.34 = **1.34 mΩ**.
At T<sub>j</sub> = 125 °C: R = **1.45 mΩ**.

Add **PCB/via copper resistance** per current path. For example, 10 mm of 2 oz copper, 3 mm wide, across 2 parallel layers gives about 0.4 mΩ:

$$R_{path} = R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + R_{Cu},\qquad R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho_{Cu}(T)\,\ell}{w\,t\,n_{layers}}$$

### 1.2 Six-step conduction topology

In any 60° sector, two phase legs are active:

- The **PWM leg** has the high-side (HS) FET on for D·T<sub>sw</sub>. The complementary low-side (LS) FET carries current for (1−D)·T<sub>sw</sub> (synchronous rectification). The leg therefore carries I<sub>ph</sub> 100% of the time, split between its two FETs.
- The **static leg** has its LS FET on for 100% of the sector.

So at every instant, **exactly two channels carry I<sub>ph</sub>**:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph,RMS}^2\,R_{path}$$

### 1.3 Ripple correction

$$\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{LL}\,f_{sw}},\qquad I_{RMS}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$
