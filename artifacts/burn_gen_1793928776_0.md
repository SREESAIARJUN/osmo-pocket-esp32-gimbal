# STM32H743 Nano AIO + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #33): Thermal, Switching, and DShot Design Model

## 0. Architecture Clarifications

These points constrain the rest of the model:

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1 design, each ESC channel has its own MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32. That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and drives four bi-directional DShot600 lines. The model is split accordingly:
   - **Power stage:** ESC MCU → gate driver → 6 MOSFETs, ×4.
   - **Control link:** H743 timers/DMA ↔ ESC MCUs.
2. **"80 A burst" must be bounded by time.** At 4 × 80 A from 6S, bus current is 320 A (about 8 kW). A nano board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) can only survive this for seconds. Section 3 derives the actual burst duration and continuous rating.
3. **The FET parameters below are placeholder assumptions**, typical of a 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm PQFN class part. Substitute your selected datasheet values; every equation is written parametrically.
