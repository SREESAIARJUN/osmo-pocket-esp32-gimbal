## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

The STM32H743 cannot directly commutate four BLDC motors at 48 kHz while also running ArduPilot. Production AIO boards use a split architecture, and this design does too:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (400 MHz) | ArduPilot. Sends DShot600 to 4 ESC MCUs and receives eRPM telemetry. |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 (120 MHz), one per motor | AM32 firmware. 6-step commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM. |
| Gate driver (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase half-bridge, bootstrap) | Drives 6 N-FETs per motor. |
| Power FETs (×24) | 40 V, DFN5×6, R_DS(on) ≈ 1.0 mΩ max at 25 °C | Bridge switches. |

> **Form-factor warning:** The 80 A burst rating below assumes a 30.5×30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz board. On a 20×20 mm "nano" board, the burst window shrinks by about 55% (see §3.4). An 80 A rating on a 20×20 board is a marketing number, not an engineering one.
