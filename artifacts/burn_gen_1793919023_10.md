## 0. Architecture Decision and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 cannot be the ESC commutation controller in this design.** ArduPilot does not run sensorless six-step or FOC motor commutation on the flight MCU. The board therefore uses the standard AIO split:

```
                ┌───────────────────────── 26 × 26 mm board, 20 × 20 mm M2 pattern ──┐
 VBAT 6S ──────►│  4× power stage (12× N-MOSFET, 4× 3-phase gate driver)             │
                │  4× ESC MCU (AT32F421K8U7, AM32 firmware, 48 kHz PWM)              │
                │        ▲ BDShot600 (1 wire per motor, bidirectional)               │
                │  STM32H743VIH6 @ 480 MHz, ArduPilot                                │
                └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
