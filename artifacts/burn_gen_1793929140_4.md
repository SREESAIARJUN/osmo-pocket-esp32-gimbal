| Rate (velocity) loop | 4 kHz | ~60–120 Hz |

## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

Phase currents satisfy $i_a + i_b + i_c = 0$.

**Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant):**
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park transform.** Let $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$ be the electrical angle.
$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park transform:**
$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Plant (SPMSM, $L_d \approx L_q = L$)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$
$$\tau = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.3 Current PI with Decoupling and Back-EMF Feedforward

Pole-zero cancellation of $1/(R+Ls)$ gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth $\omega_c$:
$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c \quad(\text{zero at } R/L)$$

Choose $\omega_c \le 2\pi f_{pwm}/10$. At 20 kHz this means $\omega_c \approx 2\pi\cdot 1.5\text{ kHz}$.

The voltage commands, including decoupling terms:
$$v_d^* = PI_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e \hat L\, i_q$$
$$v_q^* = PI_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e(\hat L\, i_d + \hat\lambda_m)$$

Use $i_d^* = 0$, since gimbal motors have no reluctance torque. Then apply circular voltage limiting:
$$|v_{dq}| \le V_{bus}/\sqrt3$$

The d-axis has priority, and $v_q$ gets the remainder.
