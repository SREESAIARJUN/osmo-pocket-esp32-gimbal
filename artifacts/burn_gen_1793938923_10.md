# Job #64: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC Reference Design

## 0. Scope and Design Basis

**System partitioning.** The STM32H743 does not drive the power stage. Each ESC channel has its own MCU, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to the ESC MCUs. The math below follows that split:

| Domain | Owner | Sections |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction and switching losses at 48 kHz | ESC MCU, gate driver, MOSFETs | 1–3 |
| Thermal model, 80 A burst | 4-in-1 PCB | 4 |
| Bidirectional DShot600, timers and DMA | STM32H743 / ArduPilot | 5 |
| Netlists | Both | 6 |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In synchronous 6-step, two phase legs carry current at every instant. During PWM-on, current flows through the high-side FET of phase A and the low-side FET of phase B. During PWM-off, the complementary low-side FET of phase A conducts, plus the low-side of phase B.

In both states, exactly **two channel resistances** sit in series with the motor current, independent of duty cycle D:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$
