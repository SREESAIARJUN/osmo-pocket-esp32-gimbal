# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Model, Job #21

## 0. Scope and Critical Design Flags

Three issues should be settled before anyone commits to layout.

1. **Architecture.** ArduPilot runs on the STM32H743 flight controller. The 4-in-1 ESC runs its own per-channel MCUs (AM32 on AT32F421 or STM32G071, or BLHeli_32), linked to the FC by bidirectional DShot600. ArduPilot does not support one H743 commutating four motors directly. This document assumes the standard split: an H743 FC board stacked on a 4-in-1 ESC board.
2. **80 A burst on a nano form factor is a transient-only rating.** On a 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm board, the model below gives about 1–2 s at 80 A per channel before junction limits are reached. The realistic continuous rating is roughly 25–30 A per channel. Marketing must state the burst duration.
3. **Parameters are representative.** MOSFET and driver values are typical of the device class, not quotes from a specific datasheet. Recompute with the actual BOM part's Rds(on)(Vgs, T), Qg, Qgd, Qoss, and Qrr.
