## 0. Reality Check

The model below leads to one conclusion that should shape every downstream decision:

> **An "80 A per motor" rating on a nano (20×20 / 25.5×25.5 mm) 4-in-1 is a ~3–4 second burst rating at best. The thermally sustainable continuous current is about 20–25 A per channel with prop-wash cooling.**

§5 derives both figures. Size the marketing claim, the firmware current limits (AM32 or BLHeli_32 current limit, plus ArduPilot `BATT_*` limits), and the QA burn-in test to these numbers.

### Architecture assumption

The STM32H743 is the flight controller. Each ESC channel runs its own commutation MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32. The H743 drives four DShot600 lines and receives bi-directional eRPM telemetry.

Driving 12 half-bridges directly from the H743 (TIM1/TIM8/HRTIM) is possible, but it is not how ArduPilot works. That approach would be a different project.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

$$R_{DS}(110\,°C) = 1.2\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.0065 \times 85) = 1.2 \times 1.553 = \mathbf{1.86\,m\Omega}$$
