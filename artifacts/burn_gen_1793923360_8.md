## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Assumptions

### Architecture

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates **bidirectional DShot600**. It does not drive the MOSFETs directly. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU (for example an AT32F421 running AM32, or BLHeli_32-class firmware). That MCU drives a 3-phase gate driver and a 6-FET bridge. This document models:

1. The **ESC power stage**: conduction, switching, recovery, dead-time and copper losses at 80 A and 48 kHz.
2. The **thermal behaviour** of a nano-format 4-in-1 under burst load.
3. The **H743 timer/DMA allocation** for 4× bidirectional DShot600 under ArduPilot.
4. **Reference netlists** for the FC core, the DShot interface and one ESC channel.
