# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #38): Loss, Thermal, DShot/DMA and Netlist Model

## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Architecture Flags

I don't have your Job #38 BOM or stack-up, so every device parameter below is a **declared assumption** with typical values for the class. The equations are general. Replace the values in §1.2 with your datasheet values and re-run the iteration in §4.

**Architecture clarification.** In every production design of this class, the STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. The 4-in-1 ESC carries its own commutation MCU, typically an STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 cannot do sensorless 6-step commutation for 4 motors at 48 kHz while also running ArduPilot. This document models:

- The H743 FC, which generates bi-directional DShot600.
- The 4-in-1 power stage, switching at 48 kHz under ESC-MCU control.

**Feasibility flag (stated up front).** 4 × 80 A on a nano (~30×30 mm) board is **thermally a burst-only rating of roughly 2–4 s**. It also implies a **320 A battery draw**. §4 quantifies this.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current Path

With 6-step trapezoidal drive and synchronous rectification, at every instant the phase current flows through exactly one high-side and one low-side device. That holds both during PWM on-time and during the complementary freewheel through the low-side FET of the chopped leg.

```
P_cond,motor(T_J) = I_ph² · 2 · R_DS(on)(T_J)
R_DS(on)(T_J)     = R_25 · [1 + α (T_J − 25)]
```

### 2.2 Numerical Values

| T_J | R_DS(on) | P_cond per motor | P_cond, 4 motors |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.000 mΩ | 12.8 W | 51.2 W |
| 100 °C | 1.338 mΩ | 17.1 W | 68.5 W |
| 125 °C | 1.450 mΩ | 18.6 W | 74.2 W |

### 2.3 Per-Device Distribution

Each device conducts for a 1/3 electrical period (120°). Its instantaneous dissipation is I²R = 8.6 W at 100 °C. The die thermal time constant (~1–5 ms) is comparable to the electrical period at high eRPM, so the average is the relevant figure:

- Mean per device ≈ 17.1 W / 6 = **2.85 W**.
- The low-side FET of the chopped leg also carries the freewheel share (1 − D)·I².
- Ripple on a 3.3×3.3 die at 8.6 W pulses is about +5 K.

### 2.4 Parasitic Path Losses (per motor)

| Element | Assumed R | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Copper (pads to phase pad, 2 oz × multilayer) | 0.4 mΩ | 2.56 W |
| Shunt (if one per channel) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.60 W |
| Solder/phase wire joint | 0.1 mΩ | 0.64 W |
| **Subtotal** | | **≈ 4.8 W** |

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one leg per motor is hard-switched at a time: the high-side PWM, with the low-side complementary.

### 3.1 Hard-Switching (V–I overlap)

```
P_sw = ½ · V_bus · I_ph · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw
     = 0.5 · 25.2 · 80 · 30e-9 · 48e3 = 1.45 W
```

### 3.2 Reverse Recovery of the Low-Side Body Diode

```
P_rr = V_bus · Q_rr · f_sw = 25.2 · 40e-9 · 48e3 = 0.048 W
```

### 3.3 Output Capacitance

```
P_oss ≈ ½ · Q_oss · V_bus · f_sw = 0.5 · 25e-9 · 25.2 · 48e3 = 0.015 W
```
