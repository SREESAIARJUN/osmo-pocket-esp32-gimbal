## 1. FOC Inner Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle is computed from the mechanical angle θ_m, the pole-pair count p, and the encoder zero offset θ_off:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 Plant and current PI with decoupling

The motor voltage equations in the rotating frame are:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

The controller cancels the cross-coupling and back-EMF terms with feedforward:

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

**Tuning by pole-zero cancellation.** Placing the PI zero on the electrical pole R/L makes the closed loop first-order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10}\;\;(\approx 2\pi\cdot 1.5\text{ kHz})$$

Use i_d* = 0. Gimbal motors are never run in field weakening.

**Voltage limiting.** The commanded voltage vector is clamped to the space-vector linear range:

$$|v_{dq}| \le V_{bus}/\sqrt3$$

When clamping, preserve v_d priority.
