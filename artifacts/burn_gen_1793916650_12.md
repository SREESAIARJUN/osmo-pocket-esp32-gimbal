## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and One Architecture Correction

**Architecture correction.** The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors itself. ArduPilot has no sensorless BLDC commutation firmware, and running 12 half-bridges with 48 kHz center-aligned PWM plus BEMF zero-cross detection, alongside the flight stack, is not a supported or sensible design. The industry-standard and ArduPilot-supported partition is:

- **U1, STM32H743VIT6:** runs ArduPilot and generates 4× bidirectional DShot600.
- **U10–U13, 4× AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071):** run AM32, which handles commutation at 48 kHz PWM and returns eRPM telemetry.
- **U20–U23, 4× three-phase gate drivers:** FD6288Q-class, bootstrap high-side.
- **Q1–Q24:** 40 V N-MOSFETs in 5×6 or 3.3×3.3 DFN.
