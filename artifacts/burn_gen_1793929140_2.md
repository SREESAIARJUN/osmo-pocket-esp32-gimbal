## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle from mechanical encoder angle $\theta_m$, with calibrated offset $\theta_0$:

$$\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_0 \pmod{2\pi}$$

The calibration procedure is: align with $v_d$ only, read $\theta_m$, and set $\theta_0 = p\,\theta_m$.

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, assuming $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant and Current Controller Design

The SPM dq model, with $\omega_e = p\,\omega_m$, is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

The controller is a PI with cross-coupling and back-EMF decoupling:

$$v_{d}^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \hat\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \hat\omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

**Gain selection.** Choose the zero to cancel the electrical pole at $R/L$. This gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth $\omega_c$:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

For $\omega_c = 2\pi\cdot 1.5\,\text{kHz}$, $f_{pwm}=20\,\text{kHz}$, and a total delay of about $1.5T_s$, the phase margin is

$$PM \approx 90^\circ - \omega_c\cdot 1.5T_s\cdot\tfrac{180}{\pi} \approx 50^\circ.$$

**Discretization.** Use backward Euler for the integrator:

$$x_k = x_{k-1} + K_iT_s e_k$$
