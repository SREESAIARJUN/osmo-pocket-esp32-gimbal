# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage and DShot Architecture Model (Job #20)

## 0. Architecture Corrections

Three issues in the brief would sink the design if left unaddressed.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no FOC or 6-step ESC firmware for the flight controller MCU. The correct partition is:
   - **H743 (flight controller):** runs ArduPilot and emits 4× bidirectional DShot600.
   - **4× ESC MCUs** (e.g. STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32, or EFM8 running BLHeli_S): each runs its own 48 kHz PWM, gate driver and 6 MOSFETs.
   
   Every model below follows this split.

2. **80 A is a burst rating, not a continuous one, on a 20×20 mm board.** The math in §3 shows roughly 1 s of burst at 80 A per motor and roughly 18–25 A continuous with propwash cooling. Any "80 A" label must state the duration.

3. **Total current is set by the battery side, not the PCB.** 4 × 80 A = 320 A of battery current is a wiring and connector problem (XT60 is about 60 A continuous). The board's bulk-input copper must be modelled as a loss term (§2.4).

All component values below are **representative assumptions**. Replace them with the datasheet values of the parts you actually select before freezing the design.

## 2. Loss Model (per motor channel)

### 2.1 Conduction loss

In 6-step operation exactly two phase legs carry current at any instant:
- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D·T; the synchronous low-side FET conducts for (1−D)·T.
- **Static leg:** the low-side FET is on for 100 % of the sector.

So, excluding dead time, the current always flows through two channels in series:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I^2\,R_{25}\,k(T_j)$$

This loss is **independent of duty cycle** with synchronous rectification:

$$P_{cond} = 2(80)^2(1.0\text{m}\Omega)(1.5) = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

The hot spot is the static low-side FET, which carries the full 9.6 W for the 120° sector. Over a full electrical cycle each FET averages about one third of that, but the burst thermal transient of the die follows the peak.

### 2.2 Switching loss (hard-switched PWM leg only)

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw | ½·25.2·80·30 ns·48 k = **1.45 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 40 nC·25.2·48 k = **0.05 W** |
| C_oss | ½·Q_oss·V·f_sw | **0.02 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw | 0.8·80·200 ns·48 k = **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs switching) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw | 2·50 nC·10·48 k = **0.05 W** (dissipated in the driver/R_g) |
| **Total** | | **≈ 2.2 W** |

Design levers:
- Overlap loss scales linearly with f_sw, so 48 kHz costs 2× a 24 kHz setting.
- Dead time is the second-largest term. Trim it to the minimum safe value (about 50–60 ns with a tight layout), which recovers about 0.3 W.

### 2.3 Per-channel and board totals at 80 A burst

| | Per motor | ×4 motors |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| Switching | 2.2 W | 8.8 W |
| **Total** | **21.4 W** | **≈ 86 W** |

### 2.4 Copper and DC-link losses (often omitted)

**DC-link capacitor ripple.** Worst case is at D = 0.5:

$$I_{C,rms} = I\sqrt{D(1-D)} = 80\cdot 0.5 = 40\text{ A rms per channel}$$

- **On board:** ceramic MLCCs (for example 8× 10 µF/50 V X7R 1210 per channel, effective ≈ 25 µF after DC-bias derating).
- **Off board (mandatory):** a low-ESR electrolytic of at least 1000 µF/35 V at the battery pads.

**Phase-pad and bus copper.** For a 3 mm × 2 oz inner trace segment, R ≈ 0.25 mΩ, giving P = 80²·0.25 m ≈ 1.6 W per motor.
- Use stacked pours on all 2 oz layers plus via arrays.
- Via capacity: about 1.5–2 A per 0.3 mm via, so at least 40 vias per 80 A node.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Lumped network (two-node Cauer per FET, board node shared)

```
P_FET ──[R_θJC 1.0]──[R_θCB ≈ 4–6 K/W*]── T_board ──[R_θBA]── T_air
          C_die ≈ 0.02 J/K                C_board
```

\*R_θCB is local spreading resistance into a 20×20 mm 8-layer stack under a 3.3×3.3 mm pad. It is a dominant term; use 5 K/W until confirmed by FEA.

### 3.2 Board heat capacity

| Component | Calculation | Heat capacity |
|---|---|---|
| FR-4 (20×20×1.6 mm) | 1.18 g × 1.1 J/g·K | 1.3 J/K |
| Copper (8 layers × 70 µm) | 2.0 g × 0.385 J/g·K | 0.77 J/K |
| FETs, caps, solder | | ≈ 0.5 J/K |
| **Total C_board** | | **≈ 2.6 J/K** |

### 3.3 Burst time-to-limit

The die-to-board rise for the static low-side FET at 9.6 W + switching share settles within about 50 ms:

$$\Delta T_{jb} \approx 10.5\text{ W}\cdot(1.0+5) \approx 63\text{ K}$$

The board ramps at:

$$\frac{dT_b}{dt} \approx \frac{86\text{ W}}{2.6\text{ J/K}} \approx 33\text{ K/s}$$

Convective loss during the burst is negligible compared to 86 W.

Taking T_j,max = 150 °C with a 10 K margin and T_board,start = 45 °C:

$$t_{burst} = \frac{(140 - 63) - 45}{33} \approx \mathbf{1.0\ s}$$

Motor-lead copper adds heat-sinking. Expect 1–1.5 s in practice. **Spec: "80 A burst ≤ 1 s, 10 % duty."**
