# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #20): Architecture Derivation

## 0. Design Review Findings

These four findings shape everything that follows.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32). The H743 is the flight controller: it generates DShot600 and decodes the bidirectional eRPM replies. Section 3 covers that interface.
2. **80 A per channel is a burst rating, not a thermal operating point.** On a 20×20 mm board, the math in Section 2 shows 4×80 A is survivable for about 1 second.
3. **48 kHz PWM roughly doubles switching loss compared with 24 kHz.** It is justified only for low-inductance motors (see Section 1.3).
4. **All device parameters below are stated assumptions.** Replace them with datasheet values for the selected MOSFET before release.

## 1. Loss Model per ESC Channel

### 1.1 Current ripple and RMS correction

With duty cycle D:

$$\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} \;\xrightarrow{D=0.5}\; \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\,\mu H \times 48\,kHz} = 6.56\,A_{pp}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2\left(1+\frac{r^2}{12}\right),\quad r=\frac{\Delta I}{I}=0.082 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{correction} = +0.056\%$$

The ripple correction is negligible at 80 A. It matters at light load, where r becomes greater than 1.

### 1.2 Conduction loss

In synchronous 6-step drive, current always flows through two conducting switches:

- the PWM leg (high side for time D, its complementary low side for time 1−D), and
- the static low side of the return phase.

Conduction loss is therefore approximately independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha (T_j-25)\right]$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 6400 \times (1.5\,m\Omega \times 1.6) = 2 \times 6400 \times 2.4\,m\Omega = \mathbf{30.7\,W}$$

At T_j = 25 °C (cold start of the burst), the figure is 19.2 W. This is a positive-feedback loop: loss rises as the junction heats. Solve it iteratively with the thermal model in Section 2.

### 1.3 Switching losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg hard-switches.

**Hard-switching V·I overlap:**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\,ns \times 48\,kHz = \mathbf{1.94\,W}$$

**Reverse recovery (low-side body diode):**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\,nC \times 25.2 \times 48\,kHz = \mathbf{0.036\,W}$$

**Output capacitance (Q_oss approximation, both FETs in the leg):**

$$P_{oss} \approx Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 25\,nC \times 25.2 \times 48\,kHz = \mathbf{0.030\,W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction (two edges per period):**

$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2 t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,ns \times 48\,kHz = \mathbf{0.61\,W}$$

**Gate drive (2 FETs switching).** Most of this power is dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not in the FETs.

$$P_g = 2\, Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} = 2 \times 30\,nC \times 10\,V \times 48\,kHz = \mathbf{0.029\,W}$$

**Total switching loss:**

$$P_{sw,total} \approx 2.65\,W \quad (\text{vs } 1.33\,W \text{ at } 24\,kHz)$$

### 1.4 Channel and board totals at 80 A burst (T_j = 125 °C)

| Loss term | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 30.7 W | 122.9 W |
| Switching + dead time | 2.65 W | 10.6 W |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ, I²R at 80 A) | 1.6 W | 6.4 W |
| Copper traces/vias (estimated 0.3 mΩ path) | 1.9 W | 7.7 W |
| **Total** | **≈36.9 W** | **≈147.6 W** |

Input power at D = 1 is 4 × 80 × 25.2 = 8064 W, so efficiency is about 98%. The problem is not efficiency; it is **heat density**: roughly 148 W dissipated into 4 cm².

### 1.5 Per-FET split (worst-case device)

Take the PWM high-side FET at D ≈ 0.9 (punch-out):

$$P_{HS} = I^2 R\, D + P_{VI} + P_{oss}/2 \approx 6400 \times 2.4\,m\Omega \times 0.9 + 1.94 + 0.015 \approx \mathbf{15.8\,W}$$

The static low-side FET carries 15.4 W. Across a 60° commutation cycle each FET averages about one third of these values, because each switch is active in only part of the electrical period. A *stalled or low-speed* rotor, however, places the full value on one device. **Design for the stalled case.**
