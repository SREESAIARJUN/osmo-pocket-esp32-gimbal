# STM32H743 AIO with 4-in-1 ESC (Job #8): Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot Architecture

## 0. Architecture and Key Findings

### 0.1 Architectural correction

An STM32H743 cannot directly commutate four BLDC motors at 48 kHz while running ArduPilot. The correct partitioning is:

```
[6S LiPo] ──► [4× 3-phase power stages] ◄── 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / AM32 or BLHeli_32-class)
                                                  ▲  48 kHz PWM, commutation, BEMF ZC, eRPM
                                                  │  bi-directional DShot600 (1 wire/motor)
                                       [STM32H743 – ArduPilot]
```

- The **48 kHz switching** happens on the ESC MCUs.
- The **H743 owns the DShot600 timers and DMA**: outbound frames and inbound GCR eRPM telemetry.

### 0.2 Headline result

**80 A burst per motor on a nano (20×20 mm) board is thermally limited to roughly 1 s.**

- Total dissipation at 4×80 A is about **125–135 W**, into roughly **2 J/K** of board heat capacity.
- That is about **60 K/s of temperature rise**.
- Sustainable continuous current is about **25–30 A per motor**, depending on airflow.
