## 0. Feasibility Findings

Four findings change the design before any numbers are run.

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** On every production 4-in-1 design, each motor bridge is commutated by a dedicated ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as an STM32G071 or AT32F421. The H743 sends DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. | The 48 kHz PWM is an ESC-firmware setting. ArduPilot has no control over it. This document models both domains separately. |
| 2 | **80 A burst per motor on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is not thermally credible.** The analysis below shows ≈30–40 W per channel and ≈130–160 W total at 80 A phase current. | Use a **30.5×30.5 mm** form factor with 2–3 oz copper. Specify 80 A as a **≤ 5 s burst**, with a continuous rating of about 45–55 A. |
| 3 | **The H743 (LQFP100, 14×14 mm) plus 24 FETs plus 4 ESC MCUs** will not fit in 20×20 mm on one board. | Use a two-board stack (FC on top, ESC below) or a 30.5 mm AIO. |
| 4 | **No specific MOSFET datasheet is assumed.** | All loss math is **parameterized**. The worked numbers use a representative 40 V, 5×6 mm PDFN device (table §1.2). Re-run the model with your selected part's datasheet values. |

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

In each 60° commutation sector, two legs conduct:

- **Leg A (PWM leg):**
  - The high-side (HS) conducts for duty fraction D.
  - The synchronous low-side (LS) conducts for (1−D), minus dead time.
- **Leg B (static leg):** the LS is fully on for the whole sector.

Phase current I_ph flows continuously through exactly two FET channels (ignoring dead time). Total bridge conduction loss is therefore **independent of duty cycle**:

$$
P_{cond,bridge} = I_{ph}^2 \, R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{ph}^2 \, R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

### 2.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]
$$

### 2.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts during a fraction of the electrical cycle:

- **HS FET:** 1/3 of the cycle, at duty D.
- **LS FET:** 1/3 of the cycle as the static leg, plus 1/3 × (1−D) as the synchronous rectifier.

$$
P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} D\, I^2 R_{DS}, \qquad
P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\left[1 + (1-D)\right] I^2 R_{DS}
$$

- **Worst-case FET:** the LS at low duty. As D → 0, P_LS → ⅔ I²R.
- **At full throttle:** D → 1, so P_LS = P_HS = ⅓ I²R.
