# Job #71: STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC Architecture Package

## 0. Architecture Baseline and Assumptions

**A note on feasibility:** an 80 A per-channel burst on a nano-class board (20×20 or 25×25 mm) is thermally marginal. Section 2.6 quantifies this: the burst is time-limited to about 1–2 s, and sustainable current is about 20 A per channel. Treat "80 A" as a short transient rating, not a continuous one.

**Architectural correction:** ArduPilot running on the H743 does not commutate motors. The standard and only supported topology is:

```
STM32H743 (ArduPilot) --DShot600 bidir x4--> 4x ESC MCU (AT32F421 / AM32 or EFM8 / Bluejay)
                                              --> 4x 3-phase gate driver --> 24x N-MOSFET
```

The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs. The H743 only generates the DShot frames and captures the eRPM telemetry.

## 1. Conduction Model

### 1.1 Six-step synchronous commutation

In each 60° sector, two phases conduct:
- **Phase A (PWM leg):** the high side conducts for fraction D, and the low side (synchronous rectification) conducts for fraction 1−D.
- **Phase B (static leg):** the low side conducts continuously.

Phase current is approximately constant over a PWM period (L_motor ≫ ripple). Battery current is related by I_bat ≈ D·I_ph.

Channel conduction loss:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 R_{hot}\big[\underbrace{D}_{HS} + \underbrace{(1-D)}_{LS\,sync} + \underbrace{1}_{static\,LS}\big] - P_{dt,corr} \approx 2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{hot}$$

With synchronous rectification, conduction loss is independent of duty. The correction term is the dead-time fraction, which is handled separately in §2.3.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{hot}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 108 °C: R_hot = 1.5 × (1 + 0.006 × 83) ≈ **2.25 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Worked numbers at 80 A

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.25\text{ m}\Omega = 2 \times 6400 \times 0.00225 = \mathbf{28.8\ W}$$

Each FET conducts on average one third of the electrical cycle:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I^2 R_{hot}}{3}\cdot 2 \cdot \tfrac{3}{6}\ \Rightarrow\ \frac{28.8}{6} = \mathbf{4.8\ W/FET}$$

### 1.4 Copper and shunt losses

| Path | Resistance | Loss at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase/bus copper (2 oz, est.) | 0.5 mΩ | 3.2 W |
| Current shunt (if fitted) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W × D ≈ 1.4 W |

### 1.5 Thermal runaway check

Equilibrium requires

$$\frac{dP_{cond}}{dT_j}\cdot R_{th,ja} < 1$$

where

$$\frac{dP}{dT} = 2I^2 R_{25}\alpha = 2 \times 6400 \times 0.0015 \times 0.006 = 0.115\ \text{W/K}$$

With effective board R_th,ja of about 10 K/W (§2.6), the loop gain is 1.15 > 1. **There is no steady-state equilibrium at 80 A.** This proves that 80 A is a transient rating only.
