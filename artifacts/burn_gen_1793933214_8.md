# Job #17: STM32H743 Nano FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC Design Package

**Rev A, Principal Architecture Baseline**

---

## 0. Architectural Ground Rules

Three points govern every number in this package.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is a flight-control stack and has no field-oriented or six-step commutation. The architecture is therefore an **STM32H743 FC driving four dedicated ESC MCUs over bi-directional DShot600**. The ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071) run AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, and each generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 owns DShot, eRPM telemetry, and the harmonic notch.
2. **"80 A" is a burst rating, not continuous.** The model below shows that a 30.5 × 30.5 mm board sustains roughly 20 to 25 A per motor continuous in prop-wash. 80 A on all four motors is survivable for about 2 seconds. Marketing must label it accordingly.
3. **Component values are representative placeholders.** The MOSFET and driver parameters are typical of 40 V, ~1 mΩ, 5 × 6 mm parts. Substitute datasheet values for the selected part before releasing the design. All equations are parametric for this reason.

**Operating point:** 6S LiPo (V_bus = 25.2 V max), f_sw = 48 kHz, I_phase = 80 A burst, T_amb = 40 °C, T_j,max (design) = 125 °C, T_board,max = 110 °C.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

**Topology:** six-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. At any instant, two phase legs carry the current I:

- **PWM leg:** current flows through the high-side FET for D·T_sw and through the low-side FET (synchronous rectification) for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Static leg:** the low-side FET is fully on.

At every instant, exactly one FET per active leg conducts. The total conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle D:

$$
P_{cond,ESC} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j) + I^2 R_{cu}
$$

**Per-FET loss.** Each FET is active for 120° of each electrical cycle. Averaged over the cycle:

$$
\bar P_{cond,FET} \approx \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2 R_{ds(on)} \quad\text{(average)}
$$

During its own 120° window, the worst FET (the low side of the static leg) carries the full I²R.

**Temperature coupling.** R_ds(on) rises with junction temperature, so the model is self-consistent only when solved iteratively:

$$
R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0067\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

The solution converges to k_T ≈ 1.5 at T_j ≈ 100 °C.

**At 80 A:**

- P_FET,cond = 2 × 80² × 1.5 mΩ = **19.20 W**
- P_Cu = 80² × 0.3 mΩ = **1.92 W**

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

With complementary PWM, only the PWM leg switches. That gives one hard turn-on and one hard turn-off per period.

| Loss term | Equation | Value at 80 A, 25.2 V |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48 k = **1.94 W** |
| Dead-time body diode | V_f · I · 2t_dt · f_sw | 0.8 × 80 × 400 ns × 48 k = **1.23 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw | 50 n × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.06 W** |
| C_oss | Q_oss · V · f_sw | 60 n × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.07 W** |
| Gate drive (in driver and R_g, not in the FET) | 2 · Q_g · V_gs · f_sw | 2 × 60 n × 10 × 48 k = **0.06 W** |

**Key sensitivities:**

- **Dead time is the second-largest loss.** Tuning t_dt from 200 ns down to 100 ns saves about 0.6 W per ESC. Verify shoot-through margin on a scope before relying on that saving.
- **48 kHz is a deliberate trade.** At 24 kHz, the switching terms halve (saving ~1.6 W per ESC), but current ripple and acoustic noise worsen with low-inductance racing motors.

## 4. Total Loss Function

The per-ESC loss is quadratic in I:

$$
P_{ESC}(I) = aI^2 + bI
$$

| Coefficient | Derivation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| a | 2R_ds(on),hot + R_cu | 3.3 mΩ |
| b | [½V(t_r+t_f) + 2V_f·t_dt] · f_sw + (Q_rr + Q_oss)·V·f_sw / I | ≈ 0.0396 W/A at 80 A |
