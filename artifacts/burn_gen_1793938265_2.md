## 1. FOC Current Loop

### 1.1 Electrical angle
$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Here $p$ is the number of pole pairs. $\theta_{off}$ is found at commissioning by applying $v_d>0$, $v_q=0$ at $\theta_e=0$ and reading the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (power-invariant variant not used; amplitude-invariant)
With $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$:
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform
$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant (surface PMSM, $L_d = L_q = L$)
$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$
$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q \equiv K_t\, i_q$$

### 1.5 Current PI with decoupling and back-EMF feed-forward
