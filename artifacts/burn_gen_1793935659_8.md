# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80 A ESC (Job #64): Power Stage, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

The numbers below use a defined set of representative assumptions. Before trusting them for layout or procurement, check every component value and pin against its datasheet. The analysis also shows that **80 A per channel cannot be sustained on a nano-format board**. It is only possible as a time-limited burst. Section 5 quantifies that limit.

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

| Item | Assumption | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Topology | STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to **4 dedicated ESC MCUs** on the same PCB | ArduPilot does not do motor commutation. Each motor needs its own commutation MCU (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32). |
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max (22.2 V nominal) | Common for 80 A-class ESCs |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification

At any commutation state, two phases carry the full phase current I_ph:
- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) switch conducts for D·T_sw and the low-side (LS) switch for (1−D)·T_sw. Dead time is handled separately.
- **Return leg:** the LS switch conducts 100% of the time.

So at every instant exactly two MOSFET channels are in series:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 RMS current including PWM ripple

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\, f_{sw}}, \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

Worst case is D = 0.5:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\,\mu H \times 48\,kHz} = 6.56\ A \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 3.6\ A^2$$

The ripple term (3.6 A²) is negligible against I² = 6400 A². At 48 kHz, ripple matters for the bus capacitors (§3), not for conduction loss.

### 1.3 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ /K$$

At T_j = 135 °C this gives R ≈ 1.5 mΩ.
