# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have the original Job #21 specification, so this design states its assumptions explicitly. Every parameter is a named constant so you can substitute your real values.

**Assumed hardware**

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× BLDC gimbal motors, 12N14P (7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5
