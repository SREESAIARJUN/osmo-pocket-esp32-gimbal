## 0. Executive Findings

| Result | Value | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Loss per channel at 80 A phase current, T_j ≈ 110 °C | ≈ 34 W | Not sustainable on a nano board |
| Board loss with all 4 channels at 80 A | ≈ 136 W | Board heats about 50 K/s adiabatically |
| Allowable 80 A burst (1 motor, from 40 °C to 110 °C) | ≈ 4–5 s | "80 A burst" is defensible only per-motor and short |
| Allowable 80 A burst (all 4 motors) | ≈ 1.1–1.3 s | Must be enforced by ESC firmware current/thermal limits |
| Switching share at 48 kHz | ≈ 1.9 W/ch (≈ 6 %) | Conduction dominates; 48 kHz is fine |
| DShot600 bidir round trip | ≈ 85 µs | Supports up to about 8 kHz loop; ArduPilot's default 1–2 kHz is trivial |
| DMA streams for 4 bidir motors on one timer | 5 (1×UP + 4×CCx) | H743 DMAMUX makes this easy. Group all motors on one timer. |

**Architectural correction.** In a 4-in-1 ESC stack the **H743 does not drive the MOSFETs**. Each bridge is commutated by a dedicated ESC MCU (AM32/BLHeli_32 class, e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071). The H743 only sources DShot and decodes eRPM telemetry. The model below reflects that split.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Bridge topology during one commutation sector

With complementary PWM in a given sector (phase A high, phase B low):

- **Q_AH** conducts for a fraction D of the cycle.
- **Q_AL** (synchronous rectifier) conducts for 1 − D.
- **Q_BL** conducts 100 % of the sector.

The phase current I_ph flows continuously through the motor winding, so:

$$
P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 R_{hot}\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{ph}^2 R_{hot} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{hot}
$$

This result is independent of duty cycle, which is the key advantage of synchronous rectification. Dead-time diode conduction is handled separately in §3.

### 2.2 Temperature dependence (positive feedback)

$$
R_{hot}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_hot = 1.6 mΩ × (1 + 0.0055 × 85) = **2.35 mΩ**.

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.35\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{30.1\ W\ per\ channel}
$$

### 2.3 Per-FET distribution

Over one electrical revolution, each phase leg spends 1/3 of the time on the high side, 1/3 on the low side, and 1/3 floating. Average loss per FET is about P_cond / 6 ≈ **5.0 W**.

The **instantaneous** peak during a low-side "always-on" sector is I²R = 80² × 2.35 mΩ = **15 W**. The relevance of this peak is covered in §5.3.
