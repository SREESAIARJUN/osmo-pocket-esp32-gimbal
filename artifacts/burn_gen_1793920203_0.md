# STM32H743 AIO 4-in-1 ESC: 80 A Burst Thermal, 48 kHz Switching, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Design Package

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I don't have the Job #52 BOM, stack-up or MOSFET selection. Every number below comes from a **stated, replaceable parameter set**. The equations are the deliverable. Substitute datasheet values and the results scale directly.

**Architecture clarification.** In an AIO, the **H743 does not generate the 48 kHz motor PWM**. The ESC side does that: typically 4× AT32F421 or similar running AM32/BLHeli_32. The H743 generates DShot600 and decodes bidirectional eRPM telemetry. The two domains are modeled separately below.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

**Commutation model.** With 6-step or FOC complementary (synchronous) PWM, exactly one high-side and one low-side path carry the phase current at every instant. One is the PWM leg, where the HS/LS FETs alternate D/(1−D); the other is the static low-side.

**Temperature dependence of on-resistance:**

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

**Per-channel conduction loss:**

$$P_{cond}=I^2\left[D\,R_{HS}+(1-D)\,R_{LS,sync}+R_{LS,static}\right]=2\,I^2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

This assumes identical FETs. The result is duty-independent because synchronous rectification always presents one R_ds(on).

**Worst case at T_j = 125 °C:**
- R_ds(on) = 1.0 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**
- P_cond = 2 × 80² × 1.5 mΩ = **19.2 W per channel**
- Copper loss: P_cu = 80² × 0.5 mΩ = **3.2 W**

**Self-consistent solution (electro-thermal feedback):**

$$T_j = T_{board} + Z_{th,jc}(t)\cdot P_{FET}(T_j)\;\Rightarrow\;T_j=\frac{T_b+Z\,I^2R_{25}(1-25\alpha)}{1-Z\,I^2R_{25}\alpha}$$

The thermal-runaway condition is Z·I²·R₂₅·α ≥ 1. With Z_jc = 1 K/W, the loop term is 1 × 6400 × 1e-3 × 0.005 = 0.032, so the design is stable.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the PWM-leg pair switches. Each loss term is listed below.

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap (hard-switched HS) | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | ½·25.2·80·30 ns·48k = **1.45 W** |
| Dead-time body-diode conduction | V_SD·I·2t_dt·f_sw | 0.8·80·200 ns·48k = **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 50 nC·25.2·48k = **0.06 W** |
| C_oss discharge | ½·C_oss,er·V²·f_sw | **0.03 W** |
| Gate drive (mostly in driver/R_g) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw | **0.058 W** |
| **Σ switching** | | **≈ 2.2 W / channel** |

**Frequency sensitivity:** dP_sw/df_sw ≈ 45 µW/Hz. Moving 24 → 48 kHz costs about +1.1 W per channel at 80 A. The benefit is lower current ripple:

$$\Delta I_{pp}=\frac{V_{bus}D(1-D)}{L_{ph}f_{sw}}$$

## 3. Total Loss and Thermal Budget

**Per channel at 80 A:**
- 19.2 W conduction + 2.2 W switching + 3.2 W copper ≈ **24.6 W**
- All four motors: **≈ 98 W**

**Junction rise above board.** The hottest FET is the PWM-leg HS: 9.6 W conduction + 1.5 W switching ≈ 11 W.

$$\Delta T_{jb} = P\cdot Z_{th,jb}(t) \approx 11\,\text{W}\times1.5\,\text{K/W}\approx 17\ \text{K (quasi-steady)}$$

**Board-level transient (lumped RC, the dominant constraint).** Representative values for a nano-class AIO:
- Board + FET thermal mass m·c_p ≈ 6 g × 0.6 J/(g·K) ≈ **3.6 J/K**
- Board-to-air with prop wash: R_th,ba ≈ **6 K/W**, so τ = R·C ≈ 22 s

The temperature response is:

$$T_b(t)=T_a+P R_{ba}\left(1-e^{-t/\tau}\right)$$

The allowable burst time to reach T_b,max is:

$$t_{burst} = -\tau\ln\!\left(1-\frac{T_{b,max}-T_a}{P R_{ba}}\right)$$

| Load case | P_total | Steady ΔT (theoretical) | t to T_b = 105 °C from 35 °C |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 × 80 A | 98 W | 588 K | **≈ 2.8 s** |
| 4 × 60 A | 56 W | 336 K | ≈ 5.0 s |
| 4 × 40 A | 26 W | 156 K | ≈ 13 s |
| 4 × 25 A | 11 W | 66 K | continuous (T_b ≈ 101 °C) |

**Verdict:** "80 A burst" on a nano form factor is defensible only as a **≤ 2–3 s, all-motor** rating, or about 10 s on a single motor. Specify the burst rating with its duration and duty cycle. Enable the ESC firmware's temperature derating: AM32 temp limit at about 100 °C on the board NTC.

## 4. Bulk Capacitor Stress

The input RMS ripple current per channel peaks at D = 0.5:

$$I_{C,rms}=I\sqrt{D(1-D)} \;\Rightarrow\; 40\ \text{A}_{rms}$$

The four channels are partially de-correlated, but the aggregate is still tens of amps.

**Requirements:**
- **On-board:** ≥ 8 × 10 µF/50 V X7R 1206, placed at each half-bridge. DC-bias derating leaves about 3 µF effective each.
- **External:** a low-ESR 35 V, ≥ 1000 µF electrolytic on the pigtail. This is mandatory, not optional.
