## 0. Architecture and Key Assumptions

**The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In any ArduPilot-compatible 4-in-1 stack, the H743 sends DShot600 commands. A dedicated ESC MCU switches the bridges at 48 kHz and returns eRPM telemetry. Typical ESC MCUs are the AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32.

ArduPilot has no firmware path for an H743 to drive 12 half-bridges directly. The design is therefore partitioned as follows:

```
[FC board: STM32H743]  --DShot600 bidir x4 (single wire, open-drain-ish w/ pull-up)-->  [ESC board: 4x ESC MCU + 4x 3-ph gate driver + 24 MOSFETs]
                       <--GCR eRPM telemetry (same wire)--
                       <--ESC serial telemetry (optional UART)--
                       <--Current sense (analog, CURR pin)--
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM, which is the AM32 default. At any instant:

- The **high-side FET of phase A** conducts for duty fraction D.
- The **low-side FET of phase A** conducts for (1 − D), as synchronous rectification.
- The **low-side FET of phase B** conducts for 100% of the sector.

As a result, the current always flows through exactly **two R_ds,on in series**, independent of D:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2 \, R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

**Temperature dependence:**

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big]
$$

### 1.2 Per-device distribution (worst device)

Within one 60° sector, the always-on low-side FET of phase B carries the full I² R. Averaged over the 6 sectors, each of the 6 FETs conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle. The high- and low-side FETs of a phase split that third according to D.

For thermal sizing, the **sector-instantaneous worst device** is what matters, because sectors are far shorter than the die thermal time constant only at high eRPM:

$$
P_{FET,peak} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad
P_{FET,avg} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\cdot 2 \;\;(\text{spread over 6 FETs})
$$

| T_j | R_ds (typ) | P_cond per channel (typ) | P_cond per channel (R_max) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 0.90 mΩ | 11.5 W | 15.4 W |
| 100 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W | 20.5 W |

Worked example at 125 °C, typical R_ds: 2 × 80² × 1.31 mΩ = **16.7 W per channel**.

### 1.4 PCB copper loss

For copper of thickness t, the sheet resistance is:

$$
R_{\square} = \frac{\rho_{Cu}(T)}{t}
$$

For 70 µm (2 oz) copper at 25 °C, R□ = 1.72e-8 / 70e-6 = **0.246 mΩ/sq**. This value rises by about +0.39 %/K.

A phase path of roughly 3 squares on 2 paralleled 2 oz layers gives R ≈ 0.37 mΩ. Battery-to-bridge bus copper adds about 0.3 mΩ shared.

- **Per-channel copper loss:** 80² × 0.37 mΩ ≈ **2.4 W**
- **Shared bus loss at 4 × 80 A = 320 A:** 320² × 0.3 mΩ ≈ **31 W**
