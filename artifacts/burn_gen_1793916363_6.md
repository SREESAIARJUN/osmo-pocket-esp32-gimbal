## Engineering Design Package, Rev A (Pre-Layout)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Red Flags

Some constraints shape everything that follows:

1. **The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC.** No production 4-in-1 runs commutation for four motors on an H743 under ArduPilot. The ESC section uses **4× dedicated ESC MCUs** running AM32, one per motor. The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600. This is the only architecture that ArduPilot supports for this feature set.
2. **"80 A burst" is interpreted as per-channel phase current for ≤ 5 s.** It is not a 320 A battery current. Battery current is roughly D·I_phase per channel.
3. **"Nano" is taken to mean a 25.5 × 25.5 mm board with 20 × 20 mm M2/M3 mounting.** The thermal analysis in §3 shows this footprint cannot sustain 80 A on all four channels for more than about 3 s. The burst rating is physically limited by board thermal mass, not by silicon. Marketing numbers must reflect this.
4. **All device parameters are representative placeholders for a 40 V, 3.3 × 3.3 mm DFN MOSFET.** They must be replaced with the selected part's datasheet values before release. The equations are general; the numbers are only as good as the inputs.
5. **ArduPilot hwdef behaviours below match current ChibiOS-based ArduPilot master as I understand it.** Validate them against the `libraries/AP_HAL_ChibiOS/hwdef/` tree and on bench hardware with `SERVO_BLH_BDMASK` / `SERVO_DSHOT_ESC` set.

        │   │        ▲                    ▲ gate drive (4 × FD6288Q-class)            │
        │   │        │                    │                                           │
        │   │  4 × AT32F421K8U7 (AM32) ◄──┘  BEMF/shunt sense                          │
        │   │        ▲ 1-wire bidir DShot600 (×4)                                      │
