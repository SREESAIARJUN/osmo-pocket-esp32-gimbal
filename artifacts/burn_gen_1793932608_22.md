## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path

In trapezoidal six-step drive, two phases conduct at any instant:

- **High-side leg (phase A):** the HS FET conducts for D·T. Its complementary LS FET conducts for (1−D)·T, minus dead time, via synchronous rectification.
- **Low-side leg (phase B):** the LS FET is fully on for the 60° electrical step.

The phase current therefore always flows through exactly **two FET channels in series**, independent of D. The exception is dead time, covered in §2.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

### 1.3 Per-motor conduction loss

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\, I_{pk}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j) + I_{pk}^2 R_{Cu}$$

Here R_Cu is the PCB copper and solder resistance in the current path, budgeted at **0.3 mΩ** for 2 oz inner plus 3 oz outer copper with via arrays.

At T_j = 100 °C, R_DS = 1.375 mΩ:

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.375\times10^{-3} = 17.6\ \text{W}$$
$$P_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.3\times10^{-3} = 1.92\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 Per-FET average

Each of the 6 FETs carries current for roughly 1/3 of the electrical cycle (120° conduction). The total is shared across 6 devices:

$$\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{17.6}{6} \approx 2.9\ \text{W}$$

The instantaneous peak while conducting is 6400 × 1.375 mΩ = **8.8 W**.

### 1.5 Self-consistent T_j iteration

Loss depends on T_j, and T_j depends on loss. Iterate:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{ref} + \bar P_{FET}\big(T_j^{(n)}\big)\cdot Z_{th}(t)$$

This converges in 3–4 iterations for any stable design. Divergence indicates thermal runaway at that burst duration (see §3.4).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing high-side FET (and its synchronous partner) switches during a commutation step.

### 2.1 Transition times

Gate current during the Miller plateau:

$$I_g = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_{g}} = \frac{10 - 3.5}{5} = 1.3\ \text{A}$$

$$t_{r} \approx t_{f} \approx \frac{Q_{gd} + Q_{gs2}}{I_g} = \frac{8 + 6}{1.3}\,\text{nC/A} \approx 10.8\ \text{ns}$$

Q_gs2 is the post-threshold portion of Q_gs, taken as about half.

### 2.2 Loss components

**V–I overlap (hard switching):**
$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{pk}(t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 21.6\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = 1.045\ \text{W}$$

**Reverse recovery** (LS body diode recovers when HS turns on):
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

**Output capacitance:**
$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 1\text{n} \times 635 \times 48\text{k} = 0.015\ \text{W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two dead-time intervals per period):
$$P_{dt} = 2\, t_{dt} V_{SD} I_{pk} f_{sw} = 2 \times 50\text{n} \times 0.8 \times 80 \times 48\text{k} = 0.307\ \text{W}$$

**Gate drive** (dissipated in the driver and R_g, not the FET), two switching FETs:
$$P_{gate} = 2\, Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} = 2 \times 50\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

### 2.3 Per-motor switching total

$$P_{sw,motor} \approx 1.045 + 0.048 + 0.015 + 0.307 = \mathbf{1.42\ W}$$

At 80 A, switching is about **7%** of power-stage loss. The design is **conduction-dominated**. Prioritize R_DS(on) and copper over faster edges. Do not reduce R_g aggressively: faster edges cost ringing and EMI against the H743's IMU and baro for little gain.

### 2.4 Frequency sensitivity

$$\frac{\partial P_{total}}{\partial f_{sw}} \approx \frac{1.42}{48\text{k}} \approx 29.6\ \mu\text{W/Hz per motor}$$

Raising PWM from 48 kHz to 96 kHz adds about **1.4 W per motor** at 80 A.
