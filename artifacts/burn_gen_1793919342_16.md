## 0. Design Basis and Assumptions

Before the math, a correction to the architecture and a feasibility warning.

**1. The H743 is not the ESC controller.** On every production AIO of this class, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot. Each motor bridge is commutated by a dedicated small ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The 48 kHz figure is the ESC-side PWM frequency. Bidirectional DShot600 is the link between the H743 and the four ESC MCUs. The model below follows this split.

**2. Component values are placeholders.** The MOSFET parameters are representative of a 40 V PDFN3.3×3 part. Replace them with your selected part's datasheet values and re-run Section 2. Every equation is written so a single parameter swap propagates through.

**3. Feasibility warning.** As derived below, "80 A burst" on a nano-sized (≤30×30 mm) board is a **~1–2 second** capability with all four motors loaded simultaneously. Treat it as a single-motor or short-transient figure, not a sustained rating.
