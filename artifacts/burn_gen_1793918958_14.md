## 0. Architectural Clarification

"STM32H743 4-in-1 ESC" is ambiguous, so this design fixes the architecture first:

| Function | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (400 MHz, LQFP100) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) |
| ESC commutation, ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071, one per motor | AM32 (supports 48 kHz PWM and bidirectional DShot) |
| Gate drive, ×4 | FD6288Q 3-phase half-bridge driver | n/a |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET (6 per motor) | n/a |

ArduPilot does not run commutation or FOC on the flight-controller MCU. The H743 emits bidirectional DShot600 to the four ESC MCUs and receives eRPM telemetry. The 48 kHz switching therefore happens on the ESC MCUs, and the DShot DMA allocation happens on the H743.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path in 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification

At any instant, exactly two phases are energized. With complementary (synchronous) PWM:

- **During ON (duty D):** the high-side FET of phase A and the low-side FET of phase B conduct.
- **During OFF (1 − D):** the low-side FET of phase A (synchronous rectifier) and the low-side FET of phase B conduct.

So the current always flows through two channel resistances in series, regardless of D:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$
