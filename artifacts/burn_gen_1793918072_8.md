# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #64): Power-Stage Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation, and Reference Netlists

## 0. Architecture Clarifications

Three points shape the rest of the analysis.

1. **The H743 does not generate the 48 kHz PWM.** In a 4-in-1 ESC stack, each channel (or one shared MCU) runs AM32 or BLHeli_32 on a small MCU such as the AT32F421 or STM32G071. That MCU produces the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and only sends DShot600 frames and receives eRPM telemetry. The losses in §1–§3 therefore belong to the ESC board, and the DMA work in §5 belongs to the FC.

2. **"Nano" and "80 A burst" conflict.** On a 20×20 mm ESC, 12 MOSFETs in 3.3×3.3 mm packages cannot conduct 80 A per channel for more than a fraction of a second. I model a **30.5×30.5 mm ESC footprint**, which is the smallest realistic size for an 80 A burst rating. The FC itself can still be a 20×20 or 30×30 board.

3. **All device parameters below are stated assumptions.** Replace them with your selected parts' datasheet values. The equations stay valid; the numbers will move.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

In each 60° sector, phase A's high side (Q_AH) is PWM-switched at duty D. Q_AL is its complement, and phase B's low side (Q_BL) is held fully on. Two devices always carry I_ph:

- During D: Q_AH and Q_BL conduct.
- During (1−D): Q_AL (synchronous rectification) and Q_BL conduct.

So per channel, instantaneous conduction is always I² · 2R_DS(on), regardless of D.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At the design junction temperature T_j = 100 °C:

$$R_{DS} = 1.0 \times (1 + 0.005 \times 75) = 1.375\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2\,(2R_{DS}(T_j) + R_{Cu})$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 6400 \times (2.75 + 0.5)\times10^{-3} = 17.6\ \text{W (FETs)} + 3.2\ \text{W (copper)} = 20.8\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 Per-device distribution

This matters for hot-spot temperature. Over one electrical revolution, each switch position is active in 2 of the 6 sectors.

- **Held-on low side** (e.g., Q_BL): 1/3 of the cycle at 100 % → average I²R/3.
- **PWM high side:** 1/3 of the cycle × D.
- **Synchronous low side:** 1/3 of the cycle × (1−D).
