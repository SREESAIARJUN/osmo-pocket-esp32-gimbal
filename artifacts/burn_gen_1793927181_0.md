# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal (Job #50)

I don't have the Job #50 specification, so this design states its assumptions in §0. Every parameter that depends on them is a named constant you can replace. The equations come first, then a complete C++17 implementation for embedded targets: no heap, no exceptions, float-only.

---

## 0. Assumptions

| Item | Assumed value | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (7 pole pairs), R ≈ 10 Ω, L ≈ 4 mH, Kt ≈ 0.12 N·m/A | Sets cogging harmonics and current-loop gains |
| Encoder | AS5048A/MT6835-class absolute magnetic, ≥14 bit, per joint | Used for commutation and cogging indexing |
| MCU | STM32G4/H7, FPU, center-aligned PWM at 20 kHz | Sets the loop rates |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI at 8 kHz ODR, rigidly mounted with a known relative pose | Used for redundancy, noise averaging and lever-arm correction |
| Joint order | Base → **Yaw (z)** → **Roll (x)** → **Pitch (y)** → camera | Used by the Jacobian |
| Vibration | Airframe/propeller vibration, 80–400 Hz, RPM optionally known | Sets notch placement |

**Loop rates:** current loop 20 kHz (in the PWM ISR); velocity loop 4 kHz; angle/attitude loop 1 kHz; IMU sampling and filtering 8 kHz, decimated to 4 kHz.
