# Job #9: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: System Architecture & Mathematical Model

**Document scope:** Architecture, electro-thermal loss model (80 A burst, 48 kHz PWM), ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer and DMA allocation, reference netlists, and thermal budget.

**Engineering position up front:** 80 A per motor on a nano-class board is a **transient rating limited by thermal mass**, not a continuous rating. The model below quantifies exactly how long the burst can last. Any marketing figure should be derived from it.

---

## 0. Architecture Decision

The H743 cannot sensibly run four sensorless commutation loops at 48 kHz *and* the ArduPilot flight stack. The standard partition is used:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| FC MCU | STM32H743 (LQFP100 or TFBGA100), 480 MHz | ArduPilot, sensors, DShot master |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | AT32F421 or STM32G071-class, running AM32 | 6-step commutation, BEMF ZC, BDShot reply |
| Gate drivers (×4) | 3-phase half-bridge driver with bootstrap (FD6288Q-class) | 10–12 V gate drive |
| Power stage (×4) | 6× N-MOSFET, 40 V (6S max) | Per-motor bridge |
| Stack-up | 8-layer, 2 oz inner power planes, 30.5×30.5 mm mounting | "Nano" mechanical class, 25.5 mm is not viable at 80 A |

**Assumed MOSFET (parametric placeholder, verify against the chosen datasheet):**

| Param | Value |
|---|---|
| V_DS | 40 V |
| R_DS(on) max @25 °C, V_GS=10 V | 1.3 mΩ |
| R_DS(on) temperature multiplier @125 °C | ×1.5 |
| Q_g / Q_gs / Q_gd | 50 / 12 / 8 nC |

**Operating point:** 6S full charge, V_bus = 25.2 V. I_burst = 80 A phase current. f_sw = 48 kHz.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path topology (6-step, complementary/synchronous PWM)

At every instant during a commutation sector, motor current flows through **exactly two FETs**:
- During the PWM on-time: HS of leg A plus LS of leg B.
- During the off-time: LS of leg A (synchronous rectification) plus LS of leg B.

Therefore, independent of duty D:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\, I^2 R_{DS}(T_J)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence (self-consistent)

$$R_{DS}(T_J) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_J - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

$$T_J = T_{board} + P_{FET}(T_J)\cdot R_{\theta J\text{-}board}$$

This loop converges if $\partial P / \partial T_J \cdot R_\theta < 1$. At 80 A it converges, but the margin is small. This is the thermal runaway boundary and the firmware must enforce it (§5).

### 1.3 Numbers @ 80 A, T_J = 125 °C

$$R_{DS} = 1.3 \times 1.5 = 1.95\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.95\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{24.96\ W}$$

**PCB copper path** (bus planes + phase pads, about 0.5 mΩ effective round-trip):

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.5\times10^{-3} = 3.2\ \text{W}$$

**Current shunt** (if a bus shunt is used): a 0.5 mΩ shunt would dissipate 3.2 W, which is unacceptable. Specify **0.2 mΩ
