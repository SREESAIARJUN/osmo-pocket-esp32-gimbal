## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) PWM

At any instant, two phases conduct:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) FET conducts for duty fraction D. The low-side (LS) FET of the same leg freewheels for (1 − D).
- **Low leg:** the LS FET is on 100% of the time.

The instantaneous conduction loss per channel is therefore:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS} = 2 I^2 R_{DS}$$

Note that this is independent of D.

### 1.2 Per-channel result at 80 A

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{23.0\ W}$$

Copper trace and phase-pad resistance adds to this. Assume about 0.3 mΩ per conduction path:

$$P_{Cu} = 2 \cdot 6400 \cdot 0.3\text{m} = 3.8\ \text{W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET average over an electrical cycle

Each phase spends one-third of the cycle in each of three states: PWM-high, held-low, and floating.

$$\bar P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} D\, I^2 R, \qquad \bar P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,(2-D)\, I^2 R$$

With I²R = 11.52 W:

| D | P̄_HS | P̄_LS | Hotspot |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 | 3.84 W | 3.84 W | Balanced |
| 0.5 | 1.92 W | **5.76 W** | LS FETs |

**Design consequence:** low-side FETs are the thermal hotspot at partial throttle with high current, such as a hard punch-out off a hover. Give the LS FETs priority for thermal vias and copper area.
