# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Nano): Power, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot Design Model

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

I don't have Job #18's requirements document, so the assumptions below are stated explicitly. Every equation is written symbolically, so you can substitute your actual BOM values.

**Architecture correction.** In a "STM32H743 4-in-1" stack, the H743 is the **flight controller**. It does not run the motors. Each ESC channel runs firmware such as AM32 or BLHeli_32 on its own MCU (typically STM32G071, AT32F421, or GD32E230). The 48 kHz PWM is generated by those ESC MCUs. The H743 only generates DShot600 and decodes the eRPM telemetry. The model below respects that split.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step

In any 60° sector, two phases conduct:

- **Switching leg:** the high-side (HS) FET conducts for duty D. The low-side (LS) FET conducts (synchronous rectification) for (1 − D), minus dead time.
- **Return leg:** the LS FET is held fully on.

Per-motor conduction loss:

$$
P_{cond} = I_{rms}^2\left[D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS} + R_{LS}\right] + I_{rms}^2 R_{Cu}
$$

With R_HS = R_LS = R:

$$
P_{cond} \approx 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R\;+\;I_{rms}^2 R_{Cu}
$$

This expression is **independent of D**. That is the key reason synchronous rectification matters.

### 1.2 Ripple correction

For phase inductance L_ph and peak-to-peak ripple ΔI:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-E_{bemf})\,D}{2L_{ph} f_{sw}}
$$

$$
I_{rms} = \sqrt{I_{dc}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}}
$$

Example: L_ph = 10 µH line-to-line, so 5 µH per phase in the formula, at D = 0.5:

- ΔI ≈ (25.2 − 12.6)(0.5) / (2 · 5 µH · 48 kHz) ≈ 13 A
- I_rms ≈ √(80² + 13²/12) = **80.09 A**, which is negligible at 48 kHz.

### 1.3 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \beta\,(T_j - 25)\right], \qquad \beta \approx 0.005\ /\mathrm{K}
$$

This creates positive feedback, so solve iteratively. A practical design value is R_hot = 1.5 mΩ.
