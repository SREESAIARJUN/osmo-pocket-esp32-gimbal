## 0. Architecture Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no sensorless BLDC commutation path. The architecture is therefore:

```
┌───────────────────────── FC section ─────────────────────────┐
│ STM32H743VIH6 (ArduPilot) ── 4× bidir DShot600 ──┐           │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────┼───────────┘
┌───────────────────────── ESC section ────────────┼───────────┐
│ 4× AT32F421K8U7 (AM32) ── FD6288Q ── 6× N-FET ── motor 1..4  │
│   48 kHz complementary PWM, sync rectification               │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

> **Feasibility flag.** 80 A on a "nano"-class board is achievable only as a **seconds-scale burst**. It is never a continuous rating. The math below quantifies exactly how many seconds.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step with complementary PWM

In each of the 6 commutation sectors, one phase is PWM-switched (HS/LS complementary), one phase has its LS held on, and one phase floats (BEMF sensing).

- **ON interval:** current flows through HS(A) → motor → LS(B).
- **Freewheel interval:** current flows through LS(A) (synchronous) → motor → LS(B).

In both intervals, exactly **two FETs carry I**. Channel conduction loss is therefore approximately independent of duty D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j) \;+\; I^2 R_{sh}$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2(80)^2(2.25\,\text{m}\Omega) + (80)^2(0.5\,\text{m}\Omega) = 28.8 + 3.2 = \mathbf{32.0\ W}$$

The ripple correction, $I_{rms}^2 = I^2(1 + r^2/12)$, adds less than 1% at typical motor inductance (r < 0.2) and is neglected.

### 1.2 Per-FET distribution

Over one electrical cycle, each phase leg conducts for 2/3 of the time. Within its active 1/3 of the cycle, the PWM leg splits current between HS (fraction D) and LS (fraction 1−D). Worst case for the hottest FET is the LS at high duty, where the LS conducts its full held 1/3 plus freewheel time:

$$P_{FET,max} \approx I^2 R_{ds}\left(\tfrac{1}{3} + \tfrac{1-D}{3}\right) \xrightarrow{D\to 0.5} 14.4 \times 0.5 = \mathbf{7.2\ W}$$

The average per FET is $28.8/6 = 4.8$ W.

### 1.3 Temperature-coupled form (for iteration)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

Solve $T_j = T_{pcb} + P(T_j)\,Z_{th}$ iteratively. There is thermal-runaway risk if $\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,Z_{th} \ge 1$. Check: $7.2 \times 0.005/1.5 \times 8\ \text{K/W} \approx 0.19 < 1$, so the system is stable.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-leg HS FET switches hard. The LS turns on via ZVS after the body diode conducts.

| Term | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | $\tfrac12 V I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw}$ | ½·25.2·80·20 ns·48 k = **0.968 W** |
| Reverse recovery | $Q_{rr} V f_{sw}$ | 40 nC·25.2·48 k = **0.048 W** |
| Coss | $\tfrac12 Q_{oss} V f_{sw}$ | **0.018 W** |
| Dead-time diode | $V_f I \cdot 2t_d f_{sw}$ | 0.8·80·100 ns·48 k = **0.307 W** |
| Gate drive (HS+LS of PWM leg) | $2 Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw}$ | **0.038 W** (dissipated mostly in driver/R_g) |
| **Total switching, per channel** | | **≈ 1.38 W** |

**Frequency sensitivity:** $\partial P_{sw}/\partial f \approx 28.7\ \mu\text{W/Hz}$. Moving 48 kHz to 96 kHz adds about 1.4 W per channel; conduction loss still dominates by roughly 20×.

### Channel and board totals at 80 A

| | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 28.8 W | 115.2 W |
| Shunt | 3.2 W | 12.8 W |
| Switching | 1.4 W | 5.5 W |
| Bus-cap ESR (see §3.4) | ~0.8 W | ~3 W |
| **Total** | **≈ 34 W** | **≈ 137 W** |

Efficiency at 80 A, D ≈ 0.9: P_out ≈ 25.2·0.9·80 ≈ 1.8 kW per channel, giving η ≈ 98.1%. Efficiency looks good, but the absolute heat density is the problem.
