## 0. Architecture Clarification and Design Assumptions

**Partitioning.** An STM32H743 is not used as the motor-commutation MCU on production 4-in-1 ESCs. It is a flight-controller-class part. The correct architecture for Job #47 is:

| Board | MCU | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| FC (stacked, 30.5 × 30.5 mm) | STM32H743VIT6 @ 480 MHz | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) | Attitude control, bi-dir DShot600 master, eRPM harmonic notch |
| 4-in-1 ESC (30.5 × 30.5 mm) | 4 × AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) | AM32 / BLHeli_32-compatible | Per-motor 6-step commutation, DShot slave, eRPM telemetry |

A single H743 commutating four motors at 48 kHz is possible in principle. However, it would break ArduPilot compatibility and centralize a single point of failure. **This document assumes the split architecture.**

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step synchronous commutation

In each 60° electrical sector, current flows through one high-side FET in phase X and one low-side FET in phase Y.

- **ON-time (D):** HS_X → motor → LS_Y.
- **OFF-time (1−D), synchronous:** LS_X (reverse conduction) → motor → LS_Y.

**Both intervals put exactly two channels' worth of R_DS(on) in series.** Conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2 \cdot I_{ph}^2 \cdot R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /\text{K}
$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**.
For the burst analysis, use an average T_j ≈ 100 °C, giving R ≈ **1.375 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Numbers

| Quantity | Equation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Channel conduction loss @ 100 °C | 2 × 80² × 1.375 mΩ | **17.6 W** |
| Channel conduction loss @ 125 °C (worst) | 2 × 80² × 1.5 mΩ | **19.2 W** |
| Instantaneous loss in one conducting FET | 80² × 1.375 mΩ | **8.8 W** |
| Average per FET (1/3 conduction share, 6 FETs) | 17.6 / 6 | **2.93 W** |
