### 0.2 Engineering Flags (read these first)

1. **The "80 A burst" definition must be pinned down.** It could mean phase current, per-channel battery current, or a marketing number. I model 80 A as phase current at near-full duty, with 4 channels loaded simultaneously. That is about 288 A and 7 kW from the pack. On a 25.5 mm board this is only survivable for roughly 1 to 2 seconds (derived in §3). The spec sheet must state burst duration and conditions.
2. **48 kHz is a trade.** It reduces motor current ripple and acoustic noise. However, it roughly doubles switching plus dead-time loss versus 24 kHz, which is about +2.6 W per channel at 80 A.
3. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** It generates DShot600 and decodes bidirectional eRPM telemetry. The ESC side runs its own firmware (AM32 recommended for 48 kHz plus bidirectional DShot support).

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current Paths in 6-Step Complementary PWM

At any commutation step, one phase is sourced (A), one is sunk (B), and one floats (C).

- **On-time (D·T):** current flows through Q_HA and Q_LB.
- **Off-time ((1−D)·T):** current flows through Q_LA (synchronous freewheel) and Q_LB.

Exactly two FETs are always in the current path. With a low-ripple phase current I_ph, the per-channel conduction loss is therefore:
