## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

### 0.1 The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs

Four independent 48 kHz six-step or FOC loops with sub-µs BEMF zero-cross timing are not run on the flight controller MCU in any production design, and ArduPilot does not support it. The partitioning used here is:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 480 MHz | ArduPilot, sensors, DShot600 bidirectional master |
| ESC MCU ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32) or STM32G071 | AM32 firmware, 48 kHz complementary PWM, BEMF, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, internal bootstrap diodes) | Drives HS and LS gates |
| MOSFETs ×24 | 40 V N-ch, PDFN 5×6 (parameters below) | 6 per motor |
