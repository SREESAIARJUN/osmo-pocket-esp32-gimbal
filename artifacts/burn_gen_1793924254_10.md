## 0. System Interpretation and Baseline Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. It does not drive the bridges. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (AM32 class, e.g. AT32F421 or STM32G071) running 48 kHz PWM. The H743 talks to those MCUs over bidirectional DShot600.
