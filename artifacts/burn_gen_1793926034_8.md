# STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): 80A Burst Thermal, 48 kHz Switching, and ArduPilot BDShot600 Design

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

This is a reference design. No specific MOSFET or ESC firmware was specified, so I picked representative parts. Every number traces back to the parameters in the table below. Swap in your datasheet values and the equations still hold.

**Architecture note.** The H743 does **not** generate the 48 kHz PWM. Each ESC channel, or one shared 4-channel ESC MCU, runs AM32 or BLHeli_32 on its own MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class). The H743 only sends DShot600 frames and receives eRPM telemetry. The two halves of this document are coupled only by the DShot link and shared thermal mass.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × 1.3375 = **1.605 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Per-FET duty in 6-step with complementary PWM

Each phase spends 120° electrical driven "high," 120° driven "low," and 120° floating.

| FET | Conducts during | Fraction of electrical period |
|---|---|---|
| High side (HS) | HS-active window, PWM on | D/3 |
| Low side (LS), same leg | HS-active window, PWM off (synchronous freewheel) | (1−D)/3 |
| Low side (LS) | LS-active window (100 % on) | 1/3 |

This gives:

$$P_{HS} = \frac{D}{3}I^2R_{DS},\qquad P_{LS} = \frac{2-D}{3}I^2R_{DS}$$

$$P_{leg} = \frac{2}{3}I^2R_{DS},\qquad P_{cond,ch} = 3P_{leg} = 2I^2R_{DS}$$

The per-channel result is duty-independent: exactly two FETs carry I at every instant.
