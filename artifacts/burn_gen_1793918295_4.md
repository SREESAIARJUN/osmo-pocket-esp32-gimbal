## 0. Architecture Clarification and Baseline Assumptions

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. Each ESC channel uses a dedicated low-cost MCU (AT32F421 running AM32) that commutates the motor and returns eRPM telemetry over bidirectional DShot. The H743 does not commutate motors directly.

## 1. Electrical Loss Model (Per ESC Channel)

### 1.1 Operating Mode

The model assumes 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM.

At any instant:
- One half-bridge is PWM-switching. Its high-side (HS) FET conducts for duty D, and its low-side (LS) FET conducts for 1−D.
- One LS FET in a second leg is held fully on.

So the current I always flows through exactly **two** FET channels in series.

### 1.2 Conduction Loss

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha_T (T_j - 25)] \approx 0.70 \times 1.5 = 1.05\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

This result is independent of D, because synchronous rectification removes the diode term.

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.05\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{13.44\ W}$$

**PCB copper loss.** Assume a 12 mm phase/bus path, 6 mm wide, 4 layers paralleled at 70 µm, with ρ_Cu(100 °C) = 2.2×10⁻⁸ Ω·m:

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho L}{w\,t\,n} = \frac{2.2\times10^{-8}\times 0.012}{0.006\times70\times10^{-6}\times4} \approx 0.16\ \text{m}\Omega$$

Taking two such paths in series:

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.31\times10^{-3} \approx \mathbf{2.0\ W}$$

### 1.3 Switching Loss (Hard-Switched Leg Only)

**Overlap (V–I crossover):**

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 35\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.69\ W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction (two edges per cycle):**

$$P_{dt} = V_F I \,(2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery:**

$$P_{rr} = V_{bus} Q_{rr} f_{sw} = 25.2 \times 50\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.060\ W}$$

**Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss} = V_{bus} Q_{oss} f_{sw} = 25.2 \times 40\text{n}\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

**Gate drive (two FETs switching per cycle).** This power is dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors:

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2\times 60\text{n}\times10\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.058\ W}$$

### 1.4 Per-Channel and Board Totals

| Loss term | W per channel | W for 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 13.44 | 53.8 |
| Copper | 2.00 | 8.0 |
| Overlap switching | 1.69 | 6.8 |
| Dead time | 0.61 | 2.4 |
| Q_rr + Q_oss + gate | 0.17 | 0.7 |
| Current shunt (§3) | — | ~2.6 (at 4×80 A, 0.1 mΩ… see §3) |
| **Total** | **≈ 17.9** | **≈ 74 W** |

**Efficiency at burst:**

$$P_{out} \approx 4 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times D_{eff}$$

At full throttle (D ≈ 0.95), input power is about 7.7 kW, giving efficiency ≈ 99.0 %. Efficiency is not the problem. **Heat density is:** 74 W in 13 cm² of board.
