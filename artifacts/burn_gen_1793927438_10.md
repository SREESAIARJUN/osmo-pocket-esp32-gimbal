## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs. The partitioning is:

| Domain | Device | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz, ArduPilot) | Attitude control. Generates **bidirectional DShot600** to four ESC MCUs. |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421 / STM32G071 class (AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware) | Commutation and **48 kHz** PWM generation. Runs the gate drivers. |
| Power stage ×4 | 3-phase gate driver + 6 N-MOSFETs per motor | Bridge switching |
