# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #23): System Architecture, Loss Model, and DShot Allocation

## 0. Architecture Assumptions

**The STM32H743 should not commutate the motors directly.** Doing so would require 12 complementary PWM outputs with dead-time, 4 sets of BEMF comparators and 4 commutation ISRs, all running alongside the EKF and the fast IMU loop. No mainstream ArduPilot build supports that, and it would throw away bidirectional DShot telemetry.

The defensible architecture is:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot. Emits 4× bidirectional DShot600. |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 | AM32 (or BLHeli_32). 48 kHz complementary PWM, BEMF sensing, eRPM telemetry. |
| Gate drivers (×4) | FD6288Q-class 3-phase half-bridge driver | Bootstrap high-side drive, roughly 1.2/1.5 A source/sink. |
| Power FETs (×24) | 40 V, ~1.2 mΩ @25 °C, PDFN 5×6 / 3.3×3.3 clip-bond | Six per channel. |

**Operating assumptions:**
- 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V.
- "80 A burst" means the per-channel phase current for ≤10 s.
- Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification.

**These are representative design values.** Every device parameter below must be replaced with the selected part's datasheet numbers, especially R_DS(on)(T), Q_gd, Q_rr and Z_th(t).

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path

In six-step commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM, exactly one high-side and one low-side FET carry the phase current at every instant, across both PWM sub-intervals. The conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle, to first order:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

The battery current is different: $I_{bat} = D \cdot I_{ph}$. Do not size the FETs from the battery current.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.0055\text{–}0.007\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

### 1.3 Self-consistent junction temperature

Because R_DS(on) rises with temperature, P and T_j must be solved together:

$$T_j = T_{ref} + P_{FET}(T_j)\, Z_{th}(t)$$

Substituting $P_{FET} = I^2 R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$ and solving gives:

$$T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + I^2 R_{25} Z_{th}(1 - 25\alpha)}{1 - I^2 R_{25}\, \alpha\, Z_{th}}$$

**Stability criterion:** a solution exists only if $I^2 R_{25}\alpha Z_{th} < 1$. If it is violated, the device goes into thermal runaway.

### 1.4 Numbers (R₂₅ = 1.2 mΩ, α = 0.006 K⁻¹)

| T_j (°C) | R_DS(on) (mΩ) | P per FET @80 A (W) | P_cond per channel (W) | 4 channels (W) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | 1.20 | 7.68 | 15.4 | 61.4 |
| 100 | 1.74 | 11.1 | 22.3 | 89.1 |
| 125 | 1.92 | 12.3 | 24.6 | 98.3 |

**Stability check:** take a 10 s transient Z_th of about 2 K/W from junction to the board hotspot. Then 6400 × 1.2e-3 × 0.006 × 2 = 0.092, which is well below 1. The model is stable, and T_j sits about 25 K above the local copper temperature.

### 1.5 PCB copper loss (usually forgotten)

$$R_{trace} = \frac{\rho\,\ell}{w\,t}, \qquad R_\square(\text{2 oz}, 25\,°C) \approx 0.245\ \text{m}\Omega/\square$$

Example phase path: 6 squares on 4 paralleled 2 oz layers gives about 0.37 mΩ. At 80 A that is about 2.4 W per phase leg, and the battery bus path adds a similar amount. **Budget about 3 W per channel of copper loss at 80 A.**

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz)

In complementary PWM, one FET per channel hard-switches. Its partner commutates softly through its body diode during dead-time.

### 2.1 V–I overlap loss

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

The transition time is set by the Miller plateau:

$$t_{r,f} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{I_g}, \qquad I_g = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{plateau}}{R_g}$$

With Q_gd = 10 nC and I_g ≈ 0.7 A, t ≈ 15 ns.

$$P_{VI} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

With Q_rr ≈ 30 nC:

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.036\ W}$$

The recovery current also adds turn-on current overshoot. That overshoot scales with Q_rr/t_r and is a ringing and EMI driver.

### 2.3 Output capacitance

With C_oss,eq ≈ 1.5 nF:

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss,eq} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = \mathbf{0.023\ W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

With V_f = 0.8 V and t_dt = 50 ns per edge:

$$P_{dt} = V_f I_{ph}(2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 100\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = \mathbf{0.31\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive (2 FETs switching per channel)

With Q_g = 50 nC and V_drv = 10 V:

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

Most of this is dissipated in R_g and in the driver, not in the FET.

### 2.6
