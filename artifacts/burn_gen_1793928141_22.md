**Engineering warning up front.** On a 25.5 mm board, "80 A" is a *burst rating limited by thermal capacitance*, not a continuous rating. The model in §3 quantifies exactly how long the burst can last. Do not put "80 A continuous" on a datasheet for this design.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.007\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

| T_j | Multiplier | R_DS(on) |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 | 1.00 mΩ |
| 100 °C | 1.53 | 1.53 mΩ |
| 125 °C | 1.70 | 1.70 mΩ ← design value |

### 1.2 Six-step synchronous conduction paths

During any commutation step, two phases carry current:

- **Leg A (PWM leg).** The high side conducts for D·T_sw. The synchronous low side conducts for (1 − D)·T_sw, excluding dead time.
- **Leg B (return leg).** The low side is held fully on.

Current therefore always flows through exactly two R_DS(on) in series:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot R_{DS}(T_j)\,[\,D + (1-D)\,] + I_{rms}^2 \cdot R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.3 Ripple correction

The motor inductance is about 15 µH (phase-to-phase, typical 1404–2207 class). At D = 0.5 and V = 25.2 V:

$$\Delta I = \frac{V\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{15\times10^{-6}\times 48\times10^{3}} = 8.75\ \text{A}_{pp}$$

$$I_{rms} = I\sqrt{1+\frac{(\Delta I/I)^2}{12}} = 80\sqrt{1 + \frac{0.01196}{12}} = 80.04\ \text{A}$$

The ripple contribution is negligible at 80 A, so use I = 80 A.

### 1.4 Numbers per channel (T_j = 125 °C)

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.70\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{21.8\ W}$$

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle:

- Average conduction loss per FET ≈ 21.8 / 6 = **3.6 W**.
- Instantaneous loss in the active FET is up to 10.9 W.

### 1.5 PCB copper conduction (often forgotten)

The phase pour is 2 oz × 4 layers paralleled, about 8 mm long and 3 mm wide.

$$R = \frac{\rho L}{A} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\times 8\times10^{-3}}{3\times10^{-3}\times 280\times10^{-6}} \approx 0.16\ \text{m}\Omega \;(\times1.4\text{ hot}) \approx 0.23\ \text{m}\Omega$$

Two phase paths plus a share of the battery bus (about 0.15 mΩ) give:

$$P_{Cu,ch} \approx 80^2 \times (2 \times 0.23 + 0.15)\times10^{-3} \approx \mathbf{3.9\ W}$$
