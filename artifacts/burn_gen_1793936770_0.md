| FC MCU | – | STM32H743 at 480 MHz |

**Architectural note.** The H743 does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own MCU, and the H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to those MCUs. A single-MCU design where the H743 drives 24 gates directly is possible in principle but unsupported by ArduPilot. I don't recommend it.

**Headline result.** 80 A per channel on a 30.5 mm board is a short burst rating only, roughly 2–3 s with all four motors loaded simultaneously. The board can sustain about 30 A per channel continuously in prop-wash cooling. Section 3 shows the derivation. The firmware must enforce a burst limit; don't market 80 A as a continuous rating.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

R_DS(T_j) = R_25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)], with α ≈ 0.0055 K⁻¹ for 40 V trench FETs.

At a design junction temperature of 115 °C, R_DS = 1.2 mΩ × 1.495 ≈ **1.8 mΩ**.
