## 0. Assumptions and Loop Timing

- Current loop: ω_c ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz
- Velocity loop: ω_v ≈ 2π·80 Hz

## 1. FOC Current Loop (Innermost, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle: θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset), wrapped to [0, 2π).

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

Inverse Park transform:

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI Design by Pole-Zero Cancellation

The motor in the dq frame:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

Choose PI gains so the controller zero cancels the electrical pole (R/L):

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \frac{I(s)}{I^*(s)} = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}
$$

Decoupling feedforward:

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e(L i_d + \lambda_m)
$$

### 1.3 Voltage Limit and Space-Vector Modulation

The maximum linear-region voltage magnitude is V_max = V_bus/√3. Saturation prioritizes the d-axis:

$$
v_d \leftarrow \text{clamp}(v_d, \pm V_{max}),\qquad |v_q| \le \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}
$$

SVPWM is implemented as min-max zero-sequence injection, which is mathematically equivalent to 7-segment SVPWM:

$$
v_{abc} = \text{invClarke}(v_\alpha, v_\beta),\quad
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max v_{abc} + \min v_{abc}\big),\quad
d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

## 2. Cascaded Angle → Velocity → Current Control

### 2.1 Mechanical Plant per Axis

$$
J\ddot\theta = K_t i_q - b\dot\theta - \tau_{cog}(\theta) - \tau_{dist},\qquad K_t = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m
$$

### 2.2 Velocity Loop (4 kHz): PI with Feedforward

The velocity feedback ω̂ is the joint-axis rate taken from the IMU gyros (Section 4.6), because that is the quantity we want to stabilize. It is blended with the encoder rate at low frequency (Section 2.5).

$$
i_q^* = \underbrace{K_{pv} e_\omega + K_{iv}\!\int\! e_\omega\,dt}_{\text{PI}} + \underbrace{\frac{J\,\dot\omega^* + b\,\omega^*}{K_t}}_{\text{model FF}} + \underbrace{i_{cog}(\theta)}_{\text{Section 3}},\qquad e_\omega = \omega^* - \hat\omega
$$

For a velocity crossover ω_v (current loop treated as unity):

$$
K_{pv} = \frac{J\,\omega_v}{K_t},\qquad K_{iv} = \frac{K_{pv}\,\omega_v}{4}\quad(\text{PI zero one octave-plus below crossover, about }60^\circ\text{ phase margin})
$$

### 2.3 Angle Loop (1 kHz): P with Optional PI and Rate Feedforward

$$
\omega^* = K_{p\theta}\,\text{wrap}(\theta^* - \hat\theta) + K_{i\theta}\!\int\! e_\theta\,dt + \dot\theta^*_{ff},\qquad K_{p\theta} = \omega_\theta
$$

### 2.4 Discretization and Anti-Windup

Integrators use the Tustin (bilinear) rule:

$$
I_k = I_{k-1} + K_i\tfrac{T_s}{2}(e_k + e_{k-1}) + K_{aw}(u_{sat,k-1} - u_{k-1})
$$
