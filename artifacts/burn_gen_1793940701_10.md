# STM32H743 Flight Controller + 80 A-Burst 4-in-1 ESC (Job #11): Design Basis, Loss Model, DShot DMA Allocation, Netlists

## 0. Feasibility Review

These issues change the architecture, so they come first.

| Issue | Finding | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| **H743 as ESC controller** | 4-in-1 ESCs use one small MCU per motor (AT32F421 / STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot to those MCUs. It does not drive gates. | H743 = flight controller. 4 × ESC MCU on the power board. |
| **80 A burst on a nano (20×20 mm) board** | Not achievable. 24 MOSFETs in 3.3×3.3 mm packages need about 260 mm² of a 400 mm² face, before the MCU, drivers and capacitors. The board's thermal mass gives under 1 s of 80 A burst (§2.6). | Power stage moves to a **30.5×30.5 mm** stack. The "nano" FC can stay 20×20 (H743 in TFBGA100) on top. |
| **Device parameters** | No MOSFET part number was given. | Parametric model with a representative 40 V, 0.8 mΩ PQFN 3.3×3.3 FET. **Re-run with datasheet values before release.** |
| **Battery** | Not specified. | 6S, V_bus = 25.2 V (full charge, worst case for switching loss). |

All numbers below are engineering estimates. Validate them with thermal-camera and thermocouple tests on first articles.

## 2. Thermal and Loss Model (per ESC channel, 80 A burst)

### 2.1 Assumed parameters

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S full |
| I_ph | 80 A | Burst phase current (flat-top, 6-step) |
| f_sw | 48 kHz | PWM frequency |
| R_ds,25 | 0.80 mΩ | Per FET at V_gs = 10 V |
| α | 0.0045 /K | R_ds temperature coefficient |
| t_r, t_f | 20 ns each | At driver I_g ≈ 1.5 A |
| t_dt | 100 ns | Dead time per edge |
| V_SD | 0.8 V | Body-diode drop at 80 A |
| Q_rr | 50 nC | Reverse-recovery charge |
| C_oss | 1.5 nF | Effective output capacitance |
| Q_g | 60 nC | Total gate charge |
| R_cu | 0.25 mΩ | Copper path per phase |
| R_θJC | 1.5 K/W | Junction to board |

### 2.2 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 0.80 × 1.383 = **1.106 mΩ**.

### 2.3 Conduction loss

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, current always flows through two legs:

- The PWM leg: high-side on for D, low-side on for 1−D.
- The return leg: low-side fully on.

$$P_{cond}=I^2\big[R_{ds}D+R_{ds}(1-D)\big]+I^2R_{ds}+I^2\cdot 2R_{cu}=I^2(2R_{ds}+2R_{cu})$$

At 80 A:

$$P_{cond}=6400\,(2.212+0.5)\times10^{-3}= \mathbf{17.36\ W}$$

- FETs: 14.16 W
- Copper: 3.20 W

### 2.4 Switching loss at 48 kHz

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f_sw = ½·25.2·80·40 ns·48 k | **1.935 W** |
| Dead-time diode | V_SD·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·200 ns·48 k | **0.614 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 0.060 W |
| C_oss | ½·C_oss·V²·f_sw | 0.023 W |
| Gate drive (2 FETs) | 2·Q_g·V_gs·f_sw | 0.058 W (dissipated in driver) |
| **P_sw total** | | **2.69 W** |

Switching loss scales linearly with f_sw. Dropping to 24 kHz saves about 1.3 W per channel at a cost in current ripple and acoustics.

### 2.5 Totals

| | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| FET (conduction + switching) | 16.85 W | **67.4 W** |
| Copper | 3.20 W | 12.8 W |
| **Total heat into board** | **20.05 W** | **≈ 80 W** |

- Average: ≈ 16.85 / 6 ≈ 2.8 W.
