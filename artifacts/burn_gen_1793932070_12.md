# STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): 80A Burst Thermal, 48 kHz Switching, and ArduPilot BDShot600 Design

## 0. Assumptions

**The STM32H743 cannot be the ESC controller under ArduPilot.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 as the flight controller. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The architecture below assumes:

```
H743 (ArduPilot) ──BDShot600 x4──► 4x AT32F421 (AM32) ──► 4x 3-phase gate drivers ──► 24 MOSFETs
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology

In 6-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification (AM32 default), current at any instant flows through:

- **During the ON phase (D):** high-side FET of phase A, then low-side FET of phase B.
- **During the OFF phase (1−D):** low-side FET of phase A (sync rect), then low-side FET of phase B.

So **exactly two FET channels carry I at all times, independent of duty**:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature-coupled solution

R_DS depends on T_j, and T_j depends on P. Iterate:

$$
T_j = T_{pcb} + P_{FET}\,R_{\theta,j\text{-}pcb},\qquad R_{DS}=R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]
$$

At I = 80 A, with T_j converging near 110 °C:

$$
R_{DS}= 1.0(1+0.0045\cdot 85)=1.38\text{ mΩ}
$$
$$
P_{cond,motor}=2\cdot 6400\cdot 1.38\times10^{-3}= \mathbf{17.7\ W}
$$

**Per-FET distribution.** Each low-side FET is fully on for 2 of 6 sectors as the return path, plus (1−D) for 2 sectors as the sync rectifier. Each high-side FET conducts D for 2 sectors.

- At full throttle (D → 1), losses sit mostly in the HS FETs plus the return LS FETs. Instantaneous FET loss during conduction is $I^2R = 8.8$ W.
- The average per FET is ≈ 17.7/6 ≈ **2.95 W**. The worst FET is ≈ 1/3 duty × 8.8 ≈ **2.95–3.5 W**.

### 1.3 Additional conduction paths (often neglected, significant at 80 A)

| Path | R | P @ 80 A (per motor) |
|---|---|---|
| Phase trace and pad (2 oz, 3 mm × 8 mm, ×2 layers) | ~0.4 mΩ | 2.6 W (×2 phases active) ≈ 5.1 W |
| Current-sense shunt (shared, 0.25 mΩ, total ≈ 4×80 A only if all burst) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 kW?? → **do not shunt the full-bus 320 A on-board**; use per-ESC or Hall sensor |
| Battery pads to bus (total 320 A) | ~0.15 mΩ | 15.4 W total |

**Design rule:** at 320 A aggregate, use a Hall-effect sensor (e.g. an ACS7xx-class, ~0.1 mΩ) or per-channel 0.2 mΩ shunts at 80 A each (1.3 W each). Do not use a single bus shunt.

## 2. Switching Loss Model @ 48 kHz

Per motor, one phase leg is hard-switched per PWM cycle (the sync-rect FET soft-commutates through its body diode).

**Overlap (V-I crossover):**
$$
P_{VI}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw}=\tfrac12(25.2)(80)(20\text{ns})(48\text{k}) = 0.97\ \text{W}
$$

**Reverse recovery** of the sync-rect body diode:
$$
P_{rr}=Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw}=40\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k}=0.048\ \text{W}
$$

**Output capacitance:**
$$
P_{oss}=\tfrac12 Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw}\times 2=30\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k}=0.036\ \text{W}
$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (two edges per cycle). This term is the dominant hidden loss:
$$
P_{dt}=V_f\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw}=0.8\cdot80\cdot400\text{n}\cdot48\text{k}=1.23\ \text{W}
$$

**Gate drive** (dissipated in the driver and gate resistors, not the FETs):
$$
P_g=Q_gV_{drv}f_{sw}\times2=40\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k}\cdot2=0.038\ \text{W}
$$

| Loss term | Per motor | ×4 |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FET) | 17.7 W | 70.8 W |
| Copper (phase) | 5.1 W | 20.4 W |
| V-I overlap | 0.97 W | 3.9 W |
| Dead time | 1.23 W | 4.9 W |
| Q_rr + Q_oss + gate | 0.12 W | 0.5 W |
| Shunt (0.2 mΩ per channel) | 1.3 W | 5.1 W |
| Battery pad / bus | — | 15.4 W |
| **Total** | **~26.4 W** | **~121 W** |

**Key insight:** at 48 kHz, switching losses (~2.3 W per motor) are about 10% of conduction losses. Raising f_sw to 96 kHz would add only ~2.3 W per motor. The thermal limit is set entirely by I²R. Spend your BOM budget on R_DS(on) and copper, not on faster switching. Cut dead time to 100 ns if the driver and FET allow it; this saves ~0.6 W per motor.

### 2.1 Input capacitor ripple

$$
I_{C,rms}\approx I\sqrt{D(1-D)}\big|_{D=0.5}=40\ \text{A per motor}
$$

With no guaranteed interleaving across the 4 ESCs, the worst case is ~80 A_rms aggregate.

- **Ceramics:** 4 × (4 × 10 µF 50 V X7R 1206) per channel.
- **Bulk:** 1000 µF / 35 V low-ESR external electrolytic, mandatory on the battery leads.
- **Check:** ceramic self-heating stays negligible at ESR ≈ 3 mΩ per bank.
