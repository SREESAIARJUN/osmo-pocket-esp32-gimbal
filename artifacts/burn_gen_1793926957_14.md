## 0. Executive Finding

The model shows that **80 A per channel on a nano-class (≤30.5 mm) board is a transient rating only.** Total power-stage dissipation at 4 × 80 A is about **105 W**. Without a heatsink, the board's thermal mass gives roughly **3 s** before copper and solder temperature limits are reached (§4).

Recommended actions:
- Specify the burst as **"80 A / ≤ 2 s, 10 % duty"**.
- Specify the continuous rating around **40–45 A**.
- Alternatively, move to a 30.5 mm board with a bonded aluminum spreader.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Commutation topology

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In each 60° sector:
- One phase leg is PWM'd. Its high-side (HS) FET conducts for duty D, and its low-side (LS) FET synchronously rectifies for (1 − D).
- The return leg's LS FET conducts for 100 % of the sector.

The conducting resistance per sector is therefore:

$$
R_{path} = R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D)] + R_{DS} = 2R_{DS}
$$

**Conduction loss is duty-independent** with synchronous rectification. This is why burst current, not throttle position, drives heating.

### 2.2 RMS current with ripple

For triangular ripple:

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_M^2\left(1 + \frac{r^2}{12}\right) = 6400 \times 1.0075 = 6448\ \text{A}^2
$$

### 2.3 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on), solved self-consistently

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha(T_j - 25)]
