# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #67): Design Model

**Scope:** Electro-thermal model of the power stage, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA plan, representative netlists, and thermal budget.

**How to read this:** All component values are representative assumptions, stated explicitly so you can substitute datasheet numbers from your chosen parts. Treat this as a design model to verify on the bench, not a signed-off schematic.

---

## 0. Feasibility Finding (Read First)

An **80 A per-motor burst on a nano (20×20 mm) form factor is not physically credible.**

- The 3.3×3.3 mm MOSFETs that fit on a 20×20 board run 2–3 mΩ.
- A 20×20 board has roughly 45% of the copper and thermal mass of a 30.5×30.5 board.
- The model below shows that even 30.5×30.5 boards with 5×6 mm FETs only survive 80 A for about 2–4 s.

**Recommendation:**
- Build the ESC on **30.5×30.5 mm** (the FC can stay nano-stacked).
- Or re-rate the nano version to about **35 A continuous / 45 A burst**.

All numbers below assume the 30.5×30.5 board with 5×6 mm FETs.
