## 1. Electrical Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation Topology

AM32 uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In every 60° electrical sector:

- One phase is high-side PWM with duty D. Its low-side FET acts as the synchronous rectifier during (1 − D).
- One phase has its low side held ON for 100% of the sector.
- One phase floats and is used for BEMF zero-crossing detection.

As a result, the motor current always flows through **exactly two FET channels in series**, regardless of D. Phase current is approximately constant within a sector (assuming high L/R), so I_rms ≈ I_ph.

### 1.2 Conduction Loss with Thermal Feedback

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)]$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2\,R_{trace}$$

At T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS}(110) = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega\,[1 + 0.0045 \times 85] = 1.38\,\text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,FET} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.38\times10^{-3} = 17.7\ \text{W per motor}$$
