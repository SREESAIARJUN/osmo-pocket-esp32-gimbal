# 3-Axis Gimbal: FOC Control Loop, Anti-Cogging Feedforward, and Dual-IMU Vibration Rejection

## 0. Assumptions

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|

## 1. FOC Inner Loop (Current / Torque)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle: θ_e = p · (θ_m − θ_offset).

**Clarke (amplitude-invariant):**

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}\qquad (i_a+i_b+i_c=0)
$$

**Park:**

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant and decoupling

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

The torque constant is K_t = (3/2) p λ_m. The controller adds a decoupling feedforward to the PI output:

$$
v_d^* = PI_d(e_d) - \hat\omega_e \hat L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = PI_q(e_q) + \hat\omega_e \hat L i_d + \hat\omega_e\hat\lambda_m
$$

### 1.3 Current PI tuning (pole-zero cancellation)

The plant is 1/(Ls+R). Choosing the controller zero to cancel the plant pole gives a closed loop of ω_c/(s+ω_c):

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10\text{–}20}
$$

At 20 kHz PWM, use ω_c ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz.

> **Note for high-R gimbal motors.** If R ≫ ω_c L, voltage-mode control (v_q = R·i_q* + ω_e λ_m) is acceptable. The code supports both modes.

### 1.4 SVPWM by min-max zero-sequence injection

This is mathematically equivalent to 7-segment SVPWM:

$$
v_a = v_\alpha,\quad v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc})+\min(v_{abc})\big),\qquad
d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

The linear range is |v_αβ| ≤ V_bus/√3. Voltage-vector saturation uses circular limiting, with q-axis priority:

$$
v_q \le \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}
$$

## 2. Velocity Estimation (Encoder PLL)

Differentiating the encoder signal directly amplifies quantization noise:

$$
\Delta\theta_q = 2\pi/2^{14} \;\Rightarrow\; \text{at 4 kHz: } 1.53\ \text{rad/s per LSB}
$$

A type-2 tracking PLL avoids this. Let e = wrap(θ_meas − θ̂). Then:

$$
\dot{\hat\omega} = K_i\, e,\qquad \dot{\hat\theta} = \hat\omega + K_p\, e,\qquad K_p = 2\zeta\omega_n,\; K_i=\omega_n^2
$$

Use ω_n ≈ 2π·150 Hz and ζ = 0.707–1. The PLL output θ̂ also serves as an interpolated angle for θ_e.
