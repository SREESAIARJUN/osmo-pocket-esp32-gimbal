# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: System Architecture Package (Job #1)

## 0. Architectural Correction and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In every production 4-in-1 design, the H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot. The ESC section has its own MCU(s), typically AM32 on STM32G071 or AT32F421, or BLHeli_32. Those MCUs generate the 48 kHz PWM and perform commutation. This model treats the system that way.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

In 6-step commutation, two legs conduct at any moment. In the PWM leg, the high side (HS) is on for duty D and the synchronous low side (LS) for 1−D. Either way, current flows through exactly one FET. In the return leg, the LS conducts continuously. Therefore:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I^2\,[D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)R_{LS}] + I^2 R_{LS} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] \approx 1.1\text{ mΩ}\times 1.6 = 1.76\text{ mΩ}$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2(80)^2(1.76\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{22.5\ W}$$

**Per-FET average.** Each FET conducts about 1/3 of an electrical cycle, so 22.5 W / 6 ≈ **3.75 W average**. The instantaneous dissipation while conducting is I²R = 11.3 W.

**PCB copper loss.** Model it as R_Cu = ρL/(wt). For a 2 oz pour that is 8 mm wide and 15 mm long per phase path:

- R_Cu ≈ 1.72e-8 × 0.015 / (0.008 × 70e-6) ≈ 0.46 mΩ per layer.
- Paralleled across 4 power layers, that gives ≈ 0.12 mΩ.
- Across 2 paths: 2 × 80² × 0.12e-3 ≈ **1.5 W per motor**.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

| Term | Equation | Value per motor |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = 0.5·25.2·80·20e-9·48e3 | 0.97 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 30e-9·25.2·48e3 | 0.036 W |
| Coss | ½·Q_oss·V·f_sw | 0.015 W |
| Dead-time diode | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·200e-9·48e3 | 0.61 W |
| Gate charge | Q_g·V_GS·f_sw × 2 switching FETs (mostly in driver) | 0.048 W |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 1.64 W** |

**Design lever.** Dead-time diode loss is 37% of switching loss. Cutting dead time to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W per motor.

**Total burst loss per motor:**

$$P_{motor} = 22.5 + 1.64 + 1.5 \approx \mathbf{25.6\ W}$$

With all four motors at 80 A: **P_total ≈ 102 W**.

**Efficiency check.** Output power is about 22.2 V × 80 A × 4 ≈ 7.1 kW, so ESC efficiency ≈ 98.6%. That is plausible, but the absolute heat is the problem.

### 3.2 Junction-to-board rise

Use R_th(j-board) ≈ 8 K/W, which is realistic for a DFN3.3 on thermal vias. It includes spreading resistance.

ΔT_j-b = 3.75 W × 8 ≈ 30 K, plus about 6 K of per-commutation ripple, so **≈ 36 K total**.

### 3.3 Convection

With prop-wash forced air, h ≈ 100 W/m²K and A ≈ 2 × 930 mm², giving G_conv ≈ 0.19 W/K. Motor leads and battery leads add roughly 0.3 W/K. Total **G ≈ 0.5 W/K**, so τ = C/G ≈ 8.4 s.
