## 0. Design Assumptions

The numbers below are only as good as these assumptions. Substitute datasheet values for your chosen parts. Every equation is written so you can do that.

### 0.1 Architecture

- **FC MCU:** STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), running ArduPilot.
  - SYSCLK 400 MHz.
  - APB1/APB2 = 100 MHz, so timer kernel clock f_TIM = 200 MHz.
- **ESC:** four independent ESC MCUs (AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. These must support bidirectional DShot and EDT.
