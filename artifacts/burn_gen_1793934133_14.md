## 1. Field-Oriented Control (inner current loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle from the mechanical encoder angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{m,0}) \pmod{2\pi}$$

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.

### 1.2 Plant and current PI design

The PMSM model in the dq frame is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

Apply cross-coupling decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$v_d^* = u_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^* = u_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

Each axis then reduces to $G(s) = 1/(Ls+R)$.

Choose the PI as $C(s) = K_p + K_i/s$ with the zero cancelling the electrical pole:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c \;\Rightarrow\; T(s) = \frac{\omega_c}{s+\omega_c}$$

Guidelines for the crossover ω_c:
- Choose ω_c ≤ 2π·f_PWM/20 so the 1.5-sample delay (ZOH plus computation) costs less than about 15° of phase.
- At 20 kHz this gives ω_c ≈ 2π·1 kHz.
- With the defaults: K_p = 11.9 V/A and K_i = 35 186 V/(A·s).

Discrete form (Tustin integrator, T_s = 1/f_PWM):

$$u[k] = K_p e[k] + I[k],\quad I[k+1] = I[k] + K_i T_s \tfrac{e[k]+e[k-1]}{2} + K_{aw}(u_{sat}[k]-u[k])$$

### 1.3 Voltage limit and SVPWM

Circular limiting preserves vector direction while prioritizing q:

$$|v| \le \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}$$

$$v_d \leftarrow \text{clamp}(v_d, \pm V_{max}), \qquad v_q \leftarrow \text{clamp}\!\left(v_q, \pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}\right)$$

SVPWM is computed by min-max zero-sequence injection, which is mathematically equivalent to 7-segment SVPWM:

$$v_{x}' = v_x - \tfrac12(\max_x v_x + \min_x v_x),\qquad d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x'}{V_{bus}}$$

**Voltage-mode fallback.** Many gimbal boards have no phase shunts. In that case set $v_q = R\,i_q^* + \omega_e\lambda_m$ and $v_d = -\omega_e L i_q^*$ (open-loop current). The outer loops are unchanged.
