**Design Package: Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, BDShot600 Timer/DMA Allocation, Netlists**

## 0. Architecture Corrections and Feasibility

Three issues change how the rest of this package should be read.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.**
In an ArduPilot stack, the STM32H743 is the flight controller. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU, which runs the commutation, the 48 kHz PWM and the eRPM telemetry encoder.
- Typical ESC MCUs: AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or EFM8 running Bluejay.
- 48 kHz is an ESC-firmware setting (AM32 and Bluejay both offer it). It is not an H743 timer.
- The H743's job is to generate DShot600 frames and capture the GCR eRPM replies.

**2. "Nano" (20×20 mm) with 80 A burst per channel is not thermally viable.**
The model in §2 shows the board can absorb an 80 A ×4 burst for only about 1 s on 20×20 mm, and about 2–3 s on 30.5×30.5 mm, before FETs exceed safe junction temperature.
- I recommend specifying **80 A burst at ≤1 s and 45–55 A continuous on a 30.5×30.5 mm board**.
- Alternatively, de-rate a 20×20 board to about 40 A burst.

**3. All device parameters below are placeholders.**
They represent the 40 V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN FET class. Replace every value with numbers from the selected datasheet (R_DS(on) at T_j, Q_g, Q_rr, Q_oss, R_θJC) before sign-off.

## 1. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 1.1 Assumptions

| Symbol | Meaning | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | Pack voltage | 25.2 V (6S, full) |
| I_ph | Phase current (burst) | 80 A |
| f_sw | PWM frequency | 48 kHz |
| D | PWM duty at burst | ~0.95 |
| R_ds25 | R_DS(on) at 25 °C | 1.2 mΩ |
| k_T | R_DS(on) temperature coefficient at T_j = 125 °C | 1.6 |
| t_r, t_f | Voltage rise/fall times | 20 ns each |
| Q_rr | Body-diode reverse-recovery charge | 50 nC |
| Q_oss | Output charge | 60 nC |
| Q_g | Total gate charge | 50 nC at V_GS = 10 V |
| t_dt | Dead time | 100 ns |
| V_F | Body-diode forward voltage | 0.8 V |
| R_shunt | Current shunt | 0.25 mΩ |
| R_cu | Copper/via path resistance per channel | 0.4 mΩ |
| Topology | Commutation scheme | 6-step trapezoidal, complementary (synchronous) PWM on one leg, opposite leg low-side held ON |

### 1.2 Conduction Loss

At any instant during a 6-step sector, current passes through:
- the PWM leg, where the high side conducts for D and the low side (synchronous) for 1−D, and
- the return leg, where the low side is ON for 100 % of the sector.

The temperature-corrected resistance is:

$$R_{ds,hot}=k_T\,R_{ds25}=1.6\times1.2\,\text{m}\Omega=1.92\,\text{m}\Omega$$

The conduction loss is:

$$P_{cond}=I^2R_{ds,hot}\big[D+(1-D)\big]+I^2R_{ds,hot}=2I^2R_{ds,hot}$$

$$P_{cond}=2(80)^2(1.92\times10^{-3})=\mathbf{24.6\ W}$$

When the sector duty is distributed over the 6 FETs in one electrical revolution, each FET conducts for about 1/3 of the time. This gives an average of about 8.2 W per FET, with an instantaneous peak of 12.3 W in the conducting FETs.

### 1.3 Switching Loss (PWM leg only)

**V–I overlap:**

$$P_{VI}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw}=\tfrac12(25.2)(80)(40\text{n})(48\text{k})=\mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

**Reverse recovery.** With synchronous rectification, the body diode conducts only during dead time, but Q_rr still applies:

$$P_{rr}=Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw}=50\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k}=\mathbf{0.060\ W}$$

**Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss}=\tfrac12Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw}=\tfrac12(60\text{n})(25.2)(48\text{k})=\mathbf{0.036\ W}$$

**Dead-time body-diode conduction** (2 dead-time events per period):

$$P_{dt}=V_F I(2t_{dt})f_{sw}=0.8\cdot80\cdot200\text{n}\cdot48\text{k}=\mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**Gate drive.** This loss falls mostly in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET. Only the PWM leg's 2 FETs switch at f_sw:

$$P_g=2Q_gV_{GS}f_{sw}=2\cdot50\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k}=\mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

### 1.4 Passive Losses

$$P_{shunt}=I^2R_{shunt}=6400\cdot0.25\text{m}=\mathbf{1.6\ W}$$

$$P_{cu}=I^2R_{cu}=6400\cdot0.4\text{m}=\mathbf{2.56\ W}$$

Bus-capacitor ripple loss is roughly 0.3 W (with low-ESR polymer + MLCC).

### 1.5 Totals at 80 A Burst

| Component | Per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction | 24.6 W | 98.4 W |
| Switching (VI + rr + oss + dt) | 2.65 W | 10.6 W |
| Gate drive | 0.05 W | 0.2 W |
| Shunt + copper | 4.16 W | 16.6 W |
| Capacitors | 0.3 W | 1.2 W |
| **Total** | **≈31.8 W** | **≈127 W** |

Delivered electrical power is about 4 × 25.2 V × 80 A × D ≈ 7.7 kW, so stage efficiency is about 98.4 %. The losses look small in percentage terms but are very large in heat density for this board area.
