# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #29): Loss, Thermal and DShot Timing Model

## 0. Architectural Reality Check

Three issues need to be on the record before the derivation, because they change the design.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every viable 4-in-1 AIO, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to four (or one quad-channel) dedicated ESC MCUs. Typical choices are the AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or the EFM8BB51 running Bluejay. This model uses **4 × AT32F421K8U7 + AM32**, with one MCU per motor for fault isolation.
2. **"Nano" and 80 A burst conflict.** Section 3 shows that four motors at 80 A dissipate about 120 W. On a 20 × 20 mm board that is roughly 3 s of thermal headroom with no realistic continuous rating. The model below assumes a **30.5 × 30.5 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz board**, which is the smallest footprint where "80 A burst" is defensible. Treat 20 × 20 as a 35–45 A burst class.
3. **The numbers depend on component choices.** All figures are derived from stated, representative parameters. Replace them with datasheet values for the selected FET and driver, then validate with thermocouple and IR testing (Section 7). Do not treat any figure here as a certified rating.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Topology

AM32 drives each motor with trapezoidal 6-step commutation and complementary (synchronous) PWM. At any instant, current in a channel flows through exactly two FET channels:

- During the PWM on-time: the high-side FET of phase A and the low-side FET of phase B.
- During the PWM off-time: the low-side FET of phase A (synchronous rectification) and the low-side FET of phase B.

So the instantaneous conduction path is always two R_ds(on), **independent of duty cycle**:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Each of the 6 FETs carries an equal share over a full electrical revolution:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6} = \frac{I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)}{3}$$

### 2.2 Ripple Contribution

Peak-to-peak ripple current is

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{LL}\,f_{sw}} = \frac{22.2 \times 0.25}{20\times10^{-6}\times 48\times10^{3}} = 5.78\ \text{A}$
