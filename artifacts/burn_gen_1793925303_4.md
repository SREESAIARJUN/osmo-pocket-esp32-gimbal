## 0. Architectural Clarifications

These points constrain everything that follows.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In a 4-in-1 ESC each channel has its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. This design uses a representative AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 to each ESC MCU. Bi-directional DShot is the link between the two.
2. **80 A per channel on a nano board is a burst rating, not a continuous one.** Section 3 shows that a 20×20 mm-class board is limited by its heat capacity. The model below gives you the allowable burst duration, which is what the datasheet should state.
3. **Design point:**
