## 0. Ground Rules, Assumptions and Two Architecture Corrections

No component datasheets were provided. Every numeric result below comes from **declared representative parameters** (Table 0). The equations are the deliverable. Substitute your real BOM values before sign-off.

### Correction 1: The H743 cannot commutate the motors

ArduPilot does not run ESC commutation, and one H743 cannot also run four sensorless 48 kHz commutation loops alongside the flight stack. The correct partition is:

| Block | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100, 400 MHz, timer clocks 200 MHz) | ArduPilot |
| ESC ×4 | STM32G071 (or AT32F421), one per motor | AM32, 48 kHz complementary PWM |
| FC to ESC link | Bi-directional DShot600, one wire per motor | — |

### Correction 2: "80 A" is a burst rating only

At nano size (≈20×20 to 25.5×25.5 mm), 80 A per channel is thermally a **burst** rating, not a continuous one. Section 5 shows the defensible continuous figure is about **22–25 A per channel**, and the 80 A burst is time-limited to roughly **1–2 s** when all four channels are loaded. The product spec and marketing must reflect this.

## 1. Electrical Operating Model (6-Step, Synchronous/Complementary PWM)

In any 60° commutation sector:
- One leg is PWM-switched with complementary high-side/low-side (HS/LS) drive at duty D.
- One leg holds its LS FET fully on.
- One leg floats (back-EMF sensing).

The phase current flows through **two series FETs at all times**:
- During D: through the HS of the PWM leg.
- During (1−D): through the LS of the PWM leg.

Over one electrical cycle, each of the 6 FETs carries an equal share of the conduction energy. This symmetry requires that AM32 rotate which leg is PWM'd (it does).
