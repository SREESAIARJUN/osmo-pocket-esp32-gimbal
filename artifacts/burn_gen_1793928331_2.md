## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path
In any 60° commutation sector, one leg is PWM-switched (HS/LS complementary) and one leg is held on. With synchronous rectification, current always flows through **exactly two MOSFET channels** in series:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \left[ D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS} + R_{static} \right] \approx 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

This is independent of duty cycle when all devices are matched.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

### 1.3 Per-device loss
Each device conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle on average, but during a burst the hottest device is the one carrying current at that moment. Two figures matter:

- **Average per device:** $P_{avg,dev} = \frac{2 I^2 R_{ds}}{6} = \frac{I^2 R_{ds}}{3}$
- **Instantaneous on-state:** $P_{inst,dev} = I^2 R_{ds}$. This drives junction ripple when the electrical period is long, i.e., at low eRPM.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond/channel | P_inst/device |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 6.4 W |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ | 17.1 W | 8.6 W |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W | 9.3 W |

### 1.5 Electrothermal fixed point
Loss and temperature are coupled, so solve iteratively:

$$T_j^{(k+1)} = T_a + R_{th,ja}\cdot P\big(R_{ds}(T_j^{(k)})\big)$$

This converges only if

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,R_{th} = 2 I^2 R_{ds,25}\,\alpha\, R_{th,ch} < 1$$

At 80 A with a channel-level R_th of 5 K/W: 2·6400·0.001·0.0045·5 = 0.29 < 1, so the result is stable. **At 80 A continuous with poor cooling (R_th,ch ≳ 17 K/W), thermal runaway becomes possible.** This is the core reason 80 A must remain a time-limited burst rating.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches in each sector, giving one HS turn-on and one turn-off per PWM period per channel.

| Mechanism | Equation | Value per channel |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = 0.5·25.2·80·40 ns·48 k | **1.94 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 50 nC·25.2·48 k | 0.06 W |
| C_oss | ½·C_oss·V²·f_sw = 0.5·1.5 nF·635·48 k | 0.02 W |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_sd·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·200 ns·48 k | **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (2 active FETs, lost in driver) | Q_g·V_gs·f_sw·2 = 50 nC·10·48 k·2 | 0.05 W |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 2.68 W** |

**Design notes:**
- Switching loss scales linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz it is about 1.3 W. 48 kHz buys smoother low-throttle behavior and less audible noise at roughly 1.3 W/channel extra at burst.
- t_r and t_f are set by gate resistor and driver strength. Typical 3-phase ESC drivers (e.g., FD6288Q class, about 1.5 A/1.8 A) with R_g ≈ 4.7–10 Ω yield 15–40 ns. Faster edges reduce loss but worsen ringing and EMI on DShot lines.
- Ringing check: with switch-node loop inductance L_loop ≈ 3 nH and di/dt = 80 A/20 ns = 4 A/ns, overshoot ≈ L·di/dt = 12 V, so the peak reaches about 37 V against a 40 V rating. **This is marginal.** Minimize the loop with ceramics (≥ 4×1 µF X7R 50 V per channel) directly at the HS drain and LS source.

## 4. Thermal Model

### 4.1 Network (Cauer-style, per device to ambient)

```
Tj ──R_jc(0.8 K/W)── Tcase ──R_pad+vias(2 K/W)── T_board ──R_board-amb── Ta
 │                     │                           │
C_j(0.02 J/K)    C_pkg(0.15 J/K)          C_board (shared, ~6 J/K)
```

### 4.2 Board heat capacity (assumed stack)
- 6-layer, 2 oz outer / 2 oz inner copper, 30.5 × 30.5 mm: copper ≈ 3.5 g × 0.385 J/g·K ≈ 1.35 J/K
- FR4 ≈ 2.2 g × 1.1 ≈ 2.4 J/K
- 24 MOSFETs + capacitors + solder ≈ 2.2 J/K
- **C_board ≈ 6 J/K**

### 4.3 Adiabatic burst (no airflow, worst case)

$$\frac{dT_{board}}{dt} = \frac{P_{tot}}{C_{board}} = \frac{100}{6} \approx 16.6\ \text{K/s}$$

Starting from 40 °C board temperature and limiting the board to 110 °C (so T_j stays below about 135 °C):

$$t_{burst,max} \approx \frac{110-40}{16.6} \approx 4.2\ \text{s}$$

With prop-wash airflow (h ≈ 80 W/m²K over ≈ 2×9.3 cm²): R_board-amb ≈ 6.7 K/W, which removes only about 10 W at a 70 K rise. **The burst limit remains about 4–5 s for all four channels simultaneously, or about 15–20 s for one channel at 80 A.**

### 4.4 Junction excursion above board
Steady per-device average at 80 A: 8.6/3 + 2.7/2 ≈ 4.2 W, giving ΔT_j-board ≈ 4.2 × 2.8 ≈ 12 K.

At low eRPM the per-device on-time can reach several milliseconds, so use the instantaneous 8.6 W with Z_th(t): about 0.5 K/W at 5 ms, giving roughly 4 K ripple. This is negligible relative to the board rise.

**Result:** T_j ≈ T_board + 12–16 K. At the burst limit that is about 125 °C, which leaves 50 K margin to a 175 °C T_j,max for derating and part spread.

### 4.5 Recommended firmware protection
- Report ESC temperature through bidirectional DShot EDT (Extended DShot Telemetry).
- Use ArduPilot `BATT_*` current monitoring plus ESC-side current limiting (in AM32, `current limit` via the configurator).
- Throttle down linearly from 100 °C to 120 °C at the ESC NTC.
- Burst current I²t budget: I²·t ≤ 80²·4 s = 25,600 A²·s, referenced to a 40 °C start.
