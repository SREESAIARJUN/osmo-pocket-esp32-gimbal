## 0. Architectural Clarifications and Assumptions

Two points need to be settled first, because they change the math.

1. **The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1 stack, the H743 is the flight controller. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or EFM8/BB21 running Bluejay). The 48 kHz figure is the **ESC MCU's PWM frequency**. ArduPilot on the H743 only generates the DShot600 command stream and decodes the bidirectional eRPM telemetry. This document models both domains separately.

2. **An "80 A" rating on a nano (20×20 mm) board is a short-burst rating only.** It is not a continuous rating. Section 3 shows that the board can sustain 80 A for about 1–5 s, depending on how many motors burst at once. That is consistent with how the industry actually rates these boards.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current Path in 6-Step Commutation with Complementary PWM

In any commutation sector, phase A is PWM'd (high side HS_A plus synchronous low side LS_A), phase B is held low (LS_B on), and phase C floats.

- **PWM-on interval (D):** current flows HS_A → motor → LS_B.
- **PWM-off interval (1−D):** current freewheels through LS_A → motor → LS_B.

The motor current therefore always flows through exactly two MOSFETs, regardless of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS,25}\,k_T(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Copper Trace and Plane Loss

The current path covers the input planes, phase pours, and solder pads.

$$
R_{Cu} \approx \rho_{Cu}(T)\frac{\ell}{w\,t\,n_{layers}}
$$

Assume roughly 0.25 mΩ effective per channel at 80 °C (paralleled 2 oz layers plus the shunt):

$$
P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.25\text{ mΩ} = 1.6\text{ W}
$$

### 1.3 Numerical Result at 80 A

With T_j = 110 °C, k_T = 1.47, and R_DS = 2.06 mΩ:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 6400 \times 2.06\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{26.4\ W}
$$

$$
P_{cond+Cu,ch} \approx 28.0\text{ W}
$$

Note the positive feedback: R_DS rises with temperature, which raises loss, which raises temperature. Section 3.3 handles this explicitly.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd phase leg switches. Hard switching occurs on the high side. The low side commutates softly via its body diode during dead time.

### 2.1 Hard-Switching V·I Overlap (HS_A)

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{ns} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}
$$

### 2.2 Body-Diode Conduction During Dead Time (LS_A)

There are two dead-time intervals per PWM period:

$$
P_{dt} = V_{SD}\,I\,(2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 400\text{ns}\times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}
$$
