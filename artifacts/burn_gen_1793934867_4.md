# STM32H743 AIO + 80A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #59): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA, Netlists, Thermal

## 0. Architectural Clarifications and Assumptions

**1. Two MCU domains.**
- The STM32H743 is the flight controller. It runs ArduPilot and generates bidirectional DShot600.
- The 48 kHz PWM and commutation run on four separate ESC MCUs, one per channel. This design uses AT32F421 running AM32.
- The H743 does not drive the gates. These domains share a PCB and a ground, not firmware.

**2. The 80A burst rating needs a duty-cycle definition.** The analysis below shows that four channels at 80A simultaneously in a 30×30 mm nano footprint is a ~1–2 s transient, not a sustained condition. I define the rating as:
- **80A per channel for ≤ 2 s, single or dual channel**
- **4 × 80A for ≤ 1 s**
- **45A continuous per channel with prop-wash airflow**

**4. Operating point.**
- Battery: 6S, analysed at the worst-case 25.2 V.
- Topology: one FET per switch position, so 6 FETs per channel and 24 total. Section 2.6 covers the paralleled variant.
- Commutation: 6-step trapezoidal with synchronous complementary PWM, f_sw = 48 kHz, dead time t_dt = 100 ns.
- Gate drive: V_drv = 10 V, R_g,on = 5 Ω, R_g,off = 2 Ω.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step current paths

During any 60° sector, two phases conduct:
- **Phase A (PWM leg):** the high-side (HS) FET conducts for D·T_sw. The low-side (LS) FET synchronously rectifies for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Phase B (static leg):** the LS FET is on for the whole sector.

The motor current I_m flows continuously through one switching-leg FET plus the static LS FET:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_m^2 R_{hot}\,[D + (1-D)] + I_m^2 R_{hot} = 2\,I_m^2\,R_{hot}$$

This is independent of duty cycle, which is a key result: burst loss does not drop at partial throttle if current is held.

The hot resistance includes the temperature coefficient:

$$R_{hot} = R_0\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] \approx R_0 \cdot k_T$$

### 1.2 Numbers at 80A

$$R_{hot} = 1.0 \times 1.5 = 1.5\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\times10^{-3} = 19.2\ \text{W per channel}$$
