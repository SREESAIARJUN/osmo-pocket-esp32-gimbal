## 1. FOC Equations

### 1.1 Motor model in the rotor (dq) frame

The PMSM is surface-mount, so L_d = L_q = L.

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q, \qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2}\,p\,\lambda_m\, i_q \equiv K_t\, i_q,\qquad \omega_e = p\,\omega_m,\qquad \theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}
$$

The mechanical axis (camera plus frame) obeys:

$$
J\dot\omega_m = K_t i_q - B\omega_m + \tau_{\text{cog}}(\theta_m) + \tau_{\text{dist}}
$$

### 1.2 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke transform, using i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park and inverse Park transforms:
