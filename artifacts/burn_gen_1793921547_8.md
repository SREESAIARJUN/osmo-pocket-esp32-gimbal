## 0. Assumptions and Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3 × gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5 Ω, L ≈ 2 mH, λ ≈ 0.01 V·s/rad |
| Commutation sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (MT6701 / AS5048A), one per joint |
| Inertial sensors | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, mounted symmetrically about the payload's rotation center |
| Gyro/accel ODR | 8 kHz, ±2000 dps / ±16 g, on-chip anti-alias filter (AAF) at about 258 Hz |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with single-precision FPU |
