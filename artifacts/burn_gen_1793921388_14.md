## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and One Architectural Correction

**Architectural correction.** The STM32H743 cannot be the ESC controller. ArduPilot does not perform BLDC commutation. Bi-directional DShot is a link *between* a flight controller and ESC MCUs, so the design is an AIO stack with two compute tiers:

| Tier | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| FC | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100, 400 MHz under ArduPilot) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, DShot600 TX, eRPM capture |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32) | AM32 | Sensorless 6-step commutation at 48 kHz PWM |

**Working assumptions.** Replace each with project-specific values where they differ.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V (max), 22.2 V (nominal) |
