## 1. Conduction Loss Model

In 6-step operation, two phases conduct in each 60° sector:

- **Switching phase:** the high-side FET conducts for D, and its complementary low-side FET conducts for (1 − D). Together that is one R_ds path for the full period.
- **Static return phase:** one low-side FET conducts for 100% of the sector.

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j) \;+\; I^2 R_{Cu}$$

Note that this result is independent of D because complementary PWM is used.

Because R_ds depends on junction temperature, P and T_j must be solved iteratively:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_a + P\!\left(T_j^{(n)}\right)\cdot R_{\theta,ja}$$

**At 80 A per motor (T_j = 125 °C):**

- R_ds = 1.2 mΩ × 1.5 = 1.8 mΩ
- P_FET = 2 × 6400 × 0.0018 = **23.0 W**
- Copper path (≈ 0.5 mΩ): 6400 × 0.0005 = **3.2 W**
- **P_cond ≈ 26.2 W per motor**

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Per motor, only the switching phase leg commutates at f_sw.

| Term | Equation | Value @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r + t_f)·f | ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 30 ns × 48k = **1.452 W** |
| Reverse recovery | V·Q_rr·f | 25.2 × 40 nC × 48k = **0.048 W** |
| C_oss | ½·C_oss·V²·f | ½ × 1.5 nF × 635 × 48k = **0.023 W** |
| Dead-time diode | 2·t_dt·V_f·I·f | 2 × 100 ns × 1.0 × 80 × 48k = **0.768 W** |
| Gate drive (driver IC) | Q_g·V_gs·f·2 | 30 nC × 10 × 48k × 2 = **0.029 W** |
| **Total** | | **≈ 2.32 W per motor** |

**Design insight.** Dead-time diode conduction is the second-largest switching term. Reducing dead time from 100 ns to 50 ns saves about 0.38 W per motor. That is worth more than any gate-resistor tuning. Raising f_sw to 96 kHz doubles every term in this table.
