# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #42): Power-Stage Thermal Model, Switching Losses, and Bi-Directional DShot600 DMA Allocation

## 0. Architecture Check and Assumptions

Two things need to be settled before the math, because they change the design.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot on the H743 is the flight controller. It sends DShot600 to the ESC MCUs and receives eRPM telemetry back. Each motor's commutation runs on a dedicated ESC MCU, typically AM32 or BLHeli_32-class on an AT32F421 or STM32G071, either one per motor or a quad-MCU arrangement. The DShot section below covers the FC-to-ESC link. If you intended the H743 to run sensorless six-step for four motors itself, that is a different, non-ArduPilot firmware architecture. Flag it now.

**2. 80 A burst on a nano board is thermally marginal.** The model below quantifies this. A 20×20 mm board can survive an 80 A burst for only about 2–3 s. Treat this as a design constraint, not a rating.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{ds} = 1.2 \times 1.6 = 1.92\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

In each 60° sector, one phase leg is PWM'd (high-side for D, complementary low-side for 1−D) and one low-side is held on. Current therefore always flows through exactly two channel resistances:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

With typical motor inductance (~20 µH) at 48 kHz, ripple is ΔI_pp ≈ 10 A. That adds only 8 A² to 6400 A², so it is negligible here.

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.92\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{24.6\ W}$$

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle, so the average per FET is:

$$P_{cond,FET} = 24.6 / 6 = 4.1\ \text{W}$$

The instantaneous load during the FET's conduction window is about 12.3 W. Electrical periods are sub-millisecond, far shorter than the junction thermal time constant (milliseconds to seconds), so the average governs.

### 1.4 Electrothermal feedback

R_ds depends on T_j and T_j depends on loss, so the junction temperature must be solved self-consistently:

$$T_j = T_{board} + P(T_j)\,R_{\theta JC}$$

$$\Rightarrow\; T_j = \frac{T_b + R_{\theta}\,(P_{sw} + k R_{25}(1 - 25\alpha))}{1 - R_{\theta}\,k R_{25}\,\alpha}, \qquad k = \tfrac{2I^2}{6}$$

Thermal runaway occurs when the denominator approaches zero:

$$R_\theta \cdot k \cdot R_{25} \cdot \alpha \to 1$$

Here that product is 1.5 × 2133 × 1.2e-3 × 0.006 ≈ 0.023, so the design is far from runaway at the FET level. The limit is board heat sinking.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches. Per motor:

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw = 0.5 · 25.2 · 80 · 27 ns · 48 kHz | **1.31 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f · I · 2t_dt · f_sw = 0.8 · 80 · 200 ns · 48 kHz | **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw = 40 nC · 25.2 · 48 kHz | 0.05 W |
| Output capacitance | ½ · Q_oss · V · f_sw | 0.03 W |
| Gate drive (mostly in driver IC) | 2 · Q_g · V_gs · f_sw = 2 · 40 nC · 10 V · 48 kHz | 0.04 W |
| **Total switching** | | **≈ 2.0 W** |

### Sensitivities

- **P_sw scales linearly with f_sw.** At 24 kHz this term drops to about 1 W, but current ripple doubles. That is still minor next to conduction loss.
- **Dead-time loss is 30% of switching loss.** Trimming t_dt to about 50 ns (AM32 dead-time setting, verified on a scope) saves about 0.3 W per motor.
- **Conduction dominates (~92%) at 80 A.** Rds(on) and copper area are the real levers, not switching frequency.

### Per-motor and total power-stage loss

$$P_{motor} = 24.6 + 2.0 \approx 26.6\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{4\text{-in-1}} \approx \mathbf{107\ W}$$

Adding PCB trace and shunt losses (~0.3 mΩ effective per phase path, 2 × 80² × 0.3 mΩ × 4 ≈ 15 W) gives a total of about **120 W** during a simultaneous 4-motor burst.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Network (lumped Cauer approximation)

```
P_FET ──[R_θJC 1.5 K/W]──┬── T_case ──[R_θCB ~2 K/W (pad + vias)]──┬── T_board
                       C_j≈0.05 J/K                                C_board ──[R_θBA]── T_amb
```

### 3.2 Steady-state limit

Board heat-sinking capacity:

- 20×20 mm, both sides: A = 8 cm².
- Prop-wash convection: h ≈ 50–80 W/m²K.

$$R_{\theta BA} = \frac{1}{hA} \approx \frac{1}{60 \times 8\times10^{-4}} \approx 21\ \text{K/W}$$

The maximum sustained dissipation for a 70 K rise is:

$$P_{cont} = 70 / 21 \approx 3.3\ \text{W total}$$

That corresponds to about **≈15–20 A continuous per motor** (P ∝ I²; check: 4 × 2 × 18² × 1.6 mΩ ≈ 4.1 W). This is consistent with real-world nano 4-in-1 ratings.
