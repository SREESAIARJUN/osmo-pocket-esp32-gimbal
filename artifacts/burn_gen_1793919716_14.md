## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology

Use 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM, which is AM32's default.

In each 60° sector:
- **PWM leg:** the high side (HS) conducts for duty D. The low side (LS) of the same leg conducts for (1−D) as the synchronous rectifier.
- **Return leg:** the LS is on for the whole sector.

Current flows through one switch in the PWM leg at all times, plus the return-leg LS. So the conducted resistance per channel is independent of D:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2\,\big[D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS}\big] + I_{rms}^2 R_{LS} \;\;\xrightarrow{R_{HS}=R_{LS}=R}\;\; P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

Because R_ds depends on T_j and T_j depends on loss, the loss must be solved self-consistently:

$$
T_j = T_a + P_{FET}(T_j)\cdot Z_{th}(t) \quad\Rightarrow\quad
P_{cond} = \frac{2I^2R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_a - 25)]}{1 - 2I^2R_{25}\,\alpha\,Z_{th}}
$$

**Stability criterion (thermal runaway):** 2 I² R₂₅ α Z_th < 1.

At 80 A with R₂₅ = 1 mΩ, Z_th must be below 13 K/W. A bare nano board in still air sits around 15–25 K/W, so it **runs away at continuous 80 A**. This is another reason 80 A is only a burst rating.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j ≈ 125 °C (R_ds ≈ 1.6 mΩ)

| Term | Expression | Per channel |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 2 · 80² · 1.6 mΩ | **20.5 W** |
| Copper/solder | 2 · 80² · 0.5 mΩ | **6.4 W** |
| Shunt (if 0.25 mΩ on DC bus) | I_bus² · R. I_bus ≈ D · I_ph; at D = 0.9 this is 72² · 0.25 m | **1.3 W** |

Per FET, the hottest device is the return-leg LS. It dissipates 80² · 1.6 m = 10.2 W while conducting. It conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle, so its average is about 3.4 W, with a peak junction ripple at low eRPM.
