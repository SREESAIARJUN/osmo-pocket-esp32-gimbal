## 1. FOC Equations

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

θ_off is found at calibration: apply V_d along α, let the rotor settle, and record θ_m.

The velocity estimate is differenced from the unwrapped θ_m, then low-pass filtered (§2.4).

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, balanced load i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant in the dq frame (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$

$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q$$

### 1.5 Current PI with decoupling (pole-zero cancellation)

With the cross-coupling and back-EMF terms cancelled, each axis reduces to G(s) = 1/(Ls + R). Choosing the PI as

$$C(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s},\qquad K_p = L\,\omega_{c,i},\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_{c,i}$$

gives a closed loop of ω_c,i/(s + ω_c,i).

**Control law:**

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \hat\omega_e \hat L i_q$$

$$v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \hat\omega_e(\hat L i_d + \hat\lambda_m)
