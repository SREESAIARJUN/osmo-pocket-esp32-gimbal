# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #30): System Model and Design Package

**Scope:** Loss model for 80 A burst at 48 kHz, thermal analysis, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer and DMA plan, and reference netlists.

**Basis of numbers:** Component values are representative of real 40 V PDFN3.3 MOSFETs and FD6288-class drivers. Before release, replace every parameter marked ⟨DS⟩ with the value from the datasheet of your selected part. The equations stay the same when you do.

---

## 0. Architecture Findings You Need Before Reading the Math

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---------|-------------|
| A1 | The H743 cannot also be the ESC controller while running ArduPilot. Commutation, BEMF zero-cross and 48 kHz PWM for 4 motors (12 half-bridges) need 4 dedicated ESC MCUs. | Use 4× AT32F421 or STM32G071, running AM32 (or BLHeli_32-compatible firmware). The H743 drives them via DShot. |
| A2 | **80 A on a nano (20×20 / 25×25 mm) board is a burst rating only.** It is limited to about 1–1.5 s, from a cool board, with one motor at peak. The thermal math in §3 proves this. | Firmware must enforce I²t limiting. Marketing must state the continuous rating honestly: ~25–35 A per motor with airflow. |
| A3 | "80 A" is defined here as **phase current** (FET current). Battery current is I_bat ≈ D·I_ph. | Losses scale with I_ph², not I_bat. |
| A4 | Bidirectional DShot requires input capture on the same pin as the output. **Do not use TIMx_CHxN complementary pins** for motor outputs; they have no input-capture path. | §4 uses TIM1_CH1–CH4 (non-N pins). |
| A5 | 6S (25.2 V max) needs 40 V FETs. 30 V parts are inadequate once regen and ringing spikes are included. | V_DS(max) = 40 V, with a TVS on the bus. |
