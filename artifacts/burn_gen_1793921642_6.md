# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #38): Loss, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Design Basis

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

Some project details were not specified, so I have chosen the values below. Every derived number follows from them, and you can re-run the equations with your actual datasheet values.

**System architecture.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller. It runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600. Each of the 4 ESC channels has its own motor-control MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 at 48 kHz PWM.

- The H743 does not commutate the motors.
- "Switching losses at 48 kHz" therefore applies to the ESC power stage.
- "DMA timer allocation" applies to the H743.

**Sanity flag.** 80 A × 25.2 V is about 2 kW per motor and 8 kW per board. That is far beyond what a "nano" footprint can dissipate continuously. The thermal analysis in §3 shows that 80 A is a transient rating limited by thermal capacitance, and it quantifies how many seconds that transient can last.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

At any instant two phases are active. Take the case where phase A is PWM-driven and phase B is held low:

| Interval | Duration | Conducting devices |
|---|---|---|
| ON | D·T_sw | Q_AH (high-side A) + Q_BL (low-side B) |
| OFF (freewheel, sync rect) | (1−D)·T_sw | Q_AL (channel, not body diode) + Q_BL |

Q_BL conducts 100% of the time and Q_AH/Q_AL share the remaining period. The total conducted I²R per channel is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \, R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Ripple contribution

The worst-case ripple occurs at D = 0.5:

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\,\mu H \times 48\,kHz} = 6.56\ A
$$

$$
I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 = 6403.6\ A^2
$$

Ripple adds less than 0.06% at 48 kHz, so it is negligible here.

### 1.3 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on): fixed-point solution

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

$$
T_j = T_{board} + P_{FET}\,(R_{\theta JC} + R_{\theta,spread})
$$

These two equations are coupled, so solve them iteratively. Using T_j ≈ 110 °C as the converged burst-end junction temperature (see §3):

$$
R_{DS}(110^\circ C) = 1.0 \times [1 + 0.0045 \times 85] = 1.38\ m\Omega \;\;(\text{design value: } 1.4\ m\Omega)
$$

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 6403.6 \times 1.4\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{17.9\ W\ per\ channel}
$$
