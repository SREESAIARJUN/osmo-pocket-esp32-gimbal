## 0. Architecture & Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture clarification

In a production "FC + 4-in-1 ESC" stack, the STM32H743 does **not** drive the MOSFET gates directly. The 48 kHz PWM, commutation and BEMF sensing run on a dedicated ESC MCU (AM32 or BLHeli_32 class). The H743 talks to that MCU over bi-directional DShot600. The loss model (§1–3) therefore applies to the ESC board, and the DMA/timer model (§4) applies to the H743.

```
 6S LiPo ──┬── Bus caps ──► 4 × [ESC MCU → 3-ph gate driver → 6 × N-FET] → motors
           │                       ▲  bidir DShot600 (1 wire/motor)
           └── BEC 5V/10V          │
                                STM32H743 FC (ArduPilot) – TIM1 CH1-4
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

For trapezoidal (6-step) commutation with complementary PWM, during each 60° sector:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** the high-side FET conducts for duty D, and the low-side FET synchronously rectifies for 1−D.
- **Phase B (return leg):** the low-side FET is on for 100% of the sector.

So the motor current always flows through exactly two R_ds(on) in series:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

Add copper and shunt resistance R_pcb (assume 0.3 mΩ per phase path → 0.6 mΩ per loop):

$$P_{cond,ESC} = I^2\,[2R_{ds}(T_j) + R_{pcb} + R_{shunt}]$$

### 1.2 Numerical evaluation at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | 2·I²·R_ds | + copper (0.6 mΩ) | Total / ESC | ×4 ESCs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 3.84 W | 16.6 W | 66.6 W |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ | 17.1 W | 3.84 W | 20.9 W | 83.8 W |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W | 3.84 W | 22.4 W | 89.6 W |

### 1.3 Thermal runaway check

Because conduction loss rises with T_j, the loop is only stable when:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}\cdot R_{th} = 2 I^2 R_{ds,25}\,\alpha\, R_{th} < 1$$

At 80 A this requires R_th(j–a, effective) < **1.7 K/W per ESC channel**. That is not achievable continuously on a nano board (see §3), which is the formal reason 80 A can only be a *burst* rating.

### 1.4 Per-FET loss (averaged over an electrical cycle)

Each FET plays three roles over a cycle: PWM high side (1/6 of the cycle), synchronous low side (1/6) and static low side (1/6). On average:

$$\bar P_{FET} \approx P_{cond,ESC}/6 \approx 3.1\ \text{W @ } T_j=125°C$$

The peak instantaneous loss in the static low-side role is I²R = **9.3 W**. This matters for Z_th(j–c) at low eRPM, where sectors last milliseconds.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches hard; the return leg is static. The terms per ESC are:

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$
$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V f_{sw}$$
$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V f_{sw}$$
$$P_{dt} = 2\,V_{SD} I\, t_{dt} f_{sw}$$
$$P_{gate} = 2\,Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw}$$

The gate-drive term is dissipated mostly in the driver and R_g, not in the FET.

| Term | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | 0.5·25.2·80·40e-9·48e3 | **1.94 W** |
| Reverse recovery | 30e-9·25.2·48e3 | 0.036 W |
| C_oss | 0.5·40e-9·25.2·48e3 | 0.024 W |
| Dead-time diode | 2·0.8·80·100e-9·48e3 | **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive | 2·50e-9·10·48e3 | 0.048 W (driver) |
| **Σ switching / ESC** | | **≈ 2.66 W** |

### 2.1 Total loss and efficiency at 80 A

- **Per ESC:** P_tot = 22.4 + 2.66 ≈ **25.1 W**
- **All four channels:** ≈ **100 W**
