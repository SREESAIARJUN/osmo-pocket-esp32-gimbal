## 0. Architecture Clarification and Design Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every production 4-in-1 design, each motor channel has its own small ESC MCU, and the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 to those MCUs. Typical ESC MCUs are the AT32F421, STM32G071 or EFM8BB51, running AM32, BLHeli_32 or Bluejay.

The architecture used in this document is:

```
6S LiPo ──► Shunt ──► 4× [ESC MCU + 3-phase driver + 6× N-MOSFET] ──► Motors
   │                        ▲  bidir DShot600 (1 wire per motor)
   └─► Buck 5V/3V3 ──► STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ── SPI IMU, baro, OSD, UARTs
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0059\ /\mathrm{K}$$

At T_j = 110 °C this gives R_ds ≈ 1.3 × 1.50 = **1.95 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Channel-level conduction (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous/complementary PWM)

At every instant, the phase current flows through two conducting devices:

- one device in the PWM'd leg (the high-side during D, or the synchronous low-side during 1−D), and
- the low-side in the return leg.

The duty cycle therefore cancels out:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

| Condition | P_cond per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 80² × 2.6 mΩ = **16.6 W** | 66.6 W |
| 110 °C | 80² × 3.9 mΩ = **25.0 W** | 99.8 W |

### 1.3 Per-device conduction

Each leg is active as the PWM leg for 120° and as the return leg for 120°, then floats for 120°. With I²R = 80² × 1.95 mΩ = 12.48 W:

- **High-side FET (PWM role, D = 0.95):** P = (1/3)·D·I²R = 3.95 W
- **Low-side FET (return role):** P = (1/3)·I²R = 4.16 W, plus synchronous freewheel (1/3)(1−D)·I²R = 0.21 W

### 1.4 Electro-thermal fixed point

The FET temperature raises R_ds, which raises loss, which raises temperature. Solve iteratively:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{pad} + R_{\theta JC}\,P\!\left(R_{ds}(T_j^{(n)})\right)$$
