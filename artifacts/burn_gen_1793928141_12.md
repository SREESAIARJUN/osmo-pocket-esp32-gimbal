## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C this gives a factor of ≈ 1.5, so R_DS ≈ 1.95 mΩ.

### 1.2 Bridge conduction (6-step, synchronous)

At any instant, exactly two phase legs carry the full phase current I_ph:

- One high-side FET, or its complementary low-side FET during the PWM off-time.
- One low-side FET, conducting fully.

So the series path always contains **two** R_DS(on) regardless of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Per-FET RMS current (each FET is active for 1/3 of the electrical period):

- High side: $I_{HS,rms}=I_{ph}\sqrt{D/3}$
- Complementary low side: $I_{LS,rms}=I_{ph}\sqrt{(1+(1-D))/3}$

The total over all six FETs still sums to 2·I²·R.

### 1.3 Copper and shunt path

$$P_{Cu}=I_{ph}^2 R_{trace} + I_{bus}^2 R_{shunt},\qquad I_{bus}\approx D\cdot I_{ph}$$

Assume R_trace ≈ 0.5 mΩ (phase pours plus vias) and a 0.25 mΩ shunt.

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

- P_cond = 2 × 80² × 1.95 mΩ = **24.96 W**
- P_Cu ≈ 80² × 0.5 mΩ + (0.8·80)² × 0.25 mΩ = 3.2 + 1.0 = **4.2 W**

**Self-consistency:** T_j and R_DS are coupled. Solve the fixed point

$$T_j = T_a + P(T_j)\,R_{th}$$

The solution converges only if $\partial P/\partial T_j \cdot R_{th} < 1$:

$$2I^2R_{25}\alpha R_{th}<1$$

At 80 A with R_th = 3 K/W per FET path: 2 × 6400 × 1.3e-3 × 0.005 × 3 = **0.25**. It converges. Thermal runaway is not the limiting factor; bulk heating is.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only one leg switches per PWM cycle in 6-step operation, so there is one hard-switched commutation pair per cycle.

### 2.1 V–I overlap

Gate currents during the Miller plateau:

$$I_{g,on}=\frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_g}=\frac{10-4.5}{5}=1.1\ \text{A},\qquad I_{g,off}=\frac{V_{pl}}{R_g}=0.9\ \text{A}$$

Transition times:

$$t_{on}=\frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}}=10\ \text{ns},\qquad t_{off}=12.2\ \text{ns}$$

Overlap loss:

$$P_{VI}=\tfrac12 V I (t_{on}+t_{off}) f_{sw}=\tfrac12(25.2)(80)(22.2\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{k})= \mathbf{1.07\ W}$$

### 2.2 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss}=\tfrac12 C_{oss,er}V^2 f_{sw}=0.5(1\,\text{nF})(635)(48\,\text{k})=\mathbf{0.015\ W}$$

### 2.3 Body-diode reverse recovery

$$P_{rr}=Q_{rr}V f_{sw}=80\,\text{nC}\times25.2\times48\,\text{k}=\mathbf{0.097\ W}$$

Reverse recovery also adds turn-on current overshoot; in practice, add about 20% to P_VI.
