Loop rates follow a ≥5× bandwidth-separation rule:

```
PWM / current (FOC) loop : 20 kHz   (BW_i ≈ 1.5–2 kHz)   ADC-triggered ISR
IMU sample + filter      :  8 kHz   (BW_imu ≈ 300–500 Hz) IMU DRDY / DMA
Velocity loop            :  4 kHz   (BW_v  ≈ 60–150 Hz)
Attitude estimate + angle:  1 kHz   (BW_θ  ≈ 10–25 Hz)
```

## 1. Field-Oriented Control

### 1.1 Transforms

Use the amplitude-invariant Clarke transform with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

The electrical angle is computed from the mechanical angle with latency compensation. T_d is the total delay from sample to PWM application, typically 1.5 T_PWM:

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{m,0}) + p\,\hat\omega_m T_d
$$

Park transform:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

Inverse Park:

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 Plant Model and Current PI Design

The motor model in the rotor frame is:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

Add decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$
v_d^{*} = \text{PI}_d(e_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{*} = \text{PI}_q(e_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

Here λ_m = Kt / (1.5 p). After decoupling, each axis is the plant 1/(Ls + R). Choosing the PI zero to cancel the plant pole (K_i/K_p = R/L) gives a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{PWM}}{10}
$$
