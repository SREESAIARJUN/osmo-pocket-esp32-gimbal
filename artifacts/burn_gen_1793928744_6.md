## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Findings

Two assumptions in the brief need correcting before the math is meaningful.

**Finding 1: The H743 does not commutate the motors.**
ArduPilot is flight-control firmware. It does not do 6-step or FOC commutation. Every production AIO board, and this design, therefore uses a two-tier architecture:

```
 STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ──4× bi-dir DShot600──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32 or BLHeli_32-class)
                                                 └─► 3-phase gate driver ─► 6× N-MOSFET per motor
```

The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs. The H743's only motor-related timer job is DShot output plus eRPM telemetry capture (Section 4).

**Finding 2: 80 A burst on a nano (20×20 mm) board is out of family.**
The model below shows why. With all four channels at 80 A, a 20×20 board reaches its thermal limit in about 1.5 s, and its steady-state capability is roughly 20–25 A per channel. Shipping products with 80 A burst ratings use 30.5×30.5 mm or larger boards with heavy copper. I carry the analysis through at 80 A so the numbers stand on their own. My recommendation is in Section 6.

**Design basis (assumptions; substitute your BOM datasheet values):**

| Parameter | Value |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

In each 60° sector, one phase is driven high (PWM), one is held low, and one floats. With complementary PWM at duty D:

- During D·T_sw: current flows through HS(A) → motor → LS(B).
- During (1−D)·T_sw: current freewheels through the LS(A) synchronous FET → motor → LS(B).

In both intervals, exactly **two** channel resistances carry the phase current. Instantaneous conduction loss is therefore independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{trace} + I_{bus}^2 R_{shunt}$$

where the battery-side current is I_bus ≈ D·I_ph.

### 1.2 Temperature-corrected R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)] = 1.0\,\text{mΩ}\,[1 + 0.0045(125-25)] = 1.45 \approx 1.5\ \text{mΩ}$$

### 1.3 At 80 A burst (D ≈ 0.95)

| Term | Expression | Value |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 2 × 80² × 1.5 mΩ | **19.2 W** |
| PCB phase/bus copper (≈ 0.15 mΩ effective) | 80² × 0.15 mΩ | 0.96 W |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ, bus side) | (0.95 × 80)² × 0.25 mΩ | 1.44 W |
| **Conduction total per channel** | | **≈ 21.6 W** |

### 1.4 Per-device distribution

Each of the 6 FETs carries current for 1/3 of the electrical period. At D near 1:

$$P_{FET} = \frac{I^2 R_{DS}}{3} = \frac{6400 \times 0.0015}{3} = 3.2\ \text{W per FET}$$

At D < 1, loss shifts from the high-side to the low-side FET. The sum stays constant.
