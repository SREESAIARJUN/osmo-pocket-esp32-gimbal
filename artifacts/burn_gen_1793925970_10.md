## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Feasibility Warning

The numbers below come from a parametric model. Every assumed value is listed here so you can substitute datasheet values for your selected parts.

### 0.1 System partitioning

ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller and emits bi-directional DShot600. Four independent ESC MCUs run AM32 or BLHeli_32 and generate 48 kHz PWM into gate drivers and MOSFETs. The model below follows that standard split.

### 0.3 Feasibility warning (read first)

A 20×20 mm "nano" stack cannot place 24 PDFN 5×6 MOSFETs:

- 24 × ~30 mm² = 720 mm² of footprint.
- The usable area is roughly 2 × 400 mm², minus the MCU, drivers and capacitors.

Nano 4-in-1 boards therefore use 3.3×3.3 mm FETs, which typically have R_ds(on) of about 1.5–2.5 mΩ. As Section 3 shows, **80 A on all four channels simultaneously is a 2–3 second event on this form factor, not a sustained rating.** The design target should be:

- 80 A on a single channel for about 10 s, or
- 80 A on all four channels for about 2 s,
- with a continuous rating of roughly 35–45 A per channel.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

The ESC uses 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In each 60° commutation sector:

- The PWM leg's high-side FET conducts for D·T.
- That leg's low-side FET conducts for (1−D)·T, synchronously rectifying.
- The return leg's low-side FET conducts for 100% of the sector.

Two MOSFETs are therefore always in series with the phase current:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2 \, R_{ds}(T_j) + I_{ph,rms}^2 R_{Cu}$$

### 1.2 Temperature-corrected R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right] = R_{ds,25}(1 + 0.0045 \cdot 85) = 1.38\,R_{ds,25}$$
