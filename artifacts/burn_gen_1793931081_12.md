## 0. Architecture Decision and Baseline Assumptions

### 0.1 Critical architecture constraint

ArduPilot cannot commutate BLDC motors from the H743. ArduPilot generates DShot commands. It has no sensorless FOC or six-step commutation engine. The integrated board is therefore a **two-tier architecture**:

```
            ┌─────────────────────── 30.5×30.5 mm stack / single PCB ───────────────────────┐
 VBAT 6S ───┤ Power entry → bulk caps → DC bus ──┬──── ESC ch1..4 (MCU + driver + 6×FET)   │
            │                                   │          ▲ DShot600 bidir (on-board net) │
            │ 5V/3V3 BEC ── STM32H743 (ArduPilot)┴── TIM3/TIM5 ─┘                          │
            └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Tier 1 (flight control):** STM32H743VIH6 or VIT6 at 400 MHz running ArduPilot. It produces 4× bidirectional DShot600.
- **Tier 2 (power stage):** 4× AT32F421K8U7 (or
