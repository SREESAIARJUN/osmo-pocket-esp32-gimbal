# Job #30: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC, Integrated Design Model

**Scope:** This covers three areas. (1) 80 A burst conduction and thermal model. (2) Switching losses at 48 kHz. (3) Bidirectional DShot600 timer and DMA allocation for ArduPilot. It also includes reference netlists and a verification plan.

> **Engineering caveats**
>
> 1. **Separate ESC MCUs drive the bridges.** On a combined board, the H743 is the flight controller. Each bridge is driven by its own ESC MCU (e.g., STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32/BLHeli_32). DShot runs H743 → ESC MCU over PCB traces, not to the FETs.
> 2. **Parameters are placeholders.** All FET, driver, and motor values are representative. Replace them with your selected part's datasheet values before release. The equations stay valid; the numbers will shift.
> 3. **Netlists are reference designs.** Verify every pin against the exact package datasheet and run ERC/DRC before fabrication.
> 4. **"80 A burst" on a 20×20 / 25.5×25.5 board is a ~1–5 second rating.** The model below shows why.
