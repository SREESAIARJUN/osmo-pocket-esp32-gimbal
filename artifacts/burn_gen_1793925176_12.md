# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #21): Design Model

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Baseline Assumptions

**The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the motor commutator.** ArduPilot does not run sensorless 6-step commutation. Each motor therefore needs its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, for example an STM32G071 or AT32F421. The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to those four MCUs.
