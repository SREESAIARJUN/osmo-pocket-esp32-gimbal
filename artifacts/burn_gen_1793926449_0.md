# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal, and DShot Architecture (Job #31)

## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Feasibility

This document assumes a common AIO split. The **STM32H743 runs ArduPilot** as the flight controller. **Four ESC microcontrollers** (AT32F421 or STM32G071 class, running AM32 or BLHeli_32) each drive one motor bridge. The H743 does not commutate the motors. It sends bidirectional DShot600 commands and receives eRPM telemetry.

**Baseline assumptions.** Replace these with your BOM datasheet values.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Bus voltage (6S, full charge) | V_bus | 25.2 V |
| Burst phase current per motor | I_pk | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET R_DS(on) at 25 °C, V_GS = 10 V | R_25 | 1.0 mΩ (40 V, DFN3.3×3.3 class) |
| R_DS(on) temperature coefficient | α | 0.0045 /K |
| Rise + fall time at the chosen gate resistor | t_r + t_f | 20 ns |
| Q_oss / Q_rr | | 40 nC / 50 nC |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 50 nC |
| Dead time per edge | t_dt | 100 ns |
| Body diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Board size | | 20×20 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz outer and inner power layers |

**Feasibility verdict.** 80 A per motor on a 20×20 board is a **seconds-scale burst rating**, not a continuous one. The model below shows this quantitatively.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

Use complementary (synchronous) 6-step PWM. At any instant, current flows through **one high-side and one low-side FET** in series, whether that is the active vector or the freewheel through the synchronous low side.

The R_DS(on) temperature dependence is:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

Conduction loss per motor, excluding dead-time intervals, is:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

For a flat-top 6-step current, I_rms is approximately equal to I_pk = 80 A.

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond per motor | Four motors |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W | 51.2 W |
| 100 °C | 1.34 mΩ | 17.1 W | 68.4 W |
| 125 °C | 1.45 mΩ | 18.6 W | 74.2 W |

**Copper loss must not be ignored.** The sheet resistance of 2 oz (70 µm) copper is:

$$R_\square = \rho/t = 1.72\times10^{-8} / 70\times10^{-6} = 0.246\ \text{mΩ/sq}$$

A phase path of about 3 squares on 2 paralleled layers gives roughly 0.37 mΩ per path. With 2 paths and 80 A, that is about **4.7 W per motor** at 25 °C. Copper loss is comparable to FET loss, so stitch power planes on 3 or more layers.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ed leg switches hard. Each term below is per motor.

**1. V-I overlap loss**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(20\text{n})(48\text{k}) = 0.97\ \text{W}$$

**2. Output charge loss**

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\cdot25.2\cdot48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}$$

**3. Reverse recovery loss**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.060\ \text{W}$$

**4. Dead-time body diode loss**

$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I\, (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8\cdot80\cdot200\text{n}\cdot48\text{k} = 0.61\ \text{W}$$

**5. Gate drive loss** (6 FETs, dissipated mostly in the driver and gate resistors)

$$P_g = 6\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 6\cdot50\text{n}\cdot10\cdot48\text{k} = 0.14\ \text{W}$$

**Total switching-related loss:** about **1.83 W per motor**, or 7.3 W for all four.

**Totals at 80 A, T_j = 100 °C, including copper:**

$$P_{tot} \approx 4\,(17.1 + 1.83 + 4.7) \approx \mathbf{94\ W}$$

The design implication is clear. At 48 kHz, switching loss is under 10 % of the total. **Conduction and copper losses dominate.** Raising f_sw to 96 kHz would add only about 7 W board-wide. Lowering R_DS(on) and copper resistance matters far more.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Lumped transient (board as a single node)

Estimate the board's heat capacity C_th from its mass of about 5.5 g:

| Component | Mass | Specific heat | Heat capacity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copper | ≈2.0 g | 0.385 J/g·K | 0.77 J/K |
| FR4 | ≈2.5 g | 1.1 J/g·K | 2.75 J/K |
| FETs and parts | ≈1.0 g | ≈0.7 J/g·K | 0.7 J/K |
| **Total** | | | **≈4.2 J/K** |

Take the board-to-ambient thermal resistance as R_θBA ≈ 12 K/W with prop-wash airflow. This value is uncertain and must be measured.

The temperature-dependent power is:

$$P(T) = P_0[1+\alpha(T-25)] + P_{sw}$$

The energy balance is:

$$C_{th}\frac{dT}{dt} = P_0\big[1+\alpha(T-25)\big] + P_{sw} - \frac{T-T_a}{R_{\theta BA}}$$

This is a linear ODE, $\dot T = aT + b$, with:

$$a = \frac{1}{C_{th}}\left(P_0\alpha - \frac{1}{R_{\theta BA}}\right), \qquad b = \frac{1}{C_{th}}\left(P_0(1-25\alpha) + P_{sw} + \frac{T_a}{R_{\theta BA}}\right)$$

**Inputs.** At 25 °C, P_0 = 4 × (12.8 + 4.7) = 70 W and P_sw = 7.3 W. Copper shares α_Cu ≈ 0.0039, which is close enough to treat as 0.0045.

**Coefficients:**

- P_0·α = 0.315 W/K
- 1/R = 0.083 W/K
- a = (0.315 − 0.083)/4.2 = **+0.055 s⁻¹**

**a is positive, which means thermal runaway at full burst.** Positive feedback from R_DS(on) outruns the cooling, and no steady state exists at 80 A on all four motors.

**Solution:**

$$T(t) = \left(T_0 + \frac{b}{a}\right)e^{at} - \frac{b}{a}$$

With T_0 = T_a = 40 °C, the initial slope is about 77.3/4.2 ≈ 18.4 K/s.

| Time | Board temperature |
|---|---|
| 1 s | ≈59 °C |
| 2 s | ≈80 °C |
| 3 s | ≈102 °C |
| ≈4 s | ≈125 °C (FET junctions are hotter still, see 3.2) |

**Burst rating: 80 A × 4 motors ≈ 2 s from a 40 °C start.** A single motor at 80 A, with the others at about 20 A, lasts roughly 6–8 s.

### 3.2 Junction rise above board (fast node)

Take Z_θJC(10 ms) ≈ 0.3 K/W and R_θJC (steady) ≈ 1.2 K/W for each DFN3.3 FET. Each FET carries about 9.5 W during its on-interval.

- Transient: ΔT_JB ≈ 9.5 × 0.3 ≈ 3 K.
- Steady: ΔT_JB ≈ 9.5 × 1.2 ≈ 11 K, before solder voiding derating.

Firmware limit: set the ESC temperature cutback at a board NTC reading of **≤ 105 °C**. This keeps T_j at or below 125 °C with margin.

### 3.3 Recommended protection

- AM32 current limit: 80 A for ≤ 1.5 s, then fold back to a continuous 35–45 A per motor.
