## 2. Electrical Loss Model (Per ESC Channel)

### 2.1 Commutation Topology

The ESC uses six-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM on the active leg. At any instant, three FET roles exist:

- **HS_pwm**: high side of the PWM leg. Conducts for duty D and switches hard.
- **LS_sync**: low side of the PWM leg. Conducts for (1 − D) as synchronous freewheel. Soft-switched, except for the dead-time diode.
- **LS_static**: low side of the return phase. Conducts 100% of the commutation sector.

### 2.2 Conduction Loss

Temperature-corrected on-resistance:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] \approx k_T R_{ds,25} = 1.45 \times 1.5 = 2.18\ \text{m}\Omega$$

Per-role conduction losses:

$$P_{HS} = I^2 R_{ds} D,\quad P_{LS,sync} = I^2 R_{ds}(1-D),\quad P_{LS,static} = I^2 R_{ds}$$

The duty-dependent terms sum to a constant:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2R_{ds}\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2I^2R_{ds}$$

At 80 A:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2(80)^2(2.18\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{27.9\ W}$$

The worst single FET is LS_static, at **13.9 W** for the duration of its sector. Over a full electrical revolution, each FET spends 1/3 in the static role, which averages per-FET heating. Hot-spot sizing must still use the sector value, since the die thermal time constant (~ms) is comparable to the sector period at low eRPM.

**PCB copper loss.** This is not negligible. A 2 oz trace is 0.25 mΩ/sq.
