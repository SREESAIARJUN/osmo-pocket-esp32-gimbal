# STM32H743 Nano AIO with 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model and Thermal Analysis (Job #12)

## 0. Architecture Corrections

Two points in the brief need fixing before any numbers are meaningful.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 as the flight controller. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Here that is 4× AT32F421 (or STM32G071). The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600. "H743 4-in-1 ESC" therefore means an **AIO stack**: an H743 FC plus 4 ESC MCUs plus 24 MOSFETs.
2. **80 A on a nano board is a short burst, not a continuous rating.** The math below shows that about 80 A on all four channels is thermally sustainable for only **~3–4 s**. Market the board as "80 A burst (≤2 s), ~35 A continuous" or similar.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0067\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 100 °C: R_ds = 1.2 × 1.50 ≈ **1.8 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current paths in 6-step synchronous operation

At any instant, the motor current I passes through exactly one high-side path (Q_H or its body diode) and one low-side FET. With complementary PWM at duty D, during each 60° commutation sector:

- The active high-side FET conducts for D.
- Its complementary low-side FET (synchronous rectification) conducts for (1−D).
- The opposite leg's low-side FET conducts 100%.

Each FET is active for 120° of every 360°. Per-FET RMS currents:

$$I_{rms,HS} = I\sqrt{\tfrac{D}{3}},\qquad I_{rms,LS} = I\sqrt{\tfrac{1}{3}\left(1 + (1-D)\right)\cdot\tfrac{1}{2}}\ \ (\text{averaged over legs})$$

These per-FET splits do not need to be tracked to get the total. Summed over the bridge, the series path always contains two R_ds(on):

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)}$$

(This excludes the dead-time interval, which is handled in §2.3.)

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.8\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{23.0\ W\ per\ channel}$$

Additional resistive paths per channel:

| Element | R | P at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pad + copper (3× ~0.15 mΩ, 2 in path) | 0.3 mΩ | 1.9 W |
| Shunt (if per-channel, 0.25 mΩ) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.6 W |
| **Total I²R per channel** | | **≈ 26.5 W** |

### 1.4 Electro-thermal fixed point

Because R_ds depends on T_j, the junction temperature must satisfy a fixed-point equation:

$$T_j = T_{pcb} + R_{\theta,jp}\cdot \frac{2I^2 R_{ds,25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]}{6} + \dots$$

This must be solved iteratively (see §4.3). Thermal runaway occurs if

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,R_\theta \ge 1$$

which is checked in §4.3.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one leg is PWM-switching at a time.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap (active high-side FET)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = \tfrac12 (25.2)(80)(40\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz}) = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

The low-side FET switches at about 0 V (ZVS via body diode), so its overlap loss is negligible.

### 2.2 Reverse recovery (low-side body diode)

$$P_{rr} = V_{bus}\, Q_{rr}\, f_{sw} = 25.2 \times 50\,\text{nC} \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.06\ W}$$

(Q_rr rises with di/dt and T_j. Budget 2× at 100 °C, i.e. 0.12 W.)

### 2.3 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_{SD}\, I\, (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.4 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 C_{oss} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 1.5\,\text{nF} \times 635 \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.023\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated in driver and gate resistors, not the FET)

$$P_g = 2\, Q_g V_{gs} f_{sw} = 2 \times 60\,\text{nC} \times 10 \times 48\,\text{k} = \mathbf{0.058\ W}$$
