## 0. Architectural Clarification and Executive Finding

The STM32H743 does not commutate the MOSFETs. Driving 24 FETs in six-step or FOC at 48 kHz on four motors while also running ArduPilot is not a credible design. The correct partitioning is:

| Block | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) or VIH6 (BGA) | ArduPilot | Sends bidirectional DShot600 to 4 ESCs |
| ESC MCU ×4 | STM32G071GBU6 (UFQFPN28) | AM32 | Six-step, 48 kHz complementary PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate driver ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) [A] | n/a | High/low drive, built-in dead time |
| Power stage | 24× 40 V N-MOSFET, PQFN 3.3×3.3 [A] | n/a | 4 × 3 half-bridges |

**Headline finding (derived in §3):** sustaining 80 A per motor simultaneously on a nano (20×20 mm) board is thermally impossible beyond about 1 s. The board dissipates roughly 110 W into about 1.8 J/K of thermal mass. The design is feasible for:

- (a) 80 A *total* burst at 20×20 mm; or
- (b) 80 A *per channel* burst of 2 s or less on a 30.5×30.5 mm board with heavy copper.

Section 3 quantifies both cases. Decide which "80 A" the requirement means before layout.

## 2. Loss Model per ESC Channel

### 2.1 Conduction Topology (Six-Step, Synchronous/Complementary PWM)

In each of the six commutation sectors, one phase is PWM-switched (HS at duty D, LS complementary at 1−D), one phase has its LS held on, and one phase floats for BEMF sensing.

The phase current $I$ flows through:
- the switched leg (HS for D·T, LS for (1−D)·T, so exactly one FET at all times, ignoring dead time), and
- the static LS FET.

Two FETs therefore conduct $I$ at all times:

$$
P_{cond} = 2\, I^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)\cdot\left(1 - \frac{2\,t_{dead} f_{sw}}{1}\right) \approx 2 I^2 R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

The dead-time fraction is $2 \cdot 200\text{ ns} \cdot 48\text{ kHz} = 1.9\%$. It is treated as negligible here and accounted for separately in §2.4.

**Temperature dependence:**

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + k\,(T_j - 25)\right],\qquad k \approx \frac{1.8-1}{100} = 0.008\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 15.4 W |
| 75 °C | 1.68 mΩ | 21.5 W |
| 125 °C | 2.16 mΩ | 27.6 W |

**Per-FET average:** each of the six FETs conducts roughly one third of the electrical cycle. At 125 °C:

$$
\bar P_{FET} \approx 27.6/6 \approx 4.6\ \text{W}
$$

The *instantaneous* dissipation in a conducting FET is $I^2 R = 13.8$ W. This value matters for $Z_{th}$ transient analysis, because the commutation period at high eRPM is much shorter than the junction time constant. The averaged value is therefore valid above about 1 kHz electrical.

### 2.2 Hard-Switching (V-I Overlap) Loss

Only the PWM-switched HS FET hard-switches. The LS FET turns on after dead time with its body diode already conducting, so it soft-switches (ZVS).

$$
P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}
= \tfrac{1}{2}(25.2)(80)(20\times10^{-9})(48\times10^{3}) = 0.97\ \text{W}
$$

### 2.3 Capacitive, Reverse-Recovery and Gate Losses

$$
P_{oss} = Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{n}\cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}
$$

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{n}\cdot 25.2\cdot 48\text{k} = 0.048\ \text{W}
$$

The gate loss is dissipated mainly in the driver and R_G, not in the FET:

$$
P_{gate} = 2\, Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} = 2\cdot 40\text{n}\cdot 10\cdot 48\text{k} = 0.038\ \text{W}
$$

### 2.4 Dead-Time Body-Diode Conduction

There are two dead-time intervals per PWM period, during which the LS body diode carries $I$:

$$
P_{dt} = 2\, V_{SD}\, I\, t_{dead}\, f_{sw} = 2(0.8)(80)(200\text{n})(48\text{k}) = 1.23\ \text{W}
$$

This loss exceeds the switching loss.
