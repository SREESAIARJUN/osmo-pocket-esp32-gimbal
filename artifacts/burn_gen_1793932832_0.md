## 1. FOC Inner Current Loop

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

θ_off comes from alignment: inject a d-axis voltage vector at θ_e = 0 and record θ_m.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 Plant in the rotating frame (surface-mounted PM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_{pm}$$
$$\tau_e = \tfrac32 p\lambda_{pm} i_q = K_t i_q$$

### 1.5 PI design by pole-zero cancellation

The plant after decoupling is $G_i(s)=\dfrac{1}{Ls+R}$. Choose $C_i(s)=K_p+\dfrac{K_i}{s}$ with $K_i/K_p = R/L$. The closed loop then becomes first order with bandwidth ω_ci:

$$K_{p,i}=L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i}=R\,\omega_{ci}$$

The PWM and ADC delay is about 1.5 T_s. For phase margin above 45°, choose

$$\omega_{ci}\le \frac{1}{3\cdot 1.5T_s}\;\Rightarrow\; f_{ci}\approx 1.5\text{–}2\ \text{kHz at } f_s = 20\ \text{kHz}.$$

### 1.6 Decoupling feedforward

$$v_d^{ff}=-\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff}=\omega_e L i_d+\omega_e\lambda_{pm}$$

### 1.7 Voltage limiting (circular)

$$\|v_{dq}\|\le \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}$$

d-axis priority: clamp v_d first, then $v_q \le \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}$. Back-calculation anti-windup is applied to both integrators.

### 1.8 Voltage-mode fallback (no shunts)

$$v_q = R\,i_q^* + \omega_e\lambda_{pm},\qquad v_d = -\omega_e L i_q^*$$

### 1.9 SVPWM via min/max zero-sequence injection

After inverse Park/Clarke to $v_a, v_b, v_c$:

$$v_0=-\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc})+\min(v_{abc})\big),\qquad d_x=\tfrac12+\frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}}$$

## 2. Cascaded Angle → Velocity → Current Control

```
θ* ─►[P(+I) angle]─ω*─►[PI velocity]─τ*─►(÷Kt)─i_q*─►[FOC PI]─►motor
        ▲  +ω_ff           ▲  +τ_ff (cogging, gravity, J·α*)
        θ (IMU/encoder)    ω (encoder, filtered)
```

### 2.1 Velocity loop

The plant from torque to velocity is $G_\omega(s)=\dfrac{1}{Js+B}\approx\dfrac{1}{Js}$, where J is the reflected inertia of the axis including the payload.

The velocity PI uses the symmetric optimum with crossover ω_cv and a spacing factor a ≈ 3–4:

$$K_{p,\omega}=J\,\omega_{cv},\qquad K_{i,\omega}=\frac{K_{p,\omega}\,\omega_{cv}}{a^2}$$

Bandwidth separation: $\omega_{cv}\le\omega_{ci}/5$, giving about 200–400 Hz.

Velocity estimation uses a PLL tracking observer on the encoder angle. This avoids the quantisation noise
