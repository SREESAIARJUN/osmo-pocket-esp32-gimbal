## 0. Architecture Clarification and Feasibility Warning

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller MCU. It does not commutate motors. Each ESC channel runs its own MCU, for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, which supports 48 kHz PWM. The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry. All loss math below applies to the ESC power stage.

**Feasibility warning.** A nano board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) cannot sustain 80 A on all four channels. The model below shows that the whole board dissipates about 92 W at 4×80 A. That limits safe burst duration to a few seconds even with prop-wash cooling. Treat "80 A burst" as single-channel or transient only, and enforce it in firmware (Section 4.5).

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × (1 + 0.6) = **1.6 mΩ**

### 1.2 Current paths in one commutation step

In each 60° step, two legs conduct:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) FET conducts for fraction D. The complementary low-side (LS) FET conducts for (1−D).
- **Return leg:** the LS FET conducts 100% of the step.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I_{rms}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

The result is **independent of duty cycle** under synchronous rectification.

Ripple correction:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}, \qquad \Delta I \approx \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

Example: L_ph = 20 µH, D = 0.5 gives ΔI ≈ 6.6 A and +3.6 A² (negligible, under 0.1%).

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.0016 = \mathbf{20.5\ W/channel}$$

### 1.4 Per-FET average loss over an electrical cycle

Each leg conducts I²R for 2/3 of the electrical cycle (HS+LS PWM pair for 1/3, static LS for 1/3).

- Per-leg average: 13.7 W, split across 2 FETs.
- HS average: (1/3)·D·I²R
- LS average: (1/3)(1−D)·I²R + (1/3)·I²R

At D = 0.9, I²R = 10.24 W:

| FET | Average loss |
|---|---|
| HS | 3.07 W |
| LS | 3.75 W |

**Worst case: locked rotor or startup.** With no commutation, a single LS FET carries the full **10.24 W** continuously. This is the design-limiting case for T_j.
