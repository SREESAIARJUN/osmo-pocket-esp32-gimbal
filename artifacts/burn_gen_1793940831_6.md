The rule I follow is a bandwidth separation of at least 5× between nested loops. Typical targets:

- Current loop: $\omega_c \approx 2\pi\cdot 1.5$ kHz
- Rate loop: $\omega_v \approx 2\pi\cdot 60$ Hz
- Angle loop: $\omega_\theta \approx 2\pi\cdot 8$ Hz

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (innermost loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle
$$\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{e,0}$$

$\theta_{e,0}$ is the encoder offset found at calibration: lock the rotor with $V_d$ applied at $\theta_e=0$, then read $\theta_m$.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$)
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform
$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$
