## 0. Architecture and scope

There are two important architectural points before the math.

1. **Two different MCUs do two different jobs.**
   - The **STM32H743 is the flight controller (FC).** It generates the DShot600 commands and decodes the eRPM telemetry.
   - The **48 kHz complementary PWM runs on the ESC MCU(s).** Typical choices are AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32, either one per motor or one shared.
   - Accordingly, Sections 1–3 apply to the ESC board and Section 4 applies to the FC.
2. **The physical form factor must match the current rating.** True "nano" 20×20 mm 4-in-1s top out around 25–35 A per channel. An **80 A burst requires a 30.5×30.5 mm footprint (36×36 mm outline), 8-layer, 2 oz copper.
