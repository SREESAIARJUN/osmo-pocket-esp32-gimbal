## 0. Scope and Assumptions

Some parameters were not in the brief, so I've fixed them explicitly. Change any of them and re-run the equations; every result below is parametric.

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller and runs ArduPilot. It does not commutate the motors. Commutation is done by four dedicated ESC MCUs, for example 4× AT32F421 running AM32 or 4× EFM8BB21 running BLHeli_S/Bluejay. The H743 talks to them over bi-directional DShot600.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C this gives R_DS = 1.0 × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

At every instant, exactly two phase legs are active. Current flows through two series MOSFET channels:

- During the PWM on-time: high side of leg A and low side of leg B.
- During the off-time: low side of leg A (synchronous freewheel) and low side of leg B.

The current always sees **2 × R_DS** regardless of duty cycle D, so the motor's conduction loss is independent of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Distribution per FET over one electrical cycle:

- Each leg is active for 2/3 of the cycle.
- Within that window, the high side carries D and the low side carries (1−D).

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS} D \qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS}(1-D) + \tfrac{1}{3} I^2 R_{DS}$$

The second low-side term is the "static-low" leg role. The low-side FETs are the thermally dominant devices.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| Condition | P_cond per motor | Worst FET (low side, D≈0.5) |
|---|---|---|
| T_j = 25 °C (cold start) | 2·6400·0.0010 = **12.8 W** | 3.2 W |
| T_j = 125 °C | 2·6400·0.0015 = **19.2 W** | 4.8 W |

Copper and trace loss also contributes. Assume 0.3 mΩ per motor (phase pads, bus planes, shunt): 6400 × 0.0003 = **1.9 W per motor**.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM-ing leg hard-switches. In complementary mode, its high-side FET hard-switches and the low side switches at ZVS after dead time.

### 2.1 Gate-current-limited transition times

Turn-on gate current during the plateau:

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_g} = \frac{10-3}{4} = 1.75\ \text{A}$$

Turn-off gate current during the plateau:

$$I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_g} = \frac{3}{4} = 0.75\ \text{A}$$

| Interval | Formula | Turn-on | Turn-off |
