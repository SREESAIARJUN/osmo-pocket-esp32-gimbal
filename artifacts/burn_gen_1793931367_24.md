## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step synchronous (complementary) PWM

In any commutation sector, phase A's leg PWMs while phase B's low-side FET is held on.

- During t_on (fraction D), current flows through HS_A and LS_B.
- During t_off (fraction 1−D), current flows through LS_A (synchronous rectification) and LS_B.

So **exactly two FETs carry the phase current at all times**, outside the dead time:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] \approx k_T R_{ds,25}$$

### 1.2 Ripple correction

The phase inductance L_ph produces a triangular ripple:

$$\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph} f_{sw}}, \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I_{dc}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

Typical values are L_ph ≈ 15 µH (line-to-line) and D ≈ 0.85 at burst, which give ΔI ≈ 4.5 A. The correction term is +1.7 A², which is negligible compared with 6400 A².

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A burst

$$R_{ds,hot} = 1.3 \times 1.6 = 2.08\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond,motor} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 2.08\text{m} = \mathbf{26.6\ W}$$

Each FET conducts for an average of one-third of the electrical cycle, so:

- **Average per FET** (6 FETs): 4.4 W
- **Instantaneous per FET** while conducting: 13.3 W

The instantaneous figure matters for the transient junction rise in §3.3.

**With 12 paralleled FETs** (two per position), each position has R/2, so total loss halves: **13.3 W per motor, 2.2 W average per FET.** For an 80 A burst rating, paralleled FETs are strongly recommended.
