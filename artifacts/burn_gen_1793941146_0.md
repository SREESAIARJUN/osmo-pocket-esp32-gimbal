## 0. Scope and Assumptions

Two points need clarifying first, because they shape everything below.

**1. The STM32H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC.** In production 4-in-1 ESCs, each phase bridge is commutated by its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32/S, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends DShot600 to those four MCUs. Commutating four motors directly from the H743 is not supported by ArduPilot and is not recommended. This document models:
- the **power stage** (ESC board), and
- the **H743 timer/DMA allocation** (FC board).

**2. 80 A per channel in a nano form factor is a burst rating, not a continuous one.** The thermal model in §3 quantifies this. The result is about 2 to 3 seconds of simultaneous 4×80 A before the board hits its thermal limit.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right]$$

At T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS} = 0.9 \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 85) = 1.244\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Bridge conduction in 6-step synchronous PWM

In each of the six sectors, one leg is PWM'd and a second leg returns current through its low-side FET held fully on.

- **PWM leg:** the high side conducts for fraction D; the synchronous low side conducts for (1 − D). Neglecting dead time, the combined loss is $I^2R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D)] = I^2R_{DS}$.
- **Return leg:** $I^2R_{DS}$.

Total bridge conduction loss is therefore independent of duty cycle:

$$\boxed{P_{cond} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + I^2 R_{Cu}}$$

At 80 A and T_j = 110 °C:

$$P_{cond,FET} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.244 \times 10^{-3} = 15.9\ \text{W}$$

**Copper path.** Estimate 0.15 mΩ for the phase and bus path per channel (2 oz plus internal planes):

$$P_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.15 \times 10^{-3} = 0.96\ \text{W}$$

**Per-FET distribution.** Averaged over an electrical cycle, the loss spreads across six FETs, about 2.65 W each. Instantaneously, during low eRPM or a stall, a single return-leg FET dissipates:

$$I^2 R_{DS} = 7.96\ \text{W}$$

Use this instantaneous value for the junction check in §3.4.

### 1.3 Electrothermal feedback

Because R_DS rises with T_j, solve iteratively:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{board} + R_{\theta,jb}\; P_{FET}\!\left(R_{DS}(T_j^{(n)})\right)$$

Runaway does not occur provided:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,R_{\theta} = I^2 R_{25}\,\alpha\,R_{\theta} < 1$$

At 80 A with R_θ = 2.6 K/W: 6400 × 0.9 × 10⁻³ × 0.0045 × 2.6 = 0.067. This is stable, with about 7 % loop gain.
