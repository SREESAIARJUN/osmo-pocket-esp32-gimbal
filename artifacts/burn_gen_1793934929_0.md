## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle from mechanical angle, with offset found at alignment:
$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{0}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$):
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

Park transform:
$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI design

The surface-PMSM voltage equations in the rotor frame are:
$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

With decoupling feedforward, each axis reduces to $G(s) = 1/(Ls+R)$. Choosing a PI zero that cancels the plant pole gives a first-order closed loop of bandwidth $\omega_c$:
$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10\text{–}20}$$

Discrete form (forward Euler on the integrator), applied per axis:
$$u_k = K_p e_k + I_k + v_{ff},\qquad I_{k+1} = I_k + T_s\big[K_i e_k + K_{aw}(\text{sat}(u_k) - u_k)\big]$$

The second term is back-calculation anti-windup, with $K_{aw} \approx K_i/K_p$.

The decoupling feedforward is:
$$v_{d,ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_{q,ff} = \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

### 1.3 Voltage limiting and SVPWM

The voltage vector is limited to the linear SVPWM hexagon's inscribed circle, $|v| \le V_{dc}/\sqrt3$. The $d$-axis gets priority:
$$v_d \leftarrow \text{clip}(v_d,\pm V_{max}),\qquad v_q \leftarrow \text{clip}\!\left(v_q, \pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}\right)$$

SVPWM uses min-max zero-sequence injection. With phase voltages $v_{a,b,c}$ from the inverse Clarke/Park transforms:
$$v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc})+\min(v_{abc})\big),\qquad d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{dc}}$$

The torque relation is $\tau = \tfrac32 p\lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q$, with $i_d^* = 0$.

## 2. Cascaded Angle / Velocity Control

### 2.1 Structure

The cascade per axis is:

> θ* →[angle P + rate FF]→ ω* →[velocity PI-D, 2-DOF]→ τ* →[÷Kt + cogging FF + gravity FF]→ i_q* → FOC

The feedback signals are chosen to match what each loop controls:

- **Angle feedback** is the camera attitude from IMU fusion, projected into joint coordinates (Section 2.3).
- **Velocity feedback** is the fused payload gyro, mapped to the joint axis. This is what rejects base disturbances. Encoder velocity would only stabilize the motor relative to the frame.

### 2.2 Laws

**Angle loop (1 kHz).** The angle error is wrapped:
$$e_\theta = \text{wrap}_\pi(\theta^* - \theta),\qquad \omega^* = K_{p\theta}\, e_\theta + \dot\theta^*_{ff}$$

**Velocity loop (4 kHz).** This is a 2-DOF PI with set-point weight $b$ and a filtered derivative on measurement:
$$\tau^* = K_{pv}(b\,\omega^* - \omega) + K_{iv}\!\int(\omega^*-\omega)\,dt - K_{dv}\frac{N s}{s+N}\omega$$

Tustin discretization of the filtered derivative gives:
$$D_k = \frac{2-NT}{2+NT}D_{k-1} - \frac{2K_{dv}N}{2+NT}(\omega_k - \omega_{k-1})$$

**Current command.** Here $\hat\tau_{cog}$ comes from Section 3 and the gravity term handles residual imbalance:
$$i_q^* = \text{sat}\!\left(\frac{\tau^* + \hat\tau_{cog}(\theta_m) + \tau_{g}}{K_t}\right)$$

**Tuning rules.** For a rigid inertia plant $J s$ with velocity bandwidth $\omega_v \approx \omega_c/5$:
$$K_{pv} = J\omega_v,\qquad K_{iv} = K_{pv}\,\omega_v/4,\qquad K_{p\theta} = \omega_v/4$$
