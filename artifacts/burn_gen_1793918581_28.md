# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage Losses, Thermal Limits, Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation, and Netlists

**Design basis.** No MOSFET part number, battery voltage, or board outline was specified. I've stated every assumption explicitly so you can substitute datasheet values. If Job #7 has a locked BOM, swap in those parameters. All equations are written so the numbers propagate.

## 0. Architecture Corrections

Two issues should be resolved before layout.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot runs on the H743 and *transmits* bi-directional DShot600. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, such as the AT32F421 or STM32G071. You could build a single-MCU FOC design, but ArduPilot does not support it, and the bi-directional DShot requirement implies separate ESC MCUs anyway.
2. **"Nano" and "80 A burst" conflict.** The thermal model in §3 shows that a 20×20 mm board cannot survive a meaningful 80 A/channel burst. A 30.5×30.5 mm board with 6 layers and 2 oz copper gets you about 2–3 s. Treat 80 A as a **≤2 s burst rating**, not a continuous rating.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (per channel, 80 A burst)

### 2.1 Conduction losses

With synchronous rectification, the phase current always flows through one high-side and one low-side FET path. During the on-time it flows through HS(A) and LS(B). During the off-time it freewheels through LS(A) and LS(B). In both states, two R_DS(on) are in series:

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

This is independent of duty cycle, to first order.

$$P_{cond} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.26\,\text{m}\Omega = 6400 \times 2.52\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{16.1\ W}$$

The **instantaneous** dissipation in a single FET is 80² × 1.26 mΩ = **8.06 W**. Over a full electrical cycle, each FET conducts roughly 1/3 to 1/2 of the time, depending on PWM placement. Use the 8 W instantaneous figure for junction-to-case transient checks, not the average.

R_DS(on) has positive thermal feedback. Solve iteratively:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0053/\text{K}$$
