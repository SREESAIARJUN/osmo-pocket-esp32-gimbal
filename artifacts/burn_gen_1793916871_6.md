## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Commutation topology

In 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM), phase current always flows through exactly two MOSFETs:

- the high-side FET (on-time D) or the low-side FET (off-time 1−D) of the PWM leg, and
- the low-side FET of the return leg (on for the full 60° sector).

Instantaneous channel conduction loss is therefore:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

### 2.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At 125 °C this gives R_DS(on) ≈ 1.92 mΩ.

### 2.3 Ripple contribution

Inductor current ripple at D = 0.5 (worst case) is:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus} D (1-D)}{L_{ph} f_{sw}}
$$

With a typical 5" motor at L_ph ≈ 20 µH:

$$
\Delta I = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{20\text{ µH} \times 48\text{ kHz}} \approx 6.6\text{ A}_{pp}
$$

The RMS current then becomes:

$$
I_{rms} = \sqrt{I_{dc}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}} = \sqrt{6400 + 3.6} \approx 80.02\text{ A}
$$

The ripple contribution is negligible at 48 kHz.

### 2.4 Numerical results at 80 A burst

| Condition | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Per channel, T_j = 25 °C | 6400 × 2 × 1.2 mΩ | 15.4 W |
| Per channel, T_j = 125 °C | 6400 × 2 × 1.92 mΩ | **24.6 W** |

Each FET conducts for 1/3 of the electrical cycle. Because the electrical frequency is about 1–3 kHz, which is far faster than the FET thermal time constants, average loss per FET is:

$$
P_{FET,cond} = \frac{24.6}{6} \approx 4.1\text{ W}
$$

### 2.5 PCB copper loss

Copper sheet resistance at 25 °C:

| Copper weight | Sheet resistance |
|---|---|
| 1 oz | ≈ 0.50 mΩ/□ |
| 2 oz | ≈ 0.25 mΩ/□ |

The bus-to-phase-to-pad current path is approximately 3 squares. With two 2 oz layers in parallel and a +30% thermal adjustment, effective resistance is about 0.4 mΩ.

$$
P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.4\text{ mΩ} \approx 2.6\text{ W per channel}
$$

Add the solder-pad motor-lead joints, which typically contribute 0.1–0.2 mΩ (about 1 W).
