# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal

I don't have the specific requirements document for "Job #12," so this design states every assumption explicitly. All motor, inertia, and mounting parameters are exposed as constants so you can substitute your real values. The equations do not depend on these specific numbers.

---

## 0. Baseline Assumptions

| Item | Assumed value | Where it's used |
|---|---|---|
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 2–5 mH | FOC, cogging |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701 class) on each joint | FOC θₑ, joint angle |
| MCU | STM32G4/H7 class, single-precision FPU, center-aligned PWM | Timing |
| Current loop | 20 kHz, synchronous to PW
