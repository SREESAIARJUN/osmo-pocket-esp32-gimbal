**Document:** Job #40, System Architecture Rev A
**Scope:** 80 A burst conduction losses, 48 kHz switching losses, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer and DMA allocation, reference netlists, thermal model.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Feasibility Constraints

Several constraints shape the rest of the design.

1. **Architecture split.** ArduPilot runs on the STM32H743 flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. The 4-in-1 ESC uses four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421 class, running AM32) that receive bidirectional DShot600 from the H743 and generate 48 kHz complementary PWM. The "48 kHz switching" and "DShot DMA" requirements therefore apply to different silicon.
2. **"Nano" vs. 80 A.** On a 20×20 mm board, 80 A per motor is not physically credible. The total loss at burst is about 85 W (Section 3). This design assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm stack**: the FC on top and the ESC below, with 5×6 mm PDFN MOSFETs on the ESC.
3. **MCU package.** Use STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100, 8×8 mm). LQFP packages do not fit.
4. **Operating point:** 6S, V_bus = 25.2 V maximum (22.2 V nominal). The 80 A burst lasts up to 5 s, with 35 A continuous.
5. **Representative MOSFET:** 40 V, R_DS(on) = 1.0 mΩ typical / 1.25 mΩ maximum at 25 °C and V_GS = 10 V. Q_g ≈ 60 nC, Q_rr ≈ 50 nC, Q_oss ≈ 60 nC, V_SD ≈ 0.8 V, R_θJC ≈ 1.0 K/W.

**Substitute your actual datasheet values before release.** All numbers below are parametric, so they update directly.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Current Path (6-step trapezoidal, synchronous complementary PWM)

During any commutation sector, two phases conduct:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET for duty D, then the low-side FET (synchronous rectification) for 1−D.
- **Return leg:** the low-side FET for the full sector.

Current therefore always flows through **exactly two R_DS(on)** at any instant, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_J)$$

### 2.2 Temperature Dependence

$$R_{DS}(T_J) = R_{DS,25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_J - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}$$

At T_J = 110 °C with a typical R_DS,25 = 1.0 mΩ:

$$R_{DS}(110) = 1.0(1 + 0.0045 \cdot 85) = 1.38\ \mathrm{m\Omega}$$

### 2.3 Per-Channel and Per-FET Values

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.38\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{17.7\ W}$$

Each FET conducts for roughly one third of the electrical period, so the averaged loss is:

$$\bar P_{FET} \approx \frac{P_{cond,ch}}{6}\cdot 2 \cdot \tfrac{1}{2}\ \text{(sector sharing)} \approx 2.9\text{–}5.9\ \mathrm{W}$$

The worst-case low-side FET (return leg plus synchronous rectification at low D) sees about 5.9 W.

### 2.4 PCB Copper Loss (not negligible)

Assume a phase path of 15 mm length and 6 mm width, with 4 layers of 2 oz (70 µm) copper in parallel:

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho L}{w t n} = \frac{1.72\times10^{-8}\cdot 0.015}{0.006\cdot 70\times10^{-6}\cdot 4} \approx 0.154\ \mathrm{m\Omega}\ (\times 1.3 \text{ at } 100^\circ\mathrm C \approx 0.20\ \mathrm{m\Omega})$$

$$P_{Cu,ch} \approx 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 0.20\,\text{m}\Omega \approx 2.6\ \mathrm{W}$$
