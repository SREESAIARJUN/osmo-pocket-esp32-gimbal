# 3-Axis Gimbal Motor Control & Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion: FOC, Cascaded Control, Anti-Cogging, and Vibration Rejection

## 0. Assumptions and Architecture

I don't have the Job #5 specification, so the design rests on the assumptions below. All values are parameters in the code. Replace them with your hardware's values.

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs $p = 7$), $R_s \approx 5.6\,\Omega$, $L_s \approx 2.1\,$mH, $K_t \approx 0.08\,$Nm/A |
| Encoder | AS5048A-class, 14-bit absolute magnetic, read on each PWM cycle |
| MCU | STM32G4/H7-class, single-precision FPU, center-aligned PWM |
| Current sense | 2 low-side shunts, sampled at PWM center |
| IMUs | 2 × ICM-42688-P on separate SPI buses, gyro and accel at 8 kHz ODR, ±2000 dps / ±16 g |
| Bus | 12–16 V |

### Rate Hierarchy

| Loop | Rate | Closed-loop bandwidth | Separation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Current (d/q PI) | 20 kHz | ~1.5 kHz | — |
| IMU acquisition + filters + fusion | 8 kHz | — | — |
| Velocity PI (+ feedforwards) | 4 kHz | ~120 Hz | ≥10× below current loop |
| Angle P/PI (attitude stabilization) | 1 kHz | ~25 Hz | ≥4–5× below velocity loop |
