| 1 | ArduPilot does not commutate BLDC motors. The H743 cannot drive gates directly with stock ArduPilot. | **Two-tier architecture.** The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600. Four ESC MCUs (AT32F421, running AM32) generate the 48 kHz 6-step PWM and drive the gate drivers. |
