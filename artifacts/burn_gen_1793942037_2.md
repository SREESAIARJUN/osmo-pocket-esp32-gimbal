# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Design Package (Job #35)

## 0. Architecture Clarification

Two corrections define the architecture.

**1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot on the H743 is the flight controller. Each motor channel needs its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. Typical choices are the AT32F421 or the STM32G071, one per motor. The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to those four MCUs. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, not by the H743.

**2. 80 A burst on a nano board is thermally limited, not silicon-limited.** The math below shows about 85 W of dissipation with all four channels at 80 A. Steady state is impossible. The rating is valid only as a time-bounded burst (≈2–4 s), and the firmware must enforce that.

Unless stated otherwise, all component values are representative engineering assumptions. Substitute datasheet values for your selected parts before release.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 Current Path

In 6-step commutation two phases conduct at any instant:
- One leg is PWM'd. With synchronous rectification, either its high-side (HS) or its low-side (LS) FET carries the current.
- One LS FET is held ON.

The phase current therefore always flows through **exactly two channels in series**:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 2.2 Temperature-Dependent R_ds

The temperature dependence is:

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{ds} = 1.0\ \text{m}\Omega \times 1.5 = 1.5\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 2.3 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 2·6400·0.0010 = **12.8 W** | 51.2 W |
| 125 °C | 1.5 mΩ | 2·6400·0.0015 = **19.2 W** | 76.8 W |

**Per-FET loss.** At 125 °C, the always-on LS FET dissipates 9.6 W during its 120° conduction interval. Rotation averages each FET over 1/3 of the electrical cycle on the held-on role. The worst instantaneous FET loss, however, is 9.6 W plus switching loss, and that figure sizes the junction.

**Thermal runaway check.** The positive tempco creates a feedback loop with gain:

$$G = \frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\,R_{\theta} = P_{cond}\,\alpha\,R_{\theta,JA,local}$$

The loop is stable only if G < 1. With P = 9.6 W, α = 0.005 and a local R_θ of about 10 K/W, G = 0.48. This is stable but amplifies the temperature rise by 1/(1−G) ≈ 1.9×.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches. The terms below are per channel.

**(a) V–I overlap (hard switching, HS FET):**

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \cdot 25.2 \cdot 80 \cdot 30\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

**(b) Dead-time body-diode conduction (LS FET):**

$$P_{dt} = V_f I_{ph}\,(2t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \cdot 80 \cdot 200\text{ns} \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

**(c) Reverse recovery (dissipated in HS FET at turn-on):**

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 40\text{nC}\cdot 25.2 \cdot 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.048\ W}$$

**(d) Output capacitance:**

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = \mathbf{0.018\ W}$$
