# STM32H743 FC + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #32): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan, Netlists

## 0. Architectural Ground Rules

Three corrections to the brief shape the rest of the design.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is a flight controller stack. It outputs DShot and consumes telemetry. It does not run BLDC commutation. The ESC section therefore uses **four dedicated ESC MCUs** running AM32 or BLHeli_32, such as AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071. The H743 talks to them over bi-directional DShot600.
2. **"Nano" and "80 A burst" conflict.** Twelve 5×6 mm MOSFETs, four 3-phase drivers and the H743 cannot fit on a 20×20 mm board. This design uses a **30.5×30.5 mm mount pattern on a 41×41 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz board**, as either a stacked FC/ESC pair or a single AIO board. The thermal model below shows that 80 A is a **burst (~10 s) rating**, not a continuous one.
3. **The figures are a model, not a measurement.** All numbers follow from the parameter table in §1. Swap in your actual MOSFET datasheet values and rerun. Validate everything with a thermocouple and IR camera on the first article.
