**Architectural point to settle first.** The H743 does **not** commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs, which do the 48 kHz PWM and BEMF commutation. Commutating four motors at 48 kHz with sensorless BEMF timing is not something ArduPilot supports on the FC MCU. All "48 kHz" analysis therefore applies to the ESC MCUs and power stage. All "DShot DMA" analysis applies to the H743.

## 1. Power Stage Topology (per motor)

```
V_BAT ──┬───────────┬───────────┬──────── (bulk + MLCC)
        Q_AH        Q_BH        Q_CH        high-side
        ├─ PH_A     ├─ PH_B     ├─ PH_C
        Q_AL        Q_BL        Q_CL        low-side
        └───────────┴───────────┴── R_SHUNT ── PGND
```

In each 60° commutation sector:
- One phase's high/low pair switches complementarily at 48 kHz.
- One low-side FET is held fully on.
- One phase floats for BEMF sensing.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

### 2.1 General form

At any instant the motor current I flows through exactly two conducting channels. Because synchronous rectification is used, the low-side FET carries the freewheel current instead of the body diode:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds(on)}(T_j) + I^2 R_{PCB}
$$

$$
R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{ds(on),25}\,\left[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right],\qquad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}
$$

The 2× factor is independent of duty cycle D. The high-side FET conducts for D of the PWM period and its complementary low-side FET for (1−D), so the switching pair together always presents one R_ds(on).

### 2.2 Per-FET average (for junction temperature)

Each FET is active in 2 of the 6 sectors (1/3 of the electrical cycle).

| FET role | Average conduction loss |
|---|---|
| PWM high-side | $\bar P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,D\,I^2 R$ |
| Complementary low-side | $\bar P_{LS,sync} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)\,I^2 R$ |
| Held-on low-side | $\bar P_{LS,on} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2 R$ |

80 A burst implies full throttle, so D → 1. Each FET then averages roughly

$$
\bar P_{FET} \approx \tfrac{1}{3}\,I^2 R = \tfrac{1}{3}(6400)(1.45\times10^{-3}) = 3.1\ \mathrm{W},
$$

with **9.3 W instantaneous** during its active sectors.

### 2.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

| Term | Equation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| FET channels | 2 × 80² × 1.45 mΩ | **18.6 W** |
| PCB copper + solder (R_PCB ≈ 0.4 mΩ round trip, 2 oz, multi-layer pour) | 80² × 0.4 mΩ | 2.6 W |
| Shunt (0.25 mΩ, average conduction ≈ 2/3 of time) | 80² × 0.25 mΩ × 2/3 | 1.1 W |
| **Conduction subtotal per motor** | | **≈ 22.3 W** |

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only one leg is hard-switched at a time. Use V = 25.2 V, I = 80 A, f = 48 kHz.

### 3.1 V–I overlap (hard switching)

$$
P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2}\,V\,I\,(t_r+t_f)\,f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(50\ \mathrm{ns})(48\ \mathrm{kHz}) = \mathbf{2.42\ W}
$$

### 3.2 Output capacitance

$$
P_{oss} = \tfrac{1}{2}C_{oss}V^2 f \times 2\ \text{FETs} = 0.5(1.5\,\mathrm{nF})(635)(48\,\mathrm{k})\times 2 = 0.05\ \mathrm{W}
$$

### 3.3 Body-diode reverse recovery

$$
P_{rr} = Q_{rr}\,V\,f = 50\,\mathrm{nC}\times25.2\times48\,\mathrm{k} = 0.06\ \mathrm{W}
$$

### 3.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$
P_{dt} = V_f\,I\,(2t_{dt})\,f = 0.8\times80\times200\,\mathrm{ns}\times48\,\mathrm{k} = 0.61\ \mathrm{W}
$$

### 3.5 Gate drive (lands in driver and gate resistors, not the FET)

$$
P_g = Q_g V_{gs} f \times 2 = 60\,\mathrm{nC}\times10\times48\,\mathrm{k}\times2 = 0.06\ \mathrm{W}
$$

### 3.6 Switching subtotal

| Term | Loss |
|---|---|
| V–I overlap | 2.42 W |
| Coss | 0.05 W |
| Qrr | 0.06 W |
| Dead time | 0.61 W |
| Gate drive | 0.06 W |
| **Total per motor** | **≈ 3.2 W** |

**Sensitivity:** P_sw scales linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz this term is about 1.6 W. At 96 kHz it is about 6
