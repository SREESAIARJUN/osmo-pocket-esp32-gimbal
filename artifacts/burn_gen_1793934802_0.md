# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: System Model (Job #17)

## 0. Assumptions and Corrections

Your request leaves some parameters open and contains one architectural point worth correcting. Everything below is parameterized, so you can substitute your real values.

| # | Item | Assumption / Correction |
|---|---|---|

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

R_ds(on)(T_j) = R_ds(on),25 · (T_j / 298 K)^α, with α ≈ 2.0–2.3 for trench silicon.

At T_j = 125 °C: factor ≈ 1.6. So R_on,hot = 0.9 mΩ × 1.6 = **1.44 mΩ** (max-spec basis).

### 1.2 Per-channel conduction

In 6-step with synchronous rectification, the current path at any instant is one leg-A device (HS or LS, depending on the PWM state) plus one leg-B LS device. That is always **two R_ds(on) in series**:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{on}(T_j) + I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{Cu}$$

At duty ≈ 1, a trapezoidal phase current gives I_rms ≈ I_pk = 80 A for the active pair.

- FET loss: 2 × 80² × 1.44 mΩ = **18.4 W**
- PCB copper (bus plane + phase pour + pad/via, R_Cu ≈ 0.5 mΩ): 80² × 0.5 mΩ = **3.2 W**
- **P_cond,ch ≈ 21.6 W**

### 1.3 Per-FET share

Each of the 6 FETs carries current for about 1/3 of an electrical cycle:

P_cond,FET ≈ 18.4 W / 6 ≈ **3.1 W average**, with a **9.2 W** instantaneous peak during its conduction interval.

### 1.4 Sensitivity

| R_ds(on),25 max | Package class | P_cond,ch (FETs + Cu) |
|---|---|---|
| 0.9 mΩ | 5×6 mm | 21.6 W |
| 1.5 mΩ | 3.3×3.3 mm | 33.9 W |
| 2.5 mΩ | 3.3×3.3 mm | 54.4 W |

If you use 3.3×3.3 mm FETs to fit a smaller board, conduction loss roughly doubles to triples.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg switches hard: one HS commutation per PWM period.

### 2.1 V·I overlap

Gate current during the Miller plateau:

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv} - V_{pl}}{R_{drv,src} + R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int}},\qquad I_{g,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{drv,snk} + R_{g,ext} + R_{g,int}}$$

With V_drv = 10 V, V_pl = 3.0 V, R_src = 3 Ω, R_snk = 1.5 Ω, R_g,ext = 2 Ω, R_g,int = 1 Ω:

- I_g,on = 7 / 6 = 1.17 A
- I_g,off = 3 / 4.5 = 0.67 A

Transition times use Q_sw = Q_gs2 + Q_gd = 15 nC:

- t_on = 15 nC / 1.17 A = 12.9 ns
- t_off = 15 nC / 0.67 A = 22.5 ns

Overlap loss:

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I_{ph}(t_{on}+t_{off}) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 35.4\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{1.71\ W}$$

### 2.2 Remaining terms

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Output capacitance | ½·Q_oss·V_bus·f_sw | 0.5 × 60 nC × 25.2 × 48k = **0.036 W** |
| Reverse recovery (LS body diode) | Q_rr·V_bus·f_sw | 50 nC × 25.2 × 48k = **0.060 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_F·I_ph·2·t_dt·f_sw | 0.8 × 80 × 2 × 100 ns × 48k = **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (dissipated mostly in driver/R_g) | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f_sw | 2 × 60 nC × 10 × 48k = **0.058 W** |

### 2.3 Switching totals

- **P_sw,ch ≈ 2.47 W**

At 48 kHz, switching is about 10% of the loss budget. **Conduction dominates.** Raising f_sw toward 96 kHz (for quieter motors) adds roughly 2.4 W per channel, which is acceptable.

- **Dead-time tuning is the cheapest lever.** Going from 100 ns to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W per channel.
