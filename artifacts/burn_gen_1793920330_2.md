## 0. Architecture Decisions and Red Flags

### 0.1 Partitioning: the H743 does not commutate the motors

The H743 runs ArduPilot. Four dedicated ESC MCUs run AM32, one per motor (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071). The H743 talks to them over bi-directional DShot600.

The H743 should not drive the bridges itself:
- It has only two advanced timers with complementary outputs (TIM1, TIM8), which covers two 3-phase bridges.
- ArduPilot does not run ESC commutation.

### 0.2 Red flag: "80 A burst" on a nano board

Define the rating explicitly as **80 A peak phase current per motor, ≤2–3 s, with airflow**.

| Interpretation | Battery-side current | Feasibility on nano board |
|---|---|---|
| 80 A *total* | 80 A | Credible (XT30/XT60, 2 oz Cu) |
| 80 A *per motor*, partial throttle (D≈0.5) | ~160 A total | Very marginal: connector, pads, shunt |
| 80 A *per motor*, full throttle (D≈1) | ~320 A total | **Not credible.** Exceeds connector, battery-pad copper, and board thermal mass |

The model below uses the hardest reasonable case: 80 A per motor phase, all four motors simultaneously. Section 3 shows this is survivable only as a short burst. **Continuous capability comes out at roughly 25–30 A per motor.** That figure belongs on the datasheet; marketing "burst" numbers that do not survive a thermal model should not.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step with synchronous rectification

In each 60° commutation sector, one phase is PWM'd (complementary HS/LS) and one phase has its low side held on.

- **ON interval** (duty D): current path is HS(A) → motor → LS(B), so two FETs conduct.
- **OFF interval** (1−D): current freewheels LS(A) → motor → LS(B), so two FETs conduct.

Therefore, at every instant, the phase current flows through exactly two R_DS(on) in series, independent of D (dead time neglected here; handled in §2):

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

Each FET conducts for an average of 1/3 of the electrical period at full current-squared weight, so the per-FET loss is:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,I_{ph,rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$
