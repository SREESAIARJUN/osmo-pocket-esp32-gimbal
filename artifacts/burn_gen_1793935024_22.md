# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #45): Power-Stage, Thermal, and DShot Architecture Model

## 0. Architecture Corrections

Three points in the request need correcting before the math is useful.

1. **The STM32H743 does not generate the 48 kHz motor PWM.** In a 4-in-1 ESC, each channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32. That MCU is typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The 48 kHz figure is that firmware's PWM frequency. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to the four ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600 only.
2. **"80 A burst" on a 20×20 mm board is thermally a transient, not an operating point.** Section 3 shows that four channels at 80 A cannot be sustained for more than about 1 s. The rating has to be defined as a duration-limited burst.
