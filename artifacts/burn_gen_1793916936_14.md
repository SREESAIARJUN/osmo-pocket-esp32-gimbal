# Job #59: STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (80A Burst, 48 kHz PWM, Bidirectional DShot600)

**Architecture Design Package, Rev A**

---

## 0. Scope Corrections and Key Findings

Three points need to be settled first because they change the design.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** A 4-in-1 ESC has four dedicated ESC MCUs, typically STM32G071, AT32F421 (AM32), or EFM8BB21 (BLHeli_S/Bluejay). Each one runs its own 48 kHz PWM and BEMF zero-crossing detection. The H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600 commands to them. The loss models in sections 2–4 apply to the ESC power stage. The timer/DMA model in section 6 applies to the H743.
2. **A 20×20 mm "nano" 80A board cannot sustain 80A.** With the numbers below, 80A on all four motors is about **1.0–1.3 s from 40 °C before the junction reaches 125 °C**. Realistic continuous capability is **20–25 A per motor**. The 80A figure should be specified as a ≤1 s burst at a defined start temperature, or the board should move to 30.5×30.5 mm. Section 5.5 shows the scaling.
3. **Component values are reference assumptions.** Every MOSFET, driver, and motor parameter used below is listed in section 1. Replace them with the datasheet values for the BOM you actually procure; the equations stay the same.
