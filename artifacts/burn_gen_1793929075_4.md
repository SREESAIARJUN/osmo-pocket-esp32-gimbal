# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC Nano Stack (Job #35): Thermal, Switching & Bi-Directional DShot Architecture

## 0. Scope, Partitioning & Stated Assumptions

**Architectural note:** The STM32H743 is the **flight controller (FC)**. The 48 kHz PWM and the MOSFET bridges belong to the **ESC side**. A 4-in-1 ESC runs four independent ESC MCUs (or one per channel pair) with AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. The H743 never switches FETs directly. It talks to the ESCs only via bi-directional DShot600.
