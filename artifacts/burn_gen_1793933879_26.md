## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology accounting

Assume six-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM, which is the AM32 default. For each phase leg over one electrical cycle:

- **120° "high" interval:**
  - The high-side FET conducts for duty D.
  - The low-side FET synchronously rectifies for (1−D), minus dead time.
  - The current I flows through exactly one FET of that leg at all times.
- **120° "low" interval:** the low-side FET is fully on and carries I.
- **120° floating interval:** no current (back-EMF sensing).

So each leg conducts I through one channel resistance for 2/3 of the cycle, regardless of D:

$$P_{cond,leg} = \tfrac{2}{3}\, I^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 3 \cdot \tfrac{2}{3} I^2 R_{ds} = 2\, I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

This is physically intuitive: at any instant, the motor current passes through two FETs in series.
