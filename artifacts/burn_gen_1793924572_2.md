## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Stated Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture clarification

The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. In this architecture:

- **The H743 runs ArduPilot.** It generates 4× bi-directional DShot600 streams and decodes eRPM telemetry.
- **The 4-in-1 ESC carries four dedicated ESC MCUs**, typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. They perform the 6-step trapezoidal commutation at 48 kHz PWM.
- **Combined board ("AIO")** if the FC and ESC share a PCB. The analysis is the same, but the H743 thermal budget couples to the power stage (see §4.6).

## 1. Commutation Model: Which FETs Conduct, and When

### 1.1 Leg conduction fractions

In 6-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM:

- Two phases are energized and one floats for back-EMF zero-crossing detection.
- The PWM leg switches its high-side (HS) FET at duty D. Its low-side (LS) FET conducts synchronously during (1 − D).
- The return leg's LS FET is held fully ON.
