## 0. Architecture Assumptions and an Early Feasibility Finding

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Flight controller MCU | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), SYSCLK 400 MHz, APB2 timer clock 200 MHz |
| ESC MCUs | 4 × separate ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 / STM32G071) running AM32 firmware. The H743 runs ArduPilot and does **not** commutate the motors. |
| Bus | 6S, V_bus = 25.2 V (full charge), 22.2 V nominal |
| PWM | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous rectification), 6-step trapezoidal commutation |
| Burst | 80 A **per motor channel**, burst duration to be determined (section 3) |
| MOSFET (per switch) | 40 V, R_DS(on),max = 1.2 mΩ at 25 °C and V_GS = 10 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3 class, Q_g ≈ 40 nC, Q_gd ≈ 6 nC, C_oss ≈ 1.5 nF, Q_rr ≈ 30 nC, V_SD ≈ 0.8 V |
| Form factor | "Nano" = 20×20 mm mounting, about 25×25 mm board outline, 8-layer, 2 oz copper |

**Feasibility finding.** 80 A per channel on a 20×20 AIO is a short-burst rating only. The thermal model in section 3 shows sustained operation is physically impossible at this board size. The design must therefore:
- enforce burst limits in firmware, and
- be marketed with an honest continuous rating, which comes out around 12–20 A per channel depending on airflow.
