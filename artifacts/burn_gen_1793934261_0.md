# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 80A ESC (Job #64): System Architecture, Loss Model, Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation, and Thermal Budget

## 0. Architectural Corrections

These corrections affect every calculation that follows.

1. **The H743 is the flight controller, not the ESC MCU.** In a 4-in-1 ESC, each commutation loop runs on a small dedicated MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600. The model below treats this as a two-board stack.
2. **"Nano" and "80A" are in tension.** A 20×20 mm 4-in-1 board cannot sustain 80A per motor. Section 4 shows it can survive an 80A burst for roughly 1 s, and only with aggressive copper. I model 80A as a transient rating and give the continuous rating separately.
3. **Assumed operating point.** These are explicit assumptions; replace them with datasheet values for your chosen parts.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology
The ESC uses six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM). At any instant, one high-side and one low-side FET carry the phase current. During PWM off-time, the opposite FET of the PWM leg conducts after dead time. So the current path always passes through **two R_ds,on in series**, regardless of duty cycle (excluding dead time).

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]$$

### 1.3 Conduction loss per motor channel

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{rms}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j) + I_{rms}^2 R_{pcb}$$

For flat-top trapezoidal current, I_rms ≈ I_pk. With T_j = 110 °C, R_ds = 0.9 × 1.51 = 1.36 mΩ. With R_pcb = 0.3 mΩ:

$$P_{cond} = 2(80^2)(1.36\times10^{-3}) + 80^2(0.3\times10^{-3}) = 17.4 + 1.9 = \mathbf{19.3\ W}$$

### 1.4 Electrothermal coupling (fixed-point solution)
Junction temperature and R_ds depend on each other. With T_ref equal to the board temperature T_b:

$$T_j = T_b + R_{\theta jb}\,P_{FET}(T_j), \qquad P_{FET} = I^2 R_{25}[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] + P_{sw,FET}$$

This is linear in T_j, so it has a closed form:

$$T_j = \frac{T_b + R_{\theta jb}\left[I^2R_{25}(1-25\alpha) + P_{sw,FET}\right]}{1 - R_{\theta jb} I^2 R_{25}\alpha}$$

**Thermal runaway** occurs when the denominator reaches zero, i.e. when R_θjb · I² · R₂₅ · α ≥ 1. With R_θjb = 1.5 K/W at 80A: 1.5 × 6400 × 0.9e-3 × 0.006 = **0.052**. This is far from runaway, so the FETs are stable. The limit is the board's heat capacity, not the device.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Per PWM leg (one hard-switched FET plus its synchronous partner):

| Mechanism | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw | ½·25.2·80·40 ns·48 kHz = **1.94 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 60 nC·25.2·48k = **0.07 W** |
| Output capacitance | ½·Q_oss·V·f_sw | **0.02 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_f·I·2t_d·f_sw | 0.8·80·200 ns·48k = **0.61 W** |
| Gate drive (2 FETs, dissipated in driver/R_g) | 2·Q_g·V_drv·f_sw | **0.05 W** |
| **Total P_sw per motor** | | **≈ 2.7 W** |

**Frequency sensitivity:** dP_sw/df ≈ 55 µW/Hz. Moving from 48 kHz to 24 kHz saves about 1.3 W per motor, but doubles the current ripple.

**Design note:** dead-time diode loss is about 23% of switching loss. Tightening t_d to 50 ns saves about 0.3 W per motor, but only if the gate driver's propagation-delay mismatch is under about 20 ns.

## 3. Total Loss Budget

| | Per motor | ×4 (all motors at 80A) |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FET) | 17.4 W | 69.6 W |
| Conduction (copper) | 1.9 W | 7.6 W |
| Switching | 2.7 W | 10.8 W |
| ESC MCU, regulators, sense | — | ~0.6 W |
| **Total** | **22.0 W** | **≈ 88.6 W** |

Electrical efficiency at 80A: 1 − 22/(25.2×80) ≈ **98.9%**. Efficiency is not the problem; power density is. About 89 W from a 4 cm² board is 22 W/cm².

### 3.1 Bus capacitor ripple
The worst case is D = 0.5:

$$I_{C,rms} = I\sqrt{D(1-D)} = 40\ \text{A per motor}$$

Phase-interleaving between motors is not controlled, so assume partial cancellation, giving ≈ 40–60 A_rms total. Required: an external low-ESR electrolytic of at least 1000 µF / 35 V at the pigtail, plus about 8 × 10 µF X7R 0805 ceramics distributed at each half-bridge.

| 24 FETs (or 12 dual-FET packages) + components | — | — | ~0.25 J/K |
| **Total** | | | **≈ 1.8 J/K** |

### 4.2 Board-to-ambient resistance
Assume prop-wash forced convection h ≈ 60 W/m²K, two faces plus pad conduction to the frame, giving an effective area of about 12 cm². Then R_θba ≈ **14 K/W** and τ = R·C ≈ **25 s**.

### 4.3 Transient response
For a step load P:

$$T_b(t) = T_a + P\,R_{\theta ba}\left(1-e^{-t/\tau}\right)$$

For t ≪ τ this approximates to T_b ≈ T_a + P·t/C. With P = 88.6 W, the board heats at **49 K/s**.

Per-FET junction offset above board: (8.7 + 1.35) W × 1.5 K/W ≈ **15 K**.
