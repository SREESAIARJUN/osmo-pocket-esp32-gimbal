## 0. Architecture Decisions and Assumptions

The STM32H743 is not used as the commutation controller. It is the flight controller running ArduPilot. Each of the four motor channels has its own ESC MCU running AM32, which generates the 48 kHz PWM and returns eRPM telemetry over bidirectional DShot600. This is the only realistic partitioning. ArduPilot does not perform sensorless FOC or 6-step commutation for four motors on the FC core.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A burst)

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step trapezoidal with synchronous rectification

At any instant two phases are active. In one 60° sector:

- **Phase A (PWM leg):** HS_A conducts for fraction D. LS_A conducts (synchronous rectification) for fraction (1−D), minus dead time.
- **Phase B (sink leg):** LS_B is on 100% of the sector.

The motor current I_ph therefore always passes through **exactly two MOSFET channels in series**, independent of D (excluding dead time). This holds because AM32 uses complementary PWM. If you disable complementary PWM, the freewheel current goes through the body diode, and the model in §2.3 then dominates.

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,(1+\alpha)^{(T_j-25)}, \qquad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ /^\circ\text{C}
$$

At T_j = 100 °C: (1.0055)^75 = e^{75·ln 1.0055} = e^{0.411} = **1.51**, so R_DS = 1.51 mΩ.

### 1.3 Ripple contribution

Phase current ripple at 48 kHz, assuming a 2207-class motor with L_ph-ph ≈ 15 µH, V_bus = 25.2 V, D = 0.5:

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2 \times 0.25}{15\times10^{-6}\times 48\,000} = 8.75\ \text{A}
$$

$$
I_{rms} = \sqrt{I^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}} = \sqrt{6400 + 6.38} = 80.04\ \text{A}
$$

The ripple term is negligible (+0.1%) at 48 kHz. This is one justification for 48 kHz over 24 kHz.

### 1.4 Per-channel conduction loss

$$
P_{cond,FET} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

$$
P_{shunt} = I_{rms}^2\,R_{sh}
$$

$$
P_{Cu} = I_{rms}^2\,R_{trace}
$$

Here R_trace ≈ 0.20 mΩ total path (bus plane → FET → phase pad → return), estimated for 2 oz × 8 layers with paralleled planes.
