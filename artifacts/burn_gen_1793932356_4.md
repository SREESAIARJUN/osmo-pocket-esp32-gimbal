## 0. Scope, Assumptions and One Critical Finding

**Architecture clarification.** ArduPilot runs on the STM32H743 as the flight controller. It does **not** commutate the motors. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small motor MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, for example an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The H743 talks to these MCUs over bi-directional DShot600. That is the only architecture ArduPilot supports for this function, and the rest of this document is built on it.

**"Job #5" context was not supplied.** I have therefore fixed the design parameters below. Substitute your real MOSFET datasheet values into the equations in §1–§2. The model is parametric, so results scale directly.

| Parameter | Symbol | Value used |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 25.2 V max (worst case for switching) |
| Burst phase current per channel | I_pk | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET (40 V, 3.3×3.3 DFN class) | R_ds(on),25 | 1.5 mΩ @ V_gs = 10 V |
| R_ds(on) temperature factor at 125 °C | k_T | 1.55 |
| Gate charges | Q_g / Q_gs2 / Q_gd | 30 / 3 / 6 nC |
| Body diode | V_f / Q_rr | 0.8 V / 30 nC |
| Effective output capacitance | C_oss(er) | 1.0 nF |
| Gate drive | V_drv / V_plateau | 10 V / 3.5 V |
| Gate path resistance (driver + R_g ext + R_g int) | R_on / R_off | 5 Ω / 4 Ω |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns |
| Modulation | — | 6-step trapezoidal, complementary (synchronous) PWM |

> ⚠️ **Critical finding.** On a nano board (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm), "80 A burst" can only be a **thermal-capacitance rating of roughly 1–4 s**, never a continuous one. §3 quantifies this. Whoever owns the spec should state the burst duration and the duty explicitly.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

With complementary PWM in a 6-step sector:

- The PWM'd phase alternates its high-side FET (duty D) and low-side FET (1−D).
- The return phase's low-side FET is held on.

At every instant, therefore, exactly **two FETs carry the phase current in series**.

### 1.2 Loss equations

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0055\,/\text{K} \Rightarrow k_T(125^\circ C)\approx 1.55
$$

The instantaneous conduction loss per channel is independent of D, because synchronous rectification replaces diode conduction:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = I^2\left[D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS} + R_{LS,ret}\right] = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j) + I^2 R_{pcb}
$$

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

- R_ds(125 °C) = 1.5 × 1.55 = **2.33 mΩ**
- FET conduction: P = 2 × 80² × 2.33 mΩ = **29.8 W per channel**
- PCB copper: take R_pcb ≈ 0.3 mΩ (2 oz, 6–8 layers, short pours), giving 80² × 0.3 mΩ = **1.9 W**
- **P_cond,ch ≈ 31.7 W**

### 1.4 Per-device view

| View | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Instantaneous, on a conducting FET | 80² × 2.33 mΩ | 14.9 W |
| Averaged over an electrical cycle (each FET conducts about 1/3 of the time) | — | ≈ 5 W |
