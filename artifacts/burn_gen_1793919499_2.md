## 1. FOC Inner Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

The Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, using i_a + i_b + i_c = 0) is:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

The Park transform uses the electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_off:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this rotation.

### 1.2 Plant and PI Design

The PMSM model in the dq frame (with L_d ≈ L_q = L, as for SPM gimbal motors) is:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

The cross-coupling and back-EMF terms are cancelled by feedforward:

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

After cancellation each axis reduces to G(s) = 1/(Ls + R). A PI controller C(s) = K_p + K_i/s with zero placement K_i/K_p = R/L cancels the plant pole. The closed loop then becomes first-order with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c \le \frac{2\pi f_{pwm}}{10}
$$

### 1.3 Discretization with Back-Calculation Anti-Windup

The integrator is discretized with sample period T_s = 1/f_s:

$$
u_k^{*} = K_p e_k + I_k,\qquad u_k = \mathrm{sat}(u_k^{*}),\qquad
I_{k+1} = I_k + T_s\left[K_i e_k + K_{aw}(u_k - u_k^{*})\right]
$$

Choose K_aw ≈ K_i/K_p. The voltage vector is limited circularly to the linear SVPWM range:

$$
|v_{dq}| \le \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}
$$

The d-axis has priority in this limit.

### 1.4 SVPWM by Min-Max Zero-Sequence Injection

Each phase voltage is shifted by a zero-sequence term and normalized to a duty cycle:

$$
v_{x}' = v_x - \frac{\max(v_a,v_b,v_c)+\min(v_a,v_b,v_c)}{2},\qquad
D_x = \frac12 + \frac{v_x'}{V_{bus}}
$$

## 2. Cascaded Velocity and Angle Control

### 2.1 Mechanical Plant

The rotor dynamics are:

$$
J\dot\omega = K_t i_q - B\omega - \tau_{cog}(\theta_m) - \tau_{fric}(\omega) - \tau_{dist}
$$

The torque constant is K_t = (3/2)·p·λ_m.

### 2.2 Velocity Estimation

Velocity comes from a PLL observer on the encoder angle, optionally blended with the IMU gyro.

The phase-locked loop on the wrapped angle error is:

$$
e_k = \mathrm{wrap}_{\pi}(\theta_{m,k} - \hat\theta_k)
$$

$$
\hat\omega_{k+1} = \hat\omega_k + K_{i,pll} T_s e_k
$$

$$
\hat\theta_{k+1} = \hat\theta_k + T_s(\hat\omega_k + K_{p,pll} e_k)
$$

Gains are chosen for a critically damped response at bandwidth ω_pll: K_p,pll = 2ω_pll and K_i,pll = ω_pll².

The stabilized axis also has a gyro rate ω_g projected onto the motor axis. A complementary blend uses the gyro at high frequency, since it measures the *inertial* rate the gimbal must reject:

$$
\omega_{fb} = \mathrm{LPF}_{f_x}(\hat\omega_{enc}) + \mathrm{HPF}_{f_x}(\omega_g)
$$

### 2.3 Velocity PI

The velocity controller combines PI feedback, inertia feedforward, and cogging feedforward:

$$
i_q^* = \underbrace{K_{pv} e_\omega + K_{iv}\!\int e_\omega}_{\text{PI}} + \underbrace{\frac{J}{K_t}\alpha_{ref}}_{\text{inertia FF}} + \underbrace{i_{q,cog}(\theta_m)}_{\text{cogging FF}}
$$
