## 0. Assumptions and a Scope Flag

### 0.1 What "80A Burst" Means

On a nano form factor (20×20 mm mounting, about 26×26 mm board), **80 A per channel is not thermally survivable for more than about 1–3 s**, as §3 shows. The analysis is therefore carried at two operating points:

| Case | Meaning | Per-channel I_burst |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 80 A per motor (aggressive marketing-style rating) | 80 A |
| **B** | 80 A total battery burst (typical for nano class) | 20 A |

Case A shows *why* the board will fail. Case B is the realistic design point.

### 0.2 Architecture

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, driving a 3-phase gate driver and 6 N-MOSFETs. The H743 does **not** commutate motors. It emits bidirectional DShot600 and receives eRPM telemetry.
