## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

An STM32H743 cannot directly commutate four BLDC motors at 48 kHz with closed-loop BEMF sensing while also running ArduPilot. The architecture is therefore:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100), 400 MHz | ArduPilot. Generates 4× bidirectional DShot600. |
| ESC MCUs (×4) | AT32F421K8U7 (QFN32) | AM32 firmware. 48 kHz complementary PWM, bidir DShot decode, eRPM telemetry. |
| Gate drivers (×4) | FD6288Q (3-phase, bootstrap) | ~1.5 A source / 1.8 A sink |
| Power stage (×4) | 6× N-MOSFET, 40 V, PQFN 5×6 or 3.3×3.3 | Three half-bridges per motor |

**Representative MOSFET parameters.** Swap in the datasheet values of your selected part. Every equation below is parametric.

| Symbol | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| R_DS(on),25 | 1.0 mΩ @ V_GS = 10 V | On-resistance |
| α | 0.007 /K | R_DS(on) temperature coefficient |
| Q_g | 50 nC | Total gate charge |
| Q_gs2 + Q_gd | 6 + 10 = 16 nC | Switching charge Q_sw |
| V_plateau | 4.5 V | Miller plateau |
| R_g,int | 1.0 Ω | Internal gate resistance |
| Q_oss | 35 nC | Output charge at 25 V |
| Q_rr | 40 nC | Body diode reverse recovery |
| V_SD | 0.8 V | Body diode forward voltage |
| R_θJC | 1.0 K/W | Junction-to-case |

**Operating point.** 6S, V_bus,max = 25.2 V. I_phase = 80 A burst per motor. f_sw = 48 kHz. Dead time t_dt = 50 ns. Gate drive V_DRV = 12 V. R_g,ext = 3 Ω.

> **Reality check:** "Nano" (20×20 mm) cannot carry 4×80 A. This design assumes a 30.5×30.5 mm mount on a ~37×37 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz board. 80 A is treated strictly as a **burst rating (≤ 5 s)**, which the thermal section quantifies.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous (complementary) PWM, exactly two half-bridge legs carry the phase current at any time:

- **PWM leg:** current flows through the high-side switch (HS) for duty D and the synchronous low-side switch (LS) for (1−D).
- **Return leg:** its LS is on continuously during the 60° sector.

At any instant, current therefore flows through **exactly two MOSFET channels in series**, regardless of D (ignoring dead time).
