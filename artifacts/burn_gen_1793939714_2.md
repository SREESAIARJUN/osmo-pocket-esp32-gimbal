# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 80 A 4-in-1 ESC: Power, Thermal, and DShot Design Package (Job #7)

## 0. Architecture Issues to Resolve First

I have no prior context for Job #7, so this package is built on stated assumptions (§1). Three points shape the rest of the design:

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no ESC commutation firmware. The standard architecture, used here, is one dedicated ESC MCU per motor (AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to these MCUs. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, not the H743.
2. **80 A on a nano (20×20 mm) board is a burst rating only, and a short one.** The math below shows about 100 W of dissipation at 4×80 A. A 20×20 board has roughly 2.3 J/K of heat capacity, so a full burst is limited to about 1.5 s. Continuous ratings in this form factor are realistically 35–45 A. If the 80 A figure is a marketing requirement, I recommend a 30×30 mm board, or 25.5×25.5 mm with a heat-spreader.
3. **24 power FETs, 4 ESC MCUs, the H743 and the IMU will not fit on one 20×20 board.** Plan a two-board stack: FC board on top, ESC board below, joined by an 8-pin board-to-board connector.

**Commutation scheme:** six-step trapezoidal with complementary (synchronous) PWM.
- Two FETs carry the motor current at every instant: one in the PWM'd leg (high-side or low-side) and the static low-side of the return leg.
- Each phase's high-side PWMs for 1/3 of the electrical cycle, and its low-side is fully on for 1/3.

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

**Temperature-dependent Rds(on):**

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At the target T_j = 120 °C: R_ds = 1.2 × 1.4275 = **1.713 mΩ**.

**Ripple correction:**

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2\left(1+\frac{r^2}{12}\right), \quad r = \Delta I / I$$

For a low-inductance quad motor at 48 kHz, r ≈ 0.1, so the correction factor is 1.0008. This is neglected below.

**Per-motor conduction loss** (two FETs always in the current path):

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2I^2R_{ds} = 2(80)^2(1.713\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{21.9\ W}$$

**Per-FET split at D = 0.9.** First compute I²R = 10.96 W.
- High-side conducts for D/3 of the cycle: P_HS = 0.300 × 10.96 = **3.29 W**
- Low-side conducts for 1/3 + (1−D)/3 = 0.367 of the cycle: P_LS = 0.367 × 10.96 = **4.02 W**
