# STM32H743 FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC: Loss Model, Thermal Budget, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Allocation

> **Note:** I don't have prior context for "Job #2," so I've stated every assumption explicitly. Replace the assumed MOSFET and PCB parameters with your datasheet values. The equations are written to be re-evaluated directly.

## 0. Architecture Clarification

An STM32H743 does not commutate motors in this class of product. The practical partition is:

| Block | Device | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (TFBGA100) or VIT6 (LQFP100) | ArduPilot; emits bi-directional DShot600 |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421 / STM32G071 (or one per channel) running AM32 or BLHeli_32 | Six-step commutation at **48 kHz PWM** |
| Gate drive ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase, 250 V, bootstrap) | Drives 6 N-FETs per channel |
| Power stage | 24× 40 V N-MOSFET, DFN5x6 or DFN3.3x3.3 | 6 per motor |

The 48 kHz switching losses therefore live on the ESC side. The H743's job is the DShot protocol timing and DMA allocation (§4).

### Form-factor warning

24 power FETs plus a 320 A aggregate bus do not physically or thermally fit a 20×20 mm "nano" board. §3 shows the math. The minimum credible footprint for 80 A burst is 30.5×30.5 mm, with an 8-layer, 2–3 oz copper stack-up. Everything below assumes that footprint.

## 2. Electrical Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Temperature-corrected on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] = 1.2\,\text{mΩ}\times1.45 = 1.74\ \text{mΩ}$$

### 2.2 Conduction loss (six-step, HS-PWM with synchronous rectification)

Over 360° electrical, each phase is driven high for 120°, low for 120°, and floats for 2×60°:

- During its **high** interval, the HS FET conducts for D and the LS FET (synchronous rectification) for (1−D).
- During its **low** interval, the LS FET conducts 100%.

Per-device losses:

$$P_{HS}=\frac{I^2R\,D}{3},\qquad P_{LS}=\frac{I^2R\,(2-D)}{3}$$

Summing over 3 phases, exactly two FETs are always in the current path:

$$\boxed{P_{cond}=2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)} = 2(80)^2(1.74\text{ mΩ}) = \mathbf{22.3\ W}$$

Per device at D = 0.9:
- P_LS = 11.14 × 1.1 / 3 = **4.
