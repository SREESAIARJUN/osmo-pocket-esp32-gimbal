## 0. System Assumptions & Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Motors | 3× gimbal BLDC (e.g. 12N14P, 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5–15 Ω, L ≈ 1–5 mH |
| Drive | 3-phase inverter with 2-shunt or inline current sensing, center-aligned PWM |
| Rotor sensing | 14-bit magnetic encoder per axis (AS5048A / MT6701 class) |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI @ 24 MHz, ODR 8 kHz, FIFO time-stamped |
| Kinematic order | Base → **Yaw (z)** → **Roll (x)** → **Pitch (y)** → Camera |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with single-precision FPU (STM32G4/H7 class) |

**Loop rates (one ISR tree, phase-locked to PWM):**

```
PWM 20 kHz ──► Current loop (FOC)        20 kHz   T_c = 50 µs
           └─► ÷5  Velocity loop          4 kHz   T_v = 250 µs
           └─► ÷20 Angle loop + attitude  1 kHz   T_a = 1 ms
IMU DRDY 8 kHz ──► FIFO read, AAF'd data → fusion prefilter (decimated to 4 kHz)
```

Rule of thumb for the bandwidth hierarchy: each outer loop runs at ≤ 1/4–1/5 of the inner loop's closed-loop bandwidth:

$$\omega_{c,i} \approx 2\pi\cdot 1.5\text{ kHz},\quad \omega_{c,v} \approx 2\pi\cdot 150\text{ Hz},\quad \omega_{c,\theta} \approx 2\pi\cdot 30\text{ Hz}$$

## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Current Loop)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Here $p$ is the pole-pair count and $\theta_{off}$ is the encoder offset found by alignment (inject $i_d$ at $\theta_e = 0$ and read $\theta_m$).

**Latency compensation:** PWM update occurs about 1.5 periods after sampling, so the angle used for the inverse Park transform is advanced:

$$\theta_e^{inv} = \theta_e + \omega_e \cdot 1.5\,T_c$$

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, 2 shunts)

$$i_\alpha = i_a, \qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.4 PMSM model in the dq frame

$$v_d = R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e
