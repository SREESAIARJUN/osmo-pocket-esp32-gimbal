# Job #57: STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC "Nano" Stack

## Design Model: 80 A Burst Thermal, 48 kHz Switching, and ArduPilot Bidirectional DShot600

---

## 0. Scope, Architecture, and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architectural clarification

The STM32H743 is the **flight controller (FC)** MCU. A 4-in-1 ESC does not commutate motors from the H743. Each motor channel (or the whole board) runs its own ESC MCU, typically STM32G071, AT32F421, or GD32 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 generates bidirectional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry. The ESC MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM that drives the MOSFETs.
