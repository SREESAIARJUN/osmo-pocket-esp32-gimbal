## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

| Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|
| The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. ArduPilot has no BLDC commutation firmware for H7. | **Topology:** H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It drives 4× bi-directional DShot600 lines to a 4-in-1 ESC section. Each ESC channel has its own MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071) running AM32 or BLHeli_32. |
