**Key conclusion up front:** 80 A per motor is a **burst** rating only, and the thermal budget limits it to about 2 s. Continuous capability on this form factor is about **25–28 A per motor**. The derivation is below.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance
R_DS(on)(T_j) = R₂₅ · [1 + α(T_j − 25)], with α ≈ 0.0045 /K

- At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.2 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.74 mΩ**
- At T_j = 85 °C: R = **1.52 mΩ**

### 1.2 Six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous rectification
In each 60° sector, two phases carry current I:

- **PWM phase:** The high side conducts for D·T. The complementary low side conducts for (1−D)·T. Together this phase always presents one R_DS in the path.
- **Static phase:** The low side is fully on and presents one R_DS.

**Total conduction loss per ESC (independent of D):**

P_cond = 2 · I² · R_DS(T_j)

At 80 A and 125 °C: P_cond = 2 × 6400 × 1.74 × 10⁻³ = **22.3 W per ESC**

### 1.3 Per-FET distribution, averaged over 360°
Each phase spends 120° high-active, 120° low-active, and 120° floating.

- P_HS = (1/3) · D · I² · R
- P_LS = (1/3) · [1 + (1 − D)] · I² · R  (low side is static for half its active time and complementary for the rest)

At D = 0.95:
- P_HS ≈ 3.53 W
- P_LS ≈ 3.90 W

The **low-side FETs are the thermal hotspot**. Place them closest to the thermal vias and ground pour.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

| Term | Equation | Value (80 A, 25.2 V) |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw | ½ × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48 k = **1.94 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw | 50 nC × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.06 W** |
| Output capacitance (×2 FETs per leg) | 2 · ½ · C_oss · V² · f_sw | **0.05 W** |
| Gate drive (×2 FETs) | 2 · Q_g · V_GS · f_sw | 2 × 50 nC × 10 V × 48 k = **0.05 W** (dissipated in the driver and R_g) |
| Dead-time body-diode conduction | V_F · I · 2t_dt · f_sw, with t_dt = 100 ns, V_F = 0.8
