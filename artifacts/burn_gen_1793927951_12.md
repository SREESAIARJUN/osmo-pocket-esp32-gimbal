# Job #53: 3-Axis Gimbal FOC Control Loop and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion

This document covers the plant model, the FOC current loop, the cascaded angle/velocity PID, anti-cogging feedforward, dual ICM-42688-P vibration rejection, and a C++17 implementation.

**Assumptions (adjust to your hardware):**
- Motor: 12N14P gimbal BLDC/PMSM (7 pole pairs) with inline current sensing on 2 phases.
- Rotor feedback: 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder per axis.
- MCU: ARM Cortex-M4F/M7 with an FPU, or similar.
- Sensors: two ICM-42688-P IMUs on the camera payload, each on its own SPI bus.

---

## 1. Timing Architecture

| Loop | Rate | Ts | Input | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | 50 µs | i_a, i_b, θ_enc | PWM duty |
| IMU acquisition | 8 kHz (ODR) | 125 µs | 2× FIFO / DRDY | fused ω, a |
| Velocity | 4 kHz | 250 µs | ω_gyro (fused, filtered) | i_q* |
| Angle / attitude | 1 kHz | 1 ms | AHRS quaternion | ω* |

Bandwidth separation rule: each outer loop's crossover should be at most 1/5 to 1/10 of the inner loop's crossover. Typical targets:
- Current loop: ω_c,i ≈ 2π·2 kHz
- Velocity loop: ω_c,v ≈ 2π·150 Hz
- Angle loop: ω_c,θ ≈ 2π·20 Hz

**Clock synchronization:** feed both IMUs a common 32.768 kHz clock on CLKIN (pin 9, with RTC_MODE=1
