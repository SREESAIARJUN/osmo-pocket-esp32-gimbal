## 0. Assumptions and Timing Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (pole pairs p = 7), R = 5 Ω, L = 2 mH (phase), K_t ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701-class), SPI |
| DC bus | 12 V; PWM 20 kHz center-aligned |
| IMUs | 2× ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, SPI, gyro/accel ODR 8 kHz |
| MCU | Cortex-M4F/M7 with FPU (single-precision `float`) |

The control is a cascade of loops at decreasing rates:

```
 20 kHz  ISR: ADC → Clarke/Park → PI(id,iq) → invPark → SVPWM         (current loop, BW ≈ 1.5 kHz)
  8 kHz  IMU FIFO → dual-IMU fusion → notch/LPF chain
  4 kHz  encoder PLL, velocity PI (+ cogging FF, + inertial-rate feedback) (BW ≈ 150 Hz)
  1 kHz  attitude estimator (Mahony) → angle P + velocity FF          (BW ≈ 30 Hz)
```

**Bandwidth separation rule:** each loop's bandwidth should be at most ~1/5–1/10 of the inner loop's bandwidth, and at most ~1/10 of its own sample rate.

## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

**Clarke transform:**

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b),\qquad (i_a+i_b+i_c=0)$$

**Park transform:**

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e$$

### 1.2 Electrical angle with delay compensation

The electrical angle comes from the mechanical angle, the pole-pair count, and the calibrated encoder offset:

$$\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}$$

The PWM update lags the sample by about 1.5 periods (one for compute, half for the PWM zero-order hold). The angle used in the inverse Park transform is advanced accordingly:

$$\theta_e^{\text{inv}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,T_s\,\omega_e$$
