# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 80 A ESC (Job #53): Loss, Thermal, and DShot Architecture

## 0. Feasibility Findings

These four points constrain everything that follows.

1. **The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In a "4-in-1 ESC" architecture, the H743 runs ArduPilot and sends bidirectional DShot600. Each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32-class firmware). That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM, does commutation, and returns eRPM telemetry. The models below follow that split.
2. **80 A is a burst rating, not continuous.** The thermal model in §3 shows that a 30.5 × 30.5 mm board survives roughly 3–5 s with all four channels at 80 A. Continuous capability is about 35–40 A per channel under prop-wash
