# FOC Motor Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion for a 3-Axis Gimbal (Job #23)

I don't have the Job #23 spec, so this design rests on the assumptions in §0. Every assumed number is a named constant in the code, so you can substitute your real values.

---

## 0. Assumptions and Architecture

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| MCU | STM32G474 class: Cortex-M4F at 170 MHz, single-precision FPU, 3 synchronized advanced timers |
| Motors | Gimbal BLDC, 12N14P (p = 7 pole pairs), R ≈ 5.6 Ω phase-phase, L ≈ 2.2 mH, Kt ≈ 0.09 N·m/A |
| Position sensor | 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder (AS5048A / MT6701), read over SPI every current-loop tick |
| Current sensing | 2 low-side shunts (or inline), sampled at PWM center |
| IMU0 | ICM-42688-P on the camera plate. This is the stabilization feedback. |
| IMU1 | ICM-42688-P on the base/handle. This is the vibration reference and the disturbance feedforward. |
| Bus | Vbus = 12 to 16 V |
