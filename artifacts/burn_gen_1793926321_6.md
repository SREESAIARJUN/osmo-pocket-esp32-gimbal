## 0. Architecture Decisions and Constraints

Before the math, two conclusions shape everything else.

1. **80 A is a burst rating only.** On a nano footprint (20×20 mm mounting, ~26×26 mm PCB), 80 A phase current sustained on all four channels requires about 130 W of dissipation. Section 3 shows that this is roughly a **2–3 s burst capability**. The continuous rating that follows from the math is about 20–25 A per channel. Marketing must not claim 80 A continuous.
2. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** Bidirectional DShot with ArduPilot implies a dedicated ESC MCU per channel. The design uses an AT32F421 running AM32 with EDT enabled. The H743 sends DShot600 and receives GCR-encoded eRPM telemetry on the same pin.

**Physical stack.** Two-board stack, 20×20 mm M2 mounting:
- **Top board (FC):** STM32H743VIT6, IMU, baro, OSD, flash. 6-layer, 1 oz.
- **Bottom board (ESC):** 4× AT32F421, 4× FD6288Q gate driver, 24× N-MOSFET. 6-layer, 3 oz outer, 2 oz inner.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Commutation topology

The ESC uses six-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In any sector, two legs carry current:
- **PWM leg:** the high side (HS) conducts for fraction D; the low side (LS) freewheels for (1−D).
- **Static leg:** the LS is on for 100% of the sector.
- **Floating leg:** used for back-EMF sensing.

Losses per sector:

$$
P_{cond} = \underbrace{I_{rms}^2 R_{DS}\,D}_{HS_{PWM}} + \underbrace{I_{rms}^2 R_{DS}(1-D)}_{LS_{PWM}} + \underbrace{I_{rms}^2 R_{DS}}_{LS_{static}} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

With synchronous rectification, conduction loss is **independent of duty cycle**. Two R_DS(on) are always in series with the phase current.

### 1.2 Ripple correction

The phase current ripple depends on motor inductance L_ph (line-to-line):

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-E)\,D}{L_{ph} f_{sw}},\qquad I_{rms}^2 = I_{ph}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}
$$

Take a typical 5-inch motor (L_ph ≈ 15 µH) at worst case D = 0.5 with E ≈ V_bus·D:

$$
\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{12.6 \times 0.5}{15\times10^{-6}\times 48000} = 8.75\text{ A} \;\Rightarrow\; \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6.4\text{ A}^2
$$

This is negligible against 6400 A², so I_rms ≈ 80 A.

### 1.3 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{DS(on),25}\,\big[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\text{ K}^{-1}
$$

$$
R_{DS(on)}(125^\circ\text{C}) = 1.5 \times 1.6 = 2.4\text{ mΩ}
$$

### 1.4 Result per channel at 80 A

$$
P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.4\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{30.7\text{ W}}\quad(19.2\text{ W at }25^\circ\text{C})
$$
