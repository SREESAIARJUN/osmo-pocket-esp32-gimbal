# STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC): Power-Stage Thermal Model, Switching Losses, and Bi-Directional DShot600 Timer/DMA Allocation

## 0. Assumptions and Constraints

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 is the flight controller and runs ArduPilot. It does **not** commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU, assumed here to be an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32. That MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM, does BEMF zero-cross detection, and replies with eRPM telemetry over bi-directional DShot. A single H743 cannot practically commutate four sensorless BLDC motors at 48 kHz while also running the EKF.

**Unverified MOSFET parameters.** The MOSFET values below are parametric targets for a 40 V PDFN 3.3×3.3 device, which is the only package that fits 16 FETs on a 20×20 or 25.5×25.5 board. Every number tagged **[A]** is an assumption. Replace it with your chosen part's datasheet values before signing off the design.

## 1. Conduction-Loss Model

### 1.1 Topology current paths
For six-step trapezoidal commutation with synchronous (complementary) PWM, two legs conduct in every 60° sector:

- **PWM leg:** the high side (HS) conducts during D·T_sw. The low side (LS) conducts during (1−D)·T_sw through the channel, not the body diode, apart from dead time.
- **Return leg:** the LS is held on for the whole sector.

The current I flows continuously through exactly two channels in series:

$$P_{cond,ch}(I,T_j) = I^2\big[D\,R_{HS} + (1-D)\,R_{LS}\big] + I^2 R_{LS} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

This result is **independent of duty**, which is the key property of synchronous rectification. Adding copper:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2I^2\big[R_{ds,25}(1+\alpha_R(T_j-25)) + R_{Cu,path}\big]$$
