## 0. Architecture and Baseline Assumptions

ArduPilot runs on the H743 and *emits* DShot. The STM32H743 does **not** commutate the motors. A bidirectional-DShot 4-in-1 therefore requires four dedicated ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware.

   TIM1_CH1..4 ──BDShot600──► AT32F421 ×4 (AM32 firmware, 48 kHz complementary PWM)

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

At any instant, two phases conduct.

- **Switching leg:** current flows through the HS FET for D·T_sw and the LS FET (synchronous) for (1−D)·T_sw.
- **Static leg:** the LS FET conducts for the full period.

$$
P_{cond,bridge}= I^2R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D+(1-D)] + I^2R_{ds}(T_j) = 2\,I^2R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

The result is **independent of duty**. This is the key reason burst capability collapses on small boards.

Each FET conducts for 120° electrical (1/3 of the electrical period), so:

$$
P_{cond,FET,avg} = \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad P_{cond,FET,inst,max}= I^2R_{ds}(T_j)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on): self-consistent solution

$$
R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]

$$
T_j = T_{pcb} + P_{FET}(T_j)\cdot R_{th,j\text{-}pcb}
