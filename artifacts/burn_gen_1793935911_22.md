## 0. Architecture

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. ArduPilot does not include ESC firmware, and running four sensorless 6-step or FOC loops on the flight-control MCU alongside the EKF would compromise both. The standard partition is:

```
[STM32H743 FC, ArduPilot] --4x BDShot600--> [4x ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071), AM32 or BLHeli_32]
                                                 |--> 3-phase gate driver --> 6x N-MOSFET per motor
```

"Nano" means a 20×20 mm or 30×30 mm stack. Section 4 shows that 80A per channel on that footprint is a short-duration burst rating, not a continuous one. That is the most important design conclusion here.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 1.0 × (1 + 0.005 × 85) = **1.425 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification

In each 60° commutation sector, one phase is PWM'd and one phase is held low. Within the PWM'd phase:

- The high-side FET conducts for duty fraction D: loss = I² R D.
- The complementary low-side FET (synchronous rectification) conducts for 1 − D: loss = I² R (1 − D).
- The return phase low-side FET is fully on: loss = I² R.

Ignoring the dead-time portion, which is handled in §2:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I^2R(D) + I^2R(1-D) + I^2R = 2I^2R_{ds}(T_j)$$

The loss is **independent of D**. This is a key result: at 80A the conduction loss is the same at low and high throttle, if the motor can actually draw 80A.

Over a full electrical cycle, each of the 6 FETs averages P_cond/6. The instantaneous peak loss on the statically-on low-side FET is I²R.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80A, T_j = 110 °C

- P_cond,ch = 2 × 80² × 1.425 mΩ = **18.24 W per channel**
- Per FET (average over the cycle) = 3.04 W
- Per FET (instantaneous peak) = 9.12 W
- Shunt: I² R_sh = 6400 × 0.25 mΩ = **1.60 W**
- Copper: I² R_Cu = 6400 × 0.5 mΩ = **3.20 W**

Conduction-class subtotal: **23.0 W per channel, 92.2 W per board.**

### 1.4 Thermal-runaway check (R_ds feedback)

$$T_j = T_{pcb} + P_{FET}(T_j)\,Z_{th,jc}$$

This converges only if

$$\alpha\,P_{FET,25}\,Z_{th} < 1$$

With P_FET ≈ 6.4 W (at 25 °C), Z_th ≈ 1 K/W, and α = 0.005, the product is 0.032. So the junction-to-board loop is stable. **The instability is board-level** (§4), not at the junction.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Turn-on:

$$I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_g} = \frac{10-3.5}{5} = 1.30\text{ A}, \qquad t_{on} = \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_{g,on}} = \frac{12}{1.30} = 9.2\text{ ns}$$
