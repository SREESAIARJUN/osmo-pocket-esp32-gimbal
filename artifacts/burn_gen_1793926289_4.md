## 0. Architectural Ground Rules and Corrections

Three facts about the brief drive the rest of the design.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** A 4-in-1 ESC needs a dedicated real-time MCU per channel for sensorless 6-step control. The H743 is the flight controller and sends DShot600 to four ESC MCUs. This design uses **AT32F421K8U7 running AM32**. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, not the H743.
2. **"Nano" and "80A burst" conflict.** A 20×20 mm board cannot physically carry 24 FETs plus bulk capacitance at 80A per channel. This design assumes the **30.5×30.5 mm footprint** with an 8-layer, 2 oz board. Section 3 shows that even this board only survives 80A as a **short burst of about 1 to 2 seconds**.
3. **Interpretation of "80A".** 80A is treated as **peak phase current per motor**. Battery current is D·I_ph, so the pack sees about 300A at full burst on all four motors. Your pack and XT90 connector must support this, or you must lower the rating.

All semiconductor values below are representative of a 40V, 3.3×3.3 mm DFN MOSFET class. **Replace them with the datasheet values of your selected part.** The equations stay valid.
