**Document scope:** This covers the conduction and switching loss model for 80 A burst, the thermal transient model, the ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and a reference netlist.

## 0. Architectural Clarification (Read First)

On a "4-in-1 ESC + FC" AIO, **the STM32H743 does not commutate the motors.** The partitioning is:

| Domain | Silicon | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz, Cortex-M7) | ArduPilot | Attitude control, DShot600 master, telemetry decode |
| ESC ×4 | 4× AT32F421 / STM32G071 (or one MCU per channel) | AM32 (or BLHeli_32-class) | 6-step commutation, 48 kHz PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Power stage ×4 | 3 half-bridges per motor (12 half-bridges, 24 MOSFETs) | — | Power conversion |

The 48 kHz switching analysis therefore applies to the ESC MCUs and power stage. The DShot/DMA analysis applies to the H743
