## 0. Feasibility Findings

The 80 A requirement does not fit a nano form factor. Sections 2–4 show the following:

| Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|
| At 80 A per motor, total loss is about **28 W per channel (~110 W board)** with good 40 V FETs | A 20×20 mm or 25.5×25.5 mm board has a heat capacity of about 5 J/K, so it **rises ~20 K/s** |
| Steady-state convective dissipation of a 30×30 board in prop wash is about 0.2 W/K | 80 A on all four channels **cannot be continuous**. It is valid only as a **≤1–2 s burst**, starting from a cool board |
| The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors | Each channel needs its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 / AM32, or EFM8BB51 / Bluejay). The H743 only generates DShot and reads eRPM telemetry |

**Recommended specification:** 30.5×30.5 mm board, 6S, 40 V FETs, **45 A continuous / 80 A burst for 1 s per motor**. Burst duration is enforced by the ESC firmware's temperature limiting and by ArduPilot's `BATT_*` / current limiting.

All component values below are **design assumptions**. Replace them with datasheet values for the parts you actually select, and validate with thermal imaging.

## 2. Conduction-Loss Model

### 2.1 Current path

The ESC uses trapezoidal (6-step) drive with synchronous rectification ("complementary PWM", the AM32/Bluejay default). In any 60° sector, current flows from one phase to another:

- **PWM on (fraction D):** current flows through HS(x) and LS(y). That is 2 FETs.
- **PWM off (1−D):** current flows through LS(x) (synchronous rectifier) and LS(y). That is also 2 FETs.

Therefore, at every instant, exactly **two FET channels** carry the phase current I_ph, ignoring dead time.

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph,rms}^2\, R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 2.2 Ripple correction

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L_{\phi\phi}\, f_{sw}}, \qquad I_{rms}^2 = I_{avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}$$

The worst case is D = 0.5, with V_bus = 25.2 V, L_φφ = 20 µH, and f_sw = 48 kHz:

- ΔI_pp = 25.2·0.25 / (20e-6·48e3) = **6.6 A**
- ΔI²/12 = 3.6 A²

This is negligible against 6400 A² (+0.06 %). Using I_rms ≈ 80 A is valid.

### 2.3 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

This gives about 1.6× at 125 °C.
