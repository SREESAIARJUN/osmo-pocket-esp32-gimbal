| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The H743 should not commutate the motors directly. It has only two advanced timers (TIM1, TIM8) with complementary outputs, so it cannot drive 4×3 half-bridges with dead time. HRTIM could, but no ArduPilot or AM32 support exists. | Use the H743 as the flight controller and **4× AT32F421 (AM32)** as ESC MCUs. They are linked by bi-directional DShot600. |
| 2 | 80 A per motor on four motors at once is about **88 W of silicon loss** and **up to ~320 A battery current**. | On a nano-class PCB (20×20 or 25.5×25.5 mm) this is a **≈1–2 s transient rating**, not a continuous one. Continuous rating with good prop-wash is about **25–30 A/motor**. |
| 3 | A single XT60 or 12 AWG lead cannot carry 320 A. | Specify the "80 A burst" as **per-motor, not simultaneous**, or enforce firmware current limiting. |
| 4 | At 48 kHz, switching loss is minor compared with conduction loss (≈1.5 W vs ≈19 W per motor at 80 A). Dead-time diode loss is comparable to switching loss. | Optimise R<sub>DS(on)</sub> and copper first. Gate speed comes second. |

## 3. Conduction Loss Model

### 3.1 Temperature-dependent resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

At T<sub>j</sub> = 125 °C, R<sub>DS</sub> = 1.0 × 1.5 = **1.5 mΩ**.

### 3.2 Trapezoidal 6-step operation with synchronous rectification

In every commutation sector, current flows through exactly two FET channels:
- the PWM'd phase, through either its high-side FET (on-time) or its complementary low-side FET (off-time);
- the static low-side FET of the return phase.

Ignoring dead time:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 3.3 Result at 80 A

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{19.2\ W\ per\ motor}$$

### 3.4 Per-FET distribution

Over one electrical cycle, each FET carries current for about 1/3 of the period:

$$P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{19.2}{6}\cdot\frac{6}{6} \approx 3.2\ \text{W}$$
