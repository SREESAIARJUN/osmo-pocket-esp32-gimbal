## 1. FOC Inner Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant):
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)\quad(\text{with } i_a+i_b+i_c=0)$$

Park transform, with $\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}$:
$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant and PI Tuning (Pole-Zero Cancellation)

The dq voltage equations of an SPMSM ($L_d = L_q = L$) are:
$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$
