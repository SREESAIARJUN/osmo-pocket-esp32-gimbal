# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 80 A ESC: Engineering Reference Model (Job #31)

I don't have the Job #31 requirements document, so every assumed parameter is marked **[A]**. Replace these with your real values. The equations still hold, but the numbers will change.

| Parameter | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal **[A]** |
| Board format | "Nano" taken as a 20×20 mm mounting pattern, ~26×26 mm outline **[A]** |
| ESC MCU | AM32 firmware on 4× AT32F421 (one per motor) **[A]** |
| MOSFET | 40 V, R_DS(on) 1.0 mΩ at 25 °C, Q_g 50 nC, Q_rr 40 nC, Q_oss 30 nC, R_θJC ≈ 1.5 K/W, 3.3×3.3 mm PQFN **[A]** |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal with synchronous rectification **[A]** |

Two architectural points need correcting before any math:

1. **The STM32H743 does not drive the FETs.** ArduPilot is flight-control firmware, not ESC firmware. The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to four separate ESC MCUs (AM32 or BLHeli_32). Those ESC MCUs run the 48 kHz PWM and commutation. The DMA analysis (§4) therefore applies to the H743, and the loss analysis (§2–3) applies to the ESC power stage.
2. **"80 A burst" on a 20×20 board is a seconds-long transient rating, not a continuous one.** §3 shows that four channels at 80 A dissipate about 90 W into roughly 2.3 J/K of thermal mass. That is survivable for about 1 s. It will not survive a sustained punch-out. Specify the rating as "80 A for ≤1 s, single channel" or move to a 30×30 board.

## 1. Power Stage Topology

Each motor channel is a 3-phase bridge of 6 FETs, so the board has 24 FETs in total.

In 6-step drive, one phase is held low and another is PWM'd high. At any instant, two FETs carry the full phase current I:
- **PWM on-time (D):** high-side of phase A plus low-side of phase B.
- **Off-time (1−D):** low-side of phase A (synchronous rectification) plus low-side of phase B.

**DC bus current:**

$$I_{bus} = D \cdot I_{ph}$$

**Input capacitor RMS ripple current** (worst case at D = 0.5):

$$I_{C,rms} = I_{ph}\sqrt{D(1-D)} = 80 \times 0.5 = 40\ \text{A per channel}$$

This 40 A RMS per channel is a key layout driver:
- Place at least 4× 10 µF X7R 50 V 1206 ceramics directly across each half-bridge pair.
- Add one shared low-ESR bulk capacitor (≥470 µF, 35 V) on the pigtail.
- The ESC MCUs' PWM is not synchronized, so treat ripple as uncorrelated: total ≈ √4 × 40 = 80 A RMS at the bulk input.

## 2. Loss Model (Per Channel, 80 A Phase Current)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

R_DS(on) rises with temperature:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.004\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

Two FETs are always in the current path, so:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{DS}(T_j)$$

| T_j | R_DS(on) | P_cond per channel |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.00 mΩ | 12.8 W |
| 100 °C | 1.30 mΩ | 16.6 W |
| 125 °C | 1.40 mΩ | 17.9 W |

Use **18 W** as the hot design value.

### 2.2 Switching Loss at f_sw = 48 kHz

Only the PWM'd leg hard-switches. With synchronous rectification, one hard turn-on and one hard turn-off occur per period:

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

With t_r = t_f = 20 ns **[A]**, which depends on gate-driver strength and R_g:

$$P_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = 1.94\ \text{W}$$

### 2.3 Reverse Recovery and C_oss Loss

$$P_{rr} + P_{oss} = (Q_{rr} + Q_{oss})\, V_{bus}\, f_{sw} = 70\text{ nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.085\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-Time Body-Diode Conduction

With two dead-time intervals per period, t_dt = 200 ns **[A]**, and V_f ≈ 0.8 V at 80 A:

$$P_{dt} = V_f I_{ph} (2 t_{dt}) f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 400\text{ ns} \times 48\text{k} = 1.23\ \text{W}$$

### 2.5 Gate Drive Loss

$$P_g = 6\, Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 6 \times 50\text{ nC} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.144\ \text{W}$$

In practice only 2 FETs switch each PWM period, so this figure is conservative.
