These boundary conditions shape everything that follows.

| Item | Decision / Assumption | Rationale |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,\big[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\big],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.2 mΩ × 1.45 = **1.74 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step trapezoidal with synchronous rectification

In any commutation sector, phase current I flows through exactly two conducting FETs at all times:
- **PWM-on (D):** HS(phase A) → winding → LS(phase B)
- **PWM-off (1−D):** LS(phase A, synchronous) → winding → LS(phase B)

So the instantaneous conduction loss is independent of duty:

$$P_{cond,ESC} = I^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j)$$

Each FET's time-averaged share follows from its sector participation. Each leg conducts 4 of 6 sectors; within those, the HS gets D and the LS gets the remainder plus its full-on sectors:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,D\,I^2R,\qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\,(2-D)\,I^2R$$

Summing over 3 legs: 3·⅓·(D + 2 − D)·I²R = 2I²R ✓.

**Worst-case FET is the low side.** At low duty (punch-out from hover), LS ≈ ⅔·I²R.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

$$P_{cond,ESC} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.74\text{ mΩ} = \mathbf{22.3\ W}$$

$$P_{LS,max} = \tfrac{2}{3}\times 6400\times 1.74\text{ mΩ} = \mathbf{7.4\ W}\ \text{(hottest FET)}$$

### 1.4 Ancillary resistive losses (per ESC)

| Element | R | P @ 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Current shunt (shared, per ESC) | 0.25 mΩ | 1.60 W |
| Phase copper (2 oz, 4 parallel layers, ~8 mm) | ~0.15 mΩ ×2 paths | 1.92 W |
| Bus copper share (160 A pair-wise... see below) | ~0.1 mΩ | 0.64 W |
| **Subtotal** | | **≈ 4.2 W** |

Total bus current is 4×80 A × D_avg. At D = 0.85 and full throttle that is about 270 A from the pack, which by itself breaks most XT60 leads (§3.6).

## 2. Switching Loss Model at f_sw = 48 kHz

### 2.1 Gate drive currents during the Miller plateau

$$R_{G,tot} = R_{drv} + R_{G,ext} + R_{G,int} = 2 + 2.2 + 1.0 = 5.2\ \Omega$$

$$I_{G,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_{G,tot}} = \frac{12-3.4}{5.2} = 1.65\text{ A},\qquad I_{G,off} = \frac{V_{pl}}{R_{G,tot}} = 0.65\text{ A}$$

### 2.2 V-I overlap times

$$t_{on} = \frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{G,on}} = \frac{17\text{ nC}}{1.65} = 10.3\text{ ns},\qquad t_{off} = \frac{17\text{ nC}}{0.65} = 26.2\text{ ns}$$
