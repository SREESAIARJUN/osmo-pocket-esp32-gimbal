## 0. Architecture Assumptions and Corrections

Three points need settling before the math, because they change the results.

| # | Issue | Resolution adopted |
|---|-------|-------------------|
| A1 | The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. 48 kHz is the ESC PWM frequency, generated by four dedicated ESC MCUs. | H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600. 4× AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) run AM32 at 48 kHz. |
| A2 | "80 A burst" is ambiguous: 80 A per motor, or 80 A total. On a 20×20 mm board these differ by roughly 10× in dissipation. | Both cases are modeled. **Case P = 80 A per motor**, **Case T = 80 A total (20 A/motor)**. |
| A3 | Battery voltage is unspecified. | 6S, V_bus = 25.2 V max. Component ratings target 30 V+ FETs and 35 V caps. |

**Feasibility verdict (justified in §3):** Case P is not thermally survivable on a 20×20 mm nano board beyond roughly 1–1.5 s. Case T is viable. The deliverables below therefore spec the board as **"80 A total burst / 4×25 A per-motor burst / 4×15 A continuous"**. If you truly need 80 A per motor, the platform must grow to 30.5×30.5 mm or larger with paralleled FETs. The math in §3 shows why.

## 2. Loss Model: 6-Step Trapezoidal, Complementary (Synchronous) PWM

### 2.1 Conduction Topology

In each 60° commutation sector, exactly one high-side FET (phase X, PWM'd) and one low-side FET (phase Y, held on) conduct:

- **PWM on-time (D):** current flows V_bus → HS_X → motor → LS_Y → GND.
- **PWM off-time (1−D):** current freewheels through LS_X (synchronous rectification) → motor → LS_Y.

In both states, **exactly two FETs are in the current path**. Per motor channel this gives:

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS,hot}}$$

This holds independent of D. Phase current relates to battery current by $I_{ph} = I_{bat}/D$.

**Per-FET RMS current.** Each FET conducts during 120° of 360° electrical:

$$I_{RMS,FET}^{HS} = I_{ph}\sqrt{\tfrac{D}{3}}, \qquad I_{RMS,FET}^{LS} = I_{ph}\sqrt{\tfrac{1}{3} + \tfrac{1-D}{3}}$$

Low-side FETs carry the freewheel current, so they run hotter at partial throttle.

### 2.2 Conduction Loss: Numerical Results

Worst case is D → 1 at full throttle (I_ph = I_bat).

| Quantity | Case P (80 A/motor) | Case T (20 A/motor) |
|---|---|---|
| P_cond per channel = I²·2·1.5 mΩ | 80²·3e-3 = **19.2 W** | 20²·3e-3 = **1.2 W** |
| Per FET (D≈1): I²·R/3 | **3.2 W** | 0.2 W |
| All 4 channels | **76.8 W** | **4.8 W** |

Check: 6 FETs × 3.2 W = 19.2 W ✓

**PCB copper adder.** Phase and bus traces add roughly 0.3–0.5 mΩ per path on a nano board (2 oz, short pours). This contributes 80²·0.4 mΩ ≈ 2.6 W per channel in Case P, and 0.16 W in Case T.
