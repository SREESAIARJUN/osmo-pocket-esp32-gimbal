# 3-Axis Gimbal FOC Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion: Design and Implementation

**Assumptions** (I don't have the original spec for Job #48, so verify these against your hardware):
- Three PMSM/BLDC gimbal motors (e.g., 12N14P), each with a 14-bit absolute magnetic encoder.
- Three-phase inverter with low-side or inline current sensing.
- Two ICM-42688-P IMUs:
  - **IMU-A** on the camera plate.
  - **IMU-B** either on the camera plate (redundant pair) or on the base handle (disturbance feedforward). Both modes are covered below.
- Cortex-M7 class MCU with an FPU.

Every symbol and rate below is a parameter you can change.

---

## 1. Timing Architecture

| Loop | Rate | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | ADC end-of-conversion, center-aligned PWM |
| IMU sampling | 8 kHz ODR (FIFO burst read) | IMU INT1 → DMA |
| Velocity (rate) | 4 kHz | Timer |
| Angle (attitude) | 1 kHz | Timer |
| Cogging-table LMS update | 1 kHz (background) | Low priority |

**Bandwidth separation rule:** each outer loop should be about 5–10× slower than the loop inside it.

- ω_c,i ≈ 2π·1.5 kHz
- ω_c,v ≈ 2π·150 Hz
- ω_c,θ ≈ 2π·20 Hz

## 2. FOC Current Loop

### 2.1 Motor model in the rotor (dq) frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e L_d i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2}p\left[\lambda_m i_q + (L_d-L_q)i_d i_q\right]\;\xrightarrow{\text{SPM}}\; K_t i_q,\quad K_t=\tfrac32 p\lambda_m
$$

Here p is the number of pole pairs, ω_e = p·ω_m, and θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset.

### 2.2 Transforms

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\quad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a+2i_b)
$$

Park transform:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\-\sin\theta_e&\cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 2.3 PI tuning by pole-zero cancellation

The plant per axis is G(s) = 1/(Ls + R). Choose the PI gains so the PI zero cancels the plant pole:

$$
C(s)=K_p+\frac{K_i}{s},\quad K_p=L\,\omega_{c,i},\quad K_i=R\,\omega_{c,i}
$$

The closed loop then becomes first-order with bandwidth ω_c,i:

$$
T(s)=\frac{\omega_{c,i}}{s+\omega_{c,i}}
$$

Add decoupling and back-EMF feedforward:

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d - \hat\omega_e \hat L_q i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \text{PI}_q + \hat\omega_e \hat L_d i_d + \hat\omega_e\hat\lambda_m
$$

**Delay compensation:** the PWM update introduces about 1.5·T_s of delay. Advance the angle used for the inverse Park transform by θ_e + 1.5·ω_e·T_s.

### 2.4 SVPWM (min/max zero-sequence injection)

$$
v_{a,b,c}=\text{InvClarke}(v_\alpha,v_\beta),\quad v_0=-\tfrac12(\max+\min),\quad d_x=\tfrac12+\frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{dc}}
$$

The linear modulation limit is |v_αβ| ≤ V_dc/√3. Saturate the voltage vector circularly and feed the excess back to PI anti-windup.

### 2.5 Discrete PI with back-calculation anti-windup

$$
u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\qquad \bar u_k=\text{sat}(u_k),\qquad I_{k+1}=I_k + K_iT_s e_k + K_{aw}(\bar u_k-u_k)
$$

A typical choice is K_aw = K_i/K_p, which equals 1/T_i.

> **High-resistance gimbal motors** (R ≈ 5–15 Ω): voltage-mode control (v_q = R·i_q* + ω_e·λ_m) is acceptable if you have no current sensors. The code supports both modes.

## 3. Cascaded Angle → Velocity Control

### 3.1 Mechanical plant (per axis)

$$
J\dot\omega = K_t i_q - B\omega - \tau_{cog}(\theta_m) - \tau_{fric}(\omega) - \tau_{dist}
$$

### 3.2 Gimbal kinematics

The loops are closed on the camera's inertial attitude, not on joint angles.

For yaw (outer, z) → roll (middle, x) → pitch (inner, y), the camera-frame angular velocity is:

$$
\boldsymbol\omega_c = R_y(q_p)^{\!T}\!\left(R_x(q_r)^{\!T}\begin{bmatrix}0\\0\\\dot q_y\end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}\dot q_r\\0\\0\end{bmatrix}\right)+\begin{bmatrix}0\\\dot q_p\\0\end{bmatrix} + R_{cb}\,\boldsymbol\omega_{base}
$$

This can be written as:

$$
\boldsymbol\omega_c = J(q)\,\dot{\mathbf q} + R_{cb}\boldsymbol\omega_b
\;\Rightarrow\;
\dot{\mathbf q}^* = J(q)^{-1}\left(\boldsymbol\omega_c^* - R_{cb}\boldsymbol\omega_b\right)
$$

J(q) is singular at q_r = ±90° (gimbal lock). Use damped least squares there:

$$
J^{\dagger} = J^T(JJ^T+\lambda^2I)^{-1}
$$

### 3.3 Angle loop (P plus rate feedforward, on SO(3))

The attitude error comes from the quaternion error q_e = q*⁻¹ ⊗ q̂:

$$
\mathbf e_\theta = 2\,\text{sgn}(q_{e,w})\,\mathbf q_{e,xyz}
$$

$$
\boldsymbol\omega_c^* = -K_\theta\,\mathbf e_\theta + \boldsymbol\omega_{ff},\qquad K_\theta=\omega_{c,\theta}
$$

### 3.4 Velocity loop (PI + inertia/friction/cogging feedforward)

$$
i_q^* = \underbrace{K_{pv}e_\omega + K_{iv}\!\int e_\omega}_{\text{PI}} + \frac{1}{K_t}\Big[\hat J\dot\omega^* + \hat B\omega + \hat\tau_c\,\text{sgn}(\omega)+\hat\tau_{cog}(\theta_m)\Big]
$$

The velocity plant is approximately K_t/(Js) in series with the current loop T(s). To place the crossover at ω_c,v with phase margin set by α (the PI zero at ω_c,v/α, with α ≈ 4):

$$
K_{pv}=\frac{\hat J\,\omega_{c,v}}{K_t},\qquad K_{iv}=\frac{K_{pv}\,\omega_{c,v}}{\alpha}
$$
