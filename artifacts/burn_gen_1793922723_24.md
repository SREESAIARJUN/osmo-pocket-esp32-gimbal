> **Feasibility flag (stated up front):** the result below is 80 A *burst* (≤ ~5 s) and roughly **30–35 A continuous** per motor in prop wash. A 20 × 20 board cannot dissipate the ~100 W generated at full 4 × 80 A. A marketing "80 A" rating on such hardware is a transient figure, and the firmware current limit must enforce that.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0055\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS} = 1.1\,\text{m}\Omega \times (1 + 0.0055 \times 85) = 1.61\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step complementary PWM

During each 60° sector, two phases conduct, for example A+ and B−.

- **Phase A:** the high side (HS) conducts for duty D. The low side (LS) synchronous rectifier conducts for (1−D).
- **Phase B:** the LS conducts for 100% of the sector.

So the instantaneous conduction loss is independent of D:

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{rms}^2 R_{DS}\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{rms}^2 R_{DS} = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}$$

### 1.3 Ripple correction

The ripple current is

$$\Delta I = \frac{(V_{bus} - V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph\text{-}ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{DC}^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12}$$

At 80 A the throttle is near full, so D ≈ 0.92 and V_bemf ≈ 21 V. With L = 20 µH, ΔI ≈ 4.0 A, and the ΔI²/12 term adds 1.3 A². That is negligible (0.02%).
