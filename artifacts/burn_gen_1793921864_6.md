## 0. Architecture Decision and Design Assumptions

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600. It does not commutate motors. Each ESC channel has its own MCU running AM32 firmware, which handles 48 kHz complementary PWM and six-step commutation. This is the only practical topology. The H743 cannot run four sensorless field-oriented-control/six-step loops alongside ArduPilot, and ArduPilot has no ESC firmware.
