# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #51): Electrothermal Model, Bi-Dir DShot600 DMA Plan, and Reference Netlist

## 0. Architecture and Assumptions

**Architecture correction.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does **not** commutate the motors. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 talks to those MCUs over bi-directional DShot600. Everything below follows that partition.

## 1. Loss Model (per channel)

### 1.1 Conduction loss

In six-step synchronous PWM, current always flows through exactly two FETs: the PWM leg (HS for D, LS for 1–D) plus the fixed low-side of the return phase. Ignoring dead-time intervals:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j),\qquad R_{DS}(T_j)=R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

| T_j | R_DS | P_cond at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.0 mΩ | 12.8 W |
| 125 °C (design point) | 1.5 mΩ | **19.2 W** |
