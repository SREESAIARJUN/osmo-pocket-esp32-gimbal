## 1. Field-Oriented Control Equations

### 1.1 Transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Clarke transform, with i_c = −i_a − i_b:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}\,(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

Park transform, with electrical angle θ_e = p·θ_m − θ_offset:

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

Inverse Park transform:

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

### 1.2 SVPWM via min–max zero-sequence injection

First compute the phase voltages:

$$
v_a=v_\alpha,\quad v_b=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

Then inject the zero-sequence offset and compute duty cycles:

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_a,v_b,v_c)+\min(v_a,v_b,v_c)\big),\qquad
D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x+v_0}{V_{bus}},\quad x\in\{a,b,c\}
$$

The linear range is $|\vec v| \le V_{bus}/\sqrt3$. Clamp the vector magnitude, not the individual components.

### 1.3 Electrical plant and current PI

The PMSM model in the rotor frame is:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$
