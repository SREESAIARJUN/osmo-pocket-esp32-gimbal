## 0. Feasibility Constraints

These constraints shape every number that follows.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In an ArduPilot AIO design, the H743 runs the flight stack and emits bidirectional DShot600. Commutation and the 48 kHz PWM run on four dedicated ESC MCUs, typically AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The 48 kHz loss model therefore belongs to the ESC power stage. The DMA model belongs to the H743.
2. **80 A on a nano footprint is a burst rating, not a continuous one.** Section 3 shows about 17–18 W of loss per channel at 80 A, or about 70 W for all four. A 20×20 mm board has roughly 2.5 J/K of thermal mass.
   - **Recommendation:** use a 25.5×25.5 mm AIO (M2 whoop pattern) or a 20×20 two-board stack (FC over ESC).
   - **Rating:** 80 A burst for ≤1–2 s, roughly 35–45 A continuous with airflow.
   - A single 20×20 board cannot physically place 24 power FETs plus the H743 at this current.
3. **All device parameters below are representative placeholders.** Replace them with values from your selected MOSFET datasheet and re-run the equations. The model is written to be parameter-driven.

## 1. System Architecture

```
 6S LiPo (25.2 V max) ──┬── TVS + 4×(bulk caps) ── VBAT bus (power planes L1/L8)
                        │
                        ├── 4× ESC channel: [ESC MCU] → [3-ph gate driver] → [6× N-MOSFET] → Motor A/B/C
                        │        ▲  BEMF dividers, shunt current sense, NTC
                        │        │ bidirectional DShot600 (single wire, open-drain-style pull-up)
                        │        │
                        └── 5 V / 3.3 V bucks ── STM32H743 (ArduPilot)
                                       ├ SPI1: IMU (ICM-42688-P)
                                       ├ SPI2: OSD (AT7456E)
                                       ├ SPI4: Flash (W25N01G)
                                       ├ I2C2: Baro (DPS310) / compass
                                       ├ UART1..8: RX, GPS, VTX, ESC-telemetry (optional)
                                       └ TIM3/TIM5: M1–M4 bidir DShot600
```
