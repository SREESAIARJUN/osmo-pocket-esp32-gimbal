## 1. Field-Oriented Control Core

### 1.1 Coordinate transforms

The electrical angle is θₑ = P·(θₘ − θ_offset).

**Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):**

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

**Park transform:**

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park transform:**

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

**SVPWM with min/max zero-sequence injection** (equivalent to sector-based SVPWM). First compute the phase voltages:

$$
v_a=v_\alpha,\quad v_b=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha+\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c=-\tfrac12 v_\alpha-\tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

Then inject the zero-sequence offset and normalize to duty cycles:

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max + \min),\qquad d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}
$$

The linear range is limited to |v_αβ| ≤ V_bus/√3.

### 1.2 Current loop design (PI with pole–zero cancellation)

**Plant.** The dq-axis model is:

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

**Controller.** For each axis, choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole at R/L:

$$
C_i(s) = K_p + \frac{K_i}{s},\qquad K_p = \omega_{ci} L,\quad K_i = \omega_{ci} R
$$

The closed loop then becomes first order:

$$
\frac{i}{i^*}=\frac{\omega_{ci}}{s+\omega_{ci}}
$$

With f_s = 20 kHz and one-sample computational delay, choose ω_ci ≤ 2π·f_s/20 ≈ 2π·1 kHz.

**Decoupling and back-EMF feedforward** (λ_m = Kt / (1.5·P)):

$$
v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m
$$

**Saturation.** The output voltage magnitude is limited to V_max = V_bus/√3. Priority goes to v_d; v_q gets the remainder:

$$
v_q \le \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}
$$

**Anti-windup.** Use back-calculation with gain K_aw = 1/K_p:

$$
\dot x_I = K_i e + K_{aw}(v_{sat}-v_{unsat})
$$

> **Gimbal note:** High-resistance gimbal windings (≥5 Ω) are often run in *voltage mode* (v_q = R·i_q* + ω_e·λ_m, no current sensing). The code supports both modes through `CURRENT_SENSING`.

### 2.1 Mechanical plant

$$
J\ddot\theta = K_t i_q - B\dot\theta - \tau_{cog}(\theta) - \tau_{dist}(t)
$$

The disturbance τ_dist includes cable drag, IMU-referenced base motion, and camera imbalance.
