# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller with 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture and Mathematical Model

## 0. Architecture Clarifications

Three points shape everything below.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In any 4-in-1 design, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600. Each motor bridge is driven by a dedicated ESC MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32) at 48 kHz PWM. The FC and ESC partitions are modeled separately.
2. **80 A in a nano (20×20 mm) footprint is a transient rating only.** The math in §3 shows the board cannot sustain it. Treat "80 A burst" as a sub-second rating, not a continuous one.
3. **The netlist in §5 is an architecture-level reference.** It must be verified against the exact datasheets, the H743 package pinout (VIT6 or VIH6), and the ArduPilot `hwdef` before layout. Do not send it to fab unreviewed.

## 2. ESC Loss Model (Per Motor Bridge)

### 2.1 Hot R_ds(on)

R_ds(T_j) = R_ds,25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 110 °C: R_ds = 1.5 × 1.425 = **2.14 mΩ**

### 2.2 Conduction Loss (Six-Step, Complementary/Synchronous PWM)

In each commutation sector two phases carry current:
- **PWM leg:** the high-side FET conducts for D, and the synchronous low-side conducts for (1−D).
- **Static leg:** the low-side FET conducts for 100% of the sector.

Total bridge conduction loss:

P_cond,bridge = I²R_ds · [D + (1−D)] + I²R_ds = **2 I² R_ds(T_j)**

This result is independent of duty cycle. Averaged over six sectors, each FET dissipates P_FET,cond = I²R_ds / 3.

At 80 A:
- P_cond,bridge = 2 × 6400 × 2.14 mΩ = **27.4 W**
- Per FET: **4.56 W** average

### 2.3 Switching Loss (One Hard-Switched Leg Per Sector)

| Mechanism | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V·I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f_sw = ½·25.2·80·30 ns·48 k | 1.45 W |
| Dead-time diode | V_f·I·2t_dt·f_sw = 0.8·80·200 ns·48 k | 0.61 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw = 30 nC·25.2·48 k | 0.036 W |
| C_oss | ½·C_oss,er·V²·f_sw | 0.023 W |
| Gate drive (driver IC) | 2·Q_g·V_gs·f_sw | 0.029 W |
| **Total P_sw** | | **≈2.15 W** |

### 2.4 Additional Losses (Often Ignored, Not Negligible at 80 A)

**Copper traces.** R_trace ≈ ρL/(wt). For 10 mm × 3 mm of 2 oz (70 µm) copper:
- R ≈ 0.82 mΩ
- I²R ≈ **5.2 W per phase path**

Use multilayer pours and stitched vias, and keep the phase-to-pad path short.

**Shunt resistor** (0.25 mΩ, if fitted):
- 80² × 0.25 m ≈ **1.6 W**

### 2.5 Totals at 80 A Burst

| Scope | Value |
|---|---|
| Per bridge (FETs only) | 27.4 + 2.15 ≈ **29.6 W** |
| All 4 motors simultaneously | ≈ **118 W** (≈140 W including copper) |
| Electrical efficiency at 80 A | P_out ≈ 25.2 V·80 A·D. At D = 0.9, losses are ~1.6% FET-only, but this is concentrated in ~4 cm² |

### 2.6 Input Capacitor Stress

I_C,rms ≈ I·√(D(1−D)):
- At D = 0.5: **40 A_rms per bridge** (interleaving across bridges helps partially)

Bulk capacitance for ΔV ≤ 1 V:
- C ≥ I·D(1−D)/(f_sw·ΔV) = 80·0.25/(48 k·1) ≈ **420 µF**

This drives the design toward:
- A low-ESR external electrolytic (≥470 µF) on the battery leads, **plus**
- ≥4×10 µF X7R per bridge, placed at the drains.
