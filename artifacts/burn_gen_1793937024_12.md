**Architectural correction.** The STM32H743 cannot do the commutation for four motors. Each ESC channel needs its own MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 is the flight controller and talks to the four ESC MCUs over bidirectional DShot600.

```
[6S] ──► 4-in-1 ESC: 4× {ESC MCU → 3-phase gate driver → 6× MOSFET}
              ▲ 8-pin JST-SH (VBAT, GND, CURR, TLM, M1..M4)
              │
         STM32H743 FC (ArduPilot) — BDShot600 on M1..M4
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\,\text{K}^{-1}$$

At a design junction temperature of T<sub>j</sub> = 110 °C:

$$R_{DS} = 0.9\,(1+0.0045\cdot 85) = 1.244\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 RMS current including ripple

The current ripple is:

$$\Delta I = \frac{V_{bus}\,D(1-D)}{L\,f_{sw}} = \frac{25.2\cdot 0.25}{20\,\mu\text{H}\cdot 48\,\text{kHz}} = 6.56\ \text{A}_{pp}\quad (D=0.5 \text{ worst case})$$

The RMS current is then:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2 + \frac{\Delta I^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 \approx 6404\ \text{A}^2$$

Ripple is negligible at 48 kHz.

### 1.3 Per-channel conduction loss

With synchronous rectification, two switches are always in the current path: the PWM leg's high-side or low-side, plus the static low-side.

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2 \cdot 2R_{DS}(T_j) = 6404 \cdot 2.488\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{15.9\ W}$$

### 1.4 Per-device loss

- **Instantaneous worst case** (low eRPM, one FET carries current for a full sector): I²R = 6400 · 1.244 mΩ = **7.96 W**.
- **Averaged** (high eRPM, where the electrical period is much shorter than the thermal time constant): 15.9 W ÷ 6 FETs ≈ **2.65 W per FET**.

### 1.5 PCB copper and current sense (do not omit)

**Copper.** Assume 1 mm² effective copper path, 15 mm long, 2 oz copper on 4 paralleled layers. That is about 0.25 mΩ round trip, which adds **1.6 W per channel**.

**Shunt.** A shared 0.25 mΩ shunt carrying 320 A dissipates 25.6 W, which is unacceptable. Use one of these instead:
- a 0.1 mΩ shunt (10 W at full burst), or
- preferably a Hall/TMR sensor such as the ACS772/ACS37002 class.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

In six-step drive, only **one** half-bridge per channel switches at any moment.

### 2.1 Transition times from gate charge

$$t_{on} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}}{(V_{drv}-V_{pl})/(R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int})} = \frac{10\,\text{nC}}{(10-3)/6} = 8.6\ \text{ns}$$

$$t_{off} \approx \frac{10\,\text{nC}}{3/6} = 20\ \text{ns}$$

Adding Q<sub>gs2</sub> margin gives t<sub>r</sub> ≈ 12 ns and t<sub>f</sub> ≈ 25 ns.
