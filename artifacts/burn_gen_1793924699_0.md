### 0.1 Architectural note

On a 4-in-1 ESC, a single STM32H743 cannot practically run four sensorless BLDC commutation loops. That needs 12 complementary PWM pairs plus 4× back-EMF comparators and zero-cross timing. Bidirectional DShot is also by definition an FC↔ESC link.

The architecture is therefore:

- **FC side:** STM32H743 running ArduPilot.
- **ESC side:** four small Cortex-M0+/M4 MCUs, one per motor, running AM32.

If Job #43 really intends a single-MCU design, tell me. That would be a different (and much harder) document.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.005\ /\text{K}$$

At design T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 0.9 mΩ × 1.5 = **1.35 mΩ**.

Add package and solder (~0.1 mΩ) and phase-trace copper (§1.3) separately.

### 1.2 Six-step synchronous commutation

In each 60° sector, two phase legs carry current I:

- **PWM leg:** high-side conducts for D, synchronous low-side for (1−D). Combined loss is I²R regardless of D.
- **Freewheel-return leg:** low-side is on continuously, giving I²R.

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

At 80 A:

$$P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.35\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{17.3\ W\ per\ ESC}$$

With sinusoidal FOC instead of six-step, use I_rms per phase: P = 3·I_rms²·R. For the same torque this is ~10–15 % lower.

### 1.3 Copper loss (often forgotten)

Assume phase/bus path length ≈ 25 mm per leg, effective width 4 mm, and 4 parallel 2 oz layers (70 µm) at 100 °C (ρ = 2.2×10⁻⁸ Ω·m):

$$R_{Cu} = \frac{\rho L}{W\cdot t\cdot n} = \frac{2.2\times10^{-8}\times0.025}{0.004\times70\times10^{-6}\times4} \approx 0.49\ \text{mΩ}$$

Two current-carrying paths gives P_Cu ≈ 2 × 6400 × 0.49 mΩ ≈ **6.3 W**.

This is comparable to the FETs. Layer count and thick copper are not optional at 80 A.

### 1.4 Shunt loss

A 0.25 mΩ shunt per ESC (DC bus) gives 6400 × 0.25 mΩ = **1.6 W**.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (f_sw = 48 kHz, V_bus = 25.2 V, I = 80 A)

### 2.1 Hard-switching V–I overlap (PWM high-side only)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

With t_r + t_f ≈ 40 ns (driver ~1 A source / 2 A sink, R_g = 4.7 Ω):

$$P_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 40\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.94\ W}$$

Rise/fall time estimate: t ≈ Q_gd·R_g,total / (V_drv − V_plateau) ≈ 12 nC × 6 Ω / (10 − 3.5 V) ≈ 11 ns ideal. The 40 ns allowance covers layout inductance and driver slew limits.

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 50\text{n} \times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.06\ \text{W}$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = \tfrac12 Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 60\text{n}\times 25.2 \times 48\text{k} = 0.04\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

Two dead-time intervals per period, t_dt = 100 ns, V_F = 0.8 V:

$$P_{dt} = V_F I \cdot 2 t_{dt} f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated mostly in driver and R_g)

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2 \times 55\text{n} \times 10 \times 48\text{k} = 0.05\ \text{W}$$

### 2.6 Loss summary per ESC at 80 A burst

| Term | 1 FET/switch | 2 FET parallel/switch |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 17.3 W | 8.6 W |
| Copper | 6.3 W | 6.3 W |
| Shunt | 1.6 W | 1.6 W |
| Switching overlap | 1.94 W | ~2.3 W (more C, shared I) |
| Dead-time diode | 0.61 W | 0.61 W |
| Q_rr + Q_oss + gate | 0.15 W | 0.30 W |
| **Total per ESC** | **27.9 W** | **19.7 W** |
| **Board total (4 motors at 80 A)** | **~112 W** | **~79 W** |
