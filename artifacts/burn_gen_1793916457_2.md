## 0. Architecture Corrections and Assumptions

### 0.1 Architecture correction

The STM32H743 cannot commutate the motors directly in an ArduPilot design. ArduPilot does not run sensorless six-step or FOC commutation on the flight-controller MCU. The correct partitioning is:

| Domain | Device | Firmware | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flight control | STM32H743VIH6 (or VIT6), 480 MHz | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) | Attitude control, BDShot600 master |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071GBU6, one per motor | AM32 | Commutation, 48 kHz complementary PWM, eRPM telemetry |
| Gate drive ×4 | Fortior FD6288Q (3-phase, 250 V, bootstrap) | n/a | 6 gates per motor |
| Power stage | 24 N-MOSFETs (6 per motor) | n/a | 3 half-bridges per motor |

The H743 only generates DShot frames and captures the GCR eRPM replies. All 48 kHz switching happens on the ESC MCUs.

### 0.3 Feasibility flag

On a 20×20 mm or 30.5×30.5 mm "nano" board, 4 × 80 A **simultaneous** is a *seconds-scale transient* rating, not a sustainable one. The model below shows the board can reach a ~110 °C limit in about 3–7 s. Specify the rating as "80 A burst, 10 s, per channel, non-simultaneous," or add a heatsink plus airflow.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step current paths with synchronous rectification

During each 60° sector, one phase (A) is PWM'd, one phase (B) is held low-side ON, and the third floats for BEMF sensing.

- **Phase A leg, complementary PWM:**
  - High-side ON for D·T_sw.
  - Low-side ON for (1 − D)·T_sw, minus dead time.
- **Phase B leg:** low-side ON for 100 % of the sector.

The motor current I flows continuously, assuming L/R ≫ T_sw. The RMS conduction losses are therefore:

$$P_{A} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$P_{B} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$$

$$\boxed{P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds,25}\,\alpha_R + I^2 R_{Cu}}$$

The loss is independent of duty because synchronous rectification makes the conducting path always "one switch + one switch."

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on)

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\left[1 + k_T (T_j - 25)\right]$$

Use k_T ≈ 0.0059 /K, which gives ≈1.5 at 110 °C.

This creates a feedback loop: P depends on R_ds(T_j), and T_j depends on P. The thermal-runaway criterion is:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}\,Z_{th} < 1 \;\Rightarrow\; 2I^2R_{ds,25}k_T\,Z_{th} < 1$$

At 80 A with Z_th(j-amb, effective per FET) ≈ 15 K/W:

$$2 \times 6400 \times 10^{-3} \times 0.0059 \times 15 = 1.13 > 1$$

**Steady-state 80 A is thermally unstable without a heatsink. It must be time-limited.**

### 1.3 Numbers per channel at 80 A, T_j = 110 °C

| MOSFET conduction | 2 × 80² × 1.5 mΩ | **19.2 W** |
