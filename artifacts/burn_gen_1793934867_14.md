# STM32H743 AIO + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #60): Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan, and Netlists

## 0. Feasibility Findings

The math in §3–§5 shows that **80 A per channel on a 20×20 mm "nano" board is not thermally or electrically feasible**, even as a burst.

- At 80 A phase current, each channel dissipates about **26–28 W**. All four channels together dissipate about **105–110 W**.
- A 20×20 board has a lumped heat capacity of only about 2–3 J/K. That gives an **adiabatic heating rate of about 40–50 K/s**, and junctions reach their limit in **about 1 s**.
- At full throttle, battery current is about 4 × 80 A × D. That approaches **300 A** through pads sized for XT30/XT60 pigtails.
- Rail ripple with on-board MLCCs alone would exceed 10 V peak-to-peak (§5.3).

**Recommendation:** treat 80 A as a **≤1 s burst, single- or dual-motor transient** on a **30.5×30.5 mm** board with 5×6 mm PDFN MOSFETs. If the board must stay 20×20, rate it at **35–40 A burst and 25 A continuous**. The full derivation follows so you can re-run it with your actual parts.

A second architectural correction: the **H743 does not commutate the motors**. A 4-in-1 ESC needs **four dedicated ESC MCUs**, for example AT32F421K8U7 running AM32. AM32 natively supports bidirectional DShot and a selectable 48 kHz PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and generates DShot600.

## 1. System Architecture

```
 6S LiPo (25.2 V max) ── XT60 ── 1000 µF low-ESR ext. cap ── VBAT plane
   │
   ├─ 4× ESC power stage (3 half-bridges each = 24 MOSFETs total)
   │    └─ AT32F421 (AM32) ← DShot600 bidir ← STM32H743 TIM3 CH1–4
   │
   ├─ Buck 12 V/1 A (gate-driver VCC) ─ Buck 5 V/2.5 A ─ LDO 3.3 V
   │
   └─ STM32H743VIH6 (ArduPilot): IMU (SPI1), Baro (I2C2), OSD (SPI2),
      Flash/SD, UARTs, total-current INA on ADC
```
