## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architecture.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel has its own commutation MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware, and that MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 sends bidirectional DShot600 to each ESC and receives eRPM telemetry back. Driving 24 FETs directly from the H743 under ArduPilot is not a supported topology, so this design treats the system as an FC plus a 4-in-1 ESC stack.
