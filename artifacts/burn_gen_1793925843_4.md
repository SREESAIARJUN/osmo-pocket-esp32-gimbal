## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Feasibility Flags

**System partitioning:** The STM32H743 is the flight controller. The 4-in-1 ESC runs its own MCUs (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). The H743 never drives the gates. It generates bi-directional DShot and decodes eRPM telemetry.
