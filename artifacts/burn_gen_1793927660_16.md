# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Model

**Scope and assumptions:** I have no access to the internal Job #16 package (BOM, stack-up, MOSFET selection). Every component value below is a **stated assumption** chosen to be representative of 30.5 × 30.5 mm AIO-class hardware. The equations are general, so you can substitute the real datasheet values. Figures marked ⚠ need verification against the actual parts and a thermal test before release.

---

## 0. Architecture Clarification

The H743 does **not** perform the 48 kHz power-stage switching. In every practical 4-in-1 design, the work splits as follows:

| Function | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight control, DShot master, telemetry decode | STM32H743VIT6 (480 MHz) | ArduPilot |
| Commutation, 48 kHz PWM, BEMF / zero-cross | 4 × ESC MCU (e.g., AT32F421 / STM32G071) | AM32 or BLHeli_32 |
| Gate drive | 4 × 3-phase driver (e.g., FD6288Q class) | — |
| Power stage | 24 × N-MOSFET (6 per motor) | — |

The loss model in Sections 1–3 applies to the ESC power stage. The DMA model in Section 4 applies to the H743.

## 2. Loss Model per Motor (80 A Burst)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

During any 60° commutation sector with complementary PWM at duty D:

- The PWM-leg high-side conducts for fraction D.
- The PWM-leg low-side (synchronous rectification) conducts for fraction 1 − D.
- The return-leg low-side conducts continuously.

$$
P_{cond} = I_{ph}^2 R_{hot}\,[D + (1-D) + 1] = 2\,I_{ph}^2 R_{hot}
$$

This result is **independent of duty**, which is a useful property of synchronous trapezoidal drive.

$$
R_{hot} = 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.5 = 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega
$$
$$
P_{cond} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 0.0015 = \mathbf{19.2\ W}
$$

**Ripple correction:** with phase inductance L_ph, the ripple adds ΔI²/12 to I²_rms. For ΔI ≈ 10 A this is about 0.1%, so it is negligible.

**Copper (PCB) loss** (two phase paths conduct):

$$
P_{Cu} = I_{ph}^2 \times 2R_{Cu} = 6400 \times 0.001 = \mathbf{6.4\ W}
$$
