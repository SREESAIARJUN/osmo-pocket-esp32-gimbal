### Rate hierarchy

The design rule is a bandwidth separation of at least 5× between nested loops.

```
ω_c(current) ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s  ≥ 5× ω_v(velocity) ≈ 2π·150-250
ω_v                          ≥ 5× ω_θ(angle)    ≈ 2π·20-40
```

## 1. FOC Current Loop (Innermost, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_{off}, \qquad \omega_e = p\,\omega_m$$

θ_off is found at calibration by locking the rotor with a d-axis current vector.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0)

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$
