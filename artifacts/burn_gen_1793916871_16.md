**Scope:** Power-stage loss model, transient thermal model, ArduPilot bi-directional DShot600 timer/DMA allocation, and reference netlists.

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Key Assumptions

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot only. It does not commutate motors. Each of the 4 ESC channels has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32 (for example STM32G071 or AT32F421) and its own 3-phase gate driver. The FC talks to the ESCs over bi-directional DShot600. Every loss calculation below happens on the ESC side.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-corrected R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 100 °C: 1.0 × (1 + 0.0045 × 75) = 1.34 mΩ. Adding package and solder contributions gives a design value of **R_hot = 1.4 mΩ**.

### 1.2 Six-step commutation with synchronous rectification

At any instant, current flows through one high-side path and one low-side path. With complementary PWM, a FET is always conducting during the PWM-off interval too (the low-side FET in synchronous mode). This means two FET resistances are in series for the full electrical cycle:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{hot} = 2(80)^2(1.4\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{17.9\ W}$$

Each of the 6 FETs conducts for 1/3 of the electrical period, so the average per FET is:

$$P_{cond,FET} = \frac{I^2 R_{hot}}{3}\cdot 2\cdot\tfrac{1}{2}\cdot... \approx \frac{17.9}{6} = \mathbf{3.0\ W}$$

Within each conduction window, the split between high-side and low-side FETs depends on duty cycle D:

$$P_{HS}=\tfrac{1}{3}I^2R\,D,\qquad P_{LS}=\tfrac{1}{3}I^2R\,(1-D)$$

### 1.3 Copper loss

$$P_{cu} = I^2 R_{cu} = 6400 \times 0.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{3.2\ W\ per\ channel}$$

This 3.2 W heats the PCB directly. It does not pass through the FET junctions.

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

### 2.1 Gate currents and transition times

$$I_{g,on}=\frac{V_{drv}-V_{pl}}{R_g}=\frac{10-3}{5}=1.4\text{ A}$$

$$I_{g,off}=\frac{V_{pl}}{R_g}=\frac{3}{5}=0.6\text{ A}$$

$$t_{on}=\frac{Q_{gs2}+Q_{gd}}{I_{g,on}}=\frac{12\text{ nC}}{1.4\text{ A}}=8.6\text{ ns}$$

$$t_{off}=\frac{12\text{ nC}}{0.6\text{ A}}=20\text{ ns}$$

### 2.2 V–I overlap loss (only the PWM-ing FET)

$$P_{sw}=\tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_{on}+t_{off}) f_{sw}=\tfrac12(25.2)(80)(28.6\text{ ns})(48\text{ kHz})=\mathbf{1.38\ W}$$
