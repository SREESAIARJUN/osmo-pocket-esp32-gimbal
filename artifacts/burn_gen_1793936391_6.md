## 0. Design Assumptions and Architectural Corrections

Before any math, three things must be stated plainly. Every number below depends on them.

### 0.1 The H743 does not commutate the motors

The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. Sensorless BLDC commutation (BEMF zero-cross detection, 48 kHz PWM, ~10 µs response loops) must run on **dedicated ESC MCUs**. The baseline uses four AT32F421K8U7 running AM32, one per motor. The H743 talks to them over bidirectional DShot600.
