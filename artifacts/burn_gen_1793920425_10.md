## 0. Architectural Correction

ArduPilot running on the STM32H743 does not commutate motors. Bidirectional DShot is a link between the flight controller and the ESC MCUs. The 48 kHz PWM, commutation, and gate drive therefore belong to the ESC microcontrollers.

```
                 ┌──────────────── STM32H743 (ArduPilot) ────────────────┐
 IMU/Baro/OSD ──►│  TIM3_CH3/CH4, TIM5_CH1/CH2  → Bidir DShot600 ×4      │
                 └────────┬──────────┬──────────┬──────────┬─────────────┘
                          │ M1       │ M2       │ M3       │ M4
                     ┌────▼───┐ ┌────▼───┐ ┌────▼───┐ ┌────▼───┐
                     │ESC MCU │ │ESC MCU │ │ESC MCU │ │ESC MCU │  AM32-class
                     │48kHz PWM│ │        │ │        │ │        │  (e.g. AT32F421)
                     └────┬───┘ └────┬───┘ └────┬───┘ └────┬───┘
                     3-ph gate drv ×4 (e.g. FD6288Q-class)
                     6× N-MOSFET per motor = 24 FETs
```

## 2. Conduction Loss Model

In 6-step operation with synchronous rectification, phase current always flows through exactly two channels in series. One is the PWM leg's high-side FET, or its low-side FET during freewheel. The other is the return leg's low-side FET.

**Per motor:**

$$P_{cond,motor} = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2 R_{DS(on)} k_T = 80^2 \cdot 2 \cdot (0.9\,\text{m}\Omega \cdot 1.6) = 6400 \cdot 2.88\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{18.4\ W}$$

**Per FET:** each FET carries current for one-third of the electrical cycle, so the average is

$$P_{cond,FET} = 18.4/6 \approx \mathbf{3.07\ W}$$

The worst-case low-side FET during low-duty freewheel can carry about 2/3 of the motor's loss share in short windows. Use **4.5 W** for hot-spot design.

**PCB copper loss.** Assume a phase path of about 15 mm, 4 mm wide, 2 oz copper on two parallel layers, giving R ≈ 0.6 mΩ ⚠:

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \cdot 0.6\,\text{m}\Omega \cdot 2 \approx 7.7\ \text{W per motor}$$

Copper is often the second-largest loss. Use 3 oz copper and stitch layers with ≥20 vias per phase.

**Battery-side current** (shared by all four motors, duty D):

$$I_{bat} \approx D \cdot I_{ph}$$

At D = 0.8, this gives 4 × 64 = **256 A** from the pack. That figure exceeds most XT60/XT90 harnesses and confirms the per-motor interpretation is extreme.

## 3. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches hard. Its complementary FET soft-switches.

| Mechanism | Formula | Per motor |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t_r+t_f)·f = ½·25.2·80·40 ns·48 k | 1.94 W |
| C_oss discharge | ½·C_oss·V²·f = ½·1.5 nF·635·48 k | 0.02 W |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f = 50 nC·25.2·48 k | 0.06 W |
| Dead-time diode | 2·V_SD·I·t_dead·f = 2·0.8·80·100 ns·48 k | 0.61 W |
