## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current Path Topology

During any 60° commutation sector, exactly two legs carry the phase current I:

- **"PWM" phase (120° per electrical cycle):** HS on for fraction D, LS (synchronous) on for (1−D).
- **"Sink" phase (120° per electrical cycle):** LS on 100%.
- **Floating phase (120°):** off, used for BEMF sensing.

Per-FET duty-weighted conduction fractions, averaged over one electrical period:

$$
\delta_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}D, \qquad \delta_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D) + \tfrac{1}{3} = \tfrac{1}{3}(2-D)
$$

### 1.2 Temperature-Dependent R_ds(on)

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

At a burst design point of T_j = 125 °C:

$$
R_{ds} = 1.3 \times (1 + 0.0045 \times 100) = 1.885\ \text{m}\Omega \;\;(\text{use } 1.9\ \text{m}\Omega)
$$

### 1.3 Per-Device and Per-ESC Conduction Loss

$$
P_{cond,HS} = I^2 R_{ds}\,\tfrac{D}{3}, \qquad P_{cond,LS} = I^2 R_{ds}\,\tfrac{2-D}{3}
$$

Summed over 3 phases (D cancels: two FETs always conduct):

$$
P_{cond,ESC} = 3\left[\tfrac{D}{3}+\tfrac{2-D}{3}\right] I^2 R_{ds} = 2\,I^2 R_{ds}
$$

At I = 80 A and R = 1.9 mΩ:

$$
P_{cond,ESC} = 2 \times 6400 \times 1.9\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{24.3\ W\ per\ ESC\ channel}
$$

**Hotspot device: the low-side FET at low D.** This is the worst case, for example during a punch-out from low throttle with high current.

$$
P_{LS,max} = 6400 \times 1.9\times10^{-3} \times \tfrac{2}{3} = \mathbf{8.1\ W}
$$

|---|---|---|
| Phase trace + pad, per ESC (2 legs active) | 2 × 0.15 mΩ | 1.9 W |
