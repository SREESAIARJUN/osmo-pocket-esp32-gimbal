## 1. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop, 20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle with latency compensation

The rotor angle is sampled at the start of the PWM period, but the applied voltage is only effective one period later, averaged over another half period. The voltage-transform angle is therefore advanced by 1.5 sample periods:

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{0}) \bmod 2\pi,\qquad
\theta_e^{\text{inv}} = \theta_e + 1.5\,T_i\,\omega_e
$$

Here θ₀ is the encoder offset found during alignment, and ω_e = p·ω_m.

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms (amplitude-invariant)

Assuming i_a + i_b + i_c = 0:

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.3 Machine model in the dq frame (surface PMSM, L_d = L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

The electromagnetic torque is:

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac32 p \lambda_m i_q = K_t i_q
$$

### 1.4 Current PI with decoupling and back-EMF feedforward

The controller cancels the cross-coupling terms and the back-EMF:

$$
v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

**Tuning by pole-zero cancellation.** After decoupling, the plant is 1/(Ls + R). Place the PI zero on the plant pole:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{ci},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{ci}
$$

This gives the closed loop ω_ci/(s + ω_ci).

- Choose ω_ci ≤ 2π·f_s/20, i.e. 2π·1 kHz at 20 kHz.
- With the assumed values: K_p = 12.6 V/A and K_i = 31 400 V/(A·s).

**Gimbal note.** Gimbal motors are high-R and low-speed. A voltage-mode fallback (v_q* = R·i_q* + ω_e·λ_m) is acceptable if you have no current sensing. The design below assumes inline current sensing.

### 1.5 Voltage limiting (circular) and SVPWM

The maximum linear-region voltage vector is |v| ≤ V_dc/√3. Prioritize d-axis:

$$
v_d \leftarrow \text{sat}(v_d, \pm V_{\max}),\qquad v_q \leftarrow \text{sat}\Big(v_q,\pm\sqrt{V_{\max}^2 - v_d^2}\Big)
$$

Inverse Park, then min-max zero-sequence injection (equivalent to SVPWM):

$$
v_a = v_\alpha,\quad v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$

$$
v_0 = -\tfrac12\big(\max(v_{abc}) + \min(v_{abc})\big),\qquad
D_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{dc}}
$$
