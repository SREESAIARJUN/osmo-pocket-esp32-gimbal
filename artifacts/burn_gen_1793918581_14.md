# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Design Model (Job #9)

## 0. Assumptions and Feasibility Notes

The numbers below depend on these assumptions. Change any of them and recompute.

| Parameter | Assumed value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus = 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal | Typical for 80 A-class ESCs |
| Board format | 30.5 × 30.5 mm mounting, ~36 × 36 mm outline, 8-layer, 2 oz inner/outer | A true 20 × 20 "nano" format cannot carry 80 A bursts (see §3.6) |
| Architecture | H743 runs ArduPilot → 4× AT32F421 ESC MCUs running AM32 → 3-phase gate drivers → 24 MOSFETs | An H743 cannot commutate four motors itself. Integrated AIO designs use dedicated ESC MCUs. |
| MOSFET | 40 V, R_DS(on) = 1.0 mΩ @ 25 °C / Vgs = 10 V, 5×6 mm power package (e.g., BSC010N04LS-class) | |
| Commutation | 6-step trapezoidal, complementary PWM (active freewheeling), f_sw = 48 kHz | |
| 80 A rating | Per-motor **phase** current, burst ≤ 5 s | Not a continuous rating, and not 4 × 80 A drawn from the battery simultaneously |

**Voltage margin warning.** 40 V parts on 6S leave about 15 V of margin for regenerative and ringing spikes. This is only acceptable with tight layout (§4) and adequate bulk capacitance. A 60 V part gives more margin but roughly doubles R_DS(on) per footprint.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step complementary PWM

In each 60° commutation sector, two phases are energized:
- Phase A's high-side FET (Q_AH) is PWM'd at duty D. Its complementary low-side (Q_AL) conducts during (1−D) as a synchronous rectifier.
- Phase B's low-side FET (Q_BL) is on for the whole sector.

So the motor current I_ph always flows through exactly **two** conducting channels:

$$
P_{cond,ESC}(t) = I_{ph}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)
$$

This holds regardless of D, because during OFF time Q_AL replaces Q_AH.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$
R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0060\ \text{K}^{-1}
$$

At the design point T_j = 100 °C: R = 1.0 × 1.45 = **1.45 mΩ**.

### 1.3 Burst conduction loss

Assume trapezoidal current, so I_rms ≈ I_ph:

$$
P_{cond,ESC} = 80^2 \times 2 \times 1.45\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{18.56\ W}
$$

**Per-FET average.** Each of the 6 FETs carries current for 120° of the electrical cycle, sharing that time with its complement according to D. Averaged over the cycle, the loss spreads approximately evenly:

- P_FET,avg ≈ 18.56 / 6 ≈ **3.1 W**
- Instantaneous peak while conducting ≈ 80² × 1.45 mΩ = **9.3 W**

The electrical period at 10 kRPM with 14 poles is about 0.86 ms. This is short compared with the die thermal time constant, so the average value governs junction temperature rise.

**Board total:** 4 × 18.56 = **74.2 W**.

### 1.4 Copper and shunt losses

| Element | Resistance | Loss per ESC at 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| Phase pad + trace (2 oz, ~0.15 mΩ effective) | 0.15 mΩ × 2 paths | 1.9 W |
| Current shunt (0.25 mΩ, per ESC, on DC link) | | ≤ 1.6 W (scales with D) |

These losses heat the same copper mass. They are included in §3 as **+3 W per ESC**.
