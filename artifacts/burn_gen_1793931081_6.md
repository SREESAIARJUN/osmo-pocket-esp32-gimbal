# STM32H743 AIO (FC + 4-in-1 ESC) Nano: Architecture, Loss Model, Thermal, and DShot DMA

**Job #26 · Design Reference**

## 0. Architecture Correction: Read This First

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no BLDC commutation layer. A working AIO needs **four dedicated ESC MCUs** (for example AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32, or EFM8 running BLHeli_S/Bluejay). The H743 drives them with bidirectional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry. This split is reflected throughout the document.
2. **"80 A burst" on a 20×20 nano is a short-duration rating, not a continuous one.** On a 20×20 board, 24 FETs must be PDFN 3.3×3.3, which limits R_DS(on) to roughly 2 mΩ. The math in §3 shows that four channels at 80 A saturate the board thermally in about **1 second**. Single-channel 80 A bursts are survivable for roughly 3–5 s. Specify the rating that way, or move to a 30.5×30.5 board with 5×6 FETs.

All device parameters below are **representative assumptions** (marked ⚙).
