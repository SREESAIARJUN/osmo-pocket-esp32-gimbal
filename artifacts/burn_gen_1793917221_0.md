# STM32H743 FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #63): Thermal, Switching and DShot Design Model

## 0. Feasibility Constraints

These constraints shape every number that follows.

| Issue | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **The H743 cannot run four ESCs.** ArduPilot does not do sensorless BLDC commutation. | Each ESC needs its own commutation MCU (STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32 or BLHeli_32). The H743 is the flight controller only. It talks to the ESCs over bidirectional DShot. |
| **80 A per motor on a "nano" (20×20 mm) board is thermally impossible beyond about 1 s.** | The model below uses a 30.5×30.5 mm ESC stack with a 36×36 mm board outline. For 20×20 mm, divide the thermal capacitance by about 3.2 and the burst duration by about the same. |
| **4 × 80 A means about 320 A of battery current at full throttle.** | The XT90 connector, battery leads, shunt and bulk capacitors dominate the design. They are part of the thermal budget. |
| **The H743 package** is LQFP100 (14×14 mm) at minimum. | It fits a 20×20 mm or 30.5×30.5 mm FC board, not an integrated ESC board. This is a stacked design. |
