## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)], \quad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At the design limit T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{DS}(125) = 1.2\ \text{m}\Omega \times 1.45 = 1.74\ \text{m}\Omega$$

### 1.2 Current path in 6-step commutation

During any commutation sector (phase A high, phase B low):

- **Phase A high-side (HA)** conducts for duty D. During 1−D, current freewheels through **LA**, which is synchronously rectified.
- **Phase B low-side (LB)** conducts 100 % of the sector.

Two R_DS are therefore always in series with the motor current, independent of D:

$$P_{cond,ch} = I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] + I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) = 2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 1.3 Ripple correction

The ripple correction uses a ripple ratio r = ΔI / I:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I^2\left(1 + \frac{r^2}{12}\right)$$

Low-inductance racing motors at 48 kHz give r ≈ 0.2, so the correction factor is 1.0033 (negligible).

### 1.4 Numbers at 80 A burst

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 1.74\times10^{-3} = 22.3\ \text{W per channel}$$

**Distribution across the 6 FETs.** Each FET is active in 2 of 6 sectors (120° electrical):

| FET role | Instantaneous loss | Average over the electrical cycle |
|---|---|---|
| Low-side "always on" in sector | 11.1 W | Each sector loads two FETs. Losses are spread evenly over the 6 FETs |
| High-side (D) + its own low-side (1−D) | 11.1 W split by D | |
| **Average per FET** | | **22.3 / 6 = 3.7 W** |
