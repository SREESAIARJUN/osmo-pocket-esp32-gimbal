## 0. Feasibility Findings to Resolve Before Layout

1. **A 20×20 mm "nano" cannot sustain 4 × 80 A, even in bursts.** The model in §3 puts the worst case at about 80 W total. A 20×20 board has about 2.5 J/K of thermal mass, so 80 W raises it by about 32 K per second, and 24 power FETs do not fit at sensible spacing. **This design is therefore baselined on a 30.5×30.5 mm board (25.5 mm mounting pattern).** On a 20×20 board, burst rating would be about 45 A per motor for 2 s or less.
2. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** In every viable 4-in-1 design, each motor channel has its own ESC MCU (AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32) that generates the 48 kHz PWM. The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bi-directional DShot600 to those four MCUs. The architecture below follows that split.
3. **Device parameters are representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 DFN values.** Substitute your chosen part's datasheet values into the equations. The equations themselves are complete.

## 1. System Architecture

```
 6S LiPo (25.2 V max) ──┬── Bulk cap + TVS ── VBAT rail (shared 4-in-1 bus)
                        │
         ┌──────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────┐
         │   ESC #1..#4 (identical):                            │
         │   AT32F421 (AM32) → FD6288Q driver → 6 × (2∥ FET)     │
         │   ← BEMF comparators, phase shunt / bus shunt         │
         └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                 ▲ bidir DShot600 (single wire per motor, open-drain style)
                 │
 STM32H743VIT6 (ArduPilot) — TIM1_CH1..CH4 → M1..M4
   IMU (SPI1), Baro (I2C2), OSD AT7456E (SPI2), Flash/SD (SDMMC1), UARTs
```

**Per-motor power stage:**
- 3 half-bridges.
- Each switch position uses **2 FETs in parallel**, giving 12 FETs per motor and 48 per board.
- Drive is six-step trapezoidal with **complementary (synchronous) PWM at f_sw = 48 kHz**.
