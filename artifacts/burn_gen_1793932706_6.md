## 1. Field‑Oriented Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

Electrical angle, with encoder offset θ_off found at calibration:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Clarke transform (amplitude‑invariant, i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant and Decoupling

The PMSM in the dq frame:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m$$

Applying the decoupling feedforward

$$v_d^{ff} = -\omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q^{ff} = \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda_m)$$

reduces each axis to the first‑order plant 1/(Ls + R).

### 1.3 PI Design by Pole‑Zero Cancellation

Choose a current bandwidth ω_c, typically 2π·1.5 kHz at 20 kHz PWM. Then:

$$K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c$$

The resulting closed loop is ω_c/(s + ω_c). Keep ω_c ≤ 2π·f_s/12 to tolerate the 1.5‑sample computation and PWM delay.

### 1.4 Discrete PI with Back‑Calculation Anti‑Windup

$$u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\qquad u_k^{sat}=\mathrm{sat}(u_k)$$

$$I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw} T_s (u^{sat}_k - u_k),\qquad K_{aw}\approx K_i/K_p$$
