## 0. Architecture Clarification and Baseline Assumptions

In a "4-in-1 ESC" stack, the **STM32H743 does not drive the MOSFETs directly.** The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600. Each of the four ESC channels has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, which performs commutation at 48 kHz PWM. Section 3 covers the H743, and Sections 1–2 cover the ESC power stage.
