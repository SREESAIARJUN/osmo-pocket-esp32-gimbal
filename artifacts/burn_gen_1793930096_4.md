# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #20): Design Analysis

## 0. Assumptions and Scope

Every number below depends on the following assumptions. Replace them with the real BOM and stack-up when they are fixed.

**Architecture**

- The STM32H743 is the flight controller and runs ArduPilot. It does **not** drive the bridges directly.
- The 4-in-1 ESC carries four independent ESC MCUs, assumed to be AM32 on STM32G071 or AT32F421.
- This split is standard. Bi-directional DShot is a protocol between the H743 and those ESC MCUs.

**Electrical**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo: 25.2 V max, 22.2 V nominal |
| Burst current | 80 A per motor (phase/leg current) |
| PWM frequency | 48 kHz, center-aligned, complementary (synchronous rectification) |
| Commutation | Six-step trapezoidal, two legs conducting at any instant |
| Bridge | 6 single N-FETs per motor, 24 total |

**Assumed 40 V DFN5x6-class MOSFET**

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| On-resistance (V_GS = 10 V, 25 °C) | R_DS(on),25 | 0.9 mΩ |
| R_DS(on) temperature coefficient | α | 0.0045 /°C |
| Total gate charge | Q_g | 60 nC |
| Reverse-recovery charge | Q_rr | 50 nC |
| Output charge | Q_oss | 60 nC |
| Effective rise/fall times | t_r / t_f | 20 ns / 20 ns |
| Body-diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Dead time | t_dt | 200 ns |
| Junction-to-case thermal resistance | R_θJC | 1.2 °C/W |

**Board**

- 30.5 × 30.5 mm ESC board, 2 oz copper.
- Estimated power-path copper and solder resistance per motor: 0.5 mΩ.

> **Design-review flag:** A true "nano" (20 × 20 mm) 4-in-1 cannot carry 4 × 80 A even for bursts. Section 4 shows why. Treat 80 A as a ≤ 3–5 s per-motor burst rating on a 30.5 mm board with prop-wash airflow.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

R_DS(on)(T_j) = R_DS(on),25 · [1 + α(T_j − 25)]

At T_j = 100 °C:

R_DS(on) = 0.9 mΩ × 1.3375 = **1.204 mΩ**

### 1.2 Per-motor conduction

In six-step drive with synchronous rectification, at every instant:

- the PWM leg conducts through either its high-side FET (on-time) or its low-side FET (off-time);
- the opposite leg's low-side FET conducts continuously.

So exactly two FET channels carry the full current I at all times:

P_cond,FET = 2 · I² · R_DS(on)(T_j) = 2 × 80² × 1.204 mΩ = **15.4 W**

P_cond,Cu = I² · R_Cu = 6400 × 0.5 mΩ = **3.2 W**

### 1.3 Per-device average

Each phase conducts for 240° of every 360° electrical cycle (120° high, 120° low). The electrical frequency (≥ several hundred Hz at burst) is much faster than the die thermal time constant (~ms), so averaging is valid:

P_FET,avg ≈ 15.4 W / 6 = **2.57 W per FET**

### 1.4 Thermal runaway check

Conduction loss rises with T_j, which raises T_j further. The loop gain is:

G = α · P_cond · R_θ(J→board) = 0.0045 × 2.57 × 4.2 ≈ 0.05

G ≪ 1, so the loop is stable at the device level. The board-level loop is closed in §4.

## 2. Switching Loss Model at 48 kHz

Each PWM period contains one hard turn-on and one hard turn-off in the switching leg.

| Term | Expression | Value at V_in = 25.2 V, I = 80 A |
|---|---|---|
| V–I overlap | ½ · V · I · (t_r + t_f) · f_sw | 0.5 × 25.2 × 80 × 40 ns × 48 k = **1.94 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V · f_sw | 50 nC × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.06 W** |
| C_oss charge | Q_oss · V · f_sw | 60 nC × 25.2 × 48 k = **0.07 W** |
