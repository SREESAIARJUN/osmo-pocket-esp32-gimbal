I don't have the Job #44 specification, so this design is built on the assumptions below. Every quantity is a parameter, so the math and code stay valid when you substitute your actual hardware values.

## 1. FOC Inner Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Transforms

**Electrical angle.** With p pole pairs (p = 11), mechanical angle θ_m and encoder offset θ_0:

$$\theta_e = p\,\theta_m - \theta_0$$

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant):
$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

**Park transform:**
$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e&\sin\theta_e\\-\sin\theta_e&\cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

The inverse Park transform is the transpose of this matrix.
