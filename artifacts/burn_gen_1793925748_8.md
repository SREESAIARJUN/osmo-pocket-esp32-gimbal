**Electrothermal model, switching analysis, bidirectional DShot600 DMA plan, and reference netlist**

## 0. Feasibility Review

Two points in the brief need resolving before anyone commits to layout.

**1. The H743 does not drive the MOSFETs.** In every 4-in-1 architecture that supports bidirectional DShot, each motor has its own ESC MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071. That MCU generates the 48 kHz PWM and commutation. The H743 only produces the DShot600 frames and decodes the eRPM telemetry. The switching math in §2 therefore applies to the ESC stage. The DMA math in §4 applies to the H743.

**2. "80 A burst" on a nano form factor only works under narrow conditions.** The model below shows that 4 × 80 A simultaneously means:

- about 320 A of battery current,
- about 100 W of loss in a board with roughly 4–5 J/K of thermal mass,
- about 22 K/s adiabatic temperature rise.

That is a **≤ 2–3 s single-event rating at best**. I recommend the spec read **"80 A per channel, 1 s burst, ≤ 1 channel at a time; 4 × 45 A, 10 s burst; 4 × 30 A continuous with airflow."** All numbers below are derived so you can re-run them with your actual FET choice.

| Symbol | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| V_bus | 25.2 V | 6S full charge |

| R_ds(on),25 | 1.2 mΩ @ V_gs = 10 V | datasheet typical |
| α_R | ≈ 0.0055 /K | R(T) = R₂₅·[1 + α(T−25)] |
| t_r, t_f | 15 ns each | 1 A-class driver, R_g ≈ 2.2 Ω, Q_gd ≈ 8 nC |
| Q_g | 30 nC | total gate charge |
| Q_rr | 30 nC | body diode |
| Q_oss | 30 nC | output charge |
| V_f (body diode) | 0.8 V | at 80 A |
| t_dead | 100 ns | per edge |
| Board | 25.5 × 25.5 mm, 6 layer, 2 oz | assumed |
| T_amb | 40 °C | in-frame |

**FET voltage margin.** A 40 V part on 6S gives only 1.59× margin. Ringing on a nano layout routinely reaches 8–10 V. Either:

- use 60 V FETs (higher R_ds(on), so redo §2), or
- enforce ≥ 1000 µF of low-ESR bulk at the pads plus 4 × 1 µF/50 V X7R per half-bridge.
