# STM32H743 Nano Flight Controller + 80 A-Burst 4-in-1 ESC: Design Basis, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan and Netlists

**Job #29, Rev A (Architecture Baseline)**

## 0. Architectural Clarifications

**Partitioning.** ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The STM32H743 is the flight controller, and it sends bi-directional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs that perform commutation and PWM. This design therefore uses:

- **FC:** STM32H743VIT6 running ArduPilot
- **ESC:** 4 × AT32F421K8U7 (or STM32G071) running AM32 at 48 kHz center-aligned PWM, with 4 × FD6288Q three-phase gate drivers and 24 × N-MOSFETs
- **Topology:** stacked FC + ESC (preferred), or an AIO board with shared ground and power planes

**The headline result, up front.** On a nano (20 × 20 mm mounting) form factor, an "80 A burst" rating is a transient thermal-capacity rating, not a steady-state one. The model below shows:

| Load case | Result |
|---|---|
| 80 A per motor, all 4 motors | ≈ 2 s to reach thermal limit |
| 80 A per motor, 1 motor | ≈ 8 s to reach thermal limit |
| Continuous, all 4 motors in prop-wash | ≈ 25 A per motor |
| Battery current, 4 × 80 A at full duty | ≈ 320 A, which exceeds any XT60/XT30 and most 6S packs |

The marketing rating must be defined against this model.

## 1. Design Assumptions

|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S, 22.2 V nominal (25.2 V max) |
| Phase burst current | I | 80 A (per motor) |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz |
| MOSFET | – | 40 V, PDFN 3.3×3.3, R_DS(on),25 = 1.4 mΩ (max, V_GS = 10 V) |
