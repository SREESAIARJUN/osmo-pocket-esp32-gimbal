# STM32H743 Nano Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Design Package (Job #35)

**Scope:** Thermal and loss model at 80 A burst, switching losses at 48 kHz, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer and DMA allocation, schematic netlists, and thermal budget.

---

## 0. Architecture Constraints

These constraints shape every model below.

**C1. The H743 does not commutate the motors.**
- ArduPilot has no on-board FOC/6-step ESC driver for gate-level control.
- The 4-in-1 section therefore contains its own ESC MCUs running AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware. Either 4× AT32F421 or 4× STM32G071 works.
- The H743 talks to them only via DShot600.
- The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs, not the H743.

**C2. 80 A per channel on a "nano" footprint (20×20 mm or 25.5×25.5 mm) is a transient rating only.**
- Section 3 shows roughly 90–100 W of total board dissipation at 4×80 A.
- That is sustainable for about 2 seconds, not continuously.
- The rating must be specified as `80 A burst ≤ 2 s at 40 °C start, ≥ 10 s recovery`. The continuous rating is about 35–40 A per channel.

**C3. Stack separation is required.**
- Place the H743 and IMU on a separate board in the stack, connected by an 8-pin JST-SH.
- The ICM-42688-P drift spec and the H743's 105–125 °C limits will not survive sharing copper with roughly 100 W of FET heating.
- The netlists below are split accordingly.

## 2. Loss Model per ESC Channel

### 2.1 Conduction Topology (6-step, synchronous rectification, complementary PWM)

During any commutation step, current I flows through two phases:
- **PWM'd phase:** high-side FET conducts for D, low-side FET (sync rectifier) for (1−D).
- **Return phase:** low-side FET conducts continuously.

Conduction loss:
```
P_cond = I²·R_ds(Tj)·[D + (1−D)] + I²·R_ds(Tj)·1  =  2·I²·R_ds(Tj)
```
This is independent of D. That is the key result: at burst current, conduction loss does not fall with throttle when synchronous rectification is used.

Temperature dependence:
```
R_ds(Tj) = R₂₅·[1 + α·(Tj − 25)]
```

### 2.2 Electrothermal Self-Consistency

Conduction loss raises Tj, which raises R_ds, which raises loss. The fixed point is:
```
Tj = T_ref + R_θ·P(Tj)
P(Tj) = 2·I²·R₂₅·[1 + α(Tj−25)] + P_sw
```
Solving:
```
Tj = [T_ref + R_θ·(2I²R₂₅(1 − 25α) + P_sw)] / [1 − R_θ·2I²R₂₅·α]
```
Thermal runaway occurs when the denominator approaches 0, i.e. when `R_θ ≥ 1/(2I²R₂₅α)`.

At I = 80 A:
```
1/(2 · 6400 · 0.001 · 0.0055) = 14.2 K/W
```
Any per-channel steady-state thermal path worse than about 14 K/W runs away at 80 A. A nano board in prop-wash is about 7 K/W for the whole board, roughly 28 K/W per channel. **This is the mathematical proof that 80 A cannot be a continuous rating.**
