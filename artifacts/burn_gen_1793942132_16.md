# STM32H743 4-in-1 ESC Nano Flight Controller (Job #35): System Architecture & Mathematical Model

## 0. Architectural Ground Truth

Three constraints determine the rest of the design.

1. **The H743 must not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot on an H743 runs a 400 Hz to 2 kHz scheduler with 10–50 µs interrupt latency jitter. Sensorless 6-step or FOC at 48 kHz needs deterministic, sub-µs ADC-to-PWM timing on four motors. The correct partition is:
   - The **H743 runs ArduPilot** and outputs bi-directional DShot600.
   - **Four dedicated ESC MCUs** run AM32. Each is an AT32F421 or STM32G071, one per motor.
2. **"Nano" plus "80 A burst" is a transient rating, not a continuous one.** The math below shows that 4 × 80 A dissipates about 110 W. A 30.5 × 30.5 mm board can sustain that for roughly 2–4 s. The honest continuous rating is about 30–35 A per channel with prop-wash airflow.
3. **At 48 kHz with an 80 A di/dt, layout parasitics are the main voltage risk.** Section 2.5 shows that 10 nH of loop inductance destroys 40 V FETs on 6S.

All component values below are representative design assumptions. You must re-verify every parameter against the specific datasheet revision you procure.

## 2. Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)]
$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R_DS = 1.30 × 1.45 = **1.885 mΩ**. Round to 1.9 mΩ for the worst-case burst.

### 2.2 Conduction losses: 6-step trapezoidal with synchronous rectification

In each 60° commutation sector, one leg is PWM'd and one leg is held low. The current path is:

- **PWM leg high-side (HS)** on for D·T_sw
- **PWM leg low-side (LS)** synchronous-rectifying for (1–D)·T_sw
- **Opposite-leg LS** on for 100 %

So exactly two FETs carry I at all times:

$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

**Burst (80 A, 125 °C):**
$$
P_{cond,ch} = 2 \cdot 80^2 \cdot 1.9\times10^{-3} = \mathbf{24.3\ W}
$$

**Per-FET averaging over the electrical period.** Each FET occupies a conducting position for 120° of 360°. At full throttle (D → 1):

$$
P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3}D\,I^2R,\qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}(1-D)I^2R + \tfrac{1}{3}I^2R
