## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path
In 6-step commutation, two phase legs carry the motor current at any instant. With synchronous rectification:
- During the on-time (fraction D), current flows through the high-side (HS) FET of phase A and the low-side (LS) FET of phase B.
- During the off-time (1−D), current freewheels through the LS FET of phase A (channel, not body diode) and the same LS FET of phase B.

So the motor current always flows through exactly **two channel resistances in series**, independent of duty:

$$P_{cond} = I_{ph,rms}^2 \cdot 2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left(\frac{T_j + 273}{298}\right)^{\alpha}, \quad \alpha \approx 2.0\text{–}2.3 \;⟨param⟩$$

For T_j = 125 °C and α = 2.1: R(125 °C) ≈ 1.1 × 1.82 ≈ **2.0 mΩ**. Use the datasheet normalized R_DS(on)-vs-T_j curve when available.

### 1.3 Ripple correction
The phase current is not pure DC. Its RMS value is:

$$I_{rms} = \sqrt{I_{avg}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12}}, \qquad \Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-E_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

Example: L_ph(line-line) = 15 µH, worst-case D = 0.5, E = 12.6 V:
ΔI = 12.6 × 0.5 / (15 µ × 48 k) = 8.75 A_pp, which adds about 0.04 A_rms. **The ripple contribution is negligible at 80 A.**

### 1.4 Copper and solder losses

$$P_{Cu} = I^2 R_{trace}, \quad R_{trace} = \frac{\rho_{Cu}(T)\,\ell}{w\,t\,n_{layers}}$$

For 2 oz copper (t = 70 µm), R_sheet ≈ 0.245 mΩ/□ at 25 °C. A 6 mm path that is 4 squares long, paralleled across 4 layers, gives ≈ 0.25 mΩ. Add about 0.3 mΩ for pads and phase-wire solder joints. The loop total is ≈ 0.55 mΩ, or ≈ 0.7 mΩ hot.

### 1.5 Results per channel at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

| Term | Expression | Value |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction | 80² × 2 × 2.0 mΩ | **25.6 W** |
| Copper/joints | 80² × 0.7 mΩ | 4.5 W |
| **Conduction subtotal** | | **≈ 30 W/channel** |

With **all 4 channels at burst**, the conduction loss is **≈ 120 W**.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the PWM-ing leg switches. One HS/LS pair commutates per period, and dead-time is handled by the driver.

### 2.1 Hard-switching V·I overlap

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I \,(t_r + t_f)\, f_{sw}$$

The rise/fall times are set by the gate charge and gate drive:

$$t_{r} \approx \frac{Q_{gd}+ Q_{gs2}}{I_{g,on}}, \quad I_{g,on} = \frac{V_{drv}-V_{plateau}}{R_{g,ext}+R_{g,int}}$$

Example: Q_gd + Q_gs2 = 12 nC, V_plateau = 3.5 V, R_g = 6.8 Ω + 1 Ω. Then I_g ≈ 0.83 A and t_r ≈ 14 ns. Take t_r + t_f ≈ 25 ns.

$$P_{VI} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 25\text{n} \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.21\ W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery
The LS body diode conducts during dead-time and is then recovered by HS turn-on.

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr}V_{bus}f_{sw} = 30\text{n}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = 0.036\text{ W}$$

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} = Q_{oss}V_{bus}f_{sw}\ (\text{charge}+\text{discharge, conservative}) = 40\text{n}\times 25.2\times 48\text{k}= 0.048\text{ W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction

$$P_{dt} = V_F\,I\,(2\,t_{dt})\,f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times (2\times 200\text{n}) \times 48\text{k} = \mathbf{1.23\ W}$$

This term is the same order as the V·I loss. Minimize dead-time to what the driver and FET shoot-through margin allow.

### 2.5 Gate drive (dissipated in driver and R_g, not in the FET die)

$$P_{gate} = 2\,Q_g V_{drv} f_{sw} = 2\times40\text{n}\times10\times48\text{k} = 0.038\text{ W}$$

### 2.6 Switching subtotal per channel
**≈ 2.5 W.** At 48 kHz and 80 A, **conduction dominates about 12:1**. Reducing R_DS(on) or paralleling FETs is worth far more than lowering f_sw.

### 2.7 Total burst dissipation

| | Per channel | 4 channels |
|---|---|---|
| Conduction (FET + Cu) | 30.1 W | 120.4 W |
| Switching + dead-time | 2.5 W | 10.0 W |
| Bulk cap ESR (≈ 15 A_rms ripple × 15 mΩ effective) | 3.4 W* | — |
| **Total** | **≈ 33 W** | **≈ 134 W** |

*The bulk capacitor loss is shared at the board level. Count it once, as ≈ 3–5 W total.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Network (per FET, Foster/Cauer reduction)

```
 P_FET ─┬─[R_θJC 1.5 K/W]─┬─[R_θCB 0.4 K/W solder+via]─┬─[R_θBA (board→air)]─ T_amb
        │                 │                            │
      C_die            C_pkg                      C_board (shared)
```

### 3.2 Junction-to-board rise (quasi-steady)
The two conducting FETs share 25.6 W, so each dissipates ≈ 12.8 W plus its switching share:

$$\Delta T_{JB} = P_{FET}(R_{\theta JC}+R_{\theta CB}) = 13.5 \times 1.9 = \mathbf{25.7\ K}$$

The 6-step rotation averages this across 6 FETs per channel. The per-FET average is ≈ 1/3 of peak conduction time. However, use the peak value at low eRPM or stall, where commutation is slow relative to the die thermal time constant (τ_die ≈ 1–5 ms).

### 3.3 Board heating during the burst (lumped capacitance)

$$T_{board}(t) = T_{amb} + P_{tot}R_{\theta BA}\left(1-e^{-t/\tau}\right), \quad \tau = R_{\theta BA}C_{board}$$
