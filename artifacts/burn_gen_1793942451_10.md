# STM32H743 AIO Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage, Thermal, and Bi-Directional DShot Design Package

This package covers the electrothermal loss model, burst thermal limits, the bi-directional DShot600 timing and DMA plan, reference netlists, and the items that must be verified on hardware. Component values are representative of this class of design. Treat every number as a model input to replace with your selected datasheets and bench data.

## 0. Architecture Clarifications and Feasibility

**Partitioning.** The STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller only. It does not commutate the motors. Each ESC channel needs its own commutation MCU, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 talks to those MCUs over bi-directional DShot600.

**Baseline assumptions:**

| Parameter | Symbol | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Battery | V_bus | 6S: 22.2 V nominal, 25.2 V worst case |
| Burst phase current per channel | I_ph | 80 A |
| PWM frequency | f_sw | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous) PWM |
| Commutation | — | 6-step trapezoidal |
| MOSFET | — | 40 V, DFN3.3×3.3, one per switch position (24 FETs total) |
| On-resistance | R_ds(on),25 | 1.5 mΩ max at V_gs = 10 V |
| On-resistance temperature coefficient | α | ≈ 0.006 /K, so R(125 °C) ≈ 1.6 × R25 = 2.4 mΩ |
| Switching times | t_r, t_f | ≈ 10 ns each (about 2 A gate drive, Q_gd ≈ 8 nC) |
| Body-diode charges | Q_rr, Q_oss | ≈ 30 nC, ≈ 40 nC |
| Body-diode forward voltage | V_f | 0.8 V |
| Dead time | t_dt | 100 ns |

**Feasibility verdict.** The model below shows about 130 W of dissipation at 4 × 80 A. That is far beyond what a 20 × 20 mm board can remove continuously. "80 A burst" on a nano board is only defensible as a time-limited rating of roughly 1 s, and only with heavy copper. The model in Section 4 quantifies this.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, the phase current always flows through exactly two FETs:

- During the on-time: the high-side FET of one phase and the low-side FET of another.
- During the off-time (freewheel): two low-side FETs.

Therefore, per channel:

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds}(T_j)$$

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right]$$

### 1.3 Numbers at T_j = 125 °C

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2 \times 80^2 \times 2.4\,\text{m}\Omega = 30.7\ \text{W}$$

$$P_{cond,total} = 4 \times 30.7 = 122.9\ \text{W}$$

### 1.4 Electrothermal coupling and runaway criterion

Combining the loss equation with T_j = T_ref + R_th · P and solving self-consistently:

$$P = \frac{2 I^2 R_{25}\left[1+\alpha(T_{ref}-25)\right]}{1 - 2\alpha I^2 R_{25} R_{th}}$$

The stability condition is:

$$2\alpha I^2 R_{25} R_{th} < 1$$

At 80 A this gives R_th < 8.7 K/W, where R_th is the effective junction-to-reference resistance seen by the conducting pair.

A single DFN3.3 on a 20 × 20 board with no forced convection is roughly 30–40 K/W junction-to-ambient. **A steady 80 A operating point does not exist. Only transient operation is possible.**

### 1.5 Copper losses (do not ignore)

Phase traces at 80 A with an assumed 2 oz, 4-layer stack and about 0.3 mΩ of effective path resistance add I²R ≈ 1.9 W per channel.

Battery current is not 4 × 80 A. To first order:

$$I_{bat} \approx \sum_k D_k\, I_{ph,k}$$

At D = 0.8, that is about 256 A for 4 × 80 A. Size the battery pads, main bus, and shunt for the actual expected peak, which is usually lower because all four motors rarely peak together.

## 2. Switching Loss Model (48 kHz)

Only the actively switching high-side FET hard-switches. The low-side transitions are ZVS-like because the body diode clamps during dead time.

### 2.1 Hard-switching overlap (V-I crossover)

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac{1}{2} V_{bus} I_{ph} (t_r + t_f) f_{sw}$$

$$= 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 20\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{kHz} = 0.968\ \text{W}$$

### 2.2 Reverse recovery

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\,\text{nC} \times 25.2 \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}$$

This also adds turn-on current overshoot, so check the SOA.

### 2.3 Output capacitance

$$P_{oss} \approx \tfrac{1}{2} Q_{oss} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 0.024\ \text{W}$$

### 2.4 Dead-time body-diode conduction (two edges per period)

$$P_{dt} = V_f\, I_{ph}\, (2 t_{dt})\, f_{sw} = 0.8 \times 80 \times 200\,\text{ns} \times 48\,\text{k} = 0.614\ \text{W}$$

### 2.5 Gate drive

$$P_g = Q_g V_{dr} f_{sw}$$

With Q_g = 40 nC and V_dr = 10 V, this is 0.019 W per switching FET. Most of it dissipates in the driver and gate resistors, not the FET.

### 2.6 Switching subtotal

**Total switching-related loss ≈ 1.64 W per channel, or 6.6 W for all four.**

Sensitivity notes:

- P_VI scales linearly with f_sw. At 24 kHz it would drop to about 0.48 W.
- The dead time should be minimized, consistent with the driver's propagation skew, because it costs nearly as much as the V-I overlap at 80 A.
