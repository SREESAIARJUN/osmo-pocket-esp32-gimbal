# STM32H743 FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #29): Loss Model, Thermal Analysis, and Bidirectional DShot600 Allocation

## 0. Architecture Corrections

Three points in the brief need correcting before any math is meaningful:

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot has no BLDC commutation firmware. The 4-in-1 ESC needs its own MCU per motor (or one MCU per pair), such as AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 or BLHeli_32. The H743 only generates bidirectional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry. The 48 kHz PWM is an ESC-firmware parameter, not an H743 timer.
2. **"Nano" form factor vs. 80A.** On a 20×20 mm board, 4×80A is not physically credible. Section 3 shows that even a 30.5×30.5 mm board survives 80A only as a burst of about 1–2 s. I assume an H743 FC stacked on a 30.5×30.5 mm 4-in-1 ESC. If you truly need 20×20, derate to about 25–35A burst.
3. **"80A burst" is interpreted as 80A per motor**, at 6S (25.2 V max).

## 2. Loss Model (per motor)

### 2.1 Conduction loss

Six-step trapezoidal drive with synchronous rectification (complementary PWM) keeps current flowing through exactly two FETs at all times:
- **PWM-on:** high side of phase X plus low side of phase Y.
- **PWM-off:** low side of X (freewheeling) plus low side of Y.

Therefore:

$$P_{cond} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS(on)}(T_j), \qquad R_{DS(on)}(T_j)=R_{25}\,k_T = 1.92\ \text{m}\Omega$$

$$P_{cond} = 2(80)^2(1.92\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{24.6\ W}$$

Instantaneously, each of the two conducting FETs dissipates about 12.3 W. Averaged over the 120° conduction window, each FET carries roughly 1/3 of the motor's loss, about 8.2 W.

### 2.2 Hard-switching loss (one leg switches per PWM edge pair)

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5(25.2)(80)(30\times10^{-9})(48\times10^3) = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

### 2.3 Secondary switching terms

| Term | Formula | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr · V_bus · f_sw | 0.048 W |
| C_oss discharge | ½ C_oss V_bus² f_sw | 0.023 W |
| Dead-time body-diode conduction | 2 · V_f · I · t_dt · f_sw | 0.61 W |
| Gate drive (2 FETs/cycle, mostly in driver) | 2 · Q_g · V_gs · f_sw | 0.048 W |

### 2.4 Totals

$$P_{motor} \approx 24.6 + 1.45 + 0.05 + 0.02 + 0.61 + 0.05 \approx \mathbf{26.8\ W}$$

$$P_{ESC,4\times} \approx \mathbf{107\ W} \quad (+\ \text{copper and cap ESR, typically } 5\text{–}10\ \text{W at this current})$$

**Key insight:** at 48 kHz, conduction loss is about 92% of the total. Reducing f_sw to 24 kHz saves only about 1 W per motor. Lower R_DS(on) or parallel FETs are the real lever.

**Sensitivity:**
- ∂P/∂I = 4·I·R ≈ 0.61 W/A at 80A.
- Halving R_DS(on) saves about 12.3 W per motor.

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Lumped board capacitance (30.5×30.5, 6-layer, 2 oz, ~80% fill)

**Copper:**
- Volume = 0.03² × 70 µm × 6 × 0.8 ≈ 3.0×10⁻⁷ m³
- Mass = 3.0×10⁻⁷ × 8960 ≈ 2.7 g
- Heat capacity = 2.7 g × 0.385 J/g·K ≈ 1.04 J/K

**FR-4 plus FETs/components:** about 1.5 J/K is effectively coupled.

**Total:** C_th ≈ 2.5 J/K.

### 3.2 Board-to-ambient resistance

| Condition | R_θba |
|---|---|
| Still air | 15–25 K/W |
| Prop-wash | ≈ 4 K/W (assumed) |

### 3.3 Transient (burst) response

$$T_b(t) = T_a + P R_{\theta ba}\left(1-e^{-t/\tau}\right), \qquad \tau = R_{\theta ba} C_{th} \approx 10\ \text{s}$$

For t ≪ τ, the board heats at about dT/dt ≈ P/C ≈ 107/2.5 ≈ **43 K/s**.

**Junction rise over board:** the conducting FET dissipates 12.3 W through about 2 K/W (θ_jc plus solder and vias), giving **ΔT_jb ≈ 25 K**.

**Limit:** T_j,max = 150 °C. Applying a 25 K design margin gives T_j ≤ 125 °C, so T_b ≤ 100 °C.

From T_a = 40 °C:

$$t_{burst} \approx -\tau \ln\!\left(1 - \frac{60}{107\cdot 4}\right) \approx \mathbf{1.5\ s}$$

### 3.4 Continuous rating (same limits)

- Allowable dissipation: P_cont = 60 K / 4 K/W = 15 W total, or 3.75 W per motor.
- Subtracting about 0.4 W of switching losses at reduced current leaves about 3.3 W of conduction:

$$I_{cont} \approx \sqrt{3.3/(2\times1.92\times10^{-3})} \approx \mathbf{29\ A}$$

**Honest rating:** "30A continuous / 80A 1.5 s burst per motor (6S, prop-wash, 40 °C ambient)."

**Design recommendations:**
- Enable firmware current limit with I²t integration matching τ ≈ 10 s.
- Add an NTC near the FETs, and enable ArduPilot `ESC temp` via ESC telemetry if a serial telemetry wire is present.
