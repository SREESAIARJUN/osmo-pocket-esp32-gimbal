## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

The STM32H743 cannot commutate four BLDC motors in firmware. ArduPilot has no ESC commutation code, and bidirectional DShot implies a separate ESC MCU that replies with eRPM telemetry. The architecture is therefore:

```
                 ┌─────────────── STM32H743VIT6 (FC, ArduPilot) ───────────────┐
                 │ TIM1 CH1..CH4 ── DShot600 bidir ──► 4× ESC MCU (AT32F421 /  │
                 │                                    STM32G071, AM32 firmware)│
                 └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
   Each ESC MCU → 3-phase gate driver (FD6288Q class) → 6× N-ch MOSFET → motor
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in synchronous 6-step commutation

In each commutation sector, two phase legs carry the motor current I:

- **PWM leg:** the high side (HS) conducts for fraction D of each period, and the low side (LS) freewheels for (1 − D).
- **Return leg:** the LS is fully on for the entire sector.

Each leg therefore always has exactly one channel carrying I. The conduction loss is independent of D:

$$
P_{cond,FET}(T_j) = 2\,I^2\,R_{ds}(T_j), \qquad R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds(25)}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]
$$

$$
P_{cond,Cu} = I^2 R_{Cu}
$$

### 1.2 Evaluation at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds | P_cond,FET (per motor) | P_Cu | Total per motor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 2·6400·0.0012 = **15.4 W** | 3.2 W | 18.6 W |
| 100 °C | 1.65 mΩ | **21.1 W** | 3.2 W* | 24.3 W |
| 125 °C | 1.80 mΩ | **23.0 W** | 3.2 W* | 26.2 W |

### 1.3 Electro-thermal stability

Conduction loss rises with temperature, so the junction temperature must satisfy a fixed-point equation:

$$
T_j = T_{ref} + R_{th}\cdot 2I^2R_{ds(25)}\left[1+\alpha(T_j-25)\right]
$$

$$
T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + R_{th}\,2I^2R_{ds(25)}(1-25\alpha)}{1 - R_{th}\,2I^2R_{ds(25)}\,\alpha}
$$

**Thermal runaway** occurs when the denominator reaches zero:

$$
R_{th}\cdot 2I^2 R_{ds(25)}\,\alpha \ge 1
$$
