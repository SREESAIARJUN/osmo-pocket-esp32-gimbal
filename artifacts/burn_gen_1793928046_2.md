# 3-Axis Gimbal FOC Control and Dual-IMU Sensor Fusion: Design Specification

Gimbal motor control and IMU-based stabilization, covering field-oriented control (FOC), cascaded PID, anti-cogging feedforward and dual ICM-42688-P vibration rejection.

> **Scope note:** I don't have the internal "Job #9" spec. The design is parameterized against a typical gimbal stack. Replace the values in §0 with your actual motor, encoder and MCU data. The equations and code are written generically.

## 0. Baseline Assumptions

| Item | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Motor | Gimbal BLDC (GBM2804-class), 12N14P → pole pairs **p = 7**, R ≈ 10 Ω, L ≈ 2 mH, K_t ≈ 0.08 N·m/A |
| Encoder | 14-bit absolute magnetic (AS5048A / MT6701), SPI, read every current-loop tick |
| MCU | STM32G4 / H7 class, FPU, center-aligned PWM at 20 kHz, ADC triggered at PWM center |
| Bus | V_dc = 12–16 V |
| IMU | 2 × ICM-42688-P on the camera plate, gyro and accel ODR = 8 kHz, shared CLKIN |

### Loop Rates

| Loop | Rate | Target bandwidth |
|---|---|---|
| Current (FOC) | 20 kHz | ω_c ≈ 2π·1500 rad/s |
| Velocity (gyro-fed) | 4 kHz | ω_v ≈ 2π·80 rad/s |
| Attitude / angle | 1 kHz | ω_θ ≈ 2π·15 rad/s |
| IMU fusion | 8 kHz in, decimated to 4 kHz out | — |

Keep roughly 1:5–1:10 bandwidth separation between nested loops.

## 1. Field-Oriented Current Loop

### 1.1 Electrical Model (dq frame, SPMSM, L_d ≈ L_q = L)

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\frac{di_d}{dt} - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\frac{di_q}{dt} + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

$$
\tau_e = \tfrac{3}{2}\,p\,\lambda_m\, i_q = K_t\, i_q,\qquad \theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{off}),\quad \omega_e = p\,\omega_m
$$

### 1.2 Transforms

**Clarke transform** (amplitude-invariant, with $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$):

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)
$$

**Park transform:**

$$
\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

**Inverse Park** is the transpose of the Park matrix.

### 1.3 Current PI with Decoupling

Pole-zero cancellation of the R/L plant yields a first-order closed loop with bandwidth ω_c:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_c
$$

The control law is:

$$
v_d^* = \mathrm{PI}_d(i_d^*-i_d) - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q^* = \mathrm{PI}_q(i_q^*-i_q) + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

The reference is $i_d^* = 0$.

**Voltage limiting.** The voltage vector is limited to the inscribed SVPWM circle:

$$
|v| \le V_{dc}/\sqrt3
$$

Priority goes to $v_d$:

$$
v_q^{max} = \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}
$$

### 1.4 Discrete PI with Back-Calculation Anti-Windup

Sample time is $T_s$:

$$
u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\qquad \bar u_k = \mathrm{sat}(u_k)
$$

$$
I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw} T_s(\bar u_k - u_k),\qquad K_{aw}\approx K_i/K_p
$$

**Delay compensation.** The one-sample PWM delay is compensated by advancing the inverse-Park angle:

$$
\theta_e' = \theta_e + 1.5\,\omega_e T_s
$$
