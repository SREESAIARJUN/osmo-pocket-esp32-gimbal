## 0. Architecture Decision and Assumptions

**The STM32H743 does not drive the gate drivers directly.** On every production "H743 4-in-1" AIO, the H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It sends bidirectional DShot600 to four dedicated ESC MCUs, which run AM32 or BLHeli_32-class firmware and handle commutation. This design follows that pattern:

```
6S LiPo ──► Power stage (4 × 3-phase bridges, 24 FETs)
                ▲ gate drive
       4 × ESC MCU (AT32F421 / STM32G071, AM32, 48 kHz PWM)
                ▲ bidir DShot600 (1 wire each)
       STM32H743VIT6 @ 400 MHz (ArduPilot)
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current paths in 6-step synchronous commutation

With complementary (synchronous-rectified) PWM, the conduction path depends on the PWM state:

* **ON interval (D):** high-side FET of phase A and low-side FET of phase B conduct.
* **OFF interval (1−D):** low-side FET of phase A (sync rectifier) and low-side FET of phase B conduct.

Exactly **two FETs carry the phase current at all times, independent of D.**

### 1.2 RMS current including PWM ripple

Peak-to-peak ripple current:

$$\Delta I_{pp} = \frac{(V_{bus}-V_{bemf})\,D}{L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}, \qquad V_{bemf}\approx D\,V_{bus} \;\Rightarrow\; \Delta I_{pp,max}=\frac{V_{bus}}{4\,L_{ph}\,f_{sw}}$$

$$\Delta I_{pp,max}=\frac{25.2}{4\cdot 20\times10^{-6}\cdot 48\times10^{3}} = 6.56\ \text{A}$$

RMS current:

$$I_{rms}^2 = I_{pk}^2 + \frac{\Delta I_{pp}^2}{12} = 6400 + 3.6 \approx 6404\ \text{A}^2$$

At 48 kHz, ripple adds less than 0.06 %. This is one argument for 48 kHz over 24 kHz.

### 1.3 Temperature-dependent R_ds(on): electro-thermal fixed point

$$R_{ds}(T_j)=R_{ds,25}\left(\frac{T_j+273}{298}\right)^{\alpha},\quad \alpha\approx 2.0\text{–}2.5$$

$$P_{cond}(T_j)=2\,I_{rms}^2\,R_{ds}(T_j)$$

Because P depends on T_j and T_j = T_board + P·R_θ, solve the fixed point iteratively:

$$T_j^{(n+1)} = T_{board} + P\!\left(T_j^{(n)}\right) R_{\theta}$$

The iteration converges and the device is thermally stable only if:

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial T_j}\,R_\theta < 1$$

Otherwise you get thermal runaway. Check this numerically for your FET at 80 A.
