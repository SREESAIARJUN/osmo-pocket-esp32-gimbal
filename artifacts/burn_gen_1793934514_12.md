## 0. Scope, Assumptions and Feasibility Findings

I have no prior record of "Job #25," so this document defines its own design basis. **Every number below follows from the assumptions in §0.2. Replace them with your actual BOM datasheet values and re-run the equations.**

### 0.1 Feasibility findings

1. **Architecture.** The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It runs ArduPilot and generates bi-directional DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, typically an AT32F421 or STM32G071. The design below uses this split, which is how every production FC/ESC stack is built.

2. **80 A per channel cannot be continuous on a nano board.** The math in §2 shows about 28 W per channel (about 112 W per board) at 80 A. A 20×20 or 30×30 board cannot reject that heat continuously. Consequences:
   - 80 A must be specified as a **time-limited burst**, about 1 to 2 s from a cool start.
   - Realistic continuous capability is about 30 to 35 A per channel.
   - Any marketing claim of "80 A continuous" on this form factor should be rejected.

3. **Form factor.** An H743 nano FC is feasible using the TFBGA100 or LQFP100 package on a 20×20 mm board. The 80 A burst ESC realistically needs a 30.5×30.5 mm footprint. A 20×20 ESC tops out at roughly 40 to 45 A burst.

## 1. Commutation and Conduction Model

### 1.1 Conducting devices per commutation step

The ESC uses six-step trapezoidal drive with complementary (synchronous) PWM. In each 60° step, two phases conduct:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) FET conducts for duty fraction D. The low-side (LS) FET conducts synchronously for (1 − D).
- **Return leg:** the LS FET is on for the full step.

The instantaneous conduction loss in one channel is:

$$
P_{cond} = I^2 R_{ds} D + I^2 R_{ds}(1-D) + I^2 R_{ds} = 2 I^2 R_{ds}
$$

This result is independent of duty cycle. That matters because 80 A bursts happen at high throttle, where D approaches 1.

### 1.2 Temperature-corrected resistance

$$
R_{ds,hot} = R_{ds,25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)] \approx k_T \cdot R_{ds,25} = 1.5 \times 1.2 = 1.8\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

### 1.3 Numerical result at 80 A

| Term | Expression | Value |
|---|---|---|
| MOSFET conduction | 2 · 80² · 1.8 mΩ | **23.0 W** |
| Copper conduction | 80² · 0.5 mΩ | **3.2 W** |
| Total conduction per channel | | **26.2 W** |

### 1.4 Per-device average

Over one electrical revolution, each of the six FETs carries about one-third of the total conduction loss across its HS, sync and return roles:

$$
P_{FET,avg} \approx \frac{23.0}{6} \approx 3.8\ \text{W}
$$
