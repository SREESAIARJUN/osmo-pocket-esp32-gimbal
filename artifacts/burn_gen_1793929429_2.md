## 1. FOC Inner Current Loop

### 1.1 Transforms

The electrical angle is θ_e = p·(θ_m − θ_offset) mod 2π.

Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, with i_a + i_b + i_c = 0):

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix}i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.2 Plant model and decoupled PI control

The dq-frame plant is:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e \lambda_m$$

Feedforward cancels the cross-coupling and back-EMF terms, leaving a first-order plant 1/(Ls+R) on each axis.

Pole-zero cancellation design for target bandwidth ω_c:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c$$

The closed loop is then ≈ ω_c/(s+ω_c). With f_s = 20 kHz and a 1.5-sample effective delay, keep ω_c ≤ 2π·1.5 kHz. That gives K_p = 19.8 V/A and K_i = 52 800 V/(A·s).

### 1.3 Voltage limiting and SVPWM

The voltage vector is limited to the inscribed circle |v| ≤ V_bus/√3. The q-axis gets priority:

$$v_q \leftarrow \text{clamp}\!\left(v_q, \pm\sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2}\right)$$

SVPWM uses min-max zero-sequence injection on the three phase voltages:

$$v_0 = -\tfrac12(\max v_x + \min v_x),\qquad d_x = \tfrac12 + \frac{v_x + v_0}{V_{bus}}$$

## 2. Cascaded Angle → Velocity → Current Control

### 2.1 Structure

```
θ_ref ─►(+)─► C_θ ─►(+)─► C_ω ─►(+)─► i_q,ref ─► [current loop] ─► motor
         ▲  -        ▲ ω_ff   ▲ -     ▲ i_ff = J·α_ref/K_t + i_cog(θ) + i_grav
         θ_meas      ω_meas            
```

For gimbal stabilization, the velocity feedback ω_meas is the joint-axis projection of the fused camera gyro, not the encoder derivative. The angle error comes from the attitude estimate (stabilize mode) or the encoder (follow/lock mode).

Joint-axis gyro projection, where $\hat a_j$ is the joint axis expressed in the IMU frame (from the encoder-derived kinematic chain):

$$\omega_j = \hat a_j^{\,T}\,\boldsymbol\omega_{IMU}$$

### 2.2 Velocity PI with filtered derivative and back-calculation anti-windup

Error: e_ω = ω_ref − ω_meas.

Continuous-time law:

$$i_q^* = K_{pv}e_\omega + \int\!\big(K_{iv}e_\omega + K_{aw}(\text{sat}(u)-u)\big)dt - K_{dv}\,\frac{N s}{s+N}\,\omega_{meas}$$

Discrete form (Tustin integrator, T_s = 1/4000):

$$\begin{aligned}
I_k &= I_{k-1} + \tfrac{T_s}{2}K_{iv}(e_k + e_{k-1}) + T_s K_{aw}(u^{sat}_{k-1} - u_{k-1})\\
D_k &= \frac{2-NT_s}{2+NT_s}D_{k-1} - \frac{2K_{dv}N}{2+NT_s}(\omega_k - \omega_{k-1})\\
u_k &= K_{pv}e_k + I_k + D_k + i_{ff,k},\qquad u^{sat}_k = \text{clamp}(u_k, \pm I_{max})
\end{aligned}$$

The derivative acts on the measurement only, so setpoint steps do not cause derivative kick.

**Tuning.** Treat the closed current loop as ideal; the plant is K_t/(Js). For velocity bandwidth ω_v (choose ω_v ≈ ω_c/8 ≈ 2π·180 Hz, further limited by the first structural mode):

$$K_{pv} = \frac{J\omega_v}{K_t},\qquad K_{iv} = K_{pv}\frac{\omega_v}{4},\qquad K_{aw}=\frac{K_{iv}}{K_{pv}}$$
