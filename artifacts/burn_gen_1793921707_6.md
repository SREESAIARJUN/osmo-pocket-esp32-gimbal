## 0. Architectural Ground Rules

Three facts constrain the whole design.

1. **The H743 does not switch the bridge.** Each motor channel has a dedicated ESC MCU (baseline: AT32F421 running AM32) that generates the 48 kHz PWM and runs commutation. The H743 sends DShot600 commands and receives eRPM telemetry over the same wire. The 48 kHz figure is an ESC firmware setting (AM32 `PWM frequency`), not an H743 timer.
2. **"Nano" and "80 A burst" conflict.** A 20×20 mm board cannot carry 4×80 A, even for a burst, with credible copper. This baseline assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm stack**: a separate FC board on top of the 4-in-1 ESC board. That layout keeps the 480 MHz MCU and IMU away from roughly 90 W of switching noise. If the customer requires 20×20, the rating must drop to about 40–45 A burst. Section 2.6 shows why.
3. **MOSFET parameters are placeholders.** They are representative of a 40 V, 5×6 mm DFN power FET. Substitute the real datasheet values for the selected part and rerun the same equations. No number below should be treated as qualification data.
