## 0. Architecture Clarification and Baseline Assumptions

### 0.1 Partitioning
The STM32H743 cannot practically run four sensorless BLDC commutation loops and ArduPilot at the same time. ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The standard AIO partition is therefore:

| Block | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIT6 (LQFP100) or VIH6 (TFBGA100, for nano) | ArduPilot |
| ESC ×4 | AT32F421K8U7 or STM32G071 (one per motor) | AM32 (or BLHeli_32-compatible) |
| Gate drive ×4 | FD6288Q (3-phase half-bridge, bootstrap) | n/a |
| Power stage | 24× N-MOSFET, 40 V | n/a |
| Link FC→ESC | 4× bi-directional DShot600 | n/a |

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step commutation with synchronous (complementary) PWM
In each 60° commutation sector, exactly two phase legs carry the full phase current I:
- **PWM leg:** the high-side conducts for duty D; the complementary low-side conducts for (1−D) as a synchronous rectifier.
- **Return leg:** the low-side is held fully on.

So at every instant, current flows through two FET channels in series, independent of D (dead time is neglected here and treated in §2).

Each leg is active for 240° of the electrical cycle (120° high-side role, 120° low-side role). The per-motor conduction loss is:

$$
P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I^2\,R_{DS}(T_j)
$$

The per-FET average (symmetric, D → 1 at burst) is:

$$
P_{cond,FET} \approx \frac{I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)}{3}
$$

The temperature dependence is:

$$
R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

### 1.2 Self-consistent junction temperature
Conduction loss and T_j are coupled:

$$
T_j = T_{ref} + R_{\theta}\,P(T_j)
\;\Rightarrow\;
T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + R_\theta\left(\tfrac{I^2 R_{25}}{3}(1-25\alpha) + P_{sw}\right)}{1 - R_\theta \tfrac{I^2R_{25}\alpha}{3}}
$$

**Thermal runaway criterion:** the denominator must stay positive.

$$
R_\theta < \frac{3}{I^2 R_{25}\alpha} = \frac{3}{6400 \cdot 0.001 \cdot 0.006} = 78\ \text{K/W}
$$

This is satisfied for any realistic junction-to-board path.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 110 °C

$$
R_{DS}(110) = 1.0\,(1 + 0.006 \cdot 85) = 1.51\ \text{m}\Omega
$$

| P_cond per motor | 2 · 6400 · 1.51 m = **19.3 W** |

|---|---|---|
