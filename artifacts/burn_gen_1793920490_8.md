An H743 does not commutate the motors directly. In a 4-in-1 design, per-motor ESC MCUs (AM32/BLHeli_32 class) run six-step commutation at 48 kHz. The H743 runs ArduPilot and talks to them over bidirectional DShot600.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Six-step current distribution

In trapezoidal (six-step) commutation, each phase is active for 240° of the 360° electrical cycle: 120° sourcing and 120° sinking. During each 120° block, current flows through:

- **High-side FET (PWM leg):** conducts for fraction D of each PWM period.
- **Complementary low-side FET (synchronous rectification):** conducts for fraction (1 − D).
- **Low-side FET of the sinking phase:** on for the full 120°.

**Per-FET RMS currents** (D = duty, I = phase current, assumed flat-topped):

$$
I_{HS,rms}^2 = \frac{1}{3} D\, I^2 \qquad
I_{LS,rms}^2 = \frac{1}{3}I^2 + \frac{1}{3}(1-D)\,I^2
$$

Per phase leg (sum of both FETs), at any duty:

$$
P_{cond,leg} = I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)\cdot\frac{2}{3}
$$

This holds because the leg carries current 2/3 of the time, always through exactly one FET (excluding dead time).

### 1.2 Temperature dependence (self-consistent)

$$
R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds25}\,[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)]
$$

Coupled with the thermal path $T_j = T_{ref} + R_{\theta}\,P(T_j)$, for a FET with conduction loss $P = k I^2 R_{ds}(T_j)$:

$$
T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + R_\theta k I^2 R_{ds25}(1 - 25\alpha)}{1 - R_\theta k I^2 R_{ds25}\,\alpha}
$$

**Thermal runaway criterion.** A finite solution exists only if

$$
R_\theta \, k \, I^2 R_{ds25}\,\alpha < 1
$$

For the worst FET (LS, k ≈ 2/3 at D → 0; at full throttle k ≈ 1/3): R_θ k I² R_ds25 α = 30 × 0.333 × 6400 × 0.7e-3 × 0.0045 ≈ 0.20. This is stable, but only because R_θ = 30 K/W is a placeholder. The real limit is the board-level transient (§4), not single-FET runaway.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T_j = 125 °C

- R_ds(125) = 0.70 × (1 + 0.0045 × 100) = **1.015 mΩ**. Use **1.12 mΩ** to cover PCB via/lead and package spread at the margin.
- P_cond,leg = 80² × 1.12e-3 × 2/3 = 6400 × 1.12e-3 × 0.667 = **4.78 W per leg**.
- P_cond,motor = 3 × 4.78 = **14.3 W**.
- P_cond,total (4 motors) = **57.3 W**.

Worst single FET at full throttle (D ≈ 1): HS = (1/3) × 6400 × 1.12e-3 = **2.39 W**; LS similar. The losses are well balanced.

### 1.4 Shunt and copper

- Shunt: 80² × 0.25e-3 = **1.6 W per motor**, **6.4 W total**. This is significant; use 2512/3920 metal-strip parts or a lower R (0.1–0.15 mΩ) with a higher-gain amplifier.
- DC bus copper: I_bus,total = 320 A burst. Each 0.1 mΩ of path costs 10.2 W. Budget **≤ 0.15 mΩ total pad-to-FET path**: about **15 W**. This requires 4 × 2 oz (or 3 oz) inner planes and solder-bulged power traces.
