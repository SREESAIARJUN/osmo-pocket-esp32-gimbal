# STM32H743 AIO "Nano" Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Engineering Model (Job #64)

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

### Interpretation of "80A burst"

The term is ambiguous, so this document models both readings:

| Case | Meaning | Per-channel current |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 80 A per motor channel (320 A aggregate) | 80 A |
| **B** | 80 A aggregate battery current | 20 A |

**Architecture.** The H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller. It does not drive the FETs directly. Each ESC channel has its own MCU running AM32, which supports 48 kHz PWM and bidirectional DShot.
