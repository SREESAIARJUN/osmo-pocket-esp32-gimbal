**Architectural note:** ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The H743 cannot directly drive the 24 gates under ArduPilot. A "4-in-1 ESC flight controller" therefore means:

- the H743 handles flight control and emits DShot;
- four local ESC MCUs handle commutation.

This is also the only path to bidirectional DShot eRPM telemetry for ArduPilot's harmonic notch.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path in six-step synchronous PWM

In each 60° commutation sector, one phase leg is PWM'd and one leg is held low.

- **On-time (D):** current flows through HS(A) → motor → LS(B).
- **Off-time (1−D):** current freewheels through LS(A) (synchronous rectification) → motor → LS(B).

So **exactly two R_ds(on) are in series at all times**, independent of duty cycle. Dead time is treated separately in §2.

Per motor channel:

$$P_{cond} = 2\, I_{ph}^2\, R_{ds(on)}(T_j)$$

Per-FET distribution, over one electrical period with 120° conduction:

$$P_{HS} = \tfrac{1}{3} D\, I^2 R, \qquad P_{LS} = \tfrac{1}{3}\left[(1-D) + 1\right] I^2 R$$

Low-side FETs run hotter at partial throttle. Size thermal relief for the LS devices.

### 1.2 Temperature dependence

$$R_{ds(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha (T_j - 25)\right], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.0045\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

This gives about 1.45× at 125 °C, typical for 40 V trench FETs.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A

| T_j | R_ds(on) | P_cond per channel | ×4 channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 °C | 1.20 mΩ | 2·6400·1.2e-3 = **15.4 W** | 61.4 W |
| 100 °C | 1.61 mΩ | **20.6 W** | 82.4 W |
| 125 °C | 1.74 mΩ | **22.3 W** | 89.1 W |

Add the copper trace and plane loss. Assume about 0.3 mΩ of phase path plus bus planes per channel:

- 6400 × 0.3e-3 ≈ **1.9 W per channel**.

### 1.4 Electrothermal steady state and runaway criterion

With P₀ = 2I²R₂₅ and a thermal resistance R_θ (junction to ambient):

$$T_j = T_a + R_\theta P_0[1+\alpha(T_j-25)]$$

$$\Rightarrow\quad T_j = \frac{T_a + R_\theta P_0 (1 - 25\alpha)}{1 - \alpha R_\theta P_0}$$

**Runaway occurs when αR_θP₀ ≥ 1.** At P₀ = 15.4 W, the limit is R_θ < 14.4 K/W per channel. Steady state will be destroyed long before that limit for other reasons (§3), which is why 80 A can only be a burst rating.
