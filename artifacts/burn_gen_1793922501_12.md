## 0. Scope, Assumptions, and Design Risks

These are engineering findings that should be settled before layout starts.

| # | Finding | Consequence |
|---|---------|-------------|
| R1 | **Four channels at 80 A on a nano board cannot be sustained.** Section 3 shows about 100 W of dissipation against a few watts of sustainable cooling. | "80 A burst" must be specified as a **time-limited, per-channel rating**, about 1–2 s depending on preheat. The continuous rating will come out near 30 A per channel with prop-wash. |
| R2 | The H743 does not commutate the motors. The 4-in-1 uses **four ESC MCUs**: AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32 (BLHeli_32 is discontinued). | The H743 runs ArduPilot and drives four bidirectional DShot600 lines. The 48 kHz PWM is generated by the ESC MCUs. |
| R3 | A 20×20 mm form factor cannot hold an H743 (TFBGA100), 24 MOSFETs, four ESC MCUs, and gate drivers. | The baseline here is a **25.5×25.5 mm (20×20 M2 mounting) AIO** with an 8-layer board.
