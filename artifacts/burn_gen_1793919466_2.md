## 0. Architecture and Feasibility Findings

Four findings shape the rest of this document:

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot outputs bidirectional DShot (BDShot) only; it does not run 6-step or FOC commutation. The 4-in-1 ESC needs four dedicated ESC MCUs, assumed here to be AT32F421 running AM32. The H743's job is to issue DShot600 commands and decode the eRPM returned on the same wire.
2. **80 A per channel is a transient rating on a 20×20 mm board, not a continuous one.** Section 2 shows the board reaches its thermal limit in roughly 2–3 s at 4×80 A. The defensible continuous rating is about 20–25 A per channel with prop-wash cooling.
3. **The IMU should not share a PCB with about 60 W of ESC dissipation.** Use a stacked architecture: a 20×20 FC board on soft mounts above a 20×20 ESC board, joined by an 8-pin JST-SH or board-to-board connector.
4. **Bulk capacitance cannot fit on the board.** Section 3.4 requires more than 800 µF, so an external low-ESR electrolytic on the battery pigtail is mandatory.

```
[6S LiPo]──[ext 1000µF/35V low-ESR]──┬── ESC board (4× AM32 MCU + 4× 3-ph driver + 24 FETs)
                                      │        ▲ DShot600 BDShot ×4, VBAT sense, CURR sense
                                      │        │
                                      └── 8-pin link ──► FC board: STM32H743 + IMU + Baro + OSD + BEC
```
