## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80 A Burst)

### 1.1 Current Path in Six-Step Complementary PWM

During each 60° commutation sector, phase X (PWM) and phase Y (low-side held on) are active:

- **PWM ON (duration D·T_sw):** current flows through HS_X, the motor, then LS_Y.
- **PWM OFF (duration (1−D)·T_sw):** current flows through LS_X (synchronous rectification), the motor, then LS_Y. During the dead time, it flows through the LS_X body diode instead.
