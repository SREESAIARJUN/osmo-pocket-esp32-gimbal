# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC, 80 A Burst (Job #58)
## Architecture, Loss Model, DShot/DMA Allocation, Netlist and Thermal Budget

---

## 0. Feasibility Findings

Four findings shape every number in this document.

1. **The H743 does not commutate the motors.** ArduPilot is a flight-control stack and does not run motor commutation. The correct architecture is:
   - H743 (ArduPilot) sends bidirectional DShot600 to four ESC MCUs.
   - The ESC MCUs (STM32G071 or AT32F421 running AM32, or BLHeli_32-class firmware) do the 6-step or sinusoidal commutation at 48 kHz.

   The 48 kHz switching analysis applies to the ESC side.

2. **80 A per motor on a "nano" board is a burst rating only.** Section 3 shows that steady-state dissipation capability is about **5–8 W total** in prop-wash. Dissipation at 4 × 80 A is **about 90–165 W**, depending on MOSFET package. The burst is energy-limited to roughly **1–3 s**.

3. **A 20 × 20 mm footprint cannot hold 24 × DFN5x6 MOSFETs.** That would need about 720 mm² of placement area against 400 mm² per side. Two variants are therefore modeled:

   | Variant | Board | MOSFET package | Rds(on) @ 25 °C |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | A | 30.5 × 30.5 mm | DFN5x6 | ~1.0 mΩ |
   | B | 20 × 20 mm | DFN3.3x3.3 | ~2.0 mΩ |

4. **Some values are representative, not authoritative.** Specific MOSFET parameters, DMAMUX request IDs and ArduPilot internal tick constants are flagged as such. Validate them against your chosen part datasheets and the ArduPilot source/hwdef for your branch before tape-out.

## 1. System Assumptions

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Battery | 6S LiPo, V_bus,max = 25.2 V (design point), 22.2 V nominal |
| Burst phase current | I = 80 A per motor (DC-link-referred, 6-step) |
| PWM | 48 kHz, synchronous (complementary) 6-step, edge-aligned |
| MOSFET V_DS rating | 40 V minimum (1.59× margin at 25.2 V; ringing must be snubbed and layout-controlled) |
| Rds(on) temperature coefficient | α ≈ 0.006 /K, so Rds(125 °C) ≈ 1.6 × Rds(25 °C) |
| Gate driver | 3-phase bootstrap driver (FD6288Q-class), ~1 A source / 1.5 A sink |
| Edge time | t_r + t_f ≈ 40 ns total (representative at this drive strength) |
| Dead time | t_dt ≈ 100 ns per edge |
