# STM32H743 AIO Flight Controller + 4-in-1 ESC: Architecture & Analysis (Job #5)

## 0. Scope and Architectural Corrections

Two assumptions need to be fixed before the math.

1. **The H743 does not generate the 48 kHz bridge PWM.** In any ArduPilot-compatible 4-in-1, the STM32H743 runs ArduPilot and emits DShot600. Four dedicated ESC MCUs (e.g., AT32F421 running AM32) perform the 6-step commutation at 48 kHz. Switching losses are therefore a property of the ESC section, not the H743 timers.
2. **"Nano" and 80 A burst conflict.** A 20×20 mm board cannot physically host 24 power MOSFETs rated for 80 A. This design assumes a **30.5×30.5 mm mounting pattern on a ~40×40 mm, 8-layer, 2 oz board**. Section 3 shows that even this size is burst-limited to single-digit seconds when all four channels are loaded together.
