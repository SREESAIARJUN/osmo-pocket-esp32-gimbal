## 0. Architectural Ground Rules

These points affect how the model is partitioned.

1. **The H743 does not switch the MOSFETs.** In a 4-in-1 stack, the 48 kHz PWM comes from the ESC's own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32/Bluejay. Typical parts are the AT32F421, STM32G071, GD32E230, or EFM8BB51. The H743 only generates DShot600 frames and decodes the bidirectional eRPM replies. Loss math applies to the ESC board. DMA and timer math applies to the FC.
2. **"80 A burst" on a nano (20×20 mm) stack is a red flag.** Silicon can survive it for milliseconds to about a second. The PCB copper, FET package, and solder joints set the real limit. Section 3 quantifies this.
3. **No BOM was provided.** I parameterize everything and then evaluate a stated reference design. Replace the assumptions in §1 with datasheet values and the equations remain valid.

## 2. Loss Model (Per ESC Channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Topology

In 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary PWM, the current always flows through one high-side and one low-side device in series. During the on-time, the active-phase HS FET and the opposite-phase LS FET conduct. During the off-time, the active-phase LS FET (synchronous rectification) and the opposite-phase LS FET conduct. In both intervals, two channels each carry I. Duty cycle therefore cancels out of the channel total:

$$P_{cond} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)\,[D + (1-D)] \times 2 = 2 I^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

For FOC/sinusoidal drive with phase RMS current I_ph:

$$P_{cond} = 3\, I_{ph}^2 R_{DS}(T_j)$$

### 2.2 Electro-Thermal Coupling

R_DS(on) depends on junction temperature, and junction temperature depends on loss:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)], \qquad T_j = T_{ref} + P_{FET}\,Z_{th}$$

Solving for the self-consistent junction temperature:

$$T_j = \frac{T_{ref} + I^2 R_{25} Z_{th}(1-25\alpha)}{1 - \alpha I^2 R_{25} Z_{th}}$$

**Thermal-runaway criterion:** the solution exists only if α·I²·R₂₅·Z_th < 1.

At 80 A with Z_th = 1.5 K/W: 0.0045 × 6400 × 0.001 × 1.5 = 0.043. This is stable at the FET level. The board-level Z_th (§3) is what can approach the limit.

### 2.3 Switching Loss (Hard-Switched Leg)

Only the active HS/LS pair switches. Linear V–I overlap:

$$P_{sw} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r + t_f) f_{sw} = 0.5 \times 25.2 \times 80 \times 30\text{ ns} \times 48\text{ kHz} = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

### 2.4 Secondary Losses

| Loss | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| C_oss | ½·C_oss·V²·f_sw | 0.5×1 nF×635×48k = **0.015 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q_rr·V·f_sw | 50 nC×25.2×48k = **0.060 W** |
| Dead-time diode conduction | V_F·I·2t_dt·f_sw | 0.8×80×400 ns×48k = **1.23 W** |
| Gate drive (2 switching FETs) | 2·Q_g·V_GS·f_sw | 2×50 nC×10×48k = **0.048 W** (dissipated in driver and R_G) |

Dead-time loss is comparable to switching loss. Reducing dead time from 200 ns to 100 ns saves about 0.6 W per channel, provided shoot-through margin is verified with the actual Q_gd.

### 2.5 Channel Totals at 80 A

Steady-state hot case, T_j ≈ 125 °C, so R_DS = 1.45 mΩ:

| Component | W |
|---|---|
| Conduction: 2 × 6400 × 1.45 mΩ | 18.56 |
| Switching | 1.45 |
| Dead time | 1.23 |
| C_oss + Q_rr + gate | 0.12 |
| **Total per channel** | **≈ 21.4 W** |
| **All 4 channels at 80 A** | **≈ 85.6 W** |

At this point, the hottest FET dissipates roughly 9.3 W conduction plus about 1.3 W switching/diode, so **≈ 10.6 W**.

**Copper loss (frequently omitted, and significant):** a 2 oz (70 µm) trace, 3 mm wide and 10 mm long, measures about 0.8 mΩ. At 80 A that is about 5 W per phase path. Use 3–4 oz inner layers and stitched planes, or this term rivals FET loss.

**Efficiency check:** a 6S channel at 80 A delivers about 2 kW mechanical-equivalent input, so 21 W is about 1% loss. Electrically, this is fine. The problem is power density (§3).

## 3. Thermal Model

### 3.1 Junction-to-Case (Fast, Millisecond Scale)

$$\Delta T_{jc} = P_{FET}\,R_{jc} = 10.6 \times 1.5 \approx 16\ \text{K}$$

This is not the limiting term.

### 3.2 Board Heat Capacity (Burst Scale, About 1–5 s)

Model the 20×20 ESC as a lumped thermal mass:

- Mass of PCB (8-layer, heavy copper) plus 24 FETs plus solder ≈ 5 g
- c_eff ≈ 0.6 J/(g·K)
- C_th ≈ 3 J/K

Adiabatic temperature rise during a burst of duration t (convection is negligible over seconds):

$$\Delta T_{board}(t) \approx \frac{P_{total}\, t}{C_{th}} = \frac{85.6\,t}{3} \approx 28.5\ \text{K/s}$$

| Burst duration | ΔT_board | T_board (start 50 °C) | T_j (+16 K) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 s | 14 K | 64 °C | 80 °C ✅ |
| 1 s | 29 K | 79 °C | 95 °C ✅ |
| 2 s | 57 K | 107 °C | 123 °C ⚠️ |
| 3 s | 86 K | 136 °C | 152 °C ❌ |

### 3.3 Continuous Rating (Cooling-Limited)

Under propwash, the stack has roughly h ≈ 60 W/(m²·K), about 2 × 400 mm² exposed area, and about 20 cm² effective area including wires. That gives:

$$R_{th,BA} \approx \frac{1}{h A} \approx 8\ \text{K/W}$$

For ΔT = 60 K, the allowable dissipation is P ≈ 7.5 W total, or about 1.9 W per channel. Solving 2I²R ≈ 1.6 W (after switching losses):

$$I_{cont} \approx \sqrt{\frac{1.6}{2 \times 1.3\text{ m}\Omega}} \approx \mathbf{25\ A\ per\ channel}$$

### 3.4 Transient Model for Simulation (Foster Network)

$$Z_{th}(t) = \sum_{i} R_i\left(1 - e^{-t/\tau_i}\right)$$

| Stage | R_i (K/W) | τ_i | Physical meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Die | 0.3 | 0.5 ms | silicon |
| Package | 1.2 | 8 ms |
