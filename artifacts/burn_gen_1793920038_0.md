## 2. Field-Oriented Control (Innermost Loop)

### 2.1 Electrical angle

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{offset}}) \bmod 2\pi$$

Latency compensation for the delay from PWM update to measurement (about 1.5 T_s):

$$\theta_e^{comp} = \theta_e + 1.5\,T_s\,\omega_e$$

### 2.2 Clarke and Park transforms

The Clarke transform is power-variant and assumes $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$:

$$i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{i_a + 2 i_b}{\sqrt3}$$

The Park transform:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}$$

### 2.3 Plant model in the dq frame

$$v_d = R i_d + L_d \dot i_d - \omega_e L_q i_q$$
$$v_q = R i_q + L_q \dot i_q + \omega_e (L_d i_d + \psi_m)$$

### 2.4 Current PI with decoupling

**Gain selection.** Placing the PI zero on the electrical pole $R/L$ gives a closed loop of $\omega_c/(s+\omega_c)$:

$$K_p = L\,\omega_c,\qquad K_i = R\,\omega_c,\qquad \omega_c = 2\pi f_{c}$$

With $f_c \approx f_{PWM}/20 \approx 1.2$ kHz, this gives $K_p ≈ 15$ V/A and $K_i ≈ 37\,700$ V/(A·s).

**Discrete form** (backward Euler, with back-calculation anti-windup):

$$u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\qquad v_k = \text{sat}(u_k)$$
$$I_{k+1} = I_k + K_i T_s e_k + K_{aw}(v_k - u_k),\qquad K_{aw} = \frac{K_i}{K_p}T_s$$

**Feed-forward decoupling:**

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d - \omega_e L_q i_q,\qquad v_q^* = \text{PI}_q + \omega_e(L_d i_d + \psi_m)$$

**Voltage-circle limit.** $v_d$ has priority (it holds $i_d=0$):

$$|v_d| \le V_{max},\qquad |v_q| \le \sqrt{V_{max}^2 - v_d^2},\qquad V_{max} = \frac{V_{bus}}{\sqrt3}\cdot 0.95$$
