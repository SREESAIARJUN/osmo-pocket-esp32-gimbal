### Architecture

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. It is the flight controller. Each ESC channel runs its own MCU, such as an AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32/BLHeli_32. The signal and power chain is:

```
[STM32H743 FC] --4x DShot600 bidir--> [4x ESC MCU] --> [3-ph gate driver] --> [6 MOSFETs] --> motor
```

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent R_DS(on)

$$R_{DS(on)}(T_j) = R_{25}\left[1 + \alpha\,(T_j - 25)\right], \qquad \alpha \approx 0.005\ \text{K}^{-1}$$

At T_j = 125 °C:

$$R_{DS(on)} = 1.5\ \text{mΩ} \times 1.5 = 2.25\ \text{mΩ}$$

This creates a positive feedback loop: higher losses raise T_j, which raises R_DS(on), which raises losses. The coupled system is solved in §3.3.
