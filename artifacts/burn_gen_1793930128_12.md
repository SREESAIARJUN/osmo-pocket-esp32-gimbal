# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #26): Electrothermal, Switching and BDShot600 DMA Design

## 0. Scope, Architecture and Assumptions

I don't have prior project records for Job #26, so this document states every assumption explicitly. Substitute your actual BOM values into the equations. Results marked **[A]** depend on an assumed parameter.

### Architecture (20×20 mm stack)

- **FC board:** STM32H743VIT6 running ArduPilot. It generates bi-directional DShot600 on 4 timer channels.
- **ESC board:** 4 independent ESC MCUs, each driving a 3-phase bridge at 48 kHz.
  - MCU: AT32F421 or STM32G071, running AM32.
  - Gate driver: one 3-phase driver per motor, e.g. FD6288Q.
  - Power stage: 6 N-MOSFETs per motor, 24 total.

ArduPilot does not commutate motors. The H743 cannot directly run the bridges under ArduPilot, so the split above is mandatory.

The realistic continuous rating is ~20–25 A per motor. Treat any "80 A" label as a burst rating backed by firmware current limiting.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Current path

In 6-step commutation with synchronous rectification, the motor current always flows through exactly two conducting channels:
- the high-side FET of the "+" phase, or its complementary low-side FET during PWM off-time;
- the low-side FET of the "−" phase.

So, independent of duty cycle:

$$P_{cond,motor} = 2\,I_{ph}^2\,R_{DS}(T_j) + I_{ph}^2 R_{Cu}$$

### 1.2 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{DS,25}\left[1+\alpha\,(T_j-25)\right]$$

At T_j = 110 °C: R_DS = 1.2 mΩ × 1.51 = **1.81 mΩ**

### 1.3 Copper path [A]

Assume phase pad, bus plane, via arrays and solder joints total R_Cu ≈ 0.25 mΩ per motor path.
