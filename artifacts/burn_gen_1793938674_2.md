# STM32H743 4-in-1 ESC Nano Flight Controller: Architecture, Loss Model, DShot DMA Plan and Netlists

**Scope notes.** All MOSFET, PCB and thermal parameters below are representative assumptions, flagged as such. Swap in datasheet values for your selected parts before release. The netlists are reference-level. They need ERC/DRC in your EDA tool and bench validation before fab.

---

## 0. Architecture Decision

ArduPilot does not run BLDC commutation on the H743. A "4-in-1 ESC + FC" board is therefore two subsystems on one PCB stack:

| Block | Device | Firmware |
|---|---|---|
| Flight controller | STM32H743VIH6 (480 MHz, DMAMUX) | ArduPilot (ChibiOS) |
| ESC ×4 | 4× small Cortex-M0+/M4 ESC MCUs (STM32G071 / AT32F421 class) | AM32 or BLHeli_32-compatible |
| Link | Bidirectional DShot600, one wire per motor | — |

**Form-factor reality check.** An 80 A burst rating per channel is physically plausible only as a short burst, as section 3 shows. On a 20×20 mm "nano" board, continuous current is thermally limited to roughly 25–30 A per channel even with prop wash. If 80 A matters beyond ~1–2 s, move to 30.5×30.5 mm or add a heatsink plate. The model below lets you re-evaluate for either footprint.

## 2. Loss Model (per ESC channel)

### 2.1 Conduction Loss

Temperature-corrected on-resistance:

$$R_{DS}(T_j) = R_{25}\,[1+\alpha (T_j-25)]$$

At T_j = 125 °C: R = 1.0 × (1 + 0.5) = **1.5 mΩ**.

In 6-step synchronous PWM, motor current always flows through exactly two FETs: the PWM leg's HS (or its complementary LS during the off-time) plus the static LS of the return phase.

$$P_{cond,dev} = I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = 80^2 \times 1.5\,\text{m}\Omega = \mathbf{9.6\ W}$$

$$P_{cond,ch} = 2\,I^2 R_{DS}(T_j) = \mathbf{19.2\ W}$$

Averaged over a commutation cycle, each phase leg conducts 2/3 of the time. Per-device average in steady rotation is about 9.6 × (2/3) × ½ ≈ 3.2 W, shared between HS and LS by duty. Thermal *peak* design must still use the 9.6 W instantaneous value, because the electrical period at low eRPM can exceed the die's thermal time constant (~1–10 ms).

**Copper and shunt add-ons:**
- Phase/bus trace (2 oz, ~0.5 mΩ effective path): 80² × 0.5 mΩ = 3.2 W
- Current shunt, if fitted (0.25 mΩ): 1.6 W

### 2.2 Switching Loss (hard-switched edge, PWM leg)

Transition times:

$$t_r \approx t_f \approx \frac{Q_{sw}}{I_g} = 12\ \text{ns}\ \ (\text{use }15\ \text{ns with layout inductance})$$

V–I overlap:

$$P_{VI} = \tfrac12 V_{bus} I (t_r+t_f) f_{sw} = \tfrac12(25.2)(80)(30\,\text{ns})(48\,\text{kHz}) = \mathbf{1.45\ W}$$

C_oss charge/discharge:

$$P_{oss} = C_{oss,er} V_{bus}^2 f_{sw} = 1\text{nF}\times 635 \times 48\text{k} = 0.03\ \text{W}$$

Reverse recovery:

$$P_{rr} = Q_{rr} V_{bus} f_{sw} = 30\text{nC}\times25.2\times48\text{k} = 0.036\ \text{W}$$

Dead-time body-diode conduction (2 edges per period):

$$P_{dt} = V_f I \,(2t_d)\, f_{sw} = 0.8\times80\times200\text{ns}\times48\text{k} = \mathbf{0.61\ W}$$

Gate drive (dissipated in driver/gate resistors, 2 switching FETs):

$$P_g = 2\,Q_g V_{GS} f_{sw} = 2\times30\text{nC}\times10\times48\text{k} = 0.029\ \text{W}$$

Switching subtotal: **≈ 2.15 W per channel.**

**Dead-time sensitivity.** Dead time is the dominant tunable term. Cutting t_d from 100 ns to 50 ns saves ~0.3 W per channel, but only if the driver propagation skew is less than 20 ns.

### 2.3 Loss Budget (all four motors at 80 A simultaneously)

| Term | Per channel | ×4 |
|---|---|---|
| FET conduction (125 °C) | 19.2 W | 76.8 W |
| Switching + dead-time + Q_rr + C_oss | 2.15 W | 8.6 W |
| Copper + shunt | ~4.8 W | 19.2 W |
| Gate drive / LDO / ESC MCU | ~0.15 W | 0.6 W |
| H743 + IMU + regulators | — | ~1.0 W |
| **Total** | **~26.3 W** | **~106 W** |

Efficiency at this point: P_out ≈ 4 × 25.2 V × 80 A × D. Losses are ~5% of input at D ≈ 0.6.
