# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC (Job #28): Reference Design Model

## 0. Scope and Assumptions

**Architecture correction:** On a 4-in-1 stack, the **STM32H743 runs ArduPilot as the flight controller.** Each ESC channel has its own small MCU (assumed here: 4× AT32F421 running AM32) that performs commutation at 48 kHz. The H743 never drives gates directly. It generates bidirectional DShot600 and decodes eRPM telemetry.

| Parameter | Assumed value |
|---|---|
| Form factor | 20×20 mm (M2), FC and ESC on separate boards |
| Bus | 6S, V<sub>bus,max</sub> = 25.2 V |
| f<sub>sw</sub> | 48 kHz, complementary (synchronous) PWM, 6-step |
| MOSFET (representative 40 V, 3.3×3.3 DFN) | R<sub>ds,on</sub> = 1.3 mΩ @ 25 °C / 10 V; Q<sub>g</sub> = 30 nC; Q<sub>gs2</sub>+Q<sub>gd</sub> = 9 nC; Q<sub>rr</sub> = 40 nC; Q<sub>oss</sub> = 25 nC; V<sub>plateau</sub> = 3 V; R<sub>g,int</sub> = 1.5 Ω |
| Gate driver | FD6288Q-class, 10 V drive, R<sub>g,ext</sub> = 10 Ω |
| Burst current | 80 A per phase current (motor current) |
| Dead time | 100 ns per edge |

**Substitute your actual FET datasheet values before sign-off.** The structure of the model holds, but the numbers are only as good as the inputs.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

$$R_{ds}(T_j) = R_{ds,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)],\quad \alpha \approx 0.006\ /\text{K}$$

$$R_{ds}(125^\circ C) = 1.3\,\text{mΩ}\times1.6 = 2.08\ \text{mΩ}$$

### 1.2 Current paths in 6-step synchronous PWM

During each 60° sector, two phases carry current:

- **PWM leg:** high-side conducts for duty D, and the complementary low-side conducts for (1−D).
- **Return leg:** the low-side conducts for 100% of the sector.

The total conduction loss is independent of D (ignoring ripple):

$$P_{cond} = I^2R_{ds}[D + (1-D)] + I^2R_{ds} = 2I^2R_{ds}$$

With ripple included, replace I² with I<sub>rms</sub>² = I² + ΔI²/12. This is typically a 1–3% correction at 48 kHz with motor inductance ≥ 10 µH.

### 1.3 Numbers at 80 A, T<sub>j</sub> = 125 °C

$$P_{cond} = 2(80)^2(2.08\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{26.6\ W\ per\ ESC\ channel}$$

### 1.4 Parasitic copper and sense loss

Assume 0.3 mΩ for the trace, via, and pad path plus a 0.25 mΩ shunt:

$$P_{Cu} = 80^2 \times 0.55\,\text{mΩ} = \mathbf{3.5\ W}$$

### 1.5 Worst single FET

The return low-side carries current 100% of its sector, i.e. 1/3 of the electrical cycle:

$$P_{FET,avg} = 13.3\,\text{W}\times\tfrac{1}{3} \approx 4.4\,\text{W}$$

However, the instantaneous 13.3 W over the sector dwell matters for Z<sub>th</sub> at low eRPM (see §3.3).

## 2. Switching Losses at 48 kHz

Only the PWM leg switches hard: one hard turn-on and one hard turn-off per period.

### 2.1 Gate-current-limited transition times

$$R_{g,tot} = 10 + 1.5 + R_{drv}(\approx0.5) = 12\ \Omega$$

| Edge | Gate current | Transition time |
|---|---|---|
| Turn-on | I<sub>g,on</sub> = (10 − 3)/12 = 0.58 A | t<sub>on</sub> = Q<sub>sw</sub>/I<sub>g,on</sub> = 9 nC/0.58 A = **15.4 ns** |
| Turn-off | I<sub>g,off</sub> = 3/12 = 0.25 A | t<sub>off</sub> = 9 nC/0.25 A = **36 ns** |

### 2.2 Loss components (per channel, 80 A, 25.2 V)

| Mechanism | Equation | Value |
|---|---|---|
| V-I overlap | ½·V·I·(t<sub>on</sub>+t<sub>off</sub>)·f<sub>sw</sub> = ½·25.2·80·51.4n·48k | **2.49 W** |
| Body-diode dead time | V<sub>f</sub>·I·2t<sub>dt</sub>·f<sub>sw</sub> = 0.8·80·200n·48k | **0.61 W** |
| Reverse recovery | Q<sub>rr</sub>·V·f<sub>sw</sub> = 40n·25.2·48k | 0.048 W |
| C<sub>oss</sub> | ½·Q<sub>oss</sub>·V·f<sub>sw</sub> | 0.015 W |
| Gate drive (in driver, not FET) | Q<sub>g</sub>·V<sub>drv</sub>·f<sub>sw</sub>·2 | 0.029 W |
| **Switching subtotal** | | **≈ 3.2 W** |

### 2.3 Design levers

- **Asymmetric turn-off:** a diode plus 2.2 Ω in parallel with R<sub>g,ext</sub> brings t<sub>off</sub> to about 10 ns and saves ~1.3 W per channel. Check the resulting dV/dt against driver V<sub>S</sub> negative-spike limits.
- **Dead time:** reduce it to 50 ns only after measuring shoot-through margin. That saves 0.3 W.
