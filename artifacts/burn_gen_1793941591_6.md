## 0. Architectural Decisions to Settle First

Two requirements in the brief conflict with physics or with standard practice. They need resolving before any math.

| Issue | Problem | Resolution in this baseline |
|---|---|---|
| **H743 cannot commutate motors directly in ArduPilot** | ArduPilot has no on-FC sensorless 6-step/FOC motor driver. An H743 driving 12 gate signals would also exhaust its advanced timers and starve the DShot budget. | **Split architecture.** The H743 runs ArduPilot and emits bidirectional DShot600. Four dedicated ESC MCUs (AT32F421K8U7, running AM32) do 48 kHz commutation. |
| **"Nano" (20×20 mm) at 80A burst** | 12 power MOSFETs, 4 gate drivers, 4 MCUs, shunts and bulk caps do not fit on 20×20 mm at an 80A rating. | **30.5×30.5 mm footprint, 8-layer, 2 oz on all layers** (detailed in §3.4). This is still the smallest viable class. |
| **"80A burst" undefined** | It is unclear whether 80A is per channel or all four simultaneously. Four channels at once means 320A from the battery. | Both cases are modeled. **Per-channel burst is the design case.** The all-channel case gives the ~2.6 s limit in §3.5. |
