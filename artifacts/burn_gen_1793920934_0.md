## 1. FOC Current Loop (20 kHz)

### 1.1 Electrical angle

$$
\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \bmod 2\pi
$$

- θ_m is the encoder mechanical angle.
- θ_off is found at commissioning: apply a d-axis voltage at θ_e = 0, let the rotor settle, and read the encoder.

### 1.2 Clarke transform (amplitude-invariant, 3 shunts, $i_a+i_b+i_c=0$)

$$
i_\alpha = i_a,\qquad i_\beta = \frac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2 i_b)
$$

### 1.3 Park transform

$$
\begin{bmatrix} i_d\\ i_q\end{bmatrix}=
\begin{bmatrix}\cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e\\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha\\ i_\beta\end{bmatrix}
$$

### 1.4 Plant in the dq frame

$$
v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q,\qquad
v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e L i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

with $\omega_e = p\,\omega_m$.

### 1.5 PI with pole-zero cancellation and decoupling

Choose the PI zero to cancel the electrical pole $R/L$. The closed loop then becomes first order with bandwidth $\omega_{c,i}$:

$$
K_{p,i} = L\,\omega_{c,i},\qquad K_{i,i} = R\,\omega_{c,i}
$$

Control law, including feedforward of the decoupling and back-EMF terms:

$$
v_d^* = K_{p,i}e_d + K_{i,i}\!\int e_d\,dt - \omega_e L\, i_q
$$

$$
v_q^* = K_{p,i}e_q + K_{i,i}\!\int e_q\,dt + \omega_e L\, i_d + \omega_e\lambda_m
$$

The setpoints are $i_d^* = 0$ (surface PMSM) and $i_q^*$ from the velocity loop.

### 1.6 Voltage limiting

The linear SVPWM region is a circle of radius $V_{dc}/\sqrt3$. Give d priority:

$$
v_d^{sat} = \text{clamp}\!\left(v_d^*,\ \pm V_{\max}\right),\qquad
v_q^{sat} = \text{clamp}\!\left(v_q^*,\ \pm\sqrt{V_{\max}^2 - (v_d^{sat})^2}\right)
$$

### 1.7 Discrete PI with back-calculation anti-windup

Sample time $T_s$ and saturation function sat(·):

$$
u_k = K_p e_k + I_k,\qquad
I_{k+1} = I_k + T_s\big[K_i e_k + K_{aw}(\text{sat}(u_k)-u_k)\big]
$$

A good default is $K_{aw} = K_i/K_p$ (back-calculation time constant equal to the integral time).

### 1.8 Inverse Park and SVPWM (min-max zero-sequence injection)

$$
v_\alpha = v_d\cos\theta_e - v_q\sin\theta_e,\qquad v_\beta = v_d\sin\theta_e + v_q\cos\theta_e
$$

Phase voltages:

$$
v_a = v_\alpha,\quad v_b = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta,\quad v_c = -\tfrac12 v_\alpha - \tfrac{\sqrt3}{2}v_\beta
$$
