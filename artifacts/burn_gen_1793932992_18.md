# STM32H743 Nano FC + 4-in-1 ESC: Power-Stage and DShot Design Model

**Scope.** This document derives the conduction, switching and thermal models at 80 A burst and 48 kHz PWM. It also covers bidirectional DShot600 timer and DMA allocation under ArduPilot, and gives representative netlists.

**Assumptions to replace with your BOM data.** No component datasheets were supplied. I used a parameterized model filled with representative values for a 40 V, ~1 mΩ, 3.3×3.3 mm MOSFET. Every equation is closed-form, so you can substitute your actual parts.

**Key findings:**
1. **Simultaneous 80 A on all four motors is not thermally credible on a nano-format board.** The board heats at about 23 K/s, which gives roughly 3 s to thermal limit.
2. **With fast gate drive and a 2 nH commutation loop, drain overshoot exceeds the rating of a 40 V MOSFET.** This is the most likely field-failure mode.

## 1. System Architecture and Operating Point

```
6S LiPo (25.2 V max, 22.2 V nom)
   │
   ├── Bulk caps + TVS ── 3-phase bridges ×4 (24 MOSFETs)
   │                          │
   │                ESC MCUs ×4 (AM32 / BLHeli_32 class): commutation, 48 kHz PWM
   │                          │  ▲ bidirectional DShot600 (eRPM telemetry)
   │                          ▼  │
   └── 5V/10V regulators ── STM32H743 (ArduPilot): attitude loop, DShot generation, RPM notch filters
```

**Division of responsibility.** The H743 does not switch the bridges. Each ESC MCU runs 6-step trapezoidal commutation with complementary (synchronous) PWM at 48 kHz. The H743 sends DShot commands to the ESC MCUs and receives eRPM telemetry back.
