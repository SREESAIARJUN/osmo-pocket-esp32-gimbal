## 0. Executive Findings

1. **An 80A burst on all four channels at 6S is about 110 W of heat in a roughly 4 J/K board.** Without a heatsink this is a **burst-limited design (about 2–3 s from 40 °C)**, not a continuous rating.
2. **The steady state is thermally unstable at 80A/ch.** The Rds(on) temperature feedback exceeds the board's ability to shed heat (loop gain about 5.6, more than 1; derived in §3.4). The ESC firmware must enforce current and temperature limiting.
3. **Switching loss at 48 kHz is minor (about 2.6 W/ch).** Conduction loss dominates (about 22–25 W/ch).
4. **Four channels at 80A means 320A from the battery.** An XT60 and 12 AWG lead cannot support this. The "80A" figure must be per-channel non-simultaneous, or a sub-second burst.
5. **The H743 shares the board with the power stage (AIO).** Board temperatures above 85–105 °C exceed STM32H743xI industrial limits (Tj max 125 °C) and will produce IMU thermal drift. This calls for thermal isolation slots and a sensor/MCU island.
6. **DShot600 bi-dir on the H743 is DMA-feasible.** It needs 6 DMA streams for 4 motors on 2 timers, out of 16 available through DMAMUX1.
