# STM32H743 Nano FC + 80A 4-in-1 ESC (Job #59): Power-Stage and DShot Design Model

## 0. Architecture and Stated Assumptions

The STM32H743 does not commutate the motors. In a 4-in-1 stack, it acts as the flight controller and sends bidirectional DShot600. Each ESC channel has its own MCU running AM32 or BLHeli_32, and that MCU generates the 48 kHz complementary PWM. The analysis is split the same way.

> **Feasibility flag:** "80A on a nano board" is only physically valid as a short burst. Section 3 shows that the steady-state thermal limit is roughly an order of magnitude lower. If "nano" means a 20×20 mm mounting pattern, 80A burst is not credible with 5×6 MOSFETs. Use a 30.5×30.5 mm pattern at minimum.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model (80A Burst)

### 1.1 Current path in 6-step with synchronous rectification
During any 60° commutation sector, two phases conduct:

- **PWM leg:** the high-side (HS) device conducts for fraction D. The low-side (LS) device of the same leg conducts for (1−D) through its channel, not its body diode.
- **Return leg:** the LS device conducts continuously for the whole sector.

As a result, exactly two MOSFET channels are in the current path at every instant, apart from dead time.

### 1.2 Equations

R_DS(on) at temperature:

$$R_{hot} = R_{DS,25}\,[1+\alpha(T_j-25)] \approx 1.0\,\text{m}\Omega \times 1.55 = 1.55\,\text{m}\Omega$$

Conduction loss for the whole bridge (one ESC channel):

$$P_{cond,bridge} = 2\,I^2 R_{hot} = 2(80)^2(1.55\times10^{-3}) = \mathbf{19.84\ W}$$

Per-device loss over a full electrical cycle. Each device spends 1/3 of the cycle as "PWM-leg" and 1/3 as "return-leg", in alternating roles depending on the commutation scheme. The worst-case device is a LS device that serves as both the return path and the freewheel path:

$$P_{LS,worst} = I^2R_{hot}\left[\tfrac{1}{3} + \tfrac{1-D}{3}\right]$$

At D = 0.5:

$$P_{LS,worst} = 6400 \times 1.55\text{e-}3 \times 0.5 = \mathbf{4.96\ W}$$

The average across all 6 devices is 19.84 / 6 = 3.31 W.

### 1.3 Copper and shunt conduction
- **Phase and bus copper:** 2 oz is about 0.245 mΩ/sq. With roughly 3 squares in the per-phase path, paralleled over 4 layers, R ≈ 0.18 mΩ.
  - P = 2 × 80² × 0.18 mΩ ≈ **2.3 W per channel**
- **Current shunt:** 0.1 mΩ in the common return.
