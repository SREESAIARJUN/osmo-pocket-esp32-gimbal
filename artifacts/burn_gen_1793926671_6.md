## 1. Field-Oriented Control

### 1.1 Electrical angle and encoder tracking PLL

The electrical angle is derived from the mechanical encoder angle:

$$\theta_e = p\,(\theta_m - \theta_{\text{off}}) \pmod{2\pi}$$

A type-II tracking PLL produces a low-noise velocity estimate without differentiating the encoder signal:

$$\varepsilon = \operatorname{wrap}_{\pi}(\theta_{\text{meas}} - \hat\theta), \qquad \hat\omega \mathrel{+}= K_{i}\,\varepsilon\,T_s, \qquad \hat\theta \mathrel{+}= (\hat\omega + K_{p}\,\varepsilon)\,T_s$$

with gains set from the desired PLL natural frequency ω_n and damping ζ:

$$K_p = 2\zeta\omega_n, \qquad K_i = \omega_n^2, \qquad \zeta = 0.707, \qquad \omega_n \approx 2\pi \cdot 300\ \text{Hz}$$

### 1.2 Clarke and Park transforms

Phase currents are measured with two shunts (i_c is inferred), then transformed to the stationary αβ frame:

$$i_\alpha = i_a, \qquad i_\beta = \tfrac{1}{\sqrt3}(i_a + 2i_b)$$

They are then rotated into the rotor-aligned dq frame:

$$\begin{bmatrix} i_d \\ i_q \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta_e & \sin\theta_e \\ -\sin\theta_e & \cos\theta_e \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} i_\alpha \\ i_\beta \end{bmatrix}$$

### 1.3 PMSM model and current PI with decoupling

The motor in dq coordinates, with λ the permanent-magnet flux linkage and ω_e the electrical speed:

$$v_d = R i_d + L\dot i_d - \omega_e L i_q, \qquad v_q = R i_q + L\dot i_q + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda)$$

The current controllers cancel the cross-coupling and back-EMF terms explicitly:

$$v_d^* = \text{PI}_d(i_d^* - i_d) - \omega_e L i_q, \qquad v_q^* = \text{PI}_q(i_q^* - i_q) + \omega_e (L i_d + \lambda)$$
