**Scope:** 80 A burst thermal model, 48 kHz switching losses, ArduPilot bidirectional DShot600 timer/DMA plan, and reference netlists.

**Status:** Reference derivation. Component values are representative of the class and are not tied to a specific BOM. Re-run every equation with your selected MOSFET datasheet values before layout freeze.

## 0. Architecture Clarification and Assumptions

In this design the STM32H743 is the **flight controller**. It does not commutate motors. Each ESC channel has its own small MCU (e.g., AT32F421 or STM32G071 running AM32), because AM32/BLHeli_32 firmware does not run on the H743. The H743 sends DShot600 to the four ESC MCUs and receives eRPM telemetry back on the same wire.

## 1. Conduction Loss Model

### 1.1 Temperature-dependent on-resistance

R_DS(on)(T_j) = R_25 · [1 + α (T_j − 25)], with α ≈ 0.0045 K⁻¹ (typical for 40 V trench parts).

At T_j = 125 °C:

R_DS(on) = 1.1 mΩ × (1 + 0.0045 × 100) = **1.6 mΩ**

This is the conservative design value. Section 4.4 checks it for consistency.
